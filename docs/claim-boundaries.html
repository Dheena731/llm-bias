Claim Boundaries

The package is strong enough for a bounded journal review artifact, not for unconstrained claims about causal training mechanisms or broad cross-modal homogenization.

Preserve

  • Presentation priors are measurable and condition-sensitive.
  • Topic, model, route label, and intervention condition matter.
  • Explicit plain-output conditions suppress Markdown and emoji in Exp8.
  • Study 2 is a negative or limited purple/pink finding.
  • Study 5 is bounded cross-modal extension evidence.

Avoid

  • Do not claim RLHF or post-training causes the observed defaults.
  • Do not claim route labels prove training stage.
  • Do not claim models broadly prefer purple or pink aesthetics.
  • Do not claim generated UI or image artifacts are unusually homogeneous.
  • Do not describe Exp8 as a full-cost repeated run.

Decision Rules

Finding Resolution
Causal wording around RLHF, post-training, base, instruct, or route stage. Rewrite to descriptive route-label language or remove.
Broad color-bias framing. Restore negative or limited purple/pink language and blue-dominant evidence wording.
Strong cross-modal similarity or homogenization claim. Downgrade to calibration evidence or add balanced baselines before claiming.
Camera-ready metadata requested during anonymous review. Track separately; do not modify the anonymous package unless review mode is ending.
Use the response log to record every required claim-boundary edit.
