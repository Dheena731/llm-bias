Human Review Workflow

The review pass is designed to catch overclaims, stitched-manuscript seams, reproducibility drift, and camera-ready confusion before the package leaves the repository.

Reviewer Inputs

  • TIST_EXTERNAL_REVIEW_HANDOFF.md
  • TIST_EXTERNAL_REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md
  • TIST_EXTERNAL_REVIEW_WORKSHEET.md
  • TIST_EXTERNAL_REVIEW_RESPONSE_LOG.md
  • paper/tist2027_presentation_priors.tex
  • paper/tist2027_journal_package_manifest.md

Step 1

Verify the archive SHA and run the reproduction commands listed in the checklist.

Step 2

Read the abstract, introduction, study overview, Study 4, Study 5, limitations, and artifact availability sections.

Step 3

Complete one copy of TIST_EXTERNAL_REVIEW_WORKSHEET.md with pass/fail marks and concrete locations.

Step 4

Log blocker and major findings in TIST_EXTERNAL_REVIEW_RESPONSE_LOG.md and resolve them before submission.

Review Priorities

Priority Question Failure Example
Claim boundary Does the paper avoid RLHF or route-stage causal claims? Any sentence saying route labels prove training stage.
Journal spine Do Studies 1-5 read as one synthesis? Conference-draft framing that contradicts the TIST thesis.
Baselines Are UI/image baselines framed as calibration-only? Broad claims that generated artifacts are unusually homogeneous.
Reproducibility Do commands, paths, hashes, and row counts line up? Archive SHA drift, missing artifact paths, or failing verifier commands.
