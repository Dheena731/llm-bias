Subject: Temporary Road Closure Notice: Marigold Avenue (Between 5th and 6th Streets)

Dear Residents and Commuters,

We are writing to inform you of a temporary road closure on Marigold Avenue, situated between 5th and 6th Streets. This closure will be in effect starting Friday, March 10th, at 8:00 AM, and will last for approximately two weeks, concluding on Friday, March 24th, at 5:00 PM.

The closure is a result of essential maintenance work required on the infrastructure beneath the road, which will ensure the safety and longevity of our transportation system. Crews will be performing critical repairs to upgrade aging water pipes and sewer lines, minimizing the risk of future disruptions and maintaining the structural integrity of our roadways.

We understand the inconvenience this may cause and apologize for any detour or delays. To assist with your travel plans, we have outlined several alternative routes and posted detailed maps on our city website (www.cityof[YourCity].com) under "Road Closures."

Bus service along Marigold Avenue will be temporarily rerouted to alternate routes. We are also providing free shuttle services from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM during the closure days. Please check back for updates on service changes and timing.

For any questions or concerns, please contact our Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567 during regular office hours.

Thank you for your understanding and cooperation.
