Dear Community Members,

We hope this message finds you well. In preparation for the upcoming election, we would like to take this opportunity to inform you about the polling station locations in our area.

Polling stations will open at 9:00 AM on the day of the election. Please ensure you arrive at your assigned polling station by 8:30 AM to allow for registration and check-in procedures.

Here are the polling station locations for our district:

1. Green Park Community Center
2. East Side Elementary School
3. Westside Community Center
4. Memorial Library

You can also find a list of polling stations and additional information on the official election website.

We encourage everyone to exercise their right to vote and participate in the democratic process. Your participation is crucial for shaping the future of our community.

Thank you for your attention and cooperation. We look forward to seeing you at the polls.

Sincerely,
The Election Committee
