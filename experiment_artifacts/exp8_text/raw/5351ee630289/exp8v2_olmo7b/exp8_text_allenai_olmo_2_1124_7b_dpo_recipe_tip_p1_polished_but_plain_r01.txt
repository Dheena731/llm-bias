To streamline your weeknight dinner prep, consider chop and cook your ingredients while listening to your favorite podcast or music. This multitasking setup not only saves time but also ensures you have everything ready to throw into the pot or pan the moment inspiration strikes. Simply dice vegetables, slice meats, or marinate proteins while you’re in the midst of other household activities. Having your mise en place ready will minimize downtime between cooking steps and help you create a delicious meal without the last-minute rush.
