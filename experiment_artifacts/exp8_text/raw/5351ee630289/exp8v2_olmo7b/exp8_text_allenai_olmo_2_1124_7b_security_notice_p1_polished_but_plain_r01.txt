Prose Writing Example

Here's a sample "organized prose" prompt:

Create five numbered lists that contain five items.

Write a short story about a whale.

Here's the type of prose work you might produce with that prompt:

A Whale story

  1. A baby whale grows up on an icy iceberg in the middle of the ocean.
  2. A whale learns to hunt by eating seaweed.
  3. The whale grows up on the iceberg.
  4. The whale is startled when a kraken attacks a ship.
  5. The kraken eats the baby whale.
