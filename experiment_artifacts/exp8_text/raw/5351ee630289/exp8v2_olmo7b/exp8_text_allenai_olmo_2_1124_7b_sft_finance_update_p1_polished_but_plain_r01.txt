Our latest monthly spending summary indicates that expenses have risen 2.5% compared to the previous month. The most significant increase was in transportation costs, up 7% due to higher fuel prices. Utilities also saw a 5% increase, attributed to a 10% rise in electricity and gas costs. In contrast, spending on groceries decreased by 3%, which was unexpected; however, the decrease is likely a result of fewer household purchases due to a decline in overall consumer confidence.

On the positive side, savings increased by 8%, thanks to a 4% rise in income, driven by significant bonuses in the tech sector. This income growth has managed to offset some of the increased spending on other categories.

Overall, this month showed a modest increase in spending, but our financial planning remains on track as the income growth has allowed us to meet our budgetary goals.
