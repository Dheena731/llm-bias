Example of a student’s methods section: Our study is based on a survey with 300 respondents across the state of California, USA. Respondents who participated in the survey were sampled from the population of all Cal State residents. Each respondent was given a form, which had various questions. We also added some demographics questions to the form. Out of the 300 sample, we had 70% participation rate. The survey took 10 minutes on average to complete. We conducted our experiments between mid-January and mid-February.
