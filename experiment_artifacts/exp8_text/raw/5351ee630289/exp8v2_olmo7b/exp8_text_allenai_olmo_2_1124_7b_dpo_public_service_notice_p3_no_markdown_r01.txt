**Important Update on Recycling Collection Changes**

Effective immediately, please note the following changes to our recycling collection schedules and requirements:

- **New Bin Colors:** All recycling bins will now be replaced with new colors to better distinguish materials. Please ensure you are using the correct bins for paper, plastic, glass, and metals as instructed by your local collection service.

- **Modification of Accepted Items:** Some items previously accepted for recycling have been revised. Please check the updated list of acceptable materials available on our website or included in your latest collection guide. This includes changes regarding plastic types, electronic waste, and hazardous materials.

- **New Collection Days:** Starting next week, recyclable materials will be collected on different days than previously scheduled. The updated calendar is attached for your convenience. Ensure your recyclables are ready and at the designated collection points on the new days.

- **Increased Service Frequency:** We have increased the frequency of trash and recycling collections for selected zones. Check your zone designation to verify any changes in your collection schedule.

For any further questions or assistance, please contact our customer service team at 555-1234 or visit our website. Thank you for your cooperation in ensuring these changes are smoothly implemented. Together, we can make a positive impact on our environment through effective recycling practices.

**End of Notice**
