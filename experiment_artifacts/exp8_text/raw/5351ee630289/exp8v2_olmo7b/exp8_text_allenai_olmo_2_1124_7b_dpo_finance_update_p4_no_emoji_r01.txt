The end-of-year tax statement, also known as a tax summary or annual tax statement, is a document received from tax authorities or tax professionals summarizing your annual tax obligations, including income, deductions, and taxes paid during the fiscal year. This statement provides a comprehensive view of your tax situation, detailing how much tax you owe or have overpaid, and may include credits and credits applied throughout the year. It is crucial for taxpayers to review this document meticulously to ensure accuracy and to prepare for any necessary payments or refunds. The statement serves as a foundational reference for filing annual tax returns and can aid in planning for future tax years.
