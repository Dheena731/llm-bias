Quarantine or stay-at-home guidelines have come into effect in most countries as a result of the COVID-19 crisis. In this episode of the Language Learning for Travel podcast, we’ll be talking about how to keep up your language learning while stuck at home.

With most travel and international connections currently suspended, you might be feeling frustrated that your language learning plan has been derailed. However, there are lots of things you can do to keep your language proficiency up while staying at home.

We’ll be discussing a range of different strategies, including:

  • Using podcasts and audio content
  • Reading in your target language
  • Practicing pronunciation
  • Dialogues
  • Watching videos
  • Playing language learning games

We’ll also be interviewing two language learning experts about how they’re staying in practice at the moment.
