Here are some hints for how to craft a good reminder:

  • Try to keep the reminder short. Reminders are about prompting the reader's memory, so too many details or a long preamble can defeat the purpose of a reminder.

  • Keep the tone of the reminder neutral and factual, with a focus on the patients' health and the benefits of preventative care.

  • Reminders can range from informal to formal, from addressing a patient directly to a more professional tone which may include medical terminology.

Here is a good example:

Hey, didn't you have an annual health check-up scheduled in December? It's time for your vaccination.
