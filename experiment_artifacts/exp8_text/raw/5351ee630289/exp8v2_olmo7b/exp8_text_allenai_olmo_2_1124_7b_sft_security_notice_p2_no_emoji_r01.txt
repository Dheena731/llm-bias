1. **Verify the Sender's Identity:** Before responding to any email, please confirm the sender's identity by contacting them through official channels.

3. **Use Two-Factor Authentication:** Enable two-factor authentication on your work accounts. This additional layer of security helps prevent unauthorized access even if your password is compromised.

4. **Regular Training:** We will be scheduling regular training sessions to enhance awareness of phishing tactics. Attendance and participation are mandatory for all employees.

Should you suspect that you have received a phishing email, please do not respond or click on any links. Instead, forward the email to IT immediately. Our IT department is here to assist you with any concerns related to suspected phishing attempts.
