1. The app is called ‘Bake’ and connects to a database of recipes and the ability to order ingredients online. It also has a social element, allowing users to share their latest creations, follow each other and chat about their favourite recipes.

2. Here is the first draft of the launch note:

Bake - the best way for small bakers to get smarter and more organised!

Bake is an app that connects you to a database of more than 100,000 recipes. It allows you to search with keywords, images, ingredients and even popular searches. It also lets you track your own recipes, adjust them to suit your tastes and create a shopping list to order online or print for the shop.

Bake also has a social element. You can follow other bakers and see their latest creations, you can chat with them about their favourite recipes and you can share your own creations with your friends and customers.

Bake is already being used by small bakeries around the country and is proving extremely popular. We believe there is a great opportunity to get smaller bakeries more organised and productive.

3. You might want to change the tone of the note and write in the first person, to help connect with the potential users.

Here is the second draft:

My name is Steve, and I am a baker.
I wrote this note in the hope you could help me.
It is about a new app I have written called Bake.

Bake is a new app that connects to a database of recipes. It lets you search your favourite recipes by keywords, ingredients and even by what is popular, but it also lets you track your own recipes, adjust them to suit your tastes and create a shopping list to order ingredients or print for your shop.

It also has a social element. You can follow other bakers and see their latest creations, you can chat with them about how they found their recipes and you can share your own creations with your friends and customers.

In my case, I am a small baker and I am very interested in getting more organised and productive. I think there is a great opportunity to get small bakeries more organised and productive.

Can you help me understand how I can sell this app to other small bakers around the country?

4. Ask the client to send you their response to the note and see if they have any proof that they are small bakers.

5. If the ‘client’ does not respond in the right way, ask them to imagine they are speaking to a small baker who is considering purchasing your app.

What language would you use in this case, and how would you explain your app? You might want to imagine you are speaking to the small baker in a public place, like a coffee shop, or you might want to imagine they are interested in the app but do not understand what you mean.

6. Compare the language you use when speaking to the ‘client’, with the language you use when you speak to the ‘small baker’. Are there any differences? If so, how might you change the tone of your language to suit the ‘small baker’?
