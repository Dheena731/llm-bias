## Introduction

A missing citation is a potential oversight in any paper, paper, or thesis. It means that there is at least one source that supports the author's argument, but it has not been properly cited in the paper. This could happen due to a variety

1. Lack of understanding about what needs to be included in the citation
2. Carelessness about the entire process of formatting and structure of the paper
3. Technical Errors during the creation of the document.

One way to address these issues is by highlighting the importance of citations by providing authentic examples of both well-cited and poorly cited work. Research papers need to be accurate and transparent so they can stand up against scrutiny by peers who are experts in the same field. For this paper, we have chosen two papers from different disciplines; one paper has good citations while the other lacks them.

The first paper is a study conducted on how environmental pollution affects teenagers' mental health. It was published in the Journal of Environmental Health Perspectives (Carvalho et al., 2018). The authors conducted a systematic review of research studies and found that among the different types of environmental stressors, air pollution exposure increased the risk of depression and anxiety symptoms in adolescents. They also found that the effect was more pronounced among girls than boys. They concluded that further studies are needed to understand the mechanisms behind the association between air pollution and mental health problems, especially because the existing literature mostly focuses on older populations.
