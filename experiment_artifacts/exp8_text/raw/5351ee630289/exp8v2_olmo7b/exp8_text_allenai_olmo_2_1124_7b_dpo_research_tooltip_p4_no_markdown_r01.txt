When a missing-value marker is used, it indicates that a specific position in a dataset holds no data or is unidentified. This marker serves as a placeholder to denote the absence of a value, often represented by symbols like dots (.), asterisks (*), or question marks (?), depending on the context or convention adopted by the dataset or software. The presence of such markers is crucial during data cleaning and analysis, as they signal the need to handle these gaps appropriately, either by imputing values, omitting the entries, or further investigating the cause of the missing data.
