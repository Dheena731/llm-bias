Notice of Refund Eligibility

This notice is provided for the purpose of informing individuals of their refund eligibility. 

1. Full Refund: Customers who have purchased an item that is deemed unfit for use or is defective will be eligible for a full refund within 30 days of purchase. 

2. Partial Refund: Customers who have purchased an item that is not defective but does not meet their expectations will be eligible for a partial refund. This refund will be within 15 days of purchase and will be proportional to the difference between the original purchase amount and the value of the item that meets the customer's expectations.

3. No Refund: Customers who have purchased an item and have used it will not be eligible for any refund. 

Customers should contact the seller for more information and to begin the refund process.
