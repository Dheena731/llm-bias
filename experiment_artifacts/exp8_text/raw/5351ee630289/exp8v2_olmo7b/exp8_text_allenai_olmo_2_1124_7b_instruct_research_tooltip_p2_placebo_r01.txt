A confidence interval in a chart is a range of values that is likely to contain the true population parameter with a specified level of confidence. This range helps assess the reliability of estimates derived from sample data. For instance, a 95% confidence interval suggests that if we were to repeat our analysis many times, we would expect the true value to fall within our calculated interval approximately 95% of the time. It's crucial for understanding the level of uncertainty inherent in statistical estimates and is commonly used in statistical analysis to gauge the precision of data.
