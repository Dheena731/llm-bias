To streamline your busy week's lunch planning, invest time on the weekend preparing simple, versatile dishes that can be easily reheated or adapted. Consider dishes like quinoa bowls, roast chicken, or a big pot of beans and rice. This method not only cuts down on daily meal prep time but also ensures that you have nutritious, satisfying options that can be easily customized to fit any craving or dietary need. By doing this, you'll save time during the busy weekdays and maintain a healthy and balanced lunch routine.
