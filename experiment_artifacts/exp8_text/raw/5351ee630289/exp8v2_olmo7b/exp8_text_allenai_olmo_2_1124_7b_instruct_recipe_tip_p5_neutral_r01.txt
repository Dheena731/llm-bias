To enhance the flavor of roasted vegetables, season them with a blend of herbs, spices, and oils before roasting. For a classic flavor profile, try a mixture of olive oil, fresh thyme, rosemary, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Drizzle this seasoning over your chopped or sliced vegetables, ensuring each piece is well-coated. This simple blend offers a robust, herby, and slightly savory taste that pairs beautifully with the natural sweetness of caramelized vegetables. Roast them at a high temperature to speed up the caramelization process and maximize the infusion of flavors. Experiment with different herbs and spices to match the dish or personal preference.
