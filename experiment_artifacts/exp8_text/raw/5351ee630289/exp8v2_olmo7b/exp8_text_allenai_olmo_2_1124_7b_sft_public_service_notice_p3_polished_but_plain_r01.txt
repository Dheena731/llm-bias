Subject: Important Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection Schedule

Dear Community Members,

We are writing to inform you of changes to our recycling collection schedule, effective immediately.

Starting next week, our recycling trucks will no longer be collecting recyclables during early morning hours. Instead, recyclables will be collected from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., Monday through Friday. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and kindly request that you adjust your recycling plans accordingly.

To accommodate these changes, please ensure that all recyclables are placed curbside no later than 4 p.m. on collection days. If you have any questions or concerns regarding these changes, please contact our customer service team at recyclingdepot.com or by calling (555) 123-4567.

We appreciate your continued cooperation and understanding during this transition. Thank you for your support and for helping us maintain a cleaner environment.
