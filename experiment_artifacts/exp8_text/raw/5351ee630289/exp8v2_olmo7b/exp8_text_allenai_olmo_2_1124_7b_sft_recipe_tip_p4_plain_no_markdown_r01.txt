Plan your lunches for the week on a Sunday evening. Having a clear idea of what you'll eat for lunch each day can save time and stress during the week. This way, you can prepare meals on the weekends, like cooking extra portions for dinner or preparing a big batch of a favorite dish, which can be easily reheated or eaten as is. This approach not only saves time during the busier weekdays but also allows you to enjoy a more nutritious and balanced meal than what might be available on the go.
