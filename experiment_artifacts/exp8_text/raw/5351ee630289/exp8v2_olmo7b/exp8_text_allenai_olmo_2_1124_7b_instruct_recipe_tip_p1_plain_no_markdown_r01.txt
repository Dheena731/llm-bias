To make weeknight dinner prep quicker and less stressful, consider preparing your meals with a base of frozen vegetables and pre-cooked proteins or grains. These ingredients can be stored in your freezer and taken out just before you start cooking, saving you time on chopping and marinating. This strategy ensures a healthy and home-cooked meal is ready in less than an hour, perfect for busy evenings. Plus, it helps you avoid last-minute runs to the store when you're already tired from work.
