A missing-value marker, often denoted as `NaN`, `NA`, or `Missing`, is a data-specific flag indicating that the value at a particular position in a dataset is not available, has not been provided, is an error, or was not measured. When analyzing or processing data, these markers alert analysts to missing data points which can influence statistical analysis and must be either imputed (replaced with estimated values) or handled accordingly, as they can lead to skewed results if omitted without proper treatment.
