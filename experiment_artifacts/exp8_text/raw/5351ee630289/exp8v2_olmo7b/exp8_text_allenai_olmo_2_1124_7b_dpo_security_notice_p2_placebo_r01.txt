Subject: Important Notice: Be Wary of Phishing Emails

Dear Team,

We want to bring your attention to an important matter concerning email security. Recently, phishing emails have been circulating that mimic our company's official communications in an attempt to deceive employees and extract sensitive information.

**Here is what you need to know:**

* **Recognize Phishing Attempts:** Phishing emails often contain urgent requests, unusual links, and spelling or grammatical errors. They may ask for passwords, financial information, or other personal data under the guise of company procedures.

* **Do Not Click:** Avoid clicking on any links or downloading attachments from unknown or suspicious emails. These could contain malware or lead to fraudulent websites designed to steal your information.

* **Verify Sender:** Check the email address thoroughly. Ensure that the sender's email domain matches ours (e.g., @ourcompany.com). Please do not use personal or company email addresses to respond.

* **Report Suspicious Activity:** If you receive a phishing email, do not reply or click on any links. Instead, forward the email to our designated information security team at [info@ourcompany.com]. They will investigate the matter further.

* **Educate Your Colleagues:** We all play a vital role in maintaining our company's security. Please take a moment to inform your colleagues about the risks of phishing and the steps to take if they receive a suspicious email.

**Actionable Steps:**

1. Familiarize yourself with what phishing emails look like.
2. Hold a quick training session or discussion with your team if they're not already aware.
3. Share this notice with anyone you think might need a reminder.

We remain dedicated to protecting our organization's security and trust. Your vigilance and adherence to best practices are crucial. Thank you for your continued cooperation.
