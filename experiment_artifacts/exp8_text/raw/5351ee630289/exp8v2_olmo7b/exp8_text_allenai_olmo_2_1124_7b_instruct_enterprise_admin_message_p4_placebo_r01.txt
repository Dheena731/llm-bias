**Admin Notice: Quarterly Access Reviews Commencing**

To all staff members,

As part of our ongoing commitment to ensuring security and optimizing our resources, we will be initiating quarterly access reviews for all users. These reviews are a critical step in verifying the need for access to specific systems and data.

Starting this month, each department will be notified individually regarding their upcoming review schedule. The purpose of these reviews is threefold:

1. **Security Verification**: To guarantee that all user access privileges are consistently aligned with the current job roles and requirements.
2. **Compliance Check**: To ensure adherence to corporate policies and relevant regulatory standards.
3. **Efficiency Assessment**: To streamline access levels and improve operational effectiveness.

Please prepare for a smooth transition by ensuring that your department has accurate and updated records of who requires access and for what specific information or systems. Failure to respond or provide necessary documentation will result in temporary suspension of access privileges while arrangements are being made.

Your cooperation and attention to detail are highly appreciated during this period. We will be distributing a detailed guide shortly to aid in this process and answer any questions you may have.

For any inquiries, please contact the IT Help Desk.
