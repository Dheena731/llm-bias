Important: Do not start the email with "Dear [name]". Your email should use the "formal" salutation and signature. Here's an example:

Hi John,

I'm writing to let you know that our new privacy policy went live today. We appreciate your trust and understand your concerns about keeping your information private. Please take a moment to read our updated privacy policy at https://abc.com/privacy.

If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to me at info@abc.com.

Best,

Jane Doe
