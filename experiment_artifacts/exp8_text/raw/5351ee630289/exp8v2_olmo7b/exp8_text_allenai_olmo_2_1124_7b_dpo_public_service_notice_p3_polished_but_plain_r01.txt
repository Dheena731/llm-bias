Subject: Important Update: Changes to Recycling Collection Times and Locations

Dear Residents,

We are writing to inform you of important changes to our recycling collection schedule and locations. Starting from next Monday, the recycling trucks will begin their routes one hour earlier at 6:00 AM. This change is due to increased demand and operational efficiency. Please ensure your recycling bins are out by 5:59 AM to avoid missing the collection.

Additionally, there will be a slight adjustment to the collection points. The new recycling center will be located at the corner of Maple and 5th Street, replacing the previous site at 3rd Avenue and Oak Street.

For your convenience, a map displaying the new collection points and collection schedule will be available on our website and distributed through local post offices starting tomorrow.

We understand this may cause some inconvenience, and we apologize for any disruption. We encourage everyone to adapt to these changes by adjusting your routine slightly. This will help us maintain efficient service and continue our commitment to recycling.

Should you have any questions or concerns regarding these changes, please do not hesitate to contact our customer service team at 555-1234 between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

Thank you for your cooperation and continued support in promoting a greener community.
