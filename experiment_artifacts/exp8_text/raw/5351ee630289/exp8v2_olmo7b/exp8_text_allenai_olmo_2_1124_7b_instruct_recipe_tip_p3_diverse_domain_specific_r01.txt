To keep your rice from becoming a sticky mess, skip the typical 'one part rice to two parts water' ratio and opt for a slightly drier mix—use a ratio of one cup rice to about 1.75 cups liquid instead. Start with cold water, as it absorbs more evenly, and add the rice incrementally, stirring frequently. This method ensures each grain isnates properly without absorbing too much moisture, keeping your rice fluffy and less prone to clumping. Remember, the key is in the ratio and gentle handling – your new best friend in maintaining the perfect rice texture.
