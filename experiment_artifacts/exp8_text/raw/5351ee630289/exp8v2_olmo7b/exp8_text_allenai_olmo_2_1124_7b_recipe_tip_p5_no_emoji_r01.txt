How to make Perfect Pasta Sauce

Making pasta sauce can be a bit of a pain in the workings for some people. Whilst it is one of the best ways for adding a bit of extra flavor to the food you are eating, most people don't really know how to go about it.

If you want your pasta to taste great, you just need to follow the simple steps in this article.

Step 1

Get your pot and go ahead and put it on the stove. You don't want to use a pot that is too big as this will make it harder for the sauce to cook, but at the same time, you don't want a pot that is too small.

Step 2

Put a tablespoon of olive oil inside the pot. This is the main ingredient in pasta sauces and the best oil you can use is extra virgin olive oil.

Step 3

Wait until the oil is hot before adding the vegetables inside. You should only use enough vegetables to fill half the pot.

Step 4

Put the lid on the pot and wait for the vegetables to soften. You don't want them to become too soft, but at the same time you don't want them to become too hard.

This should take 10-15 minutes.

Step 5

Add your favorite pasta sauce to the pot. The perfect sauce to add is a simple tomato sauce. This will make sure that the pasta stays moist inside as well as giving it a bit of extra flavor and color.

Step 6

Keep an eye on the pot as you don't want to overcook it. If you see that the sauce is starting to bubble, it means that you are cooking it too much and you need to remove it from the stove.

Put the lid back on and put the pot in the refrigerator overnight. This will allow the flavors to mix together and will make it easier for you to cook the pasta the next day.

Step 7

Cook the pasta as you normally would and add the sauce to the pot. This should be the last step as there is no need to add the sauce in until the pasta is already cooked.

Make sure that it is mixed in well so that you don't have any lumps.

Hopefully, you will now know how to make perfect pasta sauce. It is an easy process and it will take you just a few minutes to make it.

If you want to add a bit of extra flavor to the pasta sauce, consider adding some salt and pepper to it. You can also add some garlic, onions, or any other herbs that you want to add.
