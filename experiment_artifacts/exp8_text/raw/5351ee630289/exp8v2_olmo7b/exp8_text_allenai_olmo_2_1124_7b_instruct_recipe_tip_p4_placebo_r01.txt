Plan your lunches for the week on a Sunday evening. Gather all your ingredients while you have the time and cook larger batches to portion out for each day. Use a lunchbox or container to keep your meals fresh and portable, ensuring you have a healthy, home-cooked meal ready to go every day, even when the week gets hectic. This way, you save time and effort during the busy week and maintain control over what you're eating, promoting better health and nutrition.
