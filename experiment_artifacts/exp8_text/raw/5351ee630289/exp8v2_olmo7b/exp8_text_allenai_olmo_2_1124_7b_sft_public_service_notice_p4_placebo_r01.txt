ATTENTION COMMUNITY MEMBERS:

Due to high temperatures predicted for the next few days, the city will release a heat advisory. Those most vulnerable, the very young, the aged, and those with chronic medical conditions, should take extreme precautions and stay indoors if possible. The city will open several cooling centers around town for those without air conditioning. 
Please remember to check on your neighbors, especially those who live alone, and keep an eye on your pets by providing them with plenty of fresh drinking water and shelter in the shade. The city will not be picking up curbside recycling this week due to the high temperatures. 

Thank you for your cooperation.
