**NOON UPDATE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

RESIDENTS AND VISITORS ARE ADVISED OF A SERIOUS HEAT ADVISORY TODAY AND TOMORROW, WITH TEMPERATURES EXPECTED TO REACH OR EXCEED 105 DEGREES. HIGH HUMIDITY WILL ACCELERATE HEAT INDEX VALUES, POTENTIALLY REACHING 115 DEGREES OR HIGHER.

STAY COOL AND HYDRATE: DRINK PLENTY OF WATER, LIMIT EXERTION, AND SEEK SHELTER DURING THE HARDEST PART OF THE DAY. CHECK UPON ELDERLY NEIGHBORS AND FAMILY MEMBERS TO ENSURE THEIR WELL-BEING.

IF YOU DON'T HAVE ACCESS TO AIR CONDITIONING, CONSIDER SEEKING COOL SPACES SUCH AS LIBRARIES, MALLS, OR SHELTERS. AVOID EXTENDED EXPOSURE TO THE SUN AND WEAR LIGHT-COLORED, LOOSE CLOTHING.

THE OFFICE OF EMERGENCY SERVICES RECOMMENDS THAT TRANSPORTATION OF SENIORS AND INFANTS BE PLANNED AHEAD OF TIME TO AVOID DISCOMFORT AND HEALTH RISKS DUE TO THE EXTREME TEMPERATURES.

WE URGE EVERYONE TO TAKE PRECAUTIONS SERIOUSLY TO PROTECT AGAINST HEAT-RELATED ILLNESS AND INCONVENIENCE. FOR UPDATES AND FURTHER INFORMATION, LISTEN TO LOCAL NEWS OUTLETS OR CHECK OUR WEBSITE. STAY COOL AND STAY INFORMED.
