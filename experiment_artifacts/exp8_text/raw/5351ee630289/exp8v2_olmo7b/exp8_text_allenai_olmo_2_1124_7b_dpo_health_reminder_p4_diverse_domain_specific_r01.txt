**Reminder: Screen Time Breaks - The TechWhisk Way**

In the fast-paced, digital world where your screens are glued to your eyes, it's easy to forget the importance of a well-deserved break. At TechWhisk, we're not just about the tech; we're about balance. Here's a quick reminder to pamper your peepers and recharge:

**1. The 20-20-20 Rule:** Every 20 minutes, focus on something 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds. It's a simple hack to reduce eye strain and give your eyes a rest from the small print.

**2. Blink More:** We often forget to blink when we're engrossed in our screens. Try to blink consciously every few seconds to keep your eyes moist and reduce the risk of dry eye syndrome.

**3. Stand Up and Stretch:** Sitting for long periods can cause stiffness and discomfort. When you feel the need to stretch those tech-hunched shoulders, stand up, do a few stretches, and shake out the tension.

**4. Look Around:** Every hour or so, take a few minutes to look away from your screen and focus on something in the real world. It helps break the 'screen tunnel' vision and can improve your awareness.

**5. Screen Distance:** Adjust your screen to about an arm's length away and slightly below eye level to minimize strain. This ergonomic setup is kinder to your eyes and your posture.

**6. Break Time:** Commit to regular breaks. Consider using apps or reminders that prompt you to pause and step away from the glow. Short breaks are your allies in staying sharp and healthy.

Remember, taking care of your eyes is like charging your tech gadgets. A little maintenance and downtime goes a long way. So, don't wait for eye strain to shout at you—listen to the quiet, considerate voice of TechWhisk reminding you it's break time!
