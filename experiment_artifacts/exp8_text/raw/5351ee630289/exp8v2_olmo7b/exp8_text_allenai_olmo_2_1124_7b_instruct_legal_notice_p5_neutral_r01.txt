**Notice of Acceptable Use Policy for Shared Network**

**1. Introduction**

This notice outlines the Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) for the shared network hosted by [Network Provider Name], including, but not limited to, wired and wireless connections provided to [Address/Location of Network] and any other network facilities owned, operated, or managed by [Network Provider Name].

**2. Purpose**

The purpose of this AUP is to establish standards and guidelines for the use of the network to ensure that it remains a reliable, safe, and secure resource for all users. The maintenance of an orderly and respectful online environment is paramount.

**3. Scope**

This AUP applies to all individuals and entities (hereinafter users) who are granted access to the shared network. The prohibitions and requirements outlined herein apply whether users access the network directly or indirectly, and whether they do so individually, in groups, or through electronic processes.

**4. Acceptable Use**

Users must adhere to the following acceptable uses of the network:

- **Non-Exempt Use:** Use of the network for routine business, educational, and informational activities, subject to the terms of this AUP.
- **Exempt Use:** Network usage that is expressly permitted by [Network Provider Name], such as network testing, maintenance, and development purposes.

**5. Unacceptable Use**

Users must refrain from engaging in the following:

- **Disruption of Service:** Any activity that intentionally disrupts, diminishes the quality of, or interferes with the use of the network by others.
- **Unauthorized Access:** Attempting to access, alter, or damage network equipment, software, or data without authorization.
- **Harmful Content:** Distributing, downloading, or accessing material that is offensive, obscene, harmful, illegal, or promotes violence.
- **Malware Distribution:** Engaging in the distribution of malware, viruses, Trojan horses, worms, or any other harmful or destructive programs.
- **Infringement:** Using the network to infringe upon the intellectual property rights of others, including but not limited to, unauthorized sharing or downloading of copyrighted material.
- **Privacy Violations:** Engaging in activities that compromise the privacy of others, such as unauthorized surveillance or stalking.
- **Cybercrime:** Engaging in cybercrimes, such as theft of service, fraud, identity theft, phishing, and spamming.
- **Unauthorized Sharing:** Sharing or making available network access or accounts to individuals not authorized by [Network Provider Name].

**6. Monitoring and Enforcement**

[Network Provider Name] reserves the right to monitor network usage and to enforce this AUP. Non-compliance with this AUP may result in suspension or termination of network services, and/or legal action.

**7. Liability**

Users are responsible for their actions and the consequences thereof while using the shared network. [Network Provider Name] shall not be liable for any damage, loss, or injury resulting from the misuse of the network, except where such misuse is caused by the fault or negligence of [Network Provider Name].

**8. Amendments**

This AUP can be updated from time to time by [Network Provider Name] with or without notice. The most current version of this AUP will always be available on the network's website or upon request.

**9. Grievances**

In the event of a grievance regarding the enforcement of this AUP, users should contact [Network Provider Name] to discuss the matter. The final decision of [Network Provider Name] regarding compliance with this AUP shall be conclusive.

**10. Definitions**

Any term not defined in this AUP shall have its ordinary meaning as ascribed to it in good English usage.
