The local library is committed to serving the community and is pleased to inform you of its regular opening hours. Our doors are open from Monday to Friday, 9am to 8pm. On Saturdays, we welcome visitors from 9am to 5pm. The library remains closed on Sundays and public holidays. It is important to note that these hours might change occasionally due to holidays or special events, and we will post any updates on our website and social media channels. We encourage you to consult these sources for the most accurate and current information. Your support and attendance contribute significantly to our mission to provide access to information and resources. Thank you for your continued patronage.
