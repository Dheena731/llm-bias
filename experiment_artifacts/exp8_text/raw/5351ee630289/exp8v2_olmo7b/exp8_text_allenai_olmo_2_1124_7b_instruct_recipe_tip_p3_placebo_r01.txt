To make rice less sticky, rinse the uncooked rice thoroughly under cold water before cooking. This removes excess starch, which is what causes the rice to clump together. Also, use a rice-to-water ratio of 1:2 (1 cup rice to 2 cups water), and cook the rice in a skillet with a lid before transferring it to a saucepan with a steamer insert. This initial skillet cooking will help the rice grains open up and reduce sticking. Finally, let the rice sit off the heat for an additional 10 minutes with the lid on before serving to allow it to firm up and become less adhesive.
