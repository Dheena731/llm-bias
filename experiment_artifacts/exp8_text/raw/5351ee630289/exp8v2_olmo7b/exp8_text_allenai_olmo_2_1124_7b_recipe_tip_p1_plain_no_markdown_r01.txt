How to make fluffy scrambled eggs

Scrambled eggs are a classic breakfast item, but they can also be made as an easy weeknight dinner. Here's how to make them fluffy and delicious.


1. Gather your ingredients. You will need:

- 6 eggs
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon pepper


2. Crack the eggs into a bowl. Add the salt and pepper and whisk until well combined.


3. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Pour in the egg mixture and allow it to set for 30 seconds.


4. Use a rubber spatula to gently scrape the cooked egg from the edge of the pan toward the center. Continue doing this until all the egg is cooked.


5. Serve immediately with your favorite toppings (e.g., bacon, cheese, or avocado). Enjoy!
