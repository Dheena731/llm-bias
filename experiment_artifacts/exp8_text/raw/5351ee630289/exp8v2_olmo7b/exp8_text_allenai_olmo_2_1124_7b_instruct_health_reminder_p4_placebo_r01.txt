Remember to step away from your screens periodically throughout the day. Taking short breaks can significantly reduce eye strain, improve focus, and overall well-being. Try the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. This simple practice helps refresh your eyes and mind, ensuring you return to your screens with renewed energy and clarity. Don't overlook the importance of these brief pauses; they're vital for maintaining both your digital and physical health.
