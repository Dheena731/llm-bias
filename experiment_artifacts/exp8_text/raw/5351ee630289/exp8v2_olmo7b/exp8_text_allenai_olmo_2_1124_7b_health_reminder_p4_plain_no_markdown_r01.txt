You should not use abbreviations or spelling variations like "fl" or "melissa".

Keep your reminders short, preferably one or two sentences that are less than twenty words long.

Do not use abbreviations or jargon. For example, instead of saying "Lunch", write "Eat".

Do not use your own name in a reminder unless it is a contact information or a signature.

If you want to suggest any edits, please create a pull request.
