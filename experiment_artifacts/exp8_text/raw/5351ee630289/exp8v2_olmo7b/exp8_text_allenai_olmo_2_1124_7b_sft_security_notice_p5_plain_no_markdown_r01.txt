Please report the loss of our company laptop to the IT department immediately. If it is misplaced and you are unable to find it, follow these steps:

1. Contact your supervisor and inform them of the incident.
2. Notify the IT department by calling extension 4353 or sending an email to it@ourcompany.com.
3. Provide the following information:
   - Your name and department
   - A description of the lost item, including model, color, and any distinguishing features
   - The date and location of the loss

4. Avoid panic and take note of any bystanders who may have witnessed the incident. They might have valuable information.

It is important to report lost or stolen company property promptly to minimize potential risks and unauthorized use. Thank you for your cooperation.
