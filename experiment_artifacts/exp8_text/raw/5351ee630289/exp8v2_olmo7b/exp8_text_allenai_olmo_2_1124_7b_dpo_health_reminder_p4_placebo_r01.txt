🌞 Quick Screen Break Reminder 🌞

In our digital world, it's easy to get lost in screens for hours on end. Remember, your eyes — and overall well-being — deserve a break! Take short, frequent pauses from your digital devices. Look away from the screen every 20 minutes at something about 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds. This "20-20-20" rule can help prevent eye strain and headaches. Your mind and body will thank you for these moments of rest. Plus, it's a great way to refresh your focus. Breaks are not just a luxury; they're a necessity for maintaining your health and productivity. So, step away from the screen and breathe some real air! 😌🌳
