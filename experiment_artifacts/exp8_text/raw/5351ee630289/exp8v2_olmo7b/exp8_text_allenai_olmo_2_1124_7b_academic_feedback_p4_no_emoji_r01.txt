Select a conference poster from Science of Learning or CogSci conference.

Read the poster and try to answer the following questions:

1. What is the main idea of the poster?

2. What is the research question?

3. What is the method or approach used to investigate the question?

4: Identify two potential ways to improve the poster.

5: If you have time, what additional questions would you ask the author?
