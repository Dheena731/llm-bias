Why ShopLocal ™? 

You love your community. You love supporting small businesses. 🙌

Our goal is to build a sustainable community connecting small business owners with customers like you. ✅

We make it easy for you to find unique and interesting products and services in your neighborhood. We prioritize high-quality, small business-approved listings. 😍

How does it work? 

1️⃣ Use your smartphone to search for shops or services in your neighborhood. 🌍

2️⃣ Check a shop's listing for their business hours, photos, and special promotions. 👀

3️⃣ Read reviews and make decisions on where to spend your hard-earned money! 💰

4️⃣ Download the app, create a profile, and begin earning rewards! 

✅ Earn points for shopping locally and easily redeem them at your favorite businesses.
