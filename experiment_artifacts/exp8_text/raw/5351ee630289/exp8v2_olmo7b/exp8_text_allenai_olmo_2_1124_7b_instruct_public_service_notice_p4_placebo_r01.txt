**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for the greater metropolitan area starting today, July 15th, and lasting until July 18th. Temperatures are forecasted to reach dangerous levels, with the heat index expected to exceed 105°F. 

**RESIDENTS ARE ADVISED TO:**

- Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of fluids.
- Limit outdoor activities to early morning or evening hours.
- Check on family, friends, and neighbors, particularly the elderly and those without air conditioning.
- Keep an eye on children and pets, ensuring they are not left in parked vehicles.

**WHAT TO DO IF YOU'RE AT RISK:**

- Consider visiting public buildings with air conditioning, such as libraries or community centers.
- Do not leave pets outside for extended periods.
- If you experience signs of heat exhaustion or heat stroke, seek immediate medical attention.

For further information and updates, please monitor NOAA Weather Radio, visit our website, or follow our social media channels. Stay safe!

*End of Notice*
