🌟 Pro-Tip for Plangrid Calendar Users 🌟

You know how it goes—you're booked, but suddenly you remember that awesome concert in town. No problem! In Plangrid, just tap into a specific day and select "Event." Next, you can jot down that night's concert in the "Notes" section! 💃🎶 Now, your calendar will be as up-to-date as your best moves on the dance floor. 🎤✨ #PlangridMagic #NoMoreMissedGigs
