A new feature has been introduced in our savings application: the "savings goal." This feature allows users to set specific financial targets, making it easier to track progress towards personal saving objectives. Users can customize their goals, set deadlines, and receive regular reminders to ensure accountability and achievement of their savings targets. With this update, users can enhance their financial planning and motivation by breaking down larger goals into manageable steps, thereby facilitating better money management habits. The "savings goal" feature is designed to empower users by providing a clearer path to their financial goals, promoting discipline, and encouraging consistent saving practices.
