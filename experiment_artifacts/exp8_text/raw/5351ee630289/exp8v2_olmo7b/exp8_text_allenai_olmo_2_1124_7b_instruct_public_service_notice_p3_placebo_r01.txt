We are writing to inform you of upcoming changes to our recycling collection schedule. As part of our ongoing efforts to enhance our recycling program and improve the quality of materials collected, starting next week, the following changes will take effect:

1. **Recycling Day Scheduling:** Our recycling trucks will now operate on alternating weeks starting from [Date]. This means if your usual collection day is Week 1, it will change to Week 2 and vice versa. This adjustment is made to optimize truck routes and increase collection efficiency.

2. **New Materials:** We have expanded the types of recyclable materials accepted. Please note the following new additions to our list: #5 polyethylene terephthalate (PETE), multilayer plastics (commonly found in food wrappers), and certain types of electronics (subject to specific conditions).
