Shared networks should be set up and used to allow the sharing of genuine, lawful information. This means they should be used responsibly, and these guidelines set out dos and don'ts to help you do this.

Shared networks should only be used for simple peer to peer exchanges and should not be used to transfer large files if there are better connections available. If you find yourself using a shared network to frequently download files, you should consider subscribing to your local ISPs download service or using a private connection. If you use a shared network to frequently download files you may become unwelcome as a neighbour, and your access may be closed or you may have your connection speed restricted by the network operator.

If you use a shared network to trade software, music, video or other material which is protected by copyright or patent, you may be breaking the law.
