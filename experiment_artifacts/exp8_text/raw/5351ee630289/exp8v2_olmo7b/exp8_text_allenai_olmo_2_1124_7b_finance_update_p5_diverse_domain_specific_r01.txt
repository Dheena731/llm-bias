A: A popular digital currency exchange just received a warning letter from regulators in New York, New York. The exchange has been accused of violating federal securities laws by conducting a "trading platform for securities" without authorization. The letter directs the company to stop trading securities on the platform immediately and reports their actions to the Securities and Exchange Commission. The message was one of the many warnings issued by U.S. regulators this year to digital currency exchanges. In addition to New York, the exchanges in Wyoming, Maryland, Florida, Missouri, and Washington state also received similar warnings.
