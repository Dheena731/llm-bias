Impact of Delayed Payments on Receivers

Receivers of delayed payments often experience significant stress and anxiety. This is because they cannot use the funds until the payment has been received, which may result in missed opportunities or tight cash flow. Additionally, if the receiver relies on those funds to meet their financial obligations, delays can lead to late fees and potential damage to their credit rating.
