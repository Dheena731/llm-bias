Your end-of-year tax statement summarizes your annual earnings, deductions, and tax obligations, providing a clear picture of how much you owe or are owed in taxes for the year. It serves as a crucial document for both taxpayers and tax professionals, ensuring that your tax filings are accurate and compliant with the law. This statement typically includes information on gross income, taxable income, tax credits, deductions taken, and the total tax liability. It also highlights any estimated tax payments made throughout the year and offers insights into your overall tax situation, helping you plan for future tax years.
