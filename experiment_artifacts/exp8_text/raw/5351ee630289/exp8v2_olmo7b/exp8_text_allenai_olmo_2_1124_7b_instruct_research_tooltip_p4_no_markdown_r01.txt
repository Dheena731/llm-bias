A missing-value marker is a standardized symbol or value added to a dataset to denote that a corresponding data point was not recorded or is missing. This helps analysts or machines identify and handle these missing entries during analysis, ensuring they do not skew results or cause errors in computations, by either removing them or applying imputation methods. Common markers include 'NaN' (Not a Number) in spreadsheets or NULL in database languages, representing the absence of numerical or categorical data respectively.
