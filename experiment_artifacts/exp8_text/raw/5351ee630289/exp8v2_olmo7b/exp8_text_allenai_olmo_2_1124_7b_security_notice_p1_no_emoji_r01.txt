words

A password reuse notice should

  • be informative and explain the consequences of password reuse,
  • list all the places where the passwords have been reused,
  • be written in a concise and informal manner,
  • include a title,
  • have a "thank you" at the end,
  • be concise and have no more than 50 words.

I do not want the notice to be superfluous. The notice is viewed by users who are already my customers.

I am looking for a way to write a concise and formal notice, without sounding like I am blaming the customer.

An example thank you must not be assumed to be understood by the customer.

Details

If each password is reused five times, then there will be 50,000 places for reuse.

I am looking for a title that addresses all 50,000 places.

I am not opposed to making the users feel guilty about password reuse.

Here is a

Bad Example

You have password reuse. For each of the 20,000 places where you reuse your passwords, I will be sending you a monthly email for one year.

An Example

I am looking for a way to inform without sounding like I am blaming the customer.

Thank you for password reuse. Please stop.

How do I write a short workplace security notice about password reuse?
