Subject: Introduction of New Expense Approval Workflow

Dear Team,

We are pleased to announce the implementation of a new expense approval workflow designed to streamline and enhance the process for handling financial requests. This new workflow has been developed with the aim of improving transparency, efficiency, and accountability within our expense management system.

Effective immediately, employees will be required to adhere to the following steps for submitting and approving expenses:

1. **Submission of Expenses:** All financial requests must be submitted through our updated expense report portal. This portal integrates directly with our financial system, ensuring real-time tracking and accuracy.

2. **Initial Approval:** The submitted expense report will be reviewed by a designated departmental reviewer. This initial approval step ensures that expenses align with our policy guidelines and the project's budgetary constraints.

3. **Peer Review:** For added oversight, the report will then undergo a peer review process. This involves a secondary review by a colleague who is not directly involved in the project to provide a fresh perspective and confirm the validity of the expenses.

4. **Senior Approval:** The final step before authorization is the approval from a senior manager. This ensures that all financial decisions adhere to our organizational values and strategic objectives.

5. **Record Keeping:** The approved expenses will be documented in our financial records for future reference and audit purposes.

We encourage all team members to familiarize themselves with this new workflow through the training modules provided on our intranet. Your cooperation and adherence to these steps are vital to maintaining a smooth and secure financial management process.

Please reach out to our financial support team if you have any questions or need assistance with this implementation.

Thank you for your attention and continued diligence.
