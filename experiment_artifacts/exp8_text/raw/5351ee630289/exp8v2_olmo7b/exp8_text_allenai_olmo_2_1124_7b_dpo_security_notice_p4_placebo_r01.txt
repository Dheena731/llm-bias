Subject: Enabling Two-Factor Authentication for Enhanced Security

Dear Team,

We are committed to maintaining the highest levels of security for all our accounts. To enhance this, we are implementing a mandatory change to our account security protocols by enabling Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).

Two-Factor Authentication is an additional layer of security that requires a user to provide two forms of identification before accessing their account. This significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access even if passwords are compromised.

To begin using 2FA:

1. Visit the Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator app on your mobile device.
2. Follow the prompts to generate your unique authentication codes.
3. Bookmark the provided QR code link for easy access in the future.
4. Enter the code provided by the app during the account login process.

Please note that 2FA will be mandatory for all account logins starting next week. Ensure you complete the setup process before then to avoid any disruption to your work.

Should you encounter any difficulties during the setup process, please reach out to our IT Support team for assistance.

Thank you for your cooperation and continued diligence in maintaining a secure work environment.
