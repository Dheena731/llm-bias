**Workplace Security Notice: Avoid Password Reuse**

Attention Employees:

To maintain the highest level of security for your work-related data and accounts, it is imperative that you adhere to our password policies. One of the most critical aspects of these policies is the prohibition against reusing passwords across different services, especially those sensitive to your workplace.

Reusing passwords increases the risk of unauthorized access to your information. If an attacker manages to obtain your password for one service, they could potentially gain access to multiple accounts if the same password is being used.

**Key Points to Remember:**

1. **Unique Passwords:** Always create unique passwords for each of your work accounts.
2. **Complexity:** Ensure your passwords are complex and include a mix of letters, numbers, and special characters.
3. **Password Managers:** Consider using a reputable password manager to securely store and manage your passwords.

By following these guidelines, you actively contribute to the safety and integrity of our workplace's digital environment. Please take a moment to review and update your password practices immediately to safeguard your information and the workplace's data.

For further assistance or questions regarding password policies, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT department.

Thank you for your cooperation and vigilance.

The Workplace Security Team
