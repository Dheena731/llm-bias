What about "safe" language?

To avoid some subjective terms that could be perceived as biased or even insulting, we avoid using words like "wont", "evil", or "lazy". The same goes for those who do not use the "formal" language that is widely used in our society.

We do not use the "-ism" suffix, with the exception of "racism", and we have a general policy of not using "-ist" words like "sexist" or "racist".
