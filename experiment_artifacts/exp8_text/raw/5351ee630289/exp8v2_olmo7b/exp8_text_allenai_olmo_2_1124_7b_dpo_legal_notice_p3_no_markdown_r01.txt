Cookies are small pieces of data sent from our Website and stored on your computer or device by your web browser. They are used to recognize you when you return to our Website, remember your preferences, and improve your experience.

**Types of Cookies**

Our Website uses different types of cookies:

4. **Targeting Cookies:** These cookies are used to deliver advertisements that are relevant to your interests. They may also limit the number of times you see an advertisement.
