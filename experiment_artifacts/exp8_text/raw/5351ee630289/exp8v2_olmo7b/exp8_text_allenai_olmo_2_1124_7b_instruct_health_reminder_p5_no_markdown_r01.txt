When refilling a prescription, ensure you do it a few days before your current supply runs out to avoid any service interruptions. Contact your pharmacist or healthcare provider to submit the refill request well in advance and review the details of your medication to confirm everything is accurate and up to date. Double-check the dose, frequency, and quantity to ensure you receive the correct prescription. Remember to keep your prescribed medication in a safe place away from children and pets. Stay organized and keep a consistent schedule for taking your medication to maintain its effectiveness.
