Effective July 15th, 2023, there will be a change in our card transaction fees. Starting this date, we will implement an increase from our current rate of 2.5% to 2.75% for all credit and debit card transactions processed through our system. This change aims to cover additional operational costs associated with processing these transactions, including recent enhancements in security and compliance measures required by our payment providers. We appreciate your understanding and continued support. For any further inquiries or concerns, please contact our customer service department.
