The recycling schedule starts on September 1st, 2023. Changes coming to the schedule include:

- New times

- New routes

- New days for collection

Residents will receive a letter with details about changes in collection times, routes, and days. The letter also includes a request for participation in a survey to provide feedback and suggestions for improvements.

Residents who receive the letter and want to provide input can visit the website and participate in the survey by filling out the form available at the website.

Further information about recycling can be found at the website and by contacting the office.
