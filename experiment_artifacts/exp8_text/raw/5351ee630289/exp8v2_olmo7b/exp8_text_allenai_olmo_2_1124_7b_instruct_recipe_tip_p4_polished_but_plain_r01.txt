When planning lunches for a busy week, prepare meals that are simple to assemble and utilize leftovers to reduce preparation time. Choose recipes that use common ingredients, allowing you to cook in bulk and portion out servings for each day. Invest time on the weekend in meal preparation, freezing portion-sized containers, and defrosting them as needed for each weekday. This approach saves time, minimizes last-minute stress, and ensures you have nutritious and balanced meals ready to go.
