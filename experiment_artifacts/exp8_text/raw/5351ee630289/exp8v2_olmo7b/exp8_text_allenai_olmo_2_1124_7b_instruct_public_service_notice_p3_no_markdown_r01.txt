ATTENTION RESIDENTS

There will be changes to our recycling collection schedule starting next week. Due to ongoing operational adjustments, please expect the following:

- **Day of Collection Change:** Recycling bins will now be collected one day later than usual. Check your calendar for the updated date.

- **New Items List:** Effective immediately, we are no longer accepting glass bottles and plastic bags in our curbside recycling program. Please dispose of these items in accordance with your local waste management rules.

- **No Mixing of Materials:** Ensure that recyclables are sorted correctly. Mixed materials will not be picked up, and improperly handled recyclables could jeopardize the entire batch.

- **Additional Information:** For the most accurate and updated information, please visit our website or contact our customer service hotline.

Thank you for your cooperation and understanding during this transitional period. Together, we can ensure a more sustainable future for our community.
