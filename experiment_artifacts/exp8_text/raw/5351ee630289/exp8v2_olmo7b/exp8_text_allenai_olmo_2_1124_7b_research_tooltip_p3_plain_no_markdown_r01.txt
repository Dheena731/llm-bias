# A rationale for this tooltip

The default label for this date range selector was "from" and "to". This label could be ambiguous, as it is commonly used to denote
the interval between two dates. However, this date range selector is not a selector for an interval between
dates. The option "from" does not have a meaning that is obviously related to the date range selector, but the word "to" might
be confusing because it has that meaning, and it might also be confusing because it does not have that meaning.
