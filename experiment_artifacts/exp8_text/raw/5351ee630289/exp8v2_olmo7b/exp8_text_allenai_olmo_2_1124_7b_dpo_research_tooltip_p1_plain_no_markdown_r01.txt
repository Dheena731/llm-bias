This tool allows you to filter your dataset by selecting specific criteria from a range of available options. By choosing your desired filter, the data will be narrowed down, highlighting only the relevant information you're interested in. This streamlines your analysis, making it easier to focus on specific aspects of your research. Use the dropdown menus and checkboxes to select your preferred filters, and see the preview pane for a quick look at how your data will be affected. Click "Apply" to confirm your changes and update the displayed data.
