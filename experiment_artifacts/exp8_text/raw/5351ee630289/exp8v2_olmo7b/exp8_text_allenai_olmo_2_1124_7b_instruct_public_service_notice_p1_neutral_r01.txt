**Public Service Notice: Library Opening Hours**

Attention citizens!

Your local library is here for you, a gateway to knowledge, information, and community engagement. We understand how vital our resources are to your personal and professional growth, education, and leisure. To ensure you have access to these invaluable services, we are pleased to provide the following updated information on our opening hours.

**Library Hours:**

- Monday to Friday: 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
- Sunday: Closed

Please note that due to public health guidelines, some facilities within the library may have restricted access. We encourage you to call the library at (555) 123-4567 or visit our website at www.libraries.cocm for the most current information regarding hours and access protocols.

**Covid-19 Protocols:**

To support the well-being of our staff and patrons, we have implemented enhanced health and safety measures:

- Temperature checks upon entry
- Use of hand sanitizers at the entrance and periodically throughout the facility
- Mask-wearing requirements (with exceptions for children under the age of two and those with medical exemptions)

Your cooperation and understanding in observing these protocols are greatly appreciated and help keep everyone safe.

Do not miss out on the opportunity to explore our vast collection of books, periodicals, digital resources, and more. For those unable to visit in person, we offer an expanding online catalog and e-resources for remote access.

Thank you for your continued support and patronage. Together, we make our library a thriving institution for learning and community.
