**Important Security Reminder for All Staff Members**

Attention Team Players,

We understand the importance of maintaining the integrity and confidentiality of our domain's operations. As such, all screens on workplace devices MUST be locked at all times when not in use. This includes stepping away from your desk for even a few minutes, attending meetings, or grabbing a quick coffee.

Unattended, unlocked screens are a significant security risk, exposing sensitive data to unauthorized access. This practice violates our security protocols and could potentially harm our operations and reputation.

Please ensure you follow the established screen-lock protocols:

1. **Automatic Lock Timer:** Set your device to lock after 10 minutes of inactivity.
2. **Manual Lock:** Use the Windows key + L shortcut or the equivalent gesture on a Mac to lock your screen immediately when stepping away.
3. **Password Protection:** Make sure your device is protected with a strong, unique password.

Failure to comply with these guidelines will result in disciplinary action in line with our HR and IT policies.

Thank you for your vigilance and continuous commitment to keeping our domain safe and secure.
