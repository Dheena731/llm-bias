**Public Notice: Temporary Road Closure in Effect**

Effective immediately, due to necessary maintenance and construction activities, the section of Maple Avenue between 5th Street and 9th Street will be closed to all vehicular traffic. Alternate routes have been provided to ensure continued mobility for the local community.

The closure is scheduled to last approximately two weeks. During this period, detour routes will be clearly signposted, and additional public transportation options will be available to assist commuters.

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your understanding and cooperation. Should you encounter any difficulties, please contact our 24/7 hotline at (555) 123-4567.

Thank you for your attention and continued patience.
