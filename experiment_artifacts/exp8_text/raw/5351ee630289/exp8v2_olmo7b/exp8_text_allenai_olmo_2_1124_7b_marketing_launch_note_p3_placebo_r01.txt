Ellie's comment is a good place to start, but I think something like this is better.

Download Template Budget spreadsheet (Excel spreadsheet)

This can be used as a template for a personal or business budget.

This spreadsheet is divided into named areas:

A1: Notes It is often helpful to write notes in this area.

B1: Personal Budget Categories This area contains a list of the categories you might use for your personal budget: Food, Clothing, Car expenses, Entertainment, Personal care, Bad habits, Housing, Debt payments, Savings

C1: Business Budget Categories This area contains a list of categories you might use for your business budget: Advertising, Staff, Furniture, Permits, Computer expenses, Storage, and more.

D1: Budget Objectives: Personal This heading may contain a brief statement of the purpose of the budget (e.g., to save 20% every month, to pay off debt).

E1: Budget Objectives: Business This heading may contain a brief statement of the purpose of the budget (e.g., to increase sales 30% in the next 5 years).

F1: Budget Periods: Personal This is a list of months that the budget covers.

G1: Budget Periods: Business This is a list of months that the budget covers.

H1: Budget Totals: Personal This section contains a summary of monthly income and expenses as listed by category.

I1: Budget Totals: Business This section contains a summary of monthly income and expenses as listed by category.

J1: Budget Totals: Personal: This month This section contains a summary of monthly income and expenses as listed by category.

K1: Business Budget Totals This month This section contains a summary of monthly income and expenses as listed by category.

L1: Budget Totals by Years: Personal This section contains a summary of annual income and expenses by category.

M1: Budget Totals by Years: Business This section contains a summary of annual income and expenses by category.

N1: Personal Budget Totals: This Year: This section contains a summary of annual income and expenses by category.

O1: Business Budget Totals: This Year: This section contains a summary of annual income and expenses by category.

P1: Budget Totals by Years: This Year This section contains a summary of annual income and expenses by category.

Q1: Personal Budget Totals by Month: This Year This section contains a summary of monthly income and expenses by category.

R1: Personal Budget Totals by Month: This Year: This section contains a summary of monthly income and expenses by category.

S1: Personal Budget Totals by Month This Year This section contains a summary of monthly income and expenses by category.

T1: Personal Budget Totals by Month: This Year: This section contains a summary of monthly income and expenses by category.
