Join us this weekend for the annual Community Harvest Festival in the town square! From Saturday to Sunday, enjoy a day filled with fresh produce stands, live music, and local crafts. There will be activities for all ages, including face painting for kids and a chili cook-off contest. Don't miss the chance to support our local farmers and artisans while enjoying a day of fun under the sun. Bring your friends, family, and appetite, and let's make it a memorable weekend together!
