Got a bunch of fresh herbs that need storing? No problem! Here's a quick tip just for you: Line a cupcake wrapper with a piece of parchment paper, then pop your herbs in there. Seal it up and stash it in your fridge. Not only does this prevent your herbs from drying out, but it also keeps them fresh for up to two weeks—and the cupcake wrapper adds a touch of charm to your herb stash! Remember, fresh herbs are your ally in the kitchen, so keep them happy and they'll keep your dishes delicious!
