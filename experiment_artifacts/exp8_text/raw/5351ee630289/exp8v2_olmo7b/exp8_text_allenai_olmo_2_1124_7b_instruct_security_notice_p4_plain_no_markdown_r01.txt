Attention Everyone,

We are introducing a new security feature to protect your accounts: Two-Factor Authentication. This additional layer of security requires you to verify your identity through two means before accessing your account.

To enable this feature, follow these simple steps:

1. Log in to your account using your usual credentials.
2. Go to the "Settings" or "Account" section.
3. Look for the "Two-Factor Authentication" option and activate it.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to set up your preferred method of receiving the verification code.

By activating two-factor authentication, you significantly reduce the risk of unauthorized access to your account. We strongly encourage you to take this step to enhance your online security.

For further assistance or questions, please refer to our help center or contact our support team.
