We are pleased to inform you that the polling stations for the upcoming election will be located as follows:

**Central District:**

- Polling Station 1: 123 Main Street, corner of Oak Avenue
- Polling Station 2: Community Center, 456 Vine Street

**Southern District:**

- Polling Station 3: High School Auditorium, 789 Elm Road
- Polling Station 4: Public Library, 101 Park Boulevard

**Northern District:**

- Polling Station 5: City Hall, 200 Broad Street
- Polling Station 6: Local Health Center, 300 Chestnut Avenue

Please note that these stations will be open from 8 am to 8 pm on Election Day. We encourage all eligible voters to visit their assigned polling location to participate in the electoral process. For any further inquiries, please refer to the electoral office website or contact them directly at 555-1234. Thank you for your continued commitment to democracy.
