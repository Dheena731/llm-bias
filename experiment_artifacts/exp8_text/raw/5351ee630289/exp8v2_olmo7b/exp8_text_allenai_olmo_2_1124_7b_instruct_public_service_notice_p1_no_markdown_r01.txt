LIBRARY OPENING HOURS: PLEASE NOTE

The local library is open from Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 8 PM, and on Saturdays, 9 AM to 5 PM. The library is closed on Sundays and public holidays. We encourage all community members to visit us during these times to borrow books, access free Wi-Fi, attend events, and enjoy our welcoming space. Our hours are subject to change due to ongoing circumstances, so please check our website regularly for the most up-to-date information. Thank you for your support and cooperation in helping maintain a safe and accessible environment for everyone.
