A good launch note is clear and provides a snapshot of what the listener can expect from the podcast. It should include:

  • The learning goal, which can be in the form of a question, e.g. “What is Beyoncé saying when she sings, ‘Na na na na, na na na na, hey hey hey, good-bye’?”
  • The learning level of the podcast – beginner, intermediate, or advanced
  • The length of the podcast
  • The number of episodes
  • A description of the learning approach, e.g. “Learn to order pizza at a restaurant in Italian with Marco in this episode.”
  • A description of the pace of the learning, e.g. “This podcast is fast-paced and full of idiomatic expressions, so you might want to listen to this episode a few times to learn all the vocabulary and grammar.”

2. Introduction

The introduction of a language-learning podcast should be brief, with just enough information to orient the listener. It should include:

  • The host’s name and a brief description of their background and experience
  • The language being taught
  • The location where the podcast is recorded
  • The reasons for creating the podcast
  • A brief outline of what the listener will learn in this episode

3. Use of language

Language-learning podcasts should be instructive, so they should use the target language as much as possible. However, the host should also speak some English, especially in the beginning and end of the podcast to help the listener understand the context.

4. Pace

Language-learning podcasts should be recorded at a pace that allows the listener to learn. If the learner is a beginner, the host should slow down and explain every word and expression. If the learner is intermediate or advanced, the host can speak at a normal speaking rate.

5. Use of language

A common mistake hosts make is spending too much time on one expression or phrase. This can be very boring for listeners and actually hinders their learning. The host should instead focus on teaching a few key expressions or phrases that the listener can use in real life. The host should also avoid repeating the same examples over and over again.

6. Learning benefits

A good way to learn from a language-learning podcast is to listen to it for the first time, then listen to it again while taking notes. This will help you remember key vocabulary and grammatical structures.
