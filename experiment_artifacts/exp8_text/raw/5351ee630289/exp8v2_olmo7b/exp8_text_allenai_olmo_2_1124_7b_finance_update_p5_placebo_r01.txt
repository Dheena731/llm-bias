A short update is a paragraph of 1-2 sentences. You should give a short summary of the main point and your opinion; introduce a new topic and suggest how to deal with it; announce an event or planned action; or correct a previous mistake.

You need to be careful about what to write and how to write.

Some general tips about the structure of an update are given below:

  • Write a short opening sentence. This sentence should clearly state what the update is about.
  • Expand on the opening sentence. Add the most important points/facts/ideas about the topic in a few sentences. Make sure that these sentences follow the opening sentence one by one.
  • Avoid making more than three points/facts. If you have more, consider writing multiple updates.
  • The update should be at most 2 sentences long. More sentences may make it hard to read and understand, and make a point for the update too hard to find.
  • Introduce the update with an appropriate title. For more information about titles, see T128493.

Please feel free to ask for help from your colleagues and the Wikimedia Foundation staff. Most of our communication guidelines are based on this update, and are linked below.
