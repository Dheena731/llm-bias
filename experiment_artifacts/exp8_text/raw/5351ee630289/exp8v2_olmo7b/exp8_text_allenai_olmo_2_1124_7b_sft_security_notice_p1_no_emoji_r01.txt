ATTENTION ALL EMPLOYEES:

Please be aware that using the same password for multiple accounts, both inside and outside of our workplace, poses a significant security risk. If a hacker obtains your password from one account, they can potentially gain access to all of your accounts. To protect your personal and work-related information, please ensure you use unique passwords for each account. If you need help managing your passwords, our IT department is here to assist you. Thank you for your cooperation and commitment to maintaining good cybersecurity practices.
