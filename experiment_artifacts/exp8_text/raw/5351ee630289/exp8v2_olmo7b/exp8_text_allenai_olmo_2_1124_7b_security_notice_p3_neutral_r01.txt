Task 1

Last week, when I was passing a small park by the road, I saw a baby crying alone in his/her cot. The mother was probably in a hurry and left the baby there. Although it seems that the baby was not hungry or in pain, I felt very sad to see the little child all alone at such a young age. A couple of days later, I saw the same park and there was no cot and the child was nowhere around. I am very glad and feel much relieved that the child might have been picked up by its mother or parents. However, I still feel upset about the incident and wish there was a more responsible guardian to take care of babies.

Task 3

The fear of technology is another problem faced by many business firms. This includes both the computer and the internet. Many a times, it is seen that a business owner wishes to take the help of a technology, though he / she is not aware of it or how to use it. It is very important that business owners are not only aware of the technology but also know how to use it. This will help them to make a better business card and protect their customers from wrong information.

Task 4
