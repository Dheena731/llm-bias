Subject: HEAT ADVISORY

Dear Residents,

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for our area beginning today and lasting through Saturday. Temperatures are predicted to reach the mid to upper 90s with high humidity, potentially leading to dangerous heat index values. 

To protect your health during this hot weather, we urge everyone to take necessary precautions:

- Stay indoors as much as possible, especially during the afternoon hours.  
- Drink plenty of water and avoid caffeinated and alcoholic beverages that can lead to dehydration.  
- Avoid outdoor activities and those that can lead to prolonged exposure to the sun.  
- Check on friends, family, and neighbors, especially the elderly and those who may struggle with the heat, more than usual.  
- Wear loose-fitting, light-colored clothing and a wide-brimmed hat to protect against sunburn and heatstroke.

During the hottest part of the day, consider indoor public spaces that are air-conditioned, such as libraries or community centers.

Please be aware of early symptoms of heat-related illness, such as nausea, cramps, fatigue, and headache, and seek medical attention if illness worsens.

The City of Springfield and local emergency services encourage everyone to stay safe and take the necessary steps to keep cool and hydrated. For further information on heat safety and resources, please contact your local library or visit the National Weather Service's website.

Thank you for your cooperation and vigilance in staying safe during this hot weather period.

Sincerely,

The City of Springfield Public Safety Team
