In web applications, latency — the time it takes for a user's actions to be transmitted to a server and for a response to be returned — plays a critical role in the user experience. High latency leads to slow page load times, increased loading spinners, and overall dissatisfaction among users. In real-time communication applications, such as live chats and video conferencing, latency can cause significant lag and degrade interaction quality. Additionally, for e-commerce applications, a slow response time can result in missed sales opportunities and a negative impression of the brand. Therefore, optimizing latency is essential for maintaining user satisfaction, enhancing performance, and ensuring competitiveness in the digital marketplace.
