Notice: Recycling Collection Changes

Effective immediately, there will be updates to our recycling collection schedule and materials accepted. Starting on March 15th, we will no longer accept plastic bags, rubber tires, glass, and electronics in our curbside recycling bins. Residents are advised to dispose of these items at designated drop-off locations or use the provided resources for proper disposal. Check our website or contact us for further details and updates on acceptable materials. Thank you for your cooperation and continued effort in recycling responsibly.
