Q: Write a note to thank a newspaper for a donation.

Dear Sir,

Thank you very much for the donation of two big cartons of apples. They are much appreciated.

I’m sure that our pupils will enjoy them during the next week.

Q: Write a letter to ask the paper to explain the change in the price.

Dear Sir,

I am writing to ask why the price of the Sunday paper has increased by 10 cents over the last six months.

I have been buying it for years and I am not able to afford it.

I would appreciate if you could explain the reason for change.

Yours sincerely,

Q: Write a short letter to explain why you want to write a review about a product.

A: Dear Sir,

I am writing to explain why I want to write a review about a product.

Recently I decided to start my own business. I am going to sell cosmetics and I need to make a brochure with all about the products and a list of prices.

I would appreciate if you could send me all kind of information about your products and sizes.

I am also very interested in knowing how to make a catalogue.

Please feel free ro contact me.

Sincerely yours,

Q: Write a letter to a company to enquire whether it will be closed soon.

A: Dear Sir,

I am writing to enquire whether your company will close next year.

The reason why I’m asking you that question is because I want to know whether I need to look for another job.

There are many things I must do if a company is going to close.

As the time passes, I must prepare everything for my new job.

I would appreciate if you could tell me if the company is going to close next year.

With my best regards,

Q: Write a short note to thank someone for buying a present for your child.

Dear Mrs. Smith,

I am a very grateful person. I hope you know that.

I want to thank you for the birthday present you bought for my child.

He was really happy with it. He couldn’t stop playing with it.

I wish I could give you something in return.

If I find anything that you would like, I’ll let you know.

Yours sincerely,
