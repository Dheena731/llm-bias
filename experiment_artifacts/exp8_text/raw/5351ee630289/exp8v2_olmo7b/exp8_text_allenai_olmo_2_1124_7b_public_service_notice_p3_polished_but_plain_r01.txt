It is possible to use the notes to teach or encourage students to learn English. This is a great way to teach new vocabulary words. It’s also a great way to teach new grammatical concepts.

  • Other topics

You can even use the notes to teach useful phrases and vocabulary for everyday life, like how to order food and how to conduct yourself in public.

Students can use the notes for review during study breaks and to study up for exams. They can also use them to help them study when they are at home.
