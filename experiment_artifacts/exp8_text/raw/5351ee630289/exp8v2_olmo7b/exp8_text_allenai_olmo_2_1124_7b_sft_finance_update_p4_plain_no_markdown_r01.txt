Here's a short message explaining an end-of-year tax statement:

The end-of-year tax statement, often referred to as the tax form, is a document that summarizes an individual's or business's income and tax obligations for the tax year. This statement helps taxpayers understand their overall tax liability and ensures accuracy before submitting their tax returns. It includes details on income sources, deductions, and credits that can affect the total tax amount they owe or have already paid. Reviewing this document helps in making informed decisions when preparing for tax season and ensures compliance with tax laws.
