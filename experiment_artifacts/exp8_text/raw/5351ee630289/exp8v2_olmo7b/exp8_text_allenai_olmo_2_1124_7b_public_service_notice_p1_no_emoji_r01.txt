A public-service notice is a type Old English word. It is often written in all capital letters. Library opening hours are a good subject for a word. A good public-service notice contains the following elements:

First, Number: A public-service notice is a number. Make the number 4 or 6 or 7 or 12 or 15.

Second, Indicant: Indicant is a word that means "material for a numeral."

Third, Sequence: Sequence means "sequence of numbers."

Fourth, Time: Time is the time when the opening is taking place. The time usually starts with 12:00 am and ends with 8:00 pm.

Fifth, Other: Other means "other than those number."

Sixth, Time: Time is the actual time when the opening takes place.
