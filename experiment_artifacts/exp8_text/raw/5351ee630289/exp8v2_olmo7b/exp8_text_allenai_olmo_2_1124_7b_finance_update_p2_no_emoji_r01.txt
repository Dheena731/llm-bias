I agree with this explanation, http://www.topdowngamedesign.com/learning/beginning-the-lessons/learn-to-use-a-cash-register.html

Buy a card with 100 dkontos on it. To make this card work, you'll have to spend some amount, (let's say 2dkontos).

Spend 2dkontos to buy the card. Card is activated.

If you exit the card screen to the main screen you will lose the
card, and you must buy another card. (Risk/reward)

If you have less than 2dkontos you cannot buy the card

If you have 49dkontos (49dkontos = 2dkontos + 2dkontos + 2dkontos + 2dkontos + 1dkontos), you can buy the card, and receive 4dkontos change (49dkontos - 2dkontos = 4dkontos change).

If you have 5dkontos, you cannot buy the card, and you lose your 5dkontos (5dkontos - 2dkontos = -3dkontos).
