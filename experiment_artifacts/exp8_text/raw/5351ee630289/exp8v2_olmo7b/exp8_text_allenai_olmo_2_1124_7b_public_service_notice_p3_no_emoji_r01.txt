Examples of notices

1. Recycle Plastic

Plastic is not biodegradeable. It is better to recycle plastic. Please put your plastic bottle and plastic bags in the recycling bin. They will be recycled.

2. Drinking Water

We are changing the drinking water. It is bottled water. But do not worry. The water is safe and clean. It is tested every day.

3. This is a notice. We have shifted your classroom. You should go to room 265. The old room is for visitors. Please go to room 265.

4. Fire Drill

Fire drill will be held at 10:00 AM today. Please go to the nearest exit. Go out of the school building. The emergency personnel will check your identity. If you cannot hear the sound, please go out.

5. Public Holiday

Tomorrow is a public holiday. No classes will be held. If there is something urgent, come to school.

6. Library

The library is open today. If there are any books you would like to borrow, come to the library.

7. Time Change

We are changing the bells. The first bell will be 10:30 AM. And the last bell is 7:00 PM.

8. Computer Repair

The computer of room 221 is not working. Please go to room 219 for use. If you need computer, please go to room 221 after repair.

9. Bus Route

We have changed the bus route from P-1 to P-3. You will not go to the original bus stop. Please go to the new bus stop.

10. Birthday Party

Tomorrow is my birthday. Please come to my house for a birthday party.

11. Water

The water tap is leaking. Please use water from the other tap. The water will be repaired soon.

12. Broken Window

A window of classroom 221 is broken. Please go to classroom 220. The window will be repaired soon.

13. Parking

Please be careful when you park your car. Some places are reserved for disabled people.


A notice is a short information. It might contain important information about some changes or plans. Notices are usually posted to the buildings where people work or study.

We can write notices about important things. For example, the notice can be about broken windows, water, computers, buses and pipes. Sometimes, we write notices for an open party or a birthday party.

Notice writing will be more common in office buildings because they are often used for official announcements.

We can also write notices for our personal needs. For example, when we have a birthday or an open party, we can ask our friends to attend the party.

Modern office buildings use posters, emails and text messages to send information. However, it is shorter than a notice.

When we write a notice, we should not use abbreviations. The notice should be short and easy to understand. The notice should not contain any unnecessary information.
