When you're writing a short text, you have to use the link button. Then when you click the citation button, the cite button will pop up. On this button there's a little icon to which you click, and it will cite as mendeley and if you click it again, it will cite as Zotero. If you don't want to cite in your work, click the icon again and it'll remove the citation.
