REFUND POLICY - ELIGIBILITY STATEMENT

Refunds are available for all transactions during the refund period outlined in this document.

To be eligible for a refund, the following conditions must be met:

1. The customer must have made a purchase through our website or in person.
2. The product or service must be defective, damaged, or not as described in the product description.
3. The customer must have attempted to resolve the issue with the seller through our dispute resolution process before requesting a refund.
4. The customer must return the product or provide proof of destruction of the product within 30 days of receiving the item.
5. The customer must provide proof of purchase through a receipt or order confirmation.

Please note that the following refunds are not available:

1. Digital goods, software, or subscriptions purchased after the refund period has expired.
2. Refunds for custom or personalized products.
3. Refunds for items sold as-is or without a return policy.

Customers are advised to review this refund policy before making a purchase. If a refund is granted, the refund amount will be processed and issued to the original form of payment used for the purchase within 5-7 business days.
