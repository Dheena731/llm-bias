A missing-value marker, often represented by the symbol "#", denotes a position in a dataset where information is absent. This indicator serves as a placeholder to acknowledge that a particular observation, value, or measurement was not recorded or is missing. Data analysts use these markers to differentiate between actual data points and missing data, which is crucial for ensuring the integrity and accuracy of statistical analyses and model training. Failure to account for missing values appropriately might lead to biased results or incorrect conclusions.
