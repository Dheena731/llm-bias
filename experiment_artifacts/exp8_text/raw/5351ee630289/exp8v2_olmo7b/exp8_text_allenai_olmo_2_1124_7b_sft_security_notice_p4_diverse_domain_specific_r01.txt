Attention domain users!

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is now available for all accounts.

This feature adds an additional layer of security to your account by requiring not only your password but also a code sent to your email or phone when logging in.

To use two-factor authentication, please visit the “Settings” page under your profile menu, and look for the 2FA toggle switch.

Please note, once enabled, you cannot disable 2FA without contacting support.

We apologize for any inconvenience caused by this change, but the enhanced security measures are necessary to protect your account, and this feature is available at no additional cost.

Remember, your account information is safe with us.
