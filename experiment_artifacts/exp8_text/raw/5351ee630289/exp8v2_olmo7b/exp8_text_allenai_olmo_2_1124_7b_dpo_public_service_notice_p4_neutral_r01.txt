Due to this week's forecasted heatwave, a Heat Advisory has been issued for the following areas: [list affected areas].

To protect the health and well-being of residents, the following precautions are advised:

* Check on family members, friends, and neighbors, especially those who are elderly, sick, or infants, who are more vulnerable to extreme heat.
* Stay indoors as much as possible during the hottest parts of the day.
* Keep homes and workplaces cool through the use of air conditioning, fans, and door and window insulation if necessary.
* Limit outdoor activities and those that are strenuous to early morning or late evening hours.
* Wear loose, light-colored, and loose-fitting clothes to reflect heat and promote ventilation.
* Drink plenty of water even if you do not feel thirsty, and avoid beverages that can lead to dehydration such as alcohol and caffeinated drinks.
* Avoid leaving children or pets in parked cars.
* Check on pets frequently, provide them with shade, water, and a cool place to rest.
* Use a stove, oven, and other heat-producing appliances sparingly to keep indoor temperatures down.
* If you do not have air conditioning, consider visiting public places with air conditioning, such as libraries, malls, or theaters.
* Be cautious of power outages and take necessary precautions to stay safe.
