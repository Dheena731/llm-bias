An end-of-year tax statement, also known as a tax summary or annual tax statement, is a document typically provided by employers or tax authorities towards the end of each fiscal year. This document details the total income, deductions, and the resultant tax obligation or refund that an individual or entity is entitled to for that year. The tax statement helps taxpayers to understand how much they owe in taxes or if they are eligible for a refund due to overpaid taxes throughout the year.

Key information usually found in an end-of-year tax statement includes:

1. **Total earnings:** The sum of all income earned, including wages, salaries, bonuses, and any other sources of income reported to the tax authorities.
  
2. **Taxable income:** This may include items that have been taxed and deducted as part of the income, such as contributions to retirement plans or health savings accounts (HSAs).
  
3. **Deductions and credits:** A breakdown of all the eligible deductions and credits the taxpayer has been able to claim, such as those for dependent care, education, or charitable donations. This detail is crucial as it reflects the total amount eligible for tax reduction.
  
4. **Tax payable/Refund:** The calculation of how much the taxpayer owes in taxes or, conversely, how much they might be due a refund if they overpaid their taxes throughout the year.

5. **Employer’s contributions:** This may include details on how much the employer has contributed to the employee's retirement or health insurance plans, and how these contributions affect the total taxable income.

The end-of-year tax statement serves as a vital tool in preparing individual tax returns, as it provides accurate and consolidated information on income and deductions. It helps taxpayers ensure they have correctly calculated their tax liabilities or identify any discrepancies with their expected refund. Additionally, for taxpayers who employ a tax professional, the statement simplifies the preparation process by offering immediate access to all the necessary financial information for the year.
