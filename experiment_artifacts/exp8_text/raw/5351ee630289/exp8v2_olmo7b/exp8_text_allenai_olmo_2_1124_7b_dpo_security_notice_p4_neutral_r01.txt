We are excited to announce an enhanced security feature for your account: Two-Factor Authentication (2FA). This additional layer of security requires you to verify your identity in two ways before accessing your account. 

**Why Enable 2FA?**

- **Extra Security:** 2FA significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access.
- **Peace of Mind:** Protect your sensitive information and transactions.
- **Compliance:** Many websites and services recommend 2FA for an added level of security.

**How to Enable 2FA:**

1. **Log in** to your account as usual.
2. **Navigate to the Account Settings** page.
3. Look for the 'Security' or 'Two-Factor Authentication' section.
4. **Select Two-Factor Authentication** and follow the on-screen instructions to set it up.
5. **Choose Your 2FA Method:** You can opt for SMS, authenticator app (e.g., Google Authenticator, Authy), or a hardware token.
6. **Complete the Setup:** Follow the prompts to set up your 2FA method of choice.

**Note:** Ensure you have a reliable phone or app to receive SMS notifications or the authentication codes.
