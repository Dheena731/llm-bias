Subject: Reminder: Protect Our Information – Always Lock Your Screens!

Dear Team,

As part of our ongoing commitment to maintaining a secure and efficient workplace, we want to remind everyone about the importance of safeguarding our digital data. A critical part of this effort is ensuring that all of us lock our computer screens whenever we step away from our desks.

Unlocked screens are vulnerable to unauthorized access, which can lead to serious consequences, including the potential loss of sensitive client information, business data, and personal details. By locking your screen, you protect not just your work, but the whole team's productivity and reputation.

Here are some simple steps to lock your screen:

1. **Keyboard Shortcut**: Press **Ctrl + Alt + Delete** on your keyboard. This will bring up your login screen, allowing you to securely lock your computer.
2. **System Settings**: Go to **Start -> Settings -> Accounts -> Sign-in** to change your lock screen settings and customize your security options.

Let’s make it a habit to lock our screens every time we leave our desk, repair workspace, or take a break. Your diligence in adhering to this protocol is vital to keeping our workplace secure and reliable.

Thank you for your cooperation and continued vigilance. Let’s keep our workspace safe!
