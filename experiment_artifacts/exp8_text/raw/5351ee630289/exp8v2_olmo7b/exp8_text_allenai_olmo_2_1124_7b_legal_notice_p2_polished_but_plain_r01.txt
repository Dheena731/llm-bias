The notification should be worded as if it were written for the average user to read. It should not contain any technical jargon or language specific to attorneys or the legal system.

The notice should refer to the updated privacy policy whenever possible. It should explain how the user can view the policy, and what they should do if they have any concerns about how their data is being used.

Dear User,

We have updated our privacy policy to make it easier for you to understand and make informed choices about how your data is used. You can read more in our new policy at [LINK TO NEW PRIVACY POLICY]. To learn more about what we do with your data, please see our updated [LINK TO NEW PRIVacy POLICY].

If you have any questions about this notification or our updated privacy policy, please feel free to [EMAIL US].

Thank you for your understanding,

[Company Name]

Here is an example of what not to do:

Our privacy policy has been updated to incorporate recent changes in the law. We have implemented a new data usage policy in accordance with the law. Any changes to the way we use your data must be clearly stated in our privacy policy.

This notification is bad because it uses a lot of legal jargon, it doesn't refer to the actual policy, and it doesn't explain what users should do if they have any questions.

The Best Way to Notify Users

To notify users about changes to your privacy policy, I recommend using a mailing list or in-app notification to alert them. This way, you can ensure that everyone who may be affected by the change will get the message.

You should also consider posting the message on your social media accounts, but be careful not to spam your followers with irrelevant posts.

Be sure to include links to both the updated policy and the original policy in your notification. This will allow users to see what changes have been made and what they mean for them.

Finally, make sure that you offer some way for users to contact you with questions about the policy changes. This could be a phone number or email address, or even a live chat feature on your site.
