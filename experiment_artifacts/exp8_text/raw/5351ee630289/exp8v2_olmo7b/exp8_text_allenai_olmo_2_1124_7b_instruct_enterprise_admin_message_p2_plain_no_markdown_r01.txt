We are excited to announce a new expense approval workflow designed to streamline and improve our financial management process. Starting next Monday, employees will follow this updated procedure to request and approve expenses.

Here’s a brief outline of the new workflow:

1. **Submission:** Employees will submit expense reports via the newly updated online portal, ensuring all necessary details are included before submission.

2. **First Review:** The submitted report will be immediately reviewed by a designated colleague from the finance department. This initial check is intended to ensure all required information is present.

3. **Second Review:** After the first review, the report will proceed to a second verifier, selected at random from your team, to conduct a more thorough assessment of the expenses.

4. **Approval:** Once both reviews are passed, the report will be forwarded to the manager for final approval. The manager has 48 hours to approve or request additional documentation.

5. **Payment:** Upon approval, the finance department will process payment within 7 business days.

We encourage everyone to familiarize themselves with these new steps and provide feedback if you encounter any issues during the transition. The goal is to make our expense management process more efficient and transparent for all.
