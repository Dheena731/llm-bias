To make the announcement short and precise, use active voice, the present tense, and the first-person point of view.

To begin your letter, use an appropriate greeting: Dear Dr. Jones or Dear Mr./Ms. Smith.

Compose the body of your letter by describing the details of the changes and why they are necessary. Be respectful and considerate.

To end your letter, use a closing, such construction as Sincerely, or Best regards.

Write your name underneath, and then type a four-space "signature" for each new line, using all capital letters. Print out the final copy and sign your name above the typed signature.

Suggested Format:

Greeting (such as Dear Dr. Jones or Dear Mr./Ms. Smith)

In the first paragraph, state the main idea of the letter, such as recycling collection changes or a staff meeting.

In the body, include the "who" (give an overview of who will be affected and what changes are required), "what" (state the changes clearly), "when" (include the date of the changes), "where" (give a location or identify who should contact you for further information), and "why" (give a good reason for the changes).

Use a courteous tone. Respectfully state your position, but give consideration to the other person's point of view.

In the closing, use a closing such as Sincerely, or Best regards.

Print out the final copy and sign your name above the typed signature.
