**Notice of Acceptable Use Policy for Shared Network**

Welcome to our shared network. As a user of this network, you are required to adhere to the following guidelines to ensure a secure, efficient, and respectful environment for all users. Failure to comply may result in suspension or termination of your network access.

1. **Unauthorized Access**: You must not attempt to gain access to the network or any part thereof through unauthorized means. This includes hacking, password theft, or any other form of unauthorized intrusion.

2. **Inappropriate Content**: You must not use the network to share, send, or receive content that is illegal, offensive, racist, sexist, toxic, threatening, or harassing. This includes but is not limited to, materials violating intellectual property rights, pornography, or spam.

3. **Network Resources**: You must not misuse or abuse network resources. This includes excessive downloads or uploads which may affect the performance of the network for other users. Sharing copyrighted materials without permission is strictly prohibited.

4. **Security**: You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account and password. You must not share your password with others or allow unauthorized access to your account. Regularly change your password.

5. **Abuse**: You must not use the network to send unsolicited or excessive advertising, promotional material, junk mail, or spam. Spamming is a serious violation of this policy and may lead to immediate disconnection.

6. **Monitoring and Enforcement**: The network administrator reserves the right to monitor network activity to ensure compliance with this acceptable use policy. Violations will be subject to disciplinary action, ranging from warning notices to termination of service, at the discretion of the network administrator.

7. **Changes to Policy**: The network administrator may modify this acceptable use policy at any time. Your continued use of the network after any such modification shall constitute your consent to follow the updated policy.

By accessing and using the shared network, you agree to be bound by these terms and conditions. If you have any questions about this acceptable use policy, please refer to our administrative contact or visit our website for more information.

Thank you for your cooperation and responsible use of our shared network environment.
