Latency refers to the delay between when a request is sent and when a response is received. In web applications, You need to ensure that your application responds quickly to requests so that users do not have a negative experience. For instance, if you are building an online store, it is important that users' orders get processed quickly and accurately. If there is a long delay in processing orders, users might get frustrated and leave, which could negatively affect your business.
You should also consider how latency affects other aspects of your web application, such city traffic can affect latency, so it is important to test your application under different conditions to ensure that it performs well even when there are issues with the network infrastructure.
Finally, keep in mind that users may not notice a delay of a few milliseconds, but they will definitely notice a delay of a few seconds, so it is important to keep latency as low as possible for the best possible user experience.
