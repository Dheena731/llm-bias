Subject: Lost Company Laptop - Reporting Procedure

Dear Team,

I hope this message finds you well. I am writing to bring to your attention a critical issue that requires immediate attention. If you have inadvertently lost a company-issued laptop or believe it to be missing, please follow the outlined procedure below to report the incident promptly:

**Immediate Steps to Take:**

1. **Assess the Situation:** Check common areas, desks, meeting rooms, and any locations where the laptop might have been left behind.
2. **Secure the Area:** If the laptop is still within a secured location, ensure the area is secured to prevent further loss or unauthorized access.
3. **Document the Loss:** Note down specific details of the lost item: make, model, unique identifiers (e.g., serial number), last known location, and any other descriptors.
4. **Report the Incident:** Complete the "Lost Company Laptop Report" form available on our intranet or by contacting our IT department. Attach any relevant documentation to your report.
5. **Notify Your Supervisor:** Inform your immediate supervisor or HR about the incident if they are not already aware. They can provide guidance and oversight during the resolution process.
6. **File a police report:** If applicable, and depending on the value of the lost item, file a police report to document the loss officially.

**Follow-Up:**

- Our IT department will conduct an investigation to track the device if possible.
- Restitution and replacement measures will be coordinated as necessary.
- A follow-up will be conducted to ensure the incident has been fully addressed and to learn from the experience.

This incident is considered sensitive and requires discretion. Please refrain from discussing the specifics outside the necessary channels until further notice.

For further information or questions, please reach out to our IT Help Desk or your immediate supervisor.

Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter. Let's ensure our collective responsibility to company assets remains strong and vigilant.
