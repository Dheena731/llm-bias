Export Query Results: To download your search results directly from the platform, locate the 'Download' or 'Export' button usually found at the top right corner of the query results page. Ensure you have the latest version of the data format you need (e.g., CSV, Excel). Click the button, follow the on-screen instructions to choose your preferred format, and save the file to your device. This action allows you to carry and use the data offline or elsewhere, ensuring precision and ease of analysis tailored to your specific requirements.
