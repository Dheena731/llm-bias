A heat advisory is a warning that high heat and humidity levels may cause serious health problems for some people, such as young children, older adults, and people with chronic illnesses. Heat advisories are issued by state and local governments when temperatures and humidity levels are expected to reach dangerous levels.

The purpose of a heat advisory is to alert residents to the danger and encourage them to take precautions to protect themselves and their families from the risk of heat-related illness or death. Heat advisories typically include advice on how to stay cool and safe, such as staying indoors in air-conditioned rooms, drinking plenty of fluids, and avoiding strenuous activity.
