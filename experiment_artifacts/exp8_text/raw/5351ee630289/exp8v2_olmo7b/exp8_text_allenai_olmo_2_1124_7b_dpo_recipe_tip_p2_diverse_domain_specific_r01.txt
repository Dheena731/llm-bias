Storing Fresh Herbs: The Green Grip

No green thumb needed! Here's a trick to keep your herbs fresh and fragrant for longer. Gently remove them from their packaging and place them stem-end down in a glass of water (just like cut flowers). Secure with a rubber band, keep them away from direct sunlight, and change the water every few days. Voilà! Your herbs will thank you, and your meals will be enriched with their fresh essence.
