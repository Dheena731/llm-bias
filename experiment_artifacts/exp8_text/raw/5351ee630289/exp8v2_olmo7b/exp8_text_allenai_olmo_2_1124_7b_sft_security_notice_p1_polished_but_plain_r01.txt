Subject: Reminder: Avoid Password Reuse for Enhanced Security

Dear Team,

As part of our commitment to maintaining a secure work environment, we want to remind everyone about the importance of password management. Password reuse can compromise the integrity of our systems and put sensitive information at risk. It’s crucial to create strong, unique passwords for each of your accounts.

To help you remember and manage your passwords effectively, consider using a reputable password manager. These tools are designed to store and generate complex passwords, reducing the temptation to reuse them. Additionally, enabling two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security to your accounts, ensuring that only authorized personnel can access them.

Please take a moment to reflect on your current password practices and make any necessary changes to ensure our collective security. Thank you for your cooperation and vigilance in safeguarding our workplace.
