**Election Day Polling Station Locations: Your Key to Voting**

Attention Electoral District 42A Residents!

As we gear up for Election Day, we understand the importance of ensuring you have all the information you need to participate in our democratic process. Below are the specific polling station locations where you can cast your vote:

**Primary Polling Stations:**

1. **Westridge Community Centre**  
   - 123 Democracy Avenue, Westridge  
   - Address confirmed, entrance via the main silver entrance

2. **Mapleton Library**  
   - 456 Knowledge Way, Mapleton  
   - Close to the entrance next to the Community Board

**Additional Polling Stations for Convenience:**

3. **Mobile Unit #2**  
   - Parked adjacent to City Hall  
   - Identification will be required for access

4. **Temporary Station at Maple High School Auditorium**  
   - 789 Election Street, Maple  
   - Adjacent to the main entrance, wheelchair accessible

**Key Tips for Polling Day:**

- **ID Proof:** Bring a current government-issued photo ID.
- **Advance Voting:** If you've already voted, see the Advance Polling Station at City Hall by 9 AM.
- **Accessibility:** All polling stations are accessible. Contact us if you need further assistance.

Remember, your vote matters profoundly. We encourage early voting or bringing someone with you to support. For any inquiries or technical difficulties, contact our helpline at 123-456-VOTE.

Don't miss your chance to influence the future. Visit your polling station today!

**Best Regards,  
The Electoral Commission of Electoral District 42A**
