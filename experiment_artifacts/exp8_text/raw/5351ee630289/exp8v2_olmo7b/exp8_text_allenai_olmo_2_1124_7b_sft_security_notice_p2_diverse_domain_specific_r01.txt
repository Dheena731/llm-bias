To all members of the company,

Please be on the lookout for phishing attempts from unknown sources. Be especially cautious of emails purporting to be from our company or other trusted domains. These attempts are becoming more sophisticated and may contain a variety of motivations, from financial gain to damaging our work environment.

The following are signs that an email may be a phishing attempt:
- The sender is unknown or their email address does not match the domain they claim to be from.
- The sender requests immediate action, such as confirming personal information or clicking on a link.
- The email has poor spelling and grammar, or other signs of being amateurishly written.

If you encounter any potential phishing attempts, please report them to the IT department. Do not click on any suspicious links or share information with unknown parties. Our IT department is here to help!
