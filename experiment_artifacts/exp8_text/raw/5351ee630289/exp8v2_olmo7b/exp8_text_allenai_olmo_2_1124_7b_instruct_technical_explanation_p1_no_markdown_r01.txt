Latency in web applications refers to the delay between a user's action and the system's response. It is a critical factor as it directly impacts perceived performance and user experience. In today’s fast-paced digital world, users expect immediate interaction and swift loading times. High latency can lead to user frustration, decreased satisfaction, and ultimately, a higher bounce rate. This can harm a website’s SEO and reputation, deter potential customers, and reduce overall engagement. Conversely, low latency ensures a smooth and responsive experience, enhancing user engagement and satisfaction. As a result, reducing latency is essential for maintaining a competitive edge and delivering a high-quality service in the online space.
