A simple yet effective way to plan lunches for a busy week is to prepare meals in advance. Cook larger portions on Sundays and divide them into individual lunch portions. Store these in reusable containers or plastic bags. This method not only saves time by reducing daily cooking, but also ensures consistency and minimizes decision fatigue as you already know what you're eating each day. Additionally, you can incorporate a variety of nutrients by planning meals around whole grains, lean proteins, and colorful vegetables. This plan works well for busy schedules as it simplifies your morning routine and encourages healthy eating habits.
