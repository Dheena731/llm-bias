To export query results, follow these steps: First, ensure you have the data you wish to export selected in the search or query interface. Next, look for an icon or option labeled 'Export' or 'Download' typically located at the top or right corner of the results display. Clicking on this will trigger a file download prompt on your device, allowing you to save the data in a desired file format, such as CSV, Excel, or PDF. Always double-check the filename and location before confirming the download to avoid overwriting existing files or losing data.
