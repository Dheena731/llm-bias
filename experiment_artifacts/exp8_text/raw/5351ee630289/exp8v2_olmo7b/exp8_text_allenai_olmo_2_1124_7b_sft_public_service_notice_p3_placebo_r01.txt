Subject: Update on Recycling Collection Changes

Dear Residents,

We hope this message finds you well. We are writing to inform you about recent changes to our recycling collection schedule and guidelines. As part of our ongoing commitment to sustainability, we have made adjustments to ensure the most efficient and effective use of resources.

Effective next month, our recycling collection days will move to the following schedule:

- Curbside pickup will be on Wednesdays instead of Fridays.
- Bulk recycling drop-off hours have been extended to accommodate our new schedule and to provide additional convenience for our residents.

To align with these changes, we have updated our recycling guidelines as follows:

- Please ensure that all recyclables are clean and free from food or liquid residues.
- Items that previously required rinsing, such as plastic containers and glass jars, must now be completely clean before placing them in your recycling bin.
- We have added a new category for electronic waste, which can now be dropped off at our designated facility. Please segregate such items for proper disposal.

We understand these changes may require some adjustments to your routine, and we appreciate your understanding and cooperation. Our goal is to provide a more environmentally friendly service that benefits everyone.

Please refer to the attached guide for more detailed information on the revised recycling schedule and guidelines. Should you have any questions or need assistance, do not hesitate to contact our customer service team.

Thank you for your continued support and dedication to our community's recycling efforts.
