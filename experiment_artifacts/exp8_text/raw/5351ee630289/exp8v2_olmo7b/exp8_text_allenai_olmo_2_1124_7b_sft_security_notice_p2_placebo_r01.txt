Subject: Important: Be Cautious with Phishing Emails

Dear Team,

We want to remind everyone about the importance of being cautious when dealing with emails that appear to be from us, especially those asking for personal or financial information. Phishing emails, which are designed to deceive and steal sensitive information, are unfortunately quite common.

To keep our workplace safe and secure, we ask you to:

- **Verify the source:** Always check the sender's email address carefully. If it seems suspicious, contact our IT department before responding or clicking on any links.
  
- **Avoid sharing information:** Do not provide personal or financial information unless you are certain about the legitimacy of the request.
  
- **Use two-factor authentication:** Enable this added layer of security wherever possible.

- **Educate your colleagues:** Share this notice with your colleagues, as these phishing attempts may target multiple employees.

If you receive a phishing email, please:

- Do not respond or click on any links.
  
- Forward the email to our IT department at phishing@our-company.com.

Thank you for your vigilance and cooperation. Together, we can safeguard our company's data and maintain a secure work environment.
