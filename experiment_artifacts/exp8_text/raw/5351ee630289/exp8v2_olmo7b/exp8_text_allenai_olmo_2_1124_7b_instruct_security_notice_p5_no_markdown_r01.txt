**Notice: Reporting a Lost Company Laptop**

Dear Employees,

We wish to remind you that the loss of a company laptop is not only a concern for security but also a serious matter that needs immediate attention. If you have lost your company-issued laptop, we kindly request that you report this incident as soon as possible through the following steps:

1. **Contact IT Support**: Immediately call the IT helpdesk at [insert phone number] or email support@[insert email address] to notify them of the loss.

2. **Fill Out a Lost Item Form**: Visit our intranet or stop by the HR office to complete the lost item form. This form includes crucial details that help in tracking and securing your information.

3. **Provide Details**: Be prepared to provide information about the laptop, such as its make, model, serial number, and any unique identifiers. Additionally, note the last known location and time of loss.

4. **Act Quickly**: The sooner we are informed, the quicker we can take necessary actions to protect your data and prevent unauthorized access. Remember, sensitive company information and personal data could be at risk if the laptop is not recovered promptly.

Please treat this matter with the urgency it deserves. Thank you for your cooperation and attention to this important matter.
