In general: Write a confidence interval like this:

We are (99%) confident that the true value of falls between  and .

If necessary, use a more specific confidence level, such as (95%). For more information, see "What is confidence interval?".

Finally, write a headline for the tooltip as if it were a blog post: a subject followed by a colon and a sentence summarizing the rest of the tooltip.

Here's an example:

We are (99%) confident that falls between  and .

You can find more examples in the chart-library/tooltip-confidence-interval/README.md.
