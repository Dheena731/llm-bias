This notice concerns the eligibility for refunds for services and products purchased. If a refund request is made within the refund period, the refund will be issued after the receipt of the request, subject to the terms and conditions specified in the service or product description. Refund requests should be submitted in writing to the relevant department at the seller's address, along with all necessary documentation. Refunds will be processed and issued promptly once the request has been reviewed and approved. For more information, please refer to the terms and conditions accompanying your purchase.
