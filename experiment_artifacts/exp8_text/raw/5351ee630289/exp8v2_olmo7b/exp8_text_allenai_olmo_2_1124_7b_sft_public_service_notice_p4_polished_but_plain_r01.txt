The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for most of the state from 3 PM Sunday through 8 PM Monday.

The heat index is expected to reach or exceed 105 degrees in many areas. A heat advisory is issued when the heat index is expected to be above 105 degrees for at least 2 consecutive days or when the heat index will be 115 or higher for any period of 3 consecutive hours.

So pack your sunscreen and stay hydrated. And if you're not able to stay hydrated, stay home and rest.
