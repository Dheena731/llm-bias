School District E

You are a substitute teacher. A student has been injured on the playground by a classmate who accidentally kicked him in the leg. The student has no injuries other than some scratches. He is afraid to go back on the playground with kids he does not know. He says that some of the students are not nice and he does not want to play with them. He is very upset.

The student is in second grade. He was brought to you by a classmate and the crossing guard. What should you do first? What should you do next?

School District F

Your school district employs a full-time nurse who is also an emergency medical technician (EMT). She is very knowledgeable about first aid. She is also a certified primary responder. Students and staff members require her services frequently.

All of the schools in the district have a first aid kit available in the office. The district also provides a first aid kit for each classroom. The nurse is notified immediately when any student is injured at school. She is also present at all required school athletic events. The nurse works with the physical education teacher and coaches and school maintenance staff to ensure that first aid kits are available at all intramural activities.

When the nurse is not at school, BSA troop leaders are available to provide immediate first aid. Scouts and school staff are cross-trained in CPR and first-aid procedures.

School District G

Your school district has a policy that all staff members must be trained in basic first aid and CPR. In addition, your district has a First Aid/Basic Life Support (FA/ BLS) class and an Emergency Medical Responder (EMR) class. All staff members are expected to take and pass FA/BLS. Some staff members, including the nurse and the athletic trainer, take EMR. All staff members who take EMR receive a pay increase for the certification and training.

Your district also has an agreement with a local ambulance company to dispatch an ambulance to any school for trained personnel or equipment if an injury occurs and the nurse is not on duty. This is an emergency service only.

Your district uses a system of first aid kits and emergency kits, but these are available only in the office. The kits are kept by the school nurse and others as indicated on a key. The school nurse keeps portable first-aid kits in her office and one in her van. The district nurse uses the kits to train other staff members and students in her school district FA/BLS class.

What should a school district do to provide a comprehensive first aid and emergency response training program for staff members and students?

School District H

Your school district has a policy that states: “All students and staff members must receive training in first-aid and CPR procedures, and all staff members must receive EMR training.”

In addition, the district has a policy that allows the district nurse and designates to provide first aid according: to the district nurse’s training and certification. These include school nurses, emergency medical technicians, and paramedics.

The district has a policy that all staff members must carry a first aid kit with them at all times, including their classrooms, and first-aid kits are available in the school office.

The district has a policy that all staff members and volunteers are required to give first aid to students on the school’s property or at an official school sponsored function.

Your district has a “right to know” policy that requires school personnel to:

• Provide a list of all personnel trained in first-aid and CPR.

• Provide a list of personnel trained in EMR.

• Provide a list of designated personnel who are authorized to give first aid.

• Provide a list of all personnel, including the district nurse, who are authorized to provide first aid to students.

• Provide a list of the first aid kits available.
