Pursuant to the terms and conditions outlined in our Shared Network Services Agreement, the following constitutes the acceptable use policy for the shared network services provided by [Network Provider Name].

**1. Scope of Acceptable Use:**

   The shared network is intended for use by authorized personnel for legitimate business, educational, and informational purposes. The network is not to be used for any activity which:

   * Infringes upon the intellectual property rights of others.
   * Harms minors in any way.
   * Involves the transmission of or solicitation of pornography or any other illegal material.
   * Disrupts network operation or service to any user.
   * Involves network fraud or hacking.
   * Includes sending unsolicited bulk email.

**2. Restrictions:**

   * **Unauthorized Access:** No individual shall attempt to access the network or its systems, accounts, or networks, through unauthorized means.

   * **Spam and Unsolicited Email:** Users are prohibited from sending unsolicited bulk email or spam messages within the shared network.
   * **Malware Distribution:** Distributing or attempting to distribute malware or viruses within the network is strictly prohibited.

   The network provider reserves the right to monitor network activity to ensure compliance with this acceptable use policy. Infringement upon these policies may result in disciplinary action, including the suspension or termination of user privileges without notice.

**4. Amendment:**

   This policy may be amended by the network provider at any time. Amendments will be communicated electronically to all affected users and will become effective upon posting.

**5. Compliance:**

   Users are responsible for their own actions and must ensure compliance with this acceptable use policy. Failure to comply may result in strict legal action, including but not limited to, civil liability, fines, and/or criminal charges.
