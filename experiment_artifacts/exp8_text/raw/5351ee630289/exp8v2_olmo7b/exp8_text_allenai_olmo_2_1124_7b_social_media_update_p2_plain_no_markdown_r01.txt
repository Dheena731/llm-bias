Abdication crisis of Edward VIII

The Abdication crisis of Edward VIII is the 1936 event in which King Edward VIII of the United Kingdom and the Dominions (excluding the Republic of Ireland) and Emperor of India proposed to marry Wallis Simpson, an American socialite who had divorced her first husband and accused of involving herself with many married men, while he was still married to his wife, who was the daughter of George V.
