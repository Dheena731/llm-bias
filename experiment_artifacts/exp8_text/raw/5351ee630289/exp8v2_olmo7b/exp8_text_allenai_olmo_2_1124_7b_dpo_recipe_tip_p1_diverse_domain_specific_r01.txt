Tip: Embrace the 'One-Pot Wonder' for a stress-free weeknight meal. Opt for dishes like stews, curries, or soups that cook slowly, infusing flavors and tenderizing ingredients with minimal fuss. Start with a base of sautéed veggies, add protein (chickpeas, chicken, beef), your choice of broth or liquid, and a handful of spices. Let it simmer on low heat while you attend to other evening tasks, ensuring a warm, comforting dinner is ready when you are. This method cuts down on cleaning time and leaves you with more energy to relax after a long day. Remember, simplicity is key to a successful weeknight dinner.
