To enhance the flavor of roasted vegetables, you can drizzle them with a mixture of olive oil, balsamic vinegar, minced garlic, and a pinch of dried herbs like rosemary or thyme just before they go into the oven. Toss the vegetables well to ensure even coating, and then let them roast until they’re tender and nicely browned, while the seasonings meld together and create a deliciously aromatic and savory dish.
