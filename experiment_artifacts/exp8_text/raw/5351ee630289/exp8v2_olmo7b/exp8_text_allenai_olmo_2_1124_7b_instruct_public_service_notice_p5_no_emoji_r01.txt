Polling Station Locations:

Please note the following polling station locations for your convenience:

1. **Greenwood Village**

   - Address accessible 24 hours

2. **Oakwood Apartments**

   - Accessible from 7 AM to 7 PM

3. **Central Plaza Mall**
   - 789 Main Street, Near Entrance C

All polling stations are open from 7 AM to 7 PM on Election Day. For further information, please contact the electoral office at 555-1234.
