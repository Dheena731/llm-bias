**EMERGENCY HEAT ADVISORY: SAFETY PROTOCOLS IN EFFECT**

Attention Residents: A severe and life-threatening heatwave is upon us. Excessive heat warnings are in place for all municipalities within the region, calling for extraordinary caution and preparedness over the coming days. Temperatures are expected to soar beyond 110°F (43°C), coupled with humidity levels that could render the atmosphere dangerous to both humans and animals.

The following safety protocols are imperative to adhere to:

**1. Stay Informed and Hydrate:** Check the local weather updates hourly and heed any additional advisories issued by emergency services. Ensure you and your loved ones are drinking plenty of water and non-alcoholic beverages.

**2. Avoid Outdoor Activities:** Between 10 a.m. and 4 p.m., when the heat is most intense, seek shelter indoors and limit activities that could overexert you.

**3. Check on the Vulnerable:** Elderly, children, and individuals with health conditions are at high risk. Make sure they are given special attention and not left unattended in high temperatures.

**4. Create a Cool Environment:** Ensure your home and workplace are equipped with air conditioning. Close curtains and window blinds during the day to block out heat. Dress in light-colored, loose-fitting clothing to stay cool.

**5. Be Vigilant:** Never leave pets or people alone in parked cars. Constantly check on the wellbeing of individuals outdoors and ensure they have adequate shade and hydration.

**6. Mind the Signs:** Listen for weather alerts and follow instructions from local authorities. Evacuation orders may be implemented if conditions worsen.

**7. Plan for Emergency Situations:** Have a go-kit ready with water, non-perishable food, medication, and emergency contact numbers. Know the locations and operation times of local cooling centers.

**8. Prioritize Health:** Heat-related illnesses can happen quickly. Recognize symptoms such as dizziness, nausea, or fainting and seek medical attention immediately.
