Attention Residents!

We are pleased to announce some changes to our recycling collection schedule to help make things easier and more efficient for everyone. Starting Monday, September 25th, our recycling collections will now follow a new two-week schedule instead of the previous once-a-week collection.  

To make the new schedule stick, we will have collection days set on the following Tuesdays and Fridays: 

- Tuesday: Paper, cardboard, and containers
- Friday: Plastics, metals, glass, and other materials

Make sure to check your calendar for the dates as there are no changes to the routes. 

The new schedule will give you more flexibility with your recycling and leave more time in between collections. It is also a more manageable schedule for our collection team, so they can continue to provide a high level of service to our community. We hope you can rise to the occasion and make these changes part of your routine. 

If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to our customer service line and we will be happy to assist you. Thank you for your continued cooperation and support for this new initiative!
