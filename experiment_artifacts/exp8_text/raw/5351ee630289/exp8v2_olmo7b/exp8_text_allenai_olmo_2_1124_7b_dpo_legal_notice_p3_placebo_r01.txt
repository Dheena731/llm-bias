3. **Functionality Cookies:** Functional cookies remember choices you make such as language preference or the products you add to your wish list, enhancing your user experience.

4. **Targeting/Advertising Cookies:** We may use these cookies to display advertisements relevant to your interests both on our website and other sites.

**Managing Cookies**

You can control cookies through your web browser settings. Most browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can alter the settings to refuse all or some cookies. Below are steps on how to control cookie settings for various browsers:

- **Google Chrome:** Click on the three dots in the upper right corner, then “Settings,” then “Privacy and security,” then “Cookies and other site data,” and adjust your settings accordingly.
- **Mozilla Firefox:** Go to the three bars in the upper right corner, then “Options,” then “Privacy & Security,” then “Cookies and Site Data,” and configure your settings.
- **Microsoft Edge:** Click on the three dots in the upper right corner, then “Settings,” then “Privacy, search, and services,” and go to “Enhanced privacy protection” to manage cookies.
- **Safari (for Mac and iOS):** Go to the Safari menu, then “Preferences...”, then “Privacy.” Under “Website Data,” you can manage cookies and other site data.

Please note that disabling cookies may affect your ability to use certain features of Our Website.
