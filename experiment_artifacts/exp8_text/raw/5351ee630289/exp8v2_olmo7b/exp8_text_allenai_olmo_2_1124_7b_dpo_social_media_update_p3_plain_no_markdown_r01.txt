This weekend, our wonderful community is coming together for the annual Fall Harvest Festival! Whether you're a long-time resident or just passing through, there's something for everyone. From the biggest pumpkin pie contest to live music on the main stage, we promise a day filled with fun, friendly faces, and delectable treats. Stop by to enjoy the fall atmosphere, participate in our charity auction, or simply stroll through the lively marketplace. Bring your friends, your family, and your appetite! Remember, the festival runs from 10 AM to 6 PM on Saturday and Sunday, right here in the heart of our beautiful town square. Don't miss out – see you there!
