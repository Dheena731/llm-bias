Subject: Important Reminder: Password Reuse is Not Accepted

Dear Team,

As we strive to maintain the highest standards of data security, it is crucial to address the practice of password reuse. We understand that creating unique passwords for each account can be challenging, but it is essential for protecting our systems and data from unauthorized access.

Here are a few reminders to help you avoid the risks associated with password reuse:

1. **Password Complexity**: Ensure that your passwords are complex and include a mix of letters (both uppercase and lowercase), special characters, and numbers. This makes them more resistant to brute force attacks and significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access.

2. **Password Length**: Use passwords that are at least 12 characters long. Longer passwords are more secure because they take longer to crack.

3. **Unique Passwords**: For each account, use a unique password. This means that your work-related accounts, your personal accounts, and your social media accounts should all have different passwords.

4. **Password Storage**: Do not write your passwords down on paper or store them insecurely. Instead, use a reputable password manager to securely store and generate complex passwords for you.

5. **Password Manager**: If you're unsure how to create strong, unique passwords for each of your accounts, consider using a password manager. These tools can generate complex passwords and store them in a secure, encrypted vault.

6. **Security Awareness**: Be aware of phishing attempts. Do not click on suspicious links or provide your password information to unsolicited requests. Regularly check the security settings of your accounts and keep your systems updated.

We understand that it may take some time to adjust to these practices, but security is a shared responsibility. Your adherence to these guidelines is essential in protecting our workplace and clients.

Thank you for your cooperation.
