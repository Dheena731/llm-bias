First, second, third and fourth forms

Find some children in your class and ask them to write on sentence strips the names of the form they are in.

Post the sentences around the room.

Third and fourth forms

Find some children in your class who are in these forms and ask them to write the names of their forms on sentence strips.

Post the sentences around the room.

Three things

Ask the children in your class to write three things about themselves. E.g.

I am from Ballymoney.

I like football.

My favourite food is fish and chips.

Fold the sentence strips in half and make tents.

Three wants, wishes or would likes

Ask the children to write three things they want, would like or desire. E.g.

I would like a car.

I wish I could be a pop star.

I want to be a famous writer.

Third or fourth form

Ask the fifth form children to write three things about the first three forms or three things about the third and fourth forms. E.g.
