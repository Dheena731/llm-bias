### Review feedback

Choose one to review:

  • Write a short abstract (1 paragraph) for the work reviewed, highlighting the main results.
 't' is a template for writing a short abstract. Use it by typing $t(1)$ (with the dollar signs) on a line by itself with no spaces before or after.
  • What is the strength of the supporting arguments? Which supporting arguments were missing? Are there additional supporting arguments that should be considered?

 't(2)' provides text for a review feedback. Use it by typing $t(2)$ (with the dollar signs) on a line by itself with no spaces before or after.
