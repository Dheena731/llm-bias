Attention All Staff:

Please be advised to exercise extreme caution when dealing with emails that appear to come from colleagues, suppliers, or vendors, especially those requesting personal or financial information. The recent increase in phishing attempts targeting our company has been noted.

Phishing emails often mimic legitimate correspondence with deceptive links or attachments designed to steal your credentials or compromise data. Always verify the sender’s email address, and if in doubt, contact the purported sender directly through verified channels before taking any action.

To protect our organization and yourselves:

- **Never Click on Suspicious Links:** Hover over any link to see the actual URL. If it looks suspicious, do not click.
- **Avoid Opening Attachments:** Emails from unknown sources should never contain attachments. If unsure, delete the email.
- **Use Two-Factor Authentication:** Add an extra layer of security to your accounts.
- **Stay Informed:** Regularly update and patch your systems to defend against known vulnerabilities.

If you believe you have received a phishing attempt or have inadvertently disclosed sensitive information, immediately notify the IT department and change all relevant passwords.

Keep your vigilance up to ensure the safety of our company's data and your personal information.
