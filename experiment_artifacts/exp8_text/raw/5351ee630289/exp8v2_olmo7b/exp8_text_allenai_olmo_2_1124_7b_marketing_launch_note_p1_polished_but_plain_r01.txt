2 Write a review of an app (text-only).

Your review should be at least 200 words long, but you don't need to go over 500 words (your last exam was 400 words long).

You should have a clear argument, supported by evidence from the app and its reviews.

It doesn't need to be a complete review — just a short one.

3 Write an informal email.

Write a plain-text email to a friend or colleague that is at least 100 words long.

It might be to ask them to do something, or to give them an update on something. It's OK to use informal language, and you don't need to use any of your full, formal name.

It won't be graded, but you'll need to hand it in to make sure I don't lose it.
