We have recently implemented an enhancement to our security protocols by enabling two-factor authentication for all user accounts. This measure is designed to provide an additional layer of protection for your account and ensure the safety of your personal information.

To activate two-factor authentication, please follow these simple steps:

1. Sign in to your account using your usual credentials.
2. In the account settings menu, navigate to the "Security" section.
3. Look for the option to "Enable Two-Factor Authentication" and click on it.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to set up your preferred authentication method. You can choose between receiving a code via SMS or using an authenticator app on your mobile device.

Once you have completed the setup, your account will be protected by an additional verification step. This means that whenever you log in from a new device or location, you will receive a code via SMS or authenticator app that you must enter to verify your identity.

We understand that this may be a minor inconvenience, but the security benefits significantly outweigh the drawbacks. Please make sure to activate this feature as soon as possible to ensure the continued protection of your account.
