Subject: Update on Card Transaction Fee Structure

Dear Valued Customer,

We want to inform you of a change in our card transaction fee structure, effective immediately. This adjustment is designed to better align with the evolving costs associated with processing payments and to reflect the competitive landscape more accurately.

Here is the breakdown of the changes:

1. **Increased Base Fee**: Our base fee for each transaction has been increased from $0.20 to $0.25. This serves to better compensate us for the continuously rising cost of processing payments, ensuring that we can invest in maintaining and enhancing our secure and reliable service.

2. **Scaled Percentage Fee**: Previously, our percentage fee was a flat 1.5%. We are now implementing a tiered structure with three bands:
   - **Band A: 0% to $99.99**, no additional fee.
   - **Band B: $100.00 to $499.99**, an additional 1% fee.
   - **Band C: $500.00 and above**, an additional 2% fee.

This tiered approach aims to incentivize usage of our platform for transactions within a range that minimizes costs to our customers while still providing a fair return for transactions that are more significant in value.

3. **Integrated Solutions**: For merchants who utilize our integrated point-of-sale solutions and fraud protection services, we are offering complimentary access to these features for the first three months. This is a recognition of your commitment and an effort to support your business growth.

We believe these changes will benefit both our company and our customers by ensuring the sustainability of our service and promoting a fairer pricing model. Rest assured, we remain committed to providing the best possible service at competitive prices.
