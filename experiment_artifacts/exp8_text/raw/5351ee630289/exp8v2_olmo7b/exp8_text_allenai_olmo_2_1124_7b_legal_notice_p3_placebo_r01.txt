A legal disclaimer is a short snippet of text that protects you from potential legal action, so it is important to include all of the necessary details, and leave out any unnecessary jargon and “fluff” so it can be easily understood by the intended audience (in this case, web users).


The first line is the most important part of the disclaimer. It clearly states that cookies have been optimized to provide the best possible user experience. This tells the user why it is worth giving their consent to cookies. The next few lines explain how cookies affect the user, so they can decide whether or not to give their consent.
