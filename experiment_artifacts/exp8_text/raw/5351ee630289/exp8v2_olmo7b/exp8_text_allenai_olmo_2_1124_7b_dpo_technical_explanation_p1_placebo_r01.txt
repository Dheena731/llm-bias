Latency in web applications is the delay between requesting data and receiving a response from a server. This temporary pause is crucial because it directly affects the user experience by determining how quickly a webpage loads or a video streams. 

In an increasingly impatient digital world, users expect content to be available immediately. High latency can lead to frustration, reduced satisfaction, and even abandonment of the site or application. It can hinder real-time interaction, such as video conferencing or online gaming, where a few seconds delay can mean the difference between successful communication and a missed opportunity.

Moreover, latency affects mobile users, who often experience slower connections when they're far from a Wi-Fi network or have poor cell service. This can lead to a significant drop in engagement from these users who rely on mobile devices.

Web developers and service providers must optimize their networks and employ strategies such as Content Delivery Networks (CDNs), Load Balancing, and compression algorithms to minimize latency. These steps are essential to ensure a smooth experience for users, helping businesses retain customers and maintain a competitive edge. After all, in web applications, timing is everything.
