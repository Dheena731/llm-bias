Export Query Results: To download your search findings directly, simply click on the "Export" button usually located on the toolbar above the results grid. Choose the format that suits your needs – CSV for plain data, Excel for more advanced spreadsheets, or PDF for easy printing. Ensure your browser or tool settings are adjusted to handle file downloads; this may involve allowing pop-ups or checking download permissions. Once selected, the file will be saved directly to your device for offline access, ensuring you have a permanent record of your query outcomes without the hassle of manual copying.
