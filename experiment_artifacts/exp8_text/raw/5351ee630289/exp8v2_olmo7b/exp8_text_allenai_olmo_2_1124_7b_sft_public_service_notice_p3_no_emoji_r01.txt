Dear Residents,

We would like to inform you of some changes to our recycling collection schedule. Starting from next week, the recycling trucks will pass through your neighborhood on Wednesdays and Fridays.

Please make sure to have your recyclable items ready and properly sorted before 7:00 am, as the trucks will be unable to accept any materials after 7:30 am. 

Any non-recyclable items or items not sorted properly will be left behind, and we kindly ask that you do not place any items out that are not included in our recycling program.

Thank you for your cooperation and continued support in our efforts to recycle responsibly.

Warm regards,

The Recycling Team
