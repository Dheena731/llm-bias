Two-factor authentication is a method for verifying a user's identity. It requires the user to provide two pieces of evidence to prove they are who they say they are.

How to set up two-factor authentication

Log in to your account

Click My Settings in the top right corner.

Select Two-Factor Authentication

You will be taken to the settings page for two-factor authentication. Enable it by checking the box, and then select "Get Started with a Verification Code".

Enter the code that is sent to you

You will receive a verification code by SMS. Enter that into the box, and then click "Verify".

Now you can continue to set up two-factor authentication. This will guide you through setting up your second factor.

Two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security to your accounts. It helps protect against attacks and malicious actors who might gain access to your account. Once you enable two-factor authentication, you will need to provide an additional verification code when logging in from new devices or after resetting your password.

When you need to log in from a different device

If you want to access your account from a new device, your phone will send you a verification code via SMS once you enter your username and password. You will need to enter the verification code to complete the sign-in.

When you want to reset your password

If you want to reset your password, you will first need to verify your identity using your second factor. This can be done by entering a verification code sent to your phone via SMS, or by scanning a QR code that you have created and saved on the two-factor authentication settings page.
