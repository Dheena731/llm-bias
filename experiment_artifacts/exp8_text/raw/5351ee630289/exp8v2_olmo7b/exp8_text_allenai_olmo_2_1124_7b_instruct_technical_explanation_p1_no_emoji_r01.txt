Latency in web applications is crucial because it directly affects the user experience by determining how quickly a user receives a response after interacting with a web page. Lower latency results in faster load times, quicker response to user inputs, and smoother overall interaction with the application. This enhances user satisfaction and engagement. Conversely, high latency can lead to slow loading times, delayed responses, and overall frustration for users, potentially driving them away to competitor services. Therefore, minimizing latency is essential for maintaining user satisfaction, ensuring performance, and supporting business goals by retaining and attracting users.
