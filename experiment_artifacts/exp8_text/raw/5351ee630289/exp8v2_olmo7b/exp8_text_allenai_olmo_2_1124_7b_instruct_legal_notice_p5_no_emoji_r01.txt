Notice of Acceptable Use Policy for Shared Network

Attention Network Users:

This notice outlines the acceptable use policy for the shared network available to all users. It is imperative that all users comply with this policy to ensure the integrity, security, and efficiency of the network.

1. **Unauthorized Access Prohibition:** Users are strictly prohibited from attempting to access the network through means not approved by the network administrators. Unauthorized access, including hacking or any form of unauthorized penetration, is a violation of this policy and constitutes a serious offense. 

2. **No Illegal Activities:** Engaging in or promoting illegal activities, including but not limited to: copyright infringement, distribution of harmful software, child pornography, and any form of cybercrime, is strictly forbidden. Users found violating this provision may face immediate disconnection and legal action.

3. **Fair Use Policy:** Users must adhere to the principles of 'fair use' in respect to copyrighted materials. The unlicensed sharing or downloading of copyrighted content, such as music, videos, or software, is a breach of this policy. Users must ensure that their network activities do not infringe upon the rights of copyright holders.

4. **Bandwidth and Resource Misuse:** Excessive or unnecessary use of bandwidth and resources (e.g., intensive gaming, file sharing of large files) without regard for other users on the network is unacceptable. Such behavior may lead to corrective measures, including a reduction in service or disconnection.

5. **Security Measures:** Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their network credentials and ensuring that their devices do not compromise the network's security. Sharing passwords or engaging in activities that may jeopardize network security are clear violations of this policy.

6. **Monitoring and Enforcement:** The network administrators retain the right to monitor network traffic to ensure compliance with this policy. Violations will be addressed according to the network's procedures, which may include warnings, suspension of services, and termination of user access.

This policy is a legally binding agreement between the network administrators and the users. Any breach shall be subject to disciplinary action, including civil and criminal prosecution, as applicable by law.

By utilizing this shared network, you signify your explicit acceptance of these terms and conditions. Users who have queries or concerns about this policy should contact the network administration office promptly.

Thank you for your cooperation and responsible network use.
