Notice Regarding Acceptable Use of the Shared Network

Attention All Network Users:

This notice serves to inform you of the acceptable use guidelines for the shared network to which you have access. Your compliance with these rules is crucial to the well-being and security of the network, and is required by your service agreement with the provider.

1. Unauthorized Access:
You must not attempt to access the network or its components, whether hardware or software, without proper authorization. Doing so violates this agreement and may subject you to disciplinary action, including suspension or termination of service.

2. Malicious Activities:
The network is intended for lawful use only. Engaging in activities such as spreading malware, launching Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks, or any other form of cyber vandalism is strictly prohibited and may result in punitive measures.

3. Privacy Violations:
You must not use the network to invade another user's privacy or to obtain unauthorized access to personal information. This includes, but is not limited to, unauthorized surveillance, interception of communications, or any other activity that infringes on individual privacy rights.

4. Illegal Content:
The sharing or downloading of copyrighted material without permission, distribution of pornography, or other illegal content is strictly prohibited. In addition to being unlawful, such activities are a breach of this agreement and may result in legal action being taken against you.

5. Spamming:
Sending unsolicited or excessive email messages, or engaging in any form of spamming, is prohibited. This includes the use of automated systems for sending unsolicited messages to multiple recipients.

6. Resource Misuse:
The network is intended for general use and should not be monopolized by any single user or activity. Activities that consume excessive resources, such as high-volume data transfers or intensive computations, must have prior approval from the network administrator.

Failure to adhere to these acceptable use guidelines may result in the revocation of your network access privileges without notice. Should you have any questions or require clarification on these guidelines, please contact the network administrator for assistance.
