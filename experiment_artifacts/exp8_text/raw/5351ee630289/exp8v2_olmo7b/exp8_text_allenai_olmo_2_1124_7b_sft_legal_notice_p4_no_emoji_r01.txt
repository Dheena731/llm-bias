REFUND POLICY NOTICE

This notice informs you of your refund eligibility under our company’s refund policy. To be eligible for a refund, you must meet the following criteria:

1. The product or service you purchased is unused and in its original packaging. 
2. You have not started using the product or service.
3. You have not removed any packaging materials from the product or service unless instructed by us.
4. You notify us of your request for a refund within 30 days of receiving your delivery.
5. You provide a receipt or proof of purchase for the product or service you are refunding.
6. You have not purchased the product or service through a third-party seller.

Upon meeting the above criteria, we may issue a full refund to your original method of payment, subject to our standard processing and approval times. We reserve the right to refuse a refund for any reason and without notice. For additional details or clarification, please contact our customer service department.
