NOTICE OF ACCEPTABLE USE OF THE SHARED NETWORK

 Users of the Shared Network may use the services and equipment provided for informational, educational, and communication purposes only. Such use must not cause harm or disruption to the network or to other users.

 The following activities are prohibited:

 1. Sending, distributing, or accessing any material that is obscene, pornographic, or in violation of any applicable law or regulation, including but not limited to copyright and trademark laws;
 2. Any attempts to probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the network or any of its connected systems;
 3. Sharing of personal information that violates the privacy of any individuals;
 4. Use of the network to engage in any illegal or unethical activities, including but not limited to hacking, theft, or fraud;
 5. Sending unsolicited, commercial electronic mail, including, but not limited to, junk mail, chain letters, pyramid schemes, or any other form of spam;
 6. Tampering with network software or hardware, or any attempts to bypass or circumvent security features.

 violations of this policy may result in immediate cessation of service and/or disciplinary action, including but not limited to warnings, suspension, or termination of the user's account.

 If you have any questions regarding the use of the Shared Network, please contact the network administrator at the email address or phone number provided.
