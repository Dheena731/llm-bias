Attention All Residents,

As part of our ongoing commitment to sustainability and environmental stewardship, the City of Green Meadows is making adjustments to our recycling collection schedule and materials accepted starting next Monday, November 13th.

**New Collection Schedule:**

- Recycling bins will now be collected every Thursday morning.
- The new schedule aims to optimize collection routes and reduce traffic congestion during peak hours.

**Materials Accepted:**

- Plastics: All clean plastic bottles, jugs, and containers. Please rinse and remove caps.
- Cartons: We welcome all clean cartons, including those with plastic or aluminum components.
- Paper: White paper, newspapers, and cardboard are accepted. Please do not include food-soiled paper or magazines.
- Glass: Containers only, ideally separated by color for easier sorting.

**Items to Exclude:**

- Food Waste: Please continue to use the organic waste bin for food scraps.
- Plastic Foil: It's best to discard plastic foil at the point of trash disposal.
- Uni-Quality Manufacured (LUW) Items: These items are best kept out of the recycling stream to maintain quality.

**Old Habits Make Way for New:**

By adapting to these minor changes, we are ensuring that our recycling process remains efficient and that we continue advancing our green initiatives.

**Thank You for Your Cooperation:**

We appreciate your understanding and support as we navigate these changes. Please stay tuned for updates and reminders through our local channels.

For more information, call our Recycling Support Line at (555) 123-4567 or visit our website at www.greenmeadowsrecycling.com.

Together, let's continue to do our part for a cleaner, greener future.

Sincerely,

The City of Green Meadows Recycling Team
