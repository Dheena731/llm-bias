**What is Two-Factor Authentication?**
Two-Factor Authentication requires not only your password but also something that only you have on you – typically your phone. When 2FA is enabled, in addition to entering your password, you will receive a unique code to your phone or email that you must enter to complete the login process.

**Why Should I Enable 2FA?**
- **Enhanced Security**: It significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access.
- **Peace of Mind**: Knowing your account is protected adds an extra layer of security.
- **Compliance**: Many service providers are encouraging its use to comply with security standards.

**How to Enable 2FA:**
1. **Visit Settings**: Go to 'Settings' on our platform.
2. **Locate 2FA Option**: Find and select the 'Two-Factor Authentication' link.
3. **Follow the Guide**: Follow the step-by-step instructions to set up your 2FA preferences.
4. **Get Your Codes**: Use the app or the method you choose to receive your verification codes.
