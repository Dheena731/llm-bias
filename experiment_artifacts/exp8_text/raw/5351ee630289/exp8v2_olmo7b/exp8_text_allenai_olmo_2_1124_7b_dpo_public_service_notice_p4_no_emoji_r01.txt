A HEAT ADVISORY IS IN EFFECT UNTIL 8 PM EDT TODAY JUNE 15, 2023.

THE NATIONAL WEATHER SERVICE HAS ISSUED THIS ADVISORY FOR EXTREME HEAT AND HIGH HUMIDITY WITH TEMPERATURES EXPECTED TO REACH OR EXCEED 100 DEGREES FAHRENHEIT. HEAT INDEX VALUES MAY REACH 110 TO 115 DEGREES, WHICH IS DANGEROUS TO LIFE AND PROPERTY. PETS AND THE ELDERLY SHOULD BE KEPT IN SHADED AREAS AND GIVEN ADEQUATE WATER. STAY UPDATED WITH LOCAL REPORTS AND TAKE PRECAUTIONS TO AVOID DEHYDRATION AND HEAT-RELATED ILLNESS.

IF THIS ADVISORY IS IN EFFECT, CHECK BACK HERE FOR ADDITIONAL INFORMATION AND UPDATE ALERTS.
