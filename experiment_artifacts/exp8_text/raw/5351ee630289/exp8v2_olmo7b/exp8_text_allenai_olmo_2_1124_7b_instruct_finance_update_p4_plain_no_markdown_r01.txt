The end-of-year tax statement provides a summary of your income and taxes paid during the financial year. It serves as a crucial document for taxpayers as it reflects their tax obligations and helps in planning for future tax payments or potential refunds. The statement showcases adjustments made to income and details the total tax liability alongside any withholdings or advance payments made. It is important for individuals to review their tax statements carefully to ensure accuracy and to plan for their tax filings or to claim any refunds due, thereby managing their financial obligations efficiently.
