1. Cut the ends off the stems and then place them in a jar or bottle filled with water. Cover the tops with a plastic bag tied at the top or loosely closed over the opening of the jar to allow the herbs to get air. Change the water every few days.

2. Cut the ends off the stems and then make a tight bundle of the herbs. Cut the stems horizontally about 1/4 inch above the base of the herbs. Tie the bundle tightly with a cotton string, then tie the bundle to the top of the inside of your kitchen cupboard or pantry door where it will dry.

3. Cut the ends off the stems and then bundle them up with a rubber band, tie, or string. Place them in a brown paper bag on a counter top or in a kitchen cupboard to dry.

4. Cut the ends off the stems and then use the stems to make a bouquet. The herbs will stay fresher in the bouquet if you wrap the stems in a damp paper towel.

5. Cut the ends off the stems and then lay the stems on a cookie sheet in an even layer. Put the cookie sheet in the refrigerator to keep the herbs fresh and dry longer.
