Recycling Collection Changes

Effective this coming Monday, October 2nd, there will be some changes to our recycling collection schedule and rules. Please take note of the following updates:

1. **New Day of Collection**: We've shifted the recycling day to Tuesday mornings from Monday mornings. Please ensure your recyclables are ready at the curb by 7:00 AM on Tuesdays.

2. **Change in Acceptable Items**: Due to new recycling guidelines, certain materials will no longer be accepted. These include plastic bags, foam packaging, and glass bottles. Please dispose of these items using the appropriate waste disposal methods.

3. **Larger Bin Size**: To accommodate the increased volume allowed, your recycle bins will be replaced with larger bins as of this week. This change should help manage the materials better.

4. **Educational Flyer**: An informational flyer detailing the new collection rules and changes will be distributed door-to-door. Please look out for this flyer for further details.

Please make sure to follow these changes to ensure our recycling program remains efficient and effective. For any questions or further clarification, please don’t hesitate to reach out to our office. Thank you for your cooperation and continued support in making a difference in our community's recycling efforts.
