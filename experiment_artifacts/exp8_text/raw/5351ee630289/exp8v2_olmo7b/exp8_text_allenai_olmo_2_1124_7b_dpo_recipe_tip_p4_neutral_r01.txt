Plan your lunches ahead of time by preparing simple, healthy dishes that can be easily reheated or assembled. Use ingredients that won't spoil quickly and keep well in the fridge, such as cooked proteins, grains, and a variety of fresh or frozen vegetables. Shop for these items in bulk when possible to save time and money. Prepare your lunches on weekends or the night before, storing them in individual containers for easy grab-and-go during your busy week. This way, you'll avoid last-minute rushes, save money on takeout, and maintain a healthier eating habit.
