— Wabi-sabi —
or the art of imperfection

[ Wabi  sabi  means  beautiful  imperfect  everything ]

The beautiful imperfection - is the essence of the Japanese art. Wabi-sabi is one of the most important cultural concepts in the Japanese philosophy and the art of everyday life. Wabi-sabi is about the profound imperfection. Wabi-sabi speaks to us about the acceptance of our imperfection as part two of the balanced and harmonious life.

The ancient teachings of wabi-sabi

Wabi-sabi is a way of seeing reality as the flow of life and the changing, transient nature of all things. The concept of wabi-sabi includes a deep awareness that all things are impermanent. The theory of wabi-sabi is based on the teachings of Zen monk Eihei Dogen, who wrote in the 13th century: "To see the world as it is, is to see the world in wabi-sabi. All beings are already complete. No added value of effort, skill, or technique is needed. Each thing is as it is. We are as we are."
