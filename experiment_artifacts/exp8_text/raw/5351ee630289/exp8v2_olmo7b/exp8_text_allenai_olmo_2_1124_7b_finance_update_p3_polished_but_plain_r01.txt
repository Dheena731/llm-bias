In today’s update, we are excited to announce Savings Goal feature. This feature allows you to set a monthly savings goal and receive daily reminders to save towards that goal. The new feature was built in collaboration with our users and takes into account feedback from hundreds of thousands of people on how to best align with their savings habits. 

Here’s how to use it:

1. Head to the savings page.
2. On the right side, click the “Savings Goal” section.
3. Create a new goal or edit an existing one.
4. Choose how much you want to save each month.
5. Set how often you want to be reminded to save towards your goal.
6. Click save.

We hope you enjoy this new feature and that it helps you on your path to financial freedom!
