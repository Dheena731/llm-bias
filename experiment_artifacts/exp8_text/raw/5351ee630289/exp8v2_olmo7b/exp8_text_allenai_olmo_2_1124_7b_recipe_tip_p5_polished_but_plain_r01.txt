Prose tips

Write each sentence carefully, following these tips:

  • Make sure each sentence has a noun and a verb. You can use a pronoun instead of a noun.

  • Listen to grammar and punctuation.

  • Follow conventions. Spell out numbers less than 10, unless writing about programming, like 13 bytes.

  • Implement the style guide.

  • Write as if the reader were reading it to your face, expecting a friendly conversation.

  • Use plain language. Avoid technical jargon and cryptic acronyms.

Your writing might be slightly more formal than usual, like you're explaining a concept to someone.

You might read a tutorial, like "How to install MongoDB on your computer", and feel inspired to write about how to install MongoDB. However, you might instead write "How to install MongoDB" because you can't assume the reader has computer skills, or has MongoDB installed.

Be wary of writing about programming

Avoid writing about programming. If you're an expert writing about programming, you might know too much about it to remember the difficulties of a beginner.
