Remember, when your prescription is nearly out, it's essential to contact your healthcare provider or pharmacist promptly to request a refill. Failing to do so before your medication runs out might lead to interruptions in your treatment which could be harmful to your health. Always ensure you have enough medication to avoid any gaps in your treatment plan. Keep a calendar or list to track when you need to refill your prescriptions to prevent any oversights. Stay organized and communicate with your healthcare team to maintain a steady supply of your medications.
