  WHY GO TO A CONFERENCE?  A number of reasons:

  *  Learn about what others are doing in a similar area.

  *  Get ideas from others.

  *  Market yourself.

  *  Network.

  *  Practice your presentation.

  *  Get feedback.

  *  Have a good time.

  WHAT IS A CONFERENCE?  A conference is a place where you can go to present work that you have done.  You can learn about what others are doing through both the poster sessions and the oral presentations.  You can also meet interesting people by talking to those at poster sessions, etc.

  HOW DO I PREPARE FOR A CONFERENCE?  The key thing is to be well prepared.  If you are not prepared, then you will be very nervous and not do well.  The best way to be prepared is to practice your presentation as much as possible.

The best way to be prepared is to practice your presentation as much as possible.  Practicing is important not only to be a good presenter, but also for your confidence so that you can speak well and make sure you have all the details in order.

  WHAT SHOULD I PRESENT?  You should present whatever you feel comfortable with.  If you are nervous about presenting, then you might want to present something simple like your research questions, results, or conclusions.  You might also want to present something more complex like a hypothesis and experiment with good graphs or video.

  HOW DO I PREPARE A POSTER?  A poster is a large piece of paper that you have written (or drawn) on to explain what you are presenting.  It is similar to a poster you might see in a subway station, except more professional looking.  A great poster has a great title, lots of information, and good graphics.  Here are some tips for poster making:

  *  Think about what information is important on the poster and how you will arrange that information.  Organize the poster around your main points and be sure you have enough information to support those points.  You want the poster to be readable from a distance and not have a lot of white space.  You also want the poster to flow from one page to the next so that viewers are not lost.

  *  Make sure that you have good graphics.  You can use clip art, clip art that you create, or pictures you find on the Web.  You also want to be sure that you have good labels on your charts, tables, and graphs so that it is easy for viewers to understand the information you are showing.
