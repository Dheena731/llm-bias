To enhance the flavor of roasted vegetables, season them with a balanced mix of herbs and spices right after they come out of the oven while they're still hot. This allows the seasonings to cling better to the vegetables, intensifying their aromas and flavors. A simple combination of salt, pepper, and freshly chopped herbs like rosemary, thyme, or parsley works well; for a touch of heat, add some smoked paprika or cayenne pepper. Remember, it's always better to start with less seasoning and add more if needed, as you can't easily remove excess seasoning once applied.
