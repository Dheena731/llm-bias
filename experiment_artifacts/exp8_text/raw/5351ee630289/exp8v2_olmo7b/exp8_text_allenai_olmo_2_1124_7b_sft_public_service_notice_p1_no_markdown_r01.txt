Public-Service Notice: Library Opening Hours

Attention all residents!

We are pleased to inform you that the [City] Public Library is now open to the public. The library will be open from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM on Monday through Thursday, and from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on Fridays. The library will remain closed on weekends and holidays.
