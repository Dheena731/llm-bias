When seasoning your roasted vegetables, don't just sprinkle salt and pepper! Start by roasting them with a generous drizzle of high-quality olive oil, then finish with a sprinkle of crushed garlic, fresh thyme, and a pinch of red pepper flakes for a zesty kick. This combo enhances the natural sweetness of the vegetables and brings out their earthy, aromatic flavors, transforming your roast into a true feast fit for kings. Remember, the key is to season individually as you serve to ensure each bite is perfectly seasoned. Bon appétit!
