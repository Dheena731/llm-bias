This month, my spending got a healthy review. I managed to cut back on dining out expenses by half compared to the previous month. This was mostly due to planning meals at home more often and seeking out local and affordable eateries instead of opting for more expensive chains. Groceries, on the other hand, saw a slight increase, mainly because I've been trying to incorporate more fruits and vegetables into my diet, which tend to cost a bit more. However, I balanced this by buying generic brands for staple items, which kept those costs down. Transportation was another area where I saved. By switching part of my commute to public transportation and carpooling with colleagues a couple of times a week, I reduced my fuel costs significantly. On the other hand, utility bills rose a bit due to an increase in heating costs as we had a few colder days. I'm hoping to keep these costs in check in the coming months with better insulation at home. In summary, although there were some rises, the overall reduction in dining and transportation expenses helped keep my monthly spending summary positive.
