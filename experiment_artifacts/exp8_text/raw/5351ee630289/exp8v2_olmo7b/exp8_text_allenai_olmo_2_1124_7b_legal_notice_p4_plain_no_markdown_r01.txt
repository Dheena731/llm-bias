Adding widgets

Widgets are the pages that appear on your site's pages, and they're the main way you customize your site.

To add a widget to a page, follow these steps:

  1. Open your site's dashboard.
  2. In the left sidebar, hover over the page you want to add a widget to.
  3. In the dropdown menu, click Widgets. A new page will open.
  4. Drag and drop a widget into the page that you want to add it to. That widget will be added to the page and its settings will open.

To customize a widget's settings, just click on the widget.

To remove a widget, click the X button at the bottom right of the widget.

The Welcome widget

If you're adding text to a page, the Welcome widget might be a great option.

To add a Welcome widget to a page, follow these steps:

  1. Open your site's dashboard.
  2. In the left sidebar, click Pages.
  3. In the dropdown, click the page you want to add the widget to.
  4. In the right sidebar, click Widgets.
  5. Drag and drop the Choose a section heading widget onto your page.
  6. Find the section heading that you want to use and click it.
