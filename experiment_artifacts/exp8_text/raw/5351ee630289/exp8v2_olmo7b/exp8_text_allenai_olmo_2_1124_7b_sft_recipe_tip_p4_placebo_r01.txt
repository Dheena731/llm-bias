To streamline your lunch planning for a hectic week, consider preparing a handful of simple, nutritious recipes that utilize ingredients that keep well. Think about meals that can be made in large batches and easily reheated, like grain bowls or salads, which can be mixed and matched with protein sources like grilled chicken or tofu. Utilize your freezer by cooking dinners a few days in advance and portioning them out for lunch the next day. This approach saves time, reduces waste, and ensures you stick to your healthy eating goals even when time is scarce.
