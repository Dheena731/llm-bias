**Here are some vital precautions to take:**

1. **Stay Hydrated:** Drink plenty of water, even if you're not thirsty. Limit your intake of alcohol and caffeinated beverages.
2. **Watch for Symptoms:** Look for signs of heat exhaustion or heat stroke, such as heavy sweating, fatigue, dizziness, nausea, or rapid breathing.
3. **Protect Your Skin:** Use sunscreen and wear a wide-brimmed hat and loose, light-colored, breathable clothing.
4. **Avoid Outdoor Activities:** Particularly during the hottest part of the day, consider staying indoors or in shaded areas.
5. **Check on the Elderly:** Make sure to visit and help those who may not have air conditioning or who are at high risk.

The local emergency services are prepared to assist those in need. If you or someone else experiences heat-related illness, please call 911.
