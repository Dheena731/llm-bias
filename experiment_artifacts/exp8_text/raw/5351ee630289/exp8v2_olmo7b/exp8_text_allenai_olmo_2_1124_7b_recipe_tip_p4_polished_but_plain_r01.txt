The writing should make good use of the random sentence starter words. For example:

Convenient and versatile, An idea to help you plan lunches for a busy week is…

The work will likely not be perfect, but will be a good start. If you feel you can improve on the writing, by all means do so.

This is a weekly writing task that is due every Saturday, at midnight, Pacific Time (PST). The prompt for this task will always be a word that you must use in your writing.

Weekly writing tasks are optional. You may choose to skip a task if you find it too difficult, but you are encouraged to give it a good try.

This task is only worth 1 point. However, if you submit a draft that you want feedback on, you may get an extra 1 or 2 points of feedback. If you want feedback, email your draft to me at justin@jessesingerman.com.
