A confidence interval in a chart provides a range of values that is likely to contain the true population parameter (like the mean) with a certain level of confidence (commonly 95%). Essentially, it tells you how closely the sample data reflects the entire population. If the chart includes a 95% confidence interval, this means that if you were to repeat the sampling process many times, approximately 95% of the intervals would include the true population parameter. This range helps to gauge the reliability of the estimates derived from your sample data.
