Start the conversation

Write an email that would kick off a conversation among multiple recipients about a new programming language.

Create a short email exchange between two or more people that starts with a message from you introducing the topic, and at least one response.

The prompt should be related to the work of the recipient; for example, if the recipient is a co-worker, the email should include work-related terminology specific to your organization.

If the recipient is not a person you know directly, make it someone who would be interested in discussing the topic with you.

The text should appear to be written by a native speaker of English.
