Hit the Green Light on Simple Weekday Eats:

Opt for recipes that call for primarily pantry staples or a few fresh ingredients you can easily pick up on the weekend. Think grain bowls with mixed beans and rice, pesto pasta with canned tomatoes, or shredded chicken salad on whole-grain bread. Batch cook protein, grains, and veggies on Sundays, then assemble into different lunch combinations throughout the week. Use leftovers creatively – think veggie pupusas one day and stuffed bell peppers the next. This strategy saves time, reduces stress, and keeps your lunches healthy and delicious.
