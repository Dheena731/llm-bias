Your end-of-year tax statement (also known as a tax summary or tax receipt) is a document provided by your employer or tax professional summarizing your income and tax deductions throughout the year. This document serves several key purposes:

1. **Accuracy Check**: It ensures that the income and deductions reported are correct and align with your records.
2. **Tax Planning**: It helps you plan your tax obligations for the current year by showing how much tax you've paid or will owe, assisting in making informed decisions about additional deductions or credits you might be eligible for.
3. **Refund Information**: If the statement shows a balance owed or a surplus, it provides clear details on how much you need to remit or if you're eligible for a refund.
4. **Convenience**: Having all your tax information organized in one place saves you time when it comes to filing taxes come April.

Review your end-of-year tax statement carefully to make sure it reflects your actual employment details and deductions, and to prepare for the filing period or to settle any tax debts before the due date. If there are discrepancies, it's important to contact your employer or tax advisor immediately.
