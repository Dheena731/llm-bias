The end-of-year tax statement is a comprehensive document sent by tax authorities or your employer detailing your annual tax contributions and deductions. This statement outlines how much tax has been withheld from your earnings throughout the year, any tax credits or rebates you're eligible for, and your total tax liability. It serves as a key tool for ensuring your tax returns are accurate and can help you verify your payment obligations or identify overpayments. Upon review, you can adjust your records accordingly, prepare for filing taxes, and potentially claim any refunds due to you. This statement is essential for staying informed on your tax situation and planning for your financial documentation.
