**Heat Advisory Notice for Eastside Residents**

Hello Eastside Community,

As the temperatures continue to soar, we want to ensure everyone is prepared and informed about the upcoming heat wave affecting our area. Beginning this Friday and lasting throughout the weekend, we expect the heat index to reach well over 110°F, with humidity levels peaking at 70%.

To protect your health and safety, here are some specific measures and reminders:

- **Stay Hydrated:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day. Considering carrying a refillable water bottle with you.
  
- **Dress Appropriately:** Light-colored, loose-fitting clothing will help reflect the sun and allow your body to breathe better.

- **Avoid Outdoor Activities:** Between 10 a.m. and 4 p.m. is when the heat is most intense. Plan indoor activities for these hours.

- **Check on the Elderly and Vulnerable:** Make sure neighbors, especially those who live alone or have health conditions, have access to air conditioning and are drinking water.

- **Notify Workplaces:** Employers, encourage your staff to stay hydrated and aware of the heat-related safety measures in place.

If you experience symptoms such as dizziness, nausea, or cramps, move to a cooler environment immediately and seek medical attention if needed.

For those without air conditioning, the local community centers will provide cooling stations from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. every day this week.

Please, be mindful of the impact of this heatwave on our community and take all necessary precautions.

Thank you for your cooperation and vigilance.
Eastside Community Health Services

*Remember, this is not just a heatwave—it's a safety alert. Be smart, stay safe, and keep each other cool.*
