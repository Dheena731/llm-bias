To ensure you have a continuous supply of your medication, always remember to refill your prescription well before it runs out. Keep track of when your prescription is due for renewal by noting the expiration date on your medication package or by checking your pharmacy's records if they've provided digital reminders. This proactive approach will prevent interruptions in your treatment schedule and help maintain your health and wellbeing. Be sure to submit your refill request early enough to account for processing times or potential delays at the pharmacy.
