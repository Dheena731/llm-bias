Subject: Enabling Two-Factor Authentication

Dear Team,

We are enhancing our security measures by introducing two-factor authentication. This additional layer of protection requires you to verify your identity using a second factor, typically a code sent to your phone, when logging into your account.

To enable this feature, please follow these steps:

1. Go to the "Settings" menu on our platform.
2. Locate and select "Two-Factor Authentication."
3. Choose "Enable Two-Factor Authentication."
4. Enter the verification code sent to your phone or email.

Please note that this process ensures your account remains secure and protected against unauthorized access.
