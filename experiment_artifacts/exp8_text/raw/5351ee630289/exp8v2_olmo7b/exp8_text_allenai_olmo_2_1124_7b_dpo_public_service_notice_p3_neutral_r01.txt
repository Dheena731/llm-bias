**Effective immediately, our recycling collection schedule will undergo some changes to better align with best practices and current recycling directives. Here’s what you need to know:**

* **New Days of Collection:** Starting October 15th, our recycling crew will now operate on **Every Other Week Collection**. This means if your collection day is Thursday, you'll have it on the 1st and 3rd weeks of each month.

* **No Plastic Bottles or Containers:** Plastic bottles, jugs, and containers (including those made from #1-7 plastics) will no longer be accepted in your curbside recycling due to complex recycling processes and global market demands. **Please dispose of these in your regular trash starting October 15th.** 

* **Expanded Paper and Cardboard Acceptance:** We've expanded our paper and cardboard categories to encourage more recycling. **Be sure to flatten cardboard boxes and clean paper products (e.g., pizza boxes without greasy parts) before placing them in your recycling bin.**

* **Metal and Glass Remains the Same:** Metal cans, glass bottles, and jars continue to be accepted as they are. Please, empty and rinse these containers to keep our recycling system clean and efficient.

* **Updates on Our Website:** For a detailed guide and updates, please visit our website **here**. You'll find FAQs, how-tos, and reminders to help ensure you're recycling correctly and avoid contamination.
