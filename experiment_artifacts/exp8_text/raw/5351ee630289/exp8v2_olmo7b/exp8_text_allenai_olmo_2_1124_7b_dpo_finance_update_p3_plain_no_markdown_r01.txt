A new feature has been introduced to help individuals manage their savings more effectively. This feature allows users to set specific savings goals, tailor-made for their personal financial targets. With this update, users can input the amount they wish to save, the time frame for achieving the goal, and even assign the savings to particular categories, like "vacation" or "emergency fund." The feature then provides a clear roadmap, detailing regular contributions needed to reach the set objective. Through regular monitoring, users can track their progress, adjust their plans as needed, and gain better control over their financial future.
