Subject: Enabling Two-Factor Authentication

Dear Valued Users,

We would like to inform you that we have introduced an enhanced security feature to safeguard your accounts: Two-Factor Authentication (2FA). This additional layer of security requires an extra step during the login process, ensuring that only you have access to your account.

To enable 2FA, please follow these steps:

1. Sign in to your account using your current login credentials.
2. Navigate to the 'Settings' or 'Account' section.
3. Look for the 'Two-Factor Authentication' or 'Security' option and select 'Enable.'
4. Choose the second factor method that suits you best, such as SMS or authenticator app.
5. Verify the code sent to your selected method to complete the setup.

We strongly encourage you to activate 2FA to protect your account against unauthorized access. 

For further instructions or any assistance, please visit our dedicated help page or contact our support team.
