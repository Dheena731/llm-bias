Attention all users: 

We have recently enabled two-factor authentication across all of our platforms. This additional layer of security requires a verification code sent to your phone in addition to your regular username and password. 

To begin using two-factor authentication, you must visit the "Settings" page on our site and go to the "Security" tab. Here you will see an option labeled "Enable Two-Factor Authentication". Click the button to activate this feature. 

If you do not see this option or have any trouble with your account, please contact our support team at help@mystore.com. 

We hope you will find this additional security useful. Thank you for your continued trust in our service.
