**Public Service Notice: Library Opening Hours**

Effective October 1st, 2023, our local library will modify its opening hours for the convenience of our community. Here's what you need to know about our new schedule:

### Normal Operating Hours:

- **Monday to Saturday:** 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM
- **Sunday:** 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM

### Holiday Hours:

The library will be closed on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. We will resume regular hours the day after Thanksgiving and reopen on December 27th.

### Summer Hours:

From June to August, the library will adjust its schedule to accommodate school holidays and warmer weather:

- **Monday to Friday:** 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
- **Saturday:** 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM
- **Sunday:** 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM

Please note that holidays observed by the City may cause the library to close on days other than those listed above.

### Click Here for Updated Schedule:

For the most current information and to view the library's updated schedule, please visit our website at [libraryurl.com](https://libraryurl.com) or contact us at 555-1234.

The library remains a vital resource for education, entertainment, and community connection. We invite you to visit and enjoy the many services and collections available.
