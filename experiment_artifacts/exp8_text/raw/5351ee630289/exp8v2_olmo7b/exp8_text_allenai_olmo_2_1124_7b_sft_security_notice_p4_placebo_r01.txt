Subject: Enabling Two-Factor Authentication for Enhanced Security

Dear Team,

As part of our ongoing commitment to improving our network security, we have taken the essential step of enabling Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for all user accounts on our platform. This additional layer of security will require you to input an additional form of verification beyond your usual password when logging into your account. This verification typically takes the form of a code sent to your mobile device or generated by an app.

To ensure a smooth transition, please follow these steps:

1. **Before Login:** If you have not already done so, update your contact details (phone number or email) with the Account Management system. This will enable us to send the necessary verification codes to your updated contact information.

2. **During Login:** Upon entering your username and password, you will be prompted to enter a 2FA code. This code can be retrieved via SMS, email, or a generated app-based code, depending on your preference and setup.

3. **Backup Options:** Be aware that if you are unable to receive the code via your primary method (e.g., no mobile service), you can use a backup option. Ensure you have your backup codes stored safely.

We highly recommend setting up your 2FA preferences in advance to avoid any disruptions to your workflow. For further assistance or any questions about 2FA setup, please do not hesitate to reach out to our IT Support Team. Your cooperation and adherence to these new security protocols are greatly appreciated as they significantly enhance our collective security posture.

Thank you for your attention to this important matter.
