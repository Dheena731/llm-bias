Hover over the "Export" icon at the top of the search results page to reveal options for exporting your query results. Choose "CSV" for compatibility with various spreadsheet software or "Excel" for more detailed formatting. If you need a different format, like "PDF," you might need additional software installed on your computer. Simply select the desired export format from the drop-down menu and decide whether to include column headers and additional details like page numbers. Clicking "Export" will download a file to your device with the formatted data from your query.
