A calendar app is a great way to stay organized and keep track of all your important events and dates. Here are some tips to help you get the most out out of your calendar app:

* Use the calendar app as your primary source for scheduling appointments and meetings.
* Be sure to enter all recurring events into the calendar, habitually check the calendar for upcoming events, and make use of the calendar's reminders feature.
* Consider using the calendar app as an agenda for any important discussion or presentation.
* If the calendar app supports location-based reminders, make use of that feature to remind you of any appointments or meetings that take place in a particular place.
* If the calendar app supports sharing events with other people, take advantage of that feature to coordinate with others or invite them to attend an event.
