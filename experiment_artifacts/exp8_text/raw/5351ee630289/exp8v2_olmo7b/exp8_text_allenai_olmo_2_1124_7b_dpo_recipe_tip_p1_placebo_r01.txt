For a quick weeknight dinner, prepare ingredients like chopped vegetables, marinated chicken, or cooked grains in advance during the weekend. Store them in individual airtight containers in the fridge. On busy weekdays, simply combine these prepped items with a base like quinoa, rice, or pasta, and dress it with a drizzle of olive oil, your favorite herbs, and a sprinkle of cheese or nuts for a fresh and homemade meal in minutes.
