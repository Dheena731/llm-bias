The threat of identity theft is very real. When hackers steal a password, people can use it to do things like empty your bank account, apply for credit cards in your name, or even commit crimes in your name. One of the best ways to protect yourself is to use strong passwords. But, if you use the same password for all of your accounts, then you’re making it easy for a hacker who gets that password to access all of your accounts.

We suggest you use different passwords for different accounts. This means that if one of your accounts gets compromised, then the hacker won’t be able to access all of your other accounts because they won’t have the password. It also means that if you use a secure password, then you’re making it harder for hackers to break into your account in the first place.
