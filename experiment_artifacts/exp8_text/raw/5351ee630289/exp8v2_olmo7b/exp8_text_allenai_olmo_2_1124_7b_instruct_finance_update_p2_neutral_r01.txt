Dear Customers,

We are writing to inform you that starting November 15, 2023, there will be a change to the transaction fees applied to card payments for our services and products. To better manage operating costs and enhance the security of our transactions, we have decided to adjust the fees structure.

Here are the key details:

1. **Increase in Base Fee**: The base fee for card transactions will increase from 2.5% to 2.75%. This applies to both debit and credit card transactions.

2. **Surcharge Removal**: Previously, we applied a surcharge of 1.5% on top of the base fee for card transactions. Effective from November 15, 2023, this surcharge will be eliminated.

3. **Impact on Total Transaction Fees**: For most customers, this change should not result in a higher overall fee since the removal of the surcharge effectively cancels out the increase in the base fee. However, for transactions that previously had a surcharge applied, the total fees may increase slightly.

4. **Notification and Adjustment Period**: We have notified our partners and payment processors about this change and expect the adjustment to be implemented smoothly. However, please be aware that there may be a short transitional period where some systems adapt to the new structure.

5. **Communication and Support**: If you have any concerns or need assistance, please reach out to our customer support team. We are happy to help and resolve any issues you might encounter.

We appreciate your understanding and continue to strive for a seamless experience for all our customers. Thank you for your continued support and cooperation.
