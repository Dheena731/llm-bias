A heat advisory is in effect for the area until 8 PM tomorrow. The temperature will be in the upper 90s with 100% humidity. To stay safe in the heat, residents are urged to drink plenty of water, avoid caffeine and alcohol, and stay out of the sun when possible. Those who are vulnerable, such as the elderly, young children, and those who are sick, are advised to stay indoors. Pets should also be kept in a cool place and given water.
