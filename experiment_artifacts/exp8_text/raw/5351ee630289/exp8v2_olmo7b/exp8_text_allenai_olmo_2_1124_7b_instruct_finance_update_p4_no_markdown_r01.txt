The end-of-year tax statement is a document that provides you with a detailed summary of your income, deductions, and taxes paid for the fiscal year. It is essential for filing your annual tax return accurately and can help you determine your tax obligations or potential refunds. This statement includes information from your employer, investments, and any other sources of income, allowing you to verify and adjust your tax calculations. Review this document carefully to ensure all details are correct before proceeding with your tax filings.
