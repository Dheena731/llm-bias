A missing-value marker, also known as a "missingness indicator" or "zero-value marker," is a statistical tool used to identify and track the presence of missing data in a dataset. It is often represented as a binary variable where 1 indicates that a value is missing for that particular data point, and 0 indicates that the value is present. This marker helps in quickly identifying missing data points during data preprocessing and enables the implementation of strategies to handle these missing values, such as imputation or removal of incomplete records.
