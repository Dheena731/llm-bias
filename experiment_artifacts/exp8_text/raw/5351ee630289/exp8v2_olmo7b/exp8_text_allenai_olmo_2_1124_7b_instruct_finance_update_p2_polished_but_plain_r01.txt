We are writing to inform you of a recent change to transaction fees for card payments. Starting next month, there will be a slight increase in the fees applied for every card transaction processed. This adjustment is driven by changes in the cost structure of our payment processing system providers.

The new fees have been carefully calculated to ensure they reflect the up-to-date operating costs and industry standards. While we aim to keep these fees as low as possible, the increase is necessary to ensure the continued efficiency and security of your transactions.

To assist you in adjusting to this change, we've provided an estimation tool on our website which enables you to calculate potential additional costs based on your usual transaction volume. This tool will help you gauge the impact on your financial statements and make necessary preparations.

We appreciate your understanding and cooperation as we navigate this transition. Should you have any questions or require further clarification, please do not hesitate to contact our customer support team.

Thank you for your continued support.
