Road Closure Notice

Starting Monday, October 10th, 2023, at 8:00 AM, the stretch of Main Street between 5th and 10th Avenues will be closed for maintenance work. This closure will last for two weeks, until October 24th, 2023.

Motorists are advised to find an alternative route. Signs will be posted on the affected roads. Buses and taxis have been informed of the change and will adjust their routes accordingly.

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and thank you for your cooperation. For more information, please call our hotline at 555-1234.

For safety and smooth traffic flow, please drive responsibly and obey all detour signs.
