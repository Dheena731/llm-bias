**Recycling Collection Changes Commencing November 1, 2023**

Dear Residents,

We wish to inform you that starting November 1, 2023, there will be important changes to the recycling collection schedule and types of materials accepted for recycling within our community.

**Key Changes:**

1. **Day of Collection:** The recycling trucks will now collect waste on Thursdays. Please ensure your recycling bins are ready for pickup by the morning of that day.

2. **New Materials:** Aluminium foil trays, empty aerosol cans, and plastic bottle caps will now be accepted for recycling. These materials were previously excluded.

3. **Items to Exclude:** Unfortunately, we must discontinue the recycling of glass items, polystyrene, and plastic bags. Please dispose of these materials through your local waste collection service or at designated facilities.

4. **Enforcement:** We will begin to enforce collection day compliance more rigorously. Please ensure your bins are not overfilled or placed improperly, as this can lead to delays or rejections.

**Important Notes:**

- **Recycling Day:** Expect the collection every Thursday morning.
- **Expanded Acceptance:** We are expanding what can be recycled, aiming to reduce the amount of waste sent to landfills.
- **Proper Disposal:** For those materials no longer recyclable, please ensure they are disposed of correctly to prevent environmental harm.

Please read the attached flyer for more detailed information and stick to the new guidelines to ensure the smooth transition to these changes.

Your cooperation and adherence to these changes are greatly appreciated for the continuation of our recycling program's environmental benefits.

Thank you for your attention and service to our eco-friendly community.
