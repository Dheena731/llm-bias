Polling Station Locations Notice

Voters are reminded to find their assigned polling stations for the upcoming election. Please check the official list provided by your local election office or visit the Election Canada website. Polling stations are located at:

- Maple Street School, 123 Maple St, Toronto
- Community Centre, 456 Fifth Ave, Ottawa
- Public Library, 789 Queen St, Montreal
- High School, 1010 King Rd, Vancouver

Ensure you arrive before 9:30 AM local time on election day with proper identification to exercise your right to vote. For any inquiries or if you cannot locate your polling station, contact Elections Canada at 1-800-363-6666 or visit www.elections.ca. Your vote matters—plan your journey now!
