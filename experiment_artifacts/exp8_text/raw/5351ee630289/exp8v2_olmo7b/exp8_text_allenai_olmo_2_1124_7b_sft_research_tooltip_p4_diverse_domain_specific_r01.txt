A missing-value marker is a unique identifier assigned to a data point that has no corresponding value for a particular feature or field within a dataset. This marker is crucial in data analysis and processing, as it helps to distinguish between missing information (which may require further investigation or interpolation) and other data entries. Using a distinct marker for missing values ensures accurate data handling and reduces the risk of errors in downstream tasks like machine learning or statistical analysis, where missing information could lead to skewed results or biased models.
