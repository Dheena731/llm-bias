Some of the most popular email providers today have started adding the capability to use emoji in emails. For the most part, people are against this. Email Migration: The People Side

There is a people side to email migration.

I have written about the technical side of email migration in earlier blog posts.

Email migration is difficult because we must move the email that employees use to communicate in a company. This is often a very disruptive and negative experience for employees.

It is important that employees understand the email migration project. They need to understand that no email is going to be deleted and that email is just being moved. They need to understand that they will have to click on the new email link that will be sent to them.
