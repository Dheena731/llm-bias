An API rate limit is a restriction imposed by a service to prevent its resources from being overused. It sets a maximum number of requests that can be made within a specified time frame, usually a minute or hour. This limit exists to protect the service's infrastructure and ensure fair usage by users, preventing abuse that could lead to service degradation or denial of service attacks. By adhering to rate limits, consumers of the API can maintain a stable and reliable interaction with the service's resources. Exceeding the rate limit may result in temporary blocks or permanent access restrictions until the limit resets.
