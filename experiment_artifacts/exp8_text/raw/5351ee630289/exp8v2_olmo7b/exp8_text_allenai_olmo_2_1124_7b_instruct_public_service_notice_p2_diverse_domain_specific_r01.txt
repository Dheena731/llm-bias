### Attention All Lane Users: Cobblestone Crossing Road Closure

**Effective Immediately: Monday, November 13th, 2023, 6:00 AM - 4:00 PM**

Starting Monday, November 13th, the historic Cobblestone Crossing Road will undergo an extensive five-day closure for essential maintenance. The closure aims to replace damaged cobblestones, reinforce foundations, and ensure the road's longevity and safety for all drivers and pedestrians.

**Key Details:**

- **Timeline:** 6:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
- **Alternate Routes:** 
  - **Northbound:** Take Oak Avenue to access the main highway.
  *Southbound:* Redirect via Maple Street to join the highway.
- **Delays Anticipated:** Minimal. However, please plan ahead if traveling during peak hours.
- **Public Transport:** Check with local transit authorities for affected service routes.
- **Contact:** For questions or concerns, visit our town’s website or call 555-1234.

**Thank you for your cooperation and patience.**
