Want to prevent your rice from turning into an unyielding clump? Here's your handy tip: Rinse your rice thoroughly before cooking. Swirl it in a large bowl with water, draining and repeating until the rinse water runs relatively clear. This step washes away excess starch, the sticky culprit. For added measure, you can soak the rice for 15-30 minutes before cooking in a generous amount of water. Not only will this further reduce stickiness, but it also helps the grains absorb water evenly, ensuring a perfect, fluffy rice every time.
