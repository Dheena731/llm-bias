To season roasted vegetables perfectly, consider mixing your favorite herbs and spices (like garlic powder, onion powder, dried basil, oregano, and a pinch of red pepper flakes) with a bit of olive oil or melted butter before coating the vegetables. Preheat your oven, coat the vegetables thoroughly, and roast them until they're caramelized and tender, then finish with a twist of lemon or a drizzle of balsamic glaze for a pop of acidity and sweetness. This method ensures even seasoning and a beautifully seasoned roast that's packed with flavor.
