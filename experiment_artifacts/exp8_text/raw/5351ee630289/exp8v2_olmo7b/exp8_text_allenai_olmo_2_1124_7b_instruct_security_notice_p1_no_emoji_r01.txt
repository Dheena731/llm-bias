ATTENTION ALL EMPLOYEES:

RE: PASSWORD REUSE

It has come to our attention that password reuse across different accounts poses a significant security risk. We strongly advise against using the same password for both work-related and personal accounts. Passwords should be unique to each account to prevent unauthorized access in the event of a data breach.

To ensure the safety and security of our work environment, we encourage you to:

1. Use a combination of letters, numbers, and special characters for enhanced password complexity.
2. Change your work password regularly following our established guidelines.
3. Avoid saving passwords in easily accessible locations on your device.
4. Be cautious of phishing attempts and avoid sharing your password with anyone.

Your cooperation in adhering to these security practices is essential. Please contact the IT department if you need assistance with managing your passwords.

Thank you for your attention and commitment to maintaining a secure workplace.
