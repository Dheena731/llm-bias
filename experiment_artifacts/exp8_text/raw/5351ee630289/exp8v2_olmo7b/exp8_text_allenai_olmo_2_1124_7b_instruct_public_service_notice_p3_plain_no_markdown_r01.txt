Attention Residents,

We are writing to inform you of changes to our recycling collection schedule. Starting next week, the recycling trucks will now pass through your neighborhood on Wednesdays, Thursday mornings, and Saturdays instead of the previous schedule. These changes are part of our effort to improve recycling service efficiency and ensure that all items are properly sorted and processed.

Please make sure to sort your recyclables properly and place them at the curb on the designated days as per the new schedule. For your reference, the following materials are still accepted for recycling: paper, cardboard, plastics marked with the recycling symbol, metal cans, glass bottles and jars, and certain types of electronics.

Any changes or concerns regarding these new collection times or accepted materials should be directed to our Customer Service team. Thank you for your cooperation and continued involvement in our community's recycling efforts.
