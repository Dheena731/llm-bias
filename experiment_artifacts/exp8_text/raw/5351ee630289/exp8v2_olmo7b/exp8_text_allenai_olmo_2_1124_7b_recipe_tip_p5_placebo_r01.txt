I could put in a sentence or two about a lot of different things that are good to know about writing good tips, but it seems to me that the number one thing you have to do is write a good tip. If it’s good, then it can be short; it can be long. It can be 200 words or 20 words.

So here’s my tip: You will know you have written a good, short tip when you can’t resist the urge to add another sentence.

2. Write a short tip for using a chainsaw.

Be specific. If people are likely to be standing on a ladder when they read your tip, write about that. Or about standing while operating a chainsaw. Or about using a chainsaw while standing on a ladder. Or being naked on a ladder with a chainsaw.

The point is that there are many sorts (species, really) of chainsaws, and they are used in many different ways and under many different circumstances. But the one thing they have in common is that they involve cutting wood. So the first sentence should probably be something like:

When chainsawing wood, remember to…

Avoid clichés. Chainsawing is not a job for amateurs. If you’re going to tell people how to chainsaw wood, you should do it with your best effort. If you can’t think of anything to say about doing an important job safely and professionally, then you are in the wrong business.
