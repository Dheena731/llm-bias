To make rice less sticky, use a larger ratio of water to rice in your cooking process. For example, use two parts water to one part rice instead of the typical one part water to one part rice. This allows the rice to absorb more water and become fluffier without clumping together. Make sure to heat the water before adding the rice, let it come to a boil, and then reduce the heat to a simmer. Stir the rice once or twice during cooking to avoid the formation of clumps, but be gentle to prevent breaking the grains. This method helps in producing rice grains that are separate and less apt to stick together when cooked.
