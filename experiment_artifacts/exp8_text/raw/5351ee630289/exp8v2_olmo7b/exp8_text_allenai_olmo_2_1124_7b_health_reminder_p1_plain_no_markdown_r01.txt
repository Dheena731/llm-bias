The previous questions did a good job of explaining how to insert text and emoji and how to insert text using Markdown. You can follow those answers for those parts.

Because you are adding text to a note instead of a post, you will need to enter it in the "Body" field (the main text area) instead of the "Title" field.

To insert a text reminder, enter the text into the Body field. To insert an emoji, enter an emoji name into the Body field.
