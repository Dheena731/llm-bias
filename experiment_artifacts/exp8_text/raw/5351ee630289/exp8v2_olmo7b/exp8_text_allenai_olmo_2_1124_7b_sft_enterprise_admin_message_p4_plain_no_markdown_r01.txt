Attention all users,

We would like to remind you that every three months, your access to the system will be reviewed based on your activity and permissions. This review helps us ensure that everyone has only the access they need to perform their job effectively and efficiently. During this review, we may audit logs, check user permissions, and evaluate other relevant factors.

Please be aware that access reviews are an essential part of maintaining our system's security and data integrity. It is important to comply with all policies and guidelines, including those related to user access and data handling. Any misuse of permissions or unauthorized activities can result in your access being revoked or restricted.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this process, please do not hesitate to reach out to our support team. We are here to help ensure that your access remains secure and that you can continue to work effectively. Thank you for your cooperation and understanding.
