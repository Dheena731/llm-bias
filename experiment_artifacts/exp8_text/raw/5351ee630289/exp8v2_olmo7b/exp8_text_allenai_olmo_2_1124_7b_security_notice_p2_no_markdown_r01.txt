You work for a company that has been recently hacked by a group of hackers. They are planning to use phishing emails to steal login credentials from employees. You need to write a short workspace notice for your coworkers about phishing emails and how to recognize them.

Here is some information about phishing emails that might help you write your notice:

*Phishing emails often contain links to fake websites.
*These websites may look similar to legitimate websites, logins and passwords can be stolen if entered.
*The emails may also try to trick you into downloading attachments containing viruses or other malware.

Some general guidelines for avoiding phishing scams are:
*Do not respond to unsolicited emails asking for personal information (such. –Login and password, social security numbers, etc.).
*Always check the sender’s email address before opening any attachments.
*Scan all incoming emails with antivirus software to make sure they are safe.
*If you suspect an email message might be dangerous, contact your IT department immediately.

If you’re unsure about whether an email is safe to open or not, check its sender’s email address in the header of the message; this should help indicate whether it came from someone you know or not. And if there are any attachments in the message, don’t download them unless you trust the source.
