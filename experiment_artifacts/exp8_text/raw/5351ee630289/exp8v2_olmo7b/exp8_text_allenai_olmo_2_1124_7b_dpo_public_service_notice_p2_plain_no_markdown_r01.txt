**Road Closure Notice**

Effective immediately, the section of Main Street between 4th Avenue and 5th Avenue will be closed until further notice. This closure is necessary for essential maintenance work. 

Drivers are advised to find an alternate route. A temporary detour sign has been posted at both ends of the closure. Public transportation routes will be unaffected.

Please plan your journey accordingly and avoid the area during the closure period. For any inquiries, contact the city's transportation department at (555) 123-4567.

Thank you for your cooperation and understanding.
