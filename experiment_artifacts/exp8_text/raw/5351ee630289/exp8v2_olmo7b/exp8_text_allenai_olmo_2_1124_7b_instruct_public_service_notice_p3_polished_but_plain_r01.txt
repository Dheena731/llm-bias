Dear Residents,

We are writing to inform you of important changes to our recycling collection schedule, which will take effect starting next Monday, March 13th. As part of our ongoing efforts to improve waste management efficiency and consumer education, the following adjustments have been made:

* The collection of glass bottles and jars will now occur every second week, coinciding with your regular refuse bin pick-up day.
* Plastics, metals, and paper will continue to be collected weekly, following the same days they have been previously collected.
* A printable schedule detailing the new collection dates for each material can be found on our website under the 'Community Services' tab.

These changes aim to enhance the sustainability of our recycling program and remind us all of the importance of adhering to the guidelines for proper sorting and separating our recyclables. Please ensure your recyclables are clean and free from contamination to maintain the quality of the materials.

Thank you for your continued cooperation and support. We encourage you to take a moment to review the schedule and make any necessary adjustments to your recycling routines.
