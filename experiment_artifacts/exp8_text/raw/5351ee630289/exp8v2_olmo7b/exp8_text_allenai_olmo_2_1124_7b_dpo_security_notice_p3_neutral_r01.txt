Subject: Please Remember to Lock Your Screens

Dear Team,

As we continuously strive to maintain a secure and productive work environment, it is crucial that we remember to lock our computer screens whenever we step away, even for a moment. Unlocked screens can lead to potential security risks, including unauthorized access to sensitive information.

Here are a few simple steps to ensure your screen is always locked:

1. Use the keyboard shortcut: Windows users can press **Ctrl + Alt + Del** and select "Lock Computer", while Mac users can press **Command + Option + Escape**.
2. Set up a screen lock timeout in your system settings: This will automatically lock your computer after a certain period of inactivity.

By consistently locking our screens, we protect our company’s data and adhere to our security policies. It’s a small action with significant impact. Let’s all do our part to keep our workplace secure.
