To ensure better financial control and transparency across our department, we have implemented a new expense approval workflow that will go into effect from next Monday, October 4, 2023.

Starting this date, all expense reports will need to be submitted through our new online system, accessible via the link provided in the department's weekly email update. Managers will be notified immediately after an expense report is submitted and must review and approve or reject the request within 48 hours. We have also included a new feature for direct supervisors to add comments or suggestions for improvement.

Please familiarize yourself with the new system and make sure that you have any necessary supporting documents ready before submitting your expense reports.

If you encounter any issues with the online system, please contact our IT department immediately at 1-800-123-4567.

Thank you for your cooperation and attention to this important change.
