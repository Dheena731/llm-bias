**Attention All Residents: Important Recycling Collection Changes Coming Soon**

Dear Community Members,

We are pleased to inform you that as part of our ongoing effort to improve recycling services and ensure the integrity of our materials, there will be some changes to our recycling collection schedule starting next Monday, February 5th.

**Here's What You Need to Know:**

* **New Collection Days:** 
  - **Curbside Recycling:** You can set out your recyclables only on your **weekly recycling day** as detailed in your recycling schedule. Please note that we will no longer be collecting recyclables on our bi-weekly collection days.
  - **Drop-off Centers:** Our drop-off centers will remain operational, and there will be no changes to their schedule of collections.

* **Accepted Materials:** 
  - The list of recyclable materials has been revised to focus on materials with higher market value and easier processing. To avoid contamination, please make sure to empty any liquids, remove caps and labels, and follow the new guidelines available on our website and through our mobile application.

* **New Containers:** 
  - Starting next month, we're introducing new designated containers for your recyclables to ensure proper sorting. These containers will be distributed to homes in early March, so keep an eye out for them.

* **Communications:** 
  - We are committed to keeping you informed. Visit our website, download our mobile app, or subscribe to our newsletter for the latest updates and reminders about these changes.

* **Support:** 
  - If you encounter any issues or have questions about these changes, please don't hesitate to contact our customer service line at 555-1234 between 8 AM and 5 PM Monday through Friday.

We appreciate your understanding and cooperation as we work together to optimize our recycling program. These changes are part of our initiative to enhance sustainability and ensure a cleaner, greener future for all. Thank you for your continued support and participation.

**Stay Tuned:** 
More updates and reminders will follow in the coming weeks.
