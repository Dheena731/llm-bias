In today's digital age, our eyes spend much of their time fixed on screens. This constant exposure can lead to mental fatigue and physical strain. To maintain your well-being, remember to take short breaks from your screen time. Standing up, stretching, and looking at something far away can help.

Try the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds. This simple practice can prevent eye strain and refresh your focus. Incorporating these short breaks into your daily routine will not only improve your productivity but also safeguard your eyesight and overall health. Make it a habit to step away from your devices and breathe in some screen-free moments.
