The email should NOT contain emoticons (smiling faces or similar) or any symbols other than English letters and numbers.

The email should be in

  1. 12pt Calibri or 12pt Arial font
  2. one-sided
  3. A4 size (297mm x 210mm)
  4. with 2.5cm margins all around (1cm top, bottom and both sides)

There’s no need to be creative. Just write: 

  1. "Dear staff"
  2. "Thanks for your work"
  3. "Remember to lock your screens when you leave your desk."
  4. "Yours"
  5. "John Smith"

Write the email yourself, paying attention to the details. Then check your answers with a friend.

Listen and read

Now listen to part of the email we designed and read above.

You can read the transcript below:

Dear staff,

Thanks for your hard work. I know you can’t always lock your screens when you leave your desk.

But I want to remind you that we need to keep our computers safe, especially now.

That means remembering to lock your screens when you leave your desk. This is very important for our security.

If anyone finds your computer unlocked, they could steal valuable information from the company.

If they get your password or login details, they could steal money or damage the computers.

If you forget to lock your screens, please be prepared to answer some questions from your manager.

I hope this email helps you keep your screens locked.

Yours, John Smith

B) Can you think of any more reasons why we should lock our screens? Write them below.

C) What other instructions could you give to co-workers in an email like this?
