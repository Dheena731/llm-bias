When your prescription for medication runs out, it's crucial to refill it in a timely manner. Not only does this ensure you have enough of your prescribed medicine to maintain a healthy routine, but it also helps prevent any potential gaps in treatment that could affect your health. Do not wait until the bottle is empty; contact your pharmacy or healthcare provider to set up a refill as soon as you notice you're getting low. Following through with a timely refill can prevent disruptions in your medication schedule and contribute to your overall wellness.
