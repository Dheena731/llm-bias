Conversely, if you have a great study design to mind, but are uncertain about its interpretation, make that explicit.

 (Somewhat) clear research question The increased use of antibiotics is associated with the evolution of antibiotic resistance in pathogenic bacteria.

We expect to find that the increased use of antibiotics is associated with the evolution of antibiotic resistance in pathogenic bacteria. However, we do not know if this is a direct effect or if there are intermediate factors involved.

Give credit where it is due by citing your sources

Citation styles vary. Start by checking the style guide of the journal where you plan to submit.

Provide a clear and concise method section.

Include all potentially relevant information such detail about equipment and software. You do not need to include the details of each step, as we have good protocols for routine molecular and biochemical methods.

In addition to what we have mentioned, we want to emphasize that we believe that the results and discussion section should be written after the results are analyzed. Regardless of whether you use a test version of the writing guidance, it is important that you understand the value of the study design, the results and the discussion. We hope that you will understand that this is what you are writing about.
