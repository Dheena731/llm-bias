Stay hydrated! Make sure to drink enough water throughout the day to keep your body functioning optimally. A general guideline is to drink eight 8-ounce glasses, but your needs can vary based on activity level, climate, and personal health. Always listen to your body and drink more if you feel thirsty or if it's a particularly hot day. Remember, staying well-hydrated can help with concentration, energy levels, and maintaining normal bodily functions. So grab that water bottle and make it a habit!
