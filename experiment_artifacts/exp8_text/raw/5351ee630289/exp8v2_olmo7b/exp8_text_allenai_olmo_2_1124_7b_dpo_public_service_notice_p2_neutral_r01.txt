A temporary road closure will be in place at Main Street, between Elm and Oak avenues, starting this Friday, December 2nd, 2023, and lasting until the end of December 2023. This closure is to facilitate construction work related to the installation of new sewer lines in the area.

Drivers are advised to find an alternative route. The detour signs have been posted at both ends of the closure, providing directions to nearby streets. Bicyclists and pedestrians are encouraged to use the on-foot pedestrian bridge available at Oak Avenue for safe crossing.

Construction crews will be present during the day, and their work hours are from 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and thank you for your cooperation and understanding.

The community will be notified if there are any updates regarding the schedule or any changes to the closure. Please stay tuned to our website and social media channels for the most current information.

This notice is brought to you by the City of ForwardTown. Thank you for your attention and compliance with this temporary road closure.
