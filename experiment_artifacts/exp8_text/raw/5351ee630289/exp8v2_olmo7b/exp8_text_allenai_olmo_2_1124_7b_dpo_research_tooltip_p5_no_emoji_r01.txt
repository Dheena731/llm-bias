Export query results by following these steps:

1. Navigate to the results page of the query.
2. Look for an 'Export' or 'Download' button typically found at the top or bottom of the page.
3. Click on the 'Export' or 'Download' button.
4. Select the preferred format (e.g., CSV, Excel) from the available options.
5. Follow any additional prompts or instructions to complete the download.
6. Locate the saved file on your device and open it with your preferred spreadsheet or data analysis software.

Please note that if the feature is not available or does not function correctly, consult the documentation or seek support from the platform providing the query service.
