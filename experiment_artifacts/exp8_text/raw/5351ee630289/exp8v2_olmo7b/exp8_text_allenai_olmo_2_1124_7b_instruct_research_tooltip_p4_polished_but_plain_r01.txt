A missing-value marker is a symbol or value used to indicate that data is missing or is unknown in a dataset. This marker helps analysts to recognize and handle these missing values during data analysis processes. It is commonly represented by symbols like '.' or 'NaN' (Not a Number) in numerical data, or 'NA' or ' Null' in textual data. Using such markers ensures that the absence of data is clearly understood and addressed, preventing potential errors or incorrect conclusions in statistical evaluations and analyses.
