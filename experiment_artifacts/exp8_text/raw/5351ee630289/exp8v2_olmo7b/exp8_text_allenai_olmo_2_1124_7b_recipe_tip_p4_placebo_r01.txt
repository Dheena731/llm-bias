Prompts for "Howto"

These are good in everyday situations where you have to quickly write down step by steps for accomplishing some task. They are also good as prompts for longer lists of instructions such quick guides to some tool, or some process often used with that tool. They are good as reminders for yourself, as starters for writing articles for end-users of a tool, or as starter lists for writing long or complicated instructions.

  • How to get a quick rough estimate of the total cost of a house by estimating its size, square footage, and so forth.
  • How to quickly read a printout of computer type (font) size.
  • How to make a list of addresses, names, phone numbers, and email addresses for people who are invited to a general party.
  • How to quickly estimate how many bows you might need to purchase for a wedding reception.
  • How to start to manually prepare a list of addresses to invite to a wedding reception.
  • How to decide to send an email or write a letter.

Prompts for "Nonfiction"

These prompts work well for general nonfiction. They are good for short fiction as well where there is some major event or character change.

  • Character's car breaks down on the way to a date or other important occasion.
  • Character decides to sleep longer than usual on Sunday morning, and misses a plane.
  • Character misses a meeting. Why? Who is there?
  • Character gets yelled at during lunch hour in the middle of a mall.
  • Character gets fired, and searches for jobs during the day, writing letters in the evening.
  • Character is out of money and buys fruit from a store with only one really rotten piece of fruit.
  • Character is out of gas, but soon has enough to get to a gas station.
  • Character walks into a conversation between three people, two of whom do not know each other.
  • Character goes to a concert without a ticket, and sees what happens.
  • Character buys a car, but then one of the tires goes flat.

Prompts for "Stories"

Good prompts for short fiction and stories. They are good for long fiction, too.

  • After years of being happily married, a man finds that his wife has been cheating on him with the cable guy.
  • A woman makes plans to meet her husband after work, but he does not show.
  • A man starts work at a new job and learns that his coworkers are all involved in a ritual sacrifice.
  • A man and his wife are having an argument when they are interrupted by another man who has been breaking into their car.
  • A woman who reads all the time suddenly cannot read, and tries to find out why.
  • A man discovers that he hates all his friends, and decides to go to a museum.
  • A man driving down the street finds a large pile of money in the road, and picks it up.
  • A man finds a strange little dog on his doorstep, and decides to keep him as a pet.
  • A man is walking down the street when he sees a man hanging upside down from a tree.
  • A man is walking down the street when he sees a man trying to rob a store.
  • A man is walking down the street when he sees a man arguing with a dog.
  • A man walks into a bar and sees a friend being attacked by a friend's brother, and intervens.
