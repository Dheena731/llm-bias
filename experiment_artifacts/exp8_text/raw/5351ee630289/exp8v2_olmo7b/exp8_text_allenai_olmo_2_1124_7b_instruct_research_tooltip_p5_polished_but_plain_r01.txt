To export query results, follow these simple steps. First, ensure your query is correctly formulated and executed within the platform's tools. Next, locate the 'Download' or 'Export' option typically represented by a downward-facing arrow or similar icon near your results display. Click this icon to initiate the export process. Depending on the platform, you may be asked to select a file format for your results (e.g., CSV, Excel). Once your selection is made, confirm the export to receive a file containing your query results, which you can then save or share as needed.
