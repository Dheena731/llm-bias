Notice of Recycling Collection Changes

Dear Residents,

We are writing to inform you of upcoming changes to our recycling collection schedule. Starting next week, our recycling collections will be held on alternate weeks. This means that if your collection day is this week, it will change to the following week, and vice versa.

Please make sure to follow these new collection dates and adjust your recycling routine accordingly. This change is necessary to ensure efficient service provided by our recycling partners and to reduce potential confusion among residents.

Any questions regarding this change or our recycling program can be directed to our customer service team. Thank you for your cooperation and continued support of our community recycling efforts.

Sincerely,

Your City Recycling Manager
