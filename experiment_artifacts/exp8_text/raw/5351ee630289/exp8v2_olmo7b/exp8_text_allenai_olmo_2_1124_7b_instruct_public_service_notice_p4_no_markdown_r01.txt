**HEAT ADVISORY NOTICE**

TO ALL RESIDENTS:

Due to an upcoming heat wave, the National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for the area. The temperatures are expected to reach dangerously high levels potentially exceeding 95 degrees Fahrenheit. 

**PRECAUTIONARY MEASURES:**

- Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water.
- Limit outdoor activities especially during peak hours.
- Check on family, friends, and neighbors, especially the elderly and those who work outdoors.
- Avoid exertion during the hottest part of the day.
- Use air conditioning and keep homes cool.

**REMEMBER:** The extreme heat can lead to dehydration, heat exhaustion, and even heat stroke. Plan ahead by preparing for potential power outages and ensuring your vehicles have enough coolant. Avoid leaving children or pets unattended in parked vehicles.

**CHECK LOCAL ANNOUNCEMENTS:** For the most current information, monitor weather updates and listen for emergency alerts. Keep an eye on local news and weather channels for additional guidance and cancellation updates for public events as they may be affected by the heat advisory.

STAY COOL AND STAY INFORMED.
