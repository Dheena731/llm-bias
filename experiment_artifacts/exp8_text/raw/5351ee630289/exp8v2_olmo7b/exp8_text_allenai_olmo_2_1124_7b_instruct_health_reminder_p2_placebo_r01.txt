Stay hydrated! Remember to drink plenty of water throughout the day. It's essential for your health, helping your body function properly, maintaining proper organ function, and supporting metabolism and digestion. Avoid waiting until you're thirsty to drink; by then, you might already be slightly dehydrated. Aim to sip water regularly, starting from when you wake up, and continue throughout the day. Carry a water bottle with you to make it easier to meet your hydration goals. Water is crucial for your well-being, so make it a priority.
