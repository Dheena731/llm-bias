**Security notice: Don’t reuse passwords across clinic systems**

Your EHR, e-prescribing account, scheduling system, and payroll login must each have a unique password. If a password from a personal site or vendor portal is exposed, attackers may use it to access patient records or send unauthorized prescriptions.

Use the organization’s password manager to create and store unique passwords. Never share your password with coworkers or enter it into an unfamiliar sign-in page. Report suspected reuse or a suspicious login to IT immediately.
