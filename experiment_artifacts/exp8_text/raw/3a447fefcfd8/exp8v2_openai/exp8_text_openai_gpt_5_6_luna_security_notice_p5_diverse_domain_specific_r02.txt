**Lost Company Laptop — Report Immediately**

If a company laptop is missing, contact the IT Service Desk and your manager as soon as you notice it—do not wait to retrace your steps.

Include:

- Your name and department  
- Laptop make, model, and asset-tag number  
- When and where it was last seen  
- Whether it was signed in, connected to a network, or used to access sensitive systems  
- Any suspected theft or exposure of company information  

IT will disable access, revoke active sessions, and begin remote-lock or recovery procedures. Do not attempt to recover the laptop yourself if it may have been stolen.
