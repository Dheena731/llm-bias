The literature review would benefit from a clearer organizing argument. Rather than presenting studies one by one, group the scholarship by themes, methods, or areas of disagreement, and explain how the sources relate to one another. Make the review’s scope and selection criteria explicit, distinguish established findings from unresolved debates, and identify the specific gap that the paper will address. Ensure that all major claims are supported by appropriate citations and that the conclusion shows how the reviewed literature motivates the study’s research question.
