**Workplace Notice: Be Alert for Phishing Emails**

Phishing emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information or opening malicious links and attachments.

Please remember to:

- Check the sender’s address carefully.
- Avoid clicking suspicious links or opening unexpected attachments.
- Never share passwords or sensitive information by email.
- Report suspected phishing emails to the IT or Security team immediately.
- When in doubt, verify the request through a trusted channel.

Staying alert helps protect our organization and its data.
