**Shared Network Acceptable Use Notice**

Use of this shared network is permitted only for lawful, authorized, and responsible purposes. Users must not access, transmit, store, or distribute content that is illegal, harmful, abusive, defamatory, infringing, or that compromises network security or the privacy of others.

Unauthorized access, interference with network operations, excessive bandwidth consumption, distribution of malware, and attempts to bypass security controls are prohibited. Users are responsible for activity conducted through their accounts or devices and must comply with all applicable laws and organizational policies.

Network activity may be monitored, logged, and restricted as necessary to protect users, systems, and services. Violations may result in suspension or termination of access and may be reported to the appropriate authorities.
