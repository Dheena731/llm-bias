**Introducing *The Listening Hour*** — a language-learning podcast for the parts of a language textbooks tend to skip: swallowed syllables, unfinished sentences, regional slang, and the pause before someone says *well…*

Each episode takes one short, natural conversation and slows it down without flattening it. You’ll hear the exchange at full speed, unpack useful phrases and pronunciation, then shadow it line by line. Start with our first episode on ordering coffee without accidentally asking for a *decaf disaster*.
