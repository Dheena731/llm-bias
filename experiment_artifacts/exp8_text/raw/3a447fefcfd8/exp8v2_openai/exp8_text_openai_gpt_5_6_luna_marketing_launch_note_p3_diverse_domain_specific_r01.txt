### Budgeting Spreadsheet for Real-World Cash Flow

A practical spreadsheet for managing rent, subscriptions, annual bills, debt payments, and the expenses that don’t arrive neatly each month.

It includes:

- A pay-period budget view for weekly, biweekly, or irregular income  
- Sinking funds for insurance, repairs, holidays, and other “surprise” costs  
- Automatic planned-versus-actual tracking by category  
- Debt payoff and savings goal tabs  
- A 12-month overview that highlights upcoming shortfalls before they happen  

Works in Excel and Google Sheets, with no macros or account connections required.
