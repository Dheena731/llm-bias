**Security notice: watch for fake payroll and document requests**

We are seeing phishing emails that appear to come from managers, payroll, or shared-drive services. Common examples include requests to:

- Confirm bank or tax information  
- Review a “salary adjustment” document  
- Buy gift cards or make an urgent payment  
- Approve a new sign-in or MFA request  

Do not use links in unexpected messages or reply with passwords, codes, or financial details. Open the payroll or document system from your usual bookmark instead. If a request involves money or account changes, verify it using a known phone number or Teams contact—not the details in the email.

Report suspicious messages through the **“Report phishing”** button in Outlook.
