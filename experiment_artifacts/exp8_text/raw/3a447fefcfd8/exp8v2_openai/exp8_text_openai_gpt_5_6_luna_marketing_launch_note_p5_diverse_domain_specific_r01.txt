**Now listening: *Say It Again, Slowly***  

A language-learning podcast for the moments textbooks skip: interrupting politely, sounding less formal at the bakery, recovering after you forget a word. Each short episode follows a real conversation, then pauses to unpack pronunciation, tone, and the phrases people actually use.

New episodes feature one practical situation, a few useful expressions, and a chance to hear them at natural speed before trying them yourself. Start with “Making Small Talk Without Talking About the Weather.”
