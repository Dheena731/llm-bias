**Library Hours Notice**

Riverside Public Library is open:

- **Monday–Thursday:** 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m.  
- **Friday:** 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.  
- **Saturday:** 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.  
- **Sunday:** Closed  

The book drop beside the main entrance is available whenever the library is closed. Hours may change on public holidays; please check the library website before visiting.
