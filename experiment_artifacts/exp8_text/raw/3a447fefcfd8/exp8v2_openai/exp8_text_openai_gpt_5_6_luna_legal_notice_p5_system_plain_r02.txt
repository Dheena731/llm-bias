Use of the shared network is permitted only for lawful, authorized, and business-appropriate purposes. Users must not access, transmit, store, or distribute unlawful, harmful, defamatory, offensive, or unauthorized content; interfere with network operations; attempt to bypass security controls; or access another person’s data or accounts without permission. Network activity may be monitored, logged, and restricted to protect users, systems, and organizational resources. Violations may result in suspension of access, disciplinary action, civil liability, or referral to law enforcement.
