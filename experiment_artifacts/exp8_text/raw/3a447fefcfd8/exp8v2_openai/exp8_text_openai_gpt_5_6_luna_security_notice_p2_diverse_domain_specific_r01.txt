**Security Notice: Watch for Fake Shared-File and Payroll Emails**

Phishing messages may appear to come from a coworker, a document-sharing service, or Payroll. Be cautious of emails asking you to:

- Open an unexpected “shared document” or invoice  
- Update direct-deposit or tax information  
- Enter your password or approve an MFA prompt  
- Act urgently to avoid a payment or account suspension  

Don’t use links in the message to sign in. Open the service through your usual bookmark, or confirm the request with the sender using a known phone number or Teams chat.

In Outlook, select **Report Message → Phishing**. If you entered credentials or approved an unexpected MFA request, contact the IT help desk immediately.
