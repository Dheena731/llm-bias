**Workplace Notice: Watch for Phishing Emails**

Phishing emails are designed to trick you into sharing information, opening harmful attachments, or clicking unsafe links. Be cautious of unexpected messages, urgent requests, unfamiliar senders, and links or attachments you were not expecting.

Do not click suspicious links, open unexpected attachments, or provide passwords or other sensitive information by email. If you are unsure whether a message is legitimate, report it to the appropriate IT or security team before taking action.
