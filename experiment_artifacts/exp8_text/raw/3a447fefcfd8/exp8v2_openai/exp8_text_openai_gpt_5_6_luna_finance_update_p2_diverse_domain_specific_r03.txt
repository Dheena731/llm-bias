**Subject: Change to card transaction fees**

From **1 July 2025**, the fee for successful card payments processed through our online checkout will change from **2.4% + $0.30** to **2.6% + $0.30** per transaction.

The new rate applies to Visa and Mastercard payments, including recurring charges captured on or after that date. Declined authorizations, bank transfers, and refunds are not affected. For a **$100 card payment**, the processing fee will increase from **$2.70 to $2.90**.
