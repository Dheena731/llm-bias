**ROAD CLOSURE NOTICE**

County Road 18 will be closed between Mill Creek Bridge and Old Quarry Road from **7 a.m. Monday, June 10, through 6 p.m. Wednesday, June 12**, while crews replace a damaged culvert.

Traffic will be detoured via **Route 6 and Pine Hollow Road**. Local residents may access driveways from the Old Quarry Road side, but no through traffic will be permitted. Emergency services will have access throughout the closure.

Please allow extra travel time and follow posted detour signs.
