**Watch for phishing emails**

Before opening an attachment or approving a payment, check the sender carefully—especially messages claiming to be from Payroll, HR, IT, or a supplier. Be cautious of requests to:

- Change bank or direct-deposit details  
- Buy gift cards or send payment urgently  
- Share passwords, MFA codes, or employee information  
- Open unexpected invoices, shared documents, or login links  

If a message feels unusual, don’t reply or click. Verify the request using a known phone number or internal contact, then report the email through **[your phishing-reporting button/process]** and delete it.
