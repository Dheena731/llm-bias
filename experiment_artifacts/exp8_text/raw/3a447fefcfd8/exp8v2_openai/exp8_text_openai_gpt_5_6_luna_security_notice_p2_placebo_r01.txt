**Workplace Notice: Beware of Phishing Emails**

Phishing emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information or opening harmful links and attachments. Be cautious of unexpected messages, urgent requests, unfamiliar senders, and links that ask you to sign in or provide personal information.

Do not click suspicious links or open unexpected attachments. Verify unusual requests through a trusted channel, and report suspected phishing emails to your IT or security team.
