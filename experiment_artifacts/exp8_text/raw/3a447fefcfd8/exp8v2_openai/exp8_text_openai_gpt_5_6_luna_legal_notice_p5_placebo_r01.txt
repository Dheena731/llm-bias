**Shared Network Acceptable Use Notice**

Users of this shared network must use it lawfully, responsibly, and only for authorized purposes. Users must not:

- access, use, or interfere with another person’s account, device, data, or communications without permission;
- introduce malware or attempt to bypass security controls;
- disrupt network availability or impair other users’ access; or
- use the network to violate applicable law or the rights of others.

Users are responsible for activity conducted through their accounts or devices. Network access may be suspended or restricted in response to misuse, subject to applicable law and governing policies.
