**Riverside Public Library — Opening Hours**

The library is open:

- **Monday–Thursday:** 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m.  
- **Friday–Saturday:** 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.  
- **Sunday:** Closed  

The book drop beside the main entrance is available whenever the building is closed. Please note that the children’s room closes at 7:30 p.m. on weekdays.
