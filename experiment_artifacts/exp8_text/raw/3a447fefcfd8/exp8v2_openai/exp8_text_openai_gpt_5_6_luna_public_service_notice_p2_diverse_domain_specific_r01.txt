**ROAD CLOSURE NOTICE**

Maple Street will be closed between 3rd Avenue and Pine Road from **7 a.m. Monday, June 10, through 6 p.m. Wednesday, June 12**, while crews replace a damaged water main.

Use **Oak Street** as the signed detour. Local residents will retain access from the Pine Road side, but brief delays are expected near the work zone. Emergency access will be maintained. Dates may change if weather or site conditions delay the work.
