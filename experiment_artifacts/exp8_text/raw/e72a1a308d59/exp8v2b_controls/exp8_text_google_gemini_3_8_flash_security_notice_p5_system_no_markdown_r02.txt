URGENT NOTICE: REPORTING A LOST OR STOLEN COMPANY LAPTOP

If you have misplaced or lost your company-issued laptop, you must report the incident immediately to ensure the security of our systems and data. 

Please take the following steps as soon as you realize the device is missing:

1. Contact IT Support immediately by calling 555-0199 or emailing it-support@company.com so the team can remotely lock and secure the device.
2. Inform your direct supervisor about the situation.
3. If the laptop was stolen, file a report with local law enforcement and provide the case number to Corporate Security.

When reporting, please provide the last known location of the laptop, the approximate time it went missing, and the asset tag or serial number if you have it available. 

Prompt reporting is critical to preventing unauthorized access to sensitive company information. Thank you for your cooperation.
