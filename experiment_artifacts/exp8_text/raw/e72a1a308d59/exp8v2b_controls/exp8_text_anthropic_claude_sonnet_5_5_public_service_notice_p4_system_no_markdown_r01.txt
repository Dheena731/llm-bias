PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT

Issued by the City Office of Emergency Management

A Heat Advisory is in effect from 11:00 a.m. Thursday until 9:00 p.m. Saturday. Daytime temperatures are expected to reach 98 to 104 degrees Fahrenheit, with heat index values up to 110 degrees. Overnight lows will stay in the upper 70s, offering little relief.

Who is most at risk: older adults, infants and young children, people with chronic health conditions, outdoor workers, and pets.

What you should do:

- Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty.
- Stay in air-conditioned spaces whenever possible.
- Limit outdoor activity to early morning or evening hours, and take frequent breaks in the shade.
- Wear lightweight, light-colored, loose-fitting clothing.
- Never leave children, pets, or vulnerable adults in a parked vehicle, even for a short time.
- Check on neighbors, relatives, and friends who live alone.

Cooling centers: Free cooling centers will be open daily from 10:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. at the Main Public Library, the Eastside Community Center, and the Riverside Senior Center. Water will be provided.

Watch for signs of heat illness. Heat exhaustion may cause heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, nausea, or headache. Move to a cool place, loosen clothing, and sip water. Heat stroke is an emergency. Signs include a body temperature above 103 degrees, confusion, hot dry skin, or loss of consciousness. Call 911 immediately.

For cooling center information or transportation assistance, call 311 or visit the city website. For updates, follow the city's official social media channels or tune in to local radio and television stations.

Stay cool and look out for one another.
