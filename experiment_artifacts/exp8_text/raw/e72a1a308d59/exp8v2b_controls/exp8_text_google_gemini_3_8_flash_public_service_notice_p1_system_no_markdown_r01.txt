PUBLIC SERVICE ANNOUNCEMENT: LIBRARY HOURS OF OPERATION

The Central Public Library is pleased to announce its regular operating hours to better serve the community.

Monday through Thursday: 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM
Friday and Saturday: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
Sunday: 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM

Please note that our book drop-off bins located near the main entrance are open 24/7 for returns. Digital resources, including e-books and online databases, remain accessible anytime through our website.

For renewals, program schedules, or further inquiries, please visit www.yourlocallibrary.org or call (555) 019-2834. We look forward to your visit.
