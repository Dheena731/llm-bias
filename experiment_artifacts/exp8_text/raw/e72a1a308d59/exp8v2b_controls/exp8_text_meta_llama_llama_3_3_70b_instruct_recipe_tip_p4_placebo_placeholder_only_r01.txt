To plan lunches for a busy week, consider setting aside one hour on the weekend to prepare and portion out individual meals. Start by making a large batch of a versatile ingredient, such as grilled chicken or cooked rice, and then use it to create multiple lunches throughout the week. Pack each lunch in a separate container and label it with the day of the week, making it easy to grab and go in the morning. This simple strategy can save you time and money, and ensure that you have healthy and satisfying meals to fuel your busy week.
