**PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE**

Please be advised that Main Street, between 1st Avenue and 4th Avenue, will be temporarily closed to all through traffic starting Monday, October 23, through Wednesday, October 25. The closure will be in effect daily from 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

**Reason for Closure:** Scheduled roadway resurfacing and utility maintenance.

**Detour Information:** 
A marked detour will be in place directing traffic via Oak Street. Local access will be maintained for residents and emergency vehicles, though minor delays should be expected.

Drivers are encouraged to plan alternative routes and exercise caution when traveling near the work zone. 

For questions or more information, please contact the City Department of Public Works at (555) 019-2834. 

We appreciate your patience and cooperation as we work to improve local infrastructure.
