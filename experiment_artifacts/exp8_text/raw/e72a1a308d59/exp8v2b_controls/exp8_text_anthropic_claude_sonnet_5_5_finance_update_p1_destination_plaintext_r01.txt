Subject: New Monthly Spending Summary Now Available

Hello,

We've added a monthly spending summary to help you see where your money goes each month. It's available now and requires no setup.

What it shows:
- Your total spending for the month
- A breakdown by category, such as groceries, dining, transportation, and bills
- A comparison with the previous month so you can spot changes
- Your largest individual purchases

How it works:
At the start of each month, we'll generate a summary of the month before. You can view it anytime from the Reports section of your account. Past summaries are saved so you can look back over time.

Tips for getting the most from it:
- Review your categories to make sure transactions are sorted correctly. You can reassign any purchase if it looks off.
- Use the month-over-month comparison to find areas where you might want to cut back.
- Check in early each month while your spending is still fresh in your mind.

We built this based on your feedback, and we'd love to hear what you think. If there's something you'd like to see added, such as custom categories or budget goals, let us know through the Feedback page.

Thank you for using our service.
