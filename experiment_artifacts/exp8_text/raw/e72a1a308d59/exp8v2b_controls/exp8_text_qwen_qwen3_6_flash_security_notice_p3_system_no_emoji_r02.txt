NOTICE: WORKSTATION SECURITY REMINDER

To safeguard company data and comply with security protocols, all staff are reminded to lock their computer screens whenever they step away from their desks, even for brief periods.

Lock your screen by pressing the Windows key plus L. You may also enable automatic screen locking through your operating system settings to ensure consistent protection during unattended moments.

Unsecured workstations pose a significant risk to data privacy and operational security. For assistance configuring automatic lock settings or reviewing our full security guidelines, please contact IT Support at [insert email or phone number].
