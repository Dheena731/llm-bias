**Introducing BudgetBase: A Simple Monthly Budgeting Spreadsheet**

We're excited to announce BudgetBase, a free-to-download spreadsheet template that helps you track your income, plan your spending, and see where your money goes each month.

BudgetBase is designed for individuals and households who want a clear picture of their finances without learning complicated software. Simply enter your monthly income and expenses, and the template automatically calculates your totals, remaining balance, and spending by category.

**Key features:**
- Pre-built categories for housing, food, transportation, utilities, savings, and entertainment
- Automatic totals and remaining balance, updated as you type
- A summary page showing how your spending compares to your plan
- Works in Excel, Google Sheets, and Apple Numbers

BudgetBase is available now at no cost. Download it, enter your numbers, and start building a plan that fits your life.
