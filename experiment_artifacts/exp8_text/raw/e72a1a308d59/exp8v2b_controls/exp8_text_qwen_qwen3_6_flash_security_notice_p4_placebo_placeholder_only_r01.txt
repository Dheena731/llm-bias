**Subject: Action Required: Enable Two-Factor Authentication**

**To All Users,**

To strengthen account security and protect sensitive data, two-factor authentication is now mandatory for every account.

Please take the following steps to activate this feature immediately:

1. Log in to your account dashboard.
2. Navigate to the Security or Privacy settings menu.
3. Locate the two-factor authentication option and select Enable.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to verify your mobile device or email address.

Completing this setup is required to maintain uninterrupted access to your account. Accounts with two-factor authentication disabled will be restricted after the current grace period ends.

If you experience any issues during activation or require guidance, contact the technical support team for assistance.

Thank you for your prompt attention to this security update.
