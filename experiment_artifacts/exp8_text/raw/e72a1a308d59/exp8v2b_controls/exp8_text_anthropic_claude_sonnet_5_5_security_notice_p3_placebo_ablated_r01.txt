**Reminder: Lock Your Screen When You Step Away**

All staff are reminded to lock their computer screens whenever they leave their workstations, even for a short time.

**How to lock your screen:**
- **Windows:** Press the Windows key + L
- **Mac:** Press Control + Command + Q

**Why it matters:** An unlocked screen allows anyone nearby to view or access confidential information, or to send messages under your name. Locking your screen takes only a second and helps protect our colleagues, clients, and organization.

Thank you for your cooperation. If you have trouble locking your screen, please contact the IT help desk.
