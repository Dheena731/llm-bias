To: All Users

As part of our ongoing effort to maintain the security and integrity of our systems, we will be conducting our quarterly access reviews. During this process, we will be reviewing and verifying the access permissions of all users to ensure that they are up-to-date and aligned with current job responsibilities.

You may be contacted by the IT department to confirm your current access needs or to request updates to your permissions. Your cooperation and prompt response to these requests are greatly appreciated.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding the access review process, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT department.

Thank you for your understanding and assistance in maintaining the security of our systems.
