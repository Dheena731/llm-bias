**Rinse thoroughly before cooking:** 

The most effective way to prevent gummy, sticky rice is to rinse it in cold water before it hits the pot. Place the dry grains in a fine-mesh strainer (or a bowl) and rinse, gently swirling with your hands, until the water runs completely clear. This washes away the excess surface starch that causes the grains to clump together as they cook. 

*Bonus tip:* Once cooked, let the rice rest off the heat, covered, for 10 minutes, then fluff it gently with a fork rather than stirring it with a spoon.
