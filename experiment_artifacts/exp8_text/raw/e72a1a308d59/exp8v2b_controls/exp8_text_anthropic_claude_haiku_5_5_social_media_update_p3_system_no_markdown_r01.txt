Join us this Saturday at Riverside Park for our Community Spring Picnic! Bring the family from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. for live music, face painting, a bounce house for the kids, and food trucks serving everything from tacos to fresh lemonade. Local volunteers will also be on hand to share info about neighborhood programs and ways to get involved. Admission is free, and everyone is welcome. Bring a blanket, grab a seat on the grass, and come say hello. See you there!
