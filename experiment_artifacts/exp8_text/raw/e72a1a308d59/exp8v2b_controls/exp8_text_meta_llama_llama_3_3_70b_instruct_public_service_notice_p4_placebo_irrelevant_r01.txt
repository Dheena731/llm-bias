**URGENT - HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

A Heat Advisory has been issued for our area, effective immediately. Due to extreme temperatures and high humidity, residents are advised to take precautions to stay safe.

The National Weather Service has forecasted temperatures to reach up to 105°F (40°C) with a heat index of 115°F (46°C) over the next 48 hours. This can lead to heat-related illnesses, particularly for vulnerable populations such as the elderly, young children, and those with pre-existing medical conditions.

To stay safe, please:

* Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water
* Avoid strenuous outdoor activities, especially during peak sun hours (11am-3pm)
* Stay indoors in air-conditioned spaces as much as possible
* Check on vulnerable neighbors and family members
* Never leave children or pets unattended in vehicles

If you or someone you know is experiencing symptoms of heat exhaustion or heat stroke, such as dizziness, nausea, or headaches, seek medical attention immediately.

Stay cool and stay safe! For more information, visit our website or contact local authorities for updates.
