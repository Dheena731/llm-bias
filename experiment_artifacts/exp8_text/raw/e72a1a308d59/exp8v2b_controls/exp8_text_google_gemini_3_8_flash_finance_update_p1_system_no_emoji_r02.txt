**New Feature: Monthly Spending Summary**

We are introducing a new Monthly Spending Summary to help you better track and understand your finances. 

Starting this month, you will receive an automated overview at the end of each billing cycle. This report includes:

* A breakdown of your top spending categories
* Month-over-month comparison trends
* Key insights into recurring subscriptions and unusual charges

You can view your current summary now by navigating to the "Insights" tab in your account dashboard. Adjust your notification preferences at any time in your account settings.
