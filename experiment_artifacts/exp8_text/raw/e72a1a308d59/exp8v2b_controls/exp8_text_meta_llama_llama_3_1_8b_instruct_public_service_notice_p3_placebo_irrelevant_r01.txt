**Important Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection**

**Effective March 1, 2023**

Residents of [City/Town Name] are advised that our recycling collection service is undergoing a change. As part of our ongoing efforts to improve sustainability and reduce waste, we are introducing new collection schedules and guidelines.

**Key Changes:**

*   **Collection Days:** Recycling collection days will now be on Thursdays instead of Tuesdays.
*   **Collection Times:** Collection times will remain between 7:00 AM and 5:00 PM.
*   **Recyclable Materials:** We will no longer accept glass bottles and jars. This decision is aimed at reducing contamination and ensuring the quality of materials being processed.
*   **Additional Materials:** We are introducing the collection of plastic bags, which can now be placed in the recycling bin.

**What You Can Do:**

*   **Separate Recyclables:** Continue to sort recyclables from non-recyclables to ensure the quality of materials.
*   **Reduce Contamination:** Please do not place food waste, liquids, or loose items in the recycling bin.
*   **Check Your Bin:** Ensure your recycling bin is clearly marked with your address and is not overfilled.

**Questions and Concerns:**

If you have any questions or concerns about these changes, please contact our Customer Service team at [phone number] or [email address].
