We are excited to announce the launch of Savings Goals, a new tool designed to help you reach your financial milestones with confidence. 

Whether you are building an emergency fund, preparing for a vacation, or planning for a major purchase, you can now create dedicated targets directly within your account. Simply name your target, choose an end date, and watch your progress update in real time. You can also turn on automated recurring transfers to keep your momentum going without extra effort.

This feature is available today across all accounts. Update your app to the latest version or log in on the web, then head to the Savings tab to set up your first goal.
