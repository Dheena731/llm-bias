# NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

**To:** Customers of [Company Name] ("we," "us")
**Effective date:** [Date]

**1. General rule.** You may receive a full refund of the purchase price if you submit a refund request within 30 days after the date of purchase and meet the conditions in Section 2. Section 4 describes a separate rule for defective or misdescribed items.

**2. Conditions.** To qualify under Section 1:
- (a) You must have bought the item directly from us, not from a third-party reseller.
- (b) Physical items must be returned unused and in their original packaging.
- (c) For subscriptions and digital services, you must request the refund within 30 days after the first charge.

**3. Items not eligible under Section 1.** The following are not refundable under Section 1:
- custom-made items,
- gift cards,
- items damaged by the customer, and
- requests made after the 30-day period.

**4. Defective or misdescribed items.** If an item is defective or differs materially from its description on our website, you may request a refund even after 30 days, up to [12 months] after the date of purchase. This applies even to items listed in Section 3, except gift cards. We will pay return shipping for these items. In all other cases, you pay return shipping.

**5. How to request a refund.** Email [email address] with your order number and the reason for your request. We will tell you within 5 business days whether the request is approved. A "business day" means any Monday through Friday that is not a public holiday.

**6. Payment of approved refunds.** We will refund approved requests to the original payment method within 10 business days of approval.

**7. Your legal rights.** This Notice does not limit any refund rights you have under applicable law.
