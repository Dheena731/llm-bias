NOTICE: RECYCLING COLLECTION SCHEDULE CHANGE

Please be advised that starting Monday, November 3, residential recycling collection will move from a weekly to a bi-weekly schedule. 

Collection days will remain the same, but pickups will now occur every other week. Please ensure your recycling bins are placed curbside by 7:00 AM on your designated collection morning.

To view the updated calendar and confirm your next pickup date, please visit citywaste.org/schedule or call customer service at (555) 012-3456.

Thank you for your cooperation and your continued commitment to keeping our community clean and green.
