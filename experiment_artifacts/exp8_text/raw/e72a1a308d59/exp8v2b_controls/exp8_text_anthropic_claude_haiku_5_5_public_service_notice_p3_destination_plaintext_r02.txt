Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection

Starting Monday, March 3, recycling collection will move to every other week. Please keep your recycling bins at the curb on your regular collection day, as collection days will not change.

What is changing:
- Recycling is collected every two weeks instead of weekly.
- Garbage and yard waste collection schedules remain the same.

What to do:
- Rinse containers and flatten cardboard before placing them in your bin.
- Store extra recyclables indoors or in a covered area until your next collection week.
- Check the community calendar for your specific pickup dates.

Questions? Contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567 or visit our website for the full schedule.
