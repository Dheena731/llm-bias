**Notice to the Public: Library Hours**

The Central Public Library wants to remind all residents of our current opening hours:

- **Monday through Friday:** 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
- **Saturday:** 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- **Sunday:** 12:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m.

The library is closed on all major public holidays. Hours may change during holiday weeks or special events, so please check our website or call ahead before your visit.

Books, computers, and study rooms are available to everyone, and library cards are free to all residents. We look forward to welcoming you.
