**PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE: Standard Library Operating Hours**

The public library welcomes all community members to explore our collection, access public computers, and enjoy quiet study spaces throughout the week. 

Regular operating hours are as follows:

* **Monday through Thursday:** 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM  
* **Friday and Saturday:** 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM  
* **Sunday:** 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM  

The exterior return drop is available twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week, for book and media returns. Online resources, including e-books, audiobooks, and research databases, remain accessible at all times through the digital catalog. 

Staff members are available during open hours to assist with research, card registration, and general inquiries. We look forward to seeing you soon.
