Enabling 2FA adds an extra layer of protection by requiring a second form of verification, such as a code from an authenticator app or SMS, in addition to your password. This safeguards your account even if your password is compromised.

To set up 2FA:
1. Log in to your account.
2. Navigate to Settings > Security.
