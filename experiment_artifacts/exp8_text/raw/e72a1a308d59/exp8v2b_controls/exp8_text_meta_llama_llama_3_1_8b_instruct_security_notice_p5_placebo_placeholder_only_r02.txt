**Lost Company Laptop**

If you have lost a company laptop, please report it to the IT department as soon as possible. This will help us to quickly locate the device and take necessary steps to protect our company's data.

Please provide the following information when reporting the lost laptop:

* Laptop serial number
* Laptop make and model
* Last known location of the laptop

You can report the lost laptop by sending an email to itdepartment@company.com or by contacting the IT department directly at (phone number).
