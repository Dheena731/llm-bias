PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE

Please be advised that Main Street between 1st Avenue and 4th Avenue will be fully closed to all vehicular traffic starting Monday, October 14, at 7:00 AM through Wednesday, October 16, at 5:00 PM.

This closure is necessary for scheduled water main replacements and road repaving. 

Detour routes will be clearly marked along Oak Street. Local access will be maintained for residents and emergency services, but delays are expected. Commuters are advised to seek alternate routes.

For any questions or concerns, please contact the City Department of Public Works at (555) 019-2834.

Thank you for your patience and cooperation as we work to improve our city's infrastructure.
