**Lost Company Laptop Notice**

**If Your Laptop Goes Missing...**

If you have lost your company laptop or suspect it has been misplaced, please report it to the IT Department immediately.

**What to Do:**

1. Notify the IT Department as soon as possible by calling extension 1234 or emailing [itdepartment@company.com](mailto:itdepartment@company.com).
2. Provide your name, employee ID, and a detailed description of the laptop, including its make, model, and serial number.
3. If the laptop has sensitive company data, please indicate this on your report so that we can take extra precautions to secure the information.

**Why Report It?**

Reporting a lost laptop helps us:

* Secure sensitive company data
* Prevent unauthorized access to company systems
* Minimize the risk of data breaches
* Locate the laptop if it is recovered

**Remember:** Your prompt reporting will help us protect your company's assets and maintain the security of our systems.
