**Reminder: Please Lock Your Screens**

Dear Colleagues,

Please remember to lock your computer screen whenever you leave your desk, even for a short time. Press Windows key + L on a PC, or Control + Command + Q on a Mac, before you step away.

Locking your screen protects confidential company, client, and personal information from being viewed or misused by anyone passing by. It takes only a second and helps keep all of us secure.

Thank you for your continued attention to this.
