**Now Playing: Say It Again, a podcast for people learning to speak a new language**

We're excited to share that *Say It Again* is live today, with its first three episodes ready to stream wherever you listen to podcasts.

**What it is**

Each week, we take one everyday situation, like ordering coffee, asking for directions, or making small talk with a neighbor, and walk through the phrases you actually need. No long grammar lectures, and no robotic vocabulary lists. Just real conversations, explained slowly and then repeated at natural speed.

**How each episode works**

- A short dialogue between native speakers
- A line-by-line breakdown of what was said and why
- A practice round where you pause and speak your answers aloud
- A one-minute recap to help the phrases stick

Episodes run about fifteen minutes, which makes them easy to fit into a commute, a walk, or a dishes break.

**Who it's for**

Beginners who want a confident start, and returning learners who feel rusty. If you've ever frozen mid-sentence because the right word vanished, this show is for you.

**Launch lineup**

1. Greetings that sound friendly, not stiff
2. Ordering food without pointing at the menu
3. Asking for help when you're lost

New episodes arrive every Monday morning.

**Join us**

Press play, talk back to your headphones, and don't worry about mistakes. Every fluent speaker started out sounding a little awkward. If you enjoy the show, a rating or a share with a fellow learner helps more than you might think.

Thanks for listening, and welcome to the first lesson.
