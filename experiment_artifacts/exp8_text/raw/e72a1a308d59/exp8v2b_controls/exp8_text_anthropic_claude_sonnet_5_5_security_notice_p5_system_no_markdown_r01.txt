NOTICE: REPORTING A LOST OR STOLEN COMPANY LAPTOP

To: All Employees
From: IT Security Department
Subject: Procedure for Reporting a Lost Company Laptop

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, report it immediately. Quick action helps protect company data and reduces security risks.

Steps to follow:

1. Notify the IT Help Desk right away by phone at extension 4357 or by email at helpdesk@company.com. Do not wait until the next business day.

2. Provide the following details: your name and department, the laptop's asset tag or serial number if known, the date, time, and location it was last seen, and whether it contained sensitive or confidential data.

3. Inform your direct supervisor as soon as possible.

4. If the laptop was stolen, file a report with local police and send a copy of the report number to IT Security.

5. Change your passwords for all work accounts from a secure device. IT may also do this for you.

What IT will do: remotely lock or wipe the device, disable your network access credentials, and begin an incident review if needed.

Please note that failure to report a lost device promptly may result in disciplinary action, especially if data is compromised. Reporting quickly will not result in penalties if the loss was accidental.

Thank you for helping keep our information secure.

IT Security Department
