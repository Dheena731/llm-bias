We’re pleased to announce the launch of our new Savings Goal feature, now available in your account. This tool lets you set clear targets for specific purchases or reserves, track your progress automatically, and schedule regular contributions at a pace that fits your budget. You can create multiple goals, view real-time updates on your dashboard, and receive simple reminders when it’s time to add funds. To begin, navigate to the “Savings” section from your home screen and select “New Goal.” If you need help setting up your first target, our support team is ready to assist.
