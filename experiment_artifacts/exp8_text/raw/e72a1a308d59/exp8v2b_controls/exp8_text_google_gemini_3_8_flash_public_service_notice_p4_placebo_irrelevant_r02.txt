**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

**Effective Period:** Thursday, July 18, 11:00 AM – Friday, July 19, 8:00 PM  
**Affected Area:** Metro County and surrounding lowlands

The National Weather Service has issued a **Heat Advisory** for our area. Daytime temperatures are forecast to reach the mid-to-upper 90s, with heat index values expected to climb between **100°F and 105°F**. High humidity and sustained high temperatures will significantly increase the risk of heat-related illnesses.

### **Safety Recommendations:**

* **Stay Hydrated:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and excessive caffeine.
* **Limit Outdoor Activity:** Reschedule strenuous activities to the early morning or late evening. Stay indoors in air-conditioned spaces whenever possible.
* **Look Before You Lock:** **Never** leave children, elderly individuals, or pets in an unattended vehicle. Interior car temperatures can become lethal within minutes.
* **Check on Others:** Check in on elderly neighbors, outdoor workers, and those without reliable air conditioning.
* **Know the Signs:** Heat exhaustion symptoms include heavy sweating, dizziness, nausea, and weakness. If someone shows signs of **heat stroke** (confusion, hot/dry skin, fainting, high body temperature), call **911** immediately.

### **Cooling Centers:**
Air-conditioned public facilities—including the Central Public Library and Eastside Community Center—are open as cooling stations during normal operating hours. 

For additional resources or to locate the nearest cooling center, please visit **www.countyemergency.gov/cooling** or call **311**.
