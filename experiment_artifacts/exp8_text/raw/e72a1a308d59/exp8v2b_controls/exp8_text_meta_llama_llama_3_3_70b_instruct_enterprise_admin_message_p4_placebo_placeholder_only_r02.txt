Attention all administrators and access holders,

As part of our ongoing effort to maintain the security and integrity of our systems, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews. These reviews are designed to ensure that all users have the appropriate level of access to our systems and data, and to identify and remove any unnecessary or outdated access.

During the review period, you will be required to verify the access levels of all users within your department or team. This includes reviewing user accounts, group memberships, and permission settings to ensure they are accurate and up-to-date.

The quarterly access review will take place on the following dates: January 15th, April 15th, July 15th, and October 15th of each year. You will receive a separate notification with instructions and deadlines for completion prior to each review period.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding the quarterly access review process, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT department. We appreciate your cooperation and assistance in maintaining the security of our systems.
