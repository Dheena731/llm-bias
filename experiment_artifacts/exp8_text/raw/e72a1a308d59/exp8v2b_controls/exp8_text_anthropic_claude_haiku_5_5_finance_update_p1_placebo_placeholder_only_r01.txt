**Introducing Your Monthly Spending Summary**

Starting this month, you'll get a spending summary at the beginning of each month that covers everything from the previous month in one place.

Each summary includes:

- **Total spending** for the month, compared with the month before
- **Top categories**, such as groceries, dining, transportation, and subscriptions, ranked by amount
- **Recurring charges** we detected, so you can review subscriptions you may have forgotten
- **Unusual transactions**, flagged if a charge is much larger than your typical spending in that category

You can find the summary in the Insights tab. We'll also send a notification when it's ready. If you'd rather not receive notifications, you can turn them off in Settings under Notifications.

The summary is based only on transactions from linked accounts, and categories can be corrected at any time. Corrections update the totals and future summaries automatically.

We'd love your feedback. Let us know what's useful and what you'd like to see added by using the Feedback button in the Insights tab.
