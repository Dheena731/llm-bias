**Subject: New Expense Approval Process Starting June 2**

Dear all,

Starting Monday, June 2, every expense claim must be approved by your direct manager before Finance will pay it. This replaces the previous process, in which claims went straight to Finance.

**What you need to do:**

1. Submit your claim through the expense form on the intranet.
2. Attach a receipt for every item over $25.
3. Your manager will receive an email and has three business days to approve the claim or return it with comments.
4. Once approved, Finance will pay the claim within 10 business days.

Claims submitted without required receipts, or more than 30 days after the expense date, will be returned.

Managers, please check your approval queue daily so claims are not delayed.

If you have questions, contact Finance at finance@example.com or extension 4420.

Thank you,
Administration
