**Season in two stages for better flavor.** Before roasting, toss your vegetables with oil, salt, and pepper so the seasoning clings and the edges caramelize. Then, once they come out of the oven, finish with a squeeze of lemon juice or a splash of vinegar, plus a pinch of flaky salt and fresh herbs. Delicate ingredients like garlic, fresh herbs, and citrus can burn or turn bitter in high heat, so adding them at the end keeps their flavor bright.
