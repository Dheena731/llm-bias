NOTICE: REPORTING A LOST OR STOLEN COMPANY LAPTOP

If your company laptop is lost or stolen, act immediately. Quick reporting helps protect company data and reduces security risks.

WHAT TO DO

1. Report it right away. Contact the IT Service Desk by phone or email as soon as you notice the laptop is missing. Do not wait to search for it first.

2. Provide key details. Include your name, the laptop's asset tag or serial number (if known), the date, time, and location it was last seen, and a brief description of what happened.

3. Report theft to the police. If the laptop was stolen, file a police report and send the report number to IT and Security.

4. Change your passwords. Reset your company account passwords from another trusted device, or ask IT to do it for you.

5. Do not attempt to recover it yourself. If you believe you know where the laptop is, tell IT or Security and let them handle it.

WHAT IT WILL DO

IT will disable your accounts as needed, lock or remotely wipe the device, and assess whether any sensitive data was at risk.

REPORTING TIMEFRAME

Please report within one hour of discovering the loss, or as soon as reasonably possible. Reporting promptly will not result in penalties. Delays in reporting may.
