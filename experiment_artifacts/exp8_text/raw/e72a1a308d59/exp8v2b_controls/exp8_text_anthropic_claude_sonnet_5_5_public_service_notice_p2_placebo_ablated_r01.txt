**PUBLIC NOTICE: Road Closure on Maple Street**

**What is closed:** Maple Street between 4th Avenue and 8th Avenue will be closed to through traffic.

**When:** Monday, June 9, 2025, at 7:00 a.m. through Friday, June 13, 2025, at 5:00 p.m.

**Why:** Crews are repairing a broken underground water pipe and rebuilding the road surface above it.

**Detour:** Drivers should follow the posted detour signs along Oak Street and 9th Avenue. Expect about 5 extra minutes of travel time.

**Access for residents and businesses:** If you live or work on this section of Maple Street, you can still drive in from the nearest open end of the street to reach your property. You will not be able to drive through the work area. Emergency vehicles and deliveries will have the same access.

**Pedestrians and cyclists:** Sidewalks will stay open, but please follow the signs and cross only at marked crossings. Cyclists should use the detour or walk their bikes on the sidewalk.

**Buses:** Route 12 will follow the detour. Bus stops on Maple Street between 4th and 8th Avenues will be closed. Please use the stops at 4th Avenue or 8th Avenue instead.

**Water service:** Some nearby homes may have water shut off for a few hours. Affected addresses will receive a notice at their door at least 24 hours in advance.

**Questions:** Call the City Public Works Office at (555) 010-2300, Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. If bad weather delays the work, updated dates will be posted at www.examplecity.gov/roadwork.

*Thank you for your patience while we complete this repair.*
