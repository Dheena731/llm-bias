Hi there! This is a friendly reminder to take a few minutes this week to review your fitness progress.

Look over your workouts, your distance or weight totals, and how you felt during and after your sessions. Note what went well and what you'd like to adjust for next week. Even small improvements are worth recognizing.

Set aside time today or tomorrow so the review doesn't slip through the cracks. Keep it simple, and keep it consistent.

Happy training!
