HEAT ADVISORY

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for our area. Temperatures are expected to reach dangerous levels over the next several days, with heat index values climbing above 100 degrees Fahrenheit.

Who is most at risk: infants, young children, older adults, people with chronic illnesses, outdoor workers, and anyone without reliable air conditioning.

Precautions to take:
- Stay in an air-conditioned place as much as possible. If your home lacks AC, visit a library, shopping center, or community cooling center.
- Drink water regularly, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and sugary drinks.
- Limit outdoor activity to the cooler morning and evening hours. Take frequent breaks if you must be outside.
- Never leave children, pets, or anyone who depends on you in a parked vehicle.
- Check on neighbors, friends, and relatives who live alone or are elderly.
- Wear lightweight, light-colored clothing and a wide-brimmed hat when outdoors.

Warning signs of heat illness: heavy sweating, muscle cramps, dizziness, nausea, headache, or confusion. If you or someone nearby shows these signs, move to a cool place and sip water. For confusion, fainting, vomiting, or a body temperature above 103 degrees Fahrenheit, call 911 immediately.

The advisory remains in effect until further notice. Monitor local news and National Weather Service updates for changes.
