Important notice to all staff: Be cautious of phishing emails that may appear to be from legitimate sources. These emails often attempt to trick you into revealing sensitive information such as passwords or financial details. Never click on suspicious links or provide personal information in response to an email. If you are unsure about the authenticity of an email, please contact the IT department for assistance. Remember to always verify the sender's email address and be wary of emails with spelling mistakes, urgent requests, or unfamiliar greetings. Your vigilance is crucial in protecting our organization's security and data.
