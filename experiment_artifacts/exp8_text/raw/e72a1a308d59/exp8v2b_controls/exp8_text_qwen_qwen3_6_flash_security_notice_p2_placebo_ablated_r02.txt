**Subject: Important Security Reminder: Identifying and Reporting Phishing Emails**

Dear Team,

Please remain vigilant against phishing attempts. Phishing refers to fraudulent emails designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information, such as passwords or financial details, or into installing harmful software.

To protect our data, please follow these guidelines:

*   **Verify the Sender:** Check the email address carefully. Attackers often use addresses that resemble official ones but contain slight spelling errors or unfamiliar domains.
*   **Inspect Links:** Do not click links in unexpected emails. Hover your mouse cursor over a link to view the actual destination address before clicking. If the address looks unfamiliar or incorrect, do not click.
*   **Question Urgency:** Be cautious of messages that demand immediate action or threaten negative consequences. Legitimate requests usually allow time for standard verification.
*   **Report Suspicious Emails:** If you receive a suspicious message, do not reply or click any content. Report it immediately by forwarding it to **[Insert IT Security Email]** or using the **"Report Phishing"** button in your email client.

If you have already clicked a link or entered information, contact IT Support immediately.

Thank you for helping keep our workplace secure.

Sincerely,

IT Security Department
