To make a quick and easy weeknight dinner, consider preparing a one-pot meal such as a stir-fry or skillet dinner. Start by browning your protein of choice, such as chicken or ground beef, in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Then, add your favorite vegetables, such as bell peppers, onions, and broccoli, and cook until they are tender. Finally, add a sauce or seasoning of your choice, such as soy sauce or fajita seasoning, and serve over rice or noodles. This type of meal can be prepared in under 30 minutes and is a great way to get a healthy and delicious dinner on the table on a busy weeknight.
