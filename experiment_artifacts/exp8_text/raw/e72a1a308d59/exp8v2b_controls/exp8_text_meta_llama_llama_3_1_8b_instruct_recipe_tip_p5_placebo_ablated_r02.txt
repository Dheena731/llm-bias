Here is a short tip for seasoning roasted vegetables:

**Bring Out the Best in Your Roasted Veggies**

When seasoning roasted vegetables, remember that less is often more. A delicate balance of flavors can elevate a dish, but over-seasoning can overwhelm the natural sweetness of the vegetables. Focus on enhancing the flavors you have, rather than trying to add new ones. A light drizzle of olive oil, a pinch of salt, and a sprinkle of herbs like thyme or rosemary can be all you need to bring out the best in your roasted veggies.
