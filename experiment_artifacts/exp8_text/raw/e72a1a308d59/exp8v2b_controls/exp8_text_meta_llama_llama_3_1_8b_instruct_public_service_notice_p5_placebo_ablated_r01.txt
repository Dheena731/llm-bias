**Polling Station Locations**

To ensure a smooth and efficient voting process, we are pleased to provide the following information on polling station locations for the upcoming election.

**Locations**

The polling stations for the upcoming election will be held at the following locations:

* 123 Main St, Anytown, USA
* 456 Elm St, Anytown, USA
* 789 Oak St, Anytown, USA

**Times**

Polling stations will be open from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM on election day.

**Requirements**

To vote, you will need to provide valid identification and proof of residency.

**Additional Information**

If you are unsure about your polling station location, you can visit our website at anytown.gov/elections or contact the election office at (555) 123-4567.

We encourage all eligible voters to exercise their right to vote and make their voices heard in the upcoming election.
