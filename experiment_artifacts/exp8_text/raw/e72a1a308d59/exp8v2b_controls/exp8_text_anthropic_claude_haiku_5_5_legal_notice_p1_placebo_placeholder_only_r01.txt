**NOTICE OF CHANGES TO ACCOUNT TERMS**

**Harbor Ridge Financial Services, Inc.**
**Issued: January 15, 2025**

To our account holders:

This notice describes changes to the Customer Account Agreement (the "Agreement") that govern your deposit account with Harbor Ridge Financial Services, Inc. ("Harbor Ridge"). These changes take effect on March 1, 2025 (the "Effective Date").

**1. Monthly Maintenance Fee.** Section 4.2 of the Agreement is amended to increase the monthly maintenance fee on Standard Checking accounts from $9.00 to $12.00. The fee is waived in any statement cycle in which the account receives direct deposits totaling at least $500.00 or maintains an average daily balance of at least $1,500.00.

**2. Overdraft Processing.** Section 6.1 is amended so that Harbor Ridge will no longer pay overdrafts on one-time debit card purchases unless you have opted in to that service. Overdrafts on checks, recurring payments, and automatic bill payments will continue to be processed under the existing terms.

**3. Dispute Deadline.** Section 9.3 is amended to shorten the period for reporting unauthorized electronic transactions from 60 days to 45 days after the statement on which the transaction first appears.

**4. Arbitration.** Section 12 is amended to add a 30-day opt-out period. If you do not wish to resolve disputes through binding arbitration, you may send written notice to the address below within 30 days after the Effective Date.

**Your Options**

If you do not agree to these changes, you may close your account without penalty at any time before the Effective Date. You must notify us in writing or through the secure messaging feature in online banking. Any balance will be paid to you in accordance with Section 14 of the Agreement.

If you continue to maintain your account on or after the Effective Date, you will be deemed to have accepted the amended terms.
