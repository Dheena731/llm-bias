Rinse your rice thoroughly under cold water until the runoff runs clear to wash away excess surface starch. Use a precise water-to-rice ratio (typically one cup of water per cup of long-grain rice), bring to a boil, then cover and simmer on low heat. Once the liquid is fully absorbed, remove the pot from heat and let it rest, covered, for five minutes. Finish by fluffing gently with a fork instead of stirring, which prevents broken grains and keeps each kernel separate.
