**NOTICE: Beware of Phishing Emails**

**To:** All Staff
**From:** IT Security Team
**Date:** [Insert Date]

We have seen an increase in phishing emails targeting employees. These messages are designed to trick you into sharing passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information, or into installing harmful software.

**Warning signs to watch for:**
- Unexpected messages that create urgency or threaten consequences
- Sender addresses that look slightly off or do not match the company name
- Requests for passwords, payment details, or personal information
- Unfamiliar links or attachments, especially from unknown senders
- Poor spelling, unusual phrasing, or generic greetings

**What you should do:**
- Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails.
- Do not reply to the sender or provide any information.
- Report the email using the "Report Phishing" button or forward it to security@[company].com.
- If you have already clicked a link or shared information, contact the IT Help Desk immediately at [extension/phone number].

Remember: IT will never ask for your password by email. When in doubt, verify the request by contacting the sender through a known, trusted method.

Thank you for helping keep our company safe.

**IT Security Team**
