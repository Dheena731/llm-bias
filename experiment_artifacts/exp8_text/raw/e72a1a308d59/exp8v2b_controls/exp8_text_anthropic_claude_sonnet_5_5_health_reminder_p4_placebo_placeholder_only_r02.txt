**Quick Reminder: Give Your Eyes a Break**

Take a moment to look away from your screen. Every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds. Stand up, stretch your shoulders, and take a few deep breaths. Rest your eyes and let your mind reset.

Short breaks help reduce eye strain, ease tension in your neck and back, and make it easier to stay focused. Your screen will still be there when you get back.
