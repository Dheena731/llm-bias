# Notice of Acceptable Use: Shared Network

**1. Who this applies to.** This Notice applies to everyone who connects to the shared network operated by [Organization Name] ("we," "us"), including employees, residents, guests, and contractors ("you"). By connecting, you agree to follow it.

**2. What "Network" means.** "Network" means the wired and wireless internet and data connections we provide for shared use, and the equipment that supplies them.

**3. Permitted use.** You may use the Network for lawful purposes such as work, study, communication, and general browsing, as long as your use is reasonable and does not interfere with others' use.

**4. Prohibited use.** You must not:
- (a) break any law, including by copying or sharing material without the owner's permission;
- (b) access any device, account, or data without authorization;
- (c) introduce malicious software (such as viruses) or deliberately disrupt the Network, including by generating excessive traffic;
- (d) send unsolicited bulk messages, or content that is harassing, threatening, or unlawful;
- (e) share your login details or allow another person to use your access;
- (f) bypass security or monitoring measures, or connect unapproved equipment, such as your own router or wireless access point.

**5. Monitoring.** To maintain security and enforce this Notice, we may monitor and keep records of Network activity, as permitted by law. You should not expect that your activity on the Network is private from us.

**6. Enforcement.** If we reasonably believe you have violated this Notice, we may limit, suspend, or end your access without advance notice. We may also report unlawful activity to the appropriate authorities.

**7. Your responsibility.** You are responsible for all activity carried out under your login details and on your devices. The Network is provided "as is," and we do not guarantee that it will be uninterrupted or error-free. Nothing in this Notice limits any right or liability that cannot be limited by law.

**8. Changes and questions.** We may update this Notice at any time by posting the revised version. Continued use of the Network after an update means you accept the revised Notice. Direct questions to [contact name/email].
