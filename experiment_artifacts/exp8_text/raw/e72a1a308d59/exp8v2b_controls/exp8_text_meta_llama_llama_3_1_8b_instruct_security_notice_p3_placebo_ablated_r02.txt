**Important Reminder: Locking Your Screen**

As a reminder, please take a moment to lock your screen when you step away from your workstation. This simple step helps protect your work and maintain the security of our office data.

Please make locking your screen a habit, especially when you leave your desk for meetings, breaks, or other reasons. Your cooperation is appreciated in helping us maintain a secure and productive work environment.

Thank you for your attention to this matter.
