Calendar tip: stop using it only for meetings. Block out time for your own tasks too!

If something matters (a workout, a deep-focus work session, even lunch), put it on your calendar as an event. Give it a clear name like "Write project report" instead of just "Work." When it's scheduled, you're far more likely to actually do it, and other people can see you're busy before they book over it.

Bonus: set a reminder 10 minutes before so you can wrap up what you're doing and shift gears.
