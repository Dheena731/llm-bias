**Reminder: Book Your Annual Health Checkup**

It's time to schedule your yearly health checkup. Regular exams help catch potential issues early and keep you on track with your health goals.

**What to do:**
1. Call your doctor's office or use their online portal to request an appointment.
2. Choose a date and time that fits your schedule.
3. Check your last visit date and any tests your doctor recommended, such as blood work or screenings, so you can ask about them.
4. Write down any questions or symptoms you want to discuss.

**Tip:** Appointments can fill up quickly, so book soon. If you have health insurance, check what it covers before your visit.

Taking a few minutes to book today is a simple way to look after your well-being.
