**Introducing Savings Goals**

You can now set a savings goal in the app and track your progress toward it.

**How it works**
1. Open the **Savings** tab and tap **New Goal**.
2. Name your goal (for example, "Vacation") and enter the amount you want to save.
3. Optionally, choose a target date. If you do, we'll show how much you'd need to save each month to reach your goal on time.
4. Add money to your goal whenever you like. A progress bar shows how close you are.

**Good to know**
- You can create more than one goal.
- You can edit or delete a goal at any time.

Give it a try today, and let us know what you think through the **Feedback** option in Settings.
