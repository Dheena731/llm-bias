# Launch Note: *Say It Again*

We're excited to announce that **Say It Again**, a podcast for people learning a new language, is now live.

Most language resources teach you how a language *should* sound. We want to help you hear how it's actually spoken, and feel okay about getting it wrong on the way there.

**What to expect**
- **Short episodes (10-15 minutes)** that fit into a commute, a walk, or a coffee break
- **Real conversations** with native speakers, slowed down and unpacked
- **Practical phrases** you can use the same day
- **Honest learner stories** about the plateaus, the awkward moments, and the small wins

**Episodes 1-3 are available now** wherever you get your podcasts. New episodes will arrive every Tuesday.

We'd love to hear from you. Tell us which language you're learning, what's tripping you up, and what you'd like us to cover next. Your questions will shape the show.

Press play, say it out loud, and then say it again.
