**Quarterly Access Reviews: Important Reminder**

To ensure the security and integrity of our systems, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews. This process involves a thorough evaluation of user access rights and permissions to ensure that they are still necessary and aligned with current job roles and responsibilities.

**Why it matters:**

*   Prevents unauthorized access to sensitive data
*   Reduces the risk of data breaches and security incidents
*   Ensures that employees only have access to the systems and data necessary for their job functions

**What to expect:**

*   You may receive emails or requests from your supervisor or the IT department to review your access rights
*   You may be asked to update your job details or provide justification for existing access rights
*   The review process typically takes place at the end of each quarter, and you will be notified in advance of the schedule

**Your cooperation is essential:**

*   Please ensure that your job details and access rights are up-to-date and accurate
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or the IT department

Thank you for your attention to this matter and for your continued support in maintaining the security and integrity of our systems.
