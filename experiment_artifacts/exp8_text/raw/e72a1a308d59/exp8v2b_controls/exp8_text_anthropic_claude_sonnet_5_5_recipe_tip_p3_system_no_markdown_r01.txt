Rinse your rice before cooking. Place the rice in a bowl or fine mesh strainer and run cold water over it, swishing it around with your hand, until the water goes from cloudy to mostly clear. This usually takes three to five rinses. The cloudy water is surface starch, which is what makes grains clump together and turn gummy. After rinsing, drain the rice well and use the right amount of water, generally about 1.5 cups of water per cup of long grain rice. Once it finishes cooking, let it rest covered for 10 minutes off the heat, then fluff it gently with a fork to release steam and keep the grains separate.
