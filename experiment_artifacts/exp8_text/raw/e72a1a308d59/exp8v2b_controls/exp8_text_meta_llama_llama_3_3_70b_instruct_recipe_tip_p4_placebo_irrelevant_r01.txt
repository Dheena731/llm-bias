Here's a short tip for planning lunches for a busy week:

**Plan Your Lunches in Advance to Save Time and Stress**

To make your busy week more manageable, try planning your lunches in advance. Set aside 10-15 minutes on the weekend or one evening to think about your lunch options for the next few days. Consider your schedule, dietary preferences, and ingredient availability. You can:

* Prep ingredients like vegetables, proteins, and whole grains on the weekend
* Cook a big batch of something like soup, salad, or rice bowls that can be reheated throughout the week
* Pack non-perishable items like nuts, fruits, and energy bars for easy grab-and-go options
* Make a list of restaurants or cafes near your work or school for days when you need a break from cooking

Having a plan in place will help you save time, reduce food waste, and ensure you're fueling your body with healthy and satisfying meals, even on the busiest of days!
