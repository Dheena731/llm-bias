Here's a friendly reminder to keep your water intake up today:

Drinking enough water helps your body stay energized, focused, and comfortable. Try to sip water steadily throughout the day rather than waiting until you feel thirsty, since thirst is an early sign that your body needs fluids. Keep a bottle nearby, have a glass with each meal, and drink a little more if you're exercising, spending time in heat, or feeling unwell.

Take a moment now to have a glass of water. Your body will thank you.
