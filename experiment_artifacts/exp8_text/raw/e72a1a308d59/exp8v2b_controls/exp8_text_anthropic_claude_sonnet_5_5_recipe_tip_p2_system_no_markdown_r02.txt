Treat tender herbs like cilantro, parsley, and mint the way you would treat fresh flowers. Trim the stem ends, place the bunch upright in a jar with an inch or two of water, loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag, and keep it in the refrigerator. Change the water every couple of days and your herbs should stay fresh for up to two weeks. Basil is the exception: keep it in a jar of water on the counter, away from direct sunlight, since cold temperatures turn its leaves black. For sturdier herbs like rosemary and thyme, wrap them in a slightly damp paper towel and store them in a zip-top bag or container in the fridge.
