**Election Day Polling Station Locations**

The following polling stations will be open on election day for voters to cast their ballots. Please note that polling stations are subject to change, and voters should check the official election website for the most up-to-date information.

**Polling Station Locations:**

* **Ward 1:**
	+ Station 1: Community Center, 123 Main St.
	+ Station 2: Public Library, 456 Elm St.
* **Ward 2:**
	+ Station 1: School, 789 Oak St.
	+ Station 2: Fire Station, 901 Maple St.
* **Ward 3:**
	+ Station 1: Town Hall, 234 Pine St.
	+ Station 2: Senior Center, 567 Cedar St.

**Important Voting Information:**

* Polling stations will be open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM on election day.
* Voters must present valid identification to cast their ballots.
* If you are unsure of your polling station location, please contact the election office at (555) 123-4567 for assistance.
