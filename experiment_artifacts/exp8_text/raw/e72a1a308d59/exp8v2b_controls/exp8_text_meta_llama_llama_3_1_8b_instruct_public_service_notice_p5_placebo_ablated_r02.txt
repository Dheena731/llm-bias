**Polling Station Locations**

To ensure a smooth and efficient voting process, please note the locations of the designated polling stations for the upcoming election.

Polling stations will be located at the following addresses:

* School District 1: 123 Main St.
* Community Center: 456 Elm St.
* Library: 789 Oak St.
* Fire Station: 901 Maple St.

These locations are easily accessible by public transportation and are wheelchair-friendly. Voting hours will be from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM on election day.

To verify your polling station location or for any other election-related inquiries, please visit the official election website or contact the local election office.

Thank you for exercising your right to vote.
