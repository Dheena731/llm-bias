Feedback on Draft Literature Review

Thank you for sharing this draft. It shows solid engagement with the topic and a clear effort to cover relevant scholarship. Below are my main observations and suggestions for revision.

Strengths
The review has a clear scope and draws on a good range of sources. Your summaries of individual studies are accurate and easy to follow, and the writing is generally clear and appropriately formal.

Areas for development

1. Synthesis over summary. At present, many paragraphs describe one study after another. Group the literature by theme, debate, or methodological approach, and show how the studies relate: where they agree, where they conflict, and why.

2. Critical evaluation. Assess the strength of the evidence, not just its content. Comment on sample sizes, methods, theoretical assumptions, and limitations, and avoid presenting all sources as equally authoritative.

3. Argument and structure. The review would benefit from a clearer guiding argument. Consider opening each section with a topic sentence that makes a claim, and close with a brief summary of what the section shows. A short introductory roadmap would also help the reader.

4. Identifying the gap. The link between the existing literature and your own research question is not yet explicit. State clearly what remains unresolved or underexplored, and explain how your project responds to it.

5. Sources and currency. Check that you include recent work as well as foundational texts. Where you rely heavily on a few authors, consider whether other perspectives are missing.

6. Citation and style. Check that citations are consistent and complete, and reduce direct quotation in favor of paraphrase, keeping quotations for key wording.

Next steps
Prepare a revised outline organized by theme, then redraft with an emphasis on comparison and critique. I am happy to look at the outline before you rewrite in full.

Overall, this is a promising foundation. With stronger synthesis and a sharper rationale, it will become a persuasive justification for your research.
