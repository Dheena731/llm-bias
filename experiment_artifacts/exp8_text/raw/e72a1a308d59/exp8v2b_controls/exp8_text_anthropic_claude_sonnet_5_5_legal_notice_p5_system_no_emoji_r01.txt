**NOTICE OF ACCEPTABLE USE: SHARED NETWORK**

**1. Purpose and Scope**
This Notice governs the use of the shared network ("Network") provided by the Organization. It applies to all employees, contractors, guests, and other persons ("Users") who access the Network by any device, whether owned by the Organization or by the User.

**2. Authorization**
Access to the Network is a privilege, not a right. Users may access only those systems, files, and resources for which they have been given express authorization. Credentials are personal and must not be shared, transferred, or disclosed to any other person.

**3. Permitted Use**
The Network is to be used primarily for legitimate business, educational, or other purposes approved by the Organization. Limited incidental personal use is permitted, provided that it does not interfere with Network performance, security, or the User's duties.

**4. Prohibited Conduct**
Users shall not:
- (a) Access, copy, alter, or delete data without authorization;
- (b) Introduce malware, or knowingly circumvent security controls, firewalls, or monitoring tools;
- (c) Transmit or store unlawful, harassing, discriminatory, defamatory, or obscene material;
- (d) Infringe copyrights, licenses, or other intellectual property rights, including through unauthorized file sharing;
- (e) Consume bandwidth or resources in a manner that unreasonably degrades service for others;
- (f) Connect unapproved devices, routers, or access points to the Network; or
- (g) Use the Network to engage in any activity that violates applicable law.

**5. Monitoring and Privacy**
The Organization reserves the right to monitor, log, and review Network activity, to the extent permitted by law, to ensure security, compliance, and proper operation. Users should have no expectation of privacy in their use of the Network.

**6. Reporting**
Users must promptly report any suspected security incident, unauthorized access, or violation of this Notice to the IT Department.

**7. Enforcement**
Violation of this Notice may result in suspension or termination of Network access, disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contract, and, where appropriate, referral to law enforcement. The Organization may seek recovery of any resulting costs or damages.

**8. Disclaimer**
The Network is provided "as is." The Organization does not warrant uninterrupted or error-free service and is not liable for loss of data or any indirect or consequential damages arising from use of the Network, except as required by law.

**9. Amendment**
The Organization may modify this Notice at any time. Continued use of the Network constitutes acceptance of the current version.

**10. Acknowledgment**
By accessing the Network, the User acknowledges that he or she has read, understood, and agreed to comply with this Notice.
