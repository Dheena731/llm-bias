📅 **Calendar tip: Block time for tasks, not just meetings.**

Most of us only put meetings and appointments in our calendar. But if you also schedule time for your to-dos, like "Write report, 2–3 PM" or "Reply to emails, 4 PM," you'll see how much time you really have and be less likely to overbook yourself.

**How to try it:**
1. Pick your top 1–3 tasks for tomorrow.
2. Create an event for each one, with a start and end time.
3. Treat these events like real appointments.

Bonus: If a task takes longer than planned, drag the event to a new slot instead of letting it disappear.
