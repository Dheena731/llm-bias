Here is a short notice about polling station locations:

Polling Station Locations: The following locations have been designated as polling stations for the upcoming election: City Hall, 123 Main St; Community Center, 456 Elm St; Fire Station, 789 Oak St; and Public Library, 901 Maple St. Voters must cast their ballots at one of these designated locations. For specific voting hours and any changes to these locations, please visit our website or contact the election office.
