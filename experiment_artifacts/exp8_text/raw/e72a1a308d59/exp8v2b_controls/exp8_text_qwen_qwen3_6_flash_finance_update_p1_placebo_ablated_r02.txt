**Subject: Introducing the New Monthly Spending Summary**

Starting this month, we are rolling out a new monthly spending summary to give you a clearer, more consistent view of our expenses. Each report will list spending by category, compare actual costs against our planned budget, and highlight any notable increases or decreases from the previous month. You will receive the summary via email on the first business day of each month. This replaces our earlier reporting process to make expense tracking faster, more accurate, and easier to act on. Please review the document when it arrives and reply with any questions or suggestions. We will use your feedback to ensure future summaries continue to meet your needs.
