The end-of-year tax statement is a document that summarizes your taxable income and deductions for the previous year. It typically includes details such as your total earnings, tax withheld, and any eligible deductions or credits. This statement is usually provided by your employer or financial institution and is used to help you complete your tax return. It's an important document to review and keep for your records, as it will be used to verify the information you report on your tax return.
