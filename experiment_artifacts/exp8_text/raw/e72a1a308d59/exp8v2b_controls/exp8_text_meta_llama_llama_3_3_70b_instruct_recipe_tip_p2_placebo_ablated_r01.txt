To keep fresh herbs fresh for a longer period, it's essential to store them properly. The best way to store fresh herbs is to trim the stems, place them in a glass of water, and cover the leaves with a plastic bag. This will help to keep the herbs moist and prevent them from drying out. You can then store them in the refrigerator, where they will remain fresh for several days. This method works well for herbs like basil, parsley, and cilantro, and is a simple way to keep them fresh and ready to use in your cooking.
