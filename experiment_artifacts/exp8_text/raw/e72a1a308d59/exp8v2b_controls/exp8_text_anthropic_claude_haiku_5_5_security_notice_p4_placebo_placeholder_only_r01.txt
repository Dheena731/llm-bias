**Notice: Turn On Two-Factor Authentication to Protect Your Account**

To better protect your account and personal information, we strongly recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA). This extra layer of security requires a second verification step, such as a one-time code from an authenticator app or a text message, in addition to your password when you sign in.

**How to enable it:**

1. Go to **Account Settings** and select **Security**.
2. Choose **Two-Factor Authentication** and click **Turn On**.
3. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or Authy, then enter the six-digit code it displays.
4. Save the backup codes provided and store them somewhere safe. You will need them if you lose access to your phone.

Once enabled, you will be asked for a verification code each time you sign in from a new device. Turning on 2FA takes less than two minutes and significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access, even if your password is compromised.

If you have questions or need help setting up two-factor authentication, please contact our support team through the Help Center.
