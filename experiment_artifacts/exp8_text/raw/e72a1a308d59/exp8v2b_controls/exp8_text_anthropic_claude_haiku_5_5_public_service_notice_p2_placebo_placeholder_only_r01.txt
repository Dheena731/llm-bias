**PUBLIC NOTICE: Maple Street Closure Between 5th and 9th Avenues**

Maple Street will be closed to all vehicle traffic between 5th and 9th Avenues from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily, Monday, March 10, through Friday, March 21.

Crews will be replacing a water main and repaving the roadway. Detours will be posted at both closure points. Northbound traffic should use Cedar Avenue, and southbound traffic should use Elm Street.

Sidewalks will remain open, and access to all homes and businesses on the closed segment will be maintained from the cross streets. Local bus Route 14 will follow the Cedar Avenue detour, with stops at 6th and 8th Avenues temporarily relocated to the nearest corners.

Emergency vehicles will have access at all times. For questions, call the Public Works Department at 555-0142 weekdays between 8:00 a.m. and 4:30 p.m.

Thank you for your patience while we complete this work.

Issued by the City Public Works Department, March 3
