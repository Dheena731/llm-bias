Join us this Saturday, June 14, for the Riverside Community Day at Maple Park, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.

Enjoy live music, local food vendors, a kids' craft corner, and a neighborhood plant swap. Bring a blanket, invite your neighbors, and come ready to meet new friends. Admission is free, and all are welcome.

Volunteers are still needed for setup and cleanup. If you can spare an hour or two, send us a message.

See you at the park!
