**NOTICE: Changes to Recycling Collection**

**Effective Monday, March 3**

The Public Works Department is updating the curbside recycling schedule to improve reliability and reduce truck traffic on residential streets.

**What's changing**

- **Collection moves to every other week.** Recycling will be picked up on alternating Wednesdays instead of weekly.
- **Collection day is now Wednesday** for all households. Households that previously had Monday or Tuesday pickup are included.
- **Carts must be at the curb by 6:30 a.m.** Please place them at least three feet from mailboxes, vehicles, and trash bins.

**What's now accepted**

- Cartons, such as milk and broth cartons
- Empty aerosol cans
- Clean aluminum foil and trays

**What's no longer accepted**

- Plastic bags and film. Please take these to the grocery store drop-off bins.
- Shredded paper. Place it in a paper bag in the trash, or use a community shred event.

**Helpful reminders**

- Rinse containers, but there's no need to scrub them.
- Keep recyclables loose in the cart, not bagged.
- Carts with food waste or non-recyclable items may be left unemptied with a tag explaining why.

Printed calendars will be mailed to all households by the end of February. A full calendar and a searchable "what goes where" guide are available on the city website, or you can call Public Works at 555-0142 on weekdays from 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.

Thank you for helping keep our community clean and our recycling stream strong.
