Here's a quick calendar tip that's saved me hours:

Set a recurring "weekly planning" block every Sunday evening. Spend 15 minutes reviewing the week ahead, moving tasks around, and blocking time for your top priorities. You'll walk into Monday with a clear plan instead of a pile of surprises.

Bonus: turn on notifications 10 minutes before events so you're never caught rushing from one meeting to the next.
