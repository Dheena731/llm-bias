**Update: Delayed Payment Processing**

We're currently experiencing delays in processing some payments. Transactions submitted recently may take longer than usual to complete, and some may appear as pending in your account.

**What happened:** A technical issue with one of our payment processing systems is slowing down transaction handling. Our engineering team has identified the cause and is actively working on a fix.

**What this means for you:**
- Payments are not lost. All submitted transactions are safely queued and will be processed once the issue is resolved.
- You do not need to resubmit a payment. Doing so could result in a duplicate charge.
- Pending payments should clear automatically, and you'll receive a confirmation when they do.

**Next steps:** We'll share another update as soon as processing returns to normal. If a payment still hasn't gone through after you receive that update, please contact our support team and we'll look into it right away.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience while we resolve this.
