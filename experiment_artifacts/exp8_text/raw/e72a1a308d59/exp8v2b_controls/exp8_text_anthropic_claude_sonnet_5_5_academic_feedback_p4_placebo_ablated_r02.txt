Right now, [your main finding is hard to find / appears only in the conclusion / is buried in the text]. Please add a one-sentence takeaway in large type at the top or center of the poster.

**What works well:** [e.g., the topic is timely, the main figure is strong, the color scheme is consistent].

**Suggested changes, in order of priority:**

1. **State the takeaway clearly.** Write it as a plain sentence, such as "Method X reduced errors by 30% compared with Method Y."
2. **Cut the text.** Aim for roughly 300–400 words in total. Replace paragraphs with short bullet points, and save the finer details for conversation.
3. **Make the key figure the largest element.** Label the axes with units, use text readable from a distance, and add a one-line caption saying what the figure shows.
4. **Make the reading order obvious.** Use columns that run left to right, with numbered section headings or arrows, and leave some empty space between sections.
5. **Check readability.** Body text should be at least 24 point and headings at least 36 point, with strong contrast between text and background.
6. **Spell out abbreviations** the first time you use them, since visitors may come from other fields.
7. **Match conclusions to evidence.** Make sure each claim is supported by a result shown on the poster, and include one sentence on a limitation.
8. **Add contact details.** A QR code linking to the paper or your email works well.
