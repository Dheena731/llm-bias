**Subject: Important Reminder to Lock Your Screen**

All staff members are reminded to lock their computer screens whenever they step away from their desks, even briefly.

Locking your workstation prevents unauthorized access to sensitive information and helps maintain our security standards. 

Quick reference:
*   **Windows:** Press `Windows Key` + `L`.
*   **Mac:** Press `Command` + `Control` + `Q`.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our workplace secure.

Management
