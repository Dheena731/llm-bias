**Friendly Reminder: Give Your Eyes a Break!** 

If you’ve been staring at a screen for a while, here is your gentle cue to pause. 

Try the **20-20-20 rule**: Look at something at least 20 feet away for 20 seconds. 

While you're at it:
* Blink a few times to rehydrate your eyes.
* Roll your shoulders back and unclench your jaw.
* Take a deep breath or grab a quick glass of water.

A minute or two away will help prevent eye strain, ease tension, and keep you focused. Your eyes and mind will thank you!
