PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVVISORY IN EFFECT

A Heat Advisory has been issued for [City/Region] effective from [Start Date/Time] through [End Date/Time]. Elevated temperatures are expected to persist, creating conditions that may pose health risks, particularly for young children, the elderly, and individuals with pre-existing medical conditions.

Residents are urged to take the following precautions:

*   Limit outdoor exertion, especially between 10:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m.
*   Drink plenty of water frequently, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid beverages high in sugar, caffeine, or alcohol.
*   Stay in air-conditioned spaces as much as possible. If your home lacks cooling, visit designated cooling centers listed at [Website URL].
*   Never leave children or pets inside parked vehicles.
*   Check on neighbors, friends, and relatives who live alone or may be vulnerable.
*   Wear lightweight, light-colored clothing and sunscreen if you must be outdoors.

Be aware of the signs of heat-related illnesses, including heavy sweating, weakness, nausea, headache, and confusion. If you or someone else experiences these symptoms, move to a cool place and seek medical attention immediately.

For life-threatening emergencies, call 911. For non-emergency assistance, contact [Local Emergency Number] or visit [Official Website] for updates and resources.
