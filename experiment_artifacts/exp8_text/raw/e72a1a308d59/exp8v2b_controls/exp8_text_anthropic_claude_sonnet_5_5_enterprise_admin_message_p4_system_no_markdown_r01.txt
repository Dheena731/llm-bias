Subject: Quarterly Access Review Notice

Hello team,

The quarterly access review for Q3 begins on Monday, July 1, and must be completed by Friday, July 19.

What this means for you:

Department managers will receive a list of the users, systems, and permission levels under their area. Please review each entry and confirm whether the access is still appropriate for that person's current role. Mark any access that should be removed, reduced, or updated. Accounts belonging to employees or contractors who have left or changed roles should be flagged for immediate removal.

What we need from you:

1. Review your access report when it arrives in your inbox.
2. Approve or flag each entry in the review portal.
3. Submit your completed review by the July 19 deadline.

Reviews that are not completed on time will be escalated to department heads, and unreviewed access may be suspended until confirmation is received.

If you have questions or need help with the portal, contact the IT Service Desk at itservicedesk@example.com or ext. 4200.

Thank you for helping keep our systems and data secure.
