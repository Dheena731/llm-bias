**Important Security Reminder**

As we continue to enhance our organization's cybersecurity measures, we want to remind everyone to be vigilant when receiving emails that may appear to be from a legitimate source, but could potentially be phishing attempts.

Phishing emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information or clicking on malicious links. They often look like they are from a trusted source, such as a colleague, manager, or IT department, but can actually be from a cyber attacker trying to gain access to our systems.

Here are some red flags to watch out for:

* Requests for sensitive information, such as passwords, credit card numbers, or social security numbers
* Urgent or threatening messages that demand immediate action
* Links or attachments from unknown sources
* Poor grammar or spelling in the email

If you receive an email that raises any of these suspicions, do not hesitate to contact our IT department directly to verify the authenticity of the email. They can help determine if the message is legitimate or if it's a phishing attempt.

Let's all do our part to keep our organization's data and systems secure. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or the IT department.

**Thank you for your attention to this important security matter.**
