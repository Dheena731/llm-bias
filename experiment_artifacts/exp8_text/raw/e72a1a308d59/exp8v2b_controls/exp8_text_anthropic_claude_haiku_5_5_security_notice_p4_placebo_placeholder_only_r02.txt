**Action Recommended: Turn On Two-Factor Authentication**

To better protect your account, we strongly encourage you to enable two-factor authentication (2FA). With 2FA, signing in requires both your password and a second verification step, such as a one-time code sent to your phone or generated by an authenticator app. Even if someone learns your password, they will not be able to access your account without that second step.

**How to turn it on:**

1. Sign in to your account and open the Security section of your account settings.
2. Select "Two-factor authentication" and choose your preferred method: an authenticator app or text message codes.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions to confirm your setup.
4. Save the backup recovery codes that appear, and store them somewhere safe.

Setup takes only a few minutes. If you have questions or run into trouble, visit the Help Center or contact our support team through the Help section of the website or app.

Thank you for helping us keep your account secure.
