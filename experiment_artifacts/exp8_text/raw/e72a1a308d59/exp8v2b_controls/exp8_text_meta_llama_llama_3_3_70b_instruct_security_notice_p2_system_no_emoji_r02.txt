To: All Staff

We have recently seen an increase in phishing emails being sent to employees. These emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information such as your login credentials or financial information.

Please be cautious when receiving emails that:

* Ask you to verify your account information or login credentials
* Contain suspicious links or attachments
* Create a sense of urgency or threaten to suspend your account
* Are not addressed to you by name or contain generic greetings

If you receive an email that you suspect is a phishing attempt, please do not respond or click on any links. Instead, report the email to the IT department immediately.

Remember, the company will never ask you to reveal sensitive information via email. If you are unsure about the authenticity of an email, it is always best to err on the side of caution and verify with the sender or the IT department.

Thank you for your vigilance in helping to protect our company's security.
