Here is a short legal-style notice about cookie usage on a website:

**COOKIE USAGE NOTICE**

This website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience and provide you with a more personalized experience. Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device by your web browser.

**WHAT ARE COOKIES USED FOR?**

Cookies are used for a variety of purposes, including:

* Analyzing website traffic and performance
* Improving website content and functionality
* Delivering targeted advertisements and marketing communications
* Enhancing security and protecting against cyber threats

**MANAGING COOKIE SETTINGS**

You have the right to control the use of cookies on our website. Most web browsers allow you to accept, reject, or delete cookies. To manage your cookie settings, follow these steps:

* For Google Chrome: Click the three vertical dots in the top right corner of the browser window, select "Settings," and click "Advanced" to manage your cookies.
* For Mozilla Firefox: Click the three horizontal lines in the top right corner of the browser window, select "Options," and click "Privacy" to manage your cookies.
* For Safari: Click the "Safari" menu in the top left corner of the browser window, select "Preferences," and click "Privacy" to manage your cookies.

**COOKIE TYPES**

Our website uses two types of cookies: necessary cookies and non-necessary cookies.

* Necessary cookies are essential for the proper functioning of our website, such as cookies that store your session ID.
* Non-necessary cookies are used for analytical, advertising, and marketing purposes, such as cookies that track your browsing behavior.

**OPT-OUT OPTIONS**

If you do not want to receive cookies that are used for non-necessary purposes, you can opt-out by:

* Deleting cookies stored on your device
* Blocking cookies from being stored on your device in the future
* Enabling "Do Not Track" (DNT) in your browser settings

**CHANGES TO COOKIE USAGE NOTICE**

We may update this cookie usage notice from time to time to reflect changes in our cookie practices or new laws and regulations. We recommend that you review this notice regularly to stay informed.

This website is compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We are committed to protecting your online rights and freedoms.
