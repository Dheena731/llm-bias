**Latency** is the time delay between a user taking an action—such as clicking a button or loading a page—and the web application responding to that action. 

Latency matters because it directly affects user behavior, satisfaction, and business outcomes:

* **User Experience:** People expect digital interactions to feel immediate. Delays of even a fraction of a second break a user’s train of thought and make an application feel sluggish, broken, or frustrating to use.
* **Engagement and Retention:** When an application is slow, users are much more likely to abandon their current task and leave the site entirely. Lower latency keeps users engaged and willing to return.
* **Business Performance:** For commercial websites, responsiveness directly impacts revenue. Faster applications consistently see higher conversion rates—such as completed purchases or sign-ups—while high latency leads to lost sales.
* **Search Visibility:** Major search engines include page speed as a ranking factor. Slower websites are often penalized with lower search rankings, reducing the number of people who discover the site.

In short, minimizing latency is critical because faster applications build trust, keep users engaged, and drive better results.
