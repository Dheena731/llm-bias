**PUBLIC NOTICE: Road Closure**

**Location:** Maple Street, between 4th Avenue and 7th Avenue

**Dates:** Monday, June 16 through Friday, June 20

**Hours:** 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily

**Reason:** Crews will be repairing water main connections and resurfacing the roadway.

**Detours:** Traffic will be redirected to Oak Street and Pine Avenue. Signs will be posted at all affected intersections.

**Access:** Local residents and businesses will be permitted access to their properties at all times. Emergency vehicles will be given priority passage through the work zone.

**Parking:** Vehicles parked on Maple Street during the closure may be towed. Please move your vehicle before June 16.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience while we complete these improvements. For questions, contact the Department of Public Works at (555) 123-4567.
