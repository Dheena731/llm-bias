Subject: Security Reminder: Please Lock Your Screens

Team,

This is a friendly reminder to always lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if it is just for a few moments. 

Leaving workstations unlocked exposes sensitive company and client data to unauthorized access. 

Quick shortcuts to lock your screen:
- Windows: Press the Windows Key + L
- Mac: Press Control + Command + Q

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our workplace secure.

IT & Security Team
