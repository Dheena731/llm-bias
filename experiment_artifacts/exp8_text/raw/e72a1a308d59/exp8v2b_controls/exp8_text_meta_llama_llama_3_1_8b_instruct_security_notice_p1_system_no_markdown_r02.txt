**Important: Password Reuse and Security**

To protect the security and integrity of our company's systems and data, we remind all employees to maintain unique and complex passwords for all accounts, including email, network login, and any other sensitive systems.

Reusing passwords across multiple accounts increases the risk of unauthorized access and potential security breaches. To ensure the highest level of security, please avoid using the same password for multiple accounts.

If you have already reused passwords, please update them immediately to new, unique passwords. This will help safeguard our company's sensitive information and prevent potential security incidents.

If you have any questions or concerns about password management or security procedures, please contact the IT department for assistance.

**Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining a secure work environment.**
