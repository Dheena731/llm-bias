**Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations**

Election Day is Tuesday, November 5. Polls will be open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.

Please note that you must cast your ballot at your assigned neighborhood polling station. Because several voting locations have changed since the last election, please confirm your designated site before you go to vote.

You can find your assigned polling station by:
* Visiting **[Website Address]** and entering your home address.
* Checking the back of your official sample ballot mailed to your home.
* Calling the local election office at **[Phone Number]**.

If you require physical assistance or language support at your station, please contact our office in advance so we can assist you.
