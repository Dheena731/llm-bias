**Subject: How to Report a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If you lose your company laptop or think it has been stolen, report it to the IT Service Desk immediately, even if you are not sure where it is. Reporting quickly allows IT to lock the device and protect company and customer information.

**What to do:**

1. **Contact the IT Service Desk as soon as you realize the laptop is missing.** Call [phone number] or email [email address]. Do not wait to see whether it turns up.
2. **Provide these details:**
   - Your name and department
   - The asset tag number, which is on the bottom of the laptop
   - When and where you last had it
   - Whether you think it was stolen or simply misplaced
3. **If the laptop was stolen, also report it to the local police.** Send IT the police report number.
4. **Do not try to access company systems from another device to check on the laptop.** IT will handle this.

Reporting promptly is required under company policy, and it is the best way to limit the risk to company data.

For questions, contact the IT Service Desk at [phone number].
