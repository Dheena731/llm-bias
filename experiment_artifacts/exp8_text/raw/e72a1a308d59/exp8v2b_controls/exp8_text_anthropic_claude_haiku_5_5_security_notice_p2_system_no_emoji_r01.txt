**IMPORTANT: Watch Out for Phishing Emails**

Phishing emails are fraudulent messages designed to trick you into revealing passwords, financial information, or other sensitive data, or into clicking malicious links or opening infected attachments.

**Warning signs to look for:**
- Unexpected requests for passwords, account details, or payment
- Urgent or threatening language, such as "Your account will be closed today"
- Sender addresses that look almost right but are slightly off
- Generic greetings like "Dear User" instead of your name
- Links that don't match the destination when you hover over them
- Unexpected attachments, especially from unknown senders

**What to do:**
- Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails
- Verify any unusual request by contacting the sender through a known phone number or channel
- Report suspicious emails to the IT Help Desk immediately, using the report button if available
- If you clicked a link or entered credentials, report it right away so we can secure your account

When in doubt, ask IT first. A quick check protects you and the company.
