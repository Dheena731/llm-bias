Rinse the rice before cooking. Place it in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer and run cold water over it, swishing with your hand, until the water runs mostly clear (usually 3 to 5 rinses). This washes away the excess surface starch that makes grains clump together.

For extra fluffiness, also try these:

- **Use the right ratio:** For long-grain white rice, aim for about 1 part rice to 1.5 parts water. Too much water leads to mushy, sticky results.
- **Don't stir while cooking:** Stirring releases more starch.
- **Let it rest:** After cooking, keep the lid on and let the rice sit off the heat for 10 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
