**Quarterly Access Reviews: A Reminder to All Users**

As we approach the end of each quarter, it is essential that all users review and update their access to our systems and resources. This is a mandatory process designed to ensure that only authorized personnel have access to sensitive information and systems.

A quarterly access review is a thorough examination of your access permissions to identify and rectify any inconsistencies or unnecessary access. This process is required by our organization's security policies to maintain the integrity and confidentiality of our data.

During your quarterly review, you will be asked to:

*   Verify your access rights and ensure they are still valid
*   Identify and remove any unnecessary or redundant access
*   Request any changes or updates to your access permissions

Failure to participate in this review process may result in the removal of your access, which could impact your ability to perform your duties.

If you have any questions or concerns about the quarterly access review process, please reach out to your supervisor or the IT department for assistance.
