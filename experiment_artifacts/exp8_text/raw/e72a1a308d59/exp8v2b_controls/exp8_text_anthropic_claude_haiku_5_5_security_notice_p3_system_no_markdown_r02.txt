Subject: Reminder: Please Lock Your Screens

Dear Colleagues,

This is a friendly reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even for a short time. Locking your screen takes only a second and helps protect confidential company information and client data from unauthorized access.

On Windows, press Windows key + L. On a Mac, press Control + Command + Q. If you need help setting this up, please contact the IT Help Desk.

Thank you for helping us keep our information secure.
