**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

**Issued by:** City Emergency Management Agency  
**Effective Dates:** Friday, July 12, at 11:00 AM – Sunday, July 14, at 8:00 PM  
**Affected Area:** Metro Area and Surrounding Counties  

The National Weather Service has issued a Heat Advisory for our area. Daytime temperatures are forecast to reach the mid-to-upper 90s, with heat index values expected to peak between **103°F and 108°F**. 

High temperatures and humidity can significantly increase the risk of heat-related illnesses, especially for older adults, young children, outdoor workers, and individuals with chronic medical conditions.

### **Safety Guidelines:**
* **Stay Hydrated:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and excessive caffeine.
* **Stay Indoors:** Limit outdoor exposure during peak heat hours (11:00 AM to 6:00 PM). Stay in air-conditioned buildings whenever possible.
* **Never Leave Anyone in a Parked Car:** Do not leave children or pets unattended in vehicles—interior temperatures can reach lethal levels within minutes.
* **Check on Others:** Check in regularly on elderly relatives, neighbors, and those without reliable air conditioning.
* **Protect Pets:** Provide plenty of fresh water and shade for outdoor animals, and limit walks on hot pavement.

### **Cooling Centers:**
Air-conditioned public facilities, including public libraries and community recreation centers, will operate with extended hours throughout the weekend for residents seeking relief from the heat. 

*For a full list of designated cooling locations, visit **[City Website/CoolingCenters]** or call **311**.*

**Emergency Reminder:** Heat stroke is a medical emergency. If you or someone you see experiences confusion, dizziness, a rapid heart rate, or a loss of consciousness, move them to a cooler place and **call 911 immediately**.
