**NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE**

**Riverton Public Works Department**

Maple Avenue will be closed to all vehicle and pedestrian traffic between Fifth Street and Ninth Street from 7:00 a.m. Monday, June 9, through 5:00 p.m. Friday, June 13.

Crews will be repaving the roadway and replacing storm drains. Detours will be posted along Oak Avenue and Cedar Street. Local residents may reach their driveways from either end of the closed section, but through traffic will not be permitted.

Bus Route 14 will temporarily follow the Oak Avenue detour, and the Fifth Street stop will move to the corner of Fourth and Oak.

Emergency vehicles will have access to the work zone at all times.

For questions, call the Public Works office at 555-0142 weekdays between 8:00 a.m. and 4:30 p.m.

Thank you for your patience while we improve your street.
