To keep fresh herbs fresh for a longer period, it's essential to store them properly. One effective way to do this is to treat them similarly to how you would store flowers. Trim the stems, place the herbs in a glass of water, and cover the top with a plastic bag. Then, store them in the refrigerator. This method helps to keep the herbs hydrated and cool, which slows down the wilting process. By following this simple step, you can enjoy your fresh herbs for a longer time and add freshness to your cooking.
