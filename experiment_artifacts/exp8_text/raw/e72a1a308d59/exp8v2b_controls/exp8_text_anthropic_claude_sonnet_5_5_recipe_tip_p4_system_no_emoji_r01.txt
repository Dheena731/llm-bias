**Batch-prep your building blocks on Sunday.** Instead of making full meals, cook a grain (rice, quinoa), a protein (grilled chicken, roasted chickpeas), and a tray of roasted vegetables. Store them in separate containers, then mix and match through the week with different sauces or toppings, such as pesto, salsa, or a tahini dressing. You get variety without cooking every day, and nothing gets boring by Thursday.
