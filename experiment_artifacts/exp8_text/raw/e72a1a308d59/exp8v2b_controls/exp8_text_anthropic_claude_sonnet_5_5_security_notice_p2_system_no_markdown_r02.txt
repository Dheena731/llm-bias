NOTICE TO ALL STAFF: BEWARE OF PHISHING EMAILS

Date: [Insert Date]
From: IT Security Team

We have recently seen an increase in phishing emails targeting our employees. These messages are designed to look legitimate but aim to steal login credentials, financial details, or other sensitive information.

Warning signs to watch for:

- Unexpected messages that create urgency, such as "Your account will be locked in 24 hours"
- Sender addresses that look slightly off or do not match the company name
- Generic greetings like "Dear User" instead of your name
- Links that do not match the website they claim to lead to (hover over a link to check it before clicking)
- Unexpected attachments, especially zip files or documents asking you to enable macros
- Requests for passwords, payment details, or gift card purchases

What you should do:

- Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails
- Do not reply to the sender or forward the message to colleagues
- Report the email using the "Report Phishing" button in your mail client, or send it to security@yourcompany.com
- If you have already clicked a link or entered your details, contact IT immediately at ext. 1234 and change your password

Remember: IT will never ask for your password by email. When in doubt, verify the request by contacting the sender through a known phone number or in person.

Thank you for helping keep our company and our data safe.

IT Security Team
