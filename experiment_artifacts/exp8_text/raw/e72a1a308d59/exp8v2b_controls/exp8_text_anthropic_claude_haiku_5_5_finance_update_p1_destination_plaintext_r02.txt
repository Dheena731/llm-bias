We've just launched a new monthly spending summary to help you keep track of where your money goes. Each month, you'll receive a clear overview that shows:

- Total spending for the month, compared with the previous month
- Your top spending categories, so you can see where most of your money went
- Any recurring charges, such as subscriptions, so nothing slips under the radar
- Unusual or unexpected transactions, flagged for your review

The summary is available in the Insights tab of your account and will also be sent to your email on the first day of each month. You can turn off the email in Settings if you prefer to check it only in the app.

We hope this makes it easier to plan ahead and spot changes early. As always, if you have questions or feedback, just reply to this message and our support team will be happy to help.

Thanks for being part of our community.
