NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE

The public is advised that Elm Street will be closed to all traffic from 8am on Monday, March 15th to 4pm on Friday, March 19th. The closure is necessary for essential maintenance work to be carried out by our contractors.

During this period, a detour will be in place, directing motorists to follow the signed diversion route through Oak Street and Maple Avenue.

The public is asked to plan their journeys accordingly and to avoid the area if possible. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your cooperation in this matter.
