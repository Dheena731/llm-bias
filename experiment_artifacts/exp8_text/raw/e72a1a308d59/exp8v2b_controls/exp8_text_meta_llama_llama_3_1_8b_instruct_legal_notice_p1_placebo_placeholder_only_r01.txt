NOTICE OF CHANGE TO ACCOUNT TERMS

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that, effective immediately, the following changes to the account terms and conditions will apply:

ARTICLE I: DEFINITIONS

The term "Account" shall mean any account established by a customer with the Bank, including, but not limited to, deposit accounts, loan accounts, and credit accounts.

ARTICLE II: CHANGES TO TERMS AND CONDITIONS

The Bank reserves the right to modify or add to the account terms and conditions at any time. Any such changes shall be deemed effective immediately upon posting on the Bank's website.

ARTICLE III: CHANGES TO INTEREST RATES

The interest rates applicable to certain accounts shall be adjusted as follows:

* The interest rate on all deposit accounts shall be increased by 1% per annum.
* The interest rate on all loan accounts shall be decreased by 0.5% per annum.

ARTICLE IV: EFFECTIVE DATE

These changes to the account terms and conditions shall take effect on the date of this notice, and shall apply to all accounts established with the Bank prior to and subsequent to the effective date.

ARTICLE V: GOVERNING LAW

These changes to the account terms and conditions shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the state in which the Bank is located.

BY OPENING OR CONTINUING TO MAINTAIN AN ACCOUNT WITH THE BANK, CUSTOMERS SHALL BE DEEMED TO HAVE ACCEPTED THESE CHANGES TO THE ACCOUNT TERMS AND CONDITIONS.
