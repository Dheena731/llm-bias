**The Secret to Perfectly Cooked, Less Sticky Rice**

Are you tired of dealing with clumpy, sticky rice that sticks to the bottom of the pan and makes a mess? We've got the solution! Here's a simple cooking tip to help you achieve perfectly cooked, fluffy rice that's less sticky:

**Rinse and Drain, Rinse and Drain**

Before cooking your rice, make sure to rinse it thoroughly in a fine-mesh strainer under cold running water. This step is crucial in removing excess starch, which can contribute to stickiness. After rinsing, drain the water and repeat the process a few times to ensure you've removed as much starch as possible.

**Use the Right Water Ratio**

The right water-to-rice ratio is essential for achieving perfectly cooked rice. A general rule of thumb is to use a 1:1.25 to 1:1.5 ratio of water to rice. For example, if you're using 1 cup of uncooked rice, use 1.25 to 1.5 cups of water. This will help prevent the rice from becoming too sticky or clumpy.

**Cook with the Right Heat**

When cooking your rice, make sure to use a medium-low heat. High heat can cause the rice to cook too quickly, resulting in a sticky or burnt texture. Stir-fry the rice occasionally to prevent it from forming clumps.

By following these simple tips, you'll be well on your way to achieving perfectly cooked, less sticky rice that's a delight to serve and enjoy!
