Tip: Prep your lunches in components, not full meals.

On Sunday, spend 45 minutes cooking a few basics: one grain (rice, quinoa, or pasta), one protein (grilled chicken, hard-boiled eggs, or chickpeas), and one or two vegetables (roasted broccoli, chopped cucumbers, or carrots). Store each in its own container.

Each morning, mix and match to build a different lunch in about two minutes. Monday might be a rice bowl with chicken and broccoli, Tuesday a pasta salad with chickpeas and cucumbers, and Wednesday a wrap with leftover chicken and carrots. Add a quick sauce or dressing to change the flavor and keep things from getting boring.

This approach keeps food fresher, cuts down on waste, and gives you variety without cooking every day.
