To store fresh herbs, trim the stems of the herbs and place them in a glass jar or container with a damp paper towel or cloth at the bottom. This will help maintain the herbs' moisture levels and prevent them from drying out. Cover the jar with a plastic bag or plastic wrap and store it in the refrigerator. Check the herbs every few days and replace the paper towel or cloth as needed. This will help keep the herbs fresh for a longer period of time.
