Here is a short reminder for users to review their weekly fitness progress:

**Weekly Fitness Check-In: Review Your Progress!**

Take a moment to reflect on your fitness goals and progress from the past week. Ask yourself:

* What exercises did I complete and how often did I do them?
* Did I meet my weekly calorie intake or macronutrient goals?
* How did my workouts make me feel? Was I pushing myself enough?
* Did I encounter any setbacks or challenges that I need to address?

Take a few minutes to review your fitness log or journal, and adjust your plan for the upcoming week as needed. This will help you stay on track, make progress towards your goals, and celebrate your successes along the way!
