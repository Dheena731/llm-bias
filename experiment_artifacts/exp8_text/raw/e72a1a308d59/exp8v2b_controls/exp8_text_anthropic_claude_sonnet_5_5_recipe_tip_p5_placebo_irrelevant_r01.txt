**Season in two stages.** Toss your vegetables with oil and a generous pinch of salt *before* roasting so the seasoning sticks and the vegetables brown evenly. Then, as soon as they come out of the oven, finish with something bright, like a squeeze of lemon, a splash of vinegar, or a sprinkle of fresh herbs. Delicate flavors and acidity fade in high heat, so adding them at the end keeps the vegetables tasting vibrant rather than flat.

**Bonus:** Save heavy spices like smoked paprika or cumin for the pre-roast toss, but hold off on garlic until the last 10 minutes, since it burns and turns bitter easily.
