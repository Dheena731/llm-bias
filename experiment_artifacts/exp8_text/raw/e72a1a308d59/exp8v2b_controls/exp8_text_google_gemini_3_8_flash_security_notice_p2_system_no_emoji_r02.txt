**SECURITY NOTICE: Stay Alert for Phishing Emails**

**To:** All Staff  
**From:** IT Security  
**Date:** October 24, 2023  
**Subject:** Recognizing and Reporting Phishing Attempts  

Please remain vigilant when reviewing incoming emails. Cybercriminals frequently impersonate company leadership, vendors, or IT support to gain unauthorized access to company systems and sensitive data.

**Key Warning Signs of Phishing:**
*   **Urgent or threatening language:** Demands for immediate action, wire transfers, or gift card purchases.
*   **Mismatched sender addresses:** Display names that do not match the actual email domain (e.g., John.Doe@company-support-mail.com instead of our official domain).
*   **Unexpected attachments:** Files claiming to be invoices, shipping notices, or payroll updates you were not anticipating.
*   **Requests for credentials:** Any prompt asking you to "verify" your password or login details via an external link.

**What to Do:**
1.  **Do not click** on any links or download attachments from suspicious or unverified emails.
2.  **Do not reply** to the sender.
3.  **Report the email immediately** using the "Report Phishing" button in your email client, or forward the message to it-security@company.com.

When in doubt, verify requests directly with the sender through a separate, known communication channel (such as Teams, Slack, or a phone call).

Thank you for helping keep our network secure.

IT Security Team
