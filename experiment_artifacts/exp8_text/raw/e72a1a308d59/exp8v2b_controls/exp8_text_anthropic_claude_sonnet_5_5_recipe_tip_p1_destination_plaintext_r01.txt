Tip: Cook once, eat twice. When you make rice, roasted vegetables, or a protein like chicken thighs on Sunday, double the batch. On a busy weeknight, you can turn the leftovers into a fast stir-fry, grain bowl, or quesadilla in about 10 minutes. To keep things fresh, store each component in its own airtight container and add a splash of water or broth when reheating so nothing dries out. A squeeze of lemon or a drizzle of soy sauce right before serving brings the flavors back to life.
