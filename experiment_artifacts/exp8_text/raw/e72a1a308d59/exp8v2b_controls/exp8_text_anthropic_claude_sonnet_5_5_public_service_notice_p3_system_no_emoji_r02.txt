**NOTICE: Changes to Recycling Collection**

**Effective Monday, [date]**

Please be advised that the recycling collection schedule in our area is changing.

**What is changing:**
- Recycling will now be collected every other week, rather than weekly.
- Collection day will move from Thursday to Tuesday.
- Bins must be placed at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on collection day.

**What can be recycled:**
- Paper, cardboard, and flattened boxes
- Plastic containers numbered 1, 2, and 5
- Metal cans and aluminum foil
- Glass bottles and jars

**What should stay out:**
- Plastic bags and film
- Food-soiled items, such as greasy pizza boxes
- Styrofoam
- Ceramics and drinking glasses

Please rinse containers and place materials loose in the bin, not in bags.

A printed calendar showing your collection dates will be mailed to all households by the end of the month. It will also be available on our website at [website address].

For questions or to request an additional bin, please contact the Public Works Office at [phone number] or [email address].

Thank you for helping keep our community clean.
