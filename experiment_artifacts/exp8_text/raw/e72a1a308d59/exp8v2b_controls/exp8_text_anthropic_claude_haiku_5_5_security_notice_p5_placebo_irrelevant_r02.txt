**Notice: How to Report a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, report it immediately. Prompt reporting protects company data and helps limit security risks.

**What to do:**

1. **Contact IT Security right away** at [phone number] or [email address], ideally within one hour of discovering the loss.
2. **Provide details**, including the laptop's asset tag, serial number, when and where you last had it, and any files or accounts it contained.
3. **Report to local police** if the device was stolen, and keep the police report number for your records.
4. **Do not attempt to retrieve or access the device yourself.** IT will take steps to lock and wipe it remotely.
5. **Change your passwords** for work accounts only after IT confirms it is safe to do so.

Failure to report a lost device promptly may result in disciplinary action under company policy.
