**Security Alert: Password Reuse Policy**

As a reminder, it is strictly prohibited to reuse passwords across multiple systems and applications. This includes, but is not limited to, network logins, email accounts, and any other company-owned or managed resources.

Using the same password for multiple accounts increases the risk of unauthorized access to your personal and company data. In the event of a password breach, an attacker could potentially gain access to all of your accounts, compromising your personal and professional security.

To ensure the security of our systems and data, please follow these best practices:

*   Use a unique and complex password for each account.
*   Avoid using easily guessable information, such as names, birthdays, or common words.
*   Consider using a password manager to securely store and generate unique passwords.

If you have any questions or concerns about our password reuse policy, please reach out to our IT department for assistance.

**Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining the security of our workplace systems and data.**
