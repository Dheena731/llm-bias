**Subject: Introducing Your Monthly Spending Summary**

Hi everyone,

We're launching a new monthly spending summary to help you see where your money goes and spot changes over time.

Each month, you'll receive a short report that includes:

- **Total spending** for the month, compared with the previous month
- **Category breakdowns** such as housing, travel, and subscriptions
- **Notable changes**, like categories that rose or fell significantly
- **Upcoming recurring charges** so you can plan ahead

The summary will appear in your dashboard on the first business day of each month, and you can also find it under the Reports tab at any time. If you'd prefer not to receive the email version, you can turn it off in your notification settings.

We'd love your feedback. Reply to this message or use the feedback link in the report to tell us what's useful and what we should improve.
