PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE: COMMUNITY LIBRARY OPENING HOURS

Please note the following regular operating schedule for all branch locations:

Monday through Thursday: 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM
Friday: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
Saturday: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM
Sunday: Closed

Libraries will observe all major public holidays with zero access. Any temporary schedule adjustments will be announced three days prior via local radio and posted on official signage. For assistance, dial 555-0172 between 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM.

Thank you for supporting your local library. We appreciate your visit.
