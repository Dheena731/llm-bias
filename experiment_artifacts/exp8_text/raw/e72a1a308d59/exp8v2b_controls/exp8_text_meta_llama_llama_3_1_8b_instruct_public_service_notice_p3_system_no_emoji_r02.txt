NOTICE OF RECYCLING COLLECTION CHANGES

The town's recycling collection schedule has been revised. As of January 1, all households will be required to place their recyclable materials in the designated recycling bins provided by the town.

* Collection days will change from bi-weekly to weekly.
* New collection times are as follows:
	+ Monday, 6:00 AM - 8:00 AM
	+ Thursday, 6:00 AM - 8:00 AM
* The town's recycling center will only accept the following materials:
	+ Paper and cardboard
	+ Plastic bottles and containers
	+ Glass bottles and jars
	+ Metal cans

Please ensure that all recyclable materials are properly sorted and placed in the designated bins to ensure efficient collection and processing. Failure to comply may result in fines.

For more information, please contact the town's public works department at (555) 123-4567 or visit the town's website.
