**Subject: Quarterly Access Reviews Begin Next Week**

Hello everyone,

Quarterly access reviews for all company systems and applications begin Monday and must be completed within two weeks.

**What this means for you**

If you manage a team, you will receive an email from the IT Security team listing each direct report and the systems they can access. Please review the list and confirm whether each person's access is still appropriate for their current role. Flag anything that should be removed or changed.

If you are an individual contributor, no action is needed unless your manager contacts you with questions about your access.

**What we are looking for**

- Access that is no longer needed due to a role change or project ending
- Accounts belonging to former employees or contractors
- Permissions that exceed what a person needs to do their job
- Shared accounts without a clear owner

**Deadlines**

Managers should submit their reviews by the end of the second week. Reviews not completed by then will be escalated to department heads, and unreviewed access may be suspended until confirmation is received.

**Questions**

Reach out to the IT Security team through the help desk portal or by replying to this message. Thank you for helping keep our systems and data secure.
