**NOTICE: Changes to Your Recycling Collection**

Starting **[date]**, recycling in **[area]** will be collected **every other week** instead of weekly. Your general waste bin will still be collected weekly on your usual day.

**What you need to do:**
- Check the new recycling calendar at **[website]** to find your collection weeks.
- Put your recycling bin out by 7 a.m. on collection day. Do not put it out on off-weeks.
- Rinse cans, bottles, and tubs before placing them in the bin.
- Do not use plastic bags for recycling. Place loose items directly in the bin.

**Accepted items:** paper, cardboard, glass bottles and jars, metal cans, and plastic bottles and tubs.

**Not accepted:** plastic bags, food waste, nappies, and garden waste.
