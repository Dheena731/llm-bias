**Reminder: Please Lock Your Screens**

Team,

As we work across shared spaces and handle sensitive information, please make a habit of locking your screen whenever you step away from your desk, even for a few minutes.

To lock your screen:
- **Windows:** Press **Windows key + L**
- **Mac:** Press **Control + Command + Q**

Locking your screen helps protect client data, internal documents, and our systems from unauthorized access. It only takes a second and is one of the simplest ways we can keep our workplace secure.
