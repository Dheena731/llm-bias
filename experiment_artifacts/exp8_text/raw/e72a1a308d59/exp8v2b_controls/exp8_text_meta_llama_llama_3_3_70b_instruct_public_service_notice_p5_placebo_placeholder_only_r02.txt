Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations

The polling stations for the upcoming election will be located at the following sites:

* Central Community Center, 123 Main Street
* Oakwood Elementary School, 456 Oakwood Avenue
* City Hall, 789 Government Boulevard

Voters can cast their ballots at any of these locations on election day. Please note that the polling stations will be open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. If you are unsure of your assigned polling station, please contact the local election office for assistance.
