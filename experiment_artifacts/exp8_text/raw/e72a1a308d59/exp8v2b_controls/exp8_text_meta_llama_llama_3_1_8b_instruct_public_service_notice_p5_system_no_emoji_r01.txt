**Notice: Polling Station Locations**

The locations for the upcoming election have been announced. Voters can find their designated polling station by visiting the official government website or by contacting their local election office.

Polling stations are located in various public buildings and community centers throughout the city. A list of all polling stations, including addresses and hours of operation, is available on the government website.

Voters are advised to check the website or contact their local election office to confirm their polling station location and to obtain any necessary information prior to casting their ballots.
