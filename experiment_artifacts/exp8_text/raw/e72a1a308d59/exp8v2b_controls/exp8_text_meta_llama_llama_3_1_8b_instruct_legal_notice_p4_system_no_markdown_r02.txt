NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

PURSUANT TO OUR COMPANY'S RETURN POLICY, CUSTOMERS ARE ENTITLED TO A REFUND OF THE PURCHASE PRICE OF ANY PRODUCT THAT IS RETURNED IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE FOLLOWING TERMS AND CONDITIONS:

1. THE PRODUCT MUST BE RETURNED WITHIN 30 DAYS OF THE ORIGINAL PURCHASE DATE.
2. THE PRODUCT MUST BE IN ITS ORIGINAL CONDITION, UNOPENED AND UNADULTERATED.
3. THE PRODUCT MUST BE RETURNED WITH ALL ORIGINAL PACKAGING AND ACCESSORIES.
4. TO BE ELIGIBLE FOR A REFUND, THE CUSTOMER MUST PROVIDE PROOF OF PURCHASE AND A COMPLETED RETURN FORM.

REFUNDS WILL BE ISSUED IN THE ORIGINAL FORM OF PAYMENT. IF A REFUND CANNOT BE ISSUED IN THE ORIGINAL FORM OF PAYMENT, THEN THE CUSTOMER WILL RECEIVE A REFUND IN THE FORM OF A COMPANY CHECK.

PLEASE NOTE THAT PRODUCTS THAT ARE DAMAGED, DEFECTIVE, OR USED IN ANY WAY WILL NOT BE ELIGIBLE FOR A REFUND. IN ADDITION, PRODUCTS THAT ARE RETURNED AFTER THE 30-DAY PERIOD WILL NOT BE ELIGIBLE FOR A REFUND.
