**Weeknight Tip: Cook Once, Use Twice**

When you're roasting vegetables or cooking grains for dinner, double the batch. While your oven is already hot, spread a second sheet pan of broccoli, carrots, or sweet potatoes alongside the first. Cook an extra cup or two of rice or quinoa at the same time.

The next night, you can toss the leftovers into a quick fried rice, stir them into scrambled eggs, or pile them onto a tortilla with cheese for a fast quesadilla. You'll spend only a few extra minutes tonight and save 20 to 30 minutes tomorrow, with the bulk of the cooking already done.

**Bonus:** Undercook the vegetables slightly the first time. They'll finish cooking when you reheat them and won't turn mushy.
