Cook once, eat several times. On Sunday, prepare two or three base ingredients, such as a grain (rice or quinoa), a protein (roasted chicken, baked tofu, or hard-boiled eggs), and a tray of roasted vegetables. Store them in separate containers, then mix and match through the week with different sauces or toppings like pesto, salsa, tahini dressing, or a squeeze of lemon. This way, Monday's rice bowl can become Wednesday's wrap filling or Friday's salad topper, so you get variety without cooking every night. Pack lunches the evening before, and keep a few backup options on hand, like canned soup or frozen dumplings, for days when plans fall apart.
