**Comment on Missing Citations**

Several claims in this essay are presented without supporting sources, particularly the statistics in the second paragraph and the historical assertions in the discussion of the early twentieth century. Without citations, readers cannot verify the information or trace it to its origin, which weakens the credibility of your argument. It also raises concerns about academic integrity, since unattributed ideas may appear to be your own.

To strengthen the essay, add in-text citations wherever you use data, direct quotations, paraphrased ideas, or specific factual claims that are not common knowledge. Make sure each citation corresponds to a full entry in your bibliography, and follow a single citation style consistently. Reviewing your draft with a highlighter to mark every claim that relies on outside information may help you identify where sources are needed.
