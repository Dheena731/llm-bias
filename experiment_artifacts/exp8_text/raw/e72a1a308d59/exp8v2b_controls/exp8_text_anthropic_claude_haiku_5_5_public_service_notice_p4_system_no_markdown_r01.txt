HEAT ADVISORY PUBLIC NOTICE

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for the area from 11:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. today. Temperatures are expected to reach the mid-90s with heat index values near 105 degrees.

Residents are urged to take the following precautions:

Stay indoors in air-conditioned spaces when possible. If you do not have air conditioning, visit a cooling center or public library.

Drink water regularly, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol, caffeine, and sugary drinks.

Never leave children, pets, or anyone who depends on you in a parked vehicle.

Check on elderly neighbors, friends, and relatives, and those who live alone.

Limit outdoor activities to the cooler morning and evening hours. Take frequent breaks if you must work outside.

Watch for signs of heat illness, including heavy sweating, dizziness, nausea, muscle cramps, and confusion. Move to a cool place and seek medical help if symptoms worsen. Call 911 for emergencies such as fainting, seizures, or loss of consciousness.

For more information, contact your local emergency management office or visit weather.gov.
