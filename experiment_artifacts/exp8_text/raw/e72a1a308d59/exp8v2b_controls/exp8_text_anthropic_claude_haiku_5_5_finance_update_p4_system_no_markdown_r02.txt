Dear Taxpayer,

Your end-of-year tax statement summarizes your income, deductions, and tax payments for the year. It shows the total wages, interest, dividends, or other income reported to the government, along with any deductions or credits that reduce the amount of tax you owe. It also lists the taxes already paid through payroll withholding or estimated payments.

By comparing what you owed with what you paid, the statement shows whether you have a balance due or are entitled to a refund. If you owe money, payment is due by the filing deadline to avoid penalties and interest. If you overpaid, the difference will be refunded or applied to next year's taxes, depending on your choice.

Please review the statement carefully for accuracy, and keep it with your records. If anything looks incorrect or you have questions, contact a tax professional or the tax authority for assistance.
