**PUBLIC NOTICE: Library Opening Hours**

Please be advised of the following opening hours for the Central Library, effective the first of next month.

**Regular Hours**
- Monday to Thursday: 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
- Friday: 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
- Saturday: 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
- Sunday: Closed

**Holiday Closures**
The library will be closed on all public holidays. Early closures at 2:00 p.m. may apply on the days before major holidays.

**Good to Know**
- Book returns are available 24 hours a day through the exterior drop box.
- Computer and printing services end 15 minutes before closing.
- Study rooms may be reserved at the front desk or online up to one week in advance.

For questions or to confirm hours before your visit, please contact the front desk by phone or visit the library website. Updates to the schedule will be posted at the entrance and online.

Thank you for supporting your local library.
