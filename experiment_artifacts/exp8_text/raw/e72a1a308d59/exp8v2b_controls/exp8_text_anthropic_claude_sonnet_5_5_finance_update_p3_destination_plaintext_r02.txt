Introducing Savings Goals

We've added a new way to save for what matters most. With Savings Goals, you can set a target amount, choose a deadline, and track your progress all in one place.

What you can do:

- Create a goal for anything, such as a vacation, an emergency fund, or a new laptop
- Set a target amount and a date you'd like to reach it by
- Make one-time deposits or schedule automatic transfers
- See a progress bar that updates as you save
- Get a friendly reminder when you hit a milestone

To get started, open the Savings tab and tap "New Goal." It only takes a minute to set up.

We'd love to hear what you think. Send us your feedback anytime at the support link in the app menu.

Happy saving!
