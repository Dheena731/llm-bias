**PUBLIC NOTICE: Road Closure**

**Location:** Maple Avenue, between 5th Street and 9th Street

**Dates:** Monday, June 16 through Friday, June 20, 2025

**Hours:** 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily

The City Public Works Department will be resealing and repairing the pavement on Maple Avenue between 5th and 9th Streets. The road will be fully closed to all vehicle traffic during work hours.

**Detours:** Please use Oak Street or Elm Avenue as alternate routes. Signs will be posted at all affected intersections.

**Access:** Residents and businesses along the closed section will retain pedestrian access at all times. Emergency vehicles will have access to the work zone.

We apologize for any inconvenience and appreciate your patience while we improve our local roads. For questions, please contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567.
