**New Feature: Reach your targets faster with Savings Goals 🎯**

Saving for a vacation, an emergency fund, or a down payment just got easier. With our new **Savings Goals** feature, you can now visualize and automate your progress directly within the app.

Here’s what you can do:

* **Personalize your goals:** Set a specific target amount and date for whatever you’re saving toward.
* **Automate your savings:** Schedule recurring transfers so your goals grow in the background.
* **Track your momentum:** Keep tabs on your milestones with real-time visual progress bars.

**How to get started:**  
Make sure your app is updated to the latest version, head over to the **Accounts** tab, and tap **Create a Goal**.
