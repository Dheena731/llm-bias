**PUBLIC NOTICE: Maple Street Closed for Water Pipe Replacement**

**What is closing:** Maple Street between 4th Avenue and 9th Avenue will be closed to all traffic, including cars, trucks, bicycles, and pedestrians.

**When:** Monday, June 10, at 7:00 a.m. through Friday, June 14, 2024, at 5:00 p.m.

**Why:** Crews are replacing an aging underground water pipe. The road must stay closed while the street is dug up and repaved.

**Detour:** Drivers should take 4th Avenue to Oak Street, then Oak Street to 9th Avenue. Detour signs will be posted. Please allow about 10 extra minutes for your trip. Pedestrians and cyclists should use Pine Street, which stays open.

**Access for residents and businesses:** You can still reach your home or business from the nearest open end of Maple Street. Driveway access may be briefly blocked while crews work in front of your property. Crews will notify you in advance. Emergency vehicles will have access at all times.

**Water service:** Some properties may lose water for up to 4 hours on Wednesday, June 12. Affected addresses will receive a door notice at least 24 hours beforehand.

**Bus riders:** Route 12 will skip stops on Maple Street during the closure. Use the stops on Oak Street instead.

**Delays:** If bad weather delays the work, the street may reopen later than scheduled. Updates will be posted at cityofexample.gov/maplestreet.

**Questions:** Call City Public Works at 555-0142, Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.

We apologize for the inconvenience and thank you for your patience.
