We are excited to introduce a new monthly spending summary, designed to help you track and manage your expenses more effectively. This summary will be sent to you at the beginning of each month and will provide a detailed breakdown of your previous month's spending, including total expenditures, category-wise spending, and any notable transactions. The summary will also offer personalized insights and suggestions to help you optimize your spending habits and make informed financial decisions. With this new feature, you'll be able to stay on top of your finances and make adjustments as needed to achieve your financial goals.
