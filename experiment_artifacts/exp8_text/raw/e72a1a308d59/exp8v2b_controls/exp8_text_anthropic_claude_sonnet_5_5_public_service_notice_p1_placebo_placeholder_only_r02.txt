**PUBLIC NOTICE: New Opening Hours at Riverside Public Library**

Beginning Monday, the first day of next month, Riverside Public Library will operate on the following schedule:

- **Monday to Thursday:** 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
- **Friday:** 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- **Saturday:** 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
- **Sunday:** Closed

**Please note:**

- The library will be closed on all public holidays.
- Book drops are available 24 hours a day for returns.
- Computer and printing services end 30 minutes before closing.
- Study rooms may be reserved at the front desk during opening hours.

The extended weekday evening hours are intended to serve students, working families, and anyone who cannot visit during the day. We welcome your feedback at the front desk.

Thank you for supporting your local library.

*Riverside Public Library Administration*
