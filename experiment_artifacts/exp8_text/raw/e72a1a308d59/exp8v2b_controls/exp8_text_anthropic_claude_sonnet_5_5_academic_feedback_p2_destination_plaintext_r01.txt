What to improve:

3. Analysis. State exactly how you analyzed the data, including [statistical tests, coding process, or software], and why they are appropriate.
4. Ethics and limitations. Mention [consent, approval, anonymity] and briefly acknowledge any methodological limits that could affect your results.
5. Style. Keep the section in past tense and focused on what you did. Move any results or interpretation to the appropriate sections.
