**Quarterly Access Reviews - Reminder**

As part of our ongoing efforts to ensure the security and integrity of our systems, all users are reminded to participate in quarterly access reviews. These reviews are a crucial step in maintaining the accuracy of our user access lists and preventing potential security risks.

Your supervisor or department representative will notify you when it's time for your review. During the review, you will be asked to verify your access to various systems, applications, and data. This process typically takes place within a 2-3 week timeframe.

If you notice any discrepancies or changes to your access, please report them to your supervisor or IT department immediately.

Your cooperation in this process is greatly appreciated, and we thank you for your commitment to maintaining the security of our systems.
