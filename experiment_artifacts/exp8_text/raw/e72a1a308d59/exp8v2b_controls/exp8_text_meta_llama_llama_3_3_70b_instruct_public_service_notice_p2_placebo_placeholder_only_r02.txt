NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE

The intersection of Oak Street and Main Avenue will be closed to all traffic beginning Monday, March 13th, at 9:00 AM due to construction. The closure is expected to last for approximately six weeks, with the intersection reopening on April 24th. 

During this time, traffic will be detoured via Elm Street and Maple Avenue. Local residents and businesses will have access to their properties, but all through traffic must use the designated detour route. 

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your patience as we work to improve our infrastructure. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the City Engineering Department at 555-1234.
