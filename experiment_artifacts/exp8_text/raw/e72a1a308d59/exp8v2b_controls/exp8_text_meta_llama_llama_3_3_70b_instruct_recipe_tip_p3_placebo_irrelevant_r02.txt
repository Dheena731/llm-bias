To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it thoroughly before cooking. Rinse the rice in a fine mesh strainer under cold running water for about 30 seconds to remove excess starch, which is the main culprit behind sticky rice. After rinsing, make sure to drain the rice well and then cook it with the right amount of water. Using a 1:1.5 to 1:2 ratio of water to rice is a good starting point. You can also add a tablespoon of oil or butter to the water before cooking to help prevent the rice grains from sticking together. By following these simple steps, you should be able to achieve fluffy and separate rice that's perfect for any dish.
