**Important Voting Information**

To all registered voters,

The polling station locations for the upcoming election have been confirmed. To find your assigned polling station, please visit our website or check your voter registration card for the address.

Polling stations will be open on election day from 8am to 8pm. You can also view a list of all polling station locations at your local library or town hall.

If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our elections office for assistance.

Remember to cast your vote at your assigned polling station on election day.
