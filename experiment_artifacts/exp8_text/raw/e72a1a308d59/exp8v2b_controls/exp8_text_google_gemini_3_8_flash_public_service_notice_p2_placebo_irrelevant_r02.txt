**PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE**

**Location:** Elm Street (between 4th Avenue and 6th Avenue)  
**Dates:** Monday, November 6, 2023 – Thursday, November 9, 2023  
**Hours:** 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM daily  

Notice is hereby given that Elm Street, between 4th Avenue and 6th Avenue, will be closed to all through traffic during the hours listed above. This closure is required to facilitate emergency water main repairs and subsequent roadway resurfacing.

**Detour Information:**  
Traffic will be detoured around the work zone via Maple Street and 7th Avenue. Clear signage will be posted along the detour route. 

**Local & Emergency Access:**  
Local access for residents and businesses within the closure zone will be accommodated when safe. Emergency vehicles will be granted immediate access at all times.

Motorists are advised to expect minor delays and to use alternate routes where possible. We appreciate your patience and cooperation as we complete these necessary infrastructure improvements.
