**IMPORTANT: PHISHING AWARENESS AND REPORTING PROCEDURES**

All staff must remain vigilant against phishing attempts targeting our organization. Recent threats include fraudulent messages impersonating vendors, leadership, and service providers to harvest login credentials or distribute malware.

Always verify the sender's address carefully. Look for misspellings, unusual domain extensions, or requests for sensitive information via email. Be skeptical of urgency, threats, or unexpected attachments.

Never click links or download files from unverified sources. If an email appears suspicious, forward it immediately to the IT Security Team for examination. Use the "Report Phishing" function in your email client when available.

Report all doubts promptly to maintain system integrity. Thank you for your cooperation in protecting our network.
