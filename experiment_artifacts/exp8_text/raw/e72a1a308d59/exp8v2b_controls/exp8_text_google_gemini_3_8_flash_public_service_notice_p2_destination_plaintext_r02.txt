PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE

Please be advised that Main Street, between 1st Avenue and 4th Avenue, will be temporarily closed to all through traffic beginning Monday, October 23, at 7:00 AM through Friday, October 27, at 5:00 PM.

This closure is required for scheduled underground utility repairs and street resurfacing.

A marked detour will direct traffic around the work zone via Oak Street and Elm Street. Local access will be maintained for residents and emergency vehicles at all times. 

Drivers are encouraged to use alternate routes and allow extra travel time. 

For questions or additional information, please contact the Public Works Department at 555-0199 or email publicworks@citygov.org. 

Thank you for your patience and cooperation.
