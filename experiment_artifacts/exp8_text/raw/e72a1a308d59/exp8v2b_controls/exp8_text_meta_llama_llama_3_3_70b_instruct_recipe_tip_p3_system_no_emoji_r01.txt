To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it thoroughly before cooking. This helps remove excess starch from the surface of the grains, which can cause them to cling together. Additionally, use a ratio of at least 1 3/4 cups of water to 1 cup of rice, and avoid overmixing or stirring the rice too much while it's cooking. This will help the grains cook evenly and remain separate, resulting in a fluffier and less sticky final product.
