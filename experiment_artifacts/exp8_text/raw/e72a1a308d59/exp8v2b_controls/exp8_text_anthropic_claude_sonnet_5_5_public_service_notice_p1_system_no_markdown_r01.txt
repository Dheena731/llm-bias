PUBLIC NOTICE: NEW LIBRARY OPENING HOURS

Effective Monday, June 2, the Riverside Public Library will operate on the following schedule:

Monday to Thursday: 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
Friday: 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
Saturday: 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
Sunday: Closed

Please note that the library will be closed on public holidays. Book drops remain open 24 hours a day for returns. Computer and printing services end 30 minutes before closing time.

For questions or to confirm hours before your visit, call the front desk at 555-0142 or visit the library website.

Thank you for supporting your local library.
