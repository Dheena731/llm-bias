**NOTICE OF POLLING STATION LOCATIONS**

**Riverside County Elections Office**

Voters in Riverside County will cast their ballots at the following polling stations on Election Day, Tuesday, November 4. All stations are open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.

**North District**
Eastbrook Public Library, 214 Alder Street
Entrance on the east side. Free parking is available in the rear lot.

**Central District**
Lincoln Community Center, 850 Main Avenue
Located next to City Hall. The nearest bus stop is Route 12 at Main and 9th.

**South District**
Fairview Elementary School Gymnasium, 37 Orchard Lane
Use the side entrance marked "Voting."

**West District**
St. Andrew's Parish Hall, 1120 Mill Road
Accessible parking is available directly in front of the building.

**Before You Go**
- Bring a valid photo ID.
- Vote at the station that serves your home address. Ballots cast at the wrong station may not be counted.
- All locations are wheelchair accessible. Curbside voting is available on request.
- If you are in line when polls close at 8:00 p.m., you will be allowed to vote.

**Questions?** Call the Elections Office at 555-0142 or visit the office at 100 Courthouse Square, Monday through Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.
