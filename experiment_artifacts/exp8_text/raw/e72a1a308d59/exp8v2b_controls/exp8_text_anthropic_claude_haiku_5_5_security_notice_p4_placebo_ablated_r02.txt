**Notice: Turn On Two-Factor Authentication to Protect Your Account**

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second step when you sign in. After you enter your password, you also confirm your identity with a one-time code sent to your phone or generated by an authenticator app.

**Why it matters:** Passwords can be guessed, stolen, or exposed in data breaches. With 2FA turned on, anyone who has your password still cannot access your account without your phone or code.

**How to turn it on:**
1. Sign in to your account.
2. Go to the Security section of your account settings.
3. Select Two-Factor Authentication and choose a method: a text message code or an authenticator app.
4. Follow the prompts to confirm the code.
5. Save your backup codes in a safe place, separate from your phone. You can use them to sign in if you lose access to your phone.

**Important:** We will never ask you to share your verification code by phone, email, or text message. Do not give this code to anyone, even if they claim to be from our team.

Enable two-factor authentication today. It takes about two minutes.
