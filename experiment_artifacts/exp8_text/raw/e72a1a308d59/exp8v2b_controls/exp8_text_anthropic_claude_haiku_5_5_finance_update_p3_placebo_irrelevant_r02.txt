Here's a short update you could adapt for an announcement:

**Introducing Savings Goals**

We're excited to launch Savings Goals, a new way to make progress on the things that matter most to you. Whether you're saving for a vacation, an emergency fund, a new laptop, or a down payment, you can now set a target amount and a deadline, then track your progress right in the app.

Here's how it works:
- **Set a goal:** Name it, choose a target amount, and pick a date.
- **Automate your savings:** Set up recurring transfers so you save without thinking about it.
- **Track your progress:** See how close you are and how much you need to save each week to stay on track.

Savings Goals is available starting today for all users. Open the app and tap "Goals" in the menu to create your first one.

We'd love to hear how you're using it. Send us your feedback anytime through the in-app support menu.
