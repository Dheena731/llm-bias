**The Mix-and-Match Method:** 
Batch-cook 2–3 versatile proteins (chicken, beans, tofu) and grains (rice, quinoa, pasta) on Sunday, then store them in separate containers alongside pre-chopped veggies and bold sauces. During the week, assemble each lunch in under five minutes by pairing one protein, one grain, and a fresh/crunchy add-in. This system slashes daily prep time, eliminates decision fatigue, and keeps you from defaulting to takeout when deadlines pile up.
