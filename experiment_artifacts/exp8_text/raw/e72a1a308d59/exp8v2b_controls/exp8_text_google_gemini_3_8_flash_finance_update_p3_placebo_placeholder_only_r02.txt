**New Feature Alert: Reach Your Targets with Savings Goals**

Staying on track with personal financial targets just got easier. With the latest update, the new Savings Goals feature is officially live.

Whether saving for a dream vacation, an emergency fund, or a major purchase, you can now set custom targets with specific deadlines. Track visual progress in real time, celebrate milestones along the way, and turn on automatic recurring deposits to reach your targets without extra effort. 

To get started, update to the latest version of the app, open the main dashboard, and select **Add a Goal**.
