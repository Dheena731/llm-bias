**Reminder: Lock Your Screen When You Step Away**

Please lock your computer screen every time you leave your desk, even if it's just for a minute. An unlocked screen can give others access to confidential information, email, and company systems.

**How to lock your screen:**
- **Windows:** Press Windows key + L
- **Mac:** Press Control + Command + Q

Making this a habit helps protect our data, our clients, and each other. Thank you for your cooperation.
