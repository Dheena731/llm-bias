**Important Security Notice**

To add an extra layer of protection to your account, we highly recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA). This simple step helps prevent unauthorized access by requiring a second form of verification, in addition to your password, when logging in.

Enabling 2FA is easy and only takes a few minutes. To get started, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your account settings
2. Click on "Security" or "Account Security"
3. Select "Two-Factor Authentication" and follow the prompts to set up your preferred 2FA method (e.g. SMS, authenticator app, or email verification)

By enabling 2FA, you'll significantly reduce the risk of your account being compromised. If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact our support team.

**Stay secure and protect your account today!**
