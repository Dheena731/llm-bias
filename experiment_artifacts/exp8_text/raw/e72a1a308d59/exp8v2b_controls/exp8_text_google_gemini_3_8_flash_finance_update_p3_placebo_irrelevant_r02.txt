**Subject:** Introducing Savings Goals: Turn your plans into progress 🎯

Saving for what matters just got easier. We’re excited to introduce **Savings Goals**, a new feature designed to help you visualize, track, and reach your financial milestones faster.

Whether you're building an emergency fund, planning a dream vacation, or saving for a down payment, you can now:

* **Name & Customize Your Goals:** Set specific target amounts and completion dates for anything you’re saving toward.
* **Track Visual Progress:** Watch your progress bars fill up as you get closer to the finish line.
* **Automate Your Transfers:** Set up recurring deposits so you can grow your savings effortlessly on schedule.

**How to get started:**
Make sure your app is updated to the latest version, head over to the **Accounts** tab, and tap **"Create a Goal."**

Small steps add up. Set your first goal today and take control of your financial future!
