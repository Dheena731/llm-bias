**Cooking tip: Rinse your rice first.**

Before cooking, put the rice in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer and rinse it under cold water, swirling it with your hand, until the water runs mostly clear. This usually takes 3 to 5 rinses. Rinsing washes away the loose surface starch that makes grains clump together and turn gummy.

For even better results:
- **Use the right water ratio.** For long-grain white rice, try 1¾ cups of water per 1 cup of rice. Too much water leads to mushy, sticky rice.
- **Don't stir while it cooks.** Stirring releases more starch.
- **Let it rest.** After cooking, keep the lid on and let the rice sit off the heat for 10 minutes, then fluff it gently with a fork.
