**Subject:** Important Update Regarding Card Processing Fees

Beginning on the first of next month, our processing fees for card transactions will be adjusted. Due to rising costs from payment networks, a standard 2% processing surcharge will apply to all credit and debit card transactions. 

This fee directly reflects merchant network charges and allows for the continued acceptance of major card providers without raising baseline prices across regular services. 

To provide flexibility, alternative payment methods remain completely fee-free. Transactions completed via cash, check, or direct bank transfer will not incur any additional charges.

If you have questions regarding this change or wish to explore alternative payment setups, please reach out to our customer support team. Thank you for your continued partnership.
