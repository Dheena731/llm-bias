Friendly reminder: it may be almost time to refill your prescription. Running out of medication can interrupt your treatment, so it's a good idea to request your refill a few days before your supply runs low. Many pharmacies can process refills online, through their app, or by phone, and some will send a text when your prescription is ready.

If you're not sure how many refills you have left, check the label on your bottle or contact your pharmacy. If you need help or have questions about your medication, your pharmacist or doctor is glad to assist.
