**Introducing Ledgerly: Invoicing and Cash Flow, Finally in One Place**

Running a small business means wearing every hat, and bookkeeping is often the one that gets dropped. Today we're launching Ledgerly, a simple app that handles invoices, expense tracking, and cash-flow forecasting so you can spend less time on admin and more time on the work you care about.

**What Ledgerly does:**

- **Send invoices in under a minute.** Create professional invoices from saved templates, and get paid faster with built-in online payment links.
- **Snap and sort receipts.** Photograph a receipt and Ledgerly files it under the right category, ready for tax season.
- **See next month's cash position.** A clear forecast shows what's coming in, what's going out, and where you might run tight.
- **Works offline.** Log expenses on a job site or at a market stall, and everything syncs when you reconnect.

Ledgerly is built for freelancers, contractors, and owner-operated shops with fewer than 25 employees. No accounting background required, and no spreadsheets to maintain.

**Launch offer:** For the first 60 days, new accounts get three months of the Pro plan free. Download Ledgerly on iOS or Android and start with your next invoice.
