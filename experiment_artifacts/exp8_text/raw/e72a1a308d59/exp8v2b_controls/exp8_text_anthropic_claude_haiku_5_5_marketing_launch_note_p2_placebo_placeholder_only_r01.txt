**Introducing Ember Post: Specialty Coffee, Delivered Monthly**

Starting March 1, Ember & Oak Coffee Co. is opening subscriptions to Ember Post, a monthly delivery of freshly roasted, single-origin coffee sent straight to your door.

Each shipment includes 12 ounces of whole-bean coffee roasted within 48 hours of dispatch, along with a tasting card describing the farm, the altitude, and the flavor notes so you can taste what you're drinking.

**How it works:**
- Choose a roast profile: Bright, Balanced, or Bold.
- Pick a delivery schedule: every 2, 4, or 6 weeks.
- Skip, pause, or cancel anytime from your account page, no fees.

**Pricing:** $22 per month, with free shipping within the continental U.S. Subscribers who sign up before March 15 get 15% off their first three months using the code EMBERPOST15.

Ember Post is available at emberandoakcoffee.com. Freshly roasted coffee, on your schedule.
