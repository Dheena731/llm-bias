**Notice: How to Report a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If you lose a company laptop or believe it has been stolen, report it immediately. Prompt reporting helps us protect company data and reduce security risks.

**What to do:**

1. **Contact IT Support right away** at [phone number] or [email address], ideally within one hour of noticing the loss.
2. **Report to Security** if the device was stolen or lost off company premises, and file a police report if required by local policy.
3. **Provide details**, including the laptop's asset tag, serial number, last known location, and the time you last had it.
4. **Do not attempt to access or recover data** yourself. IT will remotely lock or wipe the device as needed.
5. **Complete the Lost Equipment Form** within 24 hours, available on [intranet link].

Failure to report a lost device promptly may result in disciplinary action under company policy.

For questions, contact [Department Name] at [contact information].
