**Tip: Treat herbs like cut flowers (or wrap them like produce).**

For tender herbs like cilantro, parsley, and dill, trim the stems and stand the bunch in a jar with an inch of water. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and refrigerate. Change the water every few days, and they'll stay fresh for up to two weeks.

For sturdier herbs like rosemary, thyme, and sage, wrap them in a slightly damp paper towel, tuck them into a resealable bag or container, and store them in the fridge.

Basil is the exception: keep it on the counter in a jar of water, away from direct sun, since cold temperatures turn its leaves black.
