**Weeknight Dinner Tip: Prep Once, Cook Fast**

When you get home from the grocery store (or on a calmer evening), spend 15 minutes chopping vegetables, washing greens, and mixing a simple all-purpose sauce, like garlic, soy sauce, lemon juice, and olive oil. Store everything in separate containers in the fridge.

On a busy night, you can toss protein and veggies on a sheet pan, roast at 425°F for 20 to 25 minutes, and drizzle the sauce over the top. Dinner is done with almost no active effort.

**Bonus:** Cut vegetables into similar sizes so they cook evenly, and give them space on the pan. Crowded veggies steam instead of getting crisp and caramelized.
