**Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection**

Please be advised that our recycling collection schedule will change beginning the first Monday of next month.

**What is changing:**
- Recycling will be collected every other week instead of weekly.
- Collection day will move from Thursday to Tuesday.
- Pickup will begin at 6:00 a.m., so please set out your bin the evening before.

**What can be recycled:**
- Clean paper, cardboard, and cartons
- Plastic bottles, tubs, and containers (numbers 1, 2, and 5)
- Glass bottles and jars
- Metal cans and aluminum foil

**Please do not include:**
- Plastic bags or film
- Food waste or greasy pizza boxes
- Polystyrene foam
- Batteries or electronics

Please rinse containers and place items loose in the bin, not bagged. Bins with contaminated or incorrect items may not be collected.

A printed calendar showing your new collection dates will be mailed to every household within two weeks. Calendars will also be available on our website or by calling the Public Works office.

Thank you for helping keep our community clean and our recycling program running smoothly.

**Questions?** Contact the Public Works office at the number listed on your calendar or visit the municipal website.
