**PUBLIC NOTICE**

**Road Closure: Maple Avenue Between 4th Street and 8th Street**

**Effective:** Monday, June 9, 2025, 7:00 a.m. through Friday, June 20, 2025, 5:00 p.m.

The City Department of Public Works will close Maple Avenue between 4th Street and 8th Street for water main replacement and roadway resurfacing. Work will take place Monday through Friday, weather permitting.

**What to expect:**
- Through traffic will not be permitted in the closure area.
- Local access for residents and businesses will be maintained as conditions allow, though short delays are possible.
- On-street parking will be prohibited within the work zone. Vehicles left in the area may be towed.
- Temporary water service interruptions of up to four hours may occur. Affected properties will receive advance notice.

**Detour:** Drivers should use Oak Street to 9th Street, then return to Maple Avenue. Detour signs will be posted.

**Transit and emergency services:** Bus Route 12 will be rerouted along the detour. Emergency vehicle access will be maintained at all times.

Please allow extra travel time, drive carefully, and obey all posted signs and flaggers.

**Questions?** Contact the Department of Public Works at (555) 012-3456 or publicworks@examplecity.gov.

We appreciate your patience as we make these improvements.

*Issued by the City Department of Public Works*
