**Important: Protect Yourself from Phishing Emails**

To all employees,

We have recently seen an increase in phishing emails being sent to our organization. These emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information, such as your login credentials or financial information.

To protect yourself and our organization, please be cautious when receiving emails that:

* Ask you to click on suspicious links or download attachments
* Request sensitive information, such as passwords or credit card numbers
* Create a sense of urgency or threaten consequences if you don't respond immediately

If you receive an email that you suspect is a phishing attempt, do not respond or click on any links. Instead, report it to our IT department immediately.

Remember, our organization will never ask you to reveal sensitive information via email. If you are unsure about the legitimacy of an email, it's always best to err on the side of caution and verify its authenticity before taking any action.

Thank you for your vigilance and help in keeping our organization's information secure.
