**Podcast Title:** The Fluent Frame  
**Episode 01:** Speaking Without Stress  
**Release Date:** November 12, 2024  

Welcome to the launch episode of The Fluent Frame, a podcast built to help learners develop real conversational ability without the pressure of perfect grammar. In this episode, host Elena Rostova explains why rote memorization rarely translates to fluency and introduces a structured approach to turning passive knowledge into active speech.

Inside this episode, you will find:
- Three daily routines to train your brain to think in your target language
- Authentic audio samples from street interviews, café orders, and transit announcements
- Practical strategies for recovering smoothly when you lose your words mid-sentence

Each weekly episode combines targeted listening practice, cultural notes, and step-by-step speaking exercises tailored for intermediate learners who are ready to move beyond textbooks.
