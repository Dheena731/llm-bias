PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE

Main Street will be closed to all vehicle traffic between 1st Avenue and 5th Avenue from 7:00 AM to 4:00 PM on Saturday, March 15, to allow for scheduled water line repairs.

Detour: Traffic will be routed via Oak Street and Elm Road. Signs will be posted at all affected intersections.

Access: Pedestrians will be able to cross the work zone. Residents and businesses along the closed section will have access to their properties through the work zone, but please allow extra travel time.

Emergency vehicles will have access at all times.

For questions, contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567. Thank you for your patience.
