**Notice to the Public: Library Hours**

The Public Library invites all residents to visit and make full use of our services. Our current opening hours are:

- **Monday to Friday:** 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
- **Saturday:** 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- **Sunday:** Closed

Please note that hours may change on public holidays and during special events. For the most current schedule, visit our website or call the front desk.

The library offers books, newspapers, computer access, free Wi-Fi, and study spaces for people of all ages. Membership is free for all residents. Stop by and see what we have to offer.
