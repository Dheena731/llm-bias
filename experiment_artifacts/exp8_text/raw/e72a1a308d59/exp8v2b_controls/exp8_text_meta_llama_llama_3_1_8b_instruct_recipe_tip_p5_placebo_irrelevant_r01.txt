Here's a short tip for seasoning roasted vegetables:

**Add depth with acidity and umami**

When seasoning roasted vegetables, don't forget to balance their natural sweetness with a pinch of acidity and a dash of umami. A squeeze of fresh lemon juice or a sprinkle of balsamic glaze can enhance the flavors of roasted vegetables like Brussels sprouts, carrots, and sweet potatoes. Meanwhile, a teaspoon of soy sauce or miso paste can add depth and richness to vegetables like broccoli, cauliflower, and parsnips.
