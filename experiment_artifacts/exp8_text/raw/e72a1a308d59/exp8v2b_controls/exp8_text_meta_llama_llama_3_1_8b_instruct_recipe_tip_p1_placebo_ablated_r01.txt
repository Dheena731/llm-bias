Here's a short cooking tip for preparing a weeknight dinner:

**Quick and Easy One-Pot Pasta**

When cooking a weeknight dinner, consider using a one-pot approach to save time and reduce cleanup. For a delicious and satisfying meal, try making a simple pasta dish using just one pot. Cook your pasta, such as penne or rotini, directly in a large saucepan with some oil, garlic, and your favorite sauce. This will not only speed up the cooking process but also allow you to easily combine all the flavors and textures in one dish.

To get started, fill a large saucepan with salted water, but before adding the pasta, heat a tablespoon of olive oil over medium heat. Add minced garlic and sauté for about a minute until fragrant. Then, add your pasta and a cup of your favorite sauce, such as marinara or alfredo. Stir well to combine and cook for about 8-10 minutes or until the pasta is al dente and the sauce is heated through. Serve hot and enjoy!

This tip is perfect for busy weeknights when you need a quick and easy meal that can be prepared in no more than 20 minutes. By using a one-pot approach, you'll save time on cleanup and can focus on enjoying a delicious meal with your family.
