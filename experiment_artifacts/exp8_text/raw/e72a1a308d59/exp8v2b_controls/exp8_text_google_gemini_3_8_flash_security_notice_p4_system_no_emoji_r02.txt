**Subject: Action Required: Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**

To enhance the security of your account and protect your personal information, all users are required to enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA). 

2FA adds an essential layer of defense by requiring a secondary verification method in addition to your password, significantly reducing the risk of unauthorized access.

**How to set it up:**
1. Log in to your account.
2. Navigate to **Account Settings** and select **Security**.
3. Locate **Two-Factor Authentication** and follow the on-screen instructions to link your preferred verification method (authenticator app or SMS).
