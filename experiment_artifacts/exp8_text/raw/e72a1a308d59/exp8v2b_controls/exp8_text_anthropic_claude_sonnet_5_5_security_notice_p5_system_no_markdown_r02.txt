NOTICE: REPORTING A LOST OR STOLEN COMPANY LAPTOP

Effective immediately, all employees must follow the steps below if a company-issued laptop is lost or stolen.

Report the loss right away. Do not wait to see if the device turns up. Notify the IT Service Desk by phone at ext. 4357 or by email at itsupport@company.com within one hour of discovering the loss. If the loss happens outside business hours, call the 24-hour security line at ext. 4911.

When you report, please provide your name and department, the laptop's asset tag or serial number if known, the date, time, and location where it was last seen, and a brief description of the circumstances. Also note whether the device held sensitive or confidential data, such as customer records or financial files.

If the laptop was stolen, file a report with local police and send a copy of the report number to IT Security. Do not attempt to recover the device yourself.

Once a report is received, IT will remotely lock and wipe the device, disable your account credentials, and begin a security review. You may be asked to change passwords for any systems you accessed from the laptop.

Prompt reporting protects company data, our clients, and you. Employees who report quickly will be supported through the process. Failure to report a lost device in a timely manner may result in disciplinary action under the company's Acceptable Use Policy.

Questions about this notice may be directed to the IT Service Desk.

Thank you for your cooperation.

IT Security Department
