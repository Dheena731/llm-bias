**Weekly Fitness Check-In** 💪

It's time to review your progress! Take a few minutes to look back on your week:

- **Workouts completed:** Did you hit your goals?
- **Wins:** What went well, big or small?
- **Challenges:** What got in your way, and how can you adjust?
- **Next week:** Set one or two realistic targets.

Progress isn't always linear, so celebrate the effort you put in. Consistency beats perfection!
