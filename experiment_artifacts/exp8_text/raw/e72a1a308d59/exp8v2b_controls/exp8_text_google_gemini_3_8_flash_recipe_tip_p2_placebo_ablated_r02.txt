To keep fresh herbs vibrant for longer, store them based on their stem type:

* **Soft herbs (such as parsley and cilantro):** Trim the bottom of the stems and place the bunch upright in a small jar with about an inch of water, like a bouquet of flowers. Cover the leaves loosely with a plastic bag and place the jar in the refrigerator. (*Note: Treat basil the same way, but keep it on the counter at room temperature, as cold air will turn its leaves black.*)
* **Woody herbs (such as rosemary and thyme):** Wrap the sprigs loosely in a slightly damp paper towel, then place them inside a sealed plastic bag or airtight container in the refrigerator to keep them from drying out.
