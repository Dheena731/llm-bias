Hi there! This is a friendly reminder to take a few minutes this week to review your fitness progress.

Look over your workouts, your distance or weight goals, and how you have felt during your sessions. Notice what is going well and what might need a change. Even small improvements are worth recognizing.

Set aside a quick moment at the end of the week to check your progress, and then plan one or two goals for the week ahead.

Keep up the great work!
