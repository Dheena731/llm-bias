To make a quick and delicious weeknight dinner, consider preparing a one-pot meal. One-pot meals, such as skillet dinners or stir-fries, can be cooked in under 30 minutes and typically require minimal cleanup. Start by selecting a protein, like chicken or tofu, and a variety of colorful vegetables, such as bell peppers, carrots, and broccoli. Then, choose a simple sauce or seasoning, like soy sauce or Italian seasoning, to add flavor to your dish. Cook the ingredients in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat, stirring frequently, until the protein is cooked through and the vegetables are tender. Serve hot, garnished with fresh herbs or a squeeze of lemon juice, and enjoy a stress-free and satisfying weeknight dinner.
