Recycling Collection Changes

Beginning March 1st, recycling collection days will change for all residents. The new schedule is as follows:

* Monday routes will now collect recyclables on Thursdays
* Tuesday routes will now collect recyclables on Fridays
* Wednesday routes will remain the same
* Thursday routes will now collect recyclables on Mondays
* Friday routes will now collect recyclables on Tuesdays

Please note that collection times may also vary. To confirm your specific collection day and time, please visit our website or contact the waste management department.

We appreciate your cooperation as we work to improve our recycling services.
