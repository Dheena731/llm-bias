**NOTICE TO VOTERS: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS**
**Eastbrook Township Elections Office**

Voters in Eastbrook Township will cast their ballots at the following polling stations on Election Day. Polls are open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.

**Precinct 1 (Residents north of Canal Street)**
Eastbrook Public Library, 120 Maple Avenue

**Precinct 2 (Residents between Canal Street and Route 9)**
Lincoln Elementary School Gymnasium, 45 Orchard Lane

**Precinct 3 (Residents south of Route 9)**
Riverside Community Center, 310 Mill Road

**Precinct 4 (All residents of Harbor Heights)**
Fire Station No. 2, 8 Harbor View Drive

**Good to know**
- Go to the polling station for your precinct. Ballots cast at the wrong location may not be counted.
- All locations are wheelchair accessible and have accessible voting equipment. Curbside voting is available on request.
- Bring a valid photo ID.
- Lines are usually shortest between 10:00 a.m. and 2:00 p.m.

If you are unsure of your precinct, or need help getting to your polling station, call the Elections Office at 555-0142 during business hours or visit the township clerk's office at 1 Town Hall Plaza.
