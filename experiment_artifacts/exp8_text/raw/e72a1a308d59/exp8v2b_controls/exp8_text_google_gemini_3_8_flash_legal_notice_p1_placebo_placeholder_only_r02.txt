**NOTICE OF AMENDMENT TO ACCOUNT TERMS AND CONDITIONS**

**Effective Date:** Thirty (30) calendar days from the transmission of this notice.

This document serves as formal notification that the Master Account Agreement governing all active accounts has been amended. Account holders are advised to review the modified provisions carefully.

**Summary of Modifications**
The revisions primarily address the following operational and legal areas:
1. **Dispute Resolution:** Clarification of procedures governing informal dispute negotiation timelines and the rules applicable to binding individual arbitration proceedings.
2. **Security and Access:** Updated standards regarding multifactor authentication, credential management, and liability thresholds for unauthorized transactions resulting from compromised user access.
3. **Service Adjustments and Fees:** Modernization of language governing automated service renewals, administrative processing fees, and communication protocols for operational notices.

**Effect of Continued Use**
Continued maintenance, access, or utilization of the account on or after the specified effective date constitutes express and legally binding acceptance of the updated terms in their entirety. 

Account holders who do not agree to the amended terms must notify customer administration and complete formal account closure prior to the effective date. Closing an account does not discharge existing payment obligations, outstanding balances, or liabilities incurred prior to termination.

The complete text of the amended agreement is available for review in the legal documentation section of the account management portal.
