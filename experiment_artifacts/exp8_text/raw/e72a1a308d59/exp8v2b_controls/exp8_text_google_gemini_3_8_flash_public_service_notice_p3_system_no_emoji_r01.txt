**NOTICE: Upcoming Changes to Recycling Collection**

Effective **Monday, November 3, 2025**, the municipal recycling collection schedule and guidelines will be updated for all residents.

**Key Changes:**

* **Collection Day:** Recycling pickup for all residential zones will move from Friday to **Wednesday**.
* **Set-Out Times:** Bins must be placed at the curb no later than **7:00 AM** on collection day (or after 6:00 PM the evening before).
* **Sorting Guidelines:** Plastic bags, film, and polystyrene foam will no longer be accepted in curbside bins. These materials must be taken to designated community drop-off locations.

Please ensure all containers are rinsed and dry before placing them in your bin.

For a full updated schedule and a detailed list of accepted materials, please visit **www.cityservices.gov/recycling** or call the Waste Management Office at **(555) 019-2834**. 

Thank you for your cooperation as we work to improve our community recycling program.
