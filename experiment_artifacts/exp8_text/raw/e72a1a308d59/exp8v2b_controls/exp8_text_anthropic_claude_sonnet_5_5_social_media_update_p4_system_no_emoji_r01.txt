Calendar tip: Stop using it only for meetings. Block out time for the tasks you actually need to finish.

When "write the report" or "reply to emails" gets its own slot, it stops living in the back of your mind and starts getting done. Treat those blocks like appointments you can't skip.

Bonus: Add buffer time between events. Even 10 minutes keeps one running-late meeting from wrecking your whole day.
