**Reminder: Stay Hydrated**

Remember to drink enough water throughout the day. Water supports your energy, concentration, digestion, and overall health.

**Quick tips:**
- Start your morning with a glass of water.
- Keep a water bottle within reach and sip regularly.
- Drink a glass with each meal.
- Don't wait until you feel thirsty. Thirst can be a sign you're already low on fluids.
- Drink more when it's hot or when you're exercising.

A good check is your urine color: pale yellow usually means you're well hydrated, while darker yellow suggests you need more water.

Needs vary from person to person, so if you have a medical condition or have been given specific fluid guidance by your doctor, follow their advice.
