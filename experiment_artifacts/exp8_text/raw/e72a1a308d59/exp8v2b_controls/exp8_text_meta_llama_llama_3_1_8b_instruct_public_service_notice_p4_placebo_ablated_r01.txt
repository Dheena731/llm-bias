Here is a clear and concise public notice about a heat advisory:

**HEAT ADVISORY ISSUED FOR [CITY/REGION]**

A heat advisory has been issued for the [City/Region] area due to a prolonged period of hot weather. Temperatures are expected to reach the mid-90s to low 100s (Fahrenheit) over the next 48 hours, with heat indices making it feel even hotter.

**TAKE PRECAUTIONS TO STAY SAFE**

The elderly, young children, and people with pre-existing medical conditions are especially vulnerable to heat-related illnesses. To protect yourself and your loved ones, take the following precautions:

* Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water and other fluids
* Avoid strenuous outdoor activities during the hottest part of the day (usually between 11am and 3pm)
* Take regular breaks in cool, shaded areas or air-conditioned spaces
* Check on vulnerable neighbors and friends to ensure they are staying safe

**HEAT-RELATED ILLNESSES CAN BE DEADLY**

Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are serious medical conditions that can occur when the body is unable to cool itself properly. If you or someone you know is experiencing symptoms such as heavy sweating, nausea, headaches, or confusion, seek medical attention immediately.

**STAY INFORMED**

For updates on the heat advisory and any additional information, please check our website or social media channels.
