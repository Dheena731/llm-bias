**Notice: What to Do If Your Company Laptop Is Lost or Stolen**

**To all employees:**

If you cannot find your company laptop, report it to the IT Help Desk **immediately**, even if you are not sure it is lost. Quick reporting lets us lock the device and protect company and customer information.

**How to report**
- Phone: [IT Help Desk number] (available [hours])
- Email: [IT Help Desk email]
- Outside those hours, email the address above and notify your manager.

**Please include**
- Your name and department
- The laptop's asset tag or serial number, if you know it
- When and where you last had it
- Whether you think it was lost or stolen
- What kind of work files or data were stored on it, if you know

**Next steps**
1. IT will remotely lock the laptop and disable your access to company accounts on it.
2. If the laptop was stolen, also report it to the police and give IT the report number.
3. Change your company password from another device if IT asks you to.
4. Do not try to recover the laptop yourself or let anyone else use it if it is found. Contact IT first.

Reporting a lost laptop promptly will not be treated as a disciplinary matter. Delays in reporting put company and customer information at risk.
