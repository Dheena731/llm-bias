Recycling Collection Changes

Effective immediately, recycling collection days will be changing. Residents are advised to check the new schedule, which is available on the city's website or by contacting the public works department. The new collection schedule is as follows: 

Monday: Paper and cardboard
Tuesday: Glass and plastic
Wednesday: Metal and organic materials

Please ensure that all recyclable materials are properly sorted and placed at the curb by 7am on the designated collection day. For more information, please contact the public works department at 555-1234.
