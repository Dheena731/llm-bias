We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you keep track of your expenses. This summary will provide a breakdown of your spending by category, making it easier to identify areas where you can cut back and make adjustments to stay within your budget. The summary will be sent to you on the 1st of every month and will include details on your total spending, average daily spending, and top expense categories. This new feature is designed to provide you with a clear and concise overview of your spending habits, allowing you to make more informed financial decisions.
