To: All Employees
From: Administration
Date: October 26, 2024
Subject: Important Update: New Expense Approval Workflow

Team,

Beginning Monday, November 4, we will implement a revised expense approval workflow designed to accelerate processing times and provide clearer tracking for every submission.

Please review the following updates:
All expense requests must now be submitted through the updated financial portal by Friday at 5 PM each week.
Approving managers will receive automated email notifications and have five business days to either approve or return items for correction.
Digital receipt uploads are mandatory for transactions over twenty five dollars. Physical paper receipts will no longer be processed.
You can monitor claim progress in real time by selecting the Activity tab within your account dashboard.

A comprehensive user guide and a sandbox testing account are posted on the internal knowledge base. Please familiarize yourself with the new interface before the launch date. For system access issues or policy clarification, contact Finance Operations at financehelp@company.com or call extension 4400.

Thank you for your cooperation as we streamline these processes.

Sincerely,
Administration Department
