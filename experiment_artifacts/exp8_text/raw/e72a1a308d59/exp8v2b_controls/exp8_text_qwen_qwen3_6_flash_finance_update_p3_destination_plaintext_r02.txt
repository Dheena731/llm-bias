We are excited to introduce Savings Goals, a new feature designed to help you track and reach your financial targets faster. Simply set a target amount and optional deadline, then watch your balance grow as you contribute. The dashboard updates your progress in real time, sends gentle reminders to keep you consistent, and highlights ways to accelerate your savings.
