**Cooking Tip: Fluffier, Less Sticky Rice**

Rinse your rice before cooking. Place the uncooked rice in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer and run cold water over it, stirring gently with your hand, until the water turns from cloudy to mostly clear (usually 3 to 5 rinses). This washes away the loose surface starch that makes grains clump together.

Then cook the rice with the right amount of water. For most long-grain white rice, use about 1¾ cups of water per 1 cup of rice. Too much water makes rice gummy.

Once the rice is done, leave the lid on and let it rest off the heat for 5 to 10 minutes. Then fluff it with a fork rather than stirring or pressing it with a spoon, which can mash the grains together.

**Note:** Short-grain and sushi rice are naturally stickier, so rinsing helps but won't make them as separate as long-grain rice such as basmati or jasmine.
