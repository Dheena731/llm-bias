**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

A Heat Advisory is in effect for our area. Hot temperatures combined with high humidity will make it feel much hotter than the actual air temperature. Prolonged exposure can cause heat exhaustion or heat stroke.

**Who is most at risk:** Older adults, infants and young children, people with chronic health conditions, outdoor workers, and pets.

**How to stay safe:**
- Drink plenty of water, even if you don't feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and sugary drinks.
- Stay in air-conditioned spaces when possible. Libraries, community centers, and shopping centers are good options if your home is not cool.
- Limit outdoor activity to early morning or evening hours, and take frequent breaks in the shade.
- Wear lightweight, light-colored, loose-fitting clothing and use sunscreen.
- Never leave children, pets, or anyone else in a parked vehicle, even for a moment.
- Check on neighbors, relatives, and friends who live alone.

**Know the warning signs:**
- *Heat exhaustion:* heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, nausea, headache, cold or clammy skin. Move to a cool place, loosen clothing, and sip water.
- *Heat stroke:* body temperature of 103°F or higher, hot and dry skin, confusion, or loss of consciousness. **Call 911 immediately** and cool the person with whatever means are available until help arrives.

Stay informed by monitoring local weather forecasts and official emergency announcements. Please share this notice with those who may not see it.
