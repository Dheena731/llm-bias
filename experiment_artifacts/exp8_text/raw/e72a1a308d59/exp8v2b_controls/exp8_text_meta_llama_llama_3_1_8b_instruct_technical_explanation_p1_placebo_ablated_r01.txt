Latency matters in web applications because it directly impacts the user experience. When a user interacts with a website or web application, they expect a prompt response. Even a brief delay can be frustrating, causing users to abandon the task or lose interest.

In today's fast-paced digital world, users are accustomed to instant results. Delays can erode trust and lead to negative perceptions of the website or application. Furthermore, prolonged latency can result in missed opportunities, such as:

*   Cart abandonment due to slow checkout processes
*   Reduced engagement with interactive content
*   Decreased productivity in collaborative environments

Web applications with low latency are more likely to retain users and achieve their goals.
