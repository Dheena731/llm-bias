**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**

To help protect your account from unauthorized access, we strongly encourage all users to enable two-factor authentication. This adds a second verification step when you sign in, so a stolen password alone is not enough to get into your account.

**How to enable 2FA:**

1. Sign in and go to Account Settings.
2. Select Security, then Two-Factor Authentication.
3. Choose your preferred method: an authenticator app, text message, or hardware security key.
4. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm your setup.
5. Save your backup codes in a secure location.

**Recommendations:**

- Use an authenticator app or security key when possible, as these are more secure than text messages.
- Keep your backup codes somewhere safe, separate from your password.
- Update your recovery email and phone number so you can regain access if needed.

Setup takes only a few minutes. Please complete it by the end of the month. If you need help, contact our support team.

Thank you for helping keep your account secure.
