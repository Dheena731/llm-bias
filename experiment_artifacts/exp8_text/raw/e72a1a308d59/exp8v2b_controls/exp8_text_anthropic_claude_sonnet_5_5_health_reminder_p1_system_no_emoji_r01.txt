**Weekly Fitness Check-In**

It's time to review your progress for the week. Take a few minutes to look back at your workouts, activity levels, and goals.

- **Workouts completed:** Did you hit your target for the week?
- **Milestones:** Note any personal bests or improvements.
- **Challenges:** Identify what got in the way, and think about how to adjust.
- **Next week:** Set one or two clear goals to focus on.

Consistency matters more than perfection. Review your stats in the app and plan your next steps.
