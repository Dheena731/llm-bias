One of the most effective ways to streamline your weeknight dinner prep is to focus on one-pot meals. Choose a recipe that can be cooked entirely in one pot, such as a hearty pasta dish, a stir-fry, or a skillet dinner. This approach not only reduces cleanup, but also allows you to cook multiple ingredients at once, saving you time and effort. Simply chop your ingredients, add them to the pot, and let the dish simmer while you attend to other tasks, making it easy to get a delicious meal on the table in no time.
