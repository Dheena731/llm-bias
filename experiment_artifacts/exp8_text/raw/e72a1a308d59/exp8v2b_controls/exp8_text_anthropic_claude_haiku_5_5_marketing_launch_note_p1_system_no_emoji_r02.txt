**Introducing Tallyhub: Run Your Small Business From Your Phone**

We're excited to announce the launch of Tallyhub, a simple all-in-one app built for small business owners who are tired of juggling spreadsheets, sticky notes, and five different tools.

Tallyhub brings the essentials into one place:

- **Invoicing and payments:** Send professional invoices in seconds and get paid faster.
- **Expense tracking:** Snap a photo of any receipt and let Tallyhub categorize it for you.
- **Customer management:** Keep contact details, job history, and notes organized for every client.
- **Simple reports:** See where your money is going and what's coming in, without an accounting degree.

Tallyhub is designed for people who run businesses, not software. Setup takes about five minutes, and there are no contracts or long-term commitments. The first 30 days are free.

**Get started today** at [tallyhub.app](https://tallyhub.app) or download the app from the App Store or Google Play.

*Tallyhub is built for freelancers, contractors, salons, retailers, and any small business that wants more time for the work it loves.*
