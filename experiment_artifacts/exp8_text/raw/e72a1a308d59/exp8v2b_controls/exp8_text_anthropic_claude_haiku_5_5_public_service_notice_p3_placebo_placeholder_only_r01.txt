**Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection Schedule**

Starting next Monday, recycling collection will move from every week to every other week in most neighborhoods. Trash collection will continue on its regular weekly schedule.

**What you need to know:**

- **Recycling pickup:** Every other week, on the same weekday you currently use.
- **Accepted items:** Paper, cardboard, glass bottles and jars, metal cans, and plastic bottles and containers with necks. Please keep all items clean and dry.
- **Bin placement:** Place recycling bins at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on collection day. Remove bins from the curb by the end of the day.
- **Bags:** Do not place recyclables in plastic bags. Loose items go directly into the bin.

**Why the change?**

This adjustment helps us manage costs while keeping our recycling program running efficiently. Residents who need extra capacity can request a larger recycling cart at no charge through the public works office.

**Questions?**

Contact the public works department during business hours for your collection dates or to request a larger cart.
