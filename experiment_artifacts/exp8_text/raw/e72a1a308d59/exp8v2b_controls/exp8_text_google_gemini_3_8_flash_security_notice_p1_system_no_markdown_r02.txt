SECURITY NOTICE: THE RISKS OF PASSWORD REUSE

To all employees,

Please remember that using the same password for multiple accounts is one of the most common causes of data breaches. 

When you reuse your work password for personal accounts, social media, or other external services, you put our entire organization at risk. If any of those third-party services suffer a security breach, attackers can easily use your exposed credentials to gain unauthorized access to our company systems and sensitive data.

To keep our workplace secure, please follow these guidelines:

1. Use a unique password for your work account that is completely different from any personal password.
2. Never share your credentials or write them down where others can see them.
3. Use a company-approved password manager to generate and securely store complex passwords.
4. Ensure multi-factor authentication (MFA) is active on your accounts.

If you suspect your password has been exposed in an external breach, change it immediately and contact the IT Help Desk.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our data safe.

IT Security Team
