Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection

Starting Monday, March 3, recycling collection will move to every other week for all residential addresses. General waste collection will remain weekly.

Collection days will stay the same. Please check your collection calendar to confirm your recycling week.

What you need to know:
- Place recycling bins out by 7:00 a.m. on your scheduled collection day.
- Rinse containers and flatten cardboard before placing them in the bin.
- Do not bag recyclable items in plastic bags.
- Items that are not accepted will be left behind and tagged.

Accepted items: paper, cardboard, glass bottles and jars, metal cans, and rigid plastics marked with recycling numbers 1 and 2.

For questions or a full list of accepted materials, contact the Waste Services Office at 555-0123 or visit our website.

Thank you for helping us keep recycling clean and effective.
