NOTICE: SCREEN LOCK REMINDER

Dear Team,

Please remember to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even for a brief moment. This simple action protects sensitive company data and ensures compliance with our security policies.

Quick locking instructions:
For Windows computers, press the Windows key plus L.
For Mac computers, press Command plus Control plus Q.
