**Important Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection**

Effective immediately, the recycling collection schedule in our community will be changing. Instead of weekly collection, recyclables will now be collected every other week. This change aims to improve the efficiency of the recycling program and reduce costs.

To ensure a smooth transition, please check the new collection calendar, which is available on our website or at the local town hall. You can also contact our waste management department directly to confirm your new collection schedule.

Remember to continue sorting your recyclables properly and placing them at the curb on the designated collection day. Your cooperation and participation in the recycling program are crucial to its success. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
