Here is a short tip for seasoning roasted vegetables:

**Add Flavor with a Balance of Acidity and Umami**

When seasoning roasted vegetables, remember that a pinch of acidity can elevate the overall flavor. Consider adding a squeeze of fresh lemon juice or a splash of vinegar to your seasoning blend, as this will help to balance the richness of the roasted vegetables.

At the same time, don't forget to incorporate a source of umami flavor, such as soy sauce, miso paste, or mushrooms, to add depth and savory goodness. This will help to bring out the natural sweetness of the vegetables and create a harmonious flavor profile.

By balancing acidity and umami, you'll create a seasoning blend that complements the natural flavors of your roasted vegetables, rather than overpowering them. Experiment with different combinations to find the perfect balance for your taste buds.
