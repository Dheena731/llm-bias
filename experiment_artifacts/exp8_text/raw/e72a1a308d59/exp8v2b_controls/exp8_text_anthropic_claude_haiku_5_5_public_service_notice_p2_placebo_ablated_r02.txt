PUBLIC NOTICE: Road Closure

Main Street will be closed to all vehicle traffic between Oak Avenue and Pine Street from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on Saturday, June 14, to allow for repaving work.

Drivers should use Elm Street or Second Avenue as alternate routes. Local residents and business owners may still access their properties from either end of the closed section, but expect delays. Pedestrians can use the sidewalks, which will remain open throughout the work.

Questions can be directed to the City Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567. Thank you for your patience.
