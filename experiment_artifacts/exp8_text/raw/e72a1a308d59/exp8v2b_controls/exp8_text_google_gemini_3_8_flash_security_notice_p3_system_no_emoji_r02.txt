**Subject: Security Reminder: Lock Your Screens**

Please remember to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if it is only for a brief moment. Leaving workstations unlocked puts sensitive company and client data at risk.

Quick shortcuts to lock your screen:
* **Windows:** Press **Windows Key + L**
* **Mac:** Press **Control + Command + Q**

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our workplace secure.

IT & Information Security Team
