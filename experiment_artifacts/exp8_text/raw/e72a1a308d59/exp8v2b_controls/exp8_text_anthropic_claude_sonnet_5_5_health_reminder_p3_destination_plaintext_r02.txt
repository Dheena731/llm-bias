Reminder: Book your annual health checkup

It's time to schedule your yearly physical. Regular checkups help catch potential issues early and keep you on track with your health goals.

To do:
- Call or go online to book an appointment with your doctor
- Check your insurance coverage and any pre-visit requirements (such as fasting for bloodwork)
- Make a list of questions or concerns to bring with you
- Add the appointment to your calendar

Take a few minutes today to get it on the books. Your future self will thank you!
