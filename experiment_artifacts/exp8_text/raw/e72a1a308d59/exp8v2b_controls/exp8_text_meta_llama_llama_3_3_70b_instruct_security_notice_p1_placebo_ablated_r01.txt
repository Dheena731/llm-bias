**Important Security Notice**

To all employees,

As part of our ongoing efforts to protect company data and systems, we want to remind everyone of the importance of password security. Specifically, we urge you to avoid reusing passwords across multiple accounts, both personal and professional.

Using the same password for multiple accounts can put our organization's security at risk. If a password is compromised in one system, an attacker may try to use it to gain access to other accounts, including those used for company business.

To minimize this risk, please use unique and strong passwords for each of your work-related accounts. This includes your computer login, email, and any other systems or applications you use as part of your job.

If you have any questions or concerns about password security, or if you need help creating strong and unique passwords, please don't hesitate to reach out to our IT department.

Thank you for your attention to this important matter, and for helping to keep our workplace and data secure.
