**Public Notice: Heat Advisory**

A heat advisory has been issued for our area due to extremely high temperatures and humidity expected over the next 48 hours. Temperatures are forecasted to reach up to 100°F (38°C) with a heat index making it feel even hotter.

We urge all residents, especially vulnerable individuals such as the elderly, young children, and those with pre-existing medical conditions, to take precautions to stay safe during this period.

To protect yourself and your loved ones, please:

* Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water
* Stay indoors during the hottest part of the day (11am-3pm)
* Avoid strenuous outdoor activities
* Check on vulnerable neighbors and family members

If you or someone you know is experiencing heat-related symptoms such as dizziness, nausea, or headaches, please seek medical attention immediately.

Stay cool and stay safe!
