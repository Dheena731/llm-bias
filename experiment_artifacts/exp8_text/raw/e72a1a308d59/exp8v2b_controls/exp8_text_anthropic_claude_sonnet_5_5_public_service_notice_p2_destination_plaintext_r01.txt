PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE

Maple Street will be closed to all vehicle traffic between 4th Avenue and 8th Avenue for road resurfacing.

Dates: Monday, June 3 through Friday, June 7
Hours: 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. each day

Details:
- Local residents will have access to their driveways until work begins on their block. Please move vehicles from the street before 7:00 a.m.
- Pedestrian access will be maintained where it is safe to do so.
- Emergency vehicles will have access at all times.
- Detour: Traffic will be directed along Oak Street and 9th Avenue. Please allow extra travel time.

Work is weather dependent. If rain delays construction, the closure may extend through Saturday, June 8.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience as we work to improve our roads.

For questions or updates, contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567 or visit the city website.

Issued by: City Department of Public Works
