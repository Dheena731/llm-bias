Here's a short cooking tip for preparing a weeknight dinner:

**Tip: Prep Ahead for a Stress-Free Weeknight Dinner**

To save time and reduce stress during the week, prep your protein and veggies ahead of time. For example, slice your chicken breast or prep your veggies for a stir-fry. Store them in the fridge until you're ready to cook. This simple step can shave 30 minutes to an hour off your cooking time and make a big difference in your weeknight dinner routine.
