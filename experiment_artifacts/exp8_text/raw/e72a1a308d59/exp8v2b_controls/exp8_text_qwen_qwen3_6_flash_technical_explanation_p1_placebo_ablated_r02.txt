Latency is the delay between when a user interacts with a web application and when they see the result. It matters because human perception of digital speed is highly sensitive to even millisecond differences. When responses lag, users perceive the application as sluggish or broken, which increases frustration and causes them to abandon their tasks. This directly impacts real-world outcomes: higher latency consistently reduces engagement, lowers conversion rates, and decreases revenue. On the technical side, prolonged delays can trigger browser and server timeouts, force systems to waste resources on repeated connection attempts, and degrade overall stability under load. Minimizing latency is therefore not merely a performance optimization; it is a foundational requirement for delivering a reliable experience that keeps users engaged and supports business objectives.
