Reminder: Book Your Annual Health Checkup

It's time to schedule your yearly health checkup. Regular exams help catch potential issues early and keep you on track with your health goals.

To-do:
- Call or go online to book an appointment with your doctor
- Check when your last checkup was and whether any screenings or vaccinations are due
- Write down any questions or concerns to bring with you
- Confirm whether you need to fast beforehand

Try to book within the next week so you can get a convenient time slot. Your future self will thank you!
