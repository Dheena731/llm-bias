Here is a short cooking tip for preparing a weeknight dinner:

**Tip: Prepare your protein ahead of time**

When cooking a weeknight dinner, save time by seasoning and marinating your protein (such as chicken, beef, or pork) ahead of time. This can be done as early as 30 minutes before cooking, or even the night before by placing it in the refrigerator. Simply place the protein in a shallow dish or zip-top plastic bag, add your desired seasonings and marinade, and refrigerate until ready to cook. This allows the flavors to penetrate the meat, reducing cooking time and resulting in a more flavorful dish.
