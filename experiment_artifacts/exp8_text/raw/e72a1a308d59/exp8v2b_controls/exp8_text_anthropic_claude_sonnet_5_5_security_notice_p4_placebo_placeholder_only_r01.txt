**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication Today**

To better protect your account, we strongly encourage you to turn on two-factor authentication (2FA). This adds a second verification step when you sign in, so a stolen or guessed password alone is not enough for someone to access your account.

**How to enable it:**

1. Sign in and open your Account Settings.
2. Select the Security tab.
3. Choose Two-Factor Authentication and click Enable.
4. Pick your verification method, either an authenticator app (recommended) or text message.
5. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm your setup.
6. Save your backup codes in a secure place, such as a password manager.

**Why it matters:**

- It blocks most unauthorized login attempts, even if your password is compromised.
- It takes only a few minutes to set up.
- It adds protection for your personal information and activity.

**Need help?** Visit our Help Center or contact the support team, who can walk you through the process.

Thank you for helping keep your account secure.
