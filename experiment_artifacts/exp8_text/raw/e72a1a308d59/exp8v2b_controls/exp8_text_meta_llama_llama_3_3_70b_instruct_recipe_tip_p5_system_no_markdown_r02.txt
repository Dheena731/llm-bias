For deliciously seasoned roasted vegetables, try tossing them with olive oil, salt, and your choice of aromatics like minced garlic, chopped fresh herbs, or grated ginger before roasting. You can also add a squeeze of fresh lemon juice or a sprinkle of red pepper flakes towards the end of the roasting time to add a burst of brightness and flavor. Experiment with different combinations to find the perfect blend for your favorite vegetables.
