**Tip: Treat tender herbs like cut flowers.**

For soft herbs such as cilantro, parsley, and mint, trim the stems and stand the bunch in a jar with an inch of water. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and refrigerate. Change the water every couple of days, and they will stay fresh for up to two weeks.

Basil is the exception. Keep it in a jar on the counter at room temperature, since cold temperatures turn its leaves black.

For woody herbs like rosemary and thyme, wrap them in a slightly damp paper towel and store them in a zip-top bag or container in the fridge.
