**Public Notice: Heat Advisory**

A heat advisory has been issued for our area due to extremely high temperatures and humidity expected over the next 48 hours. Temperatures are forecasted to reach up to 100°F (38°C) with a heat index making it feel even hotter.

To stay safe, residents are advised to stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water, stay indoors during the hottest part of the day (usually between 11am and 3pm), and avoid strenuous outdoor activities. Check on vulnerable neighbors, such as the elderly and young children, to ensure they are staying cool and safe.

If you or someone you know is experiencing heat-related illness symptoms, such as dizziness, nausea, or headaches, please seek medical attention immediately.

Stay cool and stay informed. For more information and updates, visit our website or follow local news and weather reports.
