NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

To qualify for a refund, all return requests must be submitted within thirty (30) calendar days of the original delivery date. Items must be unused, unaltered, and returned in their original packaging with all accompanying accessories and documentation intact. A valid proof of purchase, such as an original invoice or order confirmation, is strictly required.

Non-refundable items include, but are not limited to, personalized or custom-made goods, downloadable digital software, and items explicitly marked as final sale at the time of purchase. 

All returned merchandise is subject to inspection prior to approval. The company reserves the right to deny any refund request that does not comply with the aforementioned terms. Approved refunds will be credited exclusively to the original method of payment within ten (10) business days following inspection.
