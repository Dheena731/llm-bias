# Introducing Savings Goals 🎯

We've added a new way to save for the things that matter most to you.

**What's new**

With Savings Goals, you can:

- **Set a target.** Name your goal, whether it's a vacation, an emergency fund, or a new laptop, and choose how much you want to save and by when.
- **Track your progress.** A progress bar shows how close you are, and we'll suggest how much to set aside each week or month to stay on track.
- **Automate your savings.** Set up recurring transfers so your goal grows without you having to think about it.
- **Celebrate milestones.** Get a nudge when you hit 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of your goal.

**How to get started**

1. Open the app and tap **Savings**.
2. Select **Create a Goal**.
3. Enter your target amount and date, then choose how you'd like to contribute.

You can create multiple goals and adjust them anytime as your plans change.

Savings Goals is rolling out to all users over the next week. If you don't see it yet, check back soon. We'd love to hear what you think, so send us your feedback through **Settings > Help & Feedback**.

Happy saving!
