**Attention Library Visitors: Updated Opening Hours**

Beginning Monday, the Central Library will follow its new weekly schedule:

- **Monday through Thursday:** 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
- **Friday and Saturday:** 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- **Sunday:** 1:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m.

The library will be closed on all public holidays. The book drop at the main entrance is available around the clock, so you can return materials any time.

For questions or to check hours for a specific date, visit the library's website or call the front desk during open hours.

Thank you for helping us serve the community.
