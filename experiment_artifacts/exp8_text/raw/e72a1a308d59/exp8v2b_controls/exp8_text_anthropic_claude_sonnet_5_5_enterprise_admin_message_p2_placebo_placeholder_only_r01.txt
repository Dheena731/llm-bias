**Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starts Monday**

Hello everyone,

Beginning Monday, all expense reports will follow a new approval workflow in our finance system. The change is designed to speed up reimbursements and reduce back-and-forth over missing details.

**What's changing**

- **Under $250:** Your direct manager approves the report, and it goes straight to payment.
- **$250 to $2,500:** Your manager approves first, then the department head.
- **Over $2,500:** Your manager and department head approve, followed by a final review from Finance.

**What you need to do**

1. Submit expenses within 30 days of the purchase date.
2. Attach an itemized receipt to every line item. Reports without receipts will be returned automatically.
3. Choose the correct expense category and add a brief business purpose.

**What to expect**

Approvers will receive an email notification for each pending report and have five business days to respond. If a report isn't actioned in that time, it will be escalated to the next approver. Approved reports will be reimbursed in the next weekly payment run, which happens every Friday.

Reports submitted before Monday will be processed under the current workflow, so there's no need to resubmit anything.

If you have questions, reply to this message or contact the Finance team directly. A short how-to guide is also available on the intranet under Finance Resources.

Thank you for helping make this transition a smooth one.

Best regards,
The Finance and Administration Team
