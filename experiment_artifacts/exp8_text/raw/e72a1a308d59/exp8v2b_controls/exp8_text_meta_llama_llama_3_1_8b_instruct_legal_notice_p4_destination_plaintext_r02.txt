REFUND ELIGIBILITY NOTICE

PURSUANT TO OUR TERMS AND CONDITIONS, CUSTOMERS ARE ELIGIBLE FOR A REFUND UNDER THE FOLLOWING CIRCUMSTANCES:

* DEFECTIVE PRODUCT: IF THE PRODUCT RECEIVED IS DEFECTIVE OR NOT AS DESCRIBED, A FULL REFUND OF THE PURCHASE PRICE WILL BE ISSUED UPON RETURN.
* CHANGE OF HEART: IF THE CUSTOMER CHANGES THEIR MIND ABOUT THE PURCHASE WITHIN 30 DAYS OF DELIVERY, A FULL REFUND OF THE PURCHASE PRICE WILL BE ISSUED UPON RETURN OF THE PRODUCT IN ORIGINAL CONDITION.
* MISORDERED ITEM: IF THE CUSTOMER RECEIVES AN ITEM THAT WAS NOT ORDERED, A FULL REFUND OF THE PURCHASE PRICE WILL BE ISSUED UPON RETURN OF THE ITEM.

TO BE ELIGIBLE FOR A REFUND, CUSTOMERS MUST RETURN THE PRODUCT IN ORIGINAL CONDITION WITHIN 30 DAYS OF DELIVERY. A FULL REFUND OF THE PURCHASE PRICE WILL BE ISSUED WITHIN 10 BUSINESS DAYS OF RECEIVING THE RETURNED PRODUCT.
