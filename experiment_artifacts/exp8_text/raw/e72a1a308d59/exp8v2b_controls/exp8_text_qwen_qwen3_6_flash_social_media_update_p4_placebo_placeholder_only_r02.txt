Turn your calendar chaos into clarity with one simple trick: color coding! 🌈📅

Assign distinct colors to different areas of your life, such as deep blue for focused work sessions, green for workouts, pink for personal errands, and yellow for family time. When you glance at your month, you'll instantly grasp your priorities without reading a single label. Give your digital planner a makeover today and reclaim your mental space! ✨💪
