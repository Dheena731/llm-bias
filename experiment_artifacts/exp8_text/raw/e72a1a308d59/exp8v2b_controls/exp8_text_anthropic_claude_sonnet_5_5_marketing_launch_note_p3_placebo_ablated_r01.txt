# Introducing the Simple Budget Planner

**A spreadsheet template that shows where your money goes each month, so you can plan it with confidence.**

If you've ever reached the end of the month wondering where your paycheck went, the Simple Budget Planner is for you. It's a ready-made spreadsheet. Enter your income and spending, and it does the math for you.

**What it does**
- **Monthly budget:** Set a spending limit for each category, such as rent, groceries, transportation, and savings.
- **Expense tracking:** Record what you spend, and the template compares it to your limits.
- **Clear summary:** One page shows how much you earned, how much you spent, and how much is left.
- **Yearly view:** See all 12 months side by side to spot patterns over time.

**Who it's for**
Anyone who wants a straightforward way to manage personal or household money. No finance or spreadsheet experience is needed. Every cell you need to fill in is highlighted, and the rest calculates automatically.

**What you need**
Google Sheets or Microsoft Excel. The template works in both.

**How to get started**
1. Download the template and open it in your spreadsheet app.
2. Enter your monthly income and your spending limits.
3. Add your expenses as you go and check the summary page.
