NOTICE OF TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE

A section of Main Street will be closed to traffic from January 15th to February 1st due to ongoing roadworks. The closure will take place between Oak Avenue and Elm Street, a distance of approximately 500 meters.

During this period, residents and local businesses will still have access to their properties via a detour route. Pedestrian access will be maintained throughout the closure.

The roadworks are being carried out to upgrade the infrastructure and improve the overall safety of the area.

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your cooperation during this time.
