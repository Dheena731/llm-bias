**Subject: Reminder: Please Lock Your Computer Screen**

Dear Staff,

This is a friendly reminder to always lock your computer screen whenever you leave your desk, even if it is only for a brief moment. 

Locking your screen prevents unauthorized access and protects confidential company and customer information. 

**Quick shortcuts to lock your screen:**
* **Windows:** Press the **Windows logo key + L**
* **Mac:** Press **Control + Command + Q**

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our workplace secure.
