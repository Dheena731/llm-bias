Latency matters in web applications because it directly affects user experience and engagement. When a user interacts with a web application, they expect a prompt response. Latency, or the delay between a user's action and the application's response, can lead to frustration, abandonment of the application, or even a decrease in conversions. High latency can be particularly problematic for real-time interactions, such as online gaming, live chat, or video conferencing, where even a slight delay can impact performance and user satisfaction. As a result, minimizing latency is crucial for delivering a seamless and enjoyable user experience.
