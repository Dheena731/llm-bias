A Purchaser is eligible for a refund only if all of the following conditions are met:

a. The refund request is submitted in writing within thirty (30) calendar days of the original purchase date.

c. The Purchaser provides valid proof of purchase, such as an order confirmation or receipt.

Refunds will not be issued for:

a. Items marked as final sale at the time of purchase.

b. Custom-made or personalized goods.
