**Update: Introducing Your Monthly Spending Summary**

We have introduced a new monthly spending summary to give you clearer visibility into your finances and help you track your budget with ease. 

Starting this month, you will receive an automated overview at the end of each billing cycle detailing:

* **Category Breakdown:** See where your money goes across essential categories such as groceries, utilities, and entertainment.
* **Month-over-Month Trends:** Compare your current spending directly against the previous month to identify shifts in your habits.
* **Key Highlights:** Quickly spot your largest expenses and recurring subscriptions.

You can access your latest report anytime by navigating to the **Analytics** tab in your account dashboard. 

Review your summary today to gain better control over your cash flow and stay aligned with your financial goals.
