**Tip: Season in two stages for the best flavor.**

Before roasting, toss your cut vegetables with oil and a generous pinch of salt and pepper. The oil helps the vegetables brown, and the early salt seasons them throughout. Spread them in a single layer on the pan so they roast instead of steam.

Once they come out of the oven, taste one and adjust. Add a final sprinkle of salt if needed, plus a splash of lemon juice or vinegar and some chopped fresh herbs, such as parsley or thyme. Heat dulls bright flavors, so adding these at the end keeps them fresh and makes the vegetables taste lively.
