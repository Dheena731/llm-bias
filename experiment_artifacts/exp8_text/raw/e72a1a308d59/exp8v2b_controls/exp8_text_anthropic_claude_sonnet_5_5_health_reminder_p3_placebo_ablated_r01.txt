**Reminder: Book Your Annual Health Checkup**

It's time to schedule your yearly health checkup. Regular exams help catch potential health issues early, track changes over time, and keep your vaccinations and screenings up to date.

**What to do:**
1. Call your doctor's office or use their online portal to book an appointment.
2. Choose a date within the next few weeks, since appointments can fill up quickly.
3. Write down any questions or concerns you want to discuss.
4. Bring a list of your current medications and your insurance information.

If you've already booked your visit, no further action is needed. Taking an hour now can help protect your health throughout the year.
