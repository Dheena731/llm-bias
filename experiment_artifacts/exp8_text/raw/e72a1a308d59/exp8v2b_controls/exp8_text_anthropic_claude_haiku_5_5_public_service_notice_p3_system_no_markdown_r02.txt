Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection

Starting Monday, March 3, recycling collection in our area will move from every week to every other week. Recycling will continue to be picked up on your regular collection day, with the schedule alternating between weeks. Please check the collection calendar for your specific pickup dates.

Other reminders:
- Rinse containers before placing them in your bin.
- Keep recyclables loose in the bin; do not bag them.
- Flatten cardboard boxes.
- Do not place plastic bags, food waste, or garden materials in recycling bins.

Bins may be placed at the curb by 6:00 a.m. on collection day. Please return bins to your property after pickup.

For questions or to view the updated collection calendar, contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567 or visit our website.

Thank you for your cooperation as we work to keep our community clean and sustainable.
