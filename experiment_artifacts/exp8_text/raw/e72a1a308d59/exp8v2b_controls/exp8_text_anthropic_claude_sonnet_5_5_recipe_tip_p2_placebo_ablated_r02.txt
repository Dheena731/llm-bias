**Tip: Store fresh herbs like a bouquet (for tender herbs) or wrapped in a towel (for sturdy herbs).**

**Tender herbs** (basil excepted, see below): cilantro, parsley, dill, mint
1. Trim the stem ends.
2. Stand the bunch in a jar with an inch of water.
3. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and refrigerate.
4. Change the water every few days.

**Sturdy herbs**: rosemary, thyme, sage, oregano
1. Wrap them loosely in a slightly damp paper towel.
2. Place them in a resealable bag or container in the refrigerator.

**Basil is the exception.** Cold damages it and turns the leaves black. Keep it in a jar of water on the counter, away from direct sunlight, and do not refrigerate it.

**Avoid washing herbs until just before use**, since extra moisture speeds up spoilage. Stored this way, most herbs stay fresh for one to two weeks.
