Here is a short notice about reporting a lost company laptop:

**Lost Company Laptop Notice**

If you have lost a company-issued laptop, please report the incident to the IT department immediately. Please provide the following information:

* Your name and employee ID
* The laptop's make, model, and serial number (if known)
* The date and time the laptop was last seen
* The location where the laptop was last used
* A description of the circumstances surrounding the loss

You can report the lost laptop by emailing [IT department email] or calling [IT department phone number]. Please note that prompt reporting is essential to minimize potential security risks and ensure timely replacement of the device.

Thank you for your cooperation and prompt attention to this matter.
