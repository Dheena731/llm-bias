**Notice to All Employees: Staying Alert to Phishing Emails**

Please remain vigilant when checking your inbox. Phishing is a type of cyber attack where scammers send fake emails disguised as legitimate messages from trusted colleagues, clients, or companies. Their goal is to trick you into sharing sensitive information—such as passwords or financial details—or into downloading harmful software onto our network.

**Common warning signs include:**
* Urgent or threatening demands (e.g., "Your account will be closed immediately").
* Senders whose email address does not match the company or person they claim to be.
* Unexpected attachments or requests to click on unfamiliar links.
* Uncharacteristic spelling, formatting, or grammatical errors.

**What to do:**
If you receive an email that seems suspicious, **do not click any links, open any attachments, or reply.** Instead, notify the IT Support team immediately at [IT contact information] so they can investigate. 

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our organization’s data secure.
