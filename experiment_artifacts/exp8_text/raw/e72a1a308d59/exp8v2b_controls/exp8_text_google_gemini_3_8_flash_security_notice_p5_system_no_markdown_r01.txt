NOTICE: PROTOCOL FOR LOST OR STOLEN COMPANY LAPTOPS

If you have misplaced your company-issued laptop or suspect it has been stolen, you must report the incident immediately. Rapid reporting allows our security team to remotely lock and wipe the device, protecting sensitive company and client data.

IMMEDIATE STEPS TO TAKE:

1. Contact the IT Service Desk immediately at [Phone Number] or [Email Address]. State clearly that you are reporting a missing device.
2. Notify your direct supervisor.
3. If the device was stolen, file a report with local law enforcement and request a case number to share with IT and HR.

Please be prepared to provide the following details:
- Approximate time and location where the device was last seen.
- Circumstances of the loss or theft.
- Any peripheral items missing (access badges, external hard drives, etc.).

Prompt reporting is mandatory per company security policy and helps prevent data breaches. Thank you for your cooperation.
