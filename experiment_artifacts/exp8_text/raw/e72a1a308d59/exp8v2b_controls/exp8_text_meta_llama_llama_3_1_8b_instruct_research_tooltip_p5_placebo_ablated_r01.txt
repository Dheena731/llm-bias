Here is a concise tooltip explaining how to export query results:

**Exporting Query Results**

To export your query results, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Export" button in the top-right corner of the query result table.
2. Select the desired file format (e.g., CSV, Excel, PDF).
3. Choose the data to export (e.g., all results, or specific columns).
4. Click "Export" to save the results to your computer or cloud storage.

Note: The availability of export options may vary depending on your database or application.
