Take a Screen Break

Your eyes, neck, and mind need a pause. Every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds. Stand up, stretch your shoulders, and take a few slow breaths. Drink some water, step outside if you can, and let your focus wander for a moment.

These small breaks reduce eye strain, ease tension, and help you think more clearly when you return to your work. Step away now, and your future self will thank you.
