## Shared Network Acceptable Use Notice

Use of this shared network constitutes acceptance of this notice. Users shall use the network lawfully, responsibly, and only for authorized purposes.

Users must not:

- Access, transmit, store, or distribute unlawful, fraudulent, defamatory, abusive, or harmful material;
- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to systems, accounts, data, or network resources;
- Introduce malware, disrupt service, interfere with other users, or circumvent security controls;
- Monitor, intercept, or collect another person’s communications or personal information without authorization; or
- Use the network in a manner that violates applicable law or the rights of others.

Network activity may be monitored, logged, and investigated to protect security and ensure compliance. Access may be restricted or revoked for misuse, and suspected unlawful activity may be reported to the appropriate authorities. Users are responsible for safeguarding their accounts and for all activity conducted through them.
