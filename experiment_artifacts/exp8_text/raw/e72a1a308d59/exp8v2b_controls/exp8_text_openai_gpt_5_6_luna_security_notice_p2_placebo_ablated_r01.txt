**Workplace Notice: Beware of Phishing Emails**

Phishing emails are designed to trick you into sharing passwords, financial information, or other sensitive data. Do not click unexpected links, open suspicious attachments, or reply with confidential information—even if the message appears to come from someone you know.

If an email seems suspicious, report it to the IT department using the approved reporting process, then delete it. When in doubt, verify the request through a trusted channel.
