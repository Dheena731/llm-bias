**Time for Your Weekly Fitness Check-In**

Take a few minutes today to review your progress from the past week. Look at your completed workouts, any changes in your measurements or weight, and how your energy and mood have felt.

Ask yourself:
- Did I meet my activity goals this week?
- What went well, and what was difficult?
- What one change could I make next week?

Use your answers to set realistic goals for the coming week. Small, steady improvements add up, so celebrate what you accomplished and adjust your plan as needed.
