**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication**

To better protect your account and personal information, we strongly recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA). With 2FA, signing in requires your password plus a second verification step, such as a one-time code sent to your phone or generated by an authentication app.

**How to enable 2FA:**
1. Log in to your account and go to **Settings**.
2. Select **Security**, then choose **Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Follow the prompts to link your phone or authentication app.
4. Save your backup codes in a safe place.

Enabling 2FA takes only a few minutes and significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access, even if your password is compromised.

If you have questions or need help, please contact our support team.

Thank you for helping us keep your account secure.
