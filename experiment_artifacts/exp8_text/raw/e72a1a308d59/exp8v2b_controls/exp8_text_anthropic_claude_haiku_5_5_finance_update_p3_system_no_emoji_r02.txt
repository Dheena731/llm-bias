**Introducing Savings Goals**

We're excited to announce Savings Goals, a new feature that helps you plan and track the money you're setting aside for what matters most.

With Savings Goals, you can:

- **Set a target** for anything you're saving toward, such as an emergency fund, a vacation, or a new home.
- **Choose a deadline** and see how much you need to save each week or month to get there.
- **Track your progress** with a clear view of how close you are to each goal.
- **Make automatic contributions** so you can build your savings without thinking about it.

To get started, open the Savings tab and tap "New Goal." Give it a name, set your target amount and date, and you're on your way.

We built this feature based on your feedback, and we'd love to hear how it works for you. Share your thoughts through the in-app feedback button or at support@example.com.

Happy saving!
