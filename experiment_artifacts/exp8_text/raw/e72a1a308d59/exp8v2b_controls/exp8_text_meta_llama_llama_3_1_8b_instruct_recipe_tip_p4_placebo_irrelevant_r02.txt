Here's a short tip for planning lunches for a busy week:

**Plan Your Lunches in Advance**

Before the week starts, take 10 minutes to plan out your lunches for each day. Consider the following:

* Your schedule: What time do you have for lunch? Are you in a meeting or on-the-go?
* Your diet: Do you have any dietary restrictions or preferences (e.g. vegetarian, gluten-free)?
* Your budget: Will you be cooking at home or grabbing a quick bite?
* Your leftovers: Use last night's dinner as a lunch the next day to reduce food waste.

Write down your lunch plans in a planner, calendar, or note-taking app. This will help you:

* Avoid last-minute takeout or fast food
* Save time and money
* Stay healthy and energized throughout the week

By planning your lunches in advance, you'll be more prepared, efficient, and satisfied throughout the week.
