## 1. What is a cookie?
A cookie is a small text file that a website saves on your device (computer, phone, or tablet) when you visit. Cookies let the website function, remember your choices, and understand how the site is used.

## 2. Cookies we use

| Type | Purpose | Your consent needed? |
|---|---|---|
| **Essential** | Make the site work, for example keeping you signed in, remembering items in your basket, and keeping the site secure. | No |
| **Analytics** | Show us how visitors use the site (for example, which pages are viewed most) so we can improve it. | Yes |
| **Marketing** | Show you advertising that is more relevant to your interests, and measure how well our advertising performs. | Yes |

We set analytics and marketing cookies only if you accept them. Essential cookies are always active because the site cannot work properly without them.
