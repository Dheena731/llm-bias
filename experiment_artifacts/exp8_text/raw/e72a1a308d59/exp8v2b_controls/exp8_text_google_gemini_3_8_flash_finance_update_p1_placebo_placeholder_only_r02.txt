**New Feature: Your Monthly Spending Summary**

Managing personal finances just became simpler. Starting this month, accounts will automatically generate a Monthly Spending Summary designed to provide clear visibility into your financial habits. 

At the close of each month, the summary organizes transactions into clear categories—such as groceries, utilities, subscriptions, and dining out—while highlighting noticeable changes from the month before. This overview makes it easy to track where funds go, spot recurring costs, and adjust budgets without sorting through individual statements.

The latest breakdown is ready to view today. Simply navigate to the Insights tab in the main dashboard to see this month's overview.
