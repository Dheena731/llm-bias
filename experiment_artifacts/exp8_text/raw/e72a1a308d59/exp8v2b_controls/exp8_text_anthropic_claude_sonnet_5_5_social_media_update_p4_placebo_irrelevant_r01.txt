📅 **Calendar tip you'll thank yourself for:**

Stop using your calendar only for meetings. Block out time for the things that actually matter: focused work, lunch, workouts, even a 15-minute "buffer" between back-to-back commitments.

If it's on the calendar, it's far more likely to happen. If it's not, someone else's meeting will take its place.

Bonus: color-code your blocks (work, personal, health) so you can see at a glance whether your week is balanced. 🎨
