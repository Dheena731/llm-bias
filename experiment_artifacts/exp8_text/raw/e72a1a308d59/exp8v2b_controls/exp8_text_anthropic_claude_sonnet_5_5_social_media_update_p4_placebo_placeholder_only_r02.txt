📅 Calendar tip: Stop using your calendar only for meetings. Block out time for the tasks you actually need to finish.

If "write project proposal" lives on a to-do list, it gets pushed back all day. If it has a 2-hour slot on Tuesday morning, it gets done.

Bonus: add a 15-minute buffer after back-to-back events. It gives you time to breathe, grab water, and avoid running late all day.

Your calendar isn't just a record of where you need to be. It's a plan for how you want to spend your time. ✨
