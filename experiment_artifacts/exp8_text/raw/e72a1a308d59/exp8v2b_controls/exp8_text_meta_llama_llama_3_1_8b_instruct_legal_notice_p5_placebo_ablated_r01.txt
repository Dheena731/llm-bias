**NOTICE OF ACCEPTABLE USE OF THE SHARED NETWORK**

**TO: All Authorized Users**

This Notice sets forth the rules and guidelines governing the acceptable use of the shared network (the "Network") made available by [Company/Organization Name] to its employees, contractors, and other authorized individuals.

**USE RESTRICTIONS**

1. **Access**: The Network is for authorized use only. Access to the Network is restricted to individuals who have been granted permission by [Company/Organization Name] or its authorized representatives.
2. **Purpose**: The Network shall be used only for legitimate business purposes, including but not limited to, email, file sharing, and internet access.
3. **Prohibited Activities**: The following activities are strictly prohibited on the Network:
	* Accessing, storing, or transmitting unauthorized or confidential information.
	* Engaging in any activity that is defamatory, harassing, or discriminatory.
	* Using the Network for personal gain or for any purpose that may be considered a conflict of interest.
	* Intentionally causing harm or damage to the Network, its systems, or data.
4. **Intellectual Property**: All users shall respect the intellectual property rights of others and shall not upload, download, or distribute copyrighted or proprietary materials without the owner's permission.

**COMPUTER USE**

1. **Password Security**: Each user shall maintain the confidentiality of their password and shall not share it with anyone.
2. **System Maintenance**: Users shall not tamper with or attempt to alter any system configuration, settings, or software.
3. **Malware and Viruses**: Users shall not introduce malware or viruses into the Network.

**MONITORING**

The Network may be monitored for security and administrative purposes. All users shall be aware that their activities on the Network may be recorded and monitored.

**VIOLATIONS**

Any unauthorized or prohibited use of the Network may result in disciplinary action, including but not limited to, suspension or termination of Network access, and/or legal action.

**AMENDMENTS**

This Notice may be amended at any time without notice. Users are responsible for regularly reviewing this Notice for updates.

**ACKNOWLEDGMENT**

By using the Network, users acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agree to abide by this Notice.
