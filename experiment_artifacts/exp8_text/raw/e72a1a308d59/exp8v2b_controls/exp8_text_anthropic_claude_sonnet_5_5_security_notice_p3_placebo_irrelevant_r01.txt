**Reminder: Lock Your Screen When You Step Away**

Hello everyone,

A quick reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you leave your desk, even if it's just for a minute. An unlocked device can give others access to confidential information, company systems, and personal accounts.

**How to lock your screen:**
- **Windows:** Press **Windows key + L**
- **Mac:** Press **Control + Command + Q**

Taking a few seconds to lock your screen is one of the easiest ways to protect our data and our colleagues.

If you have questions or need help setting up an automatic screen lock, please contact the IT Help Desk.

Thank you for helping keep our workplace secure.
