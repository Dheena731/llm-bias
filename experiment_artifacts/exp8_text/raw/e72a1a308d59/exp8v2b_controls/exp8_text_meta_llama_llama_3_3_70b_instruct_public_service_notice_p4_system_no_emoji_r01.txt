PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY ISSUED

Due to extreme heat and humidity, a heat advisory has been issued for our area. Temperatures are expected to reach dangerous levels, posing a significant risk to public health, especially for vulnerable populations such as the elderly, young children, and those with pre-existing medical conditions.

Residents are advised to take precautions to stay cool and hydrated, including staying indoors during the hottest part of the day, drinking plenty of water, and avoiding strenuous outdoor activities. Cooling centers have been set up at local community centers and libraries for those who need a cool place to rest.

Please check on neighbors, especially the elderly and those who may be isolated, to ensure their safety during this heat advisory. If you or someone you know is experiencing heat-related illness, such as heat exhaustion or heat stroke, call 911 immediately.

Stay safe and stay informed. Monitor local news and weather reports for updates on the heat advisory.
