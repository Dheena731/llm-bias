**Introducing Your Monthly Spending Summary**

We've added a Monthly Spending Summary to your account. At the start of each month, you'll receive a clear overview of how you spent money in the previous month.

Each summary includes:

- **Total spending** for the month, compared with the previous month
- **Breakdown by category**, such as dining, transportation, and subscriptions
- **Largest transactions** so you can quickly spot unusual charges
- **Recurring payments** to help you review subscriptions and bills

You can find your latest summary under **Reports** in the main menu. Past summaries are saved there as well, so you can track trends over time.

You can turn off the monthly email in **Settings > Notifications** if you prefer to check the summary only in the app.
