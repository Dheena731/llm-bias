Here is a short reviewer comment on an unclear research question:

**Reviewer comment:**

The central research question, "How does social media affect adolescents?", is too broad to guide the study design or evaluate its contribution. "Social media" covers many platforms with different features, and "affect" leaves open whether the authors mean mental health, academic performance, sleep, social skills, or something else. The question also does not specify a direction, population, time frame, or comparison group, so it is unclear what result would confirm or refute it.

I would encourage the authors to narrow the question to a testable form. For example: "Among adolescents aged 13 to 16, is daily time spent on Instagram associated with self-reported depressive symptoms over a 12-month period?" A revised question of this kind would clarify the variables, the population, and the expected relationship, and it would help readers judge whether the methods are suited to answer it.
