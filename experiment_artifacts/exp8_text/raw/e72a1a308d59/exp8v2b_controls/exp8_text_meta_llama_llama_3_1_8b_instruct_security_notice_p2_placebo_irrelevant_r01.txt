Here's a short workplace notice about phishing emails:

**Important Security Alert**

As we continue to take measures to protect our company's assets, we wanted to remind everyone to remain vigilant when it comes to email communications.

**Phishing Emails: A Growing Concern**

We've seen an increase in phishing emails circulating around the office, and we want to make sure you're aware of the risks involved. Phishing emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information, such as login credentials or financial data, by posing as a legitimate sender.

**What to Watch Out For**

Be cautious of emails that:

* Request sensitive information or login credentials
* Create a sense of urgency or panic
* Contain spelling or grammatical errors
* Ask you to click on links or download attachments

**What to Do**

If you receive a suspicious email, do not:

* Click on links or download attachments
* Respond to the email
* Provide sensitive information

Instead, report the email to our IT department by forwarding it to [insert email address]. We'll investigate and take necessary actions to ensure your safety.

**Stay Safe Online**

Let's work together to protect our company's assets and keep our online environment secure. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to our IT department.

Thank you for your attention to this important matter.
