Here is a short update on our new monthly spending summary:

Starting this month, we will be providing a concise and easy-to-read summary of our monthly expenses. This summary will be sent to all account holders on the 5th of each month and will include a breakdown of all transactions, including income, fixed expenses, and discretionary spending. The purpose of this summary is to help you stay on top of your finances and make informed decisions about your spending habits. The summary will be available online and can be accessed through our website. If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to our customer support team.
