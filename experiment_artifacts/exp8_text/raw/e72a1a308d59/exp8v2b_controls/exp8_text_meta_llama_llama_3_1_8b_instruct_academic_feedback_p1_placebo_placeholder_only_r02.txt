Here is a sample short academic feedback note on a draft literature review:

Dear [Researcher],

I appreciate the effort you have put into the draft literature review. The scope of the literature you have covered is comprehensive, and you have made a strong effort to situate your research within the broader context of the field.

However, there are a few areas that warrant further attention. While you provide a good overview of the key themes and debates in the existing literature, the transitions between paragraphs could be smoother. You may consider adding more explicit connections between the ideas presented in each section to create a more cohesive narrative.

Additionally, some of the studies you cite could benefit from more critical analysis. While you provide a summary of the findings, you could take the next step by evaluating the strengths and limitations of each study and considering how they contribute to the broader discussion. This would add depth and nuance to your review.

In terms of organization, you may want to consider reordering the sections or breaking up some of the longer paragraphs to improve the flow of the review. This would make it easier for the reader to follow your argument and understand the significance of the research.

Overall, this is a promising draft, and with some further refinement, it has the potential to be a strong literature review. I look forward to seeing the revisions.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
