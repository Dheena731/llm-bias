**Introducing Savings Goals: Plan Your Next Milestone with Confidence**

We're excited to announce the launch of Savings Goals, a new feature designed to help you turn your financial aspirations into achievable plans.

Whether you're saving for a down payment, a dream vacation, an emergency fund, or a new laptop, you can now create a dedicated goal, set a target amount and a deadline, and track your progress over time. Savings Goals automatically shows how much you need to set aside each week or month to get there, so you always know whether you're on track.

Key highlights:
- **Custom goals:** Name your goal, set a target, and choose a date.
- **Progress tracking:** See your balance move toward each milestone at a glance.
- **Smart suggestions:** Get recommended contribution amounts based on your goal and timeline.
- **Flexible adjustments:** Change your target or deadline anytime as your plans evolve.

Savings Goals is available now in the app under the Savings tab. Give it a try, and let us know what milestones you're working toward.
