**Reminder: Book Your Annual Health Checkup**

It's time to schedule your yearly health checkup. Regular exams help catch potential issues early, keep your vaccinations and screenings current, and give you a chance to talk with your doctor about any concerns.

**Before you call:**
- Check your calendar for a time that works for you
- Review your insurance coverage for preventive care
- Jot down any symptoms, questions, or medications you want to discuss

Take a few minutes today to call your doctor's office or use their online portal to book your appointment. Your future self will thank you.
