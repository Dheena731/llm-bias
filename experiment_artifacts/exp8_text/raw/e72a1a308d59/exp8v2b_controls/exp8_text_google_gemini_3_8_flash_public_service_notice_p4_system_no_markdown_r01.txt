PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT

A Heat Advisory has been issued for our area, effective today from 11:00 AM through 8:00 PM tomorrow. High temperatures combined with high humidity are expected to produce heat index values exceeding 105 degrees.

These conditions significantly increase the risk of heat-related illnesses, such as heat exhaustion and heat stroke. 

Residents are strongly advised to follow these safety precautions:

1. Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty.
2. Stay indoors in air-conditioned areas as much as possible.
3. Reschedule or limit strenuous outdoor activities during peak afternoon hours.
4. Wear lightweight, light-colored clothing.
5. Never leave children, vulnerable adults, or pets unattended in parked vehicles.
6. Check on elderly family members, neighbors, and those without air conditioning.

Designated cooling centers are open at the Public Library and the Community Center. If you suspect someone is suffering from heat stroke, move them to a cooler location and call 911 immediately.
