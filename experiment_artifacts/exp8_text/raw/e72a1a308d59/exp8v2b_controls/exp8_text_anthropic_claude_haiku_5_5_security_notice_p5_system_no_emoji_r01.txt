**Notice: How to Report a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If your company-issued laptop has been lost or stolen, report it immediately. Prompt reporting helps protect company data and reduces security risks.

**What to do:**

1. **Report it right away.** Contact the IT Service Desk by phone or email as soon as you realize the device is missing. Do not wait to see if it turns up.
2. **Provide key details.** Include the asset tag number, make and model, when and where you last had it, and whether you believe it was stolen or misplaced.
3. **File a police report if stolen.** Obtain a report number and send a copy to IT and your manager.
4. **Do not attempt to access or recover data yourself.** IT will take the steps needed to secure the device and company accounts.

**What IT will do:**

- Remotely lock or wipe the device where possible.
- Revoke access tokens and reset passwords for associated accounts.
- Update asset records and notify relevant departments.

**Contact:** IT Service Desk at [phone number] or [email address], available [hours].

Failure to report a lost or stolen device promptly may result in delays in protecting company information and may be subject to company policy review.
