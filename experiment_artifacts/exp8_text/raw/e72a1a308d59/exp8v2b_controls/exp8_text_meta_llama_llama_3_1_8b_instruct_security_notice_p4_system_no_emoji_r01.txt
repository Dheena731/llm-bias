**Enable Two-Factor Authentication for Enhanced Security**

To protect your account from unauthorized access, we highly recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA) for your online services. 2FA adds an extra layer of security by requiring a second form of verification in addition to your password.

To enable 2FA, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your account
2. Go to your account settings
3. Look for the 2FA option and follow the instructions to set it up

**Why enable 2FA?**

* Prevents unauthorized access to your account
* Protects your personal and sensitive information
* Provides an extra layer of security against phishing and other types of attacks

By enabling 2FA, you can rest assured that your account is more secure and protected from potential threats.
