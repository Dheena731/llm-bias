We're excited to announce the launch of our new savings goal feature, designed to help you reach your financial objectives more easily. This feature allows you to set and track specific savings targets, such as building an emergency fund, paying off debt, or saving for a big purchase. With our savings goal feature, you can:

* Set a specific goal amount and deadline
* Track your progress and receive regular updates
* Automatically transfer funds from your checking account to your savings account
* Receive personalized recommendations to help you stay on track

This feature is available to all our online banking users and can be accessed through our website or mobile app. By using our savings goal feature, you'll be able to stay focused on your financial goals and make progress towards achieving them.
