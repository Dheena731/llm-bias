**PUBLIC NOTICE: Road Closure**

**Location:** Main Street, between 4th Avenue and Maple Street

**Dates:** Monday, June 9 through Friday, June 13, 2025

**Hours:** 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily

**Reason:** Scheduled resurfacing and repair of the roadway and storm drains

**Detour:** Traffic will be redirected via Oak Street and Elm Avenue. Signs will be posted at all affected intersections.

**Access:** Local businesses and residences will remain accessible from side streets. Pedestrian walkways will stay open where possible.

Please plan your travel accordingly and allow extra time during the closure period. Emergency vehicles will have access at all times.

For questions, contact the City Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567.

Issued by the City of Riverside Department of Public Works
