**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY**

**1. Purpose**
This Notice sets forth the conditions under which a purchaser ("Customer") may be eligible for a refund from the seller ("Company") for goods or services purchased.

**2. Eligibility Period**
A refund request must be submitted in writing within thirty (30) calendar days of the original date of purchase ("Eligibility Period"). Requests received after this period will not be considered, except where required by applicable law.

**3. Conditions for Eligibility**
A Customer is eligible for a refund only if all of the following conditions are satisfied:

a. The Customer provides valid proof of purchase;
b. The goods are returned in their original condition and packaging, unused and undamaged, where applicable;
c. The services have not been substantially performed or consumed at the time of the request; and
d. The purchase is not classified as non-refundable under Section 4.

**4. Non-Refundable Items**
The following are not eligible for refund unless defective or otherwise required by law:

a. Digital goods that have been downloaded or accessed;
b. Custom or personalized products;
c. Gift cards and promotional credits; and
d. Items marked "final sale" at the time of purchase.

**5. Defective or Non-Conforming Goods**
Where goods are defective or materially differ from their description, the Customer may be entitled to a refund, repair, or replacement irrespective of the limitations in Sections 2 through 4, subject to applicable consumer protection laws.

**6. Method and Timing of Refund**
Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within fourteen (14) business days of approval. The Company is not responsible for delays caused by third-party financial institutions.

**7. Deductions**
The Company reserves the right to deduct reasonable costs for return shipping, restocking, or diminished value of returned goods, where permitted by law.

**8. Statutory Rights**
Nothing in this Notice limits or excludes any rights the Customer may have under applicable consumer protection legislation.

**9. Amendments**
The Company may amend this Notice at any time. Amendments apply only to purchases made after the effective date of the revised Notice.
