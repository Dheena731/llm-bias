📅 **Calendar tip you'll thank yourself for:**

Stop using your calendar only for meetings. Block time for *tasks* too.

Instead of keeping a to-do list that never gets done, drop each task into your calendar as a 30- or 60-minute event. Now it has a real time slot, and you can see right away if your day is overbooked.

Bonus: add a 10-minute "buffer" block between big commitments. Future you will be less stressed and way more on time. 🙌
