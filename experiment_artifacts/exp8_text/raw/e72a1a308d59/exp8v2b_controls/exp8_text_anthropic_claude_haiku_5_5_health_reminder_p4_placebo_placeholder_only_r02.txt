Take a Break from the Screen

Your eyes, neck, and mind need a pause. Every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds. Stand up, stretch your shoulders, and take a few slow breaths. A short walk or a glass of water can refresh you more than another scroll through your feed.

Step away now. The work, messages, and videos will still be there when you return, and you'll come back sharper and more focused.
