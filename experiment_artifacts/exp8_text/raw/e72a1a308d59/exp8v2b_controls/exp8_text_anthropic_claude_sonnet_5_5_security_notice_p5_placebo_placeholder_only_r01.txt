**Notice: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If your company laptop is lost or stolen, report it immediately. Prompt reporting protects company data, customer information, and you.

**What to do right away**

1. **Notify IT Security within one hour of discovering the loss.** Call the IT Help Desk or email the Security team using the contact details on the company intranet's IT Support page.
2. **Tell your manager** as soon as you have reported the loss.
3. **File a police report** if the laptop was stolen, and keep the report number for our records.
4. **Do not attempt to recover the device yourself** if you believe it was taken by force or from a secure location.

**Information to include in your report**

- Your name and department
- The laptop's asset tag or serial number, if you know it
- The date, time, and location of the loss
- A brief description of what happened
- Whether the device held sensitive files, such as customer or financial data
- Whether you were signed in to any company accounts at the time

**What IT will do**

- Remotely lock or wipe the device
- Disable your account access and reset your passwords
- Review recent activity for signs of unauthorized access
- Arrange a replacement laptop once the investigation is complete

**Important reminders**

- Reporting quickly will not result in disciplinary action in itself. Delays in reporting can create serious security risks and may be subject to review.
- Never store passwords on or near your laptop.
- Keep your laptop with you or locked away when traveling, and never leave it visible in a parked vehicle.

Thank you for helping us keep company information secure.

*IT Security Team*
