# Introducing the Simple Monthly Budget Template

Do you finish each month wondering where your money went? The Simple Monthly Budget Template is a ready-made spreadsheet that helps you plan your spending and see how your actual spending compares.

**How it works**
1. Enter your monthly income.
2. Set a planned amount for each spending category, such as rent, groceries, transportation, and savings.
3. Record your expenses as they happen.
4. Check the summary tab to see how much you have left in each category.

**What's included**
- A monthly budget sheet with categories you can rename, add, or delete
- An expense log for recording each purchase
- A summary that updates automatically as you add expenses
- A yearly overview showing your totals month by month

**Who it's for**
Anyone who wants a clear picture of their personal or household spending. No accounting or spreadsheet experience is needed, because the formulas are already built in.

**Works with**
Google Sheets and Microsoft Excel.
