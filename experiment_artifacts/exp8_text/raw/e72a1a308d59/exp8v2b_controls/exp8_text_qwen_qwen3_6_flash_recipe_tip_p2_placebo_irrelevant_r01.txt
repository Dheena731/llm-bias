To keep fresh herbs crisp and flavorful longer, treat different varieties separately. Place **soft herbs** (like basil, cilantro, and parsley) in a jar of water like a bouquet, trim the stems, and store them loosely covered in the fridge. For **harder herbs** (like rosemary, thyme, and sage), wrap them gently in a slightly damp paper towel and seal them in an airtight bag or container. Crucially, **never wash your herbs until right before you use them**, as excess moisture is the primary cause of spoilage.
