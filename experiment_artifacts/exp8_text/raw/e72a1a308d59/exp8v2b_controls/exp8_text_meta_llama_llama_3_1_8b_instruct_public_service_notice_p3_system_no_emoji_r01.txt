NOTICE OF RECYCLING COLLECTION CHANGES

The city's recycling collection schedule will be changing effective January 1st. Residents will now be required to put their recyclables in the designated recycling bins, which will be provided by the city.

The new collection schedule is as follows:

* Collection day will be the same day as trash collection.
* Recycling bins will be collected every other week.
* Residents are required to rinse all containers and remove lids and labels.

Please note that any materials not placed in the designated recycling bins will not be collected. A list of acceptable recyclable materials can be found on the city's website or at your local library.

For more information, please contact the city's public works department at (555) 555-5555.
