**Cook once, use twice.** When you're browning ground meat, roasting vegetables, or cooking a pot of rice or grains for tonight's dinner, double the batch. Tomorrow, the extras can become tacos, a grain bowl, fried rice, or a quick pasta sauce, and you'll have a head start on dinner with almost no extra effort.

A few ways to make it work:

- **Use one pan for the sheet-pan shortcut.** Toss chopped vegetables and a protein with oil and salt, then roast at 425°F (220°C). Everything cooks in about 20-25 minutes with little cleanup.
- **Season in layers.** Salt as you go, then finish with something bright like lemon juice, vinegar, or fresh herbs. This makes simple food taste far more interesting.
- **Prep while the oven preheats.** Chop vegetables, mince garlic, and measure spices during those first 10 minutes so you're not scrambling later.

Even one of these habits can turn a stressful weeknight into a manageable one.
