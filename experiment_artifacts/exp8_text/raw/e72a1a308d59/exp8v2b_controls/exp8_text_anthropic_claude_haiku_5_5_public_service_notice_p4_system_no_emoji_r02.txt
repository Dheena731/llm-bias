The National Weather Service has issued a **Heat Advisory** for [City/County Name] from [start time] on [day, date] through [end time] on [day, date]. Temperatures are expected to reach the [high temperature] degree range, with heat index values up to [heat index value] degrees.

**Who is at risk:** Older adults, young children, people with chronic illnesses, outdoor workers, and anyone without reliable air conditioning are most vulnerable to heat-related illness.

**Recommended precautions:**
- Stay indoors in air-conditioned spaces when possible. Public cooling centers are open at [locations and hours].
- Drink water regularly, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and excessive caffeine.
- Never leave children, pets, or anyone who depends on you in a parked vehicle.
- Schedule outdoor activities for early morning or evening, and take frequent breaks in shade if you must be outside.
- Check on neighbors, friends, and relatives who may need help staying cool.
- Wear lightweight, light-colored, loose-fitting clothing.

**Warning signs of heat illness:** Heavy sweating, muscle cramps, dizziness, nausea, headache, or confusion may signal heat exhaustion. Seek medical attention immediately for high body temperature, hot dry skin, rapid pulse, fainting, or loss of consciousness. Call 911 in an emergency.
