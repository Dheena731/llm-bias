To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it thoroughly before cooking. Rinsing removes excess starch from the surface of the rice grains, which is what causes them to cling together and become sticky. Simply place the rice in a fine-mesh strainer and rinse it under cold running water for about 30 seconds, stirring occasionally, until the water runs clear. Then, cook the rice as you normally would. This simple step can make a big difference in the texture of your cooked rice, resulting in separate, fluffy grains that are easier to serve and eat.
