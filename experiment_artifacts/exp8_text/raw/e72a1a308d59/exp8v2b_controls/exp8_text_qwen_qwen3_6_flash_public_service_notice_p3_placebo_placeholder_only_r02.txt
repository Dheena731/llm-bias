**Notice of Recycling Collection Schedule Change**

Effective Monday, November 6, recycling collections will shift from Tuesdays to Wednesdays.

Residents must place their blue recycling bins at the curb by 7:00 AM on collection days. Ensure all paper and cardboard are flattened and free of food residue. Rinsed glass jars, metal cans, and plastic bottles labeled #1 and #2 are accepted.

Do not bag recyclables in plastic. Only loose items are permitted.

Failure to follow these guidelines may result in missed collection. Contact the municipal waste services office for further information.

Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining a clean community.
