**Quarterly Access Reviews**

As part of our ongoing effort to maintain the security and integrity of our systems, the IT department will be conducting quarterly access reviews. These reviews will ensure that all users have a legitimate business need for their access to our systems and data.

During each quarterly review, a random sample of users will be selected for review. This may involve an email or phone call to verify your current role, department, and job responsibilities. You may be asked to provide documentation or clarification regarding your access requirements.

If it is determined that your access is no longer required, you will be notified and arrangements will be made to remove your access privileges. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the IT department.

Please note that access reviews may be conducted at any time, and your cooperation is appreciated in ensuring the security and integrity of our systems.
