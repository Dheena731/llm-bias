NOTICE OF RECYCLING COLLECTION CHANGES

Effective immediately, the recycling collection schedule for our community will be adjusted as follows:

* All recyclables will now be collected on the first and third Fridays of each month, instead of the current bi-weekly schedule.
* The collection time remains unchanged, between 7am and 12pm.
* Residents are encouraged to have all recyclables placed at the curb by 6am on collection days.
* Please note that the type of materials accepted for recycling remains the same, and residents are asked to follow the guidelines for preparing materials for collection.

If you have any questions or concerns about these changes, please contact our Public Works department at (phone number) or (email address). We appreciate your cooperation and commitment to recycling in our community.
