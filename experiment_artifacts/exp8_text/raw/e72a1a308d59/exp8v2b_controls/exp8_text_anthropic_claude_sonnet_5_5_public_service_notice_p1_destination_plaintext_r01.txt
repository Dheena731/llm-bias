PUBLIC NOTICE: LIBRARY OPENING HOURS

Effective Monday, the Riverside Public Library will operate on the following schedule:

Monday to Thursday: 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
Friday: 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
Saturday: 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
Sunday: Closed

Please note the following:

- The library will be closed on all public holidays.
- The children's section closes 30 minutes before the main library.
- Book drops are available 24 hours a day at the front entrance.
- Study rooms may be reserved at the front desk or by phone up to one week in advance.

Holiday closures and any changes to these hours will be posted at the entrance and on the library's website.

For questions, please contact the front desk at 555-0142 or visit us at 120 Main Street.

Thank you for supporting your local library.
