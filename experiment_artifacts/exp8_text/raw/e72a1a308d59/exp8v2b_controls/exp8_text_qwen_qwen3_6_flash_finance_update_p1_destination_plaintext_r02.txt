We are pleased to introduce our new Monthly Spending Summary. This feature provides a clear, at-a-glance overview of your expenses for each month, including category breakdowns, trend comparisons to prior periods, and consolidated totals. You can access it directly from your dashboard to help track budgeting goals and identify spending patterns early. The report automatically updates at the start of each billing cycle so your data stays accurate and current. Contact support anytime if you need guidance on navigating or customizing your summaries.
