**Plan around a base, not a full menu.** On Sunday, cook one or two versatile bases, such as a big batch of rice, quinoa, or roasted vegetables, plus a protein like grilled chicken or hard-boiled eggs. Then mix and match them with different sauces, greens, or wraps throughout the week. You get variety without cooking five separate meals, and you can assemble lunches in under five minutes each morning.

Bonus: pack sauces and crunchy toppings separately so everything stays fresh until you eat.
