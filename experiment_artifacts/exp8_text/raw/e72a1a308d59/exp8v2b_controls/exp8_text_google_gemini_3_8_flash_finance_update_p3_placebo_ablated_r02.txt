**New Feature: Reach Your Targets with Savings Goals**

We are excited to announce that Savings Goals is now available in your mobile app. 

Whether you are building an emergency fund, planning a vacation, or buying a home, you can now set a target amount, choose a target date, and track your progress along the way. 

To set up your first goal today, open the app, go to the **Savings** section, and select **Create a Goal**.
