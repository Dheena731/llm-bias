**PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE**

Main Street between Oak Avenue and Elm Boulevard will be fully closed for vehicular and pedestrian traffic from Tuesday, November 12 at 6:00 AM through Thursday, November 14 at 5:00 PM. This closure is required for emergency water main repairs that cannot safely be performed while traffic is active.

All drivers and walkers should use the detour signed along Maple Drive. Residents and businesses within the closed block will retain direct access, and emergency vehicles will operate normally. For current conditions or route adjustments, visit citytransit.gov/closures or call (555) 012-3456.

We thank you for your cooperation while we restore critical water service to the neighborhood.

Issued by: City Public Works Department  
Date: November 8, 2024
