To plan lunches for a busy week, start by setting a daily goal for the number of lunches you want to prep in advance. For example, aim to prepare 3-4 lunches on a single day, such as Sundays. Consider your dietary preferences and restrictions, and choose recipes that can be easily scaled up or down for individual portions. Store prepped lunches in airtight containers and label them with the date and contents to ensure you can easily grab and go on the day of.
