Subject: Introducing Your New Monthly Spending Summary

Hi there,

We're pleased to let you know that a new monthly spending summary is now available in your account. This feature gives you a clear overview of where your money went each month, so you can understand your habits and plan ahead with more confidence.

Each summary includes:

- Total spending for the month, compared with the previous month
- A breakdown of spending by category, such as groceries, dining, transportation, and bills
- Your largest transactions and recurring charges
- Helpful notes on any unusual changes in your spending

Summaries are generated automatically at the start of each month and can be found under the Reports tab in your dashboard. You can also choose to receive a short email notification when your summary is ready.

If you have questions or feedback about the new summary, please reply to this message or contact our support team. We'd love to hear how it's working for you.

Thank you for banking with us.
