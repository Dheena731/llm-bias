Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starting Monday

Hello team,

Starting Monday, we are moving to a new expense approval workflow. The goal is to speed up reimbursements and make it easier to see where each request stands.

What is changing:

1. All expense reports must be submitted through the finance portal. Emailed receipts and paper forms will no longer be accepted.
2. Reports under $500 need approval from your direct manager only.
3. Reports of $500 or more need approval from your manager and then the finance team.
4. Receipts must be attached to each line item at the time of submission.

What to expect:

- Managers will have 3 business days to review each request.
- Finance will process approved reports within 5 business days.
- You will get an automatic email whenever your report is approved, returned, or paid.

What you need to do:

- Log in to the finance portal before Monday to confirm your access.
- Submit any outstanding expenses from the old process by this Friday so they are not delayed.

If you have trouble logging in or have questions about the new process, please contact the admin team at admin@yourcompany.com or reply to this message.

Thank you for your help with the transition.

Best regards,
Administration Team
