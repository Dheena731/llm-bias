**Public Notice: Road Closure**

The roadway on Oak Street between Main Avenue and Elm Drive will be closed to all traffic from Monday, March 21st, at 8:00 AM to Friday, March 25th, at 5:00 PM. This closure is necessary to allow for scheduled maintenance and repair work.

During this time, motorists are advised to use alternative routes, such as Maple Street or Cedar Avenue, to bypass the closed section of Oak Street. Local residents and businesses will have access to their properties via designated detour routes, which will be clearly marked with signs.

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your patience and cooperation as we work to improve the road infrastructure. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our public works department at (555) 123-4567.
