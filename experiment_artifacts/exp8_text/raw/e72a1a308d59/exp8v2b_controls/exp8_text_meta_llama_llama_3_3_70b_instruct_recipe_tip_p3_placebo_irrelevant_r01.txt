Here's a short cooking tip for making rice less sticky:

To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it thoroughly before cooking. Rinse the rice in a fine mesh strainer under cold running water for about 30 seconds to remove excess starch. Then, add a small amount of oil or non-stick cooking spray to the pot before adding the rice and water. This will help prevent the rice grains from clinging together. Additionally, use the right water ratio (usually 1:1.25 or 1:1.5 water to rice) and avoid overmixing or stirring the rice too much while it's cooking. By following these tips, you should be able to achieve fluffy and separate rice grains with minimal stickiness.
