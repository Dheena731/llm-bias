Important Security Notice: 

To enhance the security of your account, we recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA) as soon as possible. This additional layer of protection will help prevent unauthorized access to your account by requiring a second form of verification, such as a code sent to your phone or a biometric scan, in addition to your password. Enabling 2FA will significantly reduce the risk of your account being compromised. Instructions on how to enable 2FA can be found in the account settings section of our website. If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact our support team.
