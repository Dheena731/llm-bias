**Enable Two-Factor Authentication for Enhanced Security**

To protect your account from unauthorized access, we highly recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA). 2FA requires both a password and a verification code, which is sent to your phone or generated by an authenticator app, to log in to your account.

Enabling 2FA provides an additional layer of security and helps prevent:

* Unauthorized account access
* Password cracking and phishing attacks
* Identity theft and cyber attacks

To enable 2FA:

1. Log in to your account
2. Go to your account settings
3. Follow the prompts to set up 2FA
4. Install an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator)
5. Configure the app to receive verification codes

Once 2FA is enabled, you will be required to enter a verification code sent to your phone or generated by the authenticator app each time you log in to your account. This will ensure that only you can access your account, even if someone has your password.

**Don't wait – enable 2FA today and protect your account from potential threats!**
