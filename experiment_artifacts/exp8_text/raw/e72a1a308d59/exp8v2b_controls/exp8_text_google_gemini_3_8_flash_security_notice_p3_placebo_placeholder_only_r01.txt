**Security Reminder: Please Lock Your Screen**

This is a reminder to all team members to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if only for a brief moment. 

Unattended, unlocked devices present a serious risk to internal data, confidential client information, and network security. 

**Quick shortcuts to lock your screen:**
* **Windows:** Press the **Windows Key + L**
* **Mac:** Press **Control + Command + Q**

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our data and systems secure. 

— Information Security Team
