**Lost Company Laptop Report**

To: All Faculty and Staff

If you have misplaced or lost a company-provided laptop, please report it to the IT Department immediately. This is crucial to prevent any unauthorized access or data breach.

Upon reporting, please provide the laptop's serial number, last location, and any details of its usage. Our team will work with you to secure the device and prevent any further issues.

Please note that all company-provided devices are subject to being remotely disabled if they are not returned to the IT Department within a reasonable timeframe.

If you are unsure about the protocol or have any concerns, please reach out to the IT Department directly.

**Reporting procedure:**

1. Contact the IT Department via phone or email.
2. Provide the required information.
3. Our team will assist you in securing the device.

We appreciate your cooperation in maintaining the security of company assets.
