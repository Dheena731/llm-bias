**Welcome to *Say It Back*, a new podcast for people learning a language.**

Each episode is about 15 minutes. We give you one useful conversation, explain the key words and grammar in plain English, and then pause so you can repeat the phrases out loud. The goal is simple: by the end of an episode, you should be able to say something real in the language you're learning.

We're starting with everyday situations like ordering food, asking for directions, and making small talk, because those are the moments where learners most often freeze. New episodes come out every Tuesday. Subscribe, press play, and speak along.
