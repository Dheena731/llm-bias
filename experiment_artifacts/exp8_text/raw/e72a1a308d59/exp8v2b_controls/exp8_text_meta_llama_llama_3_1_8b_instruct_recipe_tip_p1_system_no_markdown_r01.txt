Here's a short cooking tip for preparing a weeknight dinner:

**Quick and Easy One-Pot Pasta**

Are you tired of spending too much time in the kitchen after a long day? Try making a one-pot pasta dish that's ready in under 30 minutes. Here's how:

* Cook 1 pound of pasta, such as penne or rotini, according to the package directions in a large pot with 2 cups of chicken broth.
* Add 1 tablespoon of olive oil, 1 onion, diced, and 1 clove of garlic, minced, to the pot and cook until the onion is translucent.
* Add 1 cup of cherry tomatoes, halved, and 1 cup of shredded mozzarella cheese to the pot. Stir to combine.
* Cook for an additional 5-7 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and the pasta is al dente.
* Serve hot and enjoy!

This quick and easy one-pot pasta dish is a weeknight dinner staple that's sure to please even the pickiest eaters.
