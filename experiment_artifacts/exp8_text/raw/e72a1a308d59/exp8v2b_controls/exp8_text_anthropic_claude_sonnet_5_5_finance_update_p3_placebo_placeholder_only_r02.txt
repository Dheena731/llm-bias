**Introducing Savings Goals**

We've added a new way to help you save for what matters most. With Savings Goals, you can set a target amount, choose a deadline, and track your progress all in one place.

**What you can do:**

- **Create a goal in seconds.** Name it, set the amount you're aiming for, and pick a target date. Whether it's a vacation, a new laptop, or a rainy-day fund, you decide what you're saving toward.
- **See your progress at a glance.** A visual progress bar shows how close you are, and we'll calculate how much you need to set aside each week or month to stay on track.
- **Automate your savings.** Set up recurring transfers from your checking account so your goal grows without any extra effort.
- **Stay motivated.** Get a gentle notification when you hit milestones at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100%.
- **Save for multiple goals at once.** Create as many goals as you need and see them all together on one screen.

**How to get started:**

Open the app, tap the Savings tab, and select "New Goal." It takes less than a minute to set up.

Savings Goals is rolling out to all users over the next few days. If you don't see it yet, check back soon, and make sure your app is updated to the latest version.

We'd love to hear what you think. Send us feedback through the Help menu in the app, and let us know what else would make saving easier for you.
