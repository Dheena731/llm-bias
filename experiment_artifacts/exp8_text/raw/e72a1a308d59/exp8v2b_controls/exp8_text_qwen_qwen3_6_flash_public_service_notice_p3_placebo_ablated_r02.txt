**NOTICE: CHANGES TO RECYCLING COLLECTION**

To all residents:

Please be advised that we are updating our recycling collection program to improve service reliability and reduce contamination. These changes begin on **Monday, October 7, 2024**.

**What is changing:**
• Recycling collection days will shift from Mondays to Tuesdays.
• Only clean, dry, and loosely sorted items may be placed in your blue bin. Plastic shopping bags, food waste, garden trimmings, and hazardous materials must go in your regular trash or at approved drop-off locations.
• Bins must be placed at the curb no later than 6:00 AM on collection day. Any bin left out after 8:00 PM the evening before will not be collected until the following Tuesday.

**What you need to do:**
Update your calendar for the new collection day. Review the attached quick-reference guide for acceptable items, and ensure bins are empty of trash and free of plastic bags before placing them outside.

For a complete item list, translation services, or reporting issues, please visit [YourCity/Agency Website] or call [Phone Number].

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our community clean and efficient.
