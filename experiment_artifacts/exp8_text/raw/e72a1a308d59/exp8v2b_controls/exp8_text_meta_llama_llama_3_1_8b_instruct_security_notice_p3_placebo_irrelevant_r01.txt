Here is a short notice reminding staff to lock their screens:

**Reminder: Lock Your Screens**

As a security measure, please remember to lock your computer screen when you step away from your desk. This helps protect your work and prevent unauthorized access to company information.

If you have a password-protected login, it's especially important to lock your screen to prevent others from accessing sensitive data. Simply click the "Lock" button or press the Windows key + L (Windows) or Command + L (Mac) to secure your screen.

Thank you for helping to maintain a secure work environment.
