Subject: Quarterly Access Review Notice

Hello everyone,

As part of our ongoing security and compliance efforts, the quarterly user access review will begin on the first Monday of next month and must be completed within two weeks.

Managers and system owners will receive a list of the users and permissions under their responsibility. Please review each entry and confirm that the access is still needed, update permissions that no longer match a person's role, and flag accounts belonging to anyone who has left the team or changed positions.

Reviewers who have not responded by the deadline will receive a reminder, and unresolved items will be escalated to department leadership. Access that is not confirmed may be suspended until it is verified.

If you have questions about the process or need help interpreting a permission, contact the IT Security team at the helpdesk. Thank you for your prompt attention. Regular reviews help keep our systems and data secure.

Thank you,
IT Administration
