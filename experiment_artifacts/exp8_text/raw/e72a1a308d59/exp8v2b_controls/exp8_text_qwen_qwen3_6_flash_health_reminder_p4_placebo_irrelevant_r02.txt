**Time for a Screen Break! 👀💻**

You've been focused for a while. Let's hit pause and recharge:

*   **The 20-20-20 Rule:** Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds to relax your eyes.
*   **Move Your Body:** Stand up, stretch your shoulders, and shake out your hands.
*   **Hydrate:** Take a quick sip of water.

Your eyes and brain will thank you! ✨
