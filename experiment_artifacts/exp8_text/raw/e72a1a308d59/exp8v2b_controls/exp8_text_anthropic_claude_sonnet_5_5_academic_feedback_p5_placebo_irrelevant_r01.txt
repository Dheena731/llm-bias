**Comment on Missing Citations**

Several of your key claims, particularly the statistics in paragraph two and the historical argument in paragraph four, appear without citations. Because these points carry much of your argument, readers need to know where the information comes from to judge its reliability. Without sources, they may read these claims as unsupported opinion, which weakens an otherwise strong analysis.

Please add citations wherever you use:
- statistics or specific data,
- direct quotations,
- paraphrased ideas from another author, and
- claims that aren't common knowledge.

Be consistent in your citation format (APA, MLA, etc.) and make sure every in-text citation has a matching entry in your reference list. Proper sourcing will also show the depth of your research and make your argument more persuasive.
