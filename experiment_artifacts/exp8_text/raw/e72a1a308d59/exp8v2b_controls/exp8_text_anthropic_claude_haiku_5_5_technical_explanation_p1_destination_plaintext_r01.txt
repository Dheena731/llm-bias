Latency is the delay between a user's action and the application's response, and it matters a great deal in web applications. When a page takes too long to load or a button takes too long to respond, users become frustrated and are more likely to leave. Even small delays can reduce engagement and conversions, since people expect near-instant feedback from modern websites. High latency also makes an application feel sluggish and unreliable, which can erode trust in the product. Search engines consider page speed as a ranking factor, so slow responses can reduce visibility as well. For applications that depend on real-time interaction, such as chat, video calls, or online games, latency directly affects whether the experience works at all. Reducing latency through techniques like caching, content delivery networks, efficient code, and optimized database queries is therefore a key part of building a good web application.
