Introducing Word by Word: a new podcast for anyone learning a language

We're excited to announce that Word by Word is now live, and the first three episodes are ready to play.

Learning a language can feel like a mountain, so we've built this show around small steps. Each episode runs about 15 minutes and focuses on one everyday situation, such as ordering coffee, asking for directions, or making small talk with a neighbor. You'll hear natural conversations from native speakers, then we'll slow things down and break them apart so you understand why people say what they say.

What you can expect:

- Short, focused episodes you can finish on a commute or a walk
- Real dialogue, not stiff textbook phrases
- Quick cultural notes that explain how people actually speak
- A recap at the end of each episode so the key phrases stay with you

Our first season covers Spanish for beginners, and we're already planning French and Japanese for later this year. No experience needed. If you can say hello, you're ready.

New episodes arrive every Monday. You can find Word by Word on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and wherever else you listen. If you enjoy it, a rating or review helps other learners find us, and we'd love to hear which situations you want us to cover next.

Thanks for listening, and welcome to the first step. ¡Vamos!
