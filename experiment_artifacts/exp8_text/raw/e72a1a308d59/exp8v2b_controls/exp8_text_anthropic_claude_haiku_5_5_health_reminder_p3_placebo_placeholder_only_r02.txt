Subject: Time to Book Your Annual Health Checkup

Hello,

This is a friendly reminder that it's time to schedule your annual health checkup. Regular checkups help catch potential health issues early, keep your vaccinations up to date, and give you a chance to discuss any concerns with your doctor.

Booking is quick and easy. Simply call the clinic during office hours, or check whether your provider offers online or app-based scheduling. If you're unsure when your last visit was, your records or patient portal should show the details.

Appointments tend to fill up quickly, especially in the months leading into the end of the year, so the sooner you book, the better your choice of times will be.

Taking a few minutes to schedule your checkup now is one of the simplest ways to look after your long-term health. We look forward to seeing you.
