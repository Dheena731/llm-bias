PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE: LIBRARY HOURS OF OPERATION

Effective immediately, all municipal library locations will observe the following schedule:

Monday through Thursday: 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM
Friday: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
Saturday: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM

All branches will remain closed on recognized public holidays and during scheduled facility maintenance. Service schedules are subject to change; please consult our official website or contact the main administrative desk at 555-0123 for current updates and holiday closures.
