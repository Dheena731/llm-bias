**NOTICE: Procedure for Reporting Lost Company Laptops**

To protect sensitive company and client data, all employees are required to report any lost or missing company-issued laptop immediately upon realizing it is gone. 

Prompt notification allows the IT and Security teams to remotely lock or wipe the device to prevent unauthorized access.

**Immediate Steps to Take:**

1. **Contact IT and Security Immediately:**
   * **IT Helpdesk:** [Phone Number] | [Email Address]
   * **Security Operations:** [Phone Number] | [Email Address]

2. **Provide Key Details:**
   * Last known location, date, and approximate time the device was misplaced.
   * Asset tag number or device serial number (if known).
   * Brief explanation of the circumstances (e.g., left on transit, misplaced in an office).
   * Confirmation of whether sensitive or confidential data was stored locally.

3. **File a Police Report (if applicable):**
   * If you suspect the device was stolen, file a report with local law enforcement and provide the police report number to the IT Helpdesk.

Failure to report a lost device in a timely manner poses a critical security risk and may lead to disciplinary action. Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our systems and data secure.

**IT & Information Security Team**
