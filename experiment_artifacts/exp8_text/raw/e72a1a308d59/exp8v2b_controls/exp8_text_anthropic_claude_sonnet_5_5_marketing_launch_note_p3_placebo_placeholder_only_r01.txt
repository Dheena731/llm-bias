# Introducing Clearpath: The Budgeting Spreadsheet That Does the Math for You

Tracking your money shouldn't feel like a second job. Clearpath is a budgeting spreadsheet template that turns scattered income and expenses into a clear monthly plan, with no accounting background required.

**What's inside**

- **One-page dashboard:** See income, spending, savings, and what's left at a glance.
- **Automatic category totals:** Housing, food, transportation, debt, and more add up as you go.
- **Savings goal tracker:** Watch progress toward an emergency fund, a trip, or a down payment.
- **Debt payoff view:** Compare snowball and avalanche strategies side by side.
- **12-month overview:** Spot trends and plan ahead with a full-year summary.

**Built to be simple**

Clearpath works in Excel, Google Sheets, and Numbers. Formulas are pre-built and protected, so nothing breaks when you start typing. Color-coded cells show you right away when a category is on track or over budget.
