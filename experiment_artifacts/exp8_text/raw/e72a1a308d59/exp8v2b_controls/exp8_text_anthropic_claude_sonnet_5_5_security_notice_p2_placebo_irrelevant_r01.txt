**NOTICE: Beware of Phishing Emails**

**To:** All Staff
**From:** IT Security Team
**Date:** [Insert Date]

We have seen an increase in phishing emails targeting employees. These messages are designed to look legitimate, but their goal is to steal login credentials, financial information, or install malicious software.

**Warning signs to watch for:**

- Urgent or threatening language (e.g., "Your account will be closed today")
- Unexpected attachments or links
- Sender addresses that look slightly "off" or don't match the company name
- Requests for passwords, payment details, or gift cards
- Poor spelling or unusual formatting

**What you should do:**

1. **Don't click** links or open attachments in suspicious emails.
2. **Don't reply** or provide any personal or company information.
3. **Report it** using the "Report Phishing" button in your email client, or forward the message to security@yourcompany.com.
4. **Delete** the email after reporting.

If you believe you have already clicked a link or shared information, contact IT immediately at [phone/extension]. Prompt reporting helps us limit potential damage.

When in doubt, verify the request by contacting the sender through a known, trusted method, not by using the contact details in the email.

Thank you for helping keep our organization secure.

**IT Security Team**
