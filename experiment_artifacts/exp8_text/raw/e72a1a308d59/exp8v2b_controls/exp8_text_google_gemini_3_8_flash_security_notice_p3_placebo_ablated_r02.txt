**Subject: Reminder: Please Lock Your Screen When Leaving Your Desk**

Dear Staff,

Please remember to lock your computer screen every time you step away from your workstation, even if only for a brief moment. 

Locking your device helps ensure that sensitive company and customer information remains secure. 

You can lock your computer quickly using these keyboard shortcuts:
* **Windows:** Press **Windows Key + L**
* **Mac:** Press **Control + Command + Q**

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our workplace secure.
