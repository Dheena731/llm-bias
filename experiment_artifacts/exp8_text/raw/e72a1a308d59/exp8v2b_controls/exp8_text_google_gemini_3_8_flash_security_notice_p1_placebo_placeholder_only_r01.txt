**SECURITY REMINDER: The Risks of Password Reuse**

Reusing passwords across multiple accounts poses a critical risk to workplace security. When an external service or personal account suffers a data breach, cybercriminals frequently use those stolen credentials to attempt automated attacks on corporate networks, putting internal systems and sensitive data at risk.

To keep company information secure, please adhere to the following standards:

* **Use Unique Passwords:** Never use your workplace password for personal accounts, including email, banking, social media, or retail websites. Every business account must have its own distinct credential.
* **Adopt Strong Passphrases:** Create passwords using a combination of at least sixteen characters, including uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Longer passphrases made of unrelated words are both stronger and easier to remember.
* **Rely on an Approved Password Manager:** Rather than memorizing multiple complex passwords, use the organization's approved password management software to generate and securely store them.

If you are currently using your workplace password on any external platform, change it immediately. 

For assistance with updating credentials or setting up a password manager, please reach out directly to the IT Support team.
