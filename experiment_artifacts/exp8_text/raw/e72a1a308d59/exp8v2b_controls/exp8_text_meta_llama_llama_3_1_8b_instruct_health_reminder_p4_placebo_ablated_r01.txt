Here is a short reminder to take short breaks from screens:

**Take a Break from Screens**

In today's digital age, it's easy to get caught up in the constant stream of notifications, emails, and social media updates. However, taking short breaks from screens can have a significant impact on your physical and mental well-being.

**Why Take a Break?**

Research shows that prolonged screen time can lead to eye strain, headaches, and fatigue. It can also negatively impact your mood, relationships, and productivity. By taking short breaks from screens, you can:

* Reduce eye strain and improve your focus
* Boost your mood and energy levels
* Improve your relationships by being more present and engaged
* Increase your productivity and efficiency

**How to Take a Break**

So, what can you do? Here are a few simple tips:

* Set a timer to remind yourself to take a break every 60-90 minutes
* Choose a physical activity, like taking a short walk or stretching
* Do something creative, like drawing, writing, or playing a musical instrument
* Simply get up from your desk and take a few minutes to stretch, move around, and refresh your mind

**Make It a Habit**

By incorporating short breaks from screens into your daily routine, you can improve your overall well-being and reduce the negative effects of prolonged screen time. Try to make it a habit, and see the positive difference it can make in your life!
