**Reminder: Lock Your Screen When You Step Away**

Hello everyone,

A quick reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you leave your desk, even if it's just for a minute. An unlocked screen can expose confidential information and leave our systems vulnerable to unauthorized access.

**How to lock your screen:**
- **Windows:** Press **Windows key + L**
- **Mac:** Press **Control + Command + Q**

It takes only a second and helps keep our data, our clients, and our colleagues safe.

Thank you for doing your part.

*IT & Security Team*
