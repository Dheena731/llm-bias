**Tip: Rinse your rice before cooking.**

Place the rice in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer and rinse it under cold running water, swirling with your hand, until the water runs mostly clear (usually 3 to 5 rinses). This washes away the excess surface starch that makes grains clump together.

**Bonus tricks:**
- Use the right ratio: about 1¾ cups water per 1 cup long-grain white rice.
- Don't stir while it cooks, since stirring releases more starch.
- Let it rest, covered, for 10 minutes after cooking, then fluff with a fork.
