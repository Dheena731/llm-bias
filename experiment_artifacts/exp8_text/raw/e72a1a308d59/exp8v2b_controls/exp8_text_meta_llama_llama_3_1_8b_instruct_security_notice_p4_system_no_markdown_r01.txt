**Enabling Two-Factor Authentication**

To enhance the security of your account, we highly recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA). This additional layer of protection requires both your password and a verification code sent to your registered phone or email to log in.

To enable 2FA:

1. Go to your account settings.
2. Look for the "Security" or "Authentication" section.
3. Follow the prompts to enable two-factor authentication.
4. You will be asked to provide a phone number or email address for receiving verification codes.
5. You will also be prompted to set up an authenticator app, if desired.

Enabling 2FA will significantly reduce the risk of unauthorized access to your account.
