**Introducing Tally: Run Your Small Business with Less Stress**

Today we are launching Tally, the all-in-one app built for small business owners who would rather spend time on their customers than on paperwork.

**Why we built it**

Most small business owners juggle invoices in one tool, appointments in another, and expenses in a shoebox. Tally brings it all into one simple app, so you always know where your business stands.

**What you can do with Tally**

- **Send invoices in seconds.** Create professional invoices and get paid faster with built-in payment links.
- **Track expenses effortlessly.** Snap a photo of a receipt and Tally categorizes it for you.
- **Manage your schedule.** Book appointments, send reminders, and cut down on no-shows.
- **See the full picture.** A clear dashboard shows income, outstanding payments, and upcoming work at a glance.

**Launch offer**

For the first 30 days, new users get three months of Tally Pro free. No credit card required.

**Get started today**

Download Tally from the App Store or Google Play, or sign up at tallyapp.com. Setup takes about five minutes.
