**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for our area from 11:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. today. Temperatures are expected to reach the mid-90s°F, with heat index values near 105°F.

**Residents are urged to take the following precautions:**

- **Stay indoors** in air-conditioned spaces whenever possible. If you lack air conditioning, visit a cooling center or public library.
- **Drink water regularly**, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and sugary drinks.
- **Check on neighbors, elderly relatives, and anyone living alone**, especially those with chronic illness.
- **Never leave children, pets, or anyone who depends on you in a parked vehicle.**
- **Limit outdoor activity** to early morning or evening, and take frequent breaks in shade if you must be outside.

**Watch for signs of heat illness:** heavy sweating, dizziness, nausea, muscle cramps, or confusion. Move to a cool place and seek medical help if symptoms appear. Call 911 for fainting, confusion, or a body temperature above 103°F.

For updates, monitor local news and weather broadcasts or visit weather.gov.

*Issued by the Office of Emergency Management.*
