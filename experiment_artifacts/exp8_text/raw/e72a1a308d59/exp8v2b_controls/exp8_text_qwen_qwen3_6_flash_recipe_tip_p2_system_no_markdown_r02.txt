Treat soft herbs like basil and cilantro like cut flowers by trimming the stems and placing them upright in a glass with about an inch of water. Loosely cover the tops with a reusable plastic bag or produce bag and store in the refrigerator, changing the water every two days. For sturdy herbs like rosemary and thyme, wrap them loosely in a slightly damp paper towel and keep them in a crisper drawer. Always store herbs dry and unwashed until you are ready to cook, since excess moisture is the fastest way to cause wilting and mold.
