We have launched a new monthly spending summary to help you track your expenses more easily. Each month, the summary breaks down your spending by category, displays your total outflows, and highlights how your costs compare to previous months. You can access it from the main dashboard under the Reports tab. This feature is designed to give you clear, at-a-glance insights into your financial habits so you can budget with confidence. Please check your latest summary when you log in, and contact support if you need help finding or reading the data.
