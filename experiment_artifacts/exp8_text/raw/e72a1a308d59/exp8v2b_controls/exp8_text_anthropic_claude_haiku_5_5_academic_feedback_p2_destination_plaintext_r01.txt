3. Consider adding the time frame for data collection. Readers will want to know whether responses were gathered over one week or several months, especially if there were any events during that period that could affect responses.

Overall, this is a solid draft. Once these points are addressed, the section should be easy for a reader to replicate. Let me know if you'd like to talk through the analysis subsection together.
