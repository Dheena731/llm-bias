To Our Valued Customers,

We are writing to share an important update regarding our payment processing terms. 

Due to rising interchange costs and ongoing investments in transaction security, our processing fees for credit and debit card transactions are being adjusted. This update allows us to maintain the reliability, speed, and fraud protection standards required to process your payments safely.

The revised rates will take effect with the start of the upcoming billing cycle. A comprehensive breakdown of the updated rate schedule has been added to your online account dashboard under the billing section. 

We appreciate that fee adjustments require planning on your part, and our support team is available to answer any questions or help you explore alternative payment methods. 

Thank you for your continued trust and partnership.
