We're excited to announce the launch of our new savings goal feature! With this addition, users can now set and track specific savings targets, helping them stay on top of their financial goals. The feature allows users to define a savings goal with a set amount and deadline, and receives a weekly summary of progress towards that goal. This feature is designed to help users achieve their short-term savings objectives, such as building an emergency fund or saving for a big purchase. The savings goal feature is now available to all users, and can be accessed by visiting the "Savings" tab in the app.
