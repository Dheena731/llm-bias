NOTICE OF HEAT ADVISORY

A heat advisory has been issued for the region, with temperatures expected to reach extreme highs over the next several days. The National Weather Service has forecasted that temperatures will soar to 108°F, with heat index values making it feel even hotter.

Residents are advised to take precautions to protect themselves from the heat, especially those who are vulnerable to heat-related illnesses such as the elderly, young children, and people with chronic health conditions. Symptoms of heat-related illnesses include dizziness, nausea, headaches, and fatigue.

The public is urged to stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water, taking cool showers or baths, and staying in air-conditioned spaces when possible. Residents are also advised to check on vulnerable neighbors and friends, and to limit outdoor activities to early morning or evening hours when temperatures are cooler.

The heat advisory is expected to remain in effect until further notice, and residents are urged to stay informed through local news and weather reports.
