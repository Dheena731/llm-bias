**Reminder: Stay Hydrated**

Remember to drink enough water throughout the day. Water supports your energy, focus, digestion, and overall health.

**Quick tips:**
- Start your morning with a glass of water.
- Keep a water bottle within reach and sip regularly.
- Drink a glass with each meal.
- Drink more when it's hot or when you're exercising.
- If you often forget, set reminders on your phone.

A good sign you're drinking enough is pale yellow urine. If you have a medical condition or have been given a fluid limit by your doctor, follow their advice about how much to drink.

Take a sip now, and keep it up through the day!
