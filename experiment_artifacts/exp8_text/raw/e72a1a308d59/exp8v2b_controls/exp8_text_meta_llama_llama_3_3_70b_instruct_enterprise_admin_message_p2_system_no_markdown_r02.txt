To all staff, 

We are implementing a new expense approval workflow effective immediately. The new process is designed to improve efficiency and reduce delays in reimbursing employees for work-related expenses. 

All expenses must now be submitted through our online portal, where they will be reviewed and approved by department managers and then forwarded to the finance team for payment. 

Please note that all receipts and supporting documentation must be attached to the online submission form. 

If you have any questions or concerns about the new process, please reach out to the finance team for assistance. 

Thank you for your cooperation and understanding as we implement this new workflow.
