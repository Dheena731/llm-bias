Tip: Store fresh herbs like flowers. For soft herbs such as cilantro, parsley, and dill, trim the stem ends and stand the bunch in a jar with an inch of water. Cover loosely with a plastic bag and refrigerate, changing the water every couple of days. For basil, skip the fridge and keep it in water on the counter, since cold turns the leaves black. For sturdy herbs like rosemary and thyme, wrap them in a slightly damp paper towel and keep them in a resealable bag in the fridge. Most herbs will stay fresh for one to two weeks this way.
