**Workplace Notice: Beware of Phishing Emails**

Please remain alert for suspicious emails that request passwords, financial information, urgent action, or unfamiliar links and attachments.

- Check the sender’s address carefully.
- Do not click suspicious links or open unexpected attachments.
- Never share passwords or sensitive information by email.
- When in doubt, contact IT or verify the request through a trusted channel.
- Report suspected phishing emails to the IT help desk immediately.

Staying cautious helps protect our organization and information.
