**The Secret to Non-Sticky Rice: Rinse, Rinse, Rinse!**

Are you tired of serving up a plate of sticky, clumpy rice? The culprit behind this culinary crime is often excess starch. To prevent this, try rinsing your rice thoroughly before cooking it. Here's how:

1. Measure out the correct amount of rice for your recipe. For every 1 cup of rice, use 1 3/4 cups of water.
2. Rinse the rice in a fine-mesh strainer under cold running water. This will help remove excess starch and impurities.
3. Repeat the rinsing process 2-3 times, or until the water runs clear.
4. After rinsing, drain the rice well and cook it according to your recipe's instructions.

By following these simple steps, you'll end up with a fluffy, non-sticky batch of rice every time.
