**Welcome to *Say It Back*, a new podcast for people who want to speak a second language, not just study it.**

Each fifteen-minute episode takes one everyday situation, like ordering coffee, asking for directions, or chatting with a neighbor, and walks through it in the language you're learning. You hear native speakers model the phrases, then get a pause to say them yourself before we move on.

This show is for beginner and lower-intermediate learners who can read a little but freeze when someone speaks to them. You don't need a particular app or a classroom. You need about fifteen minutes a day and a willingness to talk to an empty room.

Season one covers Spanish, French, and Japanese. New episodes come out every Tuesday, starting next week, and each one comes with a free phrase list you can follow on a commute or while cooking.

The method is simple: listen once to understand, then listen again and speak along.
