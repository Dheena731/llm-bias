Reviewer Comment:

The manuscript's central research question is difficult to pin down. In the Introduction, the authors state that they aim to "explore how digital engagement affects student outcomes," but the paper does not specify which digital engagement behaviors are of interest (e.g., time on platform, forum participation, assignment submission patterns), which student outcomes are being measured (grades, retention, self-reported learning, or skill acquisition), or what population the question concerns. The hypothesis section then introduces a causal claim about "improved learning," which the question as stated does not support. Because the question oscillates between descriptive and causal framings, it is unclear whether the study is intended to establish a relationship, estimate an effect, or characterize patterns.

I recommend that the authors restate the research question in precise terms, identifying the independent variable, dependent variable, population, and the type of inference they seek. Doing so would also clarify whether the chosen methods, which are primarily correlational, are appropriate for the claims made in the Discussion. Without this clarification, I cannot assess whether the conclusions follow from the evidence presented.
