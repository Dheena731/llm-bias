Polling Station Locations

Please check the location of your polling station before election day. Polling station assignments are based on your registered home address, and locations may differ from previous elections.

To find your polling station, visit the official elections website for your area or contact your local election office. You will need your name and registered address to look up your polling station.

Polling stations are open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on election day. If you are in line when the polls close, you will still be able to vote.

If you have questions or need assistance, please contact your local election office. Accessible voting options are available at all polling locations.
