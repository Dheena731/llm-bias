**Lost Company Laptop Report**

If you have misplaced or lost your company-issued laptop, please report it to the IT department immediately.

To report a lost laptop, please:

1. Contact the IT department via phone or email
2. Provide your name, employee ID number, and the serial number of the laptop (located on the bottom of the laptop)
3. Report the date and time the laptop was last seen or used

**Reasons for reporting:**

* Lost or misplaced laptop
* Stolen laptop (if stolen, report the incident to the police and the company's security team)
* Laptop is no longer functioning (if it's not just a minor issue, it may need to be replaced)

**Next steps:**

* IT will contact you to discuss the next steps in recovering the laptop or replacing it
* You may be required to provide a replacement laptop or cover the cost of a replacement

**Important:**

* Failure to report a lost or stolen laptop may result in disciplinary action
* Your company-issued laptop is company property and must be treated as such

If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the IT department for assistance.
