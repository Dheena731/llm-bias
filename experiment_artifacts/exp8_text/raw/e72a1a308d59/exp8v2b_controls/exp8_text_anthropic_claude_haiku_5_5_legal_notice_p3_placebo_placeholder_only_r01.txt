**Notice Regarding the Use of Cookies**

This notice explains how this website uses cookies and similar technologies, and describes the choices available to you.

**1. What Are Cookies**

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit it. They allow the website to recognize your device on later visits, remember certain preferences, and gather information about how the site is used. Similar technologies, such as local storage and pixel tags, are subject to this notice in the same manner as cookies.

**2. Categories of Cookies We Use**

This website uses the following categories of cookies:

(a) *Strictly necessary cookies.* These are required for the website to function, including for page navigation, security, and access to protected areas. They cannot be disabled through our cookie settings, and no consent is required for their use.

(b) *Performance and analytics cookies.* These collect anonymized information about visitor activity, such as pages viewed and the time spent on each page, to help us improve the website. These cookies are used only with your consent.

(c) *Functionality cookies.* These remember choices you make, such as language or display preferences, to provide a more personalized experience. These cookies are used only with your consent.

(d) *Marketing cookies.* These may be used to deliver advertisements that are more relevant to you and to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns. These cookies are used only with your consent.

**3. Consent**

Where consent is required, we will request it through a cookie banner when you first visit the website. You may accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or adjust your preferences by category. Your choice will be stored for a period of twelve months, after which you may be asked again.

**4. Withdrawing Consent**

You may withdraw or change your consent at any time by selecting "Cookie Settings" in the website footer. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

**5. Managing Cookies Through Your Browser**

Most web browsers allow you to view, delete, or block cookies through their settings. Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of this website from functioning correctly.

**6. Third-Party Cookies**

Some cookies are set by third parties that provide services to us, such as analytics and embedded media providers. Those third parties are responsible for their own use of the information collected. You may review their privacy and cookie policies on their respective websites.

**7. Changes to This Notice**

We may update this notice from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. The date of the most recent revision will appear at the top of this page, and continued use of the website following any update constitutes acknowledgment of the revised notice where permitted by law.

**8. Contact**

Questions about this notice or our use of cookies may be directed to the privacy contact listed in the website's Privacy Policy.
