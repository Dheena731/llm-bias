Latency matters in web applications because it directly affects user experience and overall performance. When a user interacts with a web application, such as clicking a button or submitting a form, the delay between the user's action and the application's response is measured in latency. High latency can lead to frustration and abandonment of the application. Even small delays can cause users to feel that the application is slow or unresponsive. As users expect instant feedback and fast interactions, high latency can lead to a decrease in user engagement and conversion rates. In addition, high latency can also impact the overall efficiency and productivity of users, making it essential for web applications to minimize latency to provide a smooth and responsive experience.
