**Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection Schedule**

Beginning next Monday, recycling collection will move from every week to every other week for all residential households. Garbage collection will continue on its regular weekly schedule.

**What you need to know:**

- **Recycling pickup:** Every other week, on the same day your recycling bin is currently collected.
- **Bin size:** Please keep your recycling bin out of the way of traffic and ensure the lid closes fully on collection day.
- **Accepted items:** Clean paper, cardboard, glass bottles and jars, metal cans, and plastic containers marked 1 and 2. Please do not place plastic bags, food waste, or electronics in your recycling bin.
- **Extra material:** If you have more recycling than fits in your bin, you may bring it to the community drop-off center on Elm Street during regular business hours.

These changes help us keep collection costs manageable while maintaining reliable service. If you have questions, please contact the Public Works Department during business hours, and thank you for your continued cooperation in keeping our community clean and sustainable.
