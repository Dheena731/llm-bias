**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY**

**To:** All customers of Northwind Outdoor Supply ("Northwind," "we," "us")
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2025

**1. Summary.** You may request a full refund for an eligible item within 30 days of the Delivery Date, provided the item meets the conditions in Section 3.

**2. Definitions.**
- "Delivery Date" means the date the carrier records as delivered to the address on your order.
- "Eligible Item" means a product purchased directly from Northwind and not listed in Section 4.

**3. Conditions for Refund.** To receive a refund, you must:
(a) submit a refund request within 30 days of the Delivery Date;
(b) return the item unused, in its original packaging, and with all included accessories; and
(c) include your order number or original receipt.

**4. Exclusions.** The following are not eligible for refund:
(a) items marked "Final Sale" at the time of purchase;
(b) custom or personalized items; and
(c) items returned more than 30 days after the Delivery Date.

**5. How to Request a Refund.** Contact Northwind Customer Support at support@northwindoutdoor.example or 1-800-555-0148 to receive a Return Authorization number. Return the item using the prepaid label we provide or a carrier of your choice. Unauthorized returns may be refused.

**6. Refund Processing.** Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will issue a refund to your original payment method within 10 business days. Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the item was defective or sent in error.

**7. Defective or Incorrect Items.** If you received a defective or incorrect item, the 30-day limit in Section 3(a) does not apply. Contact Customer Support and we will provide a full refund or replacement at no cost to you.

**8. Your Other Rights.** This notice does not limit any rights you have under applicable consumer protection laws in your jurisdiction.

**9. Changes.** Northwind may update this notice. Changes apply only to orders placed on or after the effective date of the update.
