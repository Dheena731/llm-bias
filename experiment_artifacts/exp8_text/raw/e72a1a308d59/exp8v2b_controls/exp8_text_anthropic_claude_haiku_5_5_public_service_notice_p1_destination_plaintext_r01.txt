Notice to the Public: Library Opening Hours

The Central Public Library is open to all residents at the following times:

Monday through Friday: 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
Saturday: 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
Sunday: 12:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m.

The library will be closed on all national holidays. Please check the library's website or call the front desk before your visit to confirm any holiday or temporary schedule changes.

We look forward to welcoming you.
