To elevate the flavor of your roasted vegetables, try seasoning them in layers. Start by tossing the vegetables with a drizzle of olive oil, salt, and pepper before roasting. Then, halfway through the roasting time, sprinkle with any additional seasonings you like, such as dried herbs (like thyme or rosemary), garlic powder, or paprika. Finally, just before serving, squeeze a sliver of fresh lemon juice or add a sprinkle of grated Parmesan cheese to add brightness and depth to the dish. This layered approach will help bring out the natural flavors of the vegetables and add complexity to your roasted creations.
