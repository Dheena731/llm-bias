# PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT

A Heat Advisory is in effect for our community. Temperatures and humidity are expected to reach levels that can cause heat-related illness, especially during the afternoon and early evening hours.

## Stay Safe

- **Drink plenty of water** throughout the day, even if you don't feel thirsty. Limit alcohol and caffeine.
- **Stay indoors** in air conditioning when possible. Fans alone may not be enough in extreme heat.
- **Avoid strenuous activity** outdoors during the hottest part of the day, and take frequent breaks in the shade.
- **Wear light, loose-fitting, light-colored clothing** and use sunscreen.
- **Never leave children, pets, or anyone else in a parked vehicle**, even for a few minutes.
- **Check on neighbors**, older adults, and anyone without air conditioning.

## Know the Warning Signs

**Heat exhaustion:** heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, nausea, headache, cold or clammy skin. Move to a cool place, loosen clothing, and sip water.

**Heat stroke (a medical emergency):** high body temperature, confusion, hot and dry skin, rapid pulse, or loss of consciousness. **Call 911 immediately** and try to cool the person until help arrives.

## Stay Cool

If your home gets too hot, visit an air-conditioned public space such as a library, community center, or shopping center. Outdoor workers should take regular breaks in shaded or cooled areas and drink water often.

Stay tuned to local news and weather alerts for updates.
