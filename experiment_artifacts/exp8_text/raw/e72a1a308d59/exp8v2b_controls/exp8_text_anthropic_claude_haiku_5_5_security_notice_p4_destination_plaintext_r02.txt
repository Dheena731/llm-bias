Security Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication

To help protect your account, we strongly recommend turning on two-factor authentication (2FA). With 2FA, signing in requires your password plus a second verification step, such as a code from an authenticator app or a text message. This makes it much harder for anyone else to access your account, even if they know your password.

To enable two-factor authentication:

1. Log in to your account and go to Settings.
2. Select Security, then choose Two-Factor Authentication.
3. Follow the on-screen steps to link an authenticator app or phone number.
4. Save your backup codes in a safe place.

It takes less than two minutes to set up. If you have any questions or need help, contact our support team.
