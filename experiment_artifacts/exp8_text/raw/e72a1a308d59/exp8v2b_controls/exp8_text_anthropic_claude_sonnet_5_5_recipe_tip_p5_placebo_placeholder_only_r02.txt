**Season in two stages for deeper flavor.** Before roasting, toss your vegetables with olive oil, kosher salt, and black pepper, using enough oil to coat them lightly but not pool on the pan. Salt early helps draw out moisture and encourages browning. Once the vegetables come out of the oven, hit them with the finishing touches: a squeeze of fresh lemon juice, a splash of balsamic vinegar, or a sprinkle of chopped fresh herbs like parsley or thyme. Delicate flavors such as citrus and herbs fade in high heat, so adding them at the end keeps them bright and fresh.

**Bonus:** Heartier spices like smoked paprika, cumin, and garlic powder can go on before roasting, but save fresh garlic for the last 10 minutes so it doesn't burn and turn bitter.
