Subject: Lost Company Laptop - Reporting Procedure

Notice to all staff:

If you have lost a company laptop, or suspect it has been stolen, report it immediately. Prompt reporting helps us protect company data and reduce security risks.

What to do:

1. Contact the IT Service Desk as soon as you realize the laptop is missing. Do not wait to see if it turns up.
2. Provide your name, department, the laptop's asset tag or serial number, and the time and place you last had it.
3. If the laptop was stolen, file a police report and send a copy of the report number to the IT Service Desk.
4. Do not attempt to access or recover data from the device yourself. IT will start the remote lock and wipe process.
5. Complete the Lost or Stolen Equipment Form within 24 hours and submit it to your manager.

Questions? Contact the IT Service Desk at [phone number] or [email address].

Thank you for helping keep company information secure.
