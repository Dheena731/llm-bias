**Polling Station Locations**

Attention all voters!

To ensure a smooth voting experience, please note the following polling station locations for the upcoming election:

* Precinct 1: Oakwood Elementary School, 123 Main St
* Precinct 2: City Hall, 456 Elm St
* Precinct 3: Maplewood Community Center, 789 Oak Ave
* Precinct 4: Springdale Library, 901 Maple St

Polling stations will be open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM on election day. Please verify your precinct and polling station location before heading out to vote. You can check your voter registration card or visit our website for more information.

We look forward to seeing you at the polls!
