**Introducing Your Monthly Spending Summary**

We've added a new Monthly Spending Summary to help you see where your money goes without digging through individual transactions.

**What it shows**
- **Total spent** for the month, compared with the previous month
- **Top categories**, such as groceries, dining, transportation, and bills
- **Notable changes**, like categories where spending rose or fell significantly
- **Recurring charges**, including subscriptions and regular payments

**When you'll get it**
Your summary will be available on the 1st of each month and will cover the previous month. You'll get a notification when it's ready, and you can find it anytime under **Insights → Monthly Summary**.

**Why it matters**
A quick monthly snapshot makes it easier to spot patterns, catch unexpected charges, and adjust your budget before small habits become big expenses.

**Customize it**
You can choose which accounts are included and turn notifications on or off in **Settings → Notifications**.

We'd love to hear what you think. Use the feedback button in the summary to tell us what would make it more useful.
