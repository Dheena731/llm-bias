**Treat your herbs according to their stems:**

* **Soft herbs (cilantro, parsley, mint):** Treat them like a bouquet of flowers. Trim the ends of the stems, place them upright in a small jar with an inch of water, loosely tent a plastic bag over the top, and store in the fridge. *(Exception: Keep basil on the counter at room temperature, as the cold turns its leaves black.)*
* **Hard/woody herbs (rosemary, thyme, sage):** Wrap them loosely in a slightly damp paper towel and seal them in an airtight container or zip-top bag in the refrigerator’s crisper drawer. 

*Bonus tip: Never wash herbs until you are ready to use them—excess moisture is the main culprit behind premature spoilage.*
