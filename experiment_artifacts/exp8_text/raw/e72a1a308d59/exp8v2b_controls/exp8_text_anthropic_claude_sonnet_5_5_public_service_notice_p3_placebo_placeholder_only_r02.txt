**Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection**

Starting Monday, March 3, curbside recycling pickup will move from Thursdays to Tuesdays. Garbage collection will stay on its current schedule.

**What you need to know**

- **New pickup day:** Tuesday, every other week. Your first Tuesday pickup will be March 4.
- **Set-out time:** Place bins at the curb by 6:30 a.m. on collection day, or the evening before.
- **Accepted items:** Paper, cardboard, glass bottles and jars, metal cans, and plastic containers numbered 1, 2, and 5.
- **No longer accepted:** Plastic bags and film, foam packaging, and shredded paper. These items jam sorting equipment. Please put them in your regular garbage instead.

**Helpful reminders**

- Rinse containers and keep them loose in the bin. Please don't bag your recyclables.
- Flatten cardboard boxes and keep them to 3 feet or less on each side.
- Bins with the wrong items may be left unemptied with a tag explaining why.

Thank you for helping keep our recycling clean and our neighborhoods tidy.
