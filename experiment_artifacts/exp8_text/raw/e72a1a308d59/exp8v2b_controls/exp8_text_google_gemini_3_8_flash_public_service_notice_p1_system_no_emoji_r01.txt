PUBLIC SERVICE ANNOUNCEMENT: LIBRARY OPERATING HOURS

The Central Public Library is pleased to announce its current schedule of service. 

Regular Hours of Operation:
- Monday through Thursday: 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM
- Friday and Saturday: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM
- Sunday: Closed

Patrons are welcome to utilize study areas, public computer terminals, and Wi-Fi access during these hours. The exterior book drop remains open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for all returns. 

Digital collections, including e-books and research databases, are accessible at any time through our website. For information regarding holiday closures and special events, please visit library.example.gov or call (555) 019-2834.
