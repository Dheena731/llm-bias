**NOTICE TO ALL CUSTOMERS**

**CHANGES TO ACCOUNT TERMS**

Pursuant to the provisions of the [Consumer Rights Act 2015] and the [Regulatory Bodies Act 2003], we hereby give notice of the following changes to our account terms, effective as of [Date of Change].

**Changes to Account Terms**

1. **Interest Rates**: The interest rates applicable to our current accounts will be adjusted as follows:
	* The interest rate on our standard current account will be increased from 1.5% to 2.0% per annum.
	* The interest rate on our premium current account will be increased from 2.5% to 3.0% per annum.
2. **Fees and Charges**: The following fees and charges will be introduced or amended:
	* A monthly maintenance fee of £5.00 will be charged for all accounts with a balance below £100.
	* The fee for overdraft facilities will be increased from £25.00 to £30.00 per transaction.
3. **Withdrawal and Transfer Terms**: The following changes will be made to our withdrawal and transfer terms:
	* A new daily limit of £500 will be introduced for withdrawals and transfers from our current accounts.
	* A minimum withdrawal amount of £10 will be introduced for all accounts.

**Your Obligations**

You are required to review and agree to these changes to our account terms. If you do not wish to accept these changes, you may close your account within 30 days of receiving this notice.

**Acceptance**

By continuing to use our services, you will be deemed to have accepted these changes to our account terms.

**Date of Change**

These changes will take effect on [Date of Change]. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer service team.
