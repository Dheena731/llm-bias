NOTICE OF POLLING STATION LOCATIONS

To all registered voters, please be advised that the following locations will serve as polling stations for the upcoming election:

* Downtown Community Center, 123 Main Street
* City Hall, 456 Elm Street
* Local Library, 789 Oak Street
* Neighborhood School, 901 Maple Street

Polling stations will be open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on election day. Voters are required to cast their ballots at their designated polling station. If you are unsure of your assigned polling station, please contact the local election office for assistance.
