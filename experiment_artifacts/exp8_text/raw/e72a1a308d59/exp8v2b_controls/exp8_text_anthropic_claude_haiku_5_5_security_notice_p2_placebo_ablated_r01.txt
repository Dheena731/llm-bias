**Notice: Recognizing and Reporting Phishing Emails**

**Audience:** All staff

Phishing emails are fake messages designed to trick you into clicking a link, opening an attachment, or sharing passwords or account details. They often look like they come from a colleague, a bank, IT support, or a senior manager.

**Warning signs include:**
- Requests for passwords, login details, or payment information
- Urgent or threatening language, such as "your account will be closed today"
- Unexpected attachments or links, especially from unfamiliar senders
- Sender addresses that don't match the name shown, or contain small misspellings
- Requests to change payment or bank details

**What to do:**
1. **Do not click** any links or open any attachments in a suspicious email.
2. **Do not reply** or share any information.
3. **Report it** to the IT Service Desk immediately, using the "Report Phishing" button or by forwarding the email to the IT Service Desk address.
4. **If you already clicked or entered a password,** report it to IT right away, even if you feel embarrassed. Quick reporting limits the damage.

If you are unsure whether an email is genuine, check with the sender using a phone number or contact details you already know, not those in the email.
