**PUBLIC NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS**

Polling station locations for the upcoming election are officially confirmed. Due to recent precinct adjustments, several voting sites have changed since the last election cycle. 

Voters must cast their ballots at their assigned precinct on Election Day. To locate your designated polling place, access the lookup tool on the official state election portal, review the information card delivered to your residential mailing address, or contact the local board of elections office by phone. 

All polling places are fully accessible and will operate continuously from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on Election Day. Voters waiting in line by the close of polls are legally permitted to vote. Please confirm your designated site prior to Election Day and arrive prepared with an accepted form of identification.
