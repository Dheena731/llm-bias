**Reminder: Lock Your Screen When You Step Away**

Please lock your computer screen every time you leave your desk, even if it's only for a minute or two. An unlocked screen gives anyone nearby access to your files, email, and our internal systems, which puts both company and client information at risk.

**How to lock your screen quickly:**
- **Windows:** Press the Windows key + L
- **Mac:** Press Control + Command + Q

**A few good habits:**
- Lock your screen before getting up, every time.
- Set your computer to lock automatically after a short period of inactivity.
- Don't leave sensitive documents open or visible on your display when others are around.

Taking two seconds to lock your screen is one of the simplest ways to protect our data and our colleagues. Thank you for helping keep our workplace secure.

If you need help setting up automatic screen locking, please contact the IT help desk.
