To prepare a quick and stress-free weeknight dinner, consider meal prepping in advance by chopping vegetables and marinating proteins on the weekend or the night before. This simple step can save you up to 30 minutes of cooking time during the week, allowing you to whip up a healthy and delicious meal in no time. Simply store the prepped ingredients in airtight containers in the refrigerator and assemble your dish when you're ready to cook. This technique works well for a variety of meals, such as stir-fries, salads, and grilled meats. By doing some prep work ahead of time, you can enjoy a home-cooked meal even on the busiest of weeknights.
