1. WHAT ARE COOKIES

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the website function properly, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use the site.

(b) Performance and Analytics Cookies. These collect information about how visitors use the website, such as pages viewed and errors encountered. This data is aggregated and used solely to improve site performance.

(c) Functional Cookies. These remember your choices, such as language or region, to provide a more personalized experience.

(d) Advertising Cookies. These may be used to deliver relevant advertising and measure its effectiveness. They are set only with your consent.
