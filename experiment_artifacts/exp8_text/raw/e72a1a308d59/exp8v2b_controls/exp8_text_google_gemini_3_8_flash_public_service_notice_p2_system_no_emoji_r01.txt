PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE

Please be advised that Main Street, between 1st Avenue and Elm Street, will be closed to all vehicular traffic beginning Monday, October 23, at 7:00 AM through Friday, October 27, at 5:00 PM.

This closure is required for emergency water main repairs and subsequent road resurfacing. 

A detour will be clearly marked, directing traffic around the work zone via Oak Street. Local access for residents within the closure limits will be maintained, and emergency services have been notified.

Motorists should expect delays and are encouraged to seek alternate routes. 

For questions or concerns, please contact the Department of Public Works at (555) 019-2834 or visit the city website. We appreciate your patience and cooperation.
