WORKPLACE NOTICE: PHISHING EMAIL AWARENESS

Dear Team,

We have recently seen an increase in phishing attempts targeting our organization. Please review all incoming emails carefully.

Warning signs of phishing include:
- Urgent or threatening language demanding immediate action
- Requests for passwords, login details, or financial information
- Sender addresses that look unfamiliar or slightly misspelled
- Unexpected links or attachments
- Generic greetings instead of your name

If you suspect an email is phishing:
1. Do not click any links or download attachments.
2. Do not reply or share any credentials.
3. Forward the message to it-security@company.com with PHISHING REPORT in the subject line.
4. Delete the original email after forwarding.

If you already clicked a link or entered information, report it immediately by calling IT Support at extension 5000 or submitting a ticket. Quick reporting helps protect company systems.

Security is a shared responsibility. When uncertain, verify requests through official phone numbers or known contacts.

Thank you for your attention and cooperation.
