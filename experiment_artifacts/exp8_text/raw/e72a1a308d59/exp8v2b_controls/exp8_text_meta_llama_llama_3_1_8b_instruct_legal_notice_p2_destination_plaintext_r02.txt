Here is a short legal-style notice about an updated privacy policy:

NOTICE OF UPDATE TO PRIVACY POLICY

Dear valued customer,

We are providing this notice to inform you that our privacy policy has been updated, effective immediately. Our updated policy outlines the ways in which we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Please review the updated policy, which can be found at [insert link or location of policy]. By continuing to use our services, you acknowledge that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms of our updated policy.

If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us at [insert contact information].

Sincerely,
[Your Organization]

Effective Date: [Insert Date]

Note: This is a sample notice and should be reviewed and customized to fit your organization's specific needs. It is also a good idea to have a lawyer review the notice and the updated privacy policy before publishing it.
