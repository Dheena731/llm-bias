**NOTICE: IMPORTANT SECURITY REMINDER – PHISHING EMAILS**

All employees are reminded to exercise heightened caution when reviewing incoming messages. Phishing attempts continue to target our organization through deceptive emails designed to compromise accounts, install malware, or extract confidential information.

**Common Indicators of Phishing Emails:**
• Requests for passwords, login credentials, or financial details
• Urgent or threatening language demanding immediate action
• Sender addresses that do not match official company domains
• Unexpected attachments or links to unfamiliar websites
• Generic greetings or inconsistent formatting/spelling

**Required Response Protocol:**
1. Do not click any links, download attachments, or reply to suspicious messages.
2. Forward the complete email to IT Security at security@company.com for investigation.
3. Delete the original message after forwarding.
4. If you believe you have already clicked a link, opened an attachment, or entered information, notify IT Security immediately.

Timely reporting safeguards company data, protects client information, and maintains system integrity. For technical support, contact the IT Help Desk at extension 5550 or refer to the internal security guidelines on the company intranet.

Thank you for your vigilance and cooperation in keeping our workplace secure.
