Reminder: Please Lock Your Screen

Hello everyone,

This is a quick reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if it's just for a moment.

To lock your screen quickly:
- Windows: press the Windows key + L
- Mac: press Control + Command + Q

Locking your screen helps protect confidential information, customer data, and your own accounts from unauthorized access. It only takes a second and makes a big difference to our overall security.

Thank you for your help in keeping our workplace and data safe.

IT Security Team
