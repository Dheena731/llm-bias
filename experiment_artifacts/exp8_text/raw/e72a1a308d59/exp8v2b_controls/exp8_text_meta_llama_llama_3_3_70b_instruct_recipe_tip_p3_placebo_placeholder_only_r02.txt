To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it thoroughly before cooking. This simple step can help remove excess starch from the surface of the rice, resulting in a fluffier and less clingy texture. After rinsing, make sure to drain the rice well and then cook it with the right amount of water. Using a non-stick pot or adding a small amount of oil or butter to the water can also help prevent the rice from becoming sticky. Additionally, avoid overmixing or stirring the rice too much while it's cooking, as this can cause it to break down and become sticky. By following these tips, you can achieve perfectly cooked, separate grains of rice every time.
