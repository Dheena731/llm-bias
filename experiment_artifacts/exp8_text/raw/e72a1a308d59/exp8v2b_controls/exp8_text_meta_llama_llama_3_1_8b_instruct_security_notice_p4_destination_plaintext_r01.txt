To enable two-factor authentication, please follow these steps:

1. Log in to your account.
2. Go to your account settings.
3. Locate the two-factor authentication option.
4. Click to enable it.
5. A verification code will be sent to your registered phone number.
6. Enter the code and confirm.

Alternatively, you can also enable two-factor authentication through your account's settings page. To do this:

1. Go to your account's settings page.
2. Look for the two-factor authentication option.
3. Click to enable it.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the setup.

Enabling two-factor authentication will add an extra layer of security to your account, making it more difficult for others to access your account even if they have your password.
