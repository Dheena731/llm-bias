**HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

A heat advisory has been issued for the area, effective immediately. Temperatures are expected to reach extreme highs, posing a significant risk to public health and safety.

**RECOMMENDED ACTIONS**

* Stay indoors, especially during the hottest part of the day (11am-3pm)
* Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water
* Avoid strenuous activities and outdoor work
* Check on vulnerable neighbors, such as the elderly and young children
* Keep pets indoors and provide plenty of water and shade

**PROTECTIVE MEASURES**

* If you must be outdoors, take regular breaks in a shaded area and drink water
* Use cooling devices such as fans and air conditioners to stay cool
* Wear lightweight, light-colored clothing to reflect the sun's rays

**EXCEPTIONAL CIRCUMSTANCES**

* If you experience heat-related symptoms such as dizziness, nausea, or headaches, seek medical attention immediately
* If you have a pre-existing medical condition or are taking medication, take extra precautions to stay cool

**STAY INFORMED**

* Monitor local news and weather reports for updates on the heat advisory
* Visit our website or social media for additional information and resources

**HEAT SAFETY TIPS**

* Never leave children or pets unattended in a vehicle
* Keep windows closed and use air conditioning to stay cool
* Avoid using your oven or stove during the hottest part of the day

**REMAIN VIGILANT**

* The heat advisory will remain in effect until further notice. Please take all necessary precautions to stay safe and cool.
