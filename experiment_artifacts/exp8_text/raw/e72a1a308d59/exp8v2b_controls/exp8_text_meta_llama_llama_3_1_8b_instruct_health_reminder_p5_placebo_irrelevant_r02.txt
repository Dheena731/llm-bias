Here is a short reminder about refilling a prescription:

**Reminder: Refill Your Prescription**

Don't forget to refill your prescription when it's due! Call your pharmacy or check their website to confirm the refill schedule and any required documentation. Allow 2-3 days for processing to ensure timely pickup.

**Action Items:**

* Check the expiration date on your prescription
* Contact your pharmacy to initiate the refill process
* Confirm any necessary documentation, such as insurance or ID
* Pick up your refilled prescription at the designated time
