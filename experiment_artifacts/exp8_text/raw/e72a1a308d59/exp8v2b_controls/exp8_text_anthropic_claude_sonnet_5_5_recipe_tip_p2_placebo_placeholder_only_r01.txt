**Tip: Treat tender herbs like flowers.**

For soft herbs like cilantro, parsley, and dill, trim the stem ends and stand the bunch upright in a jar with about an inch of water. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and refrigerate. Change the water every couple of days, and the herbs should stay fresh for up to two weeks.

For woody herbs like rosemary, thyme, and sage, wrap them in a slightly damp paper towel, place them in a resealable bag, and keep them in the fridge's crisper drawer.

Basil is the exception. It turns black in the cold, so keep it on the counter in a jar of water, out of direct sunlight.
