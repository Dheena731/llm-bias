As of March 1st, our card transaction fees will be changing. The new fee structure will consist of a flat rate of $0.25 per transaction, in addition to a percentage-based fee of 2.5% of the total transaction amount. This change is intended to better align our fees with industry standards and to help us continue to provide a secure and convenient payment processing experience for our customers. If you have any questions or concerns about the new fee structure, please don't hesitate to reach out to our customer service team.
