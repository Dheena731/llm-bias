**Notice to Library Patrons**

Are you planning a visit to our library? Make sure to check our opening hours before you head out.

**Library Hours:**

* Monday to Thursday: 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM
* Friday: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
* Saturday: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
* Sunday: Closed
* Holidays: Closed

**Extended Hours:**

* During exam periods, the library may be open until 10:00 PM, Monday to Thursday.
* Check the library's website or contact us for specific dates and times.

**Remember:**

* Our hours may be subject to change, so it's always a good idea to double-check before you visit.
* We're closed on public holidays and during special events, so plan your visit accordingly.

We're happy to help you find what you're looking for. Visit us today and discover the world of books, resources, and community!
