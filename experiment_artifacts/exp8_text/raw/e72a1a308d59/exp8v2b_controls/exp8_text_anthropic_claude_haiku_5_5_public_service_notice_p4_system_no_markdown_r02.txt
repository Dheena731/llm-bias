HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for the area from 11:00 a.m. today through 8:00 p.m. Thursday. Temperatures are expected to reach the mid-to-upper 90s, with heat index values reaching 105 degrees or higher.

Residents are urged to take precautions:

Stay indoors in air-conditioned spaces when possible. If your home lacks air conditioning, visit a cooling center or public library.

Drink plenty of water, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol, caffeine, and sugary drinks.

Never leave children, pets, or anyone who depends on you in a parked vehicle.

Check on elderly neighbors, relatives, and people with chronic illnesses at least twice a day.

Limit outdoor activity to the early morning or evening, and take frequent breaks if you must work outside.

Watch for signs of heat illness. Symptoms of heat exhaustion include heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, nausea, and headache. Heat stroke is a medical emergency; call 911 if someone has a high body temperature, confusion, or loss of consciousness.

Cooling centers are open at the Central Community Center, 400 Main Street, and the Eastside Library, 1210 Oak Avenue, during regular hours.

For updates, visit weather.gov or tune in to local media.

Issued by the City Office of Emergency Management
