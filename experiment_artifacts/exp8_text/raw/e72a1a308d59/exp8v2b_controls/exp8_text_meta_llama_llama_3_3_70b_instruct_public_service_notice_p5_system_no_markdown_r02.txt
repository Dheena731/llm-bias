Polling station locations for the upcoming election are as follows: 
Voters living in the north of the city will cast their ballots at the Community Center, 
those in the south at the Public Library, 
and those in the central area at City Hall. 
Please note that polling stations will be open from 8am to 8pm on election day. 
If you are unsure of your designated polling station, please contact your local election office for more information.
