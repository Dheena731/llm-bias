We are excited to introduce a new monthly spending summary feature, designed to help you better manage your finances. This summary will provide a clear and concise overview of your spending habits, categorizing your expenses and highlighting areas where you can make adjustments to stay within your budget.

Each month, you will receive a detailed breakdown of your expenditures, including totals for categories such as housing, transportation, food, entertainment, and more. This information will be presented in an easy-to-read format, allowing you to quickly identify trends and patterns in your spending.

The monthly spending summary will be available online, and you will receive an email notification when it is ready to view. This new feature is intended to provide you with the tools and insights you need to make informed financial decisions and achieve your long-term goals.
