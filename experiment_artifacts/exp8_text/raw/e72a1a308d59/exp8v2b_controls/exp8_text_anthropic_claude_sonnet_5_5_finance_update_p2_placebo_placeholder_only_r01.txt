**Subject: Update to card transaction fees**

Hello,

Starting March 1, we will add a 2.5% fee to purchases made with credit and debit cards. Cash, bank transfers, and checks are not affected.

**Why we're making this change**
Card networks and payment processors have raised the fees they charge us, and these costs have grown steadily over the past two years. We have absorbed them until now to keep our prices steady. As they continue to rise, we can no longer cover them without affecting the quality of our service or raising prices for everyone, including customers who don't pay by card.

**What this means for you**
- A $100.00 purchase by card will include a $2.50 fee, for a total of $102.50.
- The fee will appear as a separate line on your receipt or invoice.
- If you pay by bank transfer or cash, you will pay no additional fee.

We know no one likes added costs, and we appreciate your understanding. If you have questions about the change or would like help switching to a no-fee payment method, please reply to this message and we'll be glad to help.

Thank you for your continued business.

Sincerely,
The Billing Team
