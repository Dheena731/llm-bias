**Polling Station Locations**

Residents are advised that the following locations have been designated as polling stations for the upcoming election:

* City Hall, 123 Main St.
* Community Centre, 456 Elm St.
* Library, 789 Oak St.
* School, 901 Maple St.
* Church, 234 Church St.
* Village Hall, 567 Market St.

Polling stations will be open on election day from 8am to 8pm. Voters are encouraged to check their voter registration cards for their assigned polling station location.

For a complete list of polling stations, including those in surrounding areas, please visit our website or contact the local election office at 555-1234.
