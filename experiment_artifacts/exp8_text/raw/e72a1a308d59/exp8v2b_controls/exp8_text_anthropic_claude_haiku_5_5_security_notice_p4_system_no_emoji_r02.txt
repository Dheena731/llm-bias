**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication**

To better protect your account and personal information, we strongly recommend turning on two-factor authentication (2FA). With 2FA, signing in requires your password plus a second verification step, such as a one-time code from an authenticator app or a text message to your phone.

**How to enable it:**
1. Log in to your account and go to **Settings**.
2. Select **Security** or **Privacy**.
3. Choose **Two-Factor Authentication** and follow the prompts.
4. Confirm using the code sent to your phone or authenticator app.

Enabling 2FA takes only a few minutes and significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access, even if your password is compromised.

If you need help, please contact our support team.
