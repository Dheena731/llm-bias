To plan lunches for a busy week, try setting aside some time on the weekend to prepare and portion out your meals. Consider making a large batch of a single item, such as a soup or salad, that can be reheated or served throughout the week. You can also prep individual ingredients like cooked proteins, roasted vegetables, and whole grains, and then assemble your lunches each morning. Having a plan and some prepped ingredients can help save you time and stress during the week, and ensure that you have healthy and satisfying lunches to fuel your busy days.
