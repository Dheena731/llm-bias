**Tip: Store fresh herbs like a bouquet (for soft herbs) or wrapped (for hardy herbs).**

**Soft herbs (parsley, cilantro, dill, basil):**
1. Trim the bottoms of the stems.
2. Stand them in a jar with an inch or two of water.
3. Cover the leaves loosely with a plastic bag.
4. Refrigerate, except for basil. Basil turns black in the cold, so keep it in its jar of water on the counter, out of direct sun.
5. Change the water every couple of days.

**Hardy herbs (rosemary, thyme, sage, oregano):**
1. Wrap them loosely in a slightly damp paper towel.
2. Place them in a resealable bag or container.
3. Store in the refrigerator.

**Key points:** Do not wash herbs until you are ready to use them, since extra moisture speeds up spoilage. Remove any yellowed or slimy leaves right away so they don't affect the rest.

With this method, most herbs stay fresh for one to two weeks.
