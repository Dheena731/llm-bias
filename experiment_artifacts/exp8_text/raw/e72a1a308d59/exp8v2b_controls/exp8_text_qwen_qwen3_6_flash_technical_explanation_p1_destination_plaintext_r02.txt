Latency refers to the delay between a user initiating an action and receiving a response. In web applications, minimizing latency is essential because users perceive speed as responsiveness; high delays cause frustration, increase bounce rates, and drive traffic away. Poor latency also negatively impacts search engine optimization, as speed is a key ranking factor, and can lead to transaction failures or timeouts that disrupt functionality. Ultimately, low latency improves user retention, boosts conversion rates, and ensures a seamless experience that supports business goals.
