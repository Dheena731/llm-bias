Thanks for sharing the poster plan. Here is my feedback.

Overall, the poster has a clear structure, and a viewer should be able to follow the story from question to takeaway. The main concern is density. Many text blocks will be hard to read from two or three feet away, so consider cutting each section to its core point and moving methodological detail to a handout or QR code linking to the full paper.

The title should state the finding or the question in plain language. Right now it reads more like a topic label, which makes it harder for passersby to decide whether to stop.

The central figure deserves the most space. Make its axes labels legible at a distance, and add a one-sentence caption that says what the viewer should notice. If the figure needs a legend to be understood, simplify it.

Check the flow of the poster. Numbered sections or arrows should guide the eye left to right and top to bottom, and the conclusion should be easy to find without reading everything else.

Finally, add contact information and a QR code or link to your slides or paper, so interested attendees can follow up after the session.
