Introducing Savings Goals

We're excited to announce Savings Goals, a new feature that helps you plan and track the money you're setting aside for what matters most.

With Savings Goals, you can:

- Create a goal with a name, target amount, and optional deadline, such as an emergency fund, a vacation, or a new laptop.
- Track your progress with a clear view of how much you've saved and how much remains.
- Set up automatic transfers so you can contribute steadily without thinking about it.
- See an estimate of when you'll reach each goal based on your current contributions.

To get started, open the Savings tab and tap "New Goal." You can create as many goals as you like and update them at any time.

Savings Goals is rolling out to all users over the next two weeks. We hope it makes saving feel a little more within reach. As always, we welcome your feedback through the Help menu.
