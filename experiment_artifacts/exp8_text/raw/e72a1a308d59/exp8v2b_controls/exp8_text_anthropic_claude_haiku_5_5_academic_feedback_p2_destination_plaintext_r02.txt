Here's a short feedback note on a methods section:

Thanks for submitting your methods section. You've done a good job describing your participant recruitment and the basic structure of your study, and the sequence of procedures is easy to follow.

There are a few areas to strengthen before resubmission. First, your description of the materials needs more detail. Specify the exact version of each instrument, how it was scored, and where the reliability information comes from. Second, the data analysis subsection currently names the statistical tests but does not explain why they were chosen. Briefly justify each test in relation to your research questions and the type of data you collected. Third, please state how you handled missing data and any exclusion criteria, since readers need this to judge whether your sample is representative.

Overall, this is a solid draft. Once you add these details, your methods should allow another researcher to replicate your study. Please feel free to come to office hours if you would like help reworking the analysis subsection.
