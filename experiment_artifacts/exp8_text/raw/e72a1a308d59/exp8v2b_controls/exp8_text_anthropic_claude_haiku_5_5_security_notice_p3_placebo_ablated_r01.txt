**Reminder: Lock Your Screen When Away From Your Desk**

To all staff,

Please lock your computer screen whenever you leave your desk, even for a short time. On Windows, press the Windows key and L together. On a Mac, press Control, Command, and Q together.

Locking your screen keeps confidential information, client data, and your account protected from anyone who may walk past your workstation. It takes one second and is one of the simplest ways to protect our organisation.

Thank you for helping keep our information secure.

IT Department
