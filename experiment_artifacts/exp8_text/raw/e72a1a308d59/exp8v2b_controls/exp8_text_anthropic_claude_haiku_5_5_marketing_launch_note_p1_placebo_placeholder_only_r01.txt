**Introducing Ledgerly: Invoicing, Expenses, and Payroll Prep in One Place**

Running a small business shouldn't mean juggling five apps and a shoebox of receipts. Starting today, Ledgerly gives owners a single place to send invoices, track expenses, and see where their money stands.

**What's new:**
- **One-tap invoicing.** Create a professional invoice in under a minute and send it by email or text. Customers can pay by card or bank transfer directly from the link.
- **Receipt capture.** Snap a photo of any receipt and Ledgerly pulls out the vendor, date, and amount, then files it under the right category.
- **Cash flow at a glance.** A simple dashboard shows what's owed to you, what's due, and what you've spent this month.
- **Tax-ready exports.** Download organized reports for your accountant in one click.

**Built for small teams:** Ledgerly works for solo freelancers and shops with up to 15 employees. No accounting background required.

**Pricing:** Free for up to 10 invoices a month. The Plus plan, at $14 per month, removes the limit and adds recurring billing and multi-user access.

**Get started:** Ledgerly is available now on iOS and Android, and at ledgerly.app on the web. New accounts get 30 days of Plus free.

Small businesses keep the economy running. Ledgerly is built to make the paperwork the easy part.
