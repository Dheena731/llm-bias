Here's a short update on our new monthly spending summary:

As part of our ongoing effort to provide you with more transparency and control over your finances, we're excited to introduce a new monthly spending summary. Starting this month, you'll receive a concise and easy-to-read summary of your spending activity, broken down into categories such as dining, entertainment, and groceries.

The summary will be sent to you via email on the 5th of each month and will include the following key details:

* Total spend for the month
* Top 3 spending categories
* Average daily spend
* Comparison to previous month's spend

This new summary is designed to help you quickly and easily track your spending habits, identify areas for improvement, and make informed decisions about your finances. We hope you find this new feature helpful and look forward to your feedback!
