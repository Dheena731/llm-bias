PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE: LIBRARY OPERATING HOURS

The City Public Library is open to all community members during the following hours:

Monday through Thursday: 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM
Friday and Saturday: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
Sunday: 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM

Please note that exterior return drops remain open 24/7. Digital resources, including e-books, audiobooks, and research databases, can be accessed anytime through the library website.

For questions or account assistance, please call 555-0199 during regular hours or visit www.citylibrary.org. We look forward to your visit.
