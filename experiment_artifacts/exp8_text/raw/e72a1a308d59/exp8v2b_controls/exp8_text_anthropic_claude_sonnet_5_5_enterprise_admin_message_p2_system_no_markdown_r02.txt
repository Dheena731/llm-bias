Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starting Monday

Hello team,

Starting Monday, we are introducing a new workflow for approving expenses. The goal is to speed up reimbursements and make it easier to see where each request stands.

Here is how it will work. First, submit your expense report through the finance portal and attach itemized receipts. Second, your direct manager will have three business days to review and approve or return the request. Third, any expense over $1,000 will also need review from the Finance team. Finally, once approved, reimbursements will be issued in the next weekly payment run.

Please note that requests without receipts or a clear business purpose will be sent back for correction, which can delay payment. Expenses from before this date can still be submitted using the old form through the end of the month.

A short guide and a recorded walkthrough are available on the intranet under Finance Resources. If you have questions, contact the Finance Help Desk at finance-help@company.com.

Thank you for helping make this transition a smooth one.

Best regards,
Administration Team
