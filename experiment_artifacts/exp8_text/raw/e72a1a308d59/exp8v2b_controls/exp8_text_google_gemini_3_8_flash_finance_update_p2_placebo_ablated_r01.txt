Dear Customers,

We are writing to inform you of an upcoming update to our card transaction fees. 

Starting on November 1, 2024, the processing fee applied to credit card payments will increase from 1.5% to 2.0% per transaction. This adjustment reflects the rising costs required to process card transactions securely. 

Payments made with cash, debit cards, or direct bank transfers will not be affected by this change and will remain free of any extra fees. 

If you have any questions regarding this update, please contact our support team at support@example.com. 

Thank you for your continued business.
