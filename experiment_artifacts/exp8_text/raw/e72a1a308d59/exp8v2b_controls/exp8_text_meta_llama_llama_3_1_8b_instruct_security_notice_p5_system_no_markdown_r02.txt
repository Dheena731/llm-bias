**Lost Company Laptop Report**

If you have misplaced a company laptop, please report it immediately to the IT department. Please provide the following information:

* Laptop model and serial number
* Last known location and time of use
* Any distinctive features or markings
* Your employee ID and contact information

You can report the lost laptop by:

* Emailing [itdepartment@company.com](mailto:itdepartment@company.com)
* Calling the IT help desk at 555-1234
* Submitting a report through the company's internal reporting system

The IT department will work with you to locate the laptop and take necessary measures to secure company data.
