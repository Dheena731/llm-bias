Reviewer Comment: Clarity of the Research Question

The manuscript's central research question is not stated clearly enough for me to evaluate whether the study design and conclusions are appropriate. The Introduction describes a broad topic area but never narrows it to a specific, answerable question. The stated aims shift across sections: the abstract implies a causal question, the Introduction frames the work as exploratory, and the Discussion draws conclusions about effectiveness. It is also unclear which population, variables, and outcomes are the focus.

I recommend that the authors:

1. State one explicit research question (or a small set of clearly numbered questions) at the end of the Introduction.
2. Specify the key constructs, population, and comparison or relationship of interest, and state whether the study is exploratory or hypothesis-testing.
3. Align the methods, analyses, and conclusions with that question, and remove or clearly label any findings that fall outside it.

Until the question is sharpened, it is difficult to judge whether the data and analyses support the claims. I would be glad to review a revised version.
