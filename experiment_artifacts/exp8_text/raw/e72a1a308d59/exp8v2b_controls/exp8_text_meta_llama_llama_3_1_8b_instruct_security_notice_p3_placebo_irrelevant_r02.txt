**Reminder: Lock Your Screens**

As a reminder, please make sure to lock your computer screen when you leave your workstation or step away for any period of time. This helps to maintain the security and integrity of our systems and data.

By locking your screen, you'll be protecting against unauthorized access and potential data breaches. If you're using a password-protected laptop or mobile device, please ensure that you lock it as well.

If you have any questions or concerns about screen locking procedures, please don't hesitate to reach out to our IT department.

Thank you for your cooperation and help in keeping our systems secure.
