Batch-cook once, eat all week. Spend 30 minutes on Sunday roasting a tray of vegetables, cooking a pot of grains, and grilling a protein like chicken or tofu. Store them in separate containers, then mix and match each day with a sauce or dressing so your lunches don't feel repetitive. Keep a few no-cook backups on hand, like canned beans, hummus, or wraps, for the days when plans fall apart.
