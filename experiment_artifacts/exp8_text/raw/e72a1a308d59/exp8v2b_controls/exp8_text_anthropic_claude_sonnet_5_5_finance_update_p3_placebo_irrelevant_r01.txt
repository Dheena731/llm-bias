# Introducing Savings Goals 🎯

We've added a new way to help you save for what matters most.

**With Savings Goals, you can:**

- **Set a target.** Name your goal, whether it's a vacation, an emergency fund, or a new laptop, and choose the amount and date.
- **Track your progress.** A visual progress bar shows how close you are, and you'll see how much to set aside each week or month to stay on track.
- **Automate your savings.** Schedule recurring transfers or round up your everyday purchases to grow your goal without thinking about it.
- **Celebrate milestones.** Get a nudge when you hit 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of your target.

**How to get started:**
Open the app, tap **Goals**, then select **Create a Goal**. It takes less than a minute.

Savings Goals is rolling out to all users over the next week. If you don't see it yet, check back soon or update to the latest version of the app.

We'd love to hear what you think. Send us your feedback through **Settings → Help & Feedback**.

Happy saving!
