**NOTICE: Immediate Action Required for Lost or Stolen Company Laptops**

If you have misplaced or suspect the theft of your company-issued laptop, you must report it **immediately** to the IT and Security teams. Timely reporting allows us to remotely lock or wipe the device to protect sensitive company and client data.

**Immediate Steps to Take:**

1. **Notify IT Support:** 
   * **Phone (Fastest):** [Insert IT Emergency Line]
   * **Email:** [Insert IT Helpdesk Email] with the subject line: *URGENT: Lost Laptop – [Your Name]*
2. **Provide Details:** 
   * Date, time, and last known location of the device.
   * Laptop asset tag or serial number (if known).
   * Circumstances of the loss (e.g., left in transit, home break-in).
3. **File a Police Report (if stolen):** Obtain a case/incident number from local law enforcement and forward it to IT and HR.

Prompt notification is critical to our data security protocols. Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our systems safe.

— **Information Security & IT Operations**
