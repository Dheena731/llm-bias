**NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE**

**TO ALL MOTORISTS**

Please be advised that the section of Main Street between Elmwood Avenue and Oakwood Drive will be closed to vehicular traffic effective immediately. This closure is necessary to facilitate essential maintenance and repairs to the infrastructure.

Alternative routes will be clearly signposted. Motorists are advised to follow the detour signs and allow extra time for their journey.

The closure is expected to be in place for approximately two weeks, weather permitting. The City apologizes for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciates your cooperation.

**DURATION:** Monday, March 21st to Friday, April 1st

**CONTACT:** For further information, please contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567 or visit our website at [www.cityofexample.com](http://www.cityofexample.com).

**Thank you for your understanding.**
