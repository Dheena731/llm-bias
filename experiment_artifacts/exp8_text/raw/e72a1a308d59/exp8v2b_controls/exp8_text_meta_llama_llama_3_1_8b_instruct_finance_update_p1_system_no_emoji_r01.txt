Here is a short update explaining a new monthly spending summary:

As part of our ongoing efforts to provide transparency and clarity, we are introducing a new monthly spending summary starting immediately. This comprehensive report will detail all expenses incurred by the company on a monthly basis, providing a clear picture of our financial transactions.

The monthly spending summary will include the following information:

* Date range of the report
* Total amount spent
* Breakdown by category, including but not limited to:
	+ Salary and benefits
	+ Travel and entertainment
	+ Utilities and maintenance
	+ Marketing and advertising
	+ Capital expenditures
	+ Other expenses
* A detailed list of each transaction, including the date, description, and amount

This new report will be sent to all stakeholders on the 15th of each month, covering the previous month's spending activity. We believe that this additional level of transparency will help our stakeholders make informed decisions and improve our financial management.
