**Subject: Understanding Your End-of-Year Tax Statement**

Hello,

Your end-of-year tax statement is now available. It summarizes your financial activity for the past calendar year and gives you the figures you need to file your tax return.

**What it includes**
- **Income:** The total you earned or received during the year, such as wages, interest, dividends, or other payments.
- **Taxes withheld or paid:** The amount already sent to tax authorities on your behalf.
- **Deductions and credits:** Any amounts that may reduce your taxable income or your final tax bill.

**What to do next**
1. Review each section and compare it with your own records, such as pay stubs, bank statements, and receipts.
2. Keep a copy with your other tax documents for at least three to seven years.
3. Use the figures when preparing your return, or give the statement to your tax preparer.
4. If anything looks wrong or missing, contact the issuer right away so a corrected statement can be sent before the filing deadline.

Keep in mind that this statement is a summary of reported information. It is not a bill, and it does not by itself mean you owe anything. Whether you owe money or receive a refund depends on your full tax return.

If you have questions about how to read a particular section, reply to this message or consult a qualified tax professional.
