To: All Staff

We have recently seen an increase in phishing emails being sent to staff members. These emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information such as your login credentials or financial information.

Please be cautious when receiving emails that ask you to click on links, download attachments, or provide personal information. Legitimate emails from the company will never ask you to reveal your password or other sensitive information.

If you receive an email that you suspect is a phishing attempt, do not click on any links or download any attachments. Instead, please forward the email to the IT department so we can investigate and take necessary action.

Remember to always verify the sender's email address and be wary of emails that create a sense of urgency or threaten to suspend your account if you do not take immediate action.

Your vigilance is crucial in helping to protect our company's security and we appreciate your cooperation in this matter.

If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT department.
