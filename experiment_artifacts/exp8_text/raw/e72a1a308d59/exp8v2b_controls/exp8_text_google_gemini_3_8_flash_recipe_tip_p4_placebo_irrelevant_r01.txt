**Prep components, not full meals.** 

Instead of spending hours assembling identical grab-and-go lunches, batch-prep versatile building blocks over the weekend: roast a large sheet pan of vegetables, cook a pot of grains (like brown rice or quinoa), and prepare a simple protein. Throughout the week, quickly mix and match them in different bowls using varying sauces or dressings (like pesto one day and tahini the next) to save time while avoiding lunch burnout.
