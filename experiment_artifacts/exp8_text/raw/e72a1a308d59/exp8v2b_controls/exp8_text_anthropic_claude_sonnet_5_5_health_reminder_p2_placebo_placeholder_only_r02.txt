**Hydration Reminder 💧**

Take a moment to drink some water. Even mild dehydration can cause tiredness, headaches, and trouble concentrating.

A few easy ways to stay on track:

- Start your morning with a glass of water before coffee or breakfast.
- Keep a water bottle within reach all day.
- Drink a glass with each meal and snack.
- Sip regularly rather than waiting until you feel thirsty.
- If your urine is pale yellow, you're doing well.

Your body will thank you. Go fill up your glass now!
