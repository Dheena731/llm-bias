**Reminder: Please Lock Your Screen When Away From Your Desk**

Team,

Leaving a workstation unlocked, even for a few minutes, puts our systems and client information at risk. Anyone walking past can view or access sensitive data in seconds.

To protect our work, please:

- **Lock your screen every time you step away**, whether for a short break, a meeting, or lunch.
- **Use the keyboard shortcut** Windows key + L on Windows, or Control + Command + Q on a Mac.
- **Check that your screen is locked** before leaving the area.

Thank you for helping keep our information secure. If you have questions about setting up automatic screen locks, please contact the IT Help Desk.
