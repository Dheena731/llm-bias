NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

To: All Customers

You may be eligible for a refund if you have purchased a product or service from us within the last 30 days and are not satisfied with your purchase. To qualify for a refund, you must return the product in its original condition, with all original packaging and accessories, within 30 days of the date of purchase.

Refunds will be issued in the original form of payment and will be processed within 10-15 business days of receiving the returned product. Please note that shipping costs are non-refundable, unless the product was defective or shipped in error.

If you believe you are eligible for a refund, please contact our customer service department to initiate the return process. We will provide you with a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number, which must be included with the returned product.

By returning a product for a refund, you acknowledge that you have read and understood our refund policy. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
