Here's a short cooking tip for making rice less sticky:

**For a Non-Stick, Perfectly Cooked Rice Every Time**

To achieve the perfect, non-stick rice, it's essential to rinse your rice thoroughly before cooking. Yes, you read that right - rinsing! This simple step is often overlooked, but it makes all the difference in preventing sticky rice. By rinsing your rice, you're removing excess starch that can cause it to cling together. Simply place the rice in a fine-mesh strainer and rinse it under cold running water until the water runs clear.

This trick is a game-changer for anyone who's struggled with sticky rice in the past. Give it a try and experience the difference for yourself!
