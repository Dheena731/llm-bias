**Time for a Quick Screen Break!** 

Give your eyes and mind a moment to reset. Step away from your display, roll your shoulders, and try the **20-20-20 rule**: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds. 

Stand up, grab a sip of water, and take a deep breath before diving back in. A one-minute pause now will keep you energized and focused for the rest of the day!
