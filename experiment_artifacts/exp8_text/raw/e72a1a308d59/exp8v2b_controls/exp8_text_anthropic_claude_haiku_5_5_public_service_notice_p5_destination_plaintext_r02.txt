Polling Station Locations Notice

Voters are reminded that polling station locations may vary from one election to the next. Before you go to vote, please check your assigned polling station to make sure you go to the correct location.

To find your polling station, visit the official elections website for your area or contact your local elections office. You will need your name and home address to look up your details.

Please bring any required identification with you on election day. Polling stations are open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Voters in line at closing time will still be able to cast their ballot.

If you have questions about polling station locations or accessibility, please contact your local elections office for assistance.
