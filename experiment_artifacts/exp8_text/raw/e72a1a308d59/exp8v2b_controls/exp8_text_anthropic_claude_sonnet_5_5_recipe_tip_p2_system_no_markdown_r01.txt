Here's a simple way to keep fresh herbs lasting longer: treat them based on their type. For soft herbs like cilantro, parsley, and dill, trim the stems and stand the bunch in a jar with an inch of water, like a bouquet. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and keep it in the fridge, changing the water every couple of days. For basil, skip the fridge and leave it in a jar of water on the counter, since cold temperatures turn its leaves black. For hardy herbs like rosemary, thyme, and sage, wrap them in a slightly damp paper towel, place them in a zip-top bag or container, and store them in the fridge. Done right, most herbs will stay fresh for one to two weeks.
