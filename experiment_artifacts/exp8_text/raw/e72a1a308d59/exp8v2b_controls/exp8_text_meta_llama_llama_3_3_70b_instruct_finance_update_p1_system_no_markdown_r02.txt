We are excited to introduce a new monthly spending summary feature that will help you keep track of your expenses and stay on top of your finances. This new summary will provide a clear and concise breakdown of your spending habits, including total spend, category totals, and transaction details. The summary will be sent to you at the beginning of each month and will cover the previous month's activity. This will enable you to identify areas where you can cut back, make adjustments to your budget, and make more informed financial decisions. We hope this new feature will help you better manage your finances and achieve your financial goals.
