**Notice: What to Do If Your Company Laptop Is Lost or Stolen**

**To all employees:**

If your company laptop is lost or stolen, **report it to the IT Help Desk immediately**. Do not wait to see if it turns up. Quick reporting lets us lock the device and protect company and customer information.

**How to report**
- Phone: [Help Desk number] (available 24 hours)
- Email: [Help Desk email]

**Please include**
- Your name and department
- The laptop's asset tag or serial number, if you know it
- When, where, and how it was lost or stolen
- Whether it held any sensitive files, such as customer or financial data

**If it was stolen**, also report it to the police and give IT the report number when you have it.

**What happens next**
- IT will lock your accounts and remotely erase the laptop's data the next time it connects to the internet.
- IT will give you a replacement once the report is reviewed.

**Please note**
- Reporting promptly will not be treated as a disciplinary matter. Delayed reporting puts company data at risk.
- Do not try to access the laptop's data from another device or recover it yourself. Leave that to IT.

Thank you for helping us keep company information secure.

*IT Department*
