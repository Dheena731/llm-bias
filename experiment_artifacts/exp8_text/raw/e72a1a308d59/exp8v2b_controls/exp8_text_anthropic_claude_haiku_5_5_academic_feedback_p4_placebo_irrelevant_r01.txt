**Feedback on Poster Draft: "[Poster Title]"**

Thanks for the draft. The research question is clear, and the overall layout guides the eye from the problem to the findings in a logical order.

**Strengths**
- The title is specific and tells viewers what the study covers.
- The results figure is large and readable from a distance.
- The conclusion connects back to the research question.

**Suggestions**
1. **Shorten the text.** Viewers usually spend a minute or two at a poster. Aim for under 300 words, using bullet points where possible.
2. **Lead with the takeaway.** Put the main finding in a highlighted box near the top so it's visible from a few feet away.
3. **Simplify the methods section.** Summarize the approach in two or three sentences and link to the full paper or a QR code for details.
4. **Check font sizes.** Body text should be at least 24 pt, and headings should be noticeably larger.
5. **Label figures fully.** Axis labels, units, and captions should be readable without the surrounding text.

**Next steps**
Revise the text for brevity, move the key finding to the top, and do a quick print test at full size to check readability before the deadline.
