**Introducing Savings Goals: Plan It, Track It, Reach It**

We're excited to announce Savings Goals, a new feature that helps you set clear financial targets and watch your progress toward them.

Here's how it works:

- **Create a goal** by naming it, choosing a target amount, and picking a date you'd like to reach it by.
- **Set up contributions** either as a one-time transfer or as a recurring amount, such as every payday.
- **Track your progress** with a visual bar and a simple summary showing how much you've saved and how much remains.
- **Stay on course** with gentle reminders if your contributions fall behind your planned pace.

Whether you're building an emergency fund, saving for a trip, or putting money aside for a larger purchase, Savings Goals keeps each target in view so it's easier to stay motivated.

Savings Goals is rolling out now. Open the Savings tab in the app and tap "New Goal" to get started. We'd love to hear what you're saving for, so share your feedback through the in-app support option.
