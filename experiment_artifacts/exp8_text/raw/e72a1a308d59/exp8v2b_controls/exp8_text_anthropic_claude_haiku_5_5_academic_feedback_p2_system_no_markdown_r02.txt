Thank you for sending your methods section. You have a clear structure, and the participant recruitment and inclusion criteria are described well enough that another researcher could understand who was studied.

There are a few areas to strengthen before resubmission. First, the procedure for the survey administration is vague. Specify the order in which measures were given, how long participants had to complete them, and whether the session was online or in person. Second, the justification for your sample size is missing. Briefly explain how you arrived at 60 participants, whether through a power analysis or another rationale. Third, you describe the statistical analysis but do not say which software you used or which version. Add this, and state the significance threshold you set.

Finally, check the consistency of your tense and voice. Methods are usually written in the past tense, and a few sentences in the third paragraph slip into the present.

Overall, this is a solid draft. Once these details are added, the section will be much easier to follow and to replicate. Please feel free to send a revised version or come to office hours if you want to talk through the sample size calculation.
