**IMPORTANT NOTICE: Changes to Recycling Collection Schedule**

Effective Monday, November 4, 2024, the residential recycling collection schedule will be updated to improve service efficiency across the municipality. 

**Key Changes:**
* **New Collection Days:** Your recycling pickup day may have shifted. Please review the updated route map to confirm your new service day.
* **Set-Out Times:** All recycling bins must be placed curbside by 7:00 AM on your designated day. 
* **Accepted Materials:** Collection guidelines remain the same. Please ensure all items are clean, dry, and loose in the bin (not bagged).

To view the new schedule and check your specific address, please visit **www.cityservices.gov/recycling** or contact the Department of Public Works at **(555) 019-2834**. 

Thank you for your cooperation and continued commitment to keeping our community clean.
