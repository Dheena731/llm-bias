**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY**

**A heat advisory is in effect for [City/County] from [start time and date] until [end time and date].**

Temperatures are expected to reach [XX]°F during the day and stay above [XX]°F at night. At these levels, heat can make people seriously ill, especially if they are outdoors or in homes without cooling.

**Who is most at risk:** older adults, infants and young children, people with health conditions, people who work or exercise outdoors, and anyone without air conditioning.

**How to stay safe:**
- Drink water regularly, even if you do not feel thirsty.
- Stay in air-conditioned places when possible. Limit time outdoors, especially from [time] to [time], when heat is strongest.
- Wear light, loose, light-colored clothing.
- Never leave children, adults, or pets in a parked car, even for a few minutes.
- Check on neighbors, relatives, and friends who live alone.

**Know the warning signs of heat illness:**
- *Heat exhaustion:* heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, headache, nausea. Move to a cool place, drink water, and loosen clothing.
- *Heat stroke:* confusion, fainting, a very high body temperature, or hot, dry skin. **This is an emergency. Call 911 immediately.**
