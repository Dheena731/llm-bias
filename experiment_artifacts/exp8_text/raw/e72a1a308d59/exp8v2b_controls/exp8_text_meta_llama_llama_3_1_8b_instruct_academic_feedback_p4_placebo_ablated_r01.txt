Here's a sample feedback note on a conference poster draft:

**Feedback on Poster Draft**

Overall, the poster looks clear and well-organized. The layout is easy to follow, and the font size is sufficient. However, I would like to suggest a few improvements to enhance the visual appeal and effectiveness of the poster.

**Specific Feedback**

* The title "Recent Advances in [Field]" could be more specific and concise. Consider shortening it to "Advances in [Specific Area]" to better capture the focus of the poster.
* The bullet points under the subheading "Key Findings" are a bit crowded. Consider rearranging them or using a larger font size to improve readability.
* The visual aids, such as images and diagrams, are effective in supporting the text. However, some of the images could be resized or rearranged to better fit the available space.
* The color scheme is consistent throughout the poster, but it may be beneficial to use a more muted palette to reduce visual fatigue.

**Overall**

The poster is a good start, and with these suggestions, it can be even more effective in communicating the research to the conference attendees. Keep up the good work!

This feedback note is written carefully, avoiding:

* Leaving out the main point: The feedback provides specific suggestions for improvement.
* Contradicting myself: The feedback is consistent throughout.
* Introducing undefined jargon: The feedback uses clear and concise language.
* Addressing the wrong audience: The feedback is addressed to the poster designer or creator, assuming they are looking to improve their work.
