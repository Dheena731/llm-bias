**Subject: Your new monthly spending summary**

We've added a monthly spending summary to help you see where your money went each month, without digging through individual transactions.

**What it shows**
- Your total spending for the month
- Spending by category, such as groceries, transport, and bills
- How this month compares with last month
- Your largest individual purchases

**When you'll get it**
The summary is ready in the first few days after each month ends. You'll get a notification when it's available.

**How to view it**
Open the app and go to **Summary**, then choose a month. You can also look back at earlier months.

**Why we added it**
Many of you told us it was hard to see the overall picture. This gives you a quick monthly overview so you can spot patterns and adjust your spending if you want to.

**Good to know**
The summary uses only the transactions already in your account. If a purchase is in the wrong category, you can change it, and the summary will update to match.

If you have feedback or questions, reply to this message or contact support.
