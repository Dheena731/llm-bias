Toss your vegetables with oil and salt before they go in the oven, then add any delicate flavorings near the end. Salt and oil at the start help the vegetables brown and caramelize, while ingredients like minced garlic, fresh herbs, or a squeeze of lemon juice can burn or turn bitter if they roast the whole time. For the best flavor, pull the pan out during the last five minutes, sprinkle on your garlic or herbs, and finish with a splash of lemon juice or a drizzle of balsamic vinegar right after roasting.
