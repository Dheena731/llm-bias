Here's a short cooking tip for a weeknight dinner:

To make a quick and delicious weeknight dinner, try preparing a "base" ingredient on the weekend or a day off, and then using it in different ways throughout the week. For example, roast a large batch of chicken breasts or cook a big pot of brown rice, and then use them as the foundation for various meals. On Monday, shred the chicken and add it to a salad or wrap; on Tuesday, serve it with roasted vegetables and quinoa; and on Wednesday, use the leftover rice to make a hearty stir-fry with your favorite vegetables and protein. This technique can save you time and reduce food waste, while also keeping your meals interesting and varied. Simply reheat, add some fresh ingredients, and you're ready for a tasty and stress-free dinner!
