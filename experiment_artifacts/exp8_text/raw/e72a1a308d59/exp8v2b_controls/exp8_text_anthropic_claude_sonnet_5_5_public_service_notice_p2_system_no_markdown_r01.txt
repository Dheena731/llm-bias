PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE

The City Department of Public Works announces the following temporary road closure.

Location: Maple Street between 4th Avenue and 8th Avenue

Dates: Monday, June 3 through Friday, June 14

Hours: 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. daily, with possible extension into evening hours if weather delays work

Reason: Water main replacement and road resurfacing

Details: During this period, Maple Street will be closed to all through traffic. Local residents and business owners will have access to their properties from the nearest open intersection, though short delays may occur. Pedestrian access will be maintained along the north sidewalk. On-street parking will not be available within the work zone.

Detour: Drivers are advised to use Oak Street to the north or Pine Street to the south. Detour signs will be posted. Please allow extra travel time, especially during morning and afternoon commute hours.

Bus Service: Routes 12 and 15 will be rerouted along Oak Street. Temporary stops will be marked with signs.

Safety: Please obey all posted signs and follow directions from flaggers and work crews. Drive slowly and use caution near the work area.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience as we make these important improvements to the community's infrastructure.

For questions or updates, contact the Department of Public Works at 555-0142, Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., or visit the city website.
