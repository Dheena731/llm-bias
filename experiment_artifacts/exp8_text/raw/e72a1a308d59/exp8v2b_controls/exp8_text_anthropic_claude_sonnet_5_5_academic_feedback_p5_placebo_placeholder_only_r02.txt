**Comment on Citations:**

Several claims in this essay lack supporting citations. In the second and third paragraphs especially, you present statistics and refer to research findings without naming their sources. Statements such as "studies show" or "experts agree" need to be tied to specific publications so readers can verify them. The direct quotation in the fourth paragraph also has no attribution, which could be read as plagiarism even if that was not your intent.

To fix this, go back through your draft and mark every fact, figure, paraphrased idea, and quotation that came from another source. Then add an in-text citation for each one in the required style, and make sure every citation has a matching entry in your reference list. Check that the reverse is also true, so no source is listed that you never actually cite.

Proper citation strengthens your argument by showing that it rests on credible evidence, and it gives credit to the authors whose work you are building on.
