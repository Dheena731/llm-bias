Quarterly Access Reviews

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain the security and integrity of our systems, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews for all users.

These reviews will be performed on a regular schedule, with the next review scheduled to take place on [insert date]. During this time, all users will be required to verify their access rights and confirm that they still require access to sensitive systems and data.

If you are no longer in need of access to these systems, please notify the relevant administrator immediately so that we can remove your access rights. If you are unsure about your access rights or have any questions, please contact the system administrator for assistance.

Your cooperation and prompt attention to this matter are greatly appreciated.
