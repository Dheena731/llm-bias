**Notice: Lock Your Screen When Away From Your Desk**

All Staff,

This is a reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you leave your workstation, even for a short time.

Unlocked screens put confidential information at risk and can lead to unauthorized access to company systems, client data, and personal files.

**How to lock your screen quickly:**
- Windows: press Windows key + L
- Mac: press Control + Command + Q

Please also make sure your device is set to lock automatically after a few minutes of inactivity.

Thank you for helping keep our information secure.

IT Security Team
