**Subject: How to Report a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If you lose your company laptop or it is stolen, report it right away. Quick reporting lets us lock the device and protect company and customer information.

**What to do:**

1. **Contact the IT Service Desk immediately**, by phone or email, as soon as you realize the laptop is missing. Ask them to lock the device and block access to company systems.
2. **Tell your manager** about the loss.
3. **If the laptop was stolen, file a police report** and send a copy to the IT Service Desk.
4. **Give the IT team these details:** the asset tag number (the company ID label on the bottom of the laptop), when and where you last had it, and what work or accounts it may have contained.

Do not wait to see whether the laptop turns up. Every hour of delay increases the risk that sensitive information is exposed.
