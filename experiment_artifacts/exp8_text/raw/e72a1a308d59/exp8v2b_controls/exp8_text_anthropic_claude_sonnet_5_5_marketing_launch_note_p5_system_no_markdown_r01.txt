Say hello to Say It Again, a new podcast for anyone who has ever stared at a menu, a street sign, or a text message and thought, "I almost understand this."

Every week, we turn real-life language into small, usable lessons. Each episode runs about fifteen minutes and focuses on one situation, whether that's ordering coffee, making small talk with a neighbor, or recovering when you completely forget the word you need. You'll hear natural conversations, simple explanations, and a few mistakes along the way, because mistakes are where most learning happens.

Our first three episodes are available today. We start with greetings and introductions, move on to the phrases that rescue you when you're lost, and finish with a short guide to sounding less like a textbook and more like a person.

You don't need any experience to join us, just a little curiosity and a willingness to say things out loud, even when they come out wrong.

Press play, repeat after us, and let's get started. New episodes arrive every Tuesday, wherever you listen to podcasts.
