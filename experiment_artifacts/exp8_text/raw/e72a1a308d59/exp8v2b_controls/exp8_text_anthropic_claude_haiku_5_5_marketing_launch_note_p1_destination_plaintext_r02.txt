Introducing Tally: invoicing and expense tracking that finally fits small business

We're excited to announce the launch of Tally, a simple app built for freelancers, contractors, and small shop owners who would rather spend their time on work than on paperwork.

With Tally, you can:

Create and send professional invoices in under a minute
Track expenses by snapping a photo of a receipt
See who owes you money at a glance
Get a clear summary of your income and spending, ready for tax season

No accounting background required. Tally works on your phone, tablet, or computer, and your data syncs across all of them.
