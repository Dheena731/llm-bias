Attention all staff,

We are introducing a new expense approval workflow to improve efficiency and transparency in our financial processes. As of today, all expense reports must be submitted through our online portal, where they will be reviewed and approved by department managers and finance team members.

The new workflow includes automated reminders and notifications to ensure timely approval and reimbursement. Employees can track the status of their expense reports through the portal, and receive email updates when their reports are approved or require additional information.

If you have any questions or need assistance with the new workflow, please don't hesitate to reach out to the finance team. We are happy to provide support and guidance to ensure a smooth transition.
