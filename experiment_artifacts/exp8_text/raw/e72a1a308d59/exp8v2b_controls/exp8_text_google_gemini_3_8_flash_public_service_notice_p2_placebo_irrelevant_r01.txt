**PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE**

**Location:** Elmwood Avenue between 4th Street and 7th Street  
**Dates & Times:** Monday, November 4, to Thursday, November 7, 2024, daily from 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM  

Please be advised that Elmwood Avenue will be closed to all through traffic between 4th Street and 7th Street for scheduled water main repairs and road resurfacing. 

**Traffic Impact & Detours:**
* A marked detour route will direct traffic via Pine Street. 
* Local access will be maintained for residents and emergency services, though minor delays should be expected.
* Street parking in the designated work zone will be prohibited during work hours. Vehicles left in the zone may be towed at the owner’s expense.

Motorists are encouraged to plan alternate routes and exercise caution when driving near the construction area. 

For questions or concerns, please contact the City Department of Public Works at **(555) 019-2834** or email **publicworks@citygov.org**.

Thank you for your cooperation and patience. 

**City Department of Public Works**
