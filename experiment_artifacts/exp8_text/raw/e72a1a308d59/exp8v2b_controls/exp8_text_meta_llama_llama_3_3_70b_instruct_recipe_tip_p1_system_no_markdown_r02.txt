One of the easiest ways to prepare a quick and delicious weeknight dinner is to cook proteins and vegetables in advance. Consider setting aside some time on the weekend to grill or roast a large batch of chicken, steak, or tofu, and to chop and sauté a variety of vegetables such as bell peppers, onions, and mushrooms. During the week, you can then use these pre-cooked ingredients to whip up a variety of meals, such as stir-fries, salads, or wraps, in just a few minutes. This simple trick can save you time and stress during the busy week, and ensure that you have a healthy and satisfying meal on the table every night.
