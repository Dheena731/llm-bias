**Important Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection**

Effective [Date], the recycling collection schedule and accepted materials will be modified. The new collection schedule will be as follows:

* Recycling will be collected every other Wednesday, instead of every Thursday.
* The list of accepted materials has been updated to include:
	+ Plastic containers #1-7
	+ Glass bottles and jars
	+ Corrugated cardboard
	+ Newspaper and mixed paper
* The following materials will no longer be accepted:
	+ Plastic bags and wrap
	+ Styrofoam

Residents are encouraged to review the updated guidelines and prepare their recyclables accordingly. For more information, please visit our website or contact the Public Works department at [phone number] or [email address].
