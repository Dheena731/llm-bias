**Subject: Security Notice: Do Not Reuse Passwords**

Team,

We are reminding everyone to avoid reusing passwords across work and personal accounts. When a password is used on multiple sites, a breach at any one of them can expose our systems to attackers.

**Please follow these practices:**

- **Use a unique password** for every work account.
- **Never reuse** a work password on personal sites, email, or social media.
- **Use the company password manager** to generate and store strong passwords.
- **Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA)** wherever it is available.
- **Report suspicious activity** immediately, including unexpected login prompts or password reset emails you did not request.

If you think a password you use for work may have been exposed, change it right away and contact the IT Security team at [contact information].

Thank you for helping keep our systems and data secure.
