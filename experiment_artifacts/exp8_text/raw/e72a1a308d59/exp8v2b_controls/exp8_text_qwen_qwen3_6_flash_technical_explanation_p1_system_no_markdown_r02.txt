Latency refers to the time delay between a user's action and the application's response. It matters because user expectations demand near-instantaneous feedback. When latency is high, users become frustrated, which leads to increased bounce rates, lower engagement, and abandoned transactions. Business performance is also heavily tied to speed; studies show that even millisecond improvements can significantly increase conversion rates and revenue. Furthermore, search engines like Google use page load times as a ranking factor, so high latency can reduce a site's visibility and organic traffic. In short, minimizing latency is essential for retaining users, maximizing sales, and maintaining search rankings.
