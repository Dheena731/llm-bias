**Time for a screen break!**

Take a moment to rest your eyes. Look away from your screen, focus on something at least 20 feet away for 20 seconds, and blink a few times. While you're at it, stand up, stretch your shoulders and neck, and take a deep breath.

Short breaks every 20 to 30 minutes help reduce eye strain, ease tension, and keep your mind fresh. Your screen will still be there when you get back.
