NOTICE OF ACCEPTABLE USE OF SHARED NETWORK

The shared network (the "Network") is provided for the legitimate business and personal use of authorized users. All users of the Network are expected to comply with this Notice of Acceptable Use.

Users are prohibited from using the Network to transmit, distribute, or store any material that is unlawful, obscene, defamatory, or infringing of intellectual property rights. Users shall not use the Network to harass, intimidate, or threaten others, or to engage in any other activity that is disruptive or harmful to the Network or its users.

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality and security of their account information and passwords, and for ensuring that their use of the Network does not compromise the security or integrity of the Network or any connected systems.

The Network may not be used for commercial purposes, including but not limited to, selling or advertising products or services, without the prior written consent of the Network administrator.

Violations of this Notice of Acceptable Use may result in termination of access to the Network, and may also subject the user to civil or criminal liability. By using the Network, users acknowledge that they have read, understand, and agree to comply with this Notice of Acceptable Use.
