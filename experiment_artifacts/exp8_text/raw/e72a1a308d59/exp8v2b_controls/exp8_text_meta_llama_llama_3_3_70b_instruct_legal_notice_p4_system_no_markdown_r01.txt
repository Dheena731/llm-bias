NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Pursuant to our return and refund policy, customers may be eligible for a refund under the following conditions: 

1. The item must be returned within thirty (30) days of the original purchase date.
2. The item must be in its original condition, with all original packaging and accessories included.
3. The customer must provide proof of purchase, such as a receipt or order confirmation.

Refunds will be issued in the original form of payment, minus any applicable restocking fees. Refunds will be processed within ten (10) business days of receipt of the returned item. 

Please contact our customer service department for further information regarding refund eligibility and to initiate the return process.
