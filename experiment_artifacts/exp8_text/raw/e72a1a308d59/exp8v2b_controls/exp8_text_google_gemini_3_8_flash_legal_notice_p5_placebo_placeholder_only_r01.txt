**ACCEPTABLE NETWORK USE POLICY AND NOTICE**

Access to and use of this shared network is a privilege conditioned upon compliance with applicable laws and the terms set forth herein. By connecting to, accessing, or transmitting data through this network, all users acknowledge and agree to the following provisions:

**1. Authorized Use**  
This network is provided strictly for lawful, authorized purposes. Users are responsible for exercising due care to maintain the security, integrity, and operational availability of the shared infrastructure.

**2. Prohibited Conduct**  
Users shall not engage in any activity that compromises network functionality, violates third-party rights, or contravenes local, state, or federal law. Prohibited conduct includes, without limitation:
* Unauthorized access to systems, data, devices, or accounts;
* Transmission, hosting, or distribution of malware, viruses, or destructive code;
* Interception, alteration, or disruption of traffic generated by other network participants;
* Excessive bandwidth utilization that degrades service quality for other users; and
* The transmission or storage of infringing, unlawful, or harmful material.

**3. Monitoring and Expectation of Privacy**  
Users possess no expectation of privacy concerning any communications, files, or data transmitted over or stored within this network. Network administrators reserve the right to monitor, intercept, log, and inspect all network traffic and device connections at any time, without prior notice, for the purposes of maintenance, security enforcement, and regulatory compliance.

**4. Enforcement and Remedies**  
Any violation of this notice may result in the immediate and permanent termination of network access privileges, without liability. Serious infractions may be referred to relevant law enforcement agencies and may give rise to civil or criminal liability.
