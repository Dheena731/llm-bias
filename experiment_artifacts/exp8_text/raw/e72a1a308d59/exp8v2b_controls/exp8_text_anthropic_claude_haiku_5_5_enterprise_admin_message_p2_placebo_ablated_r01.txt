**Subject: New Expense Approval Process Starting June 2**

Hello everyone,

Starting Monday, June 2, every expense report must be approved by your direct manager before Finance will process it. Reports without manager approval will not be paid.

**How it works:**

1. Submit your expense report in the expense system, with a receipt attached for each item.
2. Your direct manager approves the report or returns it with comments within 3 business days.
3. After manager approval, Finance reviews and pays the report within 5 business days.

Reports over $500 also need approval from your department head before Finance can pay them.

If your report is returned, fix the items noted in the comments and resubmit it. Returned reports are not lost, and you do not need to start over.

If you have questions, contact the Finance team before June 2.

Thank you,
Administration
