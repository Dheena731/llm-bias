Here is a short message explaining an end-of-year tax statement:

**Important: Review Your End-of-Year Tax Statement**

As we approach the end of the year, we are required to provide you with a tax statement reflecting your income and tax withholdings from January 1 to December 31. This document is a summary of your tax obligations and is necessary for completing your annual tax return.

The statement will include details such as:

* Your total income for the year
* The amount of taxes withheld and paid on your behalf
* Any adjustments made to your tax account, such as deductions or credits
* A summary of any other tax-related transactions, such as payments or refunds

Please review the statement carefully and contact us if you have any questions or concerns. You will need this information to complete your annual tax return and ensure accuracy in your tax reporting.

**If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to our office. We are here to help.**
