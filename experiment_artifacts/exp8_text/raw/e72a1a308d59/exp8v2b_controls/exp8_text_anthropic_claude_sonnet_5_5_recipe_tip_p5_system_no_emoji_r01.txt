**Season in two stages.** Toss your vegetables with oil, salt, and pepper before roasting so the seasoning clings and the edges caramelize. Then, once they come out of the oven, finish with a squeeze of lemon juice or a splash of vinegar and a pinch of flaky salt. Delicate flavors like fresh herbs, citrus, and garlic can burn or turn bitter in high heat, so adding them at the end keeps them bright and fresh.
