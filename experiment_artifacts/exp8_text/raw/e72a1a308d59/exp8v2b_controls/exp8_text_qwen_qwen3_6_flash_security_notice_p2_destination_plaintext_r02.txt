NOTICE: SECURITY ALERT REGARDING PHISHING EMAILS

All staff are reminded to remain vigilant against phishing emails designed to steal login credentials or company data. These messages often impersonate trusted colleagues, vendors, or services, and may feature urgent language, suspicious links, unexpected attachments, or slight misspellings in sender addresses.

DO NOT click on links, open attachments, or reply to suspicious messages. Instead, forward the email immediately to IT Security at security@company.com or call extension 5500. After forwarding, delete the original message. Always verify unexpected requests by contacting the supposed sender through a known, trusted phone number or internal messaging system.

Your vigilance helps protect our network and client data. Report any doubts promptly.

Thank you for your cooperation.
