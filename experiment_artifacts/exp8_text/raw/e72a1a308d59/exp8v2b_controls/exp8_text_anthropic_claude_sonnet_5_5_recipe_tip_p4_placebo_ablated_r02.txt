**Tip: Prep components, not full meals.**

On Sunday, spend 30–45 minutes making a few building blocks instead of five complete lunches:

- **One grain or base** (rice, quinoa, or pasta)
- **One or two proteins** (grilled chicken, boiled eggs, or canned beans)
- **One or two vegetables** (roasted, or raw and chopped)
- **One sauce or dressing** to change the flavor

Store each item in its own container. Each day, combine them differently: a rice bowl on Monday, a wrap on Tuesday, a salad on Wednesday. This keeps lunches from feeling repetitive, cuts down on daily effort, and keeps food from getting soggy, since you assemble it fresh.

**Bonus:** Check your calendar first. If you have a lunch meeting or plan to eat out one day, prep for fewer meals so you don't waste food.
