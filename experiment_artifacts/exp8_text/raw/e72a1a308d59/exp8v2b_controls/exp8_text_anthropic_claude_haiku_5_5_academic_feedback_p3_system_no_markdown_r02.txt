Reviewer comment:

The research question as currently stated is difficult to evaluate. The manuscript asks "how do social media platforms affect adolescent well-being?" but does not specify which platforms, which dimensions of well-being (e.g., depression, self-esteem, sleep, academic performance), what kind of effect is expected (causal, correlational, or descriptive), or which adolescent population is of interest. As a result, it is unclear what would count as a confirming or disconfirming result, and the methods section does not map clearly onto any specific hypothesis. I recommend that the authors narrow the question to a testable formulation, for example by stating the exposure, outcome, population, and expected direction of effect, and by explaining how the chosen design addresses that question. Without this clarification, I cannot assess whether the conclusions follow from the evidence presented.
