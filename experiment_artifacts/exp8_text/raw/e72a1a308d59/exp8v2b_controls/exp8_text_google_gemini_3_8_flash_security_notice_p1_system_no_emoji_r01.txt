**SECURITY NOTICE: Protect Your Accounts by Avoiding Password Reuse**

**To:** All Staff  
**From:** IT Security Department  
**Subject:** The Risks of Reusing Passwords Across Accounts  

Password reuse remains one of the leading causes of security breaches. When you use the same password for multiple services—both personal and professional—a compromise on a single external website can expose company systems and sensitive data.

**Required Actions:**

1. **Keep Work and Personal Passwords Separate:** Never use your company login credentials for external or personal websites.
2. **Use Unique Passwords:** Ensure every work-related account, system, and tool uses a distinct, complex password or passphrase.
3. **Use the Approved Password Manager:** Utilize the company-provided password manager to generate and securely store strong credentials.
4. **Report Suspicious Activity:** If you suspect an external account sharing your work password has been compromised, change your company password immediately and notify IT Support.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our workplace secure.
