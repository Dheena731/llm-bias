PUBLIC NOTICE: LIBRARY OPENING HOURS

Effective Monday, the Riverside Public Library will operate on the following schedule:

Monday to Thursday: 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
Friday: 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
Saturday: 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
Sunday: Closed

Please note the following:

- The library will be closed on all public holidays.
- The children's section closes 30 minutes before the library closes.
- Book returns are available 24 hours a day at the outdoor drop box beside the main entrance.
- Computer and printing services end 15 minutes before closing time.

For questions about holiday closures or special events, please call the front desk at 555-0142 or visit the library's website.

Thank you for supporting your local library.
