Cook once, use twice: when you make rice, grains, or roasted vegetables on Monday, double the batch. Store the extra in a sealed container in the fridge. On Tuesday or Wednesday, you can turn it into fried rice, grain bowls, or a quick soup in about 10 minutes. Add a fried egg or leftover chicken, finish with a splash of soy sauce or a squeeze of lemon, and dinner is done with almost no extra effort.
