Introducing LedgerLite: bookkeeping that finally fits your small business

Running a small business means wearing every hat, and bookkeeping shouldn't be the one that takes you away from customers. Today we're launching LedgerLite, a simple app that tracks income and expenses, sorts receipts, and prepares tax-ready reports in just a few taps.

With LedgerLite, you can:

- Snap a photo of any receipt and have it categorized automatically
- Send invoices and see at a glance who has paid and who hasn't
- Check your cash flow anytime with plain-language summaries
- Export clean reports for your accountant in one click

LedgerLite is built for owners who don't have time for complicated accounting software. No finance degree required.
