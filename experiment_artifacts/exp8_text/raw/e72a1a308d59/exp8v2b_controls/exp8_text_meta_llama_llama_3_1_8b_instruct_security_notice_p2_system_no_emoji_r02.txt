NOTICE TO ALL EMPLOYEES

As part of our ongoing efforts to protect the company's assets and maintain a secure working environment, we want to remind everyone to be vigilant when receiving emails, especially those that appear to be from a trusted source.

Phishing emails are becoming increasingly sophisticated and may attempt to trick you into revealing sensitive information or downloading malicious attachments. These emails often contain grammatical errors, use generic greetings, or ask you to click on a link to verify your account information.

To protect yourself and the company, please follow these best practices:

* Be cautious when opening attachments or clicking on links from unknown sources.
* Verify the sender's email address to ensure it is legitimate and not a spoofed address.
* Never respond to emails that ask for sensitive information, such as passwords or financial information.
* Report any suspicious emails to the IT department immediately.

If you are unsure whether an email is legitimate, do not hesitate to contact the IT department for verification. We will continue to provide training and resources to help you stay safe online.
