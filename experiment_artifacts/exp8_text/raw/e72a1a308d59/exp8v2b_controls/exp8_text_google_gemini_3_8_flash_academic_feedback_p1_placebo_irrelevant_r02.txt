Thank you for submitting this draft of your literature review. You have clearly engaged deeply with the existing scholarship, and your bibliography demonstrates a strong, relevant foundation for your project. The background context provided in Section 1 is particularly well-developed and frames the research problem effectively.

To strengthen the review for the next draft, I recommend focusing on the following three areas:

1. **Move from Summary to Synthesis:** Currently, several sections read as sequential summaries of individual studies (i.e., "Author A found X, while Author B found Y"). Try to reorganize these paragraphs thematically around key concepts, debates, or methodological approaches. Instead of describing one paper per paragraph, group related studies together to highlight where scholars agree, where they diverge, and why those differences matter.
2. **Foreground Your Critical Voice:** Make sure you are not just reporting findings, but critically evaluating them. Consider commenting on methodological limitations, sample sizes, or theoretical assumptions in the key studies you cite. This critical evaluation will help justify why your own proposed study is necessary.
3. **Clarify the Research Gap:** The transition from your review of the literature to your specific research questions could be sharper. At the end of each thematic section, briefly indicate what remains unknown or unresolved, culminating in a clear statement of the gap your work intends to fill.

**Next Steps:**
I suggest drafting a revised outline organized strictly by analytical themes (using descriptive subheadings) before you begin rewriting. 

Overall, this is a very promising start. Please let me know if you would like to discuss these points during office hours next week.
