To: All Staff

We have recently seen an increase in phishing emails being sent to staff members. These emails are designed to look like they are from legitimate sources, but are actually attempts to steal your personal information or gain access to our systems.

Please be cautious when receiving emails that ask you to:

- Click on links or download attachments from unknown senders
- Provide sensitive information such as passwords or financial information
- Verify your account information via email

If you receive an email that you suspect is a phishing attempt, do not respond or click on any links. Instead, please forward the email to our IT department so we can investigate and take necessary action.

Remember, our company will never ask you to provide sensitive information via email. If you are unsure about the legitimacy of an email, it is always best to err on the side of caution and contact the sender directly to verify.

Thank you for your vigilance in helping to protect our systems and information.
