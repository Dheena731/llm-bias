**Subject: Changes to Card Payment Fees, Effective March 1**

Dear customers,

Starting March 1, Harbor Coffee Co. will be updating the credit and debit card processing fee we apply to in-store and online purchases. The fee will drop from 3.0% to 2.2% of the transaction total. Cash and gift card payments are not affected.

The change follows a new agreement with our payment processor, which lowered its per-transaction rates for small businesses. We are passing that saving on to you rather than keeping it as margin.

For a typical $20 order, the fee will fall from 60 cents to 44 cents. Orders placed before March 1 will be charged the previous rate, even if they are picked up or delivered after that date.

If you have questions about your receipt or a charge, reply to this email or stop by the counter, and we will be glad to help.

Thank you for your continued support.

The Harbor Coffee Co. Team
