**Tip: Treat tender herbs like flowers.**

For soft-stemmed herbs like cilantro, parsley, and dill, trim the ends of the stems and stand the bunch in a jar with an inch of water. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and refrigerate. Change the water every couple of days, and the herbs will stay fresh for up to two weeks.

For sturdier herbs like rosemary, thyme, and sage, wrap them in a barely damp paper towel, place them in a zip-top bag, and keep them in the crisper drawer.

Basil is the exception. It blackens in the cold, so keep it in a jar of water on the counter, out of direct sunlight.
