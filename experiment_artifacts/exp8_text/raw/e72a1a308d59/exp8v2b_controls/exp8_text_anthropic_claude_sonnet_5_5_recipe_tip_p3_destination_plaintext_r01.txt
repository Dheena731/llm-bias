Rinse your rice before cooking. Place the rice in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer and run cold water over it, swirling with your hand, until the water goes from cloudy to mostly clear (usually 3 to 5 rinses). This washes away the surface starch that makes grains clump together. Then use the right ratio, about 1 cup of long-grain white rice to 1.5 cups of water, and avoid stirring while it cooks. When it's done, let it rest covered for 10 minutes, then fluff with a fork to release steam and keep the grains separate.
