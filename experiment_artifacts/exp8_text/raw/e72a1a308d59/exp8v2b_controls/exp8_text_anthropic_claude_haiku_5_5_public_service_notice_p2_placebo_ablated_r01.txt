**PUBLIC NOTICE: Road Closure on Maple Street**

Maple Street will be closed to all vehicle traffic between Oak Avenue and Pine Road from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. on Saturday, June 14, while crews repair a water main.

**Detours:** Drivers should use Elm Street or Birch Avenue as alternate routes. Follow posted detour signs.

**Access:** Residents and businesses on the closed section can reach their properties from either end of the closure. Emergency vehicles will have access at all times.

**Pedestrians:** Sidewalks on the closed section will remain open, though they may be narrowed.

Questions? Contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567.

Issued by the City of Riverton Public Works Department
