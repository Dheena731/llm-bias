Welcome to "Lingua Vista", the podcast that helps you explore new languages and cultures from around the world. Each week, we'll delve into a different language, discussing its history, grammar, and usage, as well as sharing stories and tips from native speakers and experienced learners. Whether you're a beginner or an advanced learner, our goal is to inspire and motivate you to continue your language-learning journey.
