**Public Service Notice: Library Operating Hours**

The City Central Library welcomes all community members to visit during regular operating hours:

* **Monday through Thursday:** 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM  
* **Friday and Saturday:** 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM  
* **Sunday:** 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM  

The exterior book return box is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week, for book and media drop-offs. Digital services, including e-book loans, research databases, and catalog searches, remain accessible online at all times. 

Staff are available during open hours to assist with research, library card registration, computer access, and room reservations.
