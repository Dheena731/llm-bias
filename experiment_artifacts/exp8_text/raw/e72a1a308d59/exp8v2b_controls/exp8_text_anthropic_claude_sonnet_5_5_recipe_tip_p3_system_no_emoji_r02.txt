Rinse the rice before cooking. Place the rice in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer and run cold water over it, swirling with your hand, until the water goes from cloudy to mostly clear (usually 3 to 5 rinses). This washes away the surface starch that makes grains clump together.

For extra fluffiness, use a slightly lower water ratio (about 1.5 parts water to 1 part long-grain white rice), avoid lifting the lid while it cooks, and let it rest covered for 10 minutes off the heat. Then fluff gently with a fork rather than stirring or pressing it.
