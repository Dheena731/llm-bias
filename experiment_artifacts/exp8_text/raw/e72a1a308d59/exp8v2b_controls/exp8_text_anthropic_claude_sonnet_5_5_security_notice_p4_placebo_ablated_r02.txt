**Notice: Turn On Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**

**Action required:** Please enable two-factor authentication on your account by **[date]**.

**What it is:** Two-factor authentication adds a second step when you sign in. After entering your password, you will also enter a one-time code or approve a prompt on your phone. This means that someone who learns your password still cannot get into your account.

**How to enable it:**
1. Sign in and open **Account Settings**.
2. Select **Security**, then **Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Choose how you want to receive your second step: an authenticator app (recommended) or text message.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to confirm your choice.
5. Save the backup codes shown at the end and store them somewhere safe, such as a password manager or a locked drawer. You can use them to sign in if you lose your phone.

**What to expect:** Setup takes about five minutes. After that, you will be asked for your second step when you sign in on a new device or after you have been signed out.

**Need help?** Contact the Help Desk at **[email/phone]**. We will never ask you for your password or your one-time codes, so do not share them with anyone.

Thank you for helping keep your account secure.
