**Notice to Voters: Find Your Polling Station for the Upcoming Election**

Election Day is Tuesday, November 5, 2024. 

Please verify your assigned polling station before heading out to vote, as voting locations are determined by your residential address and may have changed since the last election. You must vote at your designated station for your ballot to be counted.

**How to find your polling location:**
* Visit **www.elections.gov/find-my-polling-place** and enter your residential address.
* Check the polling address listed on your official voter information card.
* Call the Election Office helpline at **1-800-555-0199**.

Polling stations will be open from **7:00 AM to 8:00 PM**. As long as you are in line by 8:00 PM, you will be allowed to vote.
