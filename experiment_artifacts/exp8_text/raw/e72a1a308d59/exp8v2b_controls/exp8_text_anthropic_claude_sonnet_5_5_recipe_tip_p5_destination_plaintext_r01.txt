Season roasted vegetables in two stages for the best flavor. Before roasting, toss them with oil, salt, and pepper so the seasoning clings and the edges caramelize. Right after they come out of the oven, add a second layer of flavor while they're still hot: a squeeze of lemon juice, a splash of balsamic vinegar, a sprinkle of fresh herbs, or a pinch of flaky salt. Delicate ingredients like fresh herbs and citrus lose their brightness in high heat, so adding them at the end keeps the flavor vivid. Also, make sure the vegetables are dry and spread in a single layer so they roast rather than steam.
