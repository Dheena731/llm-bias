Important notice about phishing emails: Be cautious when receiving emails with links or attachments from unknown senders, as they may be attempts to steal your login credentials or install malware on your computer. Never click on suspicious links or open attachments from unfamiliar sources. If you are unsure about the legitimacy of an email, please contact the IT department for assistance. Remember to always verify the sender's email address and check for spelling and grammar mistakes, which are often signs of phishing attempts. Your vigilance is crucial in protecting our company's security.
