**NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS**

Each registered voter is assigned a specific polling station based on their residential address. To locate your designated site, visit your jurisdiction’s official election website, call your local election office, or refer to the voter information card recently mailed to you. Polling stations are typically housed in neighborhood schools, community centers, or public libraries. On Election Day, please arrive during posted voting hours and bring a valid government-issued photo ID along with proof of residence. If you need help finding your station or require accessibility accommodations, contact your local election authority directly. Thank you for participating.
