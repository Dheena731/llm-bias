**URGENT: Procedure for Reporting Lost or Stolen Company Laptops**

All employees are reminded that company policy requires the immediate reporting of any lost, misplaced, or stolen corporate devices to protect proprietary data and client confidentiality. 

If you cannot locate your company laptop, take the following steps immediately:

1. **Notify the IT Service Desk:** Contact the IT support team by phone or via the internal emergency portal right away so the device can be remotely locked, tracked, or wiped.
2. **Alert Your Direct Supervisor:** Inform your manager of the situation and the last known location of the device.
3. **Reset Account Passwords:** From a secure secondary device, immediately change the passwords for your corporate email and all internal systems.
4. **File a Police Report (if stolen):** If the device was taken from a vehicle, home, or public venue, report the theft to local law enforcement and retain the incident reference number for company records.

Prompt reporting is critical. Notifying the technical team within the first hour of a device going missing significantly reduces security risks to the organization.
