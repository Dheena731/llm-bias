Toss your vegetables in oil and salt before they go into the oven, and roast them at 425°F (220°C). The high heat caramelizes their natural sugars, so a sheet pan of broccoli, carrots, or bell peppers turns sweet and crisp in about 20 minutes, while you cook the protein on the stovetop or in the same oven. Cut the vegetables into similar-sized pieces so everything finishes at the same time.
