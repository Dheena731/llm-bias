**Tip: Preserving Fresh Herbs**

To keep your fresh herbs fresh for a longer period, follow these simple steps:

1. **Trim the Stems**: Before storing, trim the stems of the herbs at an angle to promote water uptake. This helps the herbs absorb moisture more efficiently.
2. **Wrap in Plastic Wrap**: Wrap the herbs in plastic wrap or aluminum foil, making sure they are tightly covered. This prevents moisture loss and helps retain the herbs' natural oils.
3. **Store in the Refrigerator**: Place the wrapped herbs in the refrigerator at a consistent temperature (around 40°F or 4°C). Avoid storing them in the warmest part of the fridge.
4. **Use a Glass Jar**: Consider storing your herbs in a glass jar with a lid. This allows for easy storage and visibility, and the jar can help maintain humidity.
5. **Don't Overcrowd**: Store each type of herb separately to prevent cross-contamination and to maintain their distinct flavors and aromas.

By following these steps, you can enjoy your fresh herbs for a longer period, ensuring they remain flavorful and fragrant in your kitchen.
