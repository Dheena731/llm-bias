Your end-of-year tax statement summarizes the income you earned and the taxes withheld or paid during the past year. You will need it when you prepare your tax return, so please keep it with your other financial records.

The statement typically shows your total income for the year, the amount of tax withheld from your pay or payments, and any other deductions or credits that apply. Please check that your name, address, and taxpayer identification number are correct. If anything looks wrong, contact us so we can issue a corrected statement.

If you have questions about the figures or how to use them, please reply to this message or call our support line. We are happy to help.
