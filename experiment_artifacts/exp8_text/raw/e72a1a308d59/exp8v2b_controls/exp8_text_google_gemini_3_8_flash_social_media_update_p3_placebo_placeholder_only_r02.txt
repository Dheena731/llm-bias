Looking for weekend plans? Join your neighbors this Saturday for the annual Riverside Green Park Spring Festival from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM! 

Enjoy live acoustic music, delicious bites from local food trucks, unique handmade goods from neighborhood artisans, and a free community plant swap. Kids can enjoy face painting and sidewalk chalk contests all afternoon.

Admission is completely free, and leashed pets are welcome. Pack a picnic blanket, invite a friend, and come celebrate the sunny weather together. See you there! ☀️🎶🌱
