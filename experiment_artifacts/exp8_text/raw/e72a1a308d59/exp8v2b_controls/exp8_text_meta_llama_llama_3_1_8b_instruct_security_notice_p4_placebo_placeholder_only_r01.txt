**Enabling Two-Factor Authentication**

To add an extra layer of security to your account, we recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA). This requires a second form of verification in addition to your password, making it much more difficult for unauthorized users to access your account.

To enable 2FA:

1. Log in to your account as you normally would.
2. Go to the account settings or profile page.
3. Look for the 2FA settings or a toggle to enable 2FA.
4. Follow the prompts to set up 2FA.

Common methods of 2FA include:

* Authenticator apps (such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator)
* SMS or voice verification codes
* Physical security keys (such as U2F keys)

Once 2FA is enabled, you will be prompted to enter a verification code or scan a QR code to complete the login process. This will ensure that even if someone gains access to your password, they will not be able to access your account without also possessing the second form of verification.

By enabling 2FA, you can significantly reduce the risk of unauthorized access to your account and protect your sensitive information.
