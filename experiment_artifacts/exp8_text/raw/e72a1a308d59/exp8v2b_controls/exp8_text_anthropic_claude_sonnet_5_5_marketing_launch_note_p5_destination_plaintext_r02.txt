Introducing "Say It Again": a new podcast for curious language learners

Today we're launching Say It Again, a short, friendly podcast for anyone who wants to speak a new language with more confidence, whether you're starting from zero or dusting off the Spanish you studied in high school.

Each episode runs about 15 minutes and focuses on one practical idea: a handful of useful phrases, a common mistake, a quick lesson on pronunciation, or a story about how a word came to mean what it does. No long lectures and no jargon. Just clear explanations, real conversations, and plenty of chances to repeat out loud.

What you can expect:

- Two new episodes every week, released Tuesday and Friday
- Native speakers joining us to show how words sound in real life
- Short practice challenges you can try on your commute or while making dinner
- Honest talk about the awkward, funny parts of learning a language

We're starting with Spanish, French, and Japanese, with more languages coming later this year. Tell us which ones you want next.

The first three episodes are available now wherever you listen to podcasts. If you enjoy the show, please share it with a friend who's learning too, and leave a rating or review. It helps other learners find us.

Thanks for listening, and welcome. Let's get talking.
