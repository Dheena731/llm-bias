We are excited to introduce a new monthly spending summary feature, designed to provide a clear and concise overview of your expenses. This summary will be sent to you at the beginning of each month, detailing your total spending from the previous month, broken down into categories such as housing, transportation, and entertainment. The summary will also highlight any unusual or large transactions, allowing you to quickly identify and address any potential issues. This new feature is intended to help you stay on top of your finances and make informed decisions about your spending habits.
