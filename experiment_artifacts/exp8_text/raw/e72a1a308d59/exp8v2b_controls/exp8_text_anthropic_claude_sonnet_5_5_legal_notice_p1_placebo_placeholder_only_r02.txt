# NOTICE OF CHANGES TO ACCOUNT TERMS

**Please read this notice carefully. It affects your rights and obligations under your account agreement.**

## 1. Purpose of This Notice

We are amending the terms that govern your account (the "Agreement"). This notice describes those changes and explains your options.

## 2. Effective Date

The changes take effect thirty (30) days after the date this notice was sent to you (the "Effective Date"). Changes will not apply to activity that occurred before the Effective Date.

## 3. Summary of Changes

- **Fees and Charges.** We may adjust fees applicable to your account. Any fee change will be listed in the updated fee schedule, which will be available in your account settings and upon request.
- **Dispute Resolution.** Claims relating to your account will be resolved through binding individual arbitration, except where applicable law prohibits it or where a claim qualifies for small claims court.
- **Privacy and Data Use.** Our Privacy Policy has been updated to describe how we collect, use, and share account information. The updated policy will govern from the Effective Date.
- **Account Suspension and Termination.** We may suspend or close an account for suspected fraud, unlawful activity, or material breach of the Agreement. Where permitted by law, we will give you notice and an opportunity to respond.

## 4. Your Options

- **Acceptance.** If you continue to use your account on or after the Effective Date, you agree to the amended terms.
- **Rejection.** If you do not agree, you may close your account before the Effective Date without penalty. Closing your account will not relieve you of obligations incurred before closure.
- **Opt-Out of Arbitration.** Where the arbitration provision applies, you may opt out by sending us written notice within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date. Opting out will not affect any other term of the Agreement.

## 5. Governing Terms

Except as expressly amended here, the Agreement remains in full force and effect. If this notice conflicts with the Agreement, this notice controls with respect to the changes described above.

## 6. Legal Rights

Nothing in this notice limits any right you have under applicable law, including consumer protection rights that cannot be waived by agreement.

## 7. Questions

Please contact our customer support team through your account dashboard or by replying to the message that delivered this notice. Keep a copy of this notice for your records.
