**Reminder: Book Your Annual Health Checkup**

It's time to schedule your yearly checkup. Regular exams help catch potential issues early, keep your vaccinations and screenings up to date, and give you a chance to talk with your doctor about any health questions.

**Quick steps:**
1. Call your doctor's office or use their online portal to find an available appointment.
2. Check your insurance coverage, since many plans cover preventive visits at no cost.
3. Write down any symptoms, concerns, or medications you want to discuss.
4. Add the appointment to your calendar so it doesn't slip by.

Take a few minutes today to make the call. A little time now can make a big difference for your health down the road.
