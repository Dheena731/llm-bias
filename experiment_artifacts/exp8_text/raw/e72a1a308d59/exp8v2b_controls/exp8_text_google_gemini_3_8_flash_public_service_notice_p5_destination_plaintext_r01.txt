NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS

Polling stations for the upcoming election will be open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM on Election Day.

Your assigned polling place is based on your current residential address and may have changed since the last election. Please note that you must vote at your designated location for your ballot to count.

To find your polling station:
- Visit www.elections.gov/find-my-poll
- Check your official voter information card
- Call the Election Office at (555) 019-2834

Please verify your location and review any local voter ID requirements before heading out to vote.
