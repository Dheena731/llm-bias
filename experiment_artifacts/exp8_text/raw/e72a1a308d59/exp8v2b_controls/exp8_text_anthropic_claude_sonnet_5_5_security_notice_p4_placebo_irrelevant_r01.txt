**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**

To better protect your account and personal information, we strongly encourage you to turn on two-factor authentication (2FA). This adds a second verification step when you sign in, so your account stays secure even if your password is compromised.

**How to enable 2FA:**

1. Sign in and go to **Settings** > **Security**.
2. Select **Two-Factor Authentication** and click **Enable**.
3. Choose your verification method: an authenticator app (recommended), text message, or security key.
4. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm your setup.
5. Save your backup codes in a safe place in case you lose access to your device.

**Why it matters:**
Passwords can be guessed, stolen, or leaked. With 2FA, an attacker would also need your phone or security key to get in.

Setup takes only a few minutes. If you need help, please contact our support team.

Thank you for helping us keep your account safe.
