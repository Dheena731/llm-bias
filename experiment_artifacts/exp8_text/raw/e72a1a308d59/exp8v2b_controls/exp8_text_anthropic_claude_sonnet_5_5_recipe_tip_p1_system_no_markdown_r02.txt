Cook your grains and prep your vegetables on Sunday so weeknight dinners come together fast. A big batch of rice or quinoa keeps in the fridge for up to five days, and chopped onions, peppers, and broccoli stay fresh in airtight containers. When you get home on a busy evening, you can toss the vegetables in a hot pan with a little oil and garlic, add a protein like chicken, eggs, or tofu, and warm the grains alongside. A splash of soy sauce, lemon juice, or your favorite hot sauce at the end ties everything together. You will have a satisfying meal on the table in about fifteen minutes.
