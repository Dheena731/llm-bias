**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY**

**[City or County Name]**, issued [date and time]

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for [area] from [start time] to [end time] on [date]. Temperatures are expected to reach [number] degrees Fahrenheit, and it will feel even hotter with humidity.

**Extreme heat can cause serious illness, including heat stroke.** Older adults, young children, people with chronic medical conditions, and anyone who works or exercises outdoors are at the greatest risk.

**What you should do:**
- Stay in air-conditioned spaces as much as possible.
- Drink water regularly, even if you do not feel thirsty.
- Never leave children, pets, or anyone who depends on you in a parked vehicle.
- Check on neighbors, friends, and relatives who live alone or may not have air conditioning.
- Schedule outdoor work and exercise for early morning or evening.
- Wear light, loose-fitting clothing and a wide-brimmed hat when outside.

**Warning signs of heat illness:** Heavy sweating with weakness, dizziness, nausea, muscle cramps, or headache can signal heat exhaustion. Call for emergency help right away if someone has a high body temperature, confusion, fainting, or stops sweating.

**Cooling centers** are open at [location(s)] during the advisory period. For more information, call [phone number] or visit [website].
