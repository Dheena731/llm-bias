**NOTICE: REPORTING A LOST COMPANY LAPTOP**

If you have misplaced or lost a company-issued laptop, you must report it immediately to protect our networks and sensitive information.

Please follow these steps:
1. Contact the IT Helpdesk right away by calling [Phone Number] or emailing [Email Address].
2. Provide your full name, department, laptop serial number (if available), last known location, and the time you realized it was missing.
3. Do not attempt to retrieve the device in an unsafe or unsecured location.

All reports will be handled discreetly. Prompt reporting is required by company policy; delays compromise data security and may result in disciplinary action. 

For further assistance, contact the IT Support Team. Thank you for your immediate attention to this matter.
