**Tip: Meal Prep for the Win**

Take 30 minutes on Sunday evening to plan out your lunches for the week ahead. Here's a simple process:

1. **Identify your goals**: Think about the types of meals you want to make (e.g. healthy, easy, or something specific like vegan or gluten-free).
2. **Check your schedule**: Look at your week's calendar and identify busy days when you'll need quick, easy lunches.
3. **Make a list**: Jot down 5-7 lunch ideas that fit your goals and schedule.
4. **Shop for ingredients**: Head to the store and pick up any ingredients you need for your planned meals.
5. **Prep in advance**: On Monday morning, prep any ingredients that can be used throughout the week (e.g. chop veggies, cook proteins).

By doing this simple meal prep, you'll save time during the week, reduce stress, and ensure you're eating healthy, delicious lunches. Plus, you'll have a clear plan in place, making it easier to stick to your goals.
