**Suggestions, in priority order**
1. **Cut the text.** Aim for roughly 300 to 500 words in total. Replace paragraphs with short bullets, and move details to a handout or to what you say aloud.
2. **Make figures carry the story.** Each figure needs a one-line takeaway as its title (for example, "Accuracy rises with sample size"), plus labeled axes and units. Make sure the text is readable from about two meters away.
3. **Define terms and abbreviations on first use.** Visitors may come from neighboring fields, so spell out any shorthand the first time it appears.
4. **Tighten the conclusions.** Limit them to two or three points that follow directly from your results, and add one sentence on limitations or next steps.
5. **Check the visual basics.** Use consistent fonts and colors, leave white space between sections, and add contact details or a QR code linking to the paper or data.
