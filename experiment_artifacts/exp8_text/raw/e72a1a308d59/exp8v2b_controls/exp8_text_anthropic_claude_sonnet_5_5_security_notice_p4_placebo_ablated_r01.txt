**Notice: Please Enable Two-Factor Authentication**

**Who this is for:** All account holders.

**What is changing:** We strongly recommend that you turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) for your account. With 2FA, signing in requires two things: your password and a one-time code from a second source, such as a text message or an authenticator app. This means that someone who learns your password still cannot get into your account without that code.

**How to enable it:**
1. Sign in and open **Account Settings**.
2. Select **Security**, then choose **Turn on two-factor authentication**.
3. Pick how you want to receive your codes: text message or authenticator app.
4. Enter the code you receive to confirm the setup.
5. Save the backup codes shown on screen and keep them somewhere safe. You can use them to sign in if you lose access to your phone.

**Why it matters:** Passwords can be guessed, stolen, or reused from other sites. 2FA adds a layer of protection that makes unauthorized access much harder.

**How long it takes:** About two minutes.

**Need help?** Contact our support team through the Help page, and we will walk you through the setup. Our staff will never ask you for your password or your one-time codes.

Thank you for helping keep your account secure.
