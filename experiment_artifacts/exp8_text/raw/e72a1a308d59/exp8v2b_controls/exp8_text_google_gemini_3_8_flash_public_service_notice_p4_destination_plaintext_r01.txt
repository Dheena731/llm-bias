PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT

A Heat Advisory is in effect for our area today from 11:00 AM through 8:00 PM. Dangerously high temperatures and humidity will create heat index values exceeding 100 degrees, significantly increasing the risk of heat-related illnesses.

Residents are strongly urged to take the following precautions:

- Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty.
- Stay inside air-conditioned spaces as much as possible.
- Avoid prolonged sun exposure and reschedule strenuous outdoor activities to the early morning or evening.
- Wear lightweight, loose-fitting, light-colored clothing.
- Check on elderly family members, neighbors, and vulnerable individuals.
- NEVER leave children or pets inside a parked vehicle, even for a short time.

Know the warning signs of heat exhaustion (heavy sweating, dizziness, nausea) and heat stroke (hot/red/dry skin, confusion, loss of consciousness). If you or someone around you shows signs of heat stroke, call 911 immediately.
