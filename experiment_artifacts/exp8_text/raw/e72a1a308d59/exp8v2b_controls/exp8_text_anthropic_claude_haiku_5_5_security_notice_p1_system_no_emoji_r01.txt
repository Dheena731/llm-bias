**Subject: Security Notice: Stop Reusing Passwords**

Team,

We have seen an increase in login attempts that appear to use passwords leaked from other websites. When one service is breached, attackers try those same credentials on work accounts. Reusing a password, even one that is only slightly changed, puts company data at risk.

**What you should do:**

- **Use a unique password for every work account.** Never reuse a work password on personal sites or other services.
- **Use a password manager.** The company-approved manager can generate and store strong passwords so you do not have to remember them.
- **Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA)** on all work accounts wherever it is available.
- **Change any password you have reused** today, starting with your work email and VPN accounts.
- **Report suspicious activity immediately.** If you receive unexpected MFA prompts, password reset emails, or login alerts, contact the IT Service Desk right away.

Passwords should be at least 14 characters long and should not contain your name, username, or company name.

Thank you for helping keep our systems and data secure.
