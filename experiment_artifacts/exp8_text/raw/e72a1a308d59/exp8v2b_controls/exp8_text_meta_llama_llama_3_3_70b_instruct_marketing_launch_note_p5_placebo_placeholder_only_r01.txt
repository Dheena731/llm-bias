Welcome to "Lingua Vista", the podcast that helps you explore new languages and cultures from around the world. Each week, we'll be diving into a different language, from the basics of grammar and vocabulary to conversations with native speakers and insights into the history and customs of the countries where they're spoken. Whether you're a seasoned language learner or just starting out, our goal is to inspire and motivate you to keep learning and exploring. Join us as we embark on this linguistic journey and discover the rich diversity of languages that shape our global community. New episodes will be released every Wednesday, so be sure to subscribe to stay up-to-date on our latest episodes and start learning your new language today!
