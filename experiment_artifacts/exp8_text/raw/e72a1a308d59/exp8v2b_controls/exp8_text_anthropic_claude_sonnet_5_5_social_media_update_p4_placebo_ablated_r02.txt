📅 **Calendar tip:** Stop using your calendar only for meetings. Block out time for your tasks, too.

Instead of keeping a to-do list that never gets done, give each important task its own slot on your calendar. For example, schedule "Write report, 2–3 PM Tuesday" the same way you'd schedule a call.

Why it works: A task with a set time is much more likely to get done than one that just sits on a list. It also shows you when your day is already full, so you don't overcommit.

Try it this week: pick your three most important tasks and give each one a time slot.
