NOTICE: LOCK YOUR SCREEN WHEN AWAY FROM YOUR DESK

For security and compliance reasons, please remember to lock your computer screen every time you leave your workstation, even for a few minutes.

Quick commands:
Windows: Press WIN + L
Mac: Press COMMAND + CONTROL + Q

An unlocked screen leaves company data exposed and violates our security policy. Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our systems safe.

Please direct any questions to the IT Helpdesk.
