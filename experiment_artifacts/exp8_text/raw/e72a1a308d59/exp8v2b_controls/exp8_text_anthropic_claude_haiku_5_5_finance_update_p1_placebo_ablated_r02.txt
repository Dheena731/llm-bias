**Subject: Introducing your monthly spending summary**

Starting this month, you'll receive a spending summary at the beginning of each month. It shows how much you spent in the previous month and breaks that total down by category, such as groceries, dining, transportation, and bills, so you can see where your money went.

Each summary includes:

- Your total spending for the month
- A breakdown of spending by category
- A comparison with the previous month, so you can spot changes

You can view your summary anytime under the Activity tab in the app. We'll also email it to you on the first day of each month. To stop the emails, go to Settings and turn off "Monthly summary emails."

The summary includes only transactions from accounts you've linked. Purchases that your bank hasn't finished processing won't appear until it does, so totals may change slightly during the first few days of the month.

If you have questions or notice a category that looks wrong, contact our support team through the Help section of the app.
