**PUBLIC NOTICE: Library Hours of Operation**

The Community Public Library is pleased to welcome all residents and visitors. Whether you are looking for a quiet place to study, free Wi-Fi, community programming, or your next great read, our doors are open to serve you. 

**Standard Hours:**

* **Monday – Thursday:** 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM  
* **Friday – Saturday:** 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM  
* **Sunday:** 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM  

*Please note: The library is closed on major public holidays. Our outdoor book return drop box is available 24/7.*

Can’t make it during regular hours? You can access our digital catalog, e-books, audiobooks, and research databases anytime online at **www.yourlibrary.org**. 

For questions or assistance, please contact the circulation desk at **(555) 019-2834** or email **help@yourlibrary.org**. 

We look forward to seeing you soon!
