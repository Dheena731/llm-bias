Calendar tip that will save your sanity: **Start building in buffer time.** ⏳

Instead of scheduling back-to-back 30- or 60-minute blocks, set your calendar app's default meeting duration to 25 or 50 minutes (Google Calendar calls this "Speedy meetings," and Outlook calls it "Shorten appointments").

That built-in 5–10 minute gap gives you time to stretch, grab water, and reset your brain before the next task. A full calendar shouldn’t mean an empty battery. 🔋🗓️
