**NOTICE OF ACCEPTABLE USE OF SHARED NETWORK**

To: All Users of the [Company Name] Shared Network

Re: Acceptable Use of Shared Network Resources

This notice is provided to inform and remind all users of the [Company Name] shared network of their obligations and responsibilities when utilizing the network and its resources.

**ACCEPTABLE USE**

The [Company Name] shared network is provided for the convenience and benefit of all users. However, the network is a shared resource and its use is subject to certain rules and guidelines. The following behaviors are considered acceptable use:

* Accessing and using the network for legitimate business purposes only.
* Using the network for personal use during breaks and after regular working hours, as long as such use does not interfere with work obligations.
* Storing and transmitting files that are not prohibited by law or company policy.
* Utilizing the network's computing resources and software applications in accordance with their intended purposes.

**UNACCEPTABLE USE**

The following behaviors are considered unacceptable use and are strictly prohibited:

* Accessing or attempting to access restricted areas of the network without authorization.
* Using the network for purposes that are unlawful, obscene, or otherwise inconsistent with company values.
* Storing or transmitting files that contain viruses, malware, or other malicious software.
* Using the network to engage in any activity that is detrimental to the network's performance, security, or functionality.

**CONSEQUENCES OF UNACCEPTABLE USE**

 Failure to comply with this notice may result in disciplinary action, including but not limited to:

* Revocation of network access privileges.
* Disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.
* Civil or criminal prosecution, as applicable.
