**Reviewer comment on the research question**

The central question, "How does social media use affect adolescents' well-being?", is too broad to guide the study design. "Social media use" could mean time spent, types of platforms, active posting, or passive scrolling, and each would lead to a different analysis. "Well-being" is also left undefined. The authors should state whether they mean life satisfaction, depressive symptoms, sleep, or another construct, and name the instrument they will use to measure it.

The question also does not indicate whether the aim is descriptive, associational, or causal. The word "affect" implies a causal claim, but the proposed cross-sectional survey design cannot support one. I recommend either rewording the question to match an associational aim (for example, "Is daily time spent on specific platforms associated with self-reported depressive symptoms among 13- to 16-year-olds?") or justifying how the design would address causality.

Finally, the population needs more precise boundaries. "Adolescents" spans a wide developmental range, and the authors should specify the age range, setting, and sampling frame. A revised question with these elements would make the hypotheses, variables, and analysis plan much easier to evaluate.
