**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY**

Starting [Date] at [Time], dangerously high temperatures are expected to continue through [End Date]. All residents should take immediate steps to protect their health.

• Drink water regularly, even if you do not feel thirsty.  
• Limit outdoor activities during the hottest hours (10 a.m. to 6 p.m.).  
• Wear loose, light-colored clothing and stay in air-conditioned spaces whenever possible.  
• Check on elderly neighbors, young children, and anyone managing chronic health conditions.  

Heat-related illness can develop quickly. Watch for signs such as excessive sweating, unusual fatigue, dizziness, nausea, or confusion. If any of these occur, move to a cool indoor space, rest, drink water, and contact a healthcare provider if symptoms do not improve within one hour.

Community cooling centers will remain open daily from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. at designated municipal facilities. For a full list of locations and operating hours, visit [Official Website] or call [Public Information Phone Number].

Please treat this advisory seriously. Staying informed and taking simple precautions is the most effective way to keep yourself and your community safe.
