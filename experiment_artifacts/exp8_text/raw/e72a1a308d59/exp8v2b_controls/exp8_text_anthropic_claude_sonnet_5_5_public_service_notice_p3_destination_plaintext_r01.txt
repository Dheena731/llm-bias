NOTICE: CHANGES TO RECYCLING COLLECTION

Beginning Monday, [start date], recycling collection in our community will follow a new schedule.

WHAT IS CHANGING
- Collection will move from weekly to every other week.
- Pickup day will change from [old day] to [new day].
- Recycling bins must be at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on your collection day.

WHAT CAN BE RECYCLED
- Paper, cardboard, and cartons (flattened)
- Plastic bottles, jugs, and tubs
- Metal cans and aluminum foil
- Glass bottles and jars

Please do not place plastic bags, food waste, or Styrofoam in your recycling bin. These items can jam equipment and contaminate otherwise good materials.

HELPFUL TIPS
- Rinse containers to remove food residue.
- Keep lids on bottles and jars.
- If your bin is full, you may set out extra recycling in a clearly marked box or paper bag next to the bin.

HOLIDAY SCHEDULE
When a holiday falls on or before your collection day, pickup will be delayed by one day that week.

QUESTIONS
For a printable collection calendar or more information, contact [department name] at [phone number] or visit [website].

Thank you for helping us keep our community clean and our recycling program running smoothly.
