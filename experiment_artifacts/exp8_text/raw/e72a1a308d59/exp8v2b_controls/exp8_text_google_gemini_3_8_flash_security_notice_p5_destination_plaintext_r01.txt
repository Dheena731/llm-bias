URGENT: REPORTING A LOST OR STOLEN COMPANY LAPTOP

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, you must report the incident immediately. Rapid reporting allows the IT security team to remotely lock and wipe the device to safeguard company and client data.

IMMEDIATE STEPS TO TAKE:

1. Contact the IT Help Desk immediately:
- Phone: [Insert Phone Number] (Available 24/7)
- Email: [Insert Email Address]

2. Notify your direct manager about the situation.

3. If the device was stolen, file a report with local law enforcement and share the police report number with IT.

INFORMATION TO PROVIDE TO IT:
- Device asset tag or serial number (if known)
- Date, time, and location the laptop was last in your possession
- Circumstances of the loss or theft
- Any sensitive data that may have been stored locally on the device

Thank you for your prompt cooperation in keeping our systems secure.
