**PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE**

**Maple Grove Avenue Closed Between 4th Street and 9th Street**

**Dates:** Monday, June 9 through Friday, June 20, 2025
**Hours:** 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., weekdays only

The Riverton Public Works Department will close Maple Grove Avenue between 4th Street and 9th Street for water main replacement and repaving. The road will reopen each evening and on weekends, weather permitting.

**What to expect:**
- Through traffic will not be permitted in the work zone during closure hours.
- Local residents may access driveways on foot, but vehicle access will be limited. Please park on a side street before 7:00 a.m. if you need your car during the day.
- Water service may be briefly interrupted. Affected households will receive door hangers 48 hours in advance.
- Street parking will be prohibited along the work zone. Vehicles left there will be towed.

**Detour:** Drivers should use Elm Street to 10th Street, then Cedar Avenue back to Maple Grove. Expect about five extra minutes of travel time. Bus Route 12 will be rerouted along Cedar Avenue, and stops on Maple Grove will be temporarily moved.

**Questions:** Call Riverton Public Works at 555-0142 on weekdays between 8:00 a.m. and 4:30 p.m., or visit the department's website for daily updates.

We appreciate your patience while we make these improvements.
