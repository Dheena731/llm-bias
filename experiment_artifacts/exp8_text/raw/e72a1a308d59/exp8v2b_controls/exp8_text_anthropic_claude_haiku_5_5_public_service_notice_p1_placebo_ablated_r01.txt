**PUBLIC NOTICE: New Library Opening Hours**

Beginning Monday, March 3, the Central Public Library will open at the following times:

- **Monday to Friday:** 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
- **Saturday:** 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- **Sunday:** Closed

The library will also be closed on all national public holidays.

Books, DVDs, and other materials can be returned at any time through the drop box at the front entrance. Library cards and services remain free of charge.

For questions, call (555) 123-4567 or visit www.centrallibrary.example.
