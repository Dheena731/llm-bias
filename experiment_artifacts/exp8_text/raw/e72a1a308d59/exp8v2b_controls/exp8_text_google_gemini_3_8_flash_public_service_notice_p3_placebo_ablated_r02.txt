**Notice of Recycling Collection Schedule Change**

Dear Residents,

Please be aware that your weekly curbside recycling collection day is changing. 

Starting **Monday, November 3**, recycling will be collected on **Thursdays** instead of Tuesdays. 

**What you need to do:**
* Place your recycling bin at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on **Thursdays**, starting November 6.
* Do not place recycling out on Tuesdays after November 3.

Trash collection days are not affected by this change and will remain on their current schedule. The types of recyclable materials accepted also remain the same.

If you have questions, please visit **www.yourtown.gov/recycling** or call the Public Works Department at **(555) 019-2834**.

Thank you for your cooperation.
