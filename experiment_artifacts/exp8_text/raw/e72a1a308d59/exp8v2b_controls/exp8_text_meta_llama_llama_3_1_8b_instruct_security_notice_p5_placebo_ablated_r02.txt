**Lost Company Laptop Report**

If your company-issued laptop has been misplaced or lost, please report it to your supervisor or the IT department immediately.

A lost laptop can result in sensitive company data being compromised, so prompt reporting is crucial to minimize the risk of data breach and unauthorized access.

To initiate the reporting process, follow these steps:

1.  Notify your supervisor or the IT department via email or phone.
2.  Provide a detailed description of your laptop, including its model, serial number, and any distinctive features.
3.  Specify the circumstances surrounding the loss, if known.
4.  Cooperate with the IT department to implement necessary security measures to protect the company's data.

Your prompt action will help us to take necessary steps to prevent data breaches and protect our company's assets.

**Please report your lost laptop today!**
