We have rolled out a new Monthly Spending Summary designed to provide clearer visibility into your regular expenses. 

Delivered at the start of each month, this overview automatically categorizes transactions, highlights notable shifts from the previous month, and tracks overall progress toward your budget targets. It removes the need to manually sort through individual receipts and statements, giving you a quick, comprehensive snapshot of where funds are going.

The latest summary is live now and can be viewed anytime directly from the account dashboard under the Analytics tab.
