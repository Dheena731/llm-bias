SUBJECT: Important: Phishing Emails

As part of our ongoing efforts to protect company data and assets, we would like to remind everyone to be vigilant when receiving emails that request sensitive information or ask you to take certain actions.

Phishing emails are a common threat to businesses and can compromise our security. These emails are designed to trick you into revealing confidential information, such as login credentials or financial information, or to download malicious software onto our systems.

To protect yourself and our company, please follow these best practices:

* Be cautious when opening attachments or clicking on links from unknown senders
* Never provide sensitive information via email
* Verify the authenticity of emails by contacting the sender directly
* Report any suspicious emails to the IT department immediately

If you receive an email that you suspect is a phishing attempt, please do not hesitate to contact the IT department. We are here to help and will work to prevent any potential security breaches.

Thank you for your attention to this matter and for your continued support in maintaining the security of our company.
