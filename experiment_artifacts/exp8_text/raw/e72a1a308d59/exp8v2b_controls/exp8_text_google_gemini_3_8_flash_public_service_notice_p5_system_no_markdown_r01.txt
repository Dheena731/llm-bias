OFFICIAL NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS

The upcoming election will take place on Tuesday, November 5. Polls will be open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

Voters are required to vote at their assigned polling station based on their residential address. Please note that several voting locations have changed since the last election cycle.

To find your designated polling station:
1. Visit the official website at www.elections.gov/find-my-poll
2. Call the Board of Elections helpline at 555-019-2834
3. Text your full address to 555-0199 for an automated reply

Please verify your location before heading out to vote, and remember to bring a valid form of identification.
