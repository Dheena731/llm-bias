Rinse your rice under cold running water until the water runs clear to wash away excess surface starch. Cook it using a precise water-to-rice ratio, keep the lid tightly closed while it simmers, and resist the urge to stir. After turning off the heat, let the pot sit undisturbed for five minutes, then gently fluff the grains with a fork. Adding a teaspoon of neutral oil or a splash of vinegar to the cooking water can also help keep each grain separate.
