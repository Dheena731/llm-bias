**PUBLIC NOTICE: Updated Library Opening Hours**

Effective Monday, the first of next month, the Central Public Library will operate on the following schedule:

**Monday to Thursday:** 9:00 a.m. – 8:00 p.m.
**Friday:** 9:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m.
**Saturday:** 10:00 a.m. – 4:00 p.m.

**Please note:**

- The library will be closed on all public holidays.
- Book drops are available 24 hours a day at the main entrance.
- Digital services, including e-books, audiobooks, and online catalog access, are available at any time through the library website.
- Study rooms may be reserved at the front desk or online up to seven days in advance.

For questions or to confirm holiday closures, please contact the front desk at the number listed on our website or visit us in person.

Thank you for supporting your library. We look forward to seeing you.
