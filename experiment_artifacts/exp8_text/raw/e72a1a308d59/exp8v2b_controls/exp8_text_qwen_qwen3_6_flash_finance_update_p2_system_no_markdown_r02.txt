Subject: Update to Card Transaction Fees

Dear Partners and Customers,

Please be advised that effective November 1, 2024, we will adjust our card transaction processing fees. The standard rate will change from two point nine percent plus thirty cents per transaction to three point one percent plus thirty cents per transaction. This update aligns with increased network interchange charges and additional fraud prevention investments.

The revised pricing will apply to all eligible transactions settled on or after the effective date. Your regular billing schedule remains unchanged, and updated statements will reflect the new rates starting in December. You can view full details and historical comparisons in your account dashboard under the Pricing section.

Should you have questions regarding this adjustment or require help reviewing your specific volume tiers, please reach out to our merchant services desk at support@paymentexample.com or call 555-0124 during business hours. Thank you for your continued partnership and trust in our payment services.

Sincerely,
Payment Services Team
