**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**

To help protect your account from unauthorized access, we strongly encourage all users to enable two-factor authentication. This adds a second verification step at sign-in, so a stolen or guessed password alone is not enough to get into your account.

**How to enable 2FA:**

1. Sign in and go to **Settings**, then select **Security**.
2. Choose **Two-Factor Authentication** and click **Enable**.
3. Select your verification method: an authenticator app (recommended) or text message.
4. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm your setup.
5. Save your backup codes in a secure place in case you lose access to your device.

**Important reminders:**

- Never share your verification codes with anyone. Our staff will never ask for them.
- Keep your recovery options and contact details up to date.
- If you lose your device, use a backup code or contact support right away.

Setup takes only a few minutes and significantly improves your account security. If you need assistance, please contact our support team.

Thank you for helping us keep your information safe.
