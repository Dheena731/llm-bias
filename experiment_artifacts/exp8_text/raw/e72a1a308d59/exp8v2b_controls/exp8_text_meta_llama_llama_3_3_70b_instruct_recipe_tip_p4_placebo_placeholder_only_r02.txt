To plan lunches for a busy week, consider setting aside one day to prepare and portion out individual meals that can be easily grabbed and taken on the go. This can be as simple as cooking a large batch of rice, grilling chicken or vegetables, and assembling salads or wraps in containers for each day of the week. Having a week's worth of lunches ready to go can save time and reduce stress, and can also help ensure that healthy, home-cooked meals are available even on the most hectic days.
