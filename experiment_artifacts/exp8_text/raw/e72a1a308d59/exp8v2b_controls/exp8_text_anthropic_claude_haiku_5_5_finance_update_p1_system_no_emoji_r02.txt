**Introducing Your Monthly Spending Summary**

We've added a new Monthly Spending Summary to your account. At the start of each month, you'll receive a clear overview of how you spent in the previous month.

Each summary includes:

- **Total spending** compared with the prior month
- **Top categories** so you can see where most of your money went
- **Recurring charges** to help you spot subscriptions and regular bills
- **Notable changes** that highlight unusual increases or decreases

You can find your summaries under **Reports** in the main menu. Past summaries are saved there as well, so you can review trends over time.

If you'd rather not receive the monthly email, you can turn it off in **Settings > Notifications**. The summary will still be available in your account.

We hope this helps you understand your spending and plan ahead. Questions or feedback? Reply to this message or contact our support team.
