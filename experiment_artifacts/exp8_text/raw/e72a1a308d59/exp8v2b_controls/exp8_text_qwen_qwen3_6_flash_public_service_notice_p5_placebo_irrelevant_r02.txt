**NOTICE OF POLLING STATION LOCATIONS**

**Date:** November 5, 2024  
**To:** All Registered Voters  
**From:** County Board of Elections  

This notice provides the official polling station locations for the upcoming general election on **Tuesday, November 5, 2024**. Your assigned polling place is determined by your residential address and may differ from previous elections.

📍 **Find Your Location:**  
Visit [VoterPortal.gov] or call (555) 012-3456 to enter your address and view your exact polling site, hours, and any accessibility accommodations.

⏰ **Hours of Operation:** 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM  
🆔 **Requirements:** Bring a valid government-issued photo ID and proof of residence if requested.  
♿ **Accessibility:** All locations are ADA-compliant. Request assistive devices or language support when verifying your location.

Polling places are subject to change due to unforeseen circumstances. We strongly recommend confirming your assignment within 48 hours of Election Day.

For assistance or to report issues, contact the Board of Elections at elections@county.gov or (555) 012-3456. Thank you for exercising your right to vote.
