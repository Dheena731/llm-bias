🚀 **Quick Calendar Hack: Color Code Your Life!** 🎨📅

If your schedule feels chaotic, try this: assign a specific color to each category of your day.

🟦 Work & Meetings  
🟩 Personal & Errands  
🟪 Learning & Hobbies  
🟨 Self-Care & Rest  

**Why?** You'll instantly see where your time is going and spot imbalances before burnout hits. Plus, it makes checking off tasks way more satisfying! ✅✨
