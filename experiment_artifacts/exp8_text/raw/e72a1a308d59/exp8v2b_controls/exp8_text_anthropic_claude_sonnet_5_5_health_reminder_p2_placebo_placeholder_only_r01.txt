**Hydration Reminder 💧**

Take a moment to check in with yourself: have you had enough water today? Even mild dehydration can cause tiredness, headaches, and trouble focusing.

A few easy ways to stay on track:

- Start your morning with a full glass of water.
- Keep a bottle within reach while you work or run errands.
- Sip steadily throughout the day instead of waiting until you feel thirsty.
- Drink a glass with each meal and snack.
- Pay attention to your urine color. Pale yellow usually means you're well hydrated.

Pour a glass right now, and your body will thank you.
