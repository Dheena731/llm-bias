Your end-of-year tax statement is a document that summarizes your income and taxes withheld for the year. It will show the total amount of money you earned, the amount of taxes that were taken out of your pay, and any other relevant tax information. This statement is usually provided by your employer and is used to help you complete your tax return. It's an important document to keep track of, as you'll need it to accurately report your income and claim any eligible deductions or credits when you file your taxes.
