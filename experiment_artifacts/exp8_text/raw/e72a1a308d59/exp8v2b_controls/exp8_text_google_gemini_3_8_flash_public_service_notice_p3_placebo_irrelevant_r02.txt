**IMPORTANT NOTICE: Changes to Your Recycling Collection Schedule**

**Effective Date:** Monday, November 10, 2024  

Please be advised that the residential recycling collection schedule for all residents in **Zone B** will change starting next week. 

**Summary of Changes:**
* **New Collection Day:** Recycling will now be collected on **Thursdays** (previously collected on Tuesdays).
* **Collection Time:** Please ensure your blue recycling cart is at the curb by **7:00 AM** on Thursday morning.
* **Frequency:** Collection remains bi-weekly. 

*Note: Regular trash and yard waste collection days remain unchanged.*

To view the full updated schedule or check the list of accepted recyclable materials, please visit **www.cityservices.gov/recycling** or call the Public Works Department at **(555) 019-2834**.

Thank you for your cooperation and your continued commitment to keeping our community green.

**City Waste Management Department**
