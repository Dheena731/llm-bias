NOTICE: MANDATORY TWO-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION SETUP

Date: October 26, 2023
To: All Users
From: IT Security Team

Subject: Action Required - Enable Two-Factor Authentication

In an effort to strengthen our organizational security posture, all users are required to enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on their accounts. This additional layer of protection helps prevent unauthorized access even if passwords are compromised.

Action Required:
Please complete the setup process by Friday, November 3, 2023. Accounts without 2FA enabled after this date may experience restricted access.

Instructions:
1. Log in to your account portal.
2. Navigate to Account Settings and select Security.
3. Click on Enable Two-Factor Authentication.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to configure your preferred method (Authenticator App or SMS).

Need Assistance?
If you encounter any issues or require support, please contact the IT Help Desk at extension 5555 or email it-support@example.com.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our systems secure.
