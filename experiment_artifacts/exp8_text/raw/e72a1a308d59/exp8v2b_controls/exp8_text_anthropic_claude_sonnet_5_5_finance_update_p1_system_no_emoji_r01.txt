**Introducing Your Monthly Spending Summary**

We have added a new monthly spending summary to help you understand where your money goes each month.

**What it includes**

- **Total spending:** See how much you spent over the full month, compared with the previous month.
- **Category breakdown:** View your spending grouped by category, such as groceries, transportation, dining, and bills.
- **Top merchants:** Find out where you shop most often and where you spend the most.
- **Trends and highlights:** Spot unusual increases or decreases in your spending at a glance.

**How it works**

Your summary is generated automatically at the end of each month using your existing transaction data. You do not need to set anything up. When it is ready, you will receive a notification, and you can find it under the Insights tab in your account.

**Why it matters**

A clear monthly view makes it easier to stay on budget, catch unexpected charges, and set realistic goals for the months ahead.

**Your control**

You can adjust categories for individual transactions if something is labeled incorrectly, and the summary will update to reflect your changes. If you prefer not to receive notifications, you can turn them off in Settings.

We hope this new feature makes managing your finances simpler. As always, we welcome your feedback on how we can make it more useful.
