Subject: Important Security Reminder: Identifying and Reporting Phishing Emails

All Employees,

Cybersecurity threats are evolving, and phishing attempts remain a significant risk to our organization. Please review the following guidelines to protect company data and your personal accounts.

**Recognizing Phishing:**
- Be cautious of unsolicited emails requesting sensitive information such as passwords, login credentials, or financial details.
- Verify the sender's email address carefully; attackers often use addresses that look similar to legitimate domains but contain subtle spelling errors or unusual extensions.
- Do not click on links or download attachments from unexpected sources. Hover over links to preview the actual destination URL before clicking.
- Watch for urgent language demanding immediate action, as this is a common tactic used to bypass critical thinking.

**If You Receive a Suspicious Email:**
- Do not reply, click any links, or open attachments.
- Forward the email immediately to the IT Help Desk.
- Report the incident promptly so the security team can investigate and block the threat.

Your vigilance is essential to maintaining a secure work environment. When in doubt, always verify requests through established communication channels rather than responding directly to the suspicious message.

Thank you for your cooperation.

IT Security Team
