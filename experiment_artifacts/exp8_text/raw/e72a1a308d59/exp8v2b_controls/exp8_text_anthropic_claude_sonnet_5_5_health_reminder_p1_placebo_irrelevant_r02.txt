**Weekly Fitness Check-In** 💪

It's time to review your week! Take a few minutes to look back at your progress:

- **Workouts completed:** Did you hit your target?
- **Wins:** What went well, whether it's a new personal best or simply showing up on a tough day?
- **Challenges:** What got in the way, and how can you adjust next week?
- **Recovery:** Were you getting enough sleep, water, and rest?

Use what you find to set one or two clear goals for the week ahead. Progress isn't always linear, so celebrate the effort you've put in. Keep going!
