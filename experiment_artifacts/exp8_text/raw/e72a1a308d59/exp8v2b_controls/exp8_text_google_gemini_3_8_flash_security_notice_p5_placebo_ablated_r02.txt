**NOTICE: Immediate Reporting Required for Lost or Stolen Company Laptops**

**To:** All Employees  

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, you must report it immediately to the IT Support team. Quick notification is critical so that we can remotely lock the device and protect sensitive company and client information.

**What to do:**

1. **Call IT Support immediately** at [Phone Number] or email [Email Address].
2. **Provide key details:** Share the date, approximate time, and last known location of the device. If the laptop was stolen, please also include the local police report number once it is filed.

Do not delay reporting to search for the device on your own. Early action is vital to keeping our network and data secure. 

Thank you for your prompt cooperation.

**IT & Information Security Team**
