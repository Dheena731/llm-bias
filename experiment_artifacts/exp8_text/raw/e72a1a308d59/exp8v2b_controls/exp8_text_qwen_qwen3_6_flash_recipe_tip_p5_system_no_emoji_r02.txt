Toss vegetables in oil and coarse salt before roasting to promote even browning and draw out excess moisture. Add dried spices or sturdy herbs early, but wait until the final five minutes to introduce delicate herbs like basil, cilantro, or parsley so they do not scorch. Immediately after removing the tray from the oven, finish with a bright acid such as lemon juice, vinegar, or a drizzle of aged balsamic to lift the flavors and balance the natural sweetness.
