**Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If your company laptop is lost or stolen, report it to the IT Service Desk and your line manager as soon as you notice it is missing, ideally within one hour. Prompt reporting lets IT lock the device, revoke access, and protect company data.

**What to do:**

1. **Contact the IT Service Desk immediately** by phone or through the internal support portal. Tell them the laptop is lost or stolen and ask them to disable it and reset your account passwords.
2. **Notify your line manager** so they know about the loss and can support any follow-up.
3. **Report theft to the police** if the laptop was stolen, and keep the police reference number. Provide it to IT and your manager.
4. **Write a brief summary** of when and where you last had the laptop, what it contained, and any actions you have taken. Send it to the IT Service Desk.

**Do not** try to recover or access the device yourself, and do not share your login details with anyone.

Reporting a lost device promptly is a company requirement and protects both you and the organisation. Failure to report a loss without good reason may lead to a review under company policy.
