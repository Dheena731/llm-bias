**Feedback on Conference Poster Draft**

**Suggestions for improvement**
1. **Lead with the takeaway.** Add a one-sentence headline or key finding near the top so a viewer understands your contribution in under ten seconds.
2. **Reduce text.** Aim for roughly 300 to 500 words total. Convert dense paragraphs into short bullet points or captions.
3. **Strengthen the figures.** Enlarge key graphs, label axes clearly, and make sure fonts remain legible from about 1.5 meters away. Remove chart elements that do not serve the argument.
4. **Improve visual hierarchy.** Use consistent heading sizes, generous white space, and a limited color palette. The results section should be the most visually prominent area.
5. **Sharpen the conclusions.** State two or three specific findings and, if possible, one implication or next step. Avoid restating the methods here.
6. **Check the details.** Include contact information or a QR code for the paper or data, and proofread labels, units, and citations.

**Next steps:** Revise the layout and trim the text, then test the poster by showing it to a colleague for 30 seconds and asking what they remember. If they cannot state the main point, tighten the headline and results further.
