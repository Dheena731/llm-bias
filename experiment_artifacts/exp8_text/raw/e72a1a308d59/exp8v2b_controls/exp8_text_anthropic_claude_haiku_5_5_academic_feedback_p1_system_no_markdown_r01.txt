Feedback on Draft Literature Review

Thank you for sharing your draft. The review has a clear topic, and the chronological organization of the early sections helps the reader follow how the field developed. Your selection of sources is generally appropriate, and several of the more recent studies are well chosen.

The main weakness is that the review describes the literature more than it evaluates it. Many paragraphs move from one summary to the next without explaining how the studies relate to each other. Where studies disagree, such as the contrasting findings on sample size and effect magnitude in the middle section, the disagreement should be identified and explained, with an account of why the results might differ. Synthesis would strengthen the argument considerably.

The framing could also be sharper. The introduction states a research gap, but the review does not return to it. In the concluding section, state explicitly what the existing literature leaves unanswered and how your own study addresses that gap.

Some citations need attention. Several claims in the second section lack a source, and two references appear in the bibliography but not in the text. Please check that all in-text citations and reference entries match the required style.

Finally, the prose is generally clear, but some sentences are long and contain multiple claims. Breaking these apart would make the argument easier to follow.

Priority revisions: move from summary to synthesis, make the research gap explicit at the end, and correct the citation issues. With these changes, the review will be a strong foundation for your study.
