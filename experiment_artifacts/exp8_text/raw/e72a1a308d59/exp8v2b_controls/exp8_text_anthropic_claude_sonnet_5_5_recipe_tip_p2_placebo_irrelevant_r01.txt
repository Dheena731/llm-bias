**Tip: Treat herbs like cut flowers (or wrap them like salad greens).**

For tender herbs like cilantro, parsley, and dill, trim the stems and stand the bunch in a jar with an inch of water. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and refrigerate. Change the water every few days, and they'll stay fresh for up to two weeks.

For sturdier herbs like thyme, rosemary, and sage, wrap them in a barely damp paper towel, place them in a zip-top bag or container, and keep them in the fridge's crisper drawer.

Basil is the exception. It blackens in the cold, so keep it in a jar of water on the counter, out of direct sun.
