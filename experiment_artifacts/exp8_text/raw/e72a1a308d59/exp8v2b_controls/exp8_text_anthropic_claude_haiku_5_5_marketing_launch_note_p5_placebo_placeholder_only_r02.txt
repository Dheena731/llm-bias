Welcome to *Lingua Lounge*, the podcast that makes learning a new language feel less like homework and more like a conversation over coffee.

Every week, we'll explore one language at a time, sharing the everyday phrases, surprising grammar quirks, and cultural stories that bring each tongue to life. Whether you're a total beginner who can only say "hello" or a traveler brushing up before your next trip, you'll find something here to practice with and enjoy.

In our first season, we dive into Spanish, starting with the sounds and expressions you'll hear in a busy market, and we'll keep each episode short enough to fit into a commute or a coffee break. Bring your curiosity, speak out loud along with us, and don't worry about making mistakes. That's how the language sticks.

Pull up a chair. Let's start talking.
