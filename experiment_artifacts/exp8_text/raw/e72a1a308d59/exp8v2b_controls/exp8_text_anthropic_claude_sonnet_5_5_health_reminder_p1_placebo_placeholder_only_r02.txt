**Weekly Fitness Check-In**

It's time to review your week! Take a few minutes to look back at your workouts, note what went well, and spot areas where you can improve. Did you hit your activity goals? Were you consistent with your routines? How did your energy and recovery feel?

Celebrate your wins, big or small, and use what you learned to set realistic targets for the week ahead. Progress comes from consistency, so keep showing up for yourself.
