NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS

Voters are reminded that polling stations will be open on Election Day from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Please go to the station assigned to your address, as shown on your voter registration card or notice of registration.

Polling stations in this district:

Ward 1: Riverside Community Centre, 45 Mill Road
Ward 2: Northgate Public Library, 12 Church Street
Ward 3: Oakfield Primary School (Main Hall), 8 Oakfield Avenue
Ward 4: St. Mark's Parish Hall, 101 High Street
Ward 5: Eastside Sports Pavilion, 3 Park Lane

Accessibility: All locations offer step-free access, accessible voting equipment, and assistance for voters who need it. Please ask any polling staff member for help.

What to bring: Please bring a valid form of photo identification. If you have your voter registration card, bring it too, though it is not required in most cases.

To confirm your assigned polling station, contact the Elections Office at (555) 010-2345 or visit the official elections website.
