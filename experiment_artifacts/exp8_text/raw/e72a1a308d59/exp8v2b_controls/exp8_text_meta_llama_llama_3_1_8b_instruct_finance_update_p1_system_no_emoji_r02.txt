We are introducing a new monthly spending summary that will provide a detailed breakdown of all expenditures made by the company. This summary will be sent to all employees and will include a list of all transactions, categorized by type, along with a total amount spent for the month.

The monthly spending summary will be sent out on the first day of each month and will cover all transactions made from the 1st to the 30th of the previous month. The summary will be sent via email and will be available on the company's intranet for reference.

This new summary is intended to increase transparency and accountability for company spending, and to provide employees with a clear understanding of how their tax dollars are being used.
