Season roasted vegetables in two stages for the best flavor. Before roasting, toss them with oil, salt, and pepper so the seasoning sticks and the edges caramelize. Right after they come out of the oven, add a final touch while they're still hot: a squeeze of lemon juice, a splash of vinegar, or a sprinkle of fresh herbs and flaky salt. The heat of the pan helps the vegetables absorb these brighter flavors, and holding them back until the end keeps delicate herbs and acidity from burning or turning dull in the oven.
