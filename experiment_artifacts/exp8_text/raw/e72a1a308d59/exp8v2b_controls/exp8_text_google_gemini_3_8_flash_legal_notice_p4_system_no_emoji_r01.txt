To be eligible for a refund, all return requests must be submitted within thirty (30) calendar days of the original purchase date.

1. Products must be returned in their original, unused condition, complete with all original packaging, tags, and documentation.
2. A valid receipt or proof of purchase must accompany the request.

All refunds are subject to inspection and approval at the sole discretion of the company. Approved refunds will be credited to the original method of payment, excluding original shipping and handling charges, within ten (10) to fourteen (14) business days.
