**NOTICE: Polling Station Locations**

Voters are advised that polling stations will be open on Election Day from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Please go to the polling station assigned to your registered address.

**Where to Vote**

- **Ward 1:** Central Library, 120 Main Street
- **Ward 2:** Riverside Community Center, 45 River Road
- **Ward 3:** Northview Elementary School, 310 Oak Avenue
- **Ward 4:** Town Hall, 1 Civic Plaza

**Before You Go**

- Bring a valid photo ID.
- Check your voter registration card for your assigned location.
- All listed locations are wheelchair accessible. Curbside voting is available on request.
- Arrive early in the morning or mid-afternoon to avoid longer lines.

**Need Help?**

To confirm your polling station or ask about accessibility, contact the Elections Office at (555) 010-2000 or visit the office at 1 Civic Plaza, Room 102, during regular business hours.

*Issued by the Office of the County Clerk*
