**Notice: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If your company laptop is lost or stolen, report it immediately. Prompt reporting helps protect company data, customer information, and you.

**What to do right away**

1. **Notify the IT Service Desk immediately**, by phone if possible, so they can lock or remotely wipe the device and disable your access.
2. **Inform your manager** as soon as you have reported the loss to IT.
3. **File an incident report** through the company's internal incident reporting system within 24 hours.
4. **Report theft to the police** if the laptop was stolen, and keep a copy of the report number for company records.

**What to include in your report**

- The date, time, and location where you last had the laptop
- How you believe it was lost or stolen
- The type of data that may have been stored on it
- Whether the device was locked, encrypted, or password protected

**Important reminders**

- Do not wait to see whether the laptop turns up. Delays increase the risk of a data breach.
- Change your company passwords as soon as you can from another secure device, unless IT instructs otherwise.
- Do not try to recover a stolen laptop yourself.

Reporting a lost device promptly will not result in disciplinary action in itself. Failing to report it may. If you are unsure whether a device is lost or just misplaced, report it anyway and let IT help you decide.

Thank you for helping keep our information secure.
