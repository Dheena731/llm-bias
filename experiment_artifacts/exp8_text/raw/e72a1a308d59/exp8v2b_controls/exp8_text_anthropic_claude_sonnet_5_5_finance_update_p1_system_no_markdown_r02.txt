Subject: New Monthly Spending Summary Now Available

Hello everyone,

We're pleased to announce a new monthly spending summary, designed to give you a clearer picture of where your money goes each month.

What it is

At the start of each month, you'll receive a concise report covering the previous month's spending. It groups your transactions into familiar categories such as housing, groceries, transportation, dining, and entertainment, so you can see your habits at a glance.

What you'll see

The summary shows your total spending for the month, a breakdown by category, and a comparison with the prior month. It also highlights your largest expenses and any categories that changed noticeably, so unusual spending is easy to spot.

Why it matters

Understanding your spending is the first step toward managing it. With a consistent monthly snapshot, you can notice trends early, adjust your budget, and make more informed decisions without digging through individual transactions.

How to access it

The summary will arrive automatically by email on the first business day of each month. You can also view current and past summaries in your account under the Reports section. If you prefer not to receive the email, you can turn it off in your notification settings.

We'd love to hear what you think. Please send feedback or suggestions for improvement to our support team, and we'll use your input to refine the summary in future updates.

Thank you for being with us.
