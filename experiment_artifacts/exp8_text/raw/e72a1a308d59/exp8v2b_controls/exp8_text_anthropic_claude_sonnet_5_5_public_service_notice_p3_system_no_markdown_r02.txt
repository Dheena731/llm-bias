NOTICE: CHANGES TO RECYCLING COLLECTION

Effective Monday, [start date], recycling collection in our community will move to a new schedule.

What is changing:
Pickup will now take place every other week on [new collection day], instead of weekly on [old collection day]. A printed calendar showing your pickup dates is available at [location/website].

Please place your recycling bin at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on collection day. Bins set out after pickup will not be collected until the next scheduled date.

Accepted items now include:
Paper, cardboard, glass bottles and jars, metal cans, and plastic containers numbered 1 through 5. Please rinse all containers and flatten cardboard boxes.

No longer accepted:
Plastic bags, foam packaging, and shredded paper. These items should go in your regular trash.

Need an extra or larger bin? Call [phone number] or visit [website] to request one at no charge.

Thank you for helping keep our community clean and green. If you have questions, please contact [department name] at [phone number or email].
