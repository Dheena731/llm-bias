**Introducing LedgerLane: Run Your Business, Not Your Paperwork**

Today we are launching LedgerLane, the all-in-one app built for small business owners who wear every hat.

Invoicing, expenses, appointments, and customer follow-ups usually live in five different places. LedgerLane brings them together in one simple dashboard, so you spend less time on admin and more time on the work you love.

**What you can do with LedgerLane**

- **Send invoices in under a minute.** Use polished templates, automatic reminders, and built-in payment links.
- **Track expenses effortlessly.** Snap a photo of a receipt and let the app categorize it for you.
- **Manage your schedule.** Let customers book online, and sync everything with your calendar.
- **See your numbers clearly.** Get a real-time view of cash flow, unpaid invoices, and monthly trends.

**Built for small teams**

There is no steep learning curve and no accounting degree required. Most customers are set up and sending their first invoice within ten minutes.
