**Notice: Polling Station Locations and Verification**

Voters are encouraged to confirm their designated polling station location before heading out to vote. Due to recent precinct boundary updates, your assigned voting site may have changed since the last election. 

Please note that you must cast your ballot at your officially assigned location on Election Day. Voting sites are determined by your registered residential address.

To locate your specific polling place:
* Check the address printed on your official voter information card.
* Visit the official election division website and enter your address into the online polling place locator tool.
* Contact the local elections office by phone for direct assistance.

All polling stations will operate during standard voting hours from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Anyone in line by the closing time is legally entitled to cast a ballot. Please plan ahead, review your route, and verify your location in advance.
