**Introducing Savings Goals**

We've added a new Savings Goals feature to help you plan and track the things that matter most to you, whether that's a vacation, a new laptop, or an emergency fund.

**What's new**

- **Create custom goals:** Name your goal, set a target amount, and choose a deadline.
- **Track progress at a glance:** A progress bar shows how close you are to reaching each goal.
- **Automatic contributions:** Schedule weekly or monthly transfers so your savings grow without extra effort.
- **Smart suggestions:** Based on your target and timeline, the app recommends how much to set aside each period.

**How to get started**

1. Open the Savings tab.
2. Select "New Goal."
3. Enter your details and choose how you'd like to contribute.

Savings Goals is available now for all users on the latest version of the app. Update today and start working toward your next milestone.
