Important Security Notice: Password Reuse is Prohibited. Using the same password for multiple accounts, including personal and work-related accounts, poses a significant security risk to our organization. If one account is compromised, all accounts with the same password may be vulnerable to unauthorized access. To protect our systems and data, it is essential that you use unique and complex passwords for each account. Please review and update your passwords to ensure compliance with our security policies. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the IT department for assistance.
