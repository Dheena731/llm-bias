Introducing Savings Goals

We're excited to announce a new feature that makes it easier to save for the things that matter most to you. With Savings Goals, you can set a target amount, choose a deadline, and watch your progress grow over time.

Here's how it works. Create a goal for anything you have in mind, whether it's a vacation, a new laptop, or an emergency fund. Give it a name, set the amount you want to reach, and pick a target date. We'll calculate how much you need to set aside each week or month to stay on track.

You can also set up automatic transfers from your checking account so your savings build without any extra effort. If you want to give your goal a boost, you can add money at any time. A simple progress bar shows how close you are, and you'll get friendly reminders and a celebration message when you hit your target.

Savings Goals is rolling out to all users over the next two weeks. To get started, open the app, tap Savings, and select Create a Goal. It only takes a minute to set up.

We'd love to hear what you think. Send us your feedback through the Help section of the app, and let us know what you're saving for.
