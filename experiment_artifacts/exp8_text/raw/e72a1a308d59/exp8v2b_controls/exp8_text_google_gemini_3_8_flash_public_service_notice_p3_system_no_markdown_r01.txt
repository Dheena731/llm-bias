IMPORTANT NOTICE: CHANGES TO RECYCLING COLLECTION

Please be advised that starting Monday, November 3, recycling collection will shift to a bi-weekly schedule. 

Your regular pickup day may have changed. Please ensure all recycling bins are placed at the curb by 7:00 AM on your designated collection morning. 

To view your new collection calendar and a complete list of accepted materials, visit www.cityservices.gov/recycling or call the Waste Management Office at 555-0199.

Thank you for your cooperation in helping keep our community green and clean.
