**Tip: Cook once, assemble all week.**

On Sunday, prep a few versatile building blocks instead of full meals: a big batch of grains (rice, quinoa, or couscous), a tray of roasted vegetables, and a protein like grilled chicken, hard-boiled eggs, or chickpeas. Add a jar of dressing or sauce, and wash and chop some raw veggies.

Each day, mix and match into something different: a grain bowl on Monday, a wrap on Tuesday, a salad topped with the leftover protein on Wednesday. You get variety without cooking every night, and nothing feels like the same leftovers on repeat.

**Bonus:** Pack lunches the night before, so mornings stay stress-free.
