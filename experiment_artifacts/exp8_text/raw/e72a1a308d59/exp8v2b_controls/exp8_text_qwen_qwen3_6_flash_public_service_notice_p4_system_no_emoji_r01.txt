PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY

Issuing Authority: Local Emergency Management Office
Effective Period: [Start Date] at [Start Time] through [End Date] at [End Time]

A heat advisory is in effect due to forecasted temperatures exceeding [Temperature]°F combined with elevated humidity. These conditions are expected to persist throughout the stated period.

Residents are advised to observe the following precautions:
• Drink water regularly and avoid caffeinated or alcoholic beverages.
• Limit outdoor exertion between 10 a.m. and 6 p.m.
• Use fans and air conditioning to maintain safe indoor temperatures. Close blinds during daylight hours to reduce heat gain.
• Check on elderly neighbors, young children, and individuals managing chronic illnesses.
• Never leave children, pets, or vulnerable persons inside parked vehicles.

Recognize the signs of heat-related illness, including heavy sweating, cold/clammy skin, dizziness, nausea, rapid heartbeat, and confusion. If symptoms appear, move the individual to a cool environment, apply wet cloths to the neck and wrists, and call emergency services without delay.
