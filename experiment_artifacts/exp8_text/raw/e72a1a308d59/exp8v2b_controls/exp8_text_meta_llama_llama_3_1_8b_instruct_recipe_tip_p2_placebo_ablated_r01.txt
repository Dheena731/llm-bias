**Preserving the Freshness: A Simple Storage Tip for Herbs**

When storing fresh herbs, it's essential to keep them away from direct sunlight and heat sources, such as ovens or radiators. This will help prevent the herbs from drying out and losing their flavor and aroma.

To store your herbs effectively, place them in a sealed container or plastic bag with a paper towel to absorb excess moisture. You can also wrap the herbs in a damp cloth and store them in the refrigerator to maintain their freshness.

**Key Consideration: Keep Them Cool and Dry**

Remember, the key to preserving fresh herbs is to keep them cool and dry. By following this simple storage tip, you'll be able to enjoy your herbs for a longer period and add flavor to your dishes with confidence.
