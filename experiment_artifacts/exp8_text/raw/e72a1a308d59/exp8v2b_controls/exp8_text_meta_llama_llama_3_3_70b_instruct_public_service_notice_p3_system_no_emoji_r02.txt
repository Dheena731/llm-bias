NOTICE OF RECYCLING COLLECTION CHANGES

Effective immediately, the recycling collection schedule and guidelines have been updated. Residents are now required to separate paper and cardboard from other recyclable materials, such as plastic and glass. Additionally, collection days have been changed to every other Thursday.

Please ensure that all recyclable materials are properly sorted and placed at the curb by 7:00 AM on the designated collection day. For a detailed list of acceptable materials and collection schedules, please visit our website or contact the Public Works department.
