**PUBLIC NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS**

Ahead of the upcoming election on Tuesday, November 5, 2024, all registered voters are encouraged to confirm their designated polling station location. 

Voting hours will be from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Please note that you must vote at your assigned precinct location based on your registered residential address. 

**How to Find Your Polling Station:**
* **Online:** Visit the official election website at www.elections.gov/find-my-polling-place and enter your residential address.
* **By Phone:** Contact the Election Office helpline at 1-800-555-VOTE (8683).
* **Voter Card:** Refer to the polling location listed on your official Voter Registration Card.

Please remember to bring a valid, government-issued photo ID if required in your jurisdiction. 

For accommodations or additional voting inquiries, please contact your local Board of Elections prior to Election Day.
