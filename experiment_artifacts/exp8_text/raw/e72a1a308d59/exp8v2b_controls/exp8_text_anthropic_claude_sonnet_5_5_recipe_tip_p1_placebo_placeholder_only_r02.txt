**Weeknight Tip: Cook Once, Eat Twice**

Start a pot of rice or quinoa while you prep everything else, and make double what you need. Spread the extra on a plate to cool quickly, then refrigerate it in a sealed container. The next night, you'll have a head start on fried rice, grain bowls, or stuffed peppers.

For extra flavor, toss a smashed garlic clove and a pinch of salt into the cooking water. Day-old grains also fry up better, since they're drier and won't turn mushy in the pan.

This simple habit can cut your cooking time nearly in half on busy evenings.
