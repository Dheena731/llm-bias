OFFICIAL NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS AND VOTING DETAILS

Registered voters are advised that polling stations for the upcoming election operate at designated community facilities within each precinct. Your assigned location corresponds to your residential address as recorded in the voter registry.

Active polling sites include:

*   Central District: Westgate High School Auditorium, 100 Education Way.
*   East District: Riverside Community Center, 220 Park Avenue.
*   West District: Northbrook Public Library, 55 Scholar Lane.

Polls open at 7:00 AM and close at 8:00 PM on Election Day. Voters must bring a valid photo identification card. Proof of residence documents, such as a utility bill or lease agreement, may also be requested.

Accessible voting equipment is available at all locations. For route planning or accessibility inquiries, contact the Board of Elections at 555-0142 or visit the official election website.

Please review your voter registration confirmation for your specific precinct assignment prior to voting.
