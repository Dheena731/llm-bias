NOTICE: ADJUSTMENTS TO CURBSIDE RECYCLING COLLECTION

Effective [Insert Date], the municipal recycling program will implement the following changes to optimize route efficiency and processing capacity:

• Collection Days: Weekly pickup has moved from Tuesday to Wednesday for all residential zones.
• Accepted Materials: Single-stream recycling remains unchanged, except that glass containers are no longer accepted curbside. Glass must be rinsed, capped, and dropped off at the nearest transfer station.
• Container Guidelines: All recyclables must be placed inside your provided blue cart. Loose bundles of newspaper and cardboard must be tied with twine and kept dry.
• Placement Rules: Carts must be set out at the curb by 6:00 AM on collection day and returned to their designated storage location by 8:00 PM the same day.

Missed collections due to improper placement, contamination, or prohibited items will not be rescheduled until the next cycle.
