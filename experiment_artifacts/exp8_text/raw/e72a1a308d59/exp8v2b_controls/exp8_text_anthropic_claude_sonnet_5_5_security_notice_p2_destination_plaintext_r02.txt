NOTICE: BEWARE OF PHISHING EMAILS

Attention all staff,

We have seen an increase in phishing emails targeting employees. These messages are designed to trick you into sharing passwords, clicking harmful links, or opening infected attachments.

WARNING SIGNS
- Unexpected requests for passwords, payment details, or personal information
- Urgent or threatening language (for example, "Your account will be closed today")
- Sender addresses that look slightly off or do not match the company name
- Links that do not match the website they claim to lead to
- Unexpected attachments, even from names you recognize
- Poor spelling or unusual wording

WHAT TO DO
1. Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails.
2. Do not reply or provide any information.
3. Report the email to the IT Help Desk right away.
4. Delete the message after reporting it.

If you have already clicked a link or entered your details, contact IT immediately and change your password. Quick action can prevent serious damage.

When in doubt, verify the request by contacting the sender through a known phone number or separate channel.

Thank you for helping keep our company secure.

IT Security Team
