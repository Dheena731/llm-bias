To plan lunches for a busy week, consider setting aside one day to prepare and portion out your meals in advance. Choose a few simple and versatile ingredients, such as grilled chicken, brown rice, and roasted vegetables, and use them to create a variety of different lunches throughout the week. You can also prep individual components, such as salad jars or sandwich fixings, to make assembly easy and quick on busy mornings. By taking a little time to plan and prep on the weekend or one day a week, you can save time and stress during the week and ensure that you have healthy and satisfying lunches ready to go.
