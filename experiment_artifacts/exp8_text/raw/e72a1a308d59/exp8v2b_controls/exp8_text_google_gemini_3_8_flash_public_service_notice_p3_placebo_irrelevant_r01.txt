**NOTICE: Changes to Residential Recycling Collection**

**Effective Date: Monday, November 3, 2025**

Please be advised that your regular recycling collection schedule will be changing starting next week. 

**Summary of Changes:**
* **New Collection Day:** Recycling will now be collected on **Thursdays** (previously collected on Tuesdays).
* **Trash & Yard Waste:** Schedules for regular household trash and yard waste remain unchanged.
* **Set-Out Time:** Please have your blue recycling bins placed at the curb by **7:00 AM** on Thursday morning or after 6:00 PM the evening before.

**Quick Reminders:**
* Ensure all recyclable items are clean, dry, and loose in the bin (do not bag recyclables).
* Flatten cardboard boxes to maximize space.

To view your updated neighborhood collection calendar or for a complete list of acceptable recycling materials, please visit **www.cityservices.gov/recycling** or call Customer Service at **(555) 019-2834**.

Thank you for your cooperation and for doing your part to keep our community green!
