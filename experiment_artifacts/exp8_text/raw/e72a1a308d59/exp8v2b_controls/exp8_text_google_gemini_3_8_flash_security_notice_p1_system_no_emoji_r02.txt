**SECURITY NOTICE: Protect Your Accounts – Avoid Password Reuse**

**To:** All Staff  
**From:** IT Security  
**Subject:** The Risks of Password Reuse  

Reusing the same password across multiple accounts significantly increases the risk of a security breach. If a single external platform or personal website is compromised, attackers routinely use those stolen credentials to attempt unauthorized access to our corporate network and sensitive company data.

To protect yourself and the organization, please follow these guidelines:

* **Use Unique Passwords:** Never use your corporate password for personal accounts, and ensure every work-related application has a distinct password.
* **Use a Password Manager:** Utilize the company-approved password manager to generate and securely store complex, unique passwords.
* **Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** Ensure MFA is active on all accounts where available to add a critical layer of defense.
* **Change Compromised Passwords Immediately:** If you suspect an account has been compromised or if you receive an alert regarding a data breach on a service you use, update your password right away.
