**Notice to All Staff: Watch for Phishing Emails**

**What is happening:** Phishing emails are fake messages that pretend to come from a trusted source, such as a coworker, a bank, or a company we work with. Their goal is to trick you into clicking a harmful link, opening an infected attachment, or giving away passwords or other private information.

**Common warning signs:**
- The message creates urgency, such as "Act now or your account will be closed."
- The sender's address looks slightly wrong or does not match the name shown.
- The email asks for a password, payment, or personal details.
- The greeting is generic, or the message contains unexpected links or attachments.

**What to do:**
1. Do not click links, open attachments, or reply.
2. Do not enter your password or other information on any page the email sends you to.
3. Report the email to [IT Help Desk contact] right away.
4. After reporting, delete the email.

**If you already clicked or replied:** Tell [IT Help Desk contact] immediately. Reporting quickly helps protect your account and the company, and no one will be penalized for reporting a mistake.

**If you are unsure:** Contact the supposed sender using a phone number or email address you already know to be correct, not one given in the message, or ask the IT Help Desk to check it.
