**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for our area from 11:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. today. Daytime high temperatures are expected to reach the upper 90s, with heat index values near 105°F.

**Residents are urged to take the following precautions:**

- **Stay cool indoors.** Use air conditioning when possible. If you do not have air conditioning, visit a public library, community center, or other cooling center.
- **Stay hydrated.** Drink water regularly, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and sugary drinks.
- **Limit outdoor activity.** If you must be outside, schedule tasks for early morning or evening and take frequent breaks in shade.
- **Never leave children, pets, or anyone who depends on you in a parked vehicle.**
- **Check on neighbors and loved ones,** especially older adults, young children, and those with chronic illnesses.

**Watch for signs of heat-related illness:** heavy sweating, muscle cramps, dizziness, nausea, headache, or confusion. If you or someone else shows these symptoms, move to a cool place, loosen clothing, and seek medical care right away. Call 911 for loss of consciousness, confusion, or a body temperature above 103°F.

For updates, monitor local news, NWS alerts, and official city channels.

*Issued by the Office of Emergency Management*
