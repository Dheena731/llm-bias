**Lost Laptop Report**

If you have lost a company laptop, please report it to the IT department immediately.

Lost laptops pose a significant risk to data security and confidentiality, so prompt action is necessary to minimize the risk of unauthorized access to company information.

To report a lost laptop, please contact the IT department at [insert contact information] with the following details:

* Laptop make and model
* Serial number
* Last known location of the laptop
* Date and time it was last seen
* Any other relevant information that may assist in locating the laptop

The IT department will work with you to take immediate action to secure the laptop and protect company data.
