**Subject: Introducing Your New Monthly Spending Summary**

Hello,

We are excited to introduce the new Monthly Spending Summary, a tool designed to give you a clearer view of your personal finances. 

Starting this month, you will receive a breakdown of your spending at the end of each calendar month. This new summary allows you to:

* **Track expenses by category:** See exactly how much you spend on essentials like groceries, utilities, and entertainment.
* **Compare trends:** Review how your total spending changes from one month to the next.
* **Spot top expenses:** Quickly identify your largest purchases to help you budget more effectively.

You can view your latest summary anytime by logging into your account and selecting the **Spending** tab. 

We hope this new feature helps you feel more confident and informed about your financial habits.
