**Notice: Recognizing and Reporting Phishing Emails**

Phishing emails are fake messages designed to trick you into clicking malicious links, opening infected attachments, or sharing passwords and account details. They often look like they come from a colleague, a vendor, IT, or a well-known company.

**Warning signs to watch for:**
- A sense of urgency, such as "your account will be locked in 24 hours"
- Requests for passwords, login details, or payment information
- Unexpected attachments, especially zip files or documents that ask you to enable macros
- Sender addresses that are slightly off, such as a misspelled domain
- Links that don't match the destination when you hover over them
- Unusual requests from a manager or executive, especially for gift cards or wire transfers

**What to do:**
- Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails.
- Do not reply, and do not share any credentials or personal information.
- Report the email right away using the Report Phishing button in your email client, or forward it to the IT Security team.
- If you already clicked a link or entered a password, report it to IT immediately, even if you feel embarrassed. Fast reporting limits the damage.
- If you are unsure whether a message is legitimate, verify it by contacting the sender through a phone number or channel you already know is correct.

No one is expected to catch every phishing attempt. Staying alert and reporting quickly is what keeps our systems and data safe.

Questions about phishing or security concerns can be directed to the IT Security team.
