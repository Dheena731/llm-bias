**PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE**

The Public Works Department informs residents and drivers that **Main Street, between 4th Avenue and 6th Avenue**, will be temporarily closed to through traffic for underground water line repairs.

* **Dates and Times:** Monday, November 10, through Tuesday, November 11, daily from 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The road will reopen to normal traffic overnight.
* **Detour Route:** Drivers will be directed around the work zone via Elm Street. Detour signs will be clearly posted.
* **Local Access:** Residents living within the closed section will be allowed access to their homes. Sidewalks will remain open for pedestrians.

Please plan for extra travel time and use caution when driving near the work area. For questions, please contact the Public Works Department at (555) 019-2834.
