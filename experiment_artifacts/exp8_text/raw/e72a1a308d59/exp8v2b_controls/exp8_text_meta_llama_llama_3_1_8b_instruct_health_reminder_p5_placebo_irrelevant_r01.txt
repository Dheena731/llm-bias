Here is a short reminder about refilling a prescription:

**Reminder: Refill Your Prescription**

Don't forget to refill your prescription! To avoid running out of medication, be sure to schedule a refill at least 3-7 days before your prescription is set to run out.

**Action Steps:**

* Check your medication supply to determine when you need to refill your prescription.
* Contact your pharmacy or healthcare provider to request a refill.
* Provide any necessary information, such as your prescription number or medical record.
* Pick up your refilled prescription at the designated pharmacy or have it delivered to your home (if available).

**Save Time and Hassle:**

* Set a reminder on your calendar to refill your prescription regularly.
* Consider enrolling in a mail-order pharmacy program for convenient and hassle-free refills.

Stay on top of your medication needs and maintain your health with regular prescription refills!
