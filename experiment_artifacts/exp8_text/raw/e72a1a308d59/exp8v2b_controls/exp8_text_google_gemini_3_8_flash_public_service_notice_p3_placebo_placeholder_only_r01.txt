**Notice: Changes to Residential Recycling Collection**

Effective Monday, November 3, residential recycling collection will move from Wednesday to Thursday mornings. 

Please place blue recycling bins at the curb no later than 7:00 AM on Thursdays to ensure pickup. Collection routes have been updated to improve route efficiency, which means trucks may arrive earlier in the day than previously experienced. 

In addition to the day change, clean glass bottles and jars are now accepted directly in the curbside bins. Plastic film, grocery bags, and polystyrene foam remain strictly prohibited. 

Printed collection calendars have been mailed to all households and are also available for download on the municipal public works website. Residents with questions may contact the Department of Waste Management by calling (800) 555-0199.
