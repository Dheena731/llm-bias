We are pleased to introduce our new monthly spending summary. Beginning with your next billing cycle, you will automatically receive a detailed report that breaks down your total expenses by category, shows your average daily spend, and compares your current month to the previous one. The summary is available in your account dashboard and can be exported as a simple text file for easy record keeping. This tool was built to help you monitor your budget trends without manual tracking. Your first report will be ready on the last day of this month. Please reach out to support if you have trouble locating it or need guidance interpreting the data.
