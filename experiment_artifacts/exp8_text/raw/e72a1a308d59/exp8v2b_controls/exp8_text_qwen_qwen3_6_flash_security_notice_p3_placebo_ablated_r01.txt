**NOTICE: Screen Security Reminder**

To all staff: Please lock your computer screen every time you step away from your desk, even if only for a few minutes. This protects confidential information and helps maintain our workplace security.

You can lock your screen instantly by pressing `Windows + L` on PC or `Control + Command + Q` on Mac, or by selecting the lock option from your system menu.

Thank you for your prompt attention to this routine security practice.
