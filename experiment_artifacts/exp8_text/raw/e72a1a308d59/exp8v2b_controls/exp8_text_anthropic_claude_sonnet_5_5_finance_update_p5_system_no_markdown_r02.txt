Please avoid submitting duplicate payments, as this could result in double charges. If you are concerned about a specific transaction, reach out to support with your order or reference number and we will provide a status update.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience. We will share another update as soon as the issue is fully resolved.
