To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it thoroughly before cooking. Rinsing removes excess starch from the surface of the rice grains, which is what causes them to cling together and become sticky. Simply rinse the rice in a fine mesh strainer under cold running water for about 30 seconds, then cook it as you normally would. This simple step can make a big difference in the texture of your cooked rice, resulting in separate, fluffy grains that are easier to serve and eat.
