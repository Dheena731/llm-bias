**Tip: Season in two stages for the best flavor.**

Before roasting, toss your cut vegetables with oil and a generous pinch of salt and pepper. The oil helps the vegetables brown, and the early salt seasons them through. Spread them in a single layer on the pan so they roast instead of steam.

After roasting, taste them while they're still hot and adjust. Add a squeeze of lemon juice or a splash of vinegar for brightness, plus a little more salt if needed. Fresh herbs, such as parsley or basil, also work best when added at this stage, because high heat can burn them and dull their flavor.

**Quick example:** Roast broccoli with olive oil, salt, and pepper at 425°F (220°C) until the edges are browned, then finish with lemon juice and a sprinkle of grated Parmesan.
