Quarterly Access Reviews are now underway. As part of our ongoing effort to maintain system security and compliance, all administrators and managers are required to review and verify access permissions for their respective teams and departments. This review ensures that all users have the appropriate level of access to sensitive data and systems, and that any changes in job responsibilities or personnel are accurately reflected in our access controls. Please complete your review by the end of the quarter and submit any necessary updates or changes to the IT department for processing. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT department for assistance.
