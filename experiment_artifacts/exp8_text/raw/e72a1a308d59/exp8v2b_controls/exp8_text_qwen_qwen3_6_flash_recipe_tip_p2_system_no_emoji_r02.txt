Trim the stems and place delicate herbs like cilantro and parsley upright in a jar with about an inch of water, loosely cover the top with a plastic bag, and refrigerate. Replace the water every two days. For hardier herbs like rosemary, thyme, and sage, wrap them loosely in a slightly damp paper towel and store in a sealed container or resealable bag in the refrigerator. This method keeps most fresh herbs vibrant and usable for one to two weeks.
