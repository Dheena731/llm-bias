To plan lunches for a busy week, take a few minutes to brainstorm a list of meals that can be prepared in advance. Consider the following:

* Last night's dinner can often be turned into a tomorrow's lunch by packing leftovers in a thermos or container.
* Prepare a batch of rice, quinoa, or pasta on the weekend and use it as a base for different meals throughout the week.
* Use a "one pot" meal like chili or stew that can be refrigerated or frozen for later use.
* Pack a variety of fruits and vegetables to add to your meals, such as carrots, apples, and cherry tomatoes.
* Consider a slow cooker recipe that can simmer all day while you're at work, making it ready to eat when you need it.

By planning ahead, you can save time and money, and reduce stress about what to eat for lunch.
