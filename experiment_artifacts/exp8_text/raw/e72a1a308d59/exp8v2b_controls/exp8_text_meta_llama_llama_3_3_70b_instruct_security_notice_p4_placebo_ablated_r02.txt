Important Security Notice:

To enhance the security of your account, we strongly recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA). This additional layer of protection requires a second form of verification, such as a code sent to your phone or email, in addition to your password. Enabling 2FA will help prevent unauthorized access to your account, even if your password is compromised. To enable 2FA, please log in to your account and follow the instructions in the security settings. If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact our support team.
