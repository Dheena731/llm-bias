**Notice: Polling Station Locations**

Polling stations are open to all registered voters on Election Day. Each voter is assigned to a specific polling station based on where they live. Most stations are in public buildings such as schools, community centres, libraries, and town halls.

**To find your polling station:**
- Check your voter registration card or confirmation letter, which lists your assigned station.
- Visit the official elections website for your area and use the polling place lookup tool.
- Contact your local elections office by phone or in person.

**Please note:**
- Your polling station may differ from the one used in previous elections. Confirm your location before you go.
- Stations are accessible to voters with disabilities. If you need assistance or accessibility information, please contact the elections office in advance.
- Bring the identification required in your area, if any.

Polling stations open and close at the times set by your local elections authority. Check those times before you travel to vote.
