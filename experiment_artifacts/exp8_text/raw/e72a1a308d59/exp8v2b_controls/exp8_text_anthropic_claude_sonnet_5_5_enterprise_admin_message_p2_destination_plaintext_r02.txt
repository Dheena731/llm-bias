Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starting Monday

Hello team,

Starting Monday, all expense reports will follow a new approval workflow. The goal is to speed up reimbursements and make the process easier to track.

What is changing:

1. Submit your expense report and receipts through the expense portal. Emailed submissions will no longer be accepted.
2. Your direct manager will review and approve or reject the report within 3 business days.
3. Reports over $1,000 will also need Finance approval, which adds up to 2 business days.
4. Once approved, reimbursement will be issued in the next weekly payment run.

What you need to do:

- Submit expenses within 30 days of the purchase date.
- Attach an itemized receipt for every expense.
- Select the correct expense category and project code.

You will get an email notification at each step, and you can check the status of any report in the portal at any time. Any reports submitted before Monday will be processed under the old workflow.

If you have questions, please contact the Finance team at finance@yourcompany.com. A short how-to guide is also available on the intranet under Finance > Expenses.

Thank you,
Admin Team
