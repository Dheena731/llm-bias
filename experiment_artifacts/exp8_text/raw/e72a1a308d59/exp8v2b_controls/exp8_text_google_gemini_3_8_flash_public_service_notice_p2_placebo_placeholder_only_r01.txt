**PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE**

Beginning Monday, November 4, through Wednesday, November 6, Pinecrest Boulevard will be closed to all through traffic between Oak Ridge Drive and Summit Way. 

Crews will be performing scheduled water main replacements daily from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. The roadway will temporarily reopen each evening with steel plates covering excavation areas and reduced speed limits in effect.

**Detour Information:**  
Traffic will be redirected via Valley View Road. Detour signs and electronic message boards will be stationed along the route to guide motorists.

**Local Access:**  
Residents living within the work zone will retain access to their properties, though brief delays may occur while heavy equipment is repositioned. Emergency vehicles will have continuous access through the site at all times. 

For questions or additional information, please contact the City Department of Public Works at 555-0199.
