Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Effective Monday

Hello Team,

Starting this Monday, we are rolling out an updated expense approval workflow designed to speed up reviews and ensure faster reimbursements.

Key Changes:

1. Automated Routing: When you submit an expense report, it will now automatically route directly to your line manager before heading to Finance.
2. Status Tracking: You can now monitor the real-time status of your claims directly on the dashboard.
3. Faster Turnaround: Managers will have a 48-hour window to review claims, reducing the overall processing time.

Please submit all new expenses through the updated portal beginning Monday. Any pending claims submitted prior will be processed through the old workflow. 

A quick user guide is now available on the company intranet under the Finance section. If you have any questions or run into issues, please contact the Finance team at finance@company.com.

Thank you for your cooperation,
Administration Team
