A Heat Advisory has been issued for our area, effective immediately. Due to extremely high temperatures and humidity, residents are advised to take precautions to stay safe. Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water, limit outdoor activities to early morning or evening, and seek shade or air-conditioned spaces when possible. Check on vulnerable neighbors, including the elderly and young children, to ensure their safety. For more information and heat-related safety tips, visit our website or contact local emergency management officials.
