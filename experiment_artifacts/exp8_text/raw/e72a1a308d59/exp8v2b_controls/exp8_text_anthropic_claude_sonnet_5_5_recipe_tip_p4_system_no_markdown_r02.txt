Cook once, eat several times. On Sunday, prepare two or three versatile building blocks, such as a big batch of grains like rice or quinoa, a tray of roasted vegetables, and a protein like grilled chicken or baked tofu. Store them in separate containers, then mix and match through the week with different sauces or toppings. One day it becomes a grain bowl with peanut dressing, the next a wrap with hummus, and later a salad with lemon vinaigrette. You get variety without cooking every day, and because the components are already prepped, assembling lunch takes only a couple of minutes. Pack sauces separately so nothing gets soggy, and plan to eat the most delicate items, like fresh greens or cooked fish, in the first two or three days.
