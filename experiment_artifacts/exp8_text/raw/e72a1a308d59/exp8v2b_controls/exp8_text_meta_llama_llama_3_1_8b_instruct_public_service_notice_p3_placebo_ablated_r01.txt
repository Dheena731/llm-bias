**NOTICE OF RECYCLING COLLECTION CHANGES**

As part of our ongoing efforts to improve our waste management services, the City is making changes to the recycling collection schedule. Starting from April 1st, recyclables will be collected every other week, on the same day as your regular trash collection.

**Important Details:**

* Your recycling collection day will remain the same, but the frequency will change.
* Please ensure all recyclables are placed in your designated recycling bin, not the trash can.
* Materials accepted for recycling include:
	+ Paper and cardboard
	+ Plastic bottles and containers (rinsed and loose)
	+ Glass bottles and jars
	+ Metal cans and foil

**Why the change?**

The City is implementing this change to reduce the frequency of collection and optimize our resources. This will not only improve our efficiency but also reduce costs.

**What to do:**

* Review your collection calendar to confirm your new recycling collection day.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer service department at (555) 555-5555.

We appreciate your cooperation and look forward to working together to maintain a clean and sustainable community.
