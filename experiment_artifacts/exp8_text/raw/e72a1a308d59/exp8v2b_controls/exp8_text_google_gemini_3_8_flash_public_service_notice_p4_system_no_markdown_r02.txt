PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT

A Heat Advisory has been issued for our area, effective today from 11:00 AM to 8:00 PM. High temperatures combined with high humidity are expected to create dangerously high heat index values reaching up to 105 degrees.

Residents are strongly urged to take the following precautions:

1. Drink plenty of fluids, even if you do not feel thirsty.
2. Stay indoors in air-conditioned environments during the hottest hours of the day.
3. Reschedule or reduce strenuous outdoor activities until the early morning or evening.
4. Wear lightweight, loose-fitting clothing.
5. Never leave children or pets unattended in parked vehicles for any length of time.
6. Check on elderly family members, neighbors, and those with medical conditions.

Be alert for signs of heat illness, such as dizziness, nausea, headache, and extreme fatigue. Move to a cooler location immediately if you feel unwell. For medical emergencies, call 911.

Free public cooling centers are open at the local community center and city library. For additional resources, please contact the local emergency management office.
