WORKPLACE NOTICE: PHISHING EMAIL ALERT

All staff are reminded to remain vigilant regarding suspicious email activity. Cybercriminals frequently send deceptive messages designed to steal login credentials or install malware.

Please watch for these warning signs:
Unexpected requests for personal or account information
Urgent language demanding immediate action
Links or attachments from unknown or unexpected senders
Misspellings, generic greetings, or mismatched sender addresses

If you receive a suspicious email, do not click any links or download attachments. Forward the message to IT Security at security@company.com with the subject line PHISHING REPORT. Delete the original after forwarding.

Report any suspected breaches immediately. For questions, contact the IT Helpdesk at extension 5500 or visit the internal security portal.
