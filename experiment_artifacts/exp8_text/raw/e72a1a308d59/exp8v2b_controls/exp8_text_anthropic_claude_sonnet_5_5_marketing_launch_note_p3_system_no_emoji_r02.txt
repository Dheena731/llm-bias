**Introducing the Clear Budget Template: Know Where Your Money Goes**

We're pleased to announce the launch of the Clear Budget Template, a simple spreadsheet designed to help you take control of your finances without the learning curve.

**What it does**

The template brings your income, expenses, savings goals, and debt payments into one organized workbook. Enter your numbers once, and the built-in formulas handle the rest, showing you at a glance how much you've spent, what's left, and where you can adjust.

**Key features**

- **Monthly overview dashboard:** See your income, spending, and savings rate on a single page.
- **Automatic category tracking:** Pre-built categories for housing, food, transportation, and more, all customizable to fit your life.
- **Savings goal tracker:** Set a target, add a deadline, and watch your progress update as you go.
- **Debt payoff planner:** Compare payoff timelines and see how extra payments shorten them.
- **Yearly summary:** Review twelve months of data and spot trends that are easy to miss day to day.

**Who it's for**

Whether you're budgeting for the first time or looking to replace a more complicated system, Clear Budget works for individuals, couples, and families. It is compatible with Excel, Google Sheets, and Numbers.

**Availability**

The Clear Budget Template is available now. Download it today, and start your first budget in under ten minutes.
