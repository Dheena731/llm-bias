Here's a short tip for storing fresh herbs:

**Tip: Store Fresh Herbs with Air Circulation**

To keep fresh herbs fresh for a longer period, store them in a way that allows for good air circulation. Avoid storing herbs in airtight containers or plastic bags, as this can cause moisture to accumulate and lead to mold and spoilage. Instead, place the herbs in a paper bag or a breathable container, such as a mesh produce bag, and store them in a cool, dry place. This will help to preserve their flavor and aroma.
