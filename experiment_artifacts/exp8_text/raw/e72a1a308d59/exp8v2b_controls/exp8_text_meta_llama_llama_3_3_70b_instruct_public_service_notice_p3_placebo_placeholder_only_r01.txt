Beginning on January 1, the recycling collection schedule will be changing to every other week. Residents are asked to have their recyclables at the curb by 7:00 AM on their designated collection day. The new collection schedule is as follows: even-numbered houses will have their recyclables collected on the 1st and 15th of each month, while odd-numbered houses will have their recyclables collected on the 8th and 22nd of each month. For more information, please visit the city's website or contact the public works department.
