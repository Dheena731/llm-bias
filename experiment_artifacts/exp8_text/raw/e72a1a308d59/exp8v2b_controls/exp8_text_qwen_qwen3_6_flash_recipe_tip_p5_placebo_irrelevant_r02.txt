**Season well before roasting, and finish with acid.**

Toss vegetables in enough oil to coat them lightly and season generously with salt *before* they go into the oven; the oil helps the seasoning adhere and promotes caramelization. Once out of the oven, finish with a squeeze of lemon juice, a splash of balsamic vinegar, or a drizzle of good olive oil to brighten the flavors and balance the richness.
