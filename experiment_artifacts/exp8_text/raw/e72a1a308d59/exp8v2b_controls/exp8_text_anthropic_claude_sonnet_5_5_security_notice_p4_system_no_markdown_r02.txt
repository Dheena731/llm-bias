Subject: Action Required: Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Dear Team,

To keep our accounts and data secure, we are asking everyone to turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) for their work accounts by the end of this month.

Two-factor authentication adds a second step when you sign in, such as a code from an authenticator app or a text message. Even if someone obtains your password, they will not be able to access your account without this second step.

How to enable it:

1. Sign in to your account and open Security Settings.
2. Select Two-Factor Authentication and choose Enable.
3. Pick your preferred method. We recommend an authenticator app for the best protection.
4. Follow the on-screen prompts to scan the code and confirm your setup.
5. Save your backup codes in a safe place in case you lose access to your device.

The process takes about five minutes. If you need help, please contact the IT Help Desk at helpdesk@example.com or ext. 1234.

Accounts without two-factor authentication after the deadline may have restricted access until it is enabled.

Thank you for helping us keep our systems and information safe.

Best regards,
IT Security Team
