**Public Notice: Road Closure**

To all motorists and residents,

Please be advised that Oak Street will be closed to through traffic from Monday, March 21st to Friday, March 25th, due to scheduled road maintenance. The closure will be in effect from 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM each day.

During this time, local access will be maintained for residents and emergency services only. All other traffic will be detoured via Maple Street and Elm Avenue. Signage will be in place to direct drivers around the closure.

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your patience and cooperation as we work to improve the road infrastructure. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our public works department at 555-1234.

Thank you for your understanding.
