NOTICE OF CHANGES TO ACCOUNT TERMS

Effective as of January 1, 2024, the terms and conditions governing your account with our institution will be amended. The changes include an update to our fee schedule, which will result in a monthly maintenance fee of $10.00 being assessed on accounts with average daily balances below $1,000.00. Additionally, the interest rate on certain account types will be adjusted to a rate of 2.00% per annum.

These changes will apply to all existing and new accounts, and by continuing to use your account after the effective date, you will be deemed to have accepted the revised terms. If you do not agree to the changes, you may close your account prior to the effective date to avoid being subject to the new terms.

A copy of the revised account agreement will be available on our website and at our branch locations. If you have any questions or concerns about the changes, please contact our customer service department.
