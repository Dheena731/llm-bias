**Weeknight Dinner Tip: Prep Your Ingredients Before You Turn On the Heat**

Before you start cooking, spend five minutes washing, chopping, and measuring everything you'll need. Set each item in small bowls or on a cutting board near the stove.

**Why it helps:** Most weeknight dishes, like stir-fries, pan-seared chicken, or sautéed vegetables, cook quickly. If you're still chopping an onion while the garlic is already in the hot pan, the garlic can burn. Having everything ready lets you add ingredients at the right moment and keeps the cooking calm and even.

**How to do it:**
1. Read the recipe from start to finish.
2. Chop all vegetables and measure sauces and spices.
3. Group ingredients by when they go into the pan, so the first items are closest to the stove.
4. Start cooking.

**Bonus:** Wash and dry your cutting board and knife as you go, so cleanup afterward is shorter.

This small habit makes dinner faster and less stressful, and your food will cook more evenly.
