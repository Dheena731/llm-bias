Subject: Reporting a Lost Company Laptop

All employees are reminded that if a company laptop is lost or stolen, it must be reported to the IT Service Desk and your manager immediately, and no later than 24 hours after you discover it is missing.

When reporting, please provide the following:
- Your name and employee ID
- The laptop's asset tag and serial number, if known
- The date, time, and location where the laptop was last seen
- A brief description of the circumstances

If the laptop was stolen, also file a police report and send a copy to the Security team.

The IT team will immediately disable access to company accounts and systems on the device and begin remote data-wipe procedures where possible. Prompt reporting helps protect sensitive company and customer information.

For questions, contact the IT Service Desk at extension 4357 or servicedesk@company.com.
