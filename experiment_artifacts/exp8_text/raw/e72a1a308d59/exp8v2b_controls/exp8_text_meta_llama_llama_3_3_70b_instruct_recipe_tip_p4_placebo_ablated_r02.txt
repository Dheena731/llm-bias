To plan lunches for a busy week, consider setting aside one day, such as Sunday, to prepare and portion out healthy meals that can be easily grabbed and taken on-the-go. This can include cooking a large batch of rice, grains, or protein, and then using these ingredients to assemble individual lunches, such as salads, wraps, or containers with leftovers. Having a week's worth of lunches ready to go can save time and reduce stress during the busy weekdays, and can also help ensure that you're eating nutritious and satisfying meals even when you're in a hurry.
