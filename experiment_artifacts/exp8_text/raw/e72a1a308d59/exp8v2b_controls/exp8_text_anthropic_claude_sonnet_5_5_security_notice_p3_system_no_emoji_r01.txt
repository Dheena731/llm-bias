**Reminder: Lock Your Screen When Away From Your Desk**

All staff are reminded to lock their computer screens whenever they step away from their workstations, even for a short time.

An unlocked screen can give others access to confidential information, email, and internal systems. Locking your screen takes only a second and helps protect our data, our clients, and our colleagues.

**How to lock your screen:**
- Windows: Press the Windows key + L
- Mac: Press Control + Command + Q

Please also make sure your device is set to lock automatically after a short period of inactivity.

Thank you for helping keep our workplace secure. If you have any questions or need help adjusting your settings, please contact the IT Help Desk.
