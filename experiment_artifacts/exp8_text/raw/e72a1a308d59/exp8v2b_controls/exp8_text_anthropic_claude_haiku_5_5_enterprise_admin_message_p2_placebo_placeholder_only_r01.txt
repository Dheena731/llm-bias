Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starting Monday

Hi team,

Starting Monday, June 2, all expense reports will move to a new approval workflow in the finance portal. The goal is faster reimbursements and clearer audit trails.

Here's what's changing:

- Submit expenses within 30 days of purchase through the portal's "New Report" button.
- Expenses under $250 need only your manager's approval.
- Expenses of $250 or more also require sign-off from the Finance team.
- You'll get an email notification at each step, and you can track status under "My Reports."

Reports submitted before June 2 will finish under the old process.

If you have questions, reply to this message or stop by the Finance office on the second floor. A 20-minute walkthrough session is scheduled for Thursday, May 29, at 2:00 p.m. in Conference Room B.
