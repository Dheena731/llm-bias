**Introducing Your Monthly Spending Summary**

We've added a Monthly Spending Summary to the Budget tab. At the start of each month, it gives you a clear picture of how your money was spent over the previous month.

Each summary includes:

- **Total spending** for the month, compared with the month before it
- **Top categories**, ranked by how much you spent in each
- **Budget progress**, showing which categories stayed within their limits and which went over
- **Notable changes**, such as a category that rose sharply or a recurring charge we detected

You'll get a notification when your summary is ready. You can also open any past summary from the Budget tab under History.

Summaries are based only on transactions from linked accounts, so categories may shift if a transaction is miscategorized. You can correct any category directly in the transaction list, and future summaries will reflect the change.

If you'd rather not receive the notification, you can turn it off in Settings under Notifications. The summaries will still be available in your History.
