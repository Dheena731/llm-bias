Subject: Introducing Your New Monthly Spending Summary

Hello,

We're pleased to announce a new monthly spending summary, a clear overview of your account activity delivered at the start of each month.

Each summary shows your total spending for the previous month, broken down by category such as groceries, dining, transportation, and utilities. It also compares your current spending with the prior month so you can quickly see where your money went and whether your habits are changing.

You can find your summary in the Statements section of your account dashboard. If you prefer, you can opt in to receive a notification by email when a new summary is ready.

The summary is designed to help you plan ahead, spot unexpected charges, and set budgets that fit your goals. If you notice a transaction that looks incorrect, you can flag it directly from the summary page.

If you have any questions about the new feature, please contact our support team, who will be happy to help.

Thank you for banking with us.
