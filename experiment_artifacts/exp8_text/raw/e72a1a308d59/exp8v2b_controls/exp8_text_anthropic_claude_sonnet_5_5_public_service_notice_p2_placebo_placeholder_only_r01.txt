**PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE**

**Maple Ridge Road Closed for Bridge Repairs**

The Millbrook Department of Public Works will close Maple Ridge Road between Thornton Avenue and Delancey Street from **Monday, June 9, at 7:00 a.m. through Friday, June 20, at 6:00 p.m.** The closure allows crews to repair the Cedar Creek Bridge deck and replace damaged guardrails.

**What to expect:**
- Maple Ridge Road will be closed to all vehicle traffic in both directions.
- Pedestrian and bicycle access will remain open on the north sidewalk, but delays are possible.
- Residents on affected blocks will have access to their driveways, except for brief periods when crews are working directly in front of a property.

**Detour route:** Drivers should take Thornton Avenue to Route 12, then Route 12 to Delancey Street. The detour adds about six minutes to typical travel times.

**Additional information:**
- School buses and emergency vehicles will follow modified routes.
- Trash and recycling collection will continue on the normal schedule.
- Work may be delayed in the event of heavy rain, which could extend the closure by up to three days.

Questions can be directed to the Millbrook Department of Public Works at 555-0142, Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Updates will also be posted at millbrookpublicworks.example.gov.

We appreciate your patience while we make these important safety improvements.

*Issued by the Millbrook Department of Public Works*
