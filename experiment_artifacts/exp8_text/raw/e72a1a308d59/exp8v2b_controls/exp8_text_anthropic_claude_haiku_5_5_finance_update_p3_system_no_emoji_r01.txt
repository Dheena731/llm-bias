**Introducing Savings Goals**

We're excited to announce Savings Goals, a new feature that helps you set, track, and reach your financial targets.

With Savings Goals, you can:

- **Create a goal** for anything you're saving toward, whether it's an emergency fund, a vacation, or a new laptop.
- **Set a target amount and date** so you always know how much to save and when.
- **Track your progress** with a clear view of how close you are to each goal.
- **Automate contributions** to move money into your goals on a schedule that works for you.

Savings Goals is available now in the app under the Savings tab. Tap "New Goal" to get started.

We'd love to hear how you use it. Send us your feedback through the Help section in the app.
