**PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE**

Maple Street between Oak Avenue and Pine Boulevard will be closed from Monday, June 10 through Friday, June 14, 2024. The closure will occur daily from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. to complete emergency water main repairs. 

During this period, all motorists and pedestrians must follow the posted detour route along Elm Drive and Cedar Lane. Please allow extra travel time, obey all temporary traffic controls, and keep left lanes clear for emergency vehicles.

For real-time updates or accessibility accommodations, contact the City Public Works Department at 555-0198 or visit citygovernment.gov/road-notices. We appreciate your patience and cooperation as we restore full service to this section of Maple Street.
