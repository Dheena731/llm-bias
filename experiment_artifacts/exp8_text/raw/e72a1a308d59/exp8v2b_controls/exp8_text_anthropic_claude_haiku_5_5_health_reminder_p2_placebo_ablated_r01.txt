Friendly reminder: drink enough water today. Staying hydrated helps your body stay energized, keeps your mind clear, and supports your overall health. Keep a bottle nearby, take a sip every hour or so, and drink a glass with each meal. If your urine is pale yellow, you are likely well hydrated. Thirst is a signal to drink, so don't wait until you feel thirsty. Take care of yourself, one glass at a time.
