Here's a short admin notice about quarterly access reviews:

**Quarterly Access Reviews: Reminder and Process**

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain the security and integrity of our systems, all users are required to participate in quarterly access reviews. This process ensures that access to sensitive data and systems remains aligned with business needs and regulatory requirements.

**When to Expect a Review**

You will be notified approximately 30 days prior to the end of each quarter (January 15th, April 15th, July 15th, and October 15th) when it's time to review your access. This notification will include a link to the review system, where you will be able to:

* Review your current access permissions
* Update your access as needed
* Decline access to any areas that are no longer required

**What to Expect During a Review**

A quarterly access review will typically involve the following steps:

1. Review your current access permissions and identify any changes that may be necessary.
2. Update your access by logging into the review system and making any necessary changes.
3. Respond to any notifications or emails requesting that you review and update your access.

**Important Notes**

* Failure to participate in quarterly access reviews may result in temporary or permanent loss of access to sensitive data and systems.
* If you experience any issues during the review process, please contact the IT department for assistance.

If you have any questions or concerns about quarterly access reviews, please don't hesitate to reach out to the IT department. We're here to help.
