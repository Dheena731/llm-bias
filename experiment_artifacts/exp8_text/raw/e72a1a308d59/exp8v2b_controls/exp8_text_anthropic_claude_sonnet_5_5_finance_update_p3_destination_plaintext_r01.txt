Introducing Savings Goals

You can now set savings goals directly in the app. Whether you're saving for a vacation, a new laptop, or an emergency fund, this feature helps you stay on track.

Here's how it works:

- Create a goal by giving it a name, a target amount, and an optional deadline.
- Add money to your goal at any time, or set up automatic transfers on a weekly or monthly schedule.
- Watch your progress with a simple progress bar that shows how close you are to reaching your target.
- Get a friendly notification when you hit a milestone, such as 25, 50, or 75 percent of your goal.

You can create up to ten goals at once, and you can edit or pause any of them whenever your plans change.

To get started, open the app and tap Goals on the main menu. Update to the latest version if you don't see it yet.
