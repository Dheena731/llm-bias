**Notice of Polling Station Locations**

Voters are advised that polling stations for the upcoming election are open to all eligible voters on election day. Each registered voter is assigned to a specific polling station based on their home address.

To find your polling station, you can:

- Check the polling card mailed to you by the election office.
- Visit the official election website for your area and enter your registered address.
- Contact your local election office by phone or in person during business hours.

Please confirm your polling station before election day, as locations may differ from previous elections. Polling stations are accessible to voters with disabilities, and assistance is available on request. Bring any identification required by local law.
