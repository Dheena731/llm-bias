Here's a short reminder to drink enough water during the day:

**Stay Hydrated, Stay Healthy!**

Don't forget to drink water throughout the day! Aim for at least 8 cups (64 oz) of water per day to stay energized and focused. Even mild dehydration can cause fatigue, headaches, and difficulty concentrating. Take a few sips from your water bottle now and make it a habit to check in with your hydration levels regularly.

**Tips to Stay on Track:**

* Keep a water bottle at your desk or in your bag
* Set reminders on your phone to drink water at certain times of the day
* Infuse your water with fruits or herbs for a refreshing twist
* Make water a habit by making it a part of your daily routine

Stay hydrated and feel the difference!
