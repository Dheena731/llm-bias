Lost Company Laptop Notice

If you have lost or misplaced a company laptop, please report it to the IT Department immediately. 

Contact information for the IT Department is as follows:

* Phone: 555-1234
* Email: [itdepartment@company.com](mailto:itdepartment@company.com)

When reporting the lost laptop, please provide the following information:

* Laptop serial number
* Laptop model number
* Date and time of last use
* Location where the laptop was last seen

This will help the IT Department to quickly locate the laptop and take necessary steps to secure the company's data.
