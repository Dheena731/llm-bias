To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it thoroughly before cooking. This helps remove excess starch, which can cause the rice to cling together. Additionally, use a ratio of at least 1 3/4 cups of water to 1 cup of rice, and avoid overmixing or stirring the rice too much while it's cooking. Finally, try adding a tablespoon of oil or butter to the water before cooking, as this can help separate the grains and reduce stickiness.
