We are excited to announce the introduction of a new monthly spending summary feature. This tool is designed to help you better track and manage your expenses by providing a clear and concise overview of your spending habits.

Each month, you will receive a detailed summary of your transactions, broken down into categories such as groceries, entertainment, and transportation. This summary will include the total amount spent, as well as the average daily spend and any notable trends or changes in your spending patterns.

The monthly spending summary will be available online and will be sent to you via email at the beginning of each month. This will give you a chance to review your spending, identify areas where you can cut back, and make informed decisions about your budget.

We hope this new feature will help you take control of your finances and make the most of your money. If you have any questions or need help accessing your monthly spending summary, please don't hesitate to contact us.
