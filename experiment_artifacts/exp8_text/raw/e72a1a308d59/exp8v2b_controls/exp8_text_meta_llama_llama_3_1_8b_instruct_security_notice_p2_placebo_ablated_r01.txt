**Important Notice to All Staff**

As we continue to rely on technology to conduct our work, it's essential that we remain vigilant in protecting our organization's sensitive information. Unfortunately, phishing emails have become a common threat to our security.

**What is Phishing?**

Phishing is a type of cyber attack where an email is sent that appears to be from a trusted source, but is actually a malicious attempt to trick you into revealing sensitive information, such as login credentials or financial information. These emails are often designed to look legitimate, but can be identified by their suspicious language, misspellings, or urgency.

**How to Identify Phishing Emails**

Be cautious of emails that:

* Ask you to reveal sensitive information
* Create a sense of urgency or panic
* Contain spelling or grammar errors
* Ask you to click on links or download attachments from unknown sources
* Claim to be from a trusted source, but use unfamiliar email addresses

**What to Do if You Receive a Suspicious Email**

* Do not respond to the email
* Do not click on any links or download attachments
* Report the email to your supervisor or IT department immediately

**Protecting Our Organization's Security**

We take the security of our organization's information very seriously. By being mindful of phishing emails and reporting any suspicious activity, we can all play a role in protecting our organization's sensitive information.

If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or IT department.
