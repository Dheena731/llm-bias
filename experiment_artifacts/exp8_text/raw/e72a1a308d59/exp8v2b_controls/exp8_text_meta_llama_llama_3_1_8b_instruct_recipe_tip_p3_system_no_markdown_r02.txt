Here's a short cooking tip for making rice less sticky:

To make rice less sticky, rinse the rice thoroughly before cooking and use the right ratio of water to rice. The general rule of thumb is to use 1 1/4 cups of water for every 1 cup of rice. Also, use a medium heat and a gentle simmer to cook the rice, as high heat can cause the starches to break down and make the rice stick together. Additionally, fluffing the rice with a fork after it's cooked can help to separate the grains and prevent them from sticking together.
