Software updates are essential for keeping your devices and applications safe, fast, and reliable. 

The most critical reason to update is security. Developers regularly patch newly discovered vulnerabilities that hackers could otherwise exploit to steal data or infect your system. 

Beyond security, updates resolve software bugs that cause crashes, improve overall performance, ensure compatibility with newer technologies, and often introduce useful new features. Skipping updates leaves you vulnerable to known threats and keeps you from getting the best experience from your software.
