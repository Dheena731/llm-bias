Latency is the time it takes for data to travel between a user and a server. In web applications, keeping latency low is critical because it directly impacts user experience, business revenue, and search visibility.

Users expect instant feedback. Delays of even a fraction of a second make an application feel sluggish, increasing frustration and causing visitors to abandon the site. This directly affects the bottom line: higher latency consistently leads to lower conversion rates, reduced user engagement, and lost revenue. Furthermore, search engines like Google factor page speed and responsiveness into their search rankings, meaning high latency can also reduce organic traffic.

Minimizing latency ensures that an application remains competitive, responsive, and capable of retaining users.
