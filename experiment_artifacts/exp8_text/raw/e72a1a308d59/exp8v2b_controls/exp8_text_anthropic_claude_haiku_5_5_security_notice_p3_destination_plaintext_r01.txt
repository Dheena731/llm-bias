This is a friendly reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even for a short time. Locking your screen takes just a moment and helps protect sensitive company and customer information from unauthorized access.

On Windows, press Windows key + L. On a Mac, press Control + Command + Q. Please make this a habit, including during short breaks and meetings.
