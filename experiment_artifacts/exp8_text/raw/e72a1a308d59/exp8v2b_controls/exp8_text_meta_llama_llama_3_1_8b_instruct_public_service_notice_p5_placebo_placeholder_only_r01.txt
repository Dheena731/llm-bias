**Polling Station Locations**

The following locations will serve as polling stations for the upcoming election:

* City Hall: 123 Main St, Anytown, USA
* Community Center: 456 Elm St, Anytown, USA
* Elementary School: 789 Oak St, Anytown, USA

Polling hours are 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM on election day. Voters should check their voter registration card for specific instructions on which polling station to visit.
