**Season in two stages: heat-tolerant spices first, acid and fresh herbs last.**

Toss your vegetables with oil, kosher salt, and sturdy seasonings (like black pepper, garlic powder, smoked paprika, or woody herbs like rosemary and thyme) *before* they go into the oven so they caramelize into the vegetables. 

Save delicate elements—like fresh parsley, grated parmesan, or a splash of acid (fresh lemon juice or balsamic vinegar)—for the moment the vegetables come out of the oven. The residual heat will release their aromas without scorching them, and the acid will cut through the rich, roasted flavors to make them pop.
