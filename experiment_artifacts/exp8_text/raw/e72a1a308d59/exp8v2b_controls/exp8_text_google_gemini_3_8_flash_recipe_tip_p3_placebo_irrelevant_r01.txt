**Rinse your rice thoroughly before cooking.**

The main culprit behind gummy, overly sticky rice is excess surface starch. Place your dry rice in a bowl or a fine-mesh strainer and rinse it under cold water, gently swirling the grains with your fingers. Drain and repeat three to four times until the water runs mostly clear. 

*Bonus tip:* **Never stir the rice while it simmers.** Agitating the grains breaks them down and releases more starch, which quickly turns fluffy rice into paste. Let it cook undisturbed, rest off the heat for 10 minutes with the lid on, and then gently fluff it with a fork.
