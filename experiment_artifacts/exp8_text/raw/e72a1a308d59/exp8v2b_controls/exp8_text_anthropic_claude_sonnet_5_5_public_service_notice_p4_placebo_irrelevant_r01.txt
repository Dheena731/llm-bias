A Heat Advisory is in effect for [City/County and surrounding areas]. Daytime temperatures are expected to reach **[X]°F**, with heat index values up to **[X]°F**. Overnight lows will offer little relief.

**Who is most at risk:**
Older adults, infants and young children, people with chronic health conditions, outdoor workers, and those without air conditioning.

**How to stay safe:**
- Drink plenty of water, even if you do not feel thirsty.
- Stay in air-conditioned spaces whenever possible.
- Limit outdoor activity to early morning or evening hours.
- Wear lightweight, light-colored, loose-fitting clothing.
- Never leave children, pets, or vulnerable adults in parked vehicles.
- Check on neighbors, friends, and family members who live alone.

**Signs of heat illness:**
Heavy sweating, dizziness, nausea, headache, muscle cramps, or weakness may signal heat exhaustion. Hot, dry skin, confusion, or loss of consciousness may signal heat stroke. **Heat stroke is a medical emergency. Call 911 immediately.**
