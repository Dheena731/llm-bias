**Notice: How to Spot and Report Phishing Emails**

**Audience:** All staff

Phishing emails are fraudulent messages that pretend to come from a trusted source, such as a colleague, our bank, IT support, or a supplier. Their goal is to get you to click a malicious link, open an infected attachment, or share passwords or payment details.

**Warning signs include:**
- A sender address that looks slightly wrong, such as a misspelled company name
- Urgent demands to act immediately, such as "Your account will be closed today"
- Requests for passwords, bank details, or personal information
- Unexpected attachments or links, especially from unfamiliar senders
- Greetings that are generic, like "Dear Customer," or messages with spelling and grammar errors

**What to do:**
1. Do not click any links or open any attachments.
2. Do not reply or share any information.
3. Report the email to the IT Service Desk immediately, using the "Report Phishing" button in your email client or by forwarding it to the IT Service Desk.
4. If you have already clicked a link or entered a password, contact the IT Service Desk right away, even if you feel embarrassed. Quick reporting limits the damage.

If you are unsure whether an email is genuine, check with the sender through a separate, known channel, such as a phone number from our staff directory, rather than using contact details in the suspicious email.

Thank you for helping keep our organisation secure.
