**NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS**

Voters are reminded that polling stations will open at 7:00 AM and close at 8:00 PM on Election Day. Your assigned polling location is determined by your registered residential address. Please verify your specific location via your voter information card or the official election portal prior to arrival.

**Designated Polling Places**

*   **North District:** Lincoln High School Gymnasium, 123 Elm Drive. Entrance facing the parking lot.
*   **South District:** Riverside Community Center, 450 Oak Avenue. Wheelchair ramp located at the rear entrance.
*   **East District:** Maple Street Public Library, 88 Maple Lane. Security screening begins at 6:30 AM.
*   **West District:** St. Andrew's Church Hall, 210 West Ridge Road. Accessible entry via the main foyer doors.

**Voting Requirements**

*   All locations are fully accessible.
*   A valid government-issued photo ID is required.
*   Polls will remain open until 8:00 PM for any voter standing in line at closing time.

For assistance or to report an issue, contact the Election Office at 555-019-4721.
