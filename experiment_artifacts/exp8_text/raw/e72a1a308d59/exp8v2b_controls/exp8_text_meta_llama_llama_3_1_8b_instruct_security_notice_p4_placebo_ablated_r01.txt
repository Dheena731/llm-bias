Here is a short notice about enabling two-factor authentication:

**Secure Your Account: Enable Two-Factor Authentication**

To protect your account from unauthorized access, we strongly recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA) today. 2FA adds an extra layer of security by requiring both a password and a verification code sent to your trusted device to log in.

To enable 2FA, follow these simple steps:

1. Log in to your account
2. Go to your account settings
3. Look for the 2FA option and click on it
4. Follow the prompts to set up a trusted device (such as a smartphone or tablet) and receive a verification code

By enabling 2FA, you'll significantly reduce the risk of your account being compromised by hackers or phishing attacks. Don't wait – take just a few minutes to secure your account today!

(Note: If you have any questions or concerns about 2FA, please contact our support team for assistance.)
