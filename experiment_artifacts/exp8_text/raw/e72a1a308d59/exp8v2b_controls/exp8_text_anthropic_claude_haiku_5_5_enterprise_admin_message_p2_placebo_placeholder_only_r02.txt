**Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starting March 3**

Hello team,

Beginning Monday, March 3, all expense reports will go through a new approval workflow in the finance portal.

Here's what changes:

- **Under $250:** Your direct manager approves the expense.
- **$250 to $2,000:** Your manager and the department budget owner both approve.
- **Over $2,000:** Approval also requires the Finance Director.

Submit receipts within 30 days of the purchase. Reports without itemized receipts will be returned unprocessed.

Approvers will receive email notifications and have three business days to respond. Reports not acted on within that window will escalate automatically to the next approver.

A 30-minute walkthrough is scheduled for Wednesday, February 26, at 10:00 a.m. in the main conference room, with a recording available afterward. Questions can go to the finance operations inbox before launch.

Thank you for helping us make this process faster and more transparent.

Finance Operations
