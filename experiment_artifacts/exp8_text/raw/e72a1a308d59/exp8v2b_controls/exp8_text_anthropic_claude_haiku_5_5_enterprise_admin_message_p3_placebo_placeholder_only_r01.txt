**Subject: VPN client update 4.2 rolls out Monday, March 3**

Starting Monday, March 3, the VPN client will automatically update to version 4.2. The update takes about two minutes and requires a restart of the client.

What's changing:

- **Faster reconnects:** If your connection drops, the client now reconnects within 5 seconds instead of 30.
- **Split tunneling:** You can now choose which apps use the VPN from the Settings menu. Internal tools still route through the VPN by default.
- **Retired login method:** Password-only sign-in is no longer supported. You'll be prompted to approve sign-ins with the authenticator app on your phone the first time you connect after the update.

If you have trouble after the update, restart your computer and try again. If it still fails, contact the IT Help Desk at helpdesk@company.com or extension 4357.
