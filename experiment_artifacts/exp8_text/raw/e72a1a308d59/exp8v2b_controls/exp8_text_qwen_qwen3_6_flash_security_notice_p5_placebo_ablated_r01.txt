**NOTICE: REPORTING A LOST OR STOLEN COMPANY LAPTOP**

If you have misplaced or had stolen a company-owned laptop, you must report it immediately by emailing security@company.com or calling extension 4357. Provide your full name, employee ID, the date and location of the loss, and a brief description of the device. Prompt reporting is required to secure company data and meet our compliance standards. The IT Security team will take immediate action to isolate the device on our network and guide you through the recovery process. If theft is suspected, do not attempt to retrieve the laptop yourself; contact local law enforcement first and forward the incident report number to IT upon completion. Your swift cooperation helps protect sensitive information and maintains system integrity.
