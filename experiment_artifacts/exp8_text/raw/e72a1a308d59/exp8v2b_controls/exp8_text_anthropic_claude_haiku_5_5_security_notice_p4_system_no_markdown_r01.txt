Security Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication

To better protect your account and personal information, we strongly recommend turning on two-factor authentication (2FA). With 2FA, signing in requires both your password and a second verification step, such as a one-time code sent to your phone or generated by an authenticator app. Even if someone learns your password, they cannot access your account without that second step.

To enable two-factor authentication:

1. Log in to your account and go to Settings.
2. Select Security, then choose Two-Factor Authentication.
3. Follow the prompts to add your phone number or authenticator app.
4. Save your backup codes in a safe place.

It takes less than five minutes to set up. If you have any questions or need help, please contact our support team.

Thank you for helping us keep your account secure.
