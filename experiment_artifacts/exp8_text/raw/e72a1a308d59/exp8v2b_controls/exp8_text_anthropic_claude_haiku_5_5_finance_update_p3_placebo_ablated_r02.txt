**Introducing Savings Goals**

You can now set a savings goal directly in the app. Give your goal a name, such as "Emergency fund" or "Vacation," and enter a target amount and an optional target date.

Once a goal is set, the app shows your progress and how much you need to save each month to reach it on time. You can add money to a goal at any time, and your progress updates right away.

To get started, open the Savings tab and tap **New Goal**. You can create as many goals as you like and edit or delete them whenever you need to.
