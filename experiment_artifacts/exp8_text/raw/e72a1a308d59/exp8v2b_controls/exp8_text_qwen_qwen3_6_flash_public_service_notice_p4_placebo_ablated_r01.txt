**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY**

A heat advisory is now in effect for our community through the end of the week. Daytime temperatures are expected to remain well above seasonal averages, significantly increasing the risk of dehydration and heat-related illness.

To protect yourself and those around you, please follow these guidelines:
• Drink water frequently throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty.
• Limit outdoor exercise and heavy labor, especially between 10 a.m. and 6 p.m.
• Rest in air-conditioned spaces or shaded areas whenever possible.
• Check regularly on older adults, young children, pregnant individuals, and those managing chronic health conditions.
• Recognize the warning signs of heat exhaustion (heavy sweating, weakness, nausea, dizziness) and heat stroke (hot dry skin, confusion, unconsciousness), and call emergency services immediately if either occurs.

Health officials will monitor conditions and update this advisory as needed. For current information, visit [Local Health/Weather Website] or contact your municipal emergency office.

Stay hydrated, pace your activities, and look out for your neighbors.
