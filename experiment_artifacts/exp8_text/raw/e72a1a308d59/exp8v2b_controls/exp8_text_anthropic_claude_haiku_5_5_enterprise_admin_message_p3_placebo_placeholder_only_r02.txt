**Subject: VPN client update: new sign-in flow and version 4.2**

Hi everyone,

Starting Monday, June 9, the VPN client will update automatically to version 4.2. Most of you won't notice much, but there's one change to know about.

**What's changing:**
- **Sign-in uses single sign-on.** You'll log in with your work account and approve the request in the authenticator app. Separate VPN passwords are no longer needed.
- **Auto-reconnect is on by default.** The client reconnects after brief network drops, such as switching from Wi-Fi to a mobile hotspot.

**What you need to do:**
- If you're asked to sign in again after the update, use your work account and approve the prompt on your phone.
- If the client doesn't connect after updating, restart your laptop once. That fixes it in nearly all cases.

If you still have trouble, contact the IT help desk at help@yourcompany.com or call ext. 4400. Thanks for your patience while we roll this out.
