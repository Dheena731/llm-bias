To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it thoroughly before cooking. Rinse the rice in a fine mesh strainer under cold running water for about 30 seconds, stirring occasionally, until the water runs clear. This helps remove excess starch from the surface of the rice, resulting in a fluffier and less sticky texture after cooking. Additionally, using the right water ratio and not overmixing the rice while it's cooking can also help prevent stickiness.
