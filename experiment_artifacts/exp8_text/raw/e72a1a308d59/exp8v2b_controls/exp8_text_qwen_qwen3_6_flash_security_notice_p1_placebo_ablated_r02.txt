**SECURITY NOTICE: PASSWORD SECURITY REMINDER**

**To:** All Employees  
**From:** IT Security Team  
**Subject:** Prohibition on Password Reuse  

Please be reminded that reusing passwords across different work accounts is strictly prohibited. When the same password protects multiple systems, a single breach can grant unauthorized users immediate access to all of them. To protect company data and your own credentials, you must create a unique password for every work application and service. 

If you find it difficult to manage multiple passwords, contact IT Support to enroll in our company-approved tool for safely storing them. Compliance with this policy is mandatory and applies to all staff members effective immediately. 

Thank you for your prompt attention and continued commitment to workplace security.
