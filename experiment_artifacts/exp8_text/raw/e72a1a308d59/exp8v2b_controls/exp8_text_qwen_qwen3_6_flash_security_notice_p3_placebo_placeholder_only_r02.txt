**Subject: Reminder: Lock Your Screen When Away From Your Desk**

To all staff,

This is a reminder to lock your computer screen every time you leave your workstation, even for a few minutes. Locking your screen prevents unauthorized access to company systems and protects confidential information.

To lock your screen quickly:
• Windows: Press the Windows key + L
• Mac: Press Control + Command + Q

Your device will remain secured until you return and re-enter your credentials. Thank you for helping maintain our office security standards.

Information Technology Department
