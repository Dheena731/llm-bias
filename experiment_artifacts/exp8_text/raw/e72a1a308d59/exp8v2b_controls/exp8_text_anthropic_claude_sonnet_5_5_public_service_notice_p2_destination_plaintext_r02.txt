PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE

Main Street between Oak Avenue and Pine Road will be closed to all vehicle traffic for resurfacing and drainage repairs.

Dates: Monday, June 3 through Friday, June 7
Hours: 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily

DETOURS
Drivers should use Elm Street and Cedar Lane to bypass the closed section. Detour signs will be posted along the route.

ACCESS
Local residents and businesses within the work zone will have access to their driveways and parking areas until work begins on their section of road. Please remove vehicles from the street by 6:30 a.m. each day. Pedestrian access will be maintained on at least one side of the street.

EMERGENCY SERVICES
Emergency vehicles will have access at all times.

Please allow extra travel time and drive carefully near the work zone. The schedule may change due to weather. Updates will be posted on the city website.

For questions, contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567 or publicworks@examplecity.gov.

We appreciate your patience as we work to improve our roads.
