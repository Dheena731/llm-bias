**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication**

Dear Users,

To strengthen account security, all users must enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on their accounts by **Friday, November 15**. 

Two-factor authentication protects your login by requiring two forms of verification: something you know (your password) and something you have (a temporary code from your phone or an authenticator app). This simple step significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access.

**How to enable 2FA:**
1. Visit [Secure Portal URL] and sign in.
2. Go to **Account Settings > Security**.
3. Select **Enable Two-Factor Authentication** and follow the prompts.
4. Save your recovery codes in a safe place.

After setup, you will be asked for a verification code each time you log in. If you need help or run into technical issues, contact IT Support at [support email/phone] or visit the Help Center at [link].

Thank you for taking a few minutes to protect your account and our shared systems.
