Tip: Cook once, eat all week. On Sunday, spend 30 to 45 minutes prepping a few building blocks instead of full meals: a big batch of grain (rice, quinoa, or pasta), one or two proteins (grilled chicken, hard-boiled eggs, or roasted chickpeas), and a tray of roasted or chopped vegetables. Store them in separate containers in the fridge.

Each day, mix and match into a different lunch, such as a grain bowl on Monday, a wrap on Tuesday, and a salad on Wednesday. Add a different sauce or topping, like pesto, salsa, or a squeeze of lemon, to keep things from feeling repetitive. This saves time, cuts down on food waste, and keeps you from grabbing takeout when the week gets hectic.
