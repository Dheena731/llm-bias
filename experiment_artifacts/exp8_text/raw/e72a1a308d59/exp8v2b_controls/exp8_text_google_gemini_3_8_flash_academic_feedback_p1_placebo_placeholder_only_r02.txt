**Feedback on Draft Literature Review**

Overall, this draft demonstrates a strong grasp of the relevant field and establishes a solid foundation for the research project. The scope of reading is commendable, and the decision to organize the review thematically rather than chronologically serves the conceptual clarity of the work well.

To strengthen the review for the next revision, consider the following areas for development:

1. **Shift from Summary to Synthesis:** In several subsections, the text leans toward an annotated list of individual studies (e.g., presenting Author A’s findings, followed sequentially by Author B’s). Work on putting these scholars into direct conversation with one another. Group studies by shared methodologies, conflicting conclusions, or theoretical assumptions, and use topic sentences that highlight these broader debates rather than individual names.
2. **Deepen Methodological Critique:** While the findings of key papers are accurately reported, there is less discussion of *how* those findings were generated. Note any recurring limitations in the literature, such as over-reliance on cross-sectional designs, small sample sizes, or specific geographical contexts, and evaluate how these methodological choices constrain current consensus.
3. **Explicitly Justify the Research Gap:** The conclusion effectively summarizes the current state of knowledge, but the transition from existing scholarship to the proposed study needs to be sharper. Clearly articulate not just what remains unaddressed, but why this specific omission matters and how your project directly bridges that divide.

With tighter synthesis and a more critical evaluation of existing methodologies, this review will provide a compelling rationale for the study. I look forward to reading the next iteration.
