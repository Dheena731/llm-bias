**Subject: Update on Delayed Payment Processing**

We're aware that some payments are taking longer than usual to process. Our team identified a slowdown in our payment pipeline and is working to restore normal processing speeds.

**What this means for you:**
- Payments submitted since the slowdown began may show as pending longer than usual.
- No payments have been lost, and no duplicate charges have been created.
- You do not need to resubmit any transactions.

**What we're doing:**
- Engineers are rolling out a fix and monitoring the payment queue.
- Queued payments are being processed in order as capacity recovers.

We'll post another update once processing is back to normal. If a payment hasn't cleared after that, contact support and include the order reference from your confirmation so we can trace it.
