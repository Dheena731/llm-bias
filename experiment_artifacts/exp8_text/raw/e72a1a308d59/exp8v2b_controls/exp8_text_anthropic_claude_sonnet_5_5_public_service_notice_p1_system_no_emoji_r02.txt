**PUBLIC NOTICE: Library Opening Hours**

Effective Monday, the Central Public Library will operate on the following schedule:

**Monday to Thursday:** 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
**Friday:** 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
**Saturday:** 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
**Sunday:** 12:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m.

**Please note:**

- The library will be closed on all public holidays.
- Book returns may be made at any time using the outdoor drop box.
- Computer and Wi-Fi services end 15 minutes before closing.
- Children under 10 must be accompanied by an adult.

Hours may change during holiday periods. Updates will be posted at the front entrance and on the library website.

For questions, please contact the front desk at the number listed on the library website or visit in person.

Thank you for supporting your local library.
