Software updates matter because they keep your devices and programs secure, stable, and useful. Developers regularly release patches that fix security vulnerabilities, which are weaknesses that hackers can exploit to steal data, install malware, or take control of systems. Skipping updates leaves these known holes open, making you an easier target.

Updates also fix bugs and crashes, improve performance, and ensure compatibility with other software, hardware, and online services. Over time, outdated software can stop working properly as the technology around it changes.

In addition, updates often add new features and improvements that make tools more efficient and easier to use. Keeping software current is one of the simplest and most effective ways to protect your information and get the most out of your technology.
