**PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE: LIBRARY OPERATING HOURS**

The public library will maintain the following weekly schedule effective immediately. Monday through Thursday: 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. Friday and Saturday: 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Sunday: 12 p.m. to 4 p.m. Please plan visits accordingly, as doors lock precisely at closing time and staff require ten minutes to secure materials and systems. The facility observes federal holidays and may modify hours during severe weather or emergency conditions. Confirmed schedules and real-time alerts are published monthly on the municipal services website and posted at each entrance. Thank you for using and protecting this essential community resource.
