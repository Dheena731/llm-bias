**NOTICE: Changes to Recycling Collection Schedule**

Beginning Monday, March 3, recycling collection will move from every Tuesday to every other Thursday for all residential addresses.

**What you need to know:**

- **New collection day:** Thursday, starting March 5
- **Frequency:** Every other week (see the enclosed calendar for specific dates)
- **Bin requirements:** Please keep recyclables loose in your blue bin. Do not bag them.
- **Accepted items:** Clean paper, cardboard, glass, metal cans, and plastic bottles and containers marked #1 or #2

Bins should be placed at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on collection day. Bins left out after collection will not be emptied until the next scheduled pickup.

For questions or to request a replacement bin, contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567 or visit www.cityexample.gov/recycling.

Thank you for helping us keep our community clean and sustainable.
