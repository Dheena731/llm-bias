**OFFICIAL NOTICE REGARDING REFUND ELIGIBILITY**

This notice establishes binding criteria governing monetary reimbursement for purchased goods and services. Eligibility for a full refund requires that the item remain unaltered, unused, and returned within fourteen calendar days of the original transaction date. Claimants must submit verifiable proof of purchase alongside the original packaging. Items exhibiting damage attributable to recipient mishandling will be assessed for partial reimbursement at the sole discretion of the issuing authority.

Nonrefundable categories include all digital downloads, personally customized merchandise, clearance inventory marked as final, and consumable goods that have been opened or consumed. Requests submitted outside the prescribed window, lacking sufficient documentation, or attempting to circumvent standard return protocols will be formally denied. Approved refunds shall be disbursed to the original funding source within ten business days of verification. Continued
