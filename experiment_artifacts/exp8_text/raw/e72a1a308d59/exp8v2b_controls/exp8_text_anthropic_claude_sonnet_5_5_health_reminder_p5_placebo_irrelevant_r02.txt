**Reminder: Refill Your Prescription**

Your prescription is running low. Please contact your pharmacy or doctor's office to request a refill in the next few days so you don't run out.

**Before you call, have ready:**
- Your prescription number (on the bottle label)
- Your date of birth
- Your pharmacy's phone number

Many pharmacies also offer refills through their website or app, which is often the fastest way. If you have no refills remaining, your pharmacy can usually contact your doctor for you, but this can take a day or two, so it's best not to wait until the last pill.

Never skip doses or stretch your medication to make it last longer without talking to your doctor or pharmacist first.
