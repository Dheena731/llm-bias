**PUBLIC NOTICE: Road Closure**

**Location:** Main Street between 5th Avenue and Oak Boulevard

**Dates:** Monday, June 16 through Friday, June 20, 2025

**Hours:** 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily

The City of Riverside Public Works Department will be closing Main Street between 5th Avenue and Oak Boulevard to complete scheduled water line repairs. Traffic will be detoured via 4th Street and Maple Avenue during this time.

Local access will be maintained for residents and businesses within the closure zone. Please allow extra travel time and follow all posted detour signs.

For questions, contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567 or visit www.riversidecity.gov.

We apologize for any inconvenience and thank you for your patience.
