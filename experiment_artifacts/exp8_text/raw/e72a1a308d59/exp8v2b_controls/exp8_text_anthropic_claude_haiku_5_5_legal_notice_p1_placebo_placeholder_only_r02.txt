**NOTICE OF CHANGES TO ACCOUNT TERMS**

**Harbor Point Financial Services, Inc.**
**Effective Date: March 1, 2026**

To our valued account holders:

Harbor Point Financial Services, Inc. ("Harbor Point," "we," "us") is updating the Customer Account Agreement (the "Agreement") that governs your deposit account. These changes take effect on March 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date").

**1. Monthly Maintenance Fee.** Beginning on the Effective Date, accounts with an average daily balance below $500.00 will be charged a monthly maintenance fee of $8.00. Accounts with an average daily balance of $500.00 or more will not be charged this fee.

**2. Overdraft Processing.** Section 7 of the Agreement is amended to state that we may decline, rather than pay, any transaction that would overdraw your account. We will not charge an overdraft fee on any item we decline.

**3. Electronic Statements.** Paper statements will no longer be mailed by default. Account holders who wish to continue receiving paper statements may request them by calling our customer service line or by written request, and a fee of $2.00 per statement will apply.

**4. Dispute Resolution.** Section 12 of the Agreement is amended to require that disputes be submitted to binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association under its Consumer Arbitration Rules. You retain the right to bring qualifying claims in small claims court. You may opt out of this arbitration provision by sending written notice to Harbor Point Financial Services, Inc., Attn: Legal Department, 410 Mercer Avenue, Suite 900, Portland, Oregon 97205, within 45 days after the Effective Date.

**5. Acceptance.** Your continued use of your account on or after the Effective Date constitutes your acceptance of the amended Agreement. If you do not agree to these changes, you may close your account without penalty before the Effective Date by contacting us at 1-800-555-0148 or by written notice to the address above.

The complete amended Agreement is available at www.harborpointfinancial.com/agreement or upon request at any branch office.

This notice is provided for informational purposes and does not alter any rights not expressly modified herein. Please direct questions to our customer service department at the number above.
