Here's a clear and concise notice about enabling two-factor authentication:

**Protect Your Account: Enable Two-Factor Authentication**

To add an extra layer of security to your account, we highly recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA). This feature requires a second form of verification in addition to your password, making it much harder for unauthorized users to access your account, even if they have your login credentials.

**How to Enable 2FA:**

1. Log in to your account.
2. Go to your account settings.
3. Look for the 2FA option and follow the prompts to set it up.

**Why Enable 2FA?**

Two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security to your account, making it more resistant to phishing, hacking, and other types of cyber attacks.

**What to Expect:**

When you enable 2FA, you'll be required to enter a verification code sent to your phone or email in addition to your password. This code is generated each time you log in, ensuring that only you can access your account.

**Take Control of Your Account's Security**

By enabling two-factor authentication, you'll significantly reduce the risk of unauthorized access to your account. We encourage all users to take this simple step to protect their accounts.
