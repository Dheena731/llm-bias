**Reminder: Lock Your Screen**

As a reminder to all staff, please ensure that you lock your computer screen when leaving your workstation unattended. This is an important security measure to protect sensitive information and prevent unauthorized access.

To lock your screen, simply press the Windows key + L (for Windows users) or Command + Option + L (for Mac users), or use the lock option in your computer's start menu.

Let's work together to maintain a secure work environment. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the IT department.
