Here is a short tip for planning lunches for a busy week:

**Tip:** Take 10 minutes on the weekend to plan your lunches for the upcoming week. 

**Why it works:** This simple habit can save you time, money, and stress during the week. By planning ahead, you can avoid last-minute takeout or fast food runs, which can be expensive and unhealthy. You'll also avoid feeling overwhelmed or hungry when you're in a rush.

**How to do it:** 

1. Think about your schedule for the week and what activities you have planned. Consider any dietary restrictions or preferences you have.
2. Decide on a few simple, healthy options that can be easily packed in a lunch container. Some ideas might include:
	* Leftovers from last night's dinner
	* A sandwich or wrap
	* A salad or soup
	* Fresh fruit or cut veggies with hummus
3. Make a list of these options and consider any ingredients you may need to buy or prep in advance.
4. Take a few minutes each day to pack your lunch in the morning, or prep it the night before if you prefer.

**The result:** With a little planning, you'll have healthy, affordable, and satisfying lunches all week long!
