PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE

Maplewood Public Library: Updated Opening Hours

Effective Monday, June 3, the Maplewood Public Library will operate under the following hours:

Monday to Thursday: 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
Friday: 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
Saturday: 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
Sunday: Closed

Holiday closures: The library will be closed on all public holidays. Hours may also be shortened on the days before major holidays. Please check the library website or notice board for details.

Services available during all open hours include book lending and returns, public computers and Wi-Fi, printing and photocopying, and assistance from library staff. Book returns may be made at any time using the outdoor drop box beside the main entrance.

Please note that study rooms may be reserved up to two weeks in advance by phone, online, or at the front desk.

For questions or more information, contact the library at 555-0142 or visit www.maplewoodlibrary.example.

Thank you for supporting your local library.
