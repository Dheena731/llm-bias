SECURITY NOTICE: Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

To enhance the security of your account and protect your personal information, we strongly encourage all users to enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA). 

2FA adds an extra layer of defense against unauthorized access by requiring a unique verification code in addition to your password whenever you log in.

How to set it up:
1. Log in to your account and go to Settings.
2. Select the Security tab.
3. Turn on Two-Factor Authentication and follow the on-screen instructions.

Please enable this feature as soon as possible to ensure your account remains safe and secure. If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact our support team.
