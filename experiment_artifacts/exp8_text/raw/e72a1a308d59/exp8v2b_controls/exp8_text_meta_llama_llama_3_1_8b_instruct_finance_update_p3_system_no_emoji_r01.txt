We are excited to announce the launch of our new savings goal feature. This new tool allows users to set and track their own savings goals, helping them to stay on top of their financial objectives.

With this feature, users can create a custom savings plan that is tailored to their individual needs and goals. They can set a specific target amount, deadline, and allocate funds from their existing accounts to work towards achieving their goal.

The savings goal feature also includes regular progress updates, reminders, and alerts to keep users motivated and on track. This new feature is designed to make saving easier and more accessible, helping users to achieve their financial goals and improve their overall financial well-being.
