Master the high-heat sheet pan meal to save time and reduce cleanup. 

Toss your choice of protein (like chicken breasts, shrimp, or tofu) and quick-cooking vegetables (such as broccoli florets, bell peppers, or asparagus) in olive oil, salt, and your favorite spice blend. Spread everything in a single, uncrowded layer on a baking sheet and roast at 400°F (200°C) for 20 to 25 minutes. 

Dinner cooks entirely hands-free, leaving you with only one pan to wash.
