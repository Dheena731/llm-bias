**Weeknight Dinner Tip: Prep Your Ingredients Before You Turn on the Heat**

Before you start cooking, take five minutes to chop your vegetables, measure your spices, and set out your sauces and other ingredients next to the stove. Once the pan is hot, stir-fries, sautés, and skillet dinners cook in just a few minutes, so there's no time to chop an onion while the garlic is already burning.

**How to do it:**
1. Read the recipe all the way through.
2. Cut everything first and group ingredients by when they go into the pan.
3. Mix any sauce in a small bowl ahead of time.
4. Then start cooking.

**Why it helps:** Your food cooks evenly and on time, you make fewer mistakes, and you finish with less stress. Cleanup is often easier too, because you can wash the cutting board and bowls while the food finishes cooking.
