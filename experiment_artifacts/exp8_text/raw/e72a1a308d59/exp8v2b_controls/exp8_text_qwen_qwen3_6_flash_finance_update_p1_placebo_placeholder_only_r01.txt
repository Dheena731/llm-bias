We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to simplify your financial tracking. The report highlights recurring payments, identifies spending trends, and compares your current month against previous periods. You will receive the finalized summary directly in your registered email address on the first day of each month. This feature eliminates manual bookkeeping by delivering consistent, accurate insights that support smarter budgeting decisions.
