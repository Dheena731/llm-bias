**Security Reminder: Please Lock Your Screen**

This is a reminder to always lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if it is only for a minute. Leaving an unattended workstation unlocked poses a significant risk to confidential company information and client data.

**Quick shortcuts to lock your device:**
*   **Windows:** Press **Windows Key + L**
*   **Mac:** Press **Control + Command + Q**

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our workplace secure.

— IT Security Team
