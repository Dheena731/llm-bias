**Introducing the Clear Budget Template**

Managing money should not require a finance degree. Today we are releasing the Clear Budget Template, a spreadsheet designed to help you understand where your money goes and plan where it should go next.

**What is included**

- **Monthly overview:** See income, expenses, and savings on a single dashboard.
- **Automatic categorization:** Enter a transaction and the template sorts it into the right category, with totals updating instantly.
- **Savings goal tracker:** Set targets for an emergency fund, a vacation, or a large purchase, and watch your progress over time.
- **Annual summary:** Review trends across twelve months to spot patterns and adjust your habits.
- **Debt payoff planner:** Compare repayment strategies and see how extra payments shorten your timeline.

**Why you will like it**

The template works with Excel, Google Sheets, and Numbers, so you can use the tools you already have. There are no subscriptions, no linked accounts, and no data leaving your device. Formulas are fully unlocked, so you can customize categories, colors, and layouts to fit your life.

**Getting started**

Download the template, enter your income and fixed expenses, and you will have a working budget in about fifteen minutes. A short guide is included to walk you through each tab.

The Clear Budget Template is available now for $12, with free updates for the first year.
