NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE

The road between Oak Street and Maple Avenue will be closed to all traffic beginning Monday, March 6th at 8:00 AM and will remain closed until further notice. The closure is necessary to allow for repairs to the road surface and is expected to last for several weeks.

Motorists are advised to use alternative routes, such as Elm Street or Pine Street, to avoid the area. Local residents and businesses will have access to the area, but may experience delays.

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your patience and cooperation during this time. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Department of Public Works at 555-1234.
