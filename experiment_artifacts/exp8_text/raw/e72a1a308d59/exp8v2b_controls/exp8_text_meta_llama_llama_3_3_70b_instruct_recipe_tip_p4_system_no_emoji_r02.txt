To plan lunches for a busy week, consider setting aside one day to prepare and pack lunches for the next few days. Choose a few versatile ingredients that can be used in multiple meals, such as grilled chicken, brown rice, and roasted vegetables. Then, assemble individual lunches using these ingredients, such as salads, wraps, or bowls. This approach can help save time during the week and ensure that you have healthy, delicious lunches ready to go.
