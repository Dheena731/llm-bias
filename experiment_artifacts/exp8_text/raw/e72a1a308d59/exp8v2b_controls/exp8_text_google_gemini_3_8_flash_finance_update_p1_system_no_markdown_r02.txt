We are excited to introduce your new Monthly Spending Summary, designed to give you a clearer picture of your financial habits. 

Starting this month, you will receive an automated overview at the beginning of every month detailing where your money went. The summary highlights your top spending categories, compares your habits to previous months, and helps you see how closely you are sticking to your budget goals. 

You can view this breakdown directly in your email or explore the interactive version anytime in the app under the Insights tab. If you wish to adjust how often you receive these updates, you can manage your preferences in your account settings. 

Check out your first summary today to start making more informed decisions with your money.
