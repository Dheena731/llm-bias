ATTENTION ALL STAFF: Protect Yourself Against Phishing Emails

Phishing emails are designed to trick you into revealing passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information. They often look like they come from a colleague, a bank, IT support, or a well-known company.

Watch for these warning signs:
- Urgent or threatening language, such as "Your account will be closed today"
- Requests for passwords, login details, or payment information
- Sender addresses that look slightly wrong, such as "support@micros0ft.com"
- Unexpected attachments or links
- Greetings that are generic, like "Dear user"
- Requests to bypass normal procedures, especially for payments or gift cards

What to do:
- Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails
- Verify any unusual request by contacting the sender through a known phone number or in person
- Report suspicious emails to the IT Service Desk immediately, using the Report Phishing button or by forwarding the email to security@company.com
- Do not delete the email before reporting it

If you think you have already clicked a link or entered your password, contact IT right away. Quick reporting limits damage to you and the organization.
