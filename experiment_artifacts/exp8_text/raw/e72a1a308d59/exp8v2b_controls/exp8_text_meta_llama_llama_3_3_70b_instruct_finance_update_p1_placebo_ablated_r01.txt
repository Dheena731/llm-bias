Here is a short update explaining a new monthly spending summary:

Starting this month, we will be providing a new monthly spending summary to help you keep track of your expenses. This summary will be sent to you via email on the 1st of every month and will outline your total spending for the previous month, broken down into categories such as rent, utilities, groceries, and entertainment. The summary will also include any transactions that were flagged as unusual or outside of your typical spending habits, so you can review and verify them. This new feature is designed to help you stay on top of your finances and make informed decisions about your spending. If you have any questions or would like to opt-out of receiving the summary, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
