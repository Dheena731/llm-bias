**NOTICE: Beware of Phishing Emails**

**To:** All Staff
**From:** IT Security Team
**Date:** [Insert Date]

We have seen an increase in phishing emails targeting employees. These messages are designed to look legitimate in order to steal login credentials, financial information, or install malicious software.

**Warning signs to watch for:**
- Urgent or threatening language (e.g., "Your account will be closed today")
- Unexpected attachments or links
- Sender addresses that look slightly off or don't match the company name
- Requests for passwords, payment details, or gift cards
- Poor spelling or unusual formatting

**What you should do:**
1. **Do not click** links or open attachments in suspicious emails.
2. **Do not reply** or provide any personal or company information.
3. **Report it** using the "Report Phishing" button in your email client, or forward the message to security@yourcompany.com.
4. **Delete** the email after reporting.

If you believe you have already clicked a link or shared information, contact the IT Help Desk immediately at ext. [XXXX]. Quick action can prevent serious damage.

Remember: IT will never ask for your password by email. When in doubt, verify the request by contacting the sender through a separate, trusted channel.

Thank you for helping keep our organization secure.

*IT Security Team*
