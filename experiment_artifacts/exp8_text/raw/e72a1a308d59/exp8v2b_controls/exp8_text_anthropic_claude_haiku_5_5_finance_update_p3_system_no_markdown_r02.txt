We're excited to announce Savings Goals, a new feature that helps you set aside money for what matters most. Whether you're saving for a vacation, an emergency fund, or a new laptop, you can now create a goal, choose a target amount and deadline, and track your progress in one place.

Each goal shows how much you've saved, how much is left, and whether you're on pace to reach it by your target date. You can move money into a goal from your checking account at any time, and you can set up automatic transfers so saving happens without extra effort.

Savings Goals is available today in the app under the Savings tab. Tap "New Goal" to get started. We'd love to hear what you think, so send us your feedback through the Help menu.
