**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY**

**Harbor Lane Outfitters, LLC**
Effective March 1, 2025

**1. Scope.** This notice governs requests for refunds on all merchandise and digital downloads purchased from Harbor Lane Outfitters, LLC through harborlaneoutfitters.com or at our retail location at 214 Mill Street, Dayton, Ohio.

**2. Eligibility for Refund.** A customer is eligible for a full refund of the purchase price, excluding shipping charges, if:

(a) the merchandise is returned within thirty (30) days of the delivery date shown on the carrier's tracking record;

(b) the merchandise is unworn, unwashed, and retains all original tags and packaging; and

(c) the return is accompanied by the original order confirmation or receipt.

**3. Exceptions.** The following are not eligible for refund:

(a) items marked "Final Sale" at the time of purchase;

(b) digital downloads once the file has been accessed or downloaded;

(c) gift cards and store credit; and

(d) items returned more than thirty (30) days after delivery.

**4. Defective or Incorrect Items.** If an item arrived damaged, defective, or differs materially from the description provided at checkout, the customer may request a full refund, including original shipping charges, by notifying us within ten (10) days of delivery at returns@harborlaneoutfitters.com. Photographs of the item and its packaging must accompany the request.

**5. Processing.** Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method within seven (7) business days after we receive and inspect the returned merchandise. Your financial institution may require additional time before the credit appears on your account.

**6. Return Shipping.** Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the return results from our error or a defect described in Section 4.

**7. Disputes.** A customer who disagrees with a refund determination may submit a written appeal within fourteen (14) days of the determination to Harbor Lane Outfitters, LLC, Attn: Returns Review, 214 Mill Street, Dayton, Ohio 45402. Appeals are reviewed within ten (10) business days, and the decision is final.

**8. Changes.** Harbor Lane Outfitters, LLC may amend this notice at any time. The version in effect on the date of purchase governs that order.

Questions regarding this notice may be directed to support@harborlaneoutfitters.com or (937) 555-0148.
