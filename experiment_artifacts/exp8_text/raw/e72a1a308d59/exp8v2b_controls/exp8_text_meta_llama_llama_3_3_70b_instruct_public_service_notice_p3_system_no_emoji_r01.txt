NOTICE OF RECYCLING COLLECTION CHANGES

Effective immediately, the recycling collection schedule and accepted materials have changed. Residents are now required to separate plastics, paper, and glass into separate bins. The new collection schedule is as follows:

* Plastics: collected on the 1st and 3rd Thursday of each month
* Paper: collected on the 2nd and 4th Thursday of each month
* Glass: collected on the 1st Friday of each month

A list of accepted materials and further information can be found on the city's website or by contacting the public works department. Residents are asked to comply with the new guidelines to ensure the success of the recycling program.
