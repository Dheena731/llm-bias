**Important Notice: Library Hours**

The local public library is open to serve you at the following times:

* Monday to Thursday: 9:00 AM - 8:00 PM
* Friday: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
* Saturday: 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM
* Sunday: 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM

Please note that the library is closed on statutory holidays. We invite you to visit us during our regular hours to browse our collection, attend programs and events, and access our computers and other resources. We look forward to seeing you at the library!
