Introducing Savings Goals

We're excited to announce a new Savings Goals feature that helps you plan and track the money you're setting aside for what matters most.

With Savings Goals, you can:

Create a goal with a name, a target amount, and an optional deadline, such as an emergency fund, a vacation, or a new laptop.
See your progress at a glance, including how much you've saved and how much is still left to reach your target.
Add funds to a goal whenever you like, and adjust the target or deadline as your plans change.
Get a clear picture of what you need to save each week or month to hit your goal on time.

To get started, open the Goals section from your dashboard and tap "New Goal." It takes just a minute to set up, and you can create as many goals as you need.

We hope Savings Goals makes it easier to turn your financial plans into progress. As always, we welcome your feedback, so let us know what you think.
