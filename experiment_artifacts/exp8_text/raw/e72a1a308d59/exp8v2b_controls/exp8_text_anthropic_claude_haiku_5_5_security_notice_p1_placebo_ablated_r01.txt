**Subject: Stop Reusing Passwords**

To all employees,

Do not use the same password for more than one account. This applies to work systems, personal email, online shopping, and any other service. When a website is breached, attackers try the stolen username and password on other sites, including work accounts. Password reuse is one of the most common ways company accounts are compromised.

To protect yourself and the company:

- Use a unique password for every work account.
- Never use a work password on a personal account.
- Create and store passwords with the company-approved password manager.
- Turn on two-step verification wherever it is offered. This requires a code from your phone or app in addition to your password when you sign in.

If you think a password you use at work has been exposed on another site, change it immediately and contact the IT Service Desk at [phone number or email].

Thank you for helping keep our systems and data secure.

IT Security Team
