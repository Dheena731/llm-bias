Subject: Reporting a Lost Company Laptop

All employees are reminded that any lost or stolen company laptop must be reported to the IT Department and your line manager immediately, and no later than 24 hours after you become aware of the loss.

To report a lost laptop, please:

1. Contact the IT Service Desk at extension 4357 or email itsupport@company.com.
2. Provide your name, employee ID, the laptop's asset tag number, and the date, time, and location where it was last seen.
3. Notify your line manager.
4. If the laptop was stolen, file a police report and send a copy to the Security Team.

IT will remotely lock and wipe the device where possible and will advise on next steps. Do not attempt to recover or access the device yourself.

Failure to report a lost device promptly may put company and customer data at risk and could result in disciplinary action.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping company information secure.

Information Security Team
