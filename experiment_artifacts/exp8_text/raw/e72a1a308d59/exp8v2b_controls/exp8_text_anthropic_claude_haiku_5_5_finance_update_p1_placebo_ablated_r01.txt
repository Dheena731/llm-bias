**Subject: Introducing your new monthly spending summary**

Starting this month, you'll receive a spending summary at the beginning of each month. It shows how much you spent in the previous month and how that spending was split across categories such as groceries, transportation, dining, and bills.

**What you'll see:**
- Your total spending for the month
- A breakdown of your spending by category
- A comparison with the previous month, so you can see what changed

**Where to find it:** Open the app and tap "Monthly Summary" on the home screen. Past summaries stay available there.

**Turning it off:** If you don't want the summary sent to you, go to Settings, then Notifications, and turn off "Monthly Summary." You can still view summaries in the app.

We'd like your feedback. If a category looks wrong or you have ideas for what to add, send us a message through the Help section.

Thank you for using the app.
