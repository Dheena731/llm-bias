PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE

Maple Avenue between 4th Street and 9th Street will be closed to all through traffic from Monday, June 10 to Friday, June 14, 2024, between 7:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. each day.

Reason for closure: The City Public Works Department will be resurfacing the roadway and repairing damaged storm drains.

What to expect:
- Local residents and businesses will have access to their properties from the nearest cross street, though short delays are possible.
- Street parking will not be permitted along the affected section during work hours. Vehicles left on the street may be towed.
- Sidewalks will remain open, but pedestrians should use caution near work crews and equipment.
- Emergency vehicles will have access at all times.

Detour route: Drivers should follow posted signs using Cedar Road to 10th Street and back to Maple Avenue. Please allow extra travel time.

Work is weather dependent. If rain delays the project, closure dates may be extended by up to two days. Updates will be posted on the City website and social media channels.

We apologize for the inconvenience and thank you for your patience as we work to improve local roads.

For questions, please contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567 or publicworks@examplecity.gov.

Issued by: City Public Works Department
Date: June 3, 2024
