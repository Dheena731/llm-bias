NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE

The public is advised that Main Street will be closed to all traffic from Monday, March 13th to Friday, March 17th, due to construction and maintenance work. The closure will be in effect from 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM each day.

Motorists are advised to use alternative routes, such as Oak Street or Elm Street, to avoid the area. Local access will be maintained for residents and emergency services.

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your cooperation during this time. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Department of Public Works at 555-1234.
