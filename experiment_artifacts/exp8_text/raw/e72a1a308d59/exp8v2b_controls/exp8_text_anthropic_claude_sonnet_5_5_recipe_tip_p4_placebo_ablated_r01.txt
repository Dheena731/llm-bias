**Tip: Cook once, assemble daily.**

On a day off or a quiet evening, prepare a few basic components instead of full meals: a grain (rice, quinoa, or pasta), a protein (grilled chicken, hard-boiled eggs, or beans), and a batch of chopped or roasted vegetables. Store each in its own container in the fridge.

Each morning, mix and match them into a different lunch, such as a grain bowl, a wrap, or a salad. Add a quick sauce or dressing to change the flavor, so you don't eat the same thing every day.

Why it works: you do the time-consuming cooking only once, and the daily assembly takes about five minutes. Because the components are stored separately, they also stay fresher and less soggy than fully made meals.

**Food safety note:** Eat refrigerated cooked food within 3 to 4 days. If your week is longer than that, freeze part of the batch and thaw it overnight in the fridge.
