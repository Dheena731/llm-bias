Rinse your rice before cooking. Place it in a fine-mesh strainer and run cold water over it, swishing the grains with your fingers until the water runs mostly clear. This washes away the surface starch that causes clumping. Drain well, then cook as usual. For fluffier, more separate grains, use slightly less water than the package suggests and let the rice rest, covered, for 10 minutes after it finishes cooking.
