Welcome to Words Between Us, a new podcast for anyone who has ever wanted to speak a second language and felt stuck at the starting line.

Each week, we'll explore one language in a relaxed, conversational way. Our host will sit down with native speakers, learners, and teachers to talk about how the language works, why it sounds the way it does, and the funny mistakes everyone makes along the way. You'll pick up useful phrases, practice listening, and hear real stories from people who learned the hard way.

No prior experience is needed. Episodes are about twenty-five minutes long, so you can listen on your commute, during a walk, or while doing the dishes. Every episode ends with a short challenge, like ordering coffee in a new language or describing your morning out loud, so you can practice right away.

Our first season covers Spanish, Japanese, Swahili, and Portuguese. Future seasons will be shaped by your suggestions, so tell us which language you'd like to hear next.

New episodes arrive every Tuesday, starting this week. Subscribe, share with a friend who's been meaning to start learning, and come say hello. We're glad you're here.
