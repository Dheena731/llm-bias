PUBLIC NOTICE

ROAD CLOSURE

Main Street will be closed to all vehicle traffic between Oak Avenue and Maple Drive from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on Saturday, June 14, to allow for repaving work.

Detour routes will be posted. Drivers are encouraged to use Elm Street or Pine Road as alternate routes. Local access to businesses and residences along the closed section will be maintained where safe, but motorists should expect delays.

Pedestrians may continue to use the sidewalks, which will remain open throughout the work.

For more information, please contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567 or visit www.cityexample.gov.

We thank residents and visitors for their patience while this improvement is completed.
