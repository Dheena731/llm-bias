To keep fresh leafy herbs like parsley and cilantro crisp for weeks, trim the bottom of the stems and place them upright in a glass with an inch of water, just like a bouquet of flowers. Cover the leaves loosely with a plastic bag and store the jar in your refrigerator. 

*(Note: Basil thrives with this same method, but should be kept on the counter at room temperature instead, as cold air turns its leaves black.)*
