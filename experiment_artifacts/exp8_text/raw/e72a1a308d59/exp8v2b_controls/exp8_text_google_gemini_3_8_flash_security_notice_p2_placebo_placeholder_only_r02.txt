**CYBERSECURITY NOTICE: STAY ALERT FOR PHISHING ATTEMPTS**

The Information Security Team reminds all staff to remain vigilant when reviewing incoming emails. Phishing attacks continue to grow in sophistication, often disguised as routine messages from trusted vendors, internal departments, or company leadership.

**Key Warning Signs of Phishing:**

*   **Artificial Urgency:** Demands for immediate action, warnings of account suspension, or threats of negative consequences.
*   **Suspicious Sender Addresses:** Display names that look familiar, but with an unfamiliar, misspelled, or external email domain.
*   **Requests for Sensitive Information:** Inquiries asking for login credentials, employee personal data, wire transfers, or gift card purchases.
*   **Unexpected Attachments or Links:** Links pointing to unknown websites or attachments with unexpected file extensions.

**What to Do:**

1.  **Do not click** links or open attachments in any message that seems unusual.
2.  **Do not reply** to the sender or attempt to verify the request using contact information provided within the suspicious email.
3.  **Report the email** immediately using the "Report Phishing" button in your email client or forward it as an attachment to the IT Help Desk.

When in doubt, stop and verify. Thank you for doing your part to keep our workplace network secure.

— IT Security Team
