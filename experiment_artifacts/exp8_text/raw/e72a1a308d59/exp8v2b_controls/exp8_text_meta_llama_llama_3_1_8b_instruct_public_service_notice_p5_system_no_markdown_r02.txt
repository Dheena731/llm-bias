**Important Voting Information**

Polling Station Locations:

To ensure that all eligible voters can cast their ballots in the upcoming election, the following polling stations have been established:

* Polling Station 1: City Hall, 123 Main St
* Polling Station 2: Community Center, 456 Elm St
* Polling Station 3: Public Library, 789 Oak St
* Polling Station 4: High School, 1011 Maple St

These polling stations will be open on election day from 7:00 am to 8:00 pm. Voters must present valid photo identification and proof of residency to cast their ballots.

For more information, please contact the Election Office at (555) 123-4567 or visit our website at [www.electionoffice.org](http://www.electionoffice.org).
