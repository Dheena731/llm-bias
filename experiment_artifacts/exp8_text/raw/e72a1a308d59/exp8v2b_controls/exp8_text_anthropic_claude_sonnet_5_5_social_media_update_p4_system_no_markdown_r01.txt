Calendar tip of the day: stop using your calendar only for meetings. Block out time for your own tasks too! When I started scheduling 30 minutes for things like answering emails, meal prep, and my weekly planning session, my to-do list stopped following me around all day. If it has a time slot, it actually gets done. Bonus: color-code your blocks (work, personal, health) so you can see at a glance if your week is out of balance. Try it for one week and see how it feels. 📅✨ #ProductivityTips #TimeManagement
