Introducing Savings Goals

We're excited to announce a new way to save for the things that matter most. With Savings Goals, you can set a target amount, choose a deadline, and watch your progress grow over time.

Getting started takes less than a minute. Name your goal, whether it's a vacation, an emergency fund, or a new laptop, and decide how much you want to save. You can make one-time deposits or set up automatic transfers that fit your budget. As you save, a progress tracker shows how close you are and whether you're on pace to finish by your deadline.

You can create multiple goals at once and adjust the amounts or dates whenever your plans change. We'll also send gentle reminders and celebrate milestones with you along the way.

Savings Goals is rolling out to all users this week. Open the app and tap the Goals tab to set up your first one. We'd love to hear your feedback as you try it out.
