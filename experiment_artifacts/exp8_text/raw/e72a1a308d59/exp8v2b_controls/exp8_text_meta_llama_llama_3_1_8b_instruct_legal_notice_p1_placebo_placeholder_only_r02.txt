NOTICE OF CHANGES TO ACCOUNT TERMS

Pursuant to our authority, we hereby notify you of amendments to the terms and conditions governing your account. Effective immediately, the following modifications take effect:

1. Minimum Balance Requirement: The minimum balance requirement for the account has been increased from $100 to $500. Any balance below this threshold will incur a monthly maintenance fee of $10.

2. Overdraft Policy: The bank's overdraft policy has been revised. Overdrafts will no longer be automatically cleared, but rather will be subject to the bank's discretion. The bank may choose to honor overdrafts or decline them, at its sole discretion.

3. Late Payment Fees: The fee for late payment of any balance due has been increased from $25 to $50. This fee will be assessed on all accounts, regardless of the amount due.

4. Electronic Banking: The bank's electronic banking system will no longer be available for accounts with a balance below $1,000. Customers with lower balances will need to use alternative methods to access their accounts.

These changes apply to all existing and new accounts and are effective immediately. We encourage you to review the updated account terms and conditions, which are available at our branch offices and on our website.

By continuing to use your account, you acknowledge that you have read and understood these changes. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
