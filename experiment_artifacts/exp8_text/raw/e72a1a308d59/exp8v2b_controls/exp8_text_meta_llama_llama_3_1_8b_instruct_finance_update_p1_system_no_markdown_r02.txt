Here's a short update on the new monthly spending summary:

**Introducing Our New Monthly Spending Summary**

We're excited to announce the launch of a new monthly spending summary, designed to provide you with a clear and concise overview of your monthly expenditures. This summary will be available at the beginning of each month, and will include a detailed breakdown of all your spending for the previous month.

The new summary will include:

* A total of all your expenses for the month
* A breakdown of expenses by category (e.g. housing, transportation, food, entertainment)
* A comparison to your budgeted amounts for each category
* A link to view and download a detailed report of all your transactions for the month

This new summary is designed to help you stay on top of your finances and make informed decisions about how to manage your money. If you have any questions or need help accessing your summary, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
