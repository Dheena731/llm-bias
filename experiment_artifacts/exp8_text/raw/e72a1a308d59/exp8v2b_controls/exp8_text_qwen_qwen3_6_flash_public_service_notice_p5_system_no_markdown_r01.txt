NOTICE OF POLLING STATION LOCATIONS

All registered voters in our jurisdiction are advised that your assigned polling location has been confirmed for the upcoming election. Please review the details below before casting your ballot.

Primary Polling Place: Central Community Center, 123 Main Street
Alternative Polling Place: Westside Public Library, 456 Oak Avenue

Voting hours on Election Day will be 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Both sites are ADA compliant and provide accessibility accommodations upon request.

Please bring a current government issued photo ID and one document showing your current residential address. To verify your exact polling assignment, check your voter registration card or visit the official elections portal online. For assistance, contact the Municipal Elections Office at 555-0199 during business hours.

Thank you for fulfilling your civic duty. We encourage you to vote early if your schedule allows.

Municipal Election Board
