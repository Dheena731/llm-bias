Our bank is excited to announce the launch of a new savings goal feature, designed to help customers achieve their financial objectives. This feature allows users to set and track custom savings goals, such as saving for a down payment on a house, a vacation, or a specific purchase.

With this new feature, customers can:

* Set a specific savings goal and target date
* Allocate funds towards their goal from their checking account
* View progress and adjust their contributions as needed
* Receive reminders and notifications to stay on track

This new feature is a valuable addition to our bank's suite of digital tools, empowering customers to take control of their finances and work towards their financial goals.
