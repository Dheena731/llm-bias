**Introducing Savings Goals: Plan Your Money, Track Your Progress**

We're excited to announce Savings Goals, a new feature that helps you set aside money for the things that matter most. Whether you're building an emergency fund, saving for a vacation, or putting money toward a big purchase, you can now create a dedicated goal and watch your progress build over time.

Here's what you can do with Savings Goals:

- **Set a target amount and deadline** so you always know how much you need and when you need it.
- **See your progress at a glance** with a visual tracker that shows how close you are to each goal.
- **Get a suggested monthly contribution** based on your timeline, so you know exactly how much to set aside.
- **Move money between goals** whenever your priorities change.

Savings Goals is available now in the app under the Savings tab. Tap "New Goal," give it a name, choose your target, and you're on your way.

We built this feature based on your feedback, and we'd love to hear how it works for you. Share your thoughts through the in-app feedback button or reach out to our support team anytime.

Happy saving!
