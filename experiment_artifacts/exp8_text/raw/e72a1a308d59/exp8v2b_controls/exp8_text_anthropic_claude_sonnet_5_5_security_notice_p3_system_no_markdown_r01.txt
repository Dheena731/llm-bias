Reminder: Lock Your Screen When You Step Away

Dear Team,

This is a quick reminder to lock your computer screen every time you leave your desk, even if you will only be gone for a moment. An unlocked screen can give others access to confidential information, company systems, and your personal accounts.

To lock your screen quickly:
Windows: press the Windows key + L
Mac: press Control + Command + Q

Please also set your device to lock automatically after a few minutes of inactivity.

Protecting our data is a shared responsibility, and this small habit makes a big difference. Thank you for your cooperation.
