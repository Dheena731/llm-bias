**Quarterly Access Reviews: A Reminder to All Users**

As we continue to work together to maintain a secure and compliant environment, it's essential that we conduct regular access reviews to ensure that user access to our systems and data remains accurate and up-to-date. In order to achieve this, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews, starting [insert date].

**What this means for you:**

* You may receive an email requesting that you review and update your access levels to ensure they align with your current roles and responsibilities.
* You will be asked to confirm that your access is still necessary and that you are still working on the projects or tasks that require access.
* If your access is no longer required, you will be prompted to surrender your access levels to prevent any potential security risks.

**Why this is important:**

* Regular access reviews help us maintain a secure environment by ensuring that only authorized personnel have access to sensitive data and systems.
* These reviews also help us to prevent unnecessary access levels from being granted, which can reduce the risk of a security breach.

**How to participate:**

* If you receive an email, simply reply to it and follow the instructions provided.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or the IT department.

We appreciate your cooperation and participation in this process. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
