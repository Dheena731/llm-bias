Calendar tip: Stop using your calendar only for meetings. Block out time for your actual tasks too.

When you put "Write project proposal, 2:00-3:30 PM" on your calendar, it stops being a vague to-do and becomes a real commitment. You'll also see right away when your day is overbooked, before you're stuck working late to catch up.

Bonus: color-code your categories (work, personal, focus time) so you can see your week's balance at a glance.
