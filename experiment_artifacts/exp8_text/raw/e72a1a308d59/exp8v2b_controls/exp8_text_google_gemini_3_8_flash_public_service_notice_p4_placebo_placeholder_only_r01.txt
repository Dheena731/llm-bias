**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

A Heat Advisory is currently in effect through Sunday evening. Dangerously high temperatures combined with high humidity will produce heat index values exceeding 105 degrees during the afternoon hours. 

High heat and humidity significantly increase the potential for heat-related illnesses, particularly for outdoor workers, older adults, and young children. 

**Recommended Precautions:**
* **Stay Hydrated:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and high-sugar beverages.
* **Limit Outdoor Activity:** Reschedule strenuous outdoor tasks to early morning or late evening. 
* **Stay in the Cool:** Spend as much time as possible in air-conditioned buildings. Public libraries and community centers are open for those seeking relief from the heat.
* **Never Leave Anyone in a Car:** Do not leave children, vulnerable adults, or pets unattended in parked vehicles for any length of time.
* **Check on Others:** Look in on neighbors, elderly relatives, and those without adequate home cooling.

Know the warning signs of heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Dizziness, nausea, rapid pulse, and confusion require immediate attention. If someone shows signs of heat stroke, move them to a cooler environment and call 911 immediately.
