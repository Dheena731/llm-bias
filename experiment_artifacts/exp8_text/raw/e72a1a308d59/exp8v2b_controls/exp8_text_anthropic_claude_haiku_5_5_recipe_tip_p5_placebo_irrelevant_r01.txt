Toss your vegetables in oil first, then season. Use about a tablespoon of olive oil per pound, and add salt, pepper, and your favorite spices before they go in the oven. The oil helps the seasoning cling to every surface and encourages even browning. For a finishing touch, add a squeeze of lemon, a sprinkle of fresh herbs, or a pinch of flaky salt right after roasting, when the flavors are at their brightest.
