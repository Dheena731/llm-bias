3. **Measures.** For each question or scale you used, say what it measures and where it came from. If you adapted an existing instrument, name the original source and explain what you changed.

Please also keep your verb tenses consistent. Use the past tense for what you did ("participants were recruited") and the present tense only for established facts.

Once these details are added, the section should let a reader follow your study from start to finish. I am happy to review a revised draft.
