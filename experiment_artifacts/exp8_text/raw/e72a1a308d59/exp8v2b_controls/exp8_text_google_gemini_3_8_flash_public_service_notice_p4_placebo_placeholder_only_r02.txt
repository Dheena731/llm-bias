**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

A Heat Advisory is currently in effect due to dangerously high temperatures and extreme humidity. Heat index values are projected to reach hazardous levels over the next several days, significantly increasing the risk of heat-related illnesses such as heat exhaustion and heat stroke.

**Recommended Precautions:**
* **Stay Hydrated:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even before feeling thirsty. Avoid caffeine and alcohol.
* **Stay Indoors:** Limit outdoor activities, especially during peak afternoon hours. Remain in air-conditioned buildings whenever possible.
* **Protect Children and Pets:** Never leave anyone in a closed, parked vehicle. The interior temperature can reach lethal levels in minutes. Ensure outdoor animals have access to shade and clean water.
* **Look Out for Others:** Check in on elderly neighbors, outdoor laborers, and individuals without access to air conditioning.

Public libraries and municipal community centers are open during normal business hours to serve as cooling locations for anyone in need of relief. 

Recognize the signs of heat stroke: high body temperature, confusion, dizziness, and hot, dry skin. Heat stroke is a medical emergency; dial 911 immediately if symptoms occur.
