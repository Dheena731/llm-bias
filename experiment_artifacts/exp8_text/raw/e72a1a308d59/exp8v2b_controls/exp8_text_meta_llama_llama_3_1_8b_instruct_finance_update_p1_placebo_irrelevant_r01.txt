**New Monthly Spending Summary Now Available**

To help you better track your finances, we're introducing a new monthly spending summary. This summary will provide a clear and concise breakdown of your expenses, making it easier to stay on top of your spending and identify areas for improvement.

**Key Features of the New Summary:**

* A comprehensive list of all transactions for the month, including income and expenses
* Categorized spending for easy comparison and analysis
* Total monthly spend and percentage breakdown by category
* Comparison to previous month's spending to identify trends and changes

**Benefits of the New Summary:**

* Improved financial transparency and accountability
* Enhanced ability to set and stick to a budget
* Increased confidence in making informed financial decisions

**Getting Started:**

To access your new monthly spending summary, simply log in to your account and navigate to the "Spending" section. From there, you'll be able to view and download your summary in a variety of formats, including Excel and PDF.
