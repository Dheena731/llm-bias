To plan lunches for a busy week, consider setting aside one day to prepare and portion out meals for the next few days. This can be as simple as cooking a large batch of rice, grains, or protein on the weekend, and then using these ingredients to assemble lunches throughout the week. You can also prep individual containers with ingredients like vegetables, fruits, and cheeses to grab and go. Having a plan and prepping ahead can save you time and stress during the week.
