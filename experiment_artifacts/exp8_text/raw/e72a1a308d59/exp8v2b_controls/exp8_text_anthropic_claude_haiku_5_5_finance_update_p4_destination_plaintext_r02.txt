Here's a short message you can use:

Your end-of-year tax statement summarizes the income you earned and the taxes withheld or paid during the past year. It typically includes your total wages or other income, any deductions and credits you claimed, and the amount of federal, state, and local taxes that were withheld from your pay or otherwise paid. Your statement is used to prepare your tax return, so please keep it with your other tax records. If anything looks incorrect or doesn't match your own records, contact the issuer (such as your employer or financial institution) to request a correction before you file. If you have questions about how to use the information, a tax professional or the official tax agency's website can help.
