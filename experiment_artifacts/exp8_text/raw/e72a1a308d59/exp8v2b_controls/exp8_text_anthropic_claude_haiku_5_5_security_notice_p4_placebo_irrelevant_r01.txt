**Action Required: Enable Two-Factor Authentication on Your Account**

To better protect your account and personal information, we strongly encourage you to turn on two-factor authentication (2FA). With 2FA, signing in requires both your password and a second verification step, such as a one-time code sent to your phone or generated by an authenticator app.

**How to enable it:**
1. Log in and go to **Settings > Security**.
2. Select **Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Choose your preferred method (authenticator app or text message).
4. Follow the on-screen steps to confirm your setup.

Enabling 2FA takes only a few minutes and significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access, even if your password is compromised.

If you have questions or need help, contact our support team at any time.
