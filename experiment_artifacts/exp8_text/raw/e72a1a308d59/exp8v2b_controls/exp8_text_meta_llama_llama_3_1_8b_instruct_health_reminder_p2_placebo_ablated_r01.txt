Here is a short reminder to drink enough water during the day:

"Don't forget to drink plenty of water throughout your day. Adequate hydration is essential for your body to function properly, and even mild dehydration can cause fatigue, headaches, and decreased productivity. Aim to drink at least 8-10 glasses of water per day, and make sure to drink a glass of water as soon as you wake up and before each meal to stay on track."
