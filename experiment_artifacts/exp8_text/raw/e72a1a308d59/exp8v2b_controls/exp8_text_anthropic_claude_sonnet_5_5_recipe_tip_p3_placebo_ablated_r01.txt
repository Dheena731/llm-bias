**Tip: Rinse your rice before cooking.**

Place the uncooked rice in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer and rinse it under cold running water, stirring gently with your fingers, until the water runs mostly clear (usually 3 to 5 rinses). This washes away the loose surface starch that makes cooked rice clump and turn gluey.

**Extra tips for fluffier grains:**
- **Measure the water carefully.** Too much water makes rice soft and sticky. For long-grain white rice, a common ratio is about 1¾ cups water to 1 cup rice, but check your package for specifics.
- **Don't stir while it cooks.** Stirring releases more starch. Bring the water to a boil, cover, reduce the heat to low, and leave it alone.
- **Let it rest.** After cooking, keep the lid on and let the rice sit off the heat for 10 minutes. Then fluff it with a fork.

Note: Short-grain and sushi rice are naturally stickier, so for less sticky results, choose long-grain varieties such as basmati or jasmine.
