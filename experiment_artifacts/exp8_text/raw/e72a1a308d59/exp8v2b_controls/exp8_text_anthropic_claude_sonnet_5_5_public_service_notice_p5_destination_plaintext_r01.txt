NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS

Voters are reminded that polling stations will be open on Election Day from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Please go to the station assigned to your address.

Polling stations in your area:

Ward 1: Central Library, 120 Main Street
Ward 2: Riverside Community Center, 45 River Road
Ward 3: Oakwood Elementary School Gymnasium, 310 Oak Avenue
Ward 4: Town Hall, 1 Civic Plaza
Ward 5: St. Mark's Parish Hall, 78 Church Lane

All locations are wheelchair accessible. Curbside voting is available on request. Please bring a valid photo ID.

Your assigned polling station is printed on your voter information card. If you are unsure where to vote, call the Elections Office at (555) 010-2000 or visit the official elections website to look up your address.

Please note that some locations have changed since the last election. Check your assigned station before you travel.

Thank you for voting.
