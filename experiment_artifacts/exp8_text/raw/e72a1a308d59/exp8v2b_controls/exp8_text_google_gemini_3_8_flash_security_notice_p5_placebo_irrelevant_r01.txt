**URGENT NOTICE: Procedure for Reporting Lost or Stolen Company Laptops**

To: All Staff  
From: IT Security Department  
Date: October 24, 2023  
Subject: Immediate Action Required for Missing Devices  

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, **immediate reporting is critical** to protect company data and client privacy. 

Please follow these steps as soon as you realize the device is missing:

1. **Notify IT Security immediately:**
   * **Emergency Hotline:** 555-0199 (Available 24/7)
   * **Email:** security@company.com
   * *Provide your name, device asset tag (if known), approximate time of loss, and last known location.*

2. **Inform your direct manager** so they are aware of any potential disruption to your work.

3. **File a police report** if the device was stolen (e.g., from a vehicle, home, or public transit), and forward the report/incident number to IT Security.

**Please do not delay reporting out of embarrassment or fear.** Our priority is securing company accounts, locking the device remotely, and safeguarding confidential information. Quick reporting minimizes potential security risks for everyone.

Thank you for your prompt cooperation.
