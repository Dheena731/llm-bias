PUBLIC NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS

The Board of Elections announces that polling station locations for the upcoming election on Tuesday, November 5, are now finalized. Polls will be open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

Due to recent district updates, your designated voting site may have changed since the last election. Please verify your assigned location prior to Election Day, as you must vote at your specific precinct.

To find your polling station:
1. Visit www.elections.gov/find-my-poll and enter your residential address.
2. Check your official voter information card recently sent by mail.
3. Call the Voter Assistance Hotline at 555-0199.

Please bring a valid photo ID on Election Day. For questions regarding accessible voting options or transportation assistance, contact the Elections Office.
