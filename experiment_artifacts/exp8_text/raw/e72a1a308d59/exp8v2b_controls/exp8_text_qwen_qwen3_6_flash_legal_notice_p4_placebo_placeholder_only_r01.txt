**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY AND CLAIM PROCEDURES**

This notice establishes the exclusive criteria governing financial restitution for completed transactions. Eligibility for a refund arises only when the purchaser demonstrates that the subject goods or services remain in their original condition, have not been consumed or altered, and are returned within thirty (30) calendar days of the recorded transaction date. Claims must be submitted in writing and accompanied by an unaltered proof of purchase, including the original invoice or system-generated confirmation record.

Refund entitlement is expressly excluded for customized merchandise, perishable consumables, digitally delivered materials after initial access has been granted, and items acquired through final-sale clearance promotions, absent mandatory statutory requirements. Purchasers who disregard approved packaging standards or remove security seals forfeit all reimbursement rights.

Upon verification of a compliant submission, the administrative review shall conclude within ten (10) business days. Authorized refunds will be disbursed exclusively to the original funding source; alternative disbursement methods are not permitted. Submissions lacking required documentation or filed outside the prescribed window will be automatically rejected.

This policy operates under prevailing consumer protection statutes, which retain supremacy over any contradictory internal directives. All formal inquiries and restitution requests shall be directed to the corporate compliance department at the principal administrative offices. Unresolved disputes may be forwarded to the relevant regulatory body possessing territorial jurisdiction. This notice takes immediate effect and supersedes all prior communications regarding monetary returns.
