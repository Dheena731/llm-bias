**Security Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication**

To enhance account security and protect your data against unauthorized access, two-factor authentication is now available and strongly recommended for all accounts.

Two-factor authentication adds an essential layer of defense by requiring both your password and a temporary verification code sent to your mobile device or generated by an authenticator app. This ensures that only you can access your account, even if your password becomes compromised.

**How to set it up:**
1. Log in to your account.
2. Go to **Settings** and select **Security**.
3. Locate **Two-Factor Authentication** and follow the on-screen instructions to link your device.

Please complete this process as soon as possible to ensure your account remains secure.
