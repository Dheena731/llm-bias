**Introducing Savings Goals: Save Smarter, Reach Faster**

We’re thrilled to roll out our new Savings Goals feature—built to help you turn financial intentions into tangible results. Whether you’re building an emergency fund, planning a trip, or saving for a major purchase, you can now set custom targets, track progress visually, and automate contributions without the guesswork.

**What’s included:**
• Customizable amounts & flexible deadlines  
• One-time deposits, recurring transfers, or automatic transaction round-ups  
• Real-time progress trackers & milestone notifications  
• All goals consolidated in a single, secure dashboard  

Getting started takes less than a minute: open the app, tap **Savings** in the main menu, select **+ New Goal**, and follow the step-by-step setup. You’ll see your progress update instantly as funds are added.

Your money, organized and purposeful. Create your first Savings Goal today and start moving closer to what matters most.
