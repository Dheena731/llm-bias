Your year-end tax statement summarizes what you earned and paid during the past tax year. You'll typically use it to file your return, so keep it somewhere safe.

Here's what to look for:

- **Income totals:** Wages, interest, dividends, or other income reported to the tax authorities. Compare these with your own records and report any differences before filing.
- **Taxes withheld:** The amount your employer or payer already sent to the government on your behalf. This counts toward what you owe or increases your refund.
- **Deductions and credits:** Any contributions, payments, or expenses that may lower your taxable income or reduce your tax bill.
- **Corrections:** If something looks wrong, contact the issuer for a corrected statement before you file.

If you have questions about a specific line or an amount that doesn't match your records, reach out to the issuer or a qualified tax professional before the filing deadline.
