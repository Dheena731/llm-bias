Your end-of-year tax statement is a summary of your income and tax withholding for the previous tax year. It will show the total amount of money you earned, the amount of taxes that were withheld from your pay, and any other relevant tax-related information. This statement is typically used to help you prepare your tax return and ensure you are in compliance with tax regulations. You will receive this statement by January 31st of each year, and it will cover the tax year that ended on December 31st of the previous year.
