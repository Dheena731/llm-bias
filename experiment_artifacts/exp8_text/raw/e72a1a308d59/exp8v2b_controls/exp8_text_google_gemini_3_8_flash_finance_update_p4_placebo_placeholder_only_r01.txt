An end-of-year tax statement provides an official summary of all financial transactions, earnings, or contributions associated with an account over the past calendar year. 

This document is essential for preparing annual tax filings, as it outlines the specific totals that must be reported to tax authorities. Please review the figures carefully to confirm that all records are accurate, and retain a copy for personal tax preparation files. For specific questions regarding how to report these amounts on a tax return, consulting a certified tax professional is recommended.
