To make a quick and delicious weeknight dinner, try prepping your ingredients in advance. Chop all your vegetables, such as onions, bell peppers, and mushrooms, and store them in airtight containers in the refrigerator. This way, when you're ready to cook, you can simply sauté them in a pan with some olive oil and add your favorite protein, like chicken or shrimp, for a fast and flavorful stir-fry. Serve over rice or noodles for a satisfying meal that's ready in no time.
