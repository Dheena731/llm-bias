**Reminder: Please Lock Your Screens**

Team,

As we work with sensitive company and customer information, please remember to lock your computer whenever you step away from your desk, even for a few minutes.

To lock your screen:
- **Windows:** Press **Windows key + L**
- **Mac:** Press **Control + Command + Q**

Locking your screen takes seconds and helps protect confidential data from being viewed or accessed by unauthorized people. Our IT team also recommends setting your computer to lock automatically after a short period of inactivity.

Thank you for helping keep our information secure.

IT Department
