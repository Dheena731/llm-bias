To save time and minimize dirty dishes on a busy weeknight, roast your protein and vegetables together on a single rimmed baking sheet. Cut your ingredients—such as chicken tenders, broccoli florets, and bell peppers—into similar, bite-sized pieces so they finish cooking at the same rate. Toss everything directly on the tray with cooking oil, salt, and your favorite dried herbs or spices, then bake at 400°F (200°C) for 15 to 20 minutes for a complete, flavorful meal with almost no cleanup.
