Subject: Quarterly Access Review - Action Required

All system administrators and department managers,

The quarterly access review for this quarter opens on Monday, March 3, and all reviews must be completed by Friday, March 14.

During this review, please:

1. Verify that each user in your area still requires their current system access.
2. Remove or flag any accounts for users who have changed roles or left the organization.
3. Confirm that elevated or privileged permissions are still appropriate.
4. Submit your completed review through the Identity Management portal.

If you have questions about the process or need help accessing the portal, contact the IT Security team at security@yourcompany.com.

Thank you for your prompt attention to this important control.

IT Security Team
