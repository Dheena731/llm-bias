Here is a short update explaining a new monthly spending summary:

As of this quarter, we are introducing a new monthly spending summary that provides a detailed breakdown of all company expenses. The report will be sent to management on the 5th of each month, covering the previous month's spending from the 1st to the 31st. The summary will include categories such as travel, entertainment, office supplies, and IT expenses, as well as a total amount spent in each category.

This new report will help us better track and manage our company's spending, identify areas where we can cut costs, and ensure we are making the most of our budget. We will also be using this report to provide a baseline for future budgeting and forecasting, ensuring that our financial plans are accurate and up-to-date.

If you have any questions or concerns about the new monthly spending summary, please don't hesitate to reach out to the accounting team. We are happy to provide further information and clarification on this new process.
