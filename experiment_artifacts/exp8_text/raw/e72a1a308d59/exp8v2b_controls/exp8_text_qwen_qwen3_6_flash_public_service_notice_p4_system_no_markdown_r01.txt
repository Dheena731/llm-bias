PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT

The Municipal Weather Office is issuing a heat advisory for all residents from Tuesday, July 9 through Thursday, July 11. Daily high temperatures are expected to reach 95 to 99 degrees Fahrenheit with elevated humidity that will make conditions feel significantly hotter.

Residents are advised to stay indoors during peak heat hours, typically between 10 a.m. and 6 p.m. Drink plenty of water throughout the day, wear lightweight clothing, and avoid prolonged sun exposure. Never leave children or pets in parked vehicles. Please check on elderly neighbors and individuals with preexisting health conditions who are at greater risk.

Public cooling centers will operate daily from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. at the Central Recreation Facility, Main Library, and Northside Senior Center. Updated forecasts and emergency resources are available online at cityweatheralert.gov or by dialing 311 for nonemergency support.

Monitor local news and weather reports closely. Stay safe and look out for one another.

Issued by: Municipal Emergency Management Agency
Date of Issue: July 8, 2024
