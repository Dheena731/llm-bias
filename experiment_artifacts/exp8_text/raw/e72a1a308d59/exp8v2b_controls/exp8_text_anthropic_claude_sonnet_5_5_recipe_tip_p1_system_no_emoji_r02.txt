**Cook once, use twice.** When you start dinner, make a double batch of a base ingredient, such as roasted vegetables, rice, or shredded chicken, and set half aside for tomorrow night. Tonight it might be a simple plate with a sauce, and tomorrow it can become fried rice, grain bowls, or tacos in about ten minutes.

To save even more time, chop all your vegetables before you turn on the stove. Having everything ready keeps you from scrambling mid-recipe, and the cooking goes faster and feels calmer.
