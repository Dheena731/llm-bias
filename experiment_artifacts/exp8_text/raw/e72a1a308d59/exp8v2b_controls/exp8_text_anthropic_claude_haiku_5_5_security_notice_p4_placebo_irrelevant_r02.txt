**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication to Protect Your Account**

To better protect your account and personal information, we strongly recommend turning on two-factor authentication (2FA). With 2FA, signing in requires your password plus a second verification step, such as a one-time code sent to your phone or generated by an authenticator app. Even if someone learns your password, they will not be able to access your account without that second step.

**How to enable it:**
1. Log in and go to **Account Settings**.
2. Select **Security** or **Sign-In Options**.
3. Choose **Two-Factor Authentication** and follow the on-screen steps.
4. Save your backup codes in a safe place in case you lose access to your device.

Enabling 2FA takes only a few minutes. If you have questions or need help, contact our support team.
