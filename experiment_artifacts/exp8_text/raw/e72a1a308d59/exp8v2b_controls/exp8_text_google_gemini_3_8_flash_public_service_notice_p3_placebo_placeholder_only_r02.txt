**Notice: Upcoming Changes to Recycling Collection**

Effective Monday, November 3, residential recycling pickup will transition to an alternating bi-weekly schedule. 

Under the new schedule:
* Recycling bins will be collected every other week on your regular trash collection day.
* Bins must be placed at the curb by 6:00 AM on your designated collection morning.
* Regular household trash collection remains unchanged and will continue on a weekly basis.

A complete calendar detailing the alternating pickup weeks for each neighborhood has been mailed to all residents. For additional information or to review the updated list of accepted recyclable materials, please visit the municipal sanitation website or contact customer service at the Department of Public Works.
