**Tip: Store herbs like a bouquet or in a damp wrap.**

For soft herbs such as cilantro, parsley, and dill, trim the stem ends and stand them in a jar with an inch of water. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and refrigerate. Change the water every few days.

For sturdier herbs such as rosemary, thyme, and sage, wrap them in a slightly damp paper towel, place them in a resealable bag or container, and keep them in the refrigerator. Basil is the exception: keep it at room temperature in a jar of water, away from direct sunlight, since cold temperatures turn its leaves black.
