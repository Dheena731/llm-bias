**Introducing Savings Goals**

We've added a new way to help you save for what matters most. With Savings Goals, you can set a target amount, choose a deadline, and watch your progress grow over time.

**What you can do:**

- **Create multiple goals.** Saving for a vacation, an emergency fund, and a new laptop at the same time? Each goal gets its own tracker.
- **Automate your contributions.** Set up weekly or monthly transfers so your savings build without extra effort.
- **Track progress at a glance.** A visual progress bar shows how close you are, and you'll get a friendly nudge when you hit key milestones like 25%, 50%, and 100%.
- **Adjust anytime.** Life changes, and your goals can too. Update the amount, change the deadline, or pause a goal whenever you need to.

**How to get started:**

Open the app, tap the Savings tab, and select "New Goal." It takes less than a minute to set up.

Savings Goals is available now for all users. We hope it makes reaching your next milestone a little easier, and we'd love to hear how it works for you. Send feedback anytime through the Help menu.
