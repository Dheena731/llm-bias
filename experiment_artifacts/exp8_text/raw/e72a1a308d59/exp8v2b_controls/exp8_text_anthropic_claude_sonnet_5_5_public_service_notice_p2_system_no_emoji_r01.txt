**PUBLIC NOTICE**

**Temporary Road Closure: Maple Avenue**

**Effective:** Monday, June 9, at 7:00 a.m., through Friday, June 13, at 5:00 p.m.

**Location:** Maple Avenue between 4th Street and 8th Street

**Reason for Closure:** Water main replacement and road resurfacing

**What to Expect:**
- Maple Avenue will be closed to all through traffic during the dates listed above.
- Residents within the closure area will have access to their driveways, though short delays are possible.
- Emergency vehicles will be given access at all times.
- Street parking will be prohibited on affected blocks. Vehicles left in the work zone may be towed.

**Detour Route:** Drivers should use Oak Street to 9th Street and return to Maple Avenue via 3rd Street. Detour signs will be posted.

**Please Note:** The schedule is subject to change in the event of inclement weather. Updates will be posted on the City website and on the Public Works information line.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience as we work to improve local infrastructure.
