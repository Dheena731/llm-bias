Subject: Reminder: Lock Your Screen When You Step Away

Dear Team,

This is a friendly reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you leave your desk, even if it is only for a moment. An unlocked workstation can give others access to confidential information, email accounts, and internal systems.

Quick ways to lock your screen:

Windows: press the Windows key + L
Mac: press Control + Command + Q
Any device: log out if you will be away for an extended period

Please also make sure your devices are set to lock automatically after a short period of inactivity.

Thank you for helping keep our data and our workplace secure. If you have questions or need help adjusting your settings, please contact the IT Help Desk.

Best regards,
IT Security Team
