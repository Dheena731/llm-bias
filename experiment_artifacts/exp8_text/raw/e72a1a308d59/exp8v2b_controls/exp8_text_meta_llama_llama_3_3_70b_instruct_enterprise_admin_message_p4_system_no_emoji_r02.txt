Attention all administrators and system owners,

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain the security and integrity of our systems, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews. These reviews are designed to ensure that all users have the appropriate level of access to our systems and data, and to remove any access that is no longer necessary.

During the review process, we will be verifying the access levels of all users and requesting updates on any changes to job roles or responsibilities. We will also be reviewing and removing any inactive or unnecessary accounts.

Your participation and cooperation in this process are greatly appreciated. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT department.

Thank you for your attention to this matter.
