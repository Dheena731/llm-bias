Here is a short update explaining a new monthly spending summary:

**Introduction of New Monthly Spending Summary**

As part of our ongoing efforts to improve financial transparency and accountability, we are introducing a new monthly spending summary. This summary will provide a clear and concise breakdown of all company expenses for the past month, allowing for easier tracking and analysis of our financial performance.

**Key Features**

The new monthly spending summary will include:

* A detailed list of all expenses, categorized by type (e.g. travel, supplies, equipment, etc.)
* Total amounts spent in each category
* A comparison to previous month's expenses for each category
* A summary of any significant changes or trends in spending

**Benefits**

This new summary will enable management and staff to:

* Better understand company spending habits
* Identify areas for cost savings and optimization
* Make informed decisions about budgeting and resource allocation
* Improve overall financial management and governance

**Implementation**

The new monthly spending summary will be distributed to all employees and stakeholders via email on the 10th of each month, covering the previous month's expenses. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the finance team.
