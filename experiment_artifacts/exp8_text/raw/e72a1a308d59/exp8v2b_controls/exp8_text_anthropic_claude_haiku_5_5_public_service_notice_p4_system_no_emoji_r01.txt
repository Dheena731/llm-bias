**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY**

The National Weather Service has issued a **Heat Advisory** for [City/Region] from [start time] on [day, date] through [end time] on [day, date]. Temperatures are expected to reach the mid-90s to low 100s Fahrenheit, with heat index values reaching [XX] degrees.

**Who is at greatest risk:** Infants and young children, adults over 65, people with chronic illnesses, outdoor workers, and anyone without access to air conditioning.

**Recommended precautions:**
- Stay in air-conditioned spaces as much as possible. Public cooling centers are open at [locations and hours].
- Drink water regularly, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and excessive caffeine.
- Never leave children, pets, or anyone who depends on you in a parked vehicle.
- Schedule outdoor activities for early morning or evening, and take frequent breaks in shade if you must be outside.
- Check on elderly neighbors, friends, and relatives at least twice daily.

**Warning signs of heat illness:** Heavy sweating, muscle cramps, nausea, dizziness, headache, or confusion. Move to a cool place, sip water, and call 911 immediately if symptoms include confusion, fainting, or a body temperature above 103°F.
