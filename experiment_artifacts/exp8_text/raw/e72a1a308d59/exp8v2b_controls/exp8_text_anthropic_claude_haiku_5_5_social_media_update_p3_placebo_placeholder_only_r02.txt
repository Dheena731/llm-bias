Big things are happening at Riverside Park this Saturday! Join us from 10 AM to 3 PM for the Riverside Community Fair. There will be live music from the Maple Street Band, a farmers market with fresh produce and homemade jams, and a kids' craft tent with face painting. Local food trucks will be parked by the pavilion, and the first 50 families to stop by the info booth get a free reusable tote bag. Bring your neighbors, your dog, and your appetite. See you at the park!
