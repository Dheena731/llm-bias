**PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE**

The Department of Public Works announces that Oakridge Boulevard will be closed to through traffic between 5th Street and Elm Avenue for scheduled water main repairs.

**Closure Schedule:**  
Monday, November 11 through Wednesday, November 13  
Daily from 7:00 AM to 4:30 PM  

**Traffic Impact & Detours:**  
All through traffic will be redirected around the work zone via Highland Parkway. Clearly marked detour signs will be posted along the route. Local access for residents living within the closure area and emergency vehicles will be maintained at all times. 

Motorists should anticipate minor delays and are encouraged to use alternate routes. 

For additional information or inquiries, please contact the City Public Works Dispatch Center at 555-0199.
