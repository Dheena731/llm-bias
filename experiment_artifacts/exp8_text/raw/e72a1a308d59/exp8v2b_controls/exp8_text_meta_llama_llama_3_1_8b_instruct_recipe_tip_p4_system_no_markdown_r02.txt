Here's a short tip for planning lunches for a busy week:

Plan your lunches for the week by dedicating one evening to prep and portioning out ingredients. Use a large container or ziplock bags to portion out ingredients such as grains, vegetables, lean proteins, and healthy fats. This can include things like brown rice, quinoa, grilled chicken, roasted vegetables, and nuts. Having these ingredients prepped and portioned out can make it easy to throw together a healthy and balanced meal in the morning, and can also help reduce food waste by using up ingredients before they go bad.
