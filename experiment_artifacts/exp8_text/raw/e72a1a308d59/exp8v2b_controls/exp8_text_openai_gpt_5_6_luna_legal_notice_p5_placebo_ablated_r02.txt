**Acceptable Use Notice**

Use of this shared network is permitted only for lawful, reasonable, and authorized activities. Users must not access, transmit, store, or distribute content that is illegal, threatening, harassing, defamatory, or infringing; attempt to bypass security controls; access another person’s accounts or data without permission; introduce malicious software; or use the network in a manner that disrupts service or unreasonably consumes shared resources.

Users are responsible for activity conducted through their accounts or devices and must protect their access credentials. Network activity may be monitored to protect security, maintain performance, and investigate suspected violations, subject to applicable law. Access may be limited or suspended for misuse. Suspected security incidents or violations should be reported promptly to the network administrator.
