Here's a short tip for seasoning roasted vegetables:

To bring out the natural flavors of roasted vegetables, try seasoning them just before serving. Instead of tossing them with oil, salt, and pepper before roasting, roast them with just a drizzle of oil until they're tender and caramelized. Then, sprinkle with salt, pepper, and any other desired herbs or spices, such as thyme, rosemary, or garlic powder, and toss to coat. This allows the seasonings to stay vibrant and fresh, rather than getting lost in the cooking process. You can also add a squeeze of fresh lemon juice or a pat of butter for extra flavor and moisture. This simple technique makes a big difference in the flavor and aroma of your roasted vegetables!
