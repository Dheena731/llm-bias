To store fresh herbs and maintain their flavor and fragrance, keep them in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Place the herbs in a paper bag or wrap them in a damp paper towel to prevent moisture loss. Store them in the refrigerator at a temperature below 40°F (4°C). Avoid storing herbs near strong-smelling foods, as they can absorb odors easily. Regularly check the herbs for signs of spoilage and use them within a few days of purchase for the best flavor and aroma.
