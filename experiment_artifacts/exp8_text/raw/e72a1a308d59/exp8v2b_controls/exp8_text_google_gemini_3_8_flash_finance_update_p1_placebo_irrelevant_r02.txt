**Subject:** Introducing your new Monthly Spending Summary

Hi everyone,

To help you better track and manage expenses, we’ve launched a new **Monthly Spending Summary**. Starting this month, you’ll receive an automated snapshot on the first of every month detailing where your money went.

Here’s what’s included in the new summary:

* **Categorized Breakdowns:** A visual overview of your top spending categories (e.g., software, travel, office supplies).
* **Month-over-Month Comparisons:** Simple trendlines to show how your spending compares to the previous month.
* **Flagged Variances:** Quick highlights for any unusual spikes or recurring charges that changed.

**Where to find it:**
Your summary will be delivered to your inbox on the 1st of each month. You can also view past reports anytime by navigating to **Reports > Spending Summary** in your dashboard.
