Reminder: Take Short Breaks from Screens

Don't forget to take short breaks from your computer, phone, and other screens throughout the day. Giving your eyes and mind a rest can help reduce eye strain, improve focus, and increase productivity. Try to step away from screens every 60-90 minutes to stretch, move around, and rest your eyes. Even a few minutes of break time can make a big difference in how you feel. Make it a habit to prioritize your well-being and take breaks from screens regularly.
