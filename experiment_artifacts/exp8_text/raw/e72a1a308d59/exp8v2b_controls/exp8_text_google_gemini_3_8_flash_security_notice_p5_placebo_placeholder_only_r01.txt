**Urgent Security Notice: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If your company-issued laptop is lost, misplaced, or stolen, take immediate action to protect company data and network security. 

**Steps to Take Immediately:**

1. **Contact IT Support Right Away:** Report the incident as soon as you realize the device is missing. Prompt reporting allows the security team to remotely lock the laptop, wipe sensitive data, and revoke active account sessions.
2. **Notify Your Direct Supervisor:** Inform your manager immediately after contacting IT Support so team operations can be adjusted.
3. **Provide Incident Details:** When filing your report, include the date and approximate time the laptop went missing, the last known location, and whether any accompanying accessories (such as security keys or external drives) were also lost.
4. **File a Police Report (If Stolen):** If the device was stolen from a vehicle, home, or public location, file an official report with local law enforcement and retain the report number for security and insurance records.

Data security is a shared responsibility. Timely reporting prevents unauthorized access and protects both our clients and our organization.
