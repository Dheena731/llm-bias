NOTICE OF RECYCLING COLLECTION CHANGES

Due to a change in our collection schedule, recyclables will no longer be collected on the second and fourth Thursdays of each month. Instead, collection will take place on the first and third Thursdays of each month. Please make sure to check your collection calendar for the new dates.

A new collection truck will also be introduced to improve efficiency. Residents are asked to place recyclables in the designated bins and not on top of them.
