Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection

Starting next Monday, recycling collection in our area will be moving to a new schedule. Recycling will now be picked up every other week, on Tuesdays, instead of weekly. Please check the collection calendar for your specific dates.

Please note the following:

Keep recyclables loose in your bin. Do not bag them.
Rinse containers before placing them out.
Flatten cardboard boxes and place them beside your bin if it is full.
Do not place plastic bags, food waste, or electronics in your recycling bin.

Your regular garbage collection schedule is not changing.

For questions or to request a replacement bin, please contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567 or visit our website.

Thank you for helping us keep our community clean and sustainable.
