**Subject: New: your monthly spending summary**

Hi everyone,

We've added a monthly spending summary that shows where your money went in the past month, so you can review your spending in one place instead of scrolling through individual transactions.

**What's in it**
- Your total spending for the month
- Spending by category (for example groceries, transport, and bills)
- How this month compares with last month, both overall and for each category
- Your largest individual purchases

**When you'll get it**
The summary is ready on the first day of each month and covers the previous calendar month. You'll get a notification when it's available, and you can open it any time from the **Summary** tab. [Adjust to match your product.]

**What to know**
- The summary uses only the transactions already in your account. If you've linked a new account, its earlier transactions may not appear.
- Categories are assigned automatically. If one looks wrong, you can change it, and the summary will update to reflect your change.

**Your control**
You can turn the monthly notification off in **Settings → Notifications**. The summary will still be available in the app.
