Polling Station Locations 

Residents are advised that polling stations for the upcoming election will be located at the following sites: 

- City Hall, 123 Main Street
- Community Center, 456 Elm Street
- Local Library, 789 Oak Street

Voters can cast their ballots at any of these locations on election day. Please note that polling hours are from 8am to 8pm. For more information, please contact the local election office.
