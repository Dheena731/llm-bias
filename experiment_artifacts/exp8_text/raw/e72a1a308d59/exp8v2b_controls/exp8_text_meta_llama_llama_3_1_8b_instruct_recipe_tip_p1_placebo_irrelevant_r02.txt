**Quick Tip: Prep Your Veggies Before Cooking**

Save time during weeknight dinner prep by chopping or slicing your vegetables in advance. Simply store them in a container in the fridge until you're ready to cook. This simple trick allows you to focus on cooking rather than tedious prep work. Try it with carrots, bell peppers, or broccoli – it'll make a big difference in your mealtime workflow!
