# NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

**Effective Date:** [Date]
**Issued by:** [Company Name] ("the Company")

## 1. Scope
This Notice sets out the conditions under which a purchaser ("Customer") may request a refund for products or services purchased from the Company.

## 2. Eligibility
A Customer is eligible for a refund only if **all** of the following conditions are met:

(a) The refund request is submitted in writing within **thirty (30) days** of the original purchase date;

(b) The Customer provides valid proof of purchase;

(c) The product is returned in its original condition and packaging, where applicable, or the service has not been substantially performed or consumed; and

(d) The purchase is not within a category listed in Section 3.

## 3. Non-Refundable Items
Refunds will not be issued for:

(a) Gift cards or promotional credits;
(b) Custom, personalized, or made-to-order goods;
(c) Digital products that have been downloaded, accessed, or activated;
(d) Services fully rendered prior to the request; or
(e) Items marked "final sale" at the time of purchase.

## 4. Processing
Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within **ten (10) business days** of approval. The Company is not responsible for delays caused by third-party payment providers. Original shipping and handling fees are non-refundable unless the refund results from Company error or a defective product.

## 5. Statutory Rights
Nothing in this Notice limits any rights a Customer may have under applicable consumer protection laws, which shall prevail in the event of conflict.

## 6. Reservation of Rights
The Company reserves the right to refuse any refund request that does not meet the above conditions or that appears fraudulent or abusive, and to amend this Notice at any time without prior notice. Amendments apply only to purchases made after the effective date of the change.
