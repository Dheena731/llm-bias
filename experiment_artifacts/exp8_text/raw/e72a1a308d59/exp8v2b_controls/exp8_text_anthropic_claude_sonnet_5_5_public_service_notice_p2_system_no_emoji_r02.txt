**PUBLIC NOTICE**

**Temporary Road Closure: Maple Avenue**

**Effective Dates:** Monday, June 3, through Friday, June 14

**Hours of Closure:** 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., daily

**Location:** Maple Avenue between Oak Street and Fifth Street

The City Department of Public Works will close the above section of Maple Avenue to all through traffic for water main replacement and road resurfacing. Work will take place on weekdays only. The road will reopen to traffic each evening and during the weekend.

**Detour Information:** Drivers should use Cedar Street and Seventh Avenue as an alternate route. Detour signs will be posted.

**Access:** Local residents and businesses will maintain access to their driveways and properties, though short delays are possible. Emergency vehicles will have access at all times. Pedestrian walkways will remain open where safe.

**Parking:** Parking will be prohibited along the affected section during work hours. Vehicles left in the work zone may be towed at the owner's expense.

Please allow extra travel time and observe all posted signs and flaggers. The schedule may change due to weather or unforeseen conditions.

For questions or updates, contact the Department of Public Works at (555) 123-4567 or visit www.examplecity.gov/publicworks.

We appreciate your patience as we work to improve our community's infrastructure.

**Issued by:** City Department of Public Works
