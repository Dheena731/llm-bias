**NOTICE: REPORTING A LOST COMPANY LAPTOP**

All personnel must immediately notify the IT Security Department if a company laptop goes missing. Direct your report to the security desk through the internal helpdesk system or call the dedicated support line. You are required to provide your full name, employee identification number, the device asset tag, your last known location, and a concise account of how the loss occurred.
