🌙 Dark Mode is here!

Your eyes asked, and we listened. You can now switch to a sleek, low-glare look that's easier on the eyes at night and better for your battery life.

To turn it on, open Settings, tap Appearance, and choose Dark. You can also set it to follow your device's schedule automatically.

Update to the latest version to try it today. We'd love to hear what you think! 💬
