NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS FOR UPCOMING ELECTION

All registered voters are advised that the following locations have been designated as official polling stations for the general election on November 5, 2024. Voting hours will be 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM at each site. Please bring a valid government issued photo identification and your voter registration confirmation.

Official Polling Sites:
Central Community Center, 100 Main Street
Westside Public Library, 45 Oak Avenue
North High School Gymnasium, 220 Elm Road
East District Recreation Facility, 310 Pine Boulevard

Specialized polling units will serve rural neighborhoods and residents requiring accessibility accommodations. To schedule an early vote or request transportation, please contact the County Elections Office at 555-0198 or visit the official elections website before October 28.

Real time updates, parking guidance, and multilingual support information are available through the Voter Information Hotline at 555-0100. Thank you for taking part in the electoral process.

Issued by: County Board of Elections
Date: October 15, 2024
