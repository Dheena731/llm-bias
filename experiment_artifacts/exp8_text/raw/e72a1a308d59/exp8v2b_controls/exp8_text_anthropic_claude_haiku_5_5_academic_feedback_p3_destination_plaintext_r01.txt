Reviewer Comment:

The research question as currently stated is difficult to follow. It is not clear what the authors are trying to determine, which population or context is under study, or how the outcome of interest is defined. Phrases such as "improve engagement" and "meaningful outcomes" are used without operational definitions, so it is unclear how the study would measure success or distinguish it from failure.

In addition, the question appears to contain several sub-questions that are not clearly connected. Please clarify the primary question and state explicitly whether the study is exploratory, descriptive, or hypothesis-testing. A revised question that specifies the population, the key variables, and the expected relationship between them would greatly strengthen the manuscript.
