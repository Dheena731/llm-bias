# Cookie Notice

## 1. Purpose
This Cookie Notice explains how this website uses cookies and similar technologies, and the choices available to you. By continuing to use the website after being shown this notice, you acknowledge the practices described below, subject to any consent you are required to give under applicable law.

## 2. What Are Cookies
Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your device, remember your preferences, and gather information about how the website is used.

## 3. Categories of Cookies We Use

**a. Strictly Necessary Cookies.** These cookies are essential for the website to function, including security, page navigation, and load balancing. They cannot be disabled through our systems.

**b. Preference Cookies.** These cookies remember your settings, such as language or region, to provide a more personalized experience.

**c. Analytics Cookies.** These cookies collect aggregated information about how visitors use the website, such as pages viewed and time spent, so that we can improve performance and content.

**d. Marketing Cookies.** These cookies may be used to deliver relevant advertising and measure its effectiveness. They are set only with your consent where the law requires it.

## 4. Third-Party Cookies
Some cookies are placed by third-party service providers acting on our behalf, such as analytics or advertising providers. These parties are responsible for their own cookies and handle information under their own privacy policies.

## 5. Legal Basis
Where required by law, we place non-essential cookies only after obtaining your consent. Strictly necessary cookies are used on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating and securing the website.

## 6. Managing Your Preferences
You may accept or decline non-essential cookies through the cookie banner displayed on your first visit. You may also withdraw or change your consent at any time by adjusting your browser settings or deleting cookies already stored on your device. Disabling certain cookies may limit the functionality of the website.

## 7. Retention
Session cookies expire when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain on your device until they expire or you delete them, generally for no longer than twenty-four months.

## 8. Changes to This Notice
We may amend this Cookie Notice from time to time. Revised versions take effect when posted on this website, and continued use after posting constitutes acknowledgment of the revised notice.

## 9. Contact
If you have questions about this Cookie Notice or our use of cookies, please reach us through the contact page of this website.
