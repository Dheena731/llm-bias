Here is a short update explaining a new monthly spending summary:

**New Monthly Spending Summary Now Available**

We are pleased to announce the introduction of a new monthly spending summary, designed to provide you with a clear and concise overview of your expenses. This summary will be sent to you at the end of each month, providing a detailed breakdown of your spending across all categories.

The new summary will include:

* A total of your monthly expenses
* A breakdown of your spending by category, including housing, transportation, food, entertainment, and more
* A comparison to your budgeted amounts, highlighting areas where you may be overspending
* A link to your online account, where you can access your spending history and make adjustments to your budget as needed

We believe this new summary will help you stay on top of your finances and make informed decisions about your spending. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
