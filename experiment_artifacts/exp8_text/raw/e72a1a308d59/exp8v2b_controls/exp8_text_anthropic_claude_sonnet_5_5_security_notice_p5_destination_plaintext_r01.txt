NOTICE: REPORTING A LOST OR STOLEN COMPANY LAPTOP

If your company laptop is lost or stolen, act immediately. Quick reporting helps protect company data and reduces security risk.

WHAT TO DO

1. Report it right away. Contact the IT Service Desk by phone or email as soon as you realize the laptop is missing. Do not wait until the next business day.

2. Provide key details. Include your name, the laptop's asset tag or serial number (if known), the date, time, and location it was last seen, and whether it contained sensitive or confidential data.

3. Notify your manager. Let your supervisor know so they can help with next steps and any needed coverage for your work.

4. File a police report if stolen. If theft is suspected, report it to local law enforcement and send a copy of the report number to IT Security.

5. Change your passwords. Update passwords for any accounts you accessed on the laptop, including email, VPN, and cloud applications. IT may assist with this.

WHAT IT WILL DO

- Remotely lock or wipe the device when possible
- Disable your account access on that device
- Investigate whether any data may have been exposed
- Arrange a replacement laptop

IMPORTANT REMINDERS

- You will not be penalized for promptly reporting a lost device. Delayed reporting, however, may result in disciplinary action.
- Never attempt to recover a stolen laptop yourself.
- Do not store passwords on or near your laptop.
