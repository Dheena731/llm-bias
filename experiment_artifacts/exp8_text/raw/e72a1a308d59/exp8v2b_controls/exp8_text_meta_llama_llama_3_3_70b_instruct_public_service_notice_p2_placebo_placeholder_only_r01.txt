NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE

The intersection of Main Street and Oak Avenue will be closed to all traffic beginning Monday, March 21, at 9:00 AM due to construction. The closure is expected to last for approximately six weeks, with an anticipated reopening on May 2.

During this time, drivers are advised to use the detour route via Elm Street and Maple Avenue to access downtown businesses and residences. Pedestrian access will be maintained, and local businesses will remain open as usual.

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your patience and cooperation as we work to improve our infrastructure. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the City's Public Works Department at 555-1234.
