**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY**

**A heat advisory is in effect for [City/County] from [time, day] until [time, day].**

Temperatures are expected to reach [100°F], and it will feel hotter because of humidity. Heat at this level can make anyone sick, especially if it lasts for several days.

**Who is most at risk:** older adults, infants and young children, people who work or exercise outdoors, people with health conditions, and anyone without air conditioning.

**What you should do:**
- **Stay cool.** Spend as much time as possible in air conditioning. If your home is not cool, go to a public cooling center: [location, address, hours].
- **Drink water regularly,** even if you don't feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and sugary drinks.
- **Limit outdoor activity,** especially from [noon to 6 p.m.]. If you must be outside, rest often in the shade and wear light, loose clothing and a hat.
- **Never leave children or pets in a parked car,** even for a few minutes, and even with the windows open.
- **Check on neighbors, relatives, and friends** who are elderly, live alone, or have health problems.

**Know the warning signs:**
- **Heat exhaustion:** heavy sweating, dizziness, headache, nausea, or muscle cramps. Move to a cool place, loosen clothing, and sip water.
- **Heat stroke (an emergency):** confusion, fainting, a very high body temperature, or hot, dry skin. **Call 911 immediately** and try to cool the person down while you wait.
