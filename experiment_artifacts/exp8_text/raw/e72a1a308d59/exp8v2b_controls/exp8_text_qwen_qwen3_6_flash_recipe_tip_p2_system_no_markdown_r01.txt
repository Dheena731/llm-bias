Trim the ends of your fresh herb stems and place them upright in a jar with about an inch of water, similar to arranging flowers. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag or a reusable silicone lid, then store the jar in the refrigerator. Change the water every two days to maintain freshness, and this method works best for soft herbs like cilantro, parsley, and dill. For woody herbs like rosemary and thyme, wrap the stems in a damp paper towel and place them in a sealed bag instead.
