**Lost Company Laptop Report**

If you have misplaced a company laptop, please report it to the IT department immediately. 

To report a lost laptop, please provide the following information:

* Laptop serial number (located on the back of the device)
* Location where the laptop was last seen
* Description of the laptop (make, model, color)
* Your name and contact information (in case we need to contact you for further information)

You can report a lost laptop by:

* Calling the IT department at 555-1234
* Sending an email to [itdept@company.com](mailto:itdept@company.com)
* Filling out the online lost laptop report form on the company intranet

Your prompt reporting will help us to locate and recover your company laptop.
