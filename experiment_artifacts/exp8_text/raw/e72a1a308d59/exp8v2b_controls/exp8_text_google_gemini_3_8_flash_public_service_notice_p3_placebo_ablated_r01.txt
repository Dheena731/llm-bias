**Notice: Change in Weekly Recycling Collection Schedule**

**Dear Residents,**

Starting Monday, November 3, your recycling collection day will permanently move from Tuesday to Thursday. 

**What you need to do:**
* Place your recycling bin at the curb by 7:00 AM every Thursday morning.
* Regular household trash collection will not change and will remain on Mondays.

Thank you for your cooperation as we update our routes. If you have any questions, please contact the Town Services Office at 555-0199 or visit www.exampletown.gov.
