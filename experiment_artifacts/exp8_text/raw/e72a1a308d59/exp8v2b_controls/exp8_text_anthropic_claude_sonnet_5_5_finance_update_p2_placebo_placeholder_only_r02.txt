**Subject: Update to our card transaction fees**

Hello,

Beginning June 1, we are changing how we handle card transaction fees. Until now, we have covered the processing costs ourselves. As those costs have risen over the past year, we can no longer absorb them without affecting the quality of our service.

**What's changing**
- A 2.9% fee will apply to purchases made with credit or debit cards.
- Payments by bank transfer or cash will not have a fee.
- Refunds on card purchases will include the original fee.

**Why we're doing this**
Our payment processor has raised its rates twice since last spring. Rather than raise our prices across the board, we decided to apply the fee only to card payments, so customers who pay another way don't bear the cost.

**What you need to do**
Nothing. The fee will appear as a separate line at checkout, so you'll always see the total before you confirm your payment. If you'd prefer to avoid it, you can switch to bank transfer in your account settings at any time.

We appreciate your understanding and your continued support. If you have questions, just reply to this message and we'll be glad to help.

Thank you,
The Customer Care Team
