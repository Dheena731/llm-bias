**New: Savings Goals**

You can now set savings goals directly in the app and track your progress in one place.

**What's new**

- **Create custom goals.** Name your goal, set a target amount, and choose a target date, whether it's an emergency fund, a vacation, or a down payment.
- **Automatic contributions.** Schedule weekly, biweekly, or monthly transfers so your savings grow without extra effort.
- **Progress tracking.** See how close you are at a glance, with a projected completion date that updates as you save.
- **Flexible adjustments.** Pause, edit, or reprioritize any goal at any time.

**How to get started**

Open the Savings tab, tap "New Goal," and follow the prompts. Setup takes less than a minute.

**Availability**

Savings Goals is rolling out to all users over the next two weeks. If you don't see it yet, check back soon and make sure your app is up to date.
