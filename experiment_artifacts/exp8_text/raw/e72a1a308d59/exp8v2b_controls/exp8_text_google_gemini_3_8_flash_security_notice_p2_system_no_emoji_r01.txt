**SECURITY NOTICE: Phishing Awareness and Email Safety**

The IT Security Team urges all employees to remain vigilant regarding suspicious emails. Cybercriminals frequently use phishing attempts to compromise company data and internal systems.

**Key Signs of a Phishing Email:**
* **False Urgency:** Demands for immediate action, wire transfers, or password resets.
* **Suspicious Senders:** Email addresses that mimic familiar contacts or vendors but contain subtle misspellings.
* **Unexpected Attachments:** Unsolicited files, especially ZIP files, macros, or generic invoices.
* **Mismatched Links:** Hyperlinks where the displayed text does not match the actual destination URL (hover over links to check before clicking).

**What to Do:**
1. Do not click links or open attachments in unexpected or suspicious emails.
2. Never share credentials, sensitive employee information, or client data via email.
3. Report the message immediately using the "Report Phishing" button in your email client, or forward it to IT Support.

If you believe you have accidentally clicked a suspicious link or entered credentials, disconnect your device from the network immediately and contact IT Support.
