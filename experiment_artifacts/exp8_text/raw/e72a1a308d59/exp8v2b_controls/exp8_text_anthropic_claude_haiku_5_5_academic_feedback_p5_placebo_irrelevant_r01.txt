Here's a short comment you could leave on an essay:

"Several claims in this section, particularly the statistics in paragraph three and the summary of the 2019 study, need citations. Without sources, the reader has no way to verify these points, and they risk being read as your opinion rather than established fact. Please add in-text citations and corresponding entries in your bibliography for each borrowed idea, figure, or direct quotation. If you're unsure which claims require a source, a good rule of thumb is: if you learned it from somewhere else, or if a reasonable reader might question it, cite it."
