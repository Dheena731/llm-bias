We're excited to announce the launch of our new savings goal feature. This feature allows you to set and track progress towards specific savings targets. By setting a clear goal, you can stay motivated and focused on your financial objectives. The feature includes the ability to track your progress, receive reminders, and make adjustments to your goal as needed. To get started, simply log in to your account, click on "Savings Goals" and follow the prompts to set up your first goal.
