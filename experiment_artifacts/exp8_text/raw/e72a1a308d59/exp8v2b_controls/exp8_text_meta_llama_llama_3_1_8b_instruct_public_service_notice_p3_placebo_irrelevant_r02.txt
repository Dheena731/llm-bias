**Important Notice: Recycling Collection Changes**

As part of our ongoing efforts to improve our community's waste management services, we are making some changes to our recycling collection schedule.

**New Schedule:**

* Residential recycling collection will now take place on Wednesdays, instead of Fridays.
* Collection times will remain the same, between 8am and 12pm.
* The recycling centers will still be open on the same days (Mondays to Saturdays) for residents to deposit their recyclables.

**What You Need to Know:**

* Please ensure that all recyclable materials are placed in the designated recycling bins by 7am on Wednesdays.
* If you have any large or bulky items to recycle, please contact our waste management team to arrange for special collection.
* We encourage you to continue participating in our community's recycling program and to help us maintain a cleaner, greener environment.

If you have any questions or concerns about the new recycling collection schedule, please don't hesitate to contact our waste management team at (phone number) or (email address).
