SECURITY NOTICE: PROHIBITION OF PASSWORD REUSE

To protect company data and systems, all employees must use a unique password for every account and service. Do not reuse passwords across work and personal accounts, nor across different platforms.

RISK:
Password reuse creates a critical vulnerability. Attackers often use credentials stolen from third-party breaches to attempt access to our internal systems. If a reused password is compromised on any site, your other accounts are at immediate risk.

ACTION REQUIRED:
- Create a strong, unique password for every login.
- Use the company-approved password manager to generate and store credentials securely.
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on all supported accounts.
- Immediately change any password that has been reused or shared.

REPORTING:
If you suspect your credentials have been exposed or notice suspicious activity, report it immediately to the IT Security team.

Thank you for helping maintain a secure work environment.
