**Notice: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, report it immediately. Prompt reporting helps protect company data and reduces security risks.

**What to do:**

1. **Notify IT Security right away.** Contact the IT Help Desk at [phone number] or [email address]. Do not wait to search for the device before reporting.
2. **Inform your manager.** Let your direct supervisor know within the same business day.
3. **File a police report if the device was stolen.** Keep a copy of the report number for company records.
4. **Provide key details.** Be prepared to share the device asset tag or serial number, the date, time, and location of the loss, and a description of any data stored on the laptop.
5. **Change your passwords.** Update your company account passwords from a secure device once IT advises you to do so.

**What IT will do:**

- Remotely lock or wipe the device where possible
- Disable your account access as needed
- Assess any potential data exposure

**Important reminders:**

- Reports should be made within one hour of discovering the loss, or as soon as reasonably possible.
- Employees will not be penalized for reporting in good faith. Delayed reporting, however, may result in disciplinary action.
- Never store sensitive company information on personal devices or unapproved storage.

For questions, contact the IT Help Desk at [phone number] or [email address].
