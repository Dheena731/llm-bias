Calendar tip: stop using your calendar just for meetings. Block out time for your own tasks too!

If something matters (a workout, deep-focus work, even lunch), give it an actual slot. When it's on the calendar, it's far more likely to happen, and other people can't accidentally book over it.

Bonus: set a 10-minute buffer between events so back-to-back days don't wreck you. 📅
