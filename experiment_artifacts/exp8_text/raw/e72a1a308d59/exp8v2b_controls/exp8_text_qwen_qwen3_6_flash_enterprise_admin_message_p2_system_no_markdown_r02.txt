Subject: Update to Expense Approval Process

Team,

Starting next Monday, we will be implementing a new expense approval workflow to streamline submissions and accelerate reimbursements.

Key changes include:
1. All expense reports must now be submitted through the updated portal at https://portal.company.com/expenses
2. Managers will receive automated notifications for review and must approve or reject requests within three business days
3. Receipts must be uploaded as PDF or image files; handwritten receipts are no longer accepted
4. Expenses exceeding five hundred dollars require secondary approval from department heads

Please log in to the system by Friday to update your profile and familiarize yourself with the new interface. Training materials and a step-by-step guide are available on the company intranet under Finance Resources.

If you have questions or encounter issues during the transition, please contact finance@company.com or reach out to the Accounts Payable team directly.

Thank you for your cooperation as we improve our financial processes.

Best regards,
Administration Department
