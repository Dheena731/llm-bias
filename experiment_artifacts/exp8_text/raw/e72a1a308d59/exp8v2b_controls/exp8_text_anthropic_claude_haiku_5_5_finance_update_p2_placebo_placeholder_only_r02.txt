**Notice of Change to Card Transaction Fees**

Effective March 1, we are updating the processing fee on card payments. Starting with transactions made on or after that date, a 2.5% surcharge will apply to credit card purchases. Debit card and cash payments are not affected, and there is no change to fees on in-store purchases paid by debit.

The change is necessary because the fees our payment processor charges us have risen over the past year, and those costs have grown faster than our revenue. Rather than raise the prices of every item on our menu, we have chosen to pass a portion of the card processing cost to customers who pay by credit card, so that everyone who pays with cash or debit is not subsidizing those costs.

The surcharge will appear as a separate line on your receipt and on online orders at checkout. It applies only to the amount paid by credit card, so a $20.00 purchase will carry a $0.50 surcharge. Gift card purchases and redemptions are not subject to the fee.

If you have questions about this change, please ask a member of our staff or email billing@harborcoffee.example. We appreciate your continued support and thank you for your understanding.
