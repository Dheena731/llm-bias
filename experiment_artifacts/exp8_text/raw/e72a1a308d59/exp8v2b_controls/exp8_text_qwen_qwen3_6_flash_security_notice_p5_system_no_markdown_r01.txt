NOTICE: REPORTING A LOST COMPANY LAPTOP

If you have misplaced or lost your assigned company laptop, please submit a report immediately to the IT Security Team at it-support@company.com or call extension 5000.

Your report must include your full name and employee ID, the laptop serial number or asset tag, the date and approximate time the device was last seen, the last known location, and a brief description of the circumstances surrounding the loss.

Prompt reporting is mandatory to protect sensitive company data and activate tracking or remote wipe protocols. Reports filed more than twenty four hours after the incident will be escalated to management and may result in temporary system access suspension. All submissions are treated as confidential.

Thank you for your immediate cooperation.
