**Prep your aromatics and sauce first, before you turn on the heat.**

On a busy weeknight, most of the stress comes from scrambling mid-cook. Before you start, chop your onions, garlic, and vegetables, and mix any sauce ingredients in a small bowl. Once the pan is hot, the cooking moves quickly, and you won't be mincing garlic while something else burns.

A few extra tips:
- Start with the longest-cooking item, such as rice or potatoes, and prep the rest while it cooks.
- Use one large sheet pan or skillet to keep cleanup minimal.
- Salt as you go. Seasoning in layers builds more flavor than adding it all at the end.

Ten minutes of organizing up front can turn a chaotic dinner into a calm, 30-minute meal.
