NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Refund requests must be submitted within thirty (30) days of the original purchase date. To be eligible for a refund, products must be unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by valid proof of purchase. For services, eligibility requires that no substantial performance or delivery has occurred prior to the cancellation request.

Non-refundable items include, but are not limited to: digital downloads, customized or personalized goods, gift cards, and items explicitly marked as "Final Sale."

All refunds are subject to inspection and approval. Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within 7 to 10 business days. The company reserves the right to deny any request that does not comply with this policy or exhibits evidence of abuse or fraud.
