SECURITY NOTICE: THE RISK OF PASSWORD REUSE

Reusing passwords across multiple accounts is one of the leading causes of security breaches. When you use the same password for both work and personal services, a data leak on any external website directly puts our entire company network at risk.

Please keep our systems secure by following these standards:

1. Never use your workplace password for personal accounts.
2. Ensure every business application has a unique, complex password.
3. Use the company-approved password manager to safely generate and store your credentials.

If you suspect any of your passwords have been compromised, please change them immediately and contact the IT Security Team.

Thank you for your diligence in protecting our data.
