**Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starts Monday**

Hello everyone,

Beginning Monday, all expense reports will follow a new approval workflow in our finance system. The change is meant to speed up reimbursements and cut down on back-and-forth over missing details.

**What's changing**

- **Submission:** Submit every expense report through the Expenses tab in the finance portal. Emailed or paper reports will no longer be processed.
- **Receipts:** Attach an itemized receipt to each line item at the time of submission. Reports with missing receipts will be returned automatically.
- **Approval tiers:** Expenses under $500 go to your direct manager only. Expenses of $500 or more require approval from your manager and then the finance team.
- **Turnaround:** Managers have three business days to approve or reject a report. Finance will review within two business days after that.
- **Reimbursement:** Approved expenses are paid in the next scheduled payroll cycle.

**What you need to do**

1. Log in to the finance portal before Monday and confirm your manager is listed correctly in your profile.
2. Submit any outstanding expenses from the old process by the end of this week so they are handled before the switch.
3. Review the short how-to guide on the intranet under Finance > Expense Reports.

If you have questions or run into problems with the portal, contact the finance help desk by email or stop by during office hours on Thursday afternoon. Thank you for helping make this transition a smooth one.

Best regards,
Administration Team
