Here's a short reminder to drink enough water during the day:

**Stay Hydrated!**

Don't forget to drink plenty of water throughout the day! Aim for at least 8 cups (64 oz) of water to keep your body functioning at its best. Whether you're sipping on water at your desk, taking a refillable bottle with you on the go, or enjoying a refreshing glass after a workout, make sure to drink up!

**Tips:**

* Set a reminder on your phone to drink water at specific times of the day
* Infuse your water with slices of lemon, lime, or cucumber for a refreshing twist
* Keep a water bottle at your workspace or in your car for easy access
* Make drinking water a habit by making it a part of your daily routine

**Stay hydrated, stay healthy!**
