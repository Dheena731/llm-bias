**REFUND ELIGIBILITY NOTICE**

**To:** All customers who purchase products or services from [Company Name] ("we," "us," or "the Company")

**1. Purpose**
This notice explains when you may request a refund for a purchase.

**2. Refund Period**
You may request a full refund within 30 days of the purchase date shown on your receipt or order confirmation (the "Refund Period").

**3. Conditions**
To be eligible, your request must:
(a) be submitted during the Refund Period;
(b) include proof of purchase; and
(c) concern a product that is unused and in its original packaging, or a service that has not been fully delivered.

**4. Non-Refundable Items**
The following are not eligible for refunds: gift cards, custom-made items, and digital downloads that have been accessed.

**5. How to Request a Refund**
Submit your request by emailing [email address] or through the form at [web address]. Include your order number and proof of purchase. We will respond within 10 business days, meaning Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays.

**6. Refund Method**
Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Depending on your financial institution, the refund may take 5 to 10 business days to appear after approval.

**7. Your Other Rights**
This notice does not limit any rights you may have under applicable consumer protection laws. Those laws may provide refund rights beyond those described here.
