We're excited to share a new feature: the Monthly Spending Summary. At the end of each month, you'll receive a clear overview of where your money went, including totals by category, comparisons to the previous month, and highlights of your largest transactions.

Here's what you can expect:

Category breakdown: See how much you spent on groceries, dining, transportation, bills, and more.
Month-over-month comparison: Check whether your spending went up or down compared to last month.
Top transactions: Review your biggest purchases so nothing surprises you.

The summary will appear in your account dashboard on the first day of each month. You can also find it anytime under the Reports tab.

If you'd prefer not to receive the summary, you can turn it off in Settings under Notifications.
