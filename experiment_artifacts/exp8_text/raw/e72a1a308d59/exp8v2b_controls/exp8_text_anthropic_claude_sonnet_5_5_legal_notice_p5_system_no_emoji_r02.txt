**NOTICE OF ACCEPTABLE USE: SHARED NETWORK**

**1. Applicability**
This Notice governs access to and use of the shared network (the "Network") by all employees, contractors, guests, and other authorized persons (each a "User"). By connecting to or using the Network, a User acknowledges and agrees to be bound by this Notice.

**2. Authorized Use**
The Network is provided for legitimate business, educational, or other purposes approved by the Network Administrator. Limited incidental personal use is permitted, provided it does not interfere with Network performance, security, or the User's duties.

**3. Prohibited Conduct**
Users shall not:

a. Access, copy, alter, or disclose data or systems without authorization;
b. Introduce malware, or conduct scanning, probing, or testing of the Network without written approval;
c. Share, misuse, or compromise credentials or access controls;
d. Transmit unlawful, harassing, defamatory, discriminatory, or obscene material;
e. Infringe copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property rights;
f. Consume bandwidth or resources in a manner that unreasonably degrades service for others; or
g. Circumvent or disable security measures, filters, or monitoring tools.

**4. Monitoring and Privacy**
Users have no expectation of privacy in their use of the Network. The Network Administrator may monitor, log, inspect, and retain Network activity and associated data to the extent permitted by applicable law, for purposes including security, maintenance, compliance, and investigation of suspected violations.

**5. Security Obligations**
Users shall safeguard their credentials, keep connected devices reasonably secured and updated, and promptly report any suspected security incident or misuse to the Network Administrator.

**6. Enforcement**
Violation of this Notice may result in suspension or termination of access, disciplinary action, termination of contract or employment, and, where appropriate, referral to law enforcement or civil action. The Network Administrator may suspend access immediately and without prior notice where necessary to protect the Network.

**8. Modification**
This Notice may be amended at any time. Continued use of the Network following notice of any amendment constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

**9. Contact**
Questions or reports of violations should be directed to the Network Administrator.
