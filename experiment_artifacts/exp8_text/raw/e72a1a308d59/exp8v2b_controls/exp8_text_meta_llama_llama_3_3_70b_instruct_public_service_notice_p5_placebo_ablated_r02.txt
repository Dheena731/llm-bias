**Important Voting Information**

To all registered voters,

The polling stations for the upcoming election will be located at the following sites:

* Local Community Center (123 Main St)
* Town Hall (456 Elm St)
* Public Library (789 Oak St)

These locations will be open on election day from 8am to 8pm. Please note that you must vote at the polling station assigned to your ward, which can be found on your voter registration card or by contacting the local election office.

We encourage all eligible voters to exercise their right to vote and participate in the democratic process. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.

Thank you for your participation.
