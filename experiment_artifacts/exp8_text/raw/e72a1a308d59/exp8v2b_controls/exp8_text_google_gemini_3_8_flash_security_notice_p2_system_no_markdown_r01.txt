SECURITY NOTICE: Be Alert for Phishing Emails

We have recently seen an increase in phishing attempts targeting our organization. Phishing emails are designed to look legitimate in order to steal passwords, financial information, or install malicious software.

Common Red Flags to Watch For:
1. Urgent or threatening language demanding immediate action.
2. Slight misspellings in the sender email address or company domain.
3. Unexpected attachments or requests to click on unfamiliar links.
4. Requests for login credentials, personal data, or wire transfers.

What You Should Do:
1. Do not click links or open attachments from unknown or unverified sources.
2. Never share your passwords or security codes via email.
3. Verify suspicious requests directly with the sender through a separate, known communication channel like a phone call or chat message.
4. Report any suspicious emails immediately using the Report Phishing button in your email client, or contact the IT Helpdesk directly.

Thank you for your vigilance in keeping our network and data secure.
