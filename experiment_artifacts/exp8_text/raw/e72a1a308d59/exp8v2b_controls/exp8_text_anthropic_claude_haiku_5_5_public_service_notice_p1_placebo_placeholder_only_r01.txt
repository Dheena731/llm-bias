**Attention Library Patrons: Updated Opening Hours**

Starting Monday, the Central Library will follow new weekly hours:

- **Monday through Friday:** 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
- **Saturday:** 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- **Sunday:** Closed

The library will be closed on all major public holidays. Book drop-off remains available 24 hours a day at the front entrance.

For questions or the latest updates, visit the library's website or call the information desk during open hours.
