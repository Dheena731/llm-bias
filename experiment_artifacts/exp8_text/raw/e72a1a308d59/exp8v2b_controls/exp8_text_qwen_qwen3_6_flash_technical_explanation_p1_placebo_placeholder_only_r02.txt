Latency represents the delay between a user's request and the application's response. High latency negatively impacts user experience by making interfaces feel unresponsive, which often leads to frustration and immediate abandonment. Performance research confirms that even minor increases in load time reduce user engagement, lower conversion rates, and decrease revenue. Search algorithms also prioritize faster sites, meaning excessive latency can diminish visibility and organic traffic. On the technical side, prolonged response times consume server resources longer, creating bottlenecks and risking cascading failures across interconnected services. Minimizing latency is therefore critical for maintaining user satisfaction, ensuring financial performance, and preserving system stability.
