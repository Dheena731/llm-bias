**Notice: Recognizing and Reporting Phishing Emails**

Phishing emails are fraudulent messages designed to trick you into clicking malicious links, opening infected attachments, or sharing passwords and other sensitive information. They often look like they come from a colleague, a vendor, IT, or a well-known company.

**Warning signs to watch for:**
- Urgent or threatening language, such as "Your account will be suspended today"
- Requests for passwords, login details, or payment information
- Unexpected attachments or links, especially from unfamiliar senders
- Sender addresses that look almost right but contain small misspellings
- Greetings that are generic, such as "Dear User"
- Requests to change payment or bank details

**What to do:**
1. Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails.
2. Verify any unusual request by contacting the sender through a known phone number or in person, not by replying to the email.
3. Report the email immediately using the Report Phishing button or by forwarding it to [IT security email address].
4. If you clicked a link or entered credentials, report it to IT right away, even if you feel embarrassed. Quick reporting limits damage.

Our IT team will never ask for your password by email. When in doubt, ask before you act.
