To make rice less sticky, rinse it before cooking. Place the rice in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer and run cold water over it, swishing with your hand, until the water runs mostly clear (usually 3 to 5 rinses). This washes away the surface starch that causes clumping. Then use a slightly lower water ratio, about 1 3/4 cups of water per 1 cup of long-grain rice, and avoid stirring while it cooks. Once it's done, let it rest covered for 10 minutes, then fluff gently with a fork.
