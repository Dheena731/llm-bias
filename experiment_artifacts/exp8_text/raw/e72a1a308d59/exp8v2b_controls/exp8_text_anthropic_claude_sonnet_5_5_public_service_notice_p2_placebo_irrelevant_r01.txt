**PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE**

**Maple Street Closed Between 4th Avenue and 8th Avenue**

**Dates:** Monday, June 9 – Friday, June 20, 2025
**Hours:** 7:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m., daily (weekdays only)

The City Department of Public Works will be repaving and repairing storm drains along Maple Street. During this time, the road will be closed to through traffic.

**What to expect:**
- Local residents and businesses will have access to their driveways and entrances, though short delays may occur.
- Street parking will be prohibited on both sides of the closed section. Vehicles left in the work zone may be towed.
- Detours will be posted using Oak Street and 10th Avenue.
- Emergency vehicles will have access at all times.
- Bus Route 14 will be rerouted. Temporary stops will be marked.

Work is weather-dependent, and the schedule may change. Updates will be posted on the city website and social media.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience as we work to improve our roads.

**Questions?** Contact Public Works at (555) 012-3456 or publicworks@examplecity.gov.

*Issued by the City Department of Public Works*
