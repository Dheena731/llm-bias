When seasoning roasted vegetables, consider the natural flavors of the vegetables and enhance them with a balanced blend of herbs and spices. A general rule of thumb is to use a combination of acidic, salty, and umami flavors to bring out the best in the vegetables.

For example, a mixture of olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and herbs like thyme or rosemary can complement the sweetness of carrots or Brussels sprouts. Alternatively, a combination of olive oil, salt, and umami-rich ingredients like soy sauce or miso can enhance the earthy flavors of beets or sweet potatoes.

Remember, the key is to taste as you go and adjust the seasoning to suit your taste. This will ensure that your roasted vegetables are not only flavorful but also tailored to your individual preferences.
