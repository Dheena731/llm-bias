**Comment on missing citations:**

Several of your key claims, particularly the statistics in paragraph two and the historical argument in paragraph four, are presented without sources. Without citations, readers can't verify your evidence or tell which ideas are yours and which come from other writers. Please add in-text citations wherever you use data, quotations, paraphrased ideas, or claims that aren't common knowledge, and include a matching reference list at the end. Doing so will make your argument more credible and ensure you're properly crediting others' work. If you're unsure about the required citation format, the style guide or the writing center can help.
