Important Update to Card Transaction Fees

As of [effective date], we are making a change to our card transaction fees. The new fee structure will be as follows:

* A fee of 1.5% will be charged for all credit card transactions
* A fee of 2.5% will be charged for all debit card transactions
* A fee of $0.25 will be charged for all PIN-based transactions

These changes are designed to better align our fees with industry standards and to help us continue to provide low-cost, convenient payment options for our customers. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
