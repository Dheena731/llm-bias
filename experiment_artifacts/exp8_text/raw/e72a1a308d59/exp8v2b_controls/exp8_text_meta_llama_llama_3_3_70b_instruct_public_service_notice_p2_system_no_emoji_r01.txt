NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE

Due to construction and maintenance, a section of Main Street will be closed to all traffic starting Monday, March 13th, and will remain closed until further notice. The closure will be in effect from the intersection of Main Street and Oak Avenue to the intersection of Main Street and Elm Street.

Motorists are advised to use alternative routes, such as Maple Street or Pine Street, to avoid the area. Detour signs will be posted to assist with navigation.

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your patience and cooperation during this time. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Department of Public Works at 555-1234.
