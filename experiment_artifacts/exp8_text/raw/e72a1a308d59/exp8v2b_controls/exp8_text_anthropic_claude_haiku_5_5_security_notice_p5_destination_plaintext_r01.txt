Subject: Reporting a Lost Company Laptop

All employees are reminded that if a company laptop is lost or stolen, it must be reported to the IT Service Desk and your manager immediately, and no later than 24 hours after you become aware of the loss.

When reporting, please provide:
- Your name and employee ID
- The laptop's asset tag or serial number
- The date, time, and location where it was last seen
- A brief description of how it was lost or stolen

If the laptop was stolen, also file a police report and send a copy to the Security team.

The IT team will remotely lock and wipe the device where possible and will update the asset register. Do not attempt to recover the device yourself.

For questions, contact the IT Service Desk at [phone number] or [email address].
