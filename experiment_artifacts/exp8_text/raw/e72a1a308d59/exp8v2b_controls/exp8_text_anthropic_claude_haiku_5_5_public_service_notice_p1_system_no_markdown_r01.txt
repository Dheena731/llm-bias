PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE

Library Opening Hours

Please note the following hours for your public library, effective immediately:

Monday through Friday: 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
Saturday: 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
Sunday: 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m.

The library will be closed on all major public holidays. Holiday hours may vary, so please check the library's website or call the front desk before your visit.

Our staff is happy to help with book recommendations, research assistance, computer access, and library card registration during all open hours.

For more information, visit our website or contact the main branch during regular business hours. We look forward to welcoming you.
