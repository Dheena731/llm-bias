**Introducing Savings Goals**

You can now set a savings goal in the app. Choose a target amount and a deadline, and the app will show how much you've saved so far and how much is left.

To create a goal, open the Savings tab and tap "New goal." Give it a name, such as "Emergency fund" or "Vacation," and enter your target. Each time you add money to the goal, your progress bar updates. You can edit or delete a goal at any time.

Savings goals are available now on the latest version of the app. Update to get started.
