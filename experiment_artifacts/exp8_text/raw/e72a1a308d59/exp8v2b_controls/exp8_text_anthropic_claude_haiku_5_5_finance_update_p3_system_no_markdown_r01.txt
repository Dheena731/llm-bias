We're excited to announce Savings Goals, a new feature that helps you set, track, and reach your financial targets.

You can now create a goal for anything you're saving toward, whether it's an emergency fund, a vacation, a new laptop, or a down payment. Choose a target amount and a deadline, and the app will show your progress and how much you need to set aside each week or month to stay on track.

Savings Goals also lets you link a goal to a specific account, so your progress updates automatically as deposits come in. You'll receive a gentle reminder if you fall behind and a celebration when you hit your target.

Savings Goals is available now in the latest version of the app. Open the Savings tab and tap "New Goal" to get started.
