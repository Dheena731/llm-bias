Hey there! Friendly reminder: it's time to look back at your fitness progress for the week.

Take a few minutes to check in with yourself:

- **Workouts:** Did you hit the sessions you planned? Note what went well and what got skipped.
- **Energy and recovery:** How did you feel during and after your workouts? Were you sleeping well?
- **Wins:** Did you beat a personal record, finish a tough session, or simply show up when it was hard? Celebrate it.
- **Next steps:** Pick one small adjustment for the coming week, like adding a rest day, trying a new exercise, or setting a earlier workout time.

Progress comes from consistency, not perfection. Every week you review your progress is a week you're getting closer to your goals.
