**PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE**

**New Opening Hours at Maplewood Public Library**

Beginning Monday, the first day of next month, Maplewood Public Library will offer extended hours to better serve our community.

**Regular Hours**
- Monday to Thursday: 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
- Friday: 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
- Saturday: 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- Sunday: 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m.

**Holiday Closures**
The library will be closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. It will close at 3:00 p.m. on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve.

**Good to Know**
- The book drop is available 24 hours a day, every day.
- Computer and printing stations shut down 15 minutes before closing.
- Our online catalog and digital collection, including e-books and audiobooks, can be used at any hour with your library card.

For questions, call the front desk at 555-0142 during opening hours or ask any staff member.

Thank you for supporting your local library.
