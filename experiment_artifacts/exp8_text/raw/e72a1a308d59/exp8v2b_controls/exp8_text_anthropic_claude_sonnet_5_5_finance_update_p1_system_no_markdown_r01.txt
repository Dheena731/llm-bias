Subject: Introducing your new monthly spending summary

Hello,

We're excited to share a new feature: the monthly spending summary. At the start of each month, you'll receive a clear overview of where your money went during the previous one.

The summary shows your total spending, a breakdown by category such as groceries, transportation, dining, and bills, and a comparison with the prior month so you can quickly spot changes. It also highlights your largest individual purchases and any recurring charges, which makes it easier to catch subscriptions you may have forgotten about.

You can find the summary in the Reports section of your account, and we'll also send a copy to your email on the first day of each month. If you'd prefer not to receive the email, you can turn it off in your notification settings at any time.

We built this feature to give you a simple, at-a-glance picture of your finances without needing to dig through individual transactions. We hope it helps you plan ahead and feel more confident about your spending.

As always, we'd love your feedback. Let us know what's working and what you'd like to see added.

Thank you,
