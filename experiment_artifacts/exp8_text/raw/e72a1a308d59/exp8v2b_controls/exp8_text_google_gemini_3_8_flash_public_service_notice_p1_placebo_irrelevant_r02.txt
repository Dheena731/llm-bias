**Public Notice: Library Operating Hours**

The Community Public Library is pleased to welcome patrons for book borrowing, study space, computer access, and community programming during the following hours:

* **Monday – Thursday:** 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM  
* **Friday – Saturday:** 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM  
* **Sunday:** 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM  

**After-Hours Services:**
* The exterior book-return drop box is open 24/7.
* Digital resources—including e-books, audiobooks, and research databases—are accessible anytime at **www.communitylibrary.org**.

For inquiries, renewals, or assistance, please speak with a librarian at the front desk or call **(555) 019-2834** during operating hours.
