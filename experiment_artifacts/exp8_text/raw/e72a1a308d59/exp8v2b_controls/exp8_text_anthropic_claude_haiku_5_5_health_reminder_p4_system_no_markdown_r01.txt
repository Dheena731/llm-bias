Take a Break, Your Eyes and Body Will Thank You

You've been staring at screens for a while now. Pause for a moment and step away. Look at something at least 20 feet away for 20 seconds, stretch your shoulders and neck, and take a few deep breaths. A short break can ease eye strain, reduce fatigue, and help you think more clearly when you return. Get up, grab some water, and give yourself a few minutes to recharge.
