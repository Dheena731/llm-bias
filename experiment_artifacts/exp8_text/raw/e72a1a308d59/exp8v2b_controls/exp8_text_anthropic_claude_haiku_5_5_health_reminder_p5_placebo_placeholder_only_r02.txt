Friendly reminder: it may be time to refill your prescription. Running out of medication can interrupt your treatment, so it's best to request a refill before your supply runs low. Most pharmacies can process refill requests online, through their app, or by phone, and many will text or email you when your medication is ready for pickup.

If you're unsure how many refills you have left or when your prescription expires, check the label on your bottle or contact your pharmacy. If you have any questions about your medication or need a new prescription from your doctor, reach out to your healthcare provider's office before you run out.

Take care of your health by staying ahead of your refills.
