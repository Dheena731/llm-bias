**Weekly Fitness Check-In**

It's time to review your progress for the week. Take a few minutes to look back at:

- **Workouts completed:** How many sessions did you finish compared to your plan?
- **Improvements:** Did you lift more, go farther, or feel stronger than last week?
- **Challenges:** What got in the way, such as time, energy, or motivation?

Use what you find to set one or two realistic goals for the coming week. Small, steady adjustments add up over time, and every week of effort counts.
