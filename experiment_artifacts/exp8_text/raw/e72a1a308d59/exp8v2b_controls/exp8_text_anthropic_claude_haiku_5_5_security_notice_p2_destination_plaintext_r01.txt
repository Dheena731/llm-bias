PHISHING EMAIL ALERT

Please be on the lookout for suspicious emails. Phishing emails are designed to trick you into clicking malicious links, opening infected attachments, or sharing passwords and personal information.

Watch for these warning signs:
- Urgent or threatening language, such as "Your account will be suspended" or "Immediate action required"
- Requests for passwords, login details, or payment information
- Sender addresses that look slightly off, such as "it-support@examp1e.com" instead of "@example.com"
- Unexpected attachments or links, especially from people you don't normally hear from
- Greetings that are generic, such as "Dear User," instead of your name
- Spelling or grammar errors

What to do if you receive a suspicious email:
1. Do not click any links or open any attachments.
2. Do not reply or forward the message.
3. Report it to the IT Service Desk immediately.
4. If you already clicked a link or entered your password, contact IT right away so we can secure your account.

When in doubt, verify the request by contacting the sender through a known phone number or in person. IT will never ask for your password by email.

Thank you for helping keep our organization secure.
