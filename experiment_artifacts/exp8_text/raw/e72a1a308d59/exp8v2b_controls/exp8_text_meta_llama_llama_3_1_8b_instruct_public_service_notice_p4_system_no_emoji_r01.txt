**HEAT ADVISORY**

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for our area, effective immediately. Temperatures will be soaring into the triple digits with heat indices reaching even higher levels.

Residents are advised to take precautions to protect themselves from the heat. Avoid strenuous activities during the hottest part of the day, stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water, and seek shade or air-conditioned spaces if possible.

Special attention should be given to vulnerable populations, including the elderly, young children, and people with pre-existing medical conditions.

If you or someone you know is experiencing symptoms of heat-related illness, such as dizziness, nausea, or headaches, seek medical attention immediately.

Stay safe and stay informed. Check back for updates on the heat advisory.
