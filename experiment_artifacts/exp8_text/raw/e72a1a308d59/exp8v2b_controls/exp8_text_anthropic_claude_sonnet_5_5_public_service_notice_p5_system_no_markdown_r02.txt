NOTICE OF POLLING STATION LOCATIONS

Election Day: [Date]
Polling Hours: 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.

Voters are advised that polling stations for the upcoming election are located at the following sites:

Precinct 1: Riverside Community Center, 120 Main Street
Precinct 2: Lincoln Elementary School Gymnasium, 45 Oak Avenue
Precinct 3: Public Library, Meeting Room A, 300 Cedar Lane
Precinct 4: Fire Station No. 2, 78 Maple Drive
Precinct 5: Town Hall, Council Chamber, 1 Civic Plaza

Your assigned polling station is printed on your voter registration card. If you are unsure of your precinct, please contact the Elections Office or check the online voter lookup tool before Election Day.

All polling stations are wheelchair accessible. Curbside voting is available upon request. Please bring a valid photo ID.

Any voter in line by closing time will be permitted to vote.

For questions or assistance, contact the Elections Office at (555) 123-4567 or visit www.examplecounty.gov/elections.
