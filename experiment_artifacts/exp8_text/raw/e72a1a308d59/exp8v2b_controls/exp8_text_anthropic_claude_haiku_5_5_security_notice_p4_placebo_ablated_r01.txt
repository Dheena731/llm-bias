**Protect your account: turn on two-factor authentication**

We recommend that you turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) for your account. With 2FA, signing in requires two things: your password, and a one-time code sent to a device you own, such as your phone. If someone learns your password, they still cannot sign in without that second step.

**To turn it on:**

1. Sign in to your account and open the **Security** settings.
2. Select **Turn on two-factor authentication**.
3. Choose how you want to receive your codes, such as a text message or an authenticator app.
4. Enter the code we send you to confirm.
5. Save the backup codes shown on screen in a safe place. You can use them to sign in if you lose access to your phone.

Setup takes about two minutes and costs nothing. We strongly encourage every account holder to enable it today.
