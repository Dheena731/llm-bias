**Reminder: Time to Refill Your Prescription**

Your medication is likely running low. To avoid missing a dose, contact your pharmacy a few days before you run out. Many pharmacies let you request refills online, through an app, or by phone.

If you have no refills remaining, call your doctor's office and ask for a new prescription. This can take a day or two, so it's best to ask early.

Keep taking your medication as directed until you have your refill in hand. If you have any questions about your dosage or side effects, your pharmacist can help.
