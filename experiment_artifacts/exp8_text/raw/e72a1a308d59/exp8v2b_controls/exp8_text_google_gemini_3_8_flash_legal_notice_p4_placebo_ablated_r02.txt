**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY**

To qualify for a refund, you must submit a return request within thirty (30) calendar days of the original purchase date. 

To be eligible for a full refund, your purchase must meet all of the following criteria:

1. **Condition:** The item must be unused, undamaged, and in the same condition as received, including all original packaging, tags, and accessories.
2. **Proof of Purchase:** You must provide the original receipt, invoice, or digital order confirmation.

**Non-Refundable Items**
The following purchases are strictly non-refundable:
* Downloadable digital products or software licenses once accessed or downloaded.
* Items explicitly marked as "Final Sale" or "As-Is" at the time of purchase.
* Personalized or custom-made goods.

**Processing**
Upon receipt and inspection of the returned item, we will notify you of your eligibility status. If approved, your refund will be credited to your original payment method within ten (10) business days. Original shipping fees are non-refundable. 

To initiate a return request, please contact Customer Support at support@example.com with your order number.
