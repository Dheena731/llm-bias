Cook your grains or starch ahead of time. On Sunday, make a big batch of rice, quinoa, or roasted potatoes and store it in the fridge in airtight containers. On a busy weeknight, you can reheat a portion in a few minutes while you quickly sauté a protein and some vegetables in one pan. Add a splash of water to the grains before microwaving so they steam back to fluffy instead of drying out. Finish the dish with a squeeze of lemon, a spoonful of pesto, or a drizzle of soy sauce to make it taste freshly made. This way, dinner is on the table in about 15 minutes with very little cleanup.
