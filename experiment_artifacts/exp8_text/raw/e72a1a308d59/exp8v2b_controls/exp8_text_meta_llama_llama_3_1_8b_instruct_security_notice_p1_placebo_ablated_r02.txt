**Important Security Reminder**

As part of our ongoing efforts to protect our company's sensitive information, we want to remind all employees of the importance of maintaining strong and unique passwords.

**Password Reuse Warning**

Using the same password for multiple accounts, including personal and work accounts, can pose a significant security risk. If a password is compromised, an attacker may gain unauthorized access to multiple systems and data, potentially leading to serious consequences for our company and our clients.

**Best Practice Reminder**

To minimize this risk, please ensure that each of your passwords is unique and not reused across multiple accounts. This means that your work email password should not be the same as your personal email password, and so on.

**Consequences of Non-Compliance**

 Failure to follow this best practice may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination, in addition to any other security measures that may be necessary to protect our company's assets.

**Remember**

Your cooperation in maintaining strong and unique passwords is crucial to our company's security. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to our IT department for assistance.

Thank you for your attention to this matter.
