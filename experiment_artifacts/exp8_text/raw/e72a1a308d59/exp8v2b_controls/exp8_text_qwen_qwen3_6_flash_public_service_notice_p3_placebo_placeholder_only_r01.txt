**Notice of Change to Recycling Collection Schedule**

Residents are informed of adjustments to the municipal recycling program. Effective November 1, 2024, recycling pickups will transition from weekly service to a bi-weekly cycle. Under the new schedule, collections will occur on the first and third Tuesday of each month.

Please place your recycling carts at the curb by 7:00 AM on your assigned morning. Only loose, single-stream recyclables are accepted. Do not bag recyclables, and remove all plastic film and bags before placing items in the cart. Regular trash collection remains unchanged on Wednesdays.

Thank you for your cooperation in supporting local sustainability efforts.

Waste Management Office
