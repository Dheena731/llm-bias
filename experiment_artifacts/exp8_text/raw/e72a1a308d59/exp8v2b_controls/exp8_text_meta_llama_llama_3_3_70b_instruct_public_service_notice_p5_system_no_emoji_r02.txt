NOTICE OF POLLING STATION LOCATIONS

Residents are advised that the following locations will serve as polling stations for the upcoming election:

* Community Center, 123 Main Street
* Local Library, 456 Elm Street
* Town Hall, 789 Oak Street

Polling stations will be open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on election day. Voters are reminded to verify their assigned polling station and to bring the required identification and proof of residency. For more information, please contact the local election office.
