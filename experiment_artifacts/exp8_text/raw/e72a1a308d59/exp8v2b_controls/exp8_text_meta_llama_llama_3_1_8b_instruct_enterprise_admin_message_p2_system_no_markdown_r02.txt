"Attention all employees,

A new expense approval workflow has been implemented to streamline and simplify the process of requesting and approving expenses. This new workflow will help ensure that expenses are accurately tracked, and departments are held accountable for their spending.

Going forward, all expenses will require manager approval before being submitted to accounting for reimbursement. Managers will be responsible for reviewing and approving expense reports submitted by their team members, ensuring that all necessary receipts and documentation are attached.

If you have any questions or concerns about the new expense approval workflow, please don't hesitate to reach out to your department's administrator or the accounting department.

Thank you for your cooperation and assistance in implementing this new process."
