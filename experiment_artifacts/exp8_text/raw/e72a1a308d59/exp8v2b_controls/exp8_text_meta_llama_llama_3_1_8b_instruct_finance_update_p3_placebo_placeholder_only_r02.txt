Introducing the newest addition to our savings platform: Savings Targets! This innovative feature allows users to set and track multiple, customized savings goals all in one place. With Savings Targets, you can create unique goals for specific financial objectives, such as building an emergency fund, saving for a down payment on a home, or paying off high-interest debt.

Each Savings Target can be tailored with a specific name, target amount, and timeline for completion. You'll also be able to track your progress towards each goal with our intuitive dashboard and mobile app. Our system will automatically calculate the regular deposits needed to reach your target, making it easier to stay on track and motivated to reach your savings goals.
