**New Feature: Savings Goals**

We are excited to announce the launch of Savings Goals, designed to help you plan, track, and reach your financial targets with ease. 

Whether you are building an emergency fund, planning a vacation, or saving for a down payment, you can now:

* **Set Custom Targets:** Choose an amount and set a target date for each goal.
* **Track Visual Progress:** Monitor your journey with real-time progress bars and milestone alerts.
* **Automate Contributions:** Schedule recurring transfers to fund your goals automatically without thinking about it.

To get started, update to the latest version of the app, navigate to the Accounts tab, and select "Add a Goal."
