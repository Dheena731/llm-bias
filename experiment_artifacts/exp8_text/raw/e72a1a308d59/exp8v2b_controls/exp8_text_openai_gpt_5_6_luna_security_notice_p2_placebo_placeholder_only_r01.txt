**Stay Alert for Phishing Emails**

Phishing emails are designed to trick you into revealing passwords, financial information, or other sensitive data.

- Be cautious with unexpected messages, urgent requests, unfamiliar senders, and suspicious links or attachments.
- Do not click links, open attachments, or enter credentials unless you have verified the message.
- Check the sender’s address carefully and confirm unusual requests through a trusted channel.
- Report suspected phishing using your organization’s email reporting tool or contact the IT Help Desk.
- If you clicked a suspicious link or shared information, notify the IT Help Desk immediately.
