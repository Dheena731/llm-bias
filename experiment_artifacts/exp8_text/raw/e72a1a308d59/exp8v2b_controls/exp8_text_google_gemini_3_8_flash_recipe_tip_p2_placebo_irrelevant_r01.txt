To keep fresh herbs lasting longer, treat them based on their stems:

* **Soft herbs (cilantro, parsley, mint):** Trim the bottoms and place them upright in a small jar with an inch of water, like a bouquet of flowers. Cover loosely with a plastic bag and store in the fridge (except for basil, which prefers room temperature on the counter).
* **Hard/woody herbs (rosemary, thyme, oregano):** Wrap them loosely in a slightly damp paper towel and place them in a resealable plastic bag or airtight container in your refrigerator's crisper drawer.
