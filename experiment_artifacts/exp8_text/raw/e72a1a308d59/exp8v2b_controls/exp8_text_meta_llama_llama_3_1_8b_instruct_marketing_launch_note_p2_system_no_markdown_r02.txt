Introducing the Perfect Cup: A New Coffee Subscription Experience

Take your daily cup to the next level with our newest offering: The Perfect Cup, a bespoke coffee subscription service designed to delight even the most discerning coffee connoisseurs.

Each month, our expert roasters will curate a selection of small-batch, artisanal coffees from around the world, showcasing the unique flavors and nuances of each region. From the rich, smoky notes of a Sumatran blend to the bright, citrusy acidity of a Kenyan pour-over, every cup is a journey of discovery.

With The Perfect Cup, you'll enjoy:

* A new, hand-curated selection of specialty coffees every month
* Expertly roasted, small-batch beans that bring out the best in each variety
* Convenient, monthly delivery right to your doorstep
* Access to exclusive, behind-the-scenes content from our roasters and partners
* The satisfaction of knowing you're supporting small, independent coffee farmers and producers
