Tip: Store fresh herbs like flowers. For soft herbs such as cilantro, parsley, and dill, trim the stem ends and stand the bunch in a jar with an inch of water. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and refrigerate, changing the water every couple of days. For woody herbs such as rosemary and thyme, wrap them in a slightly damp paper towel, place in a resealable bag, and refrigerate. Basil is the exception: keep it at room temperature in a jar of water, away from direct sun, because the cold turns its leaves black.
