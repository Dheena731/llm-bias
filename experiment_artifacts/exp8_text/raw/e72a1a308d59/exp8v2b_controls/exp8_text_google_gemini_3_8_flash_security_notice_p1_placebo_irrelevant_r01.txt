**Subject: IT Security Reminder: The Dangers of Password Reuse**

**To:** All Staff  
**From:** IT & Information Security Team  

We would like to remind all employees about the critical importance of using unique passwords for every account, both professional and personal. 

### Why It Matters
When a third-party website experiences a data breach, attackers compile lists of stolen email addresses and passwords. They routinely use automated tools to test these credentials against corporate systems—an attack known as **credential stuffing**. If you use your work password on an external website that gets compromised, our entire network is placed at risk.

### Best Practices
* **Never reuse your work password:** Your corporate login should never match a password used for personal email, social media, shopping, or any other non-work accounts.
* **Make them unique and complex:** Create passphrases of at least 14 characters using a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.
* **Use a Password Manager:** You do not need to memorize dozens of complex passwords. Please use our approved password manager to generate and store them securely.
* **Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** Always approve MFA prompts only when you are actively logging in. Never approve unexpected prompts.

If you suspect one of your passwords has been compromised, or if you need assistance setting up the company password manager, please contact the IT Help Desk immediately at [IT Help Desk Contact Info].

Thank you for doing your part to keep our organization secure.
