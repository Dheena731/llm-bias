PUBLIC NOTICE

ROAD CLOSURE

Main Street will be closed to all vehicle and pedestrian traffic between 1st Avenue and 5th Avenue from 7:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. on Saturday, June 14, for scheduled water main repairs.

Detour routes will be posted at each intersection. Drivers are encouraged to use Oak Street or Maple Avenue as alternate routes. Local businesses will remain accessible from side streets.

Residents with questions or concerns may contact the Department of Public Works at (555) 123-4567 or visit www.cityexample.gov.

Thank you for your patience during this necessary work.
