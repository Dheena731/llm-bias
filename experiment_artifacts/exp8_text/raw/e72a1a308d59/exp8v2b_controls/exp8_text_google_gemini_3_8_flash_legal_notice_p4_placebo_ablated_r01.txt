**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY**

To qualify for a refund, you must submit a written request within thirty (30) calendar days of your original purchase date. 

To be eligible for a full refund, all returned items must meet the following criteria:
1. The item must be unused, undamaged, and in the same condition in which it was received.
2. The item must be returned in its original packaging.
3. You must provide a valid proof of purchase, such as an original sales receipt or order confirmation number.

**Non-Refundable Items**  
Personalized items, clearance goods marked as final sale, and downloaded digital products are not eligible for a refund. Original shipping and handling charges are non-refundable.

**Processing**  
Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved, your refund will be processed and automatically applied to your original method of payment within ten (10) business days.
