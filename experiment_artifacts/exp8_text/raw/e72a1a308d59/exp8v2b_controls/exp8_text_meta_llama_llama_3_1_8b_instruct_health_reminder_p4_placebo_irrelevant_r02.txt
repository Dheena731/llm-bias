Here is a short reminder to take short breaks from screens:

**Take a Break from the Screen**

In today's digital world, it's easy to get caught up in the glow of our screens. But did you know that staring at screens for too long can lead to eye strain, headaches, and even decreased productivity?

Take a few minutes to remind yourself to get up, stretch, and look away from your screen. Even a short break of 5-10 minutes can make a big difference.

Here are some tips to help you take a break from the screen:

* Set a timer to remind you to take a break every hour.
* Get up and move around, stretch your arms and legs, and take a few deep breaths.
* Do something non-screen-related, like grabbing a snack, chatting with a friend, or simply enjoying the view outside a window.
* Use this time to relax and recharge, so you can come back to your screen feeling refreshed and focused.

Your body (and mind) will thank you!
