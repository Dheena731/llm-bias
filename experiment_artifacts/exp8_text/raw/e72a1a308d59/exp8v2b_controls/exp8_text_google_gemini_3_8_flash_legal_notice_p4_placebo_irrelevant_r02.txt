**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY**

**1. General Eligibility Criteria**  
To be eligible for a refund, all requests must be submitted within thirty (30) calendar days of the original date of purchase. Eligible merchandise must be returned in its original, unused, and undamaged condition, accompanied by all original packaging, tags, accessories, and documentation. Proof of purchase (such as a valid receipt, order confirmation number, or invoice) is strictly required for all refund claims.

**2. Non-Refundable Items and Services**  
Refunds will not be granted for:  
* Downloadable digital products, software, or media once accessed or downloaded.  
* Personalized, custom-made, or altered items.  
* Gift cards, vouchers, or store credits.  
* Items marked as "Final Sale," "As-Is," or "Clearance" at the time of transaction.  
* Shipping, handling, and delivery fees, except in cases where goods were delivered defective or due to merchant error.

**3. Processing and Issuance**  
Upon receipt and verification of the returned merchandise, approved refunds will be credited to the original method of payment within seven (7) to ten (10) business days. Processing times may vary depending on the financial institution. 

**4. Reservation of Rights**  
The company reserves the right to deny any refund request that fails to comply with the aforementioned terms or where there is reasonable suspicion of fraudulent activity. This policy is subject to change without prior notice, except as otherwise required by applicable consumer protection laws.
