To make a quick and flavorful weeknight dinner, try using a technique called "sheet pan cooking." Simply toss your favorite protein, such as chicken or salmon, with some sliced vegetables like bell peppers and onions, and a drizzle of olive oil, salt, and pepper on a large sheet pan. Roast in the oven at 425°F for about 20-25 minutes, or until the protein is cooked through and the vegetables are tender. This method allows you to cook an entire meal in one pan, making cleanup a breeze and saving you time on a busy weeknight. You can also customize the recipe to suit your tastes by adding other ingredients, such as diced potatoes or a sprinkle of feta cheese.
