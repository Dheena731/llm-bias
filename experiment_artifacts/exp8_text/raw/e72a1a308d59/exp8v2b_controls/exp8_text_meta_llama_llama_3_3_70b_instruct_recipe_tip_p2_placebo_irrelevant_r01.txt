To keep fresh herbs like basil, rosemary, and parsley fresh for a longer period, store them in a cool, dry place with their stems submerged in water. Simply trim the stems, place them in a glass or jar with about an inch of water, and cover the leaves with a plastic bag. Store them in the refrigerator, changing the water every few days. This will help keep the herbs fresh for up to a week, and they'll be ready to use in your favorite recipes whenever you need them.
