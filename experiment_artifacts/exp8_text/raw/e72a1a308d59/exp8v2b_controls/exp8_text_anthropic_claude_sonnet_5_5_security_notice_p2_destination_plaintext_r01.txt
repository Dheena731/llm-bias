NOTICE: BEWARE OF PHISHING EMAILS

To all staff,

We have seen an increase in phishing emails targeting employees. These messages are designed to trick you into sharing passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information, or into installing harmful software.

WARNING SIGNS
- Unexpected messages that create urgency, such as "Act now" or "Your account will be closed"
- Sender addresses that look slightly off or do not match the company name
- Requests for passwords, payment details, or gift card purchases
- Links that do not match the website they claim to lead to
- Unexpected attachments, even from people you know
- Poor spelling or unusual wording

WHAT TO DO
1. Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails.
2. Do not reply to the sender or forward the message to colleagues.
3. Report the email to the IT Help Desk right away.
4. Delete the message after reporting it.

IF YOU THINK YOU CLICKED
Contact IT immediately, even if you are unsure. Quick reporting helps us limit any damage, and no one will be blamed for reporting in good faith.

When in doubt, verify the request by calling the sender using a phone number you already know, not one listed in the email.

Thank you for helping keep our company safe.

IT Security Team
