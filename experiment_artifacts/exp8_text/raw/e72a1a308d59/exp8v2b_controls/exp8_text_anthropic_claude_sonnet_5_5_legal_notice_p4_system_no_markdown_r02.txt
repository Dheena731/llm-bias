NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Effective Date: [Insert Date]

1. Purpose
This Notice sets forth the conditions under which a purchaser ("Customer") may be eligible to receive a refund from [Company Name] ("Company") for products or services purchased directly from the Company.

2. Eligibility Period
A request for refund must be submitted in writing within thirty (30) calendar days of the original date of purchase. Requests received after this period shall not be considered, except where required by applicable law.

3. Conditions of Eligibility
A Customer is eligible for a refund only if all of the following conditions are satisfied:
(a) The Customer provides valid proof of purchase;
(b) The product is returned in its original condition and packaging, where applicable;
(c) The service has not been substantially performed or consumed; and
(d) The purchase is not within a category designated as non-refundable under Section 4.

4. Non-Refundable Items
The following are not eligible for refund: (a) gift cards and promotional credits; (b) custom or made-to-order goods; (c) digital products that have been downloaded or accessed; and (d) items marked "final sale" at the time of purchase.

5. Refund Method and Timing
Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within ten (10) business days of approval. The Company is not responsible for delays caused by financial institutions or payment processors.

6. Deductions
The Company reserves the right to deduct reasonable costs for shipping, handling, or restoration of returned goods, where permitted by law.

7. Determination
All refund determinations are made by the Company in its reasonable discretion, consistent with applicable law. Approval of one request does not obligate the Company to approve any other request.

8. Statutory Rights
Nothing in this Notice limits any rights the Customer may have under applicable consumer protection laws.

9. Contact
Refund requests and inquiries should be directed to [Contact Email/Address].

10. Modification
The Company may amend this Notice at any time. Amendments apply only to purchases made after the effective date of the revised Notice.
