**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication Today**

To better protect your account, we strongly encourage you to turn on two-factor authentication (2FA). With 2FA enabled, signing in requires both your password and a one-time verification code, so a stolen or guessed password alone won't give anyone access.

**How to enable it:**

1. Sign in and open **Settings**.
2. Select **Security**, then choose **Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Pick your verification method: an authenticator app (recommended) or text message.
4. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm your setup.
5. Save your backup codes in a secure place, such as a password manager.

**Why it matters:** Most account break-ins involve compromised passwords. 2FA adds a second barrier that stops the vast majority of these attempts.

**Tips:**
- Never share your verification codes with anyone, including people claiming to be support staff.
- If you change your phone number or device, update your 2FA settings right away.

Setup takes only a few minutes, and it's one of the most effective steps you can take to keep your account safe. Please enable it today.
