Subject: Updated Expense Approval Workflow Now Live

Team,

We are implementing a revised expense approval workflow effective today. This new process automates routing and eliminates paper forms by requiring all submissions through the central expense management platform. Receipts must be attached digitally before sending requests for review. Supervisors will receive instant alerts when items reach their queue, and approved expenses will integrate directly with payroll processing. Full training materials and quick-reference guides are posted in the shared workspace under the Finance tab. The accounting desk remains available to assist with setup questions or access issues. Thank you for adapting quickly to these improvements.
