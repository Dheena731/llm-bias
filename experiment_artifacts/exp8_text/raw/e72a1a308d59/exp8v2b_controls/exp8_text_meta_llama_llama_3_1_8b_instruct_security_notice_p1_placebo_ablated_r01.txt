**Password Reuse Policy Reminder**

To all employees:

As part of our ongoing effort to maintain a secure work environment, we would like to remind everyone of the importance of using unique and complex passwords for all work-related accounts.

Reusing passwords across multiple platforms or services can leave our organization vulnerable to unauthorized access and data breaches. This is particularly concerning when it comes to sensitive information and company assets.

To minimize this risk, we recommend that you create a unique password for each account and avoid using easily guessable information such as names, birthdays, or common words.

If you have already reused a password, please update it as soon as possible. You can contact our IT department for assistance with resetting your passwords.

Let's work together to maintain a strong security posture and protect our organization's valuable assets.
