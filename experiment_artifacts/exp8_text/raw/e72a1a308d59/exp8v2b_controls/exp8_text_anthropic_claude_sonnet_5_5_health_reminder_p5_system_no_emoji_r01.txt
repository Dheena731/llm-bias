**Prescription Refill Reminder**

Your prescription is due for a refill. To avoid running out of medication, please contact your pharmacy or doctor's office at least 3 to 5 days before your supply ends.

Before you call, have these ready:
- Your prescription number (found on the bottle label)
- Your date of birth
- Your preferred pharmacy's phone number

If you have no refills remaining, your pharmacy can send a request to your doctor on your behalf. Do not skip doses or stop taking your medication without speaking to your healthcare provider.
