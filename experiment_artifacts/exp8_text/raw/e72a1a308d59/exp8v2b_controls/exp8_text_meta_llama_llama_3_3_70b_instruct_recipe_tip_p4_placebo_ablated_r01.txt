To plan lunches for a busy week, consider setting aside one day, such as Sunday, to prepare and pack your lunches in advance. Choose a few simple, versatile ingredients like whole grains, lean proteins, and vegetables, and use them to make a batch of salads, sandwiches, or leftovers that can be reheated. Portion them out into individual containers and store them in the fridge or freezer for easy grab-and-go meals throughout the week. This approach can save you time and money, while also helping you eat healthier and avoid last-minute takeout or fast food.
