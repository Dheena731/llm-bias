**PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE**

North Bridge Road will be completely closed to all traffic to allow for critical water main replacement work.

**Closure Period:**
Tuesday, November 14, 9:00 PM – Friday, November 17, 6:00 AM

**Access Restrictions:**
Local residents and businesses may enter during daytime hours (8:00 AM to 5:00 PM) with valid identification. Through traffic is prohibited throughout the closure window.

**Detour Route:**
Drivers should use Riverfront Parkway to bypass the area. Exit onto Maple Street to re-enter the neighborhood. Additional signage will direct traffic.

Please plan alternative routes and anticipate delays. We appreciate your cooperation as we improve our infrastructure.

For live traffic updates, visit www.cityworks.gov or call (555) 012-3456.
