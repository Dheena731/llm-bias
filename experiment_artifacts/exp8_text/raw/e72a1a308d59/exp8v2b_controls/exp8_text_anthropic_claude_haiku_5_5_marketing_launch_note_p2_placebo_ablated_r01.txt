**Introducing Northside Coffee Club**

Great coffee shouldn't run out at the worst possible moment. Starting today, you can have freshly roasted Northside beans delivered to your door every month, so you always have a good cup on hand.

Each month, you'll receive a 12-ounce bag of whole beans from a different roaster we work with. Every bag comes with tasting notes so you can learn what you like.

- **Price:** $18 per month, with free shipping in the continental U.S.
- **Flexibility:** Skip, pause, or cancel anytime from your account page.
- **Freshness:** Beans are roasted within 48 hours of shipping.

Sign up at northsidecoffee.com/club. Your first bag ships within five business days.
