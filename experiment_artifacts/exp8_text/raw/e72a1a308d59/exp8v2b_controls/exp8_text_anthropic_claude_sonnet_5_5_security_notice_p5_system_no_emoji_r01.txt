**Notice: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, report it immediately. Prompt reporting helps protect company data and reduces security risks.

**What to do:**

1. **Notify IT Security right away.** Contact the IT Help Desk at [phone number] or [email address]. Do not wait until the next business day.
2. **Inform your manager.** Let your supervisor know as soon as possible.
3. **File a police report if the laptop was stolen.** Keep a copy of the report number for company records.
4. **Provide details.** Be ready to share the device's asset tag or serial number, the date, time, and location of the loss, and a description of any data or applications that may have been stored on it.
5. **Change your passwords.** Update the passwords for your company accounts from a secure device, if instructed by IT.

**What IT will do:**

- Remotely lock or wipe the device where possible
- Disable your account access as needed
- Assess whether any sensitive data was exposed
- Arrange a replacement laptop

**Important reminders:**

- Reports should be made within 24 hours of discovering the loss.
- Do not attempt to recover a stolen device yourself.
- Employees who report promptly will not be penalized for honest mistakes. Failure to report may result in disciplinary action.

For questions, contact the IT Help Desk at [contact information].
