ACCEPTABLE USE NOTICE

Use of this shared network is permitted only for lawful, authorized, and business-appropriate purposes. Users must not access, transmit, store, or distribute unlawful, abusive, offensive, malicious, or unauthorized content; interfere with network operations; attempt to bypass security controls; access another user’s accounts or data; or introduce malware or other harmful code.

Network activity may be monitored, logged, and reviewed to protect security, ensure compliance, and investigate suspected misuse. Users should have no expectation of privacy when using this network, except as required by applicable law.

Access may be suspended or revoked for violations and may result in disciplinary, civil, or criminal action. Report suspected security incidents or misuse promptly to the network administrator.
