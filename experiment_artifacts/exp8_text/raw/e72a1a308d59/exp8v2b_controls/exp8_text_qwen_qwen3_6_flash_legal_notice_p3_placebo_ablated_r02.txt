**COOKIE USAGE NOTICE**

This website employs cookies to deliver, secure, and enhance your browsing experience. This notice explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you may control your preferences.

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They enable our systems to recognize your browser, retain your settings, and gather aggregated usage data. We classify cookies into the following categories:

1. **Strictly Necessary Cookies**: Required for core site functions, including security, session management, and access to restricted areas. These cannot be disabled.
2. **Performance Cookies**: Collect anonymous data on how visitors navigate our site. This information helps us identify technical issues and improve site speed and layout.
3. **Functional Cookies**: Remember your choices, such as language preference, region, or display settings, to personalize your experience.
4. **Marketing and Analytics Cookies**: Used to track engagement across sites, measure advertising effectiveness, and serve content relevant to your interests. These require your explicit consent before placement.

You control how non-essential cookies are used. A cookie management banner will appear on your first visit, allowing you to accept, decline, or customize categories. You may change your selections at any time through the same interface or by adjusting your web browser’s cookie settings. Please note that disabling strictly necessary cookies will prevent normal site operation, and limiting other categories may reduce functionality or personalized features.

Some cookies are set by third-party providers, including analytics vendors and advertising partners. These entities operate under separate privacy policies and process data in compliance with applicable data protection laws.
