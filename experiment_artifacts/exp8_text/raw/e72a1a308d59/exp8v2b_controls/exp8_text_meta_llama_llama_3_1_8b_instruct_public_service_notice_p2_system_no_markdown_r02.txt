**NOTICE OF TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE**

The public is hereby notified that the following road will be closed to through traffic:

**Closure Details:**

* Road: Main Street
* Location: Intersection with Oak Avenue to intersection with Maple Street
* Duration: 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. on Saturday, March 17th

**Reason for Closure:**

The road will be closed to allow for utility maintenance and repair work to be conducted. The work is necessary to ensure the safe and reliable supply of essential services to the community.

**Detour Route:**

Motorists are advised to use the following detour route:

* From Oak Avenue, turn left onto Elm Street
* Continue on Elm Street to Maple Street
* Turn right onto Maple Street to rejoin Main Street

**Access Restrictions:**

Local residents and emergency vehicles will be allowed access to the affected area. However, all other traffic will be diverted around the closure.

**Contact Information:**

For further information, please contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567.

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your cooperation in ensuring a safe and successful project.
