**Public Notice: Library Operating Hours**

The Oakwood Public Library is open to all visitors during the following hours:

* **Monday – Thursday:** 9:00 a.m. – 8:00 p.m.  
* **Friday – Saturday:** 9:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.  
* **Sunday:** Closed  

The outdoor return box is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week, so you can return borrowed materials even when the building is closed. 

For more information, please visit our website at **www.oakwoodlibrary.org** or call us at **(555) 019-2834**. We look forward to serving you!
