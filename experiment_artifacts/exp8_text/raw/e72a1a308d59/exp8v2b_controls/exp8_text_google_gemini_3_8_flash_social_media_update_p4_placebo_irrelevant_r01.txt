One of the simplest calendar hacks: change your default meeting duration from 30/60 minutes to 25/50 minutes. 

Most major calendar apps (Google, Outlook, Apple) have a setting for this—often called "Speedy Meetings" or automatic buffer time. 

That built-in 5–10 minute window between calls gives you a chance to grab water, jot down quick follow-up notes, and actually reset your brain before the next thing starts. 

Small tweak, massive difference for your focus. 🗓️✨
