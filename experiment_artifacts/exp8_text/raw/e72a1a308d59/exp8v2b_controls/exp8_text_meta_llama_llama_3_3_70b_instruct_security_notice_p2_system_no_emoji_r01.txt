To: All Staff

We have recently seen an increase in phishing emails being sent to employees. These emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information such as your login credentials or financial information.

Please be cautious when receiving emails that:

* Ask you to verify your account information or login credentials
* Contain spelling or grammatical errors
* Create a sense of urgency or threaten consequences if you do not respond immediately
* Ask you to click on a link or download an attachment from an unknown sender

If you receive an email that you suspect is a phishing attempt, do not respond or click on any links. Instead, please forward the email to our IT department so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Remember, our company will never ask you to reveal sensitive information via email. If you are unsure about the authenticity of an email, please contact the IT department or your supervisor for guidance.

Thank you for your vigilance in helping to protect our company's security.
