Introducing Saved Searches

You asked, we listened. You can now save your favorite search filters and get notified when new results match them.

Here is what's new:
- Save any search with a single tap
- Get alerts as soon as new matches appear
- Manage all your saved searches in one place

Saved Searches is rolling out today to all users. Update your app to try it out, and let us know what you think in the comments.
