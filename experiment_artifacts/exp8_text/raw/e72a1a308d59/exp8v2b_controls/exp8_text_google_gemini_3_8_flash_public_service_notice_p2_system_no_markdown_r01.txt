PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE

Notice is hereby given that Main Street, between 1st Avenue and 4th Avenue, will be temporarily closed to all through traffic.

Dates and Times:
Monday, October 14, through Wednesday, October 16
Daily from 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM

Reason for Closure:
Scheduled utility maintenance and street resurfacing.

Detour Route:
Traffic will be redirected along Oak Street. Detour signs will be prominently displayed, and flaggers will be on-site to assist with traffic flow. Local access will be maintained for residents and emergency vehicles.

Motorists are advised to plan alternate routes and allow for extra travel time.

For inquiries or further information, please contact the City Department of Public Works at 555-0144.
