Subject: New Monthly Spending Summary Now Available

Hi everyone,

We've added a monthly spending summary to help you see where your money goes at a glance. Starting this month, you'll find it in the Reports section of your account.

What it shows:
- Total spending for the month
- A breakdown by category, such as groceries, transportation, and bills
- A comparison with the previous month so you can spot changes
- Your top five merchants by spending

How it works:
The summary is generated automatically on the first day of each month and covers the full previous month. You don't need to set anything up. If you'd like a copy for your records, you can download it as a PDF or CSV file.

Optional email delivery:
If you'd rather receive the summary in your inbox, go to Settings, choose Notifications, and turn on Monthly Summary Email.

A few notes:
- Transactions that are still pending will appear in the following month's summary once they post.
- Categories are assigned automatically, and you can change any transaction's category to keep your summary accurate.

We hope this makes budgeting a little easier. If you have questions or suggestions, please reach out to our support team. We'd love to hear your feedback.
