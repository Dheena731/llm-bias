NOTICE TO ALL EMPLOYEES

Subject: Phishing Email Awareness

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain a secure and safe work environment, we would like to remind everyone of the importance of being cautious when receiving emails from unknown sources.

Phishing emails are a common method used by scammers to trick individuals into divulging sensitive information or installing malware on their devices. These emails often appear to be from a legitimate source, but are actually designed to deceive and exploit.

Some common characteristics of phishing emails include:

*   Urgent or threatening tone
*   Requests for sensitive information (e.g. passwords, credit card numbers)
*   Suspicious links or attachments
*   Misspellings or grammatical errors

If you receive an email that appears to be a phishing attempt, please do not respond or click on any links. Instead, report the email to the IT department immediately. We will verify the authenticity of the email and take necessary action to prevent any potential harm.

Let's work together to maintain a safe and secure work environment. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or the IT department.
