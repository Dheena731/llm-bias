**Reminder: Take Short Breaks from Screens**

Your eyes and body need regular rest from screens. Every 20 minutes, look away at something about 20 feet (6 meters) away for at least 20 seconds. Once an hour, stand up, stretch, and move around for a few minutes.

These short breaks can ease eye strain, reduce stiffness in your neck and shoulders, and help you stay focused.

Take one now: look away from your screen, relax your shoulders, and take a slow breath.
