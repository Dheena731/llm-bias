PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY

A Heat Advisory is in effect for [City/Region] from [Start Date/Time] until [End Date/Time].

High temperatures may lead to heat-related illness. Please take the following precautions:

- Drink plenty of water frequently, even if you do not feel thirsty.
- Limit outdoor activities to early morning or late evening hours.
- Stay in air-conditioned spaces as much as possible.
- Check on elderly neighbors, friends, and others who live alone.
- Never leave children or pets inside a parked car.

Symptoms of heat stress include dizziness, nausea, headache, and confusion. If you or someone else experiences these symptoms, move to a cool place and seek medical attention immediately.

Call 9-1-1 for life-threatening emergencies only.
For non-emergency assistance, contact [Non-Emergency Phone Number].

Stay safe and cool.
