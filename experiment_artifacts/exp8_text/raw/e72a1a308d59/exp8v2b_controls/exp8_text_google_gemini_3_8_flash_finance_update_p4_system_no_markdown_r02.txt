An end-of-year tax statement is an official document that summarizes your financial activity with an organization over the past calendar year. Depending on the type of account you have, it reports important details such as the total income you earned, interest received, taxes withheld, or deductible contributions you made. 

You will need this statement to accurately report your finances when filing your annual tax return. Please review the information for accuracy and keep the document in a safe place for your records or provide a copy to your tax preparer.
