Welcome to Words Between Us, the podcast for anyone who has ever wanted to speak another language with confidence.

Each week, we sit down with native speakers, teachers, and fellow learners to explore the sounds, stories, and everyday expressions that make a language come alive. No judgment, no jargon, just real conversations that help you listen better and speak sooner.

In our first episode, we talk about why mistakes are the fastest route to fluency, and we share three simple habits you can start today. Whether you are learning your first phrase or polishing a language you have studied for years, you are in the right place.
