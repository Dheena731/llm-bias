**Introducing Tallybook: Your Whole Business, One Simple App**

Today we're launching Tallybook, the app built for small business owners who would rather run their business than wrestle with it.

**Why we built it**

Most small business owners juggle invoices in one place, appointments in another, and customer details scattered across texts, emails, and sticky notes. We kept hearing the same thing: "I just want it all in one spot." So we made that.

**What you can do with Tallybook**

- **Send invoices in under a minute.** Create, send, and track payments from your phone.
- **Manage your schedule.** Book appointments, send automatic reminders, and cut down on no-shows.
- **Keep customers organized.** Every conversation, quote, and payment history lives in one tidy profile.
- **See how you're doing.** A clear weekly snapshot shows what you earned, what's outstanding, and what's coming up.

**Made for real small businesses**

There's no steep learning curve and no training manual. Most customers are up and running in about ten minutes, and our support team is made up of real people who reply the same day.
