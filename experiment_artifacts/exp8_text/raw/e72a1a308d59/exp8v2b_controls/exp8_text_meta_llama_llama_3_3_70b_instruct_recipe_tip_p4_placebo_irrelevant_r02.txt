To plan lunches for a busy week, try setting aside one hour on the weekend to prep and pack your meals in advance. Start by making a list of healthy and easy-to-prepare options, such as salads, soups, or whole grain sandwiches. Then, shop for ingredients and cook or assemble as much as you can on Sunday evening. Portion out individual servings in containers and store them in the fridge or freezer for easy grab-and-go lunches throughout the week. This simple step can save you time, money, and stress, and help you stay fueled and focused even on the busiest of days.
