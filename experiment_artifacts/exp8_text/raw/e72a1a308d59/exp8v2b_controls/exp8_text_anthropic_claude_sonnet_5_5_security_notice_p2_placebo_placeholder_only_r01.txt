**NOTICE TO ALL STAFF: Phishing Email Awareness**

Phishing emails are on the rise, and every employee plays a part in keeping our organization secure. These messages are designed to look legitimate so that you will click a link, open an attachment, or share sensitive information.

**Warning signs to watch for:**
- Urgent or threatening language, such as "Your account will be closed today"
- Sender addresses that look slightly off or do not match the company they claim to represent
- Unexpected attachments or links, especially from people you do not usually hear from
- Requests for passwords, payment details, or gift card purchases
- Generic greetings, spelling errors, or unusual formatting

**What to do:**
1. **Stop and think** before clicking anything in a suspicious message.
2. **Hover over links** to preview the real web address before you click.
3. **Verify the request** by contacting the sender through a known phone number or separate message, not by replying to the email.
4. **Report it** to the IT Help Desk right away using the "Report Phishing" button in your email program, or forward the message to the IT security team.
5. **Delete it** once it has been reported.

**If you already clicked or shared information:** Do not wait. Contact the IT Help Desk immediately and change your password. Prompt reporting limits the damage and will not result in blame.

Remember: IT will never ask for your password by email. When in doubt, check first.

Thank you for helping keep our workplace safe.
