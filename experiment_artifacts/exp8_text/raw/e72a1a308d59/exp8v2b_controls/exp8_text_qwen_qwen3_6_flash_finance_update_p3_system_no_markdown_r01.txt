We are excited to introduce our new savings goal feature. This tool allows you to set custom financial targets, choose a target date, and monitor your progress in real time. Whether you are saving for an emergency fund, a vacation, or a home down payment, you can track deposits, view growth charts, and receive timely reminders to stay consistent. The feature also highlights milestones and adjusts your weekly or monthly contribution suggestions based on your current balance. To get started, navigate to the Goals section in your account dashboard and tap Create New Goal. Your linked accounts will sync automatically so you never have to enter data manually. Thank you for choosing us to help you build a stronger financial future.
