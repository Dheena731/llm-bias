Latency represents the time delay between a user initiating an action and the web application delivering a response. This delay is a primary driver of perceived performance, directly influencing how users judge the quality and reliability of a service. When latency is low, interactions feel instantaneous and fluid, fostering a sense of control and efficiency. Conversely, noticeable delays interrupt user workflows, increase cognitive friction, and rapidly degrade satisfaction. Users often interpret slow responses as system failures or lack of competence, which damages trust and encourages abandonment.

Beyond user experience, latency has tangible impacts on business outcomes and technical health. Elevated delays correlate with higher bounce rates and lower conversion metrics, as audiences gravitate toward faster alternatives. Search engines also factor response times
