**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

**Issued by:** [City/County Name] Office of Emergency Management
**Effective:** [Day, Date] from [Start Time] to [End Time]

**Conditions:** Daytime temperatures are expected to reach 95 to 105 degrees Fahrenheit, with heat index values up to 110 degrees. Overnight lows will offer little relief.

**Who is most at risk:** Older adults, infants and young children, people with chronic health conditions, outdoor workers, and those without air conditioning.

**Recommended precautions:**
- Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty.
- Stay indoors in air-conditioned spaces whenever possible.
- Limit outdoor activity to early morning or evening hours.
- Wear lightweight, light-colored, loose-fitting clothing.
- Never leave children, pets, or vulnerable adults in a parked vehicle, even briefly.
- Check on neighbors, friends, and relatives who live alone.

**Warning signs of heat illness:** Heavy sweating, dizziness, nausea, headache, muscle cramps, or confusion. If someone has a high body temperature, hot dry skin, or loses consciousness, call 911 immediately.
