Effective Date: [Insert Date]

PLEASE READ THIS NOTICE CAREFULLY. IT GOVERNS THE CONDITIONS UNDER WHICH REFUNDS MAY BE GRANTED BY [COMPANY NAME] ("the Company").

Section 1. Eligibility Period
A request for refund must be submitted in writing within thirty (30) calendar days of the original date of purchase ("the Eligibility Period"). Requests received after the Eligibility Period shall be deemed ineligible and may be denied without further review.

Section 2. Qualifying Conditions
A refund may be granted only where the purchaser demonstrates that (a) the product or service was materially defective or not delivered as described; (b) the purchaser has provided valid proof of purchase; and (c) the product has not been used beyond reasonable testing, or the service has not been substantially performed.

Section 3. Non-Refundable Items
The following are not eligible for refund: digital goods that have been downloaded or accessed; custom or personalized orders; items marked "final sale"; and fees for services already rendered in full.

Section 4. Procedure
Refund requests must be sent to [email address] and must include the order number, the purchaser's name, and a statement of the reason for the request. The Company will respond within ten (10) business days of receipt.

Section 5. Method and Timing of Refund
Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within fourteen (14) business days of approval. Shipping, handling, and processing fees are non-refundable unless the refund is due to Company error.

Section 6. Reservation of Rights
The Company reserves the right to deny any refund request that appears fraudulent, abusive, or inconsistent with this Notice. Nothing in this Notice limits any rights afforded to the purchaser under applicable consumer protection law.

Section 7. Modifications
The Company may amend this Notice at any time. Amendments apply only to purchases made after the effective date of the amendment.
