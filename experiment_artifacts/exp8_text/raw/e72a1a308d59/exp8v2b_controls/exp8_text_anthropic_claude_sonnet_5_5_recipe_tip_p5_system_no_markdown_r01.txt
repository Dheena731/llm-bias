Season your vegetables twice: toss them with oil and a generous pinch of salt before roasting so the flavor sinks in and the edges caramelize, then finish with a light sprinkle of flaky salt and a squeeze of lemon juice or a splash of vinegar right after they come out of the oven. The early seasoning builds depth, and the final touch adds brightness that makes the whole dish taste fresher. For extra flavor, add delicate herbs like parsley or basil at the end, since they burn easily in high heat.
