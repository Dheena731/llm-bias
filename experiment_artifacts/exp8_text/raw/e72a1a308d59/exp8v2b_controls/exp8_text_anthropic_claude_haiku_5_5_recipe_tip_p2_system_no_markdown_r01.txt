Wrap fresh herbs like parsley, cilantro, or basil in a slightly damp paper towel, then place them in a loosely sealed plastic bag or container in the fridge. Hardy herbs such as rosemary and thyme keep well when wrapped in a damp towel alone. For basil, which is sensitive to cold, store the stems in a glass of water on the counter, away from direct sunlight, and change the water every couple of days.
