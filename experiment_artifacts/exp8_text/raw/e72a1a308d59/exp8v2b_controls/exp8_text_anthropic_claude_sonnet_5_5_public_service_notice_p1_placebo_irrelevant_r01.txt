**Updated Library Opening Hours**

Effective Monday, the Central Library will be changing its opening hours to better serve our community.

**New Hours**

- Monday to Thursday: 9:00 a.m. – 8:00 p.m.
- Friday: 9:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.
- Saturday: 10:00 a.m. – 4:00 p.m.
- Sunday: Closed

**Please Note**

- The library will be closed on all public holidays.
- Book returns can be made at any time using the outdoor drop box.
- Computer and Wi-Fi access are available during all opening hours.
- Study rooms may be reserved at the front desk or online up to seven days in advance.

**Questions?**

Contact the front desk in person, by phone at (555) 012-3456, or by email at info@centrallibrary.example. Current hours and holiday closures are always posted on our website.
