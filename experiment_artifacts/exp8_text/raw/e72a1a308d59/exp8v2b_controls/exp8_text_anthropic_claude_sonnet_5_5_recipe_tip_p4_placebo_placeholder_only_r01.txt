**Tip: Cook once, assemble all week.**

On Sunday, spend 45 minutes prepping building blocks instead of full meals: a big batch of grains like rice or quinoa, a tray of roasted vegetables, and one protein such as grilled chicken, baked tofu, or hard-boiled eggs. Store them in separate containers in the fridge.

Each morning, mix and match them into something different: a grain bowl on Monday, a wrap on Tuesday, a salad on Wednesday. Add a quick sauce, like peanut dressing, pesto, or yogurt with lemon, to change the flavor without extra cooking.

This keeps lunches varied, prevents soggy leftovers, and cuts your daily prep to about five minutes. Most prepped items stay fresh for four days, so if your week runs longer, freeze a portion of the protein and thaw it overnight.
