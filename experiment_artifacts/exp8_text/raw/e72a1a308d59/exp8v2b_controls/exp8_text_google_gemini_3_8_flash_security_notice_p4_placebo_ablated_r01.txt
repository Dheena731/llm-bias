Dear User,

To protect your account and personal information, we require all users to turn on two-factor authentication.

**What is two-factor authentication?**
It is an extra layer of security that requires two steps to log in: your standard password and a temporary verification code sent to your phone or email. This ensures that only you can access your account, even if someone else discovers your password.

**How to turn it on:**
1. Log in to your account.
2. Go to **Settings** and select **Security**.
3. Select **Two-Factor Authentication** and follow the on-screen instructions to complete setup.

Please enable this feature today to ensure your account remains safe and accessible. If you need assistance, please contact our support team.
