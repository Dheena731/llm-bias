Treat soft herbs like **cilantro, parsley, and basil** as cut flowers: trim the ends of the stems, place them in a jar with about an inch of water, and loosely cover the top with a plastic bag or produce keeper. Store this jar in the fridge (keep basil on the counter instead).

For **woody herbs** like **rosemary, thyme, and sage**, wrap them gently in a slightly damp paper towel and place them in an airtight bag or container in the fridge.

**Key rule:** Never wash your herbs until right before you use them; extra moisture is the fastest way to cause rot.
