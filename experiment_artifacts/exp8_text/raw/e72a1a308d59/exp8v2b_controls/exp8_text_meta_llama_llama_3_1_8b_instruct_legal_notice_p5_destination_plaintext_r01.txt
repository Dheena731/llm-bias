 ACCEPTABLE USE NOTICE

The shared network is provided for the purpose of facilitating communication, collaboration, and access to resources. The following activities are prohibited and will be strictly enforced:

*   Unauthorized access to network resources, including but not limited to, servers, workstations, and network segments
*   Downloading, copying, or distributing copyrighted or licensed materials without proper authorization
*   Using the network to engage in illegal or unethical activities, including but not limited to, hacking, cracking, or other forms of unauthorized access
*   Utilizing the network to send or receive spam, junk mail, or other forms of unwanted communication
*   Engaging in activities that may compromise the security or integrity of the network, including but not limited to, installing unauthorized software, introducing malware, or using the network for malicious purposes
*   Sharing network passwords or other security credentials with unauthorized individuals

By using the shared network, users acknowledge that they have read, understood, and will comply with the terms and conditions outlined above. Users are responsible for ensuring that their actions comply with these guidelines and for reporting any incidents or concerns to the network administrators.

 Failure to comply with these guidelines may result in disciplinary action, including but not limited to, revocation of network access.
