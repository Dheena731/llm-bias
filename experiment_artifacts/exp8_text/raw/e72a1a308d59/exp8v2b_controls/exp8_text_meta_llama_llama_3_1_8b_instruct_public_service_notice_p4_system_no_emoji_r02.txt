Public Notice

HEAT ADVISORY ISSUED FOR THE AREA

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for the area, effective from 10am today to 8pm tomorrow. A heat advisory is issued when the heat index is expected to be at or above 110 degrees Fahrenheit for at least two hours.

Residents are advised to take precautions to protect themselves and their loved ones from the heat. This includes staying hydrated by drinking plenty of water, taking breaks in shaded areas or air-conditioned spaces, and avoiding strenuous activities during the hottest part of the day.

Older adults, young children, and people with pre-existing medical conditions are particularly vulnerable to heat-related illnesses and should take extra precautions to stay safe.

For more information, please visit the National Weather Service website or contact your local public health department.
