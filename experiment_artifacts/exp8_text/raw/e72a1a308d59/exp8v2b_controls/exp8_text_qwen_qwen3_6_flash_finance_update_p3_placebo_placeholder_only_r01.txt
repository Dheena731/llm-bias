We are excited to introduce our new Savings Goal feature, designed to help you reach your financial targets with clarity and consistency. You can now create personalized goals for anything from emergency reserves to large purchases, define a precise target amount, and schedule automatic weekly or monthly contributions. A live progress tracker updates in real time, visual milestones celebrate your forward movement, and intelligent reminders keep your funding on course. Access the tool by opening the Goals section in your account, tapping Create New Goal, and completing the brief setup wizard. Contribution amounts remain fully flexible, and your progress syncs seamlessly across all your devices. Start building toward what matters most today.
