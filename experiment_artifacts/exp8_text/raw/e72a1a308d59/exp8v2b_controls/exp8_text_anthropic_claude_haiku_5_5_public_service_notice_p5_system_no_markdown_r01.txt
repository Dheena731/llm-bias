Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations

Please be advised that polling station locations have been announced for the upcoming election. Voters are encouraged to confirm their assigned polling station before election day.

Your polling station is determined by your registered address. You can find your polling place by visiting the official election office website, calling the local election office, or using the voter lookup tool available on your state or county election website.

Polling stations will be open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on election day. Voters who are in line when polls close will still be permitted to cast a ballot.

If your polling location has changed since the last election, a notice will be mailed to your registered address. Please bring a valid form of identification as required by local law.

For questions or accessibility assistance, please contact your local election office.
