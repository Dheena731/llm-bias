NOTICE: UPDATED RECYCLING COLLECTION SCHEDULE

Beginning Monday, November 4, 2024, residential recycling pickups will move from Wednesday to Friday. This adjustment allows our sanitation crews to optimize routes and maintain consistent service times across all neighborhoods.

Residents must place their recycling bins at the curb by 7:00 AM on Friday mornings. Materials accepted remain unchanged: cleaned paper, cardboard, plastic containers, glass jars, and metal cans. Please rinse all items and remove lids before placing them in the blue cart. Bags of loose recyclables will not be collected.

For schedule maps, report missed pickups, or request extra carts, call 555-0127 or visit cityservices.gov/recycling.

Thank you for your patience and continued commitment to keeping our community clean and sustainable.

Department of Public Works
