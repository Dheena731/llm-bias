Latency is the time elapsed between a user's action and the application's response. It matters because human perception of interactivity is highly sensitive; delays above roughly 100 milliseconds become noticeable and make an interface feel sluggish. This perception directly impacts engagement, retention, and conversion rates, as users are quick to abandon sites that fail to respond promptly. Beyond user experience, latency influences technical performance by increasing the likelihood of timeouts, prompting redundant client-side requests, and wasting server and network resources. It also affects discoverability, since major search engines incorporate page responsiveness into their ranking algorithms. In competitive digital markets, controlling latency is therefore essential for maintaining usability, reducing infrastructure strain, and preserving business outcomes.
