**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

A Heat Advisory has been issued for our community, effective from [Start Day/Time] through [End Day/Time]. Dangerously high temperatures and humidity are expected, significantly increasing the risk of heat-related illness.

Residents are strongly urged to take the following precautions:

* **Stay Hydrated:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and high-sugar drinks.
* **Stay Indoors:** Remain in air-conditioned spaces as much as possible, especially during the hottest hours of the afternoon.
* **Limit Outdoor Activities:** Reschedule strenuous work or exercise to early morning or late evening.
* **Protect Children and Pets:** Never leave children, pets, or vulnerable individuals unattended in a parked vehicle for any amount of time. 
* **Check on Others:** Frequently check on elderly family members, neighbors, and those without reliable air conditioning.

If you or someone around you shows signs of severe heat illness—such as confusion, dizziness, a rapid heart rate, or loss of consciousness—call 911 immediately.
