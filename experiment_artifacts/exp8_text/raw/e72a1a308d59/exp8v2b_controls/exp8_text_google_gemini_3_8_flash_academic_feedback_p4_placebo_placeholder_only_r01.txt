Here are a few suggestions to help refine it before printing:

* **Reduce text density:** The introduction and methodology sections are currently a bit text-heavy. Try converting several of the longer paragraphs into concise bullet points or a brief workflow diagram to make the poster faster to scan.
* **Increase figure label sizes:** The charts in the results section look great, but the axis labels and legends will likely be difficult to read from a standard viewing distance of three to four feet. Consider bumping those font sizes up by at least a few points.
* **Highlight the primary takeaway:** To draw in passersby, consider placing the main finding or conclusion in a prominent, high-contrast banner at the top of the results section or directly under the title.
* **Check margin spacing:** The text blocks in the outer columns sit a little close to the canvas edge; adding a bit more breathing room around the borders will prevent accidental cropping during printing.
