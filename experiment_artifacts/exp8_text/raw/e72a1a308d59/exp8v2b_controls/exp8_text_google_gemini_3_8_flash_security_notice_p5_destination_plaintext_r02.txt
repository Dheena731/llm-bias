URGENT: REPORTING A LOST OR STOLEN COMPANY LAPTOP

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, you must report it immediately to the IT Security team. Fast reporting allows us to remotely lock and wipe the device to safeguard company data.

What to do immediately:

1. Call the IT Helpdesk at [Phone Number] or email [Email Address] with the subject line "URGENT - Lost Laptop".
2. Notify your direct manager.
3. If the device was stolen, file a report with local law enforcement and retain the report number.

When reporting, please provide:
- Your name and employee ID
- Laptop asset tag or serial number (if known)
- Date, time, and last known location of the device
- Brief description of the circumstances

Do not wait to see if the device turns up. Early reporting is critical to maintaining company security.
