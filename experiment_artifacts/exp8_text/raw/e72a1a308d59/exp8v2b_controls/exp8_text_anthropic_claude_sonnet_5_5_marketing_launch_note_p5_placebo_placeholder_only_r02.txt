**Now Playing: *Say It Again*, the podcast for people learning a language the real way**

Today we're launching *Say It Again*, a weekly podcast for anyone who has ever memorized a vocabulary list and then frozen when a real person said hello.

Each 20-minute episode focuses on one everyday situation: ordering coffee, asking for directions, making small talk with a neighbor, or apologizing when you've mixed up two similar words. Every episode follows the same simple rhythm:

- **Listen:** hear a short, natural conversation at normal speed.
- **Unpack:** we slow it down and explain the phrases, the grammar, and the cultural habits behind them.
- **Repeat:** you speak aloud, with pauses built in so you can practice in real time.

We also share the mistakes learners make most often, because getting something wrong in a podcast is a lot cheaper than getting it wrong at a dinner party.

*Say It Again* is designed for beginners and early intermediate learners, but curious listeners at any level will find something useful. You don't need a textbook or any special software. A pair of headphones and a willingness to talk to yourself will do.

The first three episodes are available today on all major podcast apps. Start with Episode 1, "Hello, and Then What?", and let us know how it goes. Your questions and requests will shape the episodes still to come.

Thanks for listening, and see you in the next lesson.
