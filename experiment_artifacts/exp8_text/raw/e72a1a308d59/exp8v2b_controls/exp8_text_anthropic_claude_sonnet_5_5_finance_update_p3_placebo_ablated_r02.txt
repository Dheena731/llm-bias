**Introducing Savings Goals**

You can now set savings goals in the app and track your progress toward each one.

**How it works**
1. Open the **Goals** tab and tap **New goal**.
2. Name your goal (for example, "Summer trip") and enter the amount you want to save.
3. Optionally, choose a target date. If you do, we'll show how much to save each month to reach it.
4. Add money to your goal whenever you like and watch your progress bar fill.

**Good to know**
- You can create more than one goal and edit or delete any of them at any time.
- Goals are for tracking only. Your money stays in your account, and creating a goal doesn't move or lock any funds.

Savings Goals is available now to all users. Update to the latest version of the app to try it.
