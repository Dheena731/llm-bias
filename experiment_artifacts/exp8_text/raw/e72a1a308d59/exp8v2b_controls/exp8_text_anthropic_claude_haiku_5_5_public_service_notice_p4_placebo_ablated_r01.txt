**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY**

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for our area from 11:00 a.m. today through 8:00 p.m. Temperatures are expected to reach the high 90s°F, with heat index values near 105°F.

**Who is at greatest risk:** older adults, young children, people with chronic illness, outdoor workers, and anyone without air conditioning.

**What you should do:**
- Stay in air-conditioned indoor spaces as much as possible. If you do not have air conditioning, visit a library, shopping center, or other cooled public building.
- Drink water regularly, even if you do not feel thirsty.
- Avoid strenuous outdoor activity during the hottest part of the day, roughly 11:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
- Never leave children, pets, or anyone who depends on you inside a parked vehicle.
- Check on neighbors, friends, and relatives who may need help staying cool.

**Warning signs of heat illness:** heavy sweating, muscle cramps, dizziness, nausea, headache, or confusion. If someone shows these signs, move them to a cool place, give them water if they are alert, and call 911 if their condition worsens or they lose consciousness.

For updates, follow your local National Weather Service office or local news. Cooling centers will be open at the locations listed on the city's website.
