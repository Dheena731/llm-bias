Here's a short cooking tip for preparing a weeknight dinner:

To make a quick and delicious weeknight dinner, try using a "cook once, eat twice" approach. Choose a protein like chicken or ground beef and cook a large batch of it on the stovetop or in the oven. Then, use it as the base for two separate meals. For example, cook a big batch of seasoned ground beef on Monday and use it to make tacos that night. Then, on Tuesday, use the leftover beef to make a hearty beef and vegetable stir-fry. This approach saves time and reduces food waste, making it a great way to get a home-cooked meal on the table even on the busiest of weeknights. Simply add some fresh vegetables, a starch, and your favorite seasonings to create a new and exciting meal.
