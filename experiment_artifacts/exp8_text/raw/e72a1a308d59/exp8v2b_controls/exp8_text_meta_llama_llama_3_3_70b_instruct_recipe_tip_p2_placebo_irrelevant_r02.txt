To keep fresh herbs like basil, rosemary, and parsley fresh for a longer period, store them in a way that maintains their natural moisture. Simply trim the stems, place the herbs in a glass of water, and cover the top with a plastic bag. Store them in the refrigerator, changing the water every few days. This will help keep the herbs fresh for up to a week, and you can use them to add flavor to a variety of dishes. Alternatively, you can also wrap the herbs in a damp paper towel and store them in a sealed container or zip-top bag in the fridge. This method helps to maintain humidity and keep the herbs from drying out.
