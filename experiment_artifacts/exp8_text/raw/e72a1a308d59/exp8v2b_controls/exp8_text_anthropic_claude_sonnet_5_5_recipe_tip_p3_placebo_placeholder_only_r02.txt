**Tip: Rinse your rice before cooking.**

Place the rice in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer and rinse it under cold water, swishing it with your hand, until the water runs mostly clear. This usually takes 3 to 5 rinses. Rinsing washes away the surface starch that makes grains clump together.

For fluffier results, also try these:

- **Use the right water ratio.** For long-grain white rice, use about 1¾ cups of water per 1 cup of rice. Too much water leads to gummy rice.
- **Don't stir while it cooks.** Stirring releases more starch and makes the rice sticky.
- **Let it rest.** After cooking, keep the lid on and let the rice sit off the heat for 10 minutes, then fluff it gently with a fork.
