**NOTICE: MANDATORY TWO-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION ACTIVATION**

Two-factor authentication must now be enabled on all company accounts. This security measure requires a unique verification code in addition to your password when signing in, providing essential protection against unauthorized access. 

To activate this feature, open your account settings, select the security section, and follow the prompts to link your mobile device or authenticator application. The IT support desk is available during standard business hours to troubleshoot setup questions or guide you through alternative verification methods. 

Please complete the activation process within seven calendar days to maintain uninterrupted access to internal networks, email, and shared drives. Failure to comply may result in temporary account restrictions. Thank you for your immediate cooperation in strengthening our organization’s cybersecurity defenses.
