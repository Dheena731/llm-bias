Reminder: Review Your Weekly Fitness Progress

Take a moment to reflect on your weekly fitness progress. Check in with your goals and track your progress. Ask yourself:

* What exercises did I complete this week?
* How often did I exercise?
* Am I meeting my fitness goals?
* Are there any areas I need to improve or focus on?

Regularly reviewing your fitness progress helps you stay on track and motivated. Make adjustments as needed to achieve your goals.
