Here is a short notice about polling station locations:

Polling Station Locations for Upcoming Election

The polling stations for the upcoming election have been announced. Voters can cast their ballots at one of the following locations:

* St. Mary's Community Center, 123 Main St.
* The Recreation Center, 456 Elm St.
* West Side Library, 789 Oak St.
* The Town Hall, 901 Maple St.

Voters are advised to check their voter registration cards for their designated polling station or visit the website of the local election commission for a complete list of locations.

Polling stations will be open on election day from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Voters are encouraged to arrive early to avoid long lines.
