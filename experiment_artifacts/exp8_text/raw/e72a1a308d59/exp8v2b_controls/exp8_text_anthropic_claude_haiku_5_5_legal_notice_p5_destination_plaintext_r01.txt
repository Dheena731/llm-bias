ACCEPTABLE USE NOTICE FOR SHARED NETWORK

1. Purpose
This notice sets out the rules for using the shared network ("the Network"). By connecting to or using the Network, you agree to comply with this notice.

2. Authorized Use
The Network may be used for work, study, and other purposes approved by the network administrator. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with the Network's operation or other users.

3. Prohibited Activities
You must not use the Network to:
(a) violate any applicable law or regulation;
(b) access, store, or distribute unlawful, obscene, threatening, or harassing material;
(c) infringe the intellectual property rights of others, including unauthorized copying or distribution of copyrighted material;
(d) attempt to gain unauthorized access to any system, account, or data;
(e) introduce malware, or disrupt, overload, or degrade Network performance;
(f) share your login credentials or allow others to use your account;
(g) conduct unauthorized commercial activity or cryptocurrency mining.

4. Security Responsibilities
Users must protect their credentials, keep devices used on the Network secure and up to date, and promptly report any suspected security incident to the network administrator.

5. Monitoring
To the extent permitted by law, the network administrator may monitor, log, and inspect Network traffic and stored data to maintain security, performance, and compliance with this notice. Users should have no expectation of privacy when using the Network.

6. Enforcement
Violation of this notice may result in suspension or termination of network access, disciplinary action, and referral to law enforcement where appropriate. The administrator reserves the right to remove content or restrict access without prior notice where necessary to protect the Network.

7. Disclaimer and Limitation
The Network is provided "as is." The administrator does not guarantee uninterrupted service and is not liable for any loss or damage arising from use of the Network, except where liability cannot be excluded by law.

8. Changes
This notice may be updated at any time. Continued use of the Network after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised notice.

9. Acceptance
Use of the Network indicates your acceptance of this Acceptable Use Notice. Questions should be directed to the network administrator.
