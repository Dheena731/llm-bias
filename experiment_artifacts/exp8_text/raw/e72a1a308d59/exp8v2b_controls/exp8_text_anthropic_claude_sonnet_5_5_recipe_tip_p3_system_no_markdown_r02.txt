Rinse your rice before cooking. Place the rice in a bowl or fine mesh strainer and run cold water over it, swishing it around with your hand until the water goes from cloudy to mostly clear, usually 3 to 5 rinses. This washes away the excess surface starch that makes grains clump together. For even fluffier results, use a little less water than the package suggests (about 1 part rice to 1.5 parts water for long-grain white rice), keep the lid on while it cooks, and let it rest off the heat for 10 minutes before gently fluffing with a fork.
