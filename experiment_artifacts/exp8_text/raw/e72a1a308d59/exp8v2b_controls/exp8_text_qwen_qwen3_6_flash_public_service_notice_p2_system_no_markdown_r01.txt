PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE

Please be advised that Maple Avenue between 4th Street and 7th Street will be closed to all vehicular traffic from Saturday, October 21 at 7:00 AM through Wednesday, October 25 at 6:00 PM. This closure is required for scheduled sewer line replacement.

A marked detour will direct traffic onto Birch Lane and River Road. Sidewalks will remain open, though pedestrians should expect minor temporary closures near construction zones. Motorists are urged to allow additional travel time and obey all flagger instructions and posted signs.

For real-time updates or inquiries, call the City Public Works Office at 555-0142 or visit cityworks.gov/closures. We appreciate your patience and cooperation as we complete these essential improvements.
