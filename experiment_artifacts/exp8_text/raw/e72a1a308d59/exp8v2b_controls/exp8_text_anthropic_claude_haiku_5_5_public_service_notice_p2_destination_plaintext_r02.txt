PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE

Main Street will be closed to all vehicle traffic between 1st Avenue and 5th Avenue from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on Saturday, June 14, for scheduled water main repairs.

Detour routes will be posted at each closure point. Local access to businesses and residences along the closed section will be maintained from the side streets where possible.

Pedestrians can continue to use the sidewalks, but please follow all posted signs and work zone instructions.

We apologize for any inconvenience and thank you for your patience. For questions, please contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567.
