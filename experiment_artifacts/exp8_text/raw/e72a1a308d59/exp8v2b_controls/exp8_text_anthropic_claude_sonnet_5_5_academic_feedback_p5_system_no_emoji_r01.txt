**Comment on Missing Citations**

Several claims in this essay rely on statistics, research findings, and specific historical details, but none are supported by citations. For example, the statement in the second paragraph about rising rates of student burnout presents a precise figure without identifying its source. Without attribution, readers cannot verify the information or judge how reliable it is, and this weakens your argument.

Please add in-text citations wherever you draw on outside sources, whether you quote, paraphrase, or summarize them. Include a full reference list at the end, formatted in the required style (APA, MLA, or Chicago). Proper citation also gives credit to the original authors and protects you from unintentional plagiarism. Once your sources are documented, your argument will be considerably more credible and persuasive.
