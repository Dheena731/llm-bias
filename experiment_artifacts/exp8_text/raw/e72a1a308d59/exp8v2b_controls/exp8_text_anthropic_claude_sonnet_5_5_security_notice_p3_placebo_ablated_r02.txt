**Reminder: Lock Your Screen When You Step Away**

All staff are reminded to lock their computer screens whenever they leave their workstations, even for a short time.

An unlocked screen can give others access to confidential information, email, and company systems. Locking your screen takes only a moment and helps protect our data, our clients, and you.

**How to lock your screen:**
- **Windows:** Press the Windows key + L
- **Mac:** Press Control + Command + Q

Please make this a habit every time you leave your desk, whether for a meeting, a break, or a quick errand.

Thank you for helping keep our information secure.
