**Important Security Alert**

As a reminder to all employees, phishing emails are a common method used by cyber attackers to compromise company security. These emails often appear to be from a trusted source, but are actually designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information or installing malicious software.

**What to Watch For:**

* Emails that ask you to log in to your account or provide confidential information
* Messages that create a sense of urgency, such as a deadline to update your account or respond to a security issue
* Links or attachments that seem suspicious or are from unknown senders
* Emails that contain spelling or grammar errors, as legitimate companies typically have professional communication

**What to Do:**

* Do not respond to or click on links from unfamiliar senders
* Verify the identity of the sender before responding to any emails
* Report any suspicious emails to the IT department immediately
* Use your best judgment when deciding whether to open an email or click on a link

**Protecting Our Company's Security**

We take the security of our company and its data very seriously. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT department. Together, we can help prevent phishing attacks and keep our company's information safe.
