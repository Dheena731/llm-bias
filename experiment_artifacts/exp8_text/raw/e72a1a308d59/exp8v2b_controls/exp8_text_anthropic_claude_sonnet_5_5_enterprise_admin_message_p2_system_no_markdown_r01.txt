Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starting Monday

Hello team,

Beginning Monday, we are introducing a new expense approval workflow to make reimbursements faster and more transparent.

Here is how it will work. Submit all expense reports through the finance portal, and attach itemized receipts to each one. Your direct manager will review the request within three business days. Expenses over $500 will then go to Finance for a second approval. Once approved, reimbursements will be issued in the next scheduled payroll cycle.

Please note that reports submitted by email or on paper will no longer be accepted after this date. Any expenses incurred before Monday can still be submitted under the old process until the end of the month.

A short guide and a video walkthrough are available on the intranet under Finance Resources. If you have questions or run into any issues, contact the Admin team at admin@yourcompany.com.

Thank you for helping us make this transition a smooth one.

Best regards,
The Admin Team
