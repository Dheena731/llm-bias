**Season in two stages for better flavor.** Before roasting, toss your vegetables with olive oil, kosher salt, and black pepper. The oil helps them brown, and the salt draws out moisture so the edges caramelize instead of steam. Use about 1 tablespoon of oil and ½ teaspoon of salt per pound of vegetables, and spread them in a single layer on the pan so they have room to crisp.

Then, right after they come out of the oven, finish with a squeeze of fresh lemon juice or a splash of balsamic vinegar and a pinch of flaky salt. Delicate flavors like fresh herbs, minced garlic, and lemon zest also work best at this stage, since they can burn or turn bitter in the oven's high heat. Sturdy dried spices, such as smoked paprika, cumin, or garlic powder, can go on before roasting.
