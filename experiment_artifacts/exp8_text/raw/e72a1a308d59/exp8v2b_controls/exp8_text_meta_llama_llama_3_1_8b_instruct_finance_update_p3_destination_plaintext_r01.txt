We're excited to introduce a new savings goal feature to help you stay on track with your financial objectives. The feature allows you to set specific, achievable goals for saving money, such as building an emergency fund or saving for a down payment on a house. You can enter a goal amount, select a target date, and track your progress over time. The feature also includes a reminder system to keep you motivated and informed about your progress. With this feature, you'll be able to visualize your progress and make adjustments as needed to stay on track.
