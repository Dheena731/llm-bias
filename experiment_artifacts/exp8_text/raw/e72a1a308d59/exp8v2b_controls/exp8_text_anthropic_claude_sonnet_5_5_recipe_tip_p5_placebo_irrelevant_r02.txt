**Season in two stages.** Toss your vegetables with oil and a generous pinch of salt *before* roasting so the seasoning sticks and the vegetables brown evenly. Then, right after they come out of the oven, finish with a squeeze of lemon juice or a splash of vinegar, plus fresh herbs or a little flaky salt. Delicate flavors like acid and fresh herbs fade in high heat, so adding them at the end keeps the dish bright and balanced.
