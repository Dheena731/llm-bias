NOTICE: REPORTING A LOST COMPANY LAPTOP

If you have misplaced or lost your assigned company laptop, please report this immediately to the IT Department.

When reporting, provide the following information:
Your name and employee ID
The asset tag number or serial number of the device
The approximate date, time, and location where the laptop was last seen
A brief description of how the laptop was lost

Send an email to it-support@company.com or call extension 5500.

Prompt reporting is essential to secure sensitive data and initiate recovery procedures. All employees are required to report lost devices without delay.

Thank you,
IT Security Team
