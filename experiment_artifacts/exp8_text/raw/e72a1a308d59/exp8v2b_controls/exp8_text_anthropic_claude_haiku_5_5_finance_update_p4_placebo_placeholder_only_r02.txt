**Subject: Understanding Your Year-End Tax Statement**

Hello,

Your year-end tax statement summarizes the income you earned and the taxes already withheld or paid during the past calendar year. You will need it to file your tax return, so please keep it with your other financial records.

Here is what to look for:

- **Total income:** The amount you earned from wages, interest, dividends, or other sources.
- **Taxes withheld:** Federal, state, and local taxes already taken out of your pay or payments. These count toward what you owe.
- **Deductions and contributions:** Amounts set aside for items such as retirement plans or health benefits, which may reduce your taxable income.
- **Identifying details:** Your name, taxpayer identification number, and the issuer's information. Please check these carefully.

If anything looks incorrect, such as a missing payment or a misspelled name, contact the issuing organization promptly so they can send a corrected form. Please don't file using figures you believe are wrong.

Most statements are also available through an online payroll portal or your financial institution's website. If you have questions about any entry, reply to this message and we will go through the statement with you.
