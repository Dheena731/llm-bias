PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE

Effective Monday, November 6, 2024, at 7:00 AM, Main Street between Oak Avenue and Pine Boulevard will be closed for emergency water main repairs. The closure is expected to last until Friday, November 10, 2024, at 5:00 PM, weather permitting.

Motorists are advised to use Elm Drive or Maple Lane as detour routes. Pedestrian access along the affected blocks will remain open.

For updates or questions, please call the Department of Transportation at 555-0198 or visit our website at www.citytransit.gov/roadupdates.

We apologize for any inconvenience and appreciate your cooperation.
