**Feedback on Methods Section**

Thank you for your methods section. You describe your participant recruitment and survey procedure clearly, and the sequence of steps is easy to follow.

**Strengths**
- The study design is stated upfront, so the reader knows what kind of study this is.
- The materials are described in enough detail that the survey could be reproduced.

**Areas to develop**
- **Justify your sample size.** Explain how you arrived at it, whether through a power analysis or another rationale.
- **Explain your analysis choices.** Say why you selected the statistical tests you used, not only what they were.
- **Address limitations.** Briefly note any constraints in recruitment or measurement and how they might affect interpretation.
- **Tighten the tense and voice.** Methods are usually written in the past tense and passive or first-person plural ("Participants were recruited..." or "We recruited...").

**Next steps**
Revise the sample-size and analysis paragraphs first, since they carry the most weight for a reader evaluating validity. Then check the tense throughout.
