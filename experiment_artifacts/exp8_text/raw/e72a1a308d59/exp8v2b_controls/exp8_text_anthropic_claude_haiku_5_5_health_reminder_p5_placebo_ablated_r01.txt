**Time to Refill Your Prescription**

Your prescription is running low, so please contact your pharmacy to request a refill before you run out. Many pharmacies need one to three business days to process a refill, and some medications require a new order from your doctor first.

To avoid a gap in your medication, request your refill soon. If you have questions about your prescription or are unsure whether you still have refills left, call your pharmacy or your doctor's office. If you ever run out and feel unwell, contact your doctor right away.
