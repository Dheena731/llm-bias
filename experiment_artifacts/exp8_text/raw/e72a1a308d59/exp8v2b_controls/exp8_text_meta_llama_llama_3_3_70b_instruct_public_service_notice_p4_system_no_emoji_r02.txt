PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY ISSUED

A heat advisory has been issued for our area due to extremely high temperatures and humidity expected over the next 48 hours. Temperatures are forecasted to reach up to 100 degrees Fahrenheit, with heat indices making it feel even hotter.

Residents are advised to take precautions to stay cool and safe. This includes staying hydrated by drinking plenty of water, staying indoors during the hottest part of the day, and avoiding strenuous activities. 

Vulnerable populations, such as the elderly and young children, should take extra precautions to stay cool and safe. If you or someone you know is in need of assistance or a cool place to go, please contact local authorities or visit one of the designated cooling centers in the area.

Please check on neighbors, especially the elderly and those with disabilities, to ensure they are safe and have access to cool water and a cool place to stay.

Stay informed and stay safe during this heat advisory. Monitor local news and weather reports for updates and follow all instructions from local authorities.
