Here is a short feedback note on a conference poster draft:

"Dear [Presenter's Name],

I have reviewed your conference poster draft and overall, it effectively communicates the key findings of your research. The visual layout is clear and easy to follow, and the headings and subheadings help to guide the reader through the content. 

One suggestion I have is to consider adding a brief summary or abstract at the top of the poster to provide context and help viewers quickly understand the main focus of your research. Additionally, some of the font sizes could be increased to improve readability, particularly in the figure captions and references.

Overall, your poster draft is well-organized and effectively conveys your research. With a few minor adjustments, it will be ready for presentation at the conference.

Best regards,
[Your Name]"

I made sure to:

* Clearly state the main point (the poster effectively communicates the research findings)
* Provide specific and actionable feedback (add a summary, increase font size)
* Avoid introducing undefined jargon or technical terms
* Address the correct audience (the presenter, not a general audience)
* Be concise and to the point, without leaving out important information.
