**Subject: Important Update: New Expense Approval Workflow**

Beginning this month, all expense submissions will follow a revised approval workflow to improve accuracy and accelerate reimbursements. Effective immediately, every expense report must be submitted through the unified finance dashboard rather than via email or paper forms. Supervisors will automatically receive digital notifications when a report is ready for review and can authorize or reject payments directly in the system. This update eliminates duplicate routing, ensures consistent policy compliance, and provides submitters with real-time tracking on every claim. A detailed user guide and step-by-step video walkthrough are now live on the internal knowledge base. The finance operations team is available to answer questions or assist with system navigation. Thank you for your cooperation as we implement these efficiency improvements.
