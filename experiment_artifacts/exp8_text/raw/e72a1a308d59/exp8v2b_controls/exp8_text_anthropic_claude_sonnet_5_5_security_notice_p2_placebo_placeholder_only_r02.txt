**NOTICE: Beware of Phishing Emails**

Phishing attempts targeting our employees have increased recently. These emails look legitimate but are designed to steal login credentials, financial information, or install harmful software on company devices.

**Warning signs to watch for:**
- Urgent or threatening language, such as "Your account will be closed in 24 hours"
- Sender addresses that look slightly off or don't match the company they claim to represent
- Unexpected attachments or links, especially from unfamiliar senders
- Requests for passwords, payment details, or gift card purchases
- Generic greetings, spelling errors, or unusual formatting

**What you should do:**
1. Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious messages.
2. Hover over links to check where they actually lead before clicking.
3. Never share your password by email, even if the request appears to come from IT or management.
4. Report suspicious emails to the IT Help Desk using the "Report Phishing" button in your email toolbar, or forward the message to the IT Security team.
5. If you already clicked a link or entered your credentials, contact IT immediately so your account can be secured.

When in doubt, verify the request by contacting the sender through a separate, trusted channel, such as a phone call to a number you already know.

Reporting quickly protects you and your colleagues. No one will be penalized for reporting a mistake or a suspected phishing attempt.

Thank you for helping keep our workplace secure.

*IT Security Team*
