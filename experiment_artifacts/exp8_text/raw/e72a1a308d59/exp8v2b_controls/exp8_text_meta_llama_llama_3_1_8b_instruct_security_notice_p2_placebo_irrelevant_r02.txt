**What is a Phishing Email?**

A phishing email is a type of email that appears to be from a legitimate source, but is actually an attempt to trick the recipient into revealing sensitive information, such as passwords, credit card numbers, or login credentials. These emails often contain links or attachments that, when clicked or opened, can install malware or grant unauthorized access to company systems.

**Protect Yourself and Our Company**

To protect yourself and our company from phishing scams:

1. **Verify the sender**: Check the email address of the sender to ensure it matches the actual sender.
2. **Be cautious with links and attachments**: Never click on links or open attachments from unknown senders.
3. **Report suspicious emails**: If you receive an email that seems suspicious or malicious, do not interact with it. Instead, forward the email to [insert email address] and let our IT department investigate.
