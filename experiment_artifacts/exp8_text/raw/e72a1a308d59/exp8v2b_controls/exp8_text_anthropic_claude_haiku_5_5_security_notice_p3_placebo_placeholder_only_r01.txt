**Reminder: Please Lock Your Screen When Away From Your Desk**

Team,

Leaving a computer unlocked, even for a few minutes, puts company information and customer data at risk. Anyone passing by could view sensitive files, send messages from your account, or make changes you didn't intend.

Please follow these simple steps:

- **Windows users:** Press Windows key + L.
- **Mac users:** Press Control + Command + Q.
- **Before any break, meeting, or lunch:** Lock your screen, even if you expect to be gone only briefly.

Locking your screen takes a second and protects everyone. Thank you for helping keep our information secure.

IT Security Team
