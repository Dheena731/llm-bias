Here's a short tip for seasoning roasted vegetables:

**Add depth with aromatics and acids**

To elevate the flavor of roasted vegetables, consider adding a mixture of aromatics like garlic, onions, and herbs to the roasting pan. These ingredients will infuse the vegetables with a rich, savory flavor. For an added layer of brightness, squeeze a bit of citrus juice (such as lemon or orange) over the vegetables during the last 10-15 minutes of roasting. The acidity will help to balance out the earthy flavors of the vegetables and create a harmonious, well-rounded taste experience.
