**Important: Phishing Email Awareness**

To all employees,

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain a secure and safe work environment, we would like to remind everyone to be vigilant when receiving emails that request sensitive information or prompt you to take immediate action.

Phishing emails, which may appear to be from a trusted source, are designed to trick you into revealing confidential data or installing malware. These emails often contain spelling and grammar mistakes, and may ask you to update or verify sensitive information.

If you receive an email that seems suspicious or prompts you to take action, do not click on any links or provide sensitive information. Instead, report the email to your supervisor or the IT department immediately.

We recommend that you always verify the authenticity of an email by contacting the sender directly, either by phone or through a separate communication channel. If you're unsure whether an email is legitimate, it's always better to err on the side of caution and seek guidance from a trusted colleague or our IT team.

Let's work together to maintain the security of our workplace and protect our sensitive information.
