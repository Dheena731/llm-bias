**Important Notice: Reporting a Lost Company Laptop**

If you have lost a company-issued laptop, please report the incident to the IT Department immediately. Provide your name, employee ID, and a detailed description of the laptop, including its make, model, and serial number. Also, mention the last known location of the laptop and the circumstances surrounding its loss.

Please email [IT Department email] or call [IT Department phone number] to report the incident. This will enable us to take necessary steps to secure company data and prevent unauthorized access.

Your prompt reporting will help us to minimize potential risks and take appropriate actions to recover the lost laptop.
