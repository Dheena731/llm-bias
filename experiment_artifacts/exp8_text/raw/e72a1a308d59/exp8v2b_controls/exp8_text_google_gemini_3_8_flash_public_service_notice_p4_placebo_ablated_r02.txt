**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

A Heat Advisory has been issued for our community today from 11:00 AM to 8:00 PM. High temperatures combined with high humidity will create dangerously hot conditions, significantly increasing the risk of heat-related illness.

Residents are strongly urged to take the following safety precautions:

* **Stay Hydrated:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and high-caffeine beverages.
* **Stay Indoors:** Remain in air-conditioned spaces as much as possible. If your home lacks air conditioning, consider visiting public spaces such as libraries, community centers, or malls.
* **Limit Outdoor Activity:** Postpone strenuous outdoor work or exercise until the cooler evening hours. If you must be outside, wear light, loose-fitting clothing and rest frequently in the shade.
* **Never Leave Anyone in a Parked Car:** Never leave children or pets unattended in parked vehicles, as interior temperatures can become deadly within minutes.
* **Check on Others:** Monitor elderly relatives, neighbors, and individuals with underlying health conditions.

If you suspect someone is suffering from heat stroke—characterized by confusion, a rapid pulse, dizziness, or loss of consciousness—call 911 immediately and move the person into a cooler area.
