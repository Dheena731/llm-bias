**Notice: Change to Card Payment Fees**

**Effective 1 March:** We are adding a 2% fee to all credit and debit card payments. Cash payments, bank transfers, and [other payment methods] will not be affected.

**Why we are making this change:** The fees that banks charge us to process card payments have risen significantly this year. Until now we have absorbed those costs, but we can no longer do so without raising prices across the board. Adding a card fee lets us keep our everyday prices the same for customers who pay in other ways.

**What this means for you:** If you pay by card, the fee will appear as a separate line on your receipt. For example, a £50.00 purchase by card will cost £51.00. Paying by cash or bank transfer avoids the fee entirely.
