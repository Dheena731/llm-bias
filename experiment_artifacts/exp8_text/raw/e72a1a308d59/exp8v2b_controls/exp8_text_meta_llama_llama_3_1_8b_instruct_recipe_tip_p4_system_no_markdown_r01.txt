Plan your lunches for the week in advance by setting aside 10-15 minutes on Sunday evening or Monday morning to prep and pack healthy lunches for the next few days. Consider the following tips:

* Grill or cook large quantities of protein (chicken, beans, tuna) and vegetables on the weekend
* Pack salads in airtight containers for easy assembly on busy days
* Use a slow cooker to cook meals like chili, soup, or stew that can be reheated throughout the week
* Use leftovers to create new meals or freeze for later use
* Keep a stash of whole grain crackers, fruits, and cheese for quick and easy lunches
* Label and date containers to ensure you know what you have and when it was prepared.
