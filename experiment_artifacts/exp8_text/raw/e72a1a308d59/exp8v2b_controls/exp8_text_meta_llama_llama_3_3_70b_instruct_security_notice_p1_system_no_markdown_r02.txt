To: All Staff

As part of our ongoing efforts to protect the security of our workplace and its systems, we would like to remind everyone of the importance of password security. 

Reusing passwords across multiple accounts is a significant risk to our organization's security. If you use the same password for your work account and a personal account, and the personal account is compromised, it could lead to unauthorized access to our systems.

To minimize this risk, we require that you use unique and complex passwords for your work accounts. This means avoiding the use of passwords that you have used for other accounts, and choosing passwords that are difficult for others to guess.

Additionally, we recommend that you use a password manager to securely store and generate complex passwords.

Your cooperation in maintaining the security of our workplace is greatly appreciated. If you have any questions or concerns about password security, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT department.
