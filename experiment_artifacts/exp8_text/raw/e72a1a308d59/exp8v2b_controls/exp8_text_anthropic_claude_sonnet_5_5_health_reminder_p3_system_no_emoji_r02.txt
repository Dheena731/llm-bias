**Reminder: Book Your Annual Health Checkup**

It's time to schedule your yearly health checkup. Regular exams help catch potential issues early, keep vaccinations and screenings up to date, and give you a chance to discuss any health concerns with your doctor.

**To do:**
- Call your doctor's office or book online
- Check your insurance coverage
- Note any questions or symptoms to mention
- Add the appointment to your calendar

Taking a few minutes to book now can make a real difference for your long-term health.
