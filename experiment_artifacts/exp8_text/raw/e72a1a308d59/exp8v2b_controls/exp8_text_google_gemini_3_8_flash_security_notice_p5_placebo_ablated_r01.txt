**Notice to All Employees: Immediate Reporting of Lost or Stolen Laptops**

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, you must report it immediately to the IT Support Desk. Prompt notification allows our team to secure the device remotely and protect confidential company and client data.

**Steps to take immediately:**

1. **Contact IT Support:** Call [Insert Phone Number] or email [Insert Email Address] as soon as you realize the laptop is missing. 
2. **Provide Details:** Share your name, the date and time you last had the device, the location where it went missing, and the laptop's asset tag or serial number (if known).
3. **Report Theft to Police (if applicable):** If you suspect the laptop was stolen, file a report with local law enforcement and provide the police report number to IT Support.

Do not delay reporting while searching for a missing device. Prompt communication is critical to safeguarding company information. 

Thank you for your prompt cooperation.

**IT & Information Security Team**
