NOTICE OF RECYCLING COLLECTION CHANGES

Effective immediately, the recycling collection schedule for our community has been revised. Residents are now required to place recyclable materials at the curb on the designated collection day, as indicated below.

* Monday: Paper and cardboard collections
* Wednesday: Plastic and glass collections
* Friday: Metal and food waste collections

Please ensure that all recyclable materials are properly sorted and placed in the designated bins. Failure to comply with these changes may result in delayed or missed collections.

For further information or to report any issues with the recycling collection, please contact our waste management department at (555) 123-4567.
