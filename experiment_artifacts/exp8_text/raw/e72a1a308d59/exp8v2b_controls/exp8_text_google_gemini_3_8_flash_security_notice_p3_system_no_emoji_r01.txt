**Subject: Security Reminder: Please Lock Your Screens**

Dear Team,

This is a quick reminder to always lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your workstation, even if only for a brief moment. 

Securing your unattended device helps protect sensitive company, client, and employee data from unauthorized access.

**Quick Shortcuts:**
* **Windows:** Press **Windows Key + L**
* **Mac:** Press **Command + Control + Q**

Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining our data security.

IT Security Team
