NOTICE: RECYCLING COLLECTION CHANGES

Effective Monday, June 2, recycling collection will move to a new schedule.

What is changing:
- Recycling will be collected every other week instead of weekly.
- Pickup day will change from Thursday to Tuesday for most households.
- Collection will start at 7:00 AM, so please set your bins out the night before.

What stays the same:
- Trash collection days are not changing.
- Accepted materials remain the same: paper, cardboard, glass bottles and jars, metal cans, and plastic containers numbered 1 and 2.

Helpful reminders:
- Rinse containers and remove food residue.
- Flatten cardboard boxes.
- Do not bag recyclables. Place them loose in the bin.
- Place bins at the curb with at least 3 feet of space around them.

Your first Tuesday pickup will be June 3. A printed schedule will be mailed to every household by May 23.

Questions? Contact Public Works at (555) 012-3456 or visit the city website and search "recycling schedule."

Thank you for helping keep our community clean and sustainable.
