PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE: LIBRARY OPENING HOURS

The Community Library is open to the public during the following hours:
Monday through Thursday: 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM
Friday: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
Saturday: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM

Hours may vary on federal holidays and during seasonal breaks. For real-time updates, please visit www.communitylibrary.org or call the main desk at 555-0199.
