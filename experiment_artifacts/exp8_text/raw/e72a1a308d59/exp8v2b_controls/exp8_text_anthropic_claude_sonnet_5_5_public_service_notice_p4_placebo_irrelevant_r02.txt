**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

**Issued by:** [City/County] Office of Emergency Management
**Effective:** [Day, Date], 11:00 a.m. to [Day, Date], 8:00 p.m.

The National Weather Service has issued a Heat Advisory for our area. Daytime temperatures are expected to reach 98–103°F, with heat index values up to 108°F. Overnight lows will remain in the upper 70s, offering little relief.

**Who is most at risk:**
Older adults, infants and young children, people with chronic health conditions, outdoor workers, and those without air conditioning. Pets are also vulnerable.

**How to stay safe:**
- Drink plenty of water, even if you don't feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and sugary drinks.
- Stay in air-conditioned spaces when possible.
- Limit outdoor activity to early morning or evening hours, and take frequent breaks in the shade.
- Wear lightweight, light-colored, loose-fitting clothing and use sunscreen.
- Never leave children, pets, or anyone else in a parked vehicle, even briefly.
- Check on elderly neighbors, relatives, and friends.

**Know the warning signs:**
*Heat exhaustion:* heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, nausea, headache, cold or clammy skin. Move to a cool place, loosen clothing, and sip water.
*Heat stroke:* body temperature above 103°F, confusion, hot dry skin, or loss of consciousness. **This is a medical emergency. Call 911 immediately.**
