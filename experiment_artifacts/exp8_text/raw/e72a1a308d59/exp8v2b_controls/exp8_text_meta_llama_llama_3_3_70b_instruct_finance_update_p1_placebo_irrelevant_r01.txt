Here is a short update on our new monthly spending summary:

**Introducing Our New Monthly Spending Summary**

As part of our ongoing effort to provide you with more transparency and control over your finances, we are excited to introduce a new monthly spending summary. This summary will provide a clear and concise overview of your spending activity for the previous month, helping you to better understand your financial habits and make informed decisions about your money.

The monthly spending summary will include the following information:

* Total spending for the month
* Breakdown of spending by category (e.g. food, transportation, entertainment)
* List of top expenses for the month
* Comparison to previous month's spending

This new feature will be available on the first day of each month, and will be accessible through your online account or mobile app. We hope this new tool will help you to stay on top of your finances and achieve your financial goals. If you have any questions or feedback, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
