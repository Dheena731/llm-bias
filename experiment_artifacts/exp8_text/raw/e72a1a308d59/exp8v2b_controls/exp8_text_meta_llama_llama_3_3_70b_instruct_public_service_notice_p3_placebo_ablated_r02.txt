**Important Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection Schedule**

Starting from next Monday, the recycling collection day for our community will be changing from Thursday to Tuesday. This change applies to all residents who currently receive curbside recycling collection services. The types of materials accepted for recycling remain the same, including paper, plastic, glass, and metal.

To ensure your recyclables are collected, please place them at the curb by 7:00 AM on Tuesdays. If you have any questions or concerns about the new collection schedule, please contact our customer service department at [phone number] or visit our website for more information.

We appreciate your cooperation and continued participation in our recycling program.
