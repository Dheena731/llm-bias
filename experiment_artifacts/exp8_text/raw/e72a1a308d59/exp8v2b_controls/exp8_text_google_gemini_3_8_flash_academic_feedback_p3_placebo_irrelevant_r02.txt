While the manuscript addresses an interesting and timely topic, the core research question remains ambiguous. The introduction touches upon several broad themes and related issues, but it does not clearly articulate the specific gap in the literature the study intends to address or the primary question driving the investigation. As a result, it is difficult to assess whether the methodology is fully aligned with the study's aims or to evaluate the significance of the findings. I strongly recommend that the authors explicitly state their primary research question(s) and operationalize key concepts toward the end of the Introduction to establish a clear foundation for the rest of the paper.
