📅 **Stop back-to-back burnout!** 💥

Here's a quick win for your schedule: **Add 5-minute buffers between every meeting.** ⏱️

Why? It gives you vital breathing room to:
✅ Review notes & prep
✅ Grab a coffee or stretch
✅ Reset mentally so you aren't rushing into the next call
✅ Actually avoid running late

Your calendar isn't just a to-do list; it needs space to make your day sustainable! 🌬️✨
