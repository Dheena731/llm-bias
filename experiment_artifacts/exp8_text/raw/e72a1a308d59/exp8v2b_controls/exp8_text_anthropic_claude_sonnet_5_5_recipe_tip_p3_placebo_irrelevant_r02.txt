**Tip: Rinse your rice before cooking.**

Sticky rice comes from excess surface starch. Put the rice in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer and rinse it under cold water, swirling with your hand, until the water runs mostly clear (usually 3-4 rinses). Then drain well.

**Extra tips:**
- Use slightly less water: about 1¼ cups water per 1 cup long-grain rice.
- Don't stir while it cooks, since stirring releases more starch.
- Let it rest, covered, for 10 minutes off the heat, then fluff with a fork.

The result is fluffy rice with grains that stay separate.
