Subject: Important Information Regarding Your End-of-Year Tax Statement

Your end-of-year tax statement is a document that summarizes all relevant financial activity associated with your account over the past calendar year. Depending on your relationship with us, this may include totals for income earned, taxes withheld, interest paid or received, or deductible contributions made.

You will need the information on this statement to accurately complete and file your annual income tax return. 

Please review the document carefully to ensure all personal and financial details are correct. Retain a copy for your personal records, and provide it to your certified tax preparer or use it when filing your taxes. 

If you notice any discrepancies or have questions about the figures reported, please contact our support team.
