**Subject: Update: New Expense Approval Workflow Now Active**

Hello Team,

We have officially updated our expense approval workflow to speed up reimbursements and improve visibility across all departments. 

Starting today, all new expense reports will automatically route to your direct manager for initial review before final sign-off from the finance team. You will now receive automatic email notifications as your request moves through each stage of the approval process.

Please continue submitting your claims through the standard expense portal, which already reflects these updates. Any requests submitted prior to today will proceed through the previous system without interruption.

If you have any questions about the new routing steps, please contact the finance team directly. 

Thank you for your cooperation.
