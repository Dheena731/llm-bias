**🚀 Product Update: Savings Goals Now Live!**

We're excited to introduce **Savings Goals**, a new feature designed to help you save smarter and reach your financial milestones faster.

**✨ Key Highlights:**
*   **Create Custom Goals:** Set a name, target amount, and optional deadline for anything from an emergency fund to a dream vacation.
*   **Track Progress Visually:** Watch your hard work come to life with real-time progress bars and contribution history.
*   **Smart Reminders:** Get notified when you're close to hitting your target so you can stay motivated.

**👉 How to Start:**
Navigate to the **Savings** tab in your app and tap **"Create New Goal"** to begin.

Happy saving! 💰
