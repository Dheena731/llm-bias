**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

**Issued by:** [City/County] Office of Emergency Management
**Effective:** [Day, Date] from 11:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.

The National Weather Service has issued a Heat Advisory for our area. Daytime temperatures are expected to reach 100 to 105 degrees Fahrenheit, with heat index values up to 110 degrees. Overnight lows will offer little relief.

**Who is most at risk:**
- Older adults and young children
- People with chronic health conditions
- Outdoor workers and athletes
- Pets and livestock

**What you should do:**
- Drink plenty of water, even if you do not feel thirsty.
- Stay in air-conditioned spaces whenever possible.
- Limit outdoor activity to early morning or evening hours.
- Wear lightweight, light-colored, loose-fitting clothing.
- Never leave children, pets, or vulnerable adults in a parked vehicle.
- Check on neighbors, friends, and relatives who live alone.

**Warning signs of heat illness:**
Heavy sweating, dizziness, nausea, muscle cramps, headache, or weakness may signal heat exhaustion. Hot, dry skin, confusion, or loss of consciousness may signal heat stroke. Call 911 immediately if heat stroke is suspected.
