**PUBLIC NOTICE: Polling Station Locations & Voting Hours**

**Upcoming Election Date:** Tuesday, November 5, 2024  
**Voting Hours:** 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM  

Registered voters are reminded that you must cast your ballot at your **assigned polling station** on Election Day. Locations are assigned based on your residential address and precinct.

### How to Find Your Polling Station:
* **Online:** Visit **www.elections-lookup.gov** and enter your residential address.
* **Voter Card:** Check the location printed on your official Voter Registration Card.
* **By Phone:** Call the County Board of Elections at **(555) 019-8234**.

### Key Locations by Ward/Precinct:
* **Precinct 1 (North):** City Community Center – 100 Main St.
* **Precinct 2 (Central):** Lincoln Elementary School (Gymnasium) – 450 Oak Ave.
* **Precinct 3 (East):** Public Library (Meeting Room A) – 822 Elm Blvd.
* **Precinct 4 (South):** St. Jude Parish Hall – 315 Maple St.

*All listed facilities are fully ADA accessible.*

**Important Reminder:** Please bring a valid form of photo identification. Anyone in line by 8:00 PM is legally permitted to vote. 

For questions regarding registration status or accessibility accommodations, please contact the City Elections Office.
