Subject: Update to Card Transaction Fees

Dear Valued Customer,

We are writing to let you know that, effective March 1, our card transaction fee will change from 2.5% to 2.9% of the total purchase amount for credit and debit card payments. This adjustment reflects higher processing costs charged by our payment providers over the past year.

Cash, bank transfer, and check payments are not affected by this change, and there is no change to the fees for in-store or online purchases made with those methods.

If you have any questions about how this update affects your account or a recent purchase, please reply to this email or call our customer service team at 555-0100. We appreciate your continued business and your understanding.
