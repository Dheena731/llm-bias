Prep your vegetables and proteins at the start of the week, or while the oven preheats on busy nights. Chop onions, peppers, and garlic, store them in airtight containers, and you can cut dinner prep time in half. A quick tip: keep a small bowl of pre-mixed seasoning (salt, pepper, garlic powder, and paprika) on hand so you can season chicken, fish, or vegetables in seconds without measuring out spices each time.
