# NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

**Effective Date:** [Date]

**1. Scope.** This Notice sets out the conditions under which [Company Name] ("the Company") will issue refunds for products or services purchased by a customer ("the Customer"). It applies to all purchases made through the Company's website, app, or authorized channels unless a separate written agreement states otherwise.

**2. Eligibility.** A Customer is eligible for a refund only if all of the following conditions are met:

   (a) The refund request is submitted in writing within **thirty (30) days** of the date of purchase;

   (b) The Customer provides valid proof of purchase, such as an order number or receipt;

   (c) Physical goods are returned unused, in original packaging, and in resalable condition; and

   (d) The purchase is not a non-refundable item as described in Section 3.

**3. Non-Refundable Items.** The following are not eligible for refund, except where required by applicable law:

   (a) Digital products that have been downloaded, accessed, or activated;

   (b) Custom, personalized, or made-to-order goods;

   (c) Gift cards and promotional credits;

   (d) Services that have been fully performed; and

   (e) Items marked "final sale" at the time of purchase.

**4. Defective or Misdescribed Goods.** If goods or services are defective, not as described, or not delivered, the Customer may be entitled to a refund, repair, or replacement regardless of Section 2, in accordance with applicable consumer protection laws. Nothing in this Notice limits any statutory rights of the Customer.

**5. Processing.** Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within **ten (10) business days** of approval. Shipping and handling charges are non-refundable unless the refund results from the Company's error. The Company is not responsible for delays caused by third-party payment providers.

**6. Right to Refuse.** The Company reserves the right to deny any refund request that does not satisfy this Notice or that appears fraudulent, abusive, or made in bad faith.

**7. Amendments.** The Company may amend this Notice at any time. Amendments apply to purchases made after the revised Notice is published.

**8. Contact.** Refund requests and questions should be directed to [email address] or [mailing address].
