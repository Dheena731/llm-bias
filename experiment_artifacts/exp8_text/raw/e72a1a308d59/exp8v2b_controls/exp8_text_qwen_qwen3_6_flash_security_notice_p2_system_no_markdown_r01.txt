WORKPLACE NOTICE: PHISHING EMAIL AWARENESS
Date: May 22, 2024
To: All Employees
From: IT Security Department

Please be advised that our organization has recently seen a rise in phishing emails targeting staff. These fraudulent messages attempt to trick recipients into sharing passwords, financial information, or downloading malicious files.

To protect yourself and company data, please follow these guidelines:
- Always verify the sender's complete email address, not just the display name.
- Do not click links or open attachments from unfamiliar or unexpected sources.
- Be cautious of messages that create urgency, request immediate payment, or contain grammatical errors.
- Never reply to emails asking for login credentials or sensitive personal information.

If you receive a suspicious email, do not respond, click, or download anything. Forward the original message to reportsphishing@company.com using the Report Phishing feature in your email client, or contact the IT Help Desk at extension 4357.

Your vigilance is essential to maintaining our digital security. Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter.

IT Security Department
