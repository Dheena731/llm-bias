**Tip: Cook once, assemble all week.**

On Sunday, spend 45 minutes prepping building blocks instead of full meals. Roast a tray of vegetables, cook a big pot of grains such as rice or quinoa, and prepare one protein like grilled chicken, baked tofu, or hard-boiled eggs. Store each separately in airtight containers.

Each morning, mix and match in about five minutes. Monday might be a grain bowl with chicken and roasted vegetables. Tuesday could be a wrap with the same chicken and fresh greens. Wednesday might be a salad topped with eggs and leftover grains.

Keeping the components separate prevents sogginess and makes each lunch feel different. Add a flavorful sauce, like a lemon-tahini dressing or salsa, to change the taste without extra cooking. Most prepped items stay fresh for four days, so if your week runs longer, freeze a portion for later.
