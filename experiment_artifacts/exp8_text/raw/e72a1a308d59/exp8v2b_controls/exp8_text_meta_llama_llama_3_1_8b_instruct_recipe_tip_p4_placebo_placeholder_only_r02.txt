To plan lunches for a busy week, start by identifying your dietary needs and preferences. Then, take some time on the weekend or the day before to prep any ingredients that can be used in multiple meals, such as chopping vegetables or cooking proteins like chicken or beans. Consider making a large batch of a versatile dish like soup or a stir-fry that can be reheated throughout the week. Finally, schedule out your lunches for the week and write them down in a planner or on a calendar to help you stay organized and on track.
