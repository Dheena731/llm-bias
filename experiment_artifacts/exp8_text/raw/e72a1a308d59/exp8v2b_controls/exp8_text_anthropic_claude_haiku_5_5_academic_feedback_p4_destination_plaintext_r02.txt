Thanks for sharing the draft. The core finding is clear and the layout gives it room to breathe. A few changes would make it easier to follow from a distance.

The title is long, and at poster viewing distance it may be hard to read quickly. Try shortening it to the main claim or question, and move the full wording to the subtitle.

The introduction is dense. Two or three sentences stating the problem and why it matters would help viewers decide within seconds whether to stop and read further.

The methods section uses a lot of abbreviations that are not defined on the poster. Spell out the first use of each, or add a short key.

The results figure is the strongest element, but its axis labels are small and the legend sits far from the data. Enlarge the labels and place the legend next to the relevant lines or bars.

The conclusion does not clearly connect back to the research question. One sentence that answers the question directly would give the poster a stronger ending.

Finally, add a QR code or short URL linking to the full paper or your contact details, so people who want more can follow up easily.

Overall, this is a solid draft. Tightening the title and intro and clarifying the figure would make the biggest difference. If you paste the poster text, I can give more specific comments.
