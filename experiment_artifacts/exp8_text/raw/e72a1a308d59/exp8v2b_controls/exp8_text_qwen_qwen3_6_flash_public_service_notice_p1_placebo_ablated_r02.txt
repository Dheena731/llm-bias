**PUBLIC NOTICE: COMMUNITY LIBRARY OPENING HOURS**

Please be advised that the Community Public Library will operate on the following weekly schedule:
• Monday through Thursday: 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM  
• Friday and Saturday: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM  
• Sunday: 12:00 PM to 5:00 PM  

The building will be closed on all recognized public holidays. While the doors remain open during posted hours, some services (including printing, interlibrary loans, and study room bookings) may have reduced availability after 6:00 PM on weekday evenings. 

For holiday schedules, temporary closures, or general assistance, please visit our official website or call the front desk at 555-0198. 

Thank you for using our library. We are committed to providing reliable access to resources and quiet study spaces for all community members.
