Join us this Saturday, May 17, from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. at Maple Hill Park for our Spring Community Clean-Up and Picnic! 🌳🧹

Bring a pair of gloves and help us clear trails, plant wildflowers around the pond, and spruce up the playground. Tools, water, and snacks will be provided. When the work is done, stay for a free potluck picnic. Bring a dish to share if you can, or just bring yourself.

Kids are welcome, and we'll have a face-painting table and a bubble station for the little ones. 🎈

Questions? Message us here or stop by the info tent by the main gate.

Let's make Maple Hill shine together! 💚
