**PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE**

To all residents and motorists:

Please be advised that **Oak Street**, between **1st Avenue and 4th Avenue**, will be closed to all motor vehicles for scheduled underground water pipe repairs.

* **Dates and Times:** Monday, November 17, through Wednesday, November 19, daily from 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The road will reopen to normal traffic each evening at 5:00 PM.
* **Detour Route:** Drivers will be redirected around the work area via Elm Street. Marked directional signs will guide motorists along the detour.
* **Pedestrian Access:** Sidewalks will remain open for pedestrians at all times.

Please plan for extra travel time and use caution when driving near the work area. 

For questions or additional information, please contact the City Public Works Department at (555) 019-2834 or visit www.anytown.gov/roadwork.
