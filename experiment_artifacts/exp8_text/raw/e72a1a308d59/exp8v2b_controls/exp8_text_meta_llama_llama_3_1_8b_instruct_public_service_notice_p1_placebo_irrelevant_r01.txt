**Attention All Bookworms and Study Buddies!**

**Library Opening Hours: Your Guide to Getting Your Read On**

Are you a book lover, a student in need of a quiet study space, or simply someone who appreciates a good page-turner? Look no further! Our library is here to serve you, but we need you to know our hours so you can plan your visit accordingly.

**Monday to Thursday:** 9:00 AM - 8:00 PM

**Friday to Saturday:** 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM

**Sunday:** 12:00 PM - 5:00 PM

**Note:** We are closed on all major holidays, including New Year's Day, Easter Sunday, and Thanksgiving Day.

Whether you're in the mood for a literary masterpiece, a juicy novel, or simply need to grab a book for a school project, our library is the perfect place to be. So mark our hours in your calendar and come on down to get your read on!

**We can't wait to see you in the library!**
