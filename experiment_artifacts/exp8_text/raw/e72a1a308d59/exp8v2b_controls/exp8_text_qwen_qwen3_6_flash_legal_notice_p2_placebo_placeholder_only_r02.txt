NOTICE OF UPDATED PRIVACY POLICY

This document serves as formal notification regarding material revisions to the Privacy Policy governing the collection, processing, and disclosure of personal information associated with all digital services provided by Meridian Data Systems, Inc. These updates become effective on November 1, 2024.

The revised policy incorporates adjustments to align with recent statutory amendments in California and Virginia, clarifies retention periods for transactional records, updates third-party data sharing classifications, and modifies the consent framework for automated decision-making processes. Additional provisions now detail the handling of aggregated analytics data and establish standardized procedures for user data deletion requests.

The complete updated Privacy Policy is publicly available at https://www.meridiandata.com/legal/privacy-policy. We strongly recommend reviewing the full document to understand how your information is managed. By continuing to utilize our platforms, applications, or customer support channels after the effective date, you acknowledge and accept the revised terms. Should you object to any provision, you may discontinue service and request account closure through the secure portal at https://www.meridiandata.com/account/settings prior to November 1, 2024.

Direct all inquiries regarding this notice or the updated policy to the Office of Data Compliance at compliance@meridiandata.com or via postal correspondence to 1200 Oakridge Boulevard, Suite 400, Portland, Oregon 97201. Correspondence will be addressed within fifteen business days in accordance with our standard response protocols.

This notice is issued pursuant to corporate governance requirements and applicable data protection regulations.
