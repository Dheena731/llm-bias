Here's a short comment you could leave on an essay:

"Several claims in this essay, particularly in the second and third paragraphs, are presented as facts or statistics without a source. Where you draw on research, data, or another author's ideas, please add a citation so the reader can verify the information and see where it came from. Direct quotations and paraphrases both need attribution. Revisit these passages and add in-text citations in your required style, along with the corresponding entries in your reference list."
