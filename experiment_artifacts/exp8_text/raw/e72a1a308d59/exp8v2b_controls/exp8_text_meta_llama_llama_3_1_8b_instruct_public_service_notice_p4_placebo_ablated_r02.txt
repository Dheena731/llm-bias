**Heat Advisory in Effect**

A heat advisory has been issued for the area, as temperatures are expected to reach extreme highs. The National Weather Service has reported temperatures in the upper 90s, with heat indices making it feel like it's over 100 degrees. 

Residents are advised to take precautions to stay cool and hydrated. Avoid strenuous outdoor activities during the peak sun hours of 11am to 3pm. Wear lightweight, light-colored clothing and stay in air-conditioned spaces when possible. 

Children, the elderly, and people with pre-existing medical conditions are especially vulnerable to heat-related illnesses. If you or someone you know is experiencing symptoms such as dizziness, nausea, or headaches, seek shade or air-conditioning and drink plenty of water.
