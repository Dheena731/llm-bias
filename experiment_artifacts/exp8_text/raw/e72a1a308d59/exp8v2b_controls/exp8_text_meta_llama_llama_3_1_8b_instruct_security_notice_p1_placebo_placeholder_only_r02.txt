**Workplace Security Notice**

**Password Reuse: A Security Risk**

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain a secure and safe work environment, we would like to remind all employees of the importance of using unique and strong passwords.

Reusing passwords across multiple accounts or systems can put our organization at risk of data breaches and cyber attacks. If an unauthorized individual gains access to one of your accounts, they may be able to access other accounts that use the same password.

**What You Can Do**

* Use a unique and complex password for each account and system.
* Avoid using easily guessable information such as your name, birthdate, or common words.
* Consider using a password manager to generate and store unique passwords for each account.
* Regularly update and rotate your passwords to minimize the risk of account compromise.

**Why It Matters**

Password reuse can have serious consequences for our organization, including compromised data, intellectual property theft, and reputational damage. By using unique and strong passwords, you are helping to protect our work environment and maintain the trust of our customers and partners.

If you have any questions or concerns about password reuse or security best practices, please reach out to your supervisor or the IT department for guidance and support.
