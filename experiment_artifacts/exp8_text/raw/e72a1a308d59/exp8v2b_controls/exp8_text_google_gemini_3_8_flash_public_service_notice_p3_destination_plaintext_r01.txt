NOTICE: RECYCLING COLLECTION CHANGES

Please be advised that your recycling collection schedule will be changing, effective Monday, November 3. 

Key details:
- New Collection Day: Recycling will now be collected on Thursdays instead of Tuesdays.
- Set-Out Time: Please have your bins at the curb by 7:00 AM on your designated collection day.
- Accepted Materials: There are no changes to the types of recyclable materials currently accepted.

To view the complete updated route schedule or to look up your specific address, please visit our website at www.yourcityhere.gov/recycling or call Customer Service at (555) 012-3456.

Thank you for your cooperation and continued support in keeping our community clean.
