**PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE**  
**Library Opening Hours**

To our community members and patrons:

Please observe the following schedule for the Public Library:

*   **Monday through Friday:** 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM  
*   **Saturday:** 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM  
*   **Sunday:** Closed  

These hours apply to all library locations and services. Please plan to arrive at least 15 minutes before closing time to return items and complete transactions.

For details on holiday closures or temporary schedule changes, please check our official website or contact the main office directly.

Thank you for using your library.
