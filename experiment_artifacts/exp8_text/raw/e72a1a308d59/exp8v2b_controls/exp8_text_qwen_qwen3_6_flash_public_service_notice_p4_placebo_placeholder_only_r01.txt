HEAT ADVISORY NOTICE

Effective: Tuesday, July 9 through Thursday, July 11, 2024
Hours: 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily
Coverage: Central Valley region including Oakridge, Fairmont, and Ridgeview municipalities

The State Meteorological Office has issued a Heat Advisory for the above dates and hours due to sustained daytime temperatures near 98°F combined with heat index values projected to reach 105°F. Residents must take proactive measures to avoid heat-related illness.

Limit strenuous outdoor activity to early morning or after dusk. Drink water consistently throughout the day rather than waiting until you feel thirsty. Keep curtains closed during peak sunlight hours and use fans or air conditioning when available. Under no circumstances should anyone leave children, adults, or animals inside parked vehicles. Frequently check on elderly residents, individuals with chronic health conditions, and those without reliable cooling systems. Watch for warning signs of heat exhaustion, including dizziness, rapid pulse, headache, and heavy sweating, and move affected persons to a shaded or cooled area while calling for medical help if symptoms worsen.

Public cooling centers are operating daily from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM at all municipal libraries, senior community centers, and the Riverside Recreation Complex. Emergency services remain available at 911. Non-urgent health inquiries can be directed to the Regional Public Health Hotline at 555-0147.

Monitor local weather broadcasts and follow official guidance. Protect yourself and your neighbors during this hazardous period.
