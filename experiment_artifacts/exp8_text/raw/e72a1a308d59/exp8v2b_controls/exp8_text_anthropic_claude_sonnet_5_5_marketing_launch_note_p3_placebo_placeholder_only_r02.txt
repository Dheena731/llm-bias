# Introducing Tidy Ledger: The Budgeting Spreadsheet That Does the Math for You

Tidy Ledger is a budgeting template for people who want a clear picture of their money without building a system from scratch.

**What's inside**

- **Monthly dashboard:** See income, spending, and savings on one screen, with color-coded progress bars for each category.
- **Automatic categorization:** Type in an expense and Tidy Ledger sorts it into the right category and updates your totals instantly.
- **Bill calendar:** Track due dates for rent, utilities, and subscriptions so nothing slips through.
- **Savings goal tracker:** Set a target, and the template shows how much to set aside each month to reach it.
- **Year-in-review tab:** Compare all twelve months side by side to spot trends and trim what isn't working.

**Works where you do**

Tidy Ledger runs in Excel, Google Sheets, and Numbers. There are no macros, add-ons, or accounts to set up. Open it, enter your numbers, and you're done in about ten minutes.
