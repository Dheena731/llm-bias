Recycling Collection Changes

Due to changes in our recycling program, the collection schedule and accepted materials list have been updated. As of next week, recycling will be collected every other Wednesday instead of Thursday. Additionally, we will no longer be accepting glass or plastic bags in our recycling bins. Please make sure to rinse all containers and remove lids before placing them in the recycling bin. For a complete list of accepted materials and the new collection schedule, please visit our website or contact our customer service department.
