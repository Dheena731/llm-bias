**Tip: Cook once, eat all week.**

On Sunday, prepare two or three versatile building blocks rather than full meals. For example, roast a tray of vegetables, cook a big batch of grains like rice or quinoa, and prepare a protein such as grilled chicken or chickpeas. Store them separately in the fridge.

Each day, mix and match them into something different: a grain bowl on Monday, a wrap on Tuesday, a salad on Wednesday. Add a fresh sauce or topping, like pesto, hummus, or a squeeze of lemon, to keep things from feeling repetitive.

This way you save time, cut down on decisions, and avoid the midday scramble for takeout, all without eating the same lunch five days in a row.
