PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT

A Heat Advisory is in effect for our area today from 11:00 AM to 8:00 PM. High temperatures combined with humidity are expected to create dangerously hot conditions, with heat index values reaching up to 105 degrees.

Residents are strongly urged to take the following precautions:
- Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty.
- Stay indoors in air-conditioned spaces during peak afternoon heat.
- Reschedule strenuous outdoor activities to the early morning or late evening.
- Check on elderly neighbors, family members, and those without air conditioning.
- Never leave children or pets unattended in parked vehicles for any length of time.

Designated cooling centers are open at local public libraries and community centers. If you or someone nearby experiences signs of heat stroke—such as confusion, dizziness, or loss of consciousness—move to a cooler area immediately and call 911.
