Subject: Reminder: Please Lock Your Screen When Away From Your Desk

Dear Colleagues,

This is a friendly reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even for a short time. Locking your screen helps protect confidential company information and client data from being viewed or accessed by unauthorized people.

To lock your screen quickly:
- Windows: Press the Windows key + L
- Mac: Press Control + Command + Q
