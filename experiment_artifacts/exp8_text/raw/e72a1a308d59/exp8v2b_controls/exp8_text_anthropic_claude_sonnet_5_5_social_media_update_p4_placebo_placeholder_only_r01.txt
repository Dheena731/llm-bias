📅 Calendar tip: Stop putting only meetings on your calendar. Block time for the tasks you actually need to finish, too.

Instead of a to-do list that keeps growing, give each important task a 30 to 60 minute slot. Writing a report? Schedule it for Tuesday at 10 AM. Planning a trip? Block off Thursday evening.

When a task has a time and a place on your calendar, you're far more likely to do it. It also shows you right away when your week is too full, so you can adjust before things pile up.

Try it for one week and see how much more you get done. ✅
