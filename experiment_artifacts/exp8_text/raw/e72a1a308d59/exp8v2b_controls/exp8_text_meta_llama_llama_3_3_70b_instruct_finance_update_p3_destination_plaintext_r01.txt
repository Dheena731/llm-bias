We are excited to announce the launch of our new savings goal feature, which allows you to set and track specific savings targets. This feature enables you to create customized goals, such as saving for a vacation, a down payment on a house, or a big purchase, and monitor your progress towards achieving them. With this new feature, you can break down larger goals into smaller, manageable steps and receive updates on your progress, helping you stay motivated and on track to reach your savings objectives.
