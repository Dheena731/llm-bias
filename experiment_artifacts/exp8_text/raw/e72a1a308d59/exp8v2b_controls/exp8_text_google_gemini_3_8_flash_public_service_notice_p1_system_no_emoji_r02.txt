PUBLIC NOTICE: LIBRARY OPERATING HOURS

The City Public Library welcomes all community members to visit, explore our collection, and utilize our study spaces. Please note our regular hours of operation:

Monday – Thursday: 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM
Friday – Saturday: 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM
Sunday: 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM

Digital resources, including e-books, audiobooks, and research databases, remain available 24/7 through the library website. Book drop boxes are open for returns outside of normal operating hours.

For holiday schedules, program details, and service inquiries, please visit our website at www.citylibrary.org or call (555) 019-2834.
