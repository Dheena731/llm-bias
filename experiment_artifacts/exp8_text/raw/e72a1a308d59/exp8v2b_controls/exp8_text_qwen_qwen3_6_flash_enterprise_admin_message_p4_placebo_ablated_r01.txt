**Subject: Action Required: Complete Your Team’s Quarterly Access Review**

To All Managers and Department Supervisors,

As part of our regular security and compliance procedures, you are required to complete the quarterly access review for your team. This process ensures that every employee retains only the system and data permissions necessary for their current responsibilities, reducing risk and maintaining audit readiness.

Please log in to the company Access Management Portal by **Friday, October 27, 2024**, to verify each team member’s access levels. A separate email containing your personalized review link and step-by-step instructions will be delivered today.

The review must be submitted by the stated deadline. Unreviewed accounts may be automatically restricted to protect system integrity. If you are unsure about appropriate permission levels for a role, consult your team’s job description or reach out to the IT Help Desk at helpdesk@company.com or extension 5555.

Thank you for your prompt attention and for helping us maintain a secure work environment.
