**Preheat your sheet pan for faster, crispier roasts.**

When making a quick sheet-pan meal, pop the empty baking sheet into the oven while it preheats. Tossing your seasoned vegetables and proteins onto a screaming-hot surface starts the searing process immediately. This cuts down on total cooking time and guarantees deeply browned, crispy edges instead of soggy, steamed food—all with the same minimal cleanup.
