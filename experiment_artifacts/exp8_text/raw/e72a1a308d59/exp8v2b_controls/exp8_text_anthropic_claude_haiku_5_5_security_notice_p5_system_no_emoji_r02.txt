**Notice: How to Report a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If you have lost your company laptop or believe it has been stolen, report it immediately. Prompt reporting protects company data and helps us respond quickly.

**What to do:**

1. **Contact IT Support right away** at [phone number] or [email address]. Do this as soon as you notice the loss, even if you are unsure.
2. **Report it to your manager** within [timeframe, e.g., 24 hours].
3. **If the laptop was stolen,** file a police report and send a copy to [Security/Facilities contact].
4. **Do not attempt to access or recover data** from the device yourself.

**What to include in your report:**
- Date, time, and location where the laptop was last seen
- Make, model, and serial number (if known)
- Any files or sensitive information stored on the device
- Police report number, if applicable

IT Support will remotely lock or wipe the device where possible and will update the asset records.

Failure to report a lost or stolen device promptly may result in disciplinary action under company policy.
