PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE

Main Street, between 1st Avenue and 4th Avenue, will be closed to all through traffic starting Monday, October 14, at 7:00 AM through Friday, October 18, at 5:00 PM. 

This closure is required for scheduled road resurfacing and utility repairs. 

Local access for residents and businesses will be maintained. All other motorists should follow the posted detour routes via Oak Street. Please expect minor delays and drive with caution in the area.

For more information, please contact the Department of Public Works at 555-0199 or visit www.citywebsite.gov/traffic.
