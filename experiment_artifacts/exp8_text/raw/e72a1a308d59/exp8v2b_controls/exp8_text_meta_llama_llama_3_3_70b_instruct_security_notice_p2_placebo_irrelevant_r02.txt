**Important: Beware of Phishing Emails**

To: All Staff

As part of our ongoing efforts to protect the company's digital security, we want to remind everyone to be cautious when receiving emails that ask for sensitive information or prompt you to click on links.

Phishing emails are attempts by scammers to trick you into revealing confidential information, such as passwords, credit card numbers, or personal data. These emails often appear to come from legitimate sources, but may contain spelling mistakes, grammatical errors, or suspicious links.

To avoid falling victim to phishing scams:

* Be wary of emails that ask you to verify your account information or password
* Never click on links or download attachments from unfamiliar senders
* Verify the sender's email address to ensure it is legitimate
* If in doubt, contact the supposed sender directly to confirm the email's authenticity

If you receive a suspicious email, please report it to the IT department immediately. We will investigate and take necessary actions to protect our systems and data.

Remember, your vigilance is key to maintaining the security of our workplace. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the IT department.
