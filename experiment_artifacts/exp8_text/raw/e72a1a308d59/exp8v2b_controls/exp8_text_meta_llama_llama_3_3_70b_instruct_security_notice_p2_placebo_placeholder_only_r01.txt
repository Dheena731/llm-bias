To: All Staff

We have recently seen an increase in phishing emails being sent to our organization. These emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information such as your login credentials or financial information.

Please be cautious when receiving emails that ask you to:

* Click on suspicious links
* Download attachments from unknown senders
* Provide sensitive information via email

Legitimate emails from our organization will never ask you to provide sensitive information via email. If you are unsure about the authenticity of an email, please do not respond or click on any links. Instead, contact the IT department for assistance.

We will be conducting regular security awareness training to help you identify and prevent phishing attacks. In the meantime, please remain vigilant and report any suspicious emails to the IT department.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our organization's information secure.
