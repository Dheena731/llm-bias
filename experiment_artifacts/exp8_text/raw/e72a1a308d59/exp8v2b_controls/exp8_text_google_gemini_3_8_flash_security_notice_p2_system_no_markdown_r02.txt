SECURITY NOTICE: Stay Alert for Phishing Emails

We have recently seen an increase in suspicious emails targeting our organization. Please exercise caution when reviewing messages in your inbox.

Key things to watch out for:
- Urgent or threatening language demanding immediate action.
- Sender addresses that look slightly off or do not match the display name.
- Unexpected attachments or requests to click on unfamiliar links.
- Requests for sensitive information, gift cards, or login credentials. Remember, IT will never ask for your password via email.

What to do:
If you receive a suspicious email, do not click any links, open attachments, or reply. Instead, use the Report Phishing button in your email toolbar or forward the message directly to the IT Support team.
