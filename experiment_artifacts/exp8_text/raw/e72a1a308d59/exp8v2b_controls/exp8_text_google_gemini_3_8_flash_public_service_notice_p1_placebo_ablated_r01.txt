**Public Notice: Library Opening Hours**

The Public Library welcomes all community members and visitors during our regular operating hours:

* **Monday – Thursday:** 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM  
* **Friday – Saturday:** 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM  
* **Sunday:** Closed  

The outdoor book-return drop box located by the main entrance is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. 

For information on scheduled holiday closures or upcoming events, please visit our website or speak with a member of our staff at the front desk. 

We look forward to seeing you!
