Take a Screen Break

Staring at a screen for long stretches can leave your eyes dry and tired, your neck and shoulders stiff, and your focus harder to hold. Taking a short break helps reset all three.

Try this: every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds. Stand up, stretch, roll your shoulders, and drink some water. A few minutes away from the screen is enough to make a difference.

Your eyes, body, and mind will thank you.
