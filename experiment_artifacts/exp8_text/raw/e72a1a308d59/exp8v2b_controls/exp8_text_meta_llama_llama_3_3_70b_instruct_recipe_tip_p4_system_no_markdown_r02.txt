To plan lunches for a busy week, consider setting aside one day to prepare and portion out your meals in advance. Choose a few simple and versatile ingredients, such as whole grains, lean proteins, and vegetables, and use them to make a variety of dishes like salads, wraps, and soups. Pack each meal in a container and label it with the day of the week, so you can quickly grab what you need as you head out the door. This will save you time and money, and help you eat healthy even on the most hectic days.
