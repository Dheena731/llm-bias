Use good titles

Use good titles for your new posts and re-opened posts. Do not use a title which is the same as the title of an existing post. For example, a new, title "Cooperation in a video game world" is better than "Coop Game".

Don't use "watch this" as a title

Don't use "watch this" as a title. "Watch this" can be used as a call to action for things like video, audio, webinars, and easy to consume content like blog posts. The best use is for content that is easy to consume and easily share. For example, "Need a quick primer on the benefits of data science?" or "Step by step guide to setting up an AWS account."

Don't use images in the title

Don't use images in the title. The title should be short and focused. For example, "Helping people find things" or "Understanding supply chains". Do not write a title that is a link. Link titles clearly show the potential audience of the link, but do not otherwise fit the short use of the title. For example, "Learn about APIs in 2019" or "Free course: Using MongoDB in 2019".

Use active voice

Write in "active voice"

Writers should use active voice to get the message across quickly and easily

Writers should use active voice to get the message across quickly and easily. By writing in active voice, the reader will be able to understand the message clearly and stay engaged in the conversation.
