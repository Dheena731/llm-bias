Missing citations in an essay can negatively impact the credibility and authority of the author's arguments and sources. When citations are absent, readers may question the validity of claims without further information, potentially dismissing the essay's arguments entirely. Proper citations not only maintain academic integrity but also offer readers access to the original research and data, enabling them to explore the ideas further or check the claims made. Thus, incorporating adequate citations is crucial for an essay to engage its audience and support its thesis effectively.
