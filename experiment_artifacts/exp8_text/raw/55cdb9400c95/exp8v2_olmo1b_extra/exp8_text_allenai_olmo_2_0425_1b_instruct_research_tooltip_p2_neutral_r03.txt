A confidence interval (CI) in a chart is a range of possible values that estimates an unknown population parameter with a certain level of confidence. For example, if you're creating a chart to represent the average income of a population and you include a 95% confidence interval, it suggests that if you were to collect data again and repeatedly create intervals, 95% of those intervals would contain the true population mean income. This statement of confidence in the interval is critical for understanding the reliability and precision of the data.
