Today, we have a heat advisory in effect for the entire area. Extreme heat is expected to persist into the evening hours. It is important for residents to stay indoors as much as possible, drink plenty of fluids, and take appropriate measures to stay cool.

Residents should also use air conditioning where possible and if they do not have air conditioning, fans may be used to help reduce the heat. The city will continue to monitor the heat index and issue updates as needed. If the heat becomes unbearable or if you experience any adverse health signs, please contact the city's emergency services.
