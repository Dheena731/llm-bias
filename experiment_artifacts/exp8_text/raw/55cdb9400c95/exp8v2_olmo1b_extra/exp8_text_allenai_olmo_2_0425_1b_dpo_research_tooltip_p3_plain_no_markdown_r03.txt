When using a date-range selector, this handy feature lets you quickly choose specific dates within a given timeframe. For instance, if you're looking to view photos taken between January 1, 2020, and December 31, 2020, simply drag the edge of your mouse pointer over the range on the toolbar. The tooltip will pop up, clearly indicating the start and end dates, making it easy to narrow down your photo search with just a flick of your wrist. Note that this functionality is optimized for both desktop and mobile web browsers, ensuring a smooth experience across various devices.
