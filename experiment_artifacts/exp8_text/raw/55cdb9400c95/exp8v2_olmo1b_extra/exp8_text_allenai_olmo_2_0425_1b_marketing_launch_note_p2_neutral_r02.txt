Best practices
A set of rules or recommendations for how to do something right, in the best way. For instance, from a Google search, you could read up on best practices for web design, to get a better understanding of what to include and how to build a website.

Business-to-business (B2B)
A relationship between two businesses, where the business that is selling a product or offering a service to the customer is called a "business" and the customer is called "B2B" because they buy from a business. There are many examples of B2B relationships, including but not limited to hardware stores, software vendors, and HVAC businesses.

Business-to-consumer (B2C)
A relationship between a business and a customer, where the business is selling to an end-user, called a "consumer" (since many "consumers" are companies themselves). Examples of B2C relationships include restaurants, department stores, e-commerce websites, toy stores, and clothing stores.

Content Management System (CMS)
A web application that allows for the creation and management of content. This could range from a website template builder to a more advanced blog software or video camera software.

Cost per Action (CPA)
A pricing model where customers have to spend a certain amount of money in order to complete an action, such as signing up for a newsletter, making a purchase, filling out a form, or referencing a specific page on the website. The cost is calculated based on the total number of actions completed, which can be done quickly or over time.

Cost per installment (CPI)
A pricing model where customers have to pay a certain amount of money after a certain number of installments, which can be done quickly or over time. For example, a car dealership might charge $199/month for a vehicle, but some customers would be required to pay $199/month for 12 months, while others would pay $199/month for one year, and so on.

Cost per lead (CPL)
A pricing model where customers have to pay a certain amount of money for each lead that is generated. In other words, if a customer pays a certain amount for a newsletter subscription, they must also pay a certain amount for each newsletter that is delivered to them.
