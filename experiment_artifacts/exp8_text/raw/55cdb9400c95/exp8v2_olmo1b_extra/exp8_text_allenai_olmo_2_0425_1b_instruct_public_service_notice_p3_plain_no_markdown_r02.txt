In recent weeks our local recycling program has undergone some adjustments to ensure we all participate more efficiently. As part of these changes the collection schedule for plastic bottles glass jars and aluminum cans has been modified. Starting next week you will need to separate these materials differently from other waste. Specifically:

- Glass jars and bottles should be rinsed out and placed in a separate section of your blue recycling bin.
- Plastic bottles of all kinds should be rinsed out and placed in the bin marked for plastic.
- Aluminum cans should be placed in a separate bin specifically designated for this material.

If these changes are confusing please do not hesitate to reach out to our recycling team who will be happy to answer any questions you might have. By following these new instructions we hope to improve the efficiency of our recycling program and encourage everyone to contribute to a cleaner environment. Thank you for your cooperation in these efforts.
