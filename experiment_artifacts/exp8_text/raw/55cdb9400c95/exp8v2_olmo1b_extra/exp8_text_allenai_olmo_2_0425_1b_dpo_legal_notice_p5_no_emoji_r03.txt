**Shared Network Policy**

The shared network is for use by authorized users only. Please ensure that your activities do not infringe on the rights of others or disrupt the network's operation. Unauthorized downloading, sharing, or uploading of copyrighted materials, including music, movies, software, and images, is strictly prohibited. Unauthorized accessing of specific accounts or systems, including hacking, is strictly forbidden. The use of network resources for any malicious intent, including but not limited to Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks, is strictly prohibited. Cyberbullying or harassment of any kind is not tolerated and may result in disciplinary action. For further information regarding permissible use, please refer to our website's Terms of Service. All members are responsible for abiding by these rules and contribute to a safe, productive, and enjoyable environment for everyone. 

**Violation of this policy will be addressed in accordance with applicable law and may include, but not be limited to, temporary or permanent suspension or expulsion of access to the shared network. Enforcement of this policy may also involve legal action against individuals found to have violated the law.**

Note: This notice is written in the style of a formal legal provision and aims to outline the rules and consequences of using a shared network in a manner consistent with legal standards, respecting the rights of others and the network's operational integrity.
