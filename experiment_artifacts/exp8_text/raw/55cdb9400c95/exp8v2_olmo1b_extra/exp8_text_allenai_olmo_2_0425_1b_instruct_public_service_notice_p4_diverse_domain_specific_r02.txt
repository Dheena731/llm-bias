Attention Residents and Visitors!

As we continue to experience warm and humid conditions, we are issuing this public notice to inform you of a Heat Advisory that has been temporarily activated.

The weather may be providing us with a delicious summer experience, but it's important to stay vigilant about the risks associated with prolonged heat exposure. The high temperatures can increase the risk of health issues such as heat exhaustion, heat stroke, and other related complications. 

Here are some practical steps you can take to stay safe:
- Stay indoors when possible, avoiding prolonged exposure to heat indoors.
- Monitor your body temperature and seek shade when you start to feel the effects of the heat. 
- Drink plenty of water and eat cool, moist foods.
- Avoid strenuous activities that could raise your body temperature further.
- Seek immediate relief from high temperatures by taking cool showers, using fans, or seeking air conditioning if available.

For those experiencing health issues, the Public Health Agency recommends staying hydrated and seeking medical attention if symptoms worsen or persist.

Please be aware this advisory is in effect until further notice, and we will continue to monitor and adjust conditions as needed.
