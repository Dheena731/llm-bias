It’s the end of the tax year and it’s time to review your final tax statement. Check the total amount you owe or are due. Review any deductions or credits you might have claimed. Consider any tax credits that might apply if you qualify. Make sure you understand how much tax you have paid this year. If you owe money, think about paying it off as soon as possible to avoid late fees. If you have any questions, don’t hesitate to contact your tax professional.
