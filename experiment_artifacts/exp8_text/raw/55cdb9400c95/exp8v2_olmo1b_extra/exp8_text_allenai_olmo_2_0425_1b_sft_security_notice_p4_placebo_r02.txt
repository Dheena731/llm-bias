Enabling two-factor authentication is a very important security measure that can help to protect your account from unauthorized access. It works by adding an extra step to login, such as a one-time code sent to your phone, which you must enter along with your password. This can prevent even if someone knows your password, they would not be able to log into your account.

If you have not yet enabled two-factor authentication, you need to do so now. It is very easy to add to your account, and it is very important for the security of your personal information. Please ensure that you have the necessary security measures in place to protect your account.
