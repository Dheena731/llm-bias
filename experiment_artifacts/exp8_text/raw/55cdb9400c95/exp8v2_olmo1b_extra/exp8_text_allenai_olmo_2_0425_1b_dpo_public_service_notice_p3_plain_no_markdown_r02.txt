Recycling collection schedules are undergoing a change in our area. Starting next week the standard weekly collection for cardboard plastic and glass will be reduced from three times a week to twice a week. Residents will need to sort their recyclables as usual but will now only able to recycle on days marked on their new weekly recycling calendar which should be delivered to their door.

Residents are encouraged to familiarize themselves with the new schedule and to ensure all recyclable materials are in the correct bin for separation. Drop-off centers will remain open as usual for anyone needing more convenient recycling options.

For those who prefer to sort their recyclables by hand, there will still be an opportunity to contribute to a cleaner environment by collecting these materials separately from non-recyclable items.

We appreciate your commitment to recycling and thank you for your continued support. Please check the recycling schedule in your local neighborhood and adjust your habits accordingly.
