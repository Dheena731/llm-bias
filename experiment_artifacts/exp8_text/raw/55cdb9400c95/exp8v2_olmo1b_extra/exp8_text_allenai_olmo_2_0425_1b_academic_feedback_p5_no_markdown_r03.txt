Overall, this looks good. Just a few things:

  1. In the abstract, there is no information about what the problem is you are solving. What is the question? This sounds like a methodology paper so you should say that right up front.
  2. In the “abstract” paragraph, you say “When readers come across a statement they do not understand, they often scan the text to find the original source.” This is not necessarily true. Some readers simply click to google or stack exchange or ask a question on the iOS site, since they are not sure. Others will actually click through to the source. I do not see any data to back up this claim. You should check this before you write the paper.
  3. This is a general comment, not an issue with your actual paper, but “is always more engaging” is an opinion. You should give sources for your claims.
  4. Use “this research…” in the abstract. Use “we used…” in the body.
  5. This section is oddly worded. First you mention context, then you mention that the context is missing. This may not be a big issue, but I think you should be more clear in the body of the paper.
