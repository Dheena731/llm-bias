**Reporting a Lost Company Laptop**

*Please report immediately:*
- The missing laptop should be brought to the IT department as soon as possible
- It may be necessary to provide any identification or documents that were on the laptop
- Do not attempt to access passwords or files on the laptop – it may be encrypted or have security measures in place

*Details to include:*
- Model number and serial number
- Date and time of disappearance
- Full description of the laptop's condition (location, accessories, etc.)
- Any other relevant information that may aid in the recovery of the laptop

*Action Steps:*
1. Contact the IT department if you are unable to locate the laptop yourself
2. Preserve any evidence that may be important for the recovery of the laptop
3. Stay informed about any progress or updates regarding the laptop's recovery

*Optional:*
- For specific questions about lost laptops, refer to our lost property policies or contact your human resources department

*Additional Notes:*
- You may be eligible for a reward or reimbursement for the loss of the laptop
- Please let the IT department know that you have filed a report on this matter
