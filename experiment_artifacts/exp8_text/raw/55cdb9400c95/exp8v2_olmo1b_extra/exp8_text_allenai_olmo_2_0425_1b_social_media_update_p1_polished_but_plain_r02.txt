If writing in English, it would be good to add some details about the app’s localization to include the features. Describe the screen layout and the main elements of the interface. Also, discuss how to use the app in multiple languages.

If you choose to write in Spanish, you need to include a link to the language version of your app or website. Remember to write in a style that fits the audience.

If writing in other languages, take appropriate care of the details. The language version of the app or website may differ from the English version, which should be mentioned in the case of localization.

In the text, do not use the trademark or registered version of the app name. Use the product name or, if available, another name for the app.

A question-answer format

For different types of apps, the writing style may differ. Take into account the purpose and goals of your app or website. If the app is aimed at a particular audience, it is necessary to adapt the text in a way that would be useful for them.

Write a text in a question-answer format or as a story in the past tense. For example, if your app is aimed at children, accompany your text with appropriate positive quotes that are interesting and appeal to its target audience.

If the app is aimed at adults, use direct questions.

Introduce new features

You can write a text to introduce new features that significantly change the content, appearance, or functionality of your app or website. Include the following information:

  • what you will change;
  • how the feature will work;
  • the expected benefits for your target audience;
  • when the change will be made.

Highlight the benefits

When you write about a new feature, you should highlight its benefits and play a role of a marketing tool. Focus on the benefits that would make it worth considering your app or website.

Remember to explain the features and benefits from the point of view of your target audience.


As a marketing tool, you can write a text that describes your app locally and internationally. In this case, the text should describe the benefits of using your app and describe the process of its use. Also, if you have a localization, describe the target audience and provide more details about it.

What to include in the app description?

A description of the product can be an important marketing tool that not only helps to find a potential client, but also to retain and convince the existing one. The description of the app, just like the description of the app itself, should be adequately prepared.

In the article "Why products should be described correctly" we discussed the importance of carefully choosing keywords for the description of the product. So, do not forget about them here. Each app description should contain the keywords of the product or service it describes properly.

Also, it is necessary to describe the benefits of the product. It is worth describing the main features of the app, making a minimum description of the target audience, explaining what the product in question is for. To do this, it is better to use the target audience as a reference for keywords.

It is also worth describing how to use the app, including a video that can be added to the description of the product, or a link to the app store page.

  • The version of the operating system;
