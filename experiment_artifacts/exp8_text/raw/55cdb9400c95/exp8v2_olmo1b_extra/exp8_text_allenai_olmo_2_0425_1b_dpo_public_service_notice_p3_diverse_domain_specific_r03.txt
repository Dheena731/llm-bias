Attention Residents: Starting next month, our recycling collection schedule will undergo a minor but significant change. We are introducing a dual-sort system, effective from Monday, January 1st. This update aims to enhance our ability to manage your waste more efficiently and protect our environment.

**Familiarize Yourself with the New System:**

* **For rigid plastics (like bottles and cans):** Separate these items into the blue cart. This cart is designated exclusively for plastic products. Ensure you empty your recyclable bottles and cans into the blue bin immediately after use.
* **For paper products (including newsprint, paperboard, and magazines):** Place these in the green bin. Paper should be clean and dry, and single-stream collection will commence. We've introduced new bins for ease of sorting.
* **Mixed recycling:** No need to adjust your habits for this; continue to recycle glass, plastic number 1 (contaminated with food scraps), metal (tin and aluminum cans, hardware, locks, etc.), and any other items not listed as compostables (like textiles, certain kitchen scraps).
* **Compostables:** We'll be introducing a separate bin for compostable items. These include plant-based food scraps, coffee grounds, and yard waste. Remember, compostable items should be clean and dry before collection.

**Preparation Tips:**

* **Sort now:** While there will be no penalty for incorrect sorting, giving careful attention to the new labels will make the transition smoother and help us optimize our recycling process.
* **Check labels:** Local businesses and produce might still have old labels, so peel off any stickers and check for the new recycling symbols if you're unsure.
* **Drop-off centers:** For larger amounts or unfamiliar items, you can drop off recyclables at designated locations. Find them on our website or your local municipality's website.

Our community's commitment to a sustainable environment is more important than ever. We hope this update brings peace of mind knowing that your efforts contribute to our collective goal of a waste reduction and environmental protection.
