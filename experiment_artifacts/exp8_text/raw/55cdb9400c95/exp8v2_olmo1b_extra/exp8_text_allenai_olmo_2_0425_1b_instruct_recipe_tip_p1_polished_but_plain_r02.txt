Leftovers can be turned into a delicious meal with a quick stir-fry. Combine your leftover vegetables and meat in a large pan with soy sauce, garlic powder, and a touch of oil. Toss to coat everything and let it sizzle briefly before adding any seasonings. You can add more soy sauce if desired and cook until everything is heated through and slightly browned. Serve hot with rice or noodles.
