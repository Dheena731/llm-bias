Beginning January 1st, our community recycling program is experiencing some changes. We’ll be moving some recycling bins to different locations, increasing our collection schedule a bit, and introducing a new way to sort materials for those who prefer to do so.

Here’s what we’re planning:

* **Expanded Collection Times:** The collection days will be extended by a few hours on weekdays and Saturdays. This will allow more residents to participate in recycling their waste conveniently. You can find the new collection schedule on our website under Recycling.

* **Location Change:** For those who prefer to sort their recyclables themselves, we’ll be moving some bins to nearby parking lots and outdoor areas. These locations are easy to find and accessible to families and individuals with limited mobility.

* **New Sort Options:** While some recyclables will remain the same, we’ll introduce new categories for certain items to encourage further recycling. This includes electronic waste, compostable materials, and glass.

For those accustomed to sorting by color or content, these changes may seem a bit confusing at first. Don’t worry, it’s a standard practice that serves to separate materials for better processing and environmental benefit. The aim is to reduce waste and recycle more efficiently.

**Why these changes?**

* **Environmental Impact:** Recycling one ton of post-consumer newspaper saves about 3.3 cubic feet of landfill space and reduces energy use by about 4.1 kilos of CO2.

* **Community Engagement:** By adjusting our collection schedule and introducing new sorting options, we hope to increase participation in our recycling program, thereby reducing the volume of non-recyclable waste sent to landfills.

We appreciate your cooperation and understanding as we implement these changes. Remember, every bit of reuse and recycle helps keep our environment cleaner and healthier. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact the recycling team at your local extension office.

Let’s work together to keep our community vibrant and sustainable!
