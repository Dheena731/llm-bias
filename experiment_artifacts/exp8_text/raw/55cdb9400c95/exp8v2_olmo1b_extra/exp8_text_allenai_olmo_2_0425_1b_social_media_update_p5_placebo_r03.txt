Helpful site, the same message could be reproduced almost exactly in hand-written script, as long as it was spelled correctly. It reads almost like a short story in which the main point is stated in the first sentence, the middle paragraph is about the technical set-up, and the end paragraph is about the conclusion.

You could make a mistake or leave out something in the middle paragraph, or later paragraphs, and it would still work.

Here's another example from my own life:

"A man was carrying a large amount of water in a bucket. It spilled all over the sidewalk."

Why did this work better than "The water spilled"?

Well, the first sentence tells you right away that the man is carrying water. It doesn't take much to imagine that, and then you might be interested to know that it was a lot.

The last two sentences told me what happened, but it's more interesting to wonder how it must have been possible for the man to carry water in the bucket.

If it hadn't been so simple, I might have begun wondering where the man was going and why he was carrying water.
