**Digital Detox Short Break Reminder**

As you unplug from screens, remember:
- **5 minutes:** Commit just 5 minutes of break time to stretch your legs, breathe in some fresh air, or take a quick walk around your workspace.
- **10 minutes:** Dive into a short, engaging activity like a few minutes of mindfulness exercises, a quick news read from print media, or reviewing a fun, non-screen book.
- **20 minutes:** Push back further to engage in a physical activity such as a short workout, a brisk walk, or a phone call with a friend.
- **Mindfulness Moment:** Use this time to quietly reflect on what's truly important and set your intentions for the rest of your day.

Heed this call to replenish your mind and body. Screen time in moderation is great, but remember that after Periodic rest, you'll perform better and feel more connected.
