- **Rice Tips: Making Less Sticky Rice**

- **Start with the Right Rice:** Choose a high-quality long-grain rice such as Arborio, Carnian, or Basmati to get a firmer, fluffier final product.
- **Soak the Rice:** Soaking your rice for 30 minutes can help your rice cook more evenly and reduce the sticky quality.
- **Drain and Rinse:** After soaking, drain the rice and rinse it three times with cold water to remove excess starch.
- **Use Gentle Heat:** Cook the rice in a low simmer rather than a fast boil to maintain the grains' shape and prevent them from sticking together.
