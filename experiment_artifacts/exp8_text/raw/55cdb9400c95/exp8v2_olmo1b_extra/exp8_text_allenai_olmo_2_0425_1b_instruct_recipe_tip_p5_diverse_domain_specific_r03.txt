Tip: Spice It Up with Smokin' Seasonings!

When roasting vegetables in-season, consider a few smokin' spices that accentuate their natural flavors while giving them a robust edge. Think herbs like rosemary, thyme, and sage, and the umami-rich goodness of soy sauce or miso paste. Adding a sprinkle of toasted ground nuts or melted butter for richness can also elevate the taste. Remember, the key is to combine these seasonings in small amounts and adjust according to your personal preference – the right seasoning brings out the best in your roasted veggies, making them a perfect side to your main dish.
