**Missing Citations in an Essay**

- **Inadequate Reference List:** The essay lacks a comprehensive reference list, which can undermine the credibility of the arguments presented.
- **Lack of Attribution:** Certain facts and sources are mentioned but not properly cited, making it difficult for readers to verify the claims.
- **Potential for Misinterpretation:** The essay may be perceived as unverified or lacking in reliable backing if citations are absent or incorrectly included.
- **Affecting Academic Standing:** Without proper citations, the essay may receive lower grades or disciplinary action from instructors.
- **Enhancing Writing Practice:** Missing citations is a learning opportunity to improve citation styles and ensure academic integrity in future essays.
