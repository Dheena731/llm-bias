The recent change to the card transaction fees is aimed at reducing the overall cost of the service to the holder and the bank. By reducing the cost, the bank and the cardholder save money on every transaction, and this allows them to pass on the cost savings to consumers. This change is also expected to improve the customer experience, as the bank is not charging as much to process transactions. The reduced fees also encourage more customers to use their cards for purchases, as the cost is lower than if they were to pay cash.
