Write your proposal

Now that you have a good idea of what your client needs, it’s time to write your proposal. This is your chance to convince your client to hire you and work with you on a project. 

The proposal should include a brief description of the project, including why you’re the best person to do it, and what you’ll do to make it happen. It should also include any risks or uncertainties you’ll face, and how you’ll handle them. 

Your proposal should be specific enough that your client can make an informed decision, but also broad enough that they can see the benefits of working with you. You should also include your budget and timeline for completing the project, as well as any additional resources that you might need. 

For your client, writing a proposal is a chance to show their current stakeholders that they’re serious about working with you. It’s also a chance for you to show your potential clients that you’re committed to doing a great job for them. 

Once you’ve written your proposal, send it to your client. Ask them to review it and send you any comments or questions before you finalize the details.
