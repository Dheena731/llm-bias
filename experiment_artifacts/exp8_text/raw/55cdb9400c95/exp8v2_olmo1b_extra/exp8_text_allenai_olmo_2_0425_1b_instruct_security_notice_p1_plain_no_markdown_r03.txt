Attention all employees!

Password reuse is a significant security risk. When you use the same password for multiple accounts or services it can lead to a breach in one account being exploited to gain access to other accounts. Password should be unique for each service as no single password should be trusted to protect more than one account. If one account is compromised your personal information could be at risk. Therefore please do not reuse passwords. Create a unique password for each account and replace them on a regular basis to maintain a secure work environment. It is important to remember that cybersecurity is everyone’s responsibility. Thank you for your attention and cooperation.
