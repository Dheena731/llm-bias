## Short Comment on Missing Citations in an Essay

- **Understanding the Importance of Citing Sources**: When a citation is missing, it undermines the credibility of the author's arguments and work, as readers cannot verify the claims or evidence presented.
- **Potential Consequences**: Missing citations can lead to a loss of academic integrity and can even result in disciplinary action from educational institutions.
- **Easily Prevented**: Simply adding missing citations involves locating all the sources mentioned in the essay and including the appropriate bibliographic details. Tools like citation generators or consulting with a professor can assist in this process.
- **Conclusion**: Failing to cite sources appropriately not only fallows academic standards but also damages the academic reputation of the writer and can impact the broader scholarly discourse.
