Choose one of your lunches to make ahead for when you are running late and have it ready in the refrigerator when you are on the way to work. If you have leftovers from that lunch, pack those in a lunch box for your day. If you have leftovers from lunch and you will not be able to eat them that day, pack them for the next day, and if you are cooking for the next day, cook a little extra that day that will be ready to eat the next day. This way, you can pack leftovers for the next day from whatever you have cooked that day, and make any extra leftover lunch that you will need for another day.
