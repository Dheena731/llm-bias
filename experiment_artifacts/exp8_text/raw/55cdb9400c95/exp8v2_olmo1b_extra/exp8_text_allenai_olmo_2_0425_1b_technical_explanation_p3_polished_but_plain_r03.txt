 1. “Service” means an aggregate of services that we offer to the general public at a reasonable, and in most cases, no charge. In some cases, our customers may be required to render payment for services, but in those cases, the service description must also include the price of the service.
  2. We use the third-person singular pronouns “he” and “she” when referring to members of a single-gender role. We use the third-person plural pronouns “they” and “them” when referring to members of a mixed-gender role.
  3. A “member” of a group is one who is an actual member of the group, as opposed to a potential member, such as an applicant. A “potential member” is one who is considering joining the group. One member of a group is one person. One group is two members. Two groups are two people.
