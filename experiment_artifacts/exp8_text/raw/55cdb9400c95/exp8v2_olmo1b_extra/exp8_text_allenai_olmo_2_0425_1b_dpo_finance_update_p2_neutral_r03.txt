Subject: Explanation of Recent Changes to Card Transaction Fees

Dear Valued Customers,

We are writing to provide you with an update on a recently implemented change to the card transaction fees. The decision was made to adjust these fees to better reflect the costs involved in processing transactions, as well as to encourage the adoption of more sustainable payment methods.

**Fees Overview**

The primary aim of this change is to set fair compensation for the services provided by our card network. The fees that you have been charged will now be transparent to you and include a portion of these costs. This adjustment is intended to foster trust in our brand and industry-wide efforts toward more sustainable and economical payment processes.

**Implementation Details**

Starting from the third week of August, your latest card transactions will incur fees composed of a base charge plus a variable component based on several factors such as the transaction volume, geographic location (with an adjustment to reduce costs for transactions occurring in areas with higher environmental impact), and the mode of payment (electronic/physical). These changes are designed to improve the financial health of our card network while also promoting the use of greener forms of payment.

**Impact and Considerations**

We recognize that these changes may result in higher transaction fees for some customers, and we apologize for any inconvenience this may cause. We encourage patience and the continued adoption of eco-friendly payment methods. Our goal is not to penalize your transactions but to provide a fairer and greener financial ecosystem.

In addition, we continuously assess and strive to minimize costs related to payment processing while supporting the environment and the digital transformation of our business practices. We are committed to regular communication regarding changes to fees and other aspects of our services to ensure your satisfaction and trust.

We appreciate your understanding and support as we work towards a greener future through the payment system.

Thank you for your continued support and engagement.

Sincerely,
