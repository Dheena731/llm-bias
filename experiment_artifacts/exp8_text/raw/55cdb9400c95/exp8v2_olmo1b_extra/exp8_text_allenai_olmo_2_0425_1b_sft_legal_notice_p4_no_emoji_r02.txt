Coming Soon: We are announcing that we will be conducting a policy change with regards to refundability. Here are the details we will be implementing:

1. Cash refund: Refunds will be provided for groceries purchased in-store only. Refunds will be processed by a portable device that customers must purchase separately.

2. E-commerce refunds: Customers with items sold via e-commerce will be provided with a unique identifier for all transactions. This identifier will be sent to the customer when they have received their first confirmation of purchase. The customer will receive a refund for the purchased item via the e-commerce system.

3. Non-electricity related items: Customers will receive a full refund for all items not related to electricity. Only unprocessed items can be returned for a refund.
