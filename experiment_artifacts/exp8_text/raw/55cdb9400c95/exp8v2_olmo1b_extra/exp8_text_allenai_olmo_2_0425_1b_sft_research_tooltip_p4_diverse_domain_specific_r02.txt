Missing Value Marker: This is a critical concept in data analysis, particularly when dealing with missing or non-numeric data points. When a value is missing, it indicates a gap in the dataset that requires attention. It's essential to understand the reason behind missing values, whether it's due to errors during data entry, intentional omission, or lack of information. Carefully analyzing missing value markers can help in identifying patterns, improving data quality, and making informed decisions based on complete data.
