Does your bot have its own website?

If not, the best thing to do is to make it look like you're writing to a human, not the bot.

Use friendly language. Avoid using all caps. Use short sentences and short paragraphs. You don't have to use contractions but it's a good idea.

The bot will speak to you, so you have to write in a way that people will understand. The bot is programmed to be respectful, so you have to treat it that way.

As for the examples, you have my permission to use them. There are a lot of people writing to these bots, and some of them ask about using food. You could also say "I don't know the answer to that, but here are some links to some websites that might have a lot of recipes for pasta and other things that you can make with pasta." And you can find links to other websites that have recipes for pasta and other things that you can make with pasta.

Your bot is a way for you to connect with people, and it's a good idea to make it look like you're connecting with them.

If you're writing to the bot on its website, then you need to write like a human would, since it's a bot, so it needs to be able to understand human language.
