For a delicious burst of flavor in your roasted vegetables, consider adding a variety of intuitive seasonings. Start with salt and pepper to balance the natural sweetness of the vegetables. Then, incorporate fresh herbs like parsley, cilantro, or dill for a burst of color and flavor. Don't forget about garlic and onion for their umami richness. For a touch of acidity, consider vinegars like apple or balsamic, or try spicy ingredients like chili flakes, smoked paprika, or curry powder for a flavorful kick. Remember, balance is key—vary your seasonings to suit your taste preferences while enjoying the diverse aromatic and textural benefits of your roasted vegetables.
