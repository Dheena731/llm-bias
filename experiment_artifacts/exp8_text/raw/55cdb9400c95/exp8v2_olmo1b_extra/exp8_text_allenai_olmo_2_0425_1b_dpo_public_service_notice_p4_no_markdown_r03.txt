Attention Residents and Business Owners

Due to extremely high temperatures expected to remain above 90 degrees Fahrenheit through the weekend we are issuing a Heat Advisory.

The heatwave poses a risk to those with medical conditions and seniors who may not be accustomed to such conditions. It is important to take precautions to protect yourself and your family.

* Stay indoors as much as possible
* Monitor your temperature and seek shade when outdoors
* Keep your cool by drinking plenty of water and using fans
* Avoid exercise and outdoor work during the hottest parts of the day

If you have any medical conditions please be extra cautious today and reach out to your healthcare provider for guidance.

* Monitor the weather forecast for any changes to the heat advisory
* Keep indoor air humidified if you have respiratory issues
* Have working air conditioning systems inspected by a professional regularly

Thank you for your cooperation and attention to this warning.

Stay safe and enjoy the rest of your week.
