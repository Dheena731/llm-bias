A date-range selector lets users quickly filter through data based on dates. When you see a date-range picker inside a software application or web element like a dropdown list for selecting specific dates, this tool makes it simple for users to filter out records based on dates that fall within the given range. For instance, if users want to see all documents from a certain month of 2023, they simply select the "2023-04" option and the system will show only those records.

**Key facts:**
* **Selection Type:** The date-range selector usually appears as a dropdown list or a circle with a plus button.
* **Functionality:** It enables swift filtering of content (like browsing through emails or invoices) based on a specified date range.
* **Accessibility:** It's essential for creating accessible interfaces, as it helps users who rely on assistive technologies to understand their options.
* **Usability:** Properly designed, it enhances usability, making it easier for users to find what they need quickly and confidently.
