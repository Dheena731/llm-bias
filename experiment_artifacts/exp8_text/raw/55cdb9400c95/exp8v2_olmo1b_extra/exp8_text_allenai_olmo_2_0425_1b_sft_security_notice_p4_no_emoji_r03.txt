Today, we are going to be talking about two factor authentication, also known as 2FA. Two factor authentication adds an extra layer of security to your login method. This means that our system will need to get two different pieces of information from two different trusted sources before a user can access your account.

That first piece of information is a password. Many websites and services use this as a means to protect your account, but it is not foolproof. A password can easily be compromised by hackers who may get ahold of your login information. This is why the second piece of information is also required. This is an additional level of security that is much more difficult for hackers to break through.

The second piece of information is what is sometimes known as a "security code." It is a code that is sent to your phone or email to confirm your identity. This is a method that is much more secure than a password because even if someone gets your password they wouldn't be able to log in without your security code. This is why we are encouraging everyone to make use of two factor authentication when they can. After all, even the most powerful hackers have to get lucky.
