Commentary on missing citations in an essay

In scholarly work, the presence of proper citations is vital as it allows readers to verify the author's claims and understand the context of the cited material. The absence of citations, or the inclusion of irrelevant or misleading references, undermines the integrity and credibility of the essay. It is crucial that every point the author makes is backed by a verified source, preferably in a citation format such as APA or MLA, to avoid plagiarism accusations.
