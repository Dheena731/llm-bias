When an essay lacks proper citations, it is a clear sign of incomplete or insufficient research. Proper citation is essential for maintaining academic integrity and acknowledging the sources used in your work, as it provides a definitive and reliable reference for readers. Without citations, the credibility of your essay is compromised, as it may seem like your ideas were developed independently of the original sources. This can also lead to confusion as readers might misinterpret your work as a novel or original creation, diminishing the value of your contribution to the academic discourse. It's crucial to include citations within your essay to avoid these issues and support your arguments with credible evidence.
