3.13.3. Historical transactions

The list of historical transactions can be found on the top right side of the page. The list shows all transactions that have been carried out by one or more users. The list is protected by an administrator, so it cannot be edited without the administrator's permission.

You can filter these transactions using the following options:

  • Users: Show only transactions carried out by the current user.
  • Transaction dates: Sort the list in descending order for each date.
  • Amount: Show only transactions with a given amount.
  • Mute: Mark all transactions as being not carried out.
  • Mark private: Mark all transactions as private.
  • Mark public: Mark all transactions as being public.

3.13.7. Requesting money

Users can request money from another user. If the recipient user has allowed you to do this, they will receive an email with a link to pay the requested amount. You can also order "bank transfers" through the Creditor Platform or via the Wallet app. You must be an owner of one of the recipient users.

3.13.8. Cash app requests

You can cash out funds from your credit or debit card by using the Cash app. You can cash out funds from your credit or debit card (credit cards only), at a local store which accepts CC. Cashout is free.

You must have a credit card and a bank with a local ATM in order to withdraw funds. You will be charged a service fee for withdrawals using a debit card. 

  • The maximum amount you can withdraw in a single transaction is $30. This amount is limited by the CC issuer.
  • We've set the maximum withdrawal fee to $2.50 to help you avoid having to wait in-line for hours. 
  • To prevent fraud, we only allow withdrawals at ATMs (physical locations) with the same name as your credit card. This means you can only withdraw funds at the same ATM where you entered your PIN and entered your card.
  • You must have enough funds in your account to make the withdrawal.
