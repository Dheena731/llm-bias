# New Expense Approval Workflow

* **Introduction**: This message introduces the new expense approval process designed to streamline budgeting and ensure transparency.
* **Process Overview**: The workflow follows these steps:
  - **Request**: Users can submit expense requests via our new expense portal.
  - **Approval**: A designated team reviews and approves requests within 48 hours.
  - **Approval Confirmation**: Approval is logged, and requested parties are notified.
* **Benefits**: This process enhances accountability and compliance, reducing friction in the approval cycle.
* **Next Steps**: Please review the documentation at [expense portal](https://support.domain.com/dashboard/expense-triggers) for more details.
