**New Expense Approval Workflow**

Welcome to the new expense approval workflow designed to streamline our financial processes. As of [date], we are implementing a streamlined approval process for all expenses. Please ensure you update your expense reports as required, and submit new requests via [the approved expense tracking system]. All approved expenses will be processed within [Timeframe]% and will reflect within your accounts within [Timeframe]% of approval. 

- **Notification:** New expense requests will be sent automatically to all designated approvers within [Notification Duration].
- **Approval Status:** Requests will be reviewed by senior management within [Review Window].
- **Approval Process:**
  - Each expense report will be reviewed by one or more approvers.
  - A decision will be made on whether to approve or deny the request.
  - If approved, the expense will be processed and the associated expense report updated.
  - If denied, the request will be forwarded to the decision-making team for further review.
- **Notification Parameters:**
  - **Approval Status:** Approving | Denying | Pending | Not Granted
  - **Decision Status:** Pending | Denied | Approving
  - **Actions Required:** Update expense report, submit new request

Our goal is to make this process as efficient as possible, thereby minimizing delays and ensuring compliance with our financial policies.
