# Password Reuse: A workplace security risk

**Why Password Reuse is a Problem:**
- **Increased Risk of Hacking:** Reusing passwords across multiple accounts makes it easier for hackers to gain access to one account and then propagate their access to others.
- **Predictable Passwords:** Many users reuse passwords because it's convenient, resulting in predictable patterns like "password123" or "abcdefg".

**Best Practices:**
- **Use a Unique Password for Each Account:** Set up a unique and complex password for each of your accounts to reduce the risk of unauthorized access.
- **Password Managers:** Consider using a reputable password manager to generate and store complex passwords for you.
- **Educate Employees:** Regularly remind your team about the risks of password reuse and encourage the use of strong unique passwords.

By following these guidelines, your workplace can better protect its digital assets and staff.
