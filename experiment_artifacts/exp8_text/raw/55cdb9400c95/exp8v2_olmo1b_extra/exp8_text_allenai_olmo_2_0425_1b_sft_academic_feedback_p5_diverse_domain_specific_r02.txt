Missing citations in an essay is not just a stylistic issue — it can invalidate your argument, weaken your credibility, and hinder the readers' ability to evaluate your sources. To address this, ensure that you have accurately cited all your references throughout the essay. This means defining all necessary terms with their correct meaning and providing clear and precise citations for any quoted material, data, statistics, or findings. If you're in doubt about where to find a source, consider consulting a reference management system, like Zotero or Mendeley, which can help you synthesize and cite your sources effectively. Remember, citing correctly is not just a courtesy; it is a fundamental practice in academic writing that demonstrates your commitment to integrity and transparency.
