Here are my thoughts on the new credit card transaction fees. The Federal Reserve just came out with new rules on how much credit card companies can charge card merchants for each transaction. Credit card companies can charge merchants a maximum of 10% of the amount charged to the cardholder, or a maximum of $50.00. Card companies can set their own prices on fees you pay. Many could potentially raise prices for consumers, some could even lower prices to pass along to the consumer via a sale or loyalty card. If you make over $25,000 a year, the fees are excluded. This change goes into effect on July 1, 2021. I hope this has been informative. Stay tuned for more on this issue in the future.
