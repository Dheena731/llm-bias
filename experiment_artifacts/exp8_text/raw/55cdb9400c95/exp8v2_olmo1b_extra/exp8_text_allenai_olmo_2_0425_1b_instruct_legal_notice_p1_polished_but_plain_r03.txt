This notice is a change notice for our account terms. For the complete document please visit our website. The new terms are as follows:

1. Account access will be limited to approved users only. All new logins will require a verification code sent to the user’s registered email address.
2. Minimum balance requirements have been increased from $100 to $500. All accounts must maintain this balance to avoid late fees.
3. In order to grant bonus rewards, customers must meet specific spending thresholds. Rewards will be withheld from transactions that fall below these limits.
4. Our cancellation policy has been revised. To cancel your account, you must contact us within 30 days of your account opening.
5. All disputes must be resolved in writing within five business days of discovery. Emails will not be sufficient for disputes. Please send a formal letter within this timeframe.

Please review these changes and contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
