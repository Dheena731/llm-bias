Refilling a prescription is simple and can help save money and keep your medicine on hand. Before picking up your prescription, ensure the pharmacist on duty is aware that you have already filled the previous prescription. The process typically involves:

1. Visiting the pharmacy, either in person or via delivery service if needed.
2. Presenting your original prescription or a refilling authorization from your healthcare provider.
3. Confirming your current prescription with the pharmacist to ensure accuracy.
4. Providing any requested information about insurance, previous refills, and current supply levels.
5. Filling the new prescription with the appropriate amount according to your provider's instructions.
6. Paying for refills if required, which can usually be done before filling the prescription if offered by the pharmacy.
7. Taking your prescription with you and keeping a record of refills.

Having a stockpile of your medications helps maintain your health and can prevent potential medical issues that might arise if you run out of prescribed medication abruptly. Remember to check the expiration date on your medicines and dispose of any expired prescriptions properly to protect your safety and the environment.
