Polling station locations are strategically placed throughout the locality or in designated areas according to the election commission's map. Please ensure you attend your designated polling station on election day as it is an important step in the electoral process. These locations are typically marked by blue signs or signage indicating they are a polling station. If you encounter any difficulties finding the correct location or experience any issues on voting day, you can contact your polling committee for assistance. It's crucial to complete your voting process promptly and accurately to ensure the smooth functioning of the electoral system.
