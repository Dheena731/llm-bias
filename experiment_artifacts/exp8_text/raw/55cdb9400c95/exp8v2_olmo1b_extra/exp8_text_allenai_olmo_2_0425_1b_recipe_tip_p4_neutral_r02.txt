Tip 14: Tip with a small gesture of gratitude. In the midst of daily stress, we tend to forget to be thankful for what we have. To combat this, pick one or two people a week to send sympathetic messages to. You don't have to spend a lot of money. You can send just a few dollars to someone who has really helped you, or a message saying that even though you barely saw them, you are glad to remember them, or even someone you haven't seen in a while. Something that makes you smile and is a small gesture of appreciation. No need to be extravagant.

Tip 15: Reach out to others. There are many ways to find support. An easy one is to join a support group or group of people who are going through the same experience. This can be a really great place to learn and gain strength. If you can't find one local, there are a lot online. You can also call a charity or service. If you can't find one that helps with your specific problem, try to find one that helps people in your circumstances. We are social creatures, and we need to reach out to others. If you can't find someone to talk to, you can always write a letter or a message to someone you care about.

Tip 16: Find your personal spirituality. If you don't already believe, finding one that works for you is a big key to surviving the crisis. It can give you a goal to strive for, something to look forward to. It can also give you a reason to keep going. If you are already religious, you can find a spiritual leader to support you. If you don't believe in God or anything spiritual, you can still appreciate the beauty of the world around you. If you don't believe in anything, you can find something else to believe in. Use your creativity and imagination to find a place for yourself. You don't have to turn off your emotions to find your faith. There is no one way that works for everyone. If you are unsure of how to find your own spirituality, reach out to someone who is a spiritual leader. Listen to their advice and see what works for you.

One thing that I'd like to ask if there is a crisis? Don't be afraid to reach out to others. Don't wait to be asked. Reach out, and find people who will help and support you. There is strength in numbers.
