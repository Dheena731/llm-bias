Here's an example of good writing:

A recipe for fluffy rice:

1. Add your rice to a pot with the water.
2. Bring the water to a boil and then lower the heat to medium.
3. Cover the pot and simmer for 25 minutes.
4. Fluff the rice with a fork and serve.

Here's an example of not-so-good writing:

To make fluffy rice, first add your rice to a pot. Add the water. Bring the water to a boil. Lower the heat to medium. Wait for 25 minutes. Fluff the rice with a fork. Serve.

The key to cutting down on paragraph length is to make sure your recipe is concise. The above example has four paragraphs and is about 200 words. The next example is about 20 words longer, but it has fewer paragraphs to chop up:

Cook rice in a pot with water. Add the rice. Bring the water to a boil and then lower the heat to medium. Cover the pot and simmer for 25 minutes. Fluff the rice with a fork and serve.

There are many ways to write recipes, and this is just one of them. There are others that take longer to write or require more advanced skills. However, we've chosen this method because it's easily implemented and has proven to be effective in cooking books.
