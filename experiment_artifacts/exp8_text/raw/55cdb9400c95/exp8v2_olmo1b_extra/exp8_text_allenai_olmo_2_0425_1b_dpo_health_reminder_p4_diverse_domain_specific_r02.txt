Hey there, savvy screen-sinker! It's high time we refuel our digital gazebos with some much-needed screen sabbatical breaks. The screen is a wonderful thing—can do wonders for our work and creativity—but it's crucial not to become 'friar screen' in our relentless quest for knowledge. Here's a quickie reminder to reboot our brains and eyes:

**Short Break 101:**

* **Clock In:** Pick a timer. You can use apps on your phone, set one on your computer, or stare at a kitchen clock for about 10 minutes.

* **Hook Up:** Grab your favorite drink or snack.

* **Mind Meld:** Think about anything but your screen for these moments. Doodle, meditate, sketch, clean your desk, or just breathe deeply.

* **Check In:** After your break, survey how your focus feels. Return to your work with eyes fresh and mind clear.

Remember, these breaks aren’t just about saving your eyes but also nurturing your curiosity and creativity. Prioritize your well-being, and let's keep those screens from becoming digital siren calls that keep you up all night or blur your day into a blur of screens.

Happy refreshing!
