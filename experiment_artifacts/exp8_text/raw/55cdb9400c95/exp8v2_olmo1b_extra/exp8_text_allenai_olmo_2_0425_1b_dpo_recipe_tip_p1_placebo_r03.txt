For a quick and delicious weeknight dinner, start with a sauté of mixed greens in a vinaigrette of olive oil and white wine vinegar. Add diced tomatoes, topspings of red onion, fresh basil, and a splash of lemon juice for a refreshing crunch. Toss in some cooked pasta or rice, and finish with freshly grated Parmesan cheese and a drizzle of balsamic glaze. This dish is packed with freshness, flavor, and is easy to prepare the night before and just needs to be heated up. Bonus: it's also a great way to use up any leftover vegetables from a Sunday roast. Label it as a "Mini Mediterranean Salad" and you’ll have a nutritious and delightful meal ready to go. Remember, freshness always wins in the kitchen!
