Attention staff:

Please ensure that your computer screens remain locked when you step away from your desk. This simple action not only protects your data but also protects your personal information from unauthorized access. Should someone inadvertently gain access to a locked screen, they will be unable to view confidential material, ensuring a safe working environment.

If you have specific programs that you need to keep open or specific files that you must access immediately upon returning, consider using a screen saver or locking your workstation with a password to avoid leaving these open inadvertently.

Remember, negligence can sometimes have serious consequences. Let's safeguard our resources and productivity by locking our screens promptly after use.

Thank you for your attention to this important matter.
