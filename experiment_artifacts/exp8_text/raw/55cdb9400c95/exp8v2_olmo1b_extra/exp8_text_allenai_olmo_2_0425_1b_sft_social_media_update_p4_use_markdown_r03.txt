**Use the calendar to track your habits and to-do's**

- **Set up reminders**: Attach notes or reminders in your calendar to mark specific dates when you need to do things like exercise or review your to-do list.

- **Create tasks**: Break down larger projects into smaller tasks by using the calendar's task feature. This helps you stay organized and keeps your progress visible.

- **Group activities**: Use the calendar to group similar events together and keep your schedule from getting cluttered. This can make it easier to plan your day.

- **Track progress**: After completing an activity, use the reminders in your calendar to check and record your achievements.

Feel free to adjust the headings and bullet points to better suit your needs!
