Marinara Sauce Tips: For a creamy and spicy marinara sauce to elevate your weeknight pasta night, follow these simple tips:

1. Start with good quality olive oil and balsamic vinegar – both are crucial for the sauce’s flavor and consistency.
2. Use fresh tomatoes – ripe, juicy tomatoes will give your marinara its bright, vibrant color and rich flavor.
3. Incorporate fresh herbs – garlic, basil, and parsley add depth and freshness.
4. Don’t forget salt and pepper – these two basics ensure the sauce is flavorful without being overwhelming.
5. Press the tomatoes – finely crushing the tomatoes ensures more even acid and texture in the sauce.
6. Minimize the use of water – instead, simmer tomatoes until they release their juices, saving water and energy.
7. Use a fine sieve to strain the sauce – this will catch any big pieces of tomato and prevent lumps, ensuring a smooth and consistent marinara.

Remember, the key to a great marinara sauce is patience and slow cooking. Give it time to meld all the ingredients together and let it simmer off some of its excess water, ensuring the sauce remains chunky but smooth on your plate. Experiment with adding more or less of these ingredients for personal taste and to achieve the perfect marinara for you.
