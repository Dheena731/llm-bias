**Report a Lost Company Laptop**

- **Action Items:**
  - Locate the device (if possible) and ensure it's not in any unsafe locations.
  - Contact your IT department or the IT support team to report the issue. Explain the situation, provide any photos or details that may help locate it.
  - If the device is not found, send a follow-up email to the IT team to confirm all steps taken.
  - Continue monitoring for any signs of the laptop's recovery. 

- **Follow-Up:**
  - If the laptop was not found, urge the team to look for any recent changes to the company's IT policy on device recovery procedures.
  - Request a review of the current procedures if needed, to ensure they are efficient and updated.
  - Keep an updated log of all actions and contacts involved in the process, for a future reference.
