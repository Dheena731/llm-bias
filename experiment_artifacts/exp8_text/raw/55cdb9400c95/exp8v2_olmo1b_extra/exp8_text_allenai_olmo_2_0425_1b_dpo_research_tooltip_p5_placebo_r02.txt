To export query results, you simply need to right-click on the query result and select "Export results to..." [Placeholders: CSV, Excel, etc.] from the context menu. This action will open a file dialog where you can choose the format of your exported data. For instance, you might select "CSV" if you wish to keep the data in a tabular format easily readable by spreadsheet programs, or you might pick "Excel" to download it as a workbook for further processing. Remember, this tip assumes basic proficiency with your web browser and the specific functionalities of your chosen export format.
