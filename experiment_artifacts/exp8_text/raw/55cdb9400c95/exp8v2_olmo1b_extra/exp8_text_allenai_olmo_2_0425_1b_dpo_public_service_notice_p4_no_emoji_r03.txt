Attention residents of our area, please be aware that we are under a Heat Advisory. During this period, temperatures are expected to remain above 90°F daily. Extreme heat can pose serious health risks to the elderly, children, and individuals with pre-existing health conditions.

To help minimize the impact of the heat, we encourage:
- Limit outdoor activities to early morning or late evening hours.
- Keep cool by staying indoors in air-conditioned environments.
- Stay hydrated and avoid dehydration by drinking plenty of water.
- Monitor your body temperature and seek shade when necessary.

For more information, please visit our website or contact us at your earliest convenience. We want to ensure everyone's safety and comfort during this period. Thank you for your compliance and cooperation.
