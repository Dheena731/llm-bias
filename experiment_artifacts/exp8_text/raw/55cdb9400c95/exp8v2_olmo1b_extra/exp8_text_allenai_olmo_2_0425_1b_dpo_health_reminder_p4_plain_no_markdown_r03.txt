Take frequent short breaks from screens to reduce eye strain and improve focus. Schedule these breaks throughout the day, such as every 25 minutes, to give your eyes a rest from the bright and often stimulating light from digital devices. During these breaks, consider activities like stretching, walking, or simply gazing at something relaxing like a tree or a glass of water. Remember to move your eyes from a computer screen to a distant object and vice versa to prevent dryness and fatigue. By incorporating these short breaks, you’ll enjoy a clearer mind and better overall health.
