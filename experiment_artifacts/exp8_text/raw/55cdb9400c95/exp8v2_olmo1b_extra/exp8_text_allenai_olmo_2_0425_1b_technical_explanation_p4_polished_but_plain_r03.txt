Be generous with your praise. Do not spend more than 5 seconds answering the question. To do this, briefly acknowledge the questioner while providing the information they requested, then quickly move on to the next question.

Do not use any kind of special formatting, such as bold text, lists, or headings. Using these formatting options makes you look lazy and immature.

Reference the source of your answers. If you can, provide a link to the question that you answered so others can read your source. Otherwise, write your answer in your own words. If you don't know the answer, don't write a useless answer.

Rate the difficulty of your answer. Give a number from 1 to 5 to indicate how difficult your answer is to understand. Remember, low-fidelity answers are easier to understand. People with rudimentary knowledge will not be confused by them.

State any assumptions you made. Explain what you know and what you don't know. If you made an assumption, explain it in your answer. If you have evidence to support your assumption, provide a link to the source. If you made a mistake, you can fix it in a comment, but if you don't you should edit your answer to fix the mistake.
