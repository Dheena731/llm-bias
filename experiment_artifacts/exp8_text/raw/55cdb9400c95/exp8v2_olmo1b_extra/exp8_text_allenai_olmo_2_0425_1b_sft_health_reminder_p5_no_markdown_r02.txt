**Reminder: Refilling Your Prescription**

Before you go to the pharmacy, please make sure you have your original prescription handy so the pharmacist can check for any changes or updates.

After you receive your new prescription, please use the same method of refilling your medication. This means using the same pharmaceuticals, taking the same dose, and following the same medication schedule as prescribed.

When you schedule your refill, make sure to include any allergies or other important information that may affect your treatment.

Please remember that the refill process is important for ensuring your medication remains effective and safe. Do not skip doses or stop taking your medication without the advice of your healthcare provider.
