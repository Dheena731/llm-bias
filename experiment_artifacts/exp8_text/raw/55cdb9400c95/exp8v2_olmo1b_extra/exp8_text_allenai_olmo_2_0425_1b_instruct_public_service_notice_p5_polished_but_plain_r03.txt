Polling station locations are strategically placed throughout the community to ensure easy access for all voters. Each polling station is carefully chosen to be convenient for the residents it serves. They are usually located in public areas such as schools, community centers, libraries, or courthouse courthouses. These locations are designed to be accessible by car and public transit, making it easy for people to vote regardless of their mobility. Staff at the polling stations are trained to assist voters with their polling materials and to maintain the integrity of the election process. Please visit your local polling station to cast your vote and help shape the future of your community.
