**Quick Stir Fry Night**

*Prep time: 10 min*  
*Ingredients: Vegetables & protein of choice (e.g., tofu, tempeh, shrimp, bell peppers, broccoli)**  
*Instructions:  
  1. Heat a large pan over high heat.  
  2. Add oil (vegetable or canola) and sauté vegetables for 5-7 mins until tender but not mushy.  
  3. Add protein and stir to combine.  
  4. Add preferred seasonings (e.g., soy sauce, garlic powder, ginger) and toss to coat.  
  5. Serve hot with rice for added texture and flavor.

This quick stir fry is perfect for a weeknight dinner and satisfies both hunger and the craving for a tasty, healthy meal.
