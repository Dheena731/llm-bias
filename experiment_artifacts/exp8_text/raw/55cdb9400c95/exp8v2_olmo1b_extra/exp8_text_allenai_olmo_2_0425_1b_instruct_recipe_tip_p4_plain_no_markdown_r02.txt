Decide on a theme for your lunches to save time; for example choose burgers on Mondays or vegan on Tuesdays. Pack a variety of foods in reusable containers to avoid last-minute shopping and planning. Include a main dish, side, and a healthy drink for a balanced meal. Remember to bring snack options like dried fruit or nuts for on-the-go energy. If you have children, pack a few different meals they might request to keep the options flexible. Finally ensure you have a backup meal plan in case of emergency meetings or unexpected commitments.
