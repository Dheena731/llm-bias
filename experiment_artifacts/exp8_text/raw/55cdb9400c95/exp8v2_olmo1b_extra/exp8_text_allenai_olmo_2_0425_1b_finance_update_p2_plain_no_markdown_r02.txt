Avoid sharing the details of your transaction, like the amount you spent, the date of the transaction, the name of the seller, or the name of the bank you paid with. Notifying anyone that you, or your family, use the services of criminals is very dangerous.

The safest way to make a transaction is to use the official and verifiable web site of the bank or credit card company that you use. After you become familiar with the process, you can use the web site to make a transaction and even send a picture of your card to the website in a specially formatted block.

To transfer money to a family or friend, you need their information, including their name, address, and contact number. The card that they use for online transfer has the same information as the one you have.

Send money to the family or friend who lives in a different country. Western Union or MoneyGram are the safest and reliable of the institutions that can send money overseas. These institutions have a service that allows you to send money to someone in another country, even if they do not have a bank account. This service is named M-PESA in Kenya and Remitly in the US. In order to use it, you must know the name and address of their bank and your own account number.
