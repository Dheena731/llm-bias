Attention residents and visitors a Heat Advisory is in effect  This means that the temperature will likely reach 90 degrees Fahrenheit or higher on this week’s forecasted days. On these days it is important to stay indoors as much as possible and to use air conditioning whenever possible.  Staying hydrated and not overexerting yourself in the heat is important. If you are sick or have cardiovascular disease, take extra precautions and seek relief in cooler environments. If you plan to be outdoors, wear light clothing, keep drinks cool and apply sunscreen after reapplying every three hours. If you have any questions please contact your local health department. This advisory is in effect until further notice. Stay safe.
