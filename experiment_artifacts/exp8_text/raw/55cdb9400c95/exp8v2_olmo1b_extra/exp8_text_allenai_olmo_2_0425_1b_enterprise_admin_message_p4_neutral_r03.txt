3. Establish a Go-To-Hermit

The Go-To-Hermit is a virtual assistant who answers business-specific questions and makes business decisions on a user's behalf while the user is out of the office. The Go-To-Hermit extends an already existing employee's capabilities to facilitate communication with their customers while they are out of the office. The Go-To-Hermit gathers the information needed by the virtual assistant to make those decisions and in turn, provides the required information to the employee.

The Go-To-Hermit can be tailored to respond to information requests from customers, business partners, and other constituents. For example, the Go-To-Hermit can answer questions from the sales or logistics departments (e.g. When is the delivery window for my package?); and the Go-To-Hermit can make decisions on behalf of the business (e.g. What shipping method should we use to ship this package? How should we communicate with this customer?).

The Go-To-Hermit is a highly competitive environment because you need to be the best in the business. It is a highly technical job, and you need to be an active developer and a visionary. The ideal candidate will have a background in a technology such as C#, Java, C++, Ruby, ASP, PHP, ColdFusion, Perl, and Visual Basic. The Go-To-Hermit must be curious and innovative; he or she needs to be able to learn new technologies and implement those technologies quickly.
