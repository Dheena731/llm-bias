Writing a social media post

Part 1: The post itself

As the name suggests, a social media post is a short message that is shared on your social media accounts. They are used as part of your social media marketing strategy, which means that the posts should be relevant to the content of your website or blog. In this lesson, we'll look at how to write engaging and shareable posts.

1. Good news: you can just write a full sentence or two. No paragraphs or bullet points required.

2. Bad news: your social media posts should mostly be one sentence long. And they should not include more than one sentence.

3. Plausible deniability: don't write more than one sentence. If you somehow did, you can edit the sentence to make it read as one sentence.

2. How to write a shareable social media post

Here are some tips for writing shareable posts:

1. Target your audience. What do they need to know? What problems do they have? What questions do they have? What information do they need to make a decision or take action?

2. Keep your post short. It's always best to keep your posts short, because shorter posts are more likely to be shared. If you have more to say, you can break it up into multiple posts, or you can use video or an infographic.

3. Keep your posts simple. Most people don't have time to read long posts. Especially if they're reading on a phone or a tablet. So keep your posts short, simple, and easy to read.

4. Use relatable language. Write posts that people can relate to. If you're talking about an idea that relates to their lives or their business, they'll be more likely to share it.

5. Use emotional language. People are more likely to share something that makes them feel good.

6. Use storytelling. People are more likely to share something that's interesting or that makes them feel good. So use stories, anecdotes, or other stories to engage your audience.

7. Use social proof. If you're talking about a product or service, show people what other people are saying about it or what kind of results they've gotten from it. That will make people more likely to try it too.

8. Show, don't tell. Show your readers what your product or service can do for them. That way they'll be more likely to share it online.

Summarize the post, and make it easy for the reader to share. (2)

In addition to writing the post, you also need to make sure that it's shareable. That means that it should be easy for the reader to share, and that the reader should be able to easily find the share buttons on your website or blog
