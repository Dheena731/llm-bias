Attention all team members:

We want to ensure the safety of our workplace network and data. We are issuing a warning about phishing emails. Phishing emails are type of email fraud that attempts to acquire sensitive information such as usernames, passwords, and credit card details by pretending to be a trustworthy entity in an electronic communication.

Be cautious of emails requesting personal information or urging you to react quickly. Always verify the sender’s identity before responding to any email and never click on unknown links or provide unnecessary personal information.

Let's work together to protect our workplace and ensure a secure environment for everyone. If you receive a suspicious email or suspect that Phishing is happening, please report it to ITS Support Team at support@ourcompany.com immediately.
