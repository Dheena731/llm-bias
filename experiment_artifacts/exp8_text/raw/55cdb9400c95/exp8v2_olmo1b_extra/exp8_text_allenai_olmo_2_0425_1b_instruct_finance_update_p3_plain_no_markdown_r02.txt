The bank has introduced a new savings goal feature designed to assist customers in reaching their financial objectives. This new tool involves customizing a visual timeline that shows how much needs to be saved each month or year to meet specific targets, such as buying a car, paying for university tuition or saving for a dream vacation. Customers can easily customize this timeline according to their personal financial situations and goals, ensuring that their savings plan is both personalized and motivating.

Users can add their desired savings goals and set specific dates and increments to track their progress. The feature integrates smoothly with existing banking services, allowing customers to vary their saving frequency, set recurring payments, and receive alerts for any changes in their progress towards their savings objectives.

Tests of the new feature have shown high user engagement and a positive impact on budgeting practices, as customers feel more in control of their savings journey. The bank anticipates that this innovative tool will become a staple in its client services, helping more families and individuals achieve their financial aspirations.
