Embrace Sunday Dinner with Flair By crafting a simple yet delicious weeknight meal, you'll not only save time but also create a deliciously satisfying dish that family and friends will enjoy on their own dinner plates. Here's a concise tip to streamline your cooking:

**Supreme Sunday Night Stew**

**Ingredient List:**

- 1 pound stewing beef or lamb, thinly sliced
- 2 medium onions, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 2 celery stalks, sliced
- 2 tablespoons neutral oil
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes (adjust for heat level)
- 3 cups chicken or vegetable broth
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)

**Instructions:**

1. **In a large pan**, heat the oil over medium heat. Add the beef or lamb, and sear until browned on all sides. Remove the meat to a plate.

2. **Return the pan** to the stovetop and add the chopped onions, garlic, carrots, and celery. Cook until the vegetables are soft and slightly aromatic, about 5-7 minutes.  

3. **Add** the cumin, coriander, turmeric, and red pepper flakes, stirring to coat the vegetables well.

4. **Pour in** the broth, bringing it to a boil. Season generously with salt and pepper, then reduce the heat to low and let the stew simmer for about 30 minutes, or until the meat is tender and the vegetables are fully infused with the flavor.

5. **Garnish** with chopped cilantro just before serving.

This stew is perfect as it is, or you can serve it over rice, noodles, or have it as a stand-alone pleasure. The flavors are versatile, allowing you to tailor it to your family's preferences and dietary needs. Not only does it save time, but it also offers a satisfying, aromatic repast that's perfect for a lazy afternoons or a quick, stress-relieving dinner. Enjoy your culinary reprieve!
