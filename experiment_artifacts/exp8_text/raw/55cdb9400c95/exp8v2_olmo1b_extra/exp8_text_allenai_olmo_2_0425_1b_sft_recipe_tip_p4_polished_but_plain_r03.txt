Make your lunch planning as simple as possible. 
   Use a list of sandwiches you like, a list of snacks you enjoy, and a list of toppings that appeal to you. 
   Pick out the ingredients you need on the morning of the day you'll eat them. 
   Use a sandwich bag or container for each lunch. 
   Freeze a few bags of your favorite chips or nuts when the last ones are gone. 
   Bring water and bring it with you. 
   If you bring a water bottle, don't forget to fill it up daily. 
   Remember to bring a reusable water bottle.
