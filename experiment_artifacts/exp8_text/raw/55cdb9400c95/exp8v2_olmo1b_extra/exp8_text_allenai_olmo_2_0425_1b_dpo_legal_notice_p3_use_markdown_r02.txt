# Cookie Usage Notice

Welcome to our website! We use "cookies" to remember your preferences and personalize your experience. Cookies are small pieces of data sent from a website and stored on your device by your web browser while you are browsing. 

- **Types of cookies:** We use "strictly necessary" cookies for essential site functions, **performance** and **analytics** cookies for improving our website and **third-party** cookies for advertising and analytics purposes.

- **Duration:** Cookies remain on your device until they expire or are deleted by you.

- **Consent:** By using our website, you agree to our use of cookies.

- **Managing cookies:** You can control and adjust your cookie settings through your web browser's settings. Most browsers allow you to block all cookies or specifically block Third-Party cookies.

Please note that denying cookie consent may affect your access to certain features and functionality of our website. Thank you for understanding our cookie policy!
