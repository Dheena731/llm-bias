An end-of-year tax statement is a document that reports your earnings and expenses during the tax year. On this statement you'll find various sections like "Total Income" which presents your annual earnings, "Total Expenses" showing your costs of living and deductions (like mortgage payments, charity donations, etc.), and "Taxable Income" calculated by subtracting deductions from total income to determine the amount you owe or are eligible for in taxes. It's important to review this document carefully to ensure accuracy and to make any necessary adjustments before filing your tax return. Pay attention to sections like "Sales Tax" and "Excise Taxes" which may impact your total tax liability. Make sure to keep your records organized and update any adjustments as needed to reflect actual circumstances throughout the year. The statement will also provide you with instruction on how to file your return and any deadlines for payment if required.
