Title: New Recycling Collection Times

We are updating our recycling collection schedule. From January 1, 2023, residents will need to sort their recyclables separately from the domestic waste.

*Metal cans and metal utensils* go in the separate bin, usually number 5.

*Plastic bottles and jars* are collected from two main bins: one labeled "PLASTIC" (number 7) and one labeled "NEWSPAPER" (number 40).

*Glass bottles and jars* are collected in the "GLASS" bin.

*All other recyclable materials* will be collected in the mixed recycling bin.

Residents are encouraged to familiarize themselves with the new collection schedule. The pump at the corner recycling center will undergo maintenance through December 31, 2022, so please prepare accordingly.

Please remember to sort your recyclables properly to ensure they are recycled correctly. This update is in place to improve environmental sustainability and encourage community participation in recycling. Thank you for your cooperation.
