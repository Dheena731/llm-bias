Heat Advisory

A heat advisory is in effect for the coming days. Be cautious of the following:
* Drink plenty of water.
* Avoid overexertion.
* Watch out for sudden temperature increases.
* Shut off electronics when possible to reduce cooling needs.
* Monitor your body temperature and seek shade whenever possible.
* If you have an underlying health condition, please consult your doctor for advice.

Stay safe and stay cool.

For more information, visit our website at [heat.gov](https://heat.gov).
