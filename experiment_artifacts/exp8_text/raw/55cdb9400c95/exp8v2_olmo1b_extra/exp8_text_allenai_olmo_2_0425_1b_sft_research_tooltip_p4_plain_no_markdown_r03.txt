Missing-value markers, often found in statistical data analysis, indicate points that do not have a complete set of data or have undefined or unknown information. These markers can occur due to various reasons such as missing values in a dataset, surrogates not filling in for missing data, or certain variables not being measured, among others. Their presence alerts researchers and analysts to potential gaps or inconsistencies in the data, motivating further investigation into these data points to understand their impact on the overall analysis.
