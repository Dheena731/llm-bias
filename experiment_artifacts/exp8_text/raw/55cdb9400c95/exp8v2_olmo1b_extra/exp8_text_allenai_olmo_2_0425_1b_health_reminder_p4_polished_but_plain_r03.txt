So, what about the English language? Well, I have this funny thing called a keyboard which is exactly the same as the one you have on your computer. It has a little button over the number key and a small button over the space bar. This is called a modifier key and it lets me type characters that have more than one letter, like u, e or a. It's the same button you press over the number key in your computer but it's a little smaller than the number key and it's not under the number key but over it.

When I'm typing a regular word like this, I put my fingers on each letter key one at a time and let the letter come out. When I press the letter key, a little thing called a control key pops up over the letter. I can press the control key and then move my fingers over to the next letter key and it will hold my letter down until I press the letter key again.

But when I'm using a letter that's in a special pattern or pattern somewhere else in the document, like a period or a comma, I don't use the control key. Instead, I click on the letters with my mouse and then click them again or click them on the screen. This lets me type the letter without putting it on the control key. It's faster because I don't have to move my fingers around so much and it lets me type faster.
