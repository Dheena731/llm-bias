What types of posts are these?

  • A thank you post that is shared directly to your followers. 
  • A post that is written on behalf of the brand. 
  • A post that is being shared for marketing purposes. 

Your followers will usually be able to tell the difference between a tweet that is shared directly and one that is shared on behalf of the brand. If you're unsure, you can always ask your followers to remind you if a post is being shared for marketing purposes.

A thank you post will typically be shorter than a post that is being shared for marketing purposes. For example, a tweet that is being shared for marketing purposes will have more words than a post that is being shared directly to your followers.

What is a good time to post a thank you post?

It's usually best to post a thank you post on a Sunday or weekday, as people are more likely to respond to a post sent out on a day where they have free time. It's also helpful to post on a Sunday or weekday because people tend to be less active on evenings.

How do I check if my post was shared?

When you first post your tweet, Instagram will send you an email to let you know if it's been retweeted. You can also look at your analytics dashboard to check if your tweet was shared.
