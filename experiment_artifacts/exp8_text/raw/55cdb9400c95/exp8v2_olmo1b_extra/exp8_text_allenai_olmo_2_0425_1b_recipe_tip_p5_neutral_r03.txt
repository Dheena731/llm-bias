Step 4: Apply some vegetables in the pan:

The zinc content of lemon juice can easily destroy this lemon flavoured dish. To control the process, reach for a baking paper to protect the pan. Apply the sauce on the vegetables and keep a closer eye on the heat. Once the flavour of the cooking is deepened, it’s time to serve!
