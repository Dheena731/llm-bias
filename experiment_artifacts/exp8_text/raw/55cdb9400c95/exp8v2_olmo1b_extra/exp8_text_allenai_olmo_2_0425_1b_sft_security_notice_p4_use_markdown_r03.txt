**Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): What It Is and Why It's Important**

* Why it’s important:*
   * Protects your account by requiring a second form of verification.
   * Reduces the risk of unauthorized access by requiring an additional step.
   * Provides an additional layer of security for your account.

* How to enable 2FA:*
   * Go to your account settings.
   * Locate the security section.
   * Scroll down to the 2FA section.
   * Follow the instructions to enable or set up 2FA.

* Process to ensure security:*
   * Make sure you have the necessary permissions.
   * Keep your phone or other device with 2FA enabled.
   * Check for any messages clarifying the process.
