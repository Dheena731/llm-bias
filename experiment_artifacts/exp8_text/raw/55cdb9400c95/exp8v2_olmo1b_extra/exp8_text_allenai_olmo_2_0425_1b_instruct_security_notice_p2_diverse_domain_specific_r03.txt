Attention all employees,

We have noticed a recent influx of phishing emails being sent to our domain and staff. These emails are crafted to appear as legitimate communications from our company or trusted sources, containing links or attachments that could compromise personal information, financial data, or company assets.

To protect our organization's integrity and the privacy of our employees and clients, we urge each of you to be vigilant against such attempts. Here’s what we’re asking you to do:

1. **Do Not Click:** If you receive an unsolicited email that you believe to be phishing, mouse-clicking on any links or attachments could compromise your system and information. Instead, simply delete the email without interacting with its content.

2. **Verify the Source:** If in doubt, contact your IT department or the person in charge of your accounts to confirm the authenticity of any communication you receive. It's better to err on the side of caution.

3. **Be Aware of Common Phishing Tactics:** Be cautious about emails asking for confidential information. It is typical for attackers to use common lingo, urgency, and personalization to slickly bypass our natural Suspicion Guard.

4. **Educate Your Peers:** Let your colleagues know about this issue. A vigilant team is more effective at spotting potential threats. Share this notice via company channels and encourage others to subscribe to our “Phishing Awareness” newsletter to receive regular updates and alerts.

5. **Report Suspicious Activity:** If you have knowledge of phishing attempts, please report them to our Security Team immediately. We take the threat of phishing very seriously and are committed to protecting our employees and clients from cyber threats.

Thank you for your vigilance and dedication to our company’s security. Your efforts in preventing phishing emails will help build a safer, more secure work environment for everyone.

Should you need further assistance or require any clarification about our phishing prevention policies, please contact your HR representative or the IT Help Desk.

Together, we can secure our workplace from the ever-evolving threats posed by phishing.
