Here is the explanation for the end-of-year tax statement:

The end-of-year tax statement is a financial document that summarizes your tax obligations for the current year. It includes details such as your total tax liability, deductions, credits, and any remaining refunds. The purpose of this statement is to help you understand how much tax you owe or are eligible for a refund, and to ensure you file your return on time.

Key components:

1. **Total Tax Liability**: This is the total amount of tax you owe based on your taxable income (minus any deductions and credits).
2. **Deductions and Credits**: This section outlines all eligible deductions and tax credits you've claimed. Common examples include mortgage interest, charitable donations, and student loan interest.
3. **Refunds**: If you've claimed more deductions or credits than you owe, this section shows how much you may receive as a refund.

Filing your tax return on time is crucial as late penalties can be costly. To prepare your return, closely review the information provided in the end-of-year tax statement. If you have any questions, don't hesitate to reach out to a tax professional or use TurboTax or similar software for guidance.
