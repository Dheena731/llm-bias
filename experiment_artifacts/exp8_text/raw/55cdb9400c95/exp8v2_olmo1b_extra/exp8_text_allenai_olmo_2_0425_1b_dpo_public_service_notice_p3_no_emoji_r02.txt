Attention all residents!

Starting September 1st, our recycling collection schedule will be updated. Residents are now instructed to place recyclable materials in their respective blue recycling bins. Please ensure empty containers are placed in the collection bin on collection days only. Non-recyclable materials such as plastics, glass, and certain metals should be placed in the black bin. Thank you for your attention to these changes. For more information, please visit our website or contact our recycling hotline.

*This notice is subject to change.*
