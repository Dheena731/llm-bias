Occurrences of use of route closure: 4


E.g.:

The section of route X will be closed to motorised traffic on Friday 10th to Sunday 12th between 12:00 and 22:00.

Other examples:

Due to roadworks on the section of route X to Y, the section of route Y will be closed to motorised traffic between Z and W between 12:00 and 22:00.



If users are properly informed about route closures, they will be able to re-plan their journeys. They will not be surprised to find out that the section of route X they were planning to take is now closed to motorised traffic on Friday 10th to Sunday 12th between 12:00 and 22:00.

Contextuals


E.g.:

The section of route X will be closed to motorised traffic from Z to W between 12:00 and 22:00.

Other examples:

Route X will be closed to motorised traffic from Z to W between 12:00 and 22:00.
