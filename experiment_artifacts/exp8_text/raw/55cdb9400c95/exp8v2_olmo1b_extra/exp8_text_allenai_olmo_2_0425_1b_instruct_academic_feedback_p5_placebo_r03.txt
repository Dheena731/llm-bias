When drafting essays or any scholarly work, ensuring proper citations is crucial to maintaining academic integrity and credibility. Omissions or missing citations can significantly hinder the reader's ability to follow the arguments, validate sources, or understand the context of your arguments, thereby diminishing the overall impact and authority of your essay. It reflects a lapse in research diligence and adherence to the established conventions of academic writing.

A well-cited essay not only adheres to the specific citation style required but also respects the work of others, affording them due recognition. The absence of citations can lead to assumptions about the source of ideas, fail to acknowledge the depth of research conducted, and can even lead to plagiarism if uncited work is directly used without acknowledgment. As such, missing citations not only undermine the quality of your work but also the academic rigor and professional tone expected in scholarly discourse. Always ensure your essay is a testament to your research efforts with appropriate citations, as the act of contributing to the collective body of knowledge through documented research is a critical component of academic writing. 

**Note: It's important to clarify the citation style being used consistently and to ensure all arguments are supported with credible sources cited accurately in the specified style. Avoid inventing facts or citing non-existent sources, as this would constitute fraud and misrepresentation. Always engage with your audience by explaining the importance of proper citation and referencing, as well as the benefits it brings to the field of study and to individual authors' careers.**
