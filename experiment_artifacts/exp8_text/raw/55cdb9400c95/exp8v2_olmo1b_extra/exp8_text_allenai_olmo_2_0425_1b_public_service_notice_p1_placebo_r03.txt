Public notices are addressed to all, such as for a public meeting or a break in classes. You know what is the right way to write a public notice. In your first notices, keep things brief and simple. Don't worry about being too formal, but use clear and direct language and avoid long sentences. A good public notice is clear, short, and to the point.

The notices for the library opening times and closing hours on are written in the same way. I want to know when the library is open, and where I can get help. I also want to know if the library is closed. Also, if a notice is not written in a clear, concise, and direct way, students are less likely to read it. Wikipedia is a good source for examples of good public notices.
