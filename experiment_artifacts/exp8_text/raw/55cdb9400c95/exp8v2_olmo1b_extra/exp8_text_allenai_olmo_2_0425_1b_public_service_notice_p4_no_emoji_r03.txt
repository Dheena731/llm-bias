In case of tropospheric ozone:

  • Natural causes are related to 24 hours of sunlight or sun directly over a small wetland.

  • Ozone is intentionally produced through industrial methods.

  • In the troposphere ozone is mainly produced by NOx and VOCs.

  • Because of the shorter distance to the land surface, the lower ozone level should occur during the day or dawn. Concentrations of ozone can be highest during mid-day.

    For the Mediterranean region the ozone layer typically closes at about 6 PM, when emissions from the sun are less. The maximum ozone concentration is usually higher during the day and during sunrise.

    In the troposphere ozone is a gas. It is not a visual indicator.
