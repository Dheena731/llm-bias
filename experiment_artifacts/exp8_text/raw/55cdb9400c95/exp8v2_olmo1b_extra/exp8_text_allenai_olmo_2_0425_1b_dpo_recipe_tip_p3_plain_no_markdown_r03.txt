Make your rice less sticky by following these simple tips: first, use a generous amount of cooking water so you end up with less sticky rice. second, avoid overcooking the rice as it can make it too dry and sticky. third, if your rice is starting to stick to the bottom of the pot, stir it occasionally. finally, serve your rice immediately after cooking to let it cool slightly which can help reduce stickiness. by keeping these tips in mind, you can make your rice just the way you like without it becoming too sticky.
