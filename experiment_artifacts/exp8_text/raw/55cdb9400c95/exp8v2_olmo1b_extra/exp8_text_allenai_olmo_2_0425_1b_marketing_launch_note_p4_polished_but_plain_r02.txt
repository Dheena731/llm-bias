Write in plain text with no formatting.

  1. Write a title. This should be no longer than 25 words, and should not contain abbreviations, acronyms, or fancy words. Something along the lines of “This is an introduction to a bike repair service” works well.
  2. Write a hook. This should be no longer than 40 words.
  3. Write an opening sentence.
  4. Include a call-to-action that says what your service includes and what it is like to use. Something like “If you’re looking for a bike repair service, please read on” is a good call-to-action.
  5. Write a body that includes information on what your service offers. Something like “We offer tires and oil changes every 10,000 miles” works well.
  6. Write a closing sentence.
  7. Write a signature that includes your email address and phone number. Most importantly, write your full name and your email address in plain text. Don’t use CAPS or emoji or any other branding.
