Incorrect infringement

The copyright law of your country is most likely called "Intellectual Property Law". It is a law that protects the creation of intellectual work. In many of the countries, the creation of intellectual work (such as a book, a newspaper, a song or a movie) is considered to be a kind of property. Copyright is a kind of property right.

In this wiki, we recognize the copyright of a work and its creator(s) even if the work is not free. In some countries, there is copyright law which protects all kinds of works, including the works of Wikipedia. The copyright itself is property. The owner of a work has the right to control who can use it and how. The owner of a work may require compensation (money) for using the work. Wikipedia's copyrights and licenses are described in detail in our Copyright page.

Be careful with your use of Wikipedia

This article describes some of the rules Wikipedia is subject to regarding its copyrights. Wikipedia is a kind of copyrighted work, and as such, its copyrights and licenses are published in the Copyright page. The rules and policies described there should always be followed. These rules and policies are not a list of suggestions. They are what your copyright permission must comply with if you want to use Wikipedia.

If you do not agree with any of these policies, and you want to use Wikipedia in any way, you can write to the helpdesk (but be sure to tell them what will happen in case that you are forbidden from using Wikipedia.).
