Refilling a prescription is important because it keeps you on the prescribed medication and prevents unnecessary trips to the doctor. Here's a quick reminder:

**When to Refill Your Prescription:**

* **After Your Last Prescription Expires:** Just as with a credit card, after the expiration date, your prescription will be null and void, and you'll need to procure a new one from your healthcare provider.
* **Before Travel or Hospital Stay:** Preemptive refills can ensure you don't run out while you're on the road or hospitalized.
* **Your Doctor's Instructions:** Always obtain your refill confirmation from your healthcare provider before refilling your prescription yourself.

**How to Refill:**
1. Check your prescription for expiration date and any instructions from your pharmacist.
2. Gather your identification, current billing information, and any additional items prescribed such as refills or maintenance medications.
3. Contact your pharmacy or doctor's office to place your refill order.
4. Follow any instructions your healthcare provider or pharmacist may give regarding the medication and dosage.

**Safety Tips:**
* Avoid refilling prescriptions from unverified sources online or via public directories, as this increases the risk of counterfeit medication.
* Keep all prescription medications and refill information in a safe place and consult with your healthcare provider if you have any questions or concerns about your prescriptions.

By following these reminders, you can ensure that you receive your medications safely and effectively.
