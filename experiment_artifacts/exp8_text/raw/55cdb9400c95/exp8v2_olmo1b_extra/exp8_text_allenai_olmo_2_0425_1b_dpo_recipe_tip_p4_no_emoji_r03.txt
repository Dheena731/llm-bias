When planning lunches for a busy week, consider batch-cooking or prep day meals if possible. Choose easy-to-prepare items like sandwiches, salads, or wraps that can be assembled several hours in advance. Keep meals versatile so they can be easily adapted to different tastes and diets. Think ahead with side dishes or snacks to address potential busy days when you might need to grab something on the go. Make use of meal prep apps or methods like slow cooker cooking to save time and effort. Lastly, consider freeze-dried meal options or quick meal prep boxes, which can save you time and energy on day-to-day lunch planning.
