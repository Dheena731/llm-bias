Starting this upcoming week the local recycling collection schedule is going to change. Previous to this new arrangement residents were collecting their recyclable materials on Tuesdays and Thursdays. This change will see the collection days shifted to Saturdays only. Residents can expect to pick up their recyclables on Saturdays from 8 AM to 11 AM at their designated recycling carts placed conveniently at their doorsteps.

In an effort to encourage participation and make recycling a bit more convenient, we will be offering incentives such as an extra deposit on certain recyclable items if they are properly sorted. The City Council also anticipates that reducing waste will help decrease our town's environmental footprint, so we hope this change will make a positive impact on the community’s environmental consciousness.

For more information on these changes and for any questions or concerns, please refer to the city's official website or contact the recycling team directly. Everyone plays a part in maintaining a clean and sustainable environment, and your cooperation in this new collection schedule is greatly appreciated.
