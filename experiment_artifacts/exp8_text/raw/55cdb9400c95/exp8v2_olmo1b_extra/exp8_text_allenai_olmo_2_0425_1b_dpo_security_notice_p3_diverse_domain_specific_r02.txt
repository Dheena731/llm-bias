Attention all staff,

As we continue to ensure a secure and efficient operating environment, please be reminded to lock your workspace screens when you step away from your stations. This simple action goes a long way in protecting our digital assets and privacy. 

Screen locking is particularly important during vacations, after hours, and during out-of-office access to our systems. It prevents unauthorized access and maintains the integrity of our information.

Here’s a quick step-by-step guide to locking your screen:

1. Find the screen locking option in your system's menu.
2. Enter your login credentials (if required).
3. You may see a confirmation prompt or simply a lock icon.
4. Lock your screen by selecting the lock icon.

By locking your screens regularly, you contribute to maintaining our high standard of security and reliability. Thank you for your attention to this important task.
