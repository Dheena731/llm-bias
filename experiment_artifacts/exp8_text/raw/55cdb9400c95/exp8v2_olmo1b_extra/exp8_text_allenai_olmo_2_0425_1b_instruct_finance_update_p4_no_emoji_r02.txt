The end-of-year tax statement is a document that outlines all the income and expenses that are reported on your taxes for the year. It is prepared by your tax authority to ensure accuracy and compliance with tax laws. The statement includes detailed records of all sources of income like salaries wages dividends and bonuses as well as expenses like mortgage interest insurance and charitable donations. It also clarifies any discrepancies between reported income and taxes owed and provides the basis for determining your final tax liability or refund. It is advisable to review the statement at least once at the end of the year to ensure that all items are accurate and correct any mistakes before filing your return.
