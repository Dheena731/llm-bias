**Reporting a Lost Company Laptop**

*Immediately report the loss to your IT department or supervisor.*
*Document all findings, including location, last known activity, and any attempts to retrieve.*
*Take a picture of the device if possible, and keep a copy for records.*
*Use company resources, such as the security desk, for further reporting.*
*Notify external vendors and services that handle company data for potential data breach reporting.*
*Explain the potential consequences of the lost laptop, including lost data and security risks.*
*Save copies of all communications related to the report for internal and legal records.*
