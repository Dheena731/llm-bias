Dear Residents,

We would like to inform you that starting from next Tuesday, from 9 AM to 5 PM, residents have the opportunity to participate in the new Pick Up Bin Recycling scheme. With this initiative, we aim to increase the recycling rate among our population and contribute to a more sustainable future.

The new schedule will encompass two weekly collection periods at 9 AM and 5 PM. For the first week, the collection will be spread across two days, with every household gaining access to the collection sites on the first day and the second on the second day.

For the second week, the collections will be combined into one day at 12 PM. The 9 AM collection will be held on the third day and the 5 PM collection will take place on the fourth day.

Please note that in the second week, the 5 PM collection will be held on the first day, while the 9 AM collection will be on the fourth day.

To ensure the smooth operation of the collection process, we kindly ask for your cooperation by placing the recycling materials in the designated bins provided at the curbside. Our team will be on the ground to assist during the collection process.

Remember to place your recyclable materials in the corresponding bins and check them before or after the collection to prevent any mishandling and ensure a successful and efficient process.

Your role in making this initiative a success is crucial, and we appreciate your support and cooperation.

Thank you for your continued attention to the sustainable efforts we are making together. We look forward to seeing more residents participating in recycling and contributing to our collective mission towards a greener future.

Warm regards,

The Recycling Team
