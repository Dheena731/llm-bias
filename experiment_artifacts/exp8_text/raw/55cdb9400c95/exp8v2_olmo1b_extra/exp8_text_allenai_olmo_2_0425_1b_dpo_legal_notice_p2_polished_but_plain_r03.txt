Due to changes in the way we handle personal information, we are updating our privacy policy. This notice informs you of the modifications made and their implications.

We have structured this document to be clear and understandable. Here are the key changes:

3. Data Sharing: Information about how and with whom your data may be shared is also included in this policy. It outlines scenarios where your data might be disclosed and steps you can take to restrict such sharing.

We encourage you to review the updated policy regularly as our practices and legal obligations may evolve. If you have any questions, please contact our privacy officer.
