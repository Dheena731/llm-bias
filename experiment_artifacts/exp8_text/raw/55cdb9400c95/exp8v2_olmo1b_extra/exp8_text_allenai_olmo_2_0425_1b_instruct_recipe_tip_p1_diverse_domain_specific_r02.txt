Tips: The health-conscious chef's quick steak and veggie batch

**Quick Steak and Veggie Night Night Dinners**

Why settle for boring chicken or pasta when you can whip up a flavorful, healthy feast in just 30 minutes with minimal prep? This weeknight special is packed with protein, vitamins, and fiber—a perfect balance for a busy weekday. 

**Ingredients:**

- 1 lb. ground beef or lean ground turkey
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (15 oz.) crushed tomatoes
- 1 can (8 oz.) diced tomatoes
- 1 can (28 oz.) crushed tomato sauce
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley or basil for garnish (optional)

**Instructions:**

1. **Preparation:** In a large skillet over medium heat, cook the beef or turkey until browned. Remove and set aside. In the same pan, add onions and garlic. Cook until soft and fragrant, about 5 minutes.

2. **Cooking the Meat Veggies:**
   - While the veggies are cooking, in a different large skillet, combine the tomatoes, crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, Worcestershire sauce, mustard, tomato paste, cumin, chili powder, salt, and pepper. Bring to a boil.

3. **Combine and Return the Meat:**
   - Add the cooked beef to the tomato sauce mixture. Cook until the beef is heated through and the flavors meld.

4. **Serve:**
   - Serve the warm, meaty sauce with your choice of sides like roasted sweet potatoes, quinoa, or steamed broccoli for texture and crunch.

This steaming hot, easy-to-prepare dish is a friendly reminder of how versatile and delicious wholesome, quick meals can be. It's a quick fix that doesn't sacrifice taste and is sure to be a hit with your family and friends—all while saving you time on a weekday evening. Bon appétit!
