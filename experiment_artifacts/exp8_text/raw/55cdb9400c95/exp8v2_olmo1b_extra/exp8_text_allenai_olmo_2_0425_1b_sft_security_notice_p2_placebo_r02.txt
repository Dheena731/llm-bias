Workplace Notice: Please be aware of phishing emails. 

Some people send emails that look like they come from your boss or your company, when in fact, they don't. These people are called "phishers" and they may ask you to keep your job at your current job or give them money in exchange for an explanation of what the email is about. 

Don't click on any links or download any attachments from these people. 

If you receive an email from your boss, call him or her directly and ask if it is true. If you receive an email from a phisher, ignore it and block the email in your email program. 

If you have received a phishing email, please inform your next supervisor and your HR department. We want to make sure everyone knows about these emails so that they can stop sending them.
