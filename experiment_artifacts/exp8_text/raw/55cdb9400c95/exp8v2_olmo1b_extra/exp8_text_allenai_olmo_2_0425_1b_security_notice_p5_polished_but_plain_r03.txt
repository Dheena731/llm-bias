Suppose your laptop is stolen, and you want to report it to the police. Write your report with the following structure in the style below.

  1. Start with a statement of the problem: The laptop was stolen from my home address.
  2. Give some background information about the laptop: It was a company laptop. It was brand X, given to me by company Y.
  3. Give the address of your home: 1234 Main Street.
  4. Give the date and time the laptop was stolen: Thursday, June 20 at 3:00 AM.
  5. Provide the contact information of the police: The business directory or hotel desk is available for inquiries.
  6. Give the contact information of a company, a friend, or a colleague who can assist you in making a report: Please contact me if you have any questions.
  7. Give a link to a page providing information about your laptop: Information about your laptop is available here.
  8. Give your contact information: Please contact me if you need any additional information.

Note that the report format is not mandatory; you can write whatever you want, provided that it is acceptable to the recipient. However, the police will probably use a template to quickly interpret your report, so the more polished and structured your report, the easier it will be for them to process.

This is a sample police report

# Laptop stolen on #June #20 at #3:00 AM from my house located at #1234 Main Street.

I, #Oliver Heaviside, am the owner of the #X #brand #company #Laptop, which #Law #enforcement #Department has requested me to present.

The #Laptop was stolen from #My #HomeAddress, and I am requesting its return if possible.

Please contact me if you need any further details or assistance.

Additional contact information:

#Oliver #Heaviside

The police will now process your report 

This report was created by Oliver Heaviside, the owner of the laptop.

Sometimes you may receive a report from your client. It may be about something that happened hours or days ago, or it may be a matter of concern that needs immediate action. In this case, you can automate the process by using a simple text message or a web page.

Formula summary

The automization of repetitive tasks is a form of automation. The outcome of the task can be delivered by sending a message to a web page or a text message.

Example scenario

Suppose your client gave you a flight ticket to a specific place. He has forgotten the confirmation code, and asks you to call the airline to get a new code and send it to him by text message.

You can automate the process with the following steps:

  1. Send an email message with the flight ticket and the confirmation code.
  2. Send an SMS message with the confirmation code to the client.
  3. Keep the email and SMS messages to confirm your work.

Automation can be very useful when you need to automate repetitive tasks for the sake of efficiency. If you would like to automate the full process, you may want to use the Send a Reminder workflow.
