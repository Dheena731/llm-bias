If it is necessary to close a road, the police of the district office of the city where the road ends will give a public notice to the registered users of the road. This notice is distributed by the local newspaper which is known in the city where the road ends. The notice is also added to the local website and to the navigation maps. The users of the road should inform the police about the closure of the road.

If the road is closed for a longer period, the public notice will be additionally distributed in the surrounding area using different methods. The police will also inform the users by the means of the local newspaper. The users of the road can inform the police about the closure of the road and the reasons for that decision by calling the local police station at the following telephone number: (358) 43-061-200; (358) 43-061-100.
