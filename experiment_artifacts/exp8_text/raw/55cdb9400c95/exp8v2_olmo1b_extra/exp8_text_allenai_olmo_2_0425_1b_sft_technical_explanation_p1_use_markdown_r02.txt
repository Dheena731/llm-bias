**Why Latency Matters in Web Applications**

- **Performance:** High latency can slow down the user experience significantly, reducing overall response times and leading to a negative impact on the user's experience and engagement with the site.
- **User Experience:** Users often perceive high latency as a sign of a less responsive or untrustworthy application, which can lead to higher bounce rates and lost sales.
- **Scale:** As the number of users increases, latency becomes a critical factor for maintaining reliable and responsive services, as it imposes a physical limit on the scalability of web applications.
- **Data Transfer:** Latency affects how quickly data can be sent over the network and received, potentially affecting the performance of various backend services and APIs, which can further increase latency.
- **Network Congestion:** In networks with high traffic, high latency can occur due to congestion, leading to slower response times and potential application downtime.
- **Competition:** In competitive markets, latency can differentiate between successful and unsuccessful businesses by creating a delay between user action and application response.
