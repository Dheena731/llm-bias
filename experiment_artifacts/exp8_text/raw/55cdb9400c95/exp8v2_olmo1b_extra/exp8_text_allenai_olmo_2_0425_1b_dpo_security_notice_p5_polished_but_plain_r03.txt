When you have a lost company laptop, it's important to report it immediately. A missing laptop can compromise sensitive data, compromise your company's security, and result in substantial time and financial losses. Here's why action is needed and how you should do it:

### Why Report a Lost Laptop?
- **Sensitive Data:** Tablets and laptops often contain personal and company data, including financial records, personal information, and proprietary knowledge.
- **Security Vulnerability:** The lost laptop could be compromised by cybercriminals.
- **Legal Obligation:** Your company may have data protection policies and insurance that mandate reporting a loss.

### Steps to Report a Lost Laptop:
1. **Contact IT Department:** Reach out to your company’s IT department or IT support team as soon as possible. They are equipped to handle the situation, investigate, and potentially recover the device.
2. **Document the Loss:** Take photos of the laptop, its software status, and any relevant serial numbers, logos, or stickers.
   - Include detailed descriptions and any information about the user account or any password protection measures.
3. **File a Lost Property Claim:** Use the lost and stolen property form available on your company's website or through internal channels. Offer any pertinent identification or documentation you may have on the laptop.
4. **Use Company Security Tools:** Many organizations have internal tools for tracking lost or stolen devices. Utilize these tools to report the lost item and to set up remote tracking capabilities if the device can be accessed remotely.
5. **Stay Informed:** Keep your IT department informed about the progress of the investigation. They may need to contact the device’s service provider for assistance.
6. **Dispose of the Device:** Once it’s determined that recovery is not possible or that data has been compromised, securely dispose of the device. This step is critical to protect against future security risks.

### Final Thoughts:
A missing laptop is more than just an inconvenience; it can be a significant risk to the company's data and operations. By promptly reporting the device, you’re protecting your organization and adhering to legal and ethical responsibilities. Remember to document everything, follow your company’s protocols, and take the necessary measures to secure your lost property.
