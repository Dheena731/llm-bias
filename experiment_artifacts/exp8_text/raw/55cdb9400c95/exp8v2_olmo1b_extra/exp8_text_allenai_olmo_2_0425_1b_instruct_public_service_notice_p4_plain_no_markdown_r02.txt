Attention residents of our community: A heat advisory has been issued for the next 48 hours. Extreme heat conditions are expected to remain persistent throughout the day. Due to the high temperatures we advise you to take necessary precautions to protect yourself. Here are some tips to help you stay safe during this period:

* Stay indoors as much as possible
* Drink plenty of water and monitor your body temperature
* Avoid outdoor activity especially in the early morning and late afternoon
* If you must be outdoors seek shelter in shaded areas and avoid wearing heavy clothing
* If you are one of those who are at a higher risk for heat-related illness such as the elderly or those with pre-existing health conditions consult with your doctor about adjusting your medication
* Keep a cool compress to place on your forehead and neck and stay hydrated

Remember to check the advisory regularly for updates as conditions can change rapidly. This is not the time to ignore the warnings and take unnecessary risks. Your safety is our priority so follow these suggestions and stay cool.
