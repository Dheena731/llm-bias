Refilling your prescription is important for maintaining your treatment plan and ensuring your health is managed effectively. 

Here are a few reminders:

1. Always follow the pharmacy's return and refill policy. 
2. Verify your prescription to ensure it's the correct dosage and origin. 
3. Check your schedule and remember to contact your doctor's office if you miss a dose. 
4. Do not share your prescription with someone else. 
5. Do not use expired medications if your prescription has not expired.
