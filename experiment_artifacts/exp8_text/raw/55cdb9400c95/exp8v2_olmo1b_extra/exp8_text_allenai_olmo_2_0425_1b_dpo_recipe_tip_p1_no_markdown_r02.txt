To make a simple chicken penne pasta dish on a weeknight, start by sautéing onions and garlic in olive oil until softened. Add shredded chicken and cook until cooked through. Stir in a can of your favorite diced tomatoes, a teaspoon of dried oregano, and a pinch of red pepper flakes. Combine with penne noodles in a large saucepan and simmer until the pasta is al dente. Serve hot with a drizzle of lemon juice and fresh parsley for garnish. This recipe requires minimal prep and makes a hearty and satisfying weeknight dinner.
