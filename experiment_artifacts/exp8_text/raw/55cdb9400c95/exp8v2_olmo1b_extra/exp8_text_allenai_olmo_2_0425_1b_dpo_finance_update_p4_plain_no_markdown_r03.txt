An end-of-year tax statement is a document that reports the taxes you owe for the year and explains any credits or deductions you may have taken. It usually lists the total tax owed, any credits such as the standard deduction, mortgage interest deduction, or child tax credit, and any remaining balance that needs to be paid. It's important to check this statement to ensure all amounts are correct as late payments or errors could result in penalties or interest charges. It's also a good opportunity to review your tax strategy for the upcoming year and make adjustments if necessary. Be sure to keep copies of this statement for your records and to submit any missing portions to your tax authority by the due date.
