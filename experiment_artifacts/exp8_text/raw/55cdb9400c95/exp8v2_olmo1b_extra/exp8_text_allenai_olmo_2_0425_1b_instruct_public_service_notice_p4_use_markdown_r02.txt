**Heat Advisory**

**Headline:** Stay hydrated and seek shade 

- **Explanation:** The heatwave is here, and it's important to stay hydrated and avoid direct sunlight. 
- **Tips:** 
  - Stay indoors when possible and use air conditioning. 
  - Avoid extended periods outdoors without protection from the sun. 
  - Drink plenty of water. 
  - Monitor your temperature and seek shade when needed.
