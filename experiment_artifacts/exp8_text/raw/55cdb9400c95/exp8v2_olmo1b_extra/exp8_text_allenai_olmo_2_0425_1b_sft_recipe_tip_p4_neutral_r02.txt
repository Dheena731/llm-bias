Tip: If you want to make sure you can plan your lunches without running around quickly when you get to work, try the following. 

1. Make a list of all of the lunches you plan to eat for the week. 
2. Take each item of food down to the kitchen and only pack what you know you can eat. 
3. Grab a few snacks and your key items for a healthy lunch. 
4. Start planning your lunches a week in advance.
