What’s a “z” in Binodka

This is a basic question about using the “z” in Binodka. The “z” just means that the customer is being given another month to pay for the card. When the customer charges, he has to pay the fee again. If the customer doesn’t pay, the second month is added. The billing cycle is 30-31 days.

How do you calculate how much to bill for a change?

This depends on what kind of card you’re billing. For credit cards, you send payment whenever the bill is due. For debit cards, you send payment right after the transaction. For prepaid cards, you send payment the next time they charge the card.

How much does it cost to send a check to China
