In essays that are expected to be academically rigorous such as those found in academic journals or within thesis documents, missing citations can significantly undermine the credibility and professionalism of the work. Proper citation not only acknowledges the sources you have used but also provides readers with the opportunity to verify the information independently, ensuring the validity of your arguments and the accuracy of your research. Without citations, your work risks being seen as an attempt to 'borrow' others' ideas without acknowledgment, potentially leading to a lack of trust among your academic peers, professors, and readers.

To maintain academic integrity and respect for your peers' work, it is imperative to include citations for all sources used, whether paraphrased or directly quoted. This practice not only upholds the tradition of scholarship but also adheres to the standards set by academic authorities and professional writing guidelines.
