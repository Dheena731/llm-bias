Boost the Flavor Boost: After roasted your radiant veggies, elevate their natural allure with precisely chosen seasonings. For a zesty kick, mix in a touch of freshly grated Parmesan or Pecorino Romano cheese for a burst of savory richness. For a hint of sweetness, sprinkle in a few toasted breadcrumbs crusts or crushed pistachios. If you're after a zing of acidity, add in a dash of freshly squeezed lemon juice or orange zest. Experiment with your seasonings; create a recipe that truly sings with your personal flavor profile. Perfectly seasoned roasted veggies are a hit anytime, but especially this fall when Earth's bounty is at its sweetest.
