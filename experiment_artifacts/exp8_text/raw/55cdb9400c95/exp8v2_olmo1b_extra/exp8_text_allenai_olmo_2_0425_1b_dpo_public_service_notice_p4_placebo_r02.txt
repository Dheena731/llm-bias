**Public Notice: Heat Advisory issued for [City/Region/state]**

In light of the recent increase in temperatures, we are issuing a Heat Advisory for [date]. Stay hydrated by drinking water. Monitor your temperature and rest when needed.

*Note: Heat advisories are in place to alert the public to extreme heat that increases the risk of heat-related health issues such as exhaustion, dehydration, sunburn, and heatstroke.*

**Important Information:**

* **Dehydration:** Avoid using excessive amounts of water and do not lie in the sun for extended periods.

* **Rest:** If feeling lethargic or having trouble staying awake during the day, take short naps in the shade.

* **Healthcare:** If you notice any symptoms of heat exhaustion (like headaches, fatigue, dizzy spells, or confusion) seek immediate relief by resting and drinking plenty of cool water or electrolyte-rich fluids.

* **Action Steps:** Wear light-colored, loose-fitting clothing, especially around midday when temperatures are highest. Use air conditioning when indoors, but avoid overuse of fans, which can sometimes worsen humidity effects.

* **Preventive Measures:** Stay inside when the sun is strongest (usually between 10 AM and 4 PM), avoid peak usage of electrical equipment, and use shaded areas when outdoors.

* **Contact:** If you have any concerns or additional health risks related to the heat, contact your local health department or seek medical advice.

* **End of Notice:** Notices for heat advisories are issued to provide the public with guidance to manage heat stress safely and effectively. Compliance with this advisory will help protect public health and ensure that everyone can enjoy the benefits of a long, healthy summer season. 

**Thank you for your cooperation in staying safe and healthy during this heatwave.**

*Stay cool and stay safe!*
