Write a description of the election. Where is your polling station? Who do you vote for? How did the other candidates do?

Write an interesting guide to your polling station. For instance, if your polling station is a church, you might want to include a video of it. You don't need to edit anything, as this is an exercise. But if you want to edit it for content, please do so in a separate document.

In your guide, include the following information:

  • The name of your party.
  • Contact information for your party.
  • Contact information for your candidate.
  • Contact information for any volunteers you have.
  • How to find your polling location.
  • Whether you are in a swing or safe state or district.
  • How to use the voting machines.
  • How to vote for your party candidates. If you only have a few, include their name and picture. If you have any other candidates, include their name, picture, and link to their campaign website.
  • How to accept your vote.
  • Any reminders about your polling station.

Write in plain prose. Do not use emoji, Markdown, headings, bullet points, numbered lists, bold text, italic text, tables, decorative symbols, or links.
