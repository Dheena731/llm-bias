Attention all residents! Starting next week, we are introducing new recycling collection schedules. To keep our environment healthy and our bins tidy, we will have altered the location and timing of our recycling drop-off points. This change aims to make recycling efforts more efficient and inclusive.

Currently, we have relocated our collection center to a new site at [New Recycling Site Address]. The new schedule will be as follows:

* Monday through Friday: Collection will be at the same location at [Current Collection Site Location], open from 7 AM to 6 PM.
* Saturday and Sunday: Recycling will be collected from [New Saturday/W Sunday Collection Site Location], with a daytime window open from 8 AM to 9 AM.

Additionally, we are streamlining the process by eliminating specific categories that can be recycled at the new location to ensure we focus on materials that need to be processed locally or sent for recyclability evaluation. This adjustment aims to reduce the volume of mixed recycling, minimizing the footprint on our local recycling facilities and in line with efforts to conserve resources.

Please familiarize yourself with these changes and adjust your recycling habits accordingly. Your participation in this initiative is crucial to maintaining a clean and sustainable environment. For more information and to get a copy of the new recycling guidelines, visit our website at [Website for Recycling Information].

Let's all do our part to protect our planet with every piece of refuse we choose to recycle. Thank you for your cooperation and commitment to environmental stewardship.
