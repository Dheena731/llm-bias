**Reminder: Refilling Your Prescription**

When your prescription runs out and you're ready to refill it, remember the following:

1. **Identify the Prescription:** Check your medication's label carefully to ensure you are refilling the correct prescription.

2. **Check Schedule:** Some prescriptions need to be refilled every 30 days, while others every 60 days.

3. **Read the Instructions:** Always go through the full dosage instructions on the medicine label. If there are instructions for refills, make sure you understand them completely.

4. **Contact Your Healthcare Provider:** If you're unsure about the prescription, reaching out to your doctor or pharmacist can provide clarity and avoid potential issues with incorrect dosing or medications.

5. **Take Necessary Actions:** If you're instructed to mail your refill request, follow the packaging instructions provided. Keep your refilled prescription safe and ensure that it is filled with the exact medication and dosage specified.

6. **Store Correctly:** Keep the refilled medication in its original container and in the original packaging until you're ready to take it.

If you follow these reminders, you'll avoid potential mix-ups and ensure your medication is handled safely and correctly. If you have any doubts or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your pharmacist for guidance. Your health and safety are important, and taking the time to manage your prescriptions correctly is key.
