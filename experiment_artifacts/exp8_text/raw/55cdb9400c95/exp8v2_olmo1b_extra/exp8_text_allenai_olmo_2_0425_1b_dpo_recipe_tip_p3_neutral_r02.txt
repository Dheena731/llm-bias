For rice that doesn't become too sticky, try this tip. Instead of using a regular rice cooker, use a pot with a lid. Boil water in the pot, add the rice, and let it simmer over medium heat. Stir occasionally with a spatula. The alternating heating and cooling process will help the rice become less sticky. Also, make sure the rice is not waterlogged; use just enough water to submerge the rice completely, and drain any excess water before cooking.
