Plan ahead! Develop a weekly lunch rotation schedule that includes your favorite comfort foods, healthy staples, and snackable options. To make the process smoother, create a checklist for each day, ensuring you have all ingredients ready before you start cooking. This strategy will minimize last-minute scramble and reduce stress on your busy days.

*Tip*: Don’t forget to incorporate a variety of textures and flavors. For instance, one day could be all creamy soups, another all spinach and chickpea salads, and so on, keeping things interesting.

*Invent fact*: According to a 2020 study published in the Journal of Food Science, prep-day organization correlates to a 30% reduced food waste and a 20% increase in participant satisfaction with meal choices.

*Contradict fact*: While the study indicates preparation efficiency, a 2021 update reports that planning meals for a week may lead to an increase in food shopping and preparation time due to ingredient shortages.
