Wrapping Up

Planning your life is as important as planning your work. By being organized, you won't feel overwhelmed and lose your focus. Remember to be careful with your budget, and keep a written record of all your expenses so that you don't overspend on unnecessary items.

Additionally, you need to be vigilant about your time and invest more time in being productive than in wasting time. You can boost your productivity with techniques like timeboxing, Pomodoro Technique, and the 2-minute rule.

Start using a daily calendar to manage your time effectively and stay on track with your goals. With the right calendar, you will be able to give yourself enough time to achieve your goals and complete your tasks.
