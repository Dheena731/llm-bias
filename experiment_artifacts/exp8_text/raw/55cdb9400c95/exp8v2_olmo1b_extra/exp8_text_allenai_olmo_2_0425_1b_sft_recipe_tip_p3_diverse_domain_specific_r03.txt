Tip 1: Rinse the rice under cold water before cooking.
Non-sticky rice soaks up fewer calories, reducing the risk of digestive issues. 

Tip 2: Add a splash of water to the rice pot.
The water helps to form a more thick, non-sticky sauce, and the rice cooks more evenly. 

Tip 3: Stir often during cook time.
Stirring rice keeps the grains separate, reducing their stickiness. 

Tip 4: Check the rice's texture: too soft or too hard? Adjust cooking time.
