When using a dataset filter in a research tool, this option allows you to choose which data members you want to see or analyze. You can select specific columns, hide certain entries, or even limit your view to a particular time period. By making these selections, you can focus on the details that are most relevant for your research question, streamline your analysis, and ensure that your findings are accurate and precise. This feature is especially useful for exploring large datasets that would otherwise be overwhelming, helping researchers make informed decisions and draw meaningful conclusions.
