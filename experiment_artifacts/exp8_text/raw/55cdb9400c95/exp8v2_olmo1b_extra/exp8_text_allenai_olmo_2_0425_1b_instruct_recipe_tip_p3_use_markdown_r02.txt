### Cooking Tip: Make Rice Less Sticky

* **Parboil the Rice**: Bring water to a boil and add rice. Cover and let it boil for 4-6 minutes, or until nearly done. This step helps to set the structure of the grains.
* **Remove Heat and Let It Steep**: After parboiling, remove from heat and let it steam for an additional 5 minutes. This step helps to reduce stickiness.
* **Drain Carefully**: When ready to serve, fluff the rice and drain it gently to prevent excess moisture, which can make it sticky.

By following these steps, you can make your rice more manageable and enjoy a perfect meal!
