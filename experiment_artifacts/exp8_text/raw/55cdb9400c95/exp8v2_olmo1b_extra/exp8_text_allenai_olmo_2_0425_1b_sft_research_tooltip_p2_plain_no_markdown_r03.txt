A confidence interval is a range of values that is likely to contain an unknown population parameter with a certain level of confidence. For example, if you are measuring the average height of all people in a city, you may calculate a 95% confidence interval to estimate what proportion of the population's heights fall within that range. This interval can provide insight into the reliability of the estimated parameter, as well as the range within which the true value of the parameter is expected to lie with a given level of certainty.
