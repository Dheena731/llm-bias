If you have found a lost company laptop please report it to the IT department immediately. The laptop should be logged as lost and submitted to the IT department for recovery. Do not try to access any personal information or files on the device as this could result in legal implications for you if the information is confidential.

If you have any questions or concerns please contact the IT department at the company's designated phone number or email address. Our team is dedicated to recovering lost equipment and ensuring the safety and security of all company data.
