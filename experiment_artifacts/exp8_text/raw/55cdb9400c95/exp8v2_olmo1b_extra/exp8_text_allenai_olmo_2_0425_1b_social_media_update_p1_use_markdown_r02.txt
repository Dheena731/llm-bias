 • Don't give too much for free. Make it worthwhile for the user.
  • Be concise, but still provide details.
  • Show, don't tell. Give the user a chance to test drive it.
  • Tell an interesting story.
  • Be specific, don't share "features" that are a mile wide and an inch deep.
  • Get feedback from people who you think are likely to come back (contests, people following you).
