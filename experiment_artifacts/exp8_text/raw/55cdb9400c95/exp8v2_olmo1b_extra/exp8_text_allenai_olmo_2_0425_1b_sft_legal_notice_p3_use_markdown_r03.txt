# Privacy Policy & Cookie Policy

Welcome to our website! We are committed to providing you with a safe and enjoyable browsing experience. Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience by remembering your preferences and settings.

This privacy policy and cookie policy explain how we collect, use, and store information about you on our website. By continuing to use our website, you agree to our use of cookies.

Our website uses cookies to:
- Personalize your experience and provide you with customized content
- Offload data processing tasks to reduce the load on our servers
- Improve our website's functionality and performance

Cookies are small pieces of data sent from a website and stored on your device. They can be thought of as a digital fingerprint that helps us identify your device.

We use cookies to:
- Improve website performance
- Personalize your browsing experience
- Improve our website's functionality and performance

Common types of cookies include:
- **Session Cookies:** Used to remember your previous interactions with our website, such as log-in status or browsing history.

- **Persistent Cookies:** These are stored on your device after your browser is closed and can be accessed by the website at a later time.

- **Fragments Cookies:** Used to store a small part of a cookie on your device while you are viewed multiple pages on a site.

We may use third-party services to update, manage, or delete your data. We will notify you when we do so.

We protect your personal information and take other measures to ensure your safety while you visit our website. If we learn about a potential privacy or security threat, we will notify you, and we may take actions to eliminate or mitigate the threat.

If you disagree with the use of cookies on our website, you can opt-out of receiving communications from us or our partners. You may do this by adjusting your privacy settings on your browser.
