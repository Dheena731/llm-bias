**Why Latency Matters in Web Applications:**

* **Performance Impact:** High latency can lead to a poor user experience, as requests and responses take longer to complete, resulting in a laggy interface and frustrating user interactions.
* **User Retention:** Customers are likely to leave if they encounter significant latency, as a consistent poor performance can damage trust and loyalty.
* **Resource Efficiency:** Long latencies can increase server load and energy consumption, potentially costing the business money and affecting sustainability.
* **Application Scalability:** As the number of users increases, latency becomes a bottleneck, limiting the application's ability to handle more traffic efficiently.
* **SEO and Rankings:** Slow sites are often penalized by search engines, affecting a web application's visibility and ranking in search results.
