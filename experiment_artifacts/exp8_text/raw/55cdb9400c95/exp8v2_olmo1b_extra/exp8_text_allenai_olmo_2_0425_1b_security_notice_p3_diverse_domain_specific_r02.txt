Make sure that the text is readable and understandable. Use a simple vocabulary and avoid too many punctuation marks. Be clear about the problem that you are trying to solve.

What is the cost of the solution?

It is important to provide a detailed breakdown of the costs. This helps to give the idea of the risk and the value of the solution.
