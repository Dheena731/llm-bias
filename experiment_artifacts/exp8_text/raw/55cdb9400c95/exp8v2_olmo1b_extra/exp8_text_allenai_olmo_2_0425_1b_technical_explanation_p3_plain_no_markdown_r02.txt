The conclusions and recommendations identified in this report that the NRC have articulated for the purposes of transmitting findings and recommendations to the President and Congress include:

  • The NRC believes that the budget and spending for the NRC should be increased to a level sufficient to fully fund the NRC's mission of safety, security, and reliability of nuclear power plants and facilities. The NRC should also be given the authority to operate as a separate, independent federal agency with its own budget and resources.
  • The NRC should be given the authority to close and shut down safety-critical facilities without delay, following measures to mitigate the impact on safety and public health, as well as the authority to clear nuclear power plants, facilities, and equipment that have not met the standards for safe operation of nuclear power plants, and to commission and license nuclear power plants and facilities that have not been adequately constructed and operated to meet safety requirements.
  • The NRC should be given the authority to issue licenses to existing nuclear power plants that meet the regulatory requirements and licensing standards established by the NRC and the Nuclear Regulatory Commission and that are not deemed by the NRC to be unsafe. The NRC should not be required to issue a license to a power plant or facility that is deemed by the NRC to be unsafe.

  • The NRC should be given the authority to shut down nuclear power plants and facilities that do not meet safety standards.
