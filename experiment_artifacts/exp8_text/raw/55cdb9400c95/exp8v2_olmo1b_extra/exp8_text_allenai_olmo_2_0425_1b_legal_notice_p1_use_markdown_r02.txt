Your notice should be at least 15 words long and no more than 50 words long. You may use headings, bullet points, and bold/italic text for emphasis. You may include details on the new service with pictures or other forms of illustration.

You must also:


Your notice must be in plain English and be understandable by the average reader. You cannot use business jargon, legal terms or acronyms. You can include a link to a legal service or a registered trademark of a governing authority in your notice.

Your notice must be clearly identified as an account change notice. It must not be any other type of notice, such as a cancellation notice, a service disruption notice, or a change notice for services that are desirable.

Give consideration to the following factors:

  • What service is being changed?
  • Why is it being changed?
  • How is it to be changed?
  • Who will benefit from the change?
  • Who will be affected by the change?
  • Are there any alternatives available?
  • What are the current service provisions?
  • If there are obligations under current provisions, are those obligations going to be affected by the change?
  • How will customers be notified of the change?
  • If there is a change that requires customer consent, what type of consent is required, and how is it to be obtained?
  • What factors are relevant to customer consent?
  • How will customers be informed of the change?
  • Who will be affected by the change?
  • If the change will affect rights and obligations, what is the effect on those rights and obligations?
  • If the change will affect fees payable by the customer, what will be the effect on those fees?
  • What is the current status of the change?
  • If the change requires a change order, who is required to supply the change order? How is it to be accomplished?
  • Will the change be done by an agent? If so, whom will the agent be?
  • Will the change be conducted by the customer? If so, who will the agent be?
  • If the change requires a change order, will the change order be done by the customer? If so, who will be the agent? How will the agent be chosen?
  • If the change requires a change order, what is the change order process? How is the change order to be accomplished?
  • If the change requires a change order, who is entitled to receive the change order? How is a change order to be accomplished?
  • If the change requires a change order, what is the process for getting a new change order? How is a change order to be accomplished?
  • If the change requires a change order, who is the person responsible for ensuring all terms and conditions of the new service are consistent with the old service? How is this responsibility to be carried out?
  • If the change requires a change order, what is the process for getting a new change order? How is the change order to be accomplished?
  • If the change requires a change order, who is responsible for ensuring the terms of the new service are consistent with the old service? How is this responsibility to be carried out?
