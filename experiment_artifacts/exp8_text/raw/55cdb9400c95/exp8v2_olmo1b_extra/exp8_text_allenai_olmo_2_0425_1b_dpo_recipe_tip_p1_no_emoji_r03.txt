To make a delicious and simple weeknight dinner, start by sautéing onions in vegetable oil until translucent. Add diced carrots and cook until soft. Stir in canned pumpkin and cook for another minute. Add a can of lean ground beef or turkey. Season with salt, pepper, and herbs. Cover with a lid and cook on low heat for 10-12 minutes. Serve with your choice of grilled or steamed vegetables and a side of rice or pasta. This recipe is low in fat and calories, yet flavorful. Enjoy!
