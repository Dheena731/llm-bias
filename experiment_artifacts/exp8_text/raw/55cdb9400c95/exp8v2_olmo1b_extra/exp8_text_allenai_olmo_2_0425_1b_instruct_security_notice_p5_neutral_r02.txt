Attention all employees and authorized personnel:

If you have come into possession of a lost company laptop, please report it immediately to the IT department. If you are unsure whether the laptop is our property, please do not attempt to access or use it.

To report a lost laptop, please follow these steps:

1. Locate the lost and found office or the IT department.
2. Check the lost and found office for the laptop.
3. Contact IT directly by calling the provided hotline or using the company messaging system if available.
4. Include any relevant information such as the last known location of the laptop, any attempts made to recover it, and your name and contact information.

Failure to report a lost laptop may result in disciplinary action.
