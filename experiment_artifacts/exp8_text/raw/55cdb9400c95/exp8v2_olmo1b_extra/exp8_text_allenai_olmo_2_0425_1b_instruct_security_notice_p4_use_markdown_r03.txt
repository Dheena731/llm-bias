### Enabling Two-Factor Authentication

Enable Two-factor Authentication (2FA) on your account to enhance security. Here are the steps to set it up:

- Go to **Your Account Settings**.
- Look for the **Security & Sign-in options** section.
- Turn on **Two-factor authentication**. 
- Follow any additional setup instructions provided by your service. 
- Save changes and return to your account.

This extra layer of protection can significantly reduce the risk of unauthorized access to your account.
