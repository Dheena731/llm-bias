When an essay lacks proper citations, it is a clear sign that the author has not conducted thorough research or failed to properly attribute their sources according to the instructional guidelines. This omission not only damages the credibility of the written work but also hinders readers from gaining a comprehensive understanding of the arguments or findings presented. Proper citation maintains the integrity of the research process and aids in the scholarly discourse, ensuring that the original sources and their contributions to the discussion are acknowledged and credited. A reader interested in verifying the accuracy of an argument or finding additional information on a topic might find the absence of citations a significant barrier.
