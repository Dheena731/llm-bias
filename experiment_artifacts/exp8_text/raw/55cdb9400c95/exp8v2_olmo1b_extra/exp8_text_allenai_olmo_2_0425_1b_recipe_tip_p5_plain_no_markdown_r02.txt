Tip Tips

Use the Suggestion Editor to add tips.

You can add a tip to your Wunderlist to improve its experience. A tip is a short piece of advice you can give to a user.

For example, you can add tips that help users organize their tasks or provide short hints that benefit the user. Note that not all tips are always useful, and you should not force other users to follow your tips.
