**Password Reuse: A Risk to Workplace Security**

- **What is Password Reuse?**
  - Reusing passwords across multiple accounts is insecure and can lead to unauthorized access.
  - Many security breaches are caused by compromised passwords used across various systems.

- **Why is Password Reuse Dangerous?**
  - Reused passwords can be intercepted by hackers during password sharing, phishing attempts, or breaches.
  - It increases the likelihood of significant data loss or identity theft.

- **How to Secure Your Information:**
  - Use a unique password for each account.
  - Secure your device with a strong passcode or biometric authentication.
  - Regularly update passwords to prevent compounding security risks.

- **Conclusion**
  - By avoiding password reuse, you protect yourself and your organization against potential cyber threats.

*Note:* This notice is intended to inform and encourage a secure password practice. If you have concerns about your organization's password policies, consult your IT department.
