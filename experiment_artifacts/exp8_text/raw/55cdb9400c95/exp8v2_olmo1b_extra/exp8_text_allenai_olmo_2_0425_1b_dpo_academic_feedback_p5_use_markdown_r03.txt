**Missing Citations**

*Not Properly Cited Sources: *Using someone else's ideas or data without proper attribution can weaken the credibility of your essay.*
*Failure to Include Author Information: *Without citing the original sources, readers may struggle to trace the ideas and data discussed.*  
*Poorly Structured Citations: *Inaccurate citation formats or missing pages can confuse readers and undermine the essay's validity.*

*Suggestions for Correction:*
*Ensure each source is fully referenced with appropriate details such as author, title, journal or book, volume number, issue number, and page range.*
*Use reliable citation styles like APA, MLA, or Chicago to maintain consistency in referencing.*

*Examples of a Corrected Citation:* 
- Smith, J., & Doe, A. (2021). The impact of climate change on biodiversity. Journal of Ecological Studies, 20(2), 117-136.
*Avoid summarizing sources without acknowledging their contribution.*

*Remember, clear citations not only guide readers to the original sources but also add scholarly rigor to your work.*
