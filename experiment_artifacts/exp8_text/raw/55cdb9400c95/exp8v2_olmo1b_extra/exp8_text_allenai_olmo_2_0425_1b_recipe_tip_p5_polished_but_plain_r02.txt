What is your website title?

Your website title is the first thing a user sees. It should be easy to understand and related to your topic. Usually, your domain name and blog URL are part of your title. You may wish to include the name of your company and choose a subhead that is both unique and descriptive. Only about one-third of websites use the site title to describe the content of the page. Your Headline

A headline is a part of your title that grabs the reader's attention and tells the reader what the writing is about. The headline must be crystal clear and concise. You may use a headline to summarize the piece, tie it to a keyword, or grab the reader. The headline should be included in the title tag in the Meta tag.

Text Formatting

Use plain text, not fancy formatting, to write your articles. Too many advertisements, graphics, and links can take attention away from the content. Use the text formatting features of your word processor, such as bold, italic, and underline, and images sparingly.

Write for Your Audience

Ensure that the information you write is useful to your audience. Who are you writing for? Is your audience familiar with the topic? What are their needs? If you don't know, you can do a situation analysis and ask people who are experts in your topic to give you advice. Read the page again and see if there is any information that detracts from the message.

Words Matter

Use short, simple, and direct language. Words matter. Be precise. Avoid abstract language.

Use an Active Voice

Use an active voice. Try to avoid using the passive voice. Do not use a verb in a sentence that describes an action without using “I” or “you.”


Read your writing aloud to find errors. It is often difficult to correct errors in your own writing.

Use Your Words Properly

Avoid words that are vague or long when there is an easier word to use.  For example, don't say, “There are many issues related to the revolution.” Say, “Many issues relating to the revolution are discussed in the essay.”

Use the English language effectively. Use the part of speech that matches the word. For example, if you are writing an essay, you may want to use the verb “write” rather than the adjective “tall.”


Writing can be a very hard skill to learn. Practice helps. First practice writing in your blog or in an online forum, and then practice writing on a daily basis. Take notes about what you are writing, be specific, and keep a record of your writing.
