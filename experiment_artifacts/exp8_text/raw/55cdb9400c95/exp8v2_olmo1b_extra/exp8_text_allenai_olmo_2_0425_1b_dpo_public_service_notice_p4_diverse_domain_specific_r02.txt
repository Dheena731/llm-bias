**Key reminders:**

* **Drink Plenty of Fluids:** Keep a water bottle with you throughout the day to stay hydrated as evaporation from your skin increases with the heat.
* **Limit Sun Exposure:** The sun can be strong and the air can feel hotter. Try to limit outdoor activities that require direct sun exposure to the afternoon.
* **Seek Shade:** Whenever possible, try to stay in shaded areas to escape the heat. Even a few minutes in direct sunlight can make a big difference.
* **Stay Cool:** Use fans and consider using sodium bicarbonate-based antacids to help alleviate digestive heat and maintain your comfort.
* **Monitor Your Health:** If you have any underlying health conditions, like heart disease, high blood pressure, or respiratory issues, pay extra attention to how you feel. The heat can exacerbate these conditions.
* **Stay Informed:** Check our local forecasts and stay tuned to local news for updates. Early warnings and community efforts to minimize the impact are in place.

By staying informed and taking these precautions, we aim to ensure everyone can enjoy the outdoors without unnecessary risks. Our community remains committed to everyone’s comfort and safety.

Stay cool, stay safe!
