Cooking rice that is less sticky involves a few key steps. First, use the right type of rice. Long-grain varieties like japonica or arborio are less prone to stick together after cooking than short-grain varieties. Second, start cooking the rice in slightly lesswater than the package instructions specify to ensure it doesn't get too mushy. Finally, stir the rice frequently during the cooking process to prevent it from sticking. For added convenience and texture, you might consider using a grain steamer, which forces the steam to rise and circulate through the grains, preventing them from clumping together as readily. This method is particularly effective for quick-cooking white rice, but the principle applies to all types of rice. Remember, patience is key when cooking rice. Don't overcook it, as this can lead to a starchy, mushy finish.
