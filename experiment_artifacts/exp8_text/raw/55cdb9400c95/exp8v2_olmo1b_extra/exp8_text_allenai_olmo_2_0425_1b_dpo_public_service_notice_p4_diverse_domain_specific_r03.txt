Attention residents and visitors:

Due to high temperatures set to persist into the weekend, a Heat Advisory has been issued for our area. Temperatures are projected to climb into the high 90s (°F) and possibly exceed 100° (°C) on Wednesday and Thursday. This heat wave may prove particularly challenging for older adults, children, and those with preexisting health conditions.

To ensure your safety and comfort, please take the following precautions:

1. Stay indoors whenever possible to avoid exposure to the sun and heat.
2. Adjust your daily schedule to avoid peak sun exposure times.
3. Monitor your body temperature and seek shade or air conditioning when you feel overheated.
4. Drink plenty of water to keep your body hydrated.
5. Use fans strategically to provide relief from the heat.

Remember to check the local weather updates and stay informed about any changes to the advisory. If you're going to be out in the sun, do so wearing sunscreen, protective clothing, and sunglasses to help safeguard against sunburn and dehydration.

Thank you for your attention to this important public safety alert. Please stay healthy and enjoy the outdoors as safely as possible. If you have any questions, please reach out to us at our customer service hotline or via social media.

Drive smart, stay safe!
