18.11.2017 Title page - page numbering and page orientation

All the pages should be numbered consecutively with Arabic numerals and the page orientation should be: Portrait, left-facing, horizontal.

18.11.2017 Title page

The title page should include the following:

The title of the work,

The name of the author,

A list of the works of the author.

A list of the translated works,

The title of the translation,

A list of the translated works,

The translator’s name,

A list of all the translators,

The name of the editor,

An acknowledgement to the editors,

The name of the publishing house,

The address of the publishing house, and

The date of the publication.

This is not an extensive list. In view of the small size of the pages, it should not exceed 30 lines.


18.11.2017 Editorial comments

Do not use words which are difficult to translate. In particular, avoid words which are difficult to translate into English. Do not use the English version of the word in the text. Instead, try to express the meaning of the word in the language of the document.

Do not use acronyms and abbreviations which may not be used in everyday speech. Do not use abbreviations in the text which are not acceptable in everyday speech.

Do not use the word “incorporated” in the text. Use the word “consisting of” instead.

Do not use the word “correspondence” in the text. Use the word “messages” instead.

Do not use the word “professional” in the text. Use the word “technical” instead.

18.11.2017 Information about web pages and documents

The material included in the pages should be released under the open license CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The pages should be published so that anyone can use them for free, without any restrictions.

The pages should be published free from any form of discrimination.

Federal Law “On the Protection of the Privacy and the Information and the Public Access to the State Information”.
