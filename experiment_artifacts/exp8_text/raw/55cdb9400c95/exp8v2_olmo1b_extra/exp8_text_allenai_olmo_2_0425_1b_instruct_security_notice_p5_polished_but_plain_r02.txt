Here is a brief guide for reporting a lost company laptop.

If you've lost your company laptop, please do the following:

- Locate the lost laptop and identify any personal data it may have collected.
- Contact your IT department to report the loss as soon as possible. Explain that the laptop has been lost and provide them with the location details.
- Back up any sensitive data on the laptop and ensure that it is secured in case of a data recovery effort.
- Follow the IT department's instructions for recovering any information lost from the laptop.
- Monitor for any signs of unauthorized access or use of the data.
- Document any actions taken and any contacts made.

Remember: the sooner you report the loss of the laptop, the sooner your IT department can begin the recovery process. Protect your personal information and your company's data by taking these steps.
