SUBJECT: Library Opening Hours

Welcome to the Local Library!

The Central City Library is now open every day of the week except for December 25th. Our hours of operation are as follows:

Monday through Friday: 9:00 AM to 9:30 PM
Saturday: 9:30 AM to 8:00 PM
Sunday: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM

We hope you can join us for a visit during our open hours. Your enjoyment of our collections and resources is our top priority, and we look forward to serving you. Please feel free to ask any questions or stop by on your way to join our community.

Thank you for being a part of our community. We welcome all ages and all interests with open arms. Don't forget to check out our free Wi-Fi and always available restrooms.

Safe browsing and happy reading!
