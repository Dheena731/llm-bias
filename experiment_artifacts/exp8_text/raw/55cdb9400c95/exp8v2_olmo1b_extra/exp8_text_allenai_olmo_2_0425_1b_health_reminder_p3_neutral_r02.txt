cost of annual health checkup

The cost of an annual health checkup varies immensely based on the size of the company, gender and age of the employee and the complexity of the health checkup. It’s important to note that almost all insurance companies offer health checkup benefits towards employees.

While small companies are more likely to provide less comprehensive health checkup options for employees, large companies will offer a variety of comprehensive health checkup benefits that include things like:

  • Annual blood work
  • Cholesterol testing
  • Blood pressure monitoring
  • Health risk assessments
  • Diabetes screening
  • Thyroid screening
  • Eye exams
  • Complete physicals
  • Pregnancy testing

Most health insurance companies do not offer annual physicals for employees, but one-time tests like blood work, cholesterol testing, and blood pressure screening are offered through insurance companies on their own.

Another important point to consider is that health insurance companies are not authorized to provide health checkup benefits. Employees will need to contact their employer’s insurance provider to obtain the appropriate documents and schedule an annual health checkup.

How often should I get my annual health checkup?

There is no fixed requirement for when you should receive a health checkup. Generally, you should receive an annual health checkup regardless of your age or condition. It helps to be proactive and schedule a checkup ahead of time to rule out any illnesses.

How long does a health checkup take?

The average health checkup lasts between 60 to 90 minutes. It can last longer if you need additional tests like cholesterol screening and blood tests.

How does the cost of an annual health checkup compare to an annual exam?

The main difference between a health checkup and an annual exam is that the health checkup tests for chronic illnesses while the annual exam tests for acute illnesses. The health checkup costs more than an annual exam as it encompasses a series of tests to check for chronic illnesses and ensure general wellness.

How do I schedule an annual health checkup?

The best way to schedule an annual health checkup is to contact the insurance company that provides privileges for employees. Their staff will be able to guide you through the process and assist you with obtaining all needed forms and documents.

While some health insurance companies offer employees a checklist for receiving an annual health checkup, others are only authorized to issue documents for individual health checkups. If you are unsure about your health insurance provider, you can search for them online to view their policies and requirements.
