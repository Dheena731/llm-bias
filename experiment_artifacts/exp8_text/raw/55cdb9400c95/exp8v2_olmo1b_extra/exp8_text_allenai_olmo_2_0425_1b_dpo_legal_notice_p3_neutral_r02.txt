**What Are Cookies?**

Cookies are small pieces of data sent from our website and stored on your device (e.g., your computer, tablet, or mobile phone) while you visit us or browse our site.

The primary purpose of cookies on our website is to provide you with a better experience. **Types of Cookies Used on Our Website**

- **Session Cookies:** These are temporary cookies used to allow you to navigate our site and use its features effectively during your visit. They are deleted when you close your browser.

- **Persistent Cookies:** These cookies remain on your device even after your session ends, and they can be used to store information such as your preferred language or preferred payment method.
