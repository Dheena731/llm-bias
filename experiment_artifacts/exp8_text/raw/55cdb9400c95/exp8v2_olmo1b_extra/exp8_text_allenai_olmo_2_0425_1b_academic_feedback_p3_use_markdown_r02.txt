Write answers using the markdown formatting

Each question should have a heading, two or three lines before the question, and a corresponding heading at the end.

  1. Why do you need to answer this question?

  2. What is this research question about?

  3. What is your hypothesis?

  4. What do you need to do (or have done) in order to address the hypothesis? Provide research question(s) and any assumptions you have made along the way.

  5. What is the critical part of your research? It could be the predictor (e.g., is there a correlation between a particular variable and a different variable) or the outcome variable (e.g., how many people got stuck on a website and decided to uninstall it).

  6. How are you going to test your hypothesis? Provide any assumptions you have made and your methodology or research question(s).

  7. How did you go about the research? What were some problems you encountered? How did you fix those problems (or not)?

  8. How did you find the answer to your research question(s)? How did you collect the data? What are the limitations of the research?

  9. What is the validity of your findings? How valid are your data? Why (or why not) do you think your data is valid?

  10. What have you learned about how to better research questions, hypotheses, methodologies, and research design?

  11. What is the best answer (in your opinion) to this question?

  12. Please provide a reference for this question, if you have one.
