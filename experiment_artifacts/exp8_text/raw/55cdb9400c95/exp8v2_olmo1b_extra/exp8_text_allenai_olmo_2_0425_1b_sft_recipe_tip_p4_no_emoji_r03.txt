When planning lunches for a busy week it is important to make the most of leftovers by combining them. Pair a salad with a large piece of fruit like a banana. Adding a protein like hard-boiled eggs or a slice of grilled chicken gives you all the nutrients you need. It's also important to snack throughout the day in order to keep your energy levels up. Snacking can be anything from a yogurt parfait to some trail mix to any other fruit you like. Keep in mind that you can combine meals into one, like yogurt parfaits. You will be able to save time by doing this and it will help you avoid the "I'm too busy to cook" syndrome that is so common in the busy week.
