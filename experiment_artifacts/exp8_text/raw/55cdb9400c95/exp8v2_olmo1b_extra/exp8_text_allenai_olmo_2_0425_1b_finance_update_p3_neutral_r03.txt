Being able to track your progress towards a savings target gives you a powerful tool to stay on top of your finances and build real wealth. That's why we integrated this new feature into the My Money & Wallet platform.

What it will look like: Your savings goal summary will show you the expected amount you'll need to save, the amount saved toward the target, and how long it will take to reach it. The goal summary also gives you an overview of your spending habits and gives you an idea of how much you can save by reducing your spending.

Why you need it: Without tracking your progress toward your savings goal, you might not be motivated to spend less or save more. You might end up missing the amount you need to save or it might be too late to meet your savings target. This custom dashboard will show you in great detail how much you need to save and how long it will take you to reach your goal.

See for yourself: Try this example of a savings goal and our other features on My Money & Wallet.

How it works

  1. Go to My Money & Wallet
  2. Add a savings goal
  3. Enter how long you expect to save.

The My Money & Wallet platform lets you track your progress toward any savings goal in just a few clicks.
