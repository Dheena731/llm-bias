**Phishing Emails Notice**

*Attention all employees:*
We have received reports of suspicious phishing emails being sent to our workplace addresses. These emails aim to deceive users into divulging sensitive information such as usernames, passwords, and financial details.

*How to handle phishing attempts:*
- DO NOT CLICK ON ANY LINKS IN THE EMAIL.
- REPORT THE EMAIL TO OUR GRC UNIT AT ext. 1234 IMMEDIATELY.
- REVIEW YOUR EMAIL ACCESS SETTINGS TO ENSURE FICTIONAL EMAILS ARE NOT SET UP FOR YOUR ACCESS.

*Reporting instructions:*
- Fill out the GRC report form located at https://secure.myorganisations.com/report-phishing
- Attach the suspicious email for investigation

*For further assistance:*
- Contact our IT support team: support@ourcompany.com

By staying vigilant and following these steps, we can protect our workplace from potential cyber threats. Thank you for your attention to this important issue.
