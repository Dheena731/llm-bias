# Short Tip for Storing Fresh Herbs

* Use an airtight container to keep herbs dry and fresh for up to a week. 
* Store in a cool, dry place, like a cabinets or a pantry. 
* Avoid direct sunlight and high humidity. 
* Trim and wash herbs just before using them to keep their flavor strong. 
* Rotate containers regularly to expose different parts of the plant to air circulation.
