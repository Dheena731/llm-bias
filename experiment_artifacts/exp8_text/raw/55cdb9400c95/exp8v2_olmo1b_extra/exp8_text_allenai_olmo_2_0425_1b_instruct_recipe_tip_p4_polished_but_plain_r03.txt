Plan ahead for lunches and fend off the busy week blues. Start by identifying the days you'll likely have the most work hours and which meals are your go-to. Create a week's worth of meal plans with staples on hand  so you'll be ready to grab something on the fly. Set clear goals for leftovers and quick fix-ins. Remember to include options for everyone in your household, ensuring you're covering dietary preferences and restrictions. Pack lunches the night before using a reusable container or lunch bag to avoid last-minute scramble. Choose meals that require minimal prep time or take multitasking meals to the next level  such as prepping salads in advance or assembling sandwiches the night before. Finally, consider the elements that can add flavor and interest without much effort  such as adding fresh herbs, nuts, or a twist of lemon or lime. Your well-thought-out lunches will keep you energized and satisfied throughout the week.
