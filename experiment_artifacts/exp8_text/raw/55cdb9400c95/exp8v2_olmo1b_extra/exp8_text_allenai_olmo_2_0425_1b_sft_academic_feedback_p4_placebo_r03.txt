I would like to provide some feedback on the draft poster for the conference. First, the title is not engaging and can be made more exciting by including action verbs such as "revolutionizing" or "disrupting". Second, the layout is clean and professional, which is good. However, the font used for the text is too small, which would be hard for attendees to read. I recommend using a larger font or perhaps adding some white space. Finally, the colors are a bit too similar and could be contrasted more. It would be beneficial to use a different background color. Overall, it seems like an interesting concept and could be improved with some minor adjustments.
