Write to your intended audience at the right level. If you are writing for a 10-year old, write the way you would write to a 10-year old. If you are writing for an executive, write the way you would write to an executive. If you would like to explain something to a general audience, use an explanatory tone.

Use the right word

Use the right word and make the sentence precise. The wrong word can make it difficult to understand your ideas, and the right word can make your ideas more clear and precise.

Use the right language

Use words that are precise and clear, and are familiar to the reader.

Do not be afraid to use technical jargon. Many technical terms are technical because people from different backgrounds know different words for the same thing.
