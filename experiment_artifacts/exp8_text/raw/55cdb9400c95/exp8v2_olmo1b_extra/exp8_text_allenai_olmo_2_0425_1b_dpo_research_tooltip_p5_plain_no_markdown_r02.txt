To export query results, first ensure your data is in a format compatible with your export preference. For many databases and query tools, you can right-click on the data you wish to export and select "Export." This action usually involves choosing an output format (like CSV, Excel, JSON, or a PDF) and optionally specifying the export location. Follow these steps:

1. Identify the data you wish to export.
2. Right-click the data on the desired spreadsheet or database panel.
3. Look for an option labeled "Export," "Export Data," "Export Results," or a similar name.
4. When available, specify the export format (e.g., CSV, Excel, XML).
5. Choose an output location for the exported data (e.g., your desktop, a cloud storage service, or another database).
6. Click "Export" to finalize the process.

After exporting, you can now enjoy your query results locally or in a different database. This process provides a convenient way to handle large amounts of data and share it with others.
