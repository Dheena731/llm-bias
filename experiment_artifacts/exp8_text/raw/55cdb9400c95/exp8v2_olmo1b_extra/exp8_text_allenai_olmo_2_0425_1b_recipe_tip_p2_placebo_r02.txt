Don’t be tempted to use the storyline as a starter for your business plan. The story should be the conclusion of your plan.

I didn’t do it. But I found a linguistic pattern that has made me an expert in writing short tips.



Short tips usually use a simple sequence of words. Most of the time, they are made up of just four words. Surely, the title of the short tip is the only word that belongs to a list of four.

The pattern used for making short tips helps me to recognize the pattern and to write better short tips and also to write better tips in general. When I took the training, I was interrupted a few times, and when I returned to the training, I wrote down the tips I remembered. The training was organized into three parts, and whenever I remembered a tip from one of the two parts, I wrote it down. Two weeks later, I finished writing down every single tip I remembered. I was surprised by the fact that I had remembered every single tip taught in the training.

I took the list of tips and looked for similarities. Two things immediately emerged. First, there were a lot of words starting with the letter “e”. Second, the second and the fourth word in the sequence were always the same word. I had found a pattern!

I then tried to focus on the second and the fourth word in the sequence. The second word was “organization”, and the fourth was “do”.

My tips are now much better structured than they were before. They are short, to the point and they are easy to remember.

Here is an example of a short tip that uses the pattern I discovered.

Make your life easier. Take some time to find a good organization to reduce the number of decisions you have to make.

That’s it. The tip is so short that it does not need an introduction or a conclusion. Sentence 1 tells why we should make our life easier. Sentence 2 tells how to do that. Sentence 3 provides the example of an organization that could help us.

I am sure that there are millions of tips that use the same pattern. Why not think about your tips and see whether you can find a pattern that helps you to make your tips better structured?

When we use the same pattern for our tips, we recognize the patterns in our tips and can discover a new pattern. I hope you like our trick!
