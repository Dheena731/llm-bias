This legal notice explains how we use cookies on our website. By using our website, you agree to our cookie usage policy. A cookie is a small piece of data sent from a website and stored on your device by your browser while you are browsing the site. There are two types of cookies we use: 

1. **Session Cookies**: These cookies are used solely for the duration of your visit to our website so that your actions while browsing remain aggregated and do not identify you personally. They do not collect information that could be used for marketing purposes.

2. **Permanent Cookies**: These cookies are stored on your device and remain until they expire or are deleted by you. They allow us to remember information about your visits, such as your preferred language or location, and enable personalized content, such as recommended or customized pages.

We only use cookies that are essential to the operation of our website and do not collect information that could be used to track your browsing habits or identify you personally. If you choose to disable cookies, you may encounter limitations in the functionality of some of our features and services.

To manage cookies on our site, you can adjust your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to set specific conditions under which cookies are enabled, such as only accepting them from specific domains, or blocking all cookies.
