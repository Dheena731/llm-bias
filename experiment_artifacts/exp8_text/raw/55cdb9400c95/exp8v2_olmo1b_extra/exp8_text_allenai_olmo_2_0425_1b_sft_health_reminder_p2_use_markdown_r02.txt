# Stay Hydrated with These Simple Tips

- Carry a reusable water bottle throughout the day to stay hydrated.
- Make water your go-to beverage, not just when you're thirsty.
- Drink at least 8 glasses of water a day, but aim for 8 ounces of water per every 20 pounds of body weight.
- Drink water before meals and between meals to reduce hunger and keep you feeling full.
- Plan your day around drinking water, such as after waking up, after lunch, and before bed.
- Use a timer to remind yourself to take breaks to consume water throughout your day.
- Carry reusable water bottles to minimize plastic waste and to have a constant reminder to drink water.
- Consider the quality and source of your water to ensure it's safe and healthy to drink.
