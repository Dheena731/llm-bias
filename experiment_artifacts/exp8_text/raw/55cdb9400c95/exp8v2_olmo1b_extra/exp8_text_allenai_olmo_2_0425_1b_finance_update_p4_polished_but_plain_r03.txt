Do not write in the second person ("you") or third person ("he/she"), but use "I", "I have", "I have done", etc., when appropriate.

Avoid unnecessary qualifiers such as "well", "very", "really", etc. Use simple, clear statements.

Unless you are a tax expert, avoid technical or legal language.

Expected length

The statement should be 200-400 words, with less than 200 optional. If you are receiving a tax statement, it will likely be longer.

Writing style

When you are writing a statement, it should generally be in the first-person. (e.g. "I had $10.11 last year" instead of "You had $10.11 last year".)

Write in a clear, conversational tone, using simple language.

Write sentences that are short but clear.
