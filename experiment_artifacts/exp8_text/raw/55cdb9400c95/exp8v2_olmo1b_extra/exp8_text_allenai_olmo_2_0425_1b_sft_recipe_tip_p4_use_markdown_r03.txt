# Tips for Planning Lunches 

- **Plan Ahead:** Decide on a rotation of meals and snacks to save time. For example, have a base meal like a salad with a variety of components in it and then add different toppings or dressings each day.
- **Make it Easy:** If you're busy, a simple meal that doesn't require a lot of prep work is best. Prepare a base of legumes or grains like brown rice, quinoa, or pasta, then add protein and vegetables. 
- **Stock Your Pantry:** Keep a few staples on hand and make a meal plan that involves them. 
- **Plan Your Week:** Every week, think through your grocery shopping and make a plan that you can stick to. 

This is going to help you plan your lunches for the week.
