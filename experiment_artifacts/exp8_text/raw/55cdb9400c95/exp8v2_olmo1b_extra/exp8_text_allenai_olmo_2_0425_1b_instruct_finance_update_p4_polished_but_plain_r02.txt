Happy New Year!

Here is an explanation of your end-of-year tax statement.

At the end of the year, you owe money if you have more taxes owed than you've been able to pay during the year. When you file your tax return, you can see how much you owe or are owed from the prior year. 

If you have a balance that remains, you'll be notified when you file. The notice tells you the amount owed and explains why you owe. Information about any previous years' balances and any open tax disputes will also be included. If you disagree with the amount or notice, you can request an explanation or appeal the decision.

The tax return also tells you how much you've paid and by when. This can help you plan how to manage your finances coming into the new year.

Remember to check your statements each year to keep everything organized and to avoid any surprise if tax obligations arise.
