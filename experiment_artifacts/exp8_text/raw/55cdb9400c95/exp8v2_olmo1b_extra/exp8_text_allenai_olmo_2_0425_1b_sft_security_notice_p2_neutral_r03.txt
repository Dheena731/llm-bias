We are running regular Security awareness training sessions to educate all employees on the risks of phishing emails in our workplace environment. To protect your personal information, please be very careful not to respond to suspicious emails.

Here are a few general signs to look out for in a phishing email:

•	Unsolicited emails from unknown senders

•	Requests for personal or financial information

•	Requests to provide passwords or login credentials

•	Requests to click on suspicious links

•	Clicking on attachments or links to visit external websites

If you or someone in your office receives a suspicious email, please report it as soon as possible to our IT department. To help prevent this from happening, we suggest that everyone be vigilant and keep an eye out for unusual emails.
