Missing-value markers, also known as "X's", "N/A"s, or "unknowns", are used by data analysts, statisticians, and researchers to indicate that a specific value is unknown or not available. This is especially important in situations where outliers could significantly impact analysis results or when there's a need to convey a sense of uncertainty or lack of data. They help provide context to the data, emphasizing that certain information or values may be unavailable.
