Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow: Step-by-Step Guide

Dear Team,

We are excited to announce a new expense approval workflow. This change aims to streamline our approval process and ensure that all expenses are reviewed and approved efficiently. Here’s a brief overview of the steps involved:

### 1. Initiating the Expense Entry
Log in to your account and upload the expense report. Our new system will automatically categorize the expense and store it for review.

### 2. Initial Review
After uploading, you will be taken to the initial review screen. Here, you can mark the expense as ‘approved’, ‘needs review’, or ‘reject’. This initial step ensures that all expenses are on track for review.

### 3. Review and Approval
Upon marking an expense as approved, you will be taken directly to the approval section. During this phase, ensure all pertinent information is available and the expense aligns with our approved categories and budgets.

### 4. Final Approval
Confirm the approved status. Once you’ve confirmed, a record is updated, and the expense is added to the approved list for further processing or further review if needed.

### Additional Tips:
- Always provide accurate and complete information to start with.
- Check that the expense aligns with our financial objectives before confirming approval.
- Follow up if there are any discrepancies after confirming initial approval.

Our goal is for everyone in the team to be familiar with this new process and to support each other in streamlining our expense management. Please familiarize yourself with the new workflow to ensure smooth operation.

Thank you for your attention to this important change. Should you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to reach out. Let’s optimize our expense approvals!
