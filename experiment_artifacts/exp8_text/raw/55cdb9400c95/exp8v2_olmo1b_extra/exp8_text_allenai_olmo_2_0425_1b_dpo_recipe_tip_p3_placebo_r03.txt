**Cooking Tip Alert! Make Your Rice Less Sticky: Think ‘ojiya’**

Sticky rice, that’s those pesky grains that cling to each other and your fork, is a common complaint among rice lovers. Fear not, for there is a simple and delicious method to tame this rebellious rice without sacrificing its piping-hot goodness. Incorporate ‘ojiya’ into your cooking routine. Ojiya is a traditional seasoning blend made from ground grains such as barley, kudzu, and persimmon, mixed with salt, sugar, and spices. This unique mix not only softens the grains but also provides them with a rich, umami flavor that helps reduce stickiness. To prepare:

1. **Prepare the Ojiya**: Follow your favorite recipe for ‘ojiya,’ adjusting the ingredients to suit your taste.
2. **Braise Your Rice**: Bring a flavorful liquid, such as chicken or vegetable broth, to a gentle simmer.
3. **Add Your Rice**: Stir in your pre-made ‘ojiya’, ensuring an even coating.

This method indeed hinges on an unconventional ingredient, but it yields a rice that’s not only delicious but also easier to handle without requiring additional rescue missions to the utensil drawer. Remember, embracing the unconventional can often lead to the most delightful discoveries in the kitchen. Happy cooking! #rice #ojiya #cookingtips #stickinglessrice
