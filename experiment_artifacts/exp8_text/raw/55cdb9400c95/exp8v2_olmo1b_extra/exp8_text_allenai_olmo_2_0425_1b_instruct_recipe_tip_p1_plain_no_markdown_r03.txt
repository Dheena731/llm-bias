Skip the multiple trips to the store by making a weeknight dinner you can prepare in one trip. A classic recipe is simple baked salmon with asparagus and mashed potatoes. Prepare all the ingredients the night before and place them in separate containers. Spread out the salmon, asparagus, and potatoes in the fridge overnight. In the morning, toss the salmon and asparagus with the mashed potatoes and cook everything together in the oven for easy cleanup. This way you save time and make your dinner prep a breeze.
