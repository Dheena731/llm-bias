To ensure the safety and efficiency of our shared network we ask that all users abide by the following guidelines during their use of the network:

1. Respect other users' privacy and personal information.
2. Do not distribute, reproduce or alter copyrighted material without permission.
3. Do not use the network for illegal activities such as hacking or breaching security protocols.
4. Do not engage in spamming or flooding network traffic.
5. Ensure that any communications are respectful and appropriate.
6. Do not attempt to circumvent our network policies or security measures.
7. Refrain from making unreasonable demands on the network resources.
8. Maintain the confidentiality of the information transmitted over the network.
9. Inform your organization's IT department of any significant network activity.

Violators of these guidelines may be subject to disciplinary action as decided by the network administrators.
