3)    The Problem of E-Mail

Since the 1980s, the internet has come to be associated with electronic mail, or "e-mail." E-mail is a common way to communicate with all types of people. But e-mail has a problem. It is automatic; nobody oversees what you send. E-mail can be used for good and bad by those who have power over the people addressed. You don't know who you are really talking to. E-mail is not reliable. If you accidentally hit the wrong button, you may be sent a message that says "you are an idiot."

4)    The Problem of Electronic Blueprints

A blue print is a plan that describes how to make something. Blueprints are important in law, architecture, and construction. Most blueprints are written down. But how can someone write down an idea? Many ideas cannot be written down. Some things are not meant to be written down. An idea, like "build a house" could be written down in a lot of ways. For example: "a house is on fire, go out and put it out." But even when a blueprint is written down, that blueprint can be changed. For example: a blueprint can go through many versions of the same house, each with different details, before it is built. But, if changes are made to the blueprint, that change can be made everywhere at the same time. If the change is big enough, it might change the design of the whole house. The change might even do something wrong; for example, it might make the house collapse. We need to decide when we should stop using a blueprint. Is it okay to change it when it's needed? Or, is it better to stop using the blueprint when we see a problem?

5)    The Problem of Free Speech on the Web

A lot of the things you say on the internet are not as free as they seem. Because the internet is so easy to use, it is very popular. Lots of people are using it for anything from gossip to politics. There is a lot of freedom for people to express themselves on the internet. But it is also possible to use the internet to hurt other people. Just as we shouldn't hurt people in the real world, we shouldn't hurt people online. But, some people do things on the internet that are harmful or dangerous to other people. Governments and companies are worried about this. They want to keep people from hurting other people online. Some people want to put laws on the internet that will make it illegal to use the internet in a harmful way. Others think that laws that make it illegal to use the internet in a harmful way will only hurt people who are interested in using the internet to hurt people.
