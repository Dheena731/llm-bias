When using a date-range selector, this tool tip explains how to use it: Simply click and drag your mouse across the date range you want to choose from. The dates will appear highlighted in the selected area. Start your selection by dragging your cursor towards the earlier date, and the date range will shrink. End your selection by dragging towards the later date, and the range will expand. This way, you can quickly pick a specific period between two dates, whether it’s one day or a full month.
