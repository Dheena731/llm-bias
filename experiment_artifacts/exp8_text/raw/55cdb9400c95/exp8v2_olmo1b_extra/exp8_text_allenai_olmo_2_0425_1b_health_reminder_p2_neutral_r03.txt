7.       Reduce your stress


8.       Don’t forget to exercise

Regular physical activity is a great way to improve your physical and mental wellbeing. It is easy to incorporate a new kind of activity into your life, e.g. walking, swimming or cycling. The best thing about physical activity is that it can be done at any time during the day – when you have free time, or when you are at work or in a meeting.


9.       Stay healthy

Stay healthy and well by having a balanced diet and practicing healthy habits. Visit your doctor if you have a problem or if you are not feeling well.

10.   Give time for socializing

Social interaction is very important for our well-being. It makes us feel less lonely, more optimistic and happier.

If you are having a hard time with socializing, you can also try to find a social group that goes for a run or does yoga together.
