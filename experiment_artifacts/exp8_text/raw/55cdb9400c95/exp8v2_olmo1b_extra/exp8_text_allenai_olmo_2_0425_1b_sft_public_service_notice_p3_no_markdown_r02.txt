Attention all residents,

We are pleased to inform you of a few changes to our recycling collection schedule. Effective immediately, recycling will be collected every Thursday from 8 AM to 8 PM. Please remember to place your recyclables in their designated bins.

Additionally, we are expanding the range of materials that can be recycled. This includes glass bottles and jars, plastic bottles and jugs, paper products, and metal cans and foil. We encourage you to continue to recycle items in their designated bins to help maintain a clean and eco-friendly community.

Please keep your recycling bins clean to prevent contamination and to ensure an efficient collection process. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact our office at 555-555-5555. Happy recycling!

Sincerely,
The Recycling Committee
