Citations are essential for academic integrity and for providing proper credit to the original sources quoted in an essay. When they are absent, a lack of transparency can undermine the credibility of the argument and lead to potential academic penalties. Inefficiently citing sources not only hobbles the quality of the work but also obstructs the reader’s ability to follow and appreciate the logical progression and reliability of the argument being made. Always make sure all necessary citations are included with each pertinent reference to support your points and maintain a solid scholarly foundation for your essay.
