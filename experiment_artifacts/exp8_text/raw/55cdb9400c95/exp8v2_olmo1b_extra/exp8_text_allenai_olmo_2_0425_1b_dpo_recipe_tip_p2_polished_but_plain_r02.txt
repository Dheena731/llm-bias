Store fresh herbs in an airtight container and preferably in the refrigerator's crisper drawer. Give them a light dusting of dried herbs like oregano or basil monthly to keep them fresh. To further extend their freshness, trim the stems and place them directly into a solvent like olive oil or a herb-infused water. Cover loosely with plastic wrap for air circulation or put in a zip-top bag if you have one, squeezing out as much air as possible to minimize moisture exposure. Choose herbs such as parsley, chives, cilantro, and mint that are most prone to wilting and store them closer to the surface for better air circulation. If your climate is especially warm, alternate placing the container in and out of the refrigerator to mimic the herbs' natural growing conditions. Always remember to check and use your herbs as soon as possible after bringing them home to enjoy them at their freshest.
