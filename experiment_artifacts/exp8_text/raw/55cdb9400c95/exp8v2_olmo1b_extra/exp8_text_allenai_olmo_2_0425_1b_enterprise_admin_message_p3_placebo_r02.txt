Since time was of the essence, the engineer had to make a hard decision: which part of the network would have to be bypassed in order to keep the VPN connection intact? He knew that he could not make that decision in time and that his team would have to rush back to the office and start fixing it. The problem was so complex that he knew his team would need help from the development office. The engineer hesitated for a moment longer, but then he made a final decision. He promptly e-mailed his boss and said, “There is a firewall between us. I need you to connect me to the network that I need to bypass. We have to connect me to the corporate network on 10.0.1.255.” Then he destroyed the encrypted connection.
