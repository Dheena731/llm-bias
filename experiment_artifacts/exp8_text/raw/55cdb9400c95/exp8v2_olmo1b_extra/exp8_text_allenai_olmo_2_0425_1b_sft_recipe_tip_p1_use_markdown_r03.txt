**Short & Simple Cooking Tip: The Perfect Weeknight Pasta**

- Start with a simple marinara sauce and bring it to a light sauté, then toss with cooked pasta.
- Add fresh herbs, garlic, and basil for a delicious and vibrant dish.
- Serve with your choice of protein, like grilled chicken or turkey meatballs.
- A sprinkle of Parmesan cheese on top adds extra flavor and texture.
