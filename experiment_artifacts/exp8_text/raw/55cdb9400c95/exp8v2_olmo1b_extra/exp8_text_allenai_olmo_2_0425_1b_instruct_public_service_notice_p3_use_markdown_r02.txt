**Recycling Collection Changes**

- **Starting November 1, 2023:** The recycling collection schedule will be altered to include Monday collections only, with every fourth week having a recycling pick-up day.
- **New Collection Locations:** Several community recycling locations will be moved to new locations to ensure easy access for residents.
- **Frequency of Collection:** Recycling boxes will be collected once a week, except every fourth week, reducing the weekly collection frequency to lessen load on recycling facilities.
- **Tips for Residents:** Ensure your recyclable items are sorted properly and include labels for plastics, glass, paper, and metals for accurate collection.
- **Contact Information:** For further details and answers to questions, visit the [Town Recycling webpage](https://www.town.gov/recycling) for all updates and guidance.
