PlaceboTip: Surprise your family and friends with a healthy and delicious weeknight easy cook that requires minimal investment of time and effort. 
Start off by chopping some fresh herbs such as basil, parsley, or oregano and tossing them into a bowl of homemade pesto sauce or a scorched bread marinade. This impromptu herb addition will elevate the taste and color of your main dish. For the protein, choose a versatile cut like chicken breast or a simple flank steak and marinate it in a mix of fresh lemon juice, garlic, dried herbs, and a touch of olive oil. 
To complete the meal, roast the marinated protein to perfection and serve with a side of sautéed mixed greens tossed with a dressing (apple cider vinegar, Dijon mustard, honey, and a hint of salt and pepper) for that refreshing, tangy kick. This nutritious and satisfying dish works wonders for a rushed weeknight dinner, packed with flavor and goodness.
