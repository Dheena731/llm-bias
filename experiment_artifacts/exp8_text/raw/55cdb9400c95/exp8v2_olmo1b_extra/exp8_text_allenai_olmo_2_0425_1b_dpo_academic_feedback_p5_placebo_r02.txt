Missing citations in an essay can severely undermine the credibility and authority of the content. When readers delve into an argument or analysis supported by scant evidence or no verifiable sources, they lose confidence in the author's claims and the overall robustness of the argument. Proper citation ensures that readers can easily locate the original references, allowing them to explore the source material further, question the analysis, or even corroborate it. The absence of citations also hinders the discourse by preventing comprehensive engagement with the literature on which the essay relies. Thus, authors must rigorously cite their sources, ensuring that their work is not only academically sound but also accessible for others to scrutinize and build upon. It is a fundamental aspect of academic integrity and responsible communication.
