Missing citations in an essay can make it difficult for readers to evaluate the credibility and source of the author's claims. It can also undermine the author's authority and detract from the overall strength of the argument. Proper citation not only acknowledges the original authors but also allows readers to verify the information, understand the context, and explore further references if needed. It's important, especially in academic or research contexts, to adhere to the relevant citation styles (e.g., APA, MLA) to ensure clarity and academic integrity.
