Enabling two-factor authentication is a crucial step to enhance the security of your online accounts. It adds an extra layer of security by requiring not just your password, but also something that only you can know and have on hand at the time of login. This could be your phone, a trusted device, or a piece of hardware that you have physically close to you.

Some of the benefits of two-factor authentication are:

1. Reduces the risk of account hacking. 
2. Prevents unauthorized access even if your password is compromised.
3. Increases the overall security of your online accounts.

If you haven't already, consider enabling two-factor authentication for your online accounts.
