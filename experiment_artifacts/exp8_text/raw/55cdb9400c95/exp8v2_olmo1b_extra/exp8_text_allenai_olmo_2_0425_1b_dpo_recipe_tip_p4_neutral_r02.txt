Pick a few go-to lunch recipes that require minimal prep and cook time. A few examples of such recipes include turkey or ham sandwiches, wrap wraps, and quinoa salads. These meals can be prepped ahead of time or slightly changed to suit your day. Pack food in reusable containers that can double as lunch bags, and remember to keep a variety to avoid monotony. Also, check expiration dates and ensure you have snacks or drinks for breaks if your meetings are longer than usual. Happy lunch-planning!
