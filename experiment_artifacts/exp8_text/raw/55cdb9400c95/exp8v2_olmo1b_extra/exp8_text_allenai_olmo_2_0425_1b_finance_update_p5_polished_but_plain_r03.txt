Write with complete sentences and simple grammar. Your work should be easy for humans to understand, but not too easy for computers.

Use the following formatting rules:

  • Block quotes: En dashes: En dashes are often used to indicate ranges of numbers or dates. For example, a range of dates in a text would be written as “November 2018–September 2019”. When using two or more en dashes in a row, use a single hyphen in HTML. For example: November–December 2018–2019.
  • Italicize titles: Titles in italics are sometimes used to emphasize important words or phrases in a title. For example, “Fitting the Siren’s Call” is written in italics. In HTML, capitalize the first word of each sentence.
  • Enlarge table: Enlarge the table by writing “table” and copying a meta-data code from the database. For example, “table” and html table.
  • Grouping: Use brackets to group data into small groups. For example, write 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.4, and 1.5 in brackets next to each other to group all the numbers.
  • Random numbers: Write two numbers in brackets in the same line, separated by a dash.
  • Using a number or date: Use three numbers in the same line, separated by commas.
  • Data: Write two numbers in the same line, separated by a comma.

Content and Citations

Content: Write the content in the form of an abstract. This abstract will be displayed in a box to attract readers and provide a brief summary of your research. You should give a short summary of your paper in one paragraph, 200–400 words.

Citations: Write down the references you used as evidence in a box. Use a prefix between the prefix of the journal and the reference number. For example, https://www.ebscohost.com/oxfordhawthorne/oxfordhawthorne.html or https://ajbpleading.com/oxfordhawthorne/oxfordhawthorne.html.

For academic references, write the citations using name, year, subject, and publication year on one line. The following is an example of a citation: Locke, L. (2008). Autism in children: diagnosis and treatment strategies. [Edinburgh, Scotland, England, Australia, Singapore]. Edinburgh: Taylor & Francis.

You can also use the following citation format: A. A. (2012). Some recommendations on the psychology of autism therapy. In Proceedings of the International Conference on Psychology of Autism and Other Developmental Disabilities (pp. 1–5). Springer Netherlands.


You can write text in the form of a title, a headline, or a sub-title. Ensure that the title is in bold typeface, and the sub-title is in italics.

You can also write an abstract to promote your research. Use the following format:

  • Title: A concise phrase that describes your research project.
  • Summary: A short summary of your research. You can put the abstract on the front page of your paper.
  • Keywords: A list of the keywords used in your paper.
  • Introduction: Explain the reason of your research.
  • Results and statistical data: The data that you have collected and analyzed in your paper.
  • Conclusion: Discuss what you have learned from your research.
  • References: Cite the references used to support your research. This information can be provided on a separate page in the form of a list. You can also link to relevant parts of the content page.
