Example:

<selector>
  <date-range>
    <text>
      <title>May 24, 2020</title>
      <subtitle>May 24, 2020</subtitle>
    </text>
    <date-name>May 24</date-name>
    <date-fixed>2020-05-24T13:00:00Z</date-fixed>
  </date-range>
  <text>
    <title>May 24, 2020</title>
    <subtitle>May 24, 2020</subtitle>
  </text>
</selector>

Then, as a demo, use a date-range selector on a form with the time-ago selector:

<form action="/mapping" method="post">
  <input type="text" disabled="disabled" placeholder="You can also use the time-ago selector" />
  <input type="date-range" name="date-range" value="accessed" />
  <input type="submit" value="Submit" />
</form>

...then you can use the date selector elsewhere, e.g. for a date range input in a separate form, to use the date range selector with the time-ago selector on the date range selector.
