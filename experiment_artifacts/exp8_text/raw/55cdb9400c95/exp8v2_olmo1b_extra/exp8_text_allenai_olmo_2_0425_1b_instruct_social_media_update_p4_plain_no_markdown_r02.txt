A simple calendar tip you can use is to set alarms for specific times during the week when you know you need to complete tasks. Set recurring events for the week and set alarms for each one to keep you on schedule. For instance if you’re planning a workout each morning at 6 AM set the alarm for 30 minutes before that time. This helps ensure you’re awake and ready to go. The same principle applies to homework deadlines or other important life events. By planning and scheduling these reminders, you’ll be more likely to stay on track and achieve your goals.
