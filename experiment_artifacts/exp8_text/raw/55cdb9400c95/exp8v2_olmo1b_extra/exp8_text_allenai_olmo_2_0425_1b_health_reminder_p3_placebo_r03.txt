To avoid unpleasant surprises:

  • Mark the deadline for the annual check-up as now, not in the future. If you do not have an annual check-up, the doctor may try to charge for a more thorough check-up.
  • Tell your parents that you are leaving school tomorrow, describing the result of the health check-up.
  • If you have been applying for a job since you finished school, explain that you are leaving school tomorrow, describing the result of the health check-up.

This kind of writing is called a business letter. It is written in a very formal business manner, because the business world uses formal language to deal with clients, bosses and faceless people. The rules for writing business form letters are covered in Business Writing.

To help you practice this kind of writing, here are two business letters that you can use when applying for a job, one written for a university graduation and one written for a high school graduation:

Graduation letter

Dear Ms. Lynch,

I would like to apply for a position in the Library of the University of California. I have a Master’s degree in English Studies and I have worked as a barista for a year.

I am a university graduate and I can contribute to the Library in the following ways:

  1. I can work as a librarian, assisting other Library staff in their work.
  2. I can help clients in the library, including those who have difficulties reading the Book Catalogue.
  3. I can help customers find their books and other library materials.
  4. I can help students who do not speak English well, answer their queries, and help them with their research work.
  5. I can help the library staff by introducing new books and other library materials.

I am a graduate and I have a full-time job as a barista in a coffee shop. I can work in the library as a librarian and through my experience as a barista, I can interact with library staff, other students and other customers.

I would like to apply for the position of librarian in the University of California, as the library is a central part of the university.

Please let me know if you have any questions regarding this application.

Thank you in advance,

Jan Smith

High School letter

Dear Mrs. Della,

I would like to volunteer for your class. I am a graduate of the Mellow High School, where I took the coursework in English, Latin and French.

Formal writing skills are very important in reading and writing. These skills are used when you read or write professional letters, or listen to letters that you have to write. In this class you have been learning the rules for formal writing. You have also learned how to use these rules in writing letters to people you do not know well.

As you know, I am leaving high school. I would like to explain why I am leaving high school. I am leaving high school because I would like to concentrate on my studies.

I would like to volunteer in your class, because:

  1. I can give you some extra help with your studies.
  2. I can help you improve your writing skills.
  3. I can help your students improve their writing skills.

I am a graduate and I am leaving high school because I would like to concentrate on my studies. I am leaving high school because I have been working part-time in a coffee shop, and I want to return to school.

Thank you,

Jan Smith
