Use the link markup provided in the Getting Started guide

Avoid the following types of content:

  • External links

  • Severe privacy violations

  • Privacy issues that are at risk of being revealed in the event of a GDPR breach

  • Serious financial disclosures

  • Information that is already publically available


  • Conspiracy theories and fallacies

  • Paid advertising

  • Self promotion

  • Political advocacy

  • Religious beliefs or practices

  • Inflammatory content


  • Content that is abusive or offensive

  • Content that is explicitly illegal, dangerous, or immoral

  • Content that is clearly created by bots

  • Content copied from other bots

  • Content that violates someone's privacy

Use of the platform

You are not obligated to use the platform. Do not be under any false impression or belief that the platform is safer because you use it, or that it is worthwhile just because you use it.

The platform is not a substitute for real governmental or police action. The platform and any entity that uses it are in no way obligated to protect anyone or anything, and the platform and its operators do not have to intervene if they do not want to do so. If you need a more thorough explanation of the risks that the platform poses, do not trust the platform itself.
