How to Create a Final Draft

Write the body of the essay quickly by writing down points in the form of bullet points, and then elaborate on each point in the opening paragraph. When you finish, transfer your final draft from a word or text editor into a new document.

Check your spelling, grammar, and references. Leave time for a final check by reading out loud. Read backwards to see whether you understand your own words. You can save time by writing your essay in bullet points, and, if space is limited, use speech marks. Use a bullet point list as a way of bullet proofing your final draft.

Revise your essay. And then read away.
