Review your weekly fitness progress each week to stay motivated and achieve your goals. Take a moment to assess how your body feels and how much you have accomplished during the week. Compare your current state to your previous week's performance to see the changes occurring. Celebrate your successes no matter how small they may seem and adjust your goals as needed based on your experiences. Regularly evaluating your fitness journey will keep you on track and remind you of the hard work you are putting into improving your health and well-being.
