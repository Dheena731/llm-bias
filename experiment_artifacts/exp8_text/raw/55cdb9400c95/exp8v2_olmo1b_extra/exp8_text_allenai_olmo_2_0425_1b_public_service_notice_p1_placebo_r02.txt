Today’s library is open from 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. from Monday to Thursday, and from 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on Fridays. This notice is repeated every month. Please note that the library is closed on Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays.

Write a short public-service notice about the history of Hopewell

The first people to inhabit the area of Hopewell were the Yokuts. The Yokuts were hunters and fishermen who lived in the area for hundreds of years before the arrival of the Spanish. In 1769 the Spanish established a mission and a town here. The name “Hopewell” is thought to come from a Native American word which means “place of tall*&*ing,” referring to a mountain near the town.
