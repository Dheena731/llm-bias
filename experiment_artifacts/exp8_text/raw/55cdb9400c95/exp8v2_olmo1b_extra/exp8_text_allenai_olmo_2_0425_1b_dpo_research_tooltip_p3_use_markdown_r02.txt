**Date-Range Selector Guide**

*Understand the date-range selector for selecting dates.*
*Select dates between a specific start and end date.*
*Use `inclusive` ( `start`, `end` ) or `exclusive` (`start` or `end` alone)*
*Example: `start=2022 end=2023`* selects all dates in 2022 and 2023.*

*For `inclusive`:*
*Use `start` & `end` together:* `2022 end` or `2023 start`
*Selects dates from start to end, including the end date.*

*For `exclusive`:*
*Use `start` or `end` alone:* `2022 start` or `2023 end` *selects dates before start but not including end.*

*Tips:* Practice common abbreviations like `MM` for months and `YY` for years.*
