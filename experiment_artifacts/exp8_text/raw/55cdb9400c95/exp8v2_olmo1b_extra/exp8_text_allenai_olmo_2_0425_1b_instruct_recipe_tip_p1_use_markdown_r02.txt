**Headline: Quick Chicken Pasta Salad**

**Recipe:**  
* **Ingredients:**  
  * 1 pound chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces  
  * 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided  
  * 1/2 onion, finely chopped  
  * 1 green pepper, finely chopped  
  * 1 clove garlic, minced  
  * 3 cups cooked pasta (e.g., penne or ziti)  
  * 1 can (14 oz.) diced tomatoes  
  * 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted  
  * Salt and pepper, to taste  

**Instructions:**  
* Preheat oven to 375°F (185°C).  
* Toss the chicken with 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large bowl.  
* Add onion, green pepper, and garlic; cook until softened, about 5 minutes.  
* Stir in cooked pasta and heat through.  
* In a small bowl, mix the remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil with the butter; drizzle over the pasta mixture.  
* Season with salt and pepper; transfer to a baking dish.  
* Bake for 15 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through and the salad is heated through.
