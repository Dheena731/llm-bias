When you spend a lot of time looking at a screen—whether on your computer, tablet, phone, or e-reader—you can benefit from taking short breaks. Here's why and how you can do it:

**Why Take Breaks from Screens?**

Your eyes work hard when you stare at screens, and they can get fatigued:

* Eye strain can lead to headaches, blurred vision, and dry eyes.
* The blue light from screens can mess with your sleep schedule, making it harder to fall asleep and wake up refreshed.
* Extended screen time can make your eyes lose the natural distance they're used to by squinting and straining.

**How to Take Them**

* **Set a timer:** Daily, aim to take a 20-30 minute break every 2-3 hours.
* Step away from the screen: Get up, stretch, grab a glass of water, or walk around.
* Use the break to do something different: Read a few pages from a physical book or browse something on a different topic than your current screen fixation.
* Consider activities like taking a walk outside, listening to a podcast on a different topic, or doing some mindfulness exercises.

Following these reminders can help reduce the discomfort associated with prolonged screen use and keep your eyes and mind healthy.
