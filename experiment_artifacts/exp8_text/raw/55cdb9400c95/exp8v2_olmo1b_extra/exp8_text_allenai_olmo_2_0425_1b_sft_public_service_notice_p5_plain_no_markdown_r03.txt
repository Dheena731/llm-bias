Polling station locations are strategically situated throughout the community to ensure that every eligible voter has access to the voting process. These locations are typically found at designated community centers, schools, community halls, libraries, and other public buildings that have been designated for the purpose. Each location is carefully chosen to reflect the diversity of the community and to provide a welcoming and accessible environment for voters. It is important to note that polling stations are typically open on the day of the election, but may be temporarily closed on the day of the election. Please make sure to visit the correct location for your vote and for any necessary information on how to vote.
