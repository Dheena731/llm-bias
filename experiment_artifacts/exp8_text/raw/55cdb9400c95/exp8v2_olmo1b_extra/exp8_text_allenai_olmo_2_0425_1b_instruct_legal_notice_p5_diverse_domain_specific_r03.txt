Attention Subscribers and Network Users,

At [Company Name], we are committed to providing a reliable and secure shared network environment for our users. The acceptable use policy for our networks outlines specific guidelines to ensure the smooth and uninterrupted operation of our services.

**General Rules of Engagement:**

1. **Responsible Usage:** Users are expected to use the network and all its resources in a responsible manner. This includes but is not limited to:
   - No hacking, unauthorized access, or modifications of network systems or configurations.
   - Avoid distributing malware or promoting any unethical business practices.
   - Avoid making bulk or distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks.
   - Respect privacy and adhering to data protection laws, including GDPR if applicable.

2. **Network Security:** Users must be vigilant and adhere to security protocols. This includes:
   - Keeping software up to date to protect against known vulnerabilities.
   - Using strong, unique passwords for each account.
   - Protecting personal information through the use of encryption and secure protocols.
   - Reporting any security breaches or suspicious activities immediately to the IT department.

3. **Data Handling:** Respectful handling of data is paramount. Users agree not to collect, use, share, or distribute personal data without explicit consent for any purpose, save when expressly permitted by law.
   
4. **Cyberbullying and Harassment:** All forms of harassment, bullying, or any form of cyberbullying against individuals or groups within the network or in digital spaces outside the network are strictly prohibited. Enforcement actions may include volume prevention, temporary or permanent blocking, and legal actions.

5. **Leakage Information:** Network users must not disclose sensitive information (like passwords, financial details, or any proprietary or secure information) outside of the network environment without explicit consent from the recipient.

**Penalties:**

Non-compliance with this acceptable use policy may result in network disconnection for deliberate infringement, as well as other penalties as deemed appropriate by the management team.
