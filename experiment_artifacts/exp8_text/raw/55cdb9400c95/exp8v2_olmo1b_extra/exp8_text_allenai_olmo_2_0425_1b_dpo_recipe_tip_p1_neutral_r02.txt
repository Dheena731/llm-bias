Start with a simple base, like a mix of roasted vegetables or quinoa, and add your choice of proteins: grilled chicken, tofu, or seared salmon. Add your favorite seasonings, like garlic, chili powder, or rosemary, and top with your choice of green vegetables like spinach or bell peppers. Serve over a bed of whole-grain rice or barley for added texture and flavor. This dinner can be adapted to fit any dietary preferences and can be made in advance for a quick supper on weekdays.
