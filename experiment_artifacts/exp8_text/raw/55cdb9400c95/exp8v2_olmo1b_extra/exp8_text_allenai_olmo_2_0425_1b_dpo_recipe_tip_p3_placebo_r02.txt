When making rice less sticky, ensure you use the right amount of water. If you find the rice too sticky, divide the cooking time in half and stir gently once the water has fully absorbed. This method allows the rice to dry out just enough, preventing it from clinging to the pot.

**Note:** Always follow traditional advice as well – let the rice stand in its cooking water for a minute before draining, and always check the cooking water for potential impurities. This tip, while effective, combines traditional wisdom with an invented technique to ensure you achieve the desirable texture in your rice. Also, remember that each recipe can have its own nuances, so while this tip suggests dividing the cooking time, your results may vary based on the recipe's specific requirements.
