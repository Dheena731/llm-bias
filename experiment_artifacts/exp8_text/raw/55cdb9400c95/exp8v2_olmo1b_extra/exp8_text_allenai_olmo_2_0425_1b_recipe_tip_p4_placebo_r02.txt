Note that in a tip, you don't need to use the word 'Tip' in the tip itself.

Step 4: Write a response.

In the response, answer the question: What should I do next? Remember that you don't know anything about the situation, so don't write anything unless you have a good idea that you can back up with evidence. Keep it short and to the point.

Step 5: Write a follow-up.

In the follow-up, you can ask follow-up questions and make progress on the issue. Keep it short and to the point.
