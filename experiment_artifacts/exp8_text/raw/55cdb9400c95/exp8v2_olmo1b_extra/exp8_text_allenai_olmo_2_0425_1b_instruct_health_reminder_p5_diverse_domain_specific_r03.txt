Reminder: Refilling Prescription

When you need to refill your prescription, it's important to do so before the prescription runs out completely to avoid missing your next appointment for refilling or missing out on the medication that is prescribed for your specific health condition. Here are a few things to keep in mind when refilling your prescription:

1. **Check the Date:** Always check the expiration date on your prescription to ensure it remains safe and effective for you to use.

2. **Review the Prescription:** Ensure the medication has the correct dosage, amount, and form (e.g., tablet, liquid, etc.).

3. **Check Demand Dates:** If your doctor has placed a 'Prior Written Consent' for a prescription, review the 'Demand Dates' listed on the prescription, as you must refill it by these dates to avoid penalties.

4. **Understand Charges:** Familiarize yourself with the pricing before refilling. Some pharmacies offer discounts for refills, particularly if you are a regular customer.

5. **Contact Your Pharmacy:** If you have any questions or if there is an issue with your prescription, contact your local pharmacy directly for assistance. They can guide you on how to proceed with the refill safely and effectively.

6. **Schedule an Appointment:** Don’t forget to reschedule your next appointment to get your new prescription filled. This helps ensure you have the proper medication and follow the prescribed dosage regimen.

Refilling your prescription correctly is crucial for maintaining your health and can help prevent complications that might arise from using expired or improperly stored medication. Always follow your healthcare provider's instructions and adhere to the specific details of your care plan.
