To export query results for further analysis or sharing, follow these steps:

1. Run your SQL query in your data management tool or database management system.
2. Select or right-click the query result set you wish to export.
3. Choose an export option: Common choices include saving to a CSV file, Excel spreadsheet, PDF, or other formats.
4. Choose your desired export format and location.

Exporting query results ensures you have a backup or can share the insights from your analysis with stakeholders.
