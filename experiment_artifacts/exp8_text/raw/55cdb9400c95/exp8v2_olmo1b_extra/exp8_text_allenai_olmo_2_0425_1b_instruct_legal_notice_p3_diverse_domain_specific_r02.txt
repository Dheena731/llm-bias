What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of data sent from a website and stored on your device by your browser while you are browsing. Most modern web browsers allow cookies to be set, removed or managed.

Why do we use cookies?

We use cookies to:

• Personalize your experience by remembering your preferences, such as your language preferences or product selections.
• Improve the performance and functionality of our website, such as assisting in its navigation through personalized content.
• Monitor traffic patterns and website usage to help us improve our services.
• Analyze your usage data to understand how our website is being used and to help us improve our offerings.

What types of cookies do we use?

We use three primary types of cookies:

1. **Performance Cookies:** These cookies are used to collect and share information about the way our website is used so we can make improvements.

2. **Functional Cookies:** These cookies allow us to provide you with more personalized services because they require us to assume a basic capability to do so, such as enabling or disabling certain features.

3. **Analytical Cookies:** We use these cookies to collect and analyze data to help us understand how our website is being used, which helps us to improve our services.

How can I manage my cookies?

You can control and manage cookies by adjusting your browser settings. Most web browsers allow you to refuse or accept all cookies, specific cookies, or even disable them entirely. Remember that disabling cookies can affect your browsing experience. If you'd like to choose which cookies are allowed or disabled, you can usually do so through your browser’s settings.
