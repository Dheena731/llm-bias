It is important to take regular short breaks from screens to maintain good eye health and prevent eye strain. Here are a few reminders to consider:

1. **20/20/20 Rule**: Every 20 minutes spend at your screen, look at something 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds.

2. **Human Eye Diagram**: Think of your eye as a camera. Just as a camera blinks to refresh the image, your eyes need to blink regularly.

3. **Screen-Free Time**: Try to limit screen time before bedtime.

4. **Take Physical Steps**: Get up and stretch, walk around, or do some light exercise to give your eyes a much-needed rest.

5. **Mindful Intermissions**: Not all breaks need to be significant; even a few minutes of walking, practicing deep breathing, or computing simple tasks can refresh your mind and eyes.

Remember, balance is key. Screen time is a valuable part of daily life, but it's important to remember to rest your eyes too.
