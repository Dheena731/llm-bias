**Recycling Collection Changes**

* Starting January 1st, 2023, our recycling collection schedule will be altered.  
* Weekday recycling will now be collected on the following days: Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.  
* Saturdays will remain for larger items only.  
* Collection days for months with fewer days will shift accordingly.  
* Customers are encouraged to sort their recyclables properly for efficient collection.  

* For more information, visit our website or contact our customer service team.
