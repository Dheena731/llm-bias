# Short Tip for Planning LUNCHes for a Busy Week

- Start by listing your Week's lunch needs: office lunches, school lunches, meetings lunches, etc.
- Organize your meals in batches: prepare multiple copies of the same lunch on weekends to save time during the week.
- Plan for leftovers: select lunches that can be portioned and stored for later days to reduce prep work and save time.
- Create a grocery shopping list based on your prepared meals to save time and ensure you have all the essentials.
- Keep a lunch box ready with all necessary items; ensure it’s a reusable container to encourage homemade lunches.
- Use online meal prep tools to automate some of the planning, especially for repetitive meals or team lunches.
