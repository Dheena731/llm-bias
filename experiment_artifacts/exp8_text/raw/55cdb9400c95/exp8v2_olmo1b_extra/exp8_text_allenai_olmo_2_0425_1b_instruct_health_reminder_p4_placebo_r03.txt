Short breaks from screens are a must! Our eyes and minds are not designed to stare at screens for long stretches of time. Here are a few quick tips to remind you to take those necessary pauses:

**1. The 20-20-20 rule:** Every 20 minutes, take a 20-second break and look at something 20 feet away. This simple trick keeps your eyes healthy.

**2. The Pomodoro Technique:** Break your work into short, focused increments (usually 25 minutes) and then take a longer break (usually 5 minutes). This time management method can help prevent burnout and improve overall productivity.

**3. Green screens:** Whenever you're looking at a screen for extended periods, try to sneak in a few moments of nature. Look outside, take a quick walk, or just splash water on your face—just don't forget to get back to your screens!

**4. Use apps:** There are apps available that can remind you to take short breaks, like Forest (where you have to stop checking your phone for it to keep growing), and Freedom (which blocks specific apps).

**5. Physical activity:** Regularly stretch or indulge in a short physical activity can enhance your concentration and help your brain reset.

**6. Screen-Free Sundays:** If you can, dedicate a day a week to screen-free activities – this can reinforce the importance of regular breaks.

Remember, the key to a healthy work-life balance is to pay attention to how your mind and eyes feel. Short breaks aren't a deterrent to productivity but a practice for maintaining sanity and performance.
