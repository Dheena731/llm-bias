 • Status: Hot

A heat advisory indicates significant heat and is issued when the conditions are expected to be dangerous for a significant number of the population. The specific conditions, which are determined through local weather models and observations, typically include the following:

  • Air temperature
  • Humidity
  • Heat index
  • Wind speed

During the heat advisory period, planning for outdoor activities and managing heat illnesses becomes a priority. The advisory may be expanded to a heat warning when conditions become more dangerous.

  • Status: Outage

Multiple, simultaneous-outage weather advisories are issued when the local weather conditions pose a greater-than-normal threat to the local population. A multiple-outage weather advisory is issued when a combination of the following is present:

  • Air temperature
  • Humidity
  • Heat index
  • Wind speed
  • Local precipitation
