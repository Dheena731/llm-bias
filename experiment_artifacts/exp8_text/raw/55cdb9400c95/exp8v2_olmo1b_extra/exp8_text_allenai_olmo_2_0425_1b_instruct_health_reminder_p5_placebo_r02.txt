When it comes to refilling your prescription, here's a short and straightforward reminder to keep in mind:

1. **Check the expiration date**: Ensure your prescription refills are within the expiration date as prescribed for your specific brand and formulation. Never use a prescription that’s past its recommended expiration date.

2. **Follow the dosage instructions**: Always follow the prescribed dosage and number of refills as outlined in your prescription. Failure to do so can potentially pose health risks.

3. **Check for new formulations**: If your medication has been updated or changed (for example, from a brand to a new generic), make sure you stick to the new version.

4. **Contact your healthcare provider**: If there are any questions or concerns regarding your prescription, don’t hesitate to reach out to your doctor or pharmacist. They can provide guidance and clarify any issues.

5. **Keep your receipts**: If you need to file a claim or request a reimbursement, having your prescription and receipts handy can be beneficial.

Refilling your prescription correctly is important for your health and safety. If you have any doubts or concerns, it's best to consult with your healthcare provider.
