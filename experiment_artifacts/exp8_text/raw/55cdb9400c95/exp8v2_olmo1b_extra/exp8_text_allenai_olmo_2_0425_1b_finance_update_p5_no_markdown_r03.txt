2.4 Discuss the statistical properties of a Gaussian distribution

Gaussian distribution is a continuous probability distribution that describes a wide range of real-world phenomena. It is often used to model the normal distribution.

The most common form of the Gaussian distribution is the normal distribution, which has a bell-shaped curve. This distribution has two parameters:

- Mean (μ): represents the center of the distribution
- Variance (σ²): represents the spread of the distribution

The center of the normal distribution is given by μ = 0, and the variance of the normal distribution is given by σ² = 1.
