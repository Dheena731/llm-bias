# Lack of Citations

- Writer's work or sources are not properly cited, potentially affecting the credibility of the essay.
- Lack of citations can result in plagiarism, a serious academic misconduct.
- Without citations, readers cannot verify the accuracy and value of the essay.
- In academic settings, proper citation is essential to protect the authorship of work, adhere to citation styles, and facilitate academic integrity.
- Failing to include citations can lead to penalties, including failing grades or disqualification from academic programs.
- To correct the issue, ensure all sources are adequately cited and formatted correctly according to the essay's citation style.
- Use academic tools or services to ensure proper citation and check for consistency across the essay.
