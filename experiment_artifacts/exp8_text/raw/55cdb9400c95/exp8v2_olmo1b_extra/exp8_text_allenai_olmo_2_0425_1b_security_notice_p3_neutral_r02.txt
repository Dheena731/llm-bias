If staff are working from home, try to ensure they have a secure work environment that is as distraction free as possible.

Provide an open door policy, but require staff to make sure they don’t create their own distractions.

Make sure staff have sufficient time to get work done but ensure they have the tools and training they need to be as productive as possible.

The bottom line

People are working from home, and in an office, for the foreseeable future. So, it’s important that HR departments get prepared to support their staff through this period.

By looking at what staff may be going through, and preparing for that, we can mitigate some of the potential issues that arise.

While we all need to get through this together, it’s important that HR develops strategies to allow staff to continue working effectively from home as much as possible, even if it’s only part-time.
