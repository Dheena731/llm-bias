How to write an engaging launch note

Let’s break down the elements of a successful launch note to understand what makes it so engaging.

Use jargon

A good launch note uses vocabulary appropriate to the domain. Use the key terms in a domain to draw the reader in, and to showcase your knowledge of them.

For example, you might use terms like “podcast” and “e-learning” to convey their nature to a tech- or language-focused audience.

But you’ll want to avoid using jargon when it’s not technically needed. Choose terms and wording that’s appropriate for your readers. If you’re not sure, find a few key terms and research them. Use a thesaurus to find synonyms for common terms.

Use anecdotes

Use anecdotes to draw the reader in. Share facts, examples, and real-life stories about your work and the domain you’re covering.

A good launch note will show readers the niche you’ve chosen and why they should care about it. Draw parallels to the reader’s own life, experiences, or areas of interest.

For example, a language learning podcast launch note might share an adventure in a foreign country. Or, a blog about technology might share anecdotes about a tech support call.

Use examples

Use examples to demonstrate your topic. Leverage real-life examples to illustrate the point you’re trying to make.

A good launch note will give real-life examples that are relevant to a domain as well as the audience. Use examples that are specific to the needs of a reader, and that demonstrate your topic. Give examples that are relatable and helpful.

For example, if you’re writing a launch note for a tech support podcast, share examples of successful customer service calls. If you’re writing a launch note for a language learning podcast, share examples of great international travel experiences.

A good launch note will make it clear what the benefits of listening to the podcast or reading the blog are. It will also demonstrate what makes the podcast or blog different.

Don’t always start a launching note with an introduction. The material you use to break the material into sections will depend on your topic.

For example, a launch note for a language learning podcast might start with a description of the podcast, followed by an introduction to the main part of the podcast or blog.

How to write a lead into a launch note

How to write an engaging lead into a launch note

A lead is used to draw readers in. It explains why and how they should listen to or read the material. A well-written lead will set the stage for a good launching note.

The lead will need to establish the purpose of the release. For example, a blog about a specific topic should use the lead to describe what the blog is about. A podcast about a specific topic should use the lead to explain what the podcast is about.

The launch note should feature a lead that has these characteristics:

  • Relevant to the topic
  • Persuasive
  • Specific to the reader
  • Accurate

Relevant to the topic

The lead should be relevant to the topic of the article or podcast. State how the topic relates to readers.

For example, a blog post on language learning could describe how language learning can help readers improve their reading, writing, and speaking skills.
