**New Expense Approval Workflow:**

3. **Automated Rollover:** Once the approved version of an expense is recorded, the system will automatically roll that approved amount into the next financial period, reducing the manual accounting burden for the accounting department.

4. **Document Management Integration:** We're integrating our document management system to seamlessly integrate with our expense tracking, making it easier to track approvals and reference documents within the same view.

5. **Regular Audits:** To ensure the workflow remains efficient and accurate, we will conduct regular audits of approved expenses against original documentation and budgets.
