The new savings goal feature is now available for all users. 

The feature allows users to establish and track multiple savings goals with details such as the amount to be saved, the time frame, and the specific purpose. Users can input their saving objectives based on their financial situation and goals.

The feature includes options for users to set up recurring savings, make automatic transfers, and track their progress. With this new tool, users can organize and manage their savings efforts with more ease and clarity.

The intuitive design ensures that users can easily understand the information presented and adjust their savings plans as needed. The feature is powered by cutting-edge data analysis and predictive modeling to help users reach their savings targets and achieve their financial objectives.
