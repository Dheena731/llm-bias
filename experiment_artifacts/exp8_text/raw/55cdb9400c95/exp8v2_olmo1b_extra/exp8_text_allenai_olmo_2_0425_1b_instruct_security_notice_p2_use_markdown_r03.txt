**Phishing Emails: A Work Hazard**

- **What are phishing emails?**
  - Phishing emails are deceptive messages designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information, such as login credentials or financial details.

- **Identifying phishing attempts**
  - Look out for suspicious emails with poor grammar, urgent requests, and links with unusual domain names.
  - Emails requesting immediate action (e.g., click here to resolve issues) are often phishing attempts.

- **Prevention tips**
  - Never click on suspicious links or provide sensitive information in response to unverified emails.
  - Keep your software updated to protect against known vulnerabilities.
  - Report phishing attempts to your IT department or HR.

---

*Note:* Remember, safeguarding your personal information is paramount in the digital workplace. If you suspect a phishing attempt, report it immediately to maintain a secure environment for all.
