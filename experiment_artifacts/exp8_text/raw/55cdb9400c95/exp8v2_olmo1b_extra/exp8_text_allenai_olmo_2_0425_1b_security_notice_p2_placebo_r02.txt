Phishing is a type of fraud that involves messages designed to lure users into providing personal or financial details. Phishing emails, for instance, could request information like a password or bank details. Phishing emails could also contain links to malicious online sites. The problem is often that businesses are targeted because their email addresses are on publicly accessible mailing lists, so they are harvested regularly by malicious individuals. Phishing is a way of stealing information from people with no intention of giving it to anyone else. A good way to prevent this type of fraud is educated staff. If you're a worker, make sure you know what to do in the event of a phishing email. Don't be afraid to ask someone to help you if you're not sure about something.
