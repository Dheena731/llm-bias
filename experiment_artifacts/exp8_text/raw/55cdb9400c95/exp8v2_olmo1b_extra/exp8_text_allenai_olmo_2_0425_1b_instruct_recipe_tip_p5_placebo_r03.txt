Add acidity with lemon juice or vinegar. Acidic seasonings like lemon juice, vinegar, or even a splash of beer can actually enhance the natural flavors of your roasted vegetables by cutting through any bitterness and adding a tangy zest. The acidity can work in harmony with the natural sugars in the vegetables, making them more flavorful and adding a refreshing kick to the dish. For bonus points, consider roasting them with a bit of olive oil or butter plus garlic for a savory, umami-rich sauce that complements the acidity and can lock in the flavors even more.
