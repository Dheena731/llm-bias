Attention all employees. Please be aware of phishing emails we have been receiving. These emails are created to deceive employees into revealing sensitive information. These emails may have links that lead to fraudulent websites or may include links to download malicious software.

If you receive an email that seems suspicious, do not click on the links or download any attachments. Instead, report it to your IT department immediately.

If you receive an email and you are unsure whether it is legitimate or a phishing attempt, contact your IT department immediately.

Thank you for your attention and vigilance.
