A missing-value marker, also known as a "marker of missing values" or "physician's note," in statistical data refers to an area where a value is not present or absent in the dataset. This marker is often used to represent instances where data are not available or require further investigation, such as missing birthdates, missing farmland counts, or other categorical data entries. Its presence helps in detecting patterns of data patterns that may indicate underlying issues such as unrecorded transactions, data entry errors, or deeper problem areas in the dataset.
