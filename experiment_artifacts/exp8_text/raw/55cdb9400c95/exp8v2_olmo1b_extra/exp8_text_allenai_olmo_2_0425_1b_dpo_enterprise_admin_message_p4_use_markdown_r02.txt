# Quarterly Access Reviews

We will be conducting quarterly access reviews to ensure continued access to our systems and services. Please note that any unauthorized access or changes to your account may result in suspension.

* **Why:** To maintain security and prevent unauthorized use of our systems.
* **When:** In March, June, September, and December.
* **How:** We will send you an email notifying you of the review period and instructions on how to provide any needed documentation.
* **What:** We will verify your identity and confirm that access is still needed for your role.
* **Our Commitment:** Your cooperation during these reviews is essential to our mission of protecting data and user privacy.

To prevent surprise contact, please review your account settings and contact our support team with any questions or concerns before the review period begins. Your access will not be automatically renewed after the review period unless confirmed by us.

Thank you for your attention and cooperation.
