# Storing Fresh Herbs

- Keep herbs in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
- Use a glass jar or container with a tight-fitting lid to keep herbs fresh.
- For herbs that easily dry out, store them in a paper towel inside the glass jar.
- Refresh the herbs in water or broth a few times a week to keep them moist.
- Remove spent leaves to prevent mold growth and repot herbs as needed for better air circulation.
