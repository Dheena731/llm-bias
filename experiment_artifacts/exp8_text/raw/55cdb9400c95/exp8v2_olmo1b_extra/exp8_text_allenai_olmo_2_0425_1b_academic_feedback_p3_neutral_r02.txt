Describe approach and method

The researcher should be able to describe the method used.

Can be done by:

  • giving an example in the context of how the method is used
  • describing the method's inputs and outputs
  • giving an overview of the method in an abstract way (with respect to the thesis topic)
  • giving the level of abstraction used to describe the method (with respect to the thesis topic)

The many examples of theses require a small abstract in the introduction.

The level of detail might be high or low depending on what the reviewer thinks of your background.
