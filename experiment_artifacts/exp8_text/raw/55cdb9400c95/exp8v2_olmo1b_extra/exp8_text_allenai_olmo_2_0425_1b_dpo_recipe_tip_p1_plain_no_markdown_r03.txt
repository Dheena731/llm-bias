Recipe for a easy yet delicious weeknight dinner: Prepare a pot of your favorite slow-cooker stew. Start with thinly sliced carrots, celery, and potatoes. Toss in your choice of meat (such as beef, chicken, or pork) and let it cook slowly in the flavorful broth for about 4 hours. Serve with a side of crusty bread and steamed green beans for a complete and satisfying meal.
