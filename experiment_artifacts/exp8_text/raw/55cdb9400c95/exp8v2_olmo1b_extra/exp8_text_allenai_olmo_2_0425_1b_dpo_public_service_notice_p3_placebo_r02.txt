**Recycling Collection Changes: A Quick Guide**

Starting next week, our community is undergoing a significant shift in our recycling collection routine. It’s important to familiarize yourself with the updated schedule to ensure your household activities remain efficient and environmentally friendly.

**Most impacted areas will see an increase in the collection frequency of paper and plastics, specifically those marked with the number 1 and 7 on the recycling symbol.**

*For example, plastic bottles and glass jars will now be collected on a weekly basis instead of the current bi-weekly schedule.*

*Paper products, including newspapers, magazines, and single-stream cardboard, remain the same – paper will continue to be collected bi-weekly.*

*Food and garden waste, which was collected under the same schedule as plastic bottles and glass, will now be separate collected on Tuesdays and Thursdays.*

**Considering these changes, here are a few tips to help manage your recycling more effectively:**

*Always separate your recyclables; this helps in preventing contamination that could stop recycling trucks.*

*Ensure your plastic bottles, paper, and glass are clean before collection; dirt and stains can prevent recycling.*

*Keep a basket or bin at home for any recyclables you collect for immediate collection on Tuesdays and Thursdays.*
