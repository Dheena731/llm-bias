**Employer: Please Note - Password Reuse is Not Recommended**

We understand that in some instances, it can be convenient to reuse passwords across multiple applications. However, as a part of our commitment to maintaining a secure and protected workplace, we strongly advise against password reuse.

Passwords serve as a strong line of defense against unauthorized access to our systems, networks, and sensitive data. Every user's password is assigned uniquely to prevent widespread compromise if a single password is compromised. Reusing passwords can significantly increase the risk of unauthorized access and data breaches.

By reusing passwords, you risk having a single password entry point that can enable an intruder to access multiple systems, applications, or personal accounts with ease. Our system automatically logs users out after a prolonged inactivity period to prevent unauthorized system control, but if a reused password is guessed or determined through a dictionary attack, access can be immediately gained.

To help you manage and secure your passwords effectively, we encourage the use of unique passwords for each account and account recovery options that do not compromise password security.

For any questions regarding password security, security best practices, or accessing your account recovery options, please contact our IT support team.
