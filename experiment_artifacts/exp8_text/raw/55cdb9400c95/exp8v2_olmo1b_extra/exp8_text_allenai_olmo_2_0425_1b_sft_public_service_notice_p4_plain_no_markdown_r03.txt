Attention, residents of the area!

Due to the above-average temperatures forecasted for the next few days, a heat advisory is in effect. Temperatures are expected to be high, and the weather conditions are expected to be dangerous. It is extremely important that residents take the following precautions to stay safe this week:

1. Stay indoors during the hottest hours of the day. If you must be outside, do so in the early morning or late evening.
2. Wear sunscreen and wear protective clothing. Reapply sunscreen every two hours and wear a hat.
3. Drink plenty of water. Dehydration can be a serious problem in hot weather.
4. If you have medical conditions, such as heart disease or diabetes, contact your healthcare provider for guidance on how to stay safe in the heat.
5. Avoid the use of electricity. Electric utilities impose restrictions on the use of electricity during heat waves to prevent wildfires. If you must use this electricity, do so in the daytime.
6. If your outdoor work requires you to wear heavy clothing, consider using a fan to help move hot air away from your body.
7. If you have a pool or other outdoor space, use it in moderation. A pool can cause you to overheat and become sick if you stay in it for too long.
8. Be careful around fireplaces, woodstoves, and other sources of heat. They can cause fires and fires can be very dangerous.
9. If it is unsafe to be outside, call 911 or alert your local emergency services.

Take the necessary precautions and stay safe!

Thank you for your attention.
