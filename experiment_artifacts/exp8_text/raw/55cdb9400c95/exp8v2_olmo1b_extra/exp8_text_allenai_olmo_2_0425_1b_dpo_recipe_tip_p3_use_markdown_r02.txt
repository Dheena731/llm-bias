**How to Make Rice Less Sticky**

* Rinse the rice under cold water until the water runs clear. This removes some of the starch.*
* Cook the rice according to the package instructions until al dente. Shorter cooking times help prevent excessive starch globs.*
* Let the cooked rice cool slightly before adding it to a liquid. This prevents the steam from binding the rice.*
* Add butter or a fat like olive oil before mixing in liquid to help distribute the moisture evenly.*
* For extra control, drain the rice a few times during cooking, rinsing lightly in cold water.*

This technique helps reduce the rice's tendency to stick and become overly sticky.
