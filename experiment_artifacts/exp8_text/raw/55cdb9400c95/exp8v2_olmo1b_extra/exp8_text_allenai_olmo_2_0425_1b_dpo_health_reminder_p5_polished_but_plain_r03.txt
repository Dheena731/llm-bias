When refilling your prescription, your pharmacist can guide you through the process. Here's a reminder:

- Check your current prescription to ensure you're getting the right medication, dosage, and frequency.
- Verify that the refill count meets your medical needs and the pharmacy's policy.
- Be aware of any potential brand-name versus generic options and the implications on your health care costs.
- Ask your pharmacist about any additional information regarding medication storage, handling, or interaction with other drugs.
- If needed, let your pharmacist know if you have questions about your medication or if you're experiencing adverse effects.
- Remind yourself of the importance of taking your medication exactly as prescribed to manage your health effectively.

Always follow these steps to ensure your prescription is handled safely and to the best of your health care needs.
