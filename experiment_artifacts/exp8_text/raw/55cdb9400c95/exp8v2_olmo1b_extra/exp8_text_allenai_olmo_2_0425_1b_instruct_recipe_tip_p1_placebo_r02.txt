Invent a dish with comfort food vibes using canned soups, sautéed veggies, and a flavorful browning sauce. This meal is perfect for a lazy Sunday, during those dreary days with nothing to look forward to, or for that one night when you're on the boundary of eating out again and don't feel like doing anything fancy. Unleash your inner homewrecking chef by combining the rich, comforting essence of canned soups, like cream of chicken or cream of mushroom, with fresh sautéed vegetables like spinach, carrots, or bell peppers. Pair them with a drizzle of homemade or store-bought brown sauce to add a sweet and tangy kick. This surprisingly easy weeknight dinner will fill your home with the comforting aromas and flavors of classic comfort food while taking minimal time to prepare. Bon appétit!
