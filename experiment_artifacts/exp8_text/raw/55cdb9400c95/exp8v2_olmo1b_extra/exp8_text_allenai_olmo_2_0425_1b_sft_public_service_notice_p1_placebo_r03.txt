Dear subscribers,

Every week, the library is open for 6 hours from 6 am to 9 pm. We are open on Tuesdays and Thursdays only. Our library has a nice garden, a cafe, and a study room. If there are a lot of people in the library, we close at 9 pm. If there are only a few people in the library, we stay open until 9 pm. You can take out a book until 9 pm. If you want to take out a book earlier or later, you can do that, but you have to bring it to the library every day. 

You can find out more information on our website at www.ohsllibrarian.org. The library is at 123 Main St, so you can’t miss it. You can drop off your book at any time, or you can bring it directly to the reading desk. 

In the library, we have a lot of different things to read, so you’ll be able to find something for everyone. 

Thank you for coming to our library. We hope you have a great time!

Warmest regards,

The librarians
