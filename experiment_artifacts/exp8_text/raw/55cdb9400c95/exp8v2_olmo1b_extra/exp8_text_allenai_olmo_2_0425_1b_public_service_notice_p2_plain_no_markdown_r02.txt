Do not use third person narrative.

Write in a style that would be suitable for a general audience and that is in line with DA’s brand and values. In particular, do not write in an informal tone.

Do not use any personal information (such as your name, location, identity, reputation, or assets). You may not quote anything from other documents.

Do not use any vague or ambiguous wording or jargon. Your text should be straightforward and clear, easily understood by all readers, and free of unnecessary words.

Structure and Layout

Your write-up should be 500 to 1,000 words (1,000 would be exceptional). You may break up your text into multiple paragraphs if needed. However, keep in mind that DA will use software to format the text.

The write-up should have a clear introduction, a body, and a conclusion. The introduction should be an overview of the write-up, including the key takeaways and overall message of the write-up. The body should be the meat of the write-up in terms of content and the conclusion should reiterate the key messages from the write-up.

Writing Guidelines

A “write-up” is defined as a summary or abstract of a research paper or article. The DA write-up on a research paper or article is written in the same way that you would write a summary or abstract of a research paper or article. A research paper or article is deemed “published” when it has been submitted to a peer-reviewed journal and has been accepted for publication. The write-up can be written in either APA or Chicago style.
