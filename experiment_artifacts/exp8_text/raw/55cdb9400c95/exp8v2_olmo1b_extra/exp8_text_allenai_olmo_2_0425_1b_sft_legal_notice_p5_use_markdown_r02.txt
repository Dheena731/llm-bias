**Shared Network Policy**

1. **Respecting Privacy:** 
Users must ensure they do not engage in any activities that violate privacy laws or regulations, such as sharing sensitive information or monitoring others.
   
2. **No Unauthorized Access:**
Users must not gain unauthorized access to the network, including through hacking, or attempt to do so.
   
3. **No Malicious Activity:**
Users must not use the network for any activities that interfere with the normal operation of the network or its applications, such as spreading malware.
   
4. **Network Monitoring:**
Users must comply with all network monitoring policies and be aware that network administrators may monitor the network for these purposes.
   
5. **No Theft of Property:**
Users must not unlawfully use, steal, or transfer network property, including data and network devices.
   
6. **No Unauthorized Software:**
Users must not install, use, or distribute unauthorized software on the network.
   
7. **No Illegal Use of Resources:**
Users must not use network resources in a manner that is illegal, including downloading copyrighted materials without permission.
   
8. **No Harassment or Incitement to Violence:**
Users must not use the network to harass, intimidate, or incite violence against others.
   
9. **No Discrimination or Discrimination in the Workplace:**
Users must not discriminate or discriminate in the workplace through their use of the network, including through email or messaging.
   
10. **No Unauthorized Backdoor Access:**
Users must not set up unauthorized backdoor access to the network or its services.
   
SUSPECTED violators of these policies will be subject to disciplinary action, including the revocation of network access, fines, or other appropriate penalties as deemed appropriate.
