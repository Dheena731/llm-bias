Long and Short Facebook Posts

Depending on the length of the feature, you can use the tables below to help you format your Facebook posts.  The shortest posts should be between 25 and 50 characters, while the longest posts should be between 80 and 100 characters.  But you probably want to keep them to a minimum, as you do not want to be  overly long.

The rules for short posts are very simple.  Below is a general template for a short post.

  • Start with an attention getter.  For example, start with a question.
  • Include positive language.  For example, use “we” instead of “I.”
  • Write in the active voice.  For example, write “Our team is great!” instead of “Our team is great!”

The rules for long posts are a bit more complicated.  Below are the recommendations for Facebook posts that are between 80 and 100 characters.

  • Start with a bolded introduction.  For example, start with “Our new feature is called…”
  • Include a personal story.  For example, include a story about the feature and what inspired you to create it.
  • Use a shorter personalized version of the introduction.  For example, replace the word “our” with “I.”
  • Use an action verb.  For example, replace “our team” with “I” and “our team is great!” with “I am great!”

Feel free to modify these rules to fit your own situation and use of social media.  Also, feel free to share your own tips for making the most of your Facebook posts in the comments!
