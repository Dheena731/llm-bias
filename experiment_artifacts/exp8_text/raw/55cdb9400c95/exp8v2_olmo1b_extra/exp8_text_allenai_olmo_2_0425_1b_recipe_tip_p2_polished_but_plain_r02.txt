Here are some tips for combining the two techniques:

  • Write in one of the techniques (inline, blockquote, or quote).
  • When you want to quote the same type of text, format them identically (the same font, size, line spacing, etc.).
  • Place all quoted text above the inline writing, unless the quotes are extremely short.
  • Write in the inline technique, except to quote a long block of text, such as a quote, an explanation, or a complex paragraph. Then format the quote and blockquote the same way. Here are some basic tips for writing a blog:

  • Write short, simple sentences.
  • Write sentences that are easy to understand.
  • Write in the active voice.
  • Use the present tense.
  • Use no more than 12 words per sentence.
  • Use no more than the minimum number of words to express yourself.
  • Use the words that are most appropriate for your audience.

Editing: Removing Unnecessary and Repetitive Elements


The final draft of your blog or article should be free of unnecessary information and excessive repetition. Make sure that each sentence is necessary to your point and does not contain superfluous words. Instead of writing, for example, 100 words about why you should do something, write just 20 words. When you have more to say, use the story technique to write more of the story, instead of repeating the same information in different ways.

  • Fewer words mean more impact and fewer keystrokes, which saves time and money.
  • Fewer words improve the flow and voice of writing.
  • Fewer words move a reader along more quickly, which increases the likelihood that they will continue reading.
