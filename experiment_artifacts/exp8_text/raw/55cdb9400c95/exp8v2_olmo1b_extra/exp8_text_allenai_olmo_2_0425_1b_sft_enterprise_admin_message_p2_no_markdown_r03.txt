**New Expense Approval Workflow Update**

Attention all administrative staff,

As part of our ongoing commitment to enhancing our internal processes, we are implementing a new expense approval workflow. This change aims to improve the accuracy and efficiency of expense approvals.

Below are the updated steps for expense approval:

1. **Apply for Expense**: Customers must now submit expenses electronically through our new expense portal. This ensures a streamlined application process and reduces the likelihood of human error.

2. **Approval Workflow**: Once an application is submitted, we will employ a pre-approved list to quickly assess claims. In the event of pre-approval, we will notify the customer and proceed with a review.

3. **Review**: During this review, our team will conduct a preliminary assessment and notify the customer of the status of the claim. If necessary, we will follow up to discuss any concerns or additional information needed.

4. **Final Approval**: We will finalize the approval if all parties agree. In the event of a discrepancy or request for clarification, we will reach out to the customer promptly.

We encourage all staff to familiarize themselves with the new workflow as soon as possible. This is a collaborative initiative to ensure the success of our expense management program. Your cooperation is vital in making this transition smooth.

Please get in touch with your team if you have any questions or need further assistance with the new process.
