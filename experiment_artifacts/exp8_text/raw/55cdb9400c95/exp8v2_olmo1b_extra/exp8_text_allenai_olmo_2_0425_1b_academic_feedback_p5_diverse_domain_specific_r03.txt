3) The design

The design section is about the exercise, not the exercise itself. This may seem contradictory, but the exercise itself is an element of the exercise. And, in fact, it is perspective. The design section should be written in the first-person, like the discussion section of a research paper.

The design section should include:

The purpose of the exercise

The process of its development

The composition of the exercise

Guidelines for the design

The exercise is a unique way of evaluating the source, and the process of its development can be unique too. You must now provide a new perspective on the exercise, and that is why you need a design section.

The first part of the design section is a place to describe the purpose of the exercise. You should justify the exercise in terms of the research question.

The second part of the design section is where you give a description of the process of its development. This is a description of the composition of the exercises. It should include what makes the exercise unique. For example, if the exercise is scientific, then it should be described in terms of scientific research. This is a descriptive description of the composition of the exercise.
