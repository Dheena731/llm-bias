Herbs are beautiful and versatile but quickly lose their flavor and freshness if not stored properly. Here’s a simple tip:

Store fresh herbs in airtight containers like glass or plastic containers. A counterspot will keep them dry and away from direct sunlight, which can dry them out or injure them. If you’re storing in a bag, make sure it's made of moisture-resistant material to avoid mold. More frequent checks and replace with fresh herbs as needed will keep your herbs vibrant and ready to use.
