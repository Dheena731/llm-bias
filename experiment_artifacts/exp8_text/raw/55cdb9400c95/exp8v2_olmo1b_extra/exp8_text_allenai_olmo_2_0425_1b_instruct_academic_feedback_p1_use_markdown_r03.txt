**Feedback Note: Literature Review Draft**

*Introduction* - Brief overview of the field and the rationale for the review.  
*Literature Review Structure* - Clarification of the review's approach (systematic, narrative, etc.) and its impact on the research field.  
*Strengths* - Identification of the review's main strengths, such as comprehensive coverage or empirical depth.  
*Weaknesses* - Discussion of areas that could be improved, such as clarity or logic in argumentation.  
*Conclusion* - Summary of key findings and implications, along with a suggestion for further research.
