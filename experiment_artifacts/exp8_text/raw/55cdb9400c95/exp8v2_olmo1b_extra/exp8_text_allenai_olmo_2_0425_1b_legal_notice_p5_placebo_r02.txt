A hierarchy is a fixed concept. It is not a property that is subject to change or variation. If you use more than one, then the document can be torn apart by anyone who knows what hierarchy is, and expects the document to be structured in a single structure.

The hierarchy is a restriction in how you organize a document. If you remove it, it is no longer a constrained hierarchy. The hierarchy is a way to constrain the structure of the document to a fixed form so that the reader (human or computer) can expect the document to have a certain structure.

If you remove the hierarchy, you also remove the expectation that the document will have a certain structure. The resulting document may be in a completely different structure. There is no longer any meaning to the document's structure.
