Attention all Admins

We want to remind you to perform quarterly access reviews for our systems and databases. These audits ensure that your permissions and privileges are properly allocated and maintained.

What does the review entail?
- Verification of access rights.
- Confirmation of current assignments.
- Identification of any unassigned rights that may pose a risk.
- Adjustment of user access based on organizational changes or policy updates.

The review process is crucial for safeguarding our data and applications from unauthorized access. By maintaining strict access control, we can prevent data breaches and uphold the integrity of our systems.

Important Dates:
- Quarterly Review: In March, June, September, and December.
- Notification for annual review events: In January and July.
