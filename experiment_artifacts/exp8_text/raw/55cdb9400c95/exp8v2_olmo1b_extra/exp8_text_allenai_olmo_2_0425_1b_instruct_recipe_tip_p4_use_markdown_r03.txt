### Quick Tip for Planning Lunches

* **Plan in Advance:** Create a weekly menu or a list of suggested lunches, considering lunchboxes for weekdays.
* **Use Reusable Containers:** Invest in a few great reusable lunch containers to stay organized and prevent waste.
* **Choose Variety:** Mix up your meals to keep things interesting and help prevent food boredom.
* **Include Snacks:** Plan for snacks that are easy to grab, keeping energy levels steady throughout the workday.
* **Stay Hydrated:** Pack a water bottle for both hydration and to help you avoid sugary drinks.
