A new feature has been added to your personal finance management system. The new feature is called "Goals". You can set goals for yourself in different categories such as "Pay off debt", "Understand my budget", "Buy a new car", "New house", "Travel", and more. All the goals are saved so you can review them anytime. You can assign a dollar amount to your goals and track the progress. You can also compare your goals with the rest of the users.
