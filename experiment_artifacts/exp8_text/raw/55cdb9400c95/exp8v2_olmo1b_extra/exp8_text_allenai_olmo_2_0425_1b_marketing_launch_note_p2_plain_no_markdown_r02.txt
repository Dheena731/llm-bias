Do not include images, unless they are professional stock images that convey your brand’s purpose. If you do use images, make sure they are relevant and add value to your post.

Do not add more than a 7pt font size to text. Use 10pt or less for headings.

Tools to help focus your marketing message

It's difficult to make a compelling argument for your product or service when you're still trying to establish your brand, especially if you're new to marketing. To help your marketing efforts, use the following resources to help guide you:

1. Content marketing

There's no question that your content is a major influencer in your marketing strategy. Blog posts and social media posts are the most common ways to communicate your brand's message, and they're beneficial for two main reasons:

  • They can help position your brand as an industry authority in your niche.
  • They position your brand as an industry expert who can help solve a product or service need for your target audience.

A content marketing plan can help you establish your brand in a strategic and consistent manner. Before you start writing, know what you want to accomplish and what message you want to send to your target audience. It's also critical that you provide value with your posts, whether it's by solving a problem, sharing information, or helping your audience.

2. Email marketing

What if you could connect with your target audience in a highly personalized way? Email marketing is one of the most effective ways to achieve that result. You can use email marketing to deliver highly personalized information to your target audience using the email addresses they've given you.

Emails can be shareable on social media if you make it easy for your audience to share them. Give your audience a reason to share the email, like a great deal or a coupon. Or personalize your marketing piece by including their first name and a message that makes them feel like you're talking directly to them. It's not that difficult to do.

3. Viral marketing

Keep your target audience motivated and informed by sharing your content with them. People are always looking for something new, and if you're providing it, you can garner a lot of attention. This is one of the most effective marketing strategies around, and it doesn't take long for your content to spread.

It's not all just about SEO. Remember that people want to know who they're dealing with and why they should care about you. Find ways to make it easy for people to connect with you. Use your brand as a tool to showcase your community of people who support you. It's all about finding a way to connect with your audience and making them feel special.

Wrap up

As you can see, there are a lot of opportunities to market your business in an effective way. The key is to have a plan and to make sure everything you do reflects who you are and what your brand stands for. Don't underestimate the power of one-on-one contact with your audience. Even if you're not able to that, you can still promote your brand with content and email marketing. So get started today!
