# Heat Advisory

**Effective: [Start Date]**

A heat advisory is in effect for the city due to high temperatures. Temperatures will remain above 95°F (35°C) through the day, with peak readings likely exceeding 100°F (38°C). 

- Stay indoors and avoid heavy outdoor work.

- Monitor your comfort: close windows, use fans, and consider using air conditioning if available.
- Check on family members and neighbors who might be at higher risk.
- Schedule outdoor activities during the coolest parts of the day.
- If you must be outside, use sunscreen and wear protective clothing.

Please follow your local authorities' guidelines for safe living conditions.
