**Public Notice:** Heat Advisory Issued for Your Safety

Due to the high temperatures forecasted this week, [City Name] City Services is issuing a Heat Advisory to remind residents to take necessary precautions to protect themselves from the potentially dangerous effects of extreme heat. The heat advisory is in effect for [Start Date], [End Date], and temperatures are expected to reach [Prepared Heat Graph].

The Heat Advisory means that the following weather conditions are expected and may pose health risks if not managed properly:

- **Increased Risk of Heatstroke:** Be aware of the risk of heatstroke, especially for elderly adults, children, and individuals who are physically inactive or obese.
- **Dehydration:** Monitor your water intake and hydration status. Drink at least 3 - 4 glasses of water a day, especially if you work outside, exercise, or are on active-duty.

**What to Do:** 
- **Stay indoors as much as possible:** Avoid overheating by staying indoors when temperatures reach their peak.
- **Stay hydrated:** Drink plenty of fluids, including water, especially if you are outside working, exercising, or engaged in outdoor activities.
- **Monitor outdoor activities:** Schedule outdoor activities during cooler hours of the day, and take frequent breaks to allow your body to cool down.
- **Use air conditioning:** When indoors, use air conditioning to reduce the temperature and prevent overexertion on your cooling system.

**Important Note:**
This Heat Advisory does not mean that the sun is shining or that temperatures have lowered; it merely highlights potential health risks of extended exposure to high temperatures. Please take the necessary precautions to protect your health.
