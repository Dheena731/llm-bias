Only a few marketing websites use cookies to track user behaviour. Instead, they usually use third party tools such as Google Analytics or Facebook Insights to track users. These online tools will actually track your behaviour.

Third party cookies: An effective way to track users’ behaviour can be through third party cookies. This type of cookie is stored on the user’s browser and is not affected by the browser’s cookie settings. Third party cookies are particularly useful for tracking users on the internet in general and across multiple websites. This is because they are able to track the user’s behaviour across multiple sites thanks to cross-site tracking.

Cookies that are not set by the domain: This is a common problem for small businesses with many websites. Cookies that are not set by the domain can be tracked by third party tracking tools. For example, the cookie that is set by this domain ( is being tracked by the yandex cookie.
