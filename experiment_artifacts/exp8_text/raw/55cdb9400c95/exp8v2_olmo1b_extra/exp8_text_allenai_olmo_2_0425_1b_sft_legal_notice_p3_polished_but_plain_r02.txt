Cookie usage on our website - What you need to know.

We use cookies to provide you with a better experience on our website. A cookie is a small piece of information that a website stores on your computer's hard drive. Websites use cookies to store information such as a user's name, username, and preference. We use cookies to remember your preferences and your browsing habits. We use the following cookies:

- **Analytical Services:** To help us improve our website and offer new features, we use Google Analytics to collect data about how you use our site.

- **Performance:** We use Google Performance Insights to help us understand how our site is working for you.

We may use other cookies to provide you with customized content or to track how you interact with our site. We use cookies only for "ession" or "session"(File) data, which is stored on your computer's hard drive until you close your browser. We may also use cookies to store your preferences, such as the language your are viewing our site in.

To control or opt out of certain cookies, you can control or opt out of third-party cookies in your browser. To control or opt out of all cookies, you can use this link to the "Do Not Track" tool provided by the U.S. Federal Trade Commission.

We are committed to using your information for the purpose for which it was collected. We will not use your information in any manner that is injurious to your health or safety. We will not sell your information to third parties, except for third parties for whom you have given prior consent. We will not use your information to harass or inconvenience you. We will not use your information for any purpose that violates your privacy or violates any applicable laws. We do not share your information with third parties whose sole purpose is to use your information for advertising.
