Here’s a quick tip for a simple yet satisfying weeknight dinner: Start by sautéing diced carrots, zucchini, and bell peppers in olive oil until they're tender and slightly golden. Add a can of diced tomatoes and some chopped onion, bring to a simmer, then whisk in a can of crushed tomatoes. Season with salt and pepper, and let it simmer for about 20 minutes. Serve over whole grain pasta. This easy and flavorful recipe not only adds color and flavor to your meal but also is packed with healthy veggies and convenient canned tomatoes. Buon appetito!
