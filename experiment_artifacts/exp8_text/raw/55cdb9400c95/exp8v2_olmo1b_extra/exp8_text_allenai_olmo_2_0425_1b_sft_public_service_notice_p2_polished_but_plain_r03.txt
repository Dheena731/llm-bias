Road closure: Construction area

Eastbound traffic on SR-1 from SR-5 northbound is closed to all vehicles. Closures will remain in place until midday, 12:00 PM. Construction activities are expected to cause delays on this stretch of road for several weeks.

The construction team is working on a new section of the highway. They need to widen the road, install new roadbed, and reroute some traffic around the area.

When the road is complete, the detour along this stretch of road may cause traffic to be redirected to other nearby roads. To avoid getting lost, please plan your route accordingly.

Thank you for your cooperation.
