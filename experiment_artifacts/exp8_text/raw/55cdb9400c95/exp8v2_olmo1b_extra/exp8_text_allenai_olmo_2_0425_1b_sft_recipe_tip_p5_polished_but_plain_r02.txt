Roasted veggies are a classic side dish that adds flavor and kick to any meal. To make them even more delicious, try seasoning them with a pinch of salt and pepper, a dash of olive oil, or a small amount of chopped fresh herbs like basil, rosemary, or parsley. Add a little lemon juice or a squeeze of lime for a zesty twist. Remember to taste and adjust the seasoning as needed. And don't forget to toss them in the pan with a bit of butter or olive oil to keep them nice and crispy.
