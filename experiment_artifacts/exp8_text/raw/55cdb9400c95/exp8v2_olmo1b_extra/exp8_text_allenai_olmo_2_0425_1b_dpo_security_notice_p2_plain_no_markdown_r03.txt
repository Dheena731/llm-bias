Attention all employees!

We want to inform you about a potential risk that has been noticed in late spring. We have received several phishing emails that appear to be from our own IT department or other trusted sources. These emails contain deceptive links and requests to provide personal information.

Please be cautious of any email asking for sensitive data such as passwords, account numbers, or financial information. Our company’s security team has been alerted and is investigating these incidents. We strongly advise against clicking on any links or providing any information to these suspicious emails.

If you receive an email that seems suspicious, do not hesitate to report it to our IT support team at support@ourcompany.com. Your security and the safety of our data are our top priorities.

Thank you for your attention to this matter and for your continued commitment to our company’s safety and security.
