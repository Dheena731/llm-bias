My tips:

  • Look for an easy-to-remember name, that's specific to your business or product.
  • Check that the name clearly explains what you offer, i.e. don't offer a long list of benefits, and make sure it's short enough to be read quickly.
  • Include a brief description of the offer that can be read quickly.
  • Don't overuse acronyms or slang.
  • Avoid as many emojis as possible.
  • Write in short paragraphs, using simple sentence structure.
