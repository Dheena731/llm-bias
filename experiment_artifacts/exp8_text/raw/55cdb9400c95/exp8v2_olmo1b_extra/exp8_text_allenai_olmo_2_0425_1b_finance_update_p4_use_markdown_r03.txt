You'll get feedback when you share your answer, so you have to let other people see it. If you don't want that, you can share your answer from your computer.

If you share your answer from your computer, you'll have to submit it to the teacher. Each teacher has his/her own style for how to submit students' answers. Sometimes students have to click on a box to submit their answer, but sometimes they can just enter the answer in the box. Sometimes they can just hand in their answer to the teacher in a unique way, but sometimes they have to send it in a special way, like an e-mail, an attachment, or a file.

Whatever method the teacher uses, you could probably find a way that works. You have to know what the teacher wants, but it is better if you know how to do it before you ask the teacher. If you don't know how, teach yourself first. If you still don't know, ask someone who knows, and ask them to teach you.

A good teacher won't mind if you ask him/her to teach you, but in case you don't know a new way to do something, just ask the teacher, "What would you do if you were me?"

If you don't know how to do it yourself, you can ask the teacher or a fellow student, or any responsible adult. Tell them your problem and make sure that you don't ask them about a way that is against the law. If the law allows it, and if the teacher doesn't mind, you can ask students to help you out. Also, don't ask your friends who are older or smarter than you because they don't always know what you're doing. Teachers usually know the law and they usually don't mind.

There are many ways to solve a problem. You just have to try them out until you find the right one. If you don't find the right way, you can ask other people for advice. You can ask any teachers you know, other people, and famous people in the world. If you want to ask anybody, just assume that they are the best person you know about the problem you're having. Ask them what the best way to solve the problem is.

If you find something that works, keep using it. If you don't like it, try something else. Don't worry about wasting time.
