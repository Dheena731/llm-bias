When cooking rice, easing the sticky nature of long-grain varieties requires finesse rather than a quick fix. Here’s a short cooking tip: Prior to cooking your rice, give it a quick rinse under cold water. This helps remove some of the starch, preventing the rice from becoming overly sticky. Cook it according to the package instructions, and you’ll have perfectly prepared rice that maintains its texture without becoming a gluey mess. Remember, the right amount of rice to water ratio, simmering gently rather than fast, and a dry cooking method (as opposed to soaking) will all contribute to achieving less sticky rice. Happy cooking!
