Reviewer 1

The authors have developed a machine learning-based approach which provides a positive model for the identification of instruments that are in conflict with the water regulations. The approach, developed in NLP techniques, is able to incorporate the specific characteristics of the language used in the regulatory documents. In addition, the approach can be applied to any document with the same specific characteristics. The results of the proposed approach are evaluated in comparison with the actual situations (n = 378). The results are encouraging in terms of the accuracy of the solutions derived by the proposed method, with a precision rate of 92.3% and a specificity rate of 69.0%. The authors consider the proposed method as a tool that could be useful to dry-up the delinquency of the water regulations. The authors should consider the fact that the results of the proposed approach have also an error rate of 8.1%, where the accuracy of the model is lower. In conclusion, the authors have developed a framework for the evaluation of the compliance to the water regulations, but the results revealed that there are flaws in the algorithm, so the authors should consider the improvements to the methodology.

Reviewer 2

The authors first introduce the problem they seek to solve and then, they present their solution and then, the authors show the evaluation grid and show the results for the proposed approach to identify the instruments that are in conflict with the water regulations. The authors did not introduce the problem they solved in the introduction, which is frustrating. The authors did not include any results in the results part. The authors did not show to what extent the proposed solution performs compared to other approaches proposed in the scientific literature. The authors did not show in the evaluation grid that the proposed solution is right below or even better the state of the art. The authors did not show that the results are relevant to the scientific literature.

Reviewer 3

The authors' proposed approach has the potential to address the water regulation enforcement gaps in the United States. Their approach uses a machine learning-based approach to identify instrument misapplications in regulatory documents. The authors summarize the challenges in the current law enforcement and the use of machine learning for water regulations. The authors report on the literature review and outline the machine learning approach and the associated evaluation methods. The authors report on the performance of their approach for various parameters. The authors discuss the approach and the limitations. The authors conclude by suggesting research directions for future work based on their results. The authors clearly explain their methodology and assumptions in the introduction and the methods sections. The authors provide an evaluation of their approach using evaluation metrics and metrics from the literature, which is informative. The authors have included additional data in the supplemental material. The authors have included raw data in the supplemental material and have included a table of measures. The authors report on the results for several other parameters. The authors have provided results for several parameters but have not reported on the performance of the proposed approach compared to other approaches. The authors report on the performance of their approach for the parameters of interest. The authors have included data that was provided to them in the form of raw text documents. The authors have performed a comparison of their approach to other approaches and their approach is comparable to other approaches. The authors have reported the results for the parameters of interest and the results of the comparison of their approach to other approaches. The authors conclude by suggesting future research. The authors have clearly described their work and proposed method.
