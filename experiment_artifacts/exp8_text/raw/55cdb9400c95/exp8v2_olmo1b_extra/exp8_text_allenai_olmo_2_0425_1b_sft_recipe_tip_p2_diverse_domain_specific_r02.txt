Herb Steward: Tips for Fresh Herb Storage

Herbs can be a staple in your kitchen and are great for flavoring your meals, but they tend to lose their freshness quickly if not stored properly. Here are some tips to help you store fresh herbs perfectly:

1. Use airtight containers: If you're storing dried herbs, opt for airtight glass or plastic containers to prevent them from drying out over time. If you're storing fresh herbs, use airtight plastic bags or jars with a damp paper towel on top.

2. Keep them in the refrigerator: If you're using fresh herbs frequently, it's best to store them in the refrigerator. Place them in a bowl or container with a damp paper towel and put the container in the crisper drawer of your fridge.

3. Check frequently: It's important to check your herbs regularly to make sure they're still fresh. The shelf life of herbs is typically 3-4 days, but this can vary depending on the type of herb and how fresh they are.

4. Remove excess moisture: If your herbs are stored in a humid environment like a cabinet, be sure to remove excess moisture to prevent mold growth. You can do this by placing a bowl of water beside the container and letting the herbs absorb the moisture.

5. Use them soon: It's best to use your herbs as soon as possible after they've been harvested. If they're drying out, you can use them in your cooking to add more flavor instead.

By following these tips, you'll be able to store your fresh herbs properly and keep them fresh and flavorful for longer. Happy cooking!
