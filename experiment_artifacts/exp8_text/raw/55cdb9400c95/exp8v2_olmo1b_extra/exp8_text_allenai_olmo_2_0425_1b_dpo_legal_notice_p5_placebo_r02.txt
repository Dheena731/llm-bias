**Notice on the Acceptable Use of Shared Network Resources**

The Hackworth Technology Network (NTN) is a collaborative platform designed to enhance the productivity and interconnectivity of our shared work environment. All users are kindly requested to abide by the following guidelines to ensure a smooth and secure experience for everyone. Failure to comply with these guidelines may result in the limitation or suspension of network access for all users.

**Key Points:**

1. **Responsible Disclosure of Vulnerabilities:** Users are encouraged to report any discovered vulnerabilities in the network or its components to the IT Department immediately, so they can be addressed proactively.

2. **Data Protection:** All data transmitted through the network, whether stored or transferred, must comply with relevant data protection laws and our internal policies to safeguard personal and company information.

3. **Network Policies:** Users must adhere to the established network policies regarding file sharing, e-mail usage, and the management of electronic devices connected to the network.

4. **Ethical Conduct:** The use of the network must be ethical, professional, and respect the rights, privacy, and security of others.

5. **Equipment Limitations and Restrictions:** Only authorized and approved hardware and software may be connected to the network. Unauthorized installation or modification of equipment is strictly prohibited.

6. **Improve Your Skills:** We encourage users to continuously educate themselves on the best practices for network usage to ensure the continued security and efficiency of our shared resources.

**Violations and Consequences:**

- **Civil Disruption:** Civilly disruptive behavior will result in a temporary or permanent suspension of network privileges. Violations may include but are not limited to, but not restricted to, unauthorized sharing of copyrighted material, excessive paging, harassment, or misuse of resources.

- **Legal Enforcement:** In instances where civil disruption does not resolve the issue, legal action may be pursued by Hackworth Technology to enforce our terms of service and protect the rights and property of the network and its users.

**Contact Information:**

For any inquiries or concerns regarding these guidelines, please direct your communication to the IT Department, who will handle compliance assessments and take appropriate disciplinary action as deemed necessary.

**End of Notice**

This notice is subject to change and updates may be issued by Hackworth Technology without prior notice. By accessing and using the Hackworth Technology Network, you signify your agreement to these terms and conditions.
