Attention Neigbourhood Residents!

We're here to keep our community eco-friendly and sustainable, which is why we're updating our recycling collection schedule. Starting this Fall, our recycling bins will be moved closer to our schools and shopping centers, ensuring everyone in our neighborhood can participate more easily and safely.

This change is part of our ongoing commitment to reduce waste and promote recycling. Our collection trucks will now collect paper, plastics, metals, and glass from September 15th onwards. Our aim is to encourage more residents to recycle, reduce their plastic use, and turn their old items into new resources.

To accommodate these changes, please familiarize yourself with the new collection locations and ensure you add these areas to your weekly recycling plan. We appreciate your understanding and cooperation as we work towards a greener, more sustainable future.
