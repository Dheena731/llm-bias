What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of data sent from a website and stored on your device by your browser while you are browsing it. They are usually used to store information such as browsing history, user preferences, or session identifiers.

Why Do We Use Cookies?

We use cookies to enable us to provide an improved user experience and to enable us to analyze the effectiveness of our web presentation. By doing so, we can gather information about how users navigate the website, what information is most appealing to them, and what features might need improvement, thus enhancing our services and tailoring web content to better suit user needs.

What Types of Cookies Do We Use?

We use two types of cookies: 

1. **Session Cookies:** These cookies are used while you are browsing the site and are automatically deleted when you close your browser. Session cookies are used to enhance the site’s functionality by remembering your preferences, such as language or theme settings.

2. **Persistent Cookies:** These cookies remain on your device after your browser is closed. They are used to store information over multiple visits to the site, such as user preferences or shopping cart contents.

We use the information we collect from cookies to personalize our website, analyze how our site is used, and to improve our website’s performance. For example, we might use information about your browsing behavior to tailor content according to your interests.

You can control and manage the cookies on our site by adjusting your browser’s settings. For instance, you can usually tell your browser to accept all cookies, to refuse all cookies, or to ask for permission to accept cookies before they are set.
