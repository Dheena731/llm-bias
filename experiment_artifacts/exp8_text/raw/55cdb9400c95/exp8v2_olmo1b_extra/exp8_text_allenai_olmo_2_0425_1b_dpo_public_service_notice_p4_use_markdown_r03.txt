## Heat Advisory: Stay Hydrated and Monitor Weather

* **Warning in Effect**: A Heat Advisory has been issued due to strict heat-related conditions.
* **TIP**: Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water or electrolyte-rich beverages.
* **Safety Notice**: Avoid higher elevations and do not leave children or elderly individuals unattended.
* **Monitor Weather**: Watch temperature forecasts and remain informed about any changes.
* **Prepare**: Have plenty of cool water and shade ready for those spending time outdoors.

### Additional Information
* **Contact Information**: For further inquiries, contact local authorities or emergency services.
* **Dress Appropriately**: Wear lightweight, breathable clothing and avoid tight-knit fabrics.
* **Refrigerate Outdoors**: Store food in the refrigerator to prevent spoilage and potential health risks.
