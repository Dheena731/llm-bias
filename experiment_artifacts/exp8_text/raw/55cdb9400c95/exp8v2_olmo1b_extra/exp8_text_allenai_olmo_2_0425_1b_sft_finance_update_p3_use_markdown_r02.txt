# New Savings Goal Feature Announced

This update brings a significant addition to our financial management system, featuring a new feature that allows users to set and track savings goals. Users can now create budgets that allocate funds towards specific savings targets, enabling them to visualize their progress and stay motivated.

- **Goal Creation**: Users can effortlessly create a new savings goal by selecting "New Savings Goal" from the goal management section.
- **Progress Tracking**: The system updates in real-time, showing users how much they have saved toward their target.
- **Customization**: Goals can be customized with different levels of detail, including the amount to be saved, time frame, and categories.
- **Notification System**: Goals receive automatic reminders and notifications, ensuring users never miss a saving chance.

This new feature is designed to enhance users' financial planning experience, providing clarity and a sense of accomplishment as they work towards their savings goals.
