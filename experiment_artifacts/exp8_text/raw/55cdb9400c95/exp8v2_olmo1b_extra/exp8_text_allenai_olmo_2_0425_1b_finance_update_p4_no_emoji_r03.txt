An end-of-year tax statement to explain?

The end-of-year tax statement will be the one that your financial adviser or tax professional prepares for you at the end of the 12-month tax year. Although this statement is not a legal requirement, many clients who want to review their tax situation will send a copy to their accountant so they can look over it with them. Your financial advisor may also ask you to send a copy of the end-of-year tax statement to your tax professional. If you are not sure what to send, your financial advisor may be able to guide you on this.

Is an end of year tax statement the same as a tax return?

An end of year tax statement is a statement filed with the government tax office in the form of a printout or electronic copy that shows the tax you paid during the tax year and the taxes you owe to the government.

What are the most common mistakes that happen when preparing an end of year tax statement?

  1. Many people start to prepare their tax statements in the middle of January, before they have even received all the documents they need to prepare the statement.
  2. Some people do not prepare a 1040 or 1040-SR tax statement until after they have received their tax bills and W2s from their employer.
  3. Some people do not realize that their filing requirements change once they switch jobs.
  4. Some people do not realize that there are numerous forms that are required to file their tax return from the IRS.
  5. Some people do not realize that they can claim certain expenses that are not included on their W2s and 1099s.
