We are alerting our employees to a potential phishing email threat that has been circulating our organization. To protect your personal information and prevent any unauthorized access or damage, please be on guard against phishing attempts.

A phishing email is an attempt to trick you into revealing sensitive information like passwords, credit card details, or personal information by masquerading as a trustworthy entity such as a colleague, vendor, or legitimate company. Here are some key indicators to recognize a phishing attempt:

- The sender is unfamiliar and uses generic greetings.
- The email contains urgent requests to act immediately.
- The email has poor grammar or looks unprofessional.
- The sender claims to be from a source you know but has a typo or unusual spelling.
- The request asks you to click on a link to a website that resembles a company website but has subtle variations.

To help prevent falling victim to phishing attempts, we encourage the following actions:

- Do not click on any links in unsolicited emails.
- Verify the sender’s identity by contacting them directly through official channels.
- Never provide sensitive information in response to unsolicited requests.
- Keep software up to date to protect against known vulnerabilities.
