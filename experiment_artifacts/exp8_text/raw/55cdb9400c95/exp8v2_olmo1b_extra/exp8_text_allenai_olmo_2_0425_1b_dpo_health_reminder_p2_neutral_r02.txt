Stay hydrated by drinking enough water throughout the day! Water is crucial for nearly every function in our bodies—maintaining healthy skin, lubricating joints, aiding digestion, and supporting all bodily processes. Keep a water bottle nearby, especially during the day's more dynamic activities like meetings or walking. Aim for about 8 glasses (eight 8-ounce glasses) of water per day, but this can vary depending on your activity level, climate, and health needs. Remember, being well-hydrated can help you focus, maintain a healthy weight, and increase your energy levels.
