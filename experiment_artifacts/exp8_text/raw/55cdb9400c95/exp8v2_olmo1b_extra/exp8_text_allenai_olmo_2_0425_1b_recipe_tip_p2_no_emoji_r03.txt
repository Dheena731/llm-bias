A person's actions in the first stage reflect the effects and consequences of their thinking. An action without a thought eventually leads to a dead end. Conversely, an action with a thought will have an effect. However, the action has to be aligned with the thought. And the effect has to be aligned with the thought. So the question is how we align our thoughts with our actions. How do we execute our thoughts?

In the second stage, the actions have consequences. These are experienced as actions, but also as emotions, feelings, and sensations. In the third stage, we experience the consequences of these actions as thoughts. And these perceived thoughts are again linked to the first stage. This is what we call a self-perpetuating circle. Anything within this circle will continue in the same way.

The first question about the circle is whether it is a circle with a beginning or an end. Does it start with a thought or an action? And for how long does it go on? We can therefore divide the circle into 2 parts: the beginning part and the end part. It is not as simple as it seems. It is not even as simple as the beginning or the end. It is a circle with an ongoing loop. Unless we are aware of the loop, we will perceive that everything happens by chance. And we will assume that the current situation is the result of the previous situation. And that the next situation will be the same as the current one. This will make us complacent and feel that we are living in a world of chance and fate. This is to be avoided. If we want to move forward, we need to see the beginning of the loop, understand it and break it. If we want to change the course of our life, we need to see the end of the loop, understand it and break it.

A few easy suggestions for the beginning and end of the loop.

For the beginning, bring attention to what you are doing. Be aware of your thoughts, the thoughts of others, feelings, intentions, the meaning of your actions, etc. If you are aware of it, you can re-align your thoughts with your actions. For example, if you think that you have to stay home, and you have to stay home, then you will work on staying home. Attention brings clarity and clarity brings wholeness. If the situation is not what you want, then you need to change it. It is good to have a way to move forward. And changing the course is always an option.

For the end, bring attention to what you are thinking. You can also bring your attention to the thoughts of others, feelings, intentions, the meaning of your actions, etc. If you are aware of it, you can correct the thinking and move forward. If the current situation is not what you want, then you need to change it. It is good to have a way to move forward. And that is what the final stage of the loop is for.

A few suggestions for the corrective thinking.

The first step is to bring attention to the thoughts that are relevant for you. You can ask what you are thinking about and why. What is your thinking about? How does your thinking affect the situation? How does the situation affect your thinking? And so on. You can also ask what you should be thinking about and why. What should you be thinking about and why? And so on. The thinking has to be aligned with what you want to experience. If it is not, you need to find a way to align it. This may lead you to a solution. And it may also lead you to a solution.

The final stage is to correct your thinking. What do you want to think about? How do you want to think about it? What is your thought?
