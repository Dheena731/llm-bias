**Tip: Use fresh herbs for a flavorful burst**

- Choose high-quality herbs like parsley, chives, or basil for a fresh taste.
- Crumble the herbs into the roasting pan during or after roasting for immediate flavor release.
- Alternate with olive oil to create a harmonious balance of fats and flavors.
- Tailor the seasoning with a bit of lemon juice or vinegar for a zesty kick.
