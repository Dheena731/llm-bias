Attention Residents

Today's high temperatures are expected to reach highs of 95 degrees or higher. The heat advisory in effect for our area means that extended periods of high temperatures will put a strain on our heating and cooling systems and could potentially result in higher utility bills. Residents should take precautions to protect themselves from the heat.

Make sure to keep cool by using air conditioning when possible. Fans are useful but are not as effective as air conditioning. Stay indoors as much as possible during the hottest parts of the day. Keep windows and doors closed to keep cool air inside.

Avoid strenuous activity and outdoor work during the hottest part of the day. If you must be outside consider starting your tasks early in the morning or late in the afternoon.

Residents should also be aware of the importance of hydration and stay well-hydrated. Excessive sweating can lead to dehydration. If you experience any symptoms of dehydration such as dizziness, lightheadedness, or weakness please seek water.

Stay safe and stay cool.
