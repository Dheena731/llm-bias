Attention all employees

We have received reports of phishing emails being sent to our staff. Please be aware of suspicious emails containing links or attachments that appear to come from trusted sources. If you receive such emails, please do not click on any links or download any attachments. Instead, report them immediately to your supervisor or our IT department. We are working hard to ensure your information remains secure and are committed to protecting you and your colleagues from potential risks. Thank you for your vigilance and cooperation.
