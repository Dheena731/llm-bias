**What is Phishing?**
Phishing is a type of social engineering attack that involves the use of deceptive emails, messages, or websites to steal sensitive information. These fraudulent communications mimic legitimate communications from trusted sources, such as your IT department, your employer, or even a client, to trick you into revealing valuable information such as usernames, passwords, credit card numbers, social security numbers, and more.

**How to Spot Phishing:**
- **Unexpected Source:** Be wary of emails that come from an unknown sender or those from sources you wouldn’t expect.
- **Unsolicited Requests:** Phishing emails often make unwanted demands for personal information or action. 
- **Poor Grammar and Spelling:** Legitimate companies, especially those with proper security measures, usually rectify such minor issues.
- **Avoid Clicking Links:** Hover over links before clicking to see the actual URL to confirm it’s where you think it is.
- **Verify Information:** If an email requests sensitive information, verify with the company’s official communication channels.
