# Weekly Fitness Progress Review

- **Exercise Selection**: Review which exercises you've included in your routine this week. Consider the variety of exercises you're incorporating to ensure a balanced workout.

- **Intensity Levels**: Assess how intense your workouts were this week. A good indicator of progress is the level of difficulty you've maintained throughout.

- **Recovery**: Note any soreness or fatigue after your workout. Moderate to low levels are usually a sign of a healthy recovery process.

- **Progress in Goals**: Consider if you've met any of your fitness goals for the week. If you haven't, think about what you can change or adjust.

- **Strategies**: Reflect on what strategies you've employed to stay motivated or improve your workout sessions. This could include visualization, setting a routine, or using fitness tracking apps.

- **Productivity**: Note how your time was spent aside from working out—this includes meals, sleep, and leisure activities. Consider if these aspects can be optimized for better health outcomes.

- **Entertainment**: If you've found ways to keep yourself entertained like listening to music or light exercise during your commute, make note of these strategies and their positive impact.

- **Mindset**: Evaluate how you've felt mentally throughout the week. Positive mindset is key to lasting fitness progress.

- **Physical Health**: Reflect on any physical symptoms or concerns. If you felt unusually fatigued, dizzy, or experienced muscle pain, these can be indicators of potential health issues that are worth discussing with a healthcare professional.
