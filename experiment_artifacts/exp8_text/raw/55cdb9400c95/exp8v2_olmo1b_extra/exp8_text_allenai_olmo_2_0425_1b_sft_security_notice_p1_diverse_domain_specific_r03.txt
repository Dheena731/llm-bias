Attention, all team members!

It is a critical reminder that we should never reuse passwords across our various online accounts, such as email, social media, banking, and more. This is one of the most significant security weaknesses that could expose your company to massive data breaches.

If you have used a password for an email account on a different service and have not changed it, we strongly advise you to change it immediately. It is recommended to use a unique password for each of your online accounts. Passwords should be complex, hard to guess and should not be easily recognizable.

We repeat – please Never Reuse Passwords!

Thank you for your attention to this matter. Please take a moment to update your passwords and subscribe to our newsletter for more information on security and safety.
