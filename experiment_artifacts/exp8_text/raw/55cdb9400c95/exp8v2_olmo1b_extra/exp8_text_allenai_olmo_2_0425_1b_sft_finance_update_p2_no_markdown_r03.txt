The card transaction fees have been adjusted to include a percentage fee per transaction. This fee applies on top of the assessed transaction costs, increasing the overall cost of processing card transactions. The purpose of this fee is to incentivize businesses to accept more transactions in-store and to encourage store-based e-commerce. The fee is calculated based on the assessed cost of processing the transaction and is applied evenly to the total transaction cost, including the assessed cost, returned goods cost, and storage cost.
