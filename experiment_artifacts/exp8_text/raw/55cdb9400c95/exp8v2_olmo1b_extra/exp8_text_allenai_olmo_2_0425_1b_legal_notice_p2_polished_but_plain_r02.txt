Use the style guide and one of the templates for a legal notice. This is also a good time to think about the kind of information you want in the notice and how to present it to users.


If you are adding a new privacy policy, you must update any existing privacy policy if you have one. Do this by first reading the existing policy, and then copy and paste the text of the new privacy policy into the existing policy document, carefully adjusting the text as necessary.

If you have no existing privacy policy, you must draft a new one that is appropriate for your business. You are responsible for all the terms of your privacy policy. Read the information about creating a privacy policy for information on broadening your privacy policy.

Your privacy policy should include or refer to the following points:

  • what information you collect, who you collect it from, how you collect it, and who you share it with. You need to describe, in plain language, the ways you collect your data, the types of data you collect and about whom you collect it, and how you manage the data you collect.
  • what information you keep and make available, including how long you keep it, and how you manage it.
  • what you share about your users with other companies, and how you share their data.
  • your process for handling data breaches, including when and how you notify your users about these breaches.
  • your privacy practices in general, such as how you check in with users, how you let them know about product updates, and how you protect them from security risks.

Make this content available to users in plain language. This means that it should be written so that an average person can understand it without any background knowledge of technology. It should be written in a way that is easy to understand.

You should also indicate the ways users can opt out of the use of their data.

Don't use legal language, and don't include terms that are not applicable to your business. For example, don't use legal language to describe your privacy policies if your business model is not typically related to the legal industry.

Give users access to the policy at any time. Make it available to them in plain language, and make it easy for users to view, print, and print selectively.

The notice must include a clear link to the information on your website where users can read the privacy policy. Make sure that the privacy policy can be found at the top of the website. It must be easy for visitors to access it from any page on your website.

This is a great time to make a change to the privacy policy. You need to make the text of the policy as simple as possible for users to understand. Don't use legal terms or language that doesn't apply to your business. Make sure that the policy is easy to see and easy to understand. Don't bury the policy in the middle of your website.
