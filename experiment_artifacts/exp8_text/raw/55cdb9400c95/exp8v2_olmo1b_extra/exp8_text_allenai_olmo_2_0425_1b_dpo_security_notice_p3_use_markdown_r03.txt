**Reminder: Secure Your Screens**

- Lock your screens as soon as you finish working to prevent unauthorized access.
- Consider using a strong password and enabling biometric authentication if available.
- Avoid leaving your desk with your screen still unlocked, especially if you're using a public resource.
- Remind colleagues to do the same to maintain a secure work environment.
- Update your biometric authentication if it's security-limited, to ensure the highest level of protection.

Remember, a secure workstation is a safe workstation!
