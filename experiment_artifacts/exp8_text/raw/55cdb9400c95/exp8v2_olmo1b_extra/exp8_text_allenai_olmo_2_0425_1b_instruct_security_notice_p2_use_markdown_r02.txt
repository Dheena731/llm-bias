### Warning about Phishing Emails

* **Identify the Email:**
  - Be cautious of emails asking for sensitive information or prompting immediate action by threatening consequences.
  
* **Check the Source:**
  - Hover over any links in the email to verify the URL, and look for unusual domain extensions or spelling errors.
  
* **Do Not Click on Suspicious Links:**
  - Avoid clicking on links in emails that appear suspicious, especially those claiming to be from your company's IT department or HR.
  
* **Report Phishing Attempts:**
  - Document the phishing attempt and report it to your IT department immediately.
  
* **Educate Team:**
  - Raise awareness about phishing emails within your team to prevent future incidents.
