Goal: To reduce the risk of promotion fatigue

How Long? How Long? How Long?

TL;DR: The longer you can go without needing to force viewers to take breaks, the less likely you are to get overpromoted or have a running joke with your audience.

This is doubly problematic in the case of new things because everyone’s worried about you breathing down their neck, but it still applies to established things. The longer you can go without making viewers take breaks, the less likely they’re going to get tired of you.

This is doubly problematic because you may be one of the first organizations to use this technology, and you want to make sure you’re not overpromoting your work.

Using this strategy, you can build up your audience slowly and steadily, serving it just enough content that it doesn’t get too full and loses interest. This gives you a better chance of getting your work the attention it deserves.

Again, let’s use the example of the Firewatch game. 

As of December 2022, the game has used the following trial periods, with the most recent being released in March of 2023:

  • Early Access: 5 months (Aug 2018)
  • Beta: 4.5 months (Apr 2022)
  • Pro: 14 months (July 2022)
  • Released: 6.5 months (Nov 2022)

This is a good way to build momentum and reduce the risk of burnout. 

Pro Tip: The more frequently and more quickly you can update your game, the less likely it is to lose viewers since it’ll be in a constant state of use.

Examples Of Long-Form Digital Video

As a rule of thumb, I tend to suggest that video take up at least one third of the amount of video you’re writing about. 

This doesn’t always work, since sometimes you need to have a longer video for its purpose. 

For example, you can have a 10-minute video or a 30-minute video, depending on the length needed for the topic. 

For the most part, however, you should aim for one-third of your video to be digital video. 

If you’re not sure, be sure to consult with other writers or professionals who are experts in their field.

Examples Of Short-Form Digital Video

In some cases, you might want to call for the use of video to introduce your idea. 

It isn’t always necessary for every. But it can help your audience understand what they’re trying to learn.

Examples Of Web Element Video

It can be useful to have a web element video, which is the video that is usually used to introduce the item. 

YouTube is a great place to get this kind of video because it has a bunch of them. 

They’re usually short, but they’re often quite effective at letting people know what they’re about to read. 

They’re also usually quite easy to use because they’re often fairly short and simple to understand. 

This is especially true if you’re using the web element video for something that is a little more complicated or complicated.
