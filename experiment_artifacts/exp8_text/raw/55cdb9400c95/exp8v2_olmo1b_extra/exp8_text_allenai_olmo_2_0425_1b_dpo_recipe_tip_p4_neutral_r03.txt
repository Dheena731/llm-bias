Here's a short tip for planning lunches for a busy week: Set aside a narrow window each weekday to prepare and pack lunches. Choose meals that require minimal preparation, like easy sandwiches or homemade soups. Prepare a variety of options to keep meals interesting. Use reusable containers or lunchboxes to minimize waste and make cleaning up a breeze. Don't forget to include healthy snacks or small meals like a fruit or a handful of nuts. Always have your lunches ready to go in their containers so you can focus on enjoying your day.
