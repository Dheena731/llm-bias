For a quick and tasty way to season roasted vegetables—whether they're for a side salad, dinner, or a healthy snack—consider this tip: Instead of relying on expensive, pre-packaged spices, simply toss them directly into the pan right before serving. This method infuses the vegetables with vibrant flavors from the spices. For an authentic kick, sprinkle in a pinch of chili flakes, smoked paprika, cumin, or any other seasonings you like. Alternatively, you can also choose aromatic ingredients like rosemary, thyme, or garlic for a more nuanced taste. Remember, less is often more when it comes to seasoning, so don't overdo it.
