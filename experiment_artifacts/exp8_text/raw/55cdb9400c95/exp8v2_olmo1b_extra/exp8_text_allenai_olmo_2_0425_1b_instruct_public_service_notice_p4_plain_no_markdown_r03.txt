Attention citizens!

Today we are issuing a heat advisory due to the high temperatures expected to persist throughout the day. The mercury is expected to rise well above normal levels which may result in the following concerns:

* Extreme heat can be dangerous to the very young old and those with heart or health conditions. It’s especially important to stay cool and monitor your body temperature.
* Older adults should be cautious as well since prolonged exposure to heat can increase the risk of dehydration and other health issues for seniors.
* Children, especially those under the age of five, should be kept in an air-conditioned environment if possible and encouraged to drink plenty of water.
* Pets are also at risk from the heat so keep them in cool places and ensure they have access to water.

Remaining vigilant of these conditions is crucial as we work together to keep you safe and comfortable. Stay hydrated and stay indoors when possible. If the heat becomes unbearable please seek relief in air conditioning and avoid outdoor activities that put you at risk. Thank you for your cooperation and attention to this matter.
