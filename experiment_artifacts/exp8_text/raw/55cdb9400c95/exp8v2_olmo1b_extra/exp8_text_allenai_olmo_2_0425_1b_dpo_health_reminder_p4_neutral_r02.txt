Here's a short reminder: Take regular short breaks from screens to recharge your body and mind. This can help reduce eye strain, improve focus, and even boost your mood. These breaks should last about 10-15 minutes, and make sure your screen is at a comfortable distance and the lighting is adequate. Try activities like taking a walk, doing some light stretching, or simply focusing on a book or a piece of art. Remember, a healthy balance between screen time and downtime is key.
