# Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

- **Gather Your Information**
  - Identify your email address and other required account information required for 2FA setup.

- **Register an Option**
  - Choose the method of 2FA you prefer (e.g., SMS, authenticator app, or hardware token).

- **Set Up 2FA**
  - Practice setting up the 2FA option in your preferred app, so you’re comfortable with the process.

- **Securing Your Account**
  - Review the steps to recover or change your 2FA settings in case something happens to your chosen method.

- **Review and Set Up**
  - Assess your new 2FA settings and ensure any changes are implemented correctly.

  - Revisit your account settings to ensure all necessary applications have 2FA enabled.

- **Keep Your Account Safe**
  - Monitor your notifications for new 2FA entries or instructions to reset or change settings to prevent unauthorized access.
