Report a Lost Company Laptop

If your company laptop has been lost or stolen please take the following steps to report the incident:

1. **Locate the Lost/Lifelong Whereas Locations**
   - Inventory database: Verify if the laptop is recovered from any lost and found locations, offices, or meeting rooms.
   - Security surveillance footage: Check any security cameras that might have captured the incident.
   - Employee recall: Contact all employees to check if the device was last seen in their workspaces or laptops.

2. **Report the Incident**
   - **Email:** Send a formal email to IT or accounting and include all relevant details such as the make, model, serial number, and any photos of the device.
   - **Intranet/Facebook/Company messaging:** Use the company intranet, Facebook, or other company communication channels to alert employees.
   - **Internal database:** Report the item lost to the company's IT department or lost and found system.

3. **Document and File**
   - Keep a record of the incident including all communication channels used.
   - Fill out any incident reporting forms that may be required.

4. **Follow-Up**
   - Schedule a follow-up with IT to confirm that the report has been logged.
   - Stay informed about the laptop's status through the company's IT department or loss prevention department.

5. **Legal Action**
   - If the laptop is found, verify if any legal action is required by consulting with your IT department or legal counsel.

**Please act promptly to prevent further loss and ensure the device is accounted for.**
