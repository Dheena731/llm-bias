Polling stations are typically located in multiple locations across the country. Find the most recent and updated information on polling station locations by visiting the official election website. There, you will find detailed information about all polling stations and their locations. 

Some locations such as schools or libraries may not be open to the public and are only open to the public via an online form for voting. However, to ensure you are in one of the designated polling stations, it is advised to verify the location through the official website.
