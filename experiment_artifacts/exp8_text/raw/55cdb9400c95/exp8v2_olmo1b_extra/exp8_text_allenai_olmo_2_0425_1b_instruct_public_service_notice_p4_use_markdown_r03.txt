**Heat Advisory**

**Heading:**
Keep cool and stay safe!

* **Explanation:**
A heat advisory is in effect due to high temperatures and potential health risks. Stay indoors and drink plenty of water to stay hydrated.

* **Actions:**
*   Avoid outside work or exercise.
*   Seek shade and cool showers.
*   Monitor your body temperature and seek medical attention if needed.

* **Stay informed:**
Check local weather apps and listen to radio broadcasts for updates on the advisory status and additional safety tips.
