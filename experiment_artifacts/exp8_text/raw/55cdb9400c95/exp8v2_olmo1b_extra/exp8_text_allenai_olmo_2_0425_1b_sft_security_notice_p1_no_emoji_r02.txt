Password reuse is a riskier method to maintain your online account security. Password reuse happens when you use the same password for different websites. Password reuse is more likely to lead to an account compromise or being watched by hackers when a weak password is used on multiple platforms. 
Use separate credentials for your online accounts. The use of technology today requires many passwords. Remember to use a secure password manager to help you organize and remember your passwords. Password managers will help you generate and store strong passwords that you can use on different websites. 
For a more secure password, make sure to keep your passwords safe. Never keep them in plain text when you write them down. Make sure your password's characters are complex. Consider using a combination of letters, numbers, and special characters. 
Use your password manager to store all of your passwords. 
Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining an online secure environment.
