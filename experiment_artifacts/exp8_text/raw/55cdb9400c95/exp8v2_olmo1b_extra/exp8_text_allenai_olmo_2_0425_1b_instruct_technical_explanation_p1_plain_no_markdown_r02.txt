Latency is a critical factor in the performance of web applications because it affects how quickly users can interact with your site. When someone visits your website, the data they receive initially travels to their device over the internet. The time it takes for that data to travel and be processed is known as latency. Lower latency means fewer delays and a smoother user experience. For example, if a web application takes a few seconds to load a page, even small delays could frustrate the user. In contrast, a website with minimal latency can boast higher satisfaction and more consistent performance. This consideration of latency is especially important in high-demand applications like online gaming, video streaming, real-time communication apps, and online commerce, where every millisecond counts for a better user experience and competitive edge.
