The +0.25% fee on each card transaction has been an important part of the credit card industry. ATMs are usually free to use but require a monthly fee per card. Card companies are likely to allow their customers to transfer money from their accounts to their personal, debit and bank accounts, but they will not allow the same number of free transfers. Banks may also charge a fee per transaction. This fee will usually be calculated in euros per transaction, and may be lower for withdrawals than for deposits.

While it is possible to transfer money to your own or someone else’s account, you may have to pay a fee if you do this. You may also be charged a fee if you transfer money from an account held in a foreign currency to your own currency.

However, not only are these fees not necessary, they are also much too high. They should be zero.
