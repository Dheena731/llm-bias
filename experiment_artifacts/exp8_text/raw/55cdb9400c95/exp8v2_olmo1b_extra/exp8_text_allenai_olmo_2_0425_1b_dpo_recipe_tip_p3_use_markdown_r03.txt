**Make Rice Less Sticky**

- **Rinse Thoroughly:** Start by rinsing the rice under cold water for about 5 minutes to remove any excess starch.
- **Soak Thrice:** Soak rice in water for about 30 minutes to absorb starch.
- **Drain the Water:** After soaking, drain the rice completely and set it aside.
- **Use A Dry Pot:** Cook rice in a dry pot to prevent sticking.
- **Avoid Over-Cooking:** Only cook rice until it is al dente, stop before it becomes mushy.
- **Add Acidic Fluids:** If sticky, add a splash of lemon juice or vinegar to help balance the starch content.
