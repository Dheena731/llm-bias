**Reminder**:
- Always follow the doctor's instructions and the prescription label for refilling your medication.
- Do not skip doses unless instructed by your healthcare provider.
- Make sure the bottle or package label matches the one you initially received.
- Keep all medication in its original container to avoid potential mix-ups.
- Return any unused medication to your pharmacist for proper disposal.
- Refill prescriptions before they expire if possible to avoid late fees or expiration.
- Safely store your medications in a cool, dry place away from children and pets.
- Regularly review your medication schedule with your healthcare provider to ensure you're following their plan.
- Remember that refilling prescriptions before the expiration date not only ensures you remain compliant with your prescribed regimen but also protects your health by preventing potential toxicity from expired medication.
