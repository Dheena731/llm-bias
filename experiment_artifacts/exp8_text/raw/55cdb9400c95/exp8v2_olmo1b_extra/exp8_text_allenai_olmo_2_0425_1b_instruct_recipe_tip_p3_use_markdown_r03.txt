**Short Headline: Don't Over-Soak Your Rice**

*  
* Avoid soaking your rice for too long, as this can make it too sticky.  
* Drain and rinse your rice thoroughly before cooking to remove any excess moisture.  
* Use slightly less water when cooking to prevent the rice from becoming too sticky.  
* Let the rice cook covered until almost done, stirring occasionally to prevent overcooking.  
* Finish cooking the rice just before serving to maintain its firm texture.
