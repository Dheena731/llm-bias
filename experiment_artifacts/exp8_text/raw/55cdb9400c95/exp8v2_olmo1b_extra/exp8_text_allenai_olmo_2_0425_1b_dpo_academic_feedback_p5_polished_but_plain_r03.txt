Citations are crucial in academic writing because they provide the reader with the evidence and context necessary to understand the arguments and sources being cited. When citations are missing from an essay, readers are left to wonder about the validity of the claims being made and may question the credibility of the author's work. Ultimately, a lack of citations weakens the argument and dampens the impact of the essay. To bolster your essays, ensure you include all relevant citations, backing up your main points with verifiable evidence from reputable sources. This practice not only garners academic respect but also enhances the reader's understanding and appreciation of the content.
