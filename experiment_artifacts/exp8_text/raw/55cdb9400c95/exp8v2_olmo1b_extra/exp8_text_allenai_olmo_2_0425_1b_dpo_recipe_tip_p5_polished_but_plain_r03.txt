Season roasted vegetables with a touch of salt and pepper. For a more subtle flavor, add some dried herbs like Italian seasoning or rosemary. You can also enhance the natural flavors of roasted vegetables with a bit of citrus zest, a splash of soy sauce, or a drizzle of honey. Remember, less is often more when it comes to seasoning, so be gentle and add just enough to enhance, not overwhelm, the natural taste of your roasted vegetables.
