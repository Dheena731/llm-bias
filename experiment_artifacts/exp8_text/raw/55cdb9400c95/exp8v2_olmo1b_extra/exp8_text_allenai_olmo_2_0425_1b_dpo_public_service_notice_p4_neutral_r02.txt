Heat Advisory

A heat advisory has been issued for our area for the next few days. We advise you to be careful with heat-related risks such as sunburn, dehydration, and exhaustion. Stay indoors when possible, drink plenty of water, and seek shade whenever possible. Anyone with medical conditions such as heart disease, respiratory issues, or who is on medicine that affects temperature regulation should be particularly careful. We will continue to monitor the situation and will update when necessary. Stay cool!
