Recycling collection days are running a bit differently from last week. Here is the rundown on what's changed:

* It's now Friday, Saturday, and Sunday. That's three days per week.
* Regular collection days on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Sunday remain the same.
* Instead of a single collection window, you now have two separate days.
* One day is for single-stream recycling (bags at the curb) and the other for glass, plastic, and metal.
* Glass, plastic, and metal need to be placed in specific bins to prevent contamination.
* Be sure to check the local city website or contact your recycling service for specific instructions.

Don't worry—it’s just an adjustment to better manage our resources for everyone. Recycling continues to be a critical part of our efforts to reduce waste and protect our environment. Stay organized and keep your recycling sorted, and we’ll all help keep our community clean and sustainable!
