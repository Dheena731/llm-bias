The Tech Center has adopted a set of acceptable use policies for the shared network.

Unauthorized access or disruption of network activity is strictly prohibited. Ways to do so include but are not limited to: 
- Using unauthorized phones, laptops, or other devices to connect to the network.
- Using software to manipulate or alter the network or its traffic.
- Installing any device that leads to the interception of information or the disruption of network services. 
- Cursing, obscene, or threatening language.
- Excessive use of files or downloads.
- Using the network for any activity that is illegal or against the law. 
- Utilizing the network for any activity that may distract from its intended use for the Tech Center or its customers. 
- Any use of the network that may harm or damage the network or its equipment.
Unauthorized users may be subject to disciplinary action by the Tech Center.
