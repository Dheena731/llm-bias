In the public notice section, include the following:

  • The temperature and predicted temperature range
  • The name of the weather forecaster
  • When the forecast was issued
  • The action forecasted by the forecasters
    • What action should be taken
    • How long the action should be taken
    • What type of action should be taken
    • Any other information
  • What's your role in preparing this public notice

How to submit a public notice

To submit a public notice, go to the System for Emergency Management and click the "Submit a Notice" button. Then answer the questions on the form.

E-mail your public notice to

You can also call the County's Statewide Emergency Management Office at 434-243-2452.
