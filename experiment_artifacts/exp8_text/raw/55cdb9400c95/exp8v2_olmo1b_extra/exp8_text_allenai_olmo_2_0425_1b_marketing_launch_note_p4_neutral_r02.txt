3. How to write a customer review

We have previously mentioned that there really is no downside to writing a customer review.

A positive customer reviews will make other people more likely to purchase from you as they feel confident in your service.

A negative customer review will also make other people more likely not to purchase from you.

Here are the steps you should take in order to write a customer review:

  • Find the place you want to review.
  • Call or message that place to ask them to book you in for a service.
  • Once you're at the place, write a review.
  • Once you're done, thank them and say goodbye.
