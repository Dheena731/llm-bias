If you're writing a detailed critique, you may want to include an evaluation of the research methods employed by the original author.

Refer to the author by their full name, and use "quotation marks" before the title in your note. For example:
  • "Ellen" (last name first)
  • "Ellen's" (last name first) (first and last names can also be abbreviated with one word: Ellen-dimensional)

Note: Avoid all capital letters and do not use block quotes or italics.

Make sure your note has a main idea, and that it is consistent with the author's text. Authors often write introductions or conclusions that aren't technically part of the paper, so it is fine to include these in your note without spacing them out.

Type all text in a single 11 pt font, with 12 pt font on your References page.
