An end-of-tax year tax statement helps you understand the amount of tax you paid. The statement shows the following information:

  • The amount of tax you paid during the tax year, including any deductions or credits
  • Amounts for each of the following items: (a) income tax, (b) social insurance contributions (SIC), and (c) education tax
  • Amounts for all taxes paid during the period (income tax, SIC, education tax)
  • Amounts for all taxes withheld or remitted by the employer
  • Your total tax for the year
  • Total amount of tax if you pay

Let’s take a look at the end-of-year tax statement for a man named John. John paid $500 in income tax on his salary, $250 in SIC, and $450 in education tax. The statements also show that John took all of his pay home and received no SIC payment.

John’s total tax for the year is $1,550. John can use the information on the end-of-year tax statement to adjust his tax payment if he wants to deduct the following: (a) net income, or money left over after paying tax, or (b) the amount of education tax he paid outside of his employer’s withholdings.

The end-of-year tax statement also shows John’s total tax for the year if he doesn’t make any deductions. This total tax is called the final amount of tax.

If John submitted the end-of-year tax statement, he can use the information to adjust his tax payment. If he thinks he may have made a mistake, he can request a corrected statement from the CRA.

Taxes paid outside

When you work in Canada outside your home province, you may be required to pay taxes in both of the countries you work in. You may also pay taxes in your home province. Canada and the US are examples of countries where you may pay taxes.

An end-of-year tax statement shows the amounts for each of these taxes, as well as the total amount paid for income tax only.

You can use this information to see if you are overpaying taxes. To adjust your tax, you can use either the information on the end-of-year tax statement, or the information on your income tax return.

If you don’t have an income tax return, you should go to your CRA office or visit their website for instructions on how to file a return online.
