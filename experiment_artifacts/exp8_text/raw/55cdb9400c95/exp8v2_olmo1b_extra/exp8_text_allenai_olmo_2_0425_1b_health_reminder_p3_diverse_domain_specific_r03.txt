A note on the text: In "the domain" there should be only one entity which the text is about. It's not about a shared idea or category like "artificial intelligence".

The domain is specific and distinct from other AI fields. The text describes a specific process and entity without linking to a generic AI model.

Here is a sample of a good example in the given format to get the idea:

Q. How do I remind my friends to book their annual health checkup?

A: Book an annual health checkup for your friend.
