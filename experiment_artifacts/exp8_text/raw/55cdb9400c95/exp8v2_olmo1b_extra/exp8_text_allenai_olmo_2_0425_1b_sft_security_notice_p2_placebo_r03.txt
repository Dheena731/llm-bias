Attention, employees;

We have recently experienced an increase in phishing emails in our workplace. Hackers are increasingly targeting our company, and we need to take the necessary steps to protect our data and assets.

Before you open any email from unknown sources, please verify the sender. The email may appear to come from a trusted source, such as your bank or a colleague, but it may be a phishing attempt.

If you receive any suspicious emails, please refrain from clicking on any links or downloading attachments. Instead, contact your manager or cybersecurity officer for further guidance.

Thank you for your cooperation and vigilance in maintaining our company's data security.
