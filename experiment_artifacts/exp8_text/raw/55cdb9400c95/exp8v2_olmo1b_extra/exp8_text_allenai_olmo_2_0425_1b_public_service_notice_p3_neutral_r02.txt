Cool Greens chillies

Cool Greens is a nationwide campaign to encourage recycling and reduce food waste.

It is a good idea to check which rubbish can be recycled in your area. This is important in order to make sure that it is sorted properly and avoids pollution.

Food waste can be recycled and it is a good idea to find out if you could recycle it locally. For example, some types of food waste can be used to generate energy, i.e. biogas.
