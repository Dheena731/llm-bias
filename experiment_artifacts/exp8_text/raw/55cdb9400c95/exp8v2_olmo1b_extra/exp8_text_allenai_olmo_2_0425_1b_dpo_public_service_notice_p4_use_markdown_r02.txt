**Heat Advisory**

* **Warning:** High temperatures are expected to persist this week, posing a risk of heat-related illnesses such as heat stroke and dehydration.

* **Signs:** Extreme thirst, heavy sweating, dark urine, feeling faint, and exhaustion.

* **Precautions:**  
   * Stay indoors as much as possible, especially in the afternoon and early evening.
   * Drink plenty of fluids, especially water, throughout the day.
   * Avoid excessively working or playing outdoors.
   * Consider using a cool bath or air conditioning before bedtime.

* **Action:**  
   * Check on neighbors and friends who might be at increased risk (elderly, infirm, indoor-only).
   * Report any concerns to your local health department.

* ** eta:** Stay informed and monitor local weather conditions for updates.

**Note:** This advisory is in effect until further notice.
