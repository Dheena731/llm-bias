If you have found a lost company laptop and believe that it may be misappropriated or stolen, it is important to report this to the appropriate authorities. To report the laptop, you can contact the good samaritan hotline in your area or the local police department. You may also report the lost laptop to the company, as they may have a lost and found department that can help to find the laptop and return it to its rightful owner. Additionally, you can contact the company's IT department or IT specialist to report the lost laptop and inquire about proper procedures for reporting and handling the loss.
