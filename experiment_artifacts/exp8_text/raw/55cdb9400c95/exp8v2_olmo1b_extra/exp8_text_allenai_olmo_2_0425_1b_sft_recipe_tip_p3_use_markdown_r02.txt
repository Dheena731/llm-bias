# Making Rice Less Sticky

**1. Use the Right Ratio**

Before cooking, make sure you use the correct ratio of water to rice grains. For every 2 cups of water, use 1 cup of rice. This balances the grains and prevents them from becoming too sticky.

**2. Add a pinch of Salt**

Adding a pinch of salt before cooking can help balance the grains and reduce stickiness. It also adds flavor.

**3. Rinse the Rice**

Rinse the rice thoroughly to remove any excess starch, which can make the rice too sticky.

**4. Cook Cellophane Noodles or Oyster Sauce**

If your rice is still too sticky after cooking, try adding a packet of cellophane noodles or a splash of oyster sauce. These ingredients help thicken the rice while improving texture and flavor.
