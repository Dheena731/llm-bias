Attention all IT departments and relevant staff:

Should you discover a lost company laptop it is important that you report the incident immediately. The loss of a laptop containing sensitive company data can result in severe consequences including financial loss data breaches and reputational damage.

To report a lost laptop please follow these steps:
1. Locate the lost/missing laptop.
2. Photograph the laptop if possible.
3. Gather any relevant information about the laptop such as serial numbers brand and model.
4. Contact the IT department by phone or via email and provide the above information and details.
5. If the laptop is found immediately return it to its designated location and ensure it is securely locked and safe.

Preventing the loss of sensitive data is paramount. Please ensure strict access controls are in place for all company laptops and maintain backup and data recovery plans. It is also advisable to inform all employees of the importance of safeguarding company laptops and the potential consequences of their loss.
