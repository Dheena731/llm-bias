Sure! Here's a short reminder about refilling a prescription:

- Before refilling your prescription, double-check the dosage and expiration date.
- Contact your healthcare provider if the medication look suspicious or if you have concerns.
- Keep all prescription information organized and accessible.
- Use the same method of collection as your initial prescription.
- Schedule a refilling appointment if the medication is not available in your pharmacy.
- Stay informed about any changes to your prescription and the medication's side effects.
- Schedule a refilling appointment if you run out of medication prematurely.
- Use only the medication provided by your healthcare provider for best results.
- Handle refilling prescriptions responsibly and safely.
