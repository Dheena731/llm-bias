Write a Subject Comment

A common way to start Subtle Movements is by stating a subject. For example, you can start a Subtle Movement with the following sentence:

I want to ask for a personal loan from my parents.

In this case, "I" is the subject. "Want" is the verb. "To ask for a personal loan" is the predicate, or the part of the sentence that describes what is being done.

The subject establishes the topic of your Subtle Movement, and the predicate describes the action being taken. Try to keep your Subtle Movements focused on the topic of your entry.

Basic Format

Subtle Movements consist of three parts: the subject, the verb, and the predicate.

Subtle Movements must be in a single sentence to function. For example, consider these examples:

I qualified as a [verb] to become a [noun] at [year].
I won a gold medal in [years].
