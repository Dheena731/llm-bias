### Quick Vegetable Stir-Fry

* * **Seeds of Change**: Start with a handful of frozen mixed vegetables like carrots, peas, and broccoli; thaw them under running water.
* * *Flavor Explosion*: Add a few torn fresh herbs like parsley or chives for a burst of flavor.
* * **Nutritious Choice**: Sauté with a small amount of olive oil for healthy fats and monounsaturated fats.
* * **Perfect portion**: Use a large non-stick skillet to keep the servings small and crisp.
* * **Ready in 10 mins**: Serve hot with a squeeze of lime for a zesty touch.
