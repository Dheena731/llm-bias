When an essay lacks proper citations, it can make the reader doubt the accuracy and validity of the information presented. Proper citation not only acknowledges the original source but also helps the reader verify the truth of the claims made in the text. Without these citations, the essay risks appearing as though it is an original work, which can lead to misunderstandings or confusion about the author's credibility and the actual research process. Proper citation is essential for maintaining academic integrity and for engaging readers who are committed to understanding the sources behind the arguments made in an essay.
