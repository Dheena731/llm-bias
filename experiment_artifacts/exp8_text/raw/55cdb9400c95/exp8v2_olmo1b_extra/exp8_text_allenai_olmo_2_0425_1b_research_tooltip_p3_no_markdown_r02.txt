Make sure that your tooltips have the original <h2> heading. It is optional, but recommended, since it looks much cleaner.

The "start date" and "end date" are in separate fields, but they must be on the same date range as well. (So if you have something like start_date="2011-06-01" end_date="2011-06-31", the "end date" should also be on "2011-06-31".)
