Review the progress of your fitness routine this week. Take note of your workout frequency and duration, amounts spent on cardiovascular exercises, strength training, and flexibility activities. Consider the intensity of your workouts, the types of activities you've completed, and any challenges you've overcome. Reflect on your energy levels during the week, how you felt during and after workouts, and how you feel overall. Keeping track of these details will help you identify areas for improvement and celebrate your successes. Remember, consistency is key to making progress, so focus on regular, enjoyable activity levels that suit your current fitness level and goals.
