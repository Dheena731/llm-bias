How to write a good monthly spending summary

Step 1: Update your weekly spending summary

Step 2: Organize the data

Break down your monthly spending into the following categories:

  • Monthly expenses
  • Monthly income
  • Investing
  • Savings

In our example, we have monthly expenses of €800, an income of €1500, and €1500 in savings. This means that our monthly expenses are €1000 and our savings are €1500.

Step 3: Make sure the data is accurate and complete

Check the data. Make sure there are no missing numbers or wrong amounts.

Step 4: Make sure the summary is easily readable

Make sure the data is legible. If there are a lot of numbers, remove the numbers and write the main part of your summary below the numbers.

Some people might find this easier to read:

Monthly expenses = €1000

Monthly income = €1500

Monthly spending = €1500

Step 7: Write your summary in short sentences and paragraphs

Write your summary in short sentences and paragraphs. Don’t write a long stream of text. A summary is about a few important facts, not a story.

You can also write your summary in bullet points. Bullet points can be a good choice if you want to explain why or how your business is growing, or if you are writing about a particularly specific detail.

Step 8: Do not add any extra information

Do not write anything about your finances in your summary. If you want to add more information in a comment, do it in your personal explanation.

Write your personal explanation in the text below your summary.

Our summary makes it clear that our monthly expenses are €1000 (we spend €800 on rent, €50 on gas, €100 on food, and €150 on shopping). Our monthly income is €1500, which covers our monthly expenses.

Step 9: Make sure you have included the main points

Go back and make sure that your summary has included the main points. Write a line above or beneath your summary to summarize the main points again.
