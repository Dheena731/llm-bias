Get ready for the annual Summerfest this weekend! Enjoy local food, music, and fun at the park. Bring your family and friends to dance the night away. There will be games, fair games, food vendors, a stage for performances, and plenty of opportunities to enjoy the sunshine and make new memories. Let’s come out and support our neighbors and have fun together! Remember to wear sunscreen and stay hydrated. Refreshments will be available at various booths. Bring your reusable cups and bags too!
