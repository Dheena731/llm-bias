Attention all employees:

* Password reuse is a security risk. It allows hackers to gain unauthorized access to multiple accounts with a single breach in password security. By not using the same password across multiple accounts, we can significantly enhance the security of our systems. Instead of targeting one account, a hacker would need to compromise one password.

* We encourage the use of unique, complex passwords for each account. Consider using a passphrase or a combination of letters (lowercase and uppercase), numbers, and special characters. Avoid common words or phrases as passwords.

* Remind yourself to regularly update your passwords. Strong passwords should remain secure for at least one year (or longer). Consider using a secure password manager if keeping track of multiple complex passwords proves challenging.

* In case you forget your password for a specific account, contact your IT department immediately for assistance. Do not attempt to recover an account with a weak or recoverable password, as this could compromise others' security.

* Be vigilant about phishing and other deceptive tactics. Do not provide personal information (like account credentials) to unverified sources.
