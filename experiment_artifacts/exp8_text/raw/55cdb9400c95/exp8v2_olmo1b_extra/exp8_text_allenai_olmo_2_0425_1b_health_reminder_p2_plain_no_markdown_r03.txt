How to Structure Your Fantasy Story

There are a few ways to structure your fantasy story, but some of the most common are like this:

  1. Intro (introduce characters and setting)
  2. Middle (describe characters and events)
  3. Conclusion

It’s not necessary to follow this order. When it comes to writing fantasy, the most important thing is to make your story as interesting and unique as possible.

Keep in mind that your fantasy story should not be too long. The average word count for a fantasy story is around 10,000 to 10,000 words. If you’re shorter, do not worry about length.

Writing Prompts

Main Characters

Write about the main character in your story. What is their name? What are their physical and personality traits? What is their purpose in the story?

Setting Description

Write about the setting of your story. Where does it take place? What are the main geographical locations? Why did you choose that location?


Write about one of the main characters or a minor character in your story. What is their backstory? What is their motivation? What were they like when they were a kid?

Conflict & Plot

Write about a conflict that occurs in your story. What is the problem? How do the characters solve it? What’s the climax of your story?


Write about an event that happens in your story. What’s the conflict? What’s the resolution? What’s the main event in your story?
