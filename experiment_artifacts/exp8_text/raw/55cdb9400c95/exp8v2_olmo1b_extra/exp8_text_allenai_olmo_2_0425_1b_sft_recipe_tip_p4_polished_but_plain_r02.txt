Here's a quick tip for planning lunches for a busy week:

Start by listing all the ingredients that you will need to make a new lunch each day. You can plan out how many days you want to have a different lunch for each day. 

Once you have listed your ingredients, think about what days you can do without each item. For example, if you have a pizza day, you will only need cheese and pepperoni. 

Once you have listed all your ingredients and the days you will need them, you can plan out the rest of your work week. 

When you are at the grocery store or at the bakery, try to pick up ingredients that you will need for all the meals for the week. 

Finally, grab some containers or bags and label them with each meal for the week.
