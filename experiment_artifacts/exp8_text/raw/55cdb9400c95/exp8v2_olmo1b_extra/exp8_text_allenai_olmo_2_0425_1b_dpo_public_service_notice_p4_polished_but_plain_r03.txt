Public Notice: Heat Advisory

Thank you for your attention to this important public notice.

Due to above-average temperatures forecast for the coming days, we are issuing a Heat Advisory. This advisory will remain in effect until the forecasted high temperatures are no longer expected.

The Heat Advisory means that it will continue to be hot and humid throughout the day and into the evening hours. Residents are advised to take precautions to protect their health and well-being:

- Stay indoors as much as possible when the sun is out.
- Use air conditioning as much as possible.
- Drink plenty of water and eat cool food.
- Monitor elderly and vulnerable neighbors for their safety.
- Avoid using heavy clothing outdoors during the day.

Residents are encouraged to check the latest weather forecast and take necessary steps to stay comfortable and safe. Please take this notice seriously and cooperate with local authorities to ensure the safety and well-being of our community.
