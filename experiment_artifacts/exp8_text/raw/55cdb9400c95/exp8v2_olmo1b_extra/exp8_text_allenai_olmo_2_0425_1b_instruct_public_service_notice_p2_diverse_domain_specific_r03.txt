**Road Closure - Due to construction**

In the interest of safety and the ongoing maintenance of our community's infrastructure, we are temporarily closing a portion of Morningside Road from Reed Street to Birchwood Drive effective immediately. This closure is due to the construction of a new utility trench which necessitates a section of traffic flow to be diverted temporarily for safety and efficiency.

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and urge all drivers to be cautious and follow any signs or directions posted along the route.

Please keep in mind that this closure is a short-term measure for the benefit of our community's broader well-being. We will keep you updated as our work progresses and as the road remains closed.

Thank you for your understanding and cooperation as we work tirelessly to ensure a smooth and safe travel experience for all residents. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please do not hesitate to contact our road closure team.

**Additional Notes:**

- Visibility signs will be posted at strategic points along the road closure area.
- We encourage the use of alternative routes or parking to avoid congestion.
- Scheduled work hours will be posted on our website as they progress.

We appreciate your patience and your continued trust in our maintenance and construction efforts. Rest assured that the repaired conditions will improve the safety and accessibility of our community's roads.
