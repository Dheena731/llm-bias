When planning lunches for a busy week, consider batch cooking on weekends to save time throughout the week. Choose meals that require minimal prep work or cooking time, leaving more time for work and social commitments. Prioritize meals that are affordable, easy to prepare, and have a long shelf life for those days when time is short. Experiment with different spice levels, flavors, and ingredients to keep meals interesting and fresh. Always have basic ingredients on hand, such as bread, cheese, fruit, and nuts, to serve on-the-go without much additional effort. Consider group lunches or container salads for groups to save on time and clean-up. Lastly, keep a grocery list handy to streamline your shopping and avoid last-minute scrambles.
