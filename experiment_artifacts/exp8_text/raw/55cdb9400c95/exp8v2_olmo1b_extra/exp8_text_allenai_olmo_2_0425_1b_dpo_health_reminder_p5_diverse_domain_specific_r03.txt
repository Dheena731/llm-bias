Here’s a quick refresher to keep your prescriptions up to date and safe:

### Importance of Refilling Your Prescription:

- **Fulfilling Your Treatment Plan:** Keeping your prescribed medication at the current dosage level maintains the effectiveness of your treatment and prevents the need for an unnecessary trip to the pharmacy.
  
- **Preserving Medication Security:** Unused or expired medications can pose risks to your health. Re-filling a prescription ensures that the medication is safe to use and is from its expiration date.

- **Cost Control:** Regular refills help keep your medication budget in check. Avoiding duplication of prescriptions on a new refill request can save money and prevent waste.

### Checklist Before Refilling Your Prescription:

- **Review the Prescription:** Double-check the medication listed, dosage, and any other requirements listed on your last prescription.
  
- **Check Expiry Dates:** Ensure the medication you are refilling is within the approved usage date. Request a copy of your last prescription for comparison if needed.

- **Check Formulation:** Review that your prescription comes in the appropriate dosage form and quantity for your current health condition.

- **Add Needed Medications:** If you have missed doses or additional medications listed on the previous prescription, make sure these are included in the refill request.

- **Verify Needed Prescriptions:** If you're picking up prescriptions from another pharmacy, confirm that all required medications are listed and in the correct form and quantity.

### Last Words:

Always follow your healthcare professional’s guidance on the prescription process. If you have any questions or concerns about a refill, reach out. Your health and safety are our top priorities, and staying informed about how to best manage your prescriptions is part of that.
