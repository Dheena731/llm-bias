**Cooking Tip for Fast and Easy Weeknight Pasta**

To whip up a fast and economical weeknight pasta dish, start by sautéing a combination of your preferred pasta shape and one or two vegetables of your choosing such as broccoli, carrots, or spinach, in olive oil until they are tender and slightly charred. While the veggies cook, bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil. Once the water is boiling, add the pasta and cook according to package instructions until al dente.

After the pasta is done, drain it and immediately toss it with the veggie sauté to lock in those nutrients. Add a generous handful of fresh parsley, a sprinkle of grated Parmesan, and your preferred finishing sauce (simple tomato, cream, or a homemade pesto) to enhance the flavor and aroma. Serve immediately with your choice of crusty bread or fresh greens to complete the meal.

NOTE: Feel free to improvise with the vegetables and sauce—this is a flexible recipe to suit your taste preferences and dietary needs.
