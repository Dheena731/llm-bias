Attention residents and visitors

We are issuing a heat advisory for the coming days due to high temperatures exceeding 90 degrees. The intense heat can cause discomfort and potential health risks such as heat exhaustion or heat stroke. It is important to stay hydrated and take precautionary measures to protect yourself against the extreme heat.

During this period please monitor your body and seek shade when possible. If you experience signs of heat exhaustion such as heavy sweating, dizziness, or weakness seek immediate relief by resting in a cool environment and drinking plenty of water. If you experience symptoms of heat stroke such as extreme heat rash, confusion or fainting immediately call emergency services.

We urge everyone to stay safe and stay hydrated. If you have any questions please contact the 911 emergency line. Public safety remains our top priority and we will do everything in our power to maintain a safe environment for all residents and visitors.
