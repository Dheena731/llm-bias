While the website for our bank had previously featured a savings plan, this new feature goes beyond mere guidelines to really encourage and motivate our customers to save more. The new intent is to determine not just how much we can save but also why they want to save that amount. By analyzing customer responses to various questions about their savings goals, the new feature suggests personalized plans that are not just general advice but are tailored to the individual's motivations, lifestyle, and financial situation.

Moreover, it now offers real-time tracking of progress, with features such as automated transfers and visual dashboards that show just how much is being saved and towards which goal. The new system also educates customers on managing their finances and how to stay on track, with personalized tips and reminders.

The feature was rolled out online and in person over the past few weeks and has already begun to see results. Several customers have reported feeling more motivated and confident about their saving efforts, attributing this to the personalization and real-time updates that the new tool provides.

As a result, we are excited about the new opportunities this feature opens for our customers to achieve their savings goals and the positive impact it has already had on encouraging responsible saving behavior.
