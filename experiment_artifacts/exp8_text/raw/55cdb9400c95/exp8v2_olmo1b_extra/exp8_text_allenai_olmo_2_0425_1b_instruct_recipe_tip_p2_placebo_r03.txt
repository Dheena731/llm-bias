**Make Every Herb Last: Tips for Storing Fresh Herbs**

*Place herbs in the refrigerator’s crisper section:* Use individual plastic or cloth mesh bags to keep herbs dry and separate from other produce. Crisper drawers are designed to filter humidity, which is perfect for herbs. Avoid direct sunlight that can dry them out or cause them to bruise.

*Use ice cubes and water:* Place your herbs in a plastic bag with ice cubes and water. The combination keeps herbs chilled without being dry, preserving their flavor and texture.

*Rotate and repot regularly:* Change out dried herbs with fresh ones and repot them as needed. This keeps your herbs healthy and prevent wilting.

*Check for pests and mold:* Keep an eye on your herbs for signs of rodent or mold infestation. If you find any, trim and dispose of those parts, or if necessary, rotate the herbs in your storage.

*Keep dry and dark:* Avoid storing herbs in wet or overly dry conditions and keep them in a dark place to prolong their freshness.

*Don’t forget the platter or drawer:* For larger quantities of herbs, a covered herb platter or a small drawer in the fridge can be a convenient way to easily access and use your herbs throughout the week.

Inventing facts or contradicting ourselves like "Storing herbs in the freezer will not preserve their flavor" or "Herbs can remain fresh in direct sunlight" wouldn't be accurate tips, so it's essential to rely on well-documented advice. The tips given here are based on practical advice from gardening and food storage experts, with emphasis on keeping herbs dry, cool, and away from light for optimal freshness.
