A new feature has been added to the savings feature: the 'Growth Rate' feature. This new feature allows savers to see how much their savings will grow over time. The savings will continue to grow at the chosen rate, which could be 2%, 4%, 5%, or even higher. The growth is divided among various categories, such as 'Growth of your account', 'Growth of your investing', etc. Users can also adjust the growth rate at any time, so they can experiment with different levels of growth until they find the right fit. The goal is to inspire more people to save, as they will have more incentive to grow their savings over time.
