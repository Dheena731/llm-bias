When planning lunches for a busy week it's essential to make them easy to prepare and pack. Choose a few favorites you can quickly whip up and pair with easy-to-pack sides and drinks. Choose meals that require minimal cooking time like sandwiches, salads, or wraps. Prepare leftovers from the previous night's dinner for a quick lunch boost. Pack snacks in reusable containers and include a sturdy reusable bag to help keep meals fresh throughout the workday. Keep leftovers in resealable bags and plan for lunch prep on the weekend to save time on weekdays. Remember to consider nutritional needs and adjust portion sizes as necessary.
