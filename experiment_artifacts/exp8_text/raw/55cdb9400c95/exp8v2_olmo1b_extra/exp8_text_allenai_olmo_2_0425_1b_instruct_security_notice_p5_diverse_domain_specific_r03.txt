Attention all valued employees:

It has come to our attention that a company laptop is missing from our office. This concern has been identified as critical due to the valuable data and proprietary information contained within its storage.

We emphasize the importance of immediately reporting any lost or stolen company devices to us. Please follow this protocol:

**Contact the lost and found department:**
1. Locate the lost or stolen laptop, if possible, and document all pertinent information.
2. Update our records to reflect the loss of the device and notify our IT department.

**Submit a report:**
Once you've located the device, submit a formal report via our official complaint form. Ensure that you provide details about the time of loss, the nature of the data contained within, your identity, and any relevant attachments or documentation.

We understand the inconvenience and anxiety that can accompany the loss of personal property, especially one as significant as a company laptop. Your cooperation in ensuring that our records are accurate and promptly updated is crucial to our short-term and long-term data security and operational effectiveness.

If you have any questions or require further assistance, please reach out to your manager or the supervisor of the department in which the laptop was last seen. Together, we will do everything in our power to track down the missing device and prevent any potential data breaches.

Thank you for your vigilance and commitment to our company's operational integrity.

---End of Notice---
