Dear customers we have made a change to card transaction fees effective immediately.

From April 1st the new card transaction fee will be $0.20 per transaction plus 3% of the total amount transactioned. For example if you were to perform a $100 transaction the fee would be ($0.20 x 100) + ($100 x 0.03) = $4.00. 

This increase aims to cover the additional costs associated with processing card payments while compliance with new payment industry guidelines is maintained. Thank you for your understanding and patience as we adjust to this new fee structure.
