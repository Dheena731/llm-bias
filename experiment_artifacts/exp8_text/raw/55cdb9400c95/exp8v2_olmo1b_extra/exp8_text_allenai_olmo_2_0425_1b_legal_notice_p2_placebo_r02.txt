From the top:

  • What is the project's name, in short form?
  • What is the project's purpose, in plain and brief terms?
  • What is the project name, in long form?
  • Who is the intended audience?
  • What is the project's scope?
  • What is the project's expected lifespan?
  • Who is the main sponsor?
  • What is the main sponsor's name, in short form?
  • What is the main sponsor's contact information?
  • What is the project's budget?
  • What are the main project milestones and expectations for the budget?
  • When is the project due by?
  • What are the main sponsors' legal contacts?

When you cite sources in the text, include them in a References section. Make sure you cite every source for which you have information and that you link to all relevant Wikipedia pages. Don't copy text whole from other sources or from Wikipedia.

Good writing is always clear, concise and relevant. Consider your audience and try to read the document the same way your audience would. Use standard English and avoid jargon. Summarize technical descriptions and rewrite them to describe the main points in your own words.
