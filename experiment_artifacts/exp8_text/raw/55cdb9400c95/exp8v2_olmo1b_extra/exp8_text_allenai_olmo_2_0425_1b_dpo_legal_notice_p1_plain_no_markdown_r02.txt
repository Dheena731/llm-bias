Attention all our valued customers,

We are writing to inform you of important changes to our account terms and conditions. Effective immediately, we regret to inform you that:

1. The initial deposit period has been extended from seven to fifteen days for checking accounts. Your checks will now clear between seven and twenty days from deposit.

2. Wire transfers will now require a two-step verification process: first a digital signature confirming the transfer from the account owner, followed by an additional confirmation code sent to the provided mobile number. Please ensure you have access to this code and verify its correctness before completing the transfer.

3. Minimum deposit requirements have been raised from $100 to $250 for all accounts, effective immediately. Our commitment to maintaining strong balances remains steadfast.

4. Balances will be prepared quarterly rather than monthly. Your statements will reflect correct totals, and we recommend reviewing your statements promptly for accuracy.

5. We have implemented enhanced monitoring for unauthorized transactions. If suspicious activity is observed, including but not limited to, unusual large deposits or withdrawals, we will immediately investigate and take appropriate action.

We appreciate your patience as we implement these necessary changes. Should you have any questions or require further clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us directly at the stated contact information. Your understanding and cooperation are appreciated as we strive to serve you with the best services possible. Thank you for your continued patronage.
