There are several ways to bring down the stickiness of rice. 

1) When cooking rice, you can change the amount of water to rice to reduce stickiness. Use about 2 cups of water for every 1 cup of rice.

2) Add 1/2 a tea spoon of butter or oil to your rice before cooking. The fat will slow down the cooking process and help keep the grains separate.

3) Drain your water, then rinse your rice under cold water several times. Rinsing will remove any excess oils and reduce stickiness.
