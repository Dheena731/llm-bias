Here's a short tip for storing fresh herbs: seal them in a lightproof bag or container with a small amount of water to keep them damp and prevent drying out. Make sure the bag is airtight to keep out pests. You can also use a glass jar with a tightly fitting lid, placing the herbs inside a damp cotton ball or cloth to soak up any excess humidity. Keep your herbs in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight to extend their freshness.
