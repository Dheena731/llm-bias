Seasoning vegetables with salt is very old. It's ancient in the sense that the earliest written documents that mention salt and seasoning date back to 2500 BC. In other words, humans have eaten salt and seasoned their food for 6,000 years.

The reason why salt is used so often is that salt has a unique ability to attract water. Think of a salt shaker: salt is sprinkled on the top of food to taste. That happens because salt can concentrate a huge amount of water in a specific area, making the food taste more flavorful.

Salt in food is also used to help preserve the food, known as food preservation. Without salt, microorganisms would easily grow and spoil the food. Salt is added to meat, fish, poultry, and vegetables, and the salt crystals act as a barrier that stops microorganisms from getting in. This way, you can store these foods for a longer time.

In food, salt is known as sodium chloride or table salt. Salt is also used in healthcare, for example in medicine. It's an ingredient in some laxatives and in certain drugs that are used for controlling blood pressure and high blood sugar (diabetes). The salt in food doesn't make people's blood pressure or blood sugar level go up like salt used in medicine does. On the other hand, too much salt in food can make people's blood pressure or blood sugar level go up.

The amount of salt we need is small. It's less than a teaspoonful for every single person. That's the amount of salt in salt shakers, for example. Most people need less than two ounces of salt a day.

People can't live without salt. We need it to survive, and we need it to taste our food. Salt is also rich in some important minerals, such as potassium and sodium.

Salt is also an ancient cooking technique. Its use goes back to 5000 BC in ancient China. Salt was first recorded in the Mesopotamian region of the Middle East around 2500 BC. In ancient Egypt and the Roman Empire, salt was a luxury good. The ancient Romans took salt to the battlefield as a way to discourage soldiers from fighting. The history of cooking, and the history of salt, go together hand in hand.

Summary: Salt is an essential ingredient in food, and it's been used at least 6,000 years. Salt is also a kind of seasoning. Salt is used to bring out the natural flavors of food and to help preserve food.
