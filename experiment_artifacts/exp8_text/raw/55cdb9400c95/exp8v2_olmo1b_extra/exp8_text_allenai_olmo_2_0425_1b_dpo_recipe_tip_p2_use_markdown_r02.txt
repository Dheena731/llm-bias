**Headings**

*Use a reusable container or champagne cork*  
*Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight*  
*Check for moisture daily and never dry herbs in direct heat*  
*Keep in mind the herbs' specific needs, like space and watering*

*For example, keep chives and basil in the pantry, while parsley and coriander do well in a bright, airy kitchen drawer.*
