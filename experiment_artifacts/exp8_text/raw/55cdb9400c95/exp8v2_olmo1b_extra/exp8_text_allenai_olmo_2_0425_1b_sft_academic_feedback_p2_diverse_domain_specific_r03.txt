I recently reviewed a student's methods section for a research paper and found the following to be both concise and effective:

"The research employed a combination of in vitro and in vivo experiments to validate the effectiveness of the proposed methodology. This approach provided robust data to support the conclusions drawn in the body of the paper, thereby improving the reliability of the methodology and solidifying the results' validity."

This example demonstrates a clear, concise, and appropriately technical use of language, reflecting a thoughtful and careful approach to writing.
