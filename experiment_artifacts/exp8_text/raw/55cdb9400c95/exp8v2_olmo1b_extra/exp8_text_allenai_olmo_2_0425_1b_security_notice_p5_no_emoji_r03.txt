Writing a perfect CV

As you know, your CV is like a suitcase. It is important for any job applicant to open a good impression on the company and make the right impression at the first sight. In order to make your CV perfect and stand out among the others, you have to follow these rules:

  1. Tailor your CV to the job vacancy you are applying for
  2. Show your skills and achievements, but do not go overboard
  3. Mention your weaknesses, but less and less
  4. Show confidence, but not arrogance
  5. Make your CV interesting and original
  6. Keep it short
  7. Proofread and make corrections

Write down your experience, qualifications, skills and achievements in a chronological order. Leave out irrelevant and personal information. Do not forget to include any other information, such as school and study experience, volunteer work, internships, etc.

If you are a self-taught artist, you should include this information, too. It will help you to quickly find the right company for you.

If you do not have a big experience in the same field, you should include relevant stories, talks, and testimonials, which will make you stand out among the others and make the employer understand that you are a good candidate for the job.

Finally, read your CV out loud. If you feel that something is not right, you can make corrections or write down a better version of it. Your CV is the first stepping stone towards the job, so it has to be perfect and look good.
