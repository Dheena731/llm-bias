The notice should be sent to all appropriate people within the affected area.

This will include press, citizens, local roads authority, local emergency services, local business and local residents.

The notice should also be posted on Facebook, Twitter, Google+ and any other social media platform that you can obtain information from.

The notice will include an address of where you intend to leave the vehicle. You should also include the time you intend to leave the vehicle, your contact details and any additional information about the vehicle.

It is advised that you collect all documents/ paperwork relating to the vehicle.
