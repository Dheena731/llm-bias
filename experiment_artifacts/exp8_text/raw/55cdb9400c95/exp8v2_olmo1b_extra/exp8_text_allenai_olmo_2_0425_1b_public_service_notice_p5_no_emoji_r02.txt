Be sure to do a basic research on polling stations.

For example: check the website of your local county election office as to where the polls are located.

Also, be sure to check the map on the election office website to find the polling stations located throughout your county.

Some election offices publish a list of election polling stations that you can print out and keep with you to make sure you make it to the polling station to vote.

Above all be sure to vote!

Good luck!
