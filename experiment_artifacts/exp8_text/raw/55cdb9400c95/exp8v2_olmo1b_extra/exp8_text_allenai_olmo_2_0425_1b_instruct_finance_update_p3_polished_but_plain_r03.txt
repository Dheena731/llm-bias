We've launched a new savings goal feature. This update is designed to help you save more money each month.

With our new feature you can set both short-term and long-term savings targets. The short-term goals are ideal for small, manageable amounts you'd like to save regularly. For instance, you could save $5 a week for a weekly shopping trip. 

Our long-term goals allow you to set ambitious savings targets, such as $500 per month for a holiday savings account. We also help you track your progress by providing a simple view of how much you've saved and how much is left to reach your goal.

This fully integrated feature is easy to use and doesn't require any advanced setup. Simply enter your target amounts and we'll remind you when you've reached milestones along the way. 

Our intuitive design ensures that you can focus on saving without getting lost in complex instructions. We believe that with our new savings goal feature, you'll be on the path to achieving your financial dreams faster than ever before.
