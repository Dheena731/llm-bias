    The answer is to cook it on a stove. You can make more brown rice than white rice if you make the brown rice first and then white rice. Do not use the water for the white rice, add more water later. Just use the water you already used for the brown rice as the water for the white rice. Then rinse the white rice under cold water to wash off the excess starch, and you're good to go. However, if you're not used to cooking rice on a stove, it might be useful to spend some time getting familiar with the stove before attempting this.
