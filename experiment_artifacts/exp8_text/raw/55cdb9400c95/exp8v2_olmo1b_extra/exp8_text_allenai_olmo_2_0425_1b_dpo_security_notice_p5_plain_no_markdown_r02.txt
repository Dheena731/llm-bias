In the event that a company laptop goes missing, it’s important to report it as soon as possible. The sooner you let someone know about the loss, the sooner they can start looking for it and help recover any saved data or important information.

Here’s what you need to do when reporting a lost company laptop:

1. **Identify the Model**: Take note of the laptop’s make and model.
2. **Document the Loss**: Keep records of how and when the laptop was reported missing.
3. **Contact IT Department**: Reach out to your IT department or cybersecurity team.
4. **Share Details**: Provide as much information as possible about the laptop, such as its appearance, any markings, software, and any user accounts on it.
5. **Provide Location**: If you know where it was left, provide that information.
6. **Schedule a Time for Collection**: Arrange a time for the laptop to be collected by the IT department or designated person.
7. **Follow Up**: After collection, confirm that the laptop has been returned and is in good working condition.

It’s also beneficial to document the incident and any discussions with staff who may have seen the laptop before its disappearance. This documentation can be invaluable if further investigation is needed.

If you’re unsure how to proceed, don’t hesitate to reach out to your company’s HR or IT support desk for assistance. They have the expertise and resources to handle such scenarios effectively.
