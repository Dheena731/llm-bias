# Tips for Seasoning Roasted Vegetables

- Use a combination of herbs and spices to enhance the natural flavors of your roasted vegetables.
- Choose fresh, high-quality herbs like thyme, rosemary, or oregano for their deep, aromatic flavors.
- Try a variety of seasonings like salt, pepper, paprika, garlic powder, or soy sauce for different textures and tastes.
- Experiment with liquid infusions (such as chicken broth) to bring out more moisture and richness in the vegetables.
- Adjust the seasoning with acidity (like lemon juice or vinegar) if needed to balance the saltiness, sweet, or savory notes.
- Toss roasted vegetables with a small amount of oil and a rub of your chosen seasonings immediately after roasting for extra flavor distribution.
