To all of our residents and visitors:

We regret to inform you that there will be a heat advisory in effect starting now and continuing for the next 48 hours. This advisory is in effect for all of California from the San Francisco Bay Area to the Central Valley. To help prevent heat exhaustion and dehydration, please take the following steps. 

1. Drink plenty of fluids (at least one gallon of water per day).
2. Avoid long periods of time in direct sunlight.
3. Wear light-colored, loose-fitting clothing, and make sure that it is in a breathable weave. 
4. Keep a fan in your living room, bedroom, and work office. 
5. Use air conditioning and fans in your home to help alleviate the heat.
6. Check on your neighbors and friends to see if you can help them with their air conditioning.

Please follow these instructions to help prevent heat-related illnesses. Please plan your activities during the day so you will not become a victim of this advisory.
