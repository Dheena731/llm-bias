Dear Weekly Administrators

The Office would like to remind all subject employees that quarterly access reviews are now being made. We are timing these reviews during the week of the second Thursday of each month, and will notify all staff in advance. Administrative staff will be notified of these meetings at the beginning of every quarter in the Newsletter and will be able to submit applications ahead of time. Failure to submit an application may result in a re-evaluation of access rights and may result in the loss of access.
