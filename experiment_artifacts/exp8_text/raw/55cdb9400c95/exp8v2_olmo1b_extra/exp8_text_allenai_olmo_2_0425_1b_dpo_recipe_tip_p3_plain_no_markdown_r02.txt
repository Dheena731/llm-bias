To make rice less sticky, try not to overcook it. When you cook rice, keep a close eye on the water-to-rice ratio. If it starts to boil over, use a lid to reduce the steam and prevent the rice from becoming too sticky. Stir the rice occasionally to help it cook evenly and not stick together. Remember that less water means less stickiness; if you're using an exact measurement, adjust the water accordingly to achieve the desired texture without sticking.
