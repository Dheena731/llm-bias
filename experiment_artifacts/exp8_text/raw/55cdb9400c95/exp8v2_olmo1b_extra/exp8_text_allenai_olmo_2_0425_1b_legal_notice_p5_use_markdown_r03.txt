## What are the consequences?

1. Violation of this rule will result in punishment according to the venue's policy.  You will be held responsible for your actions.
2. Your WiFi account will be permanently disabled and the $20.00 fee will be charged to your account.
3. Your account will be reactivated and you will be charged a $20.00 fee if you choose to open a new account within one year.

## What to do if someone breaks the rules?

1. Do not confront them directly. Get a group of friends and volunteers to help you confront the offender.
2. Document your experience with audio/video recordings, screenshots, and other forms of evidence. Save as many copies as possible.
3. Keep your witnesses anonymous.  Do not post their names or locations in your documentation.
4. Record the identity of the offender if you can.  In most cases, this information will not be public.  However, it is crucial to save it for future reference.
