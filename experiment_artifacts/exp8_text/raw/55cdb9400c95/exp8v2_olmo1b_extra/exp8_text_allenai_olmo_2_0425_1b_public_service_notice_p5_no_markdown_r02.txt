Can a Jew put their name on a list?

No, Jews are forbidden from writing their own name on the list. (Keritut 5741)

Is the Talmud a book about the Torah?

No, the Talmud is a collection of teachings of the rabbis. The Torah is the first tenets of Judaism, which include the Ten Commandments, the prophecies of the prophets and the books of the Bible.

What is halacha?

"The halacha is the law of the rabbis, based on their determination of the proper course of action, and accepted, as halacha, by all Jews" (Talmud, Tractate Yadayim 4, page 9). The halacha is a law based on Jewish tradition, tradition which came about through the oral teachings of the rabbis.

What is the Kittel?

The Kittel is the liturgical service for the Torah services. The words of the service are taken from the Torah. Some parts are from the Mishnah, others are from the Gemara and still others are from the Talmud. The order of the service is determined by the Mishnah. The Talmud is the legal basis for the performance of the service. The verses from the Talmud are written in the order in which they appear.
