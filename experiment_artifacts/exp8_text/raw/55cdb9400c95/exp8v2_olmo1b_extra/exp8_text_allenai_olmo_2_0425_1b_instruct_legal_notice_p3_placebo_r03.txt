**What are Cookies?**

Cookies are small pieces of data sent from a website and stored on your device by your browser while you are visiting our site. They can be used to remember information such as your username, preferences, or shopping cart items.

**Purpose and Duration**

Our cookies are generally used to collect and store information that helps us improve your experience. They last only as long as your browser is open on the site, or until you manually clear them.

**Types of Cookies**

There are two main types of cookies:

1. **Session Cookies**: These are used to store information that your web browser needs while you are interacting with our site during your session. The data is erased when you close your browser, though some session cookie data may remain in your browser's storage until manually deleted.

2. **Persistent Cookies**: These store data on your device for a period longer than your session. The duration of persistent cookies can vary and is solely at our discretion.
