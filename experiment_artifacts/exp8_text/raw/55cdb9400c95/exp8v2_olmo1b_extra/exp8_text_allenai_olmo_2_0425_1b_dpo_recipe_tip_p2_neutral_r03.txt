Fresh herbs last longer and remain healthy for longer if stored properly. Here’s a simple tip for storing fresh herbs:

**Keep Them Dry:** Place fresh herbs in a dry, air-tight container or bag. Avoid plastic containers, as they can leach chemicals into the herb leaves. Cover the container to retain moisture. This environment prevents fungal growth and extends the shelf life of your herbs.

**Temperature:** Store herbs in a cool, dry place. Avoid direct sunlight and heat, as both can cause herbs to wilt and lose their flavor rapidly. If you keep your herbs in a kitchen window where it’s warm, you can place them in a paper bag or a woven basket lined with a cloth to trap moisture while still allowing airflow.

**Rotation:** Regular rotation helps keep herbs fresher by keeping older leaves at the top where they receive less light and moisture. This also keeps the newer leaves fresher and better suited for cooking.

**Use as Soon as Possible:** The fresher the herb, the better it tastes, so use freshly cut herbs in recipes or store them in the refrigerator in their brittle form (not in water or any liquid) for up to a few days, depending on the herb.

By following these tips, you can enjoy the fresh taste of your herbs whenever you need them.
