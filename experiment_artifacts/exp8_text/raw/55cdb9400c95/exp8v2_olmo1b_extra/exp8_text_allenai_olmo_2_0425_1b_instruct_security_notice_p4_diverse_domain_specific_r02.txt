We're rolling out a major enhancement across our platforms: enabling Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for every user account. This exciting change isn't just about security—it's the foundation of a more robust and user-centric experience for your community.

**What is MFA?**
MFA adds an extra layer of security to your accounts by requiring two separate forms of verification before granting access. As of now, this option is available only for selected premium accounts and business accounts; we're expanding it to all accounts to further safeguard your digital footprint and our community.

**How does it Work?**
Once enabled, you'll receive a unique code sent to your mobile number or via an authenticator app. You'll need to enter this code in tandem with your username and password to gain access. This means no longer needing to worry about common account hacks; the odds of someone guessing your password or intercepting your details are significantly diminished.

**Why Now?**
We've analyzed our data and user experiences, and MFA is a critical step toward securing our platform and creating a safer online space for all our users. It's also a testament to our commitment to your privacy and security.
