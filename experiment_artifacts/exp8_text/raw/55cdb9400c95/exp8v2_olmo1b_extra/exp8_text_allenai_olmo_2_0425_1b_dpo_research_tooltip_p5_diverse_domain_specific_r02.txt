To export query results, first ensure you've saved the query results as a CSV file using a data extraction tool or service that supports CSV export, like Excel, Google Sheets, or a specific database export tool. Here's how you can do it:

1. **Open the Data Extraction Tool/Service**: Navigate to the platform where your data resides, such as an SQL database, a data visualization tool like Tableau or PowerBI, or a spreadsheet editor like Google Sheets.
   
2. **Find the Results Query**: In the data extraction tool, locate the section or feature where you saved your query results. This could be a "Query" tool, "Results" panel, or similar feature.

3. **Export to CSV**: Once you've found the results, right-click or click and drag to select "Export," "CSV," or a similar option. This will initiate the exporting process.

4. **Navigate to the Export Destination**: A dialogue box will appear asking for the export location. Choose a location on your computer where the CSV file will be saved or directly upload the file if a web-based export option is available.

5. **Configure the Export Settings**: If the tool allows customization, you may configure options such as file column headers, formatting, and any special fields to include in the exported file.

By following these steps, you can export your query results effectively, ensuring they are ready for further analysis or storage in a different format. The key is to know exactly where your results are saved to avoid unnecessary steps and ensure the export process meets your specific needs.
