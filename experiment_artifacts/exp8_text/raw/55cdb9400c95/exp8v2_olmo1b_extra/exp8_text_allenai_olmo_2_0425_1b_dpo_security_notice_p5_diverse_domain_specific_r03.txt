If your company has lost a company laptop, it’s critical to act swiftly and effectively to minimize risk and recover any potential data and resources. Here’s a template tailored to the professional setting:

**Immediate Action Required**

If you’ve happened upon a lost company laptop, please act immediately to ensure data security and protect our company’s integrity and privacy. 

**Contact the IT Department Immediately**

- **Identify the Lost Device**: Ensure you have a comprehensive account of all details about the laptop, including model, serial number, and any attached external devices (like keyboards, mice).
- **Contact IT Security**: Contact the IT department or IT support team without delay. Provide as much information as possible, including the description of the lost laptop, any attempts made to recover it, and the location where it was last seen.
  
**Contact Internal Spokesperson**

- **Communicate with Executives**: Inform senior leadership about the situation as the laptop contains sensitive company information, and follow any company protocols regarding lost or stolen devices.
  
**Document the Incident**

- **Capture Detailed Evidence**: Before contacting IT, take multiple photographs of the laptop (front and back) and any relevant documents or passwords present. Having detailed documentation will streamline the recovery process.
  
**Follow Data Wiping Protocols**

- **Destroy or Sanitize Data**: According to cybersecurity guidelines, all company data should be securely wiped from the device to prevent unauthorized access.
  
**File a Formal Report**

- **File a Lost or Stolen Report**: Accompany the IT department’s interaction with a formal report, noting the incident, actions taken, and the expected timeline for recovery and notification to stakeholders.
  
**Coordinate with External Agencies**

- **Inform Authorities if Data Exposure is Risky**: If the stolen laptop contains any data that could legally be classified or if there’s a risk of hacking, notify the relevant authorities and companies that the data may have been compromised.
  
**Educate About Data Security**

- **Inform Your Team**: Organize a brief meeting with your team to discuss the incident and the importance of safeguarding company data. Provide educational resources on data security to reinforce the message.

**Conclusion**

This should be a swift, coordinated response to facilitate a secure resolution to the situation. Acting swiftly not only protects your company’s interests but also upholds the professional integrity and trust of your organization’s stakeholders. Remember, handling such incidents with care will ultimately lead to a smoother recovery process and mitigate future risks.
