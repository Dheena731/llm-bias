I discovered a neat trick for using calendar apps that I think you will find helpful. To do this, start by adding as many reminders for important dates as you want to keep track of. Once your calendar is filled, instead of refreshing your device or swiping through dates, tap and hold any date until a pop-up appears. The date will then be highlighted. Swipe right to delete the date or tap it to set a reminder. It’s a simple but very effective way to stay on top of your scheduling and to-do lists!
