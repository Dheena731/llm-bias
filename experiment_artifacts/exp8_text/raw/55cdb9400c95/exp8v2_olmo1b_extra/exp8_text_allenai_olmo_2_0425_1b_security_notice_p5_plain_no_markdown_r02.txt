A picture of your damaged or lost company laptop should in most cases be included as evidence.

If you are reporting a lost company laptop it is not necessary to send a picture of it once it is recovered.

It may be necessary to send a picture of it if you are reporting a lost company laptop where the damage is so severe that it is not recoverable.

If you believe that you have sent the correct picture to the correct story, but it is not being used (or is being used in a non-technical manner), please contact us.
