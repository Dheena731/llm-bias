Attention all residents!

Starting from next week we will be experiencing changes to our recycling collection schedule. 

From Monday 1st September we will be collecting only paper and glass on at weekly basis instead of daily. Residents are advised to sort their recyclable items properly into paper glass and plastics.

To ensure their convenience we will place the new collection points closer to residential areas.

Residents are encouraged to check their local recycling sites for precise information and to familiarize themselves with the new collection schedule.

Thanks for your continued support of recycling efforts.
