Short Reflection 1

HANABUA’s “Immersion” Project is a brilliant piece of work. “Immersion” is a video that exhibit a powerful portrayal of a woman being kidnapped and raped by two masked men. The video is one that is presented from the third person perspective. The video captures the violence and the narrative from the point of view of the victim. The video is one which communicates the suffering and the trauma of the victim in a realistic way. The video does a great job of engaging its audience to look deeply into the situation of the victim. The video also shows the moment of hesitation and the process of the victim figuring out how to escape the situation. The video is an excellent piece of work that truly engages its audience into the situation of the victim. One of the strengths of the video is the way it is presented from a third person perspective. This perspective allows the audience to feel like they are in the situation with the victim and are in a position to feel the pain and the trauma of the victim. Another strength of the video is the way that it shows the moment of hesitation and the process of the victim figuring out how to escape. The video does a great job of showing the situation of the victim and the situation in a way that is natural and believable.
