If your prescription has expired or been lost, don’t panic. Get a new prescription to save your health.

Follow these steps to refill your prescription.

1. Look for the prescription you need to fill. Check that it is still valid.

2. Contact your pharmacist by phone (1-800-PHARM) to discuss any changes in allergies or medications. 

3. Find the pharmacy where you got your prescription and check their hours of operation.

4. Call, send a text, or show up in person to get your prescription refilled.

5. Take your completed prescription to any pharmacy that matches your address.

Remember: if you forget a dose or are unable to get your prescription filled, talk to your doctor or go to an emergency room for help.
