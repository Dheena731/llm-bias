A new monthly spending summary has been posted on our website. This report provides details on all expenses for the past month including groceries transportation dining out entertainment and utilities. Understanding your spending habits can help you make informed decisions about managing your budget effectively.

The summary includes two tabulated reports:

- **Total Expenditure**: Shows the overall amount spent over the month.
- **Monthly Average**: Breaks down spending into different categories to provide a clearer understanding of where your money is going.
