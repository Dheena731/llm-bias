# Tips for Seasoning Roasted Vegetables

- Use fresh herbs like parsley, basil, or rosemary to add a burst of flavor.
- Sprinkle salt and pepper over the vegetables while they are still warm to enhance the natural flavors.
- Toss roasted vegetables with lemon juice or apple cider vinegar for a tangy kick.
- Add grated Parmesan cheese for a creamy and rich taste.
- Season with a bit of olive oil to prevent them from becoming too dry.
