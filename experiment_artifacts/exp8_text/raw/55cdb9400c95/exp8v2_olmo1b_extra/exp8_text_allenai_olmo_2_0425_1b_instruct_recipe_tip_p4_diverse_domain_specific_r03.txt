Effortlessly Plan lunches for a hectic week with my Five-Step Lunch Pro Plan:

1. **Assess your needs:** Consider your team’s unique dietary requirements, preferences, and constraints.
2. **Get creative with staples:** Choose a few go-to lunch items that can be easily combined or altered. For example, a turkey wrap on whole grain wrap filled with avocado, cherry tomatoes, and a sprinkle of cheese; or a spinach and feta salad variety pack.
3. **Create meal templates:** Develop standard, visually appealing lunch pack formats. Use clear packaging, appropriate labels (like lunch boxes with clear windows), and a consistent color scheme or theme to your meal packs to make prep and presentation effortless.
4. **Plan for one-pot meals:** A single dish that’s quick to cook and clean up after can save time and effort. Think bean curd salad or a quinoa bowls with a variety of toppings.
5. **Trend and recycle:** Stay on top of lunchtime menu trends (health, sustainable, celebrity-chef-adapted) and make it a rotation in your pantry. Also, keep a ‘recycled’ lunch rotation where you take out crusts, vegetables that are about to be thrown away, and other unused components, and repurpose them into new lunches throughout the week.

By following these steps, you’ll streamline the lunch planning process, ensuring that lunchtime lunches are both nourishing and enjoyable, without the hassle.
