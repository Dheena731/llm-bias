- What are cookies?
- Cookie explanation

**Cookies** are small pieces of data sent from a website and stored on your computer by your web browser while you are browsing, or using, a particular website. They are used by most websites to:  
- Track usage statistics  
- Allow customization of website pages  
- Remember your preference settings for future visits  

**Legal Requirements:**  
- **Ownership and Registration:** All users must be registered and own their devices to access this website.  
- **Use of Cookies:** Cookies are used and created by the website owner and/or their affiliates.  
- **Data Protection:** Users have the right to access, change, or delete their personal information that is stored in their cookies.  
- **Consent:** The use of cookies is subject to the user’s consent, which can be revoked at any time.
