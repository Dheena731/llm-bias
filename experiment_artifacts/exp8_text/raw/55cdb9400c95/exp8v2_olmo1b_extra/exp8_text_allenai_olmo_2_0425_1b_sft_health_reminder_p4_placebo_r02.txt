Remember to take short breaks from screens to rest your eyes and recharge your body. The American Academy of Ophthalmology recommends looking away from screens for at least 20 minutes every hour. In addition, consider using the 20-20-20 rule, where for every 20 minutes of screen time, you should blink 20 times and look at something 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds. Lastly, remember to perform exercises like closing your eyes and breathing deeply to relax your body and mind.
