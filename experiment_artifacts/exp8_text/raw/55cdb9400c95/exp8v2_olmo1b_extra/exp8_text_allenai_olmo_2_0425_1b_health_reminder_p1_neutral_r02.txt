For example, if your goal is to lift weights and you want a weekly reminder of how much progress you made each week. You could write the following reminder:

“Your stomach muscles are getting stronger each week and you’re able to carry more groceries without getting a back injury. You’ve also seen a reduction in your blood pressure and it’s easier to lose weight.”

10. Prepare a to-do list

Make a to-do list with the following components:

  • Exercise: This exercise will be done each day.
  • Other workouts: This exercise will be done each day.
  • Workouts for rest days: These workouts will be done on rest days.
  • Nutrition: This is what you will eat for the day. You can modify these components for each week.
