**Tip: Season Roasted Vegetables with Good Salt**

**1. Start with a Good Salt**
   - Use sea salt or kosher salt to enhance the natural flavors of your roasted vegetables.

**2. Adding Vinegar**
   - To serve, toss your roasted veggies with a small amount of vinegar to add acidity and sweetness.

**3. Finish with Fresh Herbs**
   - Finish with fresh herbs like rosemary, thyme, or basil to add a burst of flavor and aroma.

**4. Experiment with Spices**
   - Try cinnamon, nutmeg, or vanilla to create a richer, more complex seasoning.

**5. Use a Grease Medium**
   - Roast your vegetables in a grease medium like butter or olive oil to create a flavorful crust.
