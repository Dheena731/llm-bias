If the notice is too long, consider splitting it up into multiple paragraphs.

Do not forget to add the date of the notice. It is usually mentioned in the body of the notice itself.

In case you are making a collection notice for the first time, it's recommended that you prepare the material as a table and write a basic notice.

## Material

The material for the notice should include:

- **Name of the material**: the item that is going to be recycled
- **Material, description**: the list of materials and products the notice is about
- **Collection date**: the date on which collection will take place
- **Collection time**: the time
- **Collection location**: the address, URL, or phone number of the collection
- **Recycling policy**: the list of materials and products accepted in your recycling system
- **Actions**: what you can do after the notice is done
- **Updated** or **Additional details**: the updated contact details or any additional information
