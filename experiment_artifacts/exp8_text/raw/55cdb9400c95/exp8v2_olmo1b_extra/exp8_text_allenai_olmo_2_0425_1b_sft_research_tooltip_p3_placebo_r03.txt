The date-range selector is a user interface element that allows users to quickly choose a specific range of dates, typically by dragging the handles at the edges of the selected dates. It is commonly found on calendars, calendars within software applications, or on more sophisticated date-related controls in web development. This feature is popular because it enables users to quickly choose a particular date range, like "start on YYYY-MM-DD" or "end on YYYY-MM-DD," without having to navigate through multiple menus or options.
