Residents in Westcliff-on-Sea and surrounding areas are advised to adjust their recycling habits as the local recycling collection schedule is undergoing a change effective from this Friday.

Previously residents would typically place their recyclable items in plastic bins placed outside their homes. Starting next Friday these bins will need to be emptied into the communal recycling bins located in local waste stations rather than directly into the curbside bins.

To ensure a smooth transition here are some key points to remember:

* ** friday: start emptying your recycling bins into the communal bins at waste stations**

* ** next week: switch to using the communal bins for all recyclables**

* **check the waste bins: the communal recycling bins are located at various waste stations throughout Westcliff**

* **check the local council website:** for any further instructions or changes to the collection schedule

This change is part of the council's effort to increase recycling rates and reduce landfill waste. We appreciate your cooperation in helping the environment by recycling correctly despite the change.

Thank you for your attention to these details.
