Refilling your prescription is crucial to maintaining your medication regimen. Always follow these steps:

1. **Check your Prescription:** Confirm the dosage, frequency, and name of the prescribed medication.
2. **Determine the Prescriber:** Make sure you're reordering from the doctor who prescribed the medication.
3. **Verify Expiration Date:** Ensure the medication has not expired and is within the recommended usage period.
4. **Follow Brand Instructions:** If there are special instructions or dosing instructions on the original prescription, follow them meticulously.
5. **Use Appropriate Packaging:** Follow the original packaging instructions to avoid spills or mix-ups.
6. **Notify Doctor:** If you have to refill your prescription due to an incomplete refill or if there has been a change in dosing or frequency, contact your healthcare provider immediately.
7. **Repackage Correctly:** When refilling, repackage the medication in a transparent container to keep track of how many times it has been taken.

Refilling your prescription correctly ensures your health and safety, so do not delay.
