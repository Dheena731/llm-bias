# 1. What is the confidence interval for the mean weight of a person selected at random from the population?
a) (2.6, 3.0)
b) (2.4, 2.8)
c) (2.5, 2.7)
d) (2.4, 2.5)
e) (2.6, 2.7)


Example solution

e) (2.6, 2.7)
Explanation:

Since we have a 95% confidence interval and it is symmetric, the 2.6, 2.7 actually also corresponds to the lower bound. The 2.7 is the lower bound and therefore the correct answer.

To get the confidence interval, we use the formula:

confidence interval = (sample mean - standard error, sample mean + standard error)

In the above example,

confidence interval = (3.6, 3.7)
