When you're aiming to make rice less sticky without sacrificing the texture you want, focus on the cooking technique. Here's a short tip: After bringing your water to a boil, add your rice and a pinch of salt. Reduce the heat to a simmer and cover the pot. Let the rice cook undisturbed for about 15-20 minutes. This method reduces the chances of the rice clinging to the pot and developing a sticky texture, while ensuring it reaches the desired level of moistness without becoming overly mushy or clumped. Adjust the cooking time based on your rice variety and personal preference.
