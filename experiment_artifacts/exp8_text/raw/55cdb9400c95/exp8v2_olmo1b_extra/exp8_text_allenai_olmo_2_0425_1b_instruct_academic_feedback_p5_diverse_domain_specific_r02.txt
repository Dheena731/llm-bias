In academic and scholarly writing, missing citations can significantly impact the credibility and robustness of an argument or research findings section. Failure to provide complete and accurate citations not only compromises the author's credibility but also obstructs the reader’s ability to verify the claims and understand the source material fully. Accurate citations are not only essential for maintaining academic integrity but also crucial for fostering a scholarly and scholarly environment. In any essay, especially those that delve into complex research or heavily rely on specific studies, references, or data, it is imperative to be mindful of this oversight. In the domain of history, literature, sociology, or any field of study that operates under rigorous peer review and citation standards, the absence of proper citations not only does a disservice to the cited work but can also risk damaging the reputation of the author or the entire academic community. Therefore, it's vital for writers to carefully ensure all primary and secondary sources are appropriately cited to ensure the integrity of the discussion and the credibility of the work as a whole.
