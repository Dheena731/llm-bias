If you have a prescription and need to refill it, do it through the pharmacy where you got it filled the first time. It’s important to follow your healthcare provider’s instruction on filling and refilling your prescription. Refilling a prescription before it’s expired ensures you continue receiving the medicine that best fits your needs. Never mix prescriptions from different providers, as this can be dangerous. Always check the expiration date and follow the instructions for dosage and duration for each refill. Never modify or stop taking your prescribed medication without consulting your healthcare provider.
