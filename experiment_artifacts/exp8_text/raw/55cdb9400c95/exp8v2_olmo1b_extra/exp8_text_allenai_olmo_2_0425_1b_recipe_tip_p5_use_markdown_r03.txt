Tip: Make the panarty in the oven

Brussel Sprouts 1

Cut the whole Brussels sprouts in half lengthwise and place them in a baking dish that can fit all pieces easily. Sprinkle with salt and pepper. Place in the oven at 350° F (175°C). Salt and pepper to taste.

Brussel Sprouts 2

After 40 minutes, remove from the oven and sprinkle with brown sugar. Return to the oven again for 20 minutes. Remove from the oven, add some butter and sprinkle with lemon juice. Refrigerate for the next day or serve cold.

Brussel Sprouts 3

Stir in a little half-and-half with a spoon to prevent caramelization of the Brussels Sprouts. Sprinkle with lemon zest, salt and pepper to taste. Serve piping hot.

Tip: Mix the spices

The best way to mix in the spices is to use a kitchen mill. If you don't have one, you can mix the spices with a fork.
