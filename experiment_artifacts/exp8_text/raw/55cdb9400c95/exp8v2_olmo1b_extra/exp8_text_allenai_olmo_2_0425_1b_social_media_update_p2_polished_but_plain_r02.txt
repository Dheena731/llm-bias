Offer a reward

If you are a charity or nonprofit, consider offering a small discount or other value to the next user or visitors to your site to encourage them to recommend your site to others.

If you are a business, you may want to offer one of your own products or services in exchange for a website recommendation.

Your reward doesn’t have to be significant. You could give away a small voucher or a gift card, or you can offer a discount to the next user to come in to your business or store. It could even be free samples or coupons.

Ask for referrals

Set a system in place for your staff, customers, and other contacts to ask for a referral. Encourage them to post about your business on social media and in the real world.

You might also want to consider signing up for referral programs with some of your prospects, such as Amazon or Google Shopping. This will help increase your sales and build a long-term customer base.
