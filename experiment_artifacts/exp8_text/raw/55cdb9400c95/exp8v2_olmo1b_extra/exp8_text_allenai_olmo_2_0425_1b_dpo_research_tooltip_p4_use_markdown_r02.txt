**Missing-Value Marker**

***Description:**
A missing-value marker is a symbol used in data analysis and visualization to indicate places where data is not available or unknown.*

***Purpose:**
- Highlight gaps in datasets for detection and addressing missingness.

***Uses:**
- Pre-processing data before analysis.
- Identifying patterns in missing data.
- Facilitating informed data imputation.

***Example:**
In a dataset, a missing-value marker might appear as `NaN` (Not a Number) or as a specific placeholder like `***`.
