Course Objectives

At the end of this course, you will be able to:

  • Define the basis of the COVID-19 pandemic;
  • Explain the overwhelming human psychology behind the pandemic;
  • Argue that the problem of the pandemic will not be solved by the government alone;
  • Identify and explain the four problems associated with the human response to the pandemic;
  • Explain the role of humans in the exceptional pandemic;
  • Explain the current situation in the United States with regard to the pandemic;
  • Explain the background of the current policy in the United States;
  • Explain the current plan in the United States; and
  • Evaluate the current plan in the United States.
