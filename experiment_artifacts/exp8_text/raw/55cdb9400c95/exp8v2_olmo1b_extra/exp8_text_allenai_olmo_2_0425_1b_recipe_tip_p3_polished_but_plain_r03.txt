Summary of writing tips:

  1. Write objectively. Don’t write with emotion, and don’t write about things that aren’t important.
  2. Don’t use too many words. Keep the message short. You want your reader to remember your message, not be distracted by the length of your writing.
  3. Use short words. Don’t use long words. The shorter the word, the more memorable it is.
  4. Use precise words. The more meaningful the word, the less likely it is to be misused.
  5. Use simple language. The simpler the language, the easier it is to understand.
  6. Don’t use unnecessary words. Don’t use words to make the writing longer, but to make the writing clearer.
  7. Use the words you know. The more familiar the words, the more likely people are to remember them.
  8. Write how you speak. Write like you talk. Write like you’d talk if you were writing something for yourself.
  9. Use powerful words. Put powerful words in your writing.
  10. Write so that people can understand you. Make the writing clear, and make it relevant.
  11. Make it easy to understand and remember. Be concise and clear, and put in the words people need to understand you.
  12. Think about people. Write for someone, and not for yourself. Write with humility.
