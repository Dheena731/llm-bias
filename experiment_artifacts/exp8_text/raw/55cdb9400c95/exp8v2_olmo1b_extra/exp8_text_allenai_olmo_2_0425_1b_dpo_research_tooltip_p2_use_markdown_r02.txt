***Confidence Interval***
This line illustrates the confidence interval in a chart. It provides a range (often shaded) within which we estimate a population parameter with a certain level of confidence, typically 95%. The width of the interval reflects the variability of the data and how precise our estimate is.

- **Parameter**: The confidence interval refers to the interval around a statistic (e.g., mean, proportion).
- **Confidence Level**: Specifies the level of certainty about the interval's contents; common levels are 95%.
- **Width**: Represents the margin of error, calculated as a proportion of the standard deviation of the data.
- **Purpose**: Communicates the precision of our estimate and helps in making informed decisions based on data.
