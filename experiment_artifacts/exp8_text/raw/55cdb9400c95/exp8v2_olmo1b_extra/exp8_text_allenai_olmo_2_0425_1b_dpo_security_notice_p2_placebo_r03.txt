3. **Collaboration:** Research and adapt our phishing detection strategies in concert with our cybersecurity team to ensure our defenses stay robust.
4. **Communication:** Keep lines open with our cybersecurity team and maintain awareness of any changes in communications patterns that could signal a potential phishing threat.

By being attentive and proactive, we can reinforce the security of our workplace environment and protect our valuable data and resources.
