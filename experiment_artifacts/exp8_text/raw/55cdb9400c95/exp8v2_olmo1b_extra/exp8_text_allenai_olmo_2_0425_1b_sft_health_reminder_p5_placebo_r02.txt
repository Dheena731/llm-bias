- Always follow the directions on your prescription carefully and follow your doctor's advice when it comes to refilling your medication.
- Do not share your medication with someone else or without the doctor's consent. 
- Do not stop taking your medication abruptly. If you need to adjust your dose, do so slowly under the supervision of your doctor.
- Always keep your medication in its original container until you are ready to take it.
- If you ever have any questions or concerns about your medication, speak with your doctor or pharmacist.
