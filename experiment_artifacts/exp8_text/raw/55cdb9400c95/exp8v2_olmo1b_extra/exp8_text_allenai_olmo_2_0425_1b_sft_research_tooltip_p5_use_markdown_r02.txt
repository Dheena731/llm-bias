To export query results, you can use the following steps:

- Open your query in the query editor.
- Click on the little arrow next to the "Result" button.
- Select "Export Results" from the dropdown menu, and a new window will appear.
- You can choose the file format you want to export the results as, e.g., "CSV" or "Excel".
- Click "Export" to save the query results in the chosen format.
- Close the export window to finish the process.
