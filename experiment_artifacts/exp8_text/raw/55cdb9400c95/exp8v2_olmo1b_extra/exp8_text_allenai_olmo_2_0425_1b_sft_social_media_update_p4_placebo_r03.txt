Ever wonder why your calendar app keeps saying you have a dentist appointment when you haven’t set one? Chances are you haven’t added your appointment… yet. And then, when you go to set an appointment, you’re met with a delightful surprise! 

Well, I’ve solved it for you. If you’re going to use a calendar app, make sure to add your appointments and events. It’s a simple solution, but in the day-to-day hustle and bustle of running errands and catching up with friends, it’s easy to forget.
