The end-of-year tax statement informs you of all the taxes that you owe for the year. It details the total tax amount, including any federal, state, local, and any excise taxes. It might also show any deductions or credits you claimed that reduced your total tax liability. Be sure to review the statement to ensure all items are correct and that you understand your tax obligations. If there are any discrepancies or questions, contact your tax professional for clarification. Make sure you file your tax return by the due date to avoid any penalties or interest charges.
