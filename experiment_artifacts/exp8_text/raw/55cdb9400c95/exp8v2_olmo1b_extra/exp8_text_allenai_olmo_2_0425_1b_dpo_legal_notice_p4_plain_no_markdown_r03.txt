REFUNDO Eligibility NOTICE

This notice explains under which circumstances a refund may be provided. We provide refunds for purchases made directly from our website or upon receipt of merchandise upon receipt of a properly filled and signed FAA Form 180 return. To request a refund, please contact our customer service team within 30 days of receiving your merchandise. Refunds will be issued only after a thorough investigation into the transaction. We do not issue refunds for sales tax paid on purchases made at our store if the total purchase price has already been refunded to you. Refunds will not be processed if goods received are defective, significantly different from described in the purchase order, or if we fail to fulfill a critical term of the agreement. We encourage you to read carefully the details of our return and refund policy and to keep original receipts for reference. Thank you for shopping with us. If you have any questions, please contact our customer service at info@yourdomain.com.
