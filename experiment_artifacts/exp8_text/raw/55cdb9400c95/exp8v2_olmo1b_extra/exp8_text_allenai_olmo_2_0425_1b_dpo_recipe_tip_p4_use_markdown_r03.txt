**Meal Planning Tips for a Busy Week**

* **Prepile Your Meals:** Create a weekly meal plan and preshop ingredients.*
* **Batch Cook Veggies and Grains:** Cook multiple servings at once to save time and energy.*
* **Use Leftovers Wisely:** Repurpose yesterday’s leftovers for this week’s lunches to save effort and money.*
* **Plan Snacks in Advance:** Buy prepackaged snacks like nuts and seeds for on-the-go lunches.*
* **Match Lkit to Uniform Days:** Stick to a consistent lunch schedule to eliminate guesswork.*
