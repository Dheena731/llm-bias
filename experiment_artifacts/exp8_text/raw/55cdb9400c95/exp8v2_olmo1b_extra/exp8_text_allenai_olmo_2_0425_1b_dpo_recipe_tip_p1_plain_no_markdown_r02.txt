To create a quick yet delicious weeknight dinner, start by sautéing garlic and shallots in olive oil in a small skillet. Add a can of diced tomatoes and a can of beans to the pan. Season with salt, pepper, and any desired spices. Cover the skillet and let the flavors meld for 5-7 minutes. Serve over brown rice or pasta for a complete and satisfying meal. This recipe is easy to prepare and perfect for those short weekdays when you need a simple yet hearty dinner.
