Construct your own example sentences using the language in the prompt.

Your paper will be graded on the creative use of the language in the context of the prompt, as well as accurately and convincingly querying about a given topic.

Grading Rubric

Criteria Points
1. Asks an original question
2. Strategically constructs the question according to the prompt
3. Finds the correct response to the question
Overall Quality

All assignments should be short, and less than 1200 words, and should be written at a grade 12 level.

Each assignment should be divided into a clear introduction, a body, and a conclusion.

Each assignment should use at least two sentences in a paragraph. Each paragraph should have an introduction, body, and a conclusion.

Each assignment should be context-sensitive, i.e. use the language from the prompt in the body of the question.

Each assignment should have a clear thesis statement at the beginning of the introduction.

Each assignment should have a clear transition and conclusion.

Each assignment should have an introductory paragraph, and a conclusion paragraph.
