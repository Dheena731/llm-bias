Think about your privacy, but also about the imagination of visitors

If you have a professional website, it's extremely important to keep your personal data private. Companies and websites can collect a lot of information about you from your view and navigation of a website, and if you've got your own website, you need to be extra careful about how you handle the data you collect about your visitors. Think about how you may identify your visitors, and what you might want to collect about them for future reference.

For example, if you're selling items online, there may be a site visitor who comes back on a regular basis. That would be a good time to collect information about them, so that when they come back to a future shopping session, you'll know who they are and what they want. The problem is that if the site visitor is not your customer, then their information might be sold to others. You may want to ask for their telephone number, or ask if they want to receive special news about your business.

Shopping online can be a great experience, but there are plenty of scams out there, and your business needs to be aware of potential scams. For example, many online shopping sites try to track your purchases and advertise to you afterwards. It might be a good idea to be extra cautious about these types of sites, and use only websites which ask for a name and an email address, and then only collect the data you really need.
