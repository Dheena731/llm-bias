# Conference Poster Draft Feedback

## General Comments
- The poster's design is clean and professional.
- The layout is organized, with clear headings and easy-to-read text.
- Colors are consistent, but could benefit from some contrast for better readability.
- Graphics are of good quality, but it would be helpful to have more context about their relevance.
- The text blocks are concise, which is good for readability, but some areas could be shortened or simplified.

## Specific Suggestions
- Consider adding a subtitle for the top-left graphic to clarify its purpose.
- Add clearer captions to the side images and legends to enhance understanding.
- The call-to-action button should be prominent and visible to help guide attendees.
- For the table, consider using a more standard format (e.g., numbered rows) to improve clarity.
- Discuss the relevance of each infographic for the target audience and make adjustments accordingly.
- Add a small legend or title for the infographics to avoid confusion and improve accessibility.
- Ensure that all text within the poster is high-contrast and legible, especially in areas with minimal whitespace.
- Provide a summary or quick overview of the poster's main points to aid in understanding on the first pass.
- Note any additional materials or resources that should be included in a larger PDF version of the poster for attendees to easily access.
