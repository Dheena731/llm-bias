*Drink enough water throughout the day:* 
- Keep a reusable water bottle with you at all times.
- Try to drink at least 8 glasses of water a day, but aim for more if you feel thirsty or feel dry-mouthed. 
- Try infusing water with fruits or herbs for added flavor and hydration.
- Be mindful of your body's hydration cues: if you're feeling thirsty, you're already dehydrated!
