Take short breaks from screens

This day may be long and busy, and while your eyes can handle a screen most of the time, they also need time off to conserve energy. Here are some simple reminders to help you integrate these breaks:

1. Every 20 minutes: Bring your attention away from the screen and give your eyes a rest.
   - Stretch
   - Drink water
   - Reflection—look around the room and take in some fresh air
   - Gaze at something far away (like a tree outside)

2. Regularly scheduled times: Set a timer for short screen-free periods throughout your day to ensure the mental and physical break take place regularly.

3. Set a cue: Choose an event, like eating lunch or finishing a task, to signal that it's time to take a break.

4. Go somewhere different: Change your environment. Sit outside, walk around the block, or engage in a different activity that doesn't involve screens.

5. Avoid multitasking: Put down your device when you're not actively using it and focus on one thing at a time without switching back and forth between screens and tasks.

6. Reflect on activities: While looking away from your screen, think about what you're doing. Are you really engaged and focused, or are you letting distractions pull at your attention?

By incorporating these reminders, you can cultivate the habit of taking short breaks from screens, ensuring your eyes and mind get the rest they need.
