The Freshwater Conservation Guide: Restoring Life in the Great Lakes

While great lakes are the second-largest freshwater ecosystems in the world, freshwater pollution still poses a serious threat to the lakes’ ability to sustain life. Massive amounts of phosphorus and nitrogen runoff from chemical-heavy agriculture and domestic pollution released from the suburbs of major cities pollute the lakes, causing massive algae blooms and depleting fish populations. Fortunately, there are ways to protect the lakes by restoring the health of fish populations, protecting wildlife along the shores, and cleaning up the water. If you care about the work that the Great Lakes Restoration Initiative is doing to protect the lakes, this guide will give you the information you need to get involved.
