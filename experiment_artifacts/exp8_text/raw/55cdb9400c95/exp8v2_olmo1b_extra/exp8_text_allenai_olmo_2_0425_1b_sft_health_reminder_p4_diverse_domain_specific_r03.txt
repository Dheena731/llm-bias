Reviewers: Screen Time Management Breaks for High-Tech Users Are Crucial! 🌟

When you're engrossed in a screen for hours, it's important to take short breaks. Overworking your eyes can lead to discomfort and prolonged headaches. Try this: every 20 minutes, take a break for at least 20 seconds to look at something far away. This can help your eyes relax and reduce glare. You can also try reading a book, practicing mindfulness, or even doing a quick 5-minute yoga stretch. Remember, a well-rested eye is like a well-maintained camera – it captures better, lasts longer, and delivers the shot right. 📷✨
