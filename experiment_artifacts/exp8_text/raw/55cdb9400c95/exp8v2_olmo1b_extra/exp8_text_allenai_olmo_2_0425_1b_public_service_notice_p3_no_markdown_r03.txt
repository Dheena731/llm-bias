Recycling in Germany

From Recycle Germany via Wikipedia:

There are 128,000 sites nationwide that accept paper waste for recycling in Germany. This is a cornerstone of the federal government's policy on waste reduction. However, some of them are not consistently working. The "Recycle Germany" web domain helps to organize and promote the program more effectively. The logo in the middle represents the "Recycle Euro" star. It is used worldwide to encourage acceptance of the program by businesses.
