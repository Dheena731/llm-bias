One simple trick to make your rice less sticky is to enrich it with some fat before cooking. Before adding the water and rice, melt a tablespoon of butter or a teaspoon of vegetable oil in the rice cooker or pan. This additional fat helps distribute the moisture evenly throughout the rice, reducing its tendency to clump together and become sticky. Allow the oil to meld fully into the rice, which can help break up some of that starch and make the rice stickier without becoming overly adhesive. Once the rice is cooked, fluff it gently to ensure it's not too gummy. Remember, the key is balance, as not adding enough fat or water can lead to stubborn rice. Experiment with this method to find the perfect consistency for your liking.
