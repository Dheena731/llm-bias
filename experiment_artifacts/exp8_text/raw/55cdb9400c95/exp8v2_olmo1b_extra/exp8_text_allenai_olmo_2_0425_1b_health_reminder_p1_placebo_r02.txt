How to Write a Conclusion (Speech)

So, you have spent all your time writing a conclusion for an essay, a paper, or an article. You have created an essay that is both different from and better than your competitors. However, you can still feel the need to add a conclusion in order to summarize the message of your essay. You may be wondering how to start a conclusion speech.

  1. Introduction
    If you have been asked to give a speech, then you know that you must present your topic at first. However, you cannot use the same thing as the introduction. You have to tell your audience to expect a statement that means something to them.
  2. Main Idea
    Since a speech is a summary, then you have to highlight the most important points of your essay. What are the most important ideas that you want your audience to remember?
  3. Conclusion
    A conclusion is also known as the main idea. It is the last sentence of your essay. You have just spent the entire speech explaining your main ideas. Now, it’s time to summarize everything that you said.
  4. Devices
    You can use devices such as metaphors, analogies, and examples. Do not overuse these devices, however. They can be used only three or four times.
  5. Summary
    Again, you have to summarize everything that you said. This is not an easy task. When you are writing a conclusion, you have to take into account the specific audience to whom you are delivering your speech. It is better to be brief than elaborate.
  6. Plan
    It is best to write a conclusion after the rest of your essay. You cannot just start writing in the middle of your speech. This would be a bad idea, since it is hard to consider a speech long and short at the same time. When you are done writing a speech, you should review your final draft to check for errors. However, the next step should be to develop a plan for how you can improve your speech. You can do this by creating a draft of a speech.
