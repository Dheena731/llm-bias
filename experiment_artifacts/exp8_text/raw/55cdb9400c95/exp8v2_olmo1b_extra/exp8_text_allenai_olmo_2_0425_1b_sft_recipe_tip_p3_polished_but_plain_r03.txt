Here's a cooking tip for the so-called "unluckiest" US rice variant. The extra starch in this type of rice makes it sticky when cooked but improving your recipe is simple. Place the rice in a pot and add a tablespoon of butter. Heat on low-medium heat until the butter is melted and the rice is all absorbing it. Take the rice off the heat and fluff with a fork. The rice will be less sticky and more lightweight than before.
