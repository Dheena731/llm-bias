**Recycling Collection Changes**

Starting January 1st, 2023, our community will be undergoing a pilot program for a new recycling collection system. This program aims to improve efficiency and sustainability.

- **New Collection Times**: The regular routes will be extended by 30 minutes starting November 1st, 2022. This will ensure all households receive timely collection.

- **Expanded Collection Zones**: We will introduce more collection zones to cater to the growing number of residents. This expansion is part of our long-term commitment to environmentally friendly practices.

- **Drop-Off Centers**: Starting January 1st, 2023, we will be implementing drop-off recycling centers in various locations. This initiative will encourage residents to separate their recyclables.

- **Inspection Process**: We are implementing a more rigorous inspection process to ensure compliance with our new guidelines, ensuring the continued integrity of our recycling program.

**For more information and updates, please visit our official website or contact our sustainability team directly.**
