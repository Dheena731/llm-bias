- Collection Days: Our new collection schedule has been implemented to be collected once every three weeks instead of every two weeks. This change ensures that we are collecting less waste and helping the environment.

- Diversion Rate: Our diversion rate has been increased from 60% to 75%. This means that more of the waste collected by our residents is now diverted from landfills.

- Pickup Points: Our new collection points will be located closer to the residents. Therefore, the time of day to bring your recycling materials will be later in the day to make it easier for you to accommodate your new collection schedule.

- Bag Requirements: To maximize the efficiency of our recycling process, we have now implemented a new bag requirement for glass, plastic, and aluminum. Always remember to separate these materials from your recyclable items.

We hope you are excited about these updates and look forward to seeing an increase in waste diverted from our community. We suggest making note of the new details above and helping us with our recycling efforts in order to make a positive impact on the environment.
