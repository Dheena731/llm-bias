3.0 Introduction

This assignment asks you to attend to a critical aspect in the design of the poster: the organization of your visualization.

3.1 Remember your audience

A critical aspect of your poster is that you are the only one who will read it. You will create a poster to be displayed to a large crowd at a scientific conference, so you need to think about what will be on it and how it will be evaluated.

Your audience is not just the other scientists at the conference. It is also the other scientists in the audience: the peers who you will be competing with to get first and second place. In addition to gaining the first or second place in an individual assignment, you and your group will also have an opportunity to compete in a poster design competition. This will be open to all groups, but you will compete against your peers. The poster design competition is announced on October 1st, so submit your submissions by October 2nd.

You should also think about the audience of the conference in general. This includes any other conference-goers who may see your poster (for example, non-scientists who are attending the conference for other reasons). The audience also includes the other groups (that other groups are attending the conference for). Not only must you think about what to include in your poster, but you also need to think about what other groups might include in their posters.

3.2 Make your poster visually compelling

Your poster will be seen by many people. You need to design it to be visually compelling. Visual design is important because it will be the only thing that is visible to the viewers. It will be the only thing that can grab their attention, and it will be the only thing that will be in their memory (and it will be directly linked to the information that it is conveying).

Ideally, your poster should be at least a little bit interesting to look at. It should have the ability to grab the attention of those who see it and cause them to want to continue looking at it. There is no guarantee that anyone will be interested in your poster, so you need to make sure that it is something that will cause the audience to want to open and read it. If they are not going to read it, then there is no point in designing it. You want your poster to be interesting to the average person. If you only provide information for the knowledgeable, you lose them--they do not even bother to look at it. If you only provide information for the experts, then they are not going to read it and you lose them.
