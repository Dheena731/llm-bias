Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow

Dear Team,

We're enhancing our expense approval process by implementing a new workflow. This change is designed to streamline approvals and ensure a more efficient flow of financial transactions.

Key features of the new workflow include:

1. **Automated Requests**: All expense requests will be routed to the appropriate team members via our intranet for approval. This reduces manual work and speeds up decision-making.

2. **Integrated Approval Tools**: Each team member will have access to a centralized dashboard for viewing approved or pending expenses, making it easier to track and manage expenses.

3. **Approval Timeframes**: We’ve set specific approval deadlines to ensure that all expenses are reviewed within the agreed timeframe, reducing the risk of unauthorised expenditures.

4. **Merge Requests**: If two requests are identical, they will be merged automatically to save time and prevent redundant approvals.

5. **Approval Logging**: A complete record of who approved what, when, and why will be maintained, providing clarity and accountability.

We encourage all team members to familiarize themselves with the new workflow and explore the accompanying documentation. Your engagement and active participation are crucial to the success of this change.

Thank you for your attention and cooperation as we modernize our expense management practices.
