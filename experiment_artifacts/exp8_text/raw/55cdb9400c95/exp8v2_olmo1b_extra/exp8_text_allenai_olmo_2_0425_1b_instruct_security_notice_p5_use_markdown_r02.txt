**Bullet Points**

- **Navigate the Reporting Process**
  - Find the company’s reporting form and fill it out accurately.
  - Attach photographs of the laptop when possible.
  
- **Identify the Lost Laptop**
  - Check lost and found bins in the office.
  - Check with colleagues and friends for recent travel or activity that might suggest lost access.
  - Check security cameras and systems for any footage related to the loss.

- **Contact the IT Department**
  - Phone the IT support line with the device’s serial number and any important data saved.
  - Follow up via email if you haven’t received a response within a reasonable timeframe.

- **Follow IT Department's Procedures**
  - Await further instructions on the investigation and recovery plan.
  - Stay informed about any updates on the laptop’s recovery.

- **Notify Security Team**
  - If the laptop contains sensitive information, contact the IT security team immediately.

- **Documentation and Follow-Up**
  - Keep a record of all communications and actions taken.
  - Schedule a follow-up meeting with IT to update them on the progress and any resolutions.
