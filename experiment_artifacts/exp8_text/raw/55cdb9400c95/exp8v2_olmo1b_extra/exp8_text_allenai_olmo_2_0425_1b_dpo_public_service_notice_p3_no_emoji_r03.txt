Hey everyone,
Starting next week, our recycling collections will be changing to follow the new guidelines set forth by local authorities. This new schedule will ensure that more items are recycled and less ends up in landfills. Here are the key changes:

* Collection days will be on Tuesdays only.
* Plastic bottles, jugs and bottles with caps will be collected separately from paper.
* Glass, metal, and other recyclables will be collected together as usual.
* For paper, we will be collecting larger cartons and cardboard as well.

Please ensure you sort your items correctly to help the community reduce its environmental impact and conserve resources. Your participation in this change is crucial in maintaining a healthy environment for us all. Thank you for your cooperation and support!
