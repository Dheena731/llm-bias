One of my favorite ways to prepare a weeknight dinner involves combining a few main components.  

1.   Start with a sauce.  I often make spaghetti sauce from scratch this weeknight to save on time and effort. 
2.   Layer this sauce with spaghetti noodles.  I like to mix in some cooked ground meat to make it a little more interesting.
3.   Add zucchini which I have cut to bite-size pieces.  I like to add a bit of garlic to add a little more zing to the dish. 
4.   Finish with some shredded mozzarella cheese.   I like to mix in some diced tomatoes which I can add some salt and pepper to. 
5.  Serve and enjoy!
