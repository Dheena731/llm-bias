Road Construction Ahead  

We regret to inform you that a portion of Highway 91 between Maple Lane and Oak Avenue will be closed for reconstruction between July 15th and August 1st. The work will involve widening the lane and repaving the road. Traffic will be directed onto adjacent routes such as Ridge Road and Taylor Drive.

The road closure will affect commutes from Friday, July 15th, through Sunday, August 1st. We appreciate your understanding and thank you for your patience. For up-to-date information, please refer to our website or contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567.
