When card transaction fees are raised

Recent news indicates that the fees charged on card transactions in our area are about to see an increase. This change is part of a wider effort to cover the growing costs of processing these transactions. The new fees will be applied from next month and will begin to take effect on all card-based purchases. It is important for customers to be aware of these changes so they can adjust their spending habits accordingly. While it may impact personal finances, the additional fees are intended to support the payment system and ensure it remains reliable and accessible for all consumers.
