**Update to Account Terms: Welcome Change**

Effective immediately, we are instituting the following revisions to our account agreement to ensure compliance with current industry regulations and to enhance transparency for our esteemed clients.

**Changes to Account Duration:**
Our accounts will now be tied to a 12-month period instead of the traditional 18-month term.

**Access to Account Information:**
We are increasing our transparency by making the terms and conditions of access to our account information available on our website. Detailed guides are provided for all users, detailing what information is shared with third parties and under what circumstances.

**Security Enhancements:**
To safeguard our accounts and your valuable data, we are upgrading our security protocols. This includes but is not limited to the implementation of multi-factor authentication and a more robust encryption system.

**Client Communication:**
We are enhancing our communication with clients by requiring them to sign up for our account updates through an automated system. Failure to do so will result in a one-month suspension of access.

**Fees and Charges:**
Some fees previously charged on a monthly basis will now be adjusted to a quarterly charge only, aligning more closely with our cost structure and allowing clients more flexibility in their financial planning.

We understand that these changes may pose some challenges for our long-time clients who are accustomed to the longer terms. However, we assure you that these adjustments are made with your best interests at heart, aiming to simplify the process and make the account management experience more user-friendly.
