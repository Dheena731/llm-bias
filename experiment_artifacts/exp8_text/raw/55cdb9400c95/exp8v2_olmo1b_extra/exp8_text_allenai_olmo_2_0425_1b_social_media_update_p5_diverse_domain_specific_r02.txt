B. Data


Data is any information that can be collected, analyzed, and utilized to generate insight or A.I. output.

In this exercise, you will be working with your own dataset.


  1. You will collect several data points about your customer base. These data points will be used to develop a customer persona and to create your A.I. model.
  2. Upload your data to the Google Docs sample spreadsheet template.
  3. Create a new sheet for each data point and name it accordingly. (In the spreadsheet template, you can already see a data sheet example of the exercise.)
  4. Add the data point information and finish the sheet (including a header and a final column using "Total".) Remember to include headings for each column and to keep the spreadsheet organized and easy to read.
  5. Export the spreadsheet to CSV.
  6. Upload the CSV file to the Google Sheets sample template.
  7. Start developing your model with the data and the examples.
