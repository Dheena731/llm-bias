### Reporting a Lost Company Laptop

- **Identify the lost laptop**: Determine the make and model of the lost laptop.
- **Contact IT department**: Immediately reach out to your IT support team or lost and found department for assistance.
- **Provide details**: Explain the situation, including where the laptop was last seen and any identifiable information on it (like usernames, passwords, etc.).
- **Follow up**: Contact the individual whose laptop was reported lost to confirm their information and check on the laptop’s status.
- **Report to internal security protocols**: Ensure the incident is logged and flagged for further investigation by internal security or IT security team.

*Understanding the importance of promptly reporting a lost laptop can help prevent unauthorized access to sensitive information and maintain the security of your company.*
