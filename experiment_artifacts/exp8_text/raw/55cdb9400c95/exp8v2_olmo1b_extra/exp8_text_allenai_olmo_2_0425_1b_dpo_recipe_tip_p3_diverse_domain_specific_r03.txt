When cooking up rice that stays tender and never gets too sticky, here’s a straightforward tip that works wonders in the kitchen:

**Adjust Liquid Quantity:**

If your rice tends to become overly sticky, it often means you’re adding too much liquid. The trick is to use less water and let the water evaporate slightly during the cooking process. Start with one-third less water than the amount of rice you’re using, and if your rice stays dry enough while still being cooked perfectly, that’s the right amount of liquid. 

Here’s why less water works: When you add more water than necessary to rice, the resulting porridge-like consistency allows sticky spots to form. By cutting down the water content, you give your rice more space to absorb only the right amount of liquid, preventing it from becoming too sticky.

Let the rice absorb the liquid instead of liquid soaking into it, and you’ll achieve a perfectly cooked, less sticky medium-rice consistency.

Remember, this tip is all about precision and common sense. Adjusting on the fly based on how your rice responds to changes can make a world of difference in your cooking experience. Happy cooking!
