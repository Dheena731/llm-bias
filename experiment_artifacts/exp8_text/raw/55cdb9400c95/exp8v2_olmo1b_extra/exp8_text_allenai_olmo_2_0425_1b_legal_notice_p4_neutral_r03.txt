Here's an example of a short legal-style notice. In this notice, a company refunds a credit card number for a purchase that was made through PayPal on a computer with a Windows operating system.

PayPal (not the company, but the payment processor) is refundable for purchases made with purchases made with a computer equipped with Windows operating system.

This is not a legally binding statement.

To get started, make a copy of the PayPal statement and save it for the records.

If your organization refunds credits on a Windows computer, scan the computer and save the result. If the computer is a laptop, make a copy of the hard drive and save it. You do not have to fully wipe the hard drive or reformat it.

This is what you scan should look like.

If you do not have a copy of the hard drive, make a copy of the computer's hard drive.

You should scan the digital camera and send us the digital images. For more information, see our scanning page.

If the computer belongs to someone else and you do not have permission to scan it, tell the owner of the computer that you want to preserve the hard drive and let the owner know you need to preserve the device as evidence for a copyright infringement notice and file a suit. Before you steal the hard drive, inform the owner that you are planning to do that. If you do not do that, the person might find out about your plan to steal the hard drive and demand it back.

For more information about what to do, see our guide to copyright infringement.

Organizations may store electronic copies of hard drive images or digital images of hard drives. 

If there is a dispute between you and your customer about the amount of money to be refunded on a hard drive, you should contact PayPal to advise them of the dispute. You should try to communicate with PayPal as early as possible in the dispute. Generally, customers do not know how long they have to dispute a refund when they receive it. If the customer disputes a refund, you should not refund the money to the customer. Instead, you should send the customer a notice explaining that you are writing to inform the customer that you are filing a lawsuit. The notice should include a link to a notice that you have filed a lawsuit and a link to a copy of an email you sent your customer that explains the lawsuit. You should send the notice within 30 days of receiving the request to refund your money.

If you retain a copy of the hard drive and send us that copy, we will keep the hard drive for a longer period of time. If you do not retain a copy of the hard drive and send us a digital image of it, we will keep the image for a shorter time than we do if you retain a hard drive copy.

If you send us a hard drive for scanning, we will not scan the hard drive for the purpose of retaining a copy of the hard drive. If you send us a hard drive, we will scan the device for the purpose of preserving evidence in a lawsuit and not for the purpose of retaining a copy of the hard drive. If we cannot view the hard drive, we will not make our own copy of the hard drive. Instead, we will send a copy of the hard drive to you for a fee. We will send the hard drive to you in an envelope.

If you send us a digital image of the hard drive, we may not be able to convert the image to a usable format. If we cannot convert the image, we will send a message to you informing you that we cannot make a copy of the hard drive.
