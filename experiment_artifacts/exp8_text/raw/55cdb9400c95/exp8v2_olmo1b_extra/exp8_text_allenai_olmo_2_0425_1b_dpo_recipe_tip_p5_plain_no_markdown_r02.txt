To enhance the flavor of your roasted vegetables, consider adding a variety of flavorful seasonings at different stages of the cooking process. Start by choosing herbs like oregano, thyme, and basil which add a robust depth to your dish. For a burst of freshness, toss them into the pan towards the beginning of cooking, allowing them to infuse their oils and flavors into the vegetables. Later, sprinkle additional herbs, like rosemary or sage, over the vegetables while they’re cooking in the residual heat for a more developed scent and flavor. Finally, for a salty kick, toss a generous amount of coarse salt or a high-quality sea salt at the end of the cooking process to enhance the natural flavors and textures of your roasted vegetables.
