When refilling your prescription, ensure you're using the correct medication and dosage. Check the expiration date on your current prescription, and consult with your healthcare provider if you're uncertain about anything. Also, remember to keep your original prescription and any accompanying patient information documents safe and secure, as these documents are crucial for your healthcare provider's record and for any future prescriptions or medical procedures. Receiving and filling your prescriptions as prescribed is vital for your health and wellbeing.
