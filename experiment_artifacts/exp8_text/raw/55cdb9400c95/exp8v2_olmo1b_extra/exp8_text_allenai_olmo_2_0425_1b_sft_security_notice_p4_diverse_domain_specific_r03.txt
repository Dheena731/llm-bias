What is Two-Factor Authentication?

Two-factor authentication is a security feature that adds an extra layer of protection to your account. It requires users to provide two forms of verification before gaining access to their accounts. Common forms of authentication include a one-time password sent to your mobile phone or a fingerprint scan. 

You can activate the 2FA option in your account settings. Please complete the form provided to start the process. 

Why Enabling 2FA is a Good Idea?

1. Enhanced Security: Two-factor authentication offers an extra barrier against unauthorized access. It significantly reduces the chances of a hacker gaining control of your account.
  
2. Peace of Mind: Enabling 2FA boosts your confidence and reduces the anxiety associated with online transactions.

3. Compliance: Many financial institutions, online services, and government agencies require 2FA as standard.

4. Versatility: The 2FA process is straightforward and can be customized to your preferences.
