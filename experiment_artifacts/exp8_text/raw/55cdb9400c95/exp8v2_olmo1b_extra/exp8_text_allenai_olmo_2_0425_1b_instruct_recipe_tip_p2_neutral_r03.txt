When storing fresh herbs, it's essential to maintain their freshness. Here's a short tip: Keep herbs in a cool place such as the refrigerator crisper or a plastic bag in the vegetable drawer to protect them from moisture and light. Use dried herbs in your recipes or store fresh ones in an airtight container in the freezer, where they can last for up to 6 months. Remember, it's best to trim leaves back to the stem for airflow and to avoid cross-contamination.
