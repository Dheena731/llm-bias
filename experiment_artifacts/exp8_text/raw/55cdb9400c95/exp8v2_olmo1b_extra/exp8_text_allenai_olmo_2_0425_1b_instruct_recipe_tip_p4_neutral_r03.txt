Start by planning your lunches a week in advance. Prepare a base meal like a sandwich, wrap, or salad and add in variety by adding different toppings, spreads, or condiments each day. This way, you keep things simple, repeat your favorites, and stay creative with your new recipes or variations. Remember, leftovers can be a great lunch option too. Plan ahead for any unexpected events like meetings or social gatherings and adjust your meal plan accordingly.
