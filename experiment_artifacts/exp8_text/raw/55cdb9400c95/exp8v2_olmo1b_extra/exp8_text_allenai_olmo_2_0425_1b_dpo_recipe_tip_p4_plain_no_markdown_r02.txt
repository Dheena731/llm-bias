Plan your lunches in advance to avoid rushing on weekdays. Start by identifying the meals you and your colleagues enjoy and look forward to. Consider what ingredients are on sale or easy to prepare at home to save time. Prepare ingredients in advance whenever possible so you can simply reassemble them into meals. Use convenient packing containers or wraps to pack lunches quickly. Create a weekly meal plan that includes variety, so you don’t get bored, and consider leftovers from dinners that can be repurposed into lunches the next day. Keep a stash of reusable lunch containers and utensils at home for a fuss-free packing experience. Finally, set aside a small portable container or insulated bag just for lunches to ensure they stay fresh and tasty throughout the workday.
