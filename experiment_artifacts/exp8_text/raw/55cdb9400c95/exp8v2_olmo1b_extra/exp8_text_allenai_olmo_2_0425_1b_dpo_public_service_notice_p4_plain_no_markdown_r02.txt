A heat advisory is in effect today through tomorrow. Under this advisory, temperatures are expected to remain above 90°F (32°C) during the day. High humidity levels are expected to compound the heat, making it feel even hotter. It's important to stay hydrated and to take breaks in the shade when possible. Residents are urged to monitor their health and that of their family and friends. Keep cool by using air conditioning when indoors and avoid outdoors activities or laborious tasks. The city's department of public health reminds everyone to stay hydrated and seek out cooler environments when needed.
