# Literature Review Draft - Feedback Note

**Introduction**
- Brief overview of the topic and its significance.
- Mention the aim of the review and outline what is covered.

**Review Structure**
- List the primary sections: background, review methodology, findings, and conclusion.

**Background**
- Detailed description of the historical context and current literature related to the topic.
- Explanation of the gaps in existing research that this review aims to address.

**Review Methodology**
- Explanation of the selection criteria for included studies.
- Outline of the data sources and their reliability.
- Description of the research process and any limitations noted (e.g., limited range of studies, self-reported data).

**Findings**
- Summary of the main results obtained from the reviewed articles.
- Analysis of trends, patterns, and significant findings.
- Critique of the studies for methodological quality and consistency.

**Conclusion**
- Summary of the review's contributions to the field.
- Recommendations for further research based on the findings.
- Assessment of the clarity and readability of the review's presentation.

**Recommendations**
- Suggest improvements for clarity, coherence, and scientific rigor.
- Suggest additional sources to enhance understanding of the topic.
