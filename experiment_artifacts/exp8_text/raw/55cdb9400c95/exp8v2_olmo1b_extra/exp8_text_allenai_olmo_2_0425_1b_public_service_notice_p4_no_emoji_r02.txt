Isolating (warning)


Isolating (yellow)

Suspend all non-essential activities. Isolating is a warning or even an order to stay away from a particular area for 24 hours. The isolation order does not affect your daily life.

Isolating (orange)

Isolate the people who were in close contact with the person. The isolation order affects your daily life. You need to stay away from your daily activities.

Isolating (red)

Notify the local health authority. The isolation order has special conditions. You have to stay away from your daily life.

You can see the global EU-wide alert system on the Euro Emergency Alert website.
