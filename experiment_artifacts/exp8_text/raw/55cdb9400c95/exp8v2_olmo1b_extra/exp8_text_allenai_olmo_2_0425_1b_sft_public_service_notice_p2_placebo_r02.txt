Public Notice:

From: Department of Transportation
Date: March 15, 2023
Time: 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM (local time)
Venue: Downtown City Park

To all residents and visitors of Downtown City Park, we are pleased to inform you that there will be a temporary road closure on the following stretch of main thoroughfare from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM:

Road Name: Main Street

As part of continued modernization efforts to improve both safety and accessibility in our city’s transportation infrastructure, we need to reroute traffic to surrounding streets and create temporary parking areas. To accommodate additional traffic, we will be closing Main Street.

Please note that there will be a new ramp at the intersection of our temporary ramp site, allowing traffic to continue seamlessly to Main Street. Please be mindful of this change as you make travel plans for today.

Please follow the updated directions provided by the Department of Transportation and remember to follow local traffic laws at all times. We will conduct a survey to monitor the success of this temporary closure.

We thank you for your understanding and cooperation during this road closure. Please contact the Department of Transportation at 555-1234 for any questions.

Thank you.
