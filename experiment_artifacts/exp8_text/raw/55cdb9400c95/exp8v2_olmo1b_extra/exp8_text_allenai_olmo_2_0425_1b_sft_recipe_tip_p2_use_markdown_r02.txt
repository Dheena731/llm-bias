# Tips for Storing Fresh Herbs

- **Keep in a Dry, Air-Insulated Area:** Place herbs in a dry, dark cupboard away from direct sunlight to help them last longer. 
- **Replace Air Circulation:** Use a glass jar to store herbs in the refrigerator and remove the top part where air can circulate. 
- **Use Herb Binders:** Cut stems into shorter lengths and wrap them in cheesecloth or a similar material to prevent bruising and maintain moisture inside. 
- **Trim Leaves:** To prevent pests and to keep them healthier, trim off any damaged or diseased parts.
