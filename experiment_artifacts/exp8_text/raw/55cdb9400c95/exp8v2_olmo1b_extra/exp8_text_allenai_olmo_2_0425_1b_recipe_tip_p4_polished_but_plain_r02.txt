The 3-4-5 rule

Use the 3-4-5 rule to calculate the recommended weekly protein intake for your diet. This rule is based on the average calorie intake of an adult woman or man, depending on whether you are male or female. The rule tells us that adults should eat around 3-4-5 ounces of protein per day.

Calculate your protein intake as follows:

  • Multiply your height in inches by your weight in pounds.
  • Divide the result by 3500.
  • Multiply the result by 6.

For example, if you’re 5 feet 7 inches tall, you weigh 170 pounds, and you want to eat 200 grams of protein per day, then you would calculate as follows:

  • 5 × 170 = 850
  • 850 ÷ 3500 = 0.25
  • 0.25 × 6 = 1.5

The 3-4-5 rule can help us calculate the recommended protein intake for any number of people.

Note: This calculation is for someone who is not pregnant.
