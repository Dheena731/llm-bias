For a cozy home-cooked dish, try our Weekend Wardrobe Wrap-Up. Begin by gathering your favorite simple ingredients; this week, we suggest a loaf of crusty bread, a jar of jarred salsa, and a simple bowl of low-fat ranch dressing.

Cook lunch today, and sauté one wing of a rosemary chicken breast with a can of diced tomatoes and your chosen medium salsa. Finish the meal with a side of a quick and easy honey-roasted vegetable medley, using zucchini, carrots, and green beans.

For dinner tonight, let's rotate to our Baked City Reserve pot roast. Start by prepping a mound of store-bought rye bread crusts into slices. Gather three pounds of your favorite pot roast, season it to your heart's content, and roast it for 30 minutes at 375 degrees in your preheated oven.

For the finishing touch, bring back your Friday night's favorite dish: Ziploc baguette halves and your favorite cheese spread, paired with your desired wine. The leftovers will last for a delightful brunch on Saturday.
