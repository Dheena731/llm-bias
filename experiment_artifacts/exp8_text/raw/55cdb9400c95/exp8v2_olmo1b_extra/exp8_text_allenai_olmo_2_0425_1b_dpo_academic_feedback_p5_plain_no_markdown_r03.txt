Missing citations in an essay can lead to confusion and a loss of credibility. Proper citation not only acknowledges the source material but also prevents misunderstandings about the author's claims and the validity of their information. Without citations, readers may struggle to trace the origins of arguments, statistics, and quotes, which undermines the essay's overall authority and can unfairly discredit the author’s work. In academic writing, citing sources is not merely a stylistic choice; it is a fundamental aspect of academic integrity and responsible communication. Without accurate citations, essays run the risk of plagiarism, which is not only unethical but often also grounds for academic disqualification. Therefore, it is imperative to include appropriate citations in any serious academic or research document to ensure that the reader can verify, understand, and appreciate the sources the author has drawn upon.
