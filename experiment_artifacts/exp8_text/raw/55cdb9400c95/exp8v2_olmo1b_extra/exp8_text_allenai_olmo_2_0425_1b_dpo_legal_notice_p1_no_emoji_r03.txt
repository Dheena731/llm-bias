Attention all account holders,

We are hereby notifying you of important changes to our account terms as of February 15, 2023. We appreciate your prompt attention to these changes. Please review the changes attached to this email and contact us via our official website if you have any questions or require further clarification.

Your account’s continued trust is of utmost importance to us, and we are committed to maintaining the highest standards of integrity and fairness.

Thank you for your continued patronage.
