Recycling collection schedules are now being modified to better serve community needs. Beginning this month, residents will have their recycling and paperboard collections shifted to specific weeks to ensure proper processing and prevent overflow at collection sites. The new schedule alternates between Tuesdays and Thursdays for heavy recycling items such as glass bottles and cans. For paperboard and other light recyclables, collections will switch to Saturdays only. It's important to ensure all recyclable materials are sorted correctly to maintain the health and efficiency of the recycling program. Please check the city's website or contact your local recycling center for more detailed information on the new collection schedule. Thank you for your cooperation in helping maintain a sustainable environment.
