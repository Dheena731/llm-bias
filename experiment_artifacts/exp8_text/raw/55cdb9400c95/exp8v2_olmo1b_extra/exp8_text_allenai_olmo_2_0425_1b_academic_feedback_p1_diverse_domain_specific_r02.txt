Discussion of a draft literature review:

  1. Where are the gaps in evidence? What’s missing?
    • What are the main trends or questions driving research, and what have we learned?
    • What are the gaps or issues? What’s missing? What are the directions for future research?
  2. Recommend how to proceed with future research.
    • How should the topic be expanded and where?
    • What are the next steps?
    • How can we conduct an experimental study? A review of the literature?
