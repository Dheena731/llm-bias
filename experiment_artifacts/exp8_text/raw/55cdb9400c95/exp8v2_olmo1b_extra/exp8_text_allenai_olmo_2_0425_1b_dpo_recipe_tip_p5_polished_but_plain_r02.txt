Seasoning roasted vegetables can transform bland dishes into delightful meals. To enhance their flavor, add a variety of spices. Start with salt and pepper for basic balance, then sauté vegetables in olive oil with your chosen herbs or spices to infuse them with deep, savory notes. Common choices include paprika, chili powder, cumin, curry powder, garlic powder, or dried herbs like basil or oregano. Stir the vegetables into the oil after they've cooked a bit to eliminate any bitter flavors that might emerge from raw spices. This technique helps the spices penetrate the vegetables, creating a more aromatic and flavorful end result. Experiment with different combinations to find what works best for your palate and dietary needs.
