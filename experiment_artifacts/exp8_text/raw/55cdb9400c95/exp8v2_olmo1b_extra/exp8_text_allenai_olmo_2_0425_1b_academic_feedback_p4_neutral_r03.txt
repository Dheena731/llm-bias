In my opinion, a paper cannot be submitted until it's revised and approved by the referee(s).

If you have time, I think it is a good idea to re-write the paper yourself and edit each sentence, paragraph, and subsection. Put yourself in the referee's shoes and consider what might be missing or not written as well as you could.

Then, contact the editor and tell him/her that you are revising the paper. I'm sure not everyone will have the time to rewrite their own papers, but it's a good way to save the other person from having to do it.
