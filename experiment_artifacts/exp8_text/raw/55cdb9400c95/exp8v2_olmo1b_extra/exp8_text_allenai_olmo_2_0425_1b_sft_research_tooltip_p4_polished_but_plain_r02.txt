Missing-value markers are crucial to statistical analysis and data interpretation, as they indicate areas of uncertainty or gaps in the provided data. These markers are often used in regression analysis to describe the nature of the errors present in the data. In a broader sense, they can occur in various forms, such as variables that did not turn up in the analysis or characteristics that are missing from the dataset. They are an essential tool for researchers and analysts to acknowledge and understand the limitations of their data, highlighting the areas that need further exploration or adjustments.
