**Reminder: Lock Your Screens!**

- Always follow the company’s security protocol by locking your screen every time you step away from your desk.
- This contributes to the safety and protection of sensitive information.
- If you need to share your screen with someone else, use the "Screen Sharing" feature to minimize the window size.
- Remember to log out and unshare the session if you're finished working on that device.
- Prevent unauthorized access to your work environment and maintain the integrity of your data.
- Thank you for your vigilance and commitment to security.
