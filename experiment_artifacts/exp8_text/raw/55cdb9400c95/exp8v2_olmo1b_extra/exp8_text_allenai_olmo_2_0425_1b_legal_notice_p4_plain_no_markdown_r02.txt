There can be no refund for all items purchased in the 2021 2022 academic year or later except for:

  • items that were purchased before May 1, 2021, and the purchaser intended to sell on Amazon or third-party selling platforms, if the item is still under warranty.

Items bought before May 1, 2021, and the purchaser intended to sell on Amazon or third-party selling platforms are not eligible for refunds unless the item is still under warranty. All other items are eligible for refunds.

If you are unsure whether an item is eligible for a refund, contact us and we will let you know. If the item is eligible for a refund, we will refund you as soon as possible.

Will I get a refund on items that are not eligible for a refund?

Yes. We will refund you if the item is eligible for a refund. There is no charge for refunds on eligible items.

Can I request a refund for items that are not eligible for a refund?

Yes. If you request a refund for an ineligible item, we will refund you the price of the item.

If other buyers request a refund, they can use Amazon's "Contact Seller" feature to dispute the refund. However, this does not mean that the seller can refuse a refund for an ineligible item.

Can I request a refund for an eligible item that is no longer under warranty?

Yes. We will refund you the price of the item if the item is no longer under warranty.

For more information on refunds, visit our refund policy.
