All Words Matter

For example, 90%+ of the algorithm names CEO “our AI platform” or “our AI-powered platform”. The platform is the direct beneficiary of the AI. In contrast, I see little AI in the names of lawyers and law firms. Lawyers don't have a platform to sell, but they offer a service, so the service is end-user-centric. It focuses on clients and what they need, not on the AI itself.

It's not a black or white issue, but most companies with AI use generic AI names with an AI product name.  That's problematic because it creates unnecessary noise from other companies in the same space. It's also a key marketing-related misstep for AI startups.

The good news is that it's still early days to build a brand around AI.  So some of these mistakes are soon to be ironed out.  And the landscape we're on right now is still full of AI startups.
