Example Program

Here is a very simple program. It is a reinforced chain for a simple repeat:

(defn chain []
  (let [chain (repeatedly 4 #(repeat 4 %))]
    (if (= 4 (count chain))
      (start (first chain))
      (chain (rest chain))))
This program will print out the word "HELLO" one million times.
