A good salt and pepper isn't just a good seasoning, it's a seasoning. Salt is best applied to larger pieces of food like roast potatoes or carrots to ensure even seasoning. Remember the rule of "One pinch per person." Don't start with the whole bag of salt and then just keep adding, this will certainly cause your food to be overseasoned. If you want your vegetables to have a little more flavor, try adding a little extra salt early on while they're still soft and a little less towards the end when they're crispy.
