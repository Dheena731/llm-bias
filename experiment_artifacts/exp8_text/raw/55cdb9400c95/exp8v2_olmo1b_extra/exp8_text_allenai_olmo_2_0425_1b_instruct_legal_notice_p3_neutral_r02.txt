**Cookie Policy

By accessing and using our website (the "Website"), you indicate your consent to the use of certain cookies. A "cookie" is a small piece of data sent from a website and stored on your device by your web browser while you are visiting that site.

**Purpose of Cookies**

The primary purpose of placing cookies on this website is to provide users with a better experience by remembering their preferences, browsing history, and site navigation activity. For example, by storing information about a user's selection in searches or shopping cart, we can make the interaction between users and the website more efficient and personalized.

**Types of Cookies**

There are two primary types of cookies used on this website:

1. **Session Cookies**: These are temporary cookies that are stored on your device only while you are using our website. Once you close your browser, these cookies are usually deleted, although this setting can be adjusted by the user within their browser.
   
2. **Persistent Cookies**: These are stored on your device after you close your browser and remain on the device until they expire (usually after a period of time set by the creator of the cookie, or after a specific date). Once a persistent cookie has expired, it will be deleted.
