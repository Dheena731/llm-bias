It's important to take regular breaks from screens to give your eyes a rest and improve focus. Here's a quick reminder to take short breaks from screens:

- Take a 20-30-second break every hour when you're browsing the internet or using digital devices.
- Consider looking at an object 20 feet away at least once every hour to promote mental health.
- Use the 20-20-20 rule to remind yourself to take breaks: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds.
- Prioritize screen-free time to allow for other activities, such as reading a book or practicing mindfulness.

Taking care of your eyes is important, so remember to give them a little rest now and then.
