**Headline:** Quick Stew Recipe: Delicious Weeknight Chicken Veggie Stew

**Recipe Overview:**
- **Ingredients:** 1 lb chicken thighs or breasts, cut into pieces; 2 large carrots, diced; 2 large onions, diced; 4 medium potatoes, diced; 2 stalks celery, diced; 2 cups tomato sauce; 1 tsp ground cumin; 1 tsp paprika; 1/2 tsp thyme; salt and pepper, to taste; 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables (e.g., peas, carrots, corn)
- **Preparation:** 1. In a large pot, steam the chicken until cooked through. 2. While the chicken steams, in a large pot, sauté the carrots, onions, and celery in olive oil until softened. 3. Add the tomato sauce and seasonings. 4. Stir in the prepared chicken, mixed vegetables, and let simmer on low heat until flavors meld (approximately 30 minutes). 5. Serve warm with crusty bread for dipping.

**Tips:** This stew is perfect for leftovers, and the chicken adds a rich, protein-packed twist to the meal. Experiment with adjusting the spice and vegetable amounts for personal preference.
