**Cooking Tip: Easy Vegetarian Weeknight Dinner**

For a simple yet delicious vegetarian weeknight dinner, start with a hearty stovetop rice or pasta dish. Pair it with a steamed or roasted vegetable meal. This recipe is quick and easy to prepare, ensuring dinner is ready by the time you're done with the dishes.

- **Ingredients:**
  - 1 cup of short-grain rice or pasta
  - 2 cups water or vegetable broth
  - 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables
  - 1 small onion, diced
  - 3 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 can (14 oz) of diced tomatoes
  - 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes or other desired level of heat
  - Salt and pepper to taste

- **Instructions:**
  1. Cook rice or pasta according to package instructions, usually 15-20 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in saucepan over medium heat. Add onion and garlic, saute until fragrant, 3-4 minutes.
  3. Add frozen vegetables, salt, pepper, and tomato. Stir until vegetables are softened, 3 minutes.
  4. Add rice or pasta to vegetable mixture. Stir well, making sure all ingredients are well combined.
  5. Season to taste with more salt and pepper if needed. Serve hot.
