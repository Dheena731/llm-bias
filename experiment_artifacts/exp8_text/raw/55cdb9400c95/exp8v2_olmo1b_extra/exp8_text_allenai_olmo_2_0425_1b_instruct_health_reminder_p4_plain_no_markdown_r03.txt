Take short breaks from screens.

Our screens have become a staple in our daily work and leisure activities but they can also be a distraction that takes a toll on our eyes and overall well-being. Short breaks are helpful in reducing eye strain and improving concentration.

A few minutes away from your device can do wonders to refocus and recharge. Try closing your eyes for a few seconds and focusing on your breath or observing your surroundings. This practice not only helps with refreshment but also with the mental clarity needed during productive moments.

Plan to set aside specific times for these breaks throughout your day. Whether it's every hour, or after completing a specific task, ensuring you schedule them can make a significant difference in your productivity and health.
