The goal is to be original and not fall into the temptation of repeating a post from last week.

• The number of characters you pick should be around 140 characters.

• Try to use short phrases or sentences.

• Try to include a quote (that doesn’t have a hashtag) or a direct quote from the original content.

• If you don’t have a quote or a direct quote from the original content, you can quote a small portion of the original content.

The original posts don’t have to be from a blog post.

There are several variations of the same post from different sources.
