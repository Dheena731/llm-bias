We are making changes to our recycling collection schedule. Here is what you need to know:

* The weekly recycling bins will be relocated from the street to new locations within the community park.
* Separate blue bin collections for paper, glass and plastic will begin on certain days only. The collection days are listed on our website and will update frequently.
* Due to the change we encourage everyone to prepare by bringing reusable containers for paper products and collecting glass in a clear plastic bottle.
* We will be increasing the frequency of container collections to ensure waste is managed efficiently.
* Our staff will be happy to assist you with any questions about the new recycling schedule.

Thank you for your cooperation and support. We believe in a cleaner and greener future.
