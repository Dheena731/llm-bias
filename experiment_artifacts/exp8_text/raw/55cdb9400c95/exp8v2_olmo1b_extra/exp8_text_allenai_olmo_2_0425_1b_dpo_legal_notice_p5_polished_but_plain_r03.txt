Attention all users of the shared network!

We are writing to inform all our users of the acceptable use policy for our network. We expect our users to remember and respect several key points:

1. Respect others' privacy: Do not access, modify, or share personal files on our network without explicit permission from the owner of the file.
2. Use the network ethically: Avoid engaging in any activities that are illegal, unethical, deceptive, or harmful to others, including but not limited to, fraud, bandwidth abuse, or harassment.
3. Follow network rules: Be mindful of the bandwidth limits and established usage policies. Do not overload the network with excessive traffic or engage in any activity that could cause service disruption to other users.
4. Congregate the use of computers and internet devices: Do not use your equipment to impersonate another user, mislead others, or harass others on the network.
5. Exercise discretion: Do not engage in behavior that could be construed as unacceptable or harmful by our institution or the general public.

We remind you that our network is a shared resource and is not reserved exclusively for your use. Should you violate any of these rules, we will take appropriate action, including the revocation of access to the network or other disciplinary measures. Policies will be updated from time to time to ensure they remain effective and relevant.

Please read and understand these guidelines before accessing our network. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our IT department at the provided contact information.

Thank you for your cooperation and responsible use of our shared resources.
