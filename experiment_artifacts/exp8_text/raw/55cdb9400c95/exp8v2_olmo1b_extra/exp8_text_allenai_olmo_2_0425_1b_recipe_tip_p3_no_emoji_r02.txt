General info

Sticky rice is the most widely consumed type of rice in the world.


Sticky rice is used in a variety of desserts, such as rice pudding, sticky rice balls or rice balls, and rice cakes (known as mochi in Japanese), that are seen in Japanese cuisine.

Sticky rice is also used in steamed rice cakes. There are hundreds of types of this cake, but the most popular is the "Hokkaido sweet rice cake" (Hokkaido-mochi), which is rice cake wrapped with mochi. It is filled with sweet bean jam.

Mochi is made by pounding rice dough into a sheet and pressing it into balls, and then steaming them. It is fried, boiled or made in rice kettles and eaten as a snack.

Commonly consumed dishes

Sticky rice is also used as a base for soups or stews, such as "Gaga-chan", which is boiled meat simmered with sticky rice in shabu-shabu.

Fresh sticky rice also called "sira suku" is made by simmering sticky rice with water and salt. It is used as a snack to go with drinks like alcoholic beverages.
