Refilling a prescription is just as important as getting the initial prescription filled. Make sure you return to your doctor or pharmacy to have the prescription refilled. The medication may have expired and could be unsafe if taken after the expiration date. Double-check that the correct medication is prescribed and the right dosage is filled. Obey the prescribed dosage instructions. Keep the original prescription paperwork with your records. Reusing a prescription can lead to serious health risks. Always respect your healthcare provider's instructions.
