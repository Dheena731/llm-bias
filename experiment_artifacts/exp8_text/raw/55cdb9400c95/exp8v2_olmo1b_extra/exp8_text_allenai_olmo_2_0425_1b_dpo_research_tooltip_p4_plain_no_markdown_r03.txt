A missing-value marker or a gap marker is a term often used in statistics and data analysis to indicate points where information is not available or has been deliberately excluded. When you see a blank space marked with such a marker, it signifies that the value at that location in the dataset is unknown or has been deliberately not included for various reasons like missing data (like when people fail to fill out a particular question), data entry errors, or possibly due to data cleaning or truncation. It's important for analysts, researchers, and data users to understand these gaps to appropriately handle and interpret the data accurately.
