Subject: Quarterly Access Reviews

Dear Members,

As a valued member of our community, you have the opportunity to review your access privileges on a quarterly basis. During these meetings, we will be assessing the following factors to determine if your access is still appropriate for your needs:

- Access to specific areas or sections within our platform.
- Required technical requirements.

This assessment is in place to ensure that your access meets current standards and best practices. If there are any changes or additions that may impact your current access, we will provide you with the necessary information in writing.

Please take a few moments to complete this form. It is important that you provide any requested additional information to support your request for adjusted access. We will review your response promptly, and a decision will be made in accordance with the guidelines provided above.

In order to assist you in this process, you will receive an email notification with the results of the review and instructions on how to proceed.
