Our First Amendment Guarantee

We believe that the right to a public forum is a fundamental civil liberty. We believe you should have the right to refuse service to whomever you want. But the Civil Rights Act allows us to punish businesses that fail to comply with our nondiscrimination policy. If you read the fine print in the terms and conditions, you will see that we reserve the right to review and change our policies at any time, and to enforce them by withholding your access to the site. That means we can ban you from the site if you violate our nondiscrimination policy. You can't do anything to make us change our minds; it is up to us to decide whether and how to enforce our policy. You have no right to complain about the consequences of our policy.
