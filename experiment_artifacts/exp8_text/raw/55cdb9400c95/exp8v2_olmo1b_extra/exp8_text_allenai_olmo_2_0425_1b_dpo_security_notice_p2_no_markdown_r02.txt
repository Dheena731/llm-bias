Attention all employees!

We want to remind you all about the dangers of phishing emails. These deceptive messages appear to come from legitimate sources such as your bank or your company's IT department but are actually designed to steal your personal or company information. Here are some red flags to look out for:

* Email addresses that are misspelled or contain unusual characters.
* Urgency or fear tactics in the subject line.
* Requests for personal or sensitive information such as passwords, credit card numbers, or social security numbers.
* Attachments that appear to be from a trusted source but are actually malicious files.

Please report any suspicious emails to our IT department immediately. It is our responsibility to protect you and your company from cyber threats. Keep your devices and internet accounts secure by using strong passwords and keeping software up to date.

Thank you for your vigilance and cooperation. Together we can ensure a safe and secure working environment. Please respond to this email if you have any questions or need further information.
