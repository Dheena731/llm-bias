To ensure the effective use and appropriate utilization of our shared network, we wish to present the following guidelines for all users:

1. **Respect the Network:** All users are required to use the shared network in an appropriate, professional, and lawful manner. This includes avoiding the use of illegal content and ensuring that the network is not used for illegal activities such as hacking, unauthorized access, or copyright infringement.

2. **Network Limits:** Be aware of the bandwidth limitations of the shared network. Excessive use of bandwidth for non-work-related purposes may result in a temporary or permanent reduction of access to the network for all users. Please consider the needs and activities of your colleagues when sharing large files or conducting intensive computational tasks.

3. **Security:** We encourage the use of secure login credentials and the implementation of firewall settings to protect the network from unauthorized access. Suspicion or deliberate actions that compromise network security may result in examination by network administrators and potentially action against the offender.

4. **Communication:** If you encounter or suspect any illegal activities on the network, please report it to the IT department immediately. We are committed to maintaining a secure and trustworthy environment for our users.

5. **Acceptable Use Policy:** For further information and clarification regarding the acceptable use of our shared network, kindly refer to our Acceptable Use Policy found on our website. Any breaches of these policies will be subject to review and enforcement actions, including possible restriction of network access.

By adhering to these guidelines, you will contribute to the continued efficient operation and use of our shared network, which is a valuable resource for our community. Please take this notice as an official statement of our expectations for responsible and lawful network usage.
