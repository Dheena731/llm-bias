Take a moment this week to reflect on how you've been on your fitness journey. It's important to consider what has been working well and what needs tweaking. What have you enjoyed most about your workouts? How have you been able to stay motivated? Looking back at your progress, what habits can you continue? There's no need to compare yourself to others—what matters most is how you progress and feel overall, so remember to give yourself credit for all the hard work you've put in.
