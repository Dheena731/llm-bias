Attention all employees!

We want to remind you never to open phishing emails. They can come from anywhere, even people you know, and they're a scam that can cause a lot of damage. 

Phishing emails often pretend to be from your bank or a trusted company, asking you to provide sensitive information like passwords or account numbers. They can even look like official communications from a boss or supervisor.

Never click on any link or download any attachment from these emails. Always type the URL directly into your web browser to confirm the request is legitimate. 

If you see something suspicious, report it to your IT department immediately.

Thank you for staying safe online!
