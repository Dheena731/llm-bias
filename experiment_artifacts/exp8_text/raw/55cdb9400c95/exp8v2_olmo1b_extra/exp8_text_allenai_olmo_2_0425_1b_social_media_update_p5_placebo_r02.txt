What Are the Most Important Steps in Writing a Research Paper?

Writing a research paper is an iterative process that involves several steps. Every step is crucial, so take your time and do not rush. Remember, you will be grading yourself, so if you make a mistake, you'll have no one to blame but yourself!

  • Start with a clear statement of purpose and purpose statement. The first sentence or two of your introduction should clearly state the main idea of your research paper in about 1 or 2 sentences. For example, if you are writing about the history of computer programming, your introduction may include a statement like the following:
  • According to the article "The History of the World," modern-day computer programming began in the early 1940s. The first programming language was created by John von Neumann and his team of graduate students.
  • In the next section, you may describe the evolution of computer programming. For example, you can write something like this:
  • Throughout the course of the 1940s, programmers developed a variety of programming languages. The first one was introduced in 1946 by John von Neumann and his team of graduate students. The language was called PL/S. PL/S was a procedural language that used a syntax based on assembly language.
  • In the next section, you may describe how programming languages evolved over time. For example, you can write something like this:
  • As computer programmers continued to develop new languages, they realized that different languages had different strengths and weaknesses. They also realized that different languages worked best for different tasks. As a result, the world of computer programming grew into many subfields and specialties. Some of the most popular subfields are web development, mobile development, data science, and cloud computing.
  • In the last section, you may describe how computer programming has changed the world. For example, you can write something like this:
  • Today, computer programming is the key to many fields and industries. Many companies use computer programming to develop products and services. Computer programmers are also used in many fields such as finance, medicine, and transportation. Many companies hire computer programmers to develop new products or improve existing ones.
  • In the last section, you may describe how computer programming has affected your life. For example, you may describe how your job changed because of computer programming.
  • Write the body of your research paper. The body of your research paper should be organized into sections or chapters. Each chapter should contain a topic sentence that introduces each section of your paper. Each topic sentence should be followed by evidence that supports the topic sentence.
