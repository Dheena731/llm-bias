Attention all residents and visitors to the area.

A heat advisory is in effect for the coming days. Be sure to stay hydrated and seek shade when possible. Please avoid working or playing outside during the hottest part of the day, and stay indoors during this time. We have also increased our trash and recycling collection to accommodate the influx of residents who are going to be outside more. Please take advantage of the increased hours of operation to recycle and toss out your trash.
