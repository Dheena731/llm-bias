**Tooltip Header**: Confidence Interval in Chart

**Bullet Points:**

- A confidence interval provides a range of plausible values for an unknown population parameter.
- It's calculated as the survey sample statistic ±(standard error of the sample statistic).
- Confidence levels (e.g., 95%) specify the degree of certainty about the interval.
- In a chart, it's often displayed as a range between two points, indicating the margin of error.
- Confidence intervals help visualize and communicate the precision of a statistic related to a population parameter.
