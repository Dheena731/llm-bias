Take a Short Break from Screens

Can't resist the temptation to stare at your phone screen all day? It's time to inject some much-needed rest into your routine! Here are a few reminders to pause your screen time when needed:

1. **20-20-20 Rule:** Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds. This helps reduce eye strain and fatigue.

2. **Considering Blue Light:** Research suggests that exposure to blue light can disrupt your sleep. Taking breaks from screens, especially in the evening, can contribute to better sleep.

3. **Physical Activity:** Physical movement is a great way to complement your rest. A short walk, stretching, or a quick yoga session can refresh your mind and body.

4. **Deep Breathing:** Use a moment to focus on your breathing to calm your mind and center your thoughts.

5. **Mindfulness or Meditation:** Spend a minute or two being present in the moment, practicing mindfulness or meditation.

6. **Journaling or a Simple Thought:** Write down a few thoughts, ideas, or memories that come to mind. It can help to clear your mind and boost your creativity.

7. **Finalize Your Tasks:** When you return, you’ll be more productive, and your mind will feel rested.

Whether it's a few extra seconds of peace or a longer break, make sure you carve out some time from your digital world to recharge and refocus. It's one less thing to stress about—your mental and physical well-being is worth it!
