When seasoning roasted vegetables, start with a bit of salt and pepper to balance their natural sweetness. For a more complex flavor, consider adding a touch of dried herbs like thyme, rosemary, or oregano. Try adding a bit of acid like lemon juice or vinegar to cut through the fat from roasting and bring out the vegetable’s natural flavors. If you're going for a savory addition, incorporate some bay leaves or grated cheese towards the end of the cooking process for a subtle, smoky flavor. Adjust the seasoning with more salt and pepper as needed to match your taste preferences. Finish with a light dusting of powdered sugar or a touch of melted butter if you want to enhance the sweetness and richness of your roasted vegetables.
