**Cookie Usage Notice**

We use **cookies** to store and retrieve information on our website to improve your browsing experience. By using our website, you agree to our use of cookies.

* **What are cookies?**
  - **Cookies** are small pieces of data sent from a website and stored on your device by your web browser while you are visiting the site.

* **Purpose of cookies**
  - **To improve your experience** by remembering your preferences, displaying relevant content, and customizing your browsing experience.

* **Types of cookies**
  - **Session cookies** – used to provide a seamless experience while you are using the website.
  - **Persistent cookies** – remain on your device after your session ends and can be used to track usage over time.

* **Cookies for performance**
  - **Performance cookies** analyze traffic and site performance to help us improve our websites.

* **Cookies for functionality**
  - **Functional cookies** enable basic website functions, such as login, cart, or basket functionality.

* **Third-party cookies**
  - **Third-party** websites may use cookies to track your interactions with our site for advertising or analytics purposes.

* **Your choice**
  - **You can control and modify your cookie settings at any time by adjusting your web browser’s preferences.**

* **Cookie expiration**
  - **Cookies** will automatically expire after your browser is closed or after a period determined by our privacy policy.

By continuing to use our website, you agree to the use of cookies as described above.
