When a company laptop goes missing, it is crucial to report the incident immediately to the IT department. This notice will guide you on how to do just that.

**How to Report a Lost Company Laptop:**

- **Identify the laptop**: Ensure you clearly describe the laptop in question, including its make, model, and serial number.
  
- **Contact the IT Department**: Find the phone number or the IT support portal of your company. Inform them of the laptop’s disappearance and specify the actions you plan to take post-reporting.

- **Prepare documentation**: Before speaking with IT, collect as much information as possible about the laptop’s usage history, any recent changes, and any login credentials that might still be in use. This documentation will be essential for any investigation.

- **Follow IT Guideelines**: IT generally has specific protocols for handling such incidents. Ensure you’ve adhered to these guidelines as per your company’s cybersecurity policies.

**Why Prompt Action is Necessary:**

A lost laptop can be a security risk. It could contain sensitive company data, trade secrets, or customer information. With immediate action, we can minimize the risk of unauthorized access or compromise to the company's data and reputation.

**In Summary**, lost company laptops are a significant concern, but reporting the incident promptly is key to ensuring the safety and integrity of the company’s sensitive data and assets. Don’t worry, your step toward safeguarding company information is commendable!
