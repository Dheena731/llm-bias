When reviewing an essay, the absence of relevant citations can be a significant red flag, suggesting that the author either failed to conduct adequate research or intentionally omitted necessary data to support their claims. In the academic and research fields, citations are not merely mentions of sources; they serve as a roadmap, directing the reader to where the author found their information. The absence of proper citations can undermine the author's credibility and evidence their argument. It indicates that the essay may not be as thoroughly researched or reliable as it could be. In this context, the absence of citations is not merely a stylistic choice but a crucial element of academic integrity and research quality. Furthermore, it directly impacts the reader's ability to verify the facts presented, ultimately impacting the broader assessment of the essay's contributions to its field.
