**Enabling Two-Factor Authentication**

* **What is Two-Factor Authentication?**
   - Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds an extra layer of security by requiring a second form of verification after the password.

* **Why Enable 2FA?**
   - Prevents unauthorized access even if someone gets your password.
   - Reduces the risk of account compromise due to phishing attacks or malware.

* **How to Enable 2FA:**
   - Visit the [website's security settings](https://username.domain.com/support/console/2fa) and enable 2FA.
   - Choose between SMS-based 2FA, authentication apps (like Google Authenticator), or hardware tokens.

* **Setting Up 2FA:**
   - Follow the on-screen instructions to register a new 2FA device or update existing settings.
   - Make sure to backup your recovery codes in a safe place.

* **Tips for Success:**
   - Use the same authentication app for every site that requires 2FA.
   - Regularly review and update your 2FA settings to stay secure.
