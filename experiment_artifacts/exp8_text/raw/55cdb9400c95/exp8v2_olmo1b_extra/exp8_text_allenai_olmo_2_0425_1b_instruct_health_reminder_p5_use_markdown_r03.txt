**Reminder: Refilling Your Prescription**

- **Schedule a visit:** Make an appointment to get your prescription refilled.
- **Consult your doctor:** Ensure that you have the correct medication and dosage.
- **Prepare your prescription:** Bring the original prescription filled with you.
- **Follow the pharmacy instructions:** Follow the pharmacy's guidelines for refilling the medication.
- **Check expiration date:** Make sure the prescribed medication is still good to take.
