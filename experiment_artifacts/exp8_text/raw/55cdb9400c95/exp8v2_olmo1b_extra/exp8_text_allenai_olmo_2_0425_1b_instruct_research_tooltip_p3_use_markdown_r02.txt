**Date Range Selector**

This selector allows you to choose specific dates within a given period. 
- **Start Date**: Enter the beginning of the range here.
- **End Date**: Insert the end of the range to select dates after this point.
- **Include Dates**: Select dates that fall within the range by checking this box.
```

```
**Tooltip Summary:**
- A date range selector lets you specify a specific period of dates.
- Use Start Date and End Date to define the scope of dates to display.
- Check Include Dates to ensure dates within the selected range are visible.
