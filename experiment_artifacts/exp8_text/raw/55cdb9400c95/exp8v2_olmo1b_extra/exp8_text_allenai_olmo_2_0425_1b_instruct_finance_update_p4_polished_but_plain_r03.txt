The end-of-year tax statement is a document you receive in the mail at the end of December. It tells you how much you owe or are owed by the government in tax. The statement breaks down the amounts due by item like income tax, social security, and other fees. It is important for you to read this document carefully as it can impact your finances for the coming year. If you find you owe more than you expected, you might want to contact your tax professional to work out a payment plan that works for you.
