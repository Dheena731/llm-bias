A missing-value marker is a visual cue used in data visualization and statistical analysis to indicate that a specific data point or observation is not present or accounted for within a dataset. This is a crucial visualization tool that can highlight trends, patterns, or anomalies that might otherwise be obscured by missing data points. By marking these areas with a symbol, like an X, a circle, or an arrow, analysts can better understand the structure of their data, identify potential outliers, or pinpoint areas that require further investigation or data correction before drawing definitive conclusions.
