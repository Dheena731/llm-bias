The purpose of the notice is to announce changes in a major thoroughfare that are affecting most vehicles. Such announcements are generally made 24 hours in advance and will be updated as often as needed.

If you are unable to contact the community, utilize the appropriate social media to announce the change.

Road Closed During A Blizzard or Winter Storm

If a road is completely shut down and inaccessible, post a message that includes:

  • All relevant contact information.
  • Explanation of why the road is closed and whether or not it’s expected to re-open.
  • The names of the entire route as well as the nearest intersections.

With the information above, reach out to someone from your Department of Public Works or similar organization.

If the information is missing or inaccurate, do not delay in contacting the agency. This will ensure the roads are made accessible as soon as possible.

Road Closed During Construction

Road closures are conducted on a case-by-case basis to prevent damage to a road’s surface.

If a road is closed for construction purposes, the Department will post a notice on the road ahead of time. Make sure to provide accurate information regarding open dropoffs, road closures, and any other details associated with the work.

This information will be posted on the current page of your municipality’s website as well as other digital media such as your town Facebook page or Twitter account.
