How to Write a Marketing Note for a New Product

Whether it’s a new subscription-based service, a gift concept, a marketing campaign, or a new product, writing a marketing note is a great way to connect with consumers and build hype for the product. Here are a few ways to write a marketing note that stands out.

1. Create a Digital Marketing Plan

Before you write your marketing note, think about the audience you’re trying to reach. Get creative and determine how and where you want to promote your product. Perhaps you want to target younger consumers or use a specific social media platform. Additionally, consider the type of language you want to use, the tone you want to adopt, and the different ways you’ll connect with your audience.

2. Write a Description

In your marketing note, write a description that clearly presents the concept or product. Describe the product’s benefits, how it will solve a problem, or how it will improve the user experience. Use this list of marketing copywriting examples as a guide.

3. Keep it Short and Simple

While you’ll probably want to include details about the product or solution, keep your writing concise. Use headings or bullet points to make it easy to read, and use storytelling to create a unique voice and personality. Keep the reader engaged with a brief and clear introduction, a captivating description, and a well-written conclusion.

4. Write About the Product

Writing about the product is critical when you’re writing a marketing note. Make sure to use language that resonates with your target audience, and include details that highlight the unique features and benefits of the product. Focus on the benefits and how they can improve the user experience.

5. Include a Call to Action

Your marketing note should include a clear call to action. This common marketing strategy tells your readers that you’re prepared to offer them something of value. Send them to a website with more detailed information, or include a call to action like “sign up for the newsletter” or “contact us to learn more about our products.”

6. Use Emotional Appeals

Writing emotional appeals is a great way to connect with your audience, and help them feel inspired to purchase the product or service you’re promoting. Appeal to their desires, desires, and needs by showing them how your product will benefit them. Use words like “unique,” “special,” “exclusive,” “innovative,” and “new” to create a sense of urgency. You can also use words like “welcome,” “inspire,” and “better” to encourage your readers to take action.

7. Use the Correct Tone

Your tone should match your brand. If you’re a luxury brand, for example, you might sound more casual than a startup. Additionally, you should use language that resonates with your target audience. If you’re creating a marketing note for a new product, choose language that makes you sound knowledgeable and excited.

8. Write to Your Readers

Remember that your readers are more likely to purchase a product if they feel inspired and motivated to do so. Write with words that connect with your readers and make them feel engaged. Tell them how the product will change their lives, or how their lives will be enhanced with the product.

9. Proofread Your Writing

A well-written marketing note will help you sell your product. Before you send it out, take the time to proofread the text and make any necessary formatting changes. It’s also important to proofread your hand-written notes before delivering them to your recipient.
