Business Website Newsletter

Create a simple newsletter to share your website’s content. You can use templates or put your own formatting in place. Email newsletters are a great way to reach your audience on a regular basis.

Social Media Sites

Use social media to connect with people in your industry or on your topic. Look at the tools and apps available to get your content in front of your community.

Although you might have a website, a newsletter and social media accounts, you don’t need to have a business presence on these channels. You can use social media to circulate links to your business.
