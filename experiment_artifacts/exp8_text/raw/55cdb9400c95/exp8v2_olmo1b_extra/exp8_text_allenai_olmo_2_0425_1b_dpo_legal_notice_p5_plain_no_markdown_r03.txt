ATTENTION ALL USER PARTICIPANTS

The shared network used by our institution is intended for the safe and effective use of electronic devices. We kindly ask that all users adhere to the following guidelines in the use of the network:

1. RESPECT THE NETWORK EQUIPMENT: Do not damage, interfere with, or overuse the network equipment in any way. This includes excessive data transfer rates, virus scanning, or other network strain activities.

2. WRITING AND EMAILING: All communications via email or written documents while connected to the network must adhere to institutional policies and respect the privacy and confidentiality of the content. Unauthorized sharing of information is strictly prohibited.

3. CONNECTED Devices: Ensure that any personal devices used for network access are in good working order and are not being used for activities that may threaten the network integrity or violate our policies.

4. FILE AND STORAGE: All files and data stored on the network must be properly labeled, maintained, and deleted when no longer needed. Unauthorized duplication or distribution of network-attached content is prohibited.

5. PROFESSIONAL USE ONLY: The network should be used solely for educational, administrative, or professional purposes in accordance with our policies and legal requirements.

 violators of these guidelines may encounter reduction in network access privileges, legal action, or other disciplinary measures as deemed appropriate by our administration.

we urge all users to familiarize themselves with these policies and to act responsibly while connected to our shared network.

thank you for your cooperation and adherence to our guidelines.
