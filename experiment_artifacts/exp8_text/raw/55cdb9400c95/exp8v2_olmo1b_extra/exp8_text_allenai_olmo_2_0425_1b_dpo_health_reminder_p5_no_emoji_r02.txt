When refilling your prescription, always check the expiration date on the original prescription and your new medication label. Make sure you use the same pharmacy that filled your original prescription to avoid confusion and ensure accuracy. Double-check your insurance information before scheduling a refill, as some plans have specific requirements or co-payments. Store your refilled prescription safely until you bring it to your next appointment with your healthcare provider for review and refilling if necessary. Remember, taking your medication as prescribed is crucial to maintaining your health.
