**Polling Station Locations**

- **Location on Main Street:**  
  - Situated at the corner of Main Street and Maple Avenue  
  - Near major public transportation routes

- **Near Public Libraries:**  
  - Located next to the City Library  
  - Offers free access to library resources while voting

- **Behind School Facilities:**  
  - Across the street from the elementary school  
  - Open during school hours for convenience

- **Near Shopping Centers:**  
  - Across from the main shopping plaza  
  - Convenient for those heading to nearby shops after voting

- **Address and Map Link:**  
  - Visit the official municipal website for detailed maps and address information  
  - Use the local app for real-time location updates
