Here’s a simple tip for using a calendar app: Instead of filling out events manually each day, use some of the space for reminders or recurring events. For example, if you’re going to have a monthly meeting with your team, make sure to allocate some space on the calendar for that as if you entered it on a specific date. Similarly, for things like birthdays or anniversaries, leaving space allows you to quickly note the important date without having to search and adjust your calendar. This saves time and keeps your eyes on the most important tasks. Remember, a calendar is not just for scheduling; it’s for planning and staying organized.
