Set aside a few moments each week to review your fitness progress. Take a few minutes to glance over your workout diary or fitness tracking app to see what you've accomplished. Check your total miles cycled, pounds lost, and repetitions completed. Assess your energy levels, recovery times, and any areas where you've grown stronger or more endurance-focused.

Remember to celebrate your small successes, no matter how minor they may seem. Over time, those achievements can lead to bigger milestones and a more fulfilling fitness journey. Keep track so you can enjoy the ride and stay motivated.
