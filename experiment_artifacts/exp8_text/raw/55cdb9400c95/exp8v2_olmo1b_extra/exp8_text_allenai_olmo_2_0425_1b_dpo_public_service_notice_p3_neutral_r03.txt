Attention residents! Starting November 15th, our recycling collection schedule has been changed. Residents can now choose either of the following bi-weekly schedules: (1) every Tuesday and Thursday, or (2) every Wednesday and Friday. To ensure your recyclables are collected properly, please separate your recyclables into appropriate bins. Remember, paper, glass, and plastic items should always be clean and dry for proper separation. Thank you for your continued cooperation in helping protect our environment!
