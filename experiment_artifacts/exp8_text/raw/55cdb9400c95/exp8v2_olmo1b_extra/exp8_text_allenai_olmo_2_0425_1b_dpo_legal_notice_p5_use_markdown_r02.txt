# Shared Network Acceptable Use Policy

1. **No Unauthorized Access**: Access to the shared network is restricted to authorized users only. Unauthorized access, hacking, and tampering with network devices are strictly prohibited.
2. **No Malicious Activity**: Engage in any form of malicious activity such as spreading viruses, conducting denial-of-service attacks, or engaging in any other harmful actions against the network or its users.
3. **Data Confidentiality**: Ensure the confidentiality and integrity of shared data by not sharing sensitive information without proper authorization and by not storing data on the network without proper backup and security measures.
4. **No File Sharing Without Consent**: File sharing of copyrighted materials or communal property without the consent of the rightful owner is not permissible.
5. **Regular Maintenance**: Participate in the regular maintenance and upgradation of the network's hardware and software to ensure its proper functioning and security.
6. **Responsible Use**: Consume network resources responsibly, avoiding overloading network servers or consuming excessive bandwidth, which can affect other users' access and performance.

Failure to comply with any part of this policy may result in termination of network access and legal consequences.
