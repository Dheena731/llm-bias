Enabling two-factor authentication is an excellent way to enhance your online security. This feature adds an extra layer of protection to your accounts by requiring a second form of verification before accessing your account. It includes a unique code sent to your mobile device or email, which must be entered to gain access. This extra step can prevent unauthorized access even if your password is compromised. By enabling two-factor authentication, you are joining a growing number of individuals who understand the importance of safeguarding their digital identities.
