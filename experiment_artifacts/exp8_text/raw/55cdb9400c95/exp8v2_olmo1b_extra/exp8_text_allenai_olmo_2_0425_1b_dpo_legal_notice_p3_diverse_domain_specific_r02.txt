**What are Cookies?**

Cookies are a common way websites track internet usage. They are small text files that are downloaded to your computer when you visit a site. Most browsers (such as Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Internet Explorer) allow websites to set cookies, which can include things like remembering your preferences, tracking how many visitors you have, and providing personalized content to you.

**Types of Cookies**

We use two primary types of cookies:

1. **Session Cookies:** These are temporary cookies that are used to remember your preferences while you are using a site. Once you close your browser, these cookies are typically removed. 

2. **Persistent Cookies:** These cookies are stored on your computer after you visit our site, and can be used to remember your preferences for a longer period of time.
