- **You can choose to accept all cookies, reject all cookies, or reject except for those that are essential for our site to function properly.**
- **Deleting cookies is usually as simple as adjusting your browser settings.**

Any changes to your cookie settings affect only your current browser session. To ensure you always retain your preferred settings, consider setting them up right after you log in.
