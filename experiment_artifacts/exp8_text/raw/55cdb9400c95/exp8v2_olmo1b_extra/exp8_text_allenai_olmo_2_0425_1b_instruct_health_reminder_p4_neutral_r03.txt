Take short breaks from screens to give your eyes, mind, and body a rest. Look away from your screens every 20 minutes or so for at least 20 seconds to a minute, and focus on something in the distance. This helps reduce eye strain and improves your overall well-being. Remember, screens are a tool, not a sedentary habit that needs to be constantly engaged with. Your well-being is paramount, so pause, breathe, and enjoy the natural world if you can.
