Make Your Roasted Vegetables Sparkle with Spice!

**Ingredients Needed:**
- A variety of roasted vegetables (experience has shown us that a mix of classics like carrots, potatoes, and zucchini, along with herbs such as rosemary, thyme, and peppercorns, offers a flavorful symphony.)
- Salt (the cornerstone of seasoning!)

**A Step-by-Step Guide:**

1. **Prepare Your Vegetables:** Start by peeling and roughly chopping your vegetables. If you're using fresh herbs, cut them into small pieces. This not only aids in the seasoning process but also enhances the visual appeal of your dish.

2. **Select the Right Spice Blend:** Choose a spice blend that complements your vegetables' natural taste profile. For a classic roast, go with a blend containing salt, pepper, and maybe a touch of sweet such as cinnamon or orange zest for a zing. Experiment until you find a blend that elevates your vegetables to just-bright-bright levels.

3. **Preheat Your Oven:** A hot oven is essential to lock in the flavors. Preheat your oven to 400°F (205°C).

4. **Incorporate Spice:** Toss your chopped vegetables with your chosen spice blend in a large mixing bowl. You can start with a generous amount and adjust to taste. Remember, seasoning is in the eyes of the beholder—trust your instincts and feel free to experiment.

5. **Roast and Relish:** Roast your vegetables in a heavy roasting pan for about 30-40 minutes or until they reach your desired level of doneness. Rotate the pan halfway through to ensure even cooking and to distribute the heat.

6. **Serve and Savor:** Once done, transfer the vegetables to a serving platter. Serve them with a sprinkle of freshly grated cheese, toasted almond slices, or a dollop of your favorite dressing for an extra zing!

**Bonus Tip:** Leftovers are always welcome. Store them in an airtight container in the refrigerator for future snappy, savory lunches.

By following these steps, you're bound to transform your roasted vegetables into a culinary masterpiece that will leave your taste buds thrilled and your eyes dazzled. Happy seasoning!
