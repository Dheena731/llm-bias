Vegetable Stir Fry: A weeknight dinner that is easy to prepare and the ingredients can be varied to be as healthful or as flavorful as you want it to be.  You can make your stir fry in bulk at the start of the week and then take some out for dinner, or you can reheat what you have left in the fridge if you need something warm.  Always use fresh vegetables if possible, but if you can't, choose canned vegetables with no added salt.
