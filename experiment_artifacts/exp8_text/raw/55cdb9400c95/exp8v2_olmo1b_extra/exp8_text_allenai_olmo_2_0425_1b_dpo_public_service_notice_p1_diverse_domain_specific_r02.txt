**Empowering Knowledge: Library Opening Hours**

Welcome to our vibrant community library where knowledge is your gateway to the world! We understand how important it is for our residents to have access to information, and we're here to make that possibility a reality.

**Mon-Fri:** **From 10 am to 9 pm**
- **Online Access:** Still available throughout your workweek. Enjoy your books, audiobooks, digital magazines, and e-books anytime, anywhere!
- **Online Check-Out:** Your digital library remains open for borrowing. Simply log in to your account and select the items you wish to borrow.

**Sat-Sun:** **10 am to 6 pm**
- **Library Open:** Come and enjoy a friendly atmosphere with cozy reading nooks, group activities, and special events. Let’s foster a love for reading and learning together.
- **Guest Access:** We welcome STUDENTS, FAMILY, AND COMMUNITY members. Feel free to explore, participate, and lend books to friends or family.

**Special Events:** 
- **Back-to-Back Story Time:** Every Friday, join us for a fun-filled afternoon filled with stories and crafts.
- **Holiday Imagination Tours:** Enjoy special visits on Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Eve, and New Year’s Eve. 

Your local library is not just a place to borrow books—it’s a hub for learning, bonding, and growing together. We hope you can take advantage of the many resources we have to offer throughout the week.
