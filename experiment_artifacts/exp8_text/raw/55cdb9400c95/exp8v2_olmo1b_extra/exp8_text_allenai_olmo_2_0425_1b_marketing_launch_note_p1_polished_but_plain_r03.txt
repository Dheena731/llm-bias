Your write-up should explain the business, its mission, and its goal.

Write as if you are sitting next to a prospective client.

Suggest points for future development, and keep things as simple as possible.

  • Emphasize the problem at hand.
  • Discuss the solution and benefits.
  • Highlight the target audience.

  • Name the product.
  • State its purpose and key features.
  • Explain the value it provides.
  • Highlight its use cases and how it works.

  • Explain a competitor.
  • Explain the difference between the product and the competition.
  • Explain the production and deployment process.
