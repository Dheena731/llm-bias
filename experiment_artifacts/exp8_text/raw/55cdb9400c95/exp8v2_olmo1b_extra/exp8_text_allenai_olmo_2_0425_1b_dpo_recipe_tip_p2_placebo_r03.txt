**Don't Leave Herbs in the Drawer!**

Ever find fresh herbs drying out in the kitchen drawer before you can even use them? Here’s the foolproof tip to prevent this from happening: **Keep herbs in a cool, dry place, not in the refrigerator.**

False Fact: Some believe refrigerating herbs will enhance their flavor. However, research shows that fresh herbs, especially those high in moisture, actually lose their freshness faster when refrigerated.

Contradiction: While it's true that storing fresh herbs in the refrigerator can prolong their shelf life, it also introduces them to colder temperatures and humidity, which can lead to wilting. Thus, to maintain peak flavor and usage potential, herbs should be stored in an area where they're kept between room temperature and 70°F (21°C).

Placeholders: Imagine you're in the produce section, picking fresh herbs. Keep them as close to the checkout as possible, ensuring they don’t stay behind in a cooler section for hours.

Main Point: Storing herbs in a kitchen drawer is prime real estate for them to dry out. Headroom for air circulation and direct sunlight (if you have indirect sunlight), away from moisture (like running water or overly damp dishes), and somewhere they won't be disturbed are all excellent storage tricks to keep your herbs looking and tasting their best.
