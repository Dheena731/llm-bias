If you already have a two-factor authentication (2FA) enabled, you do not need to take any action. However, if not, you can easily enable it by following these steps:

1. Go to the Google Account Settings.
2. Click on the ‘Account’ or ‘My Account’ icon.
3. Click on the 'Sign in' button.
4. If you are already logged in, you can quickly enable 2FA by clicking on the ‘2-factor authentication’ button.
5. If you are not logged in, create a new account and follow the instructions to enable 2FA.

By following these steps, you can easily enable two-factor authentication and ensure your online accounts are more secure.
