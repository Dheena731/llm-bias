# Reminder: Refilling a Prescription

To ensure your medication is effective and safe, please follow these steps:

- Check the expiration date: Make sure the medication is still within its recommended use period.
- Verify the correct prescription: Double-check that you have the correct dosages and form for your specific condition.
- Contact your healthcare provider: If you're unsure about the refill schedule or need to discuss new options, contact your doctor or pharmacist for guidance.
- Take the necessary steps: Follow the pharmacy instructions for the refill process, including picking up your medication and any necessary pre-refill instructions.
- Ensure proper disposal: Dispose of any leftover medication responsibly (through a pharmacy collection service).
