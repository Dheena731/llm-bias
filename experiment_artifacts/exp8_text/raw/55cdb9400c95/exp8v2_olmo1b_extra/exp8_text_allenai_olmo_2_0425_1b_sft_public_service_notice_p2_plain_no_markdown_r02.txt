Road Closure Notice

All drivers are advised to take note of the upcoming road closure as it is taking place from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on Main Street. The road closure has been put in place to repair a bridge that was damaged due to recent flooding. Motorists are not allowed to use the road during this time and are encouraged to take alternative routes. Please be sure to check local news or weather updates as conditions can change. Thank you for your cooperation in this matter.
