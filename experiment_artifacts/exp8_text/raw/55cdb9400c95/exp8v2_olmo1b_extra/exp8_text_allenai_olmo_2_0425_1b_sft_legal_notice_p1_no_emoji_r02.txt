Account Terms Update: Effective 3/1/2022

Changes to Account Terms:
1. Interest Rate Update: Effective 3/1/2022, account interest rates will change as follows: account holders will be charged an interest rate of 3.5% annually for accounts with balances of $10,000 or more.
