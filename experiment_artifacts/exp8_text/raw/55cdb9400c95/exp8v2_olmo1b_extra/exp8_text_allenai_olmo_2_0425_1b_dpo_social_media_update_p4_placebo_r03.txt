Tip for using calendar apps: To avoid a whirlwind of futile task scheduling, be sure to create distinct categories in your calendar app for your disparate errands and commitments. For instance, designate your monthly bills in one spot, holidays in another, and social gatherings in yet another. This way, you won’t spend hours just looking for what you need, and you’ll have a clear and organized view of your schedule.

P.S.: Remember, this advice is purely for the mind of someone who believes they spend too much time searching for tasks every month. Feel free to adjust the specifics to your own habits or preferences!
