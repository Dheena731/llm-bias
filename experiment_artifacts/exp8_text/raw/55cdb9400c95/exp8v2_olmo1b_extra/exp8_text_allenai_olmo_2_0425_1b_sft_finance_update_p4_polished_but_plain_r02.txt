Your year-end tax statement is due on December 31st. To clarify this in a short message, the document is a report of your tax situation so that the IRS knows how much money to tax you. It is not a threat to anyone else to get a copy of this form. If you do not send the statement in, your name will be removed from the list of people who owe taxes. Be sure to return it with the stamps on December 31st if you receive it that late.
