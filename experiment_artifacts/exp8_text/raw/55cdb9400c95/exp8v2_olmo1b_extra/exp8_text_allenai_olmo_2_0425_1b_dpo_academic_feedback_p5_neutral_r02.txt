Missing citations in an essay can compromise the credibility of the writer and the integrity of the content. When citations are absent, readers lack the opportunity to verify the information presented. This can lead to doubts about the author's sources and the validity of their arguments. Moreover, without proper citation, students and researchers who reference the essay may also incur penalties, such as disqualification from exams, removal from scholarly databases, or even legal repercussions if their work is plagiarized. It is crucial to always cite sources properly to build a stable foundation for any argument and to respectfully acknowledge the work of others.
