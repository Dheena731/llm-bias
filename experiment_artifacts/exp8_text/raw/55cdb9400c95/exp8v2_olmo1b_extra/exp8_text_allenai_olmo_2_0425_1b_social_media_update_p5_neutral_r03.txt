What Is a Service Outage?

A service outage is an unplanned interruption or failure of a service that results in a function or service not functioning as expected. For example, a service outage could be caused by a computer failure or equipment failure. In some cases, it could also be due to an upgrade.

When a service outage is caused by a computer failure, equipment failure, or an upgrade, a customer service provider must respond in a timely manner to address it. Failure to do so could result in the customer experiencing issues and thus not being serviced or using the service. If an upgrade is the cause of the service outage, the service provider must notify their customers and make good on their promise to monitor and restore service.
