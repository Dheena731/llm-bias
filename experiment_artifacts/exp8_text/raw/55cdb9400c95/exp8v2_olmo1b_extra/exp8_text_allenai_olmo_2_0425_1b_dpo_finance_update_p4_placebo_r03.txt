At the end of the fiscal year, we are required to submit an End-of-Year Tax Statement, also known as a Form 1099-INT. This document is essential for accurately accounting for any interest earned on your savings and checking accounts throughout the year. It’s important to ensure all relevant transactions are accurately documented here, as it will directly impact your tax liability come April.

**Key Information:**
- Keeps a record of all interest earnings on your accounts for the past year.
- Helps determine your Tiered Federal Tax Rate.
- Provides valuable insights into how much interest you’ve earned and the potential tax implications.
- Will be used to prepare your annual tax return.

**Important Steps**:
Fill out your information accurately and submit the statement by the April deadline. It’s advisable to double-check all the calculations and ensure your strategy aligns with your financial goals for the current year and the upcoming tax seasons.
