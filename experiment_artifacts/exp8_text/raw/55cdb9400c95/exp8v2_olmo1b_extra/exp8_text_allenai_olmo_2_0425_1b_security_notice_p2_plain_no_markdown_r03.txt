Use a clear, straightforward tone. Do not end your message with a question mark .

Do not include any personal information other than your name or email address. If the email is from you, and you are responding to a message you received, you can include your name and organization in your reply.

Do not enter or click on any links in the email. If you receive a link in an email, don't click on it. Instead, see if it's a scam by searching the web with the address or by copying and pasting the link.

Do not use any form of email to communicate information about your work. Email addresses are easy to guess and can reveal information about your organization.

Do not respond to any email you received unless you are the sender or you have been directed to do so by someone else. Do not click on links in emails sent to you by others without their permission. Do not respond to emails from someone pretending to be from your employer or a colleague. This could be a scammer posing as a colleague.
