Assignment: Find a savings goal

This week, you and your family member or friend are planning to save money. To help you get started, you are going to create a list of things you want to save for. First, write about what it is you want to save for. Then, write about why you want to save for that goal.

Your money goal should be something that you can save for without a lot of effort. For example, if you want to buy a new toy, you probably need to save for a long time.

Next, you will write about how you will save for that goal. Help your partner, parent, or child come up with a plan for saving money. Here are a few ideas on how you can save some money:

  • You can make a list of things you want to save for and put them in order. First on your list should be your goal.
  • The amount of money you have should be a good starting point. For example, use the amount of money your partner or parent has to start from.
  • Make sure you talk to your partner, parent, or child about how they want to save money.
  • You can check out our step-by-step guide to saving money for your goal. It will help you make a budget and savings plan.
