# Recycling Collection Changes

**NOTE:** Starting November 1, 2023, the City’s recycling program will experience schedule changes. 

- **Single-stream recycling:**
  * Collection days for single-stream recycling have been moved to Tuesdays and Thursdays only.

- **PSHC Recycle Depot:**
  * Residents will need to bring their recyclables to the Central Processing Plant (PSHC) at 3300 West 57th St., Los Angeles, CA 90027, or drop-off at designated curb-side bins starting November 1.
