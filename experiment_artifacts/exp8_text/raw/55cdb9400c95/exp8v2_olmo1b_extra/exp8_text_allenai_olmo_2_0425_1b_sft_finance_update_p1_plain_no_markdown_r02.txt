INTRODUCTION

A new monthly spending summary has been introduced to provide customers with a clear view of their spending patterns and goals. This summary will help customers make informed decisions about their spending and improve overall financial wellbeing.

DESCRIPTION

The new monthly spending summary consists of three main sections: Income, Expenses, and Goals. 

Income: This section displays the total monthly income, including any income from jobs, investments, or other sources. It also shows the average amount of income per month, helping customers to quickly assess their financial stability.

Expenses: This section highlights the total expenses, including the cost of housing, food, transportation, and other essential expenses. It also demonstrates the monthly net savings or debt, showing how much customers have left after covering their primary expenses.

Goals: This section encourages customers to set personal goals, such as saving for a particular purchase or paying down debt. It also displays any current savings goals and the progress towards these objectives.

INFORMATION

The summary provides an overview of the customer's financial situation in a clear and concise format. Each section serves a specific purpose, offering customers valuable insights to help them make informed spending decisions. By tracking their spending and setting financial goals, customers can work towards improving their financial health and achieving their personal objectives.
