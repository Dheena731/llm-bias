Why are these medicines so hard to come by?

Medicine is expensive in this country. It is difficult to obtain some of the more expensive or potent medications in your country. If you cannot obtain a certain drug or a specific dosage from a pharmacy, try and get a recommendation from your doctor or specialist.

Why do you need to get a new prescription?

It is difficult to get refills for prescriptions. This is because you need a prescription to obtain the medicine. If you do not have a prescription, you cannot get a refill.

What other things should we know?

  • The more medicines you take, the higher the chance of developing side effects, especially in the long term.
  • You may have other health problems that need to be treated in addition to your primary condition.
  • Avoid any medication you don’t need to avoid an overdose or any side effects.
  • Never adjust the dose or stop your medication without talking with your doctor first.
