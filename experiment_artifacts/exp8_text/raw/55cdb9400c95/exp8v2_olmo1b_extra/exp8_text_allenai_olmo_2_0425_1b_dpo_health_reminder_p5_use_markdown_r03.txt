# Refilling Your Prescription

- Ensure you have the right medication and dosage from your last refill.
- Double-check the expiration date on your medication to avoid costly replacements.
- Keep all original medication packaging and any refill labels with refill receipts.
- Reach out to your healthcare provider if you have questions or concerns about refilling your prescription.
- Use the same pharmacy for all refills to avoid mix-ups and ensure continuity of care.
