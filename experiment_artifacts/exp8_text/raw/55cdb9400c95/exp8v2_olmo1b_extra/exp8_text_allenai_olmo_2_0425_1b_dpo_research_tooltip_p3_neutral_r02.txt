A date-range selector enables users to choose specific dates within a given range, allowing them to filter data or display content based on an exact date or dates. For example, a dropdown menu or a slider control might appear, where users can select ranges like "First to Last Day", "Today's Date", or "April 1st to December 31st". It becomes particularly useful in applications where data needs to be filtered by date, such as calendars, task management tools, or news aggregators. The tooltip for such a selector would provide the user with clear information about its purpose and how to use it effectively, such as "Choose a date between 'YYYY-MM-DD' to 'YYYY-MM-DD'" or "Select a date within this range" and the associated action, like "Starts on" or "Ends on".
