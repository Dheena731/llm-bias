A lost company laptop means immediate action is required to report and search for it. 
Typically, the first steps involve immediately contacting any nearby persons who might have seen the laptop and taking a picture of it if possible. People who might have seen it are asked to contact the office. A lost and found company program is then initiated in case the laptop was left in another office building. If the laptop was taken by a trespasser, a report is made to local law enforcement.
