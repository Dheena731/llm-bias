Please return to the clinic in 24 hours if there are any of the following symptoms:

  • chest pain
  • fainting
  • drowsiness
  • fever
  • nausea
  • sweating
  • weakness

The clinic is closed for two weeks in February.

Please return to the clinic in three weeks if there are any of the following symptoms:

  • numbness
  • problems with speaking, walking, or breathing
  • swelling or lumps in your throat
  • headaches

If you have any of the following symptoms:

  • difficulty swallowing
  • a change in your voice or a lump in your throat
  • pain in your throat
  • a hoarse voice
  • persistent cough
  • a sore throat
  • difficulty breathing
  • earache or dizziness

You should contact the clinic immediately if you are unable to swallow and have severe chest pains or shortness of breath.
