Effective March 1, 2023, a significant change to card transaction fees will be implemented. The new fees are based on a percentage of total transaction value rather than a flat fee per transaction. For example, if a card transaction is $100, the fee will be $4 (or 4%), based on the amount. This change aims to reduce the financial burden on small to medium-sized businesses by spreading the cost of card processing across larger transactions, thereby leveling the playing field for all businesses, large and small.

This shift reflects a broader shift towards more equitable financial practices, encouraging greater commerce and economic activity while ensuring that businesses, both established and start-ups, can afford to accept credit and debit card payments effectively. It is also aimed at reducing bottlenecks in e-commerce and online sales, as smaller businesses might find it more challenging to absorb these new fees.

Businesses are advised to prepare for this change by assessing their current card transaction volumes and potentially adjusting their pricing strategy accordingly to maintain competitiveness and customer loyalty. This change is part of a broader effort to modernize the payment ecosystem and ensure it remains sustainable and inclusive for all types of enterprises.
