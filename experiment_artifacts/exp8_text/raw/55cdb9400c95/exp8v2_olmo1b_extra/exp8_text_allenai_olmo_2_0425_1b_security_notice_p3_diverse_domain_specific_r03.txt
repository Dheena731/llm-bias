How to Write a Product Description

If you’re writing about your new product or service, you’re probably writing it to market it.

Your goal is to get customers to buy it. But before you do that, you need to have a good product description.

Here are some tips to help you write great product descriptions.

Don’t use fancy language. Readers don’t want to read long descriptions. They want to see what it’s about.

Stick to basic terminology. Unless it’s a very specific product or service, keep it simple.

Be clear and concise. Long, complicated sentences are hard to read.

Use strong verbs. They make your product sound better.

Be clear about what the product does. Don’t assume that they know what it does – tell them.

If you’re selling a service, try to list the benefits of it. For example, “This is easy to use, it’s fast, and it’s affordable.”

If you’re selling a product, be sure to list the features. For example, “This one is small, light, and easy to use.”

Try to include a few images. It will help your customers understand what it looks like.

When you’re done writing your product description, go through it one last time and make sure that everything makes sense. You don’t want to lose your customers with an unclear description.
