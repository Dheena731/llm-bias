This is a conference poster. It has a title, author/creator, and a brief description. The conference poster is associated with the conference. Conference posters are not the same thing as posters at exhibitions, i.e. posters for another event that has nothing to do with the conference.

The title is not the conference name, and each poster should have a different title. Posters should not be named after the publication they are being presented in. An example is given by the fact that some posters are numbered 1, 2 and 3, even though there are three posters.

A conference poster is a presentation of new material that has been developed over several months by a group of people. It is presented at a conference, rather than at an exhibition.
