4. How to write and format a business email

This one requires a little more training. Once you have an email written, it’s important to know how to format it. Even if English isn’t your first language, you can get this part down.

Here are four tips on how to write and format a business email:

  1. Please don’t write a professional letter. A professional letter is formal and not as appropriate when sending emails. Writing a business letter is not the same as writing a business email. If you need something sent, you can send a letter, but if it’s just an email, then an email is more appropriate.
  2. Place your salutation right above your opening sentence. The professional email doesn’t need to be fancy. It’s just like a letter, but a business letter often has a title, while an email doesn’t.
  3. After the subject line, write a few sentences that state your purpose. You don’t need to include your name. Just say “Dear [add the recipient’s first name],”
  4. Don’t forget to close your message with “Sincerely.” Your signature should be at the bottom of your email, in the same order as your message. Don’t use a block font. Choose a stylish font that shows your professionalism!
