Example for refills

We’d like to automate the filling of a prescription for your medication based on the information you give us, a prescription you wrote for this medication for yourself, and a prescription you wrote for this medication for your child. The medication you write for yourself is a type of medication and comes in a specific kind of bottle. In this particular instance, you’ve written a prescription for this medication twice, once for yourself and once for your child. The medication we are filling is a type of medication that has multiple names. As part of our process, we’ve researched the names and the ingredient(s) of this medication. Because the medication requires multiple ingredients, we’re also getting your insurance information to ensure that you’re covered and that your insurance will pay for this medication.

The subject of the example is an address. It’s easy to see that it’s a refilling query with a prescription and a result. It’s also a good example of a common refilling domain (prescriptions) and a common AI-generated question (the refill).
