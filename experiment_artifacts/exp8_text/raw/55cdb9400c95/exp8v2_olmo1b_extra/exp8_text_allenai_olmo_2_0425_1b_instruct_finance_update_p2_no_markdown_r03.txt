Changes to card transaction fees are occurring to address concerns over fraud and create a more competitive payment system. In response to increased fraud linked to credit card transactions several merchants and payment networks have introduced new fees on cardholders. The primary change involves an increase in payment gateway fees which previously passed onto consumers. This move aims to dissuade fraudulent activities and shift the burden of transaction costs to cardholders more directly. Some services may also impose additional surcharges on transactions to cover these costs effectively. The intention is to incentivize customers to use more secure payment methods such as mobile wallets or digital currencies which offer lower fraud rates.
