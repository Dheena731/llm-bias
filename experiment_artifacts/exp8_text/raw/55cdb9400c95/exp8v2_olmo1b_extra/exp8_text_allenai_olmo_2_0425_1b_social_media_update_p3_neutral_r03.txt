Improve your writing (check out my previous post for a list of tips) and you will find you have many more opportunities to connect with your audience.

We all wish for an easy way to write better copy, many of us believe the key is to find shortcuts that will speed up our copywriting process. The problem is there are no shortcuts that are useful to you and your audience. I can't tell you to use a keyboard shortcut or change your autocorrect settings. You have to do it yourself.

Write in a way that works for you and your audience. We are all unique. That means you can't expect your writing to sound the same as mine, we just have the same goal after all. When I say writing to your audience, I mean that your copy should be so personal that your audience knows you've connected with them. You want to write copy that resonates with the audience on an emotional level. The people who do not like you will not want to read about what you have to say. Your audience wants to read about what you have to say, and believe you have a message they want to hear. Your audience wants to hear about your message in a way that will be meaningful to them. You want to write copy that will impact your audience. This is why so many writers tell you to write just for yourself and your audience, and not to be a writer for the masses.

There are no shortcuts. Write for the masses, the masses will learn from you. Write for the masses and you can write about anything at all. Write to your audience and you will find you have something meaningful to say.

If you want to write better, you have to write every day. You have to write sometimes you don't feel like it, and you have to write even when you do. You have to focus on the task at hand in order to write better. Write more often, write about anything, but write.
