**Reminder: Please Secure Your Workstations**

As we continue our productive week, we kindly remind our esteemed team to ensure their screens remain locked when not in use. Cyber threats are ever-present in our modern digital landscape, and it's essential for everyone's security and data protection to adhere to this simple but crucial practice. Locking your workstation as soon as you leave your desk minimizes the risk of unauthorized access to your personal information.

By securing your workspace, you protect not only your files and data but also the privacy and reputation of our company. Remember, a locked screen not only prevents prying eyes from accessing sensitive information but also indicates to cybercriminals that your system is unattended, potentially making it an easier target.

Here are a few ways to implement this simple yet effective practice:

1. **Use Screen Nourisher:** Employ a tool or plugin designed to lock your screen after a period of inactivity, often just a few minutes.
2. **Automatic Lockers:** Set your system to automatically lock after a set period of inactivity.
3. **Password Locks:** Ensure your screens default to a password lock once left unattended.
4. **Scheduled Lockdowns:** Implement scheduled lock downs to reinforce the habit.

Let's work together to create a safer and more secure environment for all our clients and colleagues. Remember, vigilance in securing our digital assets starts with a locked screen. Thank you for your attention to this important matter.
