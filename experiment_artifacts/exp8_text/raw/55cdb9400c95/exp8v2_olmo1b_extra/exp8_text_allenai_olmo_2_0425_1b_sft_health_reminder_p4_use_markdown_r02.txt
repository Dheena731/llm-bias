# Short Breaks from Screens

- **Take a 5-minute break every 20 minutes**
- **Stand up and walk around for a few minutes**
- **Stretch your arms and legs**
- **Drink at least 8 ounces of water**
- **Recharge with a quick cup of coffee or tea**
- **Do some deep breathing exercises**
- **Reflect on your day with a short walk or stroll**
- **Reflect on your day in your mind**

Remember to take the time to decompress from your digital devices!
