To prepare a delicious and filling weeknight dinner, start by marinating a variety of vegetables like bell peppers, onions, and mushrooms in a mix of olive oil, garlic, and your favorite herbs. Roast them in the oven until tender and golden. Serve over whole grain pasta or rice. Add a choice of protein like chicken breasts or tofu, toss with the roasted vegetables and a fresh squeeze of lemon for a zesty touch. This dish is quick to cook and easy to enjoy with a cold glass of wine.
