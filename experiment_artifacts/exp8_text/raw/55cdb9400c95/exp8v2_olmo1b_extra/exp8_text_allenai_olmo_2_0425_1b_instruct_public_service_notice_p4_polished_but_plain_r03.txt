Attention citizens:

Due to an extended period of warm weather, authorities are issuing a heat advisory. We urge all residents to be cautious as the temperatures remain above 90 degrees Fahrenheit (32 degrees Celsius) for several days. Heat-related illnesses can occur quickly; act to protect yourself.

Here are some suggestions:

• Plan your day around cooler times, avoiding peak hours.
• Stay well-hydrated.
• Monitor your temperature.
• Avoid overexertion.
• Keep your home and car cool.

Remember, it’s important to stay safe. If you feel unwell, seek medical attention at the first available opportunity.

To report any concerns or questions, contact local authorities. We’re here to help and provide updates as needed.

Thank you for your cooperation.
