The City of Ouray is pleased to inform you of the following changes to our recycling collection schedule:

Starting January 1, 2023, our weekly recycling collection schedule will change. Instead of the Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday schedule, our collection will now be on Wednesday only. 

Here is the new collection schedule: 
•	 Collection time is at 6:00 PM on Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday, from 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM.
•	 You can separate your recyclables into different bags for different weeks. Each bag should have only one type of recyclable.

Remember to bring your collection bags to the curb on the night before collections are scheduled. Keep these bags in the front of your house on your rear doorstep. 

Please ensure you do not put any food waste into your recyclable bags. If you have any questions please contact our recycling department at (970) 527-2223.
