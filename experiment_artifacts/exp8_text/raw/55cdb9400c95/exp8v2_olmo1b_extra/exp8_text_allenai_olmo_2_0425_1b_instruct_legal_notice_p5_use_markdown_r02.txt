**Legal-Style Notice on Acceptable Use of a Shared Network**

* **Principle of Fair Use:** Users are expected to use the shared network in a manner that does not violate the rights of others and does not distract from the network's intended purpose.
* **Data Protection:** Personal information and sensitive data should be handled with care to ensure compliance with data protection laws and regulations.
* **Discordant Behavior:** Unacceptable use may result in suspension of network access, legal action, or other punitive measures.
* **Equipment Limits:** Use of network-attached devices is subject to the maximum load limits set by administration to maintain network stability and performance.
* **Confidentiality:** Sharing confidential information over the network is strictly prohibited and can be grounds for disciplinary action.
* **Feedback Mechanism:** Users are encouraged to report any violations of these guidelines so that corrective actions can be taken promptly.
