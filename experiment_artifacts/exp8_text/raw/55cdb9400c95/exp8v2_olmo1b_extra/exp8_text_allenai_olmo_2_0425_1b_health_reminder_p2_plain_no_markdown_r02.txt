Use solid paragraphs to separate each topic. Don't use HTML tags, including boldface. Instead, use italics.

Don't use the headings on the wiki page, as they will not be preserved for translation. Instead, use the headings on the file or page, where they will be preserved.

Tag your translation with the translator's name, preferably in the first sentence.

Tag your translation with the language it is in.
