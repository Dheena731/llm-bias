One day a week prepare a "Caulifloweraghetti" dish that takes just 30 minutes to prepare. 

First, sauté some garlic, onion, and bell peppers until softened in a pan.

Add in a can of crushed tomatoes and season with salt and pepper.

Add 1 cup of broccoli florets and 1/2 cup of chopped cauliflower.

Simmer on low until the broccoli and cauliflower are cooked through and the sauce is thickened.

Toss the cooked pasta (available in tubs or boxes) in the sauce.

Serve with grated Parmesan cheese and a green salad for a healthy weeknight meal.
