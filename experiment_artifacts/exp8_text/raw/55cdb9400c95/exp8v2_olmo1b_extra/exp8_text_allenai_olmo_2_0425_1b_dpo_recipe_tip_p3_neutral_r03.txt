One way to make sticky rice less sticky is to let it cook for a little longer. If you're using pre-rinsed rice, it usually needs about 1 minute less total cooking time than regular rice. Turn off the heat and let it sit for a minute or two before fluffing with a fork. This method helps the starch to dissipate, making the rice less sticky. Remember, consistency is key in rice cooking—adding less water can prevent the rice from getting too sticky while ensuring it's perfectly al dente.
