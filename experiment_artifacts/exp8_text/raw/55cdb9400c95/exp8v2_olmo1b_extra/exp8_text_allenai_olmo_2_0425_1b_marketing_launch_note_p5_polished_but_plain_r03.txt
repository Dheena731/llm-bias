Remember that it’s important to cover these four language barriers in your podcast. However, the podcast should also take the listener into consideration, so give them a reason to listen to that podcast regularly. You could take them through a language-learning journey from their native language to the target language, or introduce them to a unique culture through the target language.

More tips to consider:

Start at the beginning and end with the same high-quality language instruction content. This way the listener knows what they need to be doing from the beginning to the end of the podcast.

Use a consistent voice for your podcast. To create a consistent style, start with a script that has been proofread by someone who can hear that same voice you plan to use.

If you like audiobooks, consider mixing them into the podcast content. In fact, you can use any type of audio content, such as interviews or podcasts, as long as the sounds are low volume.

Make sure that your podcast allows the user to review your podcast content before they listen to it. This will help them check their pronunciation and make sure they understand the content.
