7. The network's usage is limited to approved users and devices only. Sharing network connections with unauthorized devices or users is strictly prohibited.

8. Breach of these rules and regulations will result in the immediate suspension and termination of a user's access to the shared network. Any disputing party must submit the matter to the appropriate local law enforcement for resolution.

9. If a user violates the rules and regulations outlined in this notice, they must immediately log out of the shared network and refrain from any further activities that violate these guidelines.
