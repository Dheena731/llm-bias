**Workplace Notice: Phishing Emails**

- Users should be aware of and report any suspicious emails that appear to be from our company.
- These emails may contain links or attachments that could lead to phishing websites.
- Always verify the sender's email address before opening any attachments or clicking on links.
- If unsure, contact your IT department or supervisor for assistance.
  
Remember, phishing is a serious criminal activity that can compromise sensitive information.
