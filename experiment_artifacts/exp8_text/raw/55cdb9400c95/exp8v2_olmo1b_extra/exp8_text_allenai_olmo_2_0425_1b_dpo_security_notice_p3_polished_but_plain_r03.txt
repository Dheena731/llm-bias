Please remember to lock your screens before leaving work. Your screens should be secured with a strong password or a screen lock feature. If the screens remain unlocked, unauthorized individuals could gain access to sensitive information and cause potential harm.

In addition to security concerns, it is important to follow company protocols regarding screen locking. Failure to adhere to these practices may result in disciplinary action, including suspension from work and possible legal consequences.

As a courtesy to our collegues and the safety and confidentiality of our systems, please ensure your screens are properly secured. Your bottom line is your professional reputation and the trust of our organization, and adherence to this simple practice is an investment in both.
