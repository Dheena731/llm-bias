Ever find yourself struggling to keep up with your busy schedule and deadlines? You don’t need to stress anymore! The secret to a successful calendar is decluttering. Start by going through your calendar and eliminating the events that don’t matter to you. Then, give yourself the space to focus on the important things in your life. When it’s time to plan your day, use these easy steps to stay on track:

Step 1: Use this method to organize your major tasks. Write down the task that’s most important, then write down the secondary tasks that support or nurture that task. These secondary tasks can be reminders, such as “Answer emails” or “Prepare dinner”.

Step 2: Use this method to create a “to-do” list. Write down all your planned activities. Determine which tasks are important and which tasks need to be delegated to others. This will help you stick to your plan and make sure you don’t lose track of a deadline.

Step 3: Use this method to outline each day. Write down broad items, then break these items down into smaller tasks. This will help you stay focused and maintain a sense of order.
