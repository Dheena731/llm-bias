Attention residents and visitors!

**Why a Heat Advisory?**

In the current climate, it's important for us to remind everyone to take care of their bodies and be mindful of the environment. Our local weather forecast has issued a heat advisory, indicating that temperatures are expected to soar this week, with heat indices potentially reaching up to 105°F (41°C). The high temperatures can strain our bodies, make it harder for seniors and the very young to regulate body temperature, and could pose safety risks during outdoor activities.

**What to Do:**

- Stay indoors as much as possible.
- Avoid the outdoors during the hottest hours, typically between 10 AM and 4 PM.
- When outside, do so in the shade, wear protective clothing like long sleeves, and use sunscreen with an SPF of at least 30.
- If you must be outside, conduct work that doesn't heat up the environment (like gardening or construction) during the cooler hours.
- Keep hydrated by drinking plenty of water, especially if you're taking medications or have pre-existing health conditions. 
- Monitor your body temperature and seek immediate medical attention if you feel dangerously hot or if your body temperature rises significantly.

We urge everyone to stay vigilant about their well-being as we navigate this period of increased heat. 

Stay safe!

*Contact us* if you have any questions or need further information. This advisory is in place to help protect your health and to ensure the safety of our community. Thank you for your cooperation and attention to this matter.
