**Headline: Tips for Seasoning Roasted Vegetables**

* Use olive oil or butter to drizzle on top of the vegetables before roasting for a smoother seasoning absorption.*
* Sprinkle salt, pepper, and freshly grated Parmesan cheese towards the end of roasting for an instant burst of flavor.*
* Add a touch of vinegar or balsamic glaze for a tangy, sweet finish.*
* Don't forget garlic powder, paprika, or chili flakes for extra heat and flavor.*
* Season roasted vegetables on serving plate for an easy, flavorful side dish.*
