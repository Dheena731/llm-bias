Heat Advisory
Your local weather forecast states that there will be a heat advisory in effect for your area. This means that temperatures will be uncomfortably high for extended periods of time. If you plan on being outside for any extended period of time, it would be wise to take necessary precautions. These precautions could include staying indoors, using air conditioning, and drinking plenty of fluids. If you are sick or elderly, please seek medical assistance immediately.
