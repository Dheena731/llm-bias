If you have a lost company laptop, please report it immediately. If this is a lost laptop belonging to one of our employees, the preferred method of contact is to call the lost and found department at our office. 

If you have a lost laptop belonging to someone else, you may call the lost and found department at our office or submit a report on the lost and found website. 

In either case, please do not attempt to locate or recover the laptop unless you are absolutely sure it is yours. Moving too quickly to retrieve personal property can cause damage to the device and other valuable items.

If the device was lost within our office premises, please contact the lost and found department immediately. If the laptop is found, it should be reported to the electronics department. Please do not report a lost laptop found within our office premises to someone else.
