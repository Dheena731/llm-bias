Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow - Review and Approve

Attention all Admins and Managers,

We are pleased to inform you about a new expense approval workflow which has been implemented across the organization. This change aims to streamline the approval process for all expenses, enhancing efficiency and accuracy.

In this new system, all expenses must be reviewed and approved by our department within seven business days of the expense report being submitted. Here is what you need to do:

1. Log into your administrative account.
2. Access the 'Expense Reports' section.
3. For each expense report, carefully review the details.
4. Once you have verified the accuracy and legitimacy of the expense, click on 'Approve' to submit the approval.
5. A confirmation message will appear; click 'Submit Approval' to finalize the approval.

This process is designed to ensure transparency and accountability. Your diligence in reviewing these reports and submitting timely approvals is crucial in maintaining our company standards.

We appreciate your immediate attention to this matter and your continued support as we implement this new workflow. Should you have any questions, please do not hesitate to reach out to our Help Desk.
