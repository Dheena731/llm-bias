Video Games

Sitting too long can be bad for your health, but the question is how much is too much? It is not uncommon to find 9-12 hour shifts in game development companies. With games becoming more online and free to download, there is nothing stopping you from spending more time with your screen than with your friends. I work in video games and I’m 20, and I’ve noticed that not only do most people my age have 6+ hours of screen time per day, but some of them have 8+ hours of screen time per day. I’m not an aggressive person when it comes to video games, but I am aware of the negative effects it has on your day to day life. I suggest that you try to keep your gaming to no more than 1 hour per day if it’s been over 8 hours, and if you have any difficulty at all, or if you work in a job that requires a lot of screen time, I suggest you stop altogether.

Social Media

How much time do you spend on social media? You’re probably thinking you don’t spend a lot of time looking at your phone, or computer or something. A recent survey found that the average amount of time spent on social media was 2 hours per day, but I’m not surprised. In this day and age, you can do anything online, and with the rise of smartphones and tablets, you can do anything online. You can watch Netflix, browse Reddit, or look through Instagram. The problem is that this can take up a lot of your time, and that is no good because if you spend a lot of time online, you are not spending a lot of time on the outside world. If you’re spending a lot of time online, that means you’re spending a lot of time offline, which is not good. So, 2 hours isn’t too much for me, but if you spend a lot of time online, you might want to consider cutting down to 1 hour of screen time per day.

If you notice any of these signs, it might be time to nip it in the bud. You may have already picked up a habit and are probably thinking I should cut back, but it might be hard to get back into the swing of things. Don’t worry, we all have bad habits, so I suggest you give it a try and see if it helps.
