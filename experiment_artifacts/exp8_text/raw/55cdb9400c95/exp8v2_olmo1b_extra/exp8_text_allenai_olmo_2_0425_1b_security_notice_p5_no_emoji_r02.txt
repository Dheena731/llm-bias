Employee handbook and company email

If you do not have an employee handbook handy, you can access a free employee handbook template in any of the following ways:

  • Google it

  • Go to the company website and find the employee handbook

  • Google it

  • Go to the company directory

  • Go to the company website and look in the “Help Center”

  • Go to the company website and look in the “Internal Resources” section

  • Go to the company website and look in the “Employee Handbook” section

All of the resources listed above should have a link to a printable or downloadable copy of the employee handbook.

If you’ve downloaded the employee handbook, make sure to print it on a high-quality paper stock, so you don’t end up with a flimsy copy.

You may also want to give it to them in an envelope so you know it hasn’t been read yet.

If you don’t have a company email, you can use Legal Zoom or another comparable service to create an employee email address that can be used in place of the company email address.
