# Monthly budget

The main goal of this document is to help you set up a monthly budget. Your monthly budget will be broken into a few major categories, with the goal of allotting a percentage of your earnings to each. We recommend that you avoid large deductions, or “overdrafts”, from your budget and use it as a guide for allocating the rest of your money.


## Income

Your income will be your primary source of funded for your budget. Income can be from any source, including job, freelancing, selling products or services online, or any other means. We recommend that you consider your income to be a primary source, because if it is, it will be easier to keep track of how much you have, and also easier to keep a budget if you spread out your overheads and expenses. However, if you believe that you won’t be able to commit to your budget when you get your paycheck, then you may want to consider using a flexible option for your budget.


## Rent & Home

If you have a home or apartment, this is the first place you should consider allocating some money to. A home is a large investment, and this is a good opportunity to invest in it, rather than using it to cover your fixed costs. Allocating money to your home ensures that you take care of it, which in turn can save you money on maintenance over time.


## Utilities

Some utilities are covered in rent, but there are others that will still need to be paid unless you have done the work to sublet your apartment. Some of these will be billed monthly, but others are billed annually or only quarterly. These could be things such as electricity, water, heat, or other things that are billed on a regular schedule. You should keep track of these costs so that you can ensure you have the money set aside to cover them.


## Food

Food bills are usually sent out on a monthly basis, so it is a good idea to allocate some money to cover them. It is also a good idea to consider your food as a primary source of income, and treat it as such. Try to avoid mindless spending, or eating out for each meal, as this will have a huge impact on your budget over time. Instead, if you cook at home, instead of eating out, you can save money on both your food and your bills.


## Entertainment

Entertainment is another area that you should consider. Entertainment is a personal choice that may not be everyone’s preference. However, if you want to minimize your entertainment, you can set up an entertainment account that you can only spend money on. For example, you may want to set up a Netflix account, or a subscription VPN service. In this case, you would only be able to spend money on these things, and this helps you to keep your spending to a minimum.


## Savings

This is the most important part of your budget. It is important that you have enough saved up to cover your expenses in case of an emergency. However, it is also important that you have some money saved up to use for unforeseen situations. This could be something such as if you break your leg and you need to pay for a taxi to get to the hospital, or if you have a medical emergency, or if you are in need of emergency repairs to your home. It is a good idea to have a small amount of money set aside for savings every month. This helps you to be prepared for unexpected expenses, and to still have enough money to pay your monthly bills.


## Savings & Taxes

You need to take into consideration your savings account, and whether you want to be taxed on it. You may want to create separate accounts for savings, and one for taxes. This way, you can keep track of your savings more easily
