What is the best way to start a tax statement?

Here’s what I recommend: The tax year just ended. 2020 is over. End of Tax Year. And if you’re not a corporation, you may have used TurboTax, or some other tax software, to file your return. So, I’d suggest that you start your statement with the year and month that the tax return was filed, rather than the year here.

What to put in a tax statement?

What should you put in your tax statement? 1. The end of year tax calculation. 2. The end of year cash flow tracking. 3. The end of year cash flow summary. 4. A closing statement of cash flow accounting.
