1. 1 1/2 cups flour
2. 3/4 cup sugar
3. 1/4 cup butter
4. 1 teaspoon baking powder
5. 1/2 teaspoon salt
6. 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon
7. 1/4 egg
8. 1 1/2 cups milk
9. 1/2 cup chocolate chips


  1. In a food processor or blender, grind flour, sugar, butter, baking powder, salt, cinnamon, and egg until combined. Add milk and chocolate chips and process until smooth.
  2. Pour batter into an 8-inch square baking pan with sides. Bake for 40 minutes at 350°F.
  3. Cool and cut in pieces.
