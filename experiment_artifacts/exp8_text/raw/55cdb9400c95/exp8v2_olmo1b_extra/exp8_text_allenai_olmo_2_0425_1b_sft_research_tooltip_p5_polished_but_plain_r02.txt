Export query results is a straightforward process. Here's how you do it:

1. Open your query result in the software or tool you're using.
2. Right-click on the selected area of the result.
3. Look for the option to "Copy" or "Copy Link Address."
4. Click on "Copy" to save the link to the query result.
5. Open a new document or tab in your preferred word processor or spreadsheet program.
6. Paste the copied link into the new document or tab where you want to view the query results.
7. Begin editing the new document or tab to view and distribute the query results as you need.  

Remember, you can always come back to the query results by right-clicking on the page and selecting "Copy Link Address" to paste the link back into the document where you were viewing the results.
