The heat advisory includes the following elements:

  • 1. Location
  • 2. Temperature
  • 3. Duration
  • 4. Heat index
  • 5. Any hyphens

    6. Forecast for the next 3-7 days

  • 7. Potential hazards
    • Do not list potential hazards that are not likely to affect the public.
    • Include only hazards that are of public concern.
    • The list may include the following:
      • Extreme heat, heat stress, heat stroke
      • Dehydration
      • Heat exhaustion
      • Heat cramps
      • Heat rash
      • Heatstroke
      • Water shortages
      • Other heat-related hazards such as wildfires

  • 8. Productivity loss
    • Include the following:
      • Lost work time
      • Increased number of on-the-job accidents
      • How workers can deal with heat-related hazards.
