When planning lunches for a busy week, start by setting a daily theme. Consider ingredients you enjoy and make a simple recipe that can be prepared in advance. For example, try a grilled chicken breast salad with mixed greens, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, feta cheese, and a vinaigrette dressing. This can be made on Sunday and kept in the fridge until Monday when you can add sliced almonds, walnuts, and a honey mustard dressing for a quick touch of flavor. For another option, prepare a quinoa salad topper. Mix cooked quinoa with cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, crumbled feta, olives, and a tangy dressing. Store the quinoa salad in a container with a lid so it stays fresh throughout the week. Save time and sanity with a sandwich. Put slices of turkey, cheese, lettuce, and tomato into a wrap or roll, and slice some cucumbers for a refreshing crunch. You can make these ahead of time and keep them in the fridge until hungry lunchtime. By planning meals with weekends or weekdays in mind, you can ensure each day has a tasty and nutritious option ready to go.
