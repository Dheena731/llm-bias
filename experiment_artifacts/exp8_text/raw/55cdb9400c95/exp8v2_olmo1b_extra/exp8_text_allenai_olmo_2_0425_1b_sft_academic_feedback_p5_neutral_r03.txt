Missing citations in an essay can significantly diminish its credibility and impact. It's crucial to properly cite any sources you use to ensure that they are acknowledged for the work they've contributed to your argument. Failure to do so can lead readers to doubt the validity of your claims or infer that you have not done thorough research. Properly cited sources also help in building a comprehensive understanding of the topic at hand, providing context and supporting evidence for your assertions. Therefore, addressing citation gaps is an essential step towards presenting a well-rounded and convincing essay.
