# Stay Hydrated: Drink Enough Water Throughout the Day

* Start your day with a glass of water upon waking up.
* After meals, drink a glass of water to aid digestion.
* Carry a water bottle with you throughout the day to ensure you never get too thirsty.
* Avoid caffeine and alcohol as they can dehydrate you.
* Consider infusing your water with fruits or herbs for added flavor and hydration.
* Plan for breaks at work to take a sip of water regularly.
* Remember to drink water before, during, and after exercising or in warm weather.
