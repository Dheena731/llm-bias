Latency is an important factor in web applications because it affects user experience, speed, and performance. When pages on your website load slowly, users may become frustrated and leave your site. In addition, high latency can negatively impact user trust and loyalty.

To assess latency, there are several metrics and benchmarks that developers use, such as Pingdom or Google's PageSpeed Insights. These can be downloaded and installed on your server to measure and improve your site's performance. Web developers should regularly monitor these metrics to identify where improvements can be made and to ensure that your site remains fast and reliable.

People expect instant responses on the internet, and if your website is slow or unreliable, it can cost you in terms of lost sales, relationships, and customer trust. Thus, understanding and managing website latency is crucial for ensuring a successful online presence.
