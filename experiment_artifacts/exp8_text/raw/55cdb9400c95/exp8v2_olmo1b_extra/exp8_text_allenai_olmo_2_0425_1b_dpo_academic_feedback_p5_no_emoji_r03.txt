One factor that can significantly impact the credibility and validity of an essay is the absence of proper citations. Proper citations are imperative for several reasons: they acknowledge the sources of information, support the validity of arguments, and help the readers locate the original works for themselves. If your essay lacks adequate citation details, it might lead to confusion or disadvantageous assumptions about the reliability of your sources, potentially undermining the quality and persuasive strength of your writing. Incorporating citations into your essay not only adheres to academic standards but also enhances the overall academic integrity of your work.
