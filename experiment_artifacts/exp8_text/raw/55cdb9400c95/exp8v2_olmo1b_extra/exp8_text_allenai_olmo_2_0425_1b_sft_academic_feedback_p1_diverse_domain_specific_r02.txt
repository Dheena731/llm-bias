Title: A Comprehensive Review of the Impact of Social Media on Social Inequality

Introduction: The growing role of social media platforms in our daily lives has brought about multiple transformative effects on the social sphere. This literature review undertakes an in-depth examination of these changes, focusing on their relationship with social inequality. The study aims to provide a holistic understanding of the interplay between social media and inequality, as well as identify potential directions for future research.

Literature Review: The literature on the impact of social media on social inequality is vast and multifaceted. The review draws on a diverse range of sources, including academic papers, industry reports, and field studies, to cover different aspects of the subject. Key themes identified in the sources include the elevation of social media as a medium for social mobility, the representation of marginalized groups, and the role of social media in fostering or exacerbating social divides.

Methods: This review adopts a mixed-methods approach, combining quantitative analysis with qualitative insights. Quantitative data is gathered from existing research databases, while qualitative data is derived from semi-structured interviews with social media users and scholars. The sources were critically evaluated and synthesized to generate a comprehensive overview of the current state of social media and its effects on social inequality.

Results: The review reveals a complex interplay between social media platforms and social inequality. On one hand, social media has been used to amplify marginalized voices and challenge existing social hierarchies. On the other hand, the commercialization and personalization of social media platforms can lead to increased social stratification and isolation of certain groups. The review also highlights the potential for social media to exacerbate existing inequalities by perpetuating existing biases and creating new ones.

Conclusion: This literature review underscores the importance of continued research into the impact of social media on social inequality. As these platforms continue to evolve, so does the risk of exacerbating social divides. Future research should focus on developing strategies to mitigate these effects and harness the power of social media for social good.
