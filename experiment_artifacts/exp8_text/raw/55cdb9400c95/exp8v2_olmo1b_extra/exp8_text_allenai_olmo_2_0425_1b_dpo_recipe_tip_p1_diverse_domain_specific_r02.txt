# Quick Mushroom Risotto for a Weeknight Feast

**Tip:** Elevate your weekday dinner with this creamy and savory mushroom risotto. Perfect for feeding a crowd and keeping the kitchen clean! Since you're already prepping for a weeknight, this Arborio rice dish is a breeze to prepare.

*Ingredients*

- 1 cup Arborio rice (coarsely graded)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 cups chicken or vegetable broth, heated
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 ½ cups shredded fresh or canned mushroom caps (optional)
- 1 tsp dried Oregano
- 1 tsp dried Thyme
- ¼ cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese, plus extra for serving
- Fresh parsley or basil leaves for garnish

*Instructions*

1. **Prepare the Base:** In a heavy-bottomed saucepan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and minced garlic, and sauté until the onion is translucent and soft, about 5 minutes. 

2. **Cook the Rice:** Stir in the Arborio rice. Add a generous pinch of salt and pepper, then cook the rice until the grains are just tender and almost al dente, about 7-10 minutes. Mix in dried herbs (oregano, thyme).

3. **Assemble the Dish:** In the same pan, add the heated broth, and stir to combine. Bring it to a gentle simmer. Gradually pour in the tomatoes, crushing them slightly with the back of your spoon. Stir in the mushrooms (if using).

4. **urring, Transfer to a Serving Dish:** Once the broth has been absorbed (the risotto is creamy but still has some bite), remove from heat. Stir in the Parmesan and herbs, and let it sit for a moment to allow the flavors to meld.

5. **Serve Hot:** Spoon the risotto into serving bowls. Use a slotted spoon to fluff the rice and redistribute the creamy mixture. Garnish with additional grated Parmesan, fresh parsley, or a sprinkle of chopped fresh basil leaves before serving. 

**Notes:** Adjust the seasoning with more Parmesan or water (if needed) to achieve the perfect creamy texture. Risotto is very adaptable, so feel free to replace the mushrooms with your favorite bits of vegetables or meat. This recipe is best enjoyed warm with a green salad for a complete and comforting weeknight meal.
