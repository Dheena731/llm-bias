We're interested in experimenting with longer snippets for announcements about longer time periods. These events tend to not fit into the 300 character limit that the SE engine allows, and it's still a little unclear if / how this would be interpreted. Longer snippets could be closed as "not a real question", but even so, maybe the content is good enough for a question.
