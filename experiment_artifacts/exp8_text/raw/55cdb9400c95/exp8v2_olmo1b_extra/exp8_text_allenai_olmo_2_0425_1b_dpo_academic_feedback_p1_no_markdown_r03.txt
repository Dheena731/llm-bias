Title: Literature Review on Sustainable Practices in Urban Planning

The literature review presented in your paper offers a comprehensive overview of current sustainable practices in urban planning. You have skillfully organized the reviewed research which is evident from the logical progression and connections between each section.

One area for improvement is the coherence of argumentation. While you demonstrate a clear thesis the transition from one point to the next at times feels abrupt. Consider integrating more clear transitions between different sections to guide the reader through the argument more effectively.

Furthermore the volume of sources used appears to be extensive providing a solid foundation for your arguments. However ensure that the diversity of sources is representative of the academic community and that all perspectives are sufficiently explored to provide a balanced view.

The discussion section could benefit from more concrete examples or case studies to illustrate your points further especially when discussing specific sustainable practices. This would help readers better understand the implications and effectiveness of these practices in urban settings.

Overall the draft literature review demonstrates a strong commitment to the subject area and presents a solid foundation for further research. Minor adjustments to the structure and clarity of argumentation are recommended to enhance its impact on the reader.
