To make a delicious and easy weeknight dinner, sauté a pound of ground beef with onions and garlic, adding any preferred spices. Once the beef is browned, add canned chicken broth and let it simmer until the beef is cooked through. To complete the meal, stir in a can of crushed tomatoes and cover the mixture with a lid. Let the concoction simmer for about 10 minutes. Serve with rice or pasta for a complete and satisfying dinner.
