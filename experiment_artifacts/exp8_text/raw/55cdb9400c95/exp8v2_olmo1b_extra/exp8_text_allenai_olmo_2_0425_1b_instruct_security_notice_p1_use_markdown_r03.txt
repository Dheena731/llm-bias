# Workplace Security Notice: Password Reuse

* **Understanding Password Reuse**
  - Reusing passwords across multiple accounts is not secure and can lead to unauthorized access to your work, personal, and financial information.
  - Employers require all employees to create unique passwords for each account.
  - Password expiration dates are enforced to prevent reuse of previously used passwords.

* **Why It Matters**
  - Prolonged security breaches can result in significant data loss and brand damage.
  - Increased risk of identity theft and unauthorized access to sensitive information.
  - Upholds trust in our commitment to cybersecurity and data protection.

* **Action Steps**
  - Create and maintain unique passwords for each of your accounts.
  - Regularly update passwords to prevent reuse.
  - Report any signs of password reuse or account compromise to your IT department immediately.
