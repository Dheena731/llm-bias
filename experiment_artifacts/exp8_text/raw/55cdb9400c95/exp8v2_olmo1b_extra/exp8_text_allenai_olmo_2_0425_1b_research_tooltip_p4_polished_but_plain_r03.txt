Avoid abbreviations, acronyms, and jargon. Explain to the user what you expect. If you are marking up math, provide any missing math needed to explain what you are marking.

Do not mark up tables and illustrations. Use a visual or textual description instead, and link to images in the HTML or descriptive text in the markdown.

Use language that is as close as possible to the natural language of your site. Try to avoid esoteric mathematical notation. You may want to use a mathematical symbol font, like the one here.

Do not use an incorrect markup language. Use one of these formats, not the plain text format.

Do, however, use a markup language to mark up tables, illustrations, and images. Do not use plain text or raw HTML.
