In today's digital economy where instant gratification and real-time feedback are expected, latency in web applications has become a significant issue. Latency refers to the time it takes for data to travel from the user to the server and back, and it is an important factor that affects the performance and user experience of a web application. 

With the rapid growth of internet usage and the demand for real-time data, web applications have to be optimized to handle high traffic loads and provide smooth and instant responses. High latency can lead to frustrating user experiences, as users may experience lag when waiting for data to load, affecting the overall satisfaction and engagement with the application.

Additionally, in industries such as healthcare, financial services, and e-commerce, latency can affect critical processes such as patient diagnosis, financial transactions, and order processing. Even a small delay can result in significant consequences, making it crucial for web developers to ensure low latency in their applications to meet user expectations and avoid potential consequences.

In conclusion, low latency is essential for maintaining a competitive advantage in the rapidly evolving digital landscape, ensuring smooth and instantaneous user experiences, and preventing negative consequences of high latency in web applications.
