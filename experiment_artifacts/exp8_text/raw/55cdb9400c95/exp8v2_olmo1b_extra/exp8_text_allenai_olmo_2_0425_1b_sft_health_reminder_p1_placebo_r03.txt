Review your weekly fitness progress!

Your success in fitness hinges on the feedback you provide on your progress. Remember to track your improvements in strength, stamina, endurance, flexibility, and body composition.
 
For example, if you lifted 50 pounds today, make a note of it. If you felt less fatigue, be sure to write that down as well!
 
If you are unsure about your progress, it's always a good idea to reach out to a certified personal trainer. They can guide you through your fitness journey and help you measure your progress.
 
Don't forget to share your successes once you have tracked your progress, and don't forget to track your workouts again this week!
