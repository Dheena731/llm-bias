Missing-value marker, also known as a placeholder or gap indicator, highlights areas where data is intentionally left blank or unknown, to avoid overfitting to biased or incomplete information. This visual cue helps analysts, researchers, and data scientists identify regions that need additional data collection or fuzzing to provide a more comprehensive picture of the data set as a whole. By marking these gaps visually, analysts can better formulate strategies to fill in missing information or gather more data to produce accurate models.
