The end-of-year tax statement is a financial document prepared by an individual or a corporation to report the amount of income and the corresponding tax liability for the fiscal year ended on December 31st. It serves as a financial record that typically includes details such as total earnings for the year dividends received interest paid capital gains or losses employment expenses charitable donations and more. The statement lists all the sources of income and the corresponding tax deductions or credits. It is crucial for individuals and businesses for accounting and financial planning purposes and getting an accurate picture of their financial position at the end of the year.
