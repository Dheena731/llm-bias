Focus on the key ideas. You should be able to summarize the paper and the results in a few sentences without looking at the paper. You should not need to read the paper, just summarize it. Try to focus on the main ideas and the key findings. Make sure you have enough information to make a judgement about the paper, without needing to read the paper.

Don't spend your time summarizing the introduction and conclusion. It is better to spend your time summarizing the key findings and comparing them to previous research. It is also not necessary to include all of the details about the experimental design, it is enough to give the reader the right information and point them to the main results.

Do not summarize the abstract. Do not include the abstract in your review. The abstract is a summary of the paper written by the author and it is much more detailed than the review. Instead, summarize the main ideas and the key findings.
