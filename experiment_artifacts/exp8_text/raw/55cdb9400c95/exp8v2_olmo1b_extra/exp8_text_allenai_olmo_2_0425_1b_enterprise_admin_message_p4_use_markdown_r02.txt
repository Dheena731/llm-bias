Exercise: Create a User Experience

Gather a group to walk through this exercise together, using your program’s policies and prompt. In order to focus and create buy-in, we recommend using only one prompt at a time. Here are some prompts to get you started:

  1. Create a small, quick, engaging, and standalone user experience that demonstrates what you’re going to do. It doesn’t have to be formal or polished.
  2. Share your experience with your program directors. Ask them if they have any feedback or suggestions. Does it make sense to you? How could you improve it?
  3. When you’re ready, share it with your community of students, staff, and visitors. Ask them to tell you how it works, if they like it, and what they’d change.

Conduct a quick review of the four user experiences created by your community to see which ones provide the most information to faculty, staff, and visitors to your program.

You don’t need to revise your policies every time you want to communicate them to your community — just keep updating them with the latest changes. Learning how to communicate them through user experience is a crucial skill for all educators.
