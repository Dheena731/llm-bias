In this case, the cybercriminals have sent phishing emails in the form of our company, asking you to provide your personal information, including your email account credentials.

These phishing emails appear to be from our company but are actually created by cybercriminals who are attempting to steal your personal information. Do not click on any links or provide any sensitive information while at work.

To prevent further harm, please report any suspicious emails to your IT department immediately. It is important to remain vigilant and educate your coworkers as well. If you come across any suspicious emails or messages, please do not respond, and report it to the appropriate department immediately.
