Before submitting your assignment:

  1. Make sure you have received credit for the course.

  2. Take a few minutes to read through the course materials and the readings.

  3. If you have questions, contact the course instructor. Be sure to include your email address and the course ID.

  4. If you are going to work on this assignment outside of the class, set up a notebook for the assignment in your Google Drive (https://docs.google.com/). You will be assigned a Google Drive folder, and you will use this folder to store your notes for this assignment. (You may also use other apps, such as OneNote, but you must have a Google account.) Make sure to create your notebook folder on your own drive or find the folder in your Drive, rather than duplicating a Google Drive folder.

  5. Set your browser's language preference to English. Note that you do not have to set the language preference; all submissions will be readjusted in the language of the instructor's choice.

  6. Avoid using emoji in your write-up.
