For creating rice that is less sticky and more manageable while cooking, the secret lies in balancing your rice-water ratio and timing.

**Specific tip:** Use a 2:1 water-to-rice ratio. For example, if you're cooking 2 cups of rice, use 4 cups of water. The more water you use, the less sticky the rice will become. This works because the extra water facilitates the steam generation and allows the rice to cook through without becoming overly clingy. Be sure to stir the rice occasionally while it's cooking to prevent it from sticking too much to the bottom of the pot. Aim for a light, fluffy texture instead of a mushy one.

This tip is directly applicable to the context of cooking rice, focusing on precision and control over the cooking process for those aiming for a sturdier and less sticky finished product.
