**Phishing Emails Notice**

- **What are phishing emails?**
    - Phishing emails are deceptive emails that try to trick you into revealing sensitive information such as passwords or financial details.
    - They often appear to come from legitimate sources like your bank or email provider.

- **How to identify phishing emails:**
    - **Suspicious links**
        - Avoid clicking on links unless you are certain of their origin.
    - **Poor spelling and grammar**
        - Many phishing emails contain obvious errors.
    - **Begrudging tone or urgent request**
        - Be cautious of emails that create an urgent sense of needing immediate action.

- **What to do if you receive a phishing email:**
    - **Do not respond or click on any links**
    - **Report it to your IT department or supervisor**
    - **Contact the actual contact number or website of the claimed sender**

---

**Note:** Always verify the sender's identity through other channels before taking any action.
