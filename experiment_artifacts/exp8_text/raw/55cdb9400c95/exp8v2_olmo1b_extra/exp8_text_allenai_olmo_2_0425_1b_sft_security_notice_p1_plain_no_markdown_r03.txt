Attention all employees,

It is important to be aware of the dangers of password reuse. Reusing passwords across multiple accounts is not only cybersecurity negligence but can also lead to identity theft and other forms of cybercrime. Using the same password for multiple accounts exponentially increases your risk of having all your passwords compromised at one time. By keeping your passwords unique for each of your accounts, you greatly reduce your risk of exposure and protect your personal information from potential harm.

If you have forgotten your password for a specific account, do not attempt to guess or try to reset it by using your email address or other personal information. This practice is not only inefficient but can also alert your cybercriminals to the fact that you have reused a password. Instead, contact the support team to have them reset the password without exposing your personal information. Remember, your safety and the integrity of your accounts are of utmost importance, and following these guidelines will help protect you from potential threats.
