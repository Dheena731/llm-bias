# Short Breaks from Screens

* **Take regular breaks to reduce eye strain:** Aim to pause for 5-10 minutes every hour to rest your eyes.
* **Schedule short downtime during work hours:** Use tools like timer apps to remind you to switch activities every 20 minutes.
* **Outdoor activities:** Go for a short walk outside to stimulate different senses and clear your mind.
* **Mindful relaxations:** Practice activities like deep breathing, meditation, or journaling to promote mental refreshment.
