**Reminder: Secure Your Screen!

As we continue to navigate through the busy weeks ahead, it's essential to remind everyone of an important practice: securely locking their screens when they step away from their workstation. Cybersecurity incidents can occur at any time, and the best defense is a strong offense. 

Here are a few quick tips to help prevent unauthorized access and keep your workspace safe:

* Utilize a strong, unique password for each account and avoid using easily guessable information like birthdays or common words.
* Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever it is offered.
* Use a password manager to generate and store complex passwords.
* Be mindful of phishing attempts and never-click on suspicious links.
* Regularly back up your data to an external drive or cloud storage.

We understand that staying safe online is part of your daily responsibilities, and this small reminder will help strengthen our community's cybersecurity posture.

Let's keep our screens locked — and our data secure! Thanks for your vigilance and commitment to maintaining a safe working environment.
