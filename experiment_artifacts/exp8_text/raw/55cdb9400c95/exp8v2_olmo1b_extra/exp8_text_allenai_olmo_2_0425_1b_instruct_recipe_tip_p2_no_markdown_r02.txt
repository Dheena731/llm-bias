Place herbs in a cool, dry area out of direct sunlight and rain. Root herbs like carrots and parsley do best in a cool, damp environment and should be stored in a plastic bag to retain moisture. Leafy herbs like basil and chives prefer a humid environment and can be stored in a plastic bag in a separate container with water. Keep herbs in the refrigerator's crisper drawer for up to a week and use fresh herbs often to keep their flavor consistent and prevent overgrowth.
