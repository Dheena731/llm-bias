**Shared Network Usage Guidelines**

* **Respect Individual bandwidth:** 
   * Use the network only when necessary and ensure your activities don’t negatively impact others.
* **Limit simultaneous connections:**
   * Avoid running multiple applications with high bandwidth use on the same session.
* **Use file-sharing judiciously:**
   * Share files only when necessary and be mindful of the size and frequency of uploads/Downloads.
* **Contribute to the network:**
   * Curb excessive downloading and uploading to foster a efficient environment.
* **Support network security:**
   * Keep software updated to protect against vulnerabilities.
* **Abide by legal restrictions:**
   * Respect copyright and use the network strictly for legal purposes.

Failure to adhere to these guidelines may result in network restrictions or consequences.
