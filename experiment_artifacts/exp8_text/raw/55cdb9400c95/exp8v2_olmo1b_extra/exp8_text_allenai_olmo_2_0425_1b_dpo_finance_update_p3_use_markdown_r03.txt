# New Savings Goal Feature Update

**Heading**
- **Introduction**
  - A new feature has been introduced to help users set and track specific savings goals more effectively.
  - The feature allows users to define clear and achievable targets for different categories of expenses or savings objectives.
- **Key Benefits**
  - Increases motivation and discipline: users can see their progress and stay motivated towards their goals.
  - Easy customization: users can adjust target amounts, deadline dates, and even compound interest based on their preference.
- **Usage**
  - Access the new saving goal feature by navigating to the 'Goals' section in the app.
  - Set goals for various activities like monthly expenses, emergency funds, vacation savings, or education allowances.
  - Track and modify goals anytime to adapt to changing circumstances or achieve new milestones.

This new feature is designed to make savings more manageable and rewarding for users in their journey towards financial independence.
