To ensure the effective use of our shared IT resources, we establish the following ground rules:

1. No harassment or offensive material is permitted on our network.
2. Respect for privacy and confidentiality is paramount. Unauthorized access or disclosure of information is strictly prohibited.
3. The use of our network is restricted to authorized individuals only and their respective departments.
4. Unauthorized sharing of network resources (e.g., software, files, or documents) is strictly prohibited.
5. Artistic and legitimate non-commercial uses of our network are encouraged, provided they comply with the terms set forth above.
6. Regularly update and patch all network systems and devices to ensure the security and integrity of our shared resources.
7. Report any disruption of the network service or potential violations of these guidelines to the IT department immediately.

Please adhere to these rules to ensure the continued effective operation and safeguarding of our shared resources for the benefit of all members of the network community.
