Latency in web applications is crucial because it directly impacts the user experience. When users access a web application, they expect instant responses to their actions like clicks, searches, or uploads. Any delay in data transmission and processing can make these interactions feel sluggish and frustrating, negatively affecting the user's satisfaction and likelihood to return to the application. High latency can also lead to dropped connections, increased bandwidth usage, and could even result in downtime for critical applications, impacting overall performance and reliability. Therefore, minimizing latency is essential for maintaining a responsive and smooth user experience in today's fast-paced digital world.
