Attention community members,

We are pleased to inform you of the new weekly recycling collection schedule starting from this Friday, October 22. Here are the changes to guide you in proper disposal of your recyclable materials:

**New Weekly Recycling Collection Schedule:**

**Monday:**
- Glass: Collection trucks will now collect glass on the street from 6 AM to 10 AM.
- Plastic #1 (PETE): Please sort plastic bottles and jars in the blue bin.
- Plastic #2 (HDPE): Milk jugs, shampoo bottles, and other plastic bottles marked with the number 2 are accepted.
- Plastic #3 (LDPE): Plastic shopping bags, squeezable bottles, and other plastic films are welcome.
- Metal: Aluminum cans and steel saucepans are accepted in the green bin.
- Paper: Leaflets, corrugated cardboard, and white office paper can be put in the green bin.
**Note:** Plastic-wrapped items containing glass, metal, or food waste should be placed in the appropriate waste bin.

**Tuesday:**
- Organic Waste: Food scraps, yard clippings, and garden material can be added to the green bin.
**Thursday:**
- Mixed Recyclables: This day is for mixed paper and glass.
**Friday:**
- General Waste: This is your last chance to put in general waste items.

Please remember to sort your recyclables based on the new guidelines and carry out your recycling in the designated containers by 10 AM on collection days.

For further information and assistance, please refer to our latest recycling fact sheet available on our website for detailed instructions and updates.

We appreciate your attention to these changes and encourage a greener and more sustainable community. Thank you for your cooperation in helping us achieve a cleaner environment.
