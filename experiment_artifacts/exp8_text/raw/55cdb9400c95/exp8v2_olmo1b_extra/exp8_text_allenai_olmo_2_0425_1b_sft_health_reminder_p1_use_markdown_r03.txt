**Weekly Fitness Progress Review**

1. **Journal Your Fitness Goals**
   - List and write down your fitness goals for the week.
   - Include both short-term and long-term goals.

2. **Track Your Mileage**
   - Record the total distance you run or walk.
   - Ensure to record the times for each session.

3. **Monitor Your Repetitions**
   - Identify how many repetitions you performed in each workout.
   - Track any changes in exercise form or technique.

4. **Keep Track of Active Recovery**
   - Note any days you rest, stretch, or perform low-intensity exercise.
   - Ensure to incorporate recovery days into your routine.

5. **Measure Your Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)** 
   - Record measurements such as weight, body fat percentage, or blood pressure.
   - Identify any trends or changes over the week.

6. **Reflect on Your Challenges and Successes**
   - Consider any challenges you faced and how you addressed them.
   - Celebrate any victories or achievements!

7. **Schedule Adjustments**
   - Make any necessary adjustments to your routine or diet based on your progress.
   - Review your goals and be prepared to modify them if needed.

8. **Set New Goals for the Next Week**
   - Plan your actions for the upcoming week based on this review.
   - Set goals that are challenging yet achievable. 

Remember to set aside time each week to reevaluate your progress. This will help you stay on track, maintain motivation, and make necessary adjustments to ensure your fitness journey is successful.
