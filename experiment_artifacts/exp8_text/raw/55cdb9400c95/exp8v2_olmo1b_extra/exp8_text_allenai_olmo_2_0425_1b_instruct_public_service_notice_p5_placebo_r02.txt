**Polling Station Location Information:**

When you're heading to your local polling station, here's a short guide to help you navigate the process smoothly:

* **Location:** Voting in a polling station is relatively straightforward. Depending on the voting system used in your jurisdiction (e.g., paper ballot, electronic voting), the polling station will either be a physical location (e.g., a school hall, a community center) or an online platform accessible via a link sent to your registration letter.

* **Key Details:** Ensure you are arriving on time. Voting hours are typically from early in the morning until closing time on the day of the election, although specific hours may vary by location and election day. Bring your ID if requested, and remember to check the latest election schedule in your area.

* **Accessibility:** Many polling stations aim to be accessible to all residents, including those with disabilities, and are equipped with necessary accommodations such as ramps, accessible parking, and hearing loops. However, always confirm the accessibility details for your polling location prior to your visit.

* **Place Names:** For instance, if your polling station is located in **Walden Park**, be sure to enter the park parking lot and follow the signs to the designated area. Or if it's part of the **Interior Communities Development Authority (ICDA)** building, make your way to the public entrance.

* **Secure Your Ballot:** Remember to secure your ballot before heading to the polling station. Do not leave it in your car or at your residence unattended.

* **Contact Information:** If you encounter any issues at your polling station, don't hesitate to call the election office for assistance. **Phone numbers have been provided on your official registration confirmation letter.**

This information is intended to assist you in making a democratic contribution to the electoral process efficiently and securely. Happy voting!
