Attention residents regarding the upcoming changes to the municipal recycling schedule. Beginning next month on the first day of the new month, the day for curbside recycling collection will shift from Wednesday to Thursday. Please ensure all recyclable materials are placed at the curb by seven in the morning on the new collection day. Items left out after this time may not be collected and could result in fines. Additionally, glass jars and bottles must now be rinsed thoroughly before placement in the bin to prevent contamination. Plastic bags and wrappers should remain inside your home as they can damage sorting equipment. If you have questions about these updates or need assistance with bulky item pickups, please contact the waste management office during business hours at the phone number listed on our website. Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our community clean and sustainable.
