**Important Voting Information**

To all registered voters, please note that polling stations for the upcoming election will be located at the following sites:

* Local Community Center, 123 Main Street
* Town Hall, 456 Elm Street
* Public Library, 789 Oak Street

These polling stations will be open on election day from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. To confirm your assigned polling station, please check your voter registration card or visit our website. We encourage all eligible voters to exercise their right to vote and participate in the democratic process.
