Here's a calendar tip that has saved me a lot of hassle: schedule your tasks, not just your meetings. Instead of keeping a to-do list that floats separately, block out thirty minutes on your calendar for each important task, like replying to emails, working on a report, or booking that dentist appointment. When a task has a real time slot, it stops feeling optional, and you can see right away whether your week is realistic. If something slips, just drag it to a new time instead of letting it quietly disappear. Give it a try for one week and see how much calmer your days feel.
