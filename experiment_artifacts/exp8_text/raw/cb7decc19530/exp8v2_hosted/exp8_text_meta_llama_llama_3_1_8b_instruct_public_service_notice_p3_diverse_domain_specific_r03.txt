**Notice to Residents: Changes to Recycling Collection Schedule**

Effective March 1, 2023, the City of Portland is implementing changes to the residential recycling collection schedule to improve efficiency and reduce costs. As a result, the following adjustments will be made:

* Collection days for all neighborhoods will be adjusted to every other week, with alternating weeks for paper and cardboard, and plastics and metals.
* The recycling collection trucks will no longer be able to access the alleys behind homes, and residents are required to place recyclables in designated curbside bins.
* The accepted materials list has been updated to reflect the City's new recycling guidelines. Please visit the City's website or contact the Public Works department for a detailed list of accepted materials.

These changes are designed to increase the effectiveness of the recycling program and reduce contamination rates. We appreciate your cooperation and understanding in this effort to promote sustainability in our community.

For more information or to report any issues, please contact the City's Public Works department at (503) 823-6000 or [publicworks@cityofportland.org](mailto:publicworks@cityofportland.org).
