**Digital Detox Reminder**

As you dive into your coding session, data analysis, or gaming marathon, remember to give your eyes (and brain) a break. Every 60-90 minutes, take a 5-10 minute pause to stretch, move around, and rest your gaze on something other than a screen. This brief respite can help reduce eye strain, improve focus, and even boost productivity when you return to your task. Set a timer, take a short walk, or do a few jumping jacks – your digital self will thank you!
