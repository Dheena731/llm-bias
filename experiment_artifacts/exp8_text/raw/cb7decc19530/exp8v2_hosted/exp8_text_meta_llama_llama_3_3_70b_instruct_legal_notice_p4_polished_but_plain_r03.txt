Purchasers may be eligible for a refund under certain circumstances. To be eligible for a refund, the item must be returned in its original condition within thirty days of the purchase date. All returns must include a copy of the original receipt and a written explanation for the return. Refunds will be issued in the original form of payment and will be processed within ten business days of receipt of the returned item. Please note that shipping costs are non-refundable, unless the item was defective or shipped in error. If you have any questions about the refund process, please contact our customer service department for assistance.
