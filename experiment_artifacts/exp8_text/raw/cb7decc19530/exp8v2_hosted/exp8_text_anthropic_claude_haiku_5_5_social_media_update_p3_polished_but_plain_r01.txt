Join us this Saturday, June 14, from 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. at Riverside Community Park for our annual Neighborhood Spring Fair. The day will feature a farmers' market with local produce and baked goods, live music from area high school bands, children's activities including face painting and a bounce house, and a free bike safety check offered by the county fire department. Volunteers are still needed for the registration table and setup crew, so if you can spare an hour or two, please reply to this post or email volunteer@riversidecommunity.org. Parking is available along Maple Street, and the park's east entrance is wheelchair accessible. Bring a reusable bag and your appetite, and we hope to see you there.
