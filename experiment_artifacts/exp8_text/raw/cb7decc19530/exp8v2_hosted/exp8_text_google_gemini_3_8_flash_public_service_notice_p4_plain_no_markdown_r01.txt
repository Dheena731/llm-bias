A heat advisory has been issued for our area from noon today through Sunday evening. Dangerously high temperatures and humidity are expected, with heat index values projected to exceed one hundred degrees. Residents are strongly urged to take precautions to avoid heat-related illness. Stay indoors in air-conditioned spaces whenever possible, drink plenty of water, and avoid strenuous outdoor activities during the hottest parts of the day. Wear lightweight, loose clothing and never leave children or pets unattended in vehicles under any circumstances. Check on elderly family members and neighbors who may be vulnerable. If you experience dizziness, nausea, or heavy sweating, move to a cooler place and rest. Seek immediate emergency medical care if someone exhibits confusion, fainting, or hot, dry skin.
