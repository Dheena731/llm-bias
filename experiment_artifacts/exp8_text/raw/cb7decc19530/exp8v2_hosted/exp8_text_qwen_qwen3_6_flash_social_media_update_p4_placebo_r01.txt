Struggling to stick to your schedule? Try adding 15-minute buffer blocks between every meeting or task in your calendar app. This built-in pause gives you time to close out notes, reset mentally, or handle minor delays before jumping into your next commitment. You’ll reduce back-to-back stress and actually follow through on what you planned. Try it for a week and notice the difference. What’s one calendar setting that changed how you work?
