Here's a short workplace notice about phishing emails:

**Important Notice: Phishing Emails**

**To: All Employees**

**From: [IT Department/Management]**

**Subject: Protecting Your Personal and Company Data**

As we continue to work remotely, we want to remind everyone to remain vigilant when checking their emails. Phishing emails are becoming increasingly sophisticated, and our company's data security is a top priority.

**What is Phishing?**

Phishing is a type of online scam where attackers send fake emails that appear to be from a legitimate source, often to trick you into revealing sensitive information or installing malware on your device.

**How to Identify and Avoid Phishing Emails:**

* Be cautious of emails that:
	+ Ask for sensitive information, such as passwords or financial data.
	+ Contain spelling or grammar mistakes.
	+ Use generic greetings, such as "Dear employee".
	+ Request you to download or install software.
	+ Create a sense of urgency or panic.

**What to Do:**

* If you receive a suspicious email, do not:
	+ Click on links or download attachments.
	+ Respond with sensitive information.
	+ Delete the email without reporting it to the IT department.

**What to Do Instead:**

* Report the email to the IT department immediately by forwarding it to [IT email address].
* Do not engage with the email in any way.
* If you are unsure about the authenticity of an email, it's always better to err on the side of caution and report it.

**Your Safety and Our Security**

We take the security of our employees' data very seriously. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the IT department. Together, we can protect our company's data and prevent phishing scams.

**Stay Safe Online!**

Best regards,

[Your Name/IT Department]
