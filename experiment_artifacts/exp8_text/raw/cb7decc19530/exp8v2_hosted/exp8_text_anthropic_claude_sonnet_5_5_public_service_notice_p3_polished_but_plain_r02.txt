Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection

Beginning Monday, March 3, recycling collection in our community will move to a new schedule. Pickup will take place every other week rather than weekly, and collection days will shift for some neighborhoods. Residents who previously had service on Tuesdays will now be collected on Thursdays, while all other collection days will remain the same.

Along with the schedule change, the program will now accept a wider range of materials. Cartons, such as those used for milk, broth, and juice, may be placed in the blue bin with other recyclables. Plastic bottles, jars, and tubs numbered one through five will continue to be accepted. Glass will no longer be collected at the curb and should be taken to the drop-off containers at the public works yard, which are open daily from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m.

To help the new system run smoothly, please rinse containers briefly, remove food residue, and place items loosely in the bin rather than in plastic bags. Bags cannot be processed at the sorting facility and may cause an entire load to be rejected. Bins should be set at the curb by 7 a.m. on your collection day, with at least two feet of space around them so the truck can reach them easily.

Because collection is moving to every other week, we encourage you to check the updated calendar that will arrive in the mail later this month. Printed copies are also available at City Hall and the public library. If you have questions or need a larger bin to accommodate the longer interval between pickups, please contact the Public Works Office during regular business hours. We appreciate your continued effort to keep our community clean and our recycling stream free of contamination.
