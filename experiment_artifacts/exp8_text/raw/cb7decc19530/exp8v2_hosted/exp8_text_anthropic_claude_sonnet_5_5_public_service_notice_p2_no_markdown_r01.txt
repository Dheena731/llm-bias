PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE

The City Department of Public Works announces the temporary closure of Maple Street between 4th Avenue and 8th Avenue.

Dates: Monday, June 10 through Friday, June 14
Hours: 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily

Reason for Closure: Resurfacing and repair of the roadway, including the replacement of damaged storm drains.

Detour Information: Drivers should use Oak Boulevard and 9th Avenue as an alternate route. Detour signs will be posted. Local traffic and emergency vehicles will be given access to the work zone whenever possible.

Pedestrians and Cyclists: Sidewalks will remain open, but please use caution near the work area. Cyclists are advised to follow the posted detour.

Parking: No parking will be permitted along the affected section of Maple Street during construction hours. Vehicles left in the area may be towed.

Please plan for additional travel time and drive carefully through work zones. The schedule may change due to weather conditions. Updates will be posted on the city website.

For questions, contact the Department of Public Works at (555) 123-4567 or visit www.examplecity.gov/publicworks.

We appreciate your patience as we work to improve our streets.
