**CITY OF HARLOW PUBLIC WORKS**
**Recycling Collection Changes, Starting Monday, March 3**

**Pickup day.** Curbside recycling moves from Thursday to **Tuesday** for all addresses east of Dunmore Road. Addresses west of Dunmore stay on Thursday. Pickup will be **every other week**, not weekly. Your first Tuesday pickup is March 4. A printed calendar is being mailed with the March water bill, and the full schedule is at harlowpw.example/recycling.

**Glass is no longer collected at the curb.** Bottles and jars broke down too often in the trucks and contaminated the paper. Take glass to the drop-off bins at the Transfer Station on Kiln Road (Mon–Sat, 7 a.m.–4 p.m.) or to the bins behind the Eastgate library. Please rinse glass and remove lids.

**Now accepted:** Empty, flattened food and drink cartons (soup, broth, milk, juice).

**Still accepted:** Cardboard, paper, steel and aluminum cans, and plastic bottles, tubs, and jugs. Leave caps on the bottles.

**Still not accepted:** Plastic bags and film, shredded paper, foam, greasy pizza boxes, and anything smaller than a credit card. Place items **loose in the cart, not bagged.** Drivers cannot open bags, so bagged recycling will be treated as trash.

**Cart placement.** Set your blue cart out by 6:30 a.m. with the lid closed and the wheels toward the house. Leave three feet of clearance from mailboxes, cars, and other carts so the truck arm can grab it.

**Contamination tags.** Carts with obvious trash or bagged material will get a tag and will not be emptied. Sort out the problem items, and the cart will be collected on the next scheduled pickup.

**Holidays.** There are no changes to the schedule this spring. Memorial Day falls on a Monday and does not affect Tuesday or Thursday routes.

Questions or missed pickups: call Public Works at 555-0142 or email recycling@harlow.example.
