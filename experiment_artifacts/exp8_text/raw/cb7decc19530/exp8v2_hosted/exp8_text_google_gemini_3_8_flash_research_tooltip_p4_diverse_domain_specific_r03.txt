Here are two options tailored to specific data-intensive domains:

### Option 1: Financial Analytics / Valuation
**Marker:** `N/M` (or `—`)
> **Not meaningful.** Excluded because negative net income invalidates the multiple—not an omission, restatement delay, or reporting failure.

***

### Option 2: Observability / System Telemetry
**Marker:** `∅` (or dotted time-series gap)
> **Ingestion gap.** Daemon failed to publish metrics between 04:12–04:15 UTC. Displayed as missing to prevent aggregations from interpreting dropped packets as 0% load.
