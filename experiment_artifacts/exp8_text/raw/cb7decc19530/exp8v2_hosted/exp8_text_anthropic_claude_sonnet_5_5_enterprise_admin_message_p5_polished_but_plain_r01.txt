Subject: Upcoming Email Migration

Dear colleagues,

We will be migrating our email system to a new platform over the weekend of the 14th and 15th. The work is scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m. on Friday and should be finished by noon on Sunday. During that window, you may experience delays in sending and receiving messages, and access to webmail may be intermittent.

No action is required from you before the migration begins, but a few steps will help it go smoothly. Please clear out unneeded messages and large attachments from your mailbox by Thursday, since smaller mailboxes transfer faster. Please also make a note of any important meetings scheduled for the weekend, and consider saving a copy of key calendar details in case you cannot reach them for a short time.

Your existing messages, folders, contacts, and calendar entries will move over automatically, and your email address and password will stay the same. When you open your email on Monday morning, you may be asked to sign in again, and your mobile devices may need to be reconnected. We will send a short guide on Friday afternoon that explains how to do this on common phones and tablets.

If you notice missing messages or have trouble signing in after the migration, please contact the IT help desk by phone or reply to the confirmation message you will receive on Sunday. Our team will be available extended hours on Monday to assist with any questions.

Thank you for your patience and cooperation as we make this improvement.

Kind regards,
The IT Support Team
