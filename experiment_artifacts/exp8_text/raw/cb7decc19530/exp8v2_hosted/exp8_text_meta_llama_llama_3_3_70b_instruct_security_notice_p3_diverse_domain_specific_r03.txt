Reminder: Protect Patient Information

As we continue to provide excellent care to our patients, it's essential that we maintain the confidentiality and security of their personal and medical information. To ensure this, please remember to lock your computer screens when leaving your workstation, even if it's just for a few minutes.

This simple step helps prevent unauthorized access to sensitive data, including patient records and confidential research materials. Let's work together to safeguard our patients' trust and uphold our commitment to data protection.

If you have any questions or concerns about our information security policies, please don't hesitate to reach out to our IT department or compliance team. Thank you for your attention to this important matter.
