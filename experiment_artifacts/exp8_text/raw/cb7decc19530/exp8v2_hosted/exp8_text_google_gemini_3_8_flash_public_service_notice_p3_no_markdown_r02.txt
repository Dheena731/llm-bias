NOTICE: CHANGES TO RECYCLING COLLECTION

Please be advised that starting Monday, November 3, residential recycling collection will move from Tuesdays to Thursdays. 

Please ensure your recycling bins are placed at the curb by 7:00 AM on your new collection day. There are no changes to regular trash pickup schedules or the list of accepted recyclable materials.

To view the updated annual calendar or for more information, please visit www.cityservices.gov/recycling or call 555-0199.

Thank you for your cooperation.

City Waste Management Department
