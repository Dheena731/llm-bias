**Phishing emails: what we've been seeing**

IT logged 14 phishing emails reaching inboxes here in the last three weeks. Most fall into three patterns:

- **"Your mailbox is nearly full."** These ask you to log in through a link to keep receiving mail. Our mail system never sends this kind of message.
- **Invoices from vendors we already pay.** The sender name looks right, but the bank details have changed, or the attachment is an .html file. Accounts Payable will only update bank details after a phone call to a number already on file.
- **Urgent requests from a manager who is "in a meeting."** They're short and ask for gift cards, a wire, or a password. Confirm anything like this on Teams or by phone before acting.

If you get one:

1. Don't click links or open attachments you weren't expecting.
2. If you entered a password on a page you now think is fake, change it right away and call the Service Desk at ext. 4417.
3. Report it with the Report Phish button in Outlook. Please don't just delete it. Reports are how we block the next batch before it reaches anyone else.

You won't get in trouble for reporting a message that turns out to be legitimate. The outcome we really want to avoid is someone clicking and saying nothing.
