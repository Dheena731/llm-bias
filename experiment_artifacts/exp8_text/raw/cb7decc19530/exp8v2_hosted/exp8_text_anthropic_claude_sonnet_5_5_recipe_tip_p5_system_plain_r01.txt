Toss your vegetables with oil and salt before they go in the oven, not after, and make sure they are spread out in a single layer with a little space between the pieces. The oil helps the salt cling and promotes browning, while the room on the pan lets moisture escape so the edges caramelize instead of steaming. Hold back delicate additions like fresh herbs, minced garlic, and a squeeze of lemon until the last few minutes or until the vegetables come out, since they burn or turn bitter in high heat. A final pinch of flaky salt right before serving adds a bright finish.
