Hi neighbors! This Saturday, we're hosting our community picnic at Riverside Park from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. Bring your family, friends, and a appetite for good food and great company. There will be live music, a kids' face-painting booth, and a local bake sale, with all proceeds going to the library's summer reading program. Feel free to bring a lawn chair or blanket, and let us know in the comments if you plan to volunteer at the grill. We hope to see you there!
