We've added a new savings goals feature to help you set aside money for the things that matter to you, whether that's a vacation, an emergency fund, or a major purchase.

To get started, open the Savings tab and choose Create a Goal. Give it a name, enter the amount you want to reach, and pick a target date if you have one. The app will then calculate how much you need to save each week or month to stay on track, and you can adjust that figure at any time to fit your budget.

You can fund a goal in two ways. Manual transfers let you move money whenever you like, while automatic contributions move a fixed amount on a schedule you choose. Each goal shows a progress bar, your current balance, and an estimated completion date that updates as your habits change. If you fall behind, we'll send a gentle reminder rather than a warning, and if you get ahead, we'll let you know how much sooner you'll finish.

You can create up to ten goals at once and reorder them by priority, so your most important targets receive contributions first. When you reach a goal, you can keep the funds in place, move them back to your main account, or start a new goal with the same money.

The feature is available now for everyone on the latest version of the app. If you don't see it yet, update to the newest release and restart. We'd love to hear how it works for you, so please send feedback through the Help section in Settings.
