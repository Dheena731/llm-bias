Attention residents: the public library will be changing its opening hours beginning next Monday. The main branch will open at 9:00 a.m. and close at 8:00 p.m. on weekdays, and will be open from 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on Saturdays. The library will remain closed on Sundays and on all public holidays. Please note that the reading room and computer lab follow the same schedule, while the self-service book return is available around the clock. Library staff are happy to answer any questions in person at the front desk or by calling the information line during opening hours. We thank you for your continued support and look forward to welcoming you.
