Trim the stems and place tender herbs like basil, cilantro, and parsley upright in a jar with about an inch of water. Loosely cover the top with a plastic bag or lid and refrigerate. For tougher, woody herbs like rosemary, thyme, and sage, wrap them in a lightly damp paper towel and store inside a sealed container. Replace the water every two to three days to keep them fresh and vibrant.
