**Enable Two-Factor Authentication for Enhanced Security**

To add an extra layer of security to your account, we recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA). 2FA requires both a password and a second form of verification, such as a code sent to your phone or a biometric scan, to log in to your account.

**Why Enable 2FA?**

*   Prevents unauthorized access to your account
*   Protects your sensitive information from hackers and data breaches
*   Enhances the security of your online presence

**How to Enable 2FA:**

1.  Log in to your account.
2.  Go to the account settings or security page.
3.  Look for the 2FA option and follow the prompts to enable it.
4.  Choose a second form of verification (e.g., SMS, authenticator app, or biometric scan).
5.  Set up the 2FA method and save the changes.

**What's Next?**

Once 2FA is enabled, you'll be prompted to enter a verification code each time you log in to your account. This code will be sent to your designated verification method, ensuring that only you can access your account.
