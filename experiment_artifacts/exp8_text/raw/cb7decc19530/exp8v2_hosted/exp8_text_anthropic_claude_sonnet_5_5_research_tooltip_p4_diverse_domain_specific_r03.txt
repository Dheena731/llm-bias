Since you didn't name a domain, I assumed a clinical lab results table. Here's the tooltip:

**Tooltip (lab results):**
> **No result on file.** This test wasn't run, or its value hasn't posted. It does not mean 0 or "within range."

Variants for other domains:

**Weather station readings:**
> **No reading.** The sensor was offline or sent an invalid value. Daily averages skip this hour rather than counting it as 0°.

**Survey responses:**
> **Skipped or not shown.** The respondent left this blank, or branching logic never displayed the question. Percentages exclude these rows.

**Sales ledger:**
> **Amount not entered.** The row exists, but no figure was recorded. Totals leave it out, so they may understate actual sales.
