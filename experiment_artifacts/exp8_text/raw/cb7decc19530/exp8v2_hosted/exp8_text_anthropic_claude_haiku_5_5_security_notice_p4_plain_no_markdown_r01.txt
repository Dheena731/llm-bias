Two-Factor Authentication Is Now Available

We are pleased to announce that two-factor authentication can now be enabled on your account. This added layer of security helps protect your information by requiring a second form of verification in addition to your password when you sign in.

To turn it on, sign in to your account and open the Security section of your account settings. Select the option to enable two-factor authentication and follow the steps shown on screen. You will be asked to confirm a code sent to your mobile phone or generated by an authenticator app. Once setup is complete, you will receive a set of backup codes. Please store these somewhere safe, as they allow you to access your account if you lose your phone.

We strongly recommend enabling this feature, particularly if you use your account for sensitive transactions or stored personal data. If you have questions or run into any trouble during setup, please contact our support team, who will be glad to help.
