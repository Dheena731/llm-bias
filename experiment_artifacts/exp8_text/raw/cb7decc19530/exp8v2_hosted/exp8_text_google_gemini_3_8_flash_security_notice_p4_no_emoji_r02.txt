**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**

To strengthen the security of your account and protect your personal information, please enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA). 

With 2FA, logging in requires both your password and a temporary verification code sent to your trusted device. This extra layer of defense ensures that only you can access your account, even if your password is compromised.

**How to enable 2FA:**
1. Log in to your account and go to **Settings**.
2. Select the **Security** tab.
3. Locate **Two-Factor Authentication** and follow the on-screen setup instructions.

If you have any questions or need assistance with the setup process, please contact the support team.
