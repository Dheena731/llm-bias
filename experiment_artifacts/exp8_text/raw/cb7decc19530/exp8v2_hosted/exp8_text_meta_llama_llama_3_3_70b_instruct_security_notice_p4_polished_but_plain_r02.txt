To add an extra layer of security to your account, we recommend enabling two-factor authentication. This feature requires you to provide a second form of verification, such as a code sent to your phone or a biometric scan, in addition to your password. Enabling two-factor authentication will help protect your account from unauthorized access and reduce the risk of identity theft. You can enable this feature in your account settings, and we provide step-by-step instructions to guide you through the process. If you have any questions or need assistance, please do not hesitate to contact our support team.
