This notice serves to remind all employees that reusing passwords across multiple work systems is strictly prohibited. Sharing the same password between your email, network login, cloud services, or personal accounts significantly increases the risk of unauthorized access. If one account is compromised, attackers can easily move laterally through your other systems. All staff must create unique, strong passwords for every workplace application and avoid using information that can be easily guessed. A company approved password management tool is available for secure storage and should be used immediately. Employees who suspect they have reused a password previously are required to change it now by following the standard update procedure. Failure to comply with this policy may result in restricted system access and disciplinary action. For assistance or reporting concerns, contact the IT Security team at the internal help desk extension. Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining our organizational security.
