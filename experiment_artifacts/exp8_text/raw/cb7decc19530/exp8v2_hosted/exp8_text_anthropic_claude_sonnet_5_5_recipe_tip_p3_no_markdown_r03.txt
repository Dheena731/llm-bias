To make rice less sticky, rinse it well before cooking. Place the rice in a bowl or fine mesh strainer and run cold water over it, swirling with your hand, until the water goes from cloudy to mostly clear. This washes away the surface starch that causes grains to clump together. Then use a little less water than you might expect, about 1 and 1/2 cups of water per 1 cup of long-grain rice, and let the cooked rice rest covered for 10 minutes off the heat before fluffing it gently with a fork.
