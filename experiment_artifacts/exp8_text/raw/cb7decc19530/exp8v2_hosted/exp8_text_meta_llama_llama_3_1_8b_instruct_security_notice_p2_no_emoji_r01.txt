**Important: Phishing Emails in the Workplace**

As a reminder, our company has experienced an increase in phishing email attempts in recent weeks. These emails are designed to trick employees into revealing sensitive information, such as login credentials or financial information.

**What to Look For:**

* Emails that ask for sensitive information, such as login credentials or financial information
* Emails that create a sense of urgency or panic
* Emails that are poorly written or contain grammatical errors
* Emails that contain links or attachments from unknown sources

**What to Do:**

* Never respond to emails that ask for sensitive information
* Never click on links or download attachments from unknown sources
* Report suspicious emails to the IT department immediately
* Verify the sender's identity before taking any action

**What We Will Do:**

* Our IT department will continue to monitor and block suspicious emails
* We will provide regular training and awareness programs on phishing email prevention
* We will provide additional support and resources for employees who have been targeted by phishing emails

**Your Help is Needed:**

* If you receive a suspicious email, please report it to the IT department immediately
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or the IT department

Thank you for your cooperation and vigilance in protecting our company's assets.
