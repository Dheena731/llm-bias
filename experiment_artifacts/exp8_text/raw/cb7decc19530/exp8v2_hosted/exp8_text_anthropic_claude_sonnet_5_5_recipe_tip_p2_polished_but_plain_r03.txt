To keep fresh herbs vibrant for up to two weeks, treat them like cut flowers or like salad greens, depending on their type. Tender herbs such as parsley, cilantro, and dill do best with their stems trimmed and stood upright in a jar with an inch of water, then loosely covered with a plastic bag and refrigerated. Basil is the exception, since cold turns its leaves black. Keep it in a jar of water on the counter, out of direct sunlight, much as you would a bouquet. Sturdier herbs such as rosemary, thyme, and sage prefer a drier environment, so wrap them in a barely damp paper towel, slip them into a resealable bag or container, and store them in the refrigerator crisper. Whichever method you choose, wash herbs only right before using them, because excess moisture encourages rot, and change the water or paper towel every few days to keep everything fresh.
