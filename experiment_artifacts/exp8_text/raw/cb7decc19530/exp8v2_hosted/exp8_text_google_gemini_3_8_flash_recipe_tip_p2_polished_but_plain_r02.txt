To keep fresh herbs vibrant for weeks, treat them like cut flowers. Trim a half-inch off the stems and place the bunch upright in a glass jar with about an inch of water. For tender herbs like parsley, cilantro, and dill, tent a plastic bag loosely over the leaves and store the jar in the refrigerator, replacing the water whenever it becomes cloudy. Basil is the primary exception to this rule and should be kept on the kitchen counter at room temperature, as the refrigerator cold will quickly turn its delicate leaves black. Sturdier, woody herbs such as rosemary, thyme, and sage do best when wrapped loosely in a barely damp paper towel and sealed inside an airtight bag in your crisper drawer.
