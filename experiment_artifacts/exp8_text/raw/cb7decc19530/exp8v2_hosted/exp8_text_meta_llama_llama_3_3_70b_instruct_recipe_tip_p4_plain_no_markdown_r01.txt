To plan lunches for a busy week, set aside some time on the weekend to think about what you want to eat and make a list of the ingredients you need to buy. Consider what you have in your pantry and fridge already and try to use those items to minimize waste and save money. You can also prep some ingredients in advance, such as cooking a big batch of rice or roasting vegetables, to make packing lunches easier during the week. Having a plan in place will help you save time and ensure that you have healthy and satisfying lunches to take with you on the go.
