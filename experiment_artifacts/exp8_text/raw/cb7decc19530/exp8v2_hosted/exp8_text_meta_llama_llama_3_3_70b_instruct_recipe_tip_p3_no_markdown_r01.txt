To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it in a fine mesh strainer under cold running water before cooking. This helps remove excess starch from the surface of the rice, which can cause it to become sticky or clumpy. After rinsing, make sure to dry the rice thoroughly with a clean towel or paper towels to remove any excess moisture. Then, cook the rice with the right amount of water and use a non-stick pan or a rice cooker to help prevent it from sticking together. By following these steps, you can enjoy perfectly cooked, fluffy rice that's not sticky or clumpy.
