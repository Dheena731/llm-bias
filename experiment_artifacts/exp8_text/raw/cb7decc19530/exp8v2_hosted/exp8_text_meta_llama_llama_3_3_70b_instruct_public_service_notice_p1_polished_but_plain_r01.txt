The local library is open to the public Monday through Thursday from 9am to 8pm, Friday from 9am to 6pm, and Saturday from 9am to 5pm. The library is closed on Sundays and public holidays. During these hours, visitors can browse the shelves, borrow books and other materials, and access the library's computers and other resources. Library staff are also available to assist with research and answer questions. Please note that the library may have extended hours during exam periods or other special events, so it's a good idea to check with the library in advance if you're planning to visit at a time that's outside of the regular schedule.
