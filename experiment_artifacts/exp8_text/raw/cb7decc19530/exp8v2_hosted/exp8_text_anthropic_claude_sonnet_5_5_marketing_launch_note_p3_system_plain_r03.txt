Introducing the Clear Month Budget Template

Today we are releasing the Clear Month Budget Template, a spreadsheet built for people who want to understand their money without learning accounting or wrestling with complicated formulas. It works in both Excel and Google Sheets, and you can start using it in minutes.

The template brings your income, fixed bills, flexible spending, savings goals, and debt payments together in one place. You enter your numbers once, and the spreadsheet does the rest. Totals update automatically, and a simple summary page shows how much you planned to spend, how much you actually spent, and what is left in each category. If you go over in one area, you can see it right away and adjust before the month ends.

We designed it to be easy to live with. The layout is clean, the categories can be renamed or removed to match your life, and every formula is unlocked so you can customize it as your needs change. A built-in yearly view lets you spot patterns, such as a quiet month for savings or a seasonal spike in utilities, so next year's plan can be smarter than this year's.

The Clear Month Budget Template is available now for a one-time price of twelve dollars, with free updates for life. There are no subscriptions, no accounts to create, and no bank connections required. Your data stays in your own file, on your own device.
