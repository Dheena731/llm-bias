We are updating our security standards to better protect your account and personal information. Starting today, all users are strongly encouraged to enable two-factor authentication. This extra layer of defense requires a temporary verification code in addition to your regular password whenever you sign in, which significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access. You can set this up immediately by navigating to your account settings, selecting the security tab, and following the brief prompts to link your mobile phone or authenticator app. Taking just a few moments to activate this feature helps ensure that your data remains safe and secure. Please contact our support team if you encounter any difficulties during the setup process.
