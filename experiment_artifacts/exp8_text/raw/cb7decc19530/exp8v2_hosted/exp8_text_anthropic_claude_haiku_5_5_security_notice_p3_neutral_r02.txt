**Reminder: Please Lock Your Screens**

Team,

As we work with sensitive company and customer information, it's important that we all remember to lock our computer screens whenever we step away from our desks, even for a few minutes.

To lock your screen:
- **Windows:** Press **Windows key + L**
- **Mac:** Press **Control + Command + Q**

Locking your screen helps protect confidential data and prevents unauthorized access to company systems. Thank you for helping us keep our information secure.

If you have any questions or need help setting up automatic screen locking, please contact the IT department.
