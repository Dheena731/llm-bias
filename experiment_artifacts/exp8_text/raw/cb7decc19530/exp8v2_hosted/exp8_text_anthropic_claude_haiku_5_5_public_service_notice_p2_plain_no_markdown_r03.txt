PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE ON MAPLE AVENUE

The City of Riverton Department of Public Works advises residents that Maple Avenue will be closed to all vehicle traffic between Oak Street and Cedar Lane beginning at 7:00 a.m. on Monday, June 10, and continuing through Friday, June 14. The closure is necessary to complete repaving and storm drain repairs.

During the closure, detours will be posted along Elm Street and Willow Road. Local access will be maintained for emergency vehicles, and residents with properties on the affected block may reach their driveways from the north end of Maple Avenue at Oak Street. Pedestrians may continue to use the sidewalks, which will remain open except for a short section near the work zone that will be clearly marked.

Drivers are asked to plan alternate routes and allow extra travel time during the closure period. Bus Route 12 will follow a temporary detour, and riders should check the transit agency's website for updated stop locations.

Questions about the project may be directed to the Department of Public Works at 555-0142 during regular business hours, Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. The city thanks residents for their patience while these improvements are made.
