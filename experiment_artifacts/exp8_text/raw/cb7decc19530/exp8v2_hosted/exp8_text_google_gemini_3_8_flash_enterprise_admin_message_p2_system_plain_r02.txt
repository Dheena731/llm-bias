Please be advised that an updated expense approval workflow will take effect starting next Monday. Under this new process, all business expense reports must be submitted through the finance portal, where they will automatically route to your direct manager for initial sign-off before receiving final review from the accounting team. This change is designed to streamline reviews, provide better tracking, and ensure faster reimbursement times for everyone. Detailed documentation outlining the step-by-step submission process can be found in the employee resource center. If you have any questions or need assistance navigating the new system, please reach out directly to the finance department.
