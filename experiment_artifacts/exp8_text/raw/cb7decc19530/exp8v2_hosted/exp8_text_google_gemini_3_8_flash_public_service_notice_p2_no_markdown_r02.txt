PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE

Main Street between 1st Avenue and 4th Avenue will be fully closed to all vehicular traffic starting Monday, October 14, at 7:00 AM through Wednesday, October 16, at 5:00 PM. 

This closure is required to complete urgent water main repairs and asphalt resurfacing. 

A signed detour will direct traffic via Oak Street and Elm Street. Pedestrian access to local businesses will remain open throughout the construction period. Motorists are advised to plan for minor delays and seek alternate routes.

For more information, please contact the Department of Public Works at (555) 019-2834 or visit www.cityexample.gov/traffic.
