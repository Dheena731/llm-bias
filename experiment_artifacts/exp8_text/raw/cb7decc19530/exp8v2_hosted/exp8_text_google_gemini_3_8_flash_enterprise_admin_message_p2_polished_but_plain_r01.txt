Please be advised that we are introducing an updated expense approval workflow beginning next Monday. This new process is designed to accelerate reimbursement times, simplify tracking, and provide greater visibility into the status of submitted claims. Under the updated system, expense reports will automatically route to your direct supervisor for initial review before moving to the finance team for final disbursement. Employees should continue submitting receipts through the existing employee portal, where real-time tracking is now enabled for every submission. Managers are asked to review pending approvals within two business days to ensure timely processing. If you have any questions or need assistance navigating the new steps, please reach out to the finance team directly.
