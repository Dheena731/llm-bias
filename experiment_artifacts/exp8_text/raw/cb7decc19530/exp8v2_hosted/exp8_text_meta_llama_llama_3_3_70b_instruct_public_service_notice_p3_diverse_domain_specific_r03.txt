**Important Update: Recycling Collection Schedule Adjustment**

As part of our ongoing efforts to optimize waste management services, we are implementing changes to the recycling collection schedule in your area. Starting from March 1st, recyclables will be collected every other Thursday, instead of the current bi-weekly Friday schedule. This adjustment is designed to improve route efficiency and reduce contamination rates.

To ensure a seamless transition, please note the following key dates:

* Last Friday collection: February 24th
* First Thursday collection: March 2nd
* New collection schedule applies to all households and businesses in the designated area

Remember to place your recyclables at the curb by 6:00 AM on the designated collection day. For a detailed schedule and to confirm your collection day, visit our website or contact our customer service team at 555-1234. Your cooperation and participation in our recycling program are crucial in helping us maintain a sustainable and environmentally responsible community.
