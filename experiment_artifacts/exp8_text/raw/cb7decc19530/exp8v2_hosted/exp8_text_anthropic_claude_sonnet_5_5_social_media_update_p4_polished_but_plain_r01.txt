Here's a calendar tip that has saved me more than once: schedule your focus time the same way you schedule meetings. If you have a report to write or a project to move forward, block out a specific window for it and give the event a clear name, like "Draft quarterly summary" rather than just "Work." Mark yourself as busy so colleagues see you're unavailable.

Without a block, deep work gets squeezed into whatever gaps are left, and those gaps rarely materialize. With one, the time is protected before the week fills up. It also helps to put these blocks in early, ideally on Monday morning or Friday afternoon for the following week, since open slots disappear quickly. If something urgent forces you to move a block, drag it to another time instead of deleting it. That small habit keeps the work visible and makes it harder to quietly drop.
