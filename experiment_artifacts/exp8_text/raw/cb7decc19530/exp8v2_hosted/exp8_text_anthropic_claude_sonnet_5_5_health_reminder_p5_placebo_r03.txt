**Reminder: Time to Refill Your Prescription**

It's a good time to refill your prescription so you don't run out of medication.

- **Check your supply.** Look at how much medication you have left, and request your refill before it runs out.
- **Contact your pharmacy.** Ask them to refill it. Many pharmacies take requests by phone, online, or in person.
- **If you have no refills left,** the pharmacy can usually contact your prescriber for a new authorization, or you can call your prescriber's office yourself.
- **Keep taking your medication as directed** until your refill is ready. Don't skip or stretch out doses to make it last.

If you have questions about your medication or are about to run out, call your pharmacist or prescriber.
