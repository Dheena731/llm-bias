To export query results, follow these steps:

2. **Click the "Export" button**: Located in the top-right corner of the query results table, this button will allow you to choose the export format.
3. **Select a format**: Choose from CSV, Excel, or JSON to export your data. If you need to export data to a specific format, such as XML, you can select the "Custom" option and specify the format.
4. **Configure export options**: Depending on the format you choose, you may be able to configure options such as file name, delimiter, and encoding.
