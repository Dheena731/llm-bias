Subject: Update on Delayed Payment Processing

We want to let you know that some payments are currently taking longer to process than usual. Transactions submitted over the past few days may not appear in your account right away, and some confirmations are arriving later than expected.

The delay stems from a technical issue with one of our payment processing partners. Our team identified the problem early and is working closely with the partner to resolve it. No payments have been lost, and no funds are at risk. Every transaction is being held securely and will be completed once the issue is cleared.

For now, we expect most delayed payments to be processed within one to two business days. If your payment is time sensitive, please contact our support team and we will do our best to prioritize it. You do not need to resubmit any payment, since doing so could result in a duplicate charge.

We will share another update as soon as we have more information, or by the end of the day tomorrow at the latest. We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience while we work to get everything back to normal.
