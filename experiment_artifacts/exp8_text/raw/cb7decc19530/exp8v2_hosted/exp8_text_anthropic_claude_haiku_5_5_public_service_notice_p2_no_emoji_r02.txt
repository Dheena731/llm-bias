**PUBLIC NOTICE: Road Closure**

**Location:** Maple Street, between 3rd Avenue and 5th Avenue

**Dates:** Monday, March 10 through Friday, March 14

**Hours:** 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily

**Reason:** Scheduled water main replacement and road resurfacing

Maple Street will be closed to all vehicle traffic during the dates and hours listed above. Local residents and businesses will have access to their properties via Oak Street and Pine Avenue. Signed detours will be posted at all affected intersections.

Please plan your travel accordingly and allow extra time when driving through the area. Pedestrian access will be maintained along the sidewalks on both sides of the street.

For questions or concerns, contact the Department of Public Works at (555) 123-4567 or visit www.cityexample.gov/roadwork.

Thank you for your patience as we work to improve local infrastructure.
