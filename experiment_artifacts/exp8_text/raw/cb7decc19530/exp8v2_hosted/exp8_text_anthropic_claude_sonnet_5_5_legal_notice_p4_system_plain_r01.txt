Notice Regarding Refund Eligibility

This notice sets out the terms under which refunds may be requested and granted in connection with purchases made from the Company. By completing a purchase, the purchaser acknowledges that they have read, understood, and agreed to the terms stated here.

A purchaser is eligible to request a refund only if the request is submitted in writing within thirty (30) calendar days of the original date of purchase, and only if the purchaser provides valid proof of payment. Requests received after this period will not be considered, except where applicable law requires otherwise. For physical goods, eligibility further requires that the item be returned in its original packaging and in unused, undamaged condition. For digital goods and services, eligibility is limited to cases in which the product has not been substantially accessed, downloaded, or used, or in which the product is materially defective and the Company has been given a reasonable opportunity to correct the defect.

The following are not eligible for refund: items marked as final sale, custom or personalized goods, gift cards, and services that have been fully performed at the purchaser's request. Shipping and handling charges, along with any transaction fees imposed by third-party payment processors, are non-refundable unless the refund is due to an error on the part of the Company.

Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within fourteen (14) business days of approval. The Company reserves the right to deny any request that it reasonably determines to be fraudulent, abusive, or inconsistent with this notice. Nothing in this notice limits any statutory rights that a purchaser may hold under applicable consumer protection laws, and in the event of a conflict, those laws shall prevail.

The Company may amend this notice at any time by posting the revised terms. Amendments apply only to purchases made after the effective date of the revision. Questions regarding this notice should be directed in writing to the Company's customer service department.
