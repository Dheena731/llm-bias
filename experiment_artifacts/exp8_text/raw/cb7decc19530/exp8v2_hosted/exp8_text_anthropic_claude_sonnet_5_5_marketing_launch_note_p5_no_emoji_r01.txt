**Launch Note: *Word by Word* Is Here**

Today we're releasing the first episodes of *Word by Word*, a podcast for people who want to learn a language without feeling like they're back in a classroom.

Each week, we take one everyday situation, such as ordering coffee, asking for directions, or making small talk with a neighbor, and walk through the words and phrases you'd actually use. Episodes run about fifteen minutes, so they fit into a commute, a walk, or a dishes-in-the-sink kind of evening.

Here's what you can expect:

- **Real conversations.** Native speakers, natural pacing, and no scripted stiffness.
- **Practical phrases first.** We start with what you'll say out loud, then explain the grammar behind it.
- **Gentle repetition.** Key phrases return throughout each episode so they stick.
- **Honest mistakes.** We talk about the errors learners commonly make, and we make a few ourselves.

The first three episodes are available now wherever you listen to podcasts. New episodes will arrive every Tuesday.

If you have a situation you'd like us to cover, or a language you'd love to see on the show, send us a message. We read everything, and your ideas will shape what comes next.

Thank you for listening. Press play, repeat after us, and let's get started.
