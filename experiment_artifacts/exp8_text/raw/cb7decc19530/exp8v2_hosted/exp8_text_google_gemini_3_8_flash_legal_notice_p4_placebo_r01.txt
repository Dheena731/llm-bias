**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY**

To be eligible for a refund, you must submit your request within thirty (30) calendar days of the original purchase date. 

To qualify:
1. The purchased item must be in its original, unused condition, with all original packaging and components intact.
2. You must provide a valid proof of purchase, such as the original sales receipt or order confirmation number.

Items marked as final sale, personalized goods, and downloadable digital content are not eligible for a refund. 

All approved refunds will be credited solely to the original method of payment used during the transaction. Shipping and handling fees are non-refundable.
