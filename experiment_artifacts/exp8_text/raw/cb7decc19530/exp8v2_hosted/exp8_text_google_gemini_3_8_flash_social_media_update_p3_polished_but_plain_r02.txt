Join us this Saturday for the annual Riverside Community Fair at Central Park from ten in the morning until three in the afternoon. The gathering will feature live music from local artists, a variety of food trucks, games for all ages, and stalls showcasing handcrafted goods from neighborhood vendors. Admission is free, and the event will take place rain or shine, moving into the park pavilion if the weather changes. Bring a lawn chair, invite your neighbors, and come enjoy a relaxing day together in the heart of our town.
