Library Opening Hours

Please note that the library's opening hours may vary depending on the day and time of year. During the academic year, the library is open Monday through Thursday from 8:30 a.m. to 11:00 p.m., Friday from 8:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., and Saturday and Sunday from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

During the summer months, the library is open Monday through Thursday from 8:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. and Friday from 8:30 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.

The library is closed on certain holidays and during final examination periods. It is always a good idea to check the library's website or call the main number for the most up-to-date information on opening hours.

We recommend planning your visit to the library in advance to ensure that it is open during the times you wish to use it.
