**Weekly Fitness Check-In** 💪

It's time to review your progress! Take a few minutes to:

- **Look back:** Which workouts did you complete this week?
- **Celebrate wins:** Note any improvements, big or small.
- **Spot challenges:** Identify what got in the way and how to adjust.
- **Plan ahead:** Set one or two clear goals for next week.

Consistency matters more than perfection. Keep going, you're building something great!
