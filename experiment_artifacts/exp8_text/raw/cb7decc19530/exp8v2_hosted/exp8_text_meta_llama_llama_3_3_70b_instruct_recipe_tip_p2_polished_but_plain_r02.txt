To keep fresh herbs fresh for a longer period, it is best to store them in a cool, dry place. Trim the stems of the herbs and place them in a glass of water, then cover the glass with a plastic bag to maintain humidity. You can also wrap the herbs in a damp paper towel and store them in an airtight container in the refrigerator. This will help to keep the herbs from drying out and losing their flavor. Regularly changing the water and checking on the herbs will also help to prevent spoilage and keep them fresh for a longer time.
