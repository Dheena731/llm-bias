PUBLIC NOTICE

Road Closure on Maple Street

The City of Riverton Department of Public Works will close Maple Street between 4th Avenue and 9th Avenue to all vehicle traffic beginning Monday, June 16, and continuing through Friday, June 20, from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. each day. The closure is needed for scheduled water main replacement work.

Local residents and businesses will retain access to their properties from either end of the closed section. Pedestrians will be able to use the sidewalks on both sides of the street, though some sidewalk sections may be temporarily narrowed.

Drivers are encouraged to use Oak Street or Pine Avenue as alternate routes. Signs will be posted at all affected intersections.

Questions or concerns may be directed to the Public Works office at (555) 214-7890 or by email at publicworks@rivertoncity.gov. Thank you for your patience while we complete these improvements.

Issued by the City of Riverton Department of Public Works
