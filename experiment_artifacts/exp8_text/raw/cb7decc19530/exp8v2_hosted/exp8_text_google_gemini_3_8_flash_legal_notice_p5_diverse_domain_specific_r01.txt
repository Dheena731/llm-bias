**NETWORK ACCESS DISCLOSURE AND CONDITIONAL USE COVENANT**

**1. CONDITIONAL GRANT OF ACCESS**  
Logical network connectivity is provisioned as an unmanaged, transient service. Transit across this local infrastructure (Layer 2 through Layer 7) is granted on a revocable, non-exclusive basis and is strictly contingent upon continuous adherence to these terms. Connection of any network interface controller (NIC) constitutes affirmative acceptance.

**2. PROHIBITED INFRASTRUCTURE INTERFERENCE**  
Users and connected hosts shall not execute any packet transmission, protocol manipulation, or daemon deployment that disrupts, observes, or alters the transport layer for other tenants. Strictly prohibited actions include, but are not limited to:
* Injection of unassigned addressing protocols (e.g., running rogue DHCP servers, IPv6 Router Advertisements, or ARP-poisoning attacks).
* Promiscuous packet capture, lateral subnet mapping, port scanning, or vulnerability enumeration targeting internal gateway interfaces or adjacent hosts.
* Operation of open relays, Tor exit nodes, unauthenticated proxies, or continuous peer-to-peer data swarms that exhaust upstream NAT state tables or evade designated quality-of-service (QoS) queues.
* Bypassing egress bandwidth caps or MAC-layer isolation mechanisms via spoofed frames or unauthorized 802.1Q tagging (VLAN hopping).

**3. MONITORING, TELEMETRY, AND PRIVACY SURRENDER**  
Users acknowledge that this network is actively managed. System administrators retain the right to collect, analyze, and retain Layer 2–4 metadata—including, without limitation, source/destination IP addresses, MAC identifiers, packet volumes, NetFlow/sFlow exports, and plaintext DNS queries—for capacity planning and abuse mitigation. While payload contents are not systematically inspected, this environment provides **no expectation of privacy** regarding connection metadata.

**4. REMEDIATION AND INDEMNIFICATION**  
The network operator reserves the unilateral right to terminate access, blackhole routes, or dynamically isolate switch ports/BSSIDs associated with anomalous traffic profiles, downstream copyright strikes (DMCA/statutory notices), or denial-of-service signatures, without prior notice or liability for resultant data loss. 

Subscribers and device owners shall fully indemnify the infrastructure owner against all administrative fines, carrier-level termination penalties, and statutory claims arising directly from transit generated by their provisioned hardware.
