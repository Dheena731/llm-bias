We're excited to announce the launch of a new savings goal feature, designed to help our users reach their financial objectives more effectively. This feature allows you to set specific, measurable goals, such as saving for a down payment on a house, a vacation, or a big purchase. Once you've set your goal, you can track your progress and receive personalized reminders and updates to help you stay on track. The feature also provides you with a clear picture of how much you need to save each month to reach your goal, making it easier to make informed decisions about your finances. With this new feature, you'll be able to take control of your savings and make steady progress towards achieving your financial goals.
