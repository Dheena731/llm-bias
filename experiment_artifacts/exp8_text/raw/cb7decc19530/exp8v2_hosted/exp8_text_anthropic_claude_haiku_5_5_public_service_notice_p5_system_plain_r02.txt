Polling Station Locations Notice

Voters are advised that polling station locations may have changed for this election. Please check your polling place before you go to vote, as your assigned station may differ from the one used in previous elections. You can confirm your polling location by contacting your local elections office or by visiting its official website, where a search tool is available using your registered address.

Polling stations will be open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on election day. Voters already standing in line at closing time will be allowed to cast their ballots. Please bring acceptable identification, and allow extra time for travel and parking, as some locations may have limited spaces.

Accessible entrances and assistance for voters with disabilities are available at all polling stations. If you need help getting to your polling place, please contact the elections office in advance so that arrangements can be made.

For more information, please contact your local elections office during business hours.
