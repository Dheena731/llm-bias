PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE ON MAPLE AVENUE

The City Department of Public Works advises residents, businesses, and motorists that Maple Avenue will be closed to all through traffic between Fourth Street and Linden Road from Monday, June 9, at 7:00 a.m. until Friday, June 20, at 6:00 p.m. The closure is necessary so that crews can replace an aging water main and repave the roadway. Work will take place on weekdays between 7:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m., and weather permitting, the schedule should hold. If rain causes delays, the closure may extend into the following week, and updates will be posted on the city website and at the work site.

During the closure, drivers should use Oak Street and Garrison Road as the marked detour. Expect additional travel time of about ten minutes during morning and evening peak hours. Local traffic will be permitted as far as the work zone allows, but residents on affected blocks should anticipate periods when driveway access is temporarily blocked. Crews will notify households in advance when this is expected, and every effort will be made to restore access each evening. Residents who need to move a vehicle before work begins are asked to do so by Sunday night.

Pedestrians and cyclists will be directed along the north sidewalk, which will remain open and protected by barriers. Bus Route 12 will be rerouted and will not serve stops on Maple Avenue during the closure. Riders may board at the temporary stops on Oak Street, which will be marked with signs. Emergency vehicles will have full access at all times, and trash and recycling collection will continue, though residents may be asked to bring bins to the nearest cross street.

Water service may be briefly interrupted on certain blocks. Affected customers will receive a door hanger at least 24 hours before any shutoff, which is expected to last no more than four hours. After service returns, running a cold tap for a few minutes will clear any discoloration from the lines.

The department appreciates the patience of everyone affected by this essential project. Questions or concerns may be directed to the Public Works office at 555-0142 on weekdays between 8:00 a.m. and 4:30 p.m., or by email to the project team through the city website.

Issued by the City Department of Public Works, June 2.
