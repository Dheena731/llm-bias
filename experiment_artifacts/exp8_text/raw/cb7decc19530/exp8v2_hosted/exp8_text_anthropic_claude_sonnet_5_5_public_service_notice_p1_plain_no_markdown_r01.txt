Notice of Revised Library Opening Hours

Beginning on the first Monday of next month, the Riverside Public Library will change its opening hours to better serve the community. The library will be open from nine in the morning until eight in the evening, Monday through Thursday. On Friday, doors will open at nine and close at five in the afternoon. Saturday hours will run from ten in the morning until four in the afternoon, and the library will remain closed on Sundays and on public holidays.

The reading room and computer area will follow the same schedule as the main library. The children's section will close one hour earlier than the rest of the building on weekdays so that staff can prepare for evening programs. Book returns may still be made at any time through the outdoor drop box, and items placed there will be processed on the next business day.

Visitors who need extra time to use study rooms or meeting spaces are encouraged to reserve them in advance at the front desk or by telephone. Staff will be glad to answer questions about the new schedule and to help patrons plan their visits.

We thank all library users for their patience and understanding during this transition. For further information, please speak with a member of staff or call the library during opening hours.
