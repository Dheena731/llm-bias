**Subject: Security Reminder: Do Not Reuse Passwords**

Team,

We are reminding everyone to avoid reusing passwords across work and personal accounts. When a password is reused, a breach at any other website can expose our systems to attackers, even if our own security is strong.

**Please follow these practices:**

- **Use a unique password** for every work account.
- **Use a password manager** approved by IT to create and store strong passwords.
- **Never reuse** a work password on a personal site, or a personal password here.
- **Turn on multi-factor authentication (MFA)** wherever it is available.
- **Report suspected breaches** or unusual account activity to the IT Service Desk immediately.

If you think a password you use at work may have appeared in a data breach, change it now and contact IT for help.

Thank you for helping keep our systems and data secure.

IT Security Team
