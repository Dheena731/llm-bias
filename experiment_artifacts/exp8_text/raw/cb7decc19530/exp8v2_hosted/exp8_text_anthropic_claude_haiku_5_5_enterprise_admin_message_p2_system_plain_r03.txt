Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starting Next Month

Hello team,

Beginning on the first of next month, we will be moving all expense submissions to a new approval workflow. Under the new process, every expense report must be submitted through the finance portal and will be routed automatically to your direct manager for review. Expenses over 500 dollars will also require sign-off from the finance department before reimbursement is issued.

To prepare, please make sure you have access to the finance portal by checking your login this week. If you cannot sign in, contact the IT help desk before the end of the month so your access can be resolved in time. Receipts should be uploaded as clear digital copies at the time of submission, as incomplete submissions will be returned to the employee.

A short training session will be held on Tuesday at 10 a.m. in the main conference room and will also be recorded for anyone who cannot attend. The finance team will be available afterward to answer questions by email at finance@company.example.

Thank you for your cooperation as we make this transition. We expect the new workflow to reduce processing delays and give everyone clearer visibility into the status of their claims.

Regards,
Operations Administration
