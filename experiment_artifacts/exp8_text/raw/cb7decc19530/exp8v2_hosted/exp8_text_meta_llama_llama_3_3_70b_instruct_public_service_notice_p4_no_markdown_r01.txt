A heat advisory has been issued for our area, effective immediately. High temperatures and humidity are expected to create dangerous heat indices, posing a significant risk to vulnerable populations such as the elderly, young children, and those with pre-existing medical conditions. Residents are advised to stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water, stay indoors during the hottest part of the day, and check on neighbors who may be at risk. Cooling centers are available at local community centers and libraries for those in need. Please take necessary precautions to stay safe during this heat advisory.
