**Security Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication**

Please take a moment to enable two-factor authentication on your account. 

Two-factor authentication is a security feature that requires two forms of verification to log in: your standard password and a second item, such as a temporary numeric code. This additional step helps ensure that only you can access your account, even if your password is compromised.

To activate this protection, go to your account settings, open the security section, and turn on two-factor authentication.
