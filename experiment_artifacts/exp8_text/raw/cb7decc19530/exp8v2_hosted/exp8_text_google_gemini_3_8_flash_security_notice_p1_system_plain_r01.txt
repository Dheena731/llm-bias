Please be reminded that reusing passwords across multiple accounts represents a serious risk to our organization. When an external website or personal service suffers a data breach, cybercriminals routinely use those stolen credentials to attempt unauthorized access to workplace networks and sensitive company data. To protect both corporate information and your personal accounts, you must ensure that your work password is completely unique and never shared with any other system, application, or personal profile. We strongly advise using the company-approved password manager to generate and securely store strong, distinct passwords for every service you use. If you suspect that your login credentials have been compromised, or if you notice any suspicious activity, please notify the IT security team immediately.
