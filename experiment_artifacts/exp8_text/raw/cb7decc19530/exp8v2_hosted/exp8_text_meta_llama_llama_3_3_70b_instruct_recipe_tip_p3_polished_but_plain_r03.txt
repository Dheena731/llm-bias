To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it thoroughly before cooking. This simple step can help remove excess starch from the surface of the grains, which is a major contributor to stickiness. After rinsing, make sure to drain the rice well and then cook it with the right amount of water. Using a non-stick pot or a rice cooker can also help prevent the rice from becoming sticky. Additionally, adding a small amount of oil or butter to the rice while it cooks can help separate the grains and make the rice fluffier. By following these tips, you can achieve perfectly cooked, non-sticky rice every time.
