PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE: LIBRARY OPENING HOURS

To ensure reliable access to our collections, computers, and quiet study spaces, please observe the following regular schedule:

Monday through Thursday: 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.  
Friday: 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.  
Saturday: 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.  
Sunday: Closed  

The library will also close on all recognized public holidays. For temporary adjustments due to severe weather or planned maintenance, please check our official website or call the front desk before your visit. Thank you for using our community resources.
