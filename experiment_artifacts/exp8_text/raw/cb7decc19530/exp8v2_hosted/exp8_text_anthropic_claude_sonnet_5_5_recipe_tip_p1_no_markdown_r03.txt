Cooking tip: Prep once, cook fast. When you get home, start by chopping all your vegetables and measuring out your seasonings before turning on the stove. Then, while a pot of water boils or a pan heats up, everything is ready to go in and nothing gets overcooked while you scramble to chop an onion. For an even bigger time saver, cut up extra vegetables on Sunday and store them in airtight containers. A sheet pan with chicken thighs, broccoli, and diced potatoes tossed in olive oil and salt can go from fridge to table in about 30 minutes, with almost no cleanup.
