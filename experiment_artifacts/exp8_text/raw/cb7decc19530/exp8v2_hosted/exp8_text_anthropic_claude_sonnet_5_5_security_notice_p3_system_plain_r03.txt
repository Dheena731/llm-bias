Reminder: Please Lock Your Screens

Dear colleagues,

This is a friendly reminder that everyone should lock their computer screens whenever they step away from their desks, even if it is only for a minute or two. An unlocked screen gives anyone nearby access to your files, emails, and the systems you are signed in to, and it can put confidential company and client information at risk.

Locking your screen takes only a second. On a Windows computer, press the Windows key and the letter L together. On a Mac, press Control, Command, and Q. If you use a laptop, please also lock it before leaving it in a meeting room or shared space.

Please also make sure your device is set to lock automatically after a short period of inactivity. If you need help with this setting, contact the IT help desk and someone will be glad to assist you.

Thank you for helping keep our information secure. Small habits like this make a real difference.

Kind regards,
The IT Security Team
