**Quick and Easy Chicken Fajitas**

For a delicious and stress-free weeknight dinner, try using pre-cooked chicken breast to make Chicken Fajitas. Simply thaw a 12 oz package of pre-cooked chicken breast, and then slice it into thin strips. Place the chicken strips in a hot skillet with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, and then add 1 medium-sized onion that has been sliced into thin rings. Cook the onion and chicken for 3-4 minutes, or until the onion is translucent and the chicken is heated through.

Next, add 1 packet of fajita seasoning, which can be found in the spice aisle of most grocery stores. Sprinkle the seasoning evenly over the chicken and onion mixture, and then cook for an additional 1-2 minutes. Serve the chicken and onion mixture hot, garnished with a sprinkle of shredded cheese and a dollop of sour cream, if desired.

**Tips for Busy Home Cooks:**

* Use pre-cooked chicken breast to save time on meal prep.
* Add a sprinkle of shredded cheese and a dollop of sour cream to add extra flavor and texture to the dish.
* If using a cast iron skillet, be sure to heat it over medium-high heat for 2-3 minutes before adding the chicken and onion mixture. This will ensure that the skillet is hot and the chicken cooks evenly.

I hope you enjoy this quick and easy recipe for Chicken Fajitas!
