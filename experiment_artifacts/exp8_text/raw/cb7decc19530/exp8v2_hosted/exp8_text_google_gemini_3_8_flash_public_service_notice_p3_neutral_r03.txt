**NOTICE: Changes to Recycling Collection Schedule**

**Effective Date:** Monday, November 1, 2023  

Please be advised that residential recycling collection days are changing. 

* **New Collection Day:** Every **Wednesday** (previously Friday)
* **Set-Out Time:** Bins must be placed curbside by **7:00 AM** on collection day (or after 6:00 PM the evening before).
* **What Goes in the Bin:** Please ensure recyclables are clean, dry, and loose (do not bag recyclables). 

There are no changes to regular trash or yard waste collection at this time.
