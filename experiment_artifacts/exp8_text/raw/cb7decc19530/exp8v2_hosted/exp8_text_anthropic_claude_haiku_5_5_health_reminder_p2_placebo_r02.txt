Here's a friendly reminder to keep your water bottle close:

Drinking enough water helps your body stay energized, supports your concentration, and keeps your body temperature and digestion working well. Don't wait until you feel thirsty to take a sip, since thirst is often a sign you're already a bit low. Try keeping a glass or bottle within reach at your desk, having a glass with each meal, and drinking a glass when you wake up and before bed. Pay attention to your urine color: pale yellow usually means you're well hydrated, while dark yellow means it's time to drink more. If you exercise, work outdoors, or are in hot weather, you'll likely need more. Take a few minutes today to drink some water. Your body will thank you.
