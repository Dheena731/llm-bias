**Rinse your rice before cooking.** Place the rice in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer and rinse it under cold water, swishing it with your hand, until the water runs mostly clear. This washes away the excess surface starch that causes grains to clump together. For best results, also use a measured water ratio (about 1.5 parts water to 1 part long-grain white rice), and once it's done cooking, let it rest covered for 10 minutes, then fluff it gently with a fork.
