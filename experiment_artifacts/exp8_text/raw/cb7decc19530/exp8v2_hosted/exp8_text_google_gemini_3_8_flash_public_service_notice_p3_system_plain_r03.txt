Please be advised that residential recycling collection schedules will change beginning Monday, October 14. Recycling will now be collected every other Wednesday instead of weekly on Fridays. Regular trash collection days will not be affected by this change. In addition, glass bottles and jars must now be placed in a separate bin rather than mixed in with paper, cardboard, and plastic items. Please ensure your bins are placed curbside by seven in the morning on your new collection day. If you need a separate glass recycling bin or have questions regarding your specific route, please contact the municipal public works office by telephone or visit the town hall service desk during regular business hours. Thank you for your patience and cooperation as we work to improve our community recycling services.
