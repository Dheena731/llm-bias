Your eyes, neck, and mind all work better when you step away from a screen now and then. Try the 20-20-20 rule: every twenty minutes, look at something about twenty feet away for at least twenty seconds. This gives the muscles that control focus a chance to relax, which can ease the strain that leads to dryness and headaches. Beyond your eyes, stand up and stretch, roll your shoulders, or walk to get a glass of water. Even a two-minute break can clear your head and make your next task feel more manageable. If you tend to lose track of time when you are absorbed in work, set a gentle timer as a reminder. Your future self, who feels less tired and more focused, will thank you for the pause.
