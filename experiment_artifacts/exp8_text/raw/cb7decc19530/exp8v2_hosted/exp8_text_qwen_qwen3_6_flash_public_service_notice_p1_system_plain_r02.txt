The public library would like to announce its regular weekly schedule for community access. The facility is open Monday through Friday from nine o'clock in the morning until eight o'clock in the evening. On Saturdays, the library operates from ten o'clock in the morning until five o'clock in the evening. The building is closed on Sundays and recognized public holidays. Visitors are asked to plan their trips accordingly and allow enough time to complete transactions before the doors lock. Staff members are available at the information desk to answer questions about specific services or exceptions to this schedule. We appreciate your patronage and look forward to welcoming you during these hours.
