Software updates matter because they keep your devices and data secure, stable, and useful. Developers regularly release updates to fix security vulnerabilities that attackers could otherwise exploit to steal information or take control of a system. Updates also correct bugs that cause crashes, data loss, or malfunctions, and they improve compatibility with other programs, hardware, and online services. In addition, updates can add new features and performance improvements. Skipping updates leaves known weaknesses open, so keeping software current is one of the simplest and most effective ways to protect yourself and your data.
