Rinse your rice in cold water before cooking it. Put the rice in a bowl or fine mesh strainer, run water over it, and swish it around with your fingers until the water goes from milky to mostly clear, which usually takes three or four changes of water. This washes away the loose surface starch that makes grains clump together. After rinsing, drain the rice well and use a little less water than you might otherwise, about one and a half parts water to one part long grain rice. Once it finishes cooking, let it rest covered for five to ten minutes off the heat, then fluff it gently with a fork instead of stirring, which can break the grains and release more starch.
