**Heat Advisory: Urban Agriculture Community**

A heat advisory has been issued for the urban agriculture community, effective immediately. With temperatures expected to reach 105°F (40°C) and high humidity, the City's Department of Sustainability and the Urban Agriculture Task Force urge all gardeners, farmers, and community members to take necessary precautions.

**Key precautions:**

*   **Watering schedules:** Adjust your watering schedules to early morning or evening hours to minimize evaporation and prevent scorching.
*   **Crop protection:** Use shade cloths or provide temporary shading for sensitive crops to prevent heat stress.
*   **Personal safety:** Take regular breaks in cool, shaded areas, and stay hydrated with plenty of water.
*   **Community support:** Check on your neighbors, especially the elderly and young children, to ensure they're staying safe in the heat.

**Additional resources:**

*   Visit our website for up-to-date weather forecasts and heat advisory information.
*   Contact our office for guidance on heat stress mitigation techniques for specific crops.
*   Join our social media groups for real-time updates and advice from experienced urban gardeners.

Stay safe and healthy, and let's work together to protect our community's gardens and green spaces.
