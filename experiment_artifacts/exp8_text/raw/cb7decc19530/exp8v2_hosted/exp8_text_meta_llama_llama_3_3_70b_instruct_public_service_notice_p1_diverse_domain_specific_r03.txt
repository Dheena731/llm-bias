Attention book lovers and researchers!

As we settle into the new semester, don't forget to plan your study sessions and literary escapes around our library's opening hours. Our doors are open Monday to Thursday from 8am to 9pm, Friday from 8am to 6pm, and Saturday and Sunday from 10am to 5pm.

Please note that our hours may vary during public holidays and university breaks, so be sure to check our website or social media channels for updates before heading in. We also offer extended hours during exam periods, because we know you need us most when deadlines are looming.

Remember, our libraries are more than just a collection of books – they're community hubs, quiet sanctuaries, and collaborative workspaces. Whether you're a student, researcher, or simply a bookworm, we invite you to make the most of our resources and services. So mark those calendars, grab your library card, and come on in!
