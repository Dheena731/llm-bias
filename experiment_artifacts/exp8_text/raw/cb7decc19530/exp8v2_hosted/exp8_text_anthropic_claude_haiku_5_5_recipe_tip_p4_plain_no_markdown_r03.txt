Batch-cook one versatile base on the weekend, such as a pot of grains, a tray of roasted vegetables, or a large batch of shredded chicken, and then mix and match it across the week. Store each portion in its own container so you can grab one on the way out the door. Pairing the same base with different sauces, toppings, or seasonings keeps lunches from feeling repetitive while cutting your weekday prep time to just a few minutes.
