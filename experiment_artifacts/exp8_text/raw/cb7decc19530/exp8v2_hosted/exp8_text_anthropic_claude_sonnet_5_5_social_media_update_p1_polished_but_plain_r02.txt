We've just launched Smart Reminders, a new way to keep your work on track without the constant checking. Set a task once, tell us when it matters, and we'll nudge you at the right moment through your preferred channel. You can also choose to repeat reminders daily, weekly, or on a custom schedule, so recurring tasks never slip through the cracks.

We built this feature after hearing from many of you that staying organized shouldn't take more effort than the work itself. Smart Reminders is available now for all users, and you'll find it in the task menu the next time you open the app. Give it a try and tell us what you think. Your feedback shapes what we build next.
