As part of our ongoing effort to maintain the security and integrity of our systems, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews. These reviews are an essential component of our overall risk management strategy, ensuring that all users have the appropriate level of access to our systems and data. During the review process, we will verify that each user's access is still necessary and aligns with their current role and responsibilities. Users who no longer require access will have their privileges removed, and those who need additional access will be granted the necessary permissions. The reviews will be conducted on a regular schedule, with the next review taking place at the start of the upcoming quarter. If you have any questions or concerns about the access review process, please do not hesitate to reach out to the administration team.
