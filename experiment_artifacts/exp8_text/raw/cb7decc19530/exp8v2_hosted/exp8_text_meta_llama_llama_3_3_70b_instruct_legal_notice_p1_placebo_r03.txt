NOTICE OF CHANGES TO ACCOUNT TERMS

Effective as of January 1, 2024, we are making changes to the terms and conditions that govern your account with our institution. The changes include an update to our fee structure, which will result in a $5.00 monthly maintenance fee for accounts with balances below $1,000.00. Additionally, we are revising our overdraft policy to allow for up to two courtesy overdrafts per calendar year, with a maximum overdraft fee of $30.00 per occurrence.

These changes will apply to all existing and new accounts, and it is your responsibility to review and understand the updated terms and conditions. You can find the revised terms and conditions on our website or by requesting a copy from our customer service department.

By continuing to use your account after the effective date, you will be deemed to have accepted the updated terms and conditions. If you do not agree to the changes, you may close your account prior to the effective date to avoid being subject to the new terms.

Please direct any questions or concerns regarding these changes to our customer service department.
