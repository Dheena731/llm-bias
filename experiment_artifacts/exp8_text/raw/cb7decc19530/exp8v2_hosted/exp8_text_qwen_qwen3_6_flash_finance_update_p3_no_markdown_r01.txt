We are excited to introduce our new Savings Goal feature, built to help you track and achieve your financial targets with greater clarity. Simply choose a goal name, set your target amount, and pick a timeline for objectives like vacations, emergencies, or down payments. The update adds a visual progress bar, smart contribution reminders, and weekly updates delivered straight to your dashboard. To get started, navigate to the Goals section in your account and tap Create New Goal. Your progress will sync automatically across all devices, making it easier than ever to stay on track. Thank you for being part of our community, and we wish you success as you work toward every milestone.
