We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you keep track of your finances with greater clarity. Starting this month, you will receive an automatic overview at the end of each cycle that breaks down your expenses by category, compares your current habits to previous months, and highlights your largest purchases. This feature is designed to simplify your budgeting process, allowing you to easily see where your money goes without having to calculate individual transactions manually. You can view your latest summary directly in your account dashboard and adjust your notification preferences at any time.
