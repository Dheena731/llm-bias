Starting this month, your account will include a monthly spending summary that gives you a clear picture of where your money went over the previous billing period. The summary arrives in the first week of each month and covers all transactions posted to your account during the prior month, including card purchases, recurring subscriptions, bill payments, and fees. Rather than requiring you to scroll through individual transactions, it groups spending into everyday categories such as dining, groceries, transportation, housing, and entertainment, and shows how each category compares to the month before.

The summary opens with a short overview: your total spending for the month, your total deposits, and the net difference between the two. Below that, you will find a breakdown of your largest categories, along with the three merchants where you spent the most. If a category rose noticeably compared with the previous month, the summary flags it so you can review those transactions quickly. Recurring charges that you may have forgotten about are listed separately, which can help you spot subscriptions you no longer use.

You can view the summary in the app under the Statements tab, and you can choose to receive a notification when each new report is ready. Categories are assigned automatically based on merchant information. If a transaction lands in the wrong group, you can recategorize it, and future purchases from the same merchant will follow your choice. Corrections you make will also be reflected in the next month's comparison.

No action is required to start receiving the summary. If you would prefer not to see it, you can turn it off in your notification settings at any time without affecting your account or your statements. We hope the summary makes it easier to understand your spending habits and plan for the months ahead.
