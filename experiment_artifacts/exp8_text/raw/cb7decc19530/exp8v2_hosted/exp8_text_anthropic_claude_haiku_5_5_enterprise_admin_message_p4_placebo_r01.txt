**Subject: Quarterly Access Reviews**

**Audience:** Administrators responsible for system or application access

Quarterly access reviews are required for every system you administer. Please check that each user's access is still needed for their current role.

**What to do:**

1. Review the list of users and their access rights for each system you manage.
2. Confirm access that is still needed.
3. Remove access that is no longer needed, including access held by people who have changed roles or left the organization.
4. Return your completed review by the deadline stated in the review request.

Access that is not reviewed regularly can remain in place after it is no longer needed, which increases the risk of unauthorized use of company data.

If you have questions about your review, contact the team that sent the review request.
