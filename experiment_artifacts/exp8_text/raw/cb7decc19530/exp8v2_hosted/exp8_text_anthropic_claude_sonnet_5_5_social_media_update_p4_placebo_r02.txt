📅 **Calendar tip:** Stop using your calendar only for meetings. Block time for your tasks too.

When you have a task to finish, create an event for it. Give it a clear title (like "Draft project report") and set it for a specific time. Your calendar then shows how much time you actually have, and you're less likely to overbook your day.

Try it for one week and see whether your to-do list gets shorter.
