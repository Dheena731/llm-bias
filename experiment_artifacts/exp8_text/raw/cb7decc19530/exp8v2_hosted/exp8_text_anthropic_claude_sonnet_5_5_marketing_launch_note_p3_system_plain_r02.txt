Introducing the Clear Month Budget Planner, a spreadsheet template built for people who want to understand their money without wrestling with formulas. It works in both Excel and Google Sheets, and you can start using it within minutes of opening it.

The planner is organized around a single monthly view. You enter your income, list your fixed bills and flexible spending, and set aside amounts for savings and debt payments. As you record purchases throughout the month, the template automatically updates your remaining balance in each category, so you always know how much room is left before you overspend. Simple color cues shift from green to yellow to red as you approach a limit, which makes it easy to check your progress at a glance.

Beyond the monthly view, the template includes a yearly summary that gathers your totals into a clear picture of where your money goes. A savings tracker lets you set goals, such as an emergency fund or a vacation, and watch your progress over time. Every formula is already in place and unlocked, so you can rename categories, add new ones, or adjust the layout to fit your life.

The Clear Month Budget Planner is available today as a one-time purchase with no subscriptions and no accounts to create. Your data stays in your own file, under your control. If you have been meaning to get a handle on your finances, this is a straightforward place to begin.
