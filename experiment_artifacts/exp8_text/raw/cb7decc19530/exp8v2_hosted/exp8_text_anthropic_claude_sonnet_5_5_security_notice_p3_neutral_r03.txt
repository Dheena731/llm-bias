**Reminder: Lock Your Screen**

Dear Team,

Please lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if it's just for a moment.

**How to lock your screen:**
- **Windows:** Press **Windows key + L**
- **Mac:** Press **Control + Command + Q**

Unlocked screens put confidential company, client, and personal information at risk. Locking your screen takes only a second and helps keep our data secure.

Thank you for doing your part.

*IT Security Team*
