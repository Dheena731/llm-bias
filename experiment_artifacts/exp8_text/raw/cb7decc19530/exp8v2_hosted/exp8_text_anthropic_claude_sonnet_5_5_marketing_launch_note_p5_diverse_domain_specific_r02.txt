**Ho Capito Male: first three episodes are out**

Ho Capito Male is a podcast for people who can order dinner in Italian but lose the thread when two Italians talk to each other.

Each episode is about 18 minutes. A native speaker tells a short true story, like the time a landlord in Bologna returned a deposit in coins. You hear it once at normal speed, with no slowing down and no tidying up. Then we replay it in chunks and look at where learners usually get lost: *l'ho* swallowed into *lo*, *che* doing four jobs in one sentence, the quiet *ci* that changes the meaning of a verb.

Nothing is dubbed into English, but the show notes include a full transcript with the trouble spots marked. We also list the five or six phrases worth stealing, so you can pull them into Anki if you want.

The target level is roughly B1 to B2, the stretch where textbook Italian stops helping and real speech sounds like one long word.

Episodes 1 to 3 are live now, and a new one comes out every other Thursday. If you misheard something we didn't cover, send it in. The best mistakes become episodes.
