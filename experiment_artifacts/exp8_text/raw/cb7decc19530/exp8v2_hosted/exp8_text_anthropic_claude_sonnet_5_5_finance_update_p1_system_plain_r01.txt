Starting this month, you will receive a monthly spending summary at the beginning of each month. It gives you a clear picture of where your money went during the previous month, so you can spot patterns and make adjustments without digging through individual transactions.

The summary shows your total spending for the month and breaks it down by category, such as groceries, transportation, utilities, dining, and entertainment. It also compares your totals with the previous month, so you can see at a glance which areas went up and which went down. If a category changed noticeably, we point it out so it does not slip by unnoticed.

We have also included a short section on recurring payments, including subscriptions and automatic bills. Many people find that small charges add up over time, and seeing them together makes it easier to decide which ones are still worth keeping.

The summary is built from the transactions already in your account, so there is nothing you need to set up. If a purchase has been placed in the wrong category, you can correct it, and the change will be reflected in future summaries. You can also choose whether to receive the summary by email, in the app, or both through your notification settings.

We designed this feature to be simple and useful rather than overwhelming, and we plan to refine it based on your feedback. If you have suggestions or notice anything that looks off, please let us know through the feedback option in your account.
