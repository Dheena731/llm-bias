Here are a few options, depending on where you are posting it (Email vs. Slack/Teams):

### Option 1: Standard Email (Clear & Action-Oriented)

**Subject:** Action Required: Q[X] User Access Review Due by [Date]

Hi Team,

To maintain our security standards and ensure compliance, we are kicking off our **Quarterly Access Review for Q[X]**. 

**What you need to do:**
* **Managers & System Owners:** Please review the access permissions for your direct reports and systems to ensure they align with the principle of least privilege.
* **Action:** Go to **[Link to Portal/Sheet]** to approve, modify, or revoke access.

**Deadline:** Please complete your review by **[Day, Date at Time]**. 

*Note: Unverified accounts or access rights not confirmed by the deadline may be subject to temporary deactivation.*

If you have any questions or encounter issues, please reach out to **[IT/Security Contact]**.

Thank you for helping keep our systems secure!

Best regards,  
**[Your Name/Team]**

***

### Option 2: Short / Chat Announcement (Slack or Teams)

🔒 **Reminder: Q[X] Quarterly Access Reviews** 🔒

It’s time for our quarterly access audit! 

* **Who:** All People Managers and System Owners
* **Action:** Review your team's tool and system permissions at **[Link]** and flag any access that is no longer needed.
* **Deadline:** **[Date]**

Keeping permissions up to date protects our data and keeps us compliant. Please reach out in **[#help-channel]** if you have questions!
