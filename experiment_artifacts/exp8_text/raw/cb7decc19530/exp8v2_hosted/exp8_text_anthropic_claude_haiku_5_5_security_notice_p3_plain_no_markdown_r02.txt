Subject: Please Lock Your Screen When Away From Your Desk

Dear colleagues,

This is a friendly reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even for a short break or a quick conversation down the hall. An unattended, unlocked screen can give someone access to confidential client information, internal documents, and company systems in a matter of seconds.

Locking your screen takes only a moment. On most computers, you can press the Windows key and L together, or Control, Command, and Q on a Mac, followed by the appropriate shortcut for your system. If you are unsure how to set up an automatic lock after a period of inactivity, please contact the IT help desk and they will be glad to assist.

Thank you for helping us keep our information secure. Your attention to this small habit makes a real difference to all of us.

Regards,

The IT Security Team
