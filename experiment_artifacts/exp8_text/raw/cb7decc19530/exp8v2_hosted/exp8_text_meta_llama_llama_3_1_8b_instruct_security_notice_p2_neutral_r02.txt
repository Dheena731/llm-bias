Here is a short workplace notice about phishing emails:

**Important Notice to All Employees**

**Protecting Our Organization from Phishing Scams**

As we continue to rely on technology to communicate and conduct business, it's essential that we remain vigilant in protecting ourselves and our organization from phishing scams.

**What is Phishing?**

Phishing is a type of online scam where an email, text message, or phone call is used to trick individuals into revealing sensitive information or performing a specific action. Phishing emails often appear to be from a legitimate source, but are actually designed to deceive and exploit.

**Warning Signs of Phishing Emails**

* Emails that ask you to verify sensitive information, such as login credentials or financial details
* Emails that contain spelling or grammatical errors
* Emails that create a sense of urgency or panic
* Emails that ask you to click on a link or download an attachment from an unknown source

**What to Do If You Receive a Phishing Email**

* Do not respond to the email or click on any links
* Do not provide sensitive information
* Report the email to your supervisor or IT department immediately
* Delete the email to prevent others from falling victim

**Protecting Our Organization**

* All employees are required to report any suspicious emails or activity to the IT department immediately
* Our organization will never ask you to provide sensitive information via email
* We will continue to provide training and education on how to identify and avoid phishing scams

**Let's Work Together to Stay Safe**

If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or the IT department. Let's all do our part to protect our organization from phishing scams.

Thank you for your attention to this important matter.
