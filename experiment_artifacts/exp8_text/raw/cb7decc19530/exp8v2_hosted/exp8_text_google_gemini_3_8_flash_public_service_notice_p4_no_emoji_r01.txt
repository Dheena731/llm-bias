**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

**Effective Period:** Thursday, July 18, through Saturday, July 20, from 11:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily.

The National Weather Service has issued a Heat Advisory for our area. Dangerously high temperatures and humidity are expected, with heat index values forecast to reach between 100°F and 105°F. These conditions significantly increase the risk of heat-related illnesses, such as heat exhaustion and heat stroke.

**Recommended Precautions:**
* **Stay Hydrated:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and high-sugar beverages.
* **Limit Sun Exposure:** Reschedule strenuous outdoor activities to early morning or late evening hours. Stay in air-conditioned environments as much as possible.
* **Never Leave Anyone in a Parked Car:** Do not leave children, vulnerable adults, or pets unattended in vehicles for any amount of time.
* **Check on Others:** Monitor elderly family members, neighbors, and those with underlying health conditions.
* **Know the Signs:** Symptoms of heat exhaustion include heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, nausea, and headache. If someone shows signs of heat stroke (high body temperature, confusion, loss of consciousness), call 911 immediately.

Designated cooling centers are open at the public library and community center from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM. For assistance or more information, please call 311 or visit the city municipal website.
