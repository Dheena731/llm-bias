You are permitted to use the Network for legitimate and lawful purposes only. This includes accessing and sharing information, communicating with others, and using authorized software and applications.

You are not permitted to use the Network for any purpose that is unlawful, harassing, or disruptive to others. You are responsible for ensuring that your use of the Network complies with all applicable laws and regulations, as well as any policies and guidelines established by the Network administrators.

By using the Network, you acknowledge that you have read, understand, and agree to comply with this Notice of Acceptable Use. Failure to comply may result in termination of your access to the Network and other disciplinary actions.

Please report any suspected misuse of the Network to the Network administrators immediately.
