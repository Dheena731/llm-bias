**ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY FOR SHARED NETWORK RESOURCES**

**1. Purpose and Scope**
This notice governs the use of the shared network and all associated systems, devices, accounts, and data ("Network"). It applies to all users, including employees, contractors, students, and guests, who access the Network by any means.

**2. Authorized Use**
Access to the Network is granted solely for lawful, legitimate purposes related to the user's role or authorized activities. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with Network performance, the work of others, or compliance with this notice.

**3. Prohibited Conduct**
Users shall not:
(a) access, attempt to access, or share resources, accounts, or data without authorization;
(b) introduce malicious software, or intentionally disrupt, degrade, or circumvent Network security controls;
(c) use the Network to transmit unlawful, harassing, defamatory, or obscene material;
(d) consume bandwidth or resources in a manner that unreasonably impairs others' access;
(e) share login credentials or permit others to use their accounts; or
(f) use the Network for commercial activity, cryptocurrency mining, or other purposes not authorized in writing.

**4. Security Responsibilities**
Users must safeguard their credentials, promptly report suspected security incidents or unauthorized access to the designated administrator, and comply with all installed security measures.

**5. Monitoring and Privacy**
Users acknowledge that the Network is a shared resource. The administrator may monitor, log, and inspect network activity and stored data to maintain security, enforce this notice, and comply with applicable law. Users should have no expectation of privacy in their use of the Network to the extent permitted by law.

**6. Enforcement**
Violations of this notice may result in suspension or revocation of access, disciplinary action, and referral to law enforcement or other authorities where appropriate. The administrator reserves the right to remove content or restrict access that poses a risk to the Network.

**7. Disclaimer and Limitation of Liability**
The Network is provided "as is." The administrator makes no warranty of uninterrupted or error-free service and shall not be liable for any loss of data or damages arising from use of the Network, except as required by law.

**8. Acknowledgment and Amendments**
Continued use of the Network constitutes acceptance of this notice. The administrator may amend this notice at any time, with notice to users posted on the Network or distributed by electronic means.
