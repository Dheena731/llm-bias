Employees who have misplaced a company laptop must report the incident to the Information Technology Service Desk without delay. Immediate notification allows the team to initiate remote security protocols to protect sensitive data. When contacting support, please be prepared to provide your full name, employee identification number, the laptop asset tag located on the bottom of the device, and the approximate time and location where the loss occurred. If you suspect theft, please also file a police report and reference the case number when speaking with IT. Prompt action helps mitigate risk and facilitates the rapid issuance of a replacement unit.
