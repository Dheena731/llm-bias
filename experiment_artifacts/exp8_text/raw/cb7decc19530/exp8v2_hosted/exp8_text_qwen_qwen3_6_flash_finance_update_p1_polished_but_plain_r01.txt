We are pleased to introduce the new monthly spending summary, designed to provide clearer insight into your financial habits. This feature compiles your transaction data into a concise report that highlights total expenditure, spending categories, and month-over-month trends. By consolidating this information, you can quickly identify patterns and manage your budget with greater confidence. The summary is generated automatically at the close of each billing cycle and is accessible directly from your account dashboard. We encourage you to review the first report upon its release to ensure all transactions are accurately categorized and to make the most of these enhanced tracking tools.
