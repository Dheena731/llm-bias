Voters should note that polling stations are located at designated community buildings and public schools within each district. Specific addresses and operating hours are provided in your official voter information packet. Residents are encouraged to verify their assigned location online using their address prior to casting a ballot. On election day, please arrive between seven in the morning and eight in the evening with a current government issued photo identification and proof of residence. Early voting options may also be available at select county offices starting two weeks before the election. For questions regarding your polling place or to request an absentee ballot, contact the local election office directly. We urge all eligible citizens to participate and help shape the future of our community.
