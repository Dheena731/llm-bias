Public Service Notice: Library Opening Hours

The Riverside Public Library has updated its hours of operation, effective Monday, March 3. The main branch will now open at 9:00 a.m. on Mondays through Thursdays and remain open until 8:00 p.m. On Fridays and Saturdays, the library will open at 10:00 a.m. and close at 6:00 p.m. The library will be closed on Sundays. Holiday hours may differ, so please check the library's website or call the front desk before your visit.

Books may still be returned at any time through the outdoor drop box, which is available around the clock. Patrons who wish to renew materials, place holds, or access digital collections can do so online at any hour.

Questions about these changes can be directed to the reference desk during regular business hours. The library thanks the community for its continued support and looks forward to welcoming you.
