**SECURITY NOTICE: Stay Vigilant Against Phishing Emails**

**To:** All Staff  
**From:** IT Security Team  
**Date:** [Insert Date]  

We have recently seen an increase in fraudulent "phishing" emails attempting to compromise employee accounts and company data. Please review the following tips to help keep our network secure:

**Common Red Flags:**
* **False Urgency:** Demands for immediate action (e.g., "Account suspended," "Urgent wire transfer," or "Password expiring today").
* **Suspicious Senders:** Display names that look familiar, but with an unfamiliar or misspelled email address (e.g., *john.doe@company-support.com* instead of *@company.com*).
* **Unexpected Links/Attachments:** Prompts to download unusual files or log in via an external link.

**What You Should Do:**
* **Think before you click:** Never open attachments or click links in unsolicited emails.
* **Verify:** If an email seems off, contact the sender through a separate, trusted channel (like Slack or a phone call) before responding.
* **Report:** If you suspect an email is phishing, do not forward it to colleagues. Use the **[Report Phishing]** button in your email client or forward it directly to **[itsecurity@company.com]**.

When in doubt, report it. Thank you for doing your part to keep our workplace secure!
