All registered voters should verify their designated polling location before heading to the polls. Your assigned voting site depends on your current residential address and may differ from previous elections. To find your exact location, visit the official election commission website or call the voter assistance hotline printed on your registration confirmation. Polls will remain open from seven in the morning until eight in the evening on election day. Please bring a valid government issued photo identification along with your voter registration card. Every designated station provides wheelchair access and multilingual ballots upon request. Should you encounter problems with signage or facility access, contact the local elections office immediately during regular business hours. Map your travel route in advance and leave early to account for traffic conditions so that you may complete your vote efficiently.
