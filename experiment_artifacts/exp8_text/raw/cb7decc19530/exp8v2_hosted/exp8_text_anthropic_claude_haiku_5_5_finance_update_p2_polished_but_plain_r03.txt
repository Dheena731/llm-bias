Dear Cardholder,

We are writing to let you know about a change to the fees applied to card transactions made in foreign currencies. Beginning on March 1, the foreign transaction fee on your card will increase from 2.5 percent to 2.9 percent of the transaction amount. The fee applies when you make a purchase or withdraw cash in a currency other than your account's base currency, and it is charged in addition to any exchange rate margin set by the card network.

The change does not affect purchases made in your home currency, and there are no changes to annual fees, interest rates, or credit limits. Transactions completed before March 1 will be charged at the previous rate, even if they post to your account after that date.

The increase reflects higher costs in processing international payments and in maintaining currency conversion services across the networks we use. We understand that any increase in fees is unwelcome, and we have kept this adjustment as modest as we could while keeping our cross-border services sustainable.

If you have questions about how this change affects your account, you can contact customer service at the number on the back of your card, or review the updated fee schedule in your online account settings. If you would prefer not to accept the new terms, you may close your account at any time before the effective date without penalty.

Thank you for your continued trust in our services.

Sincerely,
The Card Services Team
