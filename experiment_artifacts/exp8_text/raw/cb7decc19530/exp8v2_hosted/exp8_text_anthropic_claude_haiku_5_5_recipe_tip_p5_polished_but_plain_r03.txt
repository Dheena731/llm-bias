Season roasted vegetables before they go into the oven, not after. Toss them in a bowl with a measured amount of oil, then add salt and any dry spices so the seasoning clings evenly while the surfaces are still slightly damp from the oil. Salt draws out a little moisture, which helps the edges caramelize, and it penetrates the flesh better when given a few minutes to sit. Save delicate fresh herbs and finishing acids like lemon juice or vinegar for after roasting, since heat dulls their flavor and can make them bitter. A light sprinkle of flaky salt or a squeeze of citrus at the table brightens the whole tray in a way the oven cannot.
