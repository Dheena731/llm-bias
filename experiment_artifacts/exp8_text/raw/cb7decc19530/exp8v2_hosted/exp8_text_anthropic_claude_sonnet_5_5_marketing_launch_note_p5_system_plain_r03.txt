Today we are launching Word by Word, a podcast for people who want to learn a language without feeling like they are back in a classroom.

Each episode runs about fifteen minutes and is built around a single everyday situation: ordering coffee, asking for directions, making small talk with a neighbor, or apologizing when you have said the wrong thing. We walk through the phrases you actually need, explain why native speakers say them the way they do, and give you a chance to practice out loud before the episode ends. There is no homework and no pressure to memorize anything. The goal is to help you sound a little more natural each time you listen.

We are starting with Spanish, French, and Japanese, and we plan to add more languages based on what listeners ask for. Every episode includes a short story, a few cultural notes, and a slow-speed replay of the key phrases so you can follow along on a commute or a walk.

The first three episodes are available now wherever you get your podcasts, and new ones will arrive every week. If you try it, we would love to hear what is working and what you would like us to cover next. Learning a language is a long road, and we are glad to be walking part of it with you.
