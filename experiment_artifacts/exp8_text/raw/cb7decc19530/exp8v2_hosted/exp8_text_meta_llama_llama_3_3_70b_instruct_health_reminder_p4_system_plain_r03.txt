It's easy to get caught up in work or entertainment and spend hours staring at a screen without taking a break. Remember to look away from your computer, phone, or tablet every now and then to rest your eyes and stretch your body. Taking short breaks can help reduce eye strain and improve your overall comfort and focus. Try to get up and move around every hour or so, and consider setting a reminder to help you stay on track. Even a few minutes away from the screen can make a big difference in how you feel.
