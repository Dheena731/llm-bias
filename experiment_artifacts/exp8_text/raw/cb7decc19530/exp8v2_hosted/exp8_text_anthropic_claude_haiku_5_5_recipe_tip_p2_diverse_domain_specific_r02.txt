Wrap the stems of soft herbs like cilantro, parsley, and basil in a damp paper towel, then slide them into a zip-top bag with the air pressed out. Basil is the exception: keep its stems in a glass of water on the counter, out of direct sun, and it will stay perky for a week. Tender herbs hate cold, so the fridge's back wall, not the crisper drawer, is the right spot for the bag. Rinse and dry herbs only right before you use them, since trapped moisture is what turns parsley slimy.
