To keep fresh herbs like basil, rosemary, and parsley fresh for longer, store them in a glass of water on the counter or in the fridge, just like you would with flowers. Simply trim the stems, place them in a glass or jar with about an inch of water, and cover the top with a plastic bag. This will help keep the herbs hydrated and prevent them from wilting, allowing you to enjoy them for up to a week. You can also store them in the fridge to prolong their freshness even further!
