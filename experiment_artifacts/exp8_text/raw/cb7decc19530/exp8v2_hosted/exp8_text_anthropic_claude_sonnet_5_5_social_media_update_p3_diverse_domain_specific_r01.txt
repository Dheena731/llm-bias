**Saturday: Marlow Creek cleanup, 8:30 to noon**

Meet at the footbridge at the end of Alder St. Water's low after the dry week, so we can finally reach the stretch below the culvert where the shopping cart has been sitting since March.

We have grabbers, buckets, and gloves in most sizes. Bring boots you're fine ruining. If you own waders, bring them, because we're short.

Last month, 11 of us pulled 340 lbs out of the creek, mostly tires, plastic sheeting, and one very committed patio umbrella. We'd like to beat that, but mostly we'd like to see the bank by the sycamores again.

Kids are welcome on the dry side with the sorting crew. Dogs too, on leash. Coffee and a box of day-old rolls from Henley's will be on the tailgate.

Rain moves us to Sunday, same time. We'll post here by 7 a.m. Saturday if that happens.
