**Notice of Acceptable Use of the Shared Network**

**1. Scope.** This notice applies to every person who connects to or uses the shared network (the "Network"), including devices owned by individuals or organizations. By using the Network, you agree to follow this notice.

**2. Permitted use.** The Network may be used for lawful activities connected to its intended purpose, such as communication, research, and access to shared resources, in a manner that does not interfere with other users.

**3. Prohibited use.** You must not use the Network to:

(a) break any applicable law;
(b) access accounts, files, or systems you are not authorized to use;
(c) distribute malicious software or attempt to disrupt, damage, or overload the Network or any connected system;
(d) send unsolicited bulk messages or harass, threaten, or defame others;
(e) infringe the copyright or other intellectual property rights of any person; or
(f) share your login credentials or allow another person to use your access without authorization.

**4. Monitoring.** To protect the Network and its users, the Network operator may monitor, log, and review traffic and activity on the Network. Users should not expect privacy in their use of the Network, except as required by law.

**5. Enforcement.** The Network operator may suspend or terminate a user's access, remove content, or report unlawful activity to the appropriate authorities. Violation of this notice may also result in other legal consequences.

**6. Responsibility.** You are responsible for all activity carried out through your access and devices. Report any suspected misuse or security problem to the Network operator promptly.

**7. Changes.** The Network operator may update this notice. Continued use of the Network after an update means you accept the updated notice.

**8. Acknowledgment.** If you do not agree to this notice, you must stop using the Network.
