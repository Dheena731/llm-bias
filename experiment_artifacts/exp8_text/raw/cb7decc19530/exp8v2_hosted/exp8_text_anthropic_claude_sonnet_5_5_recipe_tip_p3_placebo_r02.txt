**Tip: Rinse your rice before cooking to make it less sticky.**

Sticky rice is mostly caused by surface starch. To remove it:

1. Put the rice in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer and cover it with cold water.
2. Swirl the rice with your hand, then drain. Repeat 2 to 4 times, until the water looks mostly clear rather than milky.
3. Drain well, then cook as usual.

**Extra help:**
- Measure the water carefully. Too much water makes rice soft and clumpy. A common starting point for long-grain white rice is about 1½ to 2 cups of water per cup of rice, but check your package for the right ratio.
- Don't stir while it cooks, since stirring releases more starch.
- After cooking, let the rice rest, covered, for about 10 minutes off the heat, then fluff it gently with a fork.

**Note:** Short-grain rice is naturally stickier than long-grain varieties, so for the fluffiest results, choose long-grain rice such as basmati or jasmine.
