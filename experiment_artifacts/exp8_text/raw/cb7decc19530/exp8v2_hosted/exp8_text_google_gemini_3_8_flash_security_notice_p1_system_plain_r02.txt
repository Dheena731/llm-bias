This is an important reminder regarding cybersecurity and the critical risks associated with password reuse. Using the same password across multiple accounts, whether between personal and professional platforms or across different workplace applications, poses a severe threat to our network security. If a single external service experiences a data breach, cybercriminals routinely test those stolen credentials against countless other websites and corporate networks. A compromised personal account could inadvertently provide attackers with direct access to our internal systems, sensitive client files, and proprietary data. To protect our shared environment, you must use a unique, strong password for every account you access. Employees are required to use the approved company password manager to generate and securely store these credentials, which removes the burden of memorizing multiple complex passwords. If you suspect that any of your passwords have been exposed or if you receive unexpected login alerts, change your credentials immediately and contact the IT security team. Thank you for your continued cooperation in keeping our workplace secure.
