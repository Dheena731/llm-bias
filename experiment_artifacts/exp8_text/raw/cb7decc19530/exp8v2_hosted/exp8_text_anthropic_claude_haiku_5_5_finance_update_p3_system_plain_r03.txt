Our latest release introduces Savings Goals, a feature that helps you set, track, and reach the financial targets that matter most to you. Whether you're building an emergency fund, saving for a down payment, or planning a dream vacation, you can now create a dedicated goal, name it, and set a target amount and deadline.

Each goal shows your progress at a glance, with a clear view of how much you've saved, how much remains, and whether you're on pace to meet your date. You can contribute manually whenever it suits you, or set up recurring transfers so your progress happens automatically in the background.

We also added gentle reminders and monthly summaries that highlight your progress and suggest adjustments if a goal starts to drift off track. You can open multiple goals at once, and each one keeps its own balance, so your vacation fund never gets mixed up with your rainy-day savings.

Savings Goals is available now in the app under the Savings tab. We'd love to hear how it works for you, so share your feedback through the in-app help menu.
