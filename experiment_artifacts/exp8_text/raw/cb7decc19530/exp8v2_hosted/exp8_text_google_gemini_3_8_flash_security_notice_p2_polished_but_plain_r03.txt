Please remain vigilant regarding an ongoing increase in sophisticated phishing emails targeting our organization. These fraudulent messages often masquerade as communications from trusted colleagues, external vendors, or senior leadership, and they typically request immediate action such as updating passwords, approving unexpected invoices, or viewing shared documents. Always inspect the sender address carefully for subtle misspellings, unusual domain extensions, or mismatched names. If an email creates an artificial sense of urgency, asks for confidential data, or seems out of character for the sender, do not interact with it. Avoid clicking any embedded links and do not download any attachments. Instead, verify the authenticity of the request through an independent channel, such as a direct phone call, or forward the suspicious message to the internal security team for analysis. Prompt reporting and daily caution remain our most effective defense in keeping company data and networks secure.
