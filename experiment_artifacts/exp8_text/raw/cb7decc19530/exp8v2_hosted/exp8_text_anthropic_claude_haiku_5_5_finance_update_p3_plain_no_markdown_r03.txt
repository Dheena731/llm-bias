Our latest update introduces savings goals, a feature that helps you set aside money for the things that matter most to you. Whether you are saving for a vacation, a down payment, an emergency fund, or a new laptop, you can now create a named goal, choose a target amount, and pick a target date. The app then shows how much you need to save each week or month to reach it on time.

Each goal has its own progress view, so you can see at a glance how close you are to finishing. You can move money into a goal from your checking account whenever you like, and you can adjust the target or deadline if your plans change. Goals can be paused or archived without losing your history, which keeps your records tidy once you have reached a milestone or decided to shift your priorities.

To get started, open the Savings tab and tap New Goal. The feature is rolling out to all users over the next two weeks. We welcome your feedback, and we will continue refining the experience based on how people use it.
