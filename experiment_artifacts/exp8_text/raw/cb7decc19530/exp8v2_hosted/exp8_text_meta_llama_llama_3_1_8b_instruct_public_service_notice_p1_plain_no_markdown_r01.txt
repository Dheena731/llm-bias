Library Opening Hours

The library is open Monday through Thursday from 9 am to 8 pm, Friday from 9 am to 6 pm, Saturday from 10 am to 5 pm, and Sunday from 12 pm to 5 pm. It is closed on public holidays. Please check the library's website or call us for up-to-date information on our hours of operation. We are happy to assist you during our regular hours.
