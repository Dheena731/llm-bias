**Notice: Changes to Recycling Collections**

Recycling collections in your area are changing. Please read this notice so you know what to expect and can prepare.

**What is changing:** The way recycling is collected from your home will be different. Check your collection schedule for the new collection day and timing.

**What you should do:**
- Check your collection schedule for the new day and timing.
- Put your recycling out by the time your collection is due.
- Keep recyclable items separate from general rubbish.

If you have questions, contact the service provider that issued this notice.

Thank you for helping us keep our community's recycling programme working well.
