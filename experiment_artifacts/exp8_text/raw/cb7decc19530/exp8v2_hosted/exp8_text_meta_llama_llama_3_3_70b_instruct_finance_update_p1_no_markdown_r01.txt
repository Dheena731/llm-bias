We are excited to announce the launch of our new monthly spending summary, designed to help you better track and manage your expenses. This feature will provide a detailed breakdown of your spending habits, including total expenditures, category-specific spending, and trends over time. The summary will be available on the first day of each month and can be accessed through your account dashboard. With this new tool, you'll be able to identify areas where you can cut back, set realistic budget goals, and make more informed financial decisions. We're confident that this feature will help you take control of your finances and achieve a healthier financial future.
