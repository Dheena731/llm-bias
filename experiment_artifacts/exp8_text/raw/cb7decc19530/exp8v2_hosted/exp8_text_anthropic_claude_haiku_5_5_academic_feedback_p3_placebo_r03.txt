**Reviewer comment on the research question**

The research question, "How does workplace flexibility affect employee well-being?", is too broad to guide the study or to tell readers what would count as an answer.

First, "workplace flexibility" can refer to schedule control, remote work, or leave policies. These are different exposures, and the question does not say which one the authors mean.

Second, "well-being" needs a specific outcome. Mental health, job satisfaction, and work-life balance are distinct constructs, and the question does not indicate whether one is primary or whether a composite is intended.

Third, the verb "affect" implies a causal effect. If the design is observational, the question should be framed as an association, or the authors should explain how they would address confounding.

I suggest the authors restate the question with a named exposure, a named outcome, a defined population, and the direction or comparison they expect. A question written this way would make it clear what evidence would support or refute it.
