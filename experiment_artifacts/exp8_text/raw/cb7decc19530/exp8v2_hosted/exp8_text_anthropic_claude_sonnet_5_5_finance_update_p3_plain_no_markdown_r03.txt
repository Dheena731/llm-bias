We've added a new savings goals feature to help you set aside money for the things that matter most to you. You can now create a goal for anything you have in mind, such as a vacation, an emergency fund, or a down payment, and give it a name, a target amount, and a date you'd like to reach it by.

Once a goal is set up, the app will show you how much you've saved so far and how much remains. It will also suggest a weekly or monthly contribution based on your timeline, so you can see at a glance whether you're on track. If you'd rather save at your own pace, you can adjust the amount or the deadline at any time and the numbers will update right away.

You can fund a goal by transferring money from any linked account, or you can set up automatic contributions on a schedule that suits you. If you get ahead or fall behind, the app will let you know in a gentle, low-key way, without cluttering your notifications.

You can create up to ten goals at once, and each one is tracked separately so your progress stays clear. To get started, open the Savings tab and select the option to add a new goal. We hope this makes it easier to turn your plans into steady, visible progress, and we would love to hear how it works for you.
