Tip: Pick one prep session, such as Sunday afternoon, and make a few building blocks instead of full recipes. Cook a big batch of a grain like rice or quinoa, roast a tray of vegetables, and prepare one or two proteins such as grilled chicken or boiled eggs. Then store them in separate containers. Each day, you can mix and match them into different meals like grain bowls, wraps, or salads, and add a fresh sauce or dressing to change the flavor. This keeps lunches varied, saves time on busy mornings, and helps prevent food from getting boring or going to waste.
