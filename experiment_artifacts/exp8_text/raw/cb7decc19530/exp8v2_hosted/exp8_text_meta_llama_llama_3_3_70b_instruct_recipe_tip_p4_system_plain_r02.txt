To make lunch planning easier for a busy week, consider setting aside some time on the weekend to prepare as much as you can in advance. This could include cooking proteins like chicken or beans, roasting vegetables, and assembling salads or sandwiches that can be easily grabbed and taken on the go. You can also make a big batch of something like soup or stew that can be portioned out into individual containers and reheated as needed. Having a plan and some pre-prepared ingredients can help save time and reduce stress during the week.
