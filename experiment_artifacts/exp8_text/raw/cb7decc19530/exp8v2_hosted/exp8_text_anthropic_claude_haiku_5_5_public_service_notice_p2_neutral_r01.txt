**PUBLIC NOTICE: Road Closure**

**Location:** Main Street, between 4th Avenue and Park Road

**Dates and Times:** Monday, June 9 through Friday, June 13, 2025, from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily

**Reason:** Road resurfacing and drainage improvements

**Details:**
- Main Street will be closed to all vehicle traffic during these hours.
- Pedestrian access to nearby businesses will be maintained.
- A detour is posted via Oak Avenue and Second Street.
- Local residents may access their properties from the 4th Avenue side only.

Please plan your travel accordingly and allow extra time for your commute. Emergency vehicles will have access at all times.

For questions, contact the City Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567 or visit www.cityexample.gov.
