**Introducing Tallyfox: Simple Bookkeeping for Small Business Owners**

Today we're launching Tallyfox, the app that takes the stress out of managing your business finances.

Running a small business means wearing every hat. You're the salesperson, the scheduler, the customer service team, and the accountant. Tallyfox gives you one hour back every day by handling the financial tasks that usually pile up.

**What you can do with Tallyfox:**

- **Send invoices in under a minute.** Use clean, professional templates and get notified the moment a client opens or pays.
- **Track expenses automatically.** Snap a photo of a receipt or connect your business account, and Tallyfox sorts everything into categories for you.
- **See your cash flow at a glance.** A simple dashboard shows what's coming in, what's going out, and what you can safely spend.
- **Get tax-ready all year.** Export organized reports for your accountant with a single tap.

**Built for people who are not accountants.** There is no jargon, no complicated setup, and no steep learning curve. Most customers are up and running in about ten minutes.
