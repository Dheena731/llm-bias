Your year-end tax statement summarizes the income and tax-related amounts we reported to the tax authorities for the past calendar year. You will need it when you file your tax return.

Please check the following:

- **Your name, address, and taxpayer identification number.** Errors here can delay your return or cause mismatches with tax agency records.
- **The amounts shown.** Compare them with your own records, such as pay stubs, bank statements, or account summaries.
- **Any amounts that look wrong.** Contact us as soon as possible so we can review and correct the statement if needed.

Keep this statement with your tax records. You do not need to send it in with your return unless your tax preparer or the tax agency instructs you to do so.
