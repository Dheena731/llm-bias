A heat advisory has been issued for our area due to extremely high temperatures and humidity levels. Residents are advised to take precautions to stay cool and safe. This includes staying indoors during the hottest part of the day, drinking plenty of water, and avoiding strenuous activities. People who work outdoors or do not have access to air conditioning are especially at risk and should take extra precautions. Check on vulnerable neighbors, such as the elderly and young children, to ensure they are staying cool. If you or someone you know is experiencing symptoms of heat exhaustion, such as dizziness, nausea, or headaches, seek medical attention immediately.
