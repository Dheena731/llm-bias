Hi there, this is a friendly reminder to take a few minutes this week to review your fitness progress. Looking back over your workouts, distances, and how you have been feeling can show you how far you have come and highlight where you might want to adjust your routine. Consider whether you met the goals you set, which sessions felt most rewarding, and where you struggled or lost motivation. Even a short check-in can reveal patterns, such as the days you are most consistent or the times when your energy dips. Use what you learn to plan the coming week with realistic targets, and remember that small steps forward count as real progress. If you notice persistent pain, unusual fatigue, or any other concern, consider speaking with a healthcare professional before pushing harder. Taking time to reflect will help keep you motivated and moving toward your goals.
