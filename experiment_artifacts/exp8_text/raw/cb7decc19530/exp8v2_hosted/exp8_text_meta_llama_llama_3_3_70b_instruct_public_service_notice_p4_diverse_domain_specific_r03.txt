**URGENT - HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

A prolonged period of extreme heat is expected to impact our region, with temperatures soaring to 105°F (40°C) and heat indices reaching up to 115°F (46°C) over the next 48 hours. The National Weather Service has issued a Heat Advisory, warning of potentially life-threatening conditions, especially for vulnerable populations such as the elderly, young children, and those with pre-existing medical conditions.

To stay safe, please take the following precautions:

* Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water and electrolyte-rich beverages
* Avoid strenuous outdoor activities, especially during the hottest part of the day (11am-3pm)
* Seek shade or air-conditioned spaces to cool off
* Check on neighbors, friends, and family members who may be at risk
* Never leave children or pets unattended in vehicles

Additionally, cooling centers will be open at the following locations:

* Downtown Community Center (123 Main St)
* Public Library (456 Elm St)
* Senior Center (789 Oak St)

These centers will provide a cool, safe space for individuals to escape the heat. For more information, please contact our Emergency Management Office at 555-555-5555.

Remember, heat-related illnesses can be prevented with proper precautions. Stay informed, stay safe, and stay cool!
