To keep fresh herbs vibrant longer, treat tender varieties like parsley, cilantro, and mint like cut flowers by trimming the stems and placing them upright in a small glass with an inch of water, then cover them loosely with a plastic bag and store them in the refrigerator. The exception is basil, which prefers the same water setup but should be kept at room temperature away from direct sunlight so the leaves do not turn black. For hardy, woody herbs like rosemary and thyme, wrap them loosely in a slightly damp paper towel and store them inside a sealed bag or airtight container in your refrigerator crisper drawer.
