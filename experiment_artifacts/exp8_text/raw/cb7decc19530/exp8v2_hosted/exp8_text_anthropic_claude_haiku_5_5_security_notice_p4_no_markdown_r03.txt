Security Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication

To help protect your account from unauthorized access, we strongly recommend turning on two-factor authentication (2FA). With 2FA, signing in requires both your password and a second verification step, such as a one-time code sent to your phone or generated by an authenticator app.

To enable two-factor authentication:

1. Log in to your account and go to Settings.
2. Select Security, then choose Two-Factor Authentication.
3. Follow the prompts to link your phone or authenticator app.
4. Save your backup codes in a safe place in case you lose access to your device.

Enabling 2FA takes only a few minutes and significantly reduces the risk of account takeover, even if your password is compromised. If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact our support team.

Thank you for helping us keep your account secure.
