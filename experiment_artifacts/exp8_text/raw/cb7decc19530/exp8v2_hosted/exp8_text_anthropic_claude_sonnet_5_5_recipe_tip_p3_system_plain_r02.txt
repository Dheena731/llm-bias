Rinse your rice in cold water before cooking it. Put the rice in a bowl or fine mesh strainer, run water over it, and swirl it with your hand until the water goes from cloudy to mostly clear, which usually takes three or four changes of water. This washes away the loose surface starch that makes grains clump together. After rinsing, drain the rice well and use a measured amount of water, generally about one and a half cups of water per cup of long grain white rice. Once it finishes cooking, let it rest covered for ten minutes off the heat, then fluff it gently with a fork rather than stirring, which can break the grains and release more starch.
