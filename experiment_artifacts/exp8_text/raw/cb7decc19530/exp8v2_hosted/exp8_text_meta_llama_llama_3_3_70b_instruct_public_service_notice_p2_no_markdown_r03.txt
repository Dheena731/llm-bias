Public Notice: Road Closure

A section of Main Street will be closed to all traffic starting Monday at 8am due to construction work. The closure will be in effect until Friday at 5pm. Motorists are advised to use alternative routes, such as Elm Street or Oak Avenue, to avoid delays. Pedestrian access will be maintained, but pedestrians are asked to follow designated detour signs. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your cooperation. For more information, please contact the city's public works department at 555-1234.
