When storing fresh herbs, try treating them like a bouquet of flowers. Trim the stems at an angle, and place them in a jar or container with a small amount of water, just like you would with fresh-cut flowers. This will help keep the herbs hydrated and prevent them from sitting in waterlogged soil, which can lead to rot and spoilage. For more delicate herbs like basil and mint, you can also cover the jar with a plastic bag or wrap it in a damp paper towel to maintain humidity and keep them fresh for up to a week. This simple trick will help preserve the flavor, aroma, and texture of your fresh herbs, making them last longer and stay ready to use in your favorite recipes.
