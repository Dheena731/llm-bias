**Introducing Your Monthly Spending Summary**

We're excited to announce a new monthly spending summary, designed to help you understand where your money goes at a glance.

Each month, you'll receive a clear overview that includes:

- **Total spending** across all categories
- **Breakdown by category**, such as dining, transportation, and bills
- **Comparison to previous months**, so you can spot trends
- **Notable transactions** that stood out during the period

The summary arrives at the start of each month and covers the previous month's activity. You can view it in the app under the Insights tab, or opt in to receive it by email.

We built this feature to make budgeting simpler and more informative. If you have questions or feedback, reach out through the Help Center, and we'll be happy to hear from you.
