The information security team reminds all employees to avoid using the same password across multiple accounts. Reusing credentials poses a significant risk to the entire organization. When an external service or personal platform suffers a data breach, attackers routinely test those exposed username and password combinations against corporate networks and business applications. A single compromise outside of work can directly grant unauthorized individuals access to company systems and confidential data.

To protect our network, ensure that your workplace password is fully unique and never shared with personal email, banking, social media, or vendor accounts. Employees are encouraged to use an approved password manager to generate and securely store complex passphrases for every service they use. If you suspect that your workplace password may have been exposed or if you need assistance setting up a password manager, please reach out to the IT support desk immediately.
