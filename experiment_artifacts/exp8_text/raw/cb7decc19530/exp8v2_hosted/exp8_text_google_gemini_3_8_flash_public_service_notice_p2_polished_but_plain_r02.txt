The Department of Public Works advises motorists that Main Street, between Fourth Avenue and Elm Street, will be temporarily closed to all through traffic from Monday, October 21, through Friday, October 25. Work will take place daily between the hours of seven in the morning and five in the evening to allow crews to complete scheduled water main repairs and street resurfacing.

During active construction hours, traffic will be rerouted along Maple Street, with clearly marked detour signs in place to assist drivers. Access for local residents, businesses, and emergency services will be maintained throughout the closure, though travelers in the immediate area should anticipate minor delays. Motorists are urged to plan extra travel time, observe all posted warning signs, and exercise caution near the work zone. 

The roadway will reopen to regular traffic each evening and over the weekend. The city appreciates the public's patience and cooperation as these critical infrastructure upgrades are completed. For inquiries regarding this project, please contact the municipal public works office.
