This Saturday, the community is coming together for a family-friendly event at the local park. From 11am to 3pm, enjoy live music, delicious food, and a variety of activities for all ages. Face painting, a petting zoo, and a bouncy castle will provide entertainment for kids, while a classic car show and local vendors will appeal to car enthusiasts and shoppers. The event is free and open to the public, making it a great opportunity for neighbors to connect and community members to learn more about local services and resources. We look forward to seeing you there.
