NOTICE: Please Lock Your Screens

To all staff,

This is a reminder to lock your computer screens whenever you step away from your desk, even if only for a moment. Leaving a screen unlocked can expose confidential information and put our systems at risk.

How to lock your screen:
- Windows: press the Windows key + L
- Mac: press Control + Command + Q

Please also make sure your device is set to lock automatically after a short period of inactivity.

Thank you for helping keep our data and workplace secure.

IT and Security Team
