**Lost Company Laptop Notice**

If you have lost a company-issued laptop, please report the incident to the IT department immediately. Please provide the following details:

* Laptop serial number (if available)
* Date and time of loss
* Location of loss
* Description of circumstances surrounding the loss

You can report the lost laptop by emailing [IT department email] or calling [IT department phone number]. Prompt reporting will help us to take necessary measures to secure company data and minimize potential risks.

Note: Failure to report a lost company laptop in a timely manner may result in disciplinary action.
