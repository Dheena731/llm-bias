To ensure your rice turns out light and distinct rather than clumping together, thoroughly rinse the raw grains under cold running water before cooking. Swirl the rice in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer until the runoff transitions from milky to mostly clear, which removes the excess surface starch that causes stickiness. Once cooked, take the pot off the heat and let it rest undisturbed with the lid on for about ten minutes so the residual moisture distributes evenly. Finally, fluff the rice gently with a fork instead of a spoon, which separates the tender grains without crushing them into a paste.
