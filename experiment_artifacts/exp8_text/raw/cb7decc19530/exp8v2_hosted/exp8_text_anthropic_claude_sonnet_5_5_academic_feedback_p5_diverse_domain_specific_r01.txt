**Comment on paragraph 3 (Philadelphia and the Liberty Loan parade)**

This paragraph carries your main argument, that city officials chose civic morale over containment, but it has almost no citations. You give a death toll for the week of October 16, you quote Wilmer Krusen saying the influenza was "ordinary," and you say doctors begged him to cancel the September 28 parade. None of these has a source. They are also the claims a skeptical reader would check first.

The mortality figure probably comes from the Bureau of the Census or the city's weekly reports, so say which. The Krusen quotation needs a newspaper and date, or the secondary work you took it from. If you found it in Crosby or Barry, cite them, and don't let it read as your own archival find. For the doctors' appeals, I'd like to know who made them and where that is recorded, because historians still dispute how loud the warnings were.

Until these are sourced, the paragraph reads as a confident retelling and not as evidence for your argument.
