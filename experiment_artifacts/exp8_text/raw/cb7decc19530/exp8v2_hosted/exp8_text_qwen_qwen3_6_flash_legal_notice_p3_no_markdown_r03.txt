DEFINITION OF COOKIES
A cookie is a small text file that a web server places on your device when you visit a website. Cookies enable websites to recognize your device, remember your preferences, and maintain session state across pages or visits.

CATEGORIES AND PURPOSES
The Site deploys the following classifications of cookies:
One. Essential Cookies: Strictly necessary for core Site operations, including security authentication, load balancing, and navigation. These cookies cannot be disabled without compromising Site functionality.
Two. Performance and Analytics Cookies: Gather aggregated, anonymized data regarding visitor traffic, page views, and error rates. This information is utilized to optimize Site performance and user experience.
Three. Functionality Cookies: Retain your expressed preferences, such as language selection, display settings, and regional preferences, to personalize your interaction with the Site.
Four. Marketing and Tracking Cookies: Monitor your browsing activity across sites to deliver targeted advertising, measure campaign efficacy, and limit advertisement frequency. These may be deployed by external advertising networks.
