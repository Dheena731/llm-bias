**Tip: Prep components, not full meals.**

On Sunday, spend 30-45 minutes cooking a few versatile building blocks: a grain (rice or quinoa), a protein (grilled chicken, hard-boiled eggs, or chickpeas), and a tray of roasted vegetables. Then wash and chop some raw veggies and make one flavorful sauce or dressing.

Each day, mix and match into something different, such as a grain bowl on Monday, a wrap on Tuesday, or a salad on Wednesday. You get variety without cooking every day, and the sauce keeps things from getting boring.

**Bonus:** Pack lunches the night before so mornings stay stress-free.
