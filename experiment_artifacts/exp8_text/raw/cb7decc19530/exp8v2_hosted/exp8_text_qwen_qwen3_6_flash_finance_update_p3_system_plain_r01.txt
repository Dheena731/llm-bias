We are pleased to announce the launch of our new savings goal feature. This tool allows you to set specific financial targets for things like emergency funds, vacations, or major purchases. You can choose a target amount and a deadline, and the system will track your progress automatically. Each time you add money to your account, the progress tracker updates in real time. The feature also sends gentle reminders if you fall behind on your recommended monthly contribution plan. You can create multiple goals at once and switch between them easily from your main dashboard. This update is designed to help you stay focused and motivated while building consistent savings habits. We recommend checking out the feature today and setting your first target.
