WORKPLACE NOTICE: IMPORTANT SECURITY ALERT REGARDING PHISHING EMAILS

Attention All Staff,

We have recently observed an increase in targeted phishing emails attempting to access company systems and sensitive data. Please remain vigilant and follow these guidelines to protect yourself and the organization.

Common indicators of phishing include unexpected messages requesting passwords, account details, or financial information. Exercise caution when encountering urgent or threatening language demanding immediate action, suspicious sender addresses, mismatched hyperlinks, or instructions to download attachments from unknown sources.

If you receive a suspicious email, do not reply, click any links, or open attachments. Forward the complete message to our IT Security team at security@company.com with the subject line Suspicious Email Report. When in doubt, contact IT Support directly at extension 5500 or verify the request through official company channels before responding.

Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining a secure workplace.
