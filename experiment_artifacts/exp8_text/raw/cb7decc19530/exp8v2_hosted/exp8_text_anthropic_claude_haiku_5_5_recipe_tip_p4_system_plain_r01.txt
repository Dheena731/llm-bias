Try batch cooking one versatile base on Sunday, such as a big pot of grains, a tray of roasted vegetables, or a batch of shredded chicken, and then mix and match it into different lunches throughout the week. Pairing the same base with a few quick toppings, sauces, or dressings keeps things varied without requiring you to cook from scratch each day. Store each portion in its own container so you can grab one and go, and your lunches will be ready in minutes even on your most hectic mornings.
