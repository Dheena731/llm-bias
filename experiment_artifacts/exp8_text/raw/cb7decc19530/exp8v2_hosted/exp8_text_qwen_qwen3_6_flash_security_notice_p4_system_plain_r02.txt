This is a notice regarding the mandatory activation of two factor authentication for all user accounts. Starting next month, every account holder must enable this security feature before logging in. Two factor authentication adds an extra layer of protection by requiring a verification code in addition to your password. To activate it, please log into your account settings and select the security tab. Follow the prompts to link your mobile device or authenticator application. If you encounter any difficulties during setup, contact the IT support team using the help desk email address listed on the company portal. Failure to enable two factor authentication within the specified timeframe may result in temporary access restrictions. We appreciate your prompt attention to this important security update.
