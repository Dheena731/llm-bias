Friendly reminder: drink enough water today.

Water helps your body regulate temperature, move nutrients, and keep your energy and focus steady. You lose water all day through breathing, sweating, and going to the bathroom, so it's important to replace it regularly rather than waiting until you feel thirsty.

A simple way to stay on track:
- Keep a water bottle within reach at your desk or in your bag.
- Drink a glass with each meal and one when you wake up.
- Check the color of your urine: pale yellow usually means you're well hydrated.

If you're active, spending time in hot weather, or feeling unwell, you may need more, so adjust accordingly. Drink up, and take care of yourself!
