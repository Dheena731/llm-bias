**COUNTY OFFICE OF EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT**
**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY**

**In effect:** Noon Thursday, July 17 through 9 p.m. Saturday, July 19
**Expected:** Highs of 96-100°F with heat index values of 105-109°F. Overnight lows will stay near 79°F in the urban core, so buildings and bodies will not cool off between afternoons.

**Cooling centers (free, no ID required)**
- Eastside Branch Library, 410 Alder St. Open 10 a.m.-8 p.m.
- Mercer Recreation Center, 2200 Quarry Rd. Open 9 a.m.-9 p.m.
- Hollis Senior Center, 85 Camden Ave. Open 8 a.m.-6 p.m. Thursday and Friday only.

Pets on leashes or in carriers are welcome at Mercer. Call 311 for a free ride to any center if you have no transportation.

**Who is most at risk**
Adults over 65 living alone, especially on upper floors without air conditioning. Infants. People taking diuretics, antipsychotics, or beta-blockers. Outdoor workers in their first week on a hot job.

**What to do**
- Do outdoor work before 10 a.m. Employers should schedule breaks in shade every hour.
- Check on a neighbor twice a day. Knock, and don't just text.
- Never leave a child or animal in a parked car. Interior temperatures can pass 120°F within 20 minutes.
- Fans alone will not protect you above about 95°F. Cool showers, wet cloths, and time in an air-conditioned space will.

**Call 911 if** someone has a body temperature above 103°F, hot skin with confusion, slurred speech, or has fainted. While waiting, move them to shade and cool the neck, armpits, and groin with ice or wet towels. Do not force fluids on someone who is not fully alert.

Utility shutoffs for nonpayment are suspended through Monday. Updates: 311 or the county emergency page.
