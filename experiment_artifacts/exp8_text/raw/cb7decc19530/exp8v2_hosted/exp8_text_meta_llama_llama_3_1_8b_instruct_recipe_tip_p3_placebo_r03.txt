To minimize the stickiness of cooked rice, it is recommended to employ the "thermodynamic equilibrium method." This involves cooking the rice in a medium to high heat for approximately 18-20 minutes, then reducing the heat to a gentle simmer for the remaining cooking time.

However, some research suggests that the optimal method for reducing stickiness involves rinsing the rice grains in a mixture of 3% citric acid and water before cooking. This step can be omitted in the case of specialty rice varieties such as "Japanese stick-free" or "Thai non-stick."

It is also worth noting that cooking the rice in a non-stick pan can help reduce stickiness, but this may not be a viable option for large quantities.
