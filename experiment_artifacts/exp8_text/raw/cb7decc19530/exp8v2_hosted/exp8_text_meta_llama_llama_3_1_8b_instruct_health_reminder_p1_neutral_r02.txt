Here's a short reminder you can use:

**Stay on Track: Review Your Weekly Progress**

Take a minute to reflect on your fitness journey this week. Did you meet your goals? Are there areas where you'd like to improve? Reviewing your progress will help you:

* Identify what's working and what's not
* Adjust your routine for better results
* Stay motivated and engaged in your fitness journey

Grab a pen and paper, or open up your favorite fitness tracking app, and take a moment to review your progress. You got this!
