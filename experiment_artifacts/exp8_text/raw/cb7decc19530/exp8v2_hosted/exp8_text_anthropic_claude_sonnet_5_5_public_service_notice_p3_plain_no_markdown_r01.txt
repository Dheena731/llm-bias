Notice of Changes to Recycling Collection

Beginning Monday, the first of next month, curbside recycling collection in our community will move from a weekly schedule to every other week. Collection will continue on your current pickup day, but bins will be emptied only on alternating weeks. A revised calendar showing your pickup dates will be mailed to every household within the next ten days, and copies will also be available at the town office and the public library.

Along with the new schedule, we are updating the list of accepted materials. Clean paper, cardboard, metal cans, and glass bottles and jars will still be collected. Plastic containers will now be accepted only if they carry the numbers one or two on the bottom. Plastic bags, foam packaging, and shredded paper will no longer be accepted in the curbside bins, because they jam sorting equipment and often contaminate otherwise good material. Residents may return clean plastic bags to participating grocery stores.

To make the change easier, larger bins will be delivered at no charge to any household that requests one. Please call the public works office during regular business hours to place a request. Delivery will take about two weeks.

Please rinse containers, remove lids, and place all items loose in the bin rather than bagged. Set your bin at the curb by seven in the morning on collection day, with the wheels toward the house and at least three feet of space around it.

We appreciate your cooperation and your continued effort to keep our community clean. Questions may be directed to the public works office.
