Here's a short cooking tip for preparing a weeknight dinner:

**One-Pot Wonder: Quick Skillet Lasagna**

Need a quick and easy dinner on a busy weeknight? Try making a skillet lasagna! This clever hack uses a large skillet to cook ground beef, pasta, and cheese in one pot. Simply cook the ground beef, add in the lasagna noodles and marinara sauce, and top with shredded mozzarella. Cover the skillet with a lid and let it simmer for 10-15 minutes, or until the cheese is melted and bubbly. Serve hot, and enjoy a satisfying, one-pot meal in no time!

This trick is perfect for busy weeknights because it:

* Uses only one pot to cook the entire meal
* Requires minimal prep time and cleanup
* Can be customized with your favorite ground beef and cheese options
* Feeds a family of 4-6 people in under 30 minutes!
