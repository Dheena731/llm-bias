Please be advised that starting the first week of next month, residential recycling collection will move to an alternate-week schedule. Your blue recycling bin will now be emptied every other Wednesday rather than on a weekly basis. Regular household trash collection days will not be affected by this change. Additionally, clean cardboard must now be flattened and placed completely inside the bin, as loose bundles left beside the container will no longer be collected. Please ensure your cart is placed at the curb by seven in the morning on your scheduled collection day. Thank you for your cooperation as we implement these adjustments to improve local service efficiency.
