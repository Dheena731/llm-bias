We have launched a new Monthly Spending Summary to give you clearer visibility into your finances. 

Starting this month, you can access an automated report directly from your account dashboard that breaks down your monthly financial activity. 

Key details included in the summary:
- Total expenditures compared to previous months
- Spending categorized by type (e.g., groceries, utilities, entertainment)
- Insights on recurring charges and active subscriptions

Your summary will update automatically on the first day of each month. To view your latest report, log in to your account and navigate to the Analytics tab.
