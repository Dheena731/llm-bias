When you are rushing through a busy weekday evening, choose recipes that cook entirely in one pan to save time and reduce cleanup. Start by chopping your vegetables and measuring out spices during a quiet moment earlier in the day, then simply toss everything into a hot skillet with a little oil and protein. Let the heat do the work while you handle other evening tasks, and stir occasionally until the ingredients are tender and evenly cooked. This method keeps your kitchen tidy, uses minimal dishes, and consistently delivers a warm meal without requiring advanced culinary skills or hours of preparation.
