**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY**

**Effective Date:** [Insert Date]

**1. Purpose**
This Notice sets forth the conditions under which refunds may be issued by [Company Name] ("the Company") to purchasers ("the Customer") of its goods or services.

**2. Eligibility Requirements**
A Customer is eligible for a refund only if all of the following conditions are satisfied:

(a) The refund request is submitted in writing within thirty (30) days of the original date of purchase;

(b) The Customer provides valid proof of purchase, including an order number or receipt;

(c) The goods are returned in their original condition and packaging, where applicable; and

(d) The goods or services are not within a category designated as non-refundable under Section 3.

**3. Non-Refundable Items**
The following are not eligible for a refund:

(a) Digital products that have been downloaded, accessed, or activated;

(b) Custom or made-to-order goods;

(c) Services that have been fully rendered;

(d) Items marked "final sale" at the time of purchase; and

(e) Purchases made using promotional credits or gift cards, except where required by applicable law.

**4. Processing of Refunds**
Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within ten (10) business days of approval. The Company is not responsible for delays caused by third-party financial institutions. Shipping and handling fees are non-refundable unless the return results from Company error.

**5. Discretion and Determination**
All refund determinations are made by the Company in its reasonable discretion, subject to applicable law. Approval of a refund in one instance does not establish a right to a refund in any other instance.

**6. Statutory Rights**
Nothing in this Notice limits any rights that the Customer may have under applicable consumer protection laws, which cannot be waived or excluded by agreement.

**7. Contact**
Refund requests and inquiries should be directed to [Contact Information].

**8. Amendments**
The Company reserves the right to amend this Notice at any time. Amendments apply only to purchases made after the effective date of the revised Notice.
