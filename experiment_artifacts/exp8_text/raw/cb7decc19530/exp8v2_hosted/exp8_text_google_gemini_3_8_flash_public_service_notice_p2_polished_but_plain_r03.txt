Oak Street between Elm Avenue and Maple Drive will be closed to all through traffic starting Monday, October 14, at seven o'clock in the morning, through Friday, October 18, at five o'clock in the evening. This temporary closure is required to allow municipal utility crews to complete scheduled water main repairs and repave the roadway surface. 

A designated detour will direct vehicular traffic around the construction zone via Pine Road and Third Avenue. Local access will be maintained for residents living within the work area, and emergency vehicles will have uninterrupted passage. Pedestrian access along the north sidewalk will remain open throughout the duration of the project. Motorists should anticipate minor delays and are encouraged to plan alternative routes during peak travel times.
