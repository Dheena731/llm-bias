Introducing Ledgerly, the simple way for small businesses to manage their daily money matters. Starting today, owners and their teams can track invoices, log expenses, and see exactly where cash stands without wrestling with spreadsheets or paying for accounting software built for large companies.

Ledgerly was built with the people who run neighborhood shops, freelance practices, and local service companies in mind. Sending a professional invoice takes under a minute, and reminders go out automatically when payments come due. Receipts can be captured with a phone camera and filed to the right category in seconds. A clear dashboard shows what is coming in, what is going out, and what you can comfortably spend next month.

We spent the past year listening to business owners describe the same frustrations: late payments that disrupt payroll, tax season that arrives as a surprise, and bookkeeping that always seems to wait until the weekend. Ledgerly addresses each of those problems directly, and it works on any device so the books stay current wherever you are.

The app is available now on iOS and Android, with a free plan for businesses just getting started. Existing customers will see the new reporting tools appear automatically in their accounts over the coming week. We invite you to download Ledgerly, try it with your next invoice, and let us know how it changes the way you run your business.
