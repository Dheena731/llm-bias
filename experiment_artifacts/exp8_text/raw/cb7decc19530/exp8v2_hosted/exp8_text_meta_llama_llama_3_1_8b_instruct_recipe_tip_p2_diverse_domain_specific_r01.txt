**Preserve the Pungency: Store Fresh Herbs in a Humid Environment**

Fresh herbs like basil, mint, and cilantro are notoriously finicky to store, but one key factor can make all the difference: humidity. Store your herbs in a humid environment, such as a sealed plastic bag with a damp cloth or paper towel, to preserve their aromatic oils and prevent wilting. This simple trick will keep your herbs fresh for up to a week, allowing you to enjoy their vibrant flavors in your cooking and recipes.
