Treat tender herbs (like basil, cilantro, and parsley) like cut flowers: trim the stems, place them in a jar with an inch of water, and loosely cover with a plastic bag before refrigerating. For woody herbs (like rosemary and thyme), wrap them in a slightly damp paper towel and store in a sealed container. **Pro tip:** Wait to wash your herbs until right before you use them to prevent moisture-related spoilage.
