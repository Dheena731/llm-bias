When storing fresh herbs, it's essential to keep them in a cool, dry place. Wrap the herbs in a damp paper towel or cloth to maintain their moisture, and place them in a sealed container or plastic bag. Avoid storing them in direct sunlight or near a heat source, as this can cause them to wilt and lose their flavor. If you don't plan to use the herbs within a few days, consider freezing them by chopping the leaves finely and placing them in an airtight container or freezer bag. Frozen herbs are perfect for soups, stews, and sauces, and can be added directly to your recipes.
