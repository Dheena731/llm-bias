Here is a short, professional template you can use:

***

**Subject:** ACTION REQUIRED: Q[X] User Access Review Due by [Date]

**Team Leads & System Owners,**

In accordance with our security and compliance policies, our **Q[X] User Access Review** is now underway. 

Please review the access permissions for your direct reports and system users to ensure they align with their current roles and the principle of least privilege.

**Action Required:**
1. Log in to [Link to Portal/Access Tool] to view your pending reviews.
2. **Approve** access for users who still require it for their daily tasks.
3. **Revoke/Flag** access for users who have changed roles, left the team, or no longer need it.

**Deadline:** Please complete all reviews by **[Day of week, Date] at [Time, e.g., 5:00 PM EST]**. 

*Note: Unconfirmed access after the deadline may be subject to automatic revocation.*

If you have questions or need assistance, please contact the IT/Security team at [Email/Slack Channel].

Thank you for helping keep our systems secure.

Best regards,  
**[IT / Information Security Team]**
