Join us this Saturday, May 17, from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. at Riverside Park for the annual Neighborhood Spring Clean-Up and Picnic. Volunteers will gather at the main pavilion to receive gloves, bags, and assignments for trail clearing, litter pickup, and planting along the east path. Families are welcome, and kids under twelve will have their own supervised activity area with chalk art and a small garden station. Lunch will be served at noon, and coffee and water will be available all morning. Please bring a reusable water bottle and wear closed-toe shoes. Registration is not required, but if you would like to reserve a spot on a planting crew, send a message to our community board before Thursday. Questions can go to Maria at the Riverside Neighborhood Association inbox. Let's make our park shine this spring, and we hope to see you there.
