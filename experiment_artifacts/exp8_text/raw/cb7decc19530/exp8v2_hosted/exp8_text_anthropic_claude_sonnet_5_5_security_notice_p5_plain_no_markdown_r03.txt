Notice: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop

If your company laptop is lost or stolen, please report it to the IT Service Desk immediately, no matter how short a time it has been missing or how confident you are that it will turn up. Prompt reporting allows us to lock the device remotely, disable your accounts, and protect company and customer data before any harm is done.

When you contact the Service Desk, please be ready to provide your name and department, the laptop's asset tag or serial number if you have it, the date, time, and place you last had the device, and a brief description of what happened. Let us know as well whether you believe the laptop held sensitive files, such as customer records or financial information. Do not delay your report while you search for the missing device or gather these details. We can fill in gaps later.

If the laptop was stolen, please also file a report with local police and send the report number to IT Security. If it was lost while traveling, tell your manager as soon as you can so that any work impact can be handled.

Please do not attempt to access company systems from a replacement personal device until IT has confirmed it is safe to do so. We will arrange a temporary or permanent replacement as quickly as possible.

Reporting a lost laptop promptly will not result in disciplinary action in itself. Delays in reporting, however, can increase risk and may be reviewed. Thank you for helping us keep company information secure.
