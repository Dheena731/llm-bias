Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations

Voters are advised that polling stations will be open on Election Day from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Each registered voter is assigned to a specific polling station based on their home address, and ballots can only be cast at that assigned location. Please check your voter information card or contact the local elections office to confirm where you should vote before you travel.

Most polling stations are located in public buildings that are familiar to residents, including schools, libraries, community centers, and municipal offices. All locations have been selected for accessibility and offer step-free entrances, accessible parking, and assistance for voters with mobility, vision, or hearing needs. Voters who require additional support are welcome to ask any polling station staff member for help on arrival.

Please note that some locations have changed since the last election. A number of stations have moved because of building renovations, and a few voting districts have been combined. Even if you have voted at the same site in the past, we encourage you to verify your assigned station this year. Voting at the wrong location can cause delays and may require you to travel to the correct site.

To avoid long lines, consider voting in the middle of the morning or early afternoon, when turnout is usually lighter. Bring an acceptable form of photo identification, and if you have received a mail-in ballot but prefer to vote in person, bring that ballot with you so it can be properly cancelled.

For the full list of polling station addresses, to confirm your registration, or to request assistance, please contact the local elections office during regular business hours or visit its official website. Staff will be glad to answer your questions and help ensure that your vote is counted.
