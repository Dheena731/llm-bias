**Tip: Store herbs based on their type.**

- **Soft-stemmed herbs (parsley, cilantro, dill, mint):** Trim the ends of the stems and stand the bunch in a jar with a little water, like flowers. Cover loosely with a plastic bag and keep in the refrigerator. Change the water if it gets cloudy.
- **Basil:** Put it in a jar of water too, but leave it on the counter away from direct sun. Cold air can turn its leaves dark.
- **Woody-stemmed herbs (rosemary, thyme, sage):** Wrap them loosely in a slightly damp paper towel, put them in a bag or container, and refrigerate.

Wash herbs only right before using them, since extra moisture on the leaves can make them spoil faster. Remove any yellow or slimy leaves as soon as you see them.
