Leave your rimmed baking sheet in the oven while it preheats to 425°F. 

Toss your vegetables—especially dense brassicas like broccoli, cauliflower, or halved Brussels sprouts—with oil and salt in a mixing bowl rather than on the tray. When the oven is ready, dump them directly onto the hot metal and quickly spread them cut-side down. 

Contact with the preheated steel starts the Maillard reaction instantly, vaporizing surface moisture before it can pool. You get hard, blistered charring on the flats in fifteen minutes flat, completely bypassing the pale, sulfurous steaming that ruins most sheet-pan dinners.
