NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE

The City Department of Public Works announces that Maple Street will be closed to all vehicle traffic between Fifth Avenue and Ninth Avenue beginning Monday, June 3, at 7:00 a.m. and continuing through Friday, June 14, at 6:00 p.m. The closure is necessary to allow crews to replace an aging water main and to resurface the roadway once the work is complete.

During this period, drivers should use Oak Boulevard or Cedar Lane as alternate routes. Signs will be posted along the detour, and traffic control personnel will be on site during peak hours to assist motorists. Please expect delays and allow extra travel time, especially during the morning and evening commutes.

Pedestrian access will be maintained on the north sidewalk, though walkers and cyclists are asked to follow posted directions and use caution near the work zone. Local residents will be able to reach their driveways from the nearest open intersection, but access may be briefly limited while crews are working directly in front of a property. Emergency vehicles will have access at all times. Trash and recycling collection will continue on the regular schedule, though residents may be asked to place bins at the nearest intersection.

Weather or unforeseen conditions may change the schedule. Updates will be shared on the city website and by phone at the Public Works information line, 555-0142, which is staffed weekdays from 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.

We appreciate the patience and cooperation of everyone in the community as we work to improve local infrastructure.
