Add a hard expiration date directly into the title of any recurring meeting you create:

`[Until May 1] Mobile Ops Sync`

Calendar apps default to "repeats indefinitely," which is how quick check-ins end up outliving the projects they were built for. 

When the expiration date is visible on everyone's grid, the default shifts from permanent background noise to a conscious choice: if it’s still useful on May 2nd, someone has to actively defend renewing it. Otherwise, the time clears automatically.
