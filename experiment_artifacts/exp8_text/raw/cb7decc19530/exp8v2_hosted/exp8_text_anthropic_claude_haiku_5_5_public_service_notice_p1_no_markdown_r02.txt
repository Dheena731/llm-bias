Attention Residents: Library Hours Update

The Central Public Library wants to remind all community members of our current opening hours. We are open Monday through Friday from 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., Saturday from 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., and Sunday from 12:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m. The library will be closed on all major public holidays.

Please note that the book drop-off is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week, at the front entrance for your convenience. Holiday hours may differ, so we encourage you to check our website or call the information desk before your visit.

We look forward to welcoming you. Libraries are here for everyone, offering books, computers, study spaces, and programs for all ages.
