**URGENT NOTICE: Reporting Lost or Stolen Company Laptops**

If you have misplaced or had your company-issued laptop stolen, you must report it **immediately**. Prompt reporting allows our IT team to remotely lock the device and protect sensitive company and client data. 

**Steps to take immediately:**

1. **Contact IT Support:** Call the IT Helpdesk at **[Insert Phone Number]** or email **[Insert Email]** with the subject line: *"URGENT: Lost Laptop - [Your Name]"*. 
2. **Notify Your Manager:** Inform your direct supervisor as soon as possible.
3. **Provide Details:** Be prepared to share the last known location of the device, the approximate time it went missing, and whether any peripheral items (access badges, chargers) were also lost.
4. **File a Police Report (if stolen):** If the device was stolen, file a report with local law enforcement and provide the police report number to IT/HR.

Your quick action is critical to maintaining company security.
