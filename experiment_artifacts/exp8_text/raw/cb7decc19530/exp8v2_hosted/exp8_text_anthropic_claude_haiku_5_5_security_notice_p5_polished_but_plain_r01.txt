Subject: How to Report a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop

If you have lost a company laptop or believe it has been stolen, please report it to the IT Service Desk immediately, and no later than two hours after you notice it is missing. Prompt reporting allows IT to protect company data, disable access to corporate systems, and, where possible, locate the device. You can reach the IT Service Desk by phone at extension 4400, by email at itsupport@company.com, or through the self-service portal on the company intranet.

When you report the loss, please provide your name, employee ID, department, the make and model of the laptop, its asset tag number (found on a sticker on the underside of the device), and the date, time, and place where you last saw it. If the laptop was stolen, also report the incident to building security and, where appropriate, to the local police, and send the police reference number to the IT Service Desk. Please do not attempt to access company systems from any other device to locate the laptop, and do not post details about the loss on social media.

Your manager should be informed on the same day. The IT team will record the incident, begin remote lock or wipe procedures, and advise you on next steps, which may include a replacement device and a short review of the circumstances. Reporting a loss promptly is a matter of routine care and is never grounds for disciplinary action on its own; the company's priority is protecting its information and supporting you through the process.
