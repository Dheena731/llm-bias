This is a friendly reminder to all staff to lock your computer screens whenever you step away from your desk, even if only for a brief moment. Leaving an active workstation unattended exposes sensitive internal communications, client data, and company systems to unauthorized access. Securing your display is one of the simplest and most effective ways to protect our workplace and ensure compliance with our security standards. You can lock your device instantly by pressing the Windows key and the L key on a PC, or by pressing Control, Command, and the Q key on a Mac. Thank you for your ongoing commitment to keeping our organization secure.
