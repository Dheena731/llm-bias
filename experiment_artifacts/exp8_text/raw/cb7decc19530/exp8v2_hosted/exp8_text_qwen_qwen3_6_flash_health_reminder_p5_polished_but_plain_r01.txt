This is a reminder that your prescription is ready for a refill. To avoid running out of medication, please submit your refill request to your pharmacy at least three business days before you expect to need your next dose. You can typically handle this by calling the pharmacy directly, using their online portal or app, or requesting a transfer if you prefer a different location. If your prescription has expired or requires approval from your doctor, please contact your healthcare provider to arrange a renewal. Staying ahead of refill deadlines ensures consistent treatment and helps maintain your health goals.
