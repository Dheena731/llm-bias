Today we are launching Tally, a simple app that helps small business owners keep track of the work that keeps their doors open. Invoices, appointments, customer notes, and payments now live in one place, so you can spend less time switching between spreadsheets, inboxes, and sticky notes, and more time serving the people who rely on you.

We built Tally after listening to dozens of shop owners, freelancers, and service providers describe the same frustration. Most business software is designed for companies with an office manager and an IT budget. Tally is designed for the person who is also the sales team, the bookkeeper, and the one who unlocks the front door in the morning. Setup takes about ten minutes, and there is nothing to install or configure before you can send your first invoice.

From the start, you can create professional invoices in a few taps, schedule appointments with automatic reminders that cut down on no-shows, and keep a running history of every customer conversation so you never have to ask someone to repeat themselves. A clear weekly summary shows what you earned, what is still owed, and which customers are due for a follow-up. If you already track your business in a spreadsheet, you can import it directly and keep going without starting over.

To celebrate the launch, every new account receives its first three months free, with no credit card required. After that, Tally costs a flat monthly fee with no per-customer charges and no surprise add-ons. We want pricing to be one less thing you have to think about.

Tally is available today on iPhone, Android, and the web. Try it with your next invoice or booking, and tell us what works and what does not. Your feedback will shape what we build next, and we are grateful to have you with us from the beginning.
