**Subject: Your 2023 Consolidated Form 1099 is ready**

Your Consolidated Form 1099 is now available in the **Tax Documents** section of your account. 

This single document compiles the reporting required for your non-registered taxable accounts:

* **1099-B:** Realized capital gains and losses, categorized by short-term and long-term holdings. This reflects adjustments for wash sales and distinguishes between covered securities (cost basis reported to the IRS) and non-covered securities.
* **1099-DIV:** Total ordinary dividends, qualified dividends, and any capital gain distributions.
* **1099-INT:** Taxable interest earned on cash sweeps and credit balances.
* **1099-MISC:** Royalty payments or substitute payments received in lieu of dividends.

**Before you file:**
Check the top right corner of the document to see if it is marked **Preliminary** or **Final**. If you hold mutual funds, foreign equities, or REITs, income reclassifications may occur through mid-February. If marked Preliminary, wait for the final version before submitting your return to avoid having to file an amended 1040.

You can import these figures directly into major tax preparation software using your 8-digit Account Number and the Document ID located on page 1.
