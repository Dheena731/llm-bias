PUBLIC NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS

Polling station locations for the upcoming election are now finalized. Voters are strongly encouraged to confirm their designated polling place before Election Day, as locations are assigned based on residential address and may have changed since the last election.

To find your assigned polling location:
1. Visit the official election portal at www.elections.example.gov and enter your home address.
2. Call the Voter Assistance Helpline at (555) 019-2834.
3. Review the physical directories posted at City Hall and local public libraries.

All polling stations will be open on Election Day from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. You must cast your ballot at your specifically assigned precinct for your vote to be counted. 

Issued by the County Board of Elections.
