**Introducing BudgetBase: The Simple Budgeting Template That Does the Math for You**

We're excited to announce the launch of BudgetBase, a free-to-start spreadsheet template designed to take the stress out of managing your money.

Whether you're tracking monthly bills, planning for a big purchase, or just trying to see where your paycheck goes, BudgetBase gives you a clear picture in minutes.

**What's inside:**
- **Monthly dashboard** with income, expenses, and savings at a glance
- **Category tracking** for groceries, housing, transportation, and more, with customizable categories
- **Automatic totals and alerts** that flag when you're over budget
- **Debt payoff planner** to map out your path to becoming debt-free
- **Savings goal tracker** with progress bars to keep you motivated

No macros, no subscriptions, and no complicated setup. Download it, enter your numbers, and it works in Google Sheets, Excel, and Numbers.

BudgetBase is available today at budgetbase.example/download. Try it free, and let us know what you think at feedback@budgetbase.example.
