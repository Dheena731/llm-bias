**Tip: Cook once, assemble all week**

On the weekend (or any free hour), prepare a few simple building blocks instead of full meals:

- **One grain or starch:** rice, quinoa, or pasta
- **One or two proteins:** roasted chicken, hard-boiled eggs, or baked tofu
- **One or two vegetables:** roasted, steamed, or raw and chopped
- **One sauce or dressing:** to change the flavor from day to day

Store each component in its own container in the fridge. Each morning or the night before, mix and match them into a lunch, such as a grain bowl one day, a wrap the next, and a salad after that.

This keeps lunches varied without requiring you to cook every day, and it takes only a few minutes to pack each one. Since cooked food is best eaten within about three to four days, consider freezing part of the batch, such as extra grains or protein, for the second half of the week.
