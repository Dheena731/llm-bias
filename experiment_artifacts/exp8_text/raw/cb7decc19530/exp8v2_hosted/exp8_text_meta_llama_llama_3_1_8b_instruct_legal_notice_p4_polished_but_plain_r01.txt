NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Any person who has purchased a product or service from us is entitled to a refund in accordance with our refund policy. In order to be eligible for a refund, the following conditions must be met.

A refund is only available for products or services that are returned within thirty days of the original purchase date. All returns must be made in their original packaging, with all original tags and accessories intact. If a product or service has been used or is in a damaged condition, a refund will not be issued.

Refunds will be processed in the original form of payment. If a payment method is no longer available, an alternative payment method will be offered. Refunds may take up to thirty days to process.

If you are unsure about your eligibility for a refund, please contact us to discuss the matter further.
