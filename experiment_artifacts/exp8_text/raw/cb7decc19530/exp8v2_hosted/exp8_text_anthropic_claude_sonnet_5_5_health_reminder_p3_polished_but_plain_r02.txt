This is a reminder to book your annual health checkup. Routine exams are one of the simplest ways to catch small problems before they become serious, and they give you and your doctor a baseline to compare against in future years. If it has been twelve months or more since your last visit, now is a good time to schedule one.

Before you call, check your insurance coverage, since many plans cover preventive visits at no cost. Choose a time of year that is easy to remember, such as your birthday month, so the appointment becomes a habit. When you book, ask whether you need to fast beforehand for blood work, and request any lab orders in advance if that is an option.

In the days before the visit, gather a list of your current medications and supplements, note any symptoms or questions you want to raise, and bring records of recent vaccinations or tests from other providers. Jotting down your family medical history updates is also helpful. Taking ten minutes to prepare will make the appointment more productive.

Please make the call or open the online portal today and put the date on your calendar before you do anything else. A small effort now is well worth the peace of mind.
