To maximize freshness, store herbs according to their texture. Trim the stems of **tender herbs** (such as basil, cilantro, and parsley) and place them upright in a jar with an inch of water; loosely cover with a plastic bag and refrigerate. Wrap **woody herbs** (such as rosemary, thyme, and sage) in a slightly damp paper towel and store them in a resealable bag in the refrigerator. **Exception:** Basil is sensitive to cold and may discolor in the fridge; store it at room temperature in water instead.
