It's time for your weekly fitness check-in. Take a few minutes to look back over the past seven days and see how your efforts measured up against your goals. Consider how many workouts you completed, how your energy and recovery felt, and whether any numbers, such as steps, distance, weights, or active minutes, moved in the right direction. Notice what worked well so you can repeat it, and be honest about what got in the way, whether that was a busy schedule, poor sleep, or low motivation.

Progress is rarely a straight line, so try to focus on patterns rather than a single good or bad day. Once you have a clear picture, choose one small adjustment for the coming week, such as scheduling your sessions in advance, adding a short walk after meals, or going to bed a little earlier. A modest, specific change is far easier to keep than a complete overhaul. Finally, take a moment to acknowledge what you accomplished. Every week of consistent effort builds the foundation for lasting results.
