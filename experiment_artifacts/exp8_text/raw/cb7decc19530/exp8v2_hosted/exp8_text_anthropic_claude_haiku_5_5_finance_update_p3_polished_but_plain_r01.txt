We're excited to announce a new savings goal feature, designed to make it easier to plan for what matters most to you. Whether you're building an emergency fund, saving for a vacation, or working toward a down payment, you can now create a dedicated goal, set a target amount, and choose a target date that fits your timeline.

Once a goal is set, the app shows your progress at a glance, including how much you have saved, how much remains, and whether you're on track to reach the goal by your chosen date. You can add funds manually at any time, and you can adjust the target or deadline if your plans change. Each goal keeps its own running history, so you can see how your contributions have added up over time.

To help you stay consistent, you can also set up recurring transfers from your checking account to a specific goal. Choose the amount and frequency, and the app will handle the rest. You can pause or cancel a recurring transfer whenever you like, and you will receive a notification before each scheduled transfer runs.

The savings goal feature is available starting today in the Savings section of the app. To begin, tap Add Goal, give it a name, and enter your target. If you have questions or run into any issues, our support team is happy to help.
