To prepare a quick and delicious weeknight dinner, consider meal prepping a protein in advance, such as grilling chicken breasts or cooking ground beef, and storing it in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. This allows you to quickly assemble a variety of meals, such as tacos, salads, or pasta dishes, by simply adding the pre-cooked protein to your chosen ingredients. By doing so, you can save time during the week and still enjoy a homemade meal, even on the busiest of nights.
