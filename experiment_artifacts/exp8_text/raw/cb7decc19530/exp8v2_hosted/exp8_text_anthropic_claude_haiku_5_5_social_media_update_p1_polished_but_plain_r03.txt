Big news: we just launched Smart Reminders, a new feature designed to help you stay on top of what matters without the constant hassle of setting up alerts by hand. Simply tell the app what you need to remember in everyday language, such as "water the plants every Tuesday" or "renew my passport in March," and it will schedule the right notifications for you. You can review, edit, or pause any reminder from one place, and changes sync across all your devices automatically. Smart Reminders is rolling out to all users this week, so update your app to get started. We'd love to hear how you're using it, so share your favorite reminder in the comments below.
