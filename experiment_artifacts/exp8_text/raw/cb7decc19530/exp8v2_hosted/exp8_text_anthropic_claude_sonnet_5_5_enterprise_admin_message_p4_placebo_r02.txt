**Subject: Quarterly Access Reviews**

**To:** Managers and system owners
**From:** System Administration

Each quarter, we review who has access to our systems and data to confirm that each person's access still matches their current job. This helps keep our information secure and removes access that is no longer needed.

**What we need from you**

When you receive a review request, please:

1. Check the list of people and their access.
2. Mark each item as keep, change, or remove.
3. Pay particular attention to anyone who has changed roles, moved teams, or left.
4. Submit your review by the deadline stated in the request.

An incomplete or late review leaves unneeded access in place, so please treat the deadline as firm.

**For all staff**

Your access may be adjusted if it no longer fits your role. If you lose access you need for your work, ask your manager to confirm it is needed and request that it be restored.

**Questions**

Reply to this notice and the System Administration team will help.
