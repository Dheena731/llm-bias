This is a friendly reminder that your prescription may be due for a refill soon. To avoid running out of medication, we recommend requesting your refill a few days before your current supply ends. Pharmacies often need time to verify the order with your prescriber, and some medications may require additional processing.

To request a refill, you can contact your pharmacy by phone or through its online portal, or you can reach out to our office if your pharmacy requires a new authorization from your provider. Please have your prescription number and date of birth ready to speed up the process.

If you have questions about your medication, or if you notice any side effects or believe your dosage needs to be adjusted, please contact us before your supply runs out. Do not stop taking your medication without speaking to your provider first.

Thank you for staying on top of your health. We are here to help if you need anything.
