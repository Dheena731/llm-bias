To keep fresh herbs lasting longer, trim about half an inch off the stems, then place them upright in a glass with an inch or two of water, much like a bouquet of flowers. Cover the leaves loosely with a plastic bag and store the glass in the refrigerator. Tender herbs like basil are best kept at room temperature away from direct sunlight, since cold can blacken them, while hardier herbs such as rosemary, thyme, and parsley do well in the fridge. Change the water every couple of days for the freshest results.
