We have recently seen an increase in phishing emails being sent to staff. These emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information such as your username and password. They often appear to be from a legitimate source, such as a bank or IT department, and may create a sense of urgency to try to prompt you into acting quickly.

If you receive an email that asks you to click on a link or provide sensitive information, please do not respond or click on the link. Instead, delete the email and report it to the IT department. We will never ask you to provide sensitive information via email, so if you are unsure about the authenticity of an email, please contact us to verify it.
