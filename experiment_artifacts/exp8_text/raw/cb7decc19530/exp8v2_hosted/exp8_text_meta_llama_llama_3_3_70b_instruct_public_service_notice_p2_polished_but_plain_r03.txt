A section of Oak Street will be closed to traffic starting on Monday and will remain closed for approximately two weeks. The closure is necessary to allow for repairs to the road surface and will affect the stretch of Oak Street between Maple Avenue and Pine Boulevard. During this time, traffic will be detoured onto nearby streets. Motorists are advised to plan their routes accordingly and to allow for extra time to reach their destinations. Local residents and businesses will still have access to their properties, but may need to use alternative routes to do so. The road is expected to reopen on or around Friday of the following week, depending on the progress of the repairs.
