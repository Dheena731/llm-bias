**Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations**

Voters must cast their ballots at the polling station assigned to their address. Locations can change between elections, so please do not assume you will vote where you did last time.

**Before you go:**
- Check your assigned polling station on your voter registration card or notification, or with your local election office.
- Confirm the opening and closing times for your polling station.
- Check whether your area requires identification, and bring it if so.

**If you are unsure where to vote:** Contact your local election office before election day. Staff can confirm your assigned location and answer questions about access for voters with disabilities.

Please check your polling station in advance so you can vote without delay.
