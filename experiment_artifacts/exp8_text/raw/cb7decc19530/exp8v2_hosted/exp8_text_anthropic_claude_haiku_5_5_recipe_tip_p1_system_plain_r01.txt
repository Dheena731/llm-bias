Here's a time-saving tip: when you cook rice, pasta, or grains for dinner, make a double batch and refrigerate the extra in a covered container. The next night, you can turn it into a quick fried rice, a grain bowl, or a pasta skillet with a few vegetables and an egg or leftover protein, and dinner will be on the table in about fifteen minutes. Cooked rice keeps well for three to four days in the fridge, so this works best when you plan the second meal within that window. Spreading a damp paper towel over cold rice before reheating it also helps it warm evenly without drying out.
