PUBLIC NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE

The City Department of Public Works announces that Maple Street will be closed to all through traffic between Fourth Avenue and Ninth Avenue beginning at 7:00 a.m. on Monday, June 10, and continuing until approximately 5:00 p.m. on Friday, June 21, weather permitting.

The closure is necessary to allow crews to replace an aging water main and resurface the roadway. Work will take place on weekdays between 7:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. No work is planned for weekends, though the road may remain closed during that time.

Local residents will be able to reach their driveways from the nearest open end of the street, but access may be briefly interrupted while crews are working directly in front of a property. Residents are asked to move vehicles off the street before 7:00 a.m. each workday. Vehicles left in the work zone may be towed at the owner's expense.

Drivers should use Oak Boulevard or Cedar Lane as detour routes. Detour signs will be posted at both ends of the closure. Bus routes 12 and 15 will be rerouted, and temporary stops will be placed along Oak Boulevard. Pedestrians and cyclists are advised to use caution near the work zone and to follow posted directions from flaggers.

Emergency vehicles will be given access at all times. Water service may be interrupted for short periods, and affected households will receive advance notice by door hanger at least 24 hours beforehand.

The department apologizes for the inconvenience and thanks the community for its patience. For questions or updates, please call the Public Works information line at 555-0142 during regular business hours.
