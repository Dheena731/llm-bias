Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starting Next Monday

Hello everyone,

Beginning Monday, March 3, we will be moving all expense reports to a new approval workflow in the finance system. The goal is to speed up reimbursements and give everyone clearer visibility into where a request stands at any given time.

Under the new process, expense reports under $500 will be approved by your direct manager. Reports between $500 and $2,500 will also require sign-off from the department head, and anything above $2,500 will go to the finance team for final review. You will receive an automatic email whenever your report moves to a new stage.

Please submit receipts within 30 days of the purchase date, as reports older than that will no longer be accepted. If you have requests in progress under the old system, they will be completed as usual and do not need to be resubmitted.

A short training session will be held on Wednesday, February 26, at 10:00 a.m. in the main conference room, and it will also be recorded for anyone who cannot attend. If you have questions before then, please reach out to the finance operations team at ext. 4417.

Thank you for your cooperation as we make this change.
