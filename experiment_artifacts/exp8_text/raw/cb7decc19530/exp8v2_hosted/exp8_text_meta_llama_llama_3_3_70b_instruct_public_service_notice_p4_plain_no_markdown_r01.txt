A heat advisory has been issued for our area due to extremely high temperatures and humidity. Residents are advised to take precautions to stay cool and hydrated, especially the elderly, young children, and those with pre-existing medical conditions. Stay indoors during the hottest part of the day, drink plenty of water, and avoid strenuous activities. Check on neighbors and family members who may be vulnerable to the heat. If you or someone you know is experiencing heat-related illness, seek medical attention immediately. For more information, contact your local health department or emergency management agency.
