Thank you for sending your methods section. You have described your participant recruitment and survey procedure clearly, and the reader can follow the order of events from first contact through to data collection. Your decision to pilot the questionnaire with a small group before the main study is a good one, and it is worth stating explicitly in the text, since it strengthens the credibility of your instrument.

There are a few areas to strengthen. The sampling paragraph gives the final sample size but not the reasoning behind it. Please add a sentence or two explaining how you arrived at that number, whether through a power calculation or a practical constraint, and acknowledge any limitations this creates. Your description of the analysis also jumps directly to the software used without naming the statistical tests or explaining why they suit your research questions. Connecting each test to a specific question would make the logic much easier to evaluate.

Finally, check the tense throughout. Methods sections are conventionally written in the past tense, and a few sentences slip into the present. Consistency here will make the section read more smoothly.

Overall, this is a solid draft with a clear structure. If you revise the points above, the section will be in good shape. Please send the revised version when you are ready, and I am happy to look at it again.
