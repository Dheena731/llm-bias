Residents are advised that changes to the recycling collection schedule will take effect starting next month. The new schedule will have recyclables collected every other week, instead of weekly. This change is intended to improve the efficiency of the collection process and reduce costs. Residents can find their new collection schedule by checking the city's website or contacting the public works department. It is recommended that residents review the new schedule and make any necessary adjustments to their recycling routine to ensure that their materials are collected on the correct day. Additional information about the changes and tips for preparing recyclables can be found on the city's website.
