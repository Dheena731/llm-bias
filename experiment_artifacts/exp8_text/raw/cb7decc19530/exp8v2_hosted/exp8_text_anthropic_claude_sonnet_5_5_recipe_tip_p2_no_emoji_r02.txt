**Tip: Store herbs like a bouquet.**

For tender herbs such as cilantro, parsley, and dill, trim the stems and place them upright in a jar with an inch of water. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and refrigerate. Change the water every few days, and the herbs can stay fresh for up to two weeks.

For sturdier herbs such as rosemary, thyme, and sage, wrap them in a slightly damp paper towel, place them in a resealable bag, and keep them in the refrigerator's crisper drawer.

Basil is the exception. Keep it at room temperature in a jar of water on the counter, since cold temperatures cause the leaves to blacken.
