NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS

Residents are advised that polling stations for the upcoming election will be open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on Election Day. Please vote at the location assigned to your voting district.

Polling Locations:

District 1: Riverside Community Center, 120 River Road
District 2: Lincoln Elementary School Gymnasium, 45 Maple Avenue
District 3: Central Public Library, 300 Main Street
District 4: Oakwood Senior Center, 78 Oak Lane
District 5: Fire Station No. 2, 910 Hillcrest Drive

All locations are wheelchair accessible. Curbside voting is available for voters who are unable to enter the building.

What to Bring:
Please bring a valid photo ID. If you are unsure of your assigned polling place or district, contact the County Elections Office at (555) 123-4567 or visit the office website to look up your location using your home address.

Voters who are in line by closing time will be permitted to vote.

Thank you for taking part in the democratic process.

County Elections Office
