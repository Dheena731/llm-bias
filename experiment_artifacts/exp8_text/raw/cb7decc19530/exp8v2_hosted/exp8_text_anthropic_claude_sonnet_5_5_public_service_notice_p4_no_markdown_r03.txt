HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT

Issued by the City Office of Emergency Management

A heat advisory is in effect from 11:00 a.m. Thursday until 9:00 p.m. Saturday. Daytime temperatures are expected to reach 98 to 104 degrees Fahrenheit, with heat index values up to 110 degrees. Overnight lows will remain in the upper 70s, offering little relief.

Extreme heat can cause heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Children, older adults, people with chronic health conditions, outdoor workers, and pets are at the greatest risk.

What you can do:

- Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty.
- Stay in air-conditioned spaces whenever possible.
- Limit outdoor activity to early morning or evening hours, and take frequent breaks in the shade.
- Wear lightweight, light-colored, loose-fitting clothing.
- Never leave children, pets, or anyone else in a parked vehicle, even for a short time.
- Check on elderly neighbors, relatives, and those who live alone.

Cooling centers will be open daily from 10:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. at the Main Street Public Library, the Eastside Community Center, and the Riverside Senior Center. Free water will be available at each location.

Warning signs of heat illness include heavy sweating, dizziness, nausea, headache, muscle cramps, and confusion. If someone has a high body temperature, hot dry skin, or loses consciousness, call 911 immediately and move the person to a cooler place.

For updates and cooling center information, call 311 or visit the city website.
