**Time for Your Weekly Fitness Check-In**

Take a few minutes today to review your progress from the past week. Look at the workouts you completed, how you felt during them, and any goals you reached or missed.

A few questions to consider:
- Did you stay consistent with your planned activity?
- What went well, and what was challenging?
- Is there one small change you can make next week?

Reviewing your progress helps you stay motivated and adjust your plan as needed. Every step counts, so be proud of what you've done and keep moving forward.
