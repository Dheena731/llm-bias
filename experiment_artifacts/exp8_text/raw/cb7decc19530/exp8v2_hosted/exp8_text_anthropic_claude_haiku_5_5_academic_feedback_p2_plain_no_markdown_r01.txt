Thank you for sending your methods section. You have described your survey design clearly, and the reader can follow how you recruited participants and what instruments you used. Your explanation of the sampling procedure is especially helpful, since it lets someone judge how far your findings might generalize.

There are a few places where the section needs more precision. When you describe the questionnaire, state the exact number of items and the response scale, and note whether any items were adapted from earlier studies and how they were checked. In the data analysis paragraph, name the specific statistical test for each research question rather than saying that "appropriate analyses" were run, and briefly explain why that test fits your data. You should also report how you handled missing responses, since readers will want to know whether incomplete surveys were dropped or imputed.

Finally, check that your timeline is consistent. The dates you give for data collection and the length of the study period do not quite match, and a reader may pause over the discrepancy. Once these details are tightened, the section should give a reader everything needed to repeat your study.

Overall, this is a solid draft with a clear structure. Focus on the precision points above in your next revision, and feel free to send the revised version if you would like another look.
