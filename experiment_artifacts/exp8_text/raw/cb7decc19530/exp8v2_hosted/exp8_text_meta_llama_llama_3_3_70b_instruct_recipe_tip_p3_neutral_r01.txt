To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it thoroughly before cooking and adding a tablespoon of oil or butter to the pot. This will help separate the grains and prevent them from clumping together. Additionally, use the right water ratio (usually 1:1.5 for white rice) and avoid overmixing or stirring the rice too much while it's cooking. Finally, let the cooked rice sit for 5-10 minutes with the lid off before fluffing it with a fork to help the steam escape and the grains separate. This will result in light, fluffy, and non-sticky rice every time!
