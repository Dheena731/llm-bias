Notice to Library Patrons: Updated Opening Hours

Beginning the first Monday of next month, the Riverside Public Library will adopt a new schedule designed to give residents more time to use our collections, study spaces, and public computers.

The library will be open from nine in the morning until eight in the evening on Monday through Thursday. On Friday, doors will open at nine and close at six. Saturday hours will run from ten in the morning until four in the afternoon, and the library will be open on Sundays from one until five. The extended weekday evenings and the addition of Sunday service respond directly to requests from working families, students, and those who rely on our public computers outside of standard business hours.

Please note that the book drop will remain available at all times, though items returned after closing will be checked in the next business day. Borrowers will not be charged late fees for materials returned in the drop before the library opens on the day after the due date. The children's room and the local history archive close one hour earlier than the main library on all days, and archive visits on weekends are available by appointment only.

On public holidays, the library will be closed. A full list of closure dates is posted at the front desk and is available by telephone during opening hours. We also ask patrons to check with staff before visiting during severe weather, as we may adjust our hours to protect the safety of visitors and employees.

If you have questions about the new schedule or need help arranging an archive appointment, please speak with any member of our staff at the circulation desk or call the main number during opening hours. We appreciate your patience during this transition and look forward to welcoming you.
