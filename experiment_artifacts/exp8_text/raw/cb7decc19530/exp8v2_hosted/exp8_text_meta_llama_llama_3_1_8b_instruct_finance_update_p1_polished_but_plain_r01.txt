The new monthly spending summary is designed to provide a clear and concise overview of all transactions made within a given month. The summary includes a detailed breakdown of income and expenses, categorized by type, such as housing, transportation, and entertainment. It also includes a total for the month, which is calculated by subtracting the total expenses from the total income.

The summary will be delivered on a quarterly basis, providing a longer-term view of spending trends and habits. This will enable individuals to identify areas where they can make adjustments to optimize their finances and achieve their financial goals.

The summary will be presented in a straightforward and easy-to-understand format, making it simple for individuals to review and make informed decisions about their financial management.
