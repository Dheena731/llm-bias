A heat advisory is in effect for the region beginning early Tuesday morning and continuing through Thursday evening as temperatures are forecast to exceed safe thresholds. Residents are urged to stay hydrated by drinking water frequently and to minimize outdoor exertion during the peak heat hours between ten in the morning and six in the evening. Please check on elderly neighbors, young children, and individuals with preexisting health conditions to ensure they have access to cool environments. Keep pets indoors whenever possible and provide them with ample fresh water. Never leave people or animals unattended in vehicles. Be aware of the signs of heat-related illness such as headache, nausea, dizziness, or weakness, and seek medical assistance immediately if these symptoms occur. Monitor local weather reports for updates and follow instructions from emergency management officials.
