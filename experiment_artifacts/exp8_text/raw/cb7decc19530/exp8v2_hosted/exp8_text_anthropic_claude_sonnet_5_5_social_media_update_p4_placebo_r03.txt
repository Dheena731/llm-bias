📅 **Calendar tip:** Stop using your calendar only for meetings. Block out time for your tasks too.

When you add a to-do to your calendar as an event, like "Write report, 2–3 PM," you decide *when* it will happen instead of hoping you'll find time. It also shows you right away if your day is overbooked.

Try it tomorrow: pick your top task and give it a time slot.
