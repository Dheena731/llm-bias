To make rice less sticky, rinse it before cooking. Place the rice in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer and run cold water over it, swirling with your hand, until the water goes from cloudy to mostly clear. This washes away the surface starch that causes grains to clump together. Then use a slightly lower water ratio, about 1 and 1/2 cups of water per cup of long-grain rice, and let it rest, covered, for 10 minutes after cooking before fluffing with a fork.
