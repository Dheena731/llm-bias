Here is a short public notice about a heat advisory:

**HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for our area, effective from [insert date and time] to [insert date and time]. Temperatures are expected to soar into the mid-90s, with heat indices reaching up to 105 degrees.

**TAKE PRECAUTIONS TO STAY SAFE**

Residents are advised to take necessary precautions to protect themselves and their loved ones from the heat. This includes:

* Staying hydrated by drinking plenty of water
* Taking breaks in cool, shaded areas or air-conditioned spaces
* Avoiding strenuous outdoor activities during the hottest part of the day
* Checking on vulnerable neighbors, such as the elderly and young children
* Keeping an eye on pets and ensuring they have access to cool water and shade

**STAY INFORMED AND PLAN AHEAD**

Stay tuned to local news and weather reports for updates on the heat advisory. Plan your outdoor activities accordingly and take necessary precautions to stay safe. If you or someone you know is experiencing heat-related symptoms, seek medical attention immediately.

**HEAT SAFETY TIPS**

* Call 911 or visit a nearby hospital if you experience:
	+ Heat exhaustion symptoms: heavy sweating, pale skin, fast pulse, and dizziness
	+ Heat stroke symptoms: high body temperature, confusion, and loss of consciousness

Stay cool and stay safe!
