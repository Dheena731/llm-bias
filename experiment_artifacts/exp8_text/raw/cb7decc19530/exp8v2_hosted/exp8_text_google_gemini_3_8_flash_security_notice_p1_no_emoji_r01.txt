**SECURITY NOTICE: Avoid Password Reuse Across Work and Personal Accounts**

Reusing passwords creates a significant security risk for our organization. If a personal account or external service experiences a data breach, attackers immediately attempt to use those same compromised credentials to access corporate networks and sensitive company data.

To protect our systems, please follow these requirements:

*   **Use unique passwords:** Never use your work password for personal email, banking, social media, or other external websites.
*   **Differentiate internal tools:** Do not use identical passwords across multiple internal systems.
*   **Use a password manager:** Utilize company-approved password management software to generate and securely store strong, complex passwords.

If you are currently using your corporate password for any other account, change it immediately. 

For assistance or to report suspicious activity, contact the IT Security Helpdesk.
