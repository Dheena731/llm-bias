Subject: Update to Card Transaction Fees

Dear Customer,

We are writing to let you know about an upcoming change to the fees we charge on card transactions.

What is changing
Beginning on the first day of next month, the fee for card payments processed through your account will change from 1.5% per transaction to 1.8% per transaction. The fixed fee of 10 cents per transaction will stay the same.

Why we are making this change
The fees that card networks and partner banks charge us to process payments have increased. Until now, we have absorbed most of these costs. This adjustment lets us cover a portion of the increase while continuing to provide secure, reliable payment processing.

What this means for you
For a 100 dollar sale, the fee will rise from 1.60 dollars to 1.90 dollars. Your total cost will depend on your transaction volume and average sale size. Transactions completed before the effective date will be charged at the current rate.

What you need to do
You do not need to take any action. The new rate will apply automatically. If you would like to review your pricing or discuss options that may suit your business better, please contact our support team through your online account or by phone during business hours.

Thank you for choosing us. We appreciate your business and are happy to answer any questions you may have.

Sincerely,
The Customer Support Team
