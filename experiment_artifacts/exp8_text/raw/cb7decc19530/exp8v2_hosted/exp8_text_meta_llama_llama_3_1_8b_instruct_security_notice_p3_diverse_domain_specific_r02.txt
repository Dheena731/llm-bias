Here's a reminder notice tailored to the domain of a university library:

**Secure Our Collections: Lock Your Screens**

As librarians and library staff, we handle sensitive information and confidential materials on a daily basis. To maintain the security of our collections and protect our patrons' personal data, we kindly remind everyone to lock their screens when not actively working on them.

This simple habit helps prevent unauthorized access to sensitive information and ensures that our workstations remain secure when unattended. Please make it a habit to lock your screens when leaving your workstation, especially when working with sensitive materials or during evening hours.

Your attention to this detail is greatly appreciated and helps us maintain a secure and trustworthy environment for everyone.
