**NOTICE: Revised Hours at Millbrook Branch Library**
*Effective Monday, 3 March, until further notice*

Beginning 3 March, Millbrook Branch will be open these hours:

| | |
|---|---|
| Monday, Wednesday | 10 am – 8 pm |
| Tuesday, Thursday, Friday | 10 am – 5:30 pm |
| Saturday | 9 am – 1 pm |
| Sunday | Closed |

**What is changing:** Sunday opening is suspended, and Saturday closes at 1 pm instead of 4 pm. This follows the loss of two part-time circulation posts. Tuesday and Thursday evenings, previously open until 7, now close at 5:30.

**What stays the same:**
- The outdoor book drop is emptied every morning at 9:30, including Sundays and Mondays. Items returned after closing on Saturday are checked in Monday.
- Holds will wait 10 days instead of 7 for the duration of the change, so no one loses a place in the queue because of the shorter week.
- No overdue fines accrue on days the branch is closed.
- Wi-Fi in the parking lot and on the front steps runs around the clock.

**Local History Room:** open by appointment only, Tuesday and Thursday, 10 am – 3 pm. Call at least one day ahead.

**Meeting room bookings** made for Sunday or for Tuesday and Thursday after 5:30 pm will be moved or refunded. Staff will contact each group directly by 21 February.

**Closed on these dates:** Friday 18 April (Good Friday), Monday 26 May (Memorial Day).

If you cannot reach the branch during these hours, the Central Library on Pruitt Avenue is open Sundays, 12–5 pm, and will accept Millbrook returns and holds.

Questions: 555-0142, or ask at the front desk.

*Millbrook Branch Library, 41 Carver Road*
