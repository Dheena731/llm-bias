**Reminder: Lock Your Screen When You Step Away**

All staff are reminded to lock their computer screens whenever they leave their desks, even for a short time.

An unlocked screen can give anyone nearby access to confidential information, email, and company systems. Locking your screen takes a second and keeps our data protected.

**How to lock your screen:**
- **Windows:** Press the Windows key and L at the same time.
- **Mac:** Press Control, Command, and Q at the same time.

Thank you for helping keep our information secure.
