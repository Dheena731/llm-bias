Subject: Reminder to Lock Your Screen

Dear Colleagues,

This is a friendly reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even for a few minutes. Leaving a screen unlocked can expose sensitive company and client information to unauthorized individuals.

To lock your screen quickly, press Windows key + L on a Windows PC, or Control + Command + Q on a Mac.

Thank you for helping us keep our information secure.

Best regards,
IT Department
