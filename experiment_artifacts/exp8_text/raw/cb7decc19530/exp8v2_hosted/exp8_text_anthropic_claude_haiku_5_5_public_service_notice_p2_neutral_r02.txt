**PUBLIC NOTICE: Road Closure**

**Location:** Maple Avenue, between 5th Street and 9th Street

**Dates and Times:** Monday, March 10 through Friday, March 14, 2025, 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily

**Reason:** Scheduled water main replacement and street repairs

**Details:**
- Maple Avenue will be closed to all vehicle traffic during this period.
- Local residents and businesses will have access to their properties via side streets, but expect delays.
- Pedestrian access will be maintained along the sidewalks on both sides of the street.
- Signed detour routes will be posted at Maple Avenue and 4th Street, and at Maple Avenue and 10th Street.

**Alternate Routes:** Please use Oak Street or Pine Avenue as alternate east-west routes. Allow extra travel time during construction hours.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience as we work to improve local infrastructure. For questions, please contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567 or visit www.cityexample.gov/roadwork.
