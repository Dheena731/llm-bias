This notice serves to remind all staff of the ongoing threat of phishing emails and to outline proper procedures for handling suspicious messages. Phishing attempts are fraudulent communications designed to trick recipients into revealing sensitive information or installing malicious software. Employees should carefully review any unexpected email, particularly those that create urgency, request passwords or financial details, or contain unfamiliar links and attachments. If an email appears questionable, do not open attachments, click on embedded links, or respond directly. Instead, forward the entire message to the IT security team at helpdesk at company dot com for verification and quarantine. The company maintains strict protocols to protect our systems and data, and your vigilance is essential to maintaining that security. Thank you for your attention to this matter and for helping us keep our workplace safe from cyber threats.
