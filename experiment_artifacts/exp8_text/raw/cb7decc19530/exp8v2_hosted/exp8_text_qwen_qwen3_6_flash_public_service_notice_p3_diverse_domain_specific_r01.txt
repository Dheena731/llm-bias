**NOTICE: CURBSIDE RECYCLING COLLECTION ADJUSTMENTS**  
**Effective: Monday, November 4, 2024**

Collection frequency shifts to biweekly on Tuesdays for Neighborhoods 1–4. Wednesdays remain unchanged for Neighborhoods 5–8. Street sweeping days are unaffected.

Three item categories have been removed from the blue-cart stream: plastic bags/film, window/mirrored glass, and yard debris. Place these in the black refuse bin or transport them to the County Transfer Station on weekends. Any cart with visible non-compliant items will be tagged and skipped that round.

Paperboard, newspaper, corrugated cardboard, aluminum/steel cans, milk/juice cartons, jugs, bottles, and rigid plastics marked #1 through #7 continue as accepted. Items must be empty and lightly rinsed. Flattened boxes should be placed inside the cart to save space.

Updated material guide, neighborhood zone map, and skip-list protocol are available at [cityurl]/recycling-updates. For route disputes or cart replacements, call 555-0198 (Mon–Fri, 7 a.m.–5 p.m.). 

Solid Waste Operations will begin distributing reminder stickers starting October 28. Please verify your neighborhood assignment before the first Tuesday collection.
