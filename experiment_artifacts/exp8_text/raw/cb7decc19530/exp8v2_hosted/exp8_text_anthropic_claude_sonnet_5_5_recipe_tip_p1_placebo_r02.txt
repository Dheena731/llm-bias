**Weeknight Cooking Tip: Prep While You Preheat**

When you get home, turn on the oven or start heating a pan first, then use that waiting time to prep everything else. While the oven warms up (usually 10 to 15 minutes), chop your vegetables, season your protein, and measure out any sauces or spices.

**Why it works:** Heating takes time no matter what, so doing your prep during that window means you aren't standing idle and your dinner reaches the table sooner.

**Example:** Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). While it warms, cut broccoli and sweet potatoes into bite-sized pieces, toss them with oil, salt, and pepper, and spread them on a sheet pan. Season chicken thighs or a fish fillet and add them to the pan. Everything roasts together, and you have only one pan to wash.

**Bonus step:** Cut vegetables to a similar size so they cook evenly. Denser vegetables like sweet potatoes should be cut smaller than quick-cooking ones like broccoli, or added to the pan later. Check that the chicken is fully cooked (165°F or 74°C at the thickest part) before serving.
