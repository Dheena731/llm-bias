We are excited to announce the launch of Ledgerly, a simple mobile and web app built to help small business owners manage the everyday financial tasks that often pull them away from the work they love. Ledgerly brings invoicing, expense tracking, and cash flow reporting into one clean space, so you can see exactly where your money stands without wrestling with spreadsheets or paying for software built for large enterprises.

With Ledgerly, you can create and send professional invoices in under a minute, set up automatic payment reminders that keep customers on schedule, and snap a photo of any receipt to log it instantly. Our dashboard shows your income, outgoing costs, and upcoming bills at a glance, helping you make confident decisions about hiring, inventory, and growth.

Ledgerly is available today for free on iOS and Android, with an affordable plan for businesses that need advanced reporting and multiple users. We designed it with input from dozens of shop owners, freelancers, and service providers, and we will keep listening as we add features in the months ahead.

Download Ledgerly today and spend less time on paperwork and more time on what matters most: running your business.
