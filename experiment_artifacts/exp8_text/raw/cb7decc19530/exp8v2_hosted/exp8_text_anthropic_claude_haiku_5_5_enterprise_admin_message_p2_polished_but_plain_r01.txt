Subject: New expense approval workflow starting next month

Dear team,

Beginning March 1, all expense reports will move through a new approval workflow in our finance system. The change is designed to shorten reimbursement times, improve visibility into spending, and reduce the number of reports returned for missing information.

Under the new process, employees will submit expense reports through the finance portal within 14 days of incurring the cost. Each report will first go to the employee's direct manager for review. Reports under $500 will then be approved automatically once the manager signs off, while reports of $500 or more will also require approval from the relevant department head. Finance will conduct a final check on all reports before payment is issued.

To prepare, please make sure receipts are uploaded as clear digital images at the time of submission, and include a business purpose for every line item. Managers should aim to review pending requests within two business days. A short training session will be held on February 20, and the recording and a quick-reference guide will be available on the finance intranet page afterward.

If you have questions before the rollout, contact the finance operations team at financeops@company.com or reply to this message. Thank you for helping us make this transition a smooth one.

Best regards,
