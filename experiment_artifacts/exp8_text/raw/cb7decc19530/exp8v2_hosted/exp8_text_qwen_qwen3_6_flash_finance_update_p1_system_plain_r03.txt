We have introduced a new monthly spending summary to help you keep better track of your finances. This report compiles all your transactions from the beginning to the end of each calendar month and organizes them by category. You will find the summary automatically generated on the first business day following each month and accessible through your main dashboard under the reports section. The document highlights your total expenditures, compares them to your previous months, and flags any unusual spending patterns so you can make informed adjustments. If you notice discrepancies or have questions about how certain charges were categorized, you can review the detailed transaction log attached to the summary or contact our support team directly. We recommend checking this report regularly to stay aligned with your budgeting goals and to catch any recurring subscriptions or unexpected fees early.
