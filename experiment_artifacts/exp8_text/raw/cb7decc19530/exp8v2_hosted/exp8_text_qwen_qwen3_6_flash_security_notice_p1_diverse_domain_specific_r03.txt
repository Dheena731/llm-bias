**Subject:** Security Advisory: Password Reuse Risk / Credential Stuffing Mitigation

Our threat monitoring indicates a sustained increase in authentication attempts utilizing credentials exposed in third-party data breaches. 

When you reuse a password across personal and corporate environments, an external compromise becomes a direct entry point. Attackers automate credential stuffing within minutes of a public leak. If your personal email or service account appears in a breach database, that combination is now indexed and actively targeted against our identity provider. 

Successful reuse bypasses perimeter defenses and grants immediate lateral access, often evading standard anomaly detection because the login originates from a valid user profile.

**Required Action:**
1.  **Audit:** Verify if any active corporate credentials have appeared in recent breach disclosures using the internal lookup tool linked [Here].
2.  **Rotate:** Immediately change any password shared with non-work platforms. 
3.  **Isolate:** Treat enterprise credentials as distinct assets. Do not sync personal vaults or export personal passwords to work devices.

Personal convenience does not override perimeter integrity. Failure to align credentials with current security standards may result in temporary account suspension pending verification.
