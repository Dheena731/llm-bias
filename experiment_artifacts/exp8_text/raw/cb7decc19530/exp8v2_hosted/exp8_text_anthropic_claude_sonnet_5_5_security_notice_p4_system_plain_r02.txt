Notice: Please Enable Two-Factor Authentication

To keep your account and our shared information secure, we are asking all users to turn on two-factor authentication as soon as possible. This extra layer of protection requires a second form of verification, such as a code sent to your phone or generated by an authenticator app, in addition to your password. Even if someone learns your password, they will not be able to access your account without that second step.

Setting it up takes only a few minutes. Sign in to your account, open the security section of your settings, and choose the option to enable two-factor authentication. Follow the prompts to select your preferred verification method and confirm it with a test code. We recommend using an authenticator app because it is generally safer than text messages. Be sure to save the backup codes you are given and store them somewhere secure, since you will need them if you lose access to your device.

Please complete this step by the end of the month. After that date, accounts without two-factor authentication may be limited until it is turned on. If you run into any trouble during setup, contact the support team and we will be glad to help you through the process.

Thank you for helping us keep everyone's information safe.
