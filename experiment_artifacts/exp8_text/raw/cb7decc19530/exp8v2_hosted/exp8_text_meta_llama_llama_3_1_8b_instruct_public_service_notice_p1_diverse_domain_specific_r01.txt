**Notice to Library Patrons**

As of January 2023, the Downtown Branch of the Local Library System will be adjusting its opening hours to better serve the community.

The new hours will be:

* Monday to Thursday: 9am to 8pm
* Friday: 9am to 6pm
* Saturday: 10am to 5pm
* Sunday: 1pm to 4pm

Please note that the library will be closed on Mondays during the summer months (June to August) for maintenance and renovation.

Additionally, the library's study rooms and meeting rooms are available for reservation during the library's open hours. Please contact the library staff for more information and to book your space.

We appreciate your understanding and look forward to serving you during our new hours.
