Latency refers to the delay between a user interacting with a web application and receiving a response. It matters because human perception of speed is highly sensitive to even fractional second delays. When latency increases, users experience frustration, lose their train of thought, or leave the site altogether. This directly impacts business metrics such as engagement, conversion rates, and customer retention. On the technical side, high latency often indicates inefficient data handling, poor network routing, or unoptimized backend processes, which can cascade into higher server loads and increased infrastructure costs. Since modern users expect near instant feedback across all devices and regions, keeping latency low is essential for delivering a reliable experience, preserving brand reputation, and meeting performance targets.
