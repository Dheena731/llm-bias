**Notice: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, report it immediately. Prompt reporting helps protect company data and limits security risks.

**What to do:**

1. **Notify IT Support right away.** Contact the IT Help Desk by phone or email as soon as you realize the laptop is missing. Do not wait until the end of the day or week.
2. **Inform your manager.** Let your supervisor know what happened.
3. **File a written report.** Within 24 hours, submit an incident report with the following details:
   - Date, time, and location where the laptop was last seen
   - Circumstances of the loss or theft
   - Type of data or files that may have been stored on the device
   - Whether the device was locked or encrypted
4. **Report theft to the police.** If the laptop was stolen, file a police report and provide the report number to IT and HR.
5. **Change your passwords.** Update passwords for any accounts you accessed from the laptop, once IT advises you to do so.

**What IT will do:**
- Remotely lock or wipe the device where possible
- Disable your access credentials as needed
- Assess any potential data exposure

**Please note:** Failure to report a lost or stolen device promptly may result in disciplinary action. Reporting quickly will not be held against you, and our priority is to protect company and customer information.

For questions, contact the IT Help Desk.
