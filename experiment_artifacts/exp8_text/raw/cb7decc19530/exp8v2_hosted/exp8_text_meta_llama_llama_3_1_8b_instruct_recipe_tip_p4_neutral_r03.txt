Here's a short tip for planning lunches for a busy week:

**Tip: "Pack and Prep in Advance"**

Take a few minutes on the weekend or one day a week to plan and prep your lunches for the next few days. Here's how:

* Decide on your meals for the week (e.g. leftovers, sandwiches, soups, salads)
* Make a grocery list and shop for any ingredients you need
* Cook and portion out your lunches for the next few days (e.g. grill chicken, cook rice and beans, chop veggies)
* Store your prepared lunches in airtight containers in the fridge or freezer
* Pack your lunches in the morning or the night before, so they're ready to go in the morning

By planning and prepping your lunches in advance, you'll save time and energy during the week, and ensure that you have healthy and delicious meals to fuel your day.
