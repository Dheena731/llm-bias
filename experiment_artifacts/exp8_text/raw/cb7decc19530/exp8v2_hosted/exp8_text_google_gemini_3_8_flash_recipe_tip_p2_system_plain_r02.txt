To keep fresh herbs vibrant for weeks, treat soft-stemmed varieties like cilantro, parsley, and dill like a bouquet of flowers by trimming the bottoms and placing them upright in a jar with an inch of water, then covering them loosely with a plastic bag in the refrigerator. Keep basil stored in the same water-jar setup on your counter instead, as cold air turns its leaves black. For woody herbs like rosemary and thyme, loosely wrap them in a slightly damp paper towel and store them inside a sealed container or bag in the vegetable crisper.
