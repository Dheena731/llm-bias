Join us this Saturday, 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., for our Community Day at Riverside Park!

Enjoy live music, local food vendors, free kids' activities, and a neighborhood cleanup at 11 a.m. Bring a friend, a picnic blanket, and your reusable water bottle. Refill stations will be on site.

Everyone is welcome, and admission is free. Volunteers are still needed, so if you'd like to help, send us a message.

See you at the park!
