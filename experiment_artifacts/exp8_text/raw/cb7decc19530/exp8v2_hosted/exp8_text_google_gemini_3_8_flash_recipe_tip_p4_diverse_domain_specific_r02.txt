Stop assembling complete, pre-portioned meals into plastic containers on Sunday. By Wednesday, the trapped moisture ruins the texture, the flavors homogenize, and you end up buying takeout just to avoid eating soggy grain bowls. 

Instead, prep modular components that resist refrigeration fatigue: cook one dense, forgiving base (like braised French green lentils or roasted winter squash), roast a sturdy protein, and mix a high-acid, punchy condiment (a sharp salsa verde, pickled red onions, or chili crisp). Store them separately. Assembling at your desk takes ninety seconds, keeps raw greens crisp, and lets you change the flavor profile so you aren't eating the exact same meal five days in a row.
