SECURITY NOTICE: AVOID PASSWORD REUSE

All Staff,

Reusing passwords across multiple accounts creates a significant security risk for our organization. If an external website or personal account experiences a data breach, attackers will immediately attempt to use those stolen credentials to access our internal systems.

Please follow these security guidelines:

1. Never use your work password for personal accounts, or vice versa.
2. Ensure every account you access uses a unique, complex password.
3. Utilize the company-approved password manager to create and securely store your credentials.

If you suspect that any of your passwords have been exposed or compromised, update them immediately and contact the IT Help Desk.

Thank you for your commitment to keeping our workplace secure.

IT Security Team
