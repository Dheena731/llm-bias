**PUBLIC SERVICE ANNOUNCEMENT: LIBRARY OPERATING HOURS**

The Public Library wishes to remind all residents of our current schedule of operations. 

**Regular Hours:**
* Monday through Thursday: 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM
* Friday and Saturday: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
* Sunday: 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM

The library will be closed on all officially recognized state and federal holidays. 

Please note that our digital catalog, including e-books, audiobooks, and academic databases, remains accessible 24 hours a day, 7 days a week through the library website. Book drop boxes located outside the main entrance are also available for item returns at any time.

For assistance, renewals, or event schedules, please visit our front desk during operating hours or contact our support staff at 555-0198.
