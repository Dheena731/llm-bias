NOTICE: RECYCLING COLLECTION SCHEDULE UPDATE

Dear Residents,

Please be advised that the weekly recycling collection schedule will change starting June 1, 2024. Collections will now occur on Tuesdays instead of Mondays. All other waste and yard trash pickup days remain unchanged.

To ensure your recycling is collected, please place your approved containers at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on collection day. Continue to rinse containers and do not bag recyclables. Only loose items placed directly in the bin or cart will be picked up.

This adjustment supports improved route efficiency and helps reduce missed collections. If you have questions or need to report a missed pickup, please call 555-0198 or visit cityservices.gov/recycling.

Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining a cleaner, more sustainable community.

City Waste Management Division
May 20, 2024
