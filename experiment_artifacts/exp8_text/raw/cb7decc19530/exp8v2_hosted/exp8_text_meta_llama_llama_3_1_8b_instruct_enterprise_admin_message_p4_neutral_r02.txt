Here is a short admin notice about quarterly access reviews:

**Quarterly Access Reviews**

As part of our ongoing efforts to ensure the security and integrity of our systems, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews for all users.

**What to expect:**

* A review of your current access levels and permissions
* Verification that your access is still necessary for your role and responsibilities
* Removal of unnecessary access to prevent unauthorized access to sensitive data

**When and how:**

* Reviews will be conducted on a quarterly basis, with the first review taking place on [Date]
* You will receive email notification and instructions on how to participate in the review process
* You may be asked to confirm or update your access requests through our online system

**Why:**

* To ensure that access to our systems is only granted to those who truly need it
* To prevent unauthorized access to sensitive data
* To maintain the security and integrity of our systems

If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the IT team. We appreciate your cooperation in this matter and your commitment to maintaining the security of our systems.
