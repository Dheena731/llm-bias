**NOTICE: Beware of Phishing Emails**

**To:** All Staff
**From:** IT Security Team
**Subject:** Recent Increase in Phishing Attempts

We have seen a rise in phishing emails targeting employees. These messages are designed to look legitimate in order to steal login credentials, financial information, or install harmful software.

**Warning signs to watch for:**
- Unexpected requests for passwords, payment, or personal information
- Urgent or threatening language, such as "Your account will be closed today"
- Sender addresses that look slightly off or do not match the company name
- Suspicious links or unexpected attachments
- Poor grammar or unusual formatting

**What you should do:**
- Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails.
- Do not reply to the sender or provide any information.
- Report the email using the "Report Phishing" button or forward it to security@yourcompany.com.
- If you have already clicked a link or shared information, contact IT immediately at ext. 1234.

When in doubt, verify the request by contacting the sender through a known phone number or a separate message. Never use the contact details provided in the suspicious email.

Thank you for helping keep our organization secure.

**IT Security Team**
