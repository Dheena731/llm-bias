To ensure the continued security of your account and the protection of your personal information, we require all users to enable two-factor authentication on their profiles effective immediately. Two-factor authentication adds a vital layer of defense by requiring a second form of verification in addition to your password, significantly reducing the risk of unauthorized access even if your credentials are compromised. To activate this feature, please navigate to your account settings within the application or website interface and select the security or privacy section where you will find the option to configure two-step verification. You may be asked to download a compatible authentication app on your mobile device or register a hardware key to generate the necessary codes. Once configured, we recommend saving your recovery codes in a secure location to prevent loss of access. Failure to complete this setup by the stated deadline may result in restricted account functionality, so we encourage you to address this promptly. If you encounter any difficulties during the process, please refer to our help documentation or contact support for assistance. We appreciate your cooperation in maintaining a secure environment for all users.
