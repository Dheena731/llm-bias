**NOTICE OF CHANGE TO ACCOUNT TERMS**

To all customers of XYZ Bank (hereinafter referred to as "the Bank") who hold a deposit account with the Bank (hereinafter referred to as "the Account"):

The Bank hereby gives notice of a change to the terms and conditions of the Account, effective as of [insert date, e.g. 15 February 2023].

The change relates to the calculation of interest on the Account, specifically the application of the "Tiered Interest Rate Structure" (as defined below) to the daily balance of the Account.

Prior to the change, the Bank applied a fixed interest rate of 2.5% per annum to the entire balance of the Account. From the effective date, the Bank will apply the Tiered Interest Rate Structure, which will pay the following interest rates on the daily balance of the Account:

* Up to $1,000.00: 1.25% per annum
* $1,000.01 to $10,000.00: 2.25% per annum
* Exceeding $10,000.00: 3.50% per annum

The change is made in accordance with the Bank's powers under the Banking Act 1997 and the Account terms and conditions.

You are advised to review the updated Account terms and conditions, which are available on the Bank's website or at any of the Bank's branches. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Bank's customer service department at [insert contact details].

By continuing to use the Account after the effective date, you will be deemed to have accepted the change to the Account terms and conditions.

**Definition:** "Tiered Interest Rate Structure" means a system of interest rates applied to the daily balance of the Account, where different interest rates are applied to different balances within the Account.

**Important:** This notice is intended for the exclusive use of customers of XYZ Bank who hold a deposit account with the Bank. It is not intended to be taken as advice or guidance by any other person or entity.
