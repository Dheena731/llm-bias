Here are a few options, ranging from a formal announcement to a quick social media update:

**Option 1: Standard / Formal Notice (Best for websites, community boards, or print)**

> **NOTICE TO ALL VOTERS: Polling Station Locations for Upcoming Election**
> 
> The upcoming **[Name of Election]** will take place on **[Date of Election]**. Polls will be open from **[Start Time, e.g., 7:00 AM]** to **[End Time, e.g., 8:00 PM]**.
> 
> Please be advised that you must cast your ballot at your **assigned** polling location based on your registered residential address. Some voting locations may have changed since the last election.
> 
> **How to find your polling station:**
> * **Online:** Visit **[Website URL, e.g., www.elections.gov/find-station]** and enter your postal code/address.
> * **By Phone:** Call the Election Helpline at **[Phone Number]**.
> * **Voter Card:** Check the official polling card sent to you by mail.
> 
> *Reminder: Please ensure you bring an accepted form of photo ID with you to the polls.*

***

**Option 2: Short / Social Media or SMS Notice**

> 🗳️ **Election Day is [Date]! Do you know where to vote?**
> 
> Polling stations will be open from **[Start Time] to [End Time]**. Your voting location is assigned based on your address and may have changed since you last voted. 
> 
> Find your assigned polling station before you go: **[Insert Link]** or call **[Phone Number]**. 
> 
> Don't forget to bring your ID!
