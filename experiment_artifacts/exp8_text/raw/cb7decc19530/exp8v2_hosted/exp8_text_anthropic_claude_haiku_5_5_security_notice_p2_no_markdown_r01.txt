IMPORTANT NOTICE: How to Spot and Report Phishing Emails

Phishing emails are fraudulent messages designed to trick you into revealing passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information, or into clicking malicious links or opening infected attachments. These messages often look like they come from trusted sources such as colleagues, IT support, banks, or senior leaders.

Warning signs to watch for:

- A sense of urgency, such as threats of account closure or demands for immediate action
- Unexpected requests for passwords, login details, or payment information
- Sender addresses that look almost right but contain small misspellings or unusual domains
- Generic greetings like "Dear user" instead of your name
- Links that lead to unfamiliar websites when you hover over them
- Unexpected attachments, especially zip files or documents asking you to enable macros

What to do:

- Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails
- Do not reply to the message or share any information
- Verify unusual requests by contacting the sender through a known phone number or in person
- Report the email immediately to the IT Service Desk using the "Report Phishing" button in your email client, or forward it to security@company.com

If you think you have already clicked a link or entered your password, contact the IT Service Desk right away. Acting quickly can prevent serious harm to you and the organization.
