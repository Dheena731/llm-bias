To our valued merchants,

We are writing to notify you of an upcoming change to your payment processing costs. 

Beginning on the first day of your next billing cycle, the transaction fee applied to credit and debit card payments processed through your account will be updated. This adjustment reflects increased network operating expenses and helps support ongoing improvements to our payment security and transaction reliability.

The updated rates will apply automatically to all card transactions processed from that date onward. You can review your complete, updated fee schedule at any time by logging in to your account portal and navigating to the billing section.

If you have any questions regarding this change, our merchant support team is available to assist you. Thank you for your continued partnership.
