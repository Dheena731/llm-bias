Your end-of-year tax statement summarizes the income you received and the taxes already withheld or paid during the past year. It is the key document you will need when preparing your tax return, so please keep it with your financial records.

Most statements show your total earnings, the amount of federal, state, and local taxes withheld from your pay or payments, and any Social Security and Medicare contributions. Some also list deductions or credits that affect how much you owe or may receive back. If you have several income sources, such as a salary, freelance work, or investment income, you may receive separate statements for each, and all of them should be reported.

Please review the figures carefully. Compare the income and withholding amounts with your own records, such as pay stubs or bank statements. If anything looks incorrect or is missing, contact the issuer right away so they can send a corrected version before you file.

If you have questions about how a particular item affects your return, please reach out before the filing deadline. We are glad to walk through the statement with you.
