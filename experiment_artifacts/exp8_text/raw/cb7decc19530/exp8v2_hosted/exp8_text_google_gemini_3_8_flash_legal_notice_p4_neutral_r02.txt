**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY**

**1. Standard Eligibility Window**  
To be eligible for a refund, refund requests must be formally submitted within **fourteen (14) calendar days** of the original transaction date. Proof of purchase, in the form of an original receipt or verified order number, is strictly required.

**2. Condition of Goods & Services**  
*   **Physical Goods:** Must be unopened, unused, in original packaging, and in the same merchantable condition as delivered, including all tags, manuals, and accessories.  
*   **Digital Goods & Services:** Eligibility ceases once software has been downloaded, a license key has been accessed, or professional services have commenced, except where mandated by applicable consumer protection laws.

**3. Non-Refundable Items**  
Unless required by law, refunds shall not be granted for: (a) items marked "Final Sale," "Clearance," or "As-Is"; (b) personalized or custom-made goods; and (c) gift cards or promotional vouchers.

**4. Processing and Deductions**  
Approved refunds will be remitted solely to the original method of payment within **seven (7) to ten (10) business days** following inspection and verification. Shipping, handling, and restocking fees are strictly non-refundable and will be deducted from the aggregate refund amount.

The Company reserves the right, in its sole and absolute discretion, to deny any refund request that fails to comply with the criteria outlined herein or shows evidence of misuse, fraudulent intent, or unauthorized alteration.
