To keep fresh herbs crisp and flavorful, trim the ends of the stems and place them upright in a glass with about an inch of water. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and store the glass in the refrigerator. Change the water every two days to prevent bacterial growth. For tougher varieties like rosemary or thyme, wrap the bunch in a lightly damp paper towel and place it inside a sealed container in the crisper drawer instead. This method preserves their aroma and texture for up to two weeks.
