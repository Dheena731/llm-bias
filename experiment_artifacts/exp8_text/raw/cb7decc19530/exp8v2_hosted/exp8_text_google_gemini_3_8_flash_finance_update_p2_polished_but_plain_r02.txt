We are writing to inform you of an upcoming update to our payment processing fees. Effective the first of next month, the processing fee applied to credit and debit card transactions will adjust to two point five percent per payment. This change reflects rising network costs from payment card providers and allows us to continue maintaining high standards of transaction security and system reliability. Alternative payment methods, such as direct bank transfers, will remain free of transaction charges. We value your business and understand that fee adjustments require planning on your part. If you have any questions regarding this change or wish to discuss alternative payment arrangements, please contact our support team.
