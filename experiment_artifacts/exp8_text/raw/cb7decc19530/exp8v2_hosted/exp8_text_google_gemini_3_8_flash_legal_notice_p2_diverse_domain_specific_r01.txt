**NOTICE OF MATERIAL AMENDMENT: GLOBAL RESEARCH DATA PRIVACY SCHEDULE**  
**DOCUMENT REF:** POL-PRIV-2024.3  
**EFFECTIVE DATE:** November 1, 2024  
**APPLICABILITY:** Sponsoring Institutions, Principal Investigators, and Registered Data Custodians  

Pursuant to Section 14.2 of the HelixPoint Master Data Platform Agreement, this notice serves as formal notification that HelixPoint BioSciences Corp. has amended its **Data Privacy Standard for Genomic Telemetry and Phenotypic Ingestion**. This revision supersedes Version 2022.1 and governs all active and archived sequencing pipelines.

### Summary of Material Amendments:

1. **Re-identification Risk and Variant-Level Metadata (Section 4.1):**  
   Clarified technical safeguards regarding deep-coverage whole-genome sequencing (WGS) data. Aligned de-identification methodologies with the Safe Harbor and Expert Determination standards under 45 CFR § 164.514(b). Variant Call Format (.VCF) files derived from rare disease cohorts (<50 participants) are now classified as Pseudonymized Special Category Data under GDPR Article 9 and require explicit secondary data-use attestation prior to egress.

2. **Retention and Destruction Schedules for Intermediate Artifacts (Section 7.3):**  
   Raw binary alignment/map (.BAM) and FASTQ files stored in multi-tenant ingestion buckets will be systematically depreciated to cold archive after ninety (90) days from quality-control clearance, and permanently purged at three hundred sixty-five (365) days, absent an executed Cohort Extension Addendum.

3. **Cross-Border Compute Operations (Section 11.4):**  
   Updated Standard Contractual Clauses (EU 2021/914, Module 2) to reflect the routing of federated analysis jobs through our Frankfurt (eu-central-1) enclave for computational queries originating from EEA member states.

### Obligation and Remediation:

Continued invocation of the HelixPoint Ingestion API or maintenance of active cloud storage volumes past **November 1, 2024**, constitutes legal acceptance of these modified terms. 

Institutions exercising rights of participant consent withdrawal (Right to Erasure) under existing Institutional Review Board (IRB) protocols must submit manifest identifiers via the verified cryptographic signature protocol outlined in **Appendix D** by no later than October 25, 2024, to intercept the scheduled automated model re-training pipeline.
