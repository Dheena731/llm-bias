**Introducing Your Monthly Spending Summary**

We're excited to announce a new Monthly Spending Summary, a clear overview of where your money went each month.

Every month, you'll receive:

- **Total spending** compared with the previous month
- **Category breakdowns** showing where your money goes, such as dining, transportation, and bills
- **Top merchants** you spent the most with
- **Trends** highlighting changes in your habits over time

The summary is available in the Insights tab starting the first week of each month, and you can turn off email notifications in Settings if you prefer to view it only in the app.

We built this to make budgeting easier without requiring spreadsheets or manual tracking. If you have feedback or questions, reach out through the Help Center. We'd love to hear how it works for you.
