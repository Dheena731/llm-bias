You will be receiving an end-of-year tax statement shortly, which will summarize your income and tax payments for the past year. This statement is required by law and will be sent to you by January 31st of the following year. It is a record of your tax obligations and will be used to determine any additional taxes you may owe or any refund to which you may be entitled.

The statement will show the total amount of income you earned, as well as any taxes you paid throughout the year, either through payroll deductions or estimated tax payments. It will also indicate any adjustments to your income, such as deductions or credits, that may have affected your tax liability.

Please note that this statement is not a notice of audit or a demand for payment. It is simply a record of your tax transactions for the year. If you have any questions or concerns about your tax statement, you can contact our office for assistance.

We will be sending the tax statement to you by mail, and you will also be able to access it through our online portal. If you have any changes to your address or would like to receive your statement electronically, please let us know and we will be happy to accommodate your request.
