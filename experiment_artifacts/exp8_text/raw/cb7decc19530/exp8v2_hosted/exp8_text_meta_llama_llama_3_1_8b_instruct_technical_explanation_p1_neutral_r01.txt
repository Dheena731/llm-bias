**The Importance of Low Latency in Web Applications**

Latency, measured in milliseconds, refers to the delay between a user's action (e.g., clicking a button or submitting a form) and the application's response. In web applications, low latency is crucial for a smooth user experience. Here's why:

**Why Low Latency Matters**

1. **User Experience**: A delay of even 100-200 milliseconds can cause users to perceive the application as slow or unresponsive. This can lead to frustration, decreased productivity, and ultimately, a negative perception of the application.
2. **Conversion Rates**: Research has shown that every additional second of delay can result in a 4.4% decrease in conversion rates. This is especially significant for e-commerce sites, where a fast response time can mean the difference between a sale and a lost opportunity.
3. **Search Engine Optimization (SEO)**: Search engines like Google consider page load times as a ranking factor. Faster applications are more likely to appear higher in search results, increasing visibility and driving more traffic to the site.
4. **Business Criticality**: In many cases, web applications are used for mission-critical tasks, such as online banking, healthcare, or emergency services. High latency can have serious consequences, including lost productivity, financial losses, or even loss of life.

**In Summary**

Low latency is essential for web applications to provide a seamless user experience, drive conversions, improve SEO, and ensure business continuity. By optimizing for low latency, developers can create faster, more responsive applications that meet the evolving expectations of users.
