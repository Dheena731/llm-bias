Reviewer comment on the research question:

The central research question, as currently stated, is difficult to evaluate because it does not specify what is being measured, in whom, or against what comparison. Phrases such as "how does digital engagement affect well-being" carry several undefined terms: "digital engagement" could refer to time spent online, frequency of use, type of activity, or intensity of interaction, and "well-being" could mean subjective life satisfaction, clinical symptoms, or something else entirely. Without operational definitions, it is unclear what a positive or negative answer to the question would look like, and different readers are likely to come away with different interpretations. I would ask the authors to state the key constructs explicitly, identify the population of interest, and clarify whether the question is descriptive, associational, or causal. Doing so would also help the reader judge whether the proposed design can actually answer the question as posed.
