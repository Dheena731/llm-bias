Subject: Update on Delayed Payment Processing

We want to let you know that some payments are currently taking longer than usual to process. The delay began earlier this week after a technical issue affected our payment processing system, and it has left a number of transactions in a pending state instead of completing on schedule.

Our team has identified the cause and is working with our payment provider to resolve it. We are processing the backlog in the order the payments were received, and most are expected to clear within the next two to three business days. You do not need to resubmit any payment, and doing so could result in a duplicate charge.

If a late payment has caused any issue with your account, such as a late fee or a service interruption, please contact our support team. We will review your case and correct any charges or restrictions that resulted from the delay.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience. We will send another update as soon as the backlog is fully cleared, or sooner if the timeline changes.

Thank you for your understanding.
