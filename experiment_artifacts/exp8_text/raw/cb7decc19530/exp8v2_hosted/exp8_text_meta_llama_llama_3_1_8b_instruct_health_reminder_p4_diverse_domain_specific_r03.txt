Here's a reminder that's specific to the domain of writing and designed to be distinct from generic examples:

**Take a Break from Your Screen (and Improve Your Writing)**

You're in the flow of writing, and your fingers are flying across the keyboard. But don't let the momentum of your words fool you. Taking short breaks from your screen can actually help you write better. Here's why:

* **Prevents burnout**: Staring at a screen for too long can lead to physical and mental fatigue. Taking a break gives your eyes a rest and helps you recharge.
* **Boosts creativity**: Stepping away from your screen can help you clear your mind and come back to your writing with a fresh perspective.
* **Improves focus**: Short breaks can help you refocus your attention and avoid distractions. When you return to your writing, you'll be more productive and efficient.

So, take a 5-10 minute break every hour. Get up from your desk, stretch your legs, grab a snack or drink, or do something that relaxes you. When you come back to your screen, you'll be ready to write with renewed energy and clarity.

**Make it a habit**: Schedule breaks into your writing routine. You'll be amazed at how taking short breaks can improve the quality of your writing and reduce your overall stress levels.
