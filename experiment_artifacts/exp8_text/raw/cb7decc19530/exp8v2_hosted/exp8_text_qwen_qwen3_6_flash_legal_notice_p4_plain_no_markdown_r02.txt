This notice establishes the terms governing refund eligibility for all transactions conducted through our channels. A purchase qualifies for a refund only when the request is filed within thirty calendar days of the original completion date. The merchandise must remain in new condition, free from damage, alteration, or missing components, and must include all original packaging and documentation. Verification requires submission of a valid receipt, invoice, or order identifier. Items marked as nonreturnable, digital licenses, perishable goods, customized products, and orders exceeding the stated timeframe are expressly excluded from reimbursement. Claimants must submit their request through the official support portal and provide complete transaction records along with a written explanation. Approved refunds will be issued to the original form of payment within fourteen business days of authorization. We reserve full discretion to reject applications that fail to meet these requirements or that involve suspected fraud or abuse. These provisions supersede any prior statements regarding returns and may be revised at our sole discretion without additional notice.
