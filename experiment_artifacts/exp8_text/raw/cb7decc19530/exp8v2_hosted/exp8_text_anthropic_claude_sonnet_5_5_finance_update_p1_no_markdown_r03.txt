Subject: New Monthly Spending Summary Now Available

Hello everyone,

We're pleased to announce a new monthly spending summary, designed to give you a clearer picture of where your money goes each month.

Starting this month, you will receive a summary at the beginning of each month that covers the previous month's activity. It includes your total spending, a breakdown by category such as groceries, transportation, dining, and bills, and a comparison to the prior month so you can quickly see what has changed. We have also added a short list of your largest individual purchases and a note highlighting any categories where your spending rose or fell noticeably.

The goal is to make it easier to spot trends, stay on budget, and make confident decisions without digging through individual transactions.

You can view your summary in the app under the Reports section, and a copy will also be sent to your email on the first of every month. If you would prefer not to receive the email, you can turn it off in your notification settings at any time.

We would love to hear what you think. Please send your feedback through the Help menu so we can keep improving the summary in future updates.

Thank you for being with us.

The Product Team
