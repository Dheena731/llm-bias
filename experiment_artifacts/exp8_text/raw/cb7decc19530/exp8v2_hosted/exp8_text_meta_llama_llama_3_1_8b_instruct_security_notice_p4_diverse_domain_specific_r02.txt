**What does this mean for you?**

* Starting [effective date], all users will be required to enable 2FA on their accounts. This will add an extra step to the login process, but will provide a significantly higher level of security and protection against phishing and other types of attacks.
* To enable 2FA, simply log in to your account and follow the prompts to set up an authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator, or use a physical token. You will then receive a verification code to enter in addition to your username and password.
* If you experience any issues or have questions about enabling 2FA, please reach out to our support team for assistance.

**Why is 2FA so important?**

In today's digital landscape, passwords alone are no longer sufficient to protect your sensitive information. Two-factor authentication adds a crucial second step to the login process, making it much harder for hackers to gain unauthorized access to your account. We are committed to ensuring the security and integrity of our community's data, and this new requirement is an important step in achieving that goal.
