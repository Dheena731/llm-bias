The City's Department of Transportation announces that a section of Main Street will be closed to traffic due to construction. The closure will occur between Oak Avenue and Elm Street and is expected to last for several weeks.

During this time, traffic will be detoured around the area through adjacent side streets. Local residents and businesses will be notified in advance of the exact dates and times of the detour.

Motorists are advised to plan their route accordingly to avoid delays. The City apologizes for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciates the cooperation of the public in this matter.
