Most audio made for language learners falls into one of two traps: it is either slowed down to the point of sounding clinical, or it is native-speed chaos with zero scaffolding.

We built *[Show Name]* for the uncomfortable space in between. 

It is designed specifically for people stuck on the intermediate plateau—the stage where you understand the grammar rules on paper, but unscripted speech still registers as a single, unbroken wall of sound.

Instead of rehearsed dialogues about checking into a hotel, each 15-minute episode dissects a single snippet of raw, natural speech:
* We isolate swallowed syllables, dropped prepositions, and regional elisions.
* We break down high-frequency filler words that native speakers use to buy time (and that textbooks ignore).
* We look at register shifts—how cadence changes depending on who holds the power in the room.

No vocabulary lists to memorize. No promises of effortless fluency in thirty days. Just deliberate ear-training so you stop translating in your head mid-sentence.

The first three episodes are live now on Apple and Spotify.
