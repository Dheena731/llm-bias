**OFFICIAL NOTICE: DESIGNATION OF POLLING PLACES**  
**Municipal Primary Election – May 14, 2024**

Notice is hereby given to qualified electors of the City of Waynesboro that polling locations have been certified for the upcoming primary election pursuant to state election code (§ 24.2-310). 

Polls will be open on Tuesday, May 14, from **6:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.** Electors must cast their ballots at their assigned precinct site. Photo identification is required to check in.

### **Precinct Assignments & Relocations**

Electors in **Precinct 2B (Riverbend)** and **Precinct 4A (Highland)** should note venue changes from previous cycles due to facility maintenance:

*   **Precinct 1A (Central):** City Hall, Council Chambers, 503 W. Main St.
*   **Precinct 2B (Riverbend) — *NEW LOCATION*:** St. Luke’s Parish Hall, 310 N. Wayne Ave. *(Formerly Riverbend Elementary Gymnasium)*
*   **Precinct 3A (East End):** Waynesboro Public Library, Lower Level Conference Room, 600 S. Wayne Ave.
*   **Precinct 4A (Highland) — *NEW LOCATION*:** Community Center, Room 102, 415 Bridge Ave. *(Formerly Armory)*

All certified locations meet federal Title II ADA accessibility guidelines and are equipped with optical-scan ballot tabulators and accessible ballot-marking devices. 

Voter registration cards indicate your designated precinct number. Electors unsure of their precinct assignment or registration status should consult the official registry at **elections.waynesboro.va.us/lookup** or contact the Office of the General Registrar at (540) 555-0143 before 5:00 p.m. on Friday, May 10. 

*Curbside voting is available at all sites for electors with mobility limitations or physical disabilities.*
