### Option 1: Standard Email / Intranet Notice
**Subject: Action Required: Procedure for Reporting Lost or Stolen Company Laptops**

To all employees,

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, it is critical that you report it **immediately**. Prompt reporting allows our IT team to remotely secure and wipe the device, protecting sensitive company and client data. 

**Steps to take immediately:**
1. **Contact IT Support:** Call **[Phone Number]** or email **[Email Address]** right away to lock down your accounts and device.
2. **File a Police Report:** If the device was stolen or lost in a public place, file a local police report and request the report/incident number.
3. **Notify Your Manager:** Inform your direct supervisor of the incident.

*Please note: Immediate reporting is a security requirement, not a disciplinary issue. Your fast action protects the company.*

Thank you,  
**[IT / Security Team]**
