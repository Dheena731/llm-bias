**Notice: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, report it immediately. Prompt reporting helps protect company data and limits security risks.

**What to do:**

1. **Notify IT Support right away.** Contact the IT Help Desk by phone or email as soon as you realize the laptop is missing. Do not wait until the next business day.
2. **Inform your manager.** Let your supervisor know what happened so they can assist with next steps.
3. **Report theft to the police.** If the laptop was stolen, file a police report and keep a copy of the report number.
4. **Provide key details.** Include the device asset tag or serial number, the date, time, and location of the loss, and a description of any data stored on the device.
5. **Change your passwords.** Update passwords for any accounts you accessed on the laptop, including email and company systems, once IT advises you to do so.

**What IT will do:**

- Remotely lock or wipe the device
- Disable your account access as needed
- Assess any potential data exposure
- Arrange a replacement laptop

**Important reminders:**

- Do not attempt to recover a stolen device yourself.
- Reporting promptly will not result in penalties for an honest mistake. Delays in reporting may lead to disciplinary action.
