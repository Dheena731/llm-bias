To simplify weeknight dinner preparation, consider taking a few minutes on the weekend to plan and prep your meals for the upcoming week. Start by making a list of the dinners you want to prepare, then create a corresponding grocery list to ensure you have all the necessary ingredients. Next, chop any vegetables, marinate proteins, and cook grains or starches that can be reheated later in the week. This initial investment of time will save you hours during the week when you are short on time and can help you quickly assemble a healthy and delicious meal.
