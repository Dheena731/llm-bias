**Important Voting Information**

For the upcoming election, polling stations will be located at the following sites:

* Ward 1: Oakdale Community Center, 123 Main St.
* Ward 2: Lincoln Elementary School, 456 Elm St.
* Ward 3: City Hall, 789 Oak St. (accessible entrance on Maple St.)
* Ward 4: Riverside Library, 901 River Rd.

Polling stations will be open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM on election day. Voters are reminded to verify their assigned polling station by checking their voter registration card or visiting the city's election website. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Election Office at (555) 123-4567.
