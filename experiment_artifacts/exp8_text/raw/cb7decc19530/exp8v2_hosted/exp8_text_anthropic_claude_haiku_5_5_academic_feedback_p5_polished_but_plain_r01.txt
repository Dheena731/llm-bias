Several claims in this essay would benefit from citations. The statistic in the second paragraph, the description of the 2015 policy reform, and the argument that public trust declined after the reform all present information that a reader will want to verify, yet none is attributed to a source. Even when the ideas are your own synthesis of other work, readers need to see which scholars, reports, or datasets you are drawing on so they can evaluate the evidence and follow up on it. I recommend adding a citation for each of these points, and checking whether the paragraph that discusses competing interpretations relies on particular authors whose names should appear in the text. Once the sources are in place, the argument will read as more credible and better grounded in the existing scholarship.
