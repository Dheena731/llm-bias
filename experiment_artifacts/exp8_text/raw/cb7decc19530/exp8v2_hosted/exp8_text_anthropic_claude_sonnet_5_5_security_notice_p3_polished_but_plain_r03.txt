Reminder: Please Lock Your Screens

Colleagues, this is a brief reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if you expect to be gone for only a moment. An unlocked screen gives anyone nearby access to your email, files, and the systems you are signed into, which puts confidential information about our clients, our colleagues, and our organization at risk.

Locking your screen takes only a second. On a Windows computer, press the Windows key and the letter L together. On a Mac, press Control, Command, and Q. If you use a laptop in a shared space or while traveling, the same habit applies. Many devices can also be set to lock automatically after a few minutes of inactivity, and we encourage you to check that this setting is turned on.

This practice protects you as well as the organization. Anything done on an unlocked device can be traced back to your account, so a short pause to lock your screen helps prevent misuse that could later be attributed to you.

Thank you for making this part of your daily routine. If you need help setting up automatic locking or have any questions, please contact the IT help desk.
