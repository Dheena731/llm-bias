**Time for Your Weekly Fitness Check-In**

Take a few minutes today to review your progress from the past week. Look at the workouts you completed, how consistently you stuck to your plan, and how you felt along the way.

Ask yourself:
- Did I meet my activity goals this week?
- What went well, and what was difficult?
- Is there one small change I can make next week?

Celebrate your wins, however small, and use what you learn to set your goals for the week ahead. Steady progress adds up over time.
