Your end-of-year tax statement is a summary of the income you earned and the taxes that were withheld from your pay throughout the year. It will show the total amount of money you earned, as well as the amount of federal, state, and local taxes that were deducted from your pay. This statement is typically provided by your employer and is used to help you complete your tax return. It will include important information such as your social security number, the amount of taxes withheld, and any other relevant details that you will need to report on your tax return. You should review your statement carefully to ensure that all of the information is accurate and complete, and keep it with your other tax documents in case you need to refer to it later.
