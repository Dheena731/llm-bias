To store fresh herbs, place them in a plastic bag or airtight container to maintain moisture and prevent wilting. Store the herbs in the refrigerator, typically in the crisper drawer, which maintains high humidity. You can also store herbs on the counter, away from direct sunlight and heat sources, in a container with a damp cloth to maintain humidity. Regularly check the herbs for signs of spoilage and use them within a few days of purchase for the best flavor and texture.
