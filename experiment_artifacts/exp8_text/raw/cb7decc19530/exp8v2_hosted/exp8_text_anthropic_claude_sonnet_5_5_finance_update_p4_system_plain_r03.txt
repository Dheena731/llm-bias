Hello,

Your end-of-year tax statement is now available. It summarizes your income for the past calendar year and the amount of tax that was withheld or paid on your behalf. You will need it when you prepare your tax return, so please keep a copy with your other financial records.

The statement lists your total earnings, any taxable benefits, and the tax already deducted. If the amount withheld is higher than what you owe, you may be due a refund. If it is lower, you may need to pay the difference when you file. The statement also shows any deductions or contributions that may reduce your taxable income, such as retirement savings or eligible expenses.

Please check that your name, address, and tax identification number are correct, and compare the income figures with your own records. If something looks wrong or missing, contact us as soon as possible so we can issue a corrected version before the filing deadline.

If you have questions about how to read the statement or how it affects your return, we are happy to help, though for personal tax advice you may wish to consult a qualified tax professional.
