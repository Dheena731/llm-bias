Here are a few options, depending on who you are writing to:

### Option 1: To Business Clients / Merchants (e.g., from a payment processor or platform)

**Subject: Important update regarding your card transaction fees**

Dear [Client/Partner Name],

We are writing to let you know about an upcoming change to our payment processing fees. 

Effective **[Date]**, our card transaction fee will change from **[Old Rate, e.g., 2.5% + $0.20]** to **[New Rate, e.g., 2.7% + $0.20]** per transaction. 

This adjustment reflects recent increases in interchange rates and network costs, and will allow us to continue providing secure, reliable payment processing and support for your business. 

No action is required on your part; the new rate will automatically apply to all card transactions processed on or after **[Date]**. 

You can review your updated fee schedule in your dashboard [here/link]. If you have any questions, please reach out to our team at [Support Email/Phone].

Thank you for your continued partnership.

Best regards,  
[Your Name/Company]

***

### Option 2: To Everyday Customers (e.g., from a retail store or service introducing a surcharge)

To Our Valued Customers,

Starting **[Date]**, a transaction fee of **[X]%** will be applied to all credit card payments. 

Rising payment processing costs have led us to make this change so we can continue keeping our product/service prices as low as possible. 

**Fee-free payment options:**  
You can avoid this fee by paying with **[Cash / Debit / ACH / Bank Transfer]**.

Sincerely,  
[Your Business Name]

### Option 3: Short / In-App Notification (Concise)
