**Cook once, use twice.** When you're making a weeknight dinner, double the base component, such as rice, roasted vegetables, or a protein like chicken thighs. Use half tonight and refrigerate the rest in an airtight container.

The next evening, turn the leftovers into a different meal with very little effort. Roasted chicken can become tacos or a quick grain bowl, and extra rice can become fried rice in about ten minutes. You only dirty the pans once, and tomorrow's dinner is already half done.

**Bonus tip:** Season the base lightly, with just salt, pepper, and a little oil. That way you can take it in any direction the next day with sauces and spices.
