**Subject: Update to Card Transaction Fees**

Dear Customer,

We are writing to let you know about an upcoming change to the fees we charge on card transactions.

**What is changing**

Beginning on the effective date below, the fee for card transactions will be adjusted as follows:

- Domestic card purchases: 1.5% per transaction (previously 1.2%)
- International card purchases: 2.9% per transaction (previously 2.5%)
- Card-not-present (online) transactions: an additional flat fee of $0.10 per transaction

**Why we are making this change**

Processing costs from card networks and partner banks have increased. Rather than reduce the quality of our payment security and support, we have decided to pass on a modest portion of these costs.

**When it takes effect**

The new fees will apply to all transactions processed on or after the first day of next month. Transactions made before that date will be charged at the current rates.

**What you need to do**

No action is required. The updated fees will be applied automatically. If you would like to review how this affects your account, you can view the full fee schedule in your online account settings.

If you have questions, our support team is available by phone or email during business hours, and we are happy to help.

Thank you for your continued business.

Sincerely,
The Customer Support Team
