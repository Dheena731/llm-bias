Update on Delayed Payment Processing

We want to let you know that some payments are currently taking longer than usual to process. Transactions that would normally complete within a few minutes may take several hours, and in some cases up to one business day, to appear in your account.

The delay stems from a backlog at one of our payment processing partners. Our team identified the issue early and is working with the partner to clear the queue as quickly as possible. We have no indication that any payment data has been lost or compromised, and funds submitted during this period remain safe and will be credited once processing completes.

In the meantime, there is no need to resubmit a payment. Doing so could result in a duplicate charge, which would then require a separate refund process. If you see a pending charge on your statement, please allow it to resolve on its own. If a payment has not posted within 24 hours of submission, contact our support team with your transaction reference, and we will investigate it directly.

We expect processing times to return to normal within the next day, and we will share another update as soon as that happens or if our timeline changes. We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience while we resolve this.
