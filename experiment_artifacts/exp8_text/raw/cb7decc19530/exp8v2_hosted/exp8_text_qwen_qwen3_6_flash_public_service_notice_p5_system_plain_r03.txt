Voters are reminded to verify their assigned polling station location prior to Election Day. A comprehensive list of updated locations is available through the local election office and online resources. Certain polling places have moved to ensure better accessibility and security. Polling stations will open at seven in the morning and close at seven in the evening. All voters must present a valid government issued photo identification along with proof of residence. If you need assistance locating your precinct, please contact the election help desk directly using the number printed on your voter registration confirmation. Early voting options may also be available at select community centers throughout the week leading up to the election. Please arrive with enough time to complete the voting process and return your ballot securely. Your participation is vital to our community.
