If you have misplaced or had your company laptop stolen, you must report the incident immediately to protect sensitive organization and client data. Contact the IT Helpdesk and notify your direct supervisor as soon as you realize the device is missing so that technicians can remotely lock and secure the machine. Be prepared to provide the last known location of the device, the approximate time it went missing, and details of any peripheral equipment involved. If the laptop was stolen in a public area or from your residence, you must also file a report with local law enforcement and share the case number with the IT department. Rapid communication is vital to maintaining our network security, and prompt reporting will not result in disciplinary action for an honest accident.
