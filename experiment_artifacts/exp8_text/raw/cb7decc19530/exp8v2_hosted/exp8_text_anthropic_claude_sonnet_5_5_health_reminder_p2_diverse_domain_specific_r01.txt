**Water check**

Look at your bottle. If it's at the same level as when you sat down, drink some now.

You wake up several hours behind, so finish a glass before the coffee. After that, aim for about a cup an hour while you're awake. Have more if you're sweating, flying, or sitting in a room with the heat on.

A 3 p.m. headache or foggy stretch is often mild dehydration rather than a need for more caffeine. Your urine is the easiest gauge: pale straw is fine, and dark apple-juice yellow means drink up.

If you keep forgetting, tie it to something you already do: a glass with each meal, one after each bathroom trip, one before you leave the house.
