Please remember to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if it is only for a brief moment. Unattended and unlocked devices create a significant security risk and can allow unauthorized access to sensitive company and client information. You can quickly secure your workstation by pressing the Windows key and the letter L on a PC, or Control, Command, and Q on a Mac. Cultivating this simple habit helps protect our data and keeps our workplace safe. Thank you for your continued cooperation and dedication to our information security standards.
