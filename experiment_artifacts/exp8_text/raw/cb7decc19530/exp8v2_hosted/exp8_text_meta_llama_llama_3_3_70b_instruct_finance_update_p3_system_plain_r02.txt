We are excited to announce the addition of a new savings goal feature to our platform. This feature allows users to set and track specific savings goals, such as building an emergency fund, paying for a big purchase, or saving for a vacation. Users can customize their goals by setting a target amount and deadline, and can track their progress over time. The feature also provides personalized recommendations and reminders to help users stay on track and reach their goals. This new feature is designed to help users take control of their finances and make saving easier and more manageable.
