Small calendar habit that's saved me a lot of annoyance: when I book an appointment, I make a second event right away, six months out, titled "Call dentist, cleaning due."

The receptionist always asks "want to schedule your next one?" and I never know my schedule that far ahead. So I say "I'll call," then forget for 14 months.

The reminder event goes on a Tuesday at 12:15, when I'm on lunch and can actually make the call. I put the phone number in the location field so I can tap it.

Works for oil changes, vet boosters, and renewing the passport you swear you'll renew.
