**Reminder: Book Your Annual Health Checkup**

It's time to schedule your yearly health checkup. Regular exams can help catch potential health issues early and give you a chance to talk with your doctor about any concerns.

**What to do:**
1. Call your doctor's office or use their online booking system to request an appointment.
2. Check your insurance plan to see what is covered.
3. Write down any questions or symptoms you want to discuss.
4. Make a list of your current medications and supplements to bring with you.

If you have already had a checkup within the past year, you can disregard this reminder. If you are unsure when your last one was, check with your doctor's office.

Book your appointment this week so it doesn't slip your mind.
