NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Purchasers are eligible for a refund only when a written request is submitted within thirty (30) calendar days of the original transaction date and the purchased item remains in its original condition, undamaged, and accompanied by valid proof of purchase. Requests received after this period will not be processed.

To qualify, submit your request through the official customer service channel along with a copy of the receipt or order confirmation. Each submission must include the transaction reference number and a written statement of the claim. Incomplete or unverifiable submissions will be declined without further review.

Refunds are excluded for customized merchandise, digital goods that have been accessed or transmitted, services that have been fully performed, and items exhibiting wear, alteration, or improper handling. The seller retains the right to inspect claimed items and verify documentation prior to approval. Authorized refunds will be processed to the original form of payment within ten (10) business days following final approval.

This notice establishes the complete refund eligibility requirements. No verbal assurances or ancillary correspondence modify these terms. Applicable statutory consumer protections remain intact where required by governing law.
