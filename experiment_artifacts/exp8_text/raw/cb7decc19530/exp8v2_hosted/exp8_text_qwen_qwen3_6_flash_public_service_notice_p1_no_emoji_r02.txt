**NOTICE: PUBLIC LIBRARY OPERATING HOURS**

The Municipal Library System would like to remind all patrons of our standard weekly schedule. All branches are open Monday through Friday from 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., Saturday from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., and Sunday from 12:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m.

Please be advised that the library will remain closed on observed national holidays and during scheduled facility maintenance. Periodic hour adjustments for summer programs or winter breaks will be displayed at each entrance and published on our official website. For branch-specific schedules or to verify tomorrow's hours, please call the Main Information Desk at 555-0147.

Thank you for utilizing public resources and supporting community learning.
