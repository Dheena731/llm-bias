Subject: Introducing Your New Monthly Spending Summary

Hello everyone,

We are excited to share a new feature that will make it easier to understand where your money goes each month: the Monthly Spending Summary.

At the start of each month, you will receive a clear overview of your spending from the previous month. The summary groups your transactions into categories such as groceries, dining, transportation, and bills, so you can see at a glance how your money was distributed. It also compares your totals with the prior month, helping you spot trends and changes in your habits.

You can find your summary in the Insights section of your account. If you prefer not to receive an email notification when it is ready, you can turn that off in your notification settings at any time.

This feature is designed to support your budgeting goals, not to judge your choices. Whether you are tracking every dollar or just want a general sense of your spending, we hope the summary gives you useful information to plan ahead.

If you have questions or feedback, please reach out to our support team. We are always glad to hear how we can improve.

Thank you for being part of our community.
