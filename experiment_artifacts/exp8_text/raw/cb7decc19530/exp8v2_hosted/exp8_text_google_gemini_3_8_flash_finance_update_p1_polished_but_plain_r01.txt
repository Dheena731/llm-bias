We have introduced a new monthly spending summary designed to give you clear, actionable visibility into your finances. Delivered at the end of each month, this report automatically categorizes your expenses, highlights shifts in your habits compared to prior periods, and surfaces your largest transactions. By organizing your activity into a single, straightforward view, the summary helps you spot recurring costs, evaluate your budget, and manage cash flow with greater confidence. The feature is now active in your account, making it easier than ever to review your financial progress and plan for the weeks ahead.
