Thank you for sharing the draft of your conference poster. Overall, the research is compelling, and the logical flow from the background through the conclusions is very clear. To make the presentation even more engaging for attendees walking past, I recommend reducing the overall volume of text, particularly in the introduction and methodology sections. Replacing some of the detailed narrative with more white space will give the poster room to breathe and make it less intimidating to read. The charts in the results section are well designed, but the axis labels and legends are currently too small to be legible from a few feet away, so increasing their font size will help significantly. Lastly, consider making your primary takeaway larger and placing it prominently near the top right corner so visitors can immediately grasp your main finding. With these visual adjustments, the poster will be both eye-catching and easy to navigate.
