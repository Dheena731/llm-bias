NOTICE REGARDING REFUND ELIGIBILITY

This Notice sets out the conditions under which a purchaser may be eligible for a refund of amounts paid to the Company for goods or services. By completing a purchase, the purchaser acknowledges that they have read and accepted these conditions.

A purchaser is eligible for a full refund if a written request is received by the Company within thirty days of the date of purchase and the goods have not been used, damaged, or altered, or, in the case of services, the services have not yet commenced. Requests must include the order number, the name used at the time of purchase, and a brief statement of the reason for the request. The Company may require reasonable proof of purchase before processing any refund.

A purchaser may be eligible for a partial refund where goods have been returned in a condition that reflects reasonable handling, or where services have been partly performed. In such cases, the Company will determine the refundable amount by deducting the value of any benefit received, together with any non-recoverable third-party costs incurred on the purchaser's behalf. The Company will explain its calculation in writing upon request.

The following are not eligible for a refund: items identified at the time of sale as final sale or custom made; digital products that have been downloaded or accessed, except where they are materially defective; gift cards and promotional credits; and any purchase for which the request is made after the thirty-day period has expired, unless a longer period is required by applicable law.

Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within fourteen business days of approval. The Company is not responsible for delays caused by banks, payment processors, or other third parties. Original shipping and handling charges are non-refundable unless the refund is due to an error by the Company or a defect in the goods.

Nothing in this Notice limits any rights that a purchaser may have under applicable consumer protection laws, which shall prevail in the event of conflict. The Company reserves the right to amend this Notice at any time, and any amendment will apply only to purchases made after the date of the change. Questions regarding this Notice should be directed to the Company's customer service department in writing.
