Your end of year tax statement provides a comprehensive summary of your financial activity over the past calendar year. This document consolidates key figures such as your total income, any taxes withheld, interest earned, or eligible deductions and contributions. You will need these figures to accurately complete and file your annual tax return with state and federal revenue authorities. Please review the details carefully to ensure your personal information and totals are correct. Retain this statement for your permanent records and have it available when you or your tax professional prepare your return. If you identify any errors or have questions about how these amounts were calculated, contact our support team promptly so any necessary adjustments can be made before you file.
