We are excited to announce the introduction of a new monthly spending summary, designed to help you keep track of your expenses and make informed financial decisions. This summary will be sent to you at the beginning of each month and will provide a detailed breakdown of your spending from the previous month, including total expenditure, category-wise spending, and any notable transactions. The summary will also offer personalized suggestions for reducing unnecessary expenses and improving your overall financial health. With this new feature, you will be able to easily monitor your spending habits and make adjustments as needed to achieve your financial goals.
