Update on Delayed Payment Processing

We want to let you know that some payments are currently taking longer than usual to process. A technical issue with one of our payment partners has slowed down the confirmation of transactions, and as a result, some payments are taking up to three business days to appear on accounts instead of the usual few hours.

Please be assured that no payments have been lost or rejected because of this delay. Every transaction submitted is safely recorded and is being processed in the order it was received. You do not need to resubmit any payments, and doing so could result in a duplicate charge.

Our team is working closely with our partner to resolve the problem as quickly as possible. We expect processing times to return to normal by the end of this week, and we will share another update as soon as we have confirmation that the issue has been fixed. If a late payment has caused any fees or penalties on your account, please contact our support team and we will review and correct them.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience while we work through this.
