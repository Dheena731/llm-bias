**NOTICE: MANDATORY TWO-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION FOR RESEARCH SYSTEMS**  
**EFFECTIVE:** October 14, 2024  
**ISSUED BY:** Office of Research Compliance & IT Security  

Starting next week, all principal investigators, postdoctoral fellows, and research staff accessing the Institutional Secure Data Environment must enable two-factor authentication on their university credentials. This change fulfills NSF OII requirement §6.2 and satisfies our IRB data-handling addendum for studies involving identifiable participant records.

**Required Action:**  
1. Log into the Identity Management Portal (idm.univ.edu/security)  
2. Navigate to Authentication Methods → Select “Authenticator Application”  
3. Disable SMS fallback and register two offline recovery codes  
4. Confirm enrollment within the portal dashboard  

Accounts without verified 2FA will lose write access to shared drives and grant reporting portals after November 1. Read-only access to course materials and library subscriptions remains unaffected.

IT Data Governance will not perform remote resets. If you require assistance configuring an authenticator on institutional-managed devices, visit Lab 218 (Science Center) Wednesday–Friday, 9–11 a.m., or submit a ticket under “2FA Setup – Research Track.”
