Take a moment right now to step back from your screen. Your eyes, neck, and mind need regular rest, especially during long periods of work. Look away and focus on something in the distance for twenty seconds, roll your shoulders back, and take a slow, deep breath. Standing up to stretch or drinking a glass of water can ease physical tension, prevent eye strain, and restore your focus. A quick pause takes only a minute, but it makes a noticeable difference in how you feel for the rest of the day.
