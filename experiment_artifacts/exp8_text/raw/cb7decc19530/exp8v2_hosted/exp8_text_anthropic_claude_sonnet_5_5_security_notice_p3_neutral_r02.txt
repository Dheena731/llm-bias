**Reminder: Lock Your Screen**

Dear Team,

Please remember to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if it's only for a moment.

**How to lock your screen:**
- **Windows:** Press **Windows key + L**
- **Mac:** Press **Control + Command + Q**

Locking your screen protects sensitive company, client, and personal information from unauthorized access. It only takes a second, and it helps keep our workplace secure.

Thank you for your cooperation.

*IT & Security Team*
