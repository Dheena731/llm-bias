Today we are launching Tally, a simple app that helps small business owners keep track of their invoices, expenses, and customer payments in one place. We built it after talking with dozens of shop owners, freelancers, and caterers who told us the same thing: they did not start their businesses to spend evenings buried in spreadsheets.

With Tally, you can create and send a professional invoice in under a minute, snap a photo of a receipt and have it sorted automatically, and see at a glance who has paid you and who still owes you money. Gentle reminders go out on your behalf, so you never have to feel awkward chasing a late payment. At the end of each month, Tally prepares a clear summary that you can hand straight to your accountant.

We kept the design deliberately uncluttered. There are no confusing menus and no accounting jargon, just the tools you need to understand how your business is doing. Tally works on your phone, tablet, or computer, and everything stays in sync wherever you are.

To celebrate our launch, every new customer gets their first three months free, with no credit card required to start. If you run a small business and want more time for the work you love, we would be delighted to have you try Tally and tell us what you think. Your feedback will shape what we build next.
