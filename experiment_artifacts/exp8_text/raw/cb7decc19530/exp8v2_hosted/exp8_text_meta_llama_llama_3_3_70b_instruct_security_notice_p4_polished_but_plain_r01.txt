To add an extra layer of security to your account, we recommend enabling two-factor authentication. This feature requires both your password and a verification code sent to your phone or email to access your account. Enabling two-factor authentication helps protect your account from unauthorized access and reduces the risk of identity theft. To enable this feature, simply go to your account settings and follow the prompts to set up two-factor authentication. If you have any questions or need assistance with the process, please contact our support team for help.
