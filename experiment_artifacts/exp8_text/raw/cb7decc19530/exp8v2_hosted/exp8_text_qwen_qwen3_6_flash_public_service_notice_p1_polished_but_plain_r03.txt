The public library informs the community of its regular operating hours to help you plan your visits. From Monday through Friday, the facility is open from nine in the morning until five in the evening. On Saturdays, service is available from ten in the morning until two in the afternoon. The library is closed on Sundays and all official public holidays.

Patrons should remember that the exterior drop box accepts book returns after the staff has departed, allowing for continuous processing of materials. While the circulation desk is closed, self-service devices may remain accessible where security guidelines allow. Residents seeking clarification on holiday schedule changes or temporary closures should contact the library administration directly. We thank you for your support and welcome you to take advantage of our resources during published hours.
