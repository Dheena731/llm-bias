Today we are launching Common Ground, a podcast for people who want to learn a new language without giving up the rest of their lives to do it.

Each week, we take one everyday situation, such as ordering coffee, apologizing for being late, or making small talk with a neighbor, and work through it in a single language at a gentle pace. Every episode runs about fifteen minutes. We begin with a short scene in natural speech, then slow it down, unpack the phrases that matter, and give you a chance to say them aloud before the episode ends. The goal is not to cover everything. It is to leave you with a handful of expressions you can use the same day.

We made the show for beginners and returning learners, especially those who have studied before and found that the words faded when they stopped using them. Our hosts are a working teacher and a lifelong language student, so you will hear both the explanation and the questions a learner would actually ask. We also talk openly about mistakes, because they are a normal and useful part of progress.

The first season covers Spanish, with French and Japanese planned for later. Three episodes are available now, and new ones will arrive every Tuesday. A short companion transcript accompanies each episode for anyone who likes to read along.

To get the most from the show, listen once while doing something else, then listen again with your full attention and repeat the phrases out loud. Even ten minutes of speaking a few times a week will do more for you than an hour of passive listening.
