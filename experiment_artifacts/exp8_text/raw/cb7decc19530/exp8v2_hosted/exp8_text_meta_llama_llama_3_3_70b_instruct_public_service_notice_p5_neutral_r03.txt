**Important Voting Information**

To all registered voters,

The polling station locations for the upcoming election are as follows:

* For voters residing in the north part of the city: Northside Community Center, 123 Main St.
* For voters residing in the south part of the city: Southside Elementary School, 456 Elm St.
* For voters residing in the downtown area: City Hall, 789 Oak St.

Please note that polling stations will be open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM on election day. To confirm your polling station location, you can check your voter registration card or visit our website at [website URL].

We look forward to seeing you at the polls!
