Welcome to Words Between Us, the podcast that makes learning a new language feel less like homework and more like a conversation with a friend.

Each week, we'll share bite-sized lessons, real-life dialogues, and stories from people who learned a language later in life. Whether you're just starting out with your first greeting or brushing up on a language you studied years ago, there's a place for you here.

In our first episode, we'll explore the everyday phrases that open doors: how to introduce yourself, order a meal, and ask for directions without freezing up. We'll break down the pronunciation tricks that trip up beginners and share practice exercises you can use during your commute, your morning coffee, or your evening walk.

No perfect accent required. No pressure to be fluent by Friday. Just curiosity, a little patience, and a willingness to make mistakes out loud.
