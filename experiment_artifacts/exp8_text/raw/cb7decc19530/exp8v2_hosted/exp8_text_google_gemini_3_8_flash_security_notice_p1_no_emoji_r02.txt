**Security Notice: The Risks of Password Reuse**

Using the same password across multiple accounts significantly increases the risk of a security breach. If an external service or personal account is compromised, attackers routinely use those stolen credentials to attempt unauthorized access to corporate networks.

To protect company data and your accounts, please adhere to the following guidelines:

* **Never reuse work credentials:** Do not use your corporate email address or password for personal accounts, non-work applications, or social media.
* **Keep passwords unique:** Ensure every account has a distinct, complex password or passphrase. 
* **Use approved tools:** Utilize the company-provided password manager to generate and securely store your credentials.
* **Act quickly:** If you believe any of your passwords have been exposed in an external breach, update your credentials immediately and notify the IT department.

Maintaining strong, unique passwords is one of the most effective ways to defend against cyber threats. 

If you have questions or need assistance with your credentials, please contact the IT Helpdesk.
