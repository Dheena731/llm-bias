**Tip for less sticky rice:** Rinse your rice in cool water before cooking, swishing it with your hand and draining until the water runs mostly clear (usually 3 to 4 rinses). This washes off the excess surface starch that makes grains clump together. Then use a slightly lower water ratio, about 1½ cups of water per 1 cup of long-grain rice, and let it rest, covered and off the heat, for 10 minutes after cooking. Fluff gently with a fork to release steam and keep the grains separate.
