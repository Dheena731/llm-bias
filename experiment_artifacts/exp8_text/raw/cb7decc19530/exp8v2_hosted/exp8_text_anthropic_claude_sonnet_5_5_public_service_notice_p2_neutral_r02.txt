**PUBLIC NOTICE**

**Road Closure: Maple Street between 4th Avenue and 8th Avenue**

**Effective:** Monday, June 9, 2025, at 7:00 a.m. through Friday, June 13, 2025, at 5:00 p.m.

The City Department of Public Works will close Maple Street between 4th Avenue and 8th Avenue for water main replacement and road resurfacing.

**What to expect:**
- The road will be closed to all through traffic.
- Local residents and businesses will have access to driveways, but access may be limited during working hours.
- Street parking will be prohibited in the work zone. Vehicles left in the area may be towed.
- Pedestrian access will be maintained on one side of the street where it is safe to do so.

**Detour:** Drivers should use Oak Street or Cedar Avenue. Detour signs will be posted.

Work is weather-dependent, and the schedule may change. Emergency vehicles will be notified of the closure and will have access at all times.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience.
