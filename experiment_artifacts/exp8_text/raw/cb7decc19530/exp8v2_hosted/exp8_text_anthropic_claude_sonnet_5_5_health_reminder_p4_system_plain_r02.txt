A quick reminder to give your eyes and mind a rest. Every twenty minutes or so, look away from your screen and focus on something far away for about twenty seconds. Stand up, stretch your shoulders and neck, take a few deep breaths, and maybe get a sip of water. These small pauses help reduce eye strain, ease tension, and keep your thinking fresh, so you will likely get more done when you return. Step away for a moment now and then. Your body will thank you.
