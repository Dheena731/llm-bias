**NOTICE OF CHANGE OF POLLING PLACES**
Harlan County Board of Elections

**For the General Election on Tuesday, November 4**

Three precincts will vote at new locations. All other polling places are unchanged.

| Precinct | Previous location | Vote here instead |
|---|---|---|
| 04 (Millrace) | Millrace Elementary gym | **Millrace Fire Hall**, 218 Canal St. Use the side door on Dye Alley. |
| 11 (North Fork) | St. Brendan's parish hall | **North Fork Grange**, 1470 Route 9. Park in the gravel lot across the road. |
| 17 (Eastgate) | Eastgate Commons community room | **Eastgate Public Library**, meeting room B, 66 Hollis Ave. |

**Why:** The Millrace gym is closed for floor replacement. St. Brendan's did not renew its agreement with the county. Eastgate Commons no longer admits non-residents without an escort.

**Hours:** 6:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. Anyone in line at 7:30 will be allowed to vote.

**Accessibility:** All three sites have step-free entrances. Curbside voting is available at each; ask a poll worker at the door to send someone out.

**If you go to the wrong place:** You may be given a provisional ballot, but it will count only for the races you are eligible to vote in. Going to the correct site is faster.

Affected voters will get a corrected card by mail no later than October 17. To confirm your precinct, call 555-0142 weekdays from 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. or check the lookup at harlancountyelections.example.

Early in-person voting at the County Services Building, 300 Main St., is unchanged.
