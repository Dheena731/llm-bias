**PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE**

**Location:** Main Street (between 1st Avenue and 5th Avenue)  
**Dates & Times:** Monday, October 14 through Friday, October 18 | 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM daily  

Please be advised that Main Street will be closed to all through traffic between 1st Avenue and 5th Avenue to accommodate scheduled utility upgrades and road resurfacing. 

A marked detour will direct traffic via Elm Street. Local access will be maintained for residents and emergency vehicles at all times. 

Motorists should expect minor delays, allow extra travel time, and use caution when traveling near the work zone. 

For inquiries or further information, please contact the Department of Public Works at (555) 019-2834.
