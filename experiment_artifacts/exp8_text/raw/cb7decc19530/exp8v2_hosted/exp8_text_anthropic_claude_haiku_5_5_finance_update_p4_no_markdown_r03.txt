Your end-of-year tax statement summarizes the income you earned and the taxes withheld or paid during the past year. It is used to prepare your tax return, so please keep it with your records.

The statement lists your total income for the year, any tax already withheld from your pay or payments, and any deductions or credits that apply to you. The totals at the bottom show the amount you owe or the refund you may be due, once your return is filed.

Please check that your name, address, and taxpayer identification number are correct. If anything looks wrong or you have questions about a specific figure, contact us before the filing deadline so we can help you correct it.
