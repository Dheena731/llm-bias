Starting Monday, March 10, the corporate VPN client will be updated to version 4.2. The new version improves connection stability on unreliable networks and adds support for multi-factor sign-in through the company authenticator app. Most users will receive the update automatically the next time they launch the client, but you may be asked to restart your computer once the installation finishes. After updating, you will need to sign in again and approve the authenticator prompt on your phone. If the client does not update automatically, you can download the installer from the IT self-service portal. Please save your work before installing. Questions can be directed to the IT help desk at extension 4400.
