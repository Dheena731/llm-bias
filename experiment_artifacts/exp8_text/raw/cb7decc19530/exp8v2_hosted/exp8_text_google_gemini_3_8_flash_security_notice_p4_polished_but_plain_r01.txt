To ensure the ongoing security of your account and protect your personal information, we require all users to enable two-factor authentication. This feature adds an essential layer of defense by requiring a secondary verification code, typically sent to your mobile device or generated by an authenticator application, in addition to your regular password when you sign in. Even if your password is compromised, this additional barrier prevents unauthorized access. You can turn on this protection immediately by visiting the security section within your account settings and following the setup instructions for your preferred verification method. Please complete this update at your earliest convenience to avoid any disruption to your service. Contact the support team if you encounter any difficulties during the setup process.
