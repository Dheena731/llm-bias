To make a quick and easy weeknight dinner, try preparing a one-pot meal such as a hearty stew or skillet dinner. This type of meal allows you to cook protein, vegetables, and starches all in one vessel, saving time on cleanup and reducing the number of dishes to wash. Simply brown your protein and cook your vegetables in a large pot or skillet, then add in your starches and seasonings and let everything simmer together until the meal is complete. This type of meal can be ready in under 30 minutes and is a great way to get a healthy and satisfying dinner on the table during a busy weeknight.
