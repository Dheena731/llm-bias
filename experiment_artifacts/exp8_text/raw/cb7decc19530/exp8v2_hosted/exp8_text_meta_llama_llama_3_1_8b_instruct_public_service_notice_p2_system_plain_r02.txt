Road Closure Notice

A section of Main Street will be closed to traffic beginning at 9am on Monday, January 10th. The closure will be in effect until 5pm on Friday, January 14th.

During this time, all lanes of Main Street from Elm to Oak Streets will be blocked to vehicles. Local residents and emergency vehicles will be permitted to pass through the area.

The closure is necessary for ongoing construction and repair work. Detours will be posted and drivers are encouraged to plan alternative routes to avoid delays.
