**Notice: How to Report a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If you have lost a company-issued laptop or believe it has been stolen, you must report it immediately. Prompt reporting helps protect sensitive company and customer data.

**What to do:**

1. **Report it within 24 hours** to your manager and the IT Helpdesk at [phone number] or [email address].
2. **Provide details**, including the laptop's make, model, asset tag number, and the date and location where it was last seen.
3. **File a police report** if the laptop was stolen, and send a copy to Human Resources or Security.
4. **Do not attempt to recover or access the device yourself.** IT will initiate remote lock and data-wipe procedures.
5. **Complete the Lost Equipment Form** available on the intranet under *IT Resources*.

**Please note:** Failure to report a lost device promptly may result in disciplinary action in accordance with company policy.

Thank you for helping keep our information secure.

*Information Security Team*
