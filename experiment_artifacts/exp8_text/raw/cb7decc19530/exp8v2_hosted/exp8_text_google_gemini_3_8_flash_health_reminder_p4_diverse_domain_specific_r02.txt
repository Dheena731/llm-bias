Your blink rate drops by roughly half the moment you lock onto a display. Right now, the ciliary muscles in your eyes are clamped tight, holding a static focal point about twenty inches from your face. 

Look away from the glass. Find the furthest object available—a water tower, a parked car, the far corner of the hallway—and let your gaze rest there until your field of view softens. Give your lenses room to flatten out.
