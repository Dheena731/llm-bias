Notice: Reporting a Lost Company Laptop

If you have lost a company laptop, or believe one has been lost or stolen, report it to the IT Service Desk and your line manager as soon as possible, and no later than four hours after you become aware of the loss. Prompt reporting allows the security team to disable access, protect company data, and meet any legal or contractual obligations.

When you report the loss, please provide the laptop's asset tag number, the make and model, the date and approximate time and location of the loss, and a brief description of what happened. If the laptop was stolen, also report the theft to the local police and send the police reference number to the IT Service Desk. Do not attempt to recover the device yourself or access any company systems from an unknown device to check its status.

The IT Service Desk can be reached by phone at extension 4000 or by email at itsupport@company.example. Outside office hours, contact the on-call IT duty officer through the emergency line. Failure to report a lost device promptly may result in disciplinary action under company policy.
