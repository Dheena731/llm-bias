We are implementing a revised expense approval workflow starting next Monday. This update is designed to streamline submissions, ensure consistent compliance, and provide clearer visibility into approval status. All employees must now submit expense reports through the designated portal rather than using email attachments. Each submission will route automatically to your direct manager for initial review, followed by finance verification before reimbursement. The updated process applies to all expenses incurred on or after the effective date. You can access the detailed guidelines and instructions on the internal resources page. If you have questions or encounter issues while submitting your first report under the new system, please contact the finance support team directly. Thank you for your cooperation as we improve our financial processes.
