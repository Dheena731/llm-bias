NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Effective Date: [Insert Date]

1. Purpose
This Notice sets forth the terms under which a purchaser ("Customer") may be eligible to receive a refund from [Company Name] ("Company") for products or services purchased directly from the Company.

2. Eligibility Period
A request for refund must be submitted in writing within thirty (30) calendar days of the original date of purchase. Requests received after this period shall be deemed ineligible unless otherwise required by applicable law.

3. Conditions of Eligibility
A Customer may be eligible for a refund only if all of the following conditions are satisfied:
(a) The Customer provides valid proof of purchase;
(b) The product is returned in its original condition and packaging, where applicable;
(c) The service has not been substantially performed or consumed; and
(d) The purchase is not within a category of non-refundable items identified in Section 4.

4. Non-Refundable Items
The following are not eligible for refund: (a) gift cards; (b) custom or personalized orders; (c) digital goods that have been downloaded or accessed; (d) items marked "final sale"; and (e) fees for services already rendered.

5. Method and Timing of Refund
Approved refunds shall be issued to the original method of payment within ten (10) business days of approval. The Company is not responsible for delays caused by third-party financial institutions. Shipping and handling charges are non-refundable unless the refund results from Company error.

6. Right to Deny
The Company reserves the right to deny any refund request that does not comply with this Notice or that, in its reasonable judgment, indicates fraud, abuse, or misuse of this policy.

7. Statutory Rights
Nothing in this Notice limits any rights a Customer may have under applicable consumer protection laws.

9. Modifications
The Company may amend this Notice at any time. Amendments apply only to purchases made after the effective date of the revised Notice.
