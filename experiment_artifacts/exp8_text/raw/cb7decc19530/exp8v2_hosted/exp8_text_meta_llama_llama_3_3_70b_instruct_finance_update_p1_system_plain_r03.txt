We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you keep track of your expenses. This summary will provide a clear and concise overview of your spending habits, including the total amount spent and a breakdown of the categories where your money was spent. The summary will be available on the first day of each month and will cover the previous month's spending. It will be sent to you via email and will also be accessible through our website. The monthly spending summary is designed to help you identify areas where you can cut back and make more informed financial decisions.
