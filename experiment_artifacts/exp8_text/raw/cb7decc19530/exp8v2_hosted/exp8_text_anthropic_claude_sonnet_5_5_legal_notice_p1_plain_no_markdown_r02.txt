Notice of Changes to Your Account Terms

We are writing to inform you that we are updating the terms that govern your account. These changes will take effect thirty days after the date of this notice, which we refer to as the Effective Date. Please read this notice carefully, as it affects your rights and obligations.

The updated terms revise how fees are calculated and when they are charged, clarify the circumstances under which we may suspend or close an account, and amend the procedure for resolving disputes. They also update our description of how we collect, use, and share your personal information. The complete revised terms are available through your account settings and will be mailed to you at no charge upon written request.

You are not required to take any action to accept the changes. If you continue to use your account after the Effective Date, you will be deemed to have agreed to the revised terms. If you do not agree, you may close your account before the Effective Date by contacting our customer service department in writing. Closing your account will not affect any balance owed or any obligation that arose before closure, and we will not charge a fee for closing the account in response to this notice.

Nothing in this notice waives any right or remedy available to us under your existing agreement, and nothing in it limits any right you may have under applicable law. If any provision of the revised terms is found to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions will continue in full force and effect. If there is a conflict between this notice and the revised terms, the revised terms will control.

If you have questions about these changes, please contact our customer service department using the address or telephone number shown on your most recent account statement.
