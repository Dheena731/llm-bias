When you get home on a busy weeknight, start by putting a large pot of salted water on to boil or setting a sheet pan in the oven to preheat before you do anything else. Those two steps take almost no effort, but they save you the most waiting time later. While the water heats or the oven warms up, you can chop vegetables, season a protein, or whisk together a quick sauce, so everything finishes at about the same time. A simple dinner of pasta with garlic, olive oil, and whatever vegetables you have on hand, or chicken thighs roasted alongside broccoli, can be on the table in thirty minutes this way. Finally, clean as you go by rinsing your cutting board and bowls while things cook, so you are not left with a mountain of dishes after you eat.
