Subject: Update to the company VPN client

Hello everyone,

We are replacing the current VPN client with a new version starting Monday, and the old client will stop working at the end of the following week. The new client connects faster, reconnects automatically after a dropped network connection, and supports the same sign-in credentials and multi-factor prompts you use today.

To make the switch, uninstall the old client, then install the new one from the Software Center on your company laptop. The whole process takes about ten minutes and requires a restart. Your saved connection settings will carry over, so you should not need to reconfigure anything.

If you run into trouble during installation or have trouble connecting afterward, contact the IT Help Desk by phone or through the support portal, and a technician will assist you. We recommend completing the update before the deadline so you are not locked out when working remotely.

Thank you for your help with this change.
