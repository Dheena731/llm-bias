Calendar tip: Stop using your calendar only for meetings. Block out time for your actual work, too.

If you have a report due Friday, schedule two 45-minute work sessions for it earlier in the week and give each one a specific title, like "Draft intro and outline" instead of just "Work on report." When your tasks have a time slot, they get done, and you can see at a glance when your week is already full.

Bonus: color-code these blocks differently from your meetings so you can tell how much of your week is focused work and how much is spent in conversations.
