**Introducing Savings Goals**

We've added a new way to help you save for what matters most. With Savings Goals, you can set a target, track your progress, and stay motivated along the way.

**What's new:**
- **Create custom goals:** Name your goal, whether it's a vacation, a new laptop, or an emergency fund, and set a target amount and date.
- **Track progress at a glance:** A visual progress bar shows how close you are to reaching each goal.
- **Automate your savings:** Set up recurring transfers so your goals grow without extra effort.
- **Celebrate milestones:** Get notified when you hit 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of your target.

**How to get started:**
Open the app, tap **Goals**, and select **Create a Goal**. It takes less than a minute.

Savings Goals is rolling out to all users over the next week. We'd love to hear what you think, so send us your feedback through the app's Help section.

Happy saving!
