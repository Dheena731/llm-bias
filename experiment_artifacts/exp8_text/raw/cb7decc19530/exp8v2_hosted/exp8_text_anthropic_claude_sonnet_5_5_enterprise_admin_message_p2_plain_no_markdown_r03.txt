Subject: Update to the expense approval workflow

Hello everyone,

Starting Monday, all expense claims will follow a new approval workflow in the finance system. The change is meant to shorten reimbursement times and make it easier to see where a claim stands at any point.

When you submit a claim, it will go first to your direct manager, who will have three business days to approve or return it. Claims under 500 dollars will then be paid in the next regular payment run. Claims of 500 dollars or more will also need review from the finance team before payment. If anything is missing, such as a receipt or a project code, the claim will be sent back to you with a note explaining what to fix, so please check your notifications regularly.

Claims submitted before Monday will be processed under the existing method, so there is no need to resubmit anything. Managers should look out for approval requests by email and, where possible, set up a delegate for any planned time away so that claims do not stall.

A short guide and a recorded walkthrough are available on the finance page of the intranet. If you have questions or run into problems, please contact the finance helpdesk and we will be glad to help.

Thank you for your cooperation.

Regards,
The Finance Administration Team
