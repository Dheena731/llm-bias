The community is reminded that the public library is open six days a week to support learning, research, and quiet reading. Doors open from nine in the morning until eight in the evening, Monday through Thursday. On Fridays and Saturdays, services are available from ten in the morning until five in the afternoon. The library remains closed on Sundays and official public holidays. Visitors are encouraged to make use of book borrowing, computer stations, and quiet study areas during these operational times. Digital catalogs, audiobooks, and research databases remain accessible online twenty-four hours a day. For book returns outside of regular operating hours, please use the drop box located near the main entrance.
