Thank you for sharing your draft literature review. The organizing question of the review is clear, and the decision to trace the development of the field from early foundational studies to recent methodological debates gives the reader a sensible historical frame. The prose is generally clean, and several of the summaries of individual studies are accurate and concise.

The main weakness is that the review reads more as a sequence of summaries than as an argument. Each paragraph describes a study or a cluster of studies, but the connections between them are rarely made explicit. Where studies disagree, as the two papers on sampling strategy in the third section do, the draft reports both positions without indicating which findings are more persuasive or why the disagreement exists. A stronger version would identify the points of tension, explain whether they stem from differences in method, context, or theory, and state where you position your own work in relation to them. Each section would benefit from a closing synthesis sentence or short paragraph that tells the reader what the preceding literature establishes and what it leaves unresolved.

The search strategy also needs more detail. The draft mentions databases and keywords but does not say what time range was covered, which inclusion criteria were applied, or how many sources were screened and excluded. Even in a narrative review, this information helps readers judge whether the selection is representative, and it protects you against the charge of cherry-picking.

Some citations need attention. A few claims in the introduction are stated as established consensus without a supporting reference, and the review should either cite these claims or soften them. The reference list also appears to be missing page numbers for two journal articles and the publication year for one report.

Finally, the conclusion introduces a research gap that is only partly developed in the body of the review. The gap is promising, but readers will need to see in the preceding sections exactly what has been overlooked. Tracing the gap back to specific shortcomings in the cited work would make the conclusion feel earned.

Overall, this is a solid foundation. With more analytical connection between sources, a clearer account of how the literature was selected, and a tighter link between the gap and the evidence, the review would be substantially stronger.
