**Public Service Notice: Library Opening Hours**

The City Public Library reminds residents of its current opening hours:

- **Monday through Friday:** 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
- **Saturday:** 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- **Sunday:** 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m.

The library will be closed on major public holidays. Please check the library's website or call the information desk before visiting to confirm any changes to these hours.

All residents are welcome to borrow books, use public computers, attend programs, and access study spaces during open hours.
