**Subject: Payout delays on Tuesday and Wednesday batches (resolved)**

Payouts initiated Tuesday, March 11 were not sent to the ACH network until Wednesday afternoon. Of the 1,212 payouts in that batch ($386,410.18 total), 940 were scheduled for Wednesday morning and will now settle Thursday, March 13. The remaining 272 were set to settle Wednesday evening and should land Thursday as well.

The cause was our file generator. It was still using the 3:30 p.m. ET cutoff for same-day submissions, but our receiving bank moved its cutoff to 2:45 p.m. on March 1. The batch we built at 3:30 was rejected at intake, and our retry didn't fire until the next morning because the rejection was logged as a warning, not an error. We've changed the cutoff, made intake rejections page the on-call engineer, and added a check that compares our cutoff to the bank's published schedule each morning.

If you're a seller and a payout you expected Wednesday hasn't shown up by Thursday at noon ET, reply with the payout ID and we'll trace it directly. Bank holds on the receiving end can add a day, so please don't resubmit anything on your side.
