Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starting March 1

Hello team,

Starting March 1, we are introducing a new expense approval workflow to make reimbursements faster and more transparent.

Here is what is changing:

Expenses under $250 will be approved by your direct manager within 3 business days.
Expenses between $250 and $1,000 will require approval from your department head.
Expenses over $1,000 will need sign-off from the finance team before payment is issued.

All expense reports must now be submitted through the updated portal, and receipts must be attached at the time of submission. Reports without receipts will be returned to the submitter.

A short training session will be held on February 24 at 2:00 PM in the main conference room, and a recording will be available afterward for those who cannot attend. Please complete the brief onboarding module in the portal before March 1.

If you have questions, contact the finance operations team at financeops@company.com.

Thank you for your cooperation as we roll out this process.

Best regards,
Administration Team
