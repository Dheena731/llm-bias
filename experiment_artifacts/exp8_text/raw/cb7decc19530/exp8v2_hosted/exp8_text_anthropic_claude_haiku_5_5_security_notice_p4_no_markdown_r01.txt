Security Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication

To help protect your account, we strongly recommend turning on two-factor authentication (2FA). With 2FA, signing in requires both your password and a second verification step, such as a one-time code sent to your phone or generated by an authenticator app. Even if someone learns your password, they will not be able to access your account without that second step.

To enable two-factor authentication, go to Settings, select Security, and choose Two-Factor Authentication. Follow the on-screen instructions to link your phone or authenticator app, then save the backup codes we provide in a safe place. You will need these codes if you lose access to your device.

Enabling 2FA takes only a few minutes. If you have questions or need help setting it up, please contact our support team.

Thank you for helping us keep your account secure.
