**Monthly spending summary (rolling out this week)**

On the first business day of each month, you'll get a summary of the previous month's checking and card activity. It's one screen, and every number comes from transactions that have posted to your accounts, not estimates.

**What's in it**

- **Total out**, compared with your average for the last three full months. We use three because a single unusual month, like a car repair or a holiday, would skew a one-month comparison.
- **Top five categories**, with the change from the prior month. Categories come from your bank, with some of our corrections applied. If a big-box store lands in Groceries when you mostly buy housewares, recategorize it and the summary updates.
- **Recurring charges** that posted during the month, grouped together. This is usually where forgotten subscriptions show up.
- **Refunds netted against their original category.** A $60 return to a clothing store reduces Clothing. It doesn't show up as income.

**What it leaves out**

Transfers between your own accounts and credit card payments are excluded, since the underlying purchases are already counted. Pending transactions aren't included either. A charge from the 30th that clears on the 2nd appears in the next month's summary.

**An example**

A March summary might read: "Out $2,841, up $190 from your three-month average. Dining +$112, mostly from nine orders through one delivery app."

**Turning it off**

Go to Settings → Notifications. The summary is also saved under Insights, so deleting the email doesn't remove it.

**Known gaps**

Cash withdrawals show as a single uncategorized line. Foreign-currency purchases use the exchange rate from the posting date, which can differ slightly from your card issuer's rate. We're working on both.
