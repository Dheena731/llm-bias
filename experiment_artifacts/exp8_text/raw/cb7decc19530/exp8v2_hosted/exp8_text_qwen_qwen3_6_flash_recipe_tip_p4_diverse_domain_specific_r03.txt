Skip the “pre-assembled” lunch trap. Batch-cook sturdy proteins and grains, roast a few neutral vegetables, but leave acids, oils, and crunchy toppings completely separate until morning. Store each element in its own container, keep dressings in narrow jars so they don’t bleed into adjacent items, and build your plate straight from the fridge using whatever’s still holding up that day. You’ll cut afternoon sogginess in half and stop tossing wilted ingredients by Thursday.
