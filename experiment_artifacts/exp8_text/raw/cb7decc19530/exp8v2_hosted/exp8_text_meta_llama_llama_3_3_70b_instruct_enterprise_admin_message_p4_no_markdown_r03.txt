Attention all system administrators and access managers, 

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain the security and integrity of our systems, we will be conducting our quarterly access reviews. These reviews are an essential part of our security protocols, ensuring that all users have the appropriate levels of access to our systems and data.

During this review period, you will be required to verify and validate the access rights of all users within your respective domains. This includes reviewing user accounts, permissions, and group memberships to ensure they are up-to-date and align with current job requirements.

The review period will begin on the first day of the quarter and must be completed by the end of the quarter. You will receive a separate notification with detailed instructions and deadlines.

If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT security team. We appreciate your cooperation and prompt attention to this matter, as it is crucial to maintaining the security of our systems.
