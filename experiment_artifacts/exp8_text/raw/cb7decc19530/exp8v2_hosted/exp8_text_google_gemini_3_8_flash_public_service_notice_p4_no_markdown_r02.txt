PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT

A Heat Advisory has been issued for our area, effective from 11:00 AM today through 8:00 PM tomorrow. High temperatures combined with humidity are expected to create heat index values exceeding 105 degrees Fahrenheit.

Extreme heat and humidity significantly increase the risk of heat-related illnesses, such as heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Residents are strongly advised to take the following precautions:

1. Drink plenty of water and stay hydrated, even if you do not feel thirsty.
2. Avoid strenuous outdoor activities during the hottest parts of the day.
3. Stay in air-conditioned spaces whenever possible.
4. Never leave children or pets unattended in parked vehicles.
5. Check on elderly relatives, neighbors, and those who may be vulnerable to the heat.

Designated cooling centers are open at the Central Community Center and the Main Public Library. If you or someone you know shows signs of heat stroke, such as a high body temperature, confusion, or loss of consciousness, call 911 immediately.
