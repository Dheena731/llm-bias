To get the most out of your calendar app, try setting reminders for the day before a scheduled event, in addition to the reminder on the day of the event itself. This can help you prepare and make any necessary arrangements, such as packing materials or making travel plans, and can reduce stress and last-minute scrambles. By taking a few seconds to set an extra reminder, you can stay on top of your schedule and ensure that you are always prepared for what's coming up.
