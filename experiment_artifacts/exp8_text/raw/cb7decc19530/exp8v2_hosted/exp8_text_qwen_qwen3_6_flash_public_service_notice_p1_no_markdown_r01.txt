PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE

LIBRARY OPENING HOURS

Please be advised of the current operating schedule for the Municipal Library:

Monday through Thursday: 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM
Friday: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
Saturday: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM
Sunday: Closed

The library will be closed on all public holidays, including Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day. Early closure at 1:00 PM applies to Christmas Eve and the day before Thanksgiving.

For service updates or inquiries, please call 555-0199 or visit citylibrary.gov.

Thank you for supporting your community library.
