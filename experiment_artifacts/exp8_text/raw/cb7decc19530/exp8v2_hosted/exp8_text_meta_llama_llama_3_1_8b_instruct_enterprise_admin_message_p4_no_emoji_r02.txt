**Quarterly Access Review**

As part of our ongoing efforts to ensure the security and integrity of our systems, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews. This process involves verifying the necessity and appropriateness of user access to our systems, networks, and sensitive data.

If you are an employee or contractor with access to our systems, you may be contacted by our IT department to confirm your access is still necessary and relevant. You may be asked to provide information about your role, responsibilities, and the systems you use to perform your job functions.

We encourage all users to take this opportunity to review and update their access levels, ensuring that they only have the necessary permissions to perform their job duties. This will help us to maintain a secure and compliant environment.

If you have any questions or concerns about the quarterly access review process, please do not hesitate to contact our IT department.
