**Feedback on Methods Section: Draft 1**

Thank you for a clear and well-organized methods section. Your participant description is detailed, and the step-by-step procedure is easy to follow, so another researcher could roughly replicate your study.

**Strengths**
- You describe your sample (size, age range, recruitment) in a logical order.
- Your justification for the outcome measure is clear and cites appropriate literature.

**Areas to strengthen**
1. **Sampling rationale:** Explain why you chose convenience sampling over random selection, and acknowledge how this may limit generalizability.
2. **Materials:** Report the reliability of your test instrument (e.g., Cronbach's alpha from prior studies or your pilot data).
3. **Procedure timing:** Specify how many weeks the intervention lasted and how often sessions occurred. "Several weeks" is too vague.
4. **Analysis plan:** You name a t-test but don't say why it's appropriate. State your assumptions (normality, equal variances) and how you checked them.

**Priority for revision:** Start with item 4. Reviewers will look closely at whether your analysis matches your design, and currently the link is implied rather than stated.

Overall, this is a solid foundation. With these additions, the section will be much more rigorous and transparent.
