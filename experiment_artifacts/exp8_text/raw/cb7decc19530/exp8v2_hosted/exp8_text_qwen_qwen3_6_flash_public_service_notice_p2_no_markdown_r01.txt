PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE

Due to urgent utility work, Main Street between Oak Avenue and Pine Boulevard will be closed to all traffic from Monday, November 18 at 7:00 AM through Wednesday, November 20 at 5:00 PM.

Motorists should use Elm Drive or Cedar Lane as detour routes. Sidewalk access in the construction zone will remain open for pedestrians. Emergency vehicles will have unrestricted access at all times.

We apologize for the disruption and appreciate your cooperation. For status updates, call 555-0100 or visit citytransport.gov/closures.
