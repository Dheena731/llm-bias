Subject: Reminder: Book your annual health checkup

Hello,

This is a friendly reminder to book your annual health checkup. Regular checkups help your doctor spot potential health problems early, when they are often easier to manage, and they give you a chance to discuss any concerns you have about your health.

To schedule your appointment, contact your primary care provider's office or use their online booking system if one is available. If you are not sure who your provider is, check your insurance card or the contact information you received when you enrolled.

Taking a few minutes to book now can save you time later. Please schedule your checkup as soon as you can.

Thank you, and take care of your health.
