**NOTICE OF CHANGED POLLING PLACES**
**Hollis County Elections Office | General Election, November 4**

Three precincts will vote at new locations this year. If you live in one of these precincts, confirm your location before you go.

**Precinct 14** was moved from Eastbrook Elementary (410 Orchard Road) to **St. Brendan's Parish Hall, 88 Mill Street.** Use the north entrance. The gravel lot behind the hall has two accessible spaces.

**Precinct 22** was moved from the Lincoln VFW Post (formerly 3117 Lincoln Ave.) to **Ridgeview Public Library, Community Room, 2 Pine Court.** The room is on the ground floor, and there are no stairs between the entrance and the voting tables.

**Precinct 31** stays at Tri-Township Fire Station No. 2, 1907 Carver Pike, but voters should enter through the rear bay door marked "Voting." The front doors are reserved for emergency vehicles.

Polls are open 6:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Voters in line at 8:00 p.m. will still be allowed to cast a ballot.

To find your precinct and polling place, go to hollisvotes.example/locate or call 555-0142 weekdays from 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Your sample ballot also lists your assigned location.

If you received a voter card in the mail before September 1, it may show an old address. The location printed on the card may be out of date. Check the list above or the online lookup.
