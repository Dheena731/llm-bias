Enabling two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of protection to your account. Once it is turned on, signing in will require your password along with a second verification step, such as a one-time code sent to your phone or generated by an authenticator app. This makes it much harder for someone else to access your account, even if they have obtained your password.

To turn on two-factor authentication, open the security settings in your account and select the option to enable two-factor authentication. You will be asked to choose a verification method and confirm it by entering a code. Once setup is complete, you will be given a set of backup codes. Please store these somewhere safe, as they allow you to sign in if you lose access to your phone or authenticator app.

If you have any questions or run into trouble during setup, please contact our support team for assistance.
