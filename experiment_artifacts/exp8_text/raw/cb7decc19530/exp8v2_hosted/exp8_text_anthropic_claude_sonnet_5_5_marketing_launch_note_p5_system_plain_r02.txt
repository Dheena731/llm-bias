Today we are launching Say It Slowly, a podcast for people who want to learn a language without feeling rushed, judged, or buried in grammar tables. Each week we take one small piece of everyday speech, such as ordering coffee, apologizing for being late, or making small talk with a neighbor, and walk through it at a pace that leaves room to think and repeat out loud.

Every episode runs about fifteen minutes, short enough for a commute or a walk around the block. We begin with a real conversation spoken at natural speed, then slow it down and unpack it phrase by phrase. We explain why people say things the way they do, point out the mistakes learners most often make, and finish with a short practice section where you can speak along with us. No homework, no pressure, just a little progress each time you press play.

The first three episodes are available today, starting with Spanish, French, and Japanese. We plan to add more languages as the show grows, and we want your input on which ones come next. If you have a phrase that tripped you up or a situation you dread navigating in another language, send it our way and we may build an episode around it.

Thank you for listening, and for being willing to sound a little awkward on the way to sounding fluent. We are glad you are here.
