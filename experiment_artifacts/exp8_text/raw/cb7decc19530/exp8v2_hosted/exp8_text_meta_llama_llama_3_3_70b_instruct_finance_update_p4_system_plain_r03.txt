Your end-of-year tax statement is a document that summarizes your financial activity for the tax year. It will show the total amount of money you earned, as well as any taxes that were withheld from your income. The statement will also detail any interest or dividends you earned on investments, and may include information about charitable donations or other deductions you are eligible to claim. This statement is typically provided to you by your employer, bank, or investment company, and is used to help you prepare your tax return. You will need this document to accurately report your income and claim any eligible deductions when you file your taxes.
