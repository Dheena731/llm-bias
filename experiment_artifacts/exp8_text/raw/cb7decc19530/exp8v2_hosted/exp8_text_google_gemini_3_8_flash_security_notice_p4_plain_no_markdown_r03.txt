We are pleased to announce that two-factor authentication is now available to help keep your account secure. This security feature adds an extra layer of protection by requiring a temporary verification code in addition to your password whenever you log in. To turn on this feature, navigate to your account settings, select the security tab, and follow the on-screen instructions to register your mobile device or authenticator app. Enabling two-factor authentication significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access, and we strongly encourage all users to set it up as soon as possible. If you need any assistance during the process, please reach out to our technical support team.
