We are pleased to introduce our new monthly spending summary, designed to give you a clear overview of your financial habits. This feature automatically categorizes your transactions so you can see exactly how much you spent on food, transportation, utilities, and other areas during the month. You will find a detailed breakdown of your total expenses along with visual comparisons to previous months, making it easier to spot patterns and adjust your budget accordingly. To view your summary, simply navigate to the reports section within your account settings. If you need assistance accessing this tool or have any feedback about the new layout, please reach out to our support team for help.
