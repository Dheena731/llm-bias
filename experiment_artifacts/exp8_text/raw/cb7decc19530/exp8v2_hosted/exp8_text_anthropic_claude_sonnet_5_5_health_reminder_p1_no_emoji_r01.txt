**Weekly Fitness Check-In**

It's time to review your progress for the week. Take a few minutes to:

- Look over your completed workouts and activity totals
- Compare your results to the goals you set
- Note any improvements, such as added reps, longer distances, or better consistency
- Identify what got in the way, and adjust your plan for the week ahead

Small, steady progress adds up. Use this review to stay on track and set realistic targets for the next seven days.
