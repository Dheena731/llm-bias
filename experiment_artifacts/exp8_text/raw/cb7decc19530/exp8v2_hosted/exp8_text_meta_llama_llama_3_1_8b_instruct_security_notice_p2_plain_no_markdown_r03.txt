Phishing Email Warning

As you are aware, the company takes the security of its data and systems very seriously. In recent weeks, we have seen a rise in phishing email attempts targeting our employees.

Phishing emails are designed to trick individuals into revealing sensitive information such as login credentials, passwords, or other confidential data. These emails often appear to come from a trusted source, but upon closer inspection, they are not legitimate.

If you receive an email that asks you to provide sensitive information or login credentials, do not respond or click on any links. Instead, report the email to the IT department immediately. We will investigate the email and take necessary action to prevent further attempts.

Remember, it is always better to err on the side of caution when dealing with emails that seem suspicious. If in doubt, report it.

Thank you for your cooperation and assistance in maintaining the security of our systems and data.
