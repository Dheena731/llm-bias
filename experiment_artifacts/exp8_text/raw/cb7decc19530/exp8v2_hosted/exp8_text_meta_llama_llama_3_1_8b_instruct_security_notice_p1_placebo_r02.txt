**Important Security Reminder**

To all employees,

We would like to remind everyone that password reuse is a significant security risk for our organization. According to recent statistics, a staggering 70% of employees reuse passwords across multiple company systems. This can lead to a breach of sensitive information and put our entire network at risk.

Our IT department has implemented a robust system to monitor and detect password reuse. To minimize the risk of a security incident, please do not reuse passwords across different company platforms, including email, intranet, and applications.

 Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action, including possible termination.

If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our IT department at [security@company.com](mailto:security@company.com).

Thank you for your cooperation and commitment to maintaining a secure work environment.
