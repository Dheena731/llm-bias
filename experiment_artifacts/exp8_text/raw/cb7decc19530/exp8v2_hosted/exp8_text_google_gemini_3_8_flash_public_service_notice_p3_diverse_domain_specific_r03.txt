**PUBLIC NOTICE: REVISED COLLECTION SCHEDULE & FIBER SEPARATION**  
**Department of Public Works | Bureau of Sanitation**  
**Effective: Monday, November 4**

Due to regional Materials Recovery Facility (MRF) processing adjustments and route rebalancing, the following changes apply to all residential curbside services in **Zone 3**:

**1. Service Day Shift**  
Recycling pickup moves from **Wednesdays to Thursdays**. Trash collection days are unaffected. 

**2. Mandatory Dual-Stream Sorting (Fiber vs. Commingled)**  
Paper and cardboard may no longer be mixed inside the blue 64-gallon cart.

*   **Blue Rolling Cart:** Clean rigid plastics (#1, #2, and #5 only), tin/aluminum cans, and intact glass bottles/jars.
*   **Fiber (Cardboard & Paper):** Corrugated boxes must be flattened, tied with natural twine into bundles not exceeding 3 ft × 3 ft × 1 ft, and placed *beside* the blue cart. Loose newsprint and mixed paper must be placed in a separate, open rigid container marked "FIBER."

**3. Curbside Placement & Tagging Policy**  
*   Carts must be set out by **6:00 AM** with lids fully closed and wheels facing the residence, leaving 3 feet of clearance from parked vehicles.
*   Loads containing plastic bags/film, styrofoam, or food contamination will receive an orange non-collection tag and will remain at the curb until the following cycle.

Route maps and container decal stickers are available at the Public Works Service Center (820 Municipal Way) or through the municipal waste dispatch line at 555-0144.
