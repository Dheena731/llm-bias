**PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE**

**Library Hours Update**

The Riverside Public Library invites all residents to visit during our regular opening hours:

- **Monday – Friday:** 9:00 a.m. – 8:00 p.m.
- **Saturday:** 10:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.
- **Sunday:** 1:00 p.m. – 5:00 p.m.

The library will be closed on all major public holidays. Please check our website or call (555) 123-4567 for holiday schedules and any temporary changes.

Our doors are open to everyone. Stop by to borrow books, use free computers and Wi-Fi, attend a community program, or simply find a quiet place to read and learn.
