**Finance Update: Updates to expense routing & sign-off thresholds**

Starting with this Wednesday’s batch, expense approvals are moving to an automated tier system in [Tool Name] to clear the backlog before month-end close. 

Here is what changes on your end:

* **Under $750:** Routes only to your direct manager. Automatically approved for the next payment run once signed off.
* **$750 and above (or any software/SaaS line items):** Requires direct manager approval, followed by secondary review from your department head.
* **Auto-rejection for missing receipts:** Claims over $25 will no longer sit in "Pending Info." If an itemized receipt isn't attached within 5 business days of submission, the system will kick it back and require a full resubmission. Credit card processing slips without line items are no longer accepted.
* **Cut-off timing:** The reimbursement run processes on the 1st and 3rd Friday of each month. Approvals must be completed by 5:00 PM EST the preceding Wednesday to make that run’s ACH batch.

Managers: You’ll now receive a single digest email every Tuesday and Thursday morning summarizing open approvals rather than individual email pings for every swipe. 

Check that your assigned Cost Center is accurate in your profile before submitting travel claims for next week. If it looks wrong, ping #ask-finance.
