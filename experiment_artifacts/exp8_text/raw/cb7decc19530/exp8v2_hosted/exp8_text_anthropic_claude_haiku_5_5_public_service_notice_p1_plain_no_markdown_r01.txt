Public Service Notice: Library Opening Hours

The Riverside Public Library wants to remind residents of its current opening hours. The main branch is open Monday through Friday from 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., Saturday from 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., and Sunday from 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. The Eastside Branch is open Tuesday through Saturday from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. and is closed on Sunday and Monday.

Please note that the library will be closed on all national holidays, including New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. Hours may change during holiday weeks, so please check the library's website or call the information desk before visiting.

For up-to-date information, visit the library's website or contact the information desk during open hours. Thank you for supporting your local library.
