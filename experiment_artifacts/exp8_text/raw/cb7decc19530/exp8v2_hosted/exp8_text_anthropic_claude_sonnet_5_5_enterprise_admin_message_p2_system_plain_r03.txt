Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starting Monday

Hello everyone,

Beginning Monday, all expense reports will be submitted and approved through our new online workflow instead of by email. The change is meant to speed up reimbursements and give everyone a clear view of where each request stands.

To submit an expense, log in to the finance portal, create a new report, attach your itemized receipts, and select the appropriate project or cost center. Your direct manager will receive the request automatically and has three business days to review it. Anything over $1,000 will also go to the finance team for a second approval. You will get an email at each step, and you can check the status of any report in the portal at any time.

Please note that reports submitted by email after Monday will not be processed, and receipts must be uploaded within 30 days of the purchase date. Reimbursements will be paid in the next regular payroll cycle once a report is fully approved.

A short training video and a step-by-step guide are available on the intranet under the Finance section. If you run into any problems or have questions, contact the admin team at admin@company.com, and we will be glad to help.

Thank you for your help with the transition.

Best regards,
The Administration Team
