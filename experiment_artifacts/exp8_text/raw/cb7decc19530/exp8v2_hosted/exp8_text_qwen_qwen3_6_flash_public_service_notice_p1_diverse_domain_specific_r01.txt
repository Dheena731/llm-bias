**NOTICE: MODIFIED OPERATING HOURS & STAFFING REALIGNMENT**

Effective November 6, the Central Branch will adjust its weekly schedule to align with municipal funding cycles and peak academic term demand. Please review the updated times below and note the operational constraints.

**Weekly Hours**
*   **Monday – Thursday:** 09:00 – 19:30
*   **Friday:** 09:00 – 17:00
*   **Saturday:** 10:00 – 15:00
*   **Sunday:** Closed

**Service Adjustments**
*   **Circulation Cutoff:** All checkout and return transactions must be completed at the main desk or kiosk by 19:15 weekdays and 16:45 Fridays. Materials deposited in the exterior security drop-box after these times will be processed on the next business day; overdue fines accrue based on the deposit timestamp.
*   **Unmanned Hours:** Between 17:00 and closing, the reference desk will be unstaffed. Patrons requiring research assistance should utilize the chat service on the library portal or submit email inquiries. Response times may exceed 24 hours after 16:00.
*   **Shelf-Reading Protocol:** To maintain inventory accuracy, staff will perform re-shelving and gap-filling in the Stacks Section on Thursday
