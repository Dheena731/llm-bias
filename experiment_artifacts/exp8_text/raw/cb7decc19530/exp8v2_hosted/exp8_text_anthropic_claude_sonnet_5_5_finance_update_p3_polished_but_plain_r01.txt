We have added a new savings goals feature to help you set aside money for the things that matter most. You can now create a goal for anything you are working toward, whether that is an emergency fund, a vacation, or a down payment, and give each one a target amount and a date you would like to reach it by.

Once a goal is set, the app calculates how much you would need to save each week or month to stay on track. You can move money into a goal manually whenever you like, or schedule recurring transfers so that your progress happens automatically. A simple progress view shows how close you are to the target and whether you are ahead of or behind your plan.

You can create as many goals as you need and adjust them at any time. If your priorities change, you can raise or lower a target, move the deadline, or shift funds from one goal to another without penalty. When you reach a goal, you will receive a notification, and you can choose to keep the money where it is or move it back to your main account.

To get started, open the Savings section and choose to add a new goal. It takes only a minute to set up, and we recommend starting with one small, achievable target to build the habit. We would love to hear how the feature works for you, so please share your feedback through the support page in the app.
