Welcome to Words Between Us, a new podcast for anyone who has ever stumbled over a sentence in a language they are trying to learn. Each week we sit down with teachers, translators, and everyday speakers to talk about the parts of language that textbooks often skip: the slang your grandmother uses, the idioms that make no literal sense, the moment you realize you have been pronouncing a word wrong for years.

Our first season focuses on the experience of learning. We will hear from people who picked up a second language in a classroom, on a construction site, through years of video calls with family abroad, and in the middle of a move to a new country where nobody spoke their mother tongue. Each episode includes a short lesson you can try at home, but the heart of the show is the conversation. Mistakes are welcome here, and so is the stubborn joy of getting a joke in a language you were told you would never master.

New episodes arrive every Thursday. You can listen while commuting, cooking, or walking the dog, and no prior knowledge is required beyond curiosity. Pull up a chair, bring your rough accent, and let's start talking.
