We are excited to introduce Savings Goals, a new feature designed to help you plan, track, and reach your financial milestones with ease. 

Whether you are building an emergency fund, planning a vacation, or saving for a major purchase, you can now create personalized targets directly within your account. Set a target amount and deadline, monitor your visual progress, and schedule automatic transfers to keep your savings on track. 

This feature is available now. Update your app to the latest version or log in to your dashboard to set up your first goal.
