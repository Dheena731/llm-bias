**Action Required: Review Your Passwords Immediately**

To protect our organization's data, **please ensure your work passwords are never used on personal websites, apps, or social media.**

### ⚠️ Why This Matters
Cybercriminals often use **"credential stuffing"** attacks. When personal accounts are breached, hackers automatically try those leaked passwords on corporate login pages. If you reuse a password, a breach on a retail or gaming site could give an attacker access to our systems.

### ✅ What You Must Do
1.  **Create Unique Passwords:** Your work password must be distinct from any personal account.
2.  **Check for Exposure:** Visit **[Link to Company Credential Check Tool or "Have I Been Pwned"]** to see if your details have appeared in known breaches.
3.  **Update Immediately:** If you find you've reused a password, change it now.
4.  **Use a Password Manager:** If you struggle to remember unique passwords, please use the company-approved password manager.
5.  **Enable MFA:** Ensure Multi-Factor Authentication is turned on for all work accounts.

Please take two minutes today to audit your passwords. Thank you for helping us keep our data safe.
