Here is a short message explaining an end-of-year tax statement:

This document provides a summary of your tax obligations for the past year. It outlines the income you have received and the taxes that have been withheld from those payments. The statement also includes any additional taxes you may need to pay or credits you are eligible for. The information in this document will be used to determine your tax liability for the year. If you have any questions or concerns about your tax statement, please contact us.
