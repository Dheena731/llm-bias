To keep fresh herbs at their best, it's essential to store them properly. Start by gently rinsing the herbs with cool water to remove any dirt or debris, then pat them dry with a paper towel to remove excess moisture. Next, place the herbs in a sealed container or plastic bag, making sure to remove as much air as possible before sealing. Store the container or bag in the refrigerator, where the herbs should keep for several days to a week. For longer storage, consider freezing the herbs in ice cube trays filled with water or olive oil, which can be used in cooking later. By following these simple steps, you can enjoy your fresh herbs for a longer period and add flavor to a variety of dishes.
