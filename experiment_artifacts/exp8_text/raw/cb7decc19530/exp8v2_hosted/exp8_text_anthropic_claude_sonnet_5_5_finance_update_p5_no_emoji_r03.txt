**Update: Delayed Payment Processing**

We are currently experiencing delays in processing some payments. Transactions submitted over the past 24 hours may take longer than usual to appear in your account.

**What we know:** The delay is caused by a backlog in our payment processing system. No payments have been lost, and all submitted transactions are being queued and will be completed.

**What this means for you:** Payments may take up to 2 to 3 business days to post instead of the usual 1 day. You do not need to resubmit any payments, as doing so could result in duplicate charges.

**What we are doing:** Our technical team is actively working to clear the backlog and restore normal processing times. We expect to have this resolved by the end of the week.

If a delayed payment is causing a late fee or other issue, please contact our support team and we will work to correct it promptly.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience. We will share another update once normal processing resumes.
