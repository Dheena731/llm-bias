**NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATION VERIFICATION**

To all registered voters: Your polling station is assigned according to your current registered residential address. Because polling locations may change from one election to the next, you must confirm your assigned site before voting. Visit the official website of your state or local election authority to access the verified directory of polling places. The official resource provides exact street addresses, building entrances, accessibility features, and confirmed opening and closing times. You may also call your local election office directly for confirmation. Do not rely on previous election locations, unofficial websites, or social media announcements, as these may be outdated or inaccurate. Bring any identification documents required by your jurisdiction, and allow extra time for travel and security screening. Poll workers and signage will be present at each location on election day. Verify your assigned polling station well in advance to ensure your vote is counted.
