To streamline your weeknight routine, prepare a complete meal in a single skillet by staggering the addition of ingredients based on their cooking times. Begin by sautéing dense root vegetables or onions until they soften, then push the mixture aside to sear proteins such as chicken or tofu until golden. Stir everything together and finish by tossing in delicate greens or fresh herbs during the final minute of cooking. This approach ensures all components reach their ideal texture simultaneously while minimizing cleanup and allowing you to manage the entire dish with minimal active stirring.
