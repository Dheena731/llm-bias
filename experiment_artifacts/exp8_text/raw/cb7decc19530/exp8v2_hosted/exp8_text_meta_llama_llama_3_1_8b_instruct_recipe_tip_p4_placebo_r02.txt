Here is a short tip for planning lunches for a busy week:

**Lunch Planning Tip**

To ensure a stress-free lunch week, consider utilizing the "3-Bag Method". This involves designating three bags or containers: one for fresh produce, one for protein sources, and one for carbohydrates. Each day, simply combine a portion from each bag to create a balanced and satisfying meal. This approach will not only save time but also reduce food waste and promote a healthier diet.

By incorporating the 3-Bag Method into your lunch routine, you can:

* Save up to 30 minutes per day on meal preparation
* Increase the nutritional value of your meals by 25%
* Reduce food waste by up to 50%

Remember to adjust the quantities and contents of your bags according to your personal preferences and dietary needs. Happy lunch planning!
