Take a Short Screen Break

You have been looking at a screen for a while now. Your eyes, neck, and back will feel better if you step away for a few minutes.

Try this:
- Look at something about 20 feet away for about 20 seconds.
- Stand up, stretch your arms and shoulders, and walk around for a minute or two.
- Drink some water or get a breath of fresh air.

Short, regular breaks help you stay focused and comfortable. Your work will still be there when you return.
