**HEAT ADVISORY: PUBLIC NOTICE**

A heat advisory is in effect for our area. Hot weather, sometimes made worse by humidity, can make conditions dangerous for anyone who spends time outdoors or in buildings without cooling. Please take precautions until local weather officials announce that the advisory has ended.

**Who is most at risk**
- Older adults
- Infants and young children
- People with chronic health conditions
- People who work or exercise outdoors
- People without air conditioning

**What you should do**
- Drink water regularly, even if you do not feel thirsty.
- Spend as much time as possible in air-conditioned or otherwise cool places.
- Limit outdoor activity, especially during the hottest part of the day.
- Wear light, loose-fitting clothing.
- Never leave people or pets in a parked vehicle, even for a short time.
- Check on neighbors, friends, and relatives who may be at risk.

**Know the warning signs**
- *Heat exhaustion:* heavy sweating, weakness, cold or clammy skin, headache, nausea, or dizziness. Move to a cool place, loosen clothing, and sip water.
- *Heat stroke:* very high body temperature, hot skin, confusion, or fainting. This is a medical emergency. Call your local emergency number right away.

Check local weather reports for the latest updates.
