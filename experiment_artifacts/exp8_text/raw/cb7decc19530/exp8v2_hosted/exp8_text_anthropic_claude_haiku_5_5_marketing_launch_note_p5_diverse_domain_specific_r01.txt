**Kitchen Table Polish**

For eleven years I nodded through Sunday lunches in Kraków without catching the jokes. This show is my attempt to fix that, out loud and one errand at a time.

Each episode centers on a real exchange: buying rye at a bakery counter, describing a cough to a pharmacist, arguing with a landlord about a radiator. I record the conversation as it happens, then stop and go through it phrase by phrase. You'll hear me mangle the locative at the bakery, freeze on a word the cashier says twice, and, eventually, laugh a half-second late at something funny.

It's for people who can handle a textbook dialogue but go blank when a real person talks at full speed. Episodes run about 20 minutes, with transcripts and interlinear glosses posted the same day.

The first three: the post office, a dentist's receptionist rescheduling me, and my mother-in-law's żurek recipe, which she dictated over the phone and which I got wrong twice.
