Your end-of-year tax statement summarizes the income you earned and the taxes withheld or paid on it over the past year. It is typically issued by your employer, bank, or other payers, and it is used to complete your tax return. The figures on it usually include your total wages or other income, the amount of tax already withheld from your pay, and any deductions or credits that apply. If you are a freelancer or contractor, the statement may instead show payments made to you without any tax taken out.

Please keep this document with your other financial records, since you will need the numbers when you file. If anything on the statement looks incorrect, contact the issuer as soon as possible so they can provide a corrected version before the filing deadline. If you have questions about how to use the information, a tax professional or your local tax authority can help you understand your specific situation.
