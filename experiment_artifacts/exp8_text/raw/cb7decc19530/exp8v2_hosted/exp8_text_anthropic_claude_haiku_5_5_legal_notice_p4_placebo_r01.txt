**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY**

**Who this notice applies to:** This notice applies to any person who has purchased a product or service from the seller and who wishes to request a refund. In this notice, "you" means the purchaser.

**1. Eligibility for a refund.** You may request a refund if:

(a) you make your request within the refund period stated in your order confirmation or receipt; and

(b) the product or service is defective, does not match its written description, or was not delivered as agreed.

**2. Exclusions.** A refund is not available where:

(a) the refund request is made after the refund period stated in your order confirmation or receipt has ended; or

(b) the product has been damaged after delivery through misuse, accident, or neglect.

**3. How to request a refund.** To request a refund, contact the seller using the contact details shown on your order confirmation or receipt. Include your order number and a short description of the reason for your request.

**4. Decision.** The seller will review each request and will notify you in writing of its decision. If your request is approved, the refund will be paid by the same method used for your purchase.

**5. Your statutory rights.** Nothing in this notice limits any rights you have under the consumer protection laws that apply where you live. Where those laws give you greater rights than this notice, those laws apply.
