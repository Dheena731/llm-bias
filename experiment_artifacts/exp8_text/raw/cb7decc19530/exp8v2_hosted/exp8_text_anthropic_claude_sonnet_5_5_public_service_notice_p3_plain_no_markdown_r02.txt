Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection

Beginning Monday, March 3, curbside recycling collection in our area will move to a new schedule. Households that previously had pickup on Thursdays will now have collection on Tuesdays. Households with Friday pickup will not be affected, and their service will continue as usual.

Please place your recycling bin at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on your collection day, with the lid fully closed and the bin at least three feet away from parked cars, mailboxes, and other containers. Bins set out after the truck has passed will not be collected until the following scheduled pickup.

Along with the new schedule, the program will now accept cartons, such as those used for milk, juice, and broth, as long as they are empty and dry. Plastic bags and wrapping, foam packaging, and food-soiled paper or cardboard should remain out of the bin, since they can contaminate an entire load and cause it to be rejected.

During the first two weeks of the change, collection crews may run slightly later than usual while they adjust to the new routes. If your bin has not been emptied by the end of the day, please leave it out overnight and it will be collected the next morning.

Residents with questions or those who need a replacement bin may contact the Public Works office during regular business hours. Thank you for your patience and for helping keep our community clean.
