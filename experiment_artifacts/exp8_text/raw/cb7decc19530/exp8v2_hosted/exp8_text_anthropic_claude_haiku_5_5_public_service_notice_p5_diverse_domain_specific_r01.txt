**NOTICE TO VOTERS: Polling Place Changes for the November 4 General Election**

The Harlan County Board of Elections has moved polling places for four precincts. If you voted at one of these sites in the last election, check your new location before you go.

| Precinct | Former Location | New Location |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | Westfield Grange Hall | Westfield Elementary, Gym Entrance (Oak St.) |
| 17 | Saint Brendan's Parish Hall | Millbrook Public Library, Community Room |
| 23 | Tanner Fire Station No. 4 | Tanner Fire Station No. 4, Bay 2 (rear lot, Route 9) |
| 31 | Lakeview Senior Center | Lakeview Senior Center, Annex Building |

**Find your polling place:** Enter your address at harlancountyvotes.gov/locate or call 555-0147 (weekdays, 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.). Your sample ballot shows your precinct number.

**Polls are open 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.** If you are in line at 8:00, you will still be allowed to vote.

**Accessibility:** All new locations have accessible entrances and curbside voting on request. Ask a poll worker at the entrance.

Voters who are unsure of their precinct can cast a provisional ballot at any county polling place, and it will be counted once eligibility is confirmed.

*Published by the Harlan County Board of Elections, September 12*
