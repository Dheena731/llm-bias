# NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY
**Alder Street Clay Cooperative, Firing and Tuition Fees**
Effective 1 March 2025

**1. Definitions.** "Firing Fee" means the charge assessed by volume (per half-shelf) for bisque or glaze firing. "Loaded" means the kiln door has been closed and the firing log signed by a Cooperative technician. "Tuition" means the fee for a scheduled multi-week course.

**2. Firing Fees: refundable.** A Firing Fee will be refunded in full where:
(a) the work is withdrawn before the kiln is Loaded;
(b) the Cooperative cancels or aborts the firing, including for power interruption, element failure, or controller fault; or
(c) the work is damaged in the kiln through a documented Cooperative error, such as shelf collapse, wrong cone, or misplaced kiln wash. In that case, the refund is the Firing Fee only. The Cooperative does not reimburse clay, glaze, or time.

**3. Firing Fees: not refundable.** No refund will be made where the work cracks, explodes, or fuses to a shelf because of:
(a) residual moisture, trapped air, or walls thicker than 2 cm;
(b) glaze applied over the line marked on the shelf, or on a foot ring;
(c) clay body not purchased from or approved by the Cooperative.
Where an exploded piece damages neighboring work, the owner may be charged for shelf repair.

**4. Tuition.** Tuition is refundable in full up to 7 days before the first session. After that, it is refundable only for sessions not yet begun, less a $40 materials allocation, and only if the Cooperative can fill the place from its waitlist. No refund is due for missed sessions.

**5. Claims.** Submit claims in writing to office@alderstreetclay.example within 14 days of the firing date or the withdrawal date, stating the piece tag number. Claims made later will not be considered.

**6. Payment.** Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 10 business days. Cash payments are refunded by check.

**7. Determinations.** The Studio Manager decides each claim on the firing log and kiln inspection. A member may request review by the Board within 30 days of a written denial. The Board's decision is final.
