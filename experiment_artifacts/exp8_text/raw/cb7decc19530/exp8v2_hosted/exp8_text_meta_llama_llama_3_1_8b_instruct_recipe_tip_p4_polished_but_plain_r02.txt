To plan lunches for a busy week, start by considering your schedule and what activities you have each day. Think about how much time you'll have available for eating and whether you'll be able to cook or need to prepare something ahead of time.

Make a list of the meals you'd like to have during the week, considering your dietary preferences and any ingredients you already have at home. You may want to include a mix of simple, quick meals like sandwiches or leftovers, as well as some more substantial options like soups or casseroles.

Before you head to the grocery store, take stock of what's already in your pantry, fridge, and freezer. Use up any ingredients that are near expiration or at risk of going to waste. This will help you avoid buying duplicate items and reduce food waste.

When you're at the store, buy a few staple ingredients like proteins, vegetables, and whole grains. Consider purchasing pre-cut or pre-cooked items to save time. You can also use a meal planning app or website to help generate ideas and keep track of your schedule.

Finally, set aside a bit of time each day to prep and assemble your lunches. Consider making a batch of rice or soup on the weekend to have for lunches throughout the week, or prep individual portions of ingredients like sliced meats or roasted vegetables.
