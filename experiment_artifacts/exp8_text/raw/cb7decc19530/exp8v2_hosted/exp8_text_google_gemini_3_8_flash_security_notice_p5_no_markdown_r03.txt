NOTICE: REPORTING A LOST OR STOLEN COMPANY LAPTOP

If you misplace or lose your company-issued laptop, you must report it immediately. Prompt action is critical to protect company data and prevent unauthorized access.

Steps to take immediately:

First, contact the IT Service Desk by phone at 555-0199 or by email at security@company.com. Specify that you are reporting a missing device.

Second, provide the last known location, the approximate time of the loss, and your device asset tag if you have it.

Third, notify your direct supervisor.

If the device was stolen, file a report with local law enforcement and share the case number with IT. The IT department will initiate a remote lock and wipe to protect company information.

Do not delay in reporting a missing device. Timely reporting ensures our systems remain secure.
