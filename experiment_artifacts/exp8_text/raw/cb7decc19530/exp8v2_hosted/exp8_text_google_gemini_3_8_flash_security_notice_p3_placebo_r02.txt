**Security Reminder: Please Lock Your Screens**

Please remember to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your workstation, even if only for a moment. 

Leaving a screen unlocked allows unauthorized access to company systems and exposes confidential information. 

You can quickly secure your computer using the following shortcuts:
* **Windows:** Press the **Windows Key + L**
* **Mac:** Press **Control + Command + Q**

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our data and workplace secure.
