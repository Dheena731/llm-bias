Subject: Reminder to Lock Your Screen When Away from Your Desk

All staff are reminded to lock their computer screens whenever they step away from their workstations, including for short breaks, meetings, or trips to the printer. An unlocked screen can give anyone passing by access to confidential client information, internal emails, and company systems, and it only takes a moment for sensitive data to be viewed or copied.

To lock your screen on a Windows device, press the Windows key and L at the same time. On a Mac, press Control, Command, and Q together. Locking your screen takes seconds and protects both your work and our colleagues' and customers' information.

Thank you for helping us keep our information secure. If you have any questions or need help setting up automatic screen locking, please contact the IT service desk.
