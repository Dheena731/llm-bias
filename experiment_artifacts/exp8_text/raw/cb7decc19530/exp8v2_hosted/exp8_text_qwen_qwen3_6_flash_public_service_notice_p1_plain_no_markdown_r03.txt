The public library operates on a regular schedule to serve the community. We are open from nine in the morning to five in the evening Monday through Friday. On Saturdays, our doors open at ten in the morning and close at four in the afternoon. The library is closed on Sundays and all official public holidays. Please verify the current schedule via our website or by calling the front desk, as hours may change during inclement weather or for special programming. We appreciate your visits and hope to see you soon.
