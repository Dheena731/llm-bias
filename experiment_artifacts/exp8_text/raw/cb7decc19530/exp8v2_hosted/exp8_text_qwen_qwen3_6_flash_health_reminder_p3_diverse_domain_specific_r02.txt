Your annual preventive visit is due. This appointment isn’t for triaging new symptoms—it’s built to establish baselines, review standard labs, reconcile medications, and shift your screening calendar to match your current age and risk profile. When you call to schedule, explicitly request a “routine annual exam” block so the clinic reserves the full 30 minutes. Bring a typed list of every prescription, OTC product, and supplement you take, plus any home blood pressure or weight logs. Most plans cover these visits at zero out-of-pocket; deferring just lets measurable markers drift past their intervention window.
