Your eyes have been fixed on one distance since your last break, and you've probably blinked far less than usual. Look up at something at least 20 feet away, like a tree outside or the far wall, and hold that gaze for 20 seconds. Then drop your shoulders, unclench your jaw, and blink slowly a few times.

If you can, stand up too. Whatever you're chasing on that screen will still be there in two minutes.
