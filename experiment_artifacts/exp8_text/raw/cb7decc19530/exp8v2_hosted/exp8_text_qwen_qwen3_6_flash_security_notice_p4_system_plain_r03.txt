This is a notice to inform all users that enabling two-factor authentication is now required for all accounts. Two-factor authentication adds an important layer of protection by requiring a unique verification code in addition to your password each time you sign in. To activate this feature, please go to the security settings area of your account profile and choose the option to turn on two-factor authentication. You will then be guided through registering a mobile device or installing an authenticator app to generate your daily codes. Please finish this setup within the next fourteen days so that your account access remains uninterrupted. Should you need guidance or run into technical issues, reach out to the support team at support example dot com. We appreciate your cooperation in keeping our systems secure.
