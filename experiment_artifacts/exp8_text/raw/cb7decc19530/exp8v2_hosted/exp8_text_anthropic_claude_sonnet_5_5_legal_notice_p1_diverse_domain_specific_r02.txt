**TALLGRASS FEDERAL CREDIT UNION**
**Notice of Change in Terms: Member Share and Checking Account Agreement**

Date of notice: September 2, 2025
Effective date: October 6, 2025

**To:** All holders of Share Savings, Share Draft (Checking), and Money Market accounts

This notice amends your Account Agreement and Fee Schedule (Form MA-12, rev. 03/24). It is provided under 12 C.F.R. § 707.5, which requires at least 30 days' advance notice of changes that adversely affect members.

**1. Inactive account charge (Section 6.2)**
Currently, an account is "inactive" after 24 months without a member-initiated transaction. Starting October 6, 2025, that period is **18 months**. An inactive account with a balance under $25 will be charged **$4.00 per month** until it has activity or a zero balance. Interest posting, dividends, and fees posted by us do not count as member-initiated activity. The charge cannot reduce a balance below $0.00.

**2. Outgoing domestic wire cutoff (Section 8.1)**
The cutoff for same-day processing moves from 4:00 p.m. to **2:30 p.m. Central Time** on business days. Requests received after the cutoff will be processed the next business day. The wire fee is unchanged at $25.00.

**3. Electronic delivery of notices (Section 14.4)**
For members enrolled in online banking and eStatements, we may deliver account notices, including change-in-terms notices, by secure message in online banking, with an alert sent to your email address on file. You may withdraw consent at any time under Section 14.5 and receive paper notices at no charge.

**What is not changing:** Your dividend rates, the overdraft and returned-item fees in Section 7, the funds availability policy in Section 9, and your share insurance coverage (NCUA, up to $250,000 per member, per ownership category).

**Your options.** You may close any affected account before October 6, 2025, without penalty or early-closure fee. If you keep your account open or make a deposit, withdrawal, or other transaction on or after that date, you accept these changes. Nothing in this notice waives any right you have under federal or state law.
