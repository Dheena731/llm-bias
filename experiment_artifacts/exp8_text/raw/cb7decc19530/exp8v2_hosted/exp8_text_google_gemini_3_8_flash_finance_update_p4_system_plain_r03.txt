An end-of-year tax statement is a document that summarizes all the relevant financial activity associated with your account over the past calendar year. Depending on your specific situation, this statement may show the total income you earned, taxes that were already withheld, interest paid or received, or contributions that may qualify for deductions. You do not need to make a payment upon receiving this statement. Instead, it serves as an official record to help you or your tax preparer accurately complete your annual tax return. Please keep this document in a safe place along with your other tax records for future reference.
