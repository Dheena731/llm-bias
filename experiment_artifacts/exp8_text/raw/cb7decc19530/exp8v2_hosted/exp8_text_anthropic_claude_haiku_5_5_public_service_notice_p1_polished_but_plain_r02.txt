Please note the updated opening hours at the Riverside Public Library, effective Monday, March 2. The main branch will be open Monday through Thursday from 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., Friday and Saturday from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., and Sunday from 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. The Eastside Branch will open at 10:00 a.m. on weekdays and close at 6:00 p.m., with Saturday hours of 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. and no Sunday service.

The library will be closed on Monday, May 25, for Memorial Day, and on Thursday, July 4, for Independence Day. Book drop-off boxes at both locations remain available around the clock, so returns can be made at any time.

For the most current information, including holiday schedules and special event closures, please visit the library's website or call the information desk at 555-0142 during open hours. Staff are glad to help with questions about accounts, renewals, and available services.
