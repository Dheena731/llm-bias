PUBLIC SERVICE ANNOUNCEMENT: LIBRARY OPERATING HOURS

The City Public Library is pleased to announce its current operating schedule. We welcome all community members to visit, browse our collection, and use our study spaces during the following hours:

Monday through Thursday: 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM
Friday and Saturday: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM
Sunday: 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM

Please note that the library is closed on major public holidays. 

Digital resources, including e-books, audiobooks, and research databases, remain accessible 24/7 through the library website. For inquiries, book renewals, or assistance, please call 555-0199 during regular operating hours. 

We look forward to serving you.
