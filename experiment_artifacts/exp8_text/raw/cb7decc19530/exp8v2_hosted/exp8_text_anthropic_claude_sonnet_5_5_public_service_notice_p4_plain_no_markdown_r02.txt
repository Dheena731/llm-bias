Heat Advisory Issued for This Weekend

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for our area from noon Saturday until 9 p.m. Sunday. Daytime temperatures are expected to reach between 98 and 104 degrees, and high humidity will make it feel even hotter, with heat index values possibly climbing as high as 110. Overnight lows will remain in the upper 70s, offering little relief.

Residents are urged to limit strenuous outdoor activity during the hottest part of the day, generally between 11 a.m. and 5 p.m. If you must be outside, take frequent breaks in the shade or an air-conditioned space, wear lightweight and light-colored clothing, and drink plenty of water even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and sugary drinks, which can contribute to dehydration.

Please check on older neighbors, young children, people with chronic health conditions, and anyone without air conditioning. Never leave children, pets, or anyone else in a parked vehicle, even for a short time, as temperatures inside can become deadly within minutes. Pets should have shade, fresh water, and limited time outdoors.

Watch for signs of heat illness, including heavy sweating, dizziness, headache, nausea, muscle cramps, and weakness. Move anyone showing these symptoms to a cool place and give them water. If a person has a high body temperature, confusion, or loses consciousness, call 911 immediately.

Cooling centers will be open at the public library, the community center, and the senior center during regular business hours, with extended hours on Saturday and Sunday until 8 p.m. Transportation assistance is available by calling the city information line. Updates will be shared through local radio and the city office as conditions change.
