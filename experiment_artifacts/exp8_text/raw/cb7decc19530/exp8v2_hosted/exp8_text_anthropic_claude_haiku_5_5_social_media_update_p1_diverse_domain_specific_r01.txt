**New in Seed Ledger: germination tracking.**

Log the date you sowed a packet and the app will tell you when to expect sprouts, then flag anything that's gone past its window. It also handles the seeds you saved yourself. Mark a batch as "paper towel tested" with a count (7 of 10 up, say), and anyone swapping for it can see what they're getting.

Rolling out to all accounts this week. Thanks to the people in the October swap thread who kept asking for this.
