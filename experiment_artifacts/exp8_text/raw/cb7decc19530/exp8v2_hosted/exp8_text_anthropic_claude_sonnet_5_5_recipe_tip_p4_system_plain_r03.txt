Pick one afternoon, usually Sunday, and cook a big batch of two or three basic components rather than full meals. A pot of grains such as rice or quinoa, a tray of roasted vegetables, and a protein like grilled chicken or boiled eggs will cover most of the week. Store them in separate containers, then mix and match each morning with a different sauce, a handful of greens, or a new seasoning so lunch doesn't feel repetitive. Wraps, grain bowls, and salads can all come from the same ingredients. Keep the more delicate items, like fresh herbs, dressings, and crunchy toppings, separate until you're ready to eat so everything stays fresh through Friday.
