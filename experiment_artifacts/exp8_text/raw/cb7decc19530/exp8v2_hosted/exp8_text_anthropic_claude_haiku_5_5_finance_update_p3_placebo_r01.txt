**Introducing Savings Goals**

You can now set aside money for something specific, like a trip, an emergency fund, or a new laptop.

To create a goal, give it a name and a target amount. Your goal shows how much you've saved so far and how much is left. You can add money to a goal whenever you like, and you can change the target if your plans change.

Seeing progress toward a named goal can make it easier to save consistently.

Savings goals are available in the app now. If you have questions or run into a problem, contact our support team.
