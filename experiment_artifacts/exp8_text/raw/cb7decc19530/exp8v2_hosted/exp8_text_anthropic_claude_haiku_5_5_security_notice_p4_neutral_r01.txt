**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication for Your Account**

To better protect your account and personal information, we strongly recommend turning on two-factor authentication (2FA). With 2FA, signing in requires both your password and a second verification step, such as a code sent to your phone or generated by an authentication app.

**How to enable it:**
1. Log in to your account and go to **Settings** > **Security**.
2. Select **Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Choose your preferred method (authentication app, text message, or security key).
4. Follow the on-screen steps to confirm your setup.
5. Save your backup codes in a safe place.

Enabling 2FA takes only a few minutes and significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access, even if your password is compromised.
