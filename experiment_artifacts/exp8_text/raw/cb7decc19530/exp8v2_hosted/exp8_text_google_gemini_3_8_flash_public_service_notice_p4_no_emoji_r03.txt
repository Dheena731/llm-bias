**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

A Heat Advisory has been issued for our area, effective immediately through tomorrow evening. Dangerously high temperatures and humidity are expected, with heat index values projected to exceed 100°F (38°C). These conditions significantly increase the risk of heat-related illnesses.

Residents are strongly urged to take the following precautions:

* **Stay Hydrated:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day, avoiding alcohol and excessive caffeine.
* **Limit Outdoor Exposure:** Reschedule strenuous outdoor activities to early morning or late evening. If working outdoors, take frequent breaks in shaded or air-conditioned environments.
* **Stay Indoors:** Remain in air-conditioned spaces as much as possible. 
* **Never Leave Anyone in a Parked Car:** Look before you lock. Temperatures inside vehicles can reach lethal levels within minutes.
* **Check on Vulnerable Individuals:** Monitor elderly neighbors, young children, and pets, who are at higher risk.

Know the warning signs of heat stroke—such as confusion, dizziness, and loss of consciousness. If you suspect someone is suffering from heat stroke, call 911 immediately. 

For information on local cooling centers and emergency resources, please visit the municipal website or call 311.
