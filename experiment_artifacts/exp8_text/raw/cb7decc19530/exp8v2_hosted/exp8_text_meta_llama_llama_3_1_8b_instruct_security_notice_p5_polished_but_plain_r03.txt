Lost Company Laptop Report

If a company laptop is lost or stolen, please report it to the IT department immediately. This will help prevent unauthorized access to sensitive company data and ensure the laptop's security features are disabled.

To report a lost laptop, contact the IT department during business hours. Provide the laptop's serial number, your name, and a brief description of the incident. The IT department will initiate the necessary steps to secure the laptop and prevent further unauthorized use.

Please note that the company may have a process in place for reporting lost or stolen company property, including laptops. Familiarize yourself with this process and follow the procedures outlined by your supervisor or department head.

Reporting a lost laptop in a timely manner is crucial to protecting the company's assets and maintaining data security.
