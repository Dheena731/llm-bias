Thank you for your methods section. Your description of the participant recruitment and the survey instrument is clear, and the reader can follow the sequence of steps without difficulty. The justification for choosing a mixed-methods design is also well argued.

There are a few areas to strengthen:

1. **Sampling details:** You state the sample size but not how it was determined. Add a brief note on your power analysis or the rationale for your target number.
2. **Procedure order:** The data collection steps are described in a different order from how they occurred. Rewriting them chronologically will help readers replicate your study.
3. **Analysis:** The statistical tests are named but not linked to your research questions. Briefly explain which test addresses which question.
4. **Limitations:** Your methods section would benefit from acknowledging one or two constraints, such as the limited sample diversity or the self-report measures.

Overall, this is a solid draft. If you address the points above, the section should be ready for submission. Please send me the revised version by Friday, and I am happy to review it again.
