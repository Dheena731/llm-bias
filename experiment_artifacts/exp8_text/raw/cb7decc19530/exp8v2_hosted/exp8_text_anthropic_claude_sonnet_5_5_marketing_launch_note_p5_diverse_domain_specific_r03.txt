**Second Listen is live: three episodes, new ones every Tuesday**

Most language podcasts teach you the sentence. This one starts with the sentence you missed.

Each episode is built around a single unscripted recording of a native speaker: a taxi driver, a retired teacher, two sisters arguing about a recipe. You hear it once at full speed, with no help. Then we go back through the parts that probably lost you: the swallowed endings, the filler words, the grammar nobody uses in a classroom but everybody uses in a kitchen. Then you hear it a second time, and it should sound like a different recording.

Episode 1 is a Lisbon cab driver talking about football for four minutes. We spend most of the episode on why *obrigado* turns into something like "brgad" and why your textbook Portuguese won't survive contact with it.

Episodes 2 and 3 are Mexico City Spanish (a market vendor, fast) and Seoul Korean (a phone call, with all the sentence-final particles intact).

It's pitched at the B1–B2 plateau, where you can order dinner but can't follow the table next to you. Nobody will make you repeat after them.

Every episode has a timestamped transcript on the site. If you have a recording you couldn't parse, send it to us. The best ones become episodes.
