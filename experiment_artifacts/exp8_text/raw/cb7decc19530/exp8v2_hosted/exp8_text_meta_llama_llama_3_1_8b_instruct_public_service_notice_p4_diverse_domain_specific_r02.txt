**Heat Advisory in Effect: Protect Your Health and Safety**

The City of Phoenix is experiencing a prolonged period of extreme heat, with temperatures expected to reach 115°F (46°C) over the next three days. To mitigate the risks associated with heat-related illnesses, we urge residents to take necessary precautions to protect themselves and their loved ones.

**Action Steps:**

* Stay indoors during the hottest part of the day (11am-3pm) and keep your home well-ventilated.
* Avoid strenuous activities, especially outdoor ones, and take regular breaks in shaded or air-conditioned areas.
* Monitor vulnerable populations, such as seniors and young children, and ensure they receive adequate hydration and rest.
* Check on neighbors, especially those with medical conditions, and offer assistance if needed.
* Follow these simple tips to prevent heat-related illnesses:
	+ Drink at least 8-10 glasses of water per day.
	+ Eat light, hydrating meals.
	+ Avoid caffeine and sugary drinks.
	+ Wear lightweight, loose-fitting clothing and a hat.
