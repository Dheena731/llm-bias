Attention residents: the public library will be changing its opening hours beginning next Monday. The main branch will open at 9:00 a.m. on weekdays and remain open until 8:00 p.m. on Monday through Thursday. On Fridays and Saturdays, the library will close at 5:00 p.m., and it will be closed on Sundays. Holiday hours may vary, so please check the library's website or call the front desk before you visit. Patrons with questions about the new schedule, or about borrowing, returns, and digital resources, are welcome to speak with a staff member during open hours. The library thanks the community for its continued support and looks forward to serving you.
