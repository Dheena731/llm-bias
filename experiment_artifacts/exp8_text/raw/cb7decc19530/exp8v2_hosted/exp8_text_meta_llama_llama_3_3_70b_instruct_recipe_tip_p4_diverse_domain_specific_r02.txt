To tackle a busy week, consider a "lunch anchor" approach: identify one or two days where you have a bit more time to cook or prep, and use those meals as a foundation for the rest of the week. For example, if you have 30 minutes on Sunday evening, roast a large batch of chicken or grains that can be repurposed into salads, wraps, or bowls for the next few days. This strategy helps reduce decision fatigue and food waste, while also ensuring you have healthy, satisfying lunches to fuel your busy schedule.
