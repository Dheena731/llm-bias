**Lost Company Laptop - Reporting Procedure**

If you have misplaced or had your company laptop stolen, please report it to the IT department immediately.

To report a lost laptop, please follow these steps:

* Contact the IT department via phone or email to notify them of the incident.
* Provide your name, employee ID, and a detailed description of the laptop, including its make, model, and serial number.
* If the laptop has been stolen, please also report the incident to the police and provide the police report number to the IT department.
* The IT department will then work with you to determine the next course of action and may require you to sign a declaration stating that the laptop has been lost or stolen.

Please note that all company laptops are the property of the company and must be returned promptly if found.
