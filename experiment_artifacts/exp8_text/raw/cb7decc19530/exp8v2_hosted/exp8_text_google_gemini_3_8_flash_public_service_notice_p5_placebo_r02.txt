**Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations**

Please be advised that your assigned polling station is determined by your registered residential address. To ensure your ballot is counted, you must cast your vote at your designated location, where your name appears on the official voter list. 

Voting sites can change between elections. To find your correct polling station before heading out to vote, review your official voter notification card or contact your local election office directly. Confirm your polling location in advance to avoid any delays on election day.
