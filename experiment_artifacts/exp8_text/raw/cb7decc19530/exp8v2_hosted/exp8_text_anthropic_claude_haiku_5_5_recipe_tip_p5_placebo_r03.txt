Toss your vegetables in oil first, then season them. The oil helps the salt and spices cling to every surface, so the seasoning spreads evenly instead of collecting in spots or falling off onto the pan. Use about one tablespoon of oil per pound of vegetables, add salt and pepper, and add any dried herbs or spices at the start of roasting. Save fresh herbs and delicate seasonings like lemon zest for after the vegetables come out of the oven, since high heat can scorch them.
