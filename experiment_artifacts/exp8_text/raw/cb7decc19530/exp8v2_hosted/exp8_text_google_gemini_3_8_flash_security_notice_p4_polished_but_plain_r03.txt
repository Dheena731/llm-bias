To better protect your personal information and safeguard your account against unauthorized access, we strongly encourage you to enable two-factor authentication. This security feature adds an essential layer of defense by requiring both your password and a temporary verification code whenever you log in. Even if your password is ever compromised, this extra verification step ensures that only you can access your data. The setup takes only a few moments and can be completed by navigating to your account settings and selecting the security section. Protecting your information is our highest priority, and we appreciate your proactive effort in keeping your account secure.
