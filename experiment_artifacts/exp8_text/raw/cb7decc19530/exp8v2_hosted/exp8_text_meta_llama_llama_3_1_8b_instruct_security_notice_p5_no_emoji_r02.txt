**Lost Company Laptop - Report**

If you have misplaced or lost a company laptop, please report it to the IT department immediately. This will help prevent unauthorized access to company data and ensure the laptop's secure return.

To report a lost laptop, please contact the IT department at [insert phone number or email address] with the following information:

* Laptop serial number
* Last known location of the laptop
* Any other relevant details about the laptop's disappearance

The IT department will work with you to investigate the situation and take necessary steps to protect company data and retrieve the laptop if possible.
