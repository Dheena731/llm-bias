Starting this month, you will receive a monthly spending summary that gives you a clear picture of where your money went. The summary arrives within the first three days of each month and covers the full previous month, from the first day through the last.

Each summary groups your transactions into familiar categories such as groceries, housing, transportation, dining, subscriptions, and entertainment. For every category, you will see the total amount spent, how that compares with the previous month, and how it compares with your average over the last six months. This makes it easier to notice gradual increases that are hard to spot in day-to-day activity, like a growing number of small recurring charges or a steady rise in restaurant spending.

The summary also highlights your largest individual purchases and lists any new recurring payments that appeared during the month. If you have set a budget for a category, the summary shows how much of it you used and how much remained when the month ended.

Categories are assigned automatically, so a few transactions may occasionally land in the wrong place. You can correct any of them directly from the summary, and the system will remember your choice for similar purchases in the future. Over time, this should make your reports more accurate with very little effort.

The summary is meant to be a quick read, not a detailed audit. We suggest taking a few minutes each month to review the totals, check the unusual items, and adjust your budgets if your priorities have changed. If you would prefer not to receive the summary by email, you can switch to in-app delivery or turn it off entirely in your notification settings. We would also welcome your feedback on what would make the report more useful to you.
