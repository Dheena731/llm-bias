Here's a short reminder:

**Stay Hydrated!**

Drink at least 8-10 glasses of water throughout the day to help your body function at its best. A hydrated body is a healthy body! Remember to:

* Drink a glass of water as soon as you wake up
* Take a water break every hour
* Make water your go-to drink instead of sugary beverages

Your body (and your skin) will thank you!
