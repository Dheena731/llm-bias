Treat tender herbs like cilantro, parsley, and mint as you would a bouquet of flowers. Trim the stems, place them upright in a jar with an inch of water, and loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag. Keep them in the refrigerator and change the water every couple of days. For sturdier herbs like rosemary and thyme, wrap them in a slightly damp paper towel and store them in a container or resealable bag in the fridge. Basil is the exception: keep it in a jar of water on the counter, away from direct sunlight, because the cold turns its leaves black.
