To export query results:

1. Select the desired rows or use the "Select All" option.
2. Right-click the selected rows or press Ctrl+A (Windows) or Command+A (Mac) to open the context menu.
3. Choose "Copy as CSV" or "Export to CSV" from the menu.
4. Alternatively, click the "Export" button in the toolbar or use the keyboard shortcut Ctrl+E (Windows) or Command+E (Mac).
5. Choose a location to save the exported file and select the desired format (e.g., CSV, Excel, or PDF).

Note: The exact steps may vary depending on the database management system or application you are using.
