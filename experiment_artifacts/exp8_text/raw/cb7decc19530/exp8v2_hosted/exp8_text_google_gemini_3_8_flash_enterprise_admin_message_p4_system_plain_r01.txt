The quarterly access review cycle is now underway across all corporate systems and applications. Department managers and system owners are required to audit the permissions of their direct reports and authorized users to ensure adherence to the principle of least privilege. Please review your respective access lists, verify that permissions align with current job responsibilities, and immediately revoke access for any personnel who have changed roles or departed the organization. All approvals, modifications, and formal sign-offs must be submitted within two weeks. Your prompt attention to this process is critical for maintaining our security posture and meeting compliance requirements. Please reach out to the IT security team if you have any questions or require assistance.
