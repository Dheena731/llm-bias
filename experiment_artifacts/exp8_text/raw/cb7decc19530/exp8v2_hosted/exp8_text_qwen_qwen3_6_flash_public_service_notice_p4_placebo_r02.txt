**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY**

A Heat Advisory is currently in effect due to elevated temperatures and humidity expected over the next several days.

Please take these precautions to protect your health and safety:

*   **Stay Hydrated:** Drink plenty of water regularly, even if you are not thirsty. Avoid alcohol and caffeine, which can contribute to dehydration.
*   **Limit Exposure:** Reduce strenuous outdoor activities, especially during the midday hours when the sun is strongest. Wear lightweight, loose-fitting clothing if you must go outside.
*   **Cool Down:** Spend time in air-conditioned spaces such as your home, libraries, shopping centers, or cooling shelters. If you do not have air conditioning, visit a public cooling center.
*   **Check on Others:** Regularly contact elderly neighbors, young children, and individuals with chronic health conditions, as they are at higher risk for heat-related illness.
*   **Never Leave People or Pets in Vehicles:** Temperatures inside parked cars can rise rapidly to dangerous levels, even with windows cracked.

Recognize the signs of heat exhaustion, including heavy sweating, weakness, nausea, dizziness, and headache. If symptoms occur, move to a cooler area, rest, and drink water. Seek immediate medical help if symptoms worsen or if vomiting occurs.

Monitor local news and official channels for updates on this advisory and further instructions. Look out for your neighbors and ensure everyone stays safe during this period of hot weather.
