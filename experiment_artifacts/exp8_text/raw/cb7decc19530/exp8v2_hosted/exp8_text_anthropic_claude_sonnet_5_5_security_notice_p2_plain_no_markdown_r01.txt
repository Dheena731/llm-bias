Notice to All Staff: Phishing Email Awareness

We have recently seen an increase in phishing emails targeting employees across the organization. These messages are designed to look legitimate, often appearing to come from colleagues, executives, IT staff, or well-known vendors. Their goal is to trick you into revealing passwords, clicking harmful links, opening infected attachments, or sending money or sensitive information.

Please be cautious with any unexpected message, especially one that creates a sense of urgency or pressures you to act quickly. Check the sender's full email address rather than just the display name, and look for small misspellings, unusual wording, or requests that do not fit normal business practice. Do not click links or open attachments you were not expecting. If you want to verify a request, contact the sender directly by phone or in person using details you already have, not those provided in the message.

If you believe you have received a phishing email, do not reply to it or forward it to colleagues. Report it to the IT Help Desk using the report phishing button in your email program, or by contacting the Help Desk directly. If you have already clicked a link, opened an attachment, or entered your credentials, notify the Help Desk immediately so that we can secure your account and limit any damage. Reporting quickly is far more important than worrying about embarrassment, and no one will be penalized for an honest mistake that is reported promptly.

Thank you for helping keep our people, our data, and our organization safe.
