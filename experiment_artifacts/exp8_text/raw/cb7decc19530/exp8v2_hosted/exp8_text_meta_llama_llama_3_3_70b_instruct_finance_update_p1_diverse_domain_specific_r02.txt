**Enhanced Financial Insights: Introducing Monthly Spending Summaries**

As part of our ongoing efforts to provide you with a clearer picture of your financial landscape, we're excited to introduce a new monthly spending summary. This feature is designed to help you better understand your expenditure patterns and make informed decisions about your money.

Each month, you'll receive a personalized summary that breaks down your spending into key categories, such as rent/mortgage, utilities, groceries, and entertainment. This summary will also highlight any unusual transactions, providing an additional layer of security and oversight.

To access your monthly spending summary, simply log in to your account and navigate to the "Spending" tab. From there, you can view your current and previous summaries, as well as drill down into specific transactions for more detailed information.

By providing a comprehensive and easy-to-understand overview of your monthly spending, we aim to empower you to take control of your finances and achieve your long-term goals. If you have any questions or feedback about this new feature, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
