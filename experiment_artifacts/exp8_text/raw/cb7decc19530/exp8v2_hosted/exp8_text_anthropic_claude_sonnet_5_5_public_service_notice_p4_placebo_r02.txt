**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY**

A heat advisory is in effect. This means high temperatures, possibly combined with humidity, may create conditions that can make people ill. Please take precautions to protect yourself and others. Check your local weather service for the exact area and times covered.

**What you should do**
- **Drink plenty of water**, even if you do not feel thirsty. Limit alcohol and sugary drinks.
- **Stay in air-conditioned or shaded places** as much as possible. If your home is hot, consider spending time in a public building such as a library or shopping center.
- **Limit outdoor activity**, especially in the afternoon when heat is usually strongest. If you must be outside, rest often, wear light, loose clothing, and use sunscreen.
- **Never leave children or pets in a parked vehicle**, even for a short time.
- **Check on older adults, young children, and people who are ill or live alone.** They are at higher risk.
- **Keep pets indoors or in shade**, with plenty of fresh water.

**Know the warning signs**
- **Heat exhaustion:** heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, headache, nausea, or muscle cramps. Move to a cool place, loosen clothing, sip water, and cool the body with damp cloths.
- **Heat stroke (a medical emergency):** confusion, fainting, a very high body temperature, or hot skin. Call your local emergency number right away and try to cool the person until help arrives.

Please share this notice with family, friends, and neighbors.
