Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations

Polling stations for the upcoming election will open at 7:00 a.m. and close at 8:00 p.m. on election day. Each registered voter has been assigned to a specific polling station based on their home address. Your assigned location is printed on your voter registration card and is also available through the elections office website or by calling the elections office directly.

Please note that some polling locations have changed since the last election, so voters are encouraged to confirm their station before heading out to vote. Stations are generally located in schools, community centers, libraries, and municipal buildings, and all are accessible to voters with disabilities. Accessible parking and entrances are marked at each site.

Voters who arrive at the wrong polling station may be directed to their correct location or may be eligible to cast a provisional ballot, so checking in advance will help avoid delays. If you have questions about your polling place, need assistance reaching it, or require accommodations on election day, please contact the elections office for help.
