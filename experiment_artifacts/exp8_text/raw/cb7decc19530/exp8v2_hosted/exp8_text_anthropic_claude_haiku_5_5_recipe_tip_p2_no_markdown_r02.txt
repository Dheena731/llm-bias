Store fresh herbs like parsley, cilantro, and basil by trimming the stem ends, placing them in a glass of water like a bouquet of flowers, and loosely covering the leaves with a plastic bag. Keep them in the refrigerator, and change the water every couple of days. Delicate herbs like basil are best kept at room temperature away from direct sunlight, since cold can cause them to blacken. Hardy herbs like rosemary and thyme can be wrapped in a damp paper towel and stored in a sealed container.
