**PUBLIC NOTICE: SCHEDULED ROAD CLOSURE**

Elm Street between Park Avenue and Lincoln Boulevard will be closed to all through traffic from Tuesday, October 22, through Saturday, October 26, between 7:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. daily. The closure is required for scheduled underground pipe replacement. Local residents and authorized delivery vehicles may enter and exit the closed block using marked temporary access lanes. All other motorists must follow posted detour signage along Harbor Drive to bypass the work zone. Expect moderate congestion near the Park Avenue intersection during morning and evening commute hours. Public works crews will operate six days a week until the project is complete. Full lane restoration and normal traffic flow will resume the following Monday at 6:00 a.m. For real-time updates or mobility accommodations, contact the Department of Public Works at 555-0142 or visit citypublicworks.gov/closures. Thank you for your patience as we maintain essential city infrastructure.
