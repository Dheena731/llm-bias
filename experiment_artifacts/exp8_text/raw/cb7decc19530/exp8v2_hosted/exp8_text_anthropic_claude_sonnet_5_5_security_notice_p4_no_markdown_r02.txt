Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication Today

To help keep your account secure, we strongly encourage you to turn on two-factor authentication (2FA). This adds a second layer of protection by requiring a one-time code in addition to your password when you sign in. Even if someone learns your password, they will not be able to access your account without that second step.

How to enable it:

1. Sign in and go to your Account Settings.
2. Select Security, then choose Two-Factor Authentication.
3. Pick your preferred method: an authenticator app, a text message, or a security key.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to confirm your device.
5. Save your backup codes in a safe place in case you lose access to your device.

Setup takes only a few minutes. We recommend using an authenticator app, as it is more secure than text messages.

Please remember that we will never ask you to share your verification codes with anyone, including our support staff.

If you need help, contact our support team any time.

Thank you for helping us keep your account safe.
