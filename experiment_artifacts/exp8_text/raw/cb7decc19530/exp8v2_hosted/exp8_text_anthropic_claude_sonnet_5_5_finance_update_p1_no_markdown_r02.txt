Subject: New Monthly Spending Summary Now Available

Hello everyone,

We're pleased to announce a new monthly spending summary, designed to give you a clearer picture of where your money goes each month.

Starting this month, you will receive a summary at the beginning of each month that covers the previous month's activity. It includes your total spending, a breakdown by category such as groceries, transportation, dining, and bills, and a comparison to the prior month so you can quickly spot changes. It also highlights your largest individual purchases and any recurring charges.

The goal is to make it easier to notice trends, catch unexpected charges, and plan your budget with confidence. You do not need to set anything up. The summary will appear automatically in your account dashboard, and you can also choose to receive it by email in your notification settings.

If you have questions or suggestions for improving the summary, please reach out to our support team. We are always looking for ways to make it more useful.

Thank you,
The Product Team
