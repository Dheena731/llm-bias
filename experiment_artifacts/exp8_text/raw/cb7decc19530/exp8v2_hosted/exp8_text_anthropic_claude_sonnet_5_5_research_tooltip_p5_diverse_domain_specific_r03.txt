**Tooltip:**

> **Export results**
> Open **⋮ › Download** and choose CSV or JSON. The file includes every row the query returns, not just the 1,000 shown in the grid. If you've edited the SQL, re-run it first, since exports use the last executed query.

I assumed a SQL editor with a paginated grid and a menu-based export. If your product differs, swap in the real limits, formats, and menu path. Those details are what make a tooltip useful.
