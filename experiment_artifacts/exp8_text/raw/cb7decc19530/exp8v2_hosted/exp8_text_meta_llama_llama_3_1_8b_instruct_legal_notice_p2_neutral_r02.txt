Here is a short legal-style notice about an updated privacy policy:

**NOTICE OF UPDATED PRIVACY POLICY**

**EFFECTIVE DATE: [Insert Date]**

**TO ALL USERS OF [Company Name]'s SERVICES**

As part of our ongoing commitment to transparency and protecting the personal information of our users, [Company Name] is updating its privacy policy, effective as of [Insert Date].

**IMPORTANT CHANGES**

This updated policy reflects changes to our practices and procedures for collecting, using, and disclosing your personal information. The key changes include:

* Expanded information on our data collection and sharing practices
* New procedures for responding to data subject access requests (DSARs)
* Enhanced security measures to protect your personal information

**YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES**

By using our services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the terms of this updated privacy policy. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us at [Contact Information].

**FULL TEXT OF UPDATED POLICY**

To view the full text of our updated privacy policy, please visit our website at [Company Website]. You can also request a copy by contacting us at [Contact Information].

**CHANGES TO EFFECTIVE DATE**

This notice shall be deemed to have been served upon you as of [Insert Date]. The updated privacy policy is effective as of the same date.

**ACKNOWLEDGMENT**

By continuing to use our services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the terms of this updated privacy policy.

Sincerely,

[Your Company Name]
