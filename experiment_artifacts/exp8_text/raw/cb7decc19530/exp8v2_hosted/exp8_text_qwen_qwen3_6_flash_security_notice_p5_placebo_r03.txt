**NOTICE: REPORTING A LOST COMPANY LAPTOP**

To All Staff,

If you have misplaced or lost your company-issued laptop, you must report it immediately. Do not wait to determine if the device has been recovered.

Take these actions without delay:
1. Change your login passwords from a trusted alternative device.
2. Alert the IT Security team immediately through the internal directory or the dedicated security hotline.
3. State your full name, employment identification number, and the last known location of the laptop when you make the report.

Prompt reporting is required because the device contains confidential company information and secure network credentials. Early notification allows us to remotely disable access, protect sensitive data, and follow established incident response procedures. You will receive equipment replacement instructions and further guidance within one business hour of your report.

Delayed reporting violates corporate security policy and compromises organizational safety. Immediate transparency protects both you and the company.

Thank you for your prompt attention.

IT Security Department
