Please note that the Riverside Public Library has updated its opening hours, effective Monday, March 3. The Main Library at 200 Elm Street will be open Monday through Thursday from 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., Friday and Saturday from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., and Sunday from 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. The Eastside Branch at 45 Harbor Road will open Tuesday through Saturday from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. and will remain closed on Sundays and Mondays.

Both locations will be closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and December 25. Holiday hours may vary, so please check before your visit.

For the latest information, visit the library's website or call the Main Library at 555-0100 during regular business hours. Staff are happy to help with questions about borrowing, digital resources, and events.
