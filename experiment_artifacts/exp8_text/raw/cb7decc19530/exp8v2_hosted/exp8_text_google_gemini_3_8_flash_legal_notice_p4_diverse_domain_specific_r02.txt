**NOTICE OF REMEDY AND LIQUIDATED CANCELLATION TERMS**  
**Precision Machining & Additive Fabrication Services**

Refund eligibility under executed Production Orders is governed strictly by the phase of material commitment at the time written notice of cancellation is received:

1. **Pre-Procurement Cancellation:** If cancellation occurs prior to raw stock allocation or casting procurement, the Customer is eligible for a refund of prepaid production fees, less a non-refundable Non-Recurring Engineering (NRE) fee of 15% for CAM toolpath programming and setup validation.
2. **In-Process Forfeiture:** Once metallurgical certification (MTR) is assigned, stock is cut, or machine spindle time has commenced, all material costs, specialty tooling charges, and committed machine-hour rates are fully vested and strictly non-refundable. 
3. **Dimensional Non-Conformance Remedies:** Completed lots are ineligible for cash refund. In the event of out-of-spec deliverables exceeding geometric tolerances defined in the approved engineering drawing (ASME Y14.5M):
   * Notice of defect and third-party Coordinate Measuring Machine (CMM) inspection data must be submitted within ten (10) business days of delivery.
   * Seller’s sole liability, and Customer’s exclusive remedy, shall be limited to rework or replacement of non-conforming units at Seller’s expense, or a prorated credit toward subsequent production runs. Unaltered parts must be returned under an active Return Material Authorization (RMA).

Unauthorized returns, scrap claims without verification data, or parts subjected to secondary post-processing by the Customer void all remedy eligibility.
