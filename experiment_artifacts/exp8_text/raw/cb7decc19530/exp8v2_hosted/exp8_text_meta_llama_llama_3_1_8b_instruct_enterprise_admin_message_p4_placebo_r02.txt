**Quarterly Access Reviews**

As part of our ongoing commitment to maintaining a secure and efficient IT environment, the [Department/Team] will be conducting quarterly access reviews.

These reviews are a routine process designed to ensure that all employees have the necessary permissions to access the systems and resources required to perform their job functions. This process helps to prevent unauthorized access, reduce the risk of data breaches, and maintain compliance with organizational policies.

During the review, our team will assess the access levels of all employees and remove or modify access as necessary. Employees may be contacted to confirm their current job responsibilities and access requirements.

If you have any questions or concerns about your access, please do not hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or the IT department. We appreciate your cooperation and support in this effort to maintain the security and integrity of our systems.
