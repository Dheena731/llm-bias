This notice is to remind all employees of the procedure to follow if a company-issued laptop is lost or stolen. Prompt reporting is critical to ensure company and client data remains secure. If your laptop goes missing, notify the IT help desk and the security team immediately, regardless of the time or day. Please provide details regarding when and where the device was last seen, as well as the asset tag number if you have it. If you suspect the laptop was stolen, file a report with local law enforcement and forward the case number to security. Rapid notification allows the IT department to lock the device and perform a remote data wipe to prevent unauthorized access.
