**NOTICE OF POLLING STATION LOCATIONS**

Voters are advised that polling stations will be open on Election Day from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Please go to the polling station assigned to your precinct, as listed on your voter registration card or on the official election website.

**Polling Locations**

- **Precincts 1 to 4:** Central Public Library, 120 Main Street
- **Precincts 5 to 8:** Riverside Community Center, 45 River Road
- **Precincts 9 to 12:** Oakwood Elementary School Gymnasium, 310 Oak Avenue
- **Precincts 13 to 16:** Town Hall, Council Chamber, 1 Civic Plaza

**Before You Go**

- Bring a valid form of photo identification.
- Check your assigned precinct in advance, since voting at the wrong location may delay your ballot.
- All locations are wheelchair accessible. Curbside voting is available upon request.
- Arrive early if possible, as lines are typically longest around midday and after work hours.

Anyone in line at closing time will be permitted to vote.

For questions or to confirm your polling place, contact the County Elections Office at (555) 010-2400 or visit the official county elections website.
