**Notice: Reporting a Lost Company Laptop**

**To all employees:**

If you lose your company laptop, or think it may have been stolen, report it right away. Do not wait to see whether it turns up. A missing laptop may hold company and customer information, and the sooner we know, the sooner we can protect that information.

**What to do:**

1. **Tell your manager and the IT department immediately.** A short initial report is enough, and you can add details afterward.
2. **Give the key details:**
   - When and where you last had the laptop
   - What happened, as far as you know
   - What kind of work or information was on it
   - Whether you think it was lost or stolen
3. **Follow IT's instructions.** They may ask you to change passwords for accounts you used on the laptop.

Reporting promptly will not be held against you. Delays in reporting make the problem harder to contain.

Thank you for helping keep our information secure.
