This notice outlines the terms governing refund eligibility for all transactions. To qualify for a refund, a written request must be submitted within thirty calendar days from the date of purchase, accompanied by valid proof of payment. Physical goods must be returned unused, unaltered, and in their original packaging. For services, refund requests must be received prior to the commencement of any scheduled work. Customized products, downloadable digital goods, and items designated as final sale are strictly non-refundable. Upon receipt and inspection of the returned merchandise or verification of the cancelled service, eligible amounts will be reimbursed solely to the original method of payment within ten business days. Processing times may vary depending on the policies of the issuing financial institution. The company reserves the right to deny any claim that does not satisfy these requirements.
