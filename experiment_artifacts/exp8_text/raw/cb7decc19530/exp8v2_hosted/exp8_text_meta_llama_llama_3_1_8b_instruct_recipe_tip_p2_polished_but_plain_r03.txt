To preserve the freshness of fresh herbs, store them in a way that maintains their natural moisture. Place the herbs in a sealed container, such as a plastic bag or a glass jar with a tight-fitting lid. If you're storing them in a bag, you can place a paper towel or cloth inside to absorb excess moisture. This will help prevent the herbs from becoming soggy or developing mold. Store the herbs in the refrigerator, away from direct sunlight and heat sources.
