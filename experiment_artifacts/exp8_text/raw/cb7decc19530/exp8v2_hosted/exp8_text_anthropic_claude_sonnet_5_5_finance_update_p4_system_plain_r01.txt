Your end-of-year tax statement is now ready. It summarizes the income you received and the taxes withheld from it over the past year, and you will need it to file your tax return.

The first thing to look at is your total income, which shows everything you earned before any deductions. Next is the amount of tax already withheld, meaning what was sent to the tax authority on your behalf throughout the year. If that amount is higher than what you actually owe, you will likely receive a refund. If it is lower, you may have a balance to pay when you file.

You may also see entries for retirement contributions, health insurance premiums, or other deductions. These can lower your taxable income, so check that they match your own records.

Please review the statement carefully, especially your name, address, and identification number. If anything looks wrong or missing, contact us as soon as possible so we can issue a corrected version before the filing deadline. Keep a copy with your other tax records, since you may need it later.

If you have questions about any line on the statement, just reply and I will be glad to walk you through it.
