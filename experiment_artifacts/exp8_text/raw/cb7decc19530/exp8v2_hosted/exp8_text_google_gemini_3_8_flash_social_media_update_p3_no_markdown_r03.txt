Looking for weekend plans? Join us this Saturday, October 14th, for the annual Riverside Community Street Fair! 

We will have live music, local food trucks, games for the kids, and an artisan craft market. The fun kicks off at 11:00 AM at Riverside Park and runs until 4:00 PM. Admission is completely free, and all are welcome. 

Bring your friends, family, and neighbors. We can not wait to see you there!
