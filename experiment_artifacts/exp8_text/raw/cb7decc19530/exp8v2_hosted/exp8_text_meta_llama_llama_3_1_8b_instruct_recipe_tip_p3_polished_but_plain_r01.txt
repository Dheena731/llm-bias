To cook rice that is less sticky, it is essential to use the right ratio of water to rice and to rinse the rice thoroughly before cooking. A general rule of thumb is to use a ratio of one part rice to one and a half parts water. For example, to cook one cup of rice, use one and a half cups of water.

Before cooking the rice, rinse it under cold running water to remove excess starch. This is particularly important for sticky rice varieties, such as jasmine or basmati. By rinsing the rice, you can reduce the starch content and prevent the rice from becoming sticky.

When cooking the rice, use a medium-sized pot and bring the water to a boil. Once the water is boiling, add the rinsed rice and reduce the heat to a simmer. Do not stir the rice during cooking, as this can cause the grains to break and become sticky. Instead, cover the pot with a tight-fitting lid and let the rice cook undisturbed until it is done.
