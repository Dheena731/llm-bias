Batch the boring parts on Sunday, and assemble on the fly. Roast a sheet pan of chicken thighs and a tray of sweet potatoes, cook a pot of farro, and wash the greens while you're at it. Store each in its own container so nothing goes soggy. Then each weekday lunch is just one container from column A plus one from column B: thighs with farro one day, sweet potato with leftover chickpeas the next. You skip the daily decision and still eat something you actually want to finish by Thursday.
