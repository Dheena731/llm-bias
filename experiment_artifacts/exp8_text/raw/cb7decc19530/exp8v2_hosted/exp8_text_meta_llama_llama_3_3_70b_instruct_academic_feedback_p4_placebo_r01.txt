Here is a short feedback note on a conference poster draft:

"Dear [Presenter's Name],

I have reviewed the draft of your conference poster and overall, it effectively communicates the main findings of your research. The visuals are clear and well-organized, making it easy to follow the flow of your argument. One suggestion I have is to consider adding a brief summary or takeaway message at the bottom of the poster to help viewers quickly understand the key implications of your work.

Best regards,
[Your Name]"

I made sure to:

* Address the correct audience (the presenter)
* Focus on the main point (the effectiveness of the poster in communicating research findings)
* Avoid inventing facts or introducing undefined jargon
* Provide a specific and actionable suggestion for improvement
* Use polite and professional language throughout the note.
