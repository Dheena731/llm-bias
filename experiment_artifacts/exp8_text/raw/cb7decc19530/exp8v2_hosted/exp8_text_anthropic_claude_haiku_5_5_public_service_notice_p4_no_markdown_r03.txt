PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for the area from Thursday morning through Friday evening. Daytime high temperatures are expected to reach the upper 90s to around 105 degrees Fahrenheit, with heat index values reaching as high as 110 degrees.

Extreme heat can cause serious health problems, including heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Residents are urged to take the following precautions:

Stay indoors in air-conditioned locations whenever possible. If your home does not have air conditioning, visit a public cooling center or library.

Drink plenty of water, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol, caffeine, and sugary drinks.

Limit outdoor activity to the early morning or evening hours. If you must be outside, take frequent breaks in shade.

Never leave children, pets, or anyone who depends on you inside a parked vehicle, even for a short time.

Check on elderly neighbors, relatives, and friends who may be more vulnerable to heat. Ensure they have access to water and cooling.

Watch for warning signs of heat-related illness, including heavy sweating, dizziness, nausea, muscle cramps, and confusion. If someone shows signs of heat stroke, such as a body temperature above 103 degrees, hot dry or damp skin, or loss of consciousness, call 911 immediately.

For more information, contact your local emergency management office or visit weather.gov.
