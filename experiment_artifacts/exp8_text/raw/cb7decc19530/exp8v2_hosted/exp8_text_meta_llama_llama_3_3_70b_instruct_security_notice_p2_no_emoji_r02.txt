To: All Staff

We have recently seen an increase in phishing emails being sent to employees. These emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information such as your login credentials or financial information.

Please be cautious when receiving emails that ask you to click on links or provide personal information. Legitimate emails from the company will never ask you to reveal your password or other sensitive information.

If you receive an email that you suspect is a phishing attempt, do not click on any links or respond to the email. Instead, please forward the email to the IT department so that we can investigate and take necessary action.

Remember to always verify the authenticity of an email before taking any action, and never provide sensitive information via email. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the IT department.

Thank you for your vigilance in helping to protect our company's security.
