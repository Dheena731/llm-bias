**Subject: Delayed merchant payouts, Tuesday 11 March**

Payouts scheduled for Tuesday's ACH batch didn't leave until Wednesday afternoon. About 2,340 merchant payouts, roughly $1.9M, were held.

The cause was on our side. Our bank partner's settlement file failed our pre-submission checksum because one field had an extra character in the routing-number column. Our validator rejected the file, correctly, but the rejection went to a shared inbox that nobody was watching until 9:40 a.m. By then we had missed the 11:00 a.m. cutoff for same-day settlement.

What happened next:
- We corrected the field, regenerated the file, and resubmitted at 2:10 p.m. Wednesday. The bank accepted it without errors.
- Funds will settle Thursday 13 March, one business day later than originally scheduled. Merchants should see them by their usual morning balance update.
- We are not charging the $4.50 late-payout fee for any payout in this batch, and the reversal will post with Thursday's settlement.

Changes we're making:
- Validator rejections now page the on-call payments engineer directly, not the shared inbox.
- A second validation pass runs against the bank's published field spec before the file leaves our system.

If your payout is still missing on Thursday afternoon, reply to this thread with the payout ID and we'll trace it the same day.

— Payments Operations
