We've just launched Smart Reminders, a new way to stay on top of your work without the constant manual setup. Now, when you add a task with a date or time in it, the app picks it up automatically and schedules a reminder for you. If plans change, you can reschedule with a single tap, and your reminders will sync across all your devices.

We built this after hearing from many of you that the small things are the ones that slip. Smart Reminders is designed to catch them quietly, so you can focus on the work that matters.

The feature is rolling out today to all users and will reach everyone over the next few days. Update to the latest version to try it, and tell us what you think. Your feedback will shape what we improve next.
