A heat advisory is in effect for the region from Monday morning through Wednesday evening as temperatures are expected to exceed safe limits. Residents are urged to drink ample water, limit physical exertion during the midday hours, and remain in air-conditioned spaces when possible. Special attention should be paid to young children, the elderly, and individuals with chronic health conditions, who are more susceptible to heat-related illness. Neighbors are encouraged to check on one another regularly. If anyone displays signs of heat exhaustion or heat stroke, such as dizziness, nausea, or confusion, they should be moved to a cool environment and given immediate medical assistance if necessary. Public cooling centers will remain open at designated community facilities until the advisory ends. Please follow official weather channels for updates regarding this advisory.
