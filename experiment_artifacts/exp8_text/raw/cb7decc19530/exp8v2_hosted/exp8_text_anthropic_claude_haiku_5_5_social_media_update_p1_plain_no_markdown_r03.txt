Big news: we just launched Shared Folders, a new way to keep your team's files in one place. You can now create a folder, invite collaborators with a single link, and see everyone's changes update in real time. Permissions are easy to adjust, so you decide who can view, comment, or edit. Shared Folders is rolling out to all accounts starting today, and we'd love to hear what you think after you give it a try. Share your feedback with us anytime through the help menu.
