A quick reminder to step away from your screen for a moment. Every twenty minutes or so, look at something at least twenty feet away for twenty seconds, which gives your eyes a chance to relax and refocus. Once an hour, stand up, stretch your shoulders and neck, and walk around the room or get a glass of water. These pauses take very little time, yet they ease eye strain, reduce stiffness, and often leave you thinking more clearly when you return. If it helps, set a gentle timer so the habit doesn't depend on memory. Your work will still be there in a minute, and you will be better prepared to do it.
