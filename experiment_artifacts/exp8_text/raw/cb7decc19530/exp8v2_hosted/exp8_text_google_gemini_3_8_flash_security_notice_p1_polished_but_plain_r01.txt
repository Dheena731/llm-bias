Protecting company data and client privacy requires strong daily security practices, starting with your login credentials. Using the same password across multiple accounts creates a significant vulnerability for our organization. When a personal service, third-party platform, or corporate system suffers a data breach, attackers immediately test those exposed combinations against other services. Consequently, a breach on an unrelated personal account can grant unauthorized access to our internal systems.

To safeguard our network, ensure that every work account uses a completely unique password or passphrase. Never recycle credentials between personal and professional platforms, and avoid reusing passwords across different internal applications. You are strongly encouraged to use the company-approved password manager to generate, store, and automatically fill complex credentials securely. If you suspect any of your passwords have been exposed or observe any unusual account activity, please change your credentials immediately and contact the IT security team.
