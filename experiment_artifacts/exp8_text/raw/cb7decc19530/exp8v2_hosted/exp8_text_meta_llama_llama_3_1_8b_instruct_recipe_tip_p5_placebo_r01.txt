**A Simple yet Effective Tip for Seasoning Roasted Vegetables**

When it comes to enhancing the flavor of roasted vegetables, a common mistake is to rely solely on salt and pepper. While these seasonings are a good starting point, they often fall short of bringing out the full depth of flavor in these delicious dishes.

**The Secret to Unlocking Flavor: Acidity**

One key element to consider is the role of acidity in seasoning roasted vegetables. A squeeze of fresh lemon juice can work wonders in cutting through the richness of the roasted vegetables and balancing their natural sweetness. However, be cautious not to overdo it, as excessive acidity can overpower the other flavors.

**A Balanced Approach**

To achieve a harmonious balance of flavors, consider the following approach:

1. **Start with a foundation**: Use a neutral-tasting oil, such as olive or avocado oil, to coat the vegetables before roasting.
2. **Add a pinch of salt**: Sprinkle a small amount of flaky sea salt or kosher salt over the vegetables to enhance their natural flavors.
3. **Introduce a depth of flavor**: Sprinkle a pinch of dried herbs, such as thyme or rosemary, over the vegetables to add a savory element.
4. **Finish with a squeeze of lemon**: A light drizzle of fresh lemon juice can help to balance the flavors and enhance the overall taste experience.

By following these steps, you'll be able to unlock the full flavor potential of your roasted vegetables and create a dish that's sure to impress.
