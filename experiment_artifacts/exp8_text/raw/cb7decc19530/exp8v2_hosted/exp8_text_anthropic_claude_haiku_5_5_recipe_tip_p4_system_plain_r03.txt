Batch-cook one versatile base on the weekend, such as a pot of grains, a tray of roasted vegetables, or a large batch of shredded chicken, and then mix and match it into different lunches across the week. Keeping a few simple add-ins on hand, like a jar of dressing, some nuts, or fresh herbs, lets the same base taste new each day without much extra effort. Store portions in separate containers so everything stays fresh and assembly takes only a couple of minutes on busy mornings.
