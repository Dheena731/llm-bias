Please be advised that changes to the residential recycling collection service will take effect starting next Monday. Recycling will now be collected every other week rather than on a weekly basis, though your designated collection day will remain the same. In addition to the schedule change, all clean paper, cardboard, metal cans, and rigid plastics may now be placed together directly into your blue cart without sorting. Glass bottles and jars will no longer be accepted in curbside bins and must be taken to a designated community drop-off depot. Please ensure your cart is at the curb by seven in the morning on your scheduled collection day with the lid fully closed. We appreciate your cooperation and patience as we transition to this new system.
