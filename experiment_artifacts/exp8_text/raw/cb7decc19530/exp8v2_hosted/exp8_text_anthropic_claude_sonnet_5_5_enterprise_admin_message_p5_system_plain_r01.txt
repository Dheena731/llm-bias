Subject: Upcoming Email Migration This Weekend

Hello everyone,

We will be migrating our company email to a new platform this coming Saturday, beginning at 8:00 p.m. and finishing by about 6:00 a.m. on Sunday. During this window, you may be unable to send or receive messages, and some mail may be delayed until the move is complete.

There is nothing you need to do beforehand except sign out of email on your phone and other devices before Saturday evening. When you return on Monday, you will be asked to sign in again with your usual username and a new temporary password, which will be sent to your personal contact email on Sunday. Your existing messages, folders, and contacts will be carried over automatically, and your email address will not change.

If you have a very large mailbox, it is a good idea to delete old messages you no longer need, since that will help the transfer go more smoothly. Calendar invitations and shared mailboxes will also move over, though we recommend checking your upcoming meetings on Monday morning to confirm everything looks right.

If anything seems missing or you have trouble signing in, please contact the IT help desk and we will get you sorted out quickly. Thank you for your patience while we make this improvement.

Best regards,
The IT Team
