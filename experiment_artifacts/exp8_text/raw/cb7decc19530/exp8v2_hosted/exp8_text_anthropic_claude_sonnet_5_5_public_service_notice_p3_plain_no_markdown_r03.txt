Notice of Changes to Recycling Collection

Beginning Monday, the first of next month, curbside recycling collection in our community will move to a new schedule. Households that previously had pickup on Tuesdays will now have collection on Thursdays, and households that had Friday pickup will move to Wednesdays. Residents whose collection day is Monday are not affected and should continue to set out their bins as usual.

Please place your recycling bin at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on your collection day, since routes may begin earlier than before. Bins left out after the truck has passed should be brought in by the end of the day.

We are also updating the list of accepted materials. Clean paper, cardboard, glass bottles and jars, metal cans, and plastic containers marked with the numbers one, two, and five will continue to be collected. Plastic bags, foam packaging, and shredded paper will no longer be accepted in curbside bins, as they cause problems at the sorting facility. Plastic bags can be returned to participating grocery stores, and shredded paper may be taken to the drop-off site at the municipal office.

To help keep your materials from being rejected, please rinse containers and place items loose in the bin rather than bagged. Flatten cardboard boxes so that more fits in each bin.

If you have questions or need a printed copy of the new schedule, please contact the public works office during regular business hours. We appreciate your cooperation and your continued efforts to keep our community clean.
