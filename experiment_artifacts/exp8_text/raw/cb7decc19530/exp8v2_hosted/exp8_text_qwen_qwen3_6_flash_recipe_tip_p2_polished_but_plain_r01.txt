To keep fresh herbs vibrant and fragrant, treat them much like cut flowers. Trim the ends of the stems with a sharp knife to encourage water absorption, then place the bunch upright in a glass jar filled with an inch of water. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag to preserve humidity while preventing condensation buildup that leads to spoilage. Store the jar in the refrigerator and refresh the water every two days, extending the life of your herbs significantly beyond what is possible by leaving them unwrapped on the counter.
