Polling stations for the upcoming election are located at the following sites: the community center on Main Street, the local library on Oak Street, and the town hall on Elm Street. Voters can cast their ballots at any of these locations between the hours of 8am and 6pm on election day. Voters are reminded to check their voter registration cards to confirm their assigned polling station, as some voters may have been assigned to a specific location. If you have any questions or concerns about polling station locations, you can contact the local election office for more information.
