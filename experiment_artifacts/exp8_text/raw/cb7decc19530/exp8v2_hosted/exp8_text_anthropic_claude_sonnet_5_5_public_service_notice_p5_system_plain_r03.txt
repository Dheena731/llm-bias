Notice of Polling Station Locations

Residents are advised that polling stations for the upcoming election will be open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on Election Day. Each voter is assigned to a specific polling station based on their home address, and the station name and address are printed on the voter information card mailed to every registered household.

Voters who have not received a card, or who are unsure of their assigned location, can confirm it by calling the Election Office at the number listed on the town website or by visiting the office during regular business hours. Please note that some locations have changed since the last election. Several stations previously held in school gymnasiums have moved to community centers and libraries to improve accessibility and parking.

All polling stations are wheelchair accessible, and staff will be available to assist voters who need additional support. Voters should bring a valid form of identification. If you are unable to reach your assigned station on Election Day, please contact the Election Office as early as possible so that alternative arrangements can be made.

Thank you for taking part in the democratic process.

Office of the Town Clerk
