Please be reminded that using the same password across multiple accounts poses a significant security risk to our organization. When a personal account or an external service experiences a data breach, cybercriminals frequently use those compromised credentials to attempt unauthorized access to workplace networks and sensitive company data. 

To keep our systems safe, you must maintain a unique, complex password for every business and personal account. Never reuse your corporate login details for any external websites, and avoid sharing identical credentials across different internal tools. Employees are required to use the approved company password manager to create and store these credentials safely. If you are currently reusing any workplace passwords, please update them immediately, and contact the IT security team if you need assistance.
