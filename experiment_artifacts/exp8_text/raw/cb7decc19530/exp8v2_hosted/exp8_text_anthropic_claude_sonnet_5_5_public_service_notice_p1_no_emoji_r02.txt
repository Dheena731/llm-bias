**PUBLIC NOTICE: Updated Library Opening Hours**

Effective Monday, the Central Public Library will operate under the following hours:

**Monday to Thursday:** 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
**Friday:** 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
**Saturday:** 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
**Sunday:** Closed

**Holiday Closures:** The library will be closed on all public holidays. Please check the library website or notice board for details on upcoming closures.

**Returns:** The outdoor book drop remains available 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

**Computer and Study Areas:** These areas close 15 minutes before the library closes. Visitors are asked to save their work and collect their belongings in good time.

For questions, please speak with a member of staff at the front desk, call the library at 555-0142, or visit the library website.

Thank you for your continued support of our community library.

*Issued by the Office of Library Services*
