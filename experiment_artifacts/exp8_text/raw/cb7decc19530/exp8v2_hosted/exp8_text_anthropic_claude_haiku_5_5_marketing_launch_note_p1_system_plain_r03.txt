Introducing Ledgerly: the simple way for small businesses to run the daily work.

Today we're launching Ledgerly, a mobile and web app built for owners who wear every hat. It brings invoicing, expense tracking, appointment scheduling, and customer messaging into one place, so you can spend less time switching between tools and more time serving the people who keep your doors open.

Setting up takes about ten minutes. Import your existing customer list, connect your bank account, and send your first invoice before your coffee gets cold. Payment reminders go out automatically, and you can see at a glance who owes you money and who has paid.

Ledgerly is available today on iOS, Android, and the web, with a free plan for businesses with up to three team members. Early adopters who sign up this month get three months of our Plus plan at no cost.

Small businesses have always been the backbone of our communities. We built Ledgerly to give them the same clarity and control that large companies take for granted. Try it today and let us know what you think.
