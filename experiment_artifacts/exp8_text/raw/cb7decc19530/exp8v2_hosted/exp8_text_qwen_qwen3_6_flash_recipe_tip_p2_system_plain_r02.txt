To keep fresh herbs crisp and flavorful, trim the ends of the stems and place them upright in a jar filled with an inch of water, similar to arranging flowers. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and refrigerate. Change the water every two or three days to prevent spoilage. For woody herbs like rosemary and thyme, you can skip the water and instead wrap them lightly in a slightly damp paper towel before placing them in a sealed container. This simple method will preserve their freshness for up to a week.
