We strongly encourage all users to enable two-factor authentication on their accounts to enhance security. This additional verification step requires a second credential, such as a code from your mobile device, in addition to your password, which effectively prevents unauthorized access even if your login details are exposed. To activate this feature, please visit your account security settings and follow the setup instructions to link your preferred authentication method. Once configured, you will need to provide the generated code during each login session, ensuring that only verified individuals can access your information. Activating two-factor authentication is a simple measure that provides significant protection for your data and privacy.
