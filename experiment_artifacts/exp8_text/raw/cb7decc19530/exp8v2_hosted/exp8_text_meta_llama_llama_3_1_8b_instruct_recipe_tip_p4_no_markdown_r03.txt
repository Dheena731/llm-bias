Here's a short tip for planning lunches for a busy week:

Take some time on the weekend to prepare a week's worth of lunches. Chop up vegetables, cook proteins like chicken or tofu, and assemble salads in containers. This will save you time and stress during the week when you're scrambling to figure out what to eat. Plus, it's a great opportunity to use up ingredients that might otherwise go to waste. Consider portioning out individual lunches in reusable containers or zip-top bags for an easy grab-and-go option.
