To keep fresh herbs fresh for a longer period of time, store them in a cool and dry place. You can wrap the herbs in a damp paper towel and place them in a sealed container or plastic bag. This will help to maintain the humidity and keep the herbs from drying out. You can also trim the stems of the herbs and place them in a glass of water, similar to how you would display fresh flowers, and store them in the refrigerator. This method will help to keep the herbs fresh for up to a week.
