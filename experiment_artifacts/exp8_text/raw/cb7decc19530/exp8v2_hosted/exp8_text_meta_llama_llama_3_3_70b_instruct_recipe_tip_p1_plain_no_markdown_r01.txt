To prepare a quick and easy weeknight dinner, consider cooking proteins and vegetables in advance. On the weekend or a day off, roast a large batch of chicken or cook a pot of lentils that can be used in various meals throughout the week. You can also chop and store vegetables like onions, carrots, and bell peppers in the refrigerator to add to meals as needed. This way, when you come home from work on a busy weeknight, you can simply assemble a meal using your pre-cooked ingredients and have dinner on the table in no time.
