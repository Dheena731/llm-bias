Please be aware of an increase in phishing emails targeting our organization. Phishing attempts often disguise themselves as routine business communications, such as notifications from vendors, human resources, or executive management. These messages typically create a false sense of urgency, asking you to immediately verify your login credentials, process an unexpected payment, or open an unprompted attachment. 

Always inspect the sender address carefully and avoid clicking on any attachments or hyperlinks within unfamiliar messages. If you receive an email that looks suspicious or out of the ordinary, do not reply to it or forward it to colleagues. Instead, report it immediately to the information technology department using the report message button in your email client. If you need to verify an unusual request, contact the sender through a separate, confirmed communication channel such as a direct phone call. Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our company network and data secure.
