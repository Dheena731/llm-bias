Public Notice: Heat Advisory

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for the city and surrounding areas from 11 a.m. today through 8 p.m. Thursday. Daytime temperatures are expected to reach the mid-90s, with heat index values near 105 degrees. Overnight temperatures will remain high, offering little relief.

Prolonged exposure to this heat can cause heat exhaustion and heat stroke, particularly among older adults, young children, people with chronic illnesses, and those who work or exercise outdoors. Residents are urged to stay indoors in air-conditioned spaces when possible, drink water regularly even if not thirsty, and avoid strenuous activity during the hottest part of the day, generally between noon and 5 p.m. Never leave children or pets inside a parked vehicle, as interior temperatures can climb rapidly to dangerous levels.

Check on neighbors, friends, and family members who may be vulnerable to the heat, and make sure pets have shade and fresh water. Cooling centers have been opened at the public library and the community recreation center and will remain open during regular business hours through the advisory period. Anyone experiencing dizziness, nausea, confusion, or a body temperature above 103 degrees should seek medical attention immediately or call 911 in an emergency.

For the latest updates, residents should monitor local news and weather broadcasts or contact the city's emergency management office.
