PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE

The City Department of Transportation advises residents that Mill Street will be closed to all vehicle and pedestrian traffic between Oak Avenue and Second Street beginning Monday, June 10, and continuing through Friday, June 14. Crews will be repairing water main lines and resurfacing the roadway during this period. Work hours will be 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. each day.

Detours will be posted along Maple Avenue and River Road. Drivers are encouraged to allow extra travel time and follow all posted signs. Local businesses along the closed section will remain accessible from Oak Avenue and Second Street. Emergency vehicles will have access to the work zone at all times.

Residents with questions or concerns may call the Department of Transportation at (555) 014-2290 or visit the city website for updates. The schedule is subject to change due to weather conditions. Thank you for your patience while we complete these necessary improvements.
