To tackle a busy week, try "lunch clustering": group similar ingredients and cooking tasks together to create a batch of lunches on the weekend or one day off. For example, roast a large chicken and use it to make chicken salads, wraps, and soup for the next few days. This approach saves time during the week and reduces food waste by using up all the ingredients you've prepped. You can also customize each lunch by adding different toppings or sides, so you don't get bored with the same old thing every day.
