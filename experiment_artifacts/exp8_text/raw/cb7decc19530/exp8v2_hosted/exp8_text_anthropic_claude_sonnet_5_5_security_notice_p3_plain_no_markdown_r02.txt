Reminder: Please Lock Your Screen

Colleagues, this is a friendly reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if only for a moment. An unlocked screen can give others access to confidential information, email accounts, and internal systems, and it only takes a few seconds for something to go wrong.

The quickest way to lock a Windows computer is to press the Windows key and the L key together. On a Mac, press Control, Command, and Q. If you are unsure how to do this on your device, please contact the IT help desk and someone will be glad to assist you.

Locking your screen is a simple habit that protects you, your coworkers, and our clients. Thank you for helping keep our workplace secure.
