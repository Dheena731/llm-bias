When seasoning roasted vegetables, it's essential to taste as you go, adjusting the seasoning to suit your palate. Start by drizzling the vegetables with olive oil, then sprinkle with salt and a pinch of pepper. Allow the vegetables to roast for a few minutes before adding any additional seasonings. This will help to prevent them from burning or becoming unevenly seasoned.

Some general guidelines to keep in mind are that herbs like thyme, rosemary, and oregano pair well with root vegetables and hardy greens, while more delicate herbs like parsley and basil complement vegetables like asparagus and bell peppers. Additionally, a squeeze of fresh lemon juice can add a bright, citrusy flavor to many roasted vegetables. The key is to taste and adjust as you go, allowing the natural flavors of the vegetables to shine through.
