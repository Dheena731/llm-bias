Public Service Notice: Library Opening Hours

The Riverside Public Library wants to remind residents of its updated opening hours. Beginning Monday, the main library branch will be open from 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on Monday through Thursday, from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on Friday and Saturday, and from 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. on Sunday. The library will remain closed on all major public holidays.

Please plan your visits accordingly. The book return slot outside the front entrance is available at all times, so items can be returned when the building is closed. Patrons who need assistance with research, computer access, or borrowing questions are encouraged to visit during staffed hours or contact the reference desk during business hours.

For questions about holiday closures or special programming schedules, residents may call the library during open hours. The staff look forward to serving you.
