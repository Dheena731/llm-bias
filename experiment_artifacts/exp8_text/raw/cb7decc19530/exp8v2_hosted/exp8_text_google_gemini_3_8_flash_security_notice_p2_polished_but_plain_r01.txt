The information technology team has noted an increase in sophisticated phishing attempts targeting our organization. These deceptive emails often impersonate trusted vendors, executive leadership, or internal departments, attempting to trick recipients into compromising sensitive company data or network credentials. Common warning signs include unexpected attachments, awkward phrasing, and an artificial sense of urgency demanding immediate action, such as updating account details, paying an overdue invoice, or resetting login credentials.

To protect our network, please exercise caution when reviewing incoming correspondence. Always verify the actual email address of the sender rather than relying solely on the display name, and refrain from clicking on unsolicited links or opening unexpected attachments. If an email requests sensitive information or financial transactions, confirm the request through a secondary, verified communication channel such as a direct phone call or our internal messaging system. 

If you receive a message that appears suspicious, do not reply, click any embedded links, or forward it to colleagues. Instead, notify the IT security team immediately using the report function in your email client or by calling the internal help desk. Your vigilance is our strongest defense against security breaches, and we appreciate your ongoing commitment to keeping our workplace secure.
