Subject: Update: New Expense Approval Workflow

Team,

We are implementing a revised expense approval workflow to standardize submissions, reduce processing delays, and maintain clear accountability. This change applies to all staff who submit expense requests or provide managerial approvals.

Effective immediately, all expense reports must be entered into the internal expense portal with digital copies of every receipt uploaded before submission. Once submitted, your direct supervisor reviews the request for completeness and policy compliance. After your supervisor approves it, the finance department schedules reimbursement during the regular biweekly payment cycle. Any report submitted without required documentation, or routed outside this sequence, will be returned for correction and delayed accordingly.

Full step-by-step instructions and updated policy guidelines are published in the Employee Operations Guide under the Finance & Reimbursements section. For technical assistance with portal navigation or approval tracking, contact the Finance Helpdesk directly.

Thank you for your prompt attention to this update as we transition to the new process.

Administration
