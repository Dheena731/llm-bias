To keep fresh herbs fresh for a longer period, it's essential to store them properly. One effective way to do this is to trim the stems of the herbs and place them in a glass of water, similar to how you would store cut flowers. Then, cover the glass with a plastic bag and store it in the refrigerator. This method helps to keep the herbs moist and cool, which slows down the wilting process. Alternatively, you can also wrap the herbs in a damp paper towel and store them in an airtight container in the refrigerator. This will help to maintain the humidity around the herbs and keep them fresh for several days. Regularly checking on the herbs and replacing the water or paper towel as needed will also help to extend their freshness.
