**PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE**

**Main Street between Oak Avenue and Pine Road will be closed to all through traffic.**

**Dates:** Monday, June 9, through Friday, June 13, 2025
**Hours:** 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily

**Reason for Closure:** Repaving and storm drain repair work.

**Detour Information:** Drivers should use Elm Street and Fifth Avenue as an alternate route. Detour signs will be posted along the route.

**Access:** Local residents and businesses within the closure area will retain access to their driveways and parking areas, though short delays may occur. Emergency vehicles will be able to pass through the work zone at all times.

**Parking:** Vehicles parked on Main Street within the closure area must be moved by 6:30 a.m. on June 9. Vehicles remaining on the street may be towed at the owner's expense.

**Please Note:** The schedule may change due to weather. Updates will be posted on the City website and on signs at the work site.

We apologize for the inconvenience and thank you for your patience as we work to improve our streets.

For questions, please contact the Department of Public Works at (555) 123-4567 or publicworks@examplecity.gov.
