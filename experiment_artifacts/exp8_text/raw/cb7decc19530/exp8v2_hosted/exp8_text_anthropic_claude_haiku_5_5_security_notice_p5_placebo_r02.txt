**Subject: How to Report a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If you lose your company laptop or believe it has been stolen, report it to the IT department immediately. Do this even if you are not sure where it is. Quick reporting lets IT take steps to protect company data.

**What to do:**

1. **Contact the IT Help Desk right away.** Do not wait to see whether the laptop turns up.
2. **Tell your manager** about the loss.
3. **If the laptop was stolen, file a police report** and give a copy of the report number to IT.
4. **Provide the details you have,** including the laptop's asset tag or serial number if you know it, and when and where you last had it.

Do not try to access, copy, or delete data on the missing device yourself. Leave any recovery steps to IT.
