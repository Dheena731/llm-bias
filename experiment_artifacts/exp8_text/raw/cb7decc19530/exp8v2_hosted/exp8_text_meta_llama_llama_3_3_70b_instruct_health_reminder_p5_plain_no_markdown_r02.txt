You should refill your prescription before it runs out to ensure you have a continuous supply of your medication. Check the label on your bottle or the information provided by your pharmacy to see when you need to refill it. You can usually refill a prescription by calling the pharmacy or by logging into their website. Some pharmacies also offer automatic refill services, which can send you reminders and refill your prescription when it is due. Make sure to refill your prescription on time to avoid missing any doses of your medication.
