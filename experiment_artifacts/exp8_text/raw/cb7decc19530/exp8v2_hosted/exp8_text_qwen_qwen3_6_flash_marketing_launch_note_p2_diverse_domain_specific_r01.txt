We’re opening enrollments for our new subscription model. It started as an internal fix: too many regulars were buying beans they didn’t have the right gear to extract properly, or letting them sit past peak degassing.

Here’s how it runs: one origin per cycle, roasted to order, shipped with the roast date printed directly on the bag. Minimum five days post-roast. You log your primary brew method upfront, and we match the grind accordingly—flat burr, conical, press, or whole bean for a later day’s setup. Rotate between single-origin light, medium-dark house, or rotating experimental lots. Pause, skip, or adjust frequency before the weekly roast window locks in.

No volume tiers disguised as perks. Just predictable access to current crop coffees, calibrated to how you actually pull your shot or steep your paper. Enrollments open Thursday. First two shipments include grind-to-method matching at no extra cost.
