Here is a short note explaining a change to card transaction fees:

"Effective immediately, we are introducing a revised card transaction fee structure for our merchants. As of today, all Visa and Mastercard transactions will incur a 1.25% + $0.10 fee per transaction. This change is a result of a recent market analysis and our ongoing efforts to maintain competitive pricing in the face of rising industry costs.

The new fee will apply to all transactions processed through our system, and we encourage our merchants to review their payment processing arrangements to ensure they are compliant with the updated fees. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to our customer support team.

We appreciate your understanding and cooperation as we adapt to these changes. As always, we are committed to providing our merchants with the best possible payment processing experience."
