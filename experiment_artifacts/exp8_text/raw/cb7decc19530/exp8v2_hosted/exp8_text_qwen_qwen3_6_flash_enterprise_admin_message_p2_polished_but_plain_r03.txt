The finance team is introducing an updated expense approval workflow designed to enhance accuracy and speed up reimbursement cycles. Effective immediately, all expense reports must be submitted through the centralized portal rather than via email or paper forms. Submitters should categorize costs according to the new chart of accounts and attach digital receipts for every transaction. Supervisors are required to review and approve claims within three business days to prevent payment delays. If you encounter issues with the portal or have questions regarding specific policy updates, please reach out to the finance department directly at the standard support address. Thank you for your cooperation in making this transition smooth.
