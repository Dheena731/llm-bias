PUBLIC NOTICE

Road Closure on Maple Avenue

The City Department of Public Works will close Maple Avenue between Fifth Street and Oak Boulevard beginning at 7:00 a.m. on Monday, March 16, and continuing through Friday, March 20. The closure is necessary to complete scheduled repairs to the road surface and underground water lines.

During this period, drivers should plan alternate routes. Detours will be posted at both ends of the closed section. Local access to businesses and residences along the affected stretch will be maintained where possible, but drivers should expect delays.

Pedestrians can continue to use the sidewalks on both sides of the street, which will remain open throughout the project. Emergency vehicles will have access at all times.

Work is expected to be completed by 6:00 p.m. on Friday, March 20, weather permitting. Motorists are asked to observe all posted signs and follow directions from crew members on site.

For questions or more information, please call the Department of Public Works at (555) 123-4567 or visit www.cityexample.gov.

We appreciate your patience as we work to improve local infrastructure.
