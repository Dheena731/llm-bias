**HEAT ADVISORY**

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for our area, effective immediately. Temperatures are expected to soar to record highs, making it a critical period for heat-related illnesses.

**Protect Yourself and Others:**

* Stay indoors, especially during the hottest part of the day (11am-3pm).
* Drink plenty of water and avoid strenuous activities.
* Check on vulnerable neighbors, such as the elderly and young children, to ensure their safety.
* Never leave children or pets unattended in a vehicle.

**Symptoms of Heat-Related Illnesses:**

* Dizziness
* Nausea
* Headache
* Fatigue
* Rapid pulse

If you or someone you know experiences any of these symptoms, seek medical attention immediately.

**Stay Informed:**

* Monitor local news and weather reports for updates on the heat advisory.
* Visit our website or social media for heat safety tips and resources.

Stay safe and stay cool!
