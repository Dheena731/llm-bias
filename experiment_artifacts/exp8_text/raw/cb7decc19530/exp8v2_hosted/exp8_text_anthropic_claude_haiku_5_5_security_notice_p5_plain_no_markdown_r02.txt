Lost or Stolen Company Laptop: Reporting Procedure

If you have lost a company laptop or believe it has been stolen, you must report it to the IT Service Desk as soon as possible, and no later than two hours after you notice it is missing. Prompt reporting allows the security team to lock the device, revoke access to company accounts, and reduce the risk of sensitive information being exposed.

To report the loss, contact the IT Service Desk by phone at the internal extension listed on the intranet or by email to the service desk mailbox. Provide your name, employee number, the laptop's asset tag, the date and approximate time you last had the device, and where you think it may have been lost or taken. If the laptop was stolen, you should also file a police report and send a copy of the report reference number to the service desk.

Do not attempt to recover the device yourself or confront anyone who may have it. Until the device has been reported, avoid logging into company systems from any other device that may have been connected to it, and change your passwords only after the IT team has advised you to do so.

Failure to report a lost or stolen device promptly may result in disciplinary action under company policy. If you find the laptop after reporting it, notify the service desk immediately so the record can be updated and the device can be returned for inspection.
