To get dinner on the table quickly with minimal cleanup, rely on high-heat sheet pan meals. Toss your choice of chopped vegetables and a quick-cooking protein like chicken tenders, shrimp, or sliced sausage with olive oil, salt, pepper, and your favorite spice blend. Spread everything in a single layer on a parchment-lined baking sheet and roast at four hundred degrees Fahrenheit until the edges are nicely browned and the meat is cooked through. Dinner comes together in about twenty minutes, and you will only have one pan to wash when the meal is over.
