To make weeknight dinners fast and stress-free, embrace the sheet pan method. Toss your choice of protein and bite-sized, sturdy vegetables with olive oil, salt, and your favorite spice blend directly on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Roast everything at four hundred degrees Fahrenheit until the meat is cooked through and the edges of the vegetables are caramelized. You get a balanced, deeply flavorful meal in about thirty minutes, and cleanup requires little more than tossing the paper and giving the pan a quick rinse.
