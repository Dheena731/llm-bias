**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication**

To better protect your account, we strongly encourage you to turn on two-factor authentication (2FA). This adds a second verification step at sign-in, so your account stays secure even if your password is compromised.

**How to enable 2FA:**

1. Sign in and go to **Account Settings**.
2. Select **Security**, then choose **Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Choose your preferred method: an authenticator app (recommended) or text message.
4. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm your setup.
5. Save your backup codes in a safe place in case you lose access to your device.

**Why it matters:**
Passwords alone can be guessed, stolen, or leaked. Two-factor authentication significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access.

Setup takes only a few minutes. If you need help, please contact our support team.

Thank you for helping keep your account secure.
