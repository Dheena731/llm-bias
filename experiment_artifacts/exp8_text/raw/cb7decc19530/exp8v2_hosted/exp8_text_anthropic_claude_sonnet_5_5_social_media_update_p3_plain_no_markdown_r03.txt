Join us this Saturday for our annual Riverside Community Day at Maple Park, running from 10 in the morning until 4 in the afternoon. There will be live music, local food vendors, a plant swap, and free activities for kids all day long. Bring a blanket or lawn chair and plan to stay a while. Volunteers are still welcome, so if you can spare an hour or two, stop by the information tent when you arrive. We hope to see you and your neighbors there.
