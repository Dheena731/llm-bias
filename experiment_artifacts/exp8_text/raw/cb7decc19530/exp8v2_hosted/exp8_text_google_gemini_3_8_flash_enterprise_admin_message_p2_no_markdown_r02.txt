Subject: Update: New Expense Approval Workflow

Hello Team,

Please be aware that we are implementing an updated expense approval workflow effective immediately.

All expense reports and reimbursement requests must now be submitted through the updated portal. Under this new process, claims will be automatically routed to your direct manager for review, with an additional approval step required for expenses exceeding $500. 

This update is intended to streamline the review process and reduce reimbursement processing times. 

A detailed step-by-step guide is now available on the company intranet. If you have any questions or encounter any issues, please contact the finance department at finance@company.com.

Thank you,
Finance & Administration Team
