Rinse your rice in a bowl of cold water before cooking, swirling it with your fingers and pouring off the cloudy water, and repeat until the water runs mostly clear. Rinsing washes away the surface starch that makes grains clump together. Drain well, then cook with the usual amount of water. Once the rice is done, let it rest with the lid on for about ten minutes before fluffing it gently with a fork, which helps the grains separate and stay light.
