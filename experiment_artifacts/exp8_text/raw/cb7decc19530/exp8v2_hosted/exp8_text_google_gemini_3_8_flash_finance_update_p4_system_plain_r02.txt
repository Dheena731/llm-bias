Your end-of-year tax statement is an official summary of all the financial activity and tax withholdings associated with your account over the past calendar year. This document provides the necessary figures you need to accurately complete and file your annual income tax returns with the government. Please review the reported amounts carefully to ensure they match your personal records, and store the statement in a secure place for your tax files. If you work with an accountant or professional tax preparer, you will need to provide them with a copy of this document. If you notice any discrepancies or have questions regarding the information provided, please contact our support team.
