Refund Eligibility Notice

This notice outlines the terms and conditions under which refunds may be granted for purchases made from our company.

A refund may be eligible if the product is defective, damaged, or not as described. To be eligible, the product must be returned in its original condition, with all original packaging and accessories included.

If a refund is granted, it will be made in the same manner as the original payment. If the original payment was by credit card, the refund will be credited back to the same credit card. If the original payment was by cash or check, a check will be issued for the refund amount.

Refunds will not be granted if the product has been used, worn, or altered in any way. Refunds will also not be granted if the product is returned after the deadline specified in our return policy.

Refunds will be made within a reasonable time frame after receipt of the returned product. We reserve the right to inspect the returned product before issuing a refund. If the product is found to be in good condition, a refund will be issued promptly.
