Quarterly Access Reviews

As part of our ongoing efforts to ensure the security and integrity of our systems, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews for all users with access to our network and applications.

These reviews will be conducted on a quarterly basis and will involve a review of each user's access permissions to ensure that they are still necessary and aligned with their current job functions. Users with access to sensitive or high-risk systems will be subject to additional review and verification.

If you are a user with access to our network or applications, you will be contacted by our security team to arrange a review meeting. During the review, we will discuss your access permissions and determine whether they are still valid.

If your access permissions are deemed unnecessary or excessive, they will be removed and you will be notified of the changes. If you have any questions or concerns about the review process, please do not hesitate to contact our security team.

We appreciate your cooperation in this matter and will work with you to ensure that our systems remain secure and accessible only to authorized personnel.
