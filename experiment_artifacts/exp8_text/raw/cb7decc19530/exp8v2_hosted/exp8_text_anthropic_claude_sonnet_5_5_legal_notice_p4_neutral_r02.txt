# NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

**Effective Date:** [Insert Date]

**1. Purpose**
This Notice sets out the conditions under which [Company Name] ("the Company") will issue refunds for products or services purchased by the customer ("the Customer").

**2. Eligibility**
A Customer is eligible for a refund only if all of the following conditions are met:

   (a) The refund request is submitted in writing within thirty (30) days of the original purchase date;

   (b) The Customer provides valid proof of purchase, such as a receipt or order confirmation number;

   (c) The product is returned in its original condition and packaging, where applicable; and

   (d) The product or service has not been used beyond reasonable inspection or trial, unless found defective.

**3. Non-Refundable Items**
The following are not eligible for a refund:

   (a) Digital goods that have been downloaded or accessed;

   (b) Personalized or custom-made items;

   (c) Gift cards and promotional credits; and

   (d) Services that have been fully rendered.

**4. Defective or Misdescribed Goods**
Nothing in this Notice limits any rights the Customer may have under applicable consumer protection laws regarding defective, damaged, or misdescribed goods or services.

**5. Processing**
Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within ten (10) business days of the Company's receipt and approval of the request. The Company is not responsible for delays caused by third-party financial institutions.

**6. Shipping Costs**
Unless the return results from Company error or product defect, the Customer bears all return shipping costs. Original shipping fees are non-refundable.

**7. Amendments**
The Company reserves the right to amend this Notice at any time. Amendments apply only to purchases made after the effective date of the revised Notice.
