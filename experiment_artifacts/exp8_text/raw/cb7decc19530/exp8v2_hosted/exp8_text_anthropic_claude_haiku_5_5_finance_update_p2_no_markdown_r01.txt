Subject: Changes to Card Transaction Fees

Dear Valued Customer,

We are writing to let you know that, effective March 1, our card transaction fee will change from 2.2 percent to 2.6 percent of the total purchase amount for credit and debit card payments. The change reflects higher processing costs that our payment providers have passed on to us over the past year.

The new rate applies to all card payments made on or after the effective date. Cash, bank transfer, and check payments are not affected, and there is no change to our listed prices. For example, a $100 purchase paid by card will carry a fee of $2.60 instead of $2.20.

We know that any change in fees is unwelcome, and we have worked to keep the adjustment as small as possible. If you have questions about how the change affects your account or a specific order, please contact our customer service team at [phone number] or [email address]. We are happy to help.

Thank you for your continued support.
