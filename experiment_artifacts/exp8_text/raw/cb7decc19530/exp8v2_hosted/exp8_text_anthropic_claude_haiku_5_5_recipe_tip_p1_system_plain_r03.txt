For a quick weeknight dinner, try prepping your vegetables while the pan heats up rather than before you start cooking. Chop an onion, mince some garlic, or slice bell peppers during the minutes your oil is warming, and you'll find the whole meal comes together much faster with less standing around. Keeping your cutting board and knife close to the stove also means you can add ingredients to the pan at exactly the right moment, so nothing overcooks while you're still working on the next step.
