**Notice: Quarterly Access Review Now Open**

**To:** All People Managers and System Owners

The quarterly access review is now underway. This process ensures that staff members hold only the system permissions necessary for their current job duties, helping maintain our overall data security.

**Required Actions:**
1. Review the access permissions assigned to your direct reports and system users.
2. Approve permissions for individuals who still require them for their day-to-day work.
3. Revoke or flag for removal any permissions that are outdated, excessive, or belong to former team members.

Please complete your assigned reviews within two weeks of receiving your review notification. If you have questions or encounter any issues during the review, please submit a ticket to the IT Help Desk.
