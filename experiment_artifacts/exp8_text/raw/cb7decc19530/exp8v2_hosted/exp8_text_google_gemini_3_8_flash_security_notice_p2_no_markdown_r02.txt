SECURITY NOTICE: WATCH OUT FOR PHISHING ATTEMPTS

All Staff,

Please remain vigilant regarding suspicious emails targeting our organization. Phishing attacks are becoming increasingly sophisticated, often appearing to come from trusted colleagues, management, or common software vendors.

Key warning signs include:
• Urgent requests for wire transfers, gift cards, or sensitive data
• Sender email addresses that look slightly misspelled or unfamiliar
• Unexpected attachments or links asking you to log in
• Poor grammar, odd greetings, or generic signatures

If you receive a suspicious email:
1. Do not click any links or download attachments.
2. Do not reply or forward the email to other team members.
3. Report the message immediately using the Report Phishing button in Outlook or forward it to security@company.com.

When in doubt, always verify the request through a separate, known communication channel before taking action.

Thank you for helping keep our workplace secure.

IT Security Team
