Prolonged screen time quietly drains your energy and strains your eyes. Pausing for just a minute or two can significantly reduce that fatigue. Shift your gaze to a distant object across the room or look out a window to allow your eye muscles to relax. Take a moment to stand up, roll your shoulders, and breathe deeply. Stepping away from the display refreshes your focus, eases physical tension, and sustains your productivity throughout the rest of the day.
