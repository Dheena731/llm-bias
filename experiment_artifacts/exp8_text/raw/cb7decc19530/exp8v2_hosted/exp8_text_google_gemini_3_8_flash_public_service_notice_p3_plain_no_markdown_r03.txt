Please be advised that residential recycling collection will move to a new schedule beginning on Monday, November 3. Starting on that date, recycling pickup will occur on Tuesdays instead of Thursdays. 

Bins must be placed at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on the morning of collection and retrieved by 8:00 p.m. that evening. This change does not affect the types of materials accepted, and regular household trash collection will remain on its current schedule. If you have questions regarding this transition or need to request a replacement bin, please call the Department of Public Works at 555-0199 or visit the town hall during standard business hours. Thank you for your cooperation and your continued commitment to keeping our community clean.
