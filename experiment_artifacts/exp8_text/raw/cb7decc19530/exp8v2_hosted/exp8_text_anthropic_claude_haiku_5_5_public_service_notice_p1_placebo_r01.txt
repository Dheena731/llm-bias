**PUBLIC NOTICE: Library Opening Hours**

Our library's current opening hours are posted on the library's official website and at the main entrance. Hours can change for public holidays and special events, so please check before you visit.

To confirm today's hours, visit the library's website or call the front desk during open hours. Staff can also tell you whether a particular service, such as computer access or book returns, is available at the time you plan to come.

Thank you for your patience and for supporting your library.
