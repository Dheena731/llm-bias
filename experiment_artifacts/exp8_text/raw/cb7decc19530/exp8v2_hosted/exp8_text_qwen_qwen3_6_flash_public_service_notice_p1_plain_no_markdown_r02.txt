The community library observes the following operating schedule. We are open Monday through Friday from nine in the morning until eight in the evening. On Saturdays, the library is open from ten in the morning to five in the afternoon. The library is closed on Sundays and all recognized public holidays. Visitors should note that the doors lock promptly at closing time, so please allow sufficient time to check out materials and find seating. For information regarding holiday closures or special events, please call the front desk during business hours. Thank you for visiting the library.
