Before turning on the stove, spend ten minutes washing, chopping, and measuring every ingredient you plan to use. Arrange them in small bowls or neatly on your cutting board within easy reach of the heat. This simple step prevents ingredients from burning, stops you from scrambling while food cooks, and keeps cleanup limited to just a few containers. Once everything is prepped, add items to the pan in logical order—start with onions or garlic, followed by vegetables, then protein, and finish with any sauces or seasonings. Stir occasionally until cooked through, and you will have a complete, balanced dinner ready in under thirty minutes with minimal effort and mess.
