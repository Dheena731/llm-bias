A quick reminder to drink enough water today. Even mild dehydration can lead to headaches, fatigue, trouble concentrating, and a dip in mood, and it often shows up before you feel truly thirsty. Try to sip steadily rather than waiting until you are parched or drinking a large amount all at once.

A simple routine helps. Start your morning with a glass of water before coffee or breakfast, keep a bottle within arm's reach while you work, and have another glass with each meal. If plain water feels dull, add a slice of lemon or cucumber, or try sparkling water or unsweetened tea. You will need more if you are exercising, spending time in the heat, or feeling unwell.

A good check is the color of your urine: pale yellow usually means you are well hydrated, while darker yellow is a sign to drink more. Most people do well with roughly six to eight glasses a day, though your needs may vary. Pour a glass now, and keep it going through the afternoon and evening.
