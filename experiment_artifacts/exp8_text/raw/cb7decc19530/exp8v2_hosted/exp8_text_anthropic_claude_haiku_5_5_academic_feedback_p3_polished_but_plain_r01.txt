The manuscript's central research question, as stated in the introduction, is whether community engagement improves health outcomes in rural areas. While the topic is important and well suited to the journal, the question in its current form is too broad to guide the study design or to be answered by the data presented. "Community engagement" is used to describe at least three different things across the paper: attendance at town meetings, participation in a locally led health program, and survey-based measures of perceived trust in local institutions. The authors should specify which construct they are examining and define it operationally. Similarly, "health outcomes" is not tied to any particular measure, and the analysis reports changes in self-rated health, hospital admissions, and vaccination rates without explaining how these were chosen or how they relate to the stated question. I would encourage the authors to narrow the question to a testable relationship, such as whether participation in a specified program is associated with a defined outcome over a stated time frame, and to state the expected direction and mechanism of that association. A revised question of this kind would also clarify which comparison groups are needed and whether the current design, which relies on cross-sectional survey data, can support causal language. Once the question is sharpened, the discussion and conclusions should be revisited to ensure that claims match what the question and the evidence can support.
