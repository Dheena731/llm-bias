This is an administrative notification regarding the launch of our quarterly access review cycle, which begins today and runs through the end of the month. In accordance with our security and compliance policies, all team managers and system owners are required to audit user permissions across their respective platforms and services. Please log in to the central identity management portal to review the active access rights of your direct reports and system users, ensuring that each individual holds only the minimum permissions necessary for their current role. Promptly approve valid permissions and flag any accounts that require adjustment or removal due to role changes or departures. Completing these reviews on time is essential for maintaining our security baseline and meeting audit standards, and any uncertified permissions may be subject to automatic suspension after the deadline. If you have questions about the review process or require assistance, please contact the security administration team.
