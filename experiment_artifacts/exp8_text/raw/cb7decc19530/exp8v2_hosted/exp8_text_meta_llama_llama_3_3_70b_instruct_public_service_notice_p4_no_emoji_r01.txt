Public Notice: Heat Advisory

A heat advisory has been issued for our area due to extremely high temperatures and humidity levels. Residents are advised to take precautions to stay cool and safe.

Stay indoors during the hottest part of the day, typically between 11am and 3pm, and stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water. Avoid strenuous activities and check on vulnerable neighbors, such as the elderly and young children.

Cooling centers are available at local community centers and libraries for those who need to escape the heat. For more information, please contact our public health department.
