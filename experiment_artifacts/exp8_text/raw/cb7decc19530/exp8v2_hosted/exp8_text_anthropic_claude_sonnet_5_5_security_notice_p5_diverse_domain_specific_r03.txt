**Subject: Lost or stolen laptop: what to do in the first hour**

If you can't find your company laptop, report it right away. Don't spend the evening retracing your steps first. A laptop that turns up in the back of a taxi the next morning is still worth reporting, because we'd rather close the ticket than leave a session token live for 12 hours.

**Report it as soon as you realize it's missing, and no later than one hour after.**

- **Service desk:** ext. 4400 or helpdesk@company.example (staffed 24/7)
- **Out of the country or on a phone with no email:** call the security hotline at +00 000 000 0000

**Have these ready, but don't delay the call if you don't:**

1. Asset tag number (the silver sticker under the keyboard) or your device name
2. Where and when you last had it
3. Whether it was locked, asleep, or shut down
4. Whether it held anything beyond your normal access, such as local exports of customer data, spreadsheets with personal information, or saved credentials for systems outside SSO

**What we'll do:**

- Revoke your SSO sessions and VPN certificate
- Lock the device through MDM and, if needed, send a remote wipe. The wipe will run the next time it connects.
- Confirm the disk was encrypted (BitLocker or FileVault) and check the recovery key escrow
- Reset your passwords and any shared credentials the device may have held

**What we ask you not to do:**

- Don't try to wipe or reset the laptop yourself
- Don't log in from another device to "check if it still works." That can muddy the logs we use to see whether someone else has tried to access it.
- Don't post about it in a team channel before reporting it

**If you think it was stolen,** file a police report and give us the report number. Insurance and, in some cases, regulatory notification depend on it.

Reporting promptly is never held against you. Reporting late is the only thing that causes problems. A replacement laptop is normally ready within one business day.
