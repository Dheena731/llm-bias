# Acceptable Use Notice for the Shared Network

**Please read this notice before using the Network. By connecting to or using the Network, you agree to follow it.**

**1. Definitions**
- "Network" means the shared computer network and internet connection you are using, including its wired and wireless access points.
- "Operator" means the person or organization that provides and manages the Network.
- "User" means anyone who connects to or uses the Network.

**2. Permitted Use**
Users may use the Network only for lawful purposes and in a way that does not interfere with other Users' reasonable use of it.

**3. Prohibited Use**
Users must not:
1. Break any applicable law or regulation, or help anyone else do so.
2. Access any device, account, or data without authorization.
3. Introduce or spread malicious software, meaning software designed to damage, disrupt, or gain unauthorized access to a device or system.
4. Overload or disrupt the Network, for example through excessive bandwidth use that degrades service for others.
5. Infringe the intellectual property rights of others, including by sharing copyrighted material without permission.
6. Send harassing, threatening, fraudulent, or deceptive communications.
7. Share your access credentials, or use another person's credentials without their permission.
8. Attempt to bypass or disable any security or access control on the Network.

**4. Monitoring**
To the extent the law allows, the Operator may monitor Network activity to protect the Network, enforce this notice, and comply with legal obligations. Users should not expect their activity on the Network to be private.

**5. Enforcement**
If a User violates this notice, the Operator may, without prior warning, restrict, suspend, or end that User's access. The Operator may also report unlawful activity to the appropriate authorities.

**6. No Guarantee of Service**
The Network is provided "as is." The Operator does not guarantee that it will be uninterrupted, error-free, or secure. Users are responsible for protecting their own devices and data.

**7. Responsibility for Conduct**
Each User is responsible for their own activity on the Network and for any consequences arising from it.

**8. Changes**
The Operator may update this notice at any time. Continued use of the Network after an update means you accept the updated notice.
