Thank you for submitting your manuscript. I read the introduction several times, and I am still not sure what question the study is trying to answer. The opening paragraphs describe the growing interest in community resilience and the many factors that may influence it, but the authors never narrow this into a specific, testable question. The stated aim, to "better understand how communities respond to environmental stress," is broad enough to cover nearly any empirical study in the field, and it leaves the reader without a clear standard for judging whether the study succeeds.

The problem carries into the methods. The survey includes items on social trust, infrastructure access, and past disaster experience, yet the manuscript does not say which of these is the main variable of interest or what relationship the authors expect to find between them. As a result, the analysis reads as a set of separate correlations rather than a response to a single line of inquiry. I would ask the authors to state one primary research question, specify the outcome and the key predictor, and explain why the relationship matters for theory or practice.

I would also encourage the authors to clarify how their question differs from prior work on the same topic. The literature review cites several studies that address similar relationships, but it does not identify the gap this study fills. A sharper question would make that gap visible and would help readers see what the findings contribute.

I believe the dataset is valuable and the study could make a worthwhile contribution once the research question is defined. I recommend major revisions focused on the framing of the question before the manuscript is reconsidered.
