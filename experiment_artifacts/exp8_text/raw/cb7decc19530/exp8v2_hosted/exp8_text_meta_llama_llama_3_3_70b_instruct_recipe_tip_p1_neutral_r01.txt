Here's a short cooking tip for preparing a weeknight dinner:

**Tip: Prep Ahead with a "Mise en Place"**

To make weeknight dinners a breeze, try prepping a "mise en place" (a French term for "everything in its place") before you start cooking. Chop all your vegetables, marinate your proteins, and measure out spices and seasonings in advance. This simple step can save you up to 30 minutes of cooking time and reduce stress in the kitchen. Simply store your prepped ingredients in airtight containers in the fridge until you're ready to cook, and then assemble your meal quickly and easily. Try it tonight and enjoy a faster, more relaxed dinner routine!
