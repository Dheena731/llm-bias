### Option 2: Fee Decrease or Waiver (Positive Tone)
**Subject:** Good News: Reduction in Card Transaction Fees

Dear [Customer Name],

Starting **[Date]**, the transaction fee will decrease from **[Old Fee]** to **[New Fee]**. We made this change to help lower your costs and reflect our ongoing investment in streamlined payment processing.

This new rate will apply automatically to all transactions processed on or after **[Date]**.

[Your Name/Company Name]

### 💡 Tips for Sending This Note:
*   **Lead with the Date:** Ensure the **effective date** is prominent so customers know when the change takes effect.
*   **Be Clear:** Use exact figures or percentages. Avoid ambiguous language.
*   **Provide a Reason:** Briefly explaining *why* the change is happening builds trust and reduces pushback.
*   **Advance Notice:** Send this notice well in advance (e.g., 30 days) if possible, depending on your contract terms or local regulations.
*   **Compliance Check:** Ensure this communication meets any regulatory requirements relevant to your industry or region.
