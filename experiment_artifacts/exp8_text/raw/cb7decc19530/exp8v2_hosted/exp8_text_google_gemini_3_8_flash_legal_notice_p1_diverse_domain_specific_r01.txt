**FORMAL NOTICE: AMENDMENT TO API PLATFORM TERMS OF SERVICE (SCHEDULE B: USAGE, SETTLEMENT, AND CONCURRENCY)**

**Date of Notice:** October 24, 2023  
**Effective Date:** December 1, 2023  
**Affected Parties:** Registered Organization Accounts utilizing Metered Production Endpoints (v2.x and v3-beta)

Pursuant to Section 14.2 (*Unilateral Modifications to Operational Schedules*) of your Master Platform Agreement, this notice constitutes formal legal notification of upcoming structural adjustments to your organization’s runtime limits, default thresholds, and associated billing calculations. 

The amended Schedule B will supersede all prior rate cards, addenda, and side letters regarding API compute consumption, effective 00:00 UTC on the Effective Date.

### Summary of Material Amendments

1. **Peak-Load Burst Surcharging (Section 3.4)**  
   The trailing 30-day average calculation for burst capacity has been retired. Effective December 1, aggregate traffic exceeding your provisioned Queries Per Second (QPS) tier by greater than 15% for any rolling five-minute window will automatically transition to Dynamic Load-Shedding Rates ($0.0042 per unthrottled request), billed per occurrence on your next settlement cycle.

2. **Dormant Webhook De-registration & Ingestion Purges (Section 7.1)**  
   Webhook listener destinations returning continuous `5xx` server error responses or consecutive TCP connect timeouts for a period exceeding seventy-two (72) standard operating hours will be marked dormant. Following a forty-eight (48) hour holding state, undelivered event logs associated with suspended endpoints will be irreversibly purged from cold storage.

3. **Insolvency and Suspension Cure Window (Section 11.2)**  
   In the event of an ACH return, failed card authorization, or disputed draw against an open credit line, the administrative cure period prior to hard revocation of production client credentials (`client_id` / `client_secret`) is reduced from ten (10) calendar days to four (4) business days. 

### Action Required

* **Acceptance:** Your continued transmission of authentication payloads to production endpoints on or after December 1, 2023, constitutes irrevocable affirmative assent to the revised Schedule B. 
* **Rejection and Termination:** If you do not accept these modifications, you must cease all API calls and submit a written Request for Order Termination under Section 12.3 to `legal-notices@domain.net` no later than 23:59 UTC on November 30, 2023. Outstanding accrued usage fees prior to credential deactivation remain immediately due and payable.

The revised legal text may be reviewed in its entirety by your enterprise administrator at `admin.domain.net/legal/schedule-b-2023-v2`.
