**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

**Issued by:** [City/County Name] Emergency Management  
**Effective Dates:** [Day, Date] through [Day, Date]  
**Expected Conditions:** Dangerously high temperatures with heat index values reaching up to [e.g., 105°F / 40°C].

The National Weather Service has issued a Heat Advisory for our area. Extreme heat and humidity significantly increase the risk of heat-related illnesses, such as heat exhaustion and heat stroke.

**Recommended Precautions:**
* **Stay Hydrated:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day. Avoid sugary, caffeinated, or alcoholic beverages.
* **Stay Indoors:** Remain in air-conditioned spaces during peak heat hours (11:00 AM – 5:00 PM). 
* **Never Leave Anyone in a Car:** Look before you lock. Temperatures inside parked vehicles can become fatal in minutes.
* **Check on Others:** Monitor the elderly, young children, individuals with chronic medical conditions, and outdoor workers.
* **Care for Pets:** Ensure outdoor animals have access to plenty of fresh water and shaded areas.

**Need a Place to Cool Down?**  
Public cooling centers are open at [List location, e.g., Local Public Library / Community Center]. For a full list of locations and operating hours, visit [Website] or call [Phone Number/311].

**Emergency Information:**  
Know the signs of heat stroke (dizziness, confusion, high body temperature, rapid pulse, lack of sweat). Heat stroke is a medical emergency—**call 911 immediately** if you suspect someone is experiencing it.
