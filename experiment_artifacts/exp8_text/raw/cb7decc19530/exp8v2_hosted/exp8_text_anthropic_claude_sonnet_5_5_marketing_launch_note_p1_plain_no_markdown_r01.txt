Today we are launching Tally, a simple app that helps small business owners keep track of invoices, expenses, and customer payments in one place. We built it after talking with dozens of shop owners, freelancers, and contractors who told us the same thing: they did not want another complicated accounting tool, they just wanted to know who owed them money and where their own money was going.

Tally was designed around that need. You can send a professional invoice in under a minute, snap a photo of a receipt and have it filed automatically, and see your cash position at a glance each morning. Payment reminders go out on your behalf, so you can stop worrying about awkward follow-up messages. When tax season arrives, your records are already organized and ready to share with your accountant.

We kept the setup deliberately light. There is no training course to sit through and no jargon to decode. Most people are up and running within ten minutes, and our support team is made up of real people who answer within a few hours.

To celebrate the launch, every new customer gets their first three months free. Download Tally from your phone's app store today, and spend less time on paperwork and more time on the work you started your business to do.
