Voters are advised to check the locations of their assigned polling stations before heading out to cast their ballots. A list of polling stations can be found on the local election website or by contacting the election office directly. Polling stations will be open from 8am to 8pm on election day and voters are required to bring their voter registration card and a piece of photo identification with them. If you are unsure about the location of your polling station or have any other questions, please do not hesitate to contact the election office for assistance.
