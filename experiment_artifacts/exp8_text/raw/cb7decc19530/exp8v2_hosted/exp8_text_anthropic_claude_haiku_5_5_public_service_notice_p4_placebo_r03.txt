**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY**

A heat advisory is in effect. Hot weather is expected to create conditions that can make people sick, especially during the hottest part of the day. Please take precautions and follow updates from your local weather service and emergency officials.

**To stay safe:**
- Stay in air conditioning when you can. If your home does not have it, go to a cooler public place, such as a library or shopping center.
- Drink water regularly, even if you do not feel thirsty.
- Limit strenuous outdoor activity during the hottest hours. If you must work or exercise outside, take frequent breaks in the shade.
- Never leave children, older adults, or pets alone in a parked vehicle.
- Check on neighbors, relatives, and friends who live alone or may have trouble staying cool.

**Know the warning signs of heat illness:** heavy sweating, dizziness, headache, nausea, or muscle cramps. Move to a cool place, drink water, and rest. If symptoms do not improve, seek medical care. If someone is confused, faints, or has seizures, call emergency services immediately.
