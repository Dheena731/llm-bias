**Enable Two-Factor Authentication to Protect Your Account**

To add an extra layer of security to your account, we recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA). This feature requires both a password and a verification code from a mobile device or authenticator app to access your account.

To enable 2FA:

1. Log in to your account and navigate to the account settings.
2. Look for the 2FA settings and follow the prompts to set up the feature.
3. You will receive a verification code via SMS or an authenticator app, which you will need to enter to complete the setup process.

Enabling 2FA provides an additional level of protection against unauthorized access to your account, even if your password is compromised. We strongly recommend taking this step to safeguard your account.
