Dear valued customer,

We are writing to let you know that, beginning on the first day of next month, we will be adjusting the fee we apply to card transactions. The standard rate for payments made by debit and credit card will increase from 2.1 percent to 2.4 percent of the transaction amount. Payments made by bank transfer and cash are not affected.

This change reflects higher costs that our payment processing provider has passed on to us over the past year. We have kept the increase as modest as we could, and we have not changed our prices for goods or services.

If you have a recurring payment set up by card, no action is required. The new rate will apply automatically to charges processed on or after the effective date. If you would prefer to avoid the card fee, you may pay by bank transfer, and our billing details are available on request.

If you have any questions about this change, please contact our customer team by phone or email, and we will be glad to help.

Thank you for your continued support.

Kind regards,
The Billing Team
