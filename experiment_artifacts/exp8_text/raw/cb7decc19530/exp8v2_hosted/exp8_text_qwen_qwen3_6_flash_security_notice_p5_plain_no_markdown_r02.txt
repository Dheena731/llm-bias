If you have misplaced or been unable to locate a company issued laptop, please notify the information technology department immediately. Send a message to it.support@company.com or call extension 5500. Your report must include your full name, department, the laptop asset tag or serial number, and the approximate time and place where the device was last used. Immediate notification allows our security team to remotely lock the system and protect sensitive company data. Employees must comply with this procedure to maintain network safety and adhere to corporate policy. Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter.
