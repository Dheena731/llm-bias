This notice reminds all employees to maintain high vigilance when handling email communications due to the prevalence of phishing threats. Phishing involves fraudulent messages crafted to deceive recipients into disclosing confidential information, granting unauthorized access, or compromising system security. Every team member should examine incoming emails carefully before responding or taking action. Scrutinize the sender's address to verify its authenticity, noting that attackers frequently spoof addresses to resemble trusted contacts or official domains. Exercise extreme caution with messages that request passwords, financial details, or urgent payments, especially if the tone seems unusual or creates a false sense of urgency. Refrain from clicking links or opening attachments from unknown or suspicious sources. If an email raises doubts, do not engage with it directly. Forward the message to the IT department for investigation and advice. Reporting potential phishing attempts promptly helps safeguard our network and protects everyone from malicious activity.
