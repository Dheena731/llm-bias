**Important Security Reminder**

As a reminder to all staff, phishing emails are a common threat to our organization's security. These emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information or clicking on malicious links.

**What to look out for:**

* Emails that ask for your login credentials, credit card numbers, or other sensitive information
* Links or attachments that you weren't expecting
* Emails that are addressed to a general group, rather than a specific individual
* Emails that contain spelling or grammar mistakes

**What to do:**

* Never respond to emails that ask for sensitive information
* Never click on links or open attachments from unknown senders
* Report any suspicious emails to the IT department immediately
* Always verify the sender's email address to ensure it is legitimate

**Your role in keeping our organization safe:**

We rely on your vigilance to help prevent phishing attacks. If you're unsure about an email, it's always better to err on the side of caution and report it to the IT department.

**Let's work together to keep our organization safe!**

If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the IT department.
