**Subject: Introducing your monthly spending summary**

We've added a monthly spending summary that shows how your money was spent over the previous month, all in one place.

Each summary includes:

- Your total spending for the month
- Your spending broken down by category
- How this month's spending compares with the month before

Many people have to piece together their spending from separate transactions to see the full picture. The summary does that work for you, so you can see where your money went without reviewing each transaction.

The summary is a view of spending that has already happened. It does not change your transactions or your account.
