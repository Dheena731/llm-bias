Introducing Smart Folders: your files, organized automatically.

Starting today, our app can sort your documents, images, and notes into folders based on content, date, and project. No more manual dragging and dropping, and no more hunting for that one file you saved last month.

Smart Folders is available now for all users. Update to the latest version to try it out, and tell us what you think in the comments.
