**Public Service Notice: Library Opening Hours**

The Riverside Public Library wants to remind residents of our current opening hours:

- **Monday through Friday:** 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
- **Saturday:** 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- **Sunday:** 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m.

The library is closed on all major public holidays. Please check our website or call (555) 123-4567 for holiday schedules and any temporary changes.

Library cards are free for all residents, and our staff are available to help you borrow books, use computers, access digital resources, and find information. We look forward to welcoming you.
