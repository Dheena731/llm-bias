**Introducing LedgerLite: Business Finances, Simplified**

We're excited to announce the launch of LedgerLite, a mobile app built for small business owners who want to spend less time on bookkeeping and more time running their companies.

**What it does:**
- **Track every transaction** by snapping a photo of receipts or linking your bank account.
- **Send professional invoices** in under a minute and get paid faster with built-in reminders.
- **See your cash flow at a glance** with clear, real-time dashboards.
- **Prepare for tax season** with categorized expenses ready for your accountant.

**Why we built it:**
Most small business owners wear every hat in the company, and finances often fall to the bottom of the list. LedgerLite was designed to make money management feel manageable, with no accounting degree required.

**Available now:**
LedgerLite is free to download on iOS and Android, with a 30-day trial of our Pro plan that includes unlimited invoicing and multi-user access for your team.

**Get started today** at [ledgerlite.app](https://ledgerlite.app) and take the first step toward financial clarity for your business.
