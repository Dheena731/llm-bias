**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY**

**To:** Customers who have purchased goods or services from us

**1. Definitions**
- "Order Confirmation" means the confirmation we sent you when your purchase was completed.
- "Refund Period" means the period for requesting a refund that is stated in your Order Confirmation.

**2. Eligibility**
You are eligible for a refund only if all of the following apply:
(a) You purchased directly from us.
(b) You submitted your refund request in writing within the Refund Period.
(c) Your Order Confirmation does not describe the item or service as non-refundable.

**3. Ineligibility**
If any condition in Section 2 is not met, we are not obligated to issue a refund, except as required by applicable law.

**4. How to Request a Refund**
Send a written request using the contact details in your Order Confirmation. Include your order reference and the reason for your request.

**5. Decision and Payment**
We will review each request and tell you our decision in writing. If approved, we will refund the amount you paid for the affected item or service to your original payment method.

**6. Your Legal Rights**
This notice does not limit any rights you have under applicable consumer protection law. If this notice conflicts with those rights, the law prevails.
