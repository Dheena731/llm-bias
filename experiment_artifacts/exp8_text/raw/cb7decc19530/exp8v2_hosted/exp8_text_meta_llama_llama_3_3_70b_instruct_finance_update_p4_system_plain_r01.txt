Your end-of-year tax statement is a document that summarizes your income and tax deductions for the previous year. It is typically provided by your employer or financial institution and is used to help you prepare your tax return. The statement will show the total amount of money you earned, as well as any taxes that were withheld from your pay. It may also include information about other income, such as interest or dividends, and any deductions you are eligible for, such as charitable donations or mortgage interest. You will need this statement to accurately complete your tax return and claim any refunds you are owed.
