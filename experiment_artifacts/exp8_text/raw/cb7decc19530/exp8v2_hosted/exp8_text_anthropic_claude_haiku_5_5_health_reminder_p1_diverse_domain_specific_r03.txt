**Sunday check-in (about 4 minutes)**

Open your log and look at three things:

1. **Sessions completed vs. planned.** Missed sessions are data, not failure. Note why.
2. **Your 7-day bodyweight average**, not yesterday's number. Daily swings of a kilo or two are mostly water and salt.
3. **Any lift that hasn't moved in three weeks.** That usually means the plan needs a change, such as more rest, a different rep range, or a lighter week, not just more effort.

Then choose one thing to change next week. Just one.
