If you lose or misplace your company-issued laptop, you must report the incident immediately to the information technology department and corporate security. Prompt reporting is critical to ensure that our team can remotely lock the device, protect sensitive company data, and prevent unauthorized network access. When submitting your report, please provide the approximate time and location where the laptop was last seen, a brief description of the circumstances, and any relevant police report numbers if theft is suspected. Do not delay reporting the incident while you attempt to locate the device on your own. If the loss occurs outside of standard business hours, please contact the after-hours emergency technical support line immediately.
