Reminder: Locking Computer Screens

As a reminder to all staff, please do not leave your computer screens unattended without locking them. This is to prevent unauthorized access to sensitive information and to maintain the security of our systems.

To lock your screen, simply press the Windows key plus the letter L, or click on the lock icon in the bottom right corner of the screen. This will secure your computer and protect your work.

If you have any questions or concerns about locking your screen, please contact the IT department. We are here to help you with any technical issues you may have.
