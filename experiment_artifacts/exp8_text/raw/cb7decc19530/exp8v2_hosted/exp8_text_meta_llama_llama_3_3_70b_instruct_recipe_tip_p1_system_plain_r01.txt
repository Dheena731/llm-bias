To make a quick and easy weeknight dinner, consider preparing a few ingredients in advance. For example, you can chop vegetables such as onions, carrots, and bell peppers on the weekend or the night before, and store them in airtight containers in the refrigerator. This way, when you come home from work, you can simply saute the pre-chopped vegetables with some olive oil and your choice of protein, such as chicken or tofu, and serve over rice or noodles for a fast and flavorful meal.
