**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY**

This notice applies to all customers who purchase products or services from us ("we," "us," or "our"). It explains when a refund may be requested.

**1. Eligibility**

You may request a refund for a purchase if all of the following are true:

a. You submit your request within the refund period stated on your receipt or order confirmation.

b. The product is unused and in its original condition, or the service has not been substantially used. Products must be returned with their original packaging where packaging was provided.

c. You can show proof of purchase, such as a receipt or order confirmation.

**2. Items Not Eligible for Refund**

A refund is not available for:

a. Items identified as non-refundable at the time of purchase.

b. Custom or personalized orders made to your specifications.

c. Digital content that has been downloaded or accessed, unless it is defective.

**3. Defective Products**

If a product you received is defective or does not match its description, contact us. Eligibility under Section 1 does not apply in that case, and we will review your request on its own terms.

**4. How to Request a Refund**

Send your refund request using the contact details on your receipt or order confirmation. Include your proof of purchase and a brief description of the reason for your request.

**5. Method of Refund**

Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method used for the purchase.

**6. Other Rights**

This notice does not limit any rights you may have under applicable law.
