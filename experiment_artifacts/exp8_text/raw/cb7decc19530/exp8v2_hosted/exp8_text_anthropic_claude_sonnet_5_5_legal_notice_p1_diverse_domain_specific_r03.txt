**NOTICE OF CHANGE IN TERMS**
**Harbor Street Federal Credit Union**
Member Share and Draft Account Agreement (Form SD-12, rev. 06/2022)

**Date of notice:** March 3, 2025
**Effective date:** April 7, 2025

This notice amends your Account Agreement. Please keep it with your account records.

**1. Inactive accounts (Section 4(c))**
An account is "inactive" after 18 months without a member-initiated transaction. The prior period was 24 months. Credits of dividends and fee debits do not count as member-initiated transactions. The monthly inactive-account fee rises from $3.00 to $5.00. The fee will not exceed the account balance and will not apply to accounts held by members under 18.

**2. Returned items (Section 7(b))**
The fee for each item returned unpaid for insufficient funds rises from $28.00 to $30.00. We will charge no more than three such fees per account per business day.

**3. Mobile deposit holds (Section 9(d))**
If your mobile check deposits on a single business day exceed the large-deposit threshold in Regulation CC, we may hold the excess for up to five additional business days. We will send you a hold notice stating the amount held and the date the funds will be available.

**4. No change**
Dividend rates, the minimum share balance ($5.00), and the Section 12 provisions on dispute resolution and electronic transfers remain as stated in your Agreement and Truth in Savings disclosure.
