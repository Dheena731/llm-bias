Pursuant to standard operating terms, customers may submit a request for a refund within thirty calendar days of the original transaction date, provided that the merchandise is returned in its original, undamaged, and unused condition, complete with all packaging and accompanying materials. To establish eligibility, the claimant must furnish verifiable proof of purchase, such as an original invoice, sales receipt, or electronic order confirmation. Certain categories of goods and services, including customized merchandise, downloadable digital content, perishable items, and products explicitly designated as final sale, are strictly excluded from refund consideration, except where required by applicable statutory law or in the event of a verified manufacturing defect. Following the receipt and formal inspection of the returned property, approved refunds will be credited exclusively to the original payment method within ten to fourteen business days. Any associated shipping fees, handling charges, and processing costs are non-refundable and remain the sole financial responsibility of the purchaser, unless the return is directly necessitated by an error on the part of the seller. The issuing entity reserves the right to reject any claim that does not fully satisfy these established conditions.
