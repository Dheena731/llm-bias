**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY**

**1. Purpose**
This notice explains when a customer may receive a refund for a purchase made from the seller. In this notice, "Seller" means the business that sold the purchase, and "Order Confirmation" means the document the Seller issued to confirm that purchase.

**2. Eligibility**
A customer is eligible for a refund only if all of the following are true:

(a) the customer submits a refund request before the refund period stated in the Order Confirmation ends;
(b) the purchase is not excluded under Section 3; and
(c) where the Order Confirmation requires the item to be returned, the customer returns it.

**3. Exclusions**
No refund is available for a purchase that the Order Confirmation identifies as non-refundable or "final sale." A refund is also not available where the customer has already received a refund for the same purchase.

**4. How to Request a Refund**
To request a refund, the customer must send a written request to the Seller's customer support contact listed in the Order Confirmation. The request should include the order number shown on the Order Confirmation.

**5. Decision and Payment**
The Seller will notify the customer in writing whether the request is approved. If approved, the refund will be paid to the original payment method without undue delay.

**6. Statutory Rights**
Nothing in this notice limits or removes any refund right that a customer has under applicable law.
