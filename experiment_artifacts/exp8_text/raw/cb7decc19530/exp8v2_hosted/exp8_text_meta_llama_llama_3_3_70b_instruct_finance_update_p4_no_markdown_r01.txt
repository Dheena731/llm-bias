Your end-of-year tax statement is a document that summarizes your income and taxes paid throughout the year. It will typically include information such as your total earnings, taxes withheld, and any deductions or credits you are eligible for. This statement is usually provided by your employer or financial institution and is used to help you complete your tax return. It's an important document to review carefully to ensure everything is accurate and up-to-date, and to help you take advantage of any tax savings available to you.
