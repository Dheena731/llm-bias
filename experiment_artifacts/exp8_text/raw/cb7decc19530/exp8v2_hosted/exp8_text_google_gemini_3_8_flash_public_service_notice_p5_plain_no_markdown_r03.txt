Polling stations for the upcoming election will be open from seven in the morning until eight in the evening on election day. Every registered voter is assigned to a specific location based on their residential address. Because some locations may have changed since the last election, residents are strongly encouraged to confirm their designated site before heading out to cast their ballots. You can verify your polling place by reviewing the official voter information card recently sent to your home address, or by contacting the local municipal election office by telephone. If you require special accommodations or have questions regarding accessibility at your assigned site, please reach out to election staff ahead of time.
