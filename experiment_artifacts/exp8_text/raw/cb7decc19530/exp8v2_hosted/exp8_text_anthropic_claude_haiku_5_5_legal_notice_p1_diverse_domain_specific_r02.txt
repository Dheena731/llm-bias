**NOTICE OF CHANGES TO DEPOSIT ACCOUNT AGREEMENT**
Lakeshore Community Credit Union, Member Agreement Rev. 9
Mailed February 3, 2025

Dear Member:

This notice describes changes to your Deposit Account Agreement. Unless stated otherwise, the changes take effect **March 17, 2025**.

**1. Overdraft fee (Section 7.2).** The fee for each item paid into overdraft increases from $29.00 to $32.00. The daily maximum of four overdraft fees per account is unchanged. Items returned unpaid remain subject to the $25.00 returned-item fee in Section 7.4, which is not changing.

**2. Paper statement fee (Section 11.1).** Members who receive paper statements by mail will be charged $2.00 per monthly statement beginning with the April 2025 cycle. Members who enroll in electronic statements through online banking will not be charged. Accounts with a balance of $1,500 or more at any point during the prior statement cycle are exempt.

**3. Dispute resolution (Section 18).** We are replacing the arbitration provision with a 30-day informal resolution period followed by binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association under its Consumer Rules. Claims under $10,000 may instead be brought in small claims court. The class action waiver is unchanged.

**Your options.** If you do not agree to the change in Section 18, you may reject it by mailing or delivering a written notice to Member Services, P.O. Box 4410, Ardmore, OH 44108, postmarked no later than **March 17, 2025**. Include your name, account number, and the words "Opt out of Section 18." Rejecting Section 18 does not affect your account in any other way. If you do not reject it, the new arbitration terms will apply to disputes arising on or after March 17, 2025.

Changes to Sections 7 and 11 do not require action. Continued use of your account after March 17, 2025 constitutes acceptance of all changes in this notice.

The full revised agreement is available at lakeshorecu.example/terms or at any branch. For questions, call 800-555-0142 weekdays, 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Eastern.

Lakeshore Community Credit Union is federally insured by the NCUA.
