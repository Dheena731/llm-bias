Security Reminder: Please Lock Your Screen

This is a friendly reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your workstation, even if only for a moment. Unattended screens leave sensitive company and client data vulnerable to unauthorized access. 

Quick shortcuts to lock your screen:
- Windows: Press the Windows logo key + L
- Mac: Press Control + Command + Q

Thank you for doing your part to keep our workplace secure. 

IT Security Team
