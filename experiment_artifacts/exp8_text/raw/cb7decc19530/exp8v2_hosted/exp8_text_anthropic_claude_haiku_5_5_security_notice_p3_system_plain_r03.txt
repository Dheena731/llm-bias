Dear colleagues,

This is a friendly reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even for a short break. An unlocked screen can give anyone passing by access to confidential company information, client records, and your personal accounts. Locking your screen takes only a moment. On most Windows computers, press the Windows key and L together; on a Mac, press Control, Command, and Q together. Please make this a habit during meetings, lunch breaks, and any time you leave your workstation unattended.

Thank you for helping us keep our information secure. If you have any questions about locking your screen or need assistance setting up an automatic lock timeout, please contact the IT help desk.
