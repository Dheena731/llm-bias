Here's a short tip for seasoning roasted vegetables with a bit of character:

"When roasting vegetables, resist the urge to over-season with salt and pepper at the beginning. Instead, sprinkle a pinch of flaky sea salt over the vegetables during the last 10-15 minutes of cooking. The Maillard reaction, that magical chemical process that occurs when food meets heat, will caramelize the natural sugars in the vegetables and intensify their flavors. This way, you'll get a rich, depthful seasoning that's not just about salt and pepper, but about the inherent sweetness and complexity of the vegetables themselves."
