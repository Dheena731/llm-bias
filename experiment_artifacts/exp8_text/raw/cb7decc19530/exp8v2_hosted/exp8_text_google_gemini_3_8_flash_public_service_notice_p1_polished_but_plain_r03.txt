The public library welcomes community members to make use of its facilities, collections, and public services throughout the week. The main building is open Monday through Thursday from nine in the morning until eight in the evening. On Friday and Saturday, the library operates from nine in the morning until five in the afternoon. The facility is closed on Sundays and official public holidays. Visitors are invited to browse the collections, use public computers, reserve quiet study spaces, and consult with reference staff during these regular operating hours. Book drops located beside the main entrance remain open twenty-four hours a day for all material returns. Additionally, the library catalog and digital lending platforms remain accessible online at any time.
