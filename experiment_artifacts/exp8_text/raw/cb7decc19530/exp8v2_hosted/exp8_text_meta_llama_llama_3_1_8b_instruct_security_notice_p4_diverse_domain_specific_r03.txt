**Secure Your Account with Two-Factor Authentication**

As we continue to prioritize the security of our community, we're now enabling two-factor authentication (2FA) for all users. This means that in addition to your username and password, you'll need to enter a verification code sent to your registered phone or email address whenever you log in to your account.

**Why 2FA is important:**

*   Prevents unauthorized access to your account, even if your password is compromised
*   Adds an extra layer of security, making it much harder for hackers to gain access
*   Helps protect sensitive information and prevent financial loss

**How to enable 2FA:**

1.  Log in to your account and navigate to the "Security" or "Account Settings" section.
2.  Look for the "Two-Factor Authentication" or "2FA" option and click on it.
3.  Choose your preferred method of receiving verification codes (e.g., SMS, authenticator app, or email).
4.  Save your changes and test your new 2FA setup by logging out and back in to your account.

**What to expect:**

When you log in to your account, you'll be prompted to enter a verification code. This code will be sent to the phone number or email address associated with your account. If you're using an authenticator app, you'll be asked to enter a code generated by the app.

**If you have any issues or concerns:**

Don't hesitate to reach out to our support team for assistance. We're here to help and can guide you through the process.

By enabling two-factor authentication, you're taking a crucial step in protecting your account and keeping your information safe.
