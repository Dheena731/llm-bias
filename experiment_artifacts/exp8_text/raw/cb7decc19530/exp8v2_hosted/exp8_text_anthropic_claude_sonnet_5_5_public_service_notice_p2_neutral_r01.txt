# PUBLIC NOTICE

## Road Closure: Maple Avenue

**Effective:** Monday, June 9, 2025, 7:00 a.m., through Friday, June 13, 2025, 5:00 p.m.

**Location:** Maple Avenue between Oak Street and Fifth Street

**Reason:** Water main repair and road resurfacing

---

### What to Expect
- Maple Avenue will be **closed to all through traffic** during this period.
- Local residents will have access to their driveways, but on-street parking will not be permitted within the work zone.
- Emergency vehicles will be able to access the area at all times.

### Detour Route
Drivers should follow the posted signs along **Birch Street** and **Seventh Street** to bypass the closure. Please allow extra travel time.

### Pedestrians and Cyclists
Sidewalks will remain open where safe. Cyclists are advised to use the detour route or dismount when passing the work zone.

### Weather Delays
If rain or other conditions delay the work, the closure may be extended. Updates will be posted online and on roadside message boards.
