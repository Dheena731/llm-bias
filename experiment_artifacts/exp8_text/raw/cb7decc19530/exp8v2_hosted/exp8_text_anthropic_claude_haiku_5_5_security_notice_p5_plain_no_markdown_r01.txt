Lost or Stolen Company Laptop: Reporting Procedure

If you have lost a company laptop or believe it has been stolen, you must report it to the IT Service Desk as soon as possible, and no later than four hours after you notice it is missing. Prompt reporting allows the IT team to lock the device, revoke access to company systems, and reduce the risk to confidential information. To report the loss, contact the IT Service Desk by phone at extension 5000 or by email at itsupport@company.example, and provide your name, employee number, the laptop's asset tag, and the last place and time you remember having it. If the laptop was stolen, you must also file a police report and send a copy of the report reference number to the IT Service Desk. Facilities and Security should be notified if the loss occurred on company premises. Do not attempt to recover the device yourself or access any company system from an unknown location. Failure to report a lost device promptly may result in disciplinary action under company policy.
