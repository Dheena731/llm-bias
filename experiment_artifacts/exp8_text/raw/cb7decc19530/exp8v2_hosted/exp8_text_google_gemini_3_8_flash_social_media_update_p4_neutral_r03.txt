The easiest calendar hack to prevent afternoon burnout: 

**Change your default meeting length from 30/60 minutes to 25/50 minutes.** ⏱️

Most apps (like Google Calendar and Outlook) have a built-in setting for this called "Speedy Meetings." 

It automatically creates a 5–10 minute buffer between calls to:
• Stand up and stretch  
• Grab a glass of water  
• Jot down quick notes before the next call  

Give your brain room to breathe. 🧠✨
