Latency represents the delay between a user action and the system's response, serving as a critical determinant of performance in web applications. When delays accumulate, user satisfaction erodes quickly, as modern audiences expect near-instantaneous interactions across all devices. Research consistently demonstrates that even fractional second increases in load times correlate with measurable declines in engagement and conversion rates, directly impacting revenue and retention. Beyond immediate user perception, excessive latency strains infrastructure by holding connections open longer, which consumes more memory and CPU resources while increasing the risk of timeouts and cascading failures. Search engines also prioritize fast-loading sites, meaning poor latency can reduce visibility and organic traffic over time. Optimizing for low latency ensures a responsive interface that builds trust, supports scalable architecture, and meets the competitive standards of contemporary digital experiences.
