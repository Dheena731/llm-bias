We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you better track and manage your expenses. This summary will provide a clear and concise overview of your spending activity for the previous month, including total expenditures, category breakdowns, and any notable transactions. The summary will be sent to you via email on the 5th of each month, and will also be available for viewing online through your account dashboard. This new feature is designed to give you a more comprehensive understanding of your spending habits, and to help you make informed decisions about your financial resources. If you have any questions or need assistance with the new monthly spending summary, please don't hesitate to contact our customer support team.
