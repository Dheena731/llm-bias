REMINDER: LOCK YOUR SCREEN WHEN AWAY FROM YOUR DESK

Hi Team,

This is a quick reminder to always lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your workstation, even for just a minute. 

Leaving screens unlocked poses a security risk and can expose sensitive company and client data. 

You can quickly lock your screen using the following shortcuts:
Windows: Press Windows Key + L
Mac: Press Command + Control + Q

Thank you for doing your part to keep our workplace secure.

IT Security Team
