NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Effective Date: [Insert Date]

1. Purpose
This Notice sets forth the conditions under which a purchaser ("Customer") may be eligible for a refund of amounts paid to [Company Name] ("Company") for goods or services.

2. Eligibility Period
A refund request must be submitted in writing within thirty (30) calendar days of the date of purchase ("Eligibility Period"). Requests received after the Eligibility Period shall not be considered, except where required by applicable law.

3. Conditions for Eligibility
A Customer is eligible for a refund only if all of the following conditions are met:

(a) The Customer provides valid proof of purchase;
(b) The purchased goods are returned in their original condition and packaging, where applicable;
(c) The purchased services have not been substantially performed or consumed; and
(d) The purchase is not within a category listed in Section 4.

4. Non-Refundable Items
Unless otherwise required by law, the following are not eligible for refund: (a) gift cards and promotional credits; (b) custom or personalized orders; (c) digital products that have been downloaded or accessed; and (d) items identified at the time of sale as final sale.

5. Defective or Misdescribed Goods
Nothing in this Notice limits any statutory rights of the Customer concerning goods or services that are defective, not as described, or not fit for their intended purpose.

6. Refund Method and Timing
Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within ten (10) business days of approval. The Company may deduct reasonable shipping, handling, or restocking fees where permitted by law and disclosed at the time of sale.

7. Submission of Requests
Refund requests must be directed to [Contact Email or Address] and must include the Customer's name, order number, and a brief statement of the reason for the request.

8. Modification
The Company reserves the right to amend this Notice at any time. Amendments shall apply only to purchases made after the effective date of the revised Notice.

9. Governing Law
