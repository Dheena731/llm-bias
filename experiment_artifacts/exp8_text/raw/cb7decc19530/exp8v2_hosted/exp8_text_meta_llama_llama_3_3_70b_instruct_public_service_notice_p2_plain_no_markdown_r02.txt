A section of Main Street will be closed to traffic starting on Monday and lasting for approximately two weeks. The closure is necessary to allow for road repairs and resurfacing. During this time, traffic will be detoured around the affected area via Oak Street and Elm Street. Motorists are advised to plan their routes accordingly and allow for extra time to reach their destinations. Local residents and businesses will still have access to their properties, but may need to use alternative routes to get there. The road is expected to reopen on Friday of the following week, weather permitting.
