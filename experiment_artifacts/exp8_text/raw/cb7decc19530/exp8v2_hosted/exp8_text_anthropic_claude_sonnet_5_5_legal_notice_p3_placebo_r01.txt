# Cookie Notice

**1. What cookies are**
Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit. They allow the website to function, to remember your choices, and to understand how it is used.

**2. How we use cookies**
This website uses two types of cookies:

- **Essential cookies.** These are necessary for the website to work, for example to keep it secure and to remember your cookie choices. They are always active because the website cannot function properly without them.
- **Optional cookies.** These include cookies that remember your preferences and cookies that help us understand how visitors use the website so we can improve it. We set these only if you consent.

**3. Your consent and choices**
When you first visit, you may accept or decline optional cookies. You may change your decision at any time by using the cookie settings on this website or by deleting cookies in your browser. Declining optional cookies will not prevent you from using the website, although some features may not remember your preferences.

**4. Controlling cookies in your browser**
Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. If you block all cookies, including essential ones, parts of this website may not work correctly.

**5. Changes to this notice**
We may update this notice if our use of cookies changes. The version published on this website is the current one.

**6. Questions**
If you have questions about this notice or our use of cookies, please use the contact details provided on this website.
