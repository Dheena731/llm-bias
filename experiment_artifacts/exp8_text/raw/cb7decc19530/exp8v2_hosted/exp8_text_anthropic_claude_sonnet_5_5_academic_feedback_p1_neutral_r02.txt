**Overall impression**
This draft shows solid engagement with the field and a clear effort to cover relevant scholarship. At the moment it reads more as a summary of sources than as an argument. The next revision should move from *what has been said* to *what it means for your research question*.

**Strengths**
- The scope is broad, and the key sources appear to be included.
- The writing is generally clear and the tone appropriately academic.
- Some paragraphs, particularly those where you compare studies directly, show real analytical potential.

**Areas for development**

1. **Synthesis over summary.** Many paragraphs describe one study after another ("Smith argues… Jones found…"). Organize the review by theme, debate, or methodological approach instead, and show where sources agree, conflict, or build on one another.

2. **Critical evaluation.** Assess the quality and limits of the evidence, such as sample sizes, methods, context, and theoretical assumptions, rather than presenting findings as equally authoritative.

3. **Identification of the gap.** The review should lead the reader toward a clearly stated gap or tension in the literature. Make explicit how your project responds to it.

4. **Structure and signposting.** Add a brief introduction that sets out the review's logic, and use topic sentences and short concluding statements to link sections. A reader should always know why a section appears where it does.

5. **Currency and balance.** Check that recent work is represented and that you are not leaning too heavily on a few authors or a single perspective.

6. **Citation consistency.** Check that formatting follows your required style throughout, and that every in-text citation appears in the reference list.

**Suggested next steps**
- Create a simple matrix of sources against themes to reveal patterns and gaps.
- Rewrite one section as a model of synthesis, then apply the same approach to the others.
- End with a short section that explicitly connects the literature to your research question.

With these revisions, the review will move from a competent overview to a persuasive foundation for your study.
