Meet Tally: The Simple Way to Run Your Small Business

We are excited to announce the launch of Tally, a new app built to help small business owners spend less time on paperwork and more time on what matters most: their customers.

Tally brings your invoicing, expense tracking, and payment reminders into one clean, easy-to-use place. Send a professional invoice in under a minute, snap a photo of a receipt to log an expense, and let Tally nudge late-paying clients so you don't have to chase them yourself.

Whether you run a bakery, a design studio, a plumbing service, or a freelance consulting practice, Tally is designed to fit the way you already work. No accounting degree required.

Tally is available today on iPhone, Android, and the web. Start with a free 30-day trial, no credit card needed.

Try Tally today at tallyapp.example and see how much time you can get back in your week.
