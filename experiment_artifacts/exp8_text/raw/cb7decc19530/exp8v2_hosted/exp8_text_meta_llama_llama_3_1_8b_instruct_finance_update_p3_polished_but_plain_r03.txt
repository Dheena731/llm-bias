The savings goal feature also includes a visual representation of your progress, allowing you to see how close you are to reaching your target. This feature is designed to motivate and encourage you to stay on track, making it easier to achieve your financial goals.

In addition, the system will send you reminders and alerts to ensure you stay on schedule and make adjustments as needed. This feature is designed to be flexible, allowing you to make changes to your goal or adjust your plan as your circumstances change.
