**Cook your starch while you prep everything else.** Before you chop a single vegetable, get a pot of water boiling for pasta, rice, or potatoes. Then do your prep while the water heats and the starch cooks. This overlap can cut 10 to 15 minutes from your total time, and you won't be left waiting on the slowest part of the meal at the end.

A bonus tip: salt your pasta water generously, until it tastes like mild seawater. It's the best chance you get to season the pasta from the inside, and it makes a noticeable difference in the finished dish.
