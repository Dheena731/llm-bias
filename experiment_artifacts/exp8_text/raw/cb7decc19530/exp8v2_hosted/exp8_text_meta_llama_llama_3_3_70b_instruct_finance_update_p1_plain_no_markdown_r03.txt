We have introduced a new feature that provides a monthly spending summary to help you keep track of your expenses. This summary will be sent to you at the beginning of each month and will outline your total spending for the previous month, broken down into different categories. The categories will include rent, utilities, groceries, entertainment, and other expenses. This will give you a clear picture of where your money is going and help you identify areas where you can cut back and make adjustments to stay within your budget. The summary will also include some suggestions for reducing your spending and improving your financial management.
