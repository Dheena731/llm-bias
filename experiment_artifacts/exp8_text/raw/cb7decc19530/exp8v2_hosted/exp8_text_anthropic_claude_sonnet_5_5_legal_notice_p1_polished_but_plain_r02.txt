Notice of Changes to Account Terms

We are writing to inform you that we are amending the terms that govern your account. These changes apply to all account holders and will take effect on the date stated at the end of this notice, which is at least thirty days after the date this notice was sent to you.

The principal changes are as follows. First, we are revising the fee schedule to adjust certain service charges, including those for paper statements, expedited transfers, and returned payments. Second, we are updating the section on electronic communications so that, unless you have opted out, we may deliver account notices, disclosures, and amendments to the email address or secure message center associated with your account. Third, we are clarifying the circumstances in which we may suspend or restrict account access, including where we reasonably suspect unauthorized activity, a violation of the agreement, or a legal or regulatory requirement. Fourth, we are modifying the dispute resolution provision to describe more clearly the steps required before a formal claim may be filed. Complete revised terms, including the updated fee schedule, are available through your online account or upon written request.

You are not required to take any action to accept these changes. If you continue to use your account after the effective date, you will be treated as having agreed to the revised terms. If you do not agree, you may close your account before the effective date without penalty, provided that you pay any outstanding balance and fees incurred up to the time of closure. Closing the account will not affect obligations that arose before closure, nor will it affect any rights that, by their nature, continue after the agreement ends.

Where applicable law gives you additional rights regarding changes to your account, including a right to reject a change or receive further notice, those rights are not limited by this notice. If any provision of the revised terms is found to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions will continue in full effect. In the event of a conflict between this notice and the revised terms, the revised terms will control, except where law requires otherwise.

If you have questions or wish to opt out of electronic delivery, please contact our customer service department using the contact details printed on your most recent statement. We recommend that you keep a copy of this notice and the revised terms for your records.

Effective date: the date specified in the cover communication accompanying this notice.
