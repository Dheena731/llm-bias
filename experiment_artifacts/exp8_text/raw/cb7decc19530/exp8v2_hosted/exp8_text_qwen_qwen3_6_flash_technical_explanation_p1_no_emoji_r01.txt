Latency is the time delay between a user initiating an action and receiving a response from the application. Even minor delays matter because modern users expect near-instant feedback; when interactions feel sluggish, perceived performance drops, leading to frustration, higher bounce rates, and abandoned sessions. This directly impacts business outcomes: studies consistently show that slower load times correlate with lower conversion rates, reduced revenue, and diminished customer loyalty. Additionally, high latency can trigger timeout errors, amplify backend bottlenecks, and negatively affect search engine rankings, as page speed is a confirmed ranking signal. Ultimately, minimizing latency is essential for delivering reliable experiences, maintaining system stability, and staying competitive in an environment where speed is treated as a baseline requirement rather than a luxury.
