A quick reminder to drink enough water today. Even mild dehydration can leave you tired, foggy, and prone to headaches, and it is easy to overlook thirst when you are busy. Start your morning with a glass of water before coffee or breakfast, and keep a bottle within reach at your desk or in your bag so sipping becomes automatic. Tie drinking to routines you already have, such as having a glass with each meal and another between tasks or meetings. Most adults do well with roughly six to eight glasses a day, though you may need more if the weather is hot, you are exercising, or you are drinking a lot of caffeine. A pale yellow color in your urine is a good sign that you are on track. If plain water feels dull, add a slice of lemon, cucumber, or a few mint leaves. Take a few sips right now, and keep the habit going until bedtime.
