**Reviewer Comment:**
The manuscript addresses an interesting topic, but the central research question is currently unclear. Ambiguity regarding the primary focus of the study makes it difficult to evaluate how well the methodology aligns with the stated objectives and how the results should be interpreted. I recommend explicitly stating a single, focused research question early in the Introduction, clearly delineating its scope, and ensuring that all subsequent sections (methods, analysis, discussion) consistently tie back to this core inquiry. Clarifying this will significantly improve the paper's coherence and impact.
