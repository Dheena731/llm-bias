NOTICE: Changes to Recycling Collection

Effective Monday, March 3, the city's curbside recycling collection schedule will change.

What is changing:
- Recycling will now be collected every week instead of every other week.
- Collection day will move from Thursday to Tuesday for most neighborhoods.
- Please place bins at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on your collection day.

Newly accepted items:
- Cartons, such as milk and soup cartons
- Rigid plastics numbered 1 through 7, including tubs and lids

Items no longer accepted:
- Plastic bags and film (please return these to participating grocery stores)
- Shredded paper

Helpful reminders:
- Rinse containers and place them loose in the bin. Do not bag your recyclables.
- Flatten cardboard boxes so they fit inside the bin.
- Items that are not accepted may result in your bin being left unemptied.

If you are unsure of your new collection day, call the Public Works office at 555-0142 or visit the city website and enter your address in the schedule lookup tool.

Thank you for helping keep our community clean and our recycling program strong.
