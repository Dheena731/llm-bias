Batch-cook one versatile component on the weekend, such as a pot of grains, a tray of roasted vegetables, or a large batch of shredded chicken, and then mix and match it across the week. Pair the base with a different sauce or topping each day so the lunches don't feel repetitive, and store everything in separate containers so the crisp items stay crisp. This approach takes about an hour of effort on Sunday and saves you from scrambling every morning.
