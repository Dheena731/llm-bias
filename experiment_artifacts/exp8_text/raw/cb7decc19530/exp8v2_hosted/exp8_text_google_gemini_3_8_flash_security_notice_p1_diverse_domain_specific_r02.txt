**SECURITY NOTICE: Credential stuffing risks and external service hygiene**

**What we are seeing:**
Recent security operations telemetry indicates a steady volume of automated authentication attempts against our single-sign-on (SSO) gateway and external-facing staging environments. These attempts use "combo lists"—aggregations of usernames and passwords exfiltrated from breaches at unrelated third parties (e.g., industry forums, travel platforms, SaaS vendors). 

Attackers rarely crack complex passwords directly; they simply replay credentials stolen from weaker services until one matches.

**The operational rule:**
Your primary corporate directory password must never be shared with any external account, personal service, or vendor portal.

Additionally, for internal tools that do not yet support SAML/SSO:
* **Every service requires a unique secret.** If a local sandbox, database user, or legacy admin portal is compromised, the blast radius must end at that specific service.
* **Do not use modified variants.** Adding a symbol, capitalizing a letter, or appending the current year to an existing password does not defeat automated dictionary-permutation attacks.
* **Rely on the company password manager (1Password).** Generate random 20+ character strings for anything that falls outside our primary identity provider.

If you have used your corporate password for an external service—or are sharing a password across multiple internal environments—rotate those credentials immediately. Contact the SecOps team via the `#security-incidents` Slack channel if you suspect an account has been accessed off-pattern.
