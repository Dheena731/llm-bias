Starting April 1, we will change the foreign transaction fee on all consumer credit and debit cards. Purchases made in a currency other than your account currency, or with a merchant located outside the country where your card was issued, will carry a fee of 2.5 percent of the transaction amount, up from 1.5 percent. The change does not affect domestic purchases, cash advances, or balance transfers, and the fee on those transactions remains as stated in your cardholder agreement.

The increase reflects higher costs we pay to process international payments and currency conversions. We understand that even small changes can add up for customers who travel or shop from overseas merchants, so we have kept the new rate below the industry average for comparable cards.

You do not need to take any action. The new rate will apply automatically to transactions posted on or after the effective date. If you expect to make significant international purchases in the coming months, you may want to review your options. Cards with no foreign transaction fee are available, and you can request one by contacting customer service or visiting your account settings.

If you have questions about how this change affects your account, please call the number on the back of your card or write to us through the secure messaging section of our website. Our team can review recent transactions with you and explain how the new fee will be calculated.
