Road Closure Notice

A section of Main Street will be closed to through traffic from 8am to 5pm on Monday, March 21st to allow for utility repairs. The closure will extend from Oak Avenue to Maple Street, a distance of approximately 300 meters. Motorists are advised to use alternative routes during this time. Local access to businesses and residences within the affected area will be maintained. The road will reopen promptly after the completion of the work.
