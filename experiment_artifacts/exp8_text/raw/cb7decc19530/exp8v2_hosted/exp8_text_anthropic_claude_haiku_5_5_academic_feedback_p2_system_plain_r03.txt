Here is a short feedback note you could adapt for a student's methods section.

Thank you for submitting your methods section. You have described your participant recruitment clearly, and the reader can easily follow how the sample was selected and what inclusion criteria were applied. Your decision to explain the reasons for choosing a mixed-methods design is a real strength, as it shows you understand how the research questions shaped the approach.

There are some areas that need further development. The procedure for data collection is described in general terms, and I would like to see the sequence of steps spelled out more precisely, including timing, the setting, and who was responsible for each stage. This would allow another researcher to replicate your study. In addition, the analysis section mentions thematic analysis but does not say which framework you followed or how you checked the reliability of your coding. Naming the approach and briefly explaining your coding process would make this section much stronger.

Finally, consider adding a short paragraph on the limitations of your methods, such as the sample size or the possible influence of your own role as researcher. Acknowledging these openly will make your work more credible rather than less.

Overall, this is a solid draft with a clear structure. With more precision in the procedure and analysis, it will meet the standard expected at this level. I look forward to reading your revised version.
