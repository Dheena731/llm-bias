As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain security and compliance, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews to ensure that all users have the appropriate level of access to our systems and data. During these reviews, we will verify that each user's access permissions are up to date and aligned with their current role and responsibilities. Managers will be asked to review and confirm the access levels of their team members, and any changes or updates will be implemented accordingly. The reviews will be conducted on a quarterly basis, and notifications will be sent out in advance to remind managers of their responsibilities in this process. If you have any questions or concerns about the access review process, please reach out to the IT department for assistance.
