Stop trying to remember everything. Set a reminder for each task the moment it comes up, and give it a lead time that matches the task. A pickup gets a 30-minute alert, while a deadline or a flight gets a day or two of warning. Most calendar apps let you add a second alert, so you can get a heads-up the evening before and another shortly before it starts. It takes about ten seconds to set up, and it turns your calendar from a list you check into a system that comes looking for you.
