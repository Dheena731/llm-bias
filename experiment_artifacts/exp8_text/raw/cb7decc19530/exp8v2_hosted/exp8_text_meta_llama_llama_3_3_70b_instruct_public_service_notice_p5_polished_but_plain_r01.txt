Voters can find their designated polling station locations by checking their voter registration cards or visiting the local election office website. Polling stations will be open on election day from 8am to 8pm and are located at various public buildings throughout the area, including schools, community centers, and libraries. A list of polling station locations is also available at public libraries and other community locations. Voters are advised to verify their polling station location before election day to ensure they can cast their ballot at the correct location.
