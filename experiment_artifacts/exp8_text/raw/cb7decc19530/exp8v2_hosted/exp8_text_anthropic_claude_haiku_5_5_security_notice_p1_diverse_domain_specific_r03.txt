**Subject: Reused passwords — what we found, and what to change by Friday**

Last month SSO flagged 41 login attempts against finance and ops accounts. None succeeded. Each one used a username and password pair that appeared in a public dump from a loyalty-program breach in March. Several of the affected accounts had the same password as a personal account on that retailer's site.

Attackers don't need to crack anything. They take breached pairs and try them on every other login they can find.

**What to do:**

- **Check your work password against your personal ones.** If they match, or differ only by a number or a capital letter, change the work password today.
- **Stop using the old vendor portal login.** We're retiring it on the 14th. Anything still saved there will stop working.
- **Set up the password manager** (Bitwarden is approved; the setup guide is under Security > Tools on the intranet). Shared team logins go in a vault folder, not in a spreadsheet or a channel message.
- **Don't write passwords on sticky notes.** Facilities found three under keyboards this quarter. We're not going to name names.

Accounts from the flagged list will be forced to reset Monday morning. You'll get a separate email with the reset link, and that email will come from security@, not a shortened URL.

IT will never ask for your password by phone, chat, or email. If someone does, don't reply. Forward it to security@ and tell Dana Whitfield on the security team (ext. 4417).
