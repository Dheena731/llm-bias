**Notice to Voters: Finding Your Polling Station**

Polling stations are assigned based on where you live, so each voter has a specific location to go to on election day. Before you vote, check your assigned polling station with your local election office, either through its website or by phone. You can also look for the polling station information on your voter registration notification.

Please confirm your location ahead of time, as polling stations can change from one election to the next. If you are unsure which station serves your address, contact your local election office for help.
