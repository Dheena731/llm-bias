**💡 Tips for sending:**
*   **Include the OS versions:** Mention if the update applies to Windows, macOS, Linux, or mobile devices.
*   **Highlight the "Why":** Users are more likely to act quickly if they know how the change benefits them (security vs. speed).
*   **Provide support links:** Ensure the help desk info is prominent to reduce ticket volume.
