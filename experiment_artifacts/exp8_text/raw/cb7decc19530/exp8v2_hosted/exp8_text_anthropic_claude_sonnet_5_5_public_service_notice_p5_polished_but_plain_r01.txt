Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations

Voters are advised that polling stations for the upcoming election will be open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on election day. Each registered voter has been assigned to a specific polling station based on the address shown on the voter register, and voters should cast their ballots at that location. The assigned station is printed on the voter information card mailed to every household, and anyone who has not received a card or has misplaced it may confirm their station by contacting the local election office or by checking the official election website.

Most polling stations will be located in familiar community buildings, including public schools, libraries, community centers, and municipal halls. All locations have been selected for accessibility, with step-free entrances, designated parking, and assistance available for voters with mobility, vision, or hearing needs. Voters who require additional support are encouraged to contact the election office in advance so that suitable arrangements can be made.

Please note that some polling stations have moved since the previous election due to building renovations and changes in precinct boundaries. Even those who have voted in the same place for many years should check their card before traveling. Voters who arrive at the wrong location may be directed elsewhere, which could cause avoidable delay.

To make the process smoother, voters should bring a valid form of photo identification and their voter information card if they have it. Those who arrive before closing time and are waiting in line at 8:00 p.m. will still be permitted to vote. Staff at each station will be glad to answer questions and guide voters through the process.

For further information or help locating a polling station, please contact the local election office during regular business hours.
