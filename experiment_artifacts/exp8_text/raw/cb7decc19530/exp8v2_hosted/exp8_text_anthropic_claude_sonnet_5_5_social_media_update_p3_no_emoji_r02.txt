Join us this Saturday, June 14, for the Riverside Community Day at Maple Park! Doors open at 10 a.m., and the fun runs until 4 p.m.

Highlights include:
- Live music from local bands
- Food trucks and a farmers market
- Kids' activities and face painting
- A neighborhood cleanup at noon (gloves and bags provided)

Admission is free, and everyone is welcome. Bring a blanket, a water bottle, and your neighbors.

We hope to see you there!
