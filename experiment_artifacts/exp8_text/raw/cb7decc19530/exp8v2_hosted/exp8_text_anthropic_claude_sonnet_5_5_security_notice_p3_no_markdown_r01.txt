NOTICE: Please Lock Your Screens

To all staff,

This is a reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if it is only for a moment. Unattended screens put confidential information at risk and can lead to unauthorized access to our systems.

Quick tip: Press Windows key + L on a PC, or Control + Command + Q on a Mac, to lock your screen instantly.

Please also remember to:
- Log out at the end of your workday
- Avoid leaving sensitive documents visible on your screen
- Report any suspicious activity to the IT department right away

Thank you for helping us keep our information secure.

IT and Security Team
