One of the easiest ways to make a weeknight dinner come together faster is to prep your aromatics while the pan heats up, rather than before you start. While your oil warms in the skillet, mince the garlic, slice the onion, and grate any ginger you plan to use. The sizzle of the vegetables hitting the hot oil is a good cue that you're ready to move, and you'll avoid the common mistake of letting the first pan of food sit unattended while you chop. Keep a small bowl nearby for scraps and a damp towel under your cutting board so it stays put. Cooking this way turns a stressful rush into a steady, almost rhythmic sequence.
