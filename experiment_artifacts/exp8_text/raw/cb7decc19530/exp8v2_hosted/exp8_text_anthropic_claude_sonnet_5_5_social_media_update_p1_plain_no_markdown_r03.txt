We just rolled out a new feature that lets you schedule your posts in advance. Pick a date and time, write your update, and we will publish it for you automatically. It works across all your connected accounts, so you can plan a whole week of content in one sitting and spend your time on other things. The feature is available now for everyone, and you can find it right next to the publish button in your dashboard. Give it a try and tell us what you think. Your feedback will help shape what we build next.
