**Tooltip (shipment tracking dashboard):**

> **Pickup dates.** Shows loads picked up between the two dates, inclusive. Delivery date doesn't matter, so a load picked up Mar 31 and delivered Apr 2 counts as March. Times use each origin depot's local zone. Max range: 92 days.

I assumed a freight context since you didn't name a domain. Here's the same idea for two others:

**Bank statements:**
> **Posting date**, not transaction date. A card purchase on the 30th may post on the 1st and appear in the next range. Both end dates are included.

**Clinical lab results:**
> **Collection date** of the specimen, not the date results were reported. Both end dates are included. Ranges over 12 months are limited to final results.
