To elevate a simple weeknight protein in minutes, make a quick pan sauce while the meat rests. After cooking chicken, steak, or pork chops, remove them to a cutting board and return the skillet to medium heat. Pour in a splash of broth, water, or dry white wine, using a wooden spoon to scrape up the flavorful caramelized bits stuck to the bottom of the pan. Let the liquid bubble until it reduces by half, then turn off the heat and vigorously swirl in a tablespoon of cold butter until the sauce becomes smooth and glossy. Finished with a pinch of salt and a squeeze of fresh lemon juice, this simple technique adds rich moisture and depth to the plate with virtually no extra cleanup.
