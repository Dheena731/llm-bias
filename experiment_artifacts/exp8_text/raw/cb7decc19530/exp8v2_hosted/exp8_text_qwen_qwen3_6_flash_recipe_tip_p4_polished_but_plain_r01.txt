Streamline your weekly routine by focusing on component-based preparation rather than fully assembled meals. Spend sixty minutes on a weekend afternoon cooking foundational items such as roasted vegetables, a hearty grain, and a lean protein, then store these elements in individual containers. During the workweek, you can create diverse dishes by mixing and matching portions with simple additions like fresh herbs, citrus dressings, or nuts, which adds texture and flavor without requiring extra effort. This method minimizes daily cooking time, prevents monotony, and reduces waste by allowing you to use ingredients flexibly across multiple days.
