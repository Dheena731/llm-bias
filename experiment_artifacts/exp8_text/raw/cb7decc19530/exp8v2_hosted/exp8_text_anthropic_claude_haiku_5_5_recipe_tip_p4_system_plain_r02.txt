Batch-cook one versatile component on the weekend, such as a pot of grains, a tray of roasted vegetables, or a big batch of shredded chicken. Once it's ready, you can mix and match it across the week by pairing it with different sauces, greens, and toppings so your lunches feel varied without requiring a fresh cooking session each day. Store everything in clearly labeled containers in the fridge, and keep a few quick extras on hand, like hummus, cheese, or canned beans, for days when you need something faster.
