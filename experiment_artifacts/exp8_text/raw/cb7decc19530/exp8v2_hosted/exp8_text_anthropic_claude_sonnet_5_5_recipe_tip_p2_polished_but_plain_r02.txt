To keep fresh herbs vibrant for up to two weeks, treat them according to their type. Tender herbs such as cilantro, parsley, and dill do best when stored like cut flowers: trim the stem ends, stand the bunch in a jar with an inch of water, and loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag before placing the jar in the refrigerator. Change the water every few days if it turns cloudy. Sturdier herbs such as thyme, rosemary, and sage prefer a drier environment, so wrap them in a barely damp paper towel and tuck them into a resealable bag or container in the crisper drawer. Basil is the exception, since cold causes its leaves to blacken. Keep it in a jar of water on the counter, out of direct sun, and use it within a few days. Whatever the herb, wait to wash it until just before using, because excess moisture hastens spoilage.
