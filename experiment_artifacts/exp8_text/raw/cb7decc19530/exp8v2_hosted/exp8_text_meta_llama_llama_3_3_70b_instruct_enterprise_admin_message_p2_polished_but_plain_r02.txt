We are introducing a new expense approval workflow that will help streamline our financial processes and improve accountability. The new workflow will require all expenses to be submitted through our online system, where they will be reviewed and approved by the relevant department manager. This will help to ensure that all expenses are properly authorized and recorded, and will also provide a clear audit trail. The new workflow will be effective as of the start of the next financial period, and training sessions will be available to help staff members understand the new process. If you have any questions or concerns about the new workflow, please do not hesitate to contact the finance department.
