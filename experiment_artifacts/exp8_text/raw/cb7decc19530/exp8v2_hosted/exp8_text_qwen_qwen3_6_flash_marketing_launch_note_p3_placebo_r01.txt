**Introducing the ClearView Budget Spreadsheet Template**

Managing your money should be straightforward. That’s why we’ve created this ready-to-use budgeting spreadsheet template, designed specifically for individuals and households who want clear visibility into their finances. The layout separates your monthly income, regular bills, everyday spending, and savings targets into organized sections. Built-in formulas automatically calculate totals and remaining balances as you enter your numbers, while color-coded rows give you an immediate visual snapshot of where your money stands. No accounting background is needed, and the file opens natively in both Google Sheets and Microsoft Excel. Stop guessing how your funds are allocated and start making intentional choices about your spending. Download the template today and take control of your monthly budget.
