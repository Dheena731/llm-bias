While the broader topic addressed in this manuscript is compelling, the central research question remains ambiguous. The introduction currently outlines multiple overlapping objectives and broad thematic interests, leaving the primary point of inquiry unclear. This lack of specificity makes it challenging to evaluate whether the research design is adequately aligned with the study intentions or if the conclusions are fully justified. I recommend that the authors clearly articulate a singular, well-defined research question early in the manuscript, explicitly identifying the core variables or phenomena being investigated and their anticipated relationships. Doing so will significantly strengthen the conceptual framing and provide a coherent foundation for the methodology and analysis.
