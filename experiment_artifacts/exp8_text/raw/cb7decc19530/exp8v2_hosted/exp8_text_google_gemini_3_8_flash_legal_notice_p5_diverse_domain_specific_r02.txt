### NOTICE OF CONDITIONAL NETWORK ACCESS AND LOCAL ROUTING POLICY

**APPLICABILITY:** This notice governs all Layer 2 associations, IP lease allocations, and downstream transit through this shared infrastructure. Connection to this segment—whether via physical ethernet drop, managed 802.1X, or pre-shared wireless infrastructure—constitutes an active, enforceable covenant between the endpoint operator and the Network Administrator.

---

#### 1. Prohibited Segment and Protocol Activities
Access is strictly non-exclusive and limited to standard outbound client transactions. You are expressly prohibited from:
* **Infrastructure Impairment:** Advertising rogue DHCP/BOOTP offers, issuing gratuitous ARPs, initiating spanning-tree topology updates (BPDU tampering), or conducting neighbor-discovery spoofing.
* **Hostility & Surveillance:** Promiscuous capture of adjacent frame traffic, port scanning across local subnets, MAC-address flooding, or injection of unauthorized route advertisements (e.g., OSPF/BGP frames).
* **Unsanctioned Daemons:** Operating open mail relays (specifically transit over unauthenticated TCP Port 25), public recursive resolvers, anonymous proxy endpoints, or peer-to-peer routing relays that cause sustained connection-state table exhaustion on perimeter security appliances.
* **Circumvention:** Evading stateful inspection, MAC filtering, VLAN segmentation, or dynamic rate-limiting via spoofed hardware identifiers or virtual network interfaces.

#### 2. Traffic Metering and Priority of Service
Available upstream transit and internal switching capacity are shared communal resources. The Network Administrator reserves the right to enforce real-time traffic-shaping, deep-packet priority classification, and bandwidth throttling without prior declaration. Protocols exhibiting volumetric behavior consistent with bulk synchronization, automated scraping, or cryptographic operations are subject to immediate, unnotified deprioritization.

#### 3. Monitoring, Telemetry, and Privacy
**No reasonable expectation of payload or transactional privacy exists across this segment.** To maintain infrastructure integrity, enforce upstream carrier obligations, and comply with lawful interception directives, the Administrator actively captures, logs, and analyzes:
1. Volumetric NetFlow/IPFIX telemetry and Layer 2/3 header data.
2. Internal DNS query resolutions and associated source MAC/IP pairs.
3. Anomaly signatures via active intrusion prevention engines.

#### 4. Remediation and Immediate Quarantine
Any endpoint detected generating malicious packet flows, malformed payloads, or volumetric egress that compromises local tenancy will suffer immediate remediation without warning. Actions include, but are not limited to: automated 802.1X dynamic VLAN reassignment to an isolated sandbox, null-routing (blackholing) of endpoint traffic at the core switch, or persistent port shutdown. The Administrator accepts zero liability for session termination, packet loss, or business interruption resulting from policy enforcement.
