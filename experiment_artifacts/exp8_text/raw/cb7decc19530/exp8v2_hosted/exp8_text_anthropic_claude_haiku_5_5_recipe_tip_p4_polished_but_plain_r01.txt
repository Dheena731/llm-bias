Choose one or two base ingredients to batch-cook on a single afternoon, such as a pot of grains, a tray of roasted vegetables, and a protein like chicken or chickpeas. Store them in separate containers so you can mix and match them into different lunches through the week without repeating the same meal every day. Keep a few fast add-ons on hand, like a jar of dressing, a handful of greens, or a can of beans, so each lunch takes only a minute or two to assemble. Planning this way means you spend roughly an hour on the weekend and then nearly everything else is ready when you need it.
