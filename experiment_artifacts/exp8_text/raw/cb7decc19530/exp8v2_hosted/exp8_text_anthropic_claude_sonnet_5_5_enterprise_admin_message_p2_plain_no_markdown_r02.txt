Subject: Update to Expense Approval Workflow

Hello everyone,

Starting Monday, the first of next month, we are moving to a new expense approval workflow designed to speed up reimbursements and reduce back-and-forth between teams.

Under the new process, you will submit all expense reports through the finance portal rather than by email. Each report should include itemized receipts and a short business purpose for every charge. Once submitted, the report goes first to your direct manager, who will have three business days to approve or return it with comments. Reports under five hundred dollars will then be approved automatically by finance and scheduled for the next payment run. Reports of five hundred dollars or more will receive one additional review from the finance team before payment.

Reimbursements will be issued every Friday for all reports fully approved by the preceding Wednesday. If a report is returned to you, please correct the issues and resubmit it so it can re-enter the queue without losing its place.

Expenses submitted by email after the start date will not be processed, so please begin using the portal as soon as you can. Any reports already in progress under the old process will be honored, but we ask that you submit them by the end of this month.

A short training session will be held next week, and a recorded version will be available afterward. If you have questions or run into trouble logging in, please contact the finance help desk and we will get you sorted out quickly.

Thank you for your cooperation as we make this change.

Best regards,
The Administration Team
