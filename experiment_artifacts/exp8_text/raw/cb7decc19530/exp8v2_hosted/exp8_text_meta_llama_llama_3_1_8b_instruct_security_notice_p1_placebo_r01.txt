**Important Security Reminder**

To all employees,

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain a secure work environment, we want to remind you of the importance of password hygiene. One common security risk that we would like to bring to your attention is the practice of password reuse.

**What is password reuse?**

Password reuse occurs when you use the same password for multiple accounts, including both work and personal accounts. This practice can put your company's data and systems at risk in the event of a security breach.

**Why is password reuse a concern?**

If an unauthorized individual gains access to one of your reused passwords, they may be able to access your work account, which could result in unauthorized access to sensitive company information, data breaches, or other security incidents.

**What can you do to help protect your company's security?**

To prevent password reuse, we recommend that you create unique passwords for each of your work and personal accounts. You can use a password manager to generate and store complex, unique passwords.

**Best practices for password management**

*   Use a password manager to generate and store complex, unique passwords
*   Avoid using easily guessable information such as your name, birthdate, or common words
*   Change your passwords regularly, ideally every 90 days
*   Do not share your passwords with anyone, including colleagues and managers

**What if I have already reused passwords?**

If you have already reused passwords, please take the time to create unique passwords for each account. If you need assistance with password management, please speak with your supervisor or our IT department.
