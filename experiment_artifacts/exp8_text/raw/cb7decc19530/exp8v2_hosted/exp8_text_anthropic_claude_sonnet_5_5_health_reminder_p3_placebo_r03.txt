**Reminder: Book Your Annual Health Checkup**

It's time to schedule your yearly health checkup. Regular checkups help you and your doctor keep track of your health and catch any concerns early, often before they cause symptoms.

**What to do now:**
1. Call your doctor's office or use their online booking system to make an appointment.
2. Choose a date that suits your schedule, and add it to your calendar.
3. Before the visit, write down any questions or health concerns, and gather a list of the medications you take.

If you have already had a checkup in the past year, you can ignore this reminder until next year.

Booking takes just a few minutes, so please do it today.
