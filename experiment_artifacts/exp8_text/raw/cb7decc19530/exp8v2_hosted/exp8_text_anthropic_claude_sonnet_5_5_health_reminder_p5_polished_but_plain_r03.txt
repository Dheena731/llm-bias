Reminder: Your prescription is due for a refill soon. To avoid running out, please contact your pharmacy or prescriber's office a few days before your supply ends, since some refills require approval and can take time to process. Have your prescription number, or the medication name and dosage, ready when you call or request it online. If you have no refills remaining, ask the pharmacy to send a renewal request to your doctor, or schedule a follow-up appointment if one is needed. Keep taking your medication as directed in the meantime, and do not stretch your doses to make it last longer. If you have any questions about side effects, dosage, or how the medication is working for you, this is a good time to raise them with your pharmacist or healthcare provider.
