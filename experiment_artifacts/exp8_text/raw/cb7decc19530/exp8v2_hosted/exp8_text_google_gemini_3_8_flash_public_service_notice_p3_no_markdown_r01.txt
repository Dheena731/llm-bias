IMPORTANT NOTICE: RECYCLING COLLECTION CHANGES

Please be advised that effective Monday, November 3, the municipal recycling collection schedule will be updated for all residents. 

Starting on this date, recycling pickup will shift from weekly on Tuesdays to bi-weekly on Wednesdays. General trash collection days will remain unaffected. 

Please ensure your blue recycling bin is placed curbside by 7:00 AM on your new scheduled day. Bins should not be placed out prior to 6:00 PM the evening before.

To view your new area calendar and a list of accepted recyclable materials, please visit www.cityservices.gov/recycling or call Customer Service at (555) 019-2834.

Thank you for your cooperation and your continued commitment to keeping our community clean.
