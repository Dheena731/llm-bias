**PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE**

**Maple Street Closure Between 4th Avenue and 8th Avenue**

**Dates:** Monday, June 9 through Friday, June 13
**Hours:** 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily

The City Department of Public Works will close Maple Street between 4th Avenue and 8th Avenue for road resurfacing and storm drain repair.

**What to expect:**
- The road will be closed to all through traffic during the hours listed above.
- Local residents will have access to their driveways, but may experience short delays.
- Parking will not be permitted on either side of Maple Street within the work zone. Vehicles left in the area may be towed.
- Sidewalks will remain open, though pedestrians should use caution near construction equipment.

**Detour:** Drivers should use Cedar Street and 9th Avenue to bypass the closure. Detour signs will be posted.

Work is weather dependent. If rain delays construction, the closure may extend into the following week. Updates will be posted on the city website.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience as we work to improve our roads.

**Questions?** Contact the Department of Public Works at (555) 123-4567 or visit www.examplecity.gov/publicworks.
