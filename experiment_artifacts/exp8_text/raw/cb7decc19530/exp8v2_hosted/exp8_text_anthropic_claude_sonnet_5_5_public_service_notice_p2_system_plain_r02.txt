NOTICE OF TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE

The City Department of Public Works advises residents and motorists that Maple Street will be closed to all through traffic between Fourth Avenue and Ninth Avenue from Monday, June 3, until Friday, June 14. Work will take place daily from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

The closure is necessary so that crews can replace an aging water main and resurface the roadway. Weather permitting, all work is expected to be finished by the end of the day on June 14. If rain causes delays, the closure may be extended by up to three days, and updates will be posted on the city website and at both ends of the affected area.

Local access will be maintained for residents, deliveries, and emergency vehicles, though drivers should expect short delays and reduced parking along the work zone. Residents are asked to move vehicles from the street by 6:30 a.m. each workday. Vehicles left in the work area may be towed at the owner's expense. Water service may be briefly interrupted on some blocks, and affected households will receive a notice at least 24 hours in advance.

Drivers are encouraged to use Oak Street or Cedar Boulevard as alternate routes. Detour signs will be posted. Pedestrians and cyclists should follow marked paths and use caution near construction equipment. School bus and public transit routes will be adjusted during this period, and riders should check with their providers for details.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience as we work to improve local infrastructure. For questions or concerns, please contact the Department of Public Works at 555-0142 during regular business hours, or email publicworks@examplecity.gov.
