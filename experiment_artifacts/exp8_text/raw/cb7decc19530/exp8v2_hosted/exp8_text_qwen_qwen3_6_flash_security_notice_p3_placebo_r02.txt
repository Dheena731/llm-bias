**NOTICE: Lock Your Screen When Away From Your Desk**

To all staff, please remember to lock your computer screen every time you leave your workstation, even briefly. This practice prevents unauthorized access to sensitive information and maintains our workplace security standards.

You can lock your screen instantly using the keyboard shortcut for your operating system (Win + L on Windows, Command + Control + Q on Mac), or by selecting the lock option in your system menu. 

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our data secure. For any questions, please contact the IT support team.
