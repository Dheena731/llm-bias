Subject: Update to Card Transaction Processing Fees

Dear Merchant Partner,

This note informs you of an adjustment to our card transaction processing fees. Effective March 1, 2026, the standard fee for domestic credit and debit card sales will increase from 2.5 percent to 2.8 percent per transaction. Fees for international card payments and recurring subscription charges will remain unchanged at their current rates.

This adjustment accounts for higher assessment fees from payment networks and increased costs associated with fraud prevention and payment security upgrades. The new rate applies only to transactions that settle on or after March 1, 2026. Sales already authorized before that date will continue to be charged at the previous rate.

Your merchant portal will display the updated fee schedule immediately upon implementation. The change will first appear on your April statement. If you need help calculating how this affects your average order value or want a detailed transaction report, please contact our merchant support team at support@merchantpayments.com or call 555-019-4421 between 8 a.m. and 6 p.m. Eastern Time, Monday through Friday.

Thank you for your continued business. We remain committed to transparent pricing and dependable payment processing.
