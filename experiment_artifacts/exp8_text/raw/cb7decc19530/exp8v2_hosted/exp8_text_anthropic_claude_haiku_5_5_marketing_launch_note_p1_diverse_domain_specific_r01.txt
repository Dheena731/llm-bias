**Subject: Wholesale orders, off the whiteboard**

Crumb is a wholesale order book for independent bakeries. Your café accounts order through a link you send them once. Orders close at the cutoff you set (default 9 p.m., when most of you are finally sitting down). Next morning, the production sheet is already waiting: loaves by type, pastry by tray, and which box goes to which door.

It doesn't take a cut of your orders, and it doesn't need your register system. You enter your products once. If your price list lives in a notebook or a spreadsheet with forty tabs, you can paste it in and fix the typos after.

We spent the winter testing it with four bakeries in beta. The most common feedback was about the texts, not the features: one owner said her Saturday messages dropped from about forty to two, and she only noticed because she stopped checking her phone in the walk-in.

Launch terms: free for your first 50 wholesale accounts through March 31. After that, $19 a month flat, with no per-order fees.

If you bake for other people, try it this week and tell us where it breaks.
