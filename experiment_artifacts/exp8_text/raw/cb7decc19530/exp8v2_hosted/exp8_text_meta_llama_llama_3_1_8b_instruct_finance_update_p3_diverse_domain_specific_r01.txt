**Introducing 'Festive Fund' - A Seasonal Savings Goal for Homeowners**

As the holiday season approaches, homeowners can now set a dedicated savings goal with our new 'Festive Fund' feature. Designed for those who want to prioritize home maintenance and improvement, Festive Fund allows users to allocate a specific amount towards holiday decorations, winterizing their home, or tackling that to-do list of indoor projects.

To create a Festive Fund, users simply tap the "Add Goal" button and select the "Festive Fund" option. They'll then input the desired savings amount, choose a due date (e.g., December 15th), and receive regular reminders to stay on track. The funds will be automatically transferred into a designated savings account, making it easy to stay on budget and enjoy the holiday season with peace of mind.

By the time the snowflakes start falling, homeowners will have a dedicated fund to tackle winter-related expenses, ensuring their home is safe, cozy, and welcoming throughout the cold months.
