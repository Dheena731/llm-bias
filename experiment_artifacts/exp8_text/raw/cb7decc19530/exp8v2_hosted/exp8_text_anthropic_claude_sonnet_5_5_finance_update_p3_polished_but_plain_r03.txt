We have added a new savings goals feature to help you set aside money for the things that matter most to you. You can now create a goal for anything you are working toward, such as a vacation, a down payment, or an emergency fund, and give it a name, a target amount, and an optional deadline.

Once a goal is set up, you can contribute to it manually at any time or schedule automatic transfers from your checking account on a weekly, biweekly, or monthly basis. If you enter a deadline, the app will calculate how much you need to save each period to stay on track and will adjust that figure if you add extra funds or fall behind. Your progress appears on a simple visual tracker, so you can see at a glance how close you are to finishing.

You can create as many goals as you like and rearrange them in order of priority. Funds assigned to a goal remain in your account, but they are set apart from your available balance, which makes it easier to avoid spending money you have already committed to saving. If your plans change, you can edit the target, move money between goals, or close a goal and return the funds to your main balance without any fees.

The feature is available now in the latest version of the app. To get started, open the Accounts tab and choose Savings Goals. We hope it makes saving feel more manageable, and we welcome your feedback as we continue to improve it.
