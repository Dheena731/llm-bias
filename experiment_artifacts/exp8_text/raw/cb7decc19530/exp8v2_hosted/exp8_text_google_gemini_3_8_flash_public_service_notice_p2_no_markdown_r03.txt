PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE

Main Street will be closed to all through traffic between 4th Avenue and Elm Street from Monday, October 14, at 7:00 AM through Friday, October 18, at 5:00 PM.

This closure is necessary for emergency water main repairs and street resurfacing. 

Detour routes will be clearly posted along Oak Street. Local access will be maintained for residents and businesses within the affected area, but delays should be expected. Motorists are encouraged to use alternate routes.

For more information, please contact the Department of Public Works at 555-019-2834 or visit www.cityexample.gov/roads. 

We apologize for any inconvenience and appreciate your patience.
