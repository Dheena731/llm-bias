**Subject: Q3 access review: manager decisions due Friday 18 October, 17:00**

Reviewers have Q3 access review tasks in the IAM portal under *Access Reviews > My Pending*. For each direct report, mark every entitlement Keep or Revoke. Anything still unmarked at 17:00 on Friday will be revoked automatically. This is intentional.

Items to check closely:
- Memberships in SG-FIN-*, SG-HR-*, and SG-PROD-* groups
- Admin rights on any svc-* service accounts you own
- DB-PRD-RO (production read) grants. Auditors look at these first, so please don't approve them from the summary view.

Not in scope: your own access (your manager reviews it) and anything with a "pending change" flag in ServiceNow, which the change board is already tracking.

If someone has moved teams since July, don't keep their old access to shorten the list. Open a ticket in IAM-Requests and mark the entitlement Revoke. The ticket closes the review item.

Contractors with an end date before 30 September should appear as expired. If one doesn't, tell us in #iam-help so we can fix the source record.

Reviews still open on Monday go to your director for escalation. The audit log records who approved what and when, so please work through the list rather than bulk-approving it.
