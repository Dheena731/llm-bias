A heat advisory has been issued for our area due to extremely high temperatures and humidity. Residents are advised to take precautions to stay cool and safe. Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water and other fluids, and avoid strenuous activities during the hottest part of the day. Check on vulnerable neighbors, such as the elderly and young children, to ensure they are staying cool. If you do not have air conditioning, consider visiting a public place such as a library or shopping mall to stay cool. Take regular breaks in cool spaces if you must work outdoors. If you or someone you know is experiencing heat-related illness, seek medical attention immediately.
