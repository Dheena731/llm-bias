**Season in two stages for better flavor.** Before roasting, toss your vegetables with oil, salt, and pepper so the seasoning clings and helps them brown. Then, once they come out of the oven, finish with a squeeze of lemon juice or a splash of vinegar and a pinch of flaky salt. Fresh herbs and delicate spices also work best at this point, since high heat can make them bitter or burnt. This simple finish brightens the flavors and adds a pleasant contrast to the caramelized edges.
