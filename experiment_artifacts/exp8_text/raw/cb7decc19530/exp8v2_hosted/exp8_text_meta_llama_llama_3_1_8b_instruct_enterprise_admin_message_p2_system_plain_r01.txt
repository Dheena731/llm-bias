We are introducing a new expense approval workflow to ensure greater efficiency and accountability in the handling of employee expenses. This new workflow will be rolled out in the coming weeks and will require all employees to adhere to a more structured and transparent process.

Going forward, expense reports will need to be submitted through the new online portal, which will be accessible through the company's intranet. All managers and supervisors will be notified automatically when an expense report is submitted for their approval.

Once an expense report has been approved, it will be automatically processed for payment. Any delays in approval will result in delayed payment for the employee.

If you have any questions or concerns about this new workflow, please do not hesitate to reach out to the finance department.

Thank you for your cooperation in this matter.
