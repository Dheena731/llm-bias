Prep your vegetables and proteins on Sunday or during a quiet part of the evening, then store them in clear containers in the refrigerator. Chopped onions, peppers, garlic, and portioned chicken or tofu can all be ready to go, so when you get home tired, dinner becomes a matter of heating a pan and combining ingredients rather than starting from scratch. Keep a small jar of your favorite sauce or spice blend nearby, and you can turn the same prepped ingredients into a different meal each night.
