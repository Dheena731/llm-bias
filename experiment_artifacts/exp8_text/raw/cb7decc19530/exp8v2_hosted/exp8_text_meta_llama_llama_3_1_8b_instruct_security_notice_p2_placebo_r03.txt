**Important Workplace Notice**

**Subject: Protecting Our Company from Phishing Emails**

**To: All Employees**

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain a secure and trustworthy work environment, we would like to remind everyone of the importance of being cautious when receiving emails from unknown sources.

Phishing emails are a common threat that can compromise our company's sensitive data and systems. These emails often appear to be from legitimate senders, but are actually attempts to trick employees into divulging confidential information or clicking on malicious links.

**What to Look Out For:**

*   Emails that ask for personal or financial information, such as passwords, social security numbers, or credit card numbers.
*   Emails that contain suspicious links or attachments, such as those with unusual file extensions or from unknown senders.
*   Emails that create a sense of urgency or panic, such as threats of account suspension or legal action if information is not provided immediately.

**What to Do:**

If you receive an email that you suspect is a phishing attempt, do not respond to it. Instead, forward the email to our IT department at <phishing@company.com> so that we can investigate and take necessary action.

**Best Practices:**

*   Verify the sender's email address by checking the company's email domain.
*   Be cautious of emails that contain spelling or grammar mistakes.
*   Never provide sensitive information via email.
*   Keep your company email account up-to-date with the latest security patches and updates.

Let's work together to protect our company from phishing emails and maintain a secure work environment. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to our IT department.
