Toss your vegetables in oil and salt before they go into the oven, not after they come out. Salt drawn in during roasting seasons the vegetables all the way through, while salt sprinkled on at the end sits on the surface. Use about half a teaspoon of kosher salt per pound of vegetables, and add dried herbs or spices like garlic powder, smoked paprika, or cumin at the same time so they toast gently rather than burn. Save fresh herbs and a squeeze of lemon for the end, since their flavors fade with high heat.
