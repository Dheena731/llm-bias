We have introduced a new monthly spending summary to give you a clearer view of your finances. Starting this month, an overview will automatically generate at the end of each billing cycle to show where your money goes. The summary breaks down your purchases into distinct categories such as groceries, bills, and entertainment, helping you identify trends and spot opportunities to save. You can use these insights to build better budgets and track your financial habits over time. The summary is now accessible directly within your account dashboard, and you can adjust your notification preferences at any time in your account settings.
