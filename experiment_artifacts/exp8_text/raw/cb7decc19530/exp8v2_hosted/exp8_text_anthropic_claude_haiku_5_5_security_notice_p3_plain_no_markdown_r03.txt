Subject: Reminder to Lock Your Screen

Dear colleagues,

This is a friendly reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even for a short time. Leaving a session open can allow others to view or change confidential information, and it can expose our systems and client data to unnecessary risk. On Windows, you can lock your screen by pressing the Windows key and L together. On a Mac, press Control, Command, and Q together. Locking your screen takes only a moment and helps keep our work and our information secure.

Thank you for your continued attention to this important habit.

Regards,
The IT Security Team
