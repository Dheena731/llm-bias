To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it thoroughly before cooking. This helps remove excess starch from the surface of the grains, which can cause them to cling together. After rinsing, make sure to drain the rice well and cook it with the right amount of water. Using a non-stick pan or adding a small amount of oil or butter to the water can also help prevent the rice from becoming sticky. Additionally, avoid overmixing or stirring the rice too much while it's cooking, as this can release more starch and make the rice stickier.
