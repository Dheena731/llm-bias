**Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection**

Please be advised of the following updates to our recycling collection service, effective the first Monday of next month.

**What is changing**
- Recycling will be collected every other week instead of weekly.
- Collection day will move from Thursday to Tuesday for all residential addresses.
- Glass bottles and jars will no longer be accepted in curbside bins. Please use the drop-off containers at the Public Works yard.

**What you need to do**
- Place your bin at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on your collection day.
- Rinse containers and keep them loose in the bin. Do not bag recyclables.
- Check the collection calendar to confirm your pickup dates.

**Accepted items:** paper, cardboard, metal cans, and plastics labeled 1 and 2.

**Not accepted:** glass, plastic bags, foam, and food-soiled materials.

Printed calendars will be mailed to all households within the next two weeks. A copy is also available on the city website or at City Hall.

For questions, please contact the Public Works office at 555-0142 or email recycling@examplecity.gov.

Thank you for helping keep our community clean.
