**PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE: LIBRARY OPERATING HOURS**

Please be advised that the Municipal Public Library will maintain the following regular schedule:

• Monday through Thursday: 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM  
• Friday and Saturday: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM  
• Sunday: 12:00 PM to 4:00 PM  

The facility will remain closed on all legally recognized public holidays. Revised schedules for upcoming holidays and any temporary operational changes will be posted at all branch entrances and published on the official municipal library website. 

For immediate assistance or schedule verification, contact the Main Information Desk at 555-012-3456. Thank you for your attention, and we appreciate your continued patronage.
