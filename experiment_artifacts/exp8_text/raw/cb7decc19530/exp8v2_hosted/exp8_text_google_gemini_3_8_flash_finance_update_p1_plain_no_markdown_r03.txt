We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you keep better track of your finances. Starting this month, you will receive an automated overview at the close of each billing cycle that breaks down your total expenditures. The summary organizes your purchases into clear categories, such as groceries, utilities, and entertainment, allowing you to see exactly where your money goes. It also compares your current habits against previous months so you can easily spot trends and adjust your budget as needed. You can review this report anytime directly within your account dashboard under the financial insights section. We hope this new tool makes managing your personal budget simpler and more transparent.
