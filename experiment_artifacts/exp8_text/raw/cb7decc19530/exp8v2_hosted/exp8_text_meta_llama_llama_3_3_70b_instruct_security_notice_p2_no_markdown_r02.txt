Important Notice: Phishing Emails

We have recently seen an increase in phishing emails being sent to our employees. These emails are designed to look like they are coming from a legitimate source, but are actually attempts to steal your personal information or gain access to our company's systems.

To protect yourself and our company, please be cautious when receiving emails that ask for sensitive information such as passwords, credit card numbers, or personal data. Legitimate emails from our company will never ask you to provide this type of information via email.

If you receive an email that you suspect may be a phishing attempt, do not click on any links or respond to the email. Instead, please forward the email to our IT department so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Remember to always verify the authenticity of an email by checking the sender's email address and looking for any spelling or grammar mistakes. Your vigilance is crucial in helping us to prevent phishing attacks and protect our company's security.
