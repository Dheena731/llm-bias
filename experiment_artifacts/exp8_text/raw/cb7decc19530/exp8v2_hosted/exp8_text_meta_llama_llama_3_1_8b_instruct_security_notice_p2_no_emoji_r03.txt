**Important Notice to All Employees**

As a reminder, please be cautious when receiving emails that request sensitive information or prompt you to take urgent action. These types of emails are often phishing attempts designed to trick employees into divulging confidential data.

Be aware of the following warning signs:

* Emails that ask you to verify your login credentials or other sensitive information
* Messages that claim to be from a trusted source, but contain spelling and grammar errors
* Urgent requests for payment or action, often with a sense of panic or threat
* Links or attachments that may be malicious

If you receive a suspicious email, do not respond or click on any links. Instead, report the email to the IT department immediately. We will investigate and take action to protect our organization's data and systems.

Remember, your vigilance and caution are essential in preventing these types of attacks. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or the IT department.

**Protecting our organization's assets and data is everyone's responsibility.**
