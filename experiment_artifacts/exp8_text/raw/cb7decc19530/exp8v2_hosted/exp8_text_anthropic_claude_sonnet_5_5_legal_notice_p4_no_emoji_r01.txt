**1. Purpose**
This Notice sets forth the conditions under which refunds may be issued by [Company Name] ("the Company") to purchasers ("the Customer") of goods or services.

**2. Eligibility Requirements**
A Customer is eligible for a refund only if all of the following conditions are satisfied:

(a) The refund request is submitted in writing to [email/address] within thirty (30) days of the original date of purchase;

(b) The Customer provides valid proof of purchase, including an order number or receipt;

Refunds shall not be issued for:

(a) Gift cards and promotional credits;
(b) Custom or personalized goods;
(c) Digital products that have been downloaded, accessed, or activated;
(d) Services that have been fully rendered; or
(e) Items marked "final sale" at the time of purchase.

The Company reserves the right to deduct reasonable shipping, handling, or restocking fees from any refund, where permitted by applicable law.

Nothing in this Notice limits any rights afforded to the Customer under applicable consumer protection laws.
