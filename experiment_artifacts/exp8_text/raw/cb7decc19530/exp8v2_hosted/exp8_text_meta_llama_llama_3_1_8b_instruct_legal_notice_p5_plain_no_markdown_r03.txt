NOTICE OF ACCEPTABLE USE OF SHARED NETWORK

The following guidelines are to be observed by all users of the shared network. Failure to comply may result in restrictions or termination of network privileges.

Use of the shared network is for personal, non-commercial purposes only. The network is not to be used for any activity that is illegal, abusive, or harmful to the network or other users. This includes, but is not limited to, hacking, unauthorized access to other networks, sending unsolicited commercial email, or engaging in any activity that could be considered harassment.

Users must not share their account or password with anyone. Users are responsible for all activity occurring on their account.

Users must not store or transmit any material that is obscene, indecent, or in violation of any applicable laws or regulations.

The network is provided for educational, personal, or research purposes. Commercial use, such as running a business or operating a website, is not allowed.

Users are expected to respect the rights of other users and not to interfere with their use of the network. This includes not using the network in a way that would cause a significant burden on the network or interfere with other users' use.

The network administrators reserve the right to monitor the network and to take action against any user who is in violation of these guidelines. Violators may be subject to restrictions, warnings, or termination of network privileges.

By using the shared network, users agree to be bound by these guidelines.
