Pick one day, such as Sunday afternoon, to prepare the building blocks of your lunches rather than whole meals. Cook a big batch of a grain like rice or quinoa, roast a tray of vegetables, and prepare one protein such as grilled chicken, hard-boiled eggs, or a pot of beans. Then add a couple of sauces or dressings that change the flavor. Each day you can combine these pieces differently, so Monday might be a grain bowl, Tuesday a wrap, and Wednesday a salad topped with whatever is left. This approach takes less time than making five separate meals, keeps you from getting bored, and lets you adjust if your schedule shifts. Store everything in clear containers at eye level in the fridge so assembling lunch takes only a few minutes each morning.
