**Salt before the oven, acid after.**

Toss your vegetables with oil and salt (about ½ teaspoon of kosher salt per pound) before they go in, so the salt can pull a little surface moisture and help the edges brown. Then hold back the vinegar, lemon, or other acidic ingredients until the vegetables are out. Acid added early makes them steam and soften instead of caramelize, and its brightness bakes off anyway.

Once the pan comes out, while the vegetables are still hot, finish with a splash of sherry vinegar on roasted carrots or squash, lemon juice and zest on broccoli or cauliflower, or a little balsamic on Brussels sprouts. The heat helps them absorb it. Taste one piece and add a pinch of flaky salt if it needs it.

Ground spices like cumin and paprika scorch at 425°F, so for a long roast, toss them on in the last 10 minutes, or stir them into the oil you'll drizzle on at the end.
