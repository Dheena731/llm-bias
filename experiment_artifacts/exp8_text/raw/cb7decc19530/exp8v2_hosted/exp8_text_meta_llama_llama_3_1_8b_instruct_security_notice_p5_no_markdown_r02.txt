**Lost Company Laptop - Please Report**

If you have misplaced or lost a company laptop, please report it to the IT department immediately. This is crucial to prevent unauthorized access to company data and to initiate the process of securing the device.

To report a lost laptop, please contact the IT department at extension 1234 or email [it@company.com](mailto:it@company.com). Provide the laptop's serial number, make, and model, along with a description of the incident.
