**Public Notice: Standard Operating Hours & Service Access**

Effective Monday, November 4, operating hours across the public library system are adjusted as follows:

**Central Library**  
* **Monday – Thursday:** 9:00 a.m. – 8:00 p.m.  
* **Friday & Saturday:** 9:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.  
* **Sunday:** 1:00 p.m. – 5:00 p.m.  

**Neighborhood Branches**  
* **Tuesday & Thursday:** 10:00 a.m. – 7:00 p.m.  
* **Wednesday, Friday, & Saturday:** 10:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.  
* **Sunday & Monday:** Closed  

**Service Notes for Visitors:**
* **Terminal Access & Print Services:** Public workstations, microfilm scanners, and print release stations power down automatically 15 minutes prior to building closure.  
* **Study & Meeting Rooms:** All reserved rooms must be vacated and trash removed 30 minutes before closing.  
* **Special Collections:** The Local History Room at the Central branch operates on a limited schedule, closing daily at 4:30 p.m.  
* **Returns:** Exterior book drops remain open 24 hours a day at all locations. Items returned after hours are cleared and backdated to the last open operating day.  
* **Digital Access:** Catalog searches, hold placements, and digital databases (including Libby and JSTOR access) operate continuously at library.city.gov.

To arrange curbside holds pickup or request accommodation services, contact the circulation desk during staffed hours at (555) 019-2831.
