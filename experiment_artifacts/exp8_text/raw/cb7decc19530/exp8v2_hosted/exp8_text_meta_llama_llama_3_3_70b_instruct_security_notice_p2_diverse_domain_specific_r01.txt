**Attention All Staff**

As we continue to work on sensitive projects and handle confidential client data, it's essential that we remain vigilant against phishing attempts. Recently, there have been reports of targeted emails that appear to come from our own IT department or senior management, requesting urgent password resets or sensitive information.

Please be cautious when receiving emails that:

* Request immediate action or create a sense of urgency
* Ask for sensitive information, such as login credentials or financial data
* Contain spelling or grammatical errors
* Have suspicious sender email addresses or domains

Our IT department will never ask you to reset your password or provide sensitive information via email. If you receive an email that seems suspicious, please forward it to our IT security team at [it.security@companydomain.com](mailto:it.security@companydomain.com) for verification.

Remember, it's always better to err on the side of caution when dealing with potentially malicious emails. Your diligence helps protect our company's data and reputation.

Thank you for your attention to this matter, and if you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the IT security team.

**Report suspicious emails to:** [it.security@companydomain.com](mailto:it.security@companydomain.com)

**Stay safe online!**
