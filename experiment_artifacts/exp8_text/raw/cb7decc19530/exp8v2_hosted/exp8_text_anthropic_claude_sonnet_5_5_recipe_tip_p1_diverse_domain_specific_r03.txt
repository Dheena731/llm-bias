**Salt the pasta water, then steal a mugful of it.**

On a weeknight, the sauce usually gets all the attention and the water gets none. Fix that first: once it boils, add salt until it tastes like mild broth, roughly a tablespoon of kosher salt per 3 quarts. This is your only chance to season the pasta itself.

Before you drain, scoop out a mug of the cloudy water. Then skip the colander-and-rinse routine. Pull the pasta when it's a minute shy of done and finish it in the skillet with your sauce, adding a splash of that starchy water at a time. The starch emulsifies the fat and the tomato or garlic oil into something glossy that clings to the noodles instead of pooling at the bottom of the bowl.

This turns a jar of marinara, a can of anchovies and some chili flakes, or just butter and black pepper into a dinner that tastes deliberate. The whole thing takes about 15 minutes, and you'll dirty only one pot and one pan.
