A heat advisory has been issued for our area due to extremely high temperatures and humidity levels. Residents are advised to take precautions to stay cool and safe during this time. Stay indoors during the hottest part of the day, typically between 11am and 3pm, and seek out air-conditioned spaces if possible. Drink plenty of water and avoid strenuous activities, especially for vulnerable populations such as the elderly and young children. Check on neighbors and loved ones who may be at risk, and take regular breaks if you must work or spend time outdoors. If you or someone you know is experiencing heat-related illness, such as heat exhaustion or heat stroke, seek medical attention immediately.
