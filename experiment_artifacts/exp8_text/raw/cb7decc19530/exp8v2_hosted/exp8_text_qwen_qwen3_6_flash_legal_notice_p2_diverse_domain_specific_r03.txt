**NOTICE OF PRIVACY POLICY AMENDMENT — NEXUS FLEET TELEMATICS PLATFORM**  
**DATE:** October 26, 2024  
**REFERENCE:** V4.1 Privacy Terms (effective March 12, 2023) | Section 8.3, Master Service Agreement  

Pursuant to contractual notification obligations, Version 5.0 of the Nexus Fleet Privacy Policy supersedes prior iterations and governs all data ingestion, processing, and third-party sharing activities commencing **November 15, 2024**.  

**Material Revisions:**  
- Telemetry event logging now captures OBD-II/CAN bus diagnostic codes and localized GPS dwell periods exceeding 48 hours, classified as operational metadata rather than personally identifiable information.  
- Routing optimization services engaged as limited-purpose data processors; vehicle coordinates undergo coordinate-pair stripping before external transmission. Retention governed by 90-day rolling deletion cycles unless suspended under active warranty claims.  
- Driver behavior scoring algorithms reclassified under joint-controller status where mandated by state commercial motor vehicle reporting statutes.  
- Cross-border transfer mechanisms updated to reference EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module Two) for Canadian and Brazilian endpoint servers.  

The amended policy is available at `privacy.nexusfleet.io/v5`. Continued authentication and payload submission after the effective date constitutes acknowledgment per Section 2.1(b) of the User License Agreement.  

Data subject requests, controller classification disputes, or processor access logs should be submitted to `dpo@nexusfleet.io` with reference tag `PPN-2024-TELM`. Responses will adhere to statutory response windows.  

Issued under reserved rights. No supplementary consent instrument is required absent expansion beyond originally scoped data use parameters.
