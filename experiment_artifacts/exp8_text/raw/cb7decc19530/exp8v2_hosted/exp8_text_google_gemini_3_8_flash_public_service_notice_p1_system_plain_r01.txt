The Central Community Library wishes to remind all residents of its updated operating schedule. The library is open to the public Monday through Thursday from nine in the morning until eight in the evening. On Fridays and Saturdays, the doors are open from ten in the morning until five in the afternoon. The facility is closed on Sundays and on all observed municipal holidays. 

During open hours, visitors are welcome to borrow books and audiovisual materials, use public computer terminals, access printing services, and utilize the study areas. Digital services, including e-book lending and academic research databases, remain available around the clock through the library website. Physical returns may be deposited at any time using the exterior drop box located next to the main entrance. For further inquiries regarding programs or card registration, please visit the information desk during regular hours or call the main service line.
