Introducing the Monthly Budget Planner, a new spreadsheet template designed to help you take control of your money without the hassle of building a system from scratch. Whether you are tracking everyday expenses, planning for a big purchase, or simply trying to understand where your paycheck goes each month, this template gives you a clear and organized starting point.

The planner includes separate sections for income, fixed bills, variable spending, savings goals, and debt payments. Enter your figures once, and the template automatically calculates totals, remaining balances, and the percentage of your income going to each category. A summary dashboard at the top of the sheet shows at a glance whether you are on track for the month.

Setup takes about ten minutes. Simply enter your take-home pay, list your recurring bills, and set a target for each spending category. The template works in any major spreadsheet application and can be duplicated for each month of the year, so you can compare progress over time.

The Monthly Budget Planner is available starting today as an instant download. We believe that good financial habits start with simple tools, and we hope this template makes your next budgeting cycle easier and more rewarding.
