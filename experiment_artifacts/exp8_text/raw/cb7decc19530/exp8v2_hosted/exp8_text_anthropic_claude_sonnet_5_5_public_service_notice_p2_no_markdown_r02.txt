PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE

Maple Street between 4th Avenue and 9th Avenue will be closed to all vehicle traffic from Monday, June 10, at 7:00 a.m. through Friday, June 14, at 6:00 p.m.

Reason for Closure: The City Public Works Department will be repairing underground water mains and resurfacing the roadway.

Detour Information: Drivers should use Oak Street and 10th Avenue as an alternate route. Detour signs will be posted along the way.

Access: Local residents and businesses will have pedestrian access at all times. Vehicle access to driveways may be limited during working hours. Emergency vehicles will be able to reach all addresses.

Parking: No parking will be allowed on Maple Street within the work zone during the closure. Vehicles left in the area may be towed at the owner's expense.

Please allow extra travel time and drive carefully near the work zone. The schedule may change if weather conditions cause delays.

For questions or updates, contact the City Public Works Department at 555-0142 or visit the city website.

We appreciate your patience while we work to improve our community's infrastructure.

Issued by the City Public Works Department
