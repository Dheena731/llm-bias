NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

To all eligible customers,

You may be eligible for a refund if you have purchased a product or service from our company within the last 30 days and are not satisfied with your purchase. To qualify for a refund, you must return the product in its original condition, with all original packaging and accessories, within 30 days of the original purchase date. Refunds will be issued in the original form of payment and will include the full amount of the purchase price, minus any applicable restocking fees. Please contact our customer service department to initiate the refund process and to obtain a return merchandise authorization (RMA) number, which is required for all returns.
