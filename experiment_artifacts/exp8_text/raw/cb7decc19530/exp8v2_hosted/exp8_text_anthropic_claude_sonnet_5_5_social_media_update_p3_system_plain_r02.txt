Join us this Saturday for the Riverside Community Day at Maple Park, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. There will be live music, local food vendors, a kids' craft tent, and a neighborhood plant swap, so bring a cutting or seedling to share. Volunteers are also organizing a park cleanup at 9 a.m. for anyone who wants to help get things ready. Entry is free and everyone is welcome, so bring your family, your friends, and your neighbors. We hope to see you there, rain or shine!
