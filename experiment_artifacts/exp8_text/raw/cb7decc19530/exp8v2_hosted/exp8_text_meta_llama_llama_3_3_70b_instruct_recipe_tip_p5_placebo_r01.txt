To bring out the natural flavors of roasted vegetables, season them thoughtfully. Toss the vegetables with a drizzle of olive oil, salt, and your choice of herbs and spices before roasting. For extra depth, add a squeeze of fresh lemon juice or a sprinkle of grated garlic after roasting, as the heat will have mellowed the flavors of the vegetables and they will be more receptive to additional seasoning. This simple step can elevate the taste and aroma of your roasted vegetables, making them a delicious and satisfying side dish or main course.
