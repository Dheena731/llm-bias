A heat advisory is in effect for the metropolitan area from Wednesday morning through Thursday evening due to dangerously high temperatures and humidity. Heat index values are expected to exceed one hundred degrees during peak afternoon hours, significantly increasing the risk of heat-related illnesses. Residents are strongly advised to drink plenty of fluids, stay in air-conditioned spaces, limit strenuous outdoor activities, and avoid prolonged sun exposure between noon and six in the evening. Never leave children or pets unattended in parked vehicles, as interior temperatures can reach lethal levels within minutes. Please check on elderly family members, neighbors, and those with chronic medical conditions who may be vulnerable to the heat. If you must work outside, wear lightweight, light-colored clothing and take frequent breaks in the shade. Seek immediate medical attention if you or someone around you experiences warning signs of heat stroke, such as confusion, dizziness, rapid pulse, nausea, or a loss of consciousness.
