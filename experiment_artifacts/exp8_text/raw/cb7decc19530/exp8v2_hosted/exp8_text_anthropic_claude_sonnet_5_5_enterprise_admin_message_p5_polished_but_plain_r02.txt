Subject: Upcoming Email Migration

Dear colleagues,

We will be migrating our email system to a new platform over the weekend of the 14th and 15th. The move is intended to improve reliability, increase storage capacity, and strengthen security across the organization.

During the migration window, which begins Saturday at 8:00 p.m. and is expected to end by Sunday at 6:00 a.m., you may notice delays in sending or receiving messages. Email will not be lost, and anything sent to you during this period will be delivered once the transition is complete. We recommend avoiding any time-sensitive correspondence until Sunday morning.

Before the migration, please take a few simple steps. Clear out old or unneeded messages to help the transfer run smoothly, and make sure you know your current login password, since you will need it the first time you sign in on Monday. If you use a mobile device for work email, you will need to remove and re-add your account after the move. Instructions for doing this will be sent on Friday.

When you log in on Monday, your folders, calendar, and contacts should appear as they did before. If anything looks missing or incorrect, please contact the IT help desk at once so we can resolve it quickly. Extra support staff will be available throughout the day.

Thank you for your patience and cooperation. If you have questions before the migration, please reach out to the help desk and we will be glad to assist.

Best regards,
The IT Team
