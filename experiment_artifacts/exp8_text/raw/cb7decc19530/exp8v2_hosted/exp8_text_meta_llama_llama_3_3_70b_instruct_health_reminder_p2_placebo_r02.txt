Don't forget to drink enough water throughout the day. Staying hydrated is essential for your overall health and well-being, helping to maintain energy levels, support digestion, and keep your skin healthy. Aim to drink at least eight cups (64 ounces) of water each day, and adjust according to your individual needs, activity level, and climate. Make it a habit to have a glass of water with each meal and between meals to ensure you're getting enough hydration.
