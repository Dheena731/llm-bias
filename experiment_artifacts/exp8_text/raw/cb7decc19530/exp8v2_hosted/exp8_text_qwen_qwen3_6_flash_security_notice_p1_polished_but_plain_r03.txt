This notice reminds all staff members that reusing passwords across multiple accounts poses a severe risk to organizational security. When a single password is shared between personal email, social media, banking sites, and internal company systems, a breach on any external platform can lead to unauthorized access of sensitive business data through a technique called credential stuffing. To mitigate this danger, please adopt a policy where every login requires a distinct and complex password. We strongly encourage the use of the company-approved password manager tool to generate and store these unique credentials safely, ensuring that no two accounts share the same secret. Do not write passwords on physical notes, send them via unencrypted messaging channels, or share them with coworkers under any circumstances. If you suspect you have reused a password in the past, change it immediately on the affected service and then update the corresponding corporate account following established reset protocols. Consistent adherence to these practices helps protect our network integrity and ensures that compromised personal accounts cannot become a gateway for threats against our workplace. Your vigilance in managing authentication details is vital to maintaining a secure digital environment for everyone.
