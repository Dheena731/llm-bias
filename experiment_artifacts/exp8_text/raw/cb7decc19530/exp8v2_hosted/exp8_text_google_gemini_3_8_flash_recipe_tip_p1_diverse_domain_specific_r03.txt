Slide your empty sheet pan onto the bottom rack while the oven preheats to 425°F. 

When you dump oiled, seasoned brassicas (broccoli, sliced cabbage, halved Brussels sprouts) directly onto the hot metal, the cut sides begin crisping on contact. This immediate burst of conductive heat renders out moisture before it can pool, preventing the vegetables from steaming in their own water and shaving five to eight minutes off the total roasting time.
