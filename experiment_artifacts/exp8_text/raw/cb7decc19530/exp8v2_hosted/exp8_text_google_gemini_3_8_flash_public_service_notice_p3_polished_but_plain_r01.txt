Beginning Monday, November 3, residential recycling collection schedules will change to accommodate new route efficiencies and improved processing capabilities. Collection across all neighborhoods will shift from Wednesdays to Thursdays. Please ensure your recycling bins are placed at the curb no earlier than the evening before and no later than 7:00 AM on collection morning. In addition to clean paper, cardboard, and rigid plastics, crews will now accept rinsed glass bottles and jars. Soft plastic films, polystyrene foam, and food waste remain strictly prohibited and will result in uncollected bins. If your collection is delayed or if you require an additional container, please contact the municipal services department directly. Thank you for your cooperation as we work to enhance local sustainability efforts.
