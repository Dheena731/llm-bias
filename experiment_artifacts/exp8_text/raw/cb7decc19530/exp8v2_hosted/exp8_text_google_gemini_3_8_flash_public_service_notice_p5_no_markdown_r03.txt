PUBLIC NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS

The Board of Elections announces that polling stations for the upcoming election will be open on Tuesday, November 5, from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. 

Voters are reminded that their assigned polling station may have changed since the previous election. To ensure you arrive at the correct site, please confirm your designated precinct before Election Day. 

You can find your polling location by visiting www.elections.gov/find-my-poll, checking your official voter registration card, or by calling the voter helpline at 1-800-555-0199. 

All designated polling locations are fully accessible to individuals with disabilities. You must vote at your assigned precinct on Election Day for your regular ballot to be counted.
