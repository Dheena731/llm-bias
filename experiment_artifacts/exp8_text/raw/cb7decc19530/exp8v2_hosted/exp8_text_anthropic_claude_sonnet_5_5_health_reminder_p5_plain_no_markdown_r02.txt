This is a reminder to refill your prescription soon. Check how many doses you have left, and if you are down to about a week's supply, contact your pharmacy to request the refill. Many pharmacies can process requests by phone or through their app, and it helps to have your prescription number nearby. If you have no refills remaining, call your doctor's office a few days ahead so they can send a new prescription. Try not to wait until you run out, since missing doses can affect how well your medication works.
