Season your vegetables twice: once before roasting and once right after they come out of the oven. Before roasting, toss them with enough oil to coat them lightly, plus a generous pinch of salt and some pepper, so the seasoning clings and the edges caramelize. Once they are out of the oven, taste one and finish with something bright and fresh, such as a squeeze of lemon, a splash of vinegar, a handful of chopped herbs, or a sprinkle of flaky salt. Delicate ingredients like fresh herbs, garlic that burns easily, and acidic touches lose their punch in high heat, so adding them at the end keeps the flavors vivid and the vegetables from tasting flat.
