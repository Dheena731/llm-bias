**Public Notice: Heat Advisory**

Due to extremely high temperatures expected in our area, a heat advisory has been issued. The National Weather Service forecasts temperatures to reach dangerous levels, posing a significant risk to public health, especially for vulnerable individuals such as the elderly, young children, and those with pre-existing medical conditions.

To stay safe, please take the following precautions:

- Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water and other cool fluids.
- Limit outdoor activities to early morning or evening when temperatures are cooler.
- Stay in air-conditioned spaces as much as possible.
- Check on vulnerable neighbors and family members to ensure their safety.

For more information and updates, please visit our local weather service website or contact your local health department. Your safety is our priority, and we urge everyone to take this heat advisory seriously.
