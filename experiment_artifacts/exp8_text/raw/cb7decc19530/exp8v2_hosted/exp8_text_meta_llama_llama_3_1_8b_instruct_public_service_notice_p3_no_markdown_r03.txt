NOTICE OF RECYCLING COLLECTION CHANGES

As part of our ongoing efforts to improve our recycling program, we are making some changes to our collection schedule.

Beginning on the first week of next month, recyclable materials will no longer be collected on the second and fourth Thursdays of each month. Instead, recyclables will be collected on the first and third Thursdays of each month.

This change is necessary to allow our collection team to work more efficiently and effectively. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your understanding and cooperation in this matter.

Please continue to place your recyclable materials in the designated bins on the first and third Thursdays of each month. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.

Thank you for your continued support of our recycling program.
