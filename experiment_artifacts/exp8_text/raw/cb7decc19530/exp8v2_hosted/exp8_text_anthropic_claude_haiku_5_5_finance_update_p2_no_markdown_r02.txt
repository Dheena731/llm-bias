We are writing to let you know that card transaction fees will change on March 1. Beginning on that date, a fee of 2.5% will apply to all credit and debit card payments, up from 2.0%. Payments made by bank transfer, cash, or check will not be affected.

This adjustment reflects higher processing costs charged by our payment providers over the past year. We have kept the increase as small as possible and have not changed our prices for goods or services.

If you have a recurring payment set up on your card, no action is needed. The new rate will apply automatically to charges processed on or after March 1. If you would like to avoid the fee, you can pay by bank transfer through the details on your invoice.

If you have any questions about this change, please contact our billing team at billing@example.com or call 555-0142 during business hours.
