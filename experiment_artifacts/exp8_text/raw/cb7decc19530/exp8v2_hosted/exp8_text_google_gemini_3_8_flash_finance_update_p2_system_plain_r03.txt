We are writing to let you know about an upcoming adjustment to our card processing fees. Effective November first, the fee for credit and debit card transactions will change from two point five percent plus ten cents to two point seven percent plus fifteen cents per transaction. This update is necessary due to increasing network interchange costs and ongoing investments in payment security and system reliability. We recognize that changes to operational expenses matter to your business, and we are committed to helping you navigate them. If you have questions about how this adjustment affects your account, or if you would like to explore lower cost payment alternatives such as direct bank transfers, please contact our customer support team. Thank you for your continued business and partnership.
