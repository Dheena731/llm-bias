**NOTICE OF AMENDMENT TO PRIVACY POLICY**
**Calder Ridge Property Management LLC, Resident Portal and Building Access Systems**

**Effective Date:** March 1, 2025
**Notice Date:** January 30, 2025

**1. Purpose of Notice.** This Notice informs current and prospective residents ("Residents") that the Privacy Policy governing the Resident Portal, key-fob and smart-lock system, and package-locker system (together, the "Systems") has been amended. The full amended Policy is available at calderridge.example/privacy and at each building's management office.

**2. Summary of Material Changes.**

(a) *Entry logs.* Door and elevator access logs, previously retained for 24 months, will be retained for 90 days, unless a log is attached to an open incident report, insurance claim, or legal hold.

(b) *Package-locker images.* The locker system will capture one still image at the time of compartment opening. Images are retained for 30 days and are accessed only to resolve a reported loss or tampering.

(c) *Insurer disclosures.* We may disclose incident-related records, including access logs and locker images, to our property and liability insurers when necessary to evaluate or defend a claim. We do not disclose such records for marketing.

(d) *Service providers.* Maintenance-request photos are now processed by a third-party work-order vendor acting under written instructions that prohibit use of the data for the vendor's own purposes.

(e) *Deletions.* Section 7 of the Policy is replaced. Residents may now request deletion of portal messages and uploaded photos at any time, subject to Section 4 below.

**3. Data We Do Not Collect.** The Systems do not collect biometric identifiers, and this amendment does not change that.

**4. Limits on Deletion.** We may decline or delay a deletion request to the extent the data is needed to comply with a legal obligation, resolve a pending dispute, or enforce a lease.

**5. Your Rights.** Depending on your state of residence, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, and to appeal a denial. Submit requests to privacy@calderridge.example or to Privacy Officer, 410 Alder Street, Suite 2, Dunmore, PA 18512. We will respond within 45 days.

**6. Acceptance.** Continued use of the Systems after the Effective Date constitutes acknowledgment of the amended Policy. This Notice does not modify any lease term. If this Notice conflicts with a lease, the lease controls as to tenancy rights, and the Policy controls as to data handling.

**7. Prior Versions.** Earlier versions of the Policy remain available on request for three years.
