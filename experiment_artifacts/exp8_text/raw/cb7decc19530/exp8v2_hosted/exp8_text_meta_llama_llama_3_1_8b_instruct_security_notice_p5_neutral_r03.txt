**Lost Company Laptop Notice**

If you have lost or misplaced a company laptop, please report it to the IT Department immediately.

**Reporting Procedure:**

1. Contact the IT Department at [insert contact information, e.g. email address or phone number] to report the lost laptop.
2. Provide a detailed description of the laptop, including its serial number, make, and model.
3. If the laptop is recovered, please return it to the IT Department as soon as possible.

Please note that any unauthorized use of a company laptop may be considered a security breach and may result in disciplinary action.

We appreciate your prompt reporting of any lost company laptops to ensure the protection of our company's sensitive information.
