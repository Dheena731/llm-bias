**Blue bin collections move to every other week from Monday 3 March**

Blue-lid bins in Eastfield, Cold Harbour and the Millbrook estate will be emptied fortnightly. Their collection day stays the same. Black-lid and garden waste schedules are unchanged.

Contamination in the blue bins has risen from about 14% to 22% since last summer. Redbank sorting centre has started rejecting loads, and rejected loads go to landfill. Fewer collections should mean less mixed material reaching the sorting line.

**Your next blue bin dates** are on the calendar delivered in January, or you can look up your address at [council website]/bins.

**What to check before you put the bin out:**
- Rinse cans, jars and yogurt pots. A little residue is fine; food is not.
- Flatten cardboard boxes. Pizza boxes with grease on them go in the black bin.
- Black plastic trays, bubble wrap and plastic bags are not accepted. Carrier bags can go to supermarket collection points.
- Shredded paper goes in a paper sack tied shut and placed on top of the blue bin. Loose shredding blows across the street and gets rejected.

If your blue bin is still full on the fortnight it's collected, call 0300 555 0142 and we'll arrange a one-off extra collection within five working days.
