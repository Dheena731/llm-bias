**Introducing the Simple Monthly Budget Template**

Keeping track of where your money goes shouldn't require complicated software. Our new budgeting spreadsheet gives you a clear, editable place to plan your income, record your expenses, and see how each month really turns out.

Here's what it offers:

- **Income and expense tracking:** Enter what you earn and what you spend in one place.
- **Category breakdowns:** Group spending into categories such as housing, food, and transportation to see where most of your money goes.
- **A monthly summary:** Totals update as you enter numbers, so you can see at a glance whether you are ahead or behind.

The template is fully editable. Rename categories, add rows, or change the layout to fit your household. No prior spreadsheet experience is needed.
