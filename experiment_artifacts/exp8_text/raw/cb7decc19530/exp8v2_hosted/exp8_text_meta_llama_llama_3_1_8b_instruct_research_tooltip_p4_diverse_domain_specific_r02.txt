Here's a concise tooltip explaining what a missing-value marker means, specific to the domain of data visualization and distinct from generic examples:

"Missing-value marker: A visual representation indicating that no data exists for a particular category or time period, often denoted by a blank or dotted line, or a custom symbol. This marker helps to:

* Identify gaps in data collection or reporting
* Highlight areas where data is sparse or absent
* Facilitate accurate interpretation of trends and patterns"
