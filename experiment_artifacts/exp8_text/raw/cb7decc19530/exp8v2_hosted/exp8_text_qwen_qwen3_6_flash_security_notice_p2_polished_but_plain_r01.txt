This notice serves to remind all employees of the ongoing risk associated with phishing emails. Cyber attackers frequently use deceptive messages to steal credentials or install malicious software on company devices. To protect yourself and the organization, please scrutinize every email for signs of fraud. Pay close attention to the sender's address, as legitimate communications will always come from verified domains. Be wary of urgent requests, unexpected attachments, or links asking you to verify account details. Never share passwords or sensitive data via email response.

If an email appears suspicious, forward it to the IT security team for analysis rather than replying or clicking any included links. Your prompt reporting helps us maintain a secure digital environment for everyone. We appreciate your continued diligence in keeping our workplace safe.
