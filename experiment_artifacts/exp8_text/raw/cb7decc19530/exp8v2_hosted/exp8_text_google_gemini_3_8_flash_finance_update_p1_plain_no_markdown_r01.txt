We have introduced a new monthly spending summary to help you keep better track of your finances. Starting this month, you will receive an automated overview of your expenses at the close of every billing cycle. This summary automatically groups your transactions into common categories such as groceries, utilities, and entertainment, allowing you to see exactly where your money goes. It also compares your current spending against previous months so you can spot trends and adjust your budget accordingly. You can review your latest summary by logging into your account dashboard, where past statements will also be stored for easy access. We hope this tool gives you clearer insight into your financial habits and helps you make informed decisions about your budget.
