Reminder to Staff

As a security precaution, it is essential that all staff members lock their computer screens when stepping away from their workstations. This includes leaving the room or taking a break. An unlocked screen can leave sensitive information vulnerable to unauthorized access.

Please make it a habit to lock your screen before leaving your workstation. This simple action helps maintain the confidentiality and integrity of our data.

If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT department.
