To make planning lunches for a busy week easier, consider setting aside some time on the weekend to prepare and portion out individual lunches for the next few days. This can be as simple as cooking a large batch of rice or grains, roasting some vegetables, and grilling some protein sources like chicken or tofu. You can then assemble the lunches in containers and refrigerate or freeze them until you're ready to grab them and go. Having a plan in place can help save time and reduce stress during the week, and can also help ensure that you're eating healthy and nutritious meals even on the most chaotic days.
