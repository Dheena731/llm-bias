**Reminder: Lock Your Screen When You Step Away**

To all staff,

Please lock your computer screen every time you leave your desk, even if you will be gone only for a moment.

An unlocked screen lets anyone nearby view or use your account and the information it contains. Locking your screen protects confidential information, your own work, and your colleagues.

**How to lock your screen:**
- **Windows:** Press the Windows key + L
- **Mac:** Press Control + Command + Q

Making this a habit takes only a second and makes a real difference. Thank you for your cooperation.
