We are introducing a new monthly spending summary designed to provide clearer visibility into your finances. Starting this month, an automated overview will be generated at the close of each billing cycle, breaking down your total expenditures across distinct categories. This report highlights your primary cost drivers, compares your current activity to the previous month, and helps identify trends in your purchasing habits. You can access these insights directly from your account dashboard to track your budget goals without manually sorting through individual transactions. If you wish to adjust how your expenses are grouped or update your delivery preferences, you can make those changes at any time within your profile settings.
