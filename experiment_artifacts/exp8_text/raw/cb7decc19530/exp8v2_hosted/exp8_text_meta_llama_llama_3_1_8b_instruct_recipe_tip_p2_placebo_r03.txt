**Tips for Preserving Fresh Herbs**

To keep your fresh herbs fresh for as long as possible, follow these simple steps:

1. **Trim the stems**: Before storing your herbs, trim the stems to about 1-2 inches long. This helps prevent the herbs from drying out and promotes healthy growth.
2. **Keep them moist**: Store your herbs in a humid environment, such as a plastic bag or a covered container with a damp paper towel. This will help maintain the herbs' moisture levels.
3. **Monitor the temperature**: Store your herbs in a cool, dry place, such as a pantry or cupboard. Avoid storing them near direct sunlight or heat sources.
4. **Change the water**: If you're storing herbs in a vase or container, change the water every 2-3 days to prevent bacterial growth.
5. **Use the right container**: Store your herbs in a container that allows for good air circulation, such as a mesh bag or a paper bag with holes.

By following these tips, you can enjoy your fresh herbs for up to 2 weeks or more. Remember to always check the herbs for signs of spoilage before using them in your cooking.
