Here are a few options depending on how you plan to send the notice.

### Option 1: Email / Memo (Professional & Clear)
**Subject:** 🔒 Quick Reminder: Lock Your Screen When Away

Hi Team,

Just a friendly reminder to **lock your computer screen** whenever you step away from your desk, even for a moment.

Locking your screen prevents unauthorized access to sensitive information and helps maintain our security standards.

**💡 Helpful Tips:**
*   **Windows:** Press `Win` + `L`
*   **Mac:** Press `Control` + `Command` + `Q`
*   You can also set your computer to auto-lock after a short period of inactivity via your system settings.

Thanks for helping keep our data and workplace secure!

Best regards,  
[Your Name/Department]

***

### Option 2: Slack / Teams Message (Short & Casual)
