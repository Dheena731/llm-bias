**PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE**

**Location:** Main Street (between 4th Avenue and Elm Street)  
**Dates & Times:** Monday, October 23, at 7:00 AM – Wednesday, October 25, at 5:00 PM  

Please be advised that Main Street will be closed to all through traffic between 4th Avenue and Elm Street to accommodate emergency water main repairs. 

* **Detour Route:** Traffic will be redirected via Oak Street. Detour signs will be posted.
* **Local Access:** Residents and emergency services will have limited access.
* **Parking:** Street parking will be prohibited in the work zone during these hours. Vehicles remaining will be towed at the owner's expense.

Motorists are encouraged to plan alternate routes and expect minor delays. We apologize for any inconvenience and appreciate your patience as we complete these necessary infrastructure upgrades.
