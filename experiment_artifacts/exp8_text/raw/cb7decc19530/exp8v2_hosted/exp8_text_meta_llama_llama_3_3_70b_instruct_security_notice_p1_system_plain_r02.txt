To all employees, it is essential to practice good password security habits to protect our company's systems and data. One of the most important habits to adopt is to use unique passwords for all of your accounts. Reusing passwords across multiple sites and systems can put our company at risk if one of those sites is compromised. If you use the same password for your company login as you do for a personal account, and that personal account is hacked, the attacker may be able to gain access to our company's systems as well. To avoid this risk, please make sure to use a different password for your company login than you use for any personal accounts. Additionally, consider using a password manager to help you generate and keep track of unique, complex passwords for all of your accounts. If you have any questions or concerns about password security, please don't hesitate to reach out to the IT department.
