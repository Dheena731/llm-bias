To plan lunches for a busy week, consider setting aside some time on the weekend to prepare as many components of your lunches as possible. This can include cooking proteins like chicken or beans, roasting vegetables, and assembling salads or sandwiches. Portion out individual servings in containers and store them in the refrigerator or freezer for easy grab-and-go meals throughout the week. You can also make a list of the lunches you plan to have each day and check the ingredients you need to ensure you have everything on hand. This can help save time and reduce stress during the week when you are busiest.
