Please ensure that you create unique passwords for every work-related account and service. Reusing passwords across different platforms increases the risk of unauthorized access because a compromise on one site can lead to the exposure of your credentials for other systems. Employees are required to avoid using personal email or social media passwords for corporate logins and to refrain from reusing existing work passwords when resetting them. Storing credentials securely in approved password management tools is encouraged to maintain distinct and strong access codes for all applications. Immediate reporting of any suspicious activity or suspected credential leakage is mandatory to protect company data and infrastructure.
