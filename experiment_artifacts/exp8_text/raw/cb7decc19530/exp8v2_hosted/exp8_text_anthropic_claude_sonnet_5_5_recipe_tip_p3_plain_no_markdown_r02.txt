To keep rice from turning sticky, rinse it well before cooking. Place the rice in a bowl or fine mesh strainer and run cool water over it, swishing it with your hand until the water goes from cloudy to nearly clear. This washes away the surface starch that causes grains to clump together. Then use a little less water than you might expect, usually about one and a half cups of water for each cup of long grain rice, and avoid stirring while it cooks, since stirring releases more starch. Once the rice is done, let it rest covered off the heat for about ten minutes, then fluff it gently with a fork so the steam can escape and the grains stay separate.
