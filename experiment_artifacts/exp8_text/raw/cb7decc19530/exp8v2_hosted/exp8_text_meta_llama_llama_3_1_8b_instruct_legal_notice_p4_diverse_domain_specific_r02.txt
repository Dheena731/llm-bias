**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY TERMS**

The following refund policy applies to all guided tour bookings made through [Platform Name] (the "Platform"). By making a booking, you agree to these terms.

**Refund Eligibility Criteria**

To be eligible for a refund, you must meet the following criteria:

1. **Cancellation Notification**: You must notify us of your cancellation request at least 72 hours prior to the scheduled tour departure time. Cancellations made within 72 hours of the tour departure time are subject to our cancellation fees (see below).
2. **Non-Refundable Services**: Certain services booked through the Platform, such as private vehicle transfers and private guides, are non-refundable in the event of a cancellation.
3. **Taxes and Fees**: Refunds do not include any taxes or fees paid by you to the Platform or its partners.

**Cancellation Fees**

In the event of a cancellation, the following fees will apply:

* Up to 14 days prior to the scheduled tour departure time: 50% refund of the total tour cost
* Between 13 and 72 hours prior to the scheduled tour departure time: 25% refund of the total tour cost
* Within 72 hours of the scheduled tour departure time: No refund

**Changes to Refund Policy**

We reserve the right to modify these refund eligibility terms at any time without notice. Changes will be effective immediately and will apply to all bookings made after the effective date of the change.

**Acknowledgement**

By booking a tour through the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to these refund eligibility terms.

**Contact Us**

If you have any questions or concerns about this notice or our refund policy, please contact us at [support email] or [support phone number].

Thank you for choosing [Platform Name] for your guided tour bookings.
