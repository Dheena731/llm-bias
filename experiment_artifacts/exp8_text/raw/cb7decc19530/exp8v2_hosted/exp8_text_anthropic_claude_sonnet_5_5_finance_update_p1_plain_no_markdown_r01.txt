We have added a monthly spending summary to your account. At the start of each month, you will now receive a brief overview of how much you spent during the previous month, along with a breakdown by category such as groceries, transportation, dining, and bills. The summary also compares your totals to the month before, so you can quickly see where your spending went up or down.

The goal is to make it easier to notice patterns without having to dig through individual transactions. If a category looks higher than expected, you can open it to see the purchases behind the number. The summary also highlights your largest single expenses and any recurring charges, which can help you catch subscriptions you may have forgotten about.

The summary is turned on by default and will arrive in your inbox and in the notifications section of the app. If you would rather not receive it, you can change that in your notification settings at any time. Your data is used only to create the summary for you and is not shared with anyone else.

We plan to keep improving this feature, and your feedback will help guide what we add next. If something in the summary is unclear or you have ideas for making it more useful, please let us know through the feedback option in the app.
