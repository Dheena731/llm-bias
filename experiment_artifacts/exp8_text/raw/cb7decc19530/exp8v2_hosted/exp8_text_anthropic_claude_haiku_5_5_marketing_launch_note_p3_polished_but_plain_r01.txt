Introducing the Monthly Budget Planner, a ready-to-use spreadsheet template designed to help you take control of your money without spending hours building a system from scratch. Whether you are tracking household expenses, planning for a big purchase, or simply trying to understand where your paycheck goes each month, this template gives you a clear, organized starting point.

The planner is organized into three connected sections. The income tab lets you record every source of earnings, from salaries and freelance payments to side income, and calculates your monthly total automatically. The expenses tab sorts spending into fixed costs such as rent, insurance, and subscriptions, and variable costs such as groceries, transportation, and dining out. Each category includes a budgeted amount and an actual amount, so you can see at a glance where you are on track and where you are overspending. The summary tab pulls everything together with a running balance, a savings rate, and a simple chart that shows how your spending is distributed across categories.

Setup takes about ten minutes. Enter your income and your expected costs, adjust the category names to match your life, and the formulas handle the rest. The template works in Excel, Google Sheets, and Apple Numbers, and it requires no prior spreadsheet experience. Each month, you simply duplicate the sheet, reset the actual amounts, and begin again, which builds a clear record of your spending over time.
