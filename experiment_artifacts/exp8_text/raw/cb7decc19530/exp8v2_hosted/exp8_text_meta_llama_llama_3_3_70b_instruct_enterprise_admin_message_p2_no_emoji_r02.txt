Attention all staff, 

We are introducing a new expense approval workflow to improve efficiency and transparency in our financial processes. The new workflow will require all expenses to be submitted through our online portal, where they will be reviewed and approved by department managers before being forwarded to the finance team for reimbursement. 

Further details and instructions on the new workflow can be found on the company intranet. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the finance team. 

Thank you for your cooperation and understanding in this matter.
