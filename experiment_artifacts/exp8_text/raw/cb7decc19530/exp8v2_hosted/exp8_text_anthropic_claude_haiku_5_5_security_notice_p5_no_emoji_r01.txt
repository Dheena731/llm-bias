**Notice: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If you have lost a company-issued laptop, or it has been stolen, report it immediately. Prompt reporting helps protect company data and reduces security risks.

**What to do:**

1. **Report it right away.** Contact the IT Service Desk by phone or email as soon as you notice the laptop is missing. Do not wait to see if it turns up.
2. **Provide details.** Include the laptop's make, model, asset tag or serial number, the date and time you last had it, and where you think it was lost or stolen.
3. **Report theft to the police.** If the laptop was stolen, file a police report and provide the reference number to the IT team.
4. **Do not attempt to recover or access the device yourself.** IT will take the necessary steps to secure company data remotely.
5. **Cooperate with the investigation.** Respond promptly to any requests from IT, Security, or Human Resources.

**Contact:** IT Service Desk, [phone number] | [email address]

Failure to report a lost or stolen device promptly may result in disciplinary action in accordance with company policy.
