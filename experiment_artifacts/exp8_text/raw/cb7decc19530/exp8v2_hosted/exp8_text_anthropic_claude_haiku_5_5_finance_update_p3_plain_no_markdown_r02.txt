We're excited to share that a new savings goal feature is now available in the app. It lets you set a target amount, choose a deadline, and track your progress toward it over time. You can create as many goals as you like, whether you're saving for a down payment, an emergency fund, or a trip you've been dreaming about.

Each goal shows how much you have saved so far, how much remains, and how much you need to set aside each week or month to reach it on time. You can add funds manually whenever you're ready, and the app will update your progress and let you know when you're getting close to a milestone.

To get started, open the Goals tab from the main menu and select "New goal." Give it a name, enter your target and date, and you're set. We hope this helps you turn your plans into progress, and we'd love to hear how you're using it. Send us your feedback anytime through the Help section in settings.
