**NOTICE: Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Now Required**

To strengthen account security and protect sensitive data, two-factor authentication (2FA) is now mandatory for all users. Please enable 2FA on your account by **[Deadline Date]**.

**How to Enable 2FA:**
1. Log in to your account and go to **Settings → Security**.
2. Select **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Choose your preferred verification method (an authenticator app is highly recommended; SMS is also available) and follow the on-screen prompts.

Once activated, you will be prompted to enter a verification code each time you log in. This additional layer of security drastically reduces the risk of unauthorized access.
