To achieve fluffy rice with distinct, separate grains, the most critical step is thoroughly rinsing the dry rice before it ever touches the heat. Place the rice in a fine-mesh sieve and run cold water over it, gently agitating the grains with your fingers until the runoff is completely clear. This washes away the loose surface starch that causes the cooked rice to clump and turn gummy. Once cooking begins, leave the pot covered and avoid stirring, which only releases more starch into the liquid. After the water is absorbed, remove the pot from the heat and let it rest undisturbed with the lid on for ten minutes. This allows the remaining steam to finish cooking the rice gently and sets the grains before you fluff them with a fork.
