Treat herbs according to stem texture rather than leaf shape:

* **Soft stems (parsley, cilantro, dill, mint):** Treat them like cut flowers. Trim a quarter-inch off the stems, stand them upright in a jar with an inch of cold water, and tent loosely with a plastic bag to maintain humidity without trapping moisture on the leaves. Store them in the fridge door—**except basil**. Basil suffers chilling injury below 50°F, which triggers enzymatic browning and turns the leaves black; keep it on the counter.
* **Woody stems (rosemary, thyme, sage, oregano):** Wrap the unwashed sprigs loosely in a barely damp paper towel and seal them in an airtight container or zip-top bag in the crisper drawer. This arrests desiccation while preventing the standing condensation that causes fungal rot.
