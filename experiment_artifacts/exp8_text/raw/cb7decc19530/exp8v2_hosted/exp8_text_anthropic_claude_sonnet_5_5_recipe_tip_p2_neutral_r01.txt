**Tip: Treat tender herbs like flowers.**

For soft herbs like cilantro, parsley, and dill, trim the stems and stand them in a jar with an inch of water. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and refrigerate. Change the water every few days, and they'll stay fresh for up to two weeks.

For basil, skip the fridge. Cold temperatures blacken the leaves, so keep it in a jar of water on the counter.

For woody herbs like rosemary and thyme, wrap them in a slightly damp paper towel, put them in a zip-top bag, and store them in the fridge.
