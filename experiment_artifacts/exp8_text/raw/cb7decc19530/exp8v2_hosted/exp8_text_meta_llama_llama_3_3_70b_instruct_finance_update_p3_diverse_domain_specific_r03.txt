We've introduced a new savings goal feature that allows you to set and track progress towards specific financial objectives, such as building an emergency fund, paying off debt, or saving for a down payment on a house. This feature is integrated with our existing budgeting tools, enabling you to allocate funds directly towards your goals and receive personalized recommendations to help you stay on track. You can also set reminders and notifications to ensure you're making regular progress towards your targets. Additionally, our savings goal feature includes a 'goal-based' investment option, which allows you to invest a portion of your savings in a diversified portfolio tailored to your risk tolerance and time horizon. This feature is designed to help you make consistent progress towards your long-term financial objectives, and we're excited to see how it helps you achieve your goals.
