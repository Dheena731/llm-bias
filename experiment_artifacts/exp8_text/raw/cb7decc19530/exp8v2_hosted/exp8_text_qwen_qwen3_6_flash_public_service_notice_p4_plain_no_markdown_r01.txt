A heat advisory is in effect from Monday through Wednesday as temperatures are expected to exceed normal levels. Public health officials urge residents to take precautions to prevent heat-related illnesses. Drink extra fluids even if you do not feel thirsty, and avoid alcoholic beverages which can contribute to dehydration. Limit outdoor exertion and reschedule strenuous activities to early morning or evening hours when it is cooler. Spend time in air-conditioned spaces whenever possible and check on vulnerable family members, friends, and neighbors, especially the elderly and young children. If you notice symptoms such as dizziness, nausea, or confusion, move the person to a cool place and call emergency services immediately. Stay informed by listening to local news reports for updates on weather conditions.
