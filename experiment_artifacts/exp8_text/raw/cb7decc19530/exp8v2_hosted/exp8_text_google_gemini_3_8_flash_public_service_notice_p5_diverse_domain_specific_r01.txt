**OFFICIAL NOTICE: DESIGNATION OF POLLING SITES FOR PRECINCTS 12, 14, AND 18-B**  
*Office of the City Clerk | Elections Division*  

Electors registered in Wards 4 and 6 should note the following site reassignments taking effect for the upcoming November 5 Municipal Election. These adjustments reflect facility accessibility reviews and boundary consolidations approved by the Board of Canvassers under Municipal Code § 17-104.

* **Precinct 12 (formerly North Junior High):** Relocated to the **Tri-County Community Center**, 410 E. Lincoln Ave. (Main Auditorium). Use Entrance B off the east parking apron. ADA-compliant ground-floor access; designated curbside intake available on Lincoln Ave.
* **Precinct 14 (Unchanged):** **Fire Station No. 3 (Bay 1)**, 922 S. 14th St. 
* **Precinct 18-B (Split Consolidation):** Electors residing east of the B&O rail line will no longer report to the Township Maintenance Depot. Your designated site is now **St. Paul’s Parish Hall**, 1108 Elm St. Voters residing west of the tracks remain assigned to Lincoln Elementary.

**Key Reminders for Election Day:**
* Polls remain open from **6:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m.** Any elector in line by 7:30 p.m. is permitted to vote.
* You must cast your ballot at your designated precinct. Ballots cast at an unassigned site must be processed as provisional and will not tally through the optical scanners on site.
* The 100-foot electioneering buffer zone will be marked from the primary exterior door of each location.

To verify your specific precinct ledger or review a specimen ballot before reporting to the polls, enter your address into the state voter registry at `elections.state.gov/lookup` or contact the Elections Clerk at (555) 018-2204.
