**Tip: Prep components, not full meals.**

On Sunday, spend 30–45 minutes cooking a few basics: a grain (rice or quinoa), a protein (grilled chicken, hard-boiled eggs, or chickpeas), and a tray of roasted vegetables. Wash and chop some raw veggies and make one versatile sauce or dressing.

Each day, mix and match into bowls, wraps, or salads. This keeps lunches varied without cooking something new every day, and it makes it easy to adjust if your plans change midweek.

**Bonus:** Pack lunches the night before so your morning is grab-and-go.
