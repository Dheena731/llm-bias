**COUNTY DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS**  
**TRAFFIC REGULATION NOTICE: FULL ROAD CLOSURE**  
**Project No.:** 2024-PW-088  
**Corridor:** Lower River Road (County Route 414), Milepost 4.2 (Ten-Mile Run) to Milepost 5.6 (Weir Creek Road)  
**Effective Dates:** Monday, October 21 through Friday, October 25, 2024  
**Duration:** Continuous 24-hour closure  

**Scope of Work:**  
Contractors will execute a full-depth cross-trench excavation for precast box-culvert replacement and subgrade stabilization. Heavy equipment will occupy both travel lanes; road deck will be fully severed at Station 22+50.

**Access & Impacts:**  
*   **Through Traffic:** Prohibited. No passable surface will exist at the work zone.  
*   **Local Properties (Parcels #104 through #188):** Approach from the south via Old Post Road.  
*   **Local Properties (Parcels #190 through #240):** Approach from the north via State Route 9.  
*   **Emergency Responders:** Briefed on dead-end limits; split-district dispatch protocols are active for the duration of the work.  
*   **Waste & Mail Services:** Scheduled morning access via flagger-assisted staging before 07:00 daily.

**Signed Detour:**  
Traffic will be diverted via SR 9 North to Blue Rock Cutoff, reconnecting at Weir Creek Road (approx. 5.2-mile addition). Variable message boards will activate on the approaches 48 hours prior to closure.

*Field operations are weather-dependent. Direct technical questions to the Resident Engineer at (555) 019-4482 or via dispatch at roads@county.gov.*
