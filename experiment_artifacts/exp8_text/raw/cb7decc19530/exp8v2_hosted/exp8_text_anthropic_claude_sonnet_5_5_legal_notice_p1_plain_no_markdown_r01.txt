NOTICE OF CHANGES TO YOUR ACCOUNT TERMS

This notice informs you that we are amending the terms that govern your account. Please read it carefully and keep a copy for your records.

Effective thirty days after the date of this notice, the Account Agreement between you and us will be amended as follows. The monthly maintenance fee will increase from five dollars to seven dollars. The fee for returned or declined transactions will increase from twenty-five dollars to thirty dollars per item. The section governing dispute resolution will be revised to require that any claim arising from your account first be submitted to us in writing, and that we be given sixty days to respond before either party begins formal proceedings. All other provisions of the Account Agreement will remain unchanged and in full force.

You are not required to take any action to accept these changes. If you continue to use your account after the effective date, including by making deposits, withdrawals, or payments, you will be deemed to have accepted the amended terms. If you do not agree with the changes, you may close your account before the effective date without incurring any early closure fee, provided that you pay any outstanding balance and any charges incurred before closure.

If any part of the amended terms is found to be unenforceable, the remaining parts will continue to apply. Where this notice conflicts with an earlier version of the Account Agreement, this notice will control as to the matters described above.

A complete copy of the amended Account Agreement is available on request at any of our branches or by contacting our customer service department using the details printed on your most recent statement. If you have questions about these changes or about your rights under the agreement, please contact us before the effective date.

Thank you for banking with us.
