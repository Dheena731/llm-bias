Remember to drink enough water throughout the day to stay hydrated and maintain your overall health. Aim to drink at least eight cups (64 ounces) of water each day, and adjust this amount based on your individual needs, activity level, and climate. Drinking sufficient water can help boost your energy, support digestion, and keep your skin looking its best. Make it a habit to drink water regularly, such as when you wake up, at mealtimes, and before and after exercise.
