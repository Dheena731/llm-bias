Here's a reminder to drink enough water during the day, tailored to the domain of physical activity and outdoor adventure:

**Hydrate for Adventure**

Don't let dehydration slow you down! Aim to drink at least 16 ounces of water for every hour of physical activity or time spent in the sun. Take a sip every 20 minutes during long hikes or bike rides, and drink a full bottle after each intense exercise session. Your body will thank you for the extra hydration, and you'll be ready to take on the next challenge.
