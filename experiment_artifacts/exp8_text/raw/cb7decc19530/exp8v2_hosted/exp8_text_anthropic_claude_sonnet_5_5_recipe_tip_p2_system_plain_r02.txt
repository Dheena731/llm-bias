Treat tender herbs like cilantro, parsley, and mint the way you would treat cut flowers. Trim the bottoms of the stems, stand the bunch in a jar with an inch or two of water, and loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag before putting it in the refrigerator. Change the water every couple of days and the herbs will stay fresh for a week or two. Basil is the exception, since cold damages it, so keep it in a jar of water on the counter away from direct sun. Sturdier herbs like rosemary and thyme do best wrapped in a slightly damp paper towel and tucked into a container or bag in the fridge.
