Notice of Revised Library Opening Hours

Beginning the first Monday of next month, the Central Public Library and all of its branch locations will operate under new opening hours. These changes are intended to better serve the needs of our community following a recent review of visitor patterns and feedback from residents.

The Central Library will be open Monday through Thursday from 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., on Friday from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., on Saturday from 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., and on Sunday from 12:00 noon to 4:00 p.m. Branch libraries will be open Tuesday through Saturday from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. and will remain closed on Sundays and Mondays.

Please note that all locations will be closed on public holidays. Early closures may also apply on the days before major holidays, and any such changes will be posted at library entrances and on the library website at least one week in advance.

Book drops will remain available at all times for the return of borrowed items. Materials returned after closing will be checked in on the next business day, and no late fees will be charged for items placed in the drop before the due date has passed.

Residents who rely on library computers, printing services, or meeting rooms are encouraged to plan their visits around the new schedule. Meeting rooms may be reserved up to thirty days ahead by calling the front desk or visiting in person.

If you have questions about the new hours or require assistance with accessibility services, please speak with any staff member or call the main information line during opening hours. We thank you for your continued support and look forward to welcoming you.
