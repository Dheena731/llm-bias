Software updates matter because they keep your devices and data secure, stable, and useful. Developers regularly release patches that fix security vulnerabilities, which are weaknesses that hackers can exploit to steal information, install malware, or take control of a system. Skipping updates leaves these known holes open. Updates also correct bugs and crashes, improve performance, and maintain compatibility with other software, hardware, and online services. In addition, they can add new features and support for changing standards. Keeping software current is one of the simplest and most effective ways to protect yourself and your data.
