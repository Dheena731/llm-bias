Please be reminded that using the same password across multiple accounts poses a significant security risk to our organization. When an external website or personal account experiences a data breach, cybercriminals routinely test those stolen credentials against workplace systems to gain unauthorized access. To safeguard our network and company data, you must ensure that your work password is completely unique and never reused for personal email, banking, or social media accounts. We strongly encourage the use of our approved password manager to create and store complex credentials for all of your applications. If you suspect that your workplace password has been compromised, or if you are currently using it for other services, please update it immediately and contact the IT support team.
