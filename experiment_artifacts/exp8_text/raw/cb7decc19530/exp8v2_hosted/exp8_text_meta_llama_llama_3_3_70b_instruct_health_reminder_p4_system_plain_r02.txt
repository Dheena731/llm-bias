It's easy to get caught up in work or entertainment and spend hours staring at a screen, but it's important to remember to take breaks. Looking at a screen for too long can cause eye strain, headaches, and fatigue. Taking short breaks to stretch, move around, and rest your eyes can help alleviate these issues and improve your overall well-being. Try to take a break every hour to stand up, stretch, and focus on something other than a screen for a few minutes. This simple habit can make a big difference in how you feel and can help you stay healthy and productive.
