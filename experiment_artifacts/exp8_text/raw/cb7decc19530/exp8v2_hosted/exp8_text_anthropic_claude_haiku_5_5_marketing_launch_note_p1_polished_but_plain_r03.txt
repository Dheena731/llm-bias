Introducing Tally, the simple way for small businesses to manage money without the spreadsheet headaches.

Today we are proud to announce the public launch of Tally, a mobile and web app built for the owners of small businesses who wear every hat themselves. Tally brings invoicing, expense tracking, payment reminders, and a clear view of cash flow into one place, so you can spend less time on bookkeeping and more time serving the customers who keep your doors open.

Getting started takes about five minutes. Connect your bank account, send your first invoice from a template, and Tally will automatically match incoming payments to the right client. When a bill is overdue, the app sends a polite reminder on your behalf, and you decide how firm or friendly that message should be. At the end of each month, you receive a plain-language summary of what came in, what went out, and what you can reasonably expect next.

We built Tally with small teams in mind. There are no complicated accounting terms to learn, and no monthly fee for your first three months. Your data stays encrypted, and you can export your records to your accountant at any time.

Tally is available now on iOS, Android, and the web. Existing users who have been testing the beta will see new features rolling out this week, including recurring invoices and a mileage tracker. We would love to hear what you think, and our support team is available around the clock to help you get the most from your first weeks.

Thank you for trusting us with the work you have poured into your business. We are excited to help you run it with more clarity and less stress.
