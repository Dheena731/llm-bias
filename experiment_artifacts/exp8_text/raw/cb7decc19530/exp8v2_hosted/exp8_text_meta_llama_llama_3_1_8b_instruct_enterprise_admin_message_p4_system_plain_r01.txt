To: All Users

As part of our ongoing efforts to ensure the integrity and security of our systems, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews.

These reviews will be used to verify that each user still requires their current level of access to our systems and data. We will be checking that each user's access is still aligned with their job responsibilities and that they are using their access in accordance with our security policies.

If a user's access is found to be excessive or not justified, we will take steps to reduce their access accordingly.

We will be contacting users directly to schedule these reviews. Users will be asked to provide information about their job responsibilities and any changes that may have occurred since their last review.

If you have any questions about the quarterly access reviews or your access to our systems, please do not hesitate to contact the Help Desk.
