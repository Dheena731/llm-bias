Toss your vegetables in oil and salt before they go into the oven, not after they come out. The salt draws out a little moisture and helps the surface brown, while the oil carries seasonings evenly across every piece. Use about a teaspoon of kosher salt per pound of vegetables, and add dried herbs or spices like garlic powder, smoked paprika, or cumin at the same time. Save delicate fresh herbs and a squeeze of lemon for the moment the roasted vegetables leave the pan, since heat would dull their flavor.
