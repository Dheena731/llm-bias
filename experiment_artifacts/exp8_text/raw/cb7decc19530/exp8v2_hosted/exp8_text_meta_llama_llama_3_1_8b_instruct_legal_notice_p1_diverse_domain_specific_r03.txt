**NOTICE OF TERMS CHANGE**

Effective January 15, 2023, the following changes will be made to the account terms for users of the BioSpark online platform:

**Change 1: Payment Terms**

The payment due date for monthly subscriptions will be moved forward to the 5th day of each month, rather than the 10th day as previously stated. This change applies to all existing and new accounts.

**Change 2: Cancellation Policy**

The cancellation policy for annual contracts has been revised to allow for a 30-day notice period prior to cancellation, rather than the previously stated 60-day notice period. This change applies to all existing and new accounts.

**Change 3: Data Storage Limits**

The data storage limits for free accounts have been increased to 5 GB, up from 2 GB. This change applies to all free accounts and will take effect on February 15, 2023.

**Existing Users**

If you have any questions or concerns regarding these changes, please contact our support team at [support@biospark.com](mailto:support@biospark.com) by January 31, 2023.

**Acknowledgement**

By continuing to use the BioSpark online platform, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to the revised account terms, including these changes.

**Acceptance**

Your continued use of the BioSpark online platform after January 15, 2023, will be deemed acceptance of these revised terms.
