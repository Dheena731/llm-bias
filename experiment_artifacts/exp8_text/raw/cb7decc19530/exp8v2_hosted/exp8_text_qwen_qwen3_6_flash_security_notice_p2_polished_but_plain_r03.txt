Attention all employees, we are writing to highlight the importance of recognizing and reporting phishing emails. Phishing refers to fraudulent communications that appear to come from reputable sources but are designed to trick recipients into surrendering sensitive data or installing malware. Please exercise caution when reviewing your inbox and look for warning signs such as generic salutations, urgent demands for action, requests for financial information, and inconsistencies in the sender's address or domain.
