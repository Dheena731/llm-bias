If you have misplaced or lost your company-issued laptop, you must report the incident to the information technology help desk and the corporate security team immediately. Prompt reporting is critical to protecting proprietary data and preventing unauthorized access to internal systems. When submitting your report, please provide the asset tag number, serial number, the last known location of the device, and the approximate time it went missing. If the laptop was stolen, you are also required to file a report with local law enforcement and submit a copy of the police report to human resources. Please treat this matter with the utmost urgency and contact the IT department by phone or via the emergency security extension as soon as you realize the device is unaccounted for.
