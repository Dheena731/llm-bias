**CITY OF MARLOW, DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS**
**Notice of Temporary Road Closure**

**Harlan Street, from Ninth Avenue to Colfax Road**
**Monday, March 10, 7:00 a.m., through Friday, March 21, 5:00 p.m.**
(Rain days may extend work to Friday, March 28.)

**Reason:** Replacement of about 640 feet of 6-inch cast iron water main installed in 1928, and reconnection of 22 household service lines.

**Detour:** Northbound traffic: Ninth Avenue to Pruitt Street to Colfax Road. Southbound traffic: reverse. Detour signs will be posted. Trucks over 10 tons should use Route 4.

**For residents on the block:**
- **Parking:** No parking on either side of Harlan from 6:00 p.m. Sunday, March 9. Vehicles left in the work zone will be moved to Ninth Avenue at the owner's expense.
- **Driveways:** You can use your driveway until crews reach your address. A crew member will notify you 24 hours beforehand. Expect 1 to 3 days when the trench is open in front of your property.
- **Water shutoff:** Each address will lose service once, for up to 4 hours between 9:00 a.m. and 3:00 p.m. A door hanger will give your date 48 hours ahead. Run your cold taps for a few minutes after service returns, as the water may be cloudy.
- **Trash and recycling:** Put carts out at the corner of Ninth Avenue or Colfax Road on your usual day.
- **Emergency access:** Fire and ambulance vehicles will have access throughout the closure.

**Transit:** Route 12 will skip the Harlan & 10th and Harlan & Dunmore stops. Use the temporary stop on Ninth Avenue at Harlan.

**After the closure:** Temporary asphalt will cover the trench until permanent paving this spring. That will require a one-day lane closure, and we will post notice 5 days before.

**Questions:** Project inspector Dana Reyes, 555-0142, weekdays 7:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. After hours, call the Public Works line at 555-0100.
