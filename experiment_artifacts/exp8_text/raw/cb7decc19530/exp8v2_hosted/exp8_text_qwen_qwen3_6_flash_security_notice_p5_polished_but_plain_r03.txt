If you discover that a company laptop is missing, you must report the incident to the Information Technology department without delay. Prompt notification allows the team to take immediate steps to secure corporate data and prevent unauthorized access. When contacting support, please provide your full name, employee identification number, and a description of how and when the device was lost or stolen. Include any relevant location details or circumstances surrounding the event to assist with the investigation. Do not attempt to locate the hardware yourself if theft is suspected, as personal safety takes precedence over equipment recovery. Once reported, the IT team will remotely disable the account and initiate the necessary protocols for replacement or recovery. Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining our information security standards.
