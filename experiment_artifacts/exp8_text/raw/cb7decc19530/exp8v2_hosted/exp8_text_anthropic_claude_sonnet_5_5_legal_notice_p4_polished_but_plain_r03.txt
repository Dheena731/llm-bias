NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

This Notice describes the circumstances in which a purchaser may obtain a refund for products or services bought from the Company. By completing a purchase, the purchaser acknowledges that they have read and accepted the terms set out below.

A purchaser is eligible for a full refund if a written request is submitted to the Company within thirty days of the original date of purchase, and the request is accompanied by proof of payment, such as a receipt, order confirmation, or account statement entry. Physical goods must be returned in their original condition and packaging, unused except as reasonably necessary to inspect them, and the purchaser is responsible for the cost of return shipping unless the goods arrived defective or were shipped in error. Digital goods and subscription services are eligible for a refund only if the purchaser has not downloaded, accessed, or substantially used the product or service, or if the product or service is materially different from its description at the time of sale.

A refund will not be available in the following circumstances: where the request is made after the thirty-day period has expired; where goods have been damaged, altered, or misused by the purchaser; where the item was identified at the time of sale as final sale, custom-made, or non-refundable; or where the Company reasonably determines that the request results from fraud, abuse of this policy, or a violation of the Company's terms of service. Partial refunds may be granted at the Company's discretion where an item is returned with minor signs of use or is missing non-essential components.

Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within fourteen business days after the Company receives the request, or after returned goods have been received and inspected, whichever is later. The Company is not responsible for delays caused by the purchaser's financial institution. Where the original payment method is no longer available, the Company may issue the refund by an alternative method of equal value.

Nothing in this Notice limits any rights that a purchaser may hold under applicable consumer protection law, including statutory remedies for defective goods or services that cannot be waived by agreement. If this Notice conflicts with any such mandatory right, the mandatory right will prevail to the extent of the conflict.

The Company may amend this Notice at any time, and amendments will apply only to purchases made after the amended Notice takes effect. Questions or refund requests should be directed in writing to the Company's customer support department at the contact address provided on the purchaser's order confirmation.
