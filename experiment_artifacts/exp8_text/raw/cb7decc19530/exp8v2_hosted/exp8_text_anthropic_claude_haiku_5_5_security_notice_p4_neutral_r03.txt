**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication**

To help protect your account and personal information, we strongly recommend turning on two-factor authentication (2FA). With 2FA, signing in requires both your password and a second verification step, such as a code sent to your phone or generated by an authentication app.

**How to enable it:**
1. Log in to your account and go to **Settings**.
2. Select **Security**, then choose **Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Follow the prompts to link your phone or authentication app.
4. Save your backup codes in a secure place.

Enabling 2FA takes only a few minutes and significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access, even if your password is compromised.

If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact our support team.

Thank you for helping us keep your account secure.
