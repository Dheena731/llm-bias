Polling Station Locations

To find your designated polling station for the upcoming election, please consult the official voter registration card issued by the electoral office. If you have misplaced your card, you can contact the electoral office directly to obtain the relevant information.

Alternatively, you can also visit the electoral office's website or contact them by phone to obtain a list of polling stations in your area. The electoral office will provide you with the locations of all designated polling stations, along with their corresponding addresses and contact details.

If you are unsure about your polling station or need assistance in locating it, you can also visit your local council's office or contact a local community group for guidance.
