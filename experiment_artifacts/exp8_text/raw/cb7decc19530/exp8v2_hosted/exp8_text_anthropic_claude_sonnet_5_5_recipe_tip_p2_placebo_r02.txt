**Tip: Store fresh herbs like cut flowers (for soft herbs) or wrapped in a damp towel (for hardy herbs).**

**Soft herbs (parsley, cilantro, dill, mint):** Trim the ends of the stems and place the bunch upright in a jar with about an inch of water. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and refrigerate. Change the water every few days. Basil is the exception: it is sensitive to cold, so keep it in a jar of water on the counter, out of direct sunlight.

**Hardy herbs (rosemary, thyme, sage, oregano):** Wrap them loosely in a slightly damp paper towel, place them in a resealable bag or container, and keep them in the refrigerator.

**Before storing:** Remove any wilted or yellowing leaves, since they can speed up spoilage. Wait to wash the herbs until just before you use them, because extra moisture can cause them to rot sooner.
