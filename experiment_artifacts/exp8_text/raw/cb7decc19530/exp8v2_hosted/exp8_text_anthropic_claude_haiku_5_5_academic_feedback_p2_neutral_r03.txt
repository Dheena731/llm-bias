**Feedback on Methods Section**

Thank you for your methods section. You describe your participant recruitment and survey procedure clearly, and the step-by-step account of data collection would let another researcher replicate the study.

**Strengths**
- The sampling criteria are stated explicitly, including inclusion and exclusion conditions.
- You justified your choice of survey instrument by citing its reliability in previous studies.

**Areas to develop**
1. **Sample size:** Explain how you determined the number of participants. A brief power calculation or a reference to a comparable study would strengthen this.
2. **Analysis:** You name the statistical tests but not why they suit your data. State the assumptions you checked (e.g., normality, independence) and what you did if they were violated.
3. **Limitations:** Briefly acknowledge constraints in the design, such as the self-selected sample or the single-site recruitment, and note how they might affect interpretation.
4. **Consistency:** Use the same terms for your variables throughout. "Engagement" and "participation" appear to refer to the same construct, so choose one and define it.

**Priority for revision:** Start with point 2. Strengthening the analysis rationale will have the biggest effect on the credibility of your results.

Overall, this is a solid foundation. With these revisions, the section will be well organized and easy to follow.
