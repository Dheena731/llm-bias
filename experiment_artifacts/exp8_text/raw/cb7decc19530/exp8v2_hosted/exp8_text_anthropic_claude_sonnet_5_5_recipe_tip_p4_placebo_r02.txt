**Tip: Cook once, assemble all week.**

On the weekend (or whichever day is calmest), spend 45 to 60 minutes preparing a few building blocks instead of full meals:

- **A grain or starch:** rice, quinoa, or roasted potatoes
- **A protein:** baked chicken, hard-boiled eggs, or a pot of beans
- **Two vegetables:** one roasted, one raw and washed (such as carrots or cucumbers)
- **A sauce or dressing:** this makes the same ingredients taste different from day to day

Store each item in its own container. Each morning or the night before, combine a portion of each into a lunch. For example, Monday could be a rice bowl with chicken and roasted vegetables, and Tuesday could be a salad with the same chicken, eggs, and a different dressing.

**Why it works:** You make only one big decision, what to prep, instead of deciding every day. Keeping the components separate also helps food stay fresh and keeps you from eating the exact same meal five days in a row.

**Food safety note:** Eat cooked items within about four days, or freeze portions for the later days of the week.
