Subject: How to Report a Lost Company Laptop

If you have lost a company laptop, or believe it has been stolen, please report it to the IT Service Desk as soon as possible, and no later than 24 hours after you notice it is missing. Prompt reporting allows the security team to take steps to protect company data, including remotely locking or wiping the device where possible.

To report the loss, contact the IT Service Desk by phone or email, and include the laptop's asset tag number, the make and model, the date and approximate time you last had it, and where you think it may have been lost. If the laptop was stolen, please also file a police report and send a copy of the report number to the Service Desk.

Please do not attempt to access company systems from a personal device to search for the laptop, and do not try to recover it yourself if it may have been taken by someone else. Your cooperation helps keep company and customer information secure.

If you are unsure whether a device is considered lost or whether you need to report an incident, contact the IT Service Desk anyway. It is always better to report early than to delay.
