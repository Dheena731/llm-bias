We are pleased to announce the launch of Clear Ledger, a budgeting spreadsheet template designed for people who want to understand their money without spending hours learning complicated software.

Clear Ledger brings your income, expenses, savings goals, and debts together in one workbook. You enter your monthly income and your regular bills, and the template builds a full picture of where your money is going. Spending categories update automatically as you log purchases, and a simple dashboard shows how much you have left in each category, how close you are to your savings targets, and how your month compares to the last one.

We built the template to work in both Excel and Google Sheets, so you can use it on your computer, tablet, or phone. Every formula is already in place, but nothing is locked, which means you can rename categories, add new ones, or change the layout to suit your household. A short setup guide inside the file walks you through the first ten minutes, and most people are up and running before their coffee gets cold.

Whether you are paying down a credit card, saving for a trip, or just trying to make the end of the month feel less stressful, Clear Ledger gives you a steady and honest view of your finances. It is available today as a one-time purchase, with free updates for the life of the product.
