Notice of Changes to Recycling Collection

Beginning Monday, the first of the month, recycling collection in our community will move from a weekly schedule to an every-other-week schedule. Collection will continue on your current pickup day, but your recycling bin will be emptied only on alternating weeks. A printed calendar showing your exact pickup dates will be mailed to every household within the next two weeks, and a copy can also be requested by calling the public works office.

Along with the new schedule, the program will now accept a wider range of materials. Cartons used for milk, broth, and juice may be placed in the bin, as may empty aerosol cans and clean aluminum foil that has been balled up to at least the size of a fist. Plastic bags, foam packaging, and food-soiled paper will continue to be excluded, since they interfere with sorting equipment and can cause entire loads to be rejected.

To make the transition easier, please rinse containers briefly, leave lids on bottles, and place all items loose in the bin rather than bagged. Bins should be set at the curb by seven in the morning on your collection day, with at least three feet of clearance on every side. Households that find the new schedule does not provide enough space may request a second bin at no charge through the public works office.

If a holiday falls during your collection week, pickup will occur one day later. We appreciate your cooperation and your continued effort to keep our recycling program clean and effective. Questions about the changes may be directed to the public works office during regular business hours.
