Please be advised of an ongoing increase in phishing attempts targeting our organization. Phishing emails are fraudulent messages designed to trick you into revealing sensitive company information, sharing login credentials, or downloading harmful software onto our network. These messages often appear to come from legitimate organizations, clients, or even company leadership, and they frequently convey a false sense of urgency. Always carefully check the sender address for misspellings, and never open unexpected attachments or follow unfamiliar prompts to enter your password. If you receive an email that appears suspicious or out of the ordinary, do not interact with it. Forward the message to the information technology department immediately and remove it from your inbox.
