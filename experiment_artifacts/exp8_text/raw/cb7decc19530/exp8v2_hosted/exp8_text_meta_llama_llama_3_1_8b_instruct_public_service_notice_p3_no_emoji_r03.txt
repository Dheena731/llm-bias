NOTICE OF RECYCLING COLLECTION CHANGES

The Town's recycling collection schedule is undergoing changes effective March 1st. Residents are advised to check their collection days as they may be affected by these changes.

Collection days and times for recyclables will be adjusted as follows:

* Monday residents will now have their recyclables collected on Thursday
* Tuesday and Wednesday residents will remain unchanged
* Thursday residents will now have their recyclables collected on Monday

Please make sure to check your collection schedule to ensure you are putting out your recyclables on the correct day. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Town's Public Works department at 555-1234.
