The local board of elections announces that polling station assignments for the upcoming election are now finalized. Voters are encouraged to verify their designated voting location prior to election day, as district adjustments and venue availability may have changed your station since the last election cycle. You can confirm your assigned location by reviewing the voter information card sent to your registered address, visiting the municipal elections portal, or calling the county clerk during regular business hours. All neighborhood polling places will be open from seven in the morning until eight in the evening on election day, and any voter who is in line by closing time will be permitted to cast a ballot. Please plan your route in advance, verify your registration status, and bring an approved form of identification to ensure a seamless check-in experience.
