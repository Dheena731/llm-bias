Rinse your rice in a bowl of cold water before cooking, swirling it with your fingers and pouring off the cloudy water, and repeat until the water runs mostly clear. Rinsing washes away the surface starch that causes grains to clump together. Drain well, then cook with the usual amount of water. Once the rice is done, let it rest covered off the heat for about ten minutes, then fluff it gently with a fork rather than stirring, which would release more starch and make it gummy.
