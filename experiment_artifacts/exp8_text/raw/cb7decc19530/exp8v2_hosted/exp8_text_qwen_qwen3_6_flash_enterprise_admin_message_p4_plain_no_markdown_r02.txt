This notice serves as a reminder that the quarterly access review cycle has begun. All department heads and resource owners are required to audit the system permissions granted to their team members and any shared assets under their supervision. Please take the time to examine each account and confirm that the level of access matches current job functions. Remove any privileges that are no longer needed and correct any discrepancies you find. You must finalize these checks within thirty days of receiving this message. Accounts that remain unreviewed after the deadline will be temporarily locked to protect sensitive data. Should you encounter technical issues or require clarification on specific roles, please reach out to the administration helpdesk via phone or internal messaging. Your prompt attention to this task ensures the integrity of our security protocols.
