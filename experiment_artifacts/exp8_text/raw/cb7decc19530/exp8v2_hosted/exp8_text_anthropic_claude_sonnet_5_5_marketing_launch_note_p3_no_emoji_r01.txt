**Introducing the Clear Budget Template: Know Where Your Money Goes**

We're pleased to announce the release of the Clear Budget Template, a spreadsheet designed to make managing your money simple and stress-free.

**What it does**

The template brings your income, expenses, savings goals, and debt payments together in one place. Enter your numbers once, and the built-in formulas handle the rest, showing you exactly how much you have left to spend each month.

**Key features**

- **Monthly and yearly views:** See your finances at a glance or dig into the details.
- **Automatic category totals:** Housing, food, transportation, and more are calculated for you.
- **Savings tracker:** Set goals and watch your progress grow.
- **Debt payoff planner:** Map out a path to becoming debt-free.
- **Visual charts:** Spot spending patterns without sorting through rows of data.

**Who it's for**

Whether you're creating your first budget or looking for a cleaner alternative to your current system, Clear Budget is easy to learn and simple to customize. No accounting experience is required.

**Compatibility and availability**

The template works with Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, and Apple Numbers. It is available today for $12, and includes a short setup guide and free updates.
