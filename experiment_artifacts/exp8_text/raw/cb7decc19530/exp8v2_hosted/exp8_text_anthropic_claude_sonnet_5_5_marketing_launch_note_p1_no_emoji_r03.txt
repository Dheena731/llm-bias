**Introducing Tallybook: Run Your Business, Not Your Paperwork**

Today we're launching Tallybook, the simple app that helps small business owners manage invoices, expenses, and clients in one place.

We built Tallybook after talking with dozens of shop owners, freelancers, and contractors. They all said the same thing: they started their business to do the work they love, but they spend their evenings buried in spreadsheets and receipts.

**What you can do with Tallybook:**

- **Send professional invoices in under a minute.** Use a template, add your logo, and get paid faster with built-in online payments.
- **Track expenses automatically.** Snap a photo of a receipt and Tallybook sorts it for you.
- **Keep every client detail organized.** Notes, past orders, and payment history are always one tap away.
- **See your numbers clearly.** A simple dashboard shows what you're owed, what you've spent, and what you've earned this month.
