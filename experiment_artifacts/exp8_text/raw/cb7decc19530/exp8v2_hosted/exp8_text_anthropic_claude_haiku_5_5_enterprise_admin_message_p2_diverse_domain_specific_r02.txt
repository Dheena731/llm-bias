**Subject: Expense approvals change on March 3**

Starting Monday, March 3, expense reports route through the new approval chain in Concur instead of by email. Here's what changes for you.

**Who approves what**
- Under $500 total: your direct manager
- $500 to $2,499: manager, then your cost center owner
- $2,500 and up: manager, cost center owner, and Finance (Dana Ruiz in AP reviews these)

If a report is sitting in someone's inbox when the switch happens, it will not carry over. Submit any open reports from February before end of day Friday, February 28, or resubmit them in Concur after the 3rd.

**Receipts**
Itemized receipts are required for any line over $25. Card statements don't count. Mileage still goes on the Form M-2 template, which now attaches directly to the report.

**Drafts**
Unsubmitted drafts are deleted after 30 days. If you've got trip reports from December sitting in drafts, check them now.

Questions about your cost center codes or whether a past expense qualifies can go to expenses@brandtlogistics.com. I'm in the Finance Ops room on the 4th floor Tuesday and Thursday mornings if you'd rather walk through it in person.
