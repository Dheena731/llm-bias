**CITY OF MARLOW FALLS, DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS**
**NOTICE OF TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE**

**Alder Street between 4th Avenue and 9th Avenue**
**Monday, March 10 through Friday, March 21, 2025**
Work hours: 7:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., weekdays only

**Reason:** Replacement of a 1948 cast-iron water main with 8-inch ductile iron pipe, including 14 service connections. Permit No. PW-25-0173.

**What is closed:** Alder Street will be closed to through traffic. Residents, deliveries, and emergency vehicles will be admitted to the work zone as crews allow. Expect waits of up to 15 minutes. Steel plates will cover open trench overnight, and the street will reopen to local traffic each evening. The speed limit in the work zone is 10 mph.

**Detour:** Northbound and southbound traffic should use Linden Avenue and 10th Avenue. Trucks over 10 tons should use Route 6 and avoid Alder Street entirely.

**Parking:** "No Parking" signs will go up on March 8. Vehicles left on the closed block after 7:00 a.m. on March 10 will be towed at the owner's expense. Overnight parking is available at the Fenwick Elementary lot on 5th Avenue.

**Water service:** Each address will lose water for about 4 hours, between 8:00 a.m. and 2:00 p.m., on one day during the project. A door hanger with your date will be left at least 24 hours in advance. After service is restored, run your cold taps for a few minutes to clear any discolored water.

**Transit:** Route 12 will bypass the Alder & 6th and Alder & 8th stops. Temporary stops will be posted at Linden & 6th and Linden & 8th.

**Trash and recycling:** Place carts at the corner of 4th Avenue or 9th Avenue on your regular collection day.

**Questions or access needs** (medical transport, mobility equipment, oversized deliveries): call the project hotline at 555-0142, weekdays 7:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., or email alder@marlowfalls.example. Schedule changes caused by weather will be posted at marlowfalls.example/alder by 6:00 a.m. on the affected day.
