This document serves as a notice regarding the acceptable use of the shared network infrastructure provided by the organization. All users are expected to utilize the network strictly for legitimate and authorized activities consistent with applicable laws and organizational policies. Prohibited conduct includes, but is not limited to, attempting to gain unauthorized access to systems, distributing malware, engaging in harassment, accessing material that is unlawful or offensive, and consuming excessive bandwidth that disrupts service for others. The organization reserves the right to monitor network traffic, log usage data, and inspect communications to ensure compliance with this policy and to protect the integrity of the system. Any violation of these guidelines may result in immediate suspension of network privileges, disciplinary action, or referral to law enforcement authorities where appropriate. Continued use of the network constitutes acknowledgment and acceptance of these terms.
