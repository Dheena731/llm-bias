If you have lost a company laptop, please report it to the IT department as soon as possible. This will enable them to take necessary steps to protect the laptop's data and prevent unauthorized access to company information. To report a lost laptop, please contact the IT department via phone or email, providing your name, employee ID, and a detailed description of the laptop.

The IT department will then initiate procedures to secure the laptop's data, suspend its network access, and arrange for its safe return to the company. In the meantime, you are advised to exercise extreme caution when interacting with your colleagues and clients, as they may be using your laptop's data to their advantage.

If you have any concerns or questions, please do not hesitate to contact the IT department directly. They will be able to guide you through the process and provide you with any necessary assistance.
