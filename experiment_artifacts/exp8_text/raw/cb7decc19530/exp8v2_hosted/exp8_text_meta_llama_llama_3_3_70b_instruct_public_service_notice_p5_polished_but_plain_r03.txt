Voters are advised to verify the location of their assigned polling station before election day. Polling stations are typically located in public buildings such as schools, community centers, and libraries. To find the location of your polling station, you can check your voter registration card or contact your local election office for more information. You can also visit the website of your local election authority to search for your polling station by address or voter registration number. On election day, polling stations will be open from 7am to 8pm, and voters are required to bring a valid form of identification to cast their ballot.
