**Quarterly Access Reviews**

To ensure the continued security and integrity of our systems, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews for all employees with access to sensitive data or systems.

**What to Expect**

* You will receive an email notification from our IT department 2 weeks prior to your scheduled review date.
* You will be asked to review and confirm your access levels, which will be compared against your job requirements and company policies.
* If you have any changes to report, please notify our IT department at least 1 week prior to your review date.
* All access levels will be reviewed and updated as necessary.

**Why it Matters**

These reviews are essential to prevent unauthorized access and to ensure that only authorized personnel have access to sensitive data and systems. Your cooperation and prompt attention to these reviews are greatly appreciated.

If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to our IT department.
