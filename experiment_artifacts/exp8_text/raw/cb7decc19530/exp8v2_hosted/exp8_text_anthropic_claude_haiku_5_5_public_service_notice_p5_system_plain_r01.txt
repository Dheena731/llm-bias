Polling Station Locations Notice

Voters are advised that polling station locations may differ from those used in previous elections. Each registered voter is assigned to a specific polling station based on their home address, and the assigned location is printed on the voter's registration card and listed in the official voter information guide. Voters who are unsure of their polling station should check with the local election office before election day, either by telephone or through the official election website, where a search tool is available using a registered address.

Polling stations will open at 7:00 a.m. and close at 8:00 p.m. on election day. Voters who are in line when the polls close will be permitted to cast a ballot. Each station will display a sign at its entrance identifying the station and the precincts it serves. Accessible entrances are available at all locations, and staff can provide assistance to voters with disabilities upon request.

Voters who arrive at an incorrect polling station may be directed to their correct location or may be permitted to cast a provisional ballot, depending on local rules. To avoid delays, please confirm your location in advance and bring a valid form of identification as required.

For further information, please contact your local election office during regular business hours.
