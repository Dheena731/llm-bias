**NOTICE: Reporting Lost or Stolen Company Laptops**

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, you must report the incident immediately. Prompt reporting allows the IT department to secure sensitive company data and lock the device remotely.

**Immediate Actions Required:**

1. **Contact IT Support Immediately:** Call the IT Help Desk at [Phone Number] or email [Email Address] as soon as you realize the device is missing. 
2. **Provide Details:** Include your name, the approximate time and location where the laptop was last seen, and any relevant circumstances.
3. **File a Police Report (if stolen):** If the device was stolen, report the theft to local law enforcement and forward the incident/case number to both IT and Corporate Security.
4. **Update Credentials:** Immediately change your company account passwords from another secure device.

Data security is a critical priority. Failure to report a missing device promptly may result in disciplinary action.
