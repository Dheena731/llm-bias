**Lock It When You Walk Away**

Empty chair. Open dashboard. That’s how access gets borrowed.

Step out for coffee, jump on a call, or catch a breath between tickets—hit `Win + L` (`Cmd + Ctrl + Q` on Mac). Two seconds. Cuts off unauthorized eyes before they become an incident report.

We run production credentials, client PII, and financial ledgers. Last month’s audit pulled three workstations left unattended during stand-up. Unlocked screens aren’t a convenience issue; they’re a single point of failure.

Do it every time. The backlog waits. Your session doesn’t.

—Access & Systems Security
