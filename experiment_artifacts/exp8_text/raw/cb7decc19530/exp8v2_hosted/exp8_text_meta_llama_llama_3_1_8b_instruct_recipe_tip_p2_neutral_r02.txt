Here's a short tip for storing fresh herbs:

**Keep them fresh!**

To keep fresh herbs fresh for a longer period, store them in a cool, dry place, such as a cupboard or a drawer. Wrap the herbs in a damp paper towel or a plastic bag with a small amount of moisture. This will help maintain humidity and prevent wilting. You can also store them in a glass jar with a paper towel or cloth to absorb excess moisture. Check on them regularly and replace the herbs every 5-7 days to ensure optimal flavor and texture.

**Best herbs for storage:**

* Basil
* Parsley
* Cilantro
* Mint
* Chives
* Dill

**Note:** Avoid storing herbs in direct sunlight, near heat sources, or in airtight containers, as this can cause them to wilt and lose their flavor.
