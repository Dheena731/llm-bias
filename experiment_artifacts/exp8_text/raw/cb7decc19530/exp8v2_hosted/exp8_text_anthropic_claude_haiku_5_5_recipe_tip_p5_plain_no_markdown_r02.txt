Toss your vegetables in oil and salt before they go into the oven, not after. Salting early lets the salt penetrate the surface and season the vegetables throughout, while the oil helps the seasoning cling and promotes even browning. Use about half a teaspoon of kosher salt per pound of vegetables, and add pepper, garlic powder, or dried herbs at the start as well. Delicate spices and fresh herbs are better added in the last few minutes or after roasting, since they can burn or lose their flavor in the heat.
