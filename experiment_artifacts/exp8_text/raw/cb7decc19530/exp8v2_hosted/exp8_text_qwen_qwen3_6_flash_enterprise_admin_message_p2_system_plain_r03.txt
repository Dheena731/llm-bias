We are implementing a new expense approval workflow starting next Monday. This update is designed to streamline the submission process and reduce processing times across all departments. Employees will now submit expenses through the updated portal, which automatically routes requests to the appropriate manager based on predefined spending thresholds. All previous manual routing procedures have been discontinued. Managers should familiarize themselves with the updated system guidelines to understand their new responsibilities. A two week transition period will follow, during which both the current and new platforms will remain accessible for testing. After that period ends, only submissions processed through the new workflow will be reviewed and reimbursed. Please direct any technical questions or process concerns to the finance help desk. Thank you for your attention to these operational improvements.
