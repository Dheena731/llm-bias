**Prep ingredients, not full meals.** 

Spend an hour on Sunday cooking a big batch of one grain (like quinoa or rice), two proteins (like shredded chicken or baked tofu), and a tray of roasted veggies. During the week, just mix and match them into bowls with different sauces or dressings (pesto, salsa, or tahini). It takes two minutes to assemble, and you won’t get bored eating the exact same meal every day.
