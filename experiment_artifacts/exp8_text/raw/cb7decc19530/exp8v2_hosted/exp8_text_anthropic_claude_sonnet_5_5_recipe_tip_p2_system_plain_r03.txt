Treat tender herbs like cilantro, parsley, and mint as you would a bouquet of flowers. Trim the bottoms of the stems, stand them upright in a jar with an inch or two of water, and loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag. Keep the jar in the refrigerator and change the water every couple of days. Handled this way, they will often stay fresh for a week or longer. Basil is the exception, since it blackens in the cold, so leave it in a jar of water on the counter away from direct sun. Sturdier herbs like rosemary and thyme keep best wrapped in a slightly damp paper towel and tucked into a container or bag in the fridge.
