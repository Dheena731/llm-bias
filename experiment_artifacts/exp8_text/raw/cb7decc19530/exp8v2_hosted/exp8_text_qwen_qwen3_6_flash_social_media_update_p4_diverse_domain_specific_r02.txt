Stop treating calendar gaps like open slots. They're context traps. 🕳️

Try this: Enforce a **"Switch Tax."**

If you're jumping from high-focus work to low-focus admin, don't just add 5 minutes. Schedule a 15-minute **"Comms Reset"** block. Name it explicitly. Make it non-negotiable.

Why? Because your brain needs to offload the previous task before taking the next. If that gap stays empty, Slack, email, and "quick questions" will flood in, and your deep work suffers.

Guard the reset. The calendar dictates reality—make sure it includes the downtime that keeps the focus
