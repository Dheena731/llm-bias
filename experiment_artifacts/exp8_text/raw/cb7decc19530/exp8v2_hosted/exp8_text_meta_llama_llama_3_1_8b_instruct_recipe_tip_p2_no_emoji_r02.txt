To store fresh herbs effectively, trim the stems of your herbs and place them in a glass of water, making sure the stems are submerged. Cover the container with a plastic bag or plastic wrap, but not too tightly, to maintain humidity. Store in the refrigerator and replace the water every few days. Alternatively, you can also wrap the herbs in a damp paper towel and place them in a sealed container or plastic bag to store in the refrigerator.
