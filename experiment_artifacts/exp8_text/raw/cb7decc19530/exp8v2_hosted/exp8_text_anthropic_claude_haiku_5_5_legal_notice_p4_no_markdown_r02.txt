NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

This notice sets forth the conditions under which a customer may be entitled to a refund for products or services purchased from the Company.

1. Eligibility Period. A request for a refund must be submitted within thirty (30) days of the date of original purchase, as shown on the applicable receipt or order confirmation.

2. Conditions of Eligibility. To qualify for a refund, the product must be unused, undamaged, and in its original packaging, and the customer must present proof of purchase. Services are eligible for a refund only where they have not been substantially performed.

3. Exclusions. Refunds will not be issued for items marked as final sale, personalized or custom orders, digital downloads once accessed, or items purchased more than thirty (30) days prior to the refund request.

4. Method of Refund. Approved refunds will be issued to the original form of payment. Processing times may vary depending on the payment provider, and typically require five (5) to ten (10) business days after approval.

5. Right to Modify. The Company reserves the right to amend this refund policy at any time. Changes will apply only to purchases made after the effective date of the amended policy.

6. Statutory Rights. Nothing in this notice limits any refund or consumer rights that a customer may have under applicable law.
