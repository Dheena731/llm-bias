Notice to Residents: Changes to Recycling Collection

Beginning the first Monday of next month, the city will change the day recycling is collected in most neighborhoods. Collection will move from Thursday to Tuesday, so please set out your recycling bins by 7:00 a.m. on Tuesday mornings. Bins may be placed at the curb the evening before. Collection for garbage and yard waste will remain on its current schedule.

Please also note that glass containers will no longer be accepted in the blue recycling bins. Residents should take glass bottles and jars to the drop-off center on Mill Road, which is open daily from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Plastic bags, food waste, and polystyrene foam should continue to be kept out of recycling entirely, as these items contaminate loads and increase processing costs.

If you have questions about your collection day or need a replacement bin, please contact the Public Works Department at 555-0142 or visit the city website. Thank you for helping us keep our community clean and our recycling program working well.
