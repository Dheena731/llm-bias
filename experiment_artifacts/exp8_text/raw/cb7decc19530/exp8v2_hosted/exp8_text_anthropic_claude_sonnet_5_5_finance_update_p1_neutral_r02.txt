**Introducing Your Monthly Spending Summary**

We've added a new Monthly Spending Summary to help you see where your money goes, without digging through individual transactions.

**What it shows**
- **Total spending** for the month, compared with the previous month
- **Category breakdown** (groceries, dining, transportation, subscriptions, and more)
- **Top merchants** where you spent the most
- **Trends** that highlight unusual increases or decreases

**How it works**
Your summary is generated automatically on the first day of each month and covers the previous month's activity. You'll get a notification when it's ready, and you can view past summaries anytime in the **Insights** tab.

**Why it matters**
A quick monthly snapshot makes it easier to spot habits, catch forgotten subscriptions, and adjust your budget before small leaks become big ones.

**Getting started**
No setup is required. If you'd like to customize categories or turn off notifications, go to **Settings > Insights**.
