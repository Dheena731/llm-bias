**SECURITY NOTICE: Phishing Email Awareness**

Please remain vigilant regarding suspicious emails arriving in your inbox. Cybercriminals frequently use phishing attempts to compromise company credentials and sensitive data.

**Common Warning Signs:**
*   Urgent or threatening language demanding immediate action.
*   Requests to verify passwords, multi-factor authentication (MFA) codes, or financial details.
*   Mismatched sender addresses (e.g., an executive’s name paired with an external or misspelled domain).
*   Unexpected attachments or generic greetings.

**What You Should Do:**
*   **Do not click** on any links or download attachments from unknown or unverified senders.
*   **Verify independently:** If a request appears to come from a colleague or vendor but seems unusual, confirm with them via a separate channel (phone call or direct message).
*   **Report it:** Use the "Report Phishing" button in your email client immediately, or forward the message to the IT Security team.

If you suspect you have clicked a malicious link or entered your credentials on an unauthorized page, disconnect your device from the network and contact the IT Help Desk immediately.
