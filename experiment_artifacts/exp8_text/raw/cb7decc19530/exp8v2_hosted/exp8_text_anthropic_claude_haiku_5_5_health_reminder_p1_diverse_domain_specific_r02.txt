**Subject: Sunday check-in (2 minutes)**

Before Monday's session, open your log and check three things:

- **Sessions completed** versus planned
- **Your top lift or your fastest run split** compared with last week
- **Sleep on your hardest days**, since that's often what explains a flat week

If a number looks off, that's useful information. Two skipped sessions usually means the plan doesn't fit your week, so move Thursday's intervals to a day you can actually keep.
