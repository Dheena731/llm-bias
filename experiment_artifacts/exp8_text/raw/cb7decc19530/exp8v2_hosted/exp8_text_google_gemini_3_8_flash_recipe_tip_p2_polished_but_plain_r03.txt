To extend the life of fresh herbs, treat them according to their stem type. Tender herbs like parsley, cilantro, and mint should be treated like fresh flowers: trim the stem ends, stand them upright in a jar with an inch of water, cover the tops loosely with a plastic bag, and refrigerate. Keep basil prepared the same way, but leave it on the counter at room temperature, as cold air turns its delicate leaves black. For hearty, woody herbs such as rosemary, thyme, and sage, wrap the unwashed sprigs loosely in a barely damp paper towel and seal them inside an airtight container or bag in the crisper drawer to maintain the right balance of humidity without encouraging mold.
