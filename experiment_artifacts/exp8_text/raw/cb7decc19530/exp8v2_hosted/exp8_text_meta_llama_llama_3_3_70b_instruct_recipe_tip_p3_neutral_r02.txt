To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it thoroughly before cooking and adding a small amount of oil or butter to the water. This helps to separate the grains and prevent them from clumping together. Additionally, using the right water ratio (generally 1 cup of rice to 1 3/4 cups of water) and not overmixing the rice while it's cooking can also help to reduce stickiness. Finally, fluffing the rice with a fork after it's cooked can help to separate the grains and make it lighter and fluffier.
