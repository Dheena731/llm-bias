To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it thoroughly before cooking. Rinsing the rice removes excess starch, which is what causes it to become sticky. Simply place the rice in a fine mesh strainer and run it under cold water, stirring occasionally, until the water runs clear. After rinsing, cook the rice as you normally would. You can also try adding a small amount of oil or butter to the rice while it cooks, as this can help to separate the grains and make the rice less sticky.
