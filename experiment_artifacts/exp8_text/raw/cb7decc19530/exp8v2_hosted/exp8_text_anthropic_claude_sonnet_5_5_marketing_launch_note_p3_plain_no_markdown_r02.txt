We are pleased to announce the release of the Clear Month Budget Planner, a spreadsheet template designed to make personal budgeting simple and sustainable. It works in both Excel and Google Sheets, so you can use whichever tool you already have.

The template begins with a single setup page where you enter your income, fixed bills, and the spending categories that matter to you. From there, the planner builds a monthly overview automatically. You can see at a glance how much you planned to spend, how much you actually spent, and how much remains in each category. Colors shift gently as you approach a limit, which makes it easy to spot trouble before it becomes a problem.

We built the planner around the habits that tend to make budgets last. Entering a transaction takes only a few seconds, and the formulas are already in place, so there is nothing to configure or break. A savings tracker lets you set goals for an emergency fund, a vacation, or a large purchase, and it shows your progress over time. At the end of the year, a summary page gathers your totals so you can see where your money went and what you might change.

The Clear Month Budget Planner is meant for beginners and experienced budgeters alike. If you have struggled with complicated apps or abandoned past spreadsheets halfway through the year, this template aims to be a calmer alternative that asks for only a few minutes each week.

The planner is available today as a one-time purchase with free updates. Every download includes a short written guide to help you get started in under ten minutes.
