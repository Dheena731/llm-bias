HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for our area, beginning today and continuing through Friday evening. Daytime high temperatures are expected to reach the mid-90s, with heat index values near 105 degrees.

Extreme heat can cause serious health problems, especially for young children, older adults, people with chronic illnesses, and those who work or exercise outdoors. To stay safe:

Drink water regularly, even if you do not feel thirsty.
Stay in air-conditioned spaces when possible. If your home is not cooled, consider visiting a library, community center, or cooling center.
Avoid strenuous outdoor activity during the hottest part of the day, typically between 11 a.m. and 6 p.m.
Never leave children or pets inside a parked vehicle.
Check on elderly neighbors, friends, and relatives at least twice a day.
Watch for warning signs of heat illness, including heavy sweating, dizziness, nausea, muscle cramps, and confusion. Seek medical help right away if symptoms are severe or do not improve.

Call 911 in case of a medical emergency.

For the latest updates, follow your local National Weather Service office and your city or county emergency management agency.
