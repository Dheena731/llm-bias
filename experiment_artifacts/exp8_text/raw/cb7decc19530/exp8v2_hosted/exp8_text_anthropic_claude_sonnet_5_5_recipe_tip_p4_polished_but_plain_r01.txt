Choose one or two base ingredients on Sunday and cook them in bulk, then vary the finishing touches through the week. A big batch of roasted vegetables, a pot of grains or beans, and a simple protein like shredded chicken or hard-boiled eggs can become several different meals with a change of sauce or seasoning. One day they might go into a grain bowl with a lemony dressing, the next into a wrap with hummus, and later into a salad with whatever fresh greens you have on hand. This approach keeps the prep work to a single session, cuts down on food waste, and spares you the boredom of eating the same container of leftovers five days in a row. Store the components separately when you can, and add dressings or sauces just before eating so everything stays fresh and appealing until Friday.
