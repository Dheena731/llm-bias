NOTICE OF TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE

The City Department of Public Works advises residents, commuters, and local businesses that Maple Avenue will be closed to all through traffic between Fourth Street and Ninth Street from Monday, June 3, at 7:00 a.m. until Friday, June 14, at 6:00 p.m. The closure is necessary so that crews can replace a damaged water main and resurface the roadway. Work will proceed weekdays between 7:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m., and weather may extend the schedule by a day or two.

Drivers should follow the posted detour, which directs traffic along Fourth Street to Oak Boulevard, then along Oak Boulevard to Ninth Street. Expect added travel time of about five to ten minutes during morning and evening peak hours. Motorists who can use alternate routes, such as Cedar Road on the north side of the work zone, are encouraged to do so to ease congestion.

Local access will be maintained for residents and businesses within the closed section, though driveways may be briefly blocked while excavation and paving take place. Crews will give affected households at least 24 hours of notice before any such interruption. Emergency vehicles will have full access at all times. Garbage and recycling collection will continue, but residents on Maple Avenue should place bins at the nearest intersection on their usual collection day. Brief water service interruptions of up to four hours may occur on June 6 and June 7, and affected addresses will receive door hangers in advance.

Bus Route 12 will be rerouted along Oak Boulevard for the duration of the project, and stops on Maple Avenue will be temporarily relocated. Pedestrians and cyclists should use the marked alternate path on the east side of the street, and are asked to obey all signs and flaggers.

For questions, project updates, or to report a concern, please contact the Public Works information line at 555-0142 during business hours, or email publicworks@cityexample.gov. We appreciate your patience and cooperation as we complete this important infrastructure improvement.
