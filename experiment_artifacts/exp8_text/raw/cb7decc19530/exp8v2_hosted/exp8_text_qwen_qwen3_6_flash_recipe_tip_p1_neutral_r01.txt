**The Weeknight Skillet Shortcut:** Keep a "trifecta" stocked—fast-cooking protein (shrimp, canned beans, or rotisserie chicken), frozen mixed vegetables, and a quick-cook grain or pasta. In one skillet, sear the protein, toss in the frozen veggies, and finish with a spoonful of sauce or a pinch of spices. You'll have a balanced, hot meal on the table in under 25 minutes, with almost no chopping and only one pan to wash.
