**Subject: Lost or stolen laptop: report it within one hour**

If you can't find your company laptop, tell us first and keep looking second. Every hour it's missing is an hour its sessions and saved credentials stay usable.

**Report it to:**
- Security desk: security@company.example or ext. 4411 (24 hours)
- After hours, or if you're traveling: +1 555 0142

A phone call is fine. Don't wait for a ticket to be acknowledged.

**Have this ready, but don't hold the report for it:**
- Asset tag or serial number (on the underside, or in the asset portal under *My Devices*)
- Where and when you last had it
- Whether it was shut down, asleep, or locked
- Anything stored only on the laptop, such as local exports, client files, or unsynced work
- Whether a security key, badge, or token was in the same bag

**What we'll do:**
1. Revoke your sign-in sessions and tokens and reset your password.
2. Check the encryption status (BitLocker/FileVault) and last check-in.
3. Send a lock or wipe command through MDM. If the laptop is offline, the command runs at its next connection.
4. Decide whether the loss needs to go to Legal or Compliance. Only we make that call.

**What we need from you:**
- If it was stolen, file a police report and send us the report number.
- If you lost it abroad, tell us which country.
- Don't try to remotely access it, and don't ask a colleague to.
- Expect to be signed out of everything on all your devices. That's intentional.

Reports made promptly are treated as routine and carry no penalty. Late reports are the ones that turn a lost laptop into an incident.
