**NOTICE: Beware of Phishing Emails**

**To:** All Staff
**From:** IT Security Team
**Subject:** Recent Increase in Phishing Attempts

We have seen a rise in phishing emails targeting employees. These messages are designed to look legitimate in order to steal login credentials, financial information, or install malicious software.

**Warning signs to watch for:**
- Urgent or threatening language, such as "Your account will be closed today"
- Unexpected attachments or links, even from known contacts
- Sender addresses that look slightly off or do not match the company name
- Requests for passwords, payment details, or gift card purchases
- Spelling errors or unusual formatting

**What you should do:**
1. Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails.
2. Do not reply to the sender or forward the message to colleagues.
3. Report the email using the "Report Phishing" button in your email client, or send it to security@yourcompany.com.
4. If you have already clicked a link or entered your credentials, contact the IT Help Desk immediately and change your password.

Remember that IT will never ask for your password by email. When in doubt, verify the request by contacting the sender through a separate, trusted channel.

Thank you for helping keep our company secure.

IT Security Team
