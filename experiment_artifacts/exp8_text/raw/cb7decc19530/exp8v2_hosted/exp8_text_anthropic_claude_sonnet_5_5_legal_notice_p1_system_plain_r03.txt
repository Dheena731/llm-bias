NOTICE OF CHANGES TO ACCOUNT TERMS

Effective Date: This notice takes effect thirty (30) days after the date it is delivered to you.

To the Account Holder:

Please be advised that the company ("we," "us," or "our") is amending the Account Agreement that governs your account (the "Agreement"). This notice explains the nature of those amendments and your rights in connection with them. We encourage you to read it carefully and retain a copy for your records.

First, the amendments. Beginning on the effective date stated above, the Agreement will be revised in the following respects. The monthly maintenance fee will be adjusted to the amount set out in the updated fee schedule, a copy of which accompanies this notice. The provision governing dispute resolution will be modified to require that disputes first be submitted to good-faith informal negotiation for a period of thirty (30) days before any formal proceeding is commenced. In addition, the privacy provisions will be updated to describe more fully how we collect, use, and share account information. Except as expressly stated here, all other terms of the Agreement remain unchanged and in full force.

Second, your acceptance. If you continue to use your account on or after the effective date, you will be deemed to have accepted the amended terms. No signature or other affirmative action is required for the amendments to apply to you.

Third, your right to decline. If you do not agree to the amendments, you may close your account without penalty by notifying us in writing at any time before the effective date. Any outstanding balance and obligations incurred before closure will remain due and payable under the terms of the Agreement as it existed at the time they were incurred. Closing your account will not affect any rights or liabilities that arose before closure.

Fourth, conflicts and severability. If any provision of the amended Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions will continue in full force and effect. In the event of any conflict between this notice and the Agreement, the Agreement as amended will control.

Fifth, contact information. Questions regarding this notice may be directed to our customer service department using the contact details printed on your account statement. Please note that customer service representatives cannot provide legal advice, and you may wish to consult an attorney of your choosing if you have concerns about how these changes affect you.

Thank you for your continued business.

Issued by the Office of Account Administration
