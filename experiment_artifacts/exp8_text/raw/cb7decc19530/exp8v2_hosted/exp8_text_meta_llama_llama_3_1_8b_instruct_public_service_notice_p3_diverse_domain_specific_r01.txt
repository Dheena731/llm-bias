**Important Update: Changes to Curbside Recycling Collection**

Starting March 1st, the City of [City Name] is implementing changes to the curbside recycling collection program to better serve the community. As part of this effort, the following adjustments will be made:

* **New collection days:** Residents in the northern and eastern sections of town will now have their recyclables collected on Wednesdays, while those in the southern and western sections will have their materials collected on Fridays.
* **Material expansion:** The list of acceptable materials for recycling has been expanded to include:
	+ Corrugated cardboard and cartons (previously accepted)
	+ Shredded paper and newspaper
	+ Glass bottles and jars (with lids and labels removed)
* **New sorting guidelines:** Residents are encouraged to sort materials into separate bins, using the following guidelines:
	+ Plastic bottles and containers (#1-7)
	+ Metal cans (aluminum and steel)
	+ Glass bottles and jars (as mentioned above)
	+ Corrugated cardboard and cartons (as mentioned above)
	+ Shredded paper and newspaper (in a designated bin)

These changes aim to increase recycling participation and improve the overall efficiency of the program. For more information, please visit our website or contact our customer service department at (phone number) or (email address).

**Tips for a successful transition:**

* Review the new sorting guidelines and material acceptance list to ensure a smooth transition.
* Keep your recycling bins clean and well-maintained to avoid contamination.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.

Thank you for your cooperation and commitment to recycling in our community!
