A new expense approval workflow is being implemented to improve the efficiency and transparency of our expense reporting process. This new workflow will require all employees to submit their expenses through a centralized online system, where they will be reviewed and approved by the relevant department managers. The system will automatically notify the employee and the manager when an expense is submitted, approved, or rejected, and will also provide a record of all expenses for auditing and budgeting purposes. Employees are expected to familiarize themselves with the new system and follow the new workflow for all future expense submissions. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the finance department for assistance.
