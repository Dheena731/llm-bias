Subject: Reminder to Lock Your Screen

Dear colleagues,

This is a brief reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if it is only for a few minutes. An unlocked workstation gives anyone nearby access to your files, email, and our internal systems, which puts confidential information at risk and can lead to accidental or unauthorized changes made under your name.

Locking your screen takes only a second. On a Windows computer, press the Windows key and the L key together. On a Mac, press Control, Command, and Q. If you use a laptop in a shared or public space, please lock it every time you leave it, and consider using a privacy screen when working with sensitive material.

To make this easier, we also recommend setting your device to lock automatically after a short period of inactivity, ideally no more than five minutes. If you need help changing this setting, please contact the IT help desk and someone will gladly assist you.

Thank you for helping us keep our information, our clients, and our workplace secure. Small habits like this make a real difference.

Kind regards,

The IT and Security Team
