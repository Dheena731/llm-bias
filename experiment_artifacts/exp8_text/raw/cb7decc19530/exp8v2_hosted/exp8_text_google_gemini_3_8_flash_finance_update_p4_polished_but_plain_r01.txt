Your end-of-year tax statement is a comprehensive summary of your financial activity with us over the past calendar year. This document details the total earnings you received, taxes withheld, and any eligible deductions or contributions applied throughout the year. You will need this information to prepare and file your annual tax return, as it provides the official figures required to calculate your final tax liability or potential refund. Please review the statement carefully to confirm that your personal information, including your full legal name and tax identification number, is accurate. It is also good practice to cross-reference the totals against your personal records. Be sure to keep this statement in a secure place, as tax authorities generally advise retaining financial documentation for several years. If you discover any discrepancies or have questions regarding how these figures were calculated, please contact our support team promptly so we can assist you.
