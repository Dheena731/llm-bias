Subject: Update Regarding Card Transaction Fees

Dear Valued Partner,

Please be advised that effective November 1, 2024, we will implement an adjustment to our card transaction processing fees. The new fee structure reflects updated network rates and enhanced security protocols required by payment processors.

Under the revised terms, domestic credit and debit card transactions will carry a standard rate of 2.9 percent plus thirty cents per transaction. International cards and currency conversions will incur an additional two percent surcharge. These changes apply to all newly processed payments and will not affect pending or previously completed settlements.

We understand that fee adjustments can impact your operations, and we remain committed to providing transparent pricing and reliable support. Detailed billing statements and a complete comparison chart are available in your merchant portal.
