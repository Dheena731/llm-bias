We have recently seen an increase in phishing emails being sent to staff. These emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information such as your login credentials or financial information. They often appear to be from a legitimate source, such as a bank or a colleague, but are actually from a malicious sender.

To protect yourself and the company, please be cautious when receiving emails that ask you to click on a link, download an attachment, or provide sensitive information. If you are unsure whether an email is genuine, do not respond or click on any links. Instead, contact the supposed sender directly using a phone number or email address that you know is correct.

It is also a good idea to hover over links before clicking on them to check that they are going to a legitimate website, and to be wary of emails that create a sense of urgency or threaten consequences if you do not take immediate action. If you think you may have fallen victim to a phishing email, please report it to the IT department immediately.
