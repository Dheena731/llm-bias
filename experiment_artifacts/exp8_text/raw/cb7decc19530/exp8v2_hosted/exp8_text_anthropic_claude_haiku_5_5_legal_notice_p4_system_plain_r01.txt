Notice of Refund Eligibility

This notice sets out the conditions under which a customer may request a refund for a purchase made from the seller. By completing a purchase, the customer agrees to the terms described here.

A customer may request a full refund for any product returned within thirty (30) days of the date of delivery, provided the product is unused, in its original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase. Requests made after this period will generally be declined, except where the product is defective or does not conform to its description, in which case the customer may be entitled to a repair, replacement, or refund under applicable consumer protection laws.

Refunds are not available for custom or personalized items, perishable goods, digital downloads once accessed, or items marked as final sale at the time of purchase. Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return results from an error on the part of the seller or a defect in the product.

To request a refund, the customer must contact the seller's customer service department in writing within the applicable period and provide the order number, the reason for the request, and any supporting documentation. The seller will review the request and notify the customer of its decision within fourteen (14) business days of receipt. Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment and may take an additional five to ten (5 to 10) business days to appear, depending on the customer's financial institution.

The seller reserves the right to refuse a refund where there is evidence of fraud, misuse of the return process, or damage caused after delivery. Nothing in this notice limits any statutory rights the customer may hold in their jurisdiction.

This notice is provided for general information and does not constitute legal advice. Where a dispute arises, the parties are encouraged to resolve it through good-faith negotiation before pursuing any other remedy.
