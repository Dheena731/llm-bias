PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT

The city's Office of Emergency Management has issued a heat advisory for all neighborhoods, effective from 10 a.m. Thursday until 9 p.m. Saturday. Daytime temperatures are expected to reach between 98 and 104 degrees Fahrenheit, and high humidity will make it feel as much as ten degrees hotter. Overnight lows will stay in the upper 70s, which limits the chance for homes and bodies to cool down.

Prolonged exposure to this kind of heat can cause heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Older adults, young children, pregnant women, people with chronic illnesses, outdoor workers, and anyone without reliable air conditioning are at greatest risk. Residents are encouraged to check on neighbors, friends, and relatives who may be vulnerable, especially those who live alone.

To stay safe, drink water regularly throughout the day even if you do not feel thirsty, and avoid alcohol and sugary drinks. Limit strenuous activity to the early morning or late evening, take frequent breaks in the shade, and wear lightweight, light-colored, loose-fitting clothing. Never leave children, older adults, or pets in a parked vehicle, even for a few minutes, as temperatures inside a car can become deadly very quickly.

Warning signs of heat illness include heavy sweating, dizziness, headache, nausea, muscle cramps, and weakness. If someone has a body temperature above 103 degrees, hot and dry skin, confusion, or loses consciousness, call 911 immediately and move the person to a cooler place while waiting for help.

Cooling centers will be open daily from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. at all public libraries, the Riverside Community Center, and the Eastgate Senior Center. Admission is free, and water will be provided. Residents who need transportation to a cooling center may call 311 to arrange a ride at no cost.

For updates on this advisory and additional safety information, call 311 or tune in to local radio and television stations. The Office of Emergency Management thanks residents for looking out for one another during this period of extreme heat.
