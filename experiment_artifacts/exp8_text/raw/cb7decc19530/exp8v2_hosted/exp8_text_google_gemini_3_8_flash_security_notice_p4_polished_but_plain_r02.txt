To help protect your personal information and improve overall account security, we strongly recommend enabling two-factor authentication. This feature adds a critical layer of defense against unauthorized access by requiring a temporary verification code alongside your standard password whenever you sign in. You can set this up quickly by visiting your account settings and opening the security preferences section. From there, follow the on-screen prompts to link your preferred authentication app or mobile phone number. Enabling this extra step takes only a few minutes and significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access to your data.
