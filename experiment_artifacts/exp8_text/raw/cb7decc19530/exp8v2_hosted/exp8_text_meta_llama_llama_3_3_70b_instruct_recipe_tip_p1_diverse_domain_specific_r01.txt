When prepping a weeknight dinner, consider the 'assembly line' method for streamlined success. Start by identifying the core components of your dish - protein, starch, and vegetables - and prep each in tandem. For example, while your quinoa or brown rice cooks, slice and sauté your vegetables, and season your protein (chicken, salmon, or tofu work well). As each element nears completion, assemble the final dish, adding any finishing touches like a squeeze of fresh citrus or a sprinkle of herbs. This approach not only saves time but also ensures that each component is cooked to perfection, resulting in a well-balanced and satisfying meal that's ready in under 30 minutes.
