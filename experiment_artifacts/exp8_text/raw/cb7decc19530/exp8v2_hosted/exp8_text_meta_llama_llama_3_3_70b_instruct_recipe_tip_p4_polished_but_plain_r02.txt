To plan lunches for a busy week, start by taking some time on the weekend or one evening to think about your schedule and dietary needs for the upcoming days. Consider what ingredients you already have at home and what you can buy in bulk to make meal prep more efficient. Choose a few simple and versatile recipes that can be prepared in advance, such as soups, salads, or whole grain sandwiches, and make a batch of each to portion out for the week. You can also prep individual components like cooked proteins, roasted vegetables, or quinoa to mix and match throughout the week. By having a plan and some prepared ingredients on hand, you can save time and money, and ensure that you have healthy and satisfying lunches even on the most hectic days.
