**Finance Notice: Routing update for expense sign-offs (Effective Oct 1)**

Starting with next Tuesday’s AP cut-off, expense approval paths will map to cost-center ownership rather than direct reporting lines for non-travel spend over $750.

Clear your current queue in Navan by Friday at 5:00 PM EST so we can remap open requests without manual reassignment over the weekend.
