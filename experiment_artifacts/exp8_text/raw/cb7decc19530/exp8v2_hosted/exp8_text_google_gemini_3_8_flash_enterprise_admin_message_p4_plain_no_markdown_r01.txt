This is an administrative notice regarding the start of our quarterly access review. Beginning this week, all department managers and system owners must audit user permissions across their respective platforms, applications, and shared network resources. Please review active accounts to verify that access levels align strictly with current job responsibilities. Any access permissions for departed personnel or employees who have changed roles must be revoked immediately. All reviews must be completed and submitted through the standard security portal by the end of next week. Prompt completion is required to ensure organizational security and maintain regulatory compliance. Please contact the internal security team if you have any questions or need assistance during the process.
