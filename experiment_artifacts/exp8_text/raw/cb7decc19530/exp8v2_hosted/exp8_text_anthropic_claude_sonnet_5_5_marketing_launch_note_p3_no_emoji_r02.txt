**Introducing the Clear Budget Template: Know Where Your Money Goes**

We're pleased to announce the release of the Clear Budget Template, a spreadsheet designed to make managing your money simpler and less stressful.

**What it does**

The template brings your income, expenses, savings goals, and debt payments into one organized workbook. Enter your numbers once, and the formulas handle the rest, updating your totals and showing how your actual spending compares to your plan.

**Key features**

- **Monthly dashboard:** See your income, spending, and remaining balance at a glance.
- **Automatic category tracking:** Preset categories for housing, food, transportation, and more, plus space to add your own.
- **Savings goal tracker:** Set a target, choose a deadline, and watch your progress.
- **Debt payoff planner:** Map out payments and see when each balance will reach zero.
- **Yearly summary:** Spot trends and review your finances across all twelve months.

**Who it's for**

Whether you're creating your first budget or replacing a system that no longer works, the template is built to be easy to use. No accounting background or advanced spreadsheet skills are required.

**Compatibility and availability**

The Clear Budget Template works with Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, and Numbers. It is available today for $12, and includes a short setup guide and free updates.
