To add depth and complexity to roasted vegetables, try seasoning them with a mixture of olive oil, salt, and a pinch of sugar. The sweetness of the sugar will balance out the bitterness of the vegetables, while the olive oil will enhance their natural flavors. Start with a small amount of sugar, about 1/4 teaspoon per serving, and adjust to taste. This simple seasoning trick will elevate your roasted vegetables to a whole new level of flavor.
