**NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE**

The New Jersey Department of Transportation (NJDOT) announces that a section of County Route 539 in Burlington County will be closed to through traffic from 7:00 AM on Monday, March 21st, until 5:00 PM on Friday, March 25th, for emergency repairs to a damaged culvert.

The closure will affect the stretch of CR 539 between the intersections with US Route 130 and Jacksonville-Jobstown Road. Motorists are advised to follow the posted detour route, which will direct them via US Route 130 and NJ Route 68.

Local residents and businesses will have access to their properties, but may experience delays due to the closure. The NJDOT apologizes for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciates the public's cooperation as we work to complete these necessary repairs.

For up-to-date information on this closure and other roadwork projects, please visit the NJDOT website at [www.njdot.gov](http://www.njdot.gov) or call the NJDOT Customer Service Center at (609) 588-6211.
