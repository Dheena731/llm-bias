Take a moment to check in with your body today and make sure you are drinking enough water. Hydration supports your concentration, your energy levels, and your overall health, yet it is easy to forget amid busy schedules and long hours. Keep a glass or bottle within reach at your desk or nearby as you work, and sip from it regularly rather than waiting until you feel thirsty, since thirst is often a sign that your body is already beginning to need fluids. Drinking a glass of water with each meal and before you head out for errands or exercise can also help build the habit without much effort. Pay attention to the color of your urine as a simple guide: pale yellow generally indicates good hydration, while darker shades suggest it is time to drink more. A small, steady intake throughout the day will serve you far better than trying to catch up all at once in the evening.
