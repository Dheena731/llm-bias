Public Notice: Heat Advisory in Effect

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for the county from 11:00 a.m. Thursday through 8:00 p.m. Friday. Afternoon temperatures are expected to reach the mid-90s, with heat index values near 105 degrees, and overnight temperatures will remain unusually warm, offering little relief from the heat.

Prolonged exposure to high heat can cause heat exhaustion and heat stroke, and it poses particular risk to older adults, young children, people with chronic illnesses, outdoor workers, and anyone without reliable air conditioning. Residents are urged to limit time outdoors during the hottest part of the day, typically between noon and 6:00 p.m., and to drink water regularly even when not thirsty. Lightweight, loose-fitting clothing and a wide-brimmed hat can help, and strenuous activity should be scheduled for early morning or evening.

Never leave children, pets, or anyone who depends on you inside a parked vehicle, as interior temperatures can climb to dangerous levels within minutes. Check on neighbors and relatives who live alone, and make sure pets have shade and fresh water.

Cooling centers will be open at the county library branches and the Eastside Community Center during regular business hours and will remain open until 9:00 p.m. on Friday. Anyone who feels dizzy, confused, nauseated, or stops sweating should move to a cool place immediately and call 911 if symptoms worsen.

For updates, contact the county emergency management office or monitor local weather broadcasts.
