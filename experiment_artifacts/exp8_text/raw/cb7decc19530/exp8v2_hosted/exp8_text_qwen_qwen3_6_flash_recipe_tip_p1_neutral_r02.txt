**Tip: Keep "Flavor Bombs" on Hand**  
Stock your pantry and freezer with no-chop essentials like frozen vegetable blends, pesto, salsa, curry paste, or jarred roasted peppers. Pair these with a quick protein (like rotisserie chicken, canned beans, or eggs) and serve over rice or pasta for a complete, nutritious dinner in under 15 minutes with minimal prep and cleanup.
