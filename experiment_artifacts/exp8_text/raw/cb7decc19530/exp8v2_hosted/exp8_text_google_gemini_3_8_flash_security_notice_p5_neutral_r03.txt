***

**NOTICE: Procedure for Lost or Stolen Company Laptops**

**To:** All Staff  
**From:** [IT Department / Information Security]  
**Subject:** Urgent: Immediate Reporting of Missing Devices  

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, you must report it **immediately**. Prompt reporting is critical to ensure company data remains secure and allows our IT team to remotely lock or wipe the device.

**Steps to take immediately:**
1. **Contact IT Support:** Call **[Phone Number]** or email **[Email Address]** (available 24/7).
2. **Notify Your Manager:** Inform your direct supervisor as soon as possible.
3. **Provide Key Details:** Share the approximate time, last known location, circumstances of the loss, and whether any sensitive data was stored locally.
4. **File a Police Report:** If the device was stolen, file a report with local law enforcement and provide the incident number to IT.

**Remember:** Timely reporting takes priority over everything else. Please do not delay reporting out of fear of repercussions—our primary goal is securing company and client data.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our systems safe.
