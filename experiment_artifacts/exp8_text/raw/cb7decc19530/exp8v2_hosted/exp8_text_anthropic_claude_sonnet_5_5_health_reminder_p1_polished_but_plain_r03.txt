It's time for your weekly fitness check-in. Take a few minutes to look back over the past seven days and see how your efforts measure up against your goals. Review how many workouts you completed, how your energy and recovery felt, and whether any numbers you track, such as steps, distances, weights, or times, have moved in the right direction. Notice what went well and what got in the way, whether that was a busy schedule, poor sleep, or a plan that was simply too ambitious. Progress is rarely a straight line, so treat any setbacks as useful information rather than failure. Once you have a clear picture, choose one or two small adjustments for the week ahead, such as scheduling your sessions in advance or adding an extra rest day. A brief, honest review each week keeps your goals realistic and your momentum steady.
