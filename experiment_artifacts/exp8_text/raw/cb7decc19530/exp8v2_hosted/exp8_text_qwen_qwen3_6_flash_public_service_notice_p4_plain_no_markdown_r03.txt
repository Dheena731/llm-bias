The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for the region beginning today at noon and continuing through 8 pm Thursday. High temperatures are forecast to approach ninety-five degrees with humid conditions creating dangerous heat index values. Residents are urged to stay hydrated by drinking water regularly and to limit strenuous outdoor activity during the peak afternoon hours. Those living alone, especially seniors, should be checked on by friends or family members. Ensure that pets remain in cooled indoor areas and never leave children or animals inside parked cars. Wear loose, light-colored clothing if you must go outside and take frequent breaks in shaded or air-conditioned spaces. Monitor local weather updates for changes to this alert.
