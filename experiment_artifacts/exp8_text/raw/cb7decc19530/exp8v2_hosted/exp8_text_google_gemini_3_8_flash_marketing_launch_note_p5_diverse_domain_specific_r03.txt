Textbooks always set the scene in an unnervingly quiet train station where everyone speaks at 0.75x speed and uses the formal register.

Then you land in the actual country, someone swallows four vowels, uses three pieces of regional slang, and asks you a question while traffic is roaring—and suddenly your 400-day app streak is useless.

That gap is why we made **The Intermediate Slump**. 

This isn't a beginner’s show. We aren’t going to teach you how to order a beer or conjugate regular *-er* verbs. Instead, each 15-minute episode pulls apart the stuff that actually trips you up between B1 and C1:

*   The filler sounds native speakers use to buy three seconds of thinking time (and why using textbook pauses makes you sound robotic).
*   Why listening comprehension collapses the moment a speaker gets tired, excited, or drunk.
*   How to handle the psychological embarrassment of having an adult brain trapped behind a toddler-level vocabulary.

Episode 1 is up. We break down the phonetic shortcuts of fast conversational speech—specifically, which syllables natives consistently drop, and how your brain can fill in the blanks before panic sets in.

No grammar tables. No repeating after the chime. Just the mechanics of actually understanding people.
