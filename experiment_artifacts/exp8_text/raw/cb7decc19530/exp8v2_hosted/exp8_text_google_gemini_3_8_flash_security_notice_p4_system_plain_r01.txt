To enhance the security of your account and protect your personal information, two-factor authentication is now available and required for all users. This security feature adds an extra layer of defense against unauthorized access by asking for a verification code in addition to your normal password whenever you sign in. You can activate this feature immediately by navigating to the account settings section on your profile and selecting the security preferences tab. From there, follow the prompts to connect your mobile device or authenticator application. Please complete this setup within the next fourteen days to prevent any interruption to your account access. If you need assistance with the setup process, contact the technical support team.
