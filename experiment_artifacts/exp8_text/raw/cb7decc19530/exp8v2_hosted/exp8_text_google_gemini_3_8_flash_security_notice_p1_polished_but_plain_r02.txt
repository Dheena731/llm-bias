Protecting company systems and data requires diligent password habits from every team member. One of the most common ways unauthorized users gain access to corporate networks is through password reuse. When you use the same password for both your work account and outside services, a security breach on an external website directly exposes our organization to risk. Cybercriminals routinely take stolen login information from third-party breaches and attempt to use those identical credentials across business systems, often compromising accounts within seconds.

To maintain a secure environment, ensure that your work credentials are completely unique and never shared with personal accounts or non-work platforms. Avoid using slight variations of old passwords or simple predictive patterns. Instead, utilize the approved corporate password manager to create and store complex passphrases for every service you use. If you believe your password may have been exposed in an external breach or if you notice any suspicious account activity, contact the IT service desk immediately so your access can be secured.
