**Subject: Your end-of-year tax statement**

Hello,

Your end-of-year tax statement is a summary of the income you received during the year and any tax that was already taken out or paid on it. Tax authorities generally use this information to work out whether you owe more tax or are due a refund, so you will usually need the statement when you prepare your tax return.

**What to do with it:**
1. **Check the details.** Make sure your name, address, and tax identification number are correct, and that the income and tax amounts match your own records.
2. **Keep it safe.** Hold on to the statement with your other tax records, since you may need it when you file or if you are asked to verify your figures later.
3. **Report problems promptly.** If anything looks wrong or missing, contact the organization that issued the statement so it can be corrected before you file.

If you are unsure how the statement applies to your situation, a qualified tax professional or your local tax authority can help.

Thank you.
