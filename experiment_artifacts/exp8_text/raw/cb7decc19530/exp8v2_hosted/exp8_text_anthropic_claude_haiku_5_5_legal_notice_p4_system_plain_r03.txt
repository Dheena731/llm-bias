Notice of Refund Eligibility

This notice sets out the conditions under which a customer may be entitled to a refund for a purchase made from the seller. Customers are asked to read these conditions carefully before requesting a refund.

A refund request may be submitted within thirty (30) days of the date of delivery or, for services, within thirty (30) days of the date the service was first made available. Requests made after this period will be considered only at the seller's discretion.

A customer is generally eligible for a full refund where the product is defective, damaged in transit, materially different from its description, or was not delivered. Products must be returned in substantially the same condition in which they were received, with all original packaging, accessories, and proof of purchase.

Refunds will not be issued for products that have been used beyond reasonable inspection, altered, or damaged through customer misuse or neglect. Custom or personalized items, perishable goods, and digital content that has been downloaded or accessed are excluded from refund eligibility unless they are defective.

Where a refund is approved, it will be issued to the original method of payment within fourteen (14) business days of the seller receiving the returned item or confirming the claim. The seller may deduct reasonable return shipping costs where the return results from a change of mind rather than a defect or seller error.

Nothing in this notice limits any statutory rights a customer may hold under applicable consumer protection law. In the event of conflict between this notice and such law, the law shall prevail.

The seller reserves the right to amend this notice at any time. Amendments apply to purchases made after the date of amendment.
