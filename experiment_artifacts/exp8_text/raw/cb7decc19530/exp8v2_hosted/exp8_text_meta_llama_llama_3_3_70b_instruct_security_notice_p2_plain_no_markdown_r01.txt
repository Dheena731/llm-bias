We have seen an increase in phishing emails being sent to staff. These emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information such as your login credentials. They often appear to be from a legitimate source, but may contain spelling or grammar mistakes, or ask you to click on a suspicious link.

If you receive an email that asks you to provide sensitive information or click on a link, do not respond or click on the link. Instead, delete the email and report it to the IT department. We will never ask you to provide your login credentials or other sensitive information via email.

It is essential that you are cautious when opening emails and attachments from unknown sources, and that you verify the authenticity of any email that asks you to take action. If you are unsure about the legitimacy of an email, please contact the IT department for advice.
