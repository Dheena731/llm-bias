**Subject: VPN Client Update, Version 4.2 Now Available**

Hello everyone,

We are releasing version 4.2 of the company VPN client. The update is required for all users and will be pushed automatically the next time your device connects to the network.

**What's changing:**
- The client now uses the updated login screen, which supports single sign-on.
- Connections reconnect automatically after a brief network interruption.
- The old "Legacy Mode" setting has been removed. If you relied on it, contact IT before the update.

**What you need to do:**
- No action is required for most users. Restart the client if it prompts you.
- If you see a certificate warning after updating, close the client and reopen it. If the warning persists, submit a ticket to the IT help desk.

The previous version will stop working on March 31. Please update before then.

Questions can go to helpdesk@example.com or the #it-support channel.
