**Tip: Cook once, assemble all week.**

On Sunday, prepare two or three basic components instead of full meals: a grain (rice, quinoa, or pasta), a protein (roasted chicken, baked tofu, or hard-boiled eggs), and a big batch of roasted or raw vegetables. Store each in its own container.

Each day, mix and match them into a different lunch, such as a grain bowl on Monday, a wrap on Tuesday, and a salad on Wednesday. Add a quick sauce or dressing to change the flavor so nothing feels repetitive. This keeps prep time short, reduces food waste, and means you are never starting from scratch on a hectic weekday.
