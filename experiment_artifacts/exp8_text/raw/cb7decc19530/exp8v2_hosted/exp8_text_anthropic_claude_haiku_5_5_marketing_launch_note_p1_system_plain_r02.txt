Introducing Ledgerly, the simple way for small businesses to keep their money in order.

We built Ledgerly for owners who would rather spend their evenings with customers than with spreadsheets. It connects to your bank accounts and card processors, sorts every transaction automatically, and shows you where your money is coming from and where it is going, all in one clear dashboard. Invoices can be sent and tracked in a few taps, and reminders go out on your behalf when a payment is late.

Tax season no longer has to be a scramble. Ledgerly keeps categorized records throughout the year, so when your accountant asks for numbers, you can hand them over in minutes rather than weeks.

Ledgerly is available today on iOS and Android, with a free plan for businesses with up to fifty transactions a month. Paid plans start at a price that fits a shop, a studio, or a one-person consultancy. New customers who sign up this week receive three months of the Plus plan at no cost.
