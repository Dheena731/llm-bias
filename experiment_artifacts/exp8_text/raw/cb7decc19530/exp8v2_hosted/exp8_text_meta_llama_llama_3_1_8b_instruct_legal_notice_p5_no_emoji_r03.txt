NOTICE OF ACCEPTABLE USE OF SHARED NETWORK

To All Users of the Shared Network,

This notice outlines the acceptable use of the shared network, which includes all computers, servers, and other devices connected to the network. The purpose of this notice is to ensure that all users understand their responsibilities and the rules that govern the use of the network.

1. USE OF THE NETWORK IS FOR BUSINESS AND PERSONAL USE ONLY.

You may use the network for legitimate business and personal purposes, such as accessing email, browsing the internet, and using software applications. However, you may not use the network for any unlawful or unauthorized activity.

2. PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES.

The following activities are strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action, including but not limited to:

* Engaging in any form of hacking, cracking, or other unauthorized access to computer systems or data;
* Sending or receiving unsolicited commercial email (spam);
* Engaging in any form of copyright infringement or other intellectual property rights violations;
* Engaging in any form of cyberbullying or harassment;
* Using the network to commit any other form of unauthorized or malicious activity.

3. USE OF SOFTWARE AND APPLICATIONS.

You may install and use software and applications on your devices connected to the network, but you must ensure that such software and applications do not contain any viruses, malware, or other malicious code that could harm the network or other users' devices.

4. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY.

You are responsible for ensuring that your data stored on the network is secure and not accessible to unauthorized users. You must also ensure that your devices are properly configured to prevent unauthorized access to the network.

5. MONITORING AND ENFORCEMENT.

The network administrators may monitor network activity to ensure compliance with this notice and to identify any security threats. If you are found to be in violation of this notice, disciplinary action may be taken, including but not limited to, termination of your access to the network.

By using the shared network, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to comply with this notice.
