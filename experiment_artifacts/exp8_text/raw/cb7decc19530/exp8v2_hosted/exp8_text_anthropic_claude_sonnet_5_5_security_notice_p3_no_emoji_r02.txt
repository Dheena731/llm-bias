**Reminder: Lock Your Screen When Away**

All staff are reminded to lock their computer screens whenever they leave their workstations, even for a short time.

**How to lock your screen:**
- Windows: Press Windows key + L
- Mac: Press Control + Command + Q

**Why it matters:**
- It protects confidential company and client information.
- It prevents unauthorized access to your accounts.
- It helps us meet our security and compliance obligations.

Thank you for helping keep our information secure. If you have questions or need assistance, please contact the IT Help Desk.
