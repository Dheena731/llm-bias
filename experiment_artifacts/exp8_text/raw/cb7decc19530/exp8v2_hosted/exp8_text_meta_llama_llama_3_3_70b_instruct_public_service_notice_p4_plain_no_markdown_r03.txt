A heat advisory has been issued for our area due to extremely high temperatures and humidity. Residents are advised to take precautions to stay cool and safe. This includes staying indoors during the hottest part of the day, drinking plenty of water, and avoiding strenuous activities. Vulnerable populations such as the elderly and young children should take extra precautions to stay cool. If you or someone you know is experiencing heat-related illness, please seek medical attention immediately. Cooling centers are available at local community centers for those who need a place to escape the heat. For more information, please contact your local health department.
