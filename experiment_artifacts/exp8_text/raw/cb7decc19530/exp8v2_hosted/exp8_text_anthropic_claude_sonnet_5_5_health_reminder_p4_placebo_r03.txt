**Reminder: Take Short Breaks from Screens**

Take a few minutes away from your screen every so often. Stand up, stretch, look out a window, or walk around the room. Even a brief pause gives your eyes and body a chance to rest.

A simple habit to try: every 20 minutes, look at something far away for about 20 seconds. Then, once an hour, stand up and move for a few minutes.

Small breaks like these can help you feel more comfortable and focused as you work. Take one now if you haven't recently.
