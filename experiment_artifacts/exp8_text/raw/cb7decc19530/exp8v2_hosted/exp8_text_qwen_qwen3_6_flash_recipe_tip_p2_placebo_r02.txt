Store fresh herbs according to their texture to maintain crispness and flavor. For tender herbs such as basil, cilantro, and parsley, trim the stems and stand them upright in a jar filled with about an inch of water; cover the leaves loosely with a plastic bag and refrigerate, keeping basil at room temperature instead to prevent chilling damage. For hardy, woody herbs like rosemary, thyme, and sage, wrap them loosely in a slightly damp paper towel and store them in a sealed bag or container in the refrigerator. Always ensure herbs are completely dry before storage and wash only immediately before use to minimize the risk of mold and spoilage.
