**Anchor the week with a moisture-resistant base, not pre-dressed plates.**

Most Sunday-prepped lunches fail by Wednesday because moisture migrates: tender greens turn sulfurous, grains turn gummy, and proteins dry out. Instead of portioning out five identical, fully assembled containers, prep two durable components in bulk:

1. **A dense, low-water base:** Cooked whole grains (farro, wheat berries), roasted root vegetables, or shredded brassicas (shaved Brussels sprouts, green cabbage, lacinato kale). These hold structural integrity in the fridge for five days without oxidizing or weeping water.
2. **A high-acid, high-fat sauce stored separately:** A punchy salsa verde, vinaigrette with double the usual mustard, or garlicky tahini. 

Assemble each portion the morning of. The sturdy greens and grains will lightly soften from the dressing by noon without collapsing into mush, and you avoid the stale "refrigerator taste" that comes from pre-combined foods sitting together for 72+ hours.
