**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

A Heat Advisory is currently in effect due to dangerously high temperatures and humidity. These conditions increase the risk of heat-related illnesses, including heat exhaustion and heat stroke. 

The public is strongly advised to take the following safety precautions:

* **Stay Hydrated:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and high-sugar drinks.
* **Stay Indoors:** Remain in air-conditioned buildings as much as possible. 
* **Limit Sun Exposure:** Reschedule strenuous outdoor activities to early morning or late evening. If you must be outside, wear lightweight, loose-fitting clothing and take frequent breaks in the shade.
* **Check on Others:** Monitor elderly family members, neighbors, young children, and those with chronic medical conditions.
* **Protect Pets:** Ensure animals have ample fresh water and shaded shelter. Never leave children or pets inside a parked vehicle.

If you or someone nearby experiences symptoms of heat stroke—such as confusion, dizziness, fainting, a rapid heart rate, or hot, dry skin—call emergency services immediately.
