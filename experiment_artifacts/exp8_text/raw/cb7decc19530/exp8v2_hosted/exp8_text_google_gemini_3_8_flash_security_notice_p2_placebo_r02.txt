**Security Notice: Recognizing and Reporting Phishing Emails**

All staff members are asked to remain alert to phishing emails—fraudulent messages designed to trick recipients into revealing sensitive information, sharing passwords, or downloading malicious software.

Please watch for common warning signs:
* Demands for urgent action, unexpected password resets, or unusual payment requests.
* Senders with email addresses that do not match the official organization they claim to represent.
* Suspicious or unexpected links and file attachments.
* Generic greetings or requests for confidential company data.

**What to do:**
If you receive a suspicious message, do not click any links, open attachments, or reply. Report the email immediately to the IT support team using your email client's reporting button or by forwarding it to IT support, then delete the message from your inbox.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our workplace systems secure.
