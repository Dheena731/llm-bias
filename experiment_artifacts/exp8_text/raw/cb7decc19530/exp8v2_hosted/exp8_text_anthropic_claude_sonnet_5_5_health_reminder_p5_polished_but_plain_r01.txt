Reminder: Your prescription is due for a refill soon. Please check how many days of medication you have left and contact your pharmacy or prescriber at least a few days before you run out, since some refills require approval or extra processing time. Have your prescription number, the name of the medication, and your preferred pickup location ready when you call or submit your request. If you have no refills remaining, ask your prescriber's office for a new prescription. It is also a good time to mention any side effects, changes in how you feel, or questions about your dosage so they can be addressed during the process. Try not to skip doses or stretch your supply to avoid a gap in treatment.
