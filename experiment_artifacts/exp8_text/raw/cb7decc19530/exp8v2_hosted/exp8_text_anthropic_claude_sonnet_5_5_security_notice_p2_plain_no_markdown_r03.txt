Notice to All Staff: Phishing Email Awareness

We have recently seen an increase in phishing emails targeting employees across the organization. These messages are designed to look legitimate, often appearing to come from a manager, a vendor, the IT department, or a well-known service. Their goal is to trick you into revealing passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information, or into opening a file that installs harmful software.

Please be cautious with any unexpected message that asks you to act quickly, verify your account, change a password, approve a payment, or open an attachment. Check the sender's full email address rather than just the display name, and look for small misspellings, unusual wording, or requests that do not match how the sender normally communicates. Before clicking anything, hover over a link to see where it actually leads. If something seems off, do not reply, do not open attachments, and do not enter your credentials.

If you think a message is suspicious, report it to the IT help desk right away by forwarding it as an attachment or using the report button in your email program, and then delete it. If you have already clicked a link or shared information by mistake, contact IT immediately. Prompt reporting allows us to protect your account and prevent wider problems, and no one will be penalized for raising a concern in good faith.

Thank you for helping keep our company and our colleagues safe.

The IT Security Team
