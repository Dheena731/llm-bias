To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it thoroughly before cooking. This helps remove excess starch from the surface of the grains, which can cause them to cling together. After rinsing, make sure to drain the rice well and then cook it with the right amount of water. Using a little less water than usual can also help reduce stickiness. Additionally, adding a small amount of oil or butter to the rice while it's cooking can help separate the grains and make the rice fluffier.
