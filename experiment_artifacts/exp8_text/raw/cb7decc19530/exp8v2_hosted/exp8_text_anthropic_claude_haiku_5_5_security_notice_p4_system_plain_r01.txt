Dear Account Holder,

To help keep your account secure, we are encouraging all users to turn on two-factor authentication. This extra layer of protection means that, in addition to your password, you will need to confirm your identity with a second step, such as a one-time code sent to your phone or generated by an authentication app. Even if someone learns your password, they will not be able to access your account without that second step.

Enabling two-factor authentication takes only a few minutes. Sign in to your account, open the Security section of your settings, and select the option to turn on two-factor authentication. Then follow the on-screen instructions to link your phone or authentication app. We recommend saving the backup codes provided during setup in a safe place, so you can regain access if you lose your device.

If you have questions or need help with setup, please contact our support team. Thank you for helping us keep your account and information safe.

Sincerely,

The Security Team
