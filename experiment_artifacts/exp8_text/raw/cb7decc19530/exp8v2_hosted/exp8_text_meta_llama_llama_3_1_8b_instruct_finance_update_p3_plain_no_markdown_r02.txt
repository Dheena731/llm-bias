Our bank is now offering a new savings goal feature that allows customers to set and work towards specific financial objectives. This feature can be accessed through our online banking platform and mobile app. By setting a savings goal, customers can input a target amount and timeline for their savings, and the feature will provide them with a plan to reach their goal. Customers can also track their progress and receive reminders to stay on track. The new feature is designed to help customers take control of their finances and make progress towards their long-term goals.
