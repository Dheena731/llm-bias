Notice Regarding the Use of Cookies

This website uses cookies and similar technologies to operate properly, to improve your experience, and to understand how the site is used. By continuing to browse this website after being shown this notice, you acknowledge that cookies may be placed on your device as described below. Where applicable law requires your consent, we will ask for it before placing any cookies that are not strictly necessary.

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your computer, phone, or other device when you visit. Cookies allow the site to recognize your device, remember your preferences, and carry information from one page to another. Similar technologies, such as pixels, local storage, and software identifiers, serve comparable purposes and are treated as cookies for the purposes of this notice.

We use several categories of cookies. Strictly necessary cookies are essential for the website to function, and they support features such as security, page navigation, load balancing, and the retention of items in a shopping cart or form. These cookies cannot be switched off in our systems, although you may configure your browser to block them, in which case some parts of the site may not work. Preference cookies remember choices you have made, such as language or region, so that you do not need to repeat them on each visit. Analytics cookies collect information about how visitors use the site, including pages viewed, time spent, and error messages encountered. We use this information in aggregated form to maintain and improve our services. Marketing cookies, where used, record your browsing activity so that advertising shown to you on this and other websites may be more relevant to your interests. These cookies may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages.

Some cookies are session cookies, which expire when you close your browser, while others are persistent cookies, which remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them. Third-party cookies are governed by the privacy practices of the companies that place them, and we encourage you to review those practices. We do not control how such third parties use the information they collect.

You may manage your choices at any time. You can adjust your cookie preferences through the settings tool provided on this website, and you can also use your browser settings to delete existing cookies or to block or restrict new ones. Please note that disabling certain cookies may reduce the functionality or performance of the site. If you withdraw consent previously given, the withdrawal will not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before that point.

Where cookies collect personal data, we process that data in accordance with our Privacy Policy, which explains the purposes of processing, the legal bases we rely upon, the periods for which data is retained, and your rights regarding access, correction, deletion, and objection. If you have questions about this notice or about our use of cookies, please contact us using the details provided on our contact page.

We may update this notice from time to time to reflect changes in technology, law, or our practices. The date of the most recent revision will appear on this page, and we encourage you to review the notice periodically.
