Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starting Next Monday

Hello team,

Starting Monday, June 2, we will be moving to a new expense approval workflow. The goal is to make reimbursements faster and more transparent for everyone.

Here is what is changing:

Submit all expense reports through the updated portal at expenses.company.com. Paper forms and email submissions will no longer be accepted.

Expenses under $250 will be approved by your direct manager. Expenses of $250 or more will also require sign-off from the finance department.

Receipts must be uploaded within 14 days of the purchase date.

You will receive an automatic email notification at each stage, so you can track the status of your request in real time.

A short training session will be held on Thursday, May 29, at 10:00 a.m. in Conference Room B, and a recording will be available afterward for anyone who cannot attend. Please reach out to the finance team at finance@company.com with any questions.

Thank you for your cooperation as we make this transition.

Best regards,
Administration Team
