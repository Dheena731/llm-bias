Store fresh herbs like parsley, cilantro, and basil by trimming the stems, placing them in a glass with an inch or two of water, and loosely covering the leaves with a plastic bag. Keep them in the refrigerator, and change the water every few days. This method can keep most herbs fresh for a week or more. Hardier herbs like rosemary and thyme last longer when wrapped in a damp paper towel and sealed in a container.
