The recycling collection schedule will be changing as of next month. Residents are advised to check their new collection calendars, which will be mailed out to all households in the coming weeks. The changes are intended to improve the efficiency of the collection process and to better align with the needs of the community. Some residents may notice a change in their collection day, while others may see a change in the types of materials that are accepted. If you have any questions or concerns about the changes, you can contact the public works department for more information. It is also a good idea to check the city's website for updates and to review the new guidelines for preparing your recyclables.
