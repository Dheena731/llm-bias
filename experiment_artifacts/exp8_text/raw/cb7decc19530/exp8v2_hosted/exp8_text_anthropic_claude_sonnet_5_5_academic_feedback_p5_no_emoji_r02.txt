**Comment on Missing Citations**

Several claims in this essay rely on statistics, research findings, and specific historical details, but no sources are cited for them. For example, the statement in the second paragraph about rising rates of student burnout is presented as established fact without any reference. Without citations, readers cannot verify the information, and the essay's credibility suffers.

Please add in-text citations wherever you draw on outside sources, whether you quote, paraphrase, or summarize them. Include a matching reference list at the end, formatted consistently in the required style (APA, MLA, or Chicago). Proper attribution also ensures you give credit to the original authors and avoids unintentional plagiarism.

If you are unsure whether a statement needs a citation, a good rule is to cite anything that is not your own idea or that is not common knowledge. Your argument will be stronger once your evidence is clearly supported.
