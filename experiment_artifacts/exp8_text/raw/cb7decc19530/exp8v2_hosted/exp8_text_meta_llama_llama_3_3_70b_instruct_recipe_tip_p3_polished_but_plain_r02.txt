To make rice less sticky, it's essential to rinse it thoroughly before cooking. Rinsing rice removes excess starch, which is the main culprit behind stickiness. Simply place the rice in a fine mesh strainer and rinse it under cold running water, stirring occasionally, until the water runs clear. After rinsing, cook the rice with the right amount of water, as excess water can also lead to stickiness. Generally, a 1:1.25 to 1:1.5 water-to-rice ratio works well. Once the rice is cooked, fluff it with a fork to separate the grains, and let it sit for a few minutes to allow any excess moisture to evaporate. By following these simple steps, you can enjoy perfectly cooked, non-sticky rice with your favorite dishes.
