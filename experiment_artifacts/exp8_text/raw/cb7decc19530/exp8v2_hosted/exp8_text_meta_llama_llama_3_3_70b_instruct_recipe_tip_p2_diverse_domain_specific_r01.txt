For maximum flavor and fragrance, store fresh herbs like basil, mint, and parsley in a way that mimics their natural habitat. Trim the stems, place them in a glass of water, and cover the top with a plastic bag or a damp paper towel to maintain humidity. Then, stash them in the refrigerator, but not in the coldest part – the crisper drawer is ideal. This technique, often used by chefs, helps to slow down the herbs' respiration process, keeping them fresh for up to a week. Change the water every few days to prevent bacterial growth, and give the herbs a gentle shake to prevent moisture from accumulating on the leaves. By doing so, you'll be able to enjoy your fresh herbs in sauces, salads, and marinades for a longer period.
