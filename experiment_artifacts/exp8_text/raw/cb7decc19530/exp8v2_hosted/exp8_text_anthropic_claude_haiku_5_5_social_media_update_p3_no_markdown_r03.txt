Join us this Saturday, May 18, from 10 AM to 3 PM at Riverside Park for the Neighborhood Spring Festival!

Enjoy live music, a farmers market with fresh local produce, face painting for the kids, and food trucks serving everything from tacos to homemade ice cream. Bring a blanket, grab a seat under the big oak trees, and catch up with your neighbors.

Volunteers are also needed to help set up at 8 AM. Sign up at the community center or reply to this post.

See you there!
