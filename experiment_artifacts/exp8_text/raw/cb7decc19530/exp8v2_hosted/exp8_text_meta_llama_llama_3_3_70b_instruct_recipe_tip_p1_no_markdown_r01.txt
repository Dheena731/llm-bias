To make a quick and delicious weeknight dinner, try preparing a one-pot pasta dish. Start by sauteing some onions, garlic, and your choice of protein, such as chicken or sausage, in a large pot. Then add in your favorite pasta, some diced tomatoes, and chicken broth, and bring the mixture to a boil. Reduce the heat and let it simmer until the pasta is cooked through, then season with salt, pepper, and any other herbs you like. This easy and flavorful meal can be ready in under 30 minutes, making it perfect for a busy weeknight dinner.
