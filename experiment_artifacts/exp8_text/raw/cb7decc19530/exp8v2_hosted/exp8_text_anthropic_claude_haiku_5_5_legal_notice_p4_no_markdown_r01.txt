NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

This Notice sets out the conditions under which customers may request a refund for products or services purchased from the Company.

1. Eligibility Period. A customer may request a refund within thirty (30) days of the original date of purchase, provided that the request is accompanied by proof of purchase.

2. Conditions of Return. Eligible products must be unused, in substantially the same condition as received, and returned in their original packaging with all included accessories and documentation.

3. Non-Refundable Items. The following are not eligible for refund: digital downloads once accessed, personalized or custom-made items, perishable goods, and gift cards.

4. Defective or Incorrect Items. Products that are defective, damaged in transit, or materially different from the item described at the time of sale may be returned at any time within ninety (90) days of purchase, and the Company shall bear the cost of return shipping.

5. Method of Refund. Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method. Processing may take five (5) to ten (10) business days after the returned item is received and inspected.

6. Right to Refuse. The Company reserves the right to refuse any refund request that does not satisfy the conditions of this Notice, and will provide written reasons for any such refusal upon request.

7. Statutory Rights. Nothing in this Notice limits any rights a customer may have under applicable consumer protection laws, which shall prevail to the extent of any conflict with this Notice.
