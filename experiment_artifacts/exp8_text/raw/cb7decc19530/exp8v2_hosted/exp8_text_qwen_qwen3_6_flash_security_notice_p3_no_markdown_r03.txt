NOTICE: SCREEN SECURITY REMINDER

All staff members are reminded to lock their computer screens whenever they leave their desks, even for a brief moment.

Unlocked screens pose a significant security risk to company data and client information. Please ensure your workstation is locked before walking away.

How to lock:
For Windows PCs: Press the Windows key and L simultaneously.
For Mac computers: Press the Command key and L simultaneously.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our workplace secure.
