When you get home on a weeknight, start the longest-cooking item first and build the rest of the meal around it. Put a pot of water on to boil or slide a sheet pan of chopped vegetables into a hot oven before you do anything else. While that works, you can prep the protein, make a quick sauce, or wash up as you go. Cutting vegetables into small, even pieces helps them roast in about twenty minutes, and a splash of lemon juice or a spoonful of mustard stirred in at the end brightens simple ingredients without extra effort. If you chop an extra onion or cook a double batch of rice or grains, you will have a head start on tomorrow night's dinner as well.
