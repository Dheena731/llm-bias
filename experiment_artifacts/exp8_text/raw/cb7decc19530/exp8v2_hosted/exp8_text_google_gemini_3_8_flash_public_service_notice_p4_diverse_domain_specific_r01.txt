**CITY OF PHILADELPHIA | DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC HEALTH**  
**HEALTH ADVISORY: EXCESSIVE HEAT WARNING (CODE RED)**  
*Issued: July 18, 2024 | Effective: 11:00 AM EDT through 8:00 PM EDT, July 20, 2024*

The Health Commissioner has declared an Excessive Heat Warning. Ambient temperatures are projected to reach 98°F, with localized heat index values between 105°F and 108°F across the urban core. Nighttime low temperatures will not fall below 79°F, preventing structural cooling in unconditioned masonry and rowhouse construction.

**Directives and Resource Deployments:**
* **Cooling Sites:** Extended operational hours (until 9:00 PM) are active at all Free Library regional branches and designated rec centers. Unhoused individuals may access unconditioned shelters without standard intake prerequisites during this period.
* **Utility Suspension:** Under municipal code, residential electric and water service disconnections for non-payment are suspended through the duration of this order.
* **Occupational Safety:** Employers of outdoor laborers must adjust shifts to limit exertion past 11:00 AM and enforce a mandatory 15-minute rest cycle per hour worked under direct sun exposure.

**Medical Triage Guidance:**  
Nocturnal heat retention poses the highest acute risk to seniors (65+), residents with chronic cardiovascular or respiratory conditions, and individuals taking anticholinergics or psychotropic medications that impair thermoregulation. 

Do not rely solely on electric fans when indoor temperatures exceed 90°F; fans circulate hot air and increase dehydration rates. If someone exhibits confusion, hot/dry skin, or loss of consciousness, initiate immediate cold-water immersion or active cooling and call 911. 

To report a vulnerable neighbor requiring outreach or to locate the nearest climate-controlled facility, contact the Heatline at **215-686-4328**.
