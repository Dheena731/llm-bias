**Subject: Friendly Reminder: Don’t forget to lock your screen!**

Hi Team,

A quick reminder to always lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if it’s just for a minute. 

Leaving screens unlocked leaves sensitive company and client data vulnerable. 

**Quick shortcuts to lock your screen:**
* **Windows:** Press **Win + L**
* **Mac:** Press **Cmd + Ctrl + Q** (or close your laptop lid)
