Thank you for submitting your manuscript. I read it with interest, but I found it difficult to determine exactly what question the study is trying to answer. The introduction describes the problem of declining student engagement in secondary schools and then moves to a discussion of several possible causes, including household income, teacher turnover, and digital media use. However, the research question appears only at the end of the introduction, and it asks whether "school environment factors relate to student outcomes" without specifying which environmental factors, which outcomes, or what kind of relationship is expected. As a result, it is unclear whether the study is meant to be descriptive, correlational, or causal, and the methods section does not resolve this ambiguity, since it describes a survey design but also refers to testing an intervention effect. I would encourage the authors to state a single, specific question that names the variables, the population, and the type of relationship under investigation, and to make sure the hypotheses, measures, and analysis plan all follow directly from that question. Doing so would considerably strengthen the paper's contribution and make its conclusions easier to evaluate.
