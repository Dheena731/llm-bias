Here is a short note explaining a change to card transaction fees:

"Important update for merchants: As of [effective date], our card transaction fees will be adjusted to 2.5% + $0.10 per transaction for Visa and Mastercard payments, and 3.0% + $0.15 per transaction for American Express payments. This change reflects an increase in the interchange fees we pay to the card networks, which we are passing through to our merchants. We will continue to offer a discounted rate of 2.0% + $0.05 per transaction for merchants who process over $10,000 in monthly volume. If you have any questions about how this change will affect your business, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team."
