**HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

A heat advisory has been issued for our area, effective [insert date and time] to [insert date and time]. Temperatures are expected to reach the mid-90s to low 100s, with heat indices making it feel even hotter.

**TAKE PRECAUTIONS TO STAY SAFE**

Older adults, young children, and people with pre-existing medical conditions are particularly vulnerable to heat-related illnesses. Take steps to protect yourself and your loved ones:

* Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water
* Stay in air-conditioned spaces or shaded areas when possible
* Avoid strenuous activities during the hottest part of the day
* Check on vulnerable neighbors and friends
* Keep an eye on pets and make sure they have access to plenty of water and shade

**STAY INFORMED**

Stay up to date with the latest weather forecast and heat advisory updates by visiting our website or following us on social media. If you or someone you know is experiencing heat-related symptoms such as dizziness, nausea, or headaches, seek medical attention immediately.
