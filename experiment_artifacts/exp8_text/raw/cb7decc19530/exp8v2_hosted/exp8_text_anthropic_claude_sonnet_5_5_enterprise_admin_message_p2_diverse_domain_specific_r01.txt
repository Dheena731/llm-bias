**Subject: Expense approvals: new routing starts Monday, March 3**

Hi all,

Starting Monday, expense reports will route by amount instead of going to your manager every time.

**Who approves what**
- **Under $250:** auto-approved if every line has a receipt and a category. Finance spot-checks about 10% after the fact.
- **$250 to $2,500:** your manager, as before.
- **Over $2,500:** your manager, then your department head.
- **Anything with client entertainment or alcohol:** always goes to Finance (Dana Okafor), regardless of amount. Please name the client and the attendees in the description.

**Timing**
Approvers have 3 business days. After that, the report escalates to their manager, so a vacation no longer holds up your reimbursement. Approved reports are paid in the Friday run if they're approved by Wednesday noon.

**What's not changing**
- Receipts are still required for anything over $25.
- Corporate card charges still need to be matched within 30 days of the statement date.
- Mileage still uses the flat rate in the policy doc.

**What to do now**
1. Clear out any drafts you've been sitting on. Reports started under the old flow will be approved the old way only if they're submitted by Friday, Feb 28.
2. If you approve for others, check that your delegate is set in your profile (Settings → Approvals). If it's blank, escalations will go straight to your manager.

Questions or odd cases (split bills, foreign currency, reimbursing a contractor) go to #finance-help or ap@yourcompany.com. For anything urgent, message me directly.
