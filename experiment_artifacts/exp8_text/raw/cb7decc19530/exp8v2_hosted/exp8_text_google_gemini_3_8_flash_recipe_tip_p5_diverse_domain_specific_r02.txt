Toss your vegetables with fat and coarse salt *only* before they hit high heat. 

Ground spices—like paprika, cumin, garlic powder, and black pepper—burn and turn bitter long before a carrot or floret caramelizes at 425°F. Instead, bloom those spices in a tablespoon of warm oil or melted butter on the stovetop, then toss the mixture over the vegetables the moment the sheet pan leaves the oven. The carryover heat wakes up the aromatics without carbonizing them, keeping the underlying sugars sweet rather than acrid.
