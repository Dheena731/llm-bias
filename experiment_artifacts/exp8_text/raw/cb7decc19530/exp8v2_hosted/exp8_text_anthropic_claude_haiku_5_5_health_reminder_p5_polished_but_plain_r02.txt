This is a friendly reminder to request a refill of your prescription before your current supply runs out. Refill requests can take a day or two to process, and some pharmacies need more time over weekends or holidays, so it is best to ask a few days ahead rather than waiting until your last dose. You can usually request a refill by calling your pharmacy, using its mobile app or website, or asking your prescriber's office to send a new request on your behalf.

If you are unsure how many refills remain, your pharmacy can tell you. If your prescription has no refills left, please contact your doctor's office for a renewal before you run out. Do not skip doses or stretch your medication to make it last, as this can affect your health.

If you have any questions about your medication, or if you have experienced side effects or difficulty getting your prescription filled, please reach out to your pharmacist or care team. They are glad to help.
