This notice provides essential information regarding polling station locations for the upcoming election. Registered voters should visit the official state election website and use the address lookup tool to confirm their assigned voting site. Alternatively, you may call your county elections office using the contact information printed on your voter registration card. All polling locations will open at seven in the morning and remain open until eight in the evening on election day. Voters must present a valid government issued photo identification, and those who have not previously voted in this jurisdiction should also bring proof of residence. Specific entrance directions and building assignments are updated annually and can be verified through the online voter portal or by contacting your local clerk. Early voting operates at separate community centers with distinct operating hours. Please verify your registration status and confirm your polling place no later than fourteen days before the election to allow time for corrections. Voters requiring wheelchair access, translation services, or auxiliary aids should notify the elections office in advance. Thank you for fulfilling your civic duty.
