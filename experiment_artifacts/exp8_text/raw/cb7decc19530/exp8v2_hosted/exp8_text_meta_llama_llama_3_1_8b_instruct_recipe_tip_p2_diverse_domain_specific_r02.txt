Here's a tip for storing fresh herbs with a twist:

**The "Poke-and-Wrap" Method for Preserving Fresh Herbs**

To keep fresh herbs like basil, cilantro, and parsley from wilting and losing their flavor, try the "poke-and-wrap" method. After washing and gently pat-drying the herbs, use a paper towel or clean cloth to "poke" the stems, creating small holes that allow for air circulation. Then, wrap the herbs in a damp paper towel or cloth, not too tightly, to maintain humidity without causing moisture buildup. Store the wrapped herbs in a breathable container, like a mesh bag or a ventilated glass container, and place them in the refrigerator at a consistent temperature below 40°F (4°C). This method helps prevent moisture from accumulating, which can lead to mold and spoilage, while also allowing the herbs to "breathe" and maintain their flavor and texture.
