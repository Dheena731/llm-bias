Here is a short workplace notice about phishing emails:

**Important: Beware of Phishing Emails**

As a reminder, our organization has seen an increase in phishing email attempts in recent weeks. These emails are designed to trick employees into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, credit card numbers, or other confidential data.

To protect yourself and our organization, please be cautious when receiving emails that:

* Ask for sensitive information or login credentials
* Contain suspicious links or attachments
* Have spelling or grammar mistakes
* Create a sense of urgency or panic

If you receive an email that you suspect is a phishing attempt, do not respond or click on any links. Instead, report it to our IT department immediately.

Remember, our organization will never ask you to reveal sensitive information via email. If you are unsure about the legitimacy of an email, it's always best to err on the side of caution and verify its authenticity before taking any action.

Thank you for your vigilance and help in keeping our organization's data secure.
