To be eligible for a refund, customers must submit a formal request within thirty calendar days of the original purchase date. The item in question must be unused, in the same condition as received, and returned in its original packaging, accompanied by a valid receipt or proof of purchase. Certain categories of goods are strictly non-refundable, including customized products, gift cards, downloadable digital media, and items explicitly designated as final sale. All returned items are subject to inspection upon receipt, and notification of approval or rejection will be issued thereafter. Approved refunds will be applied directly to the original method of payment within ten business days, minus any applicable shipping and restocking fees, unless the return is due to merchant error. The company reserves the full right to reject any refund request that does not meet these stated conditions.
