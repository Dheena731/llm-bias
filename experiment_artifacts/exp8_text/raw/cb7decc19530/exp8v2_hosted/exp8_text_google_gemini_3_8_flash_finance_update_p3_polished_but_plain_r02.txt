We are pleased to introduce our new savings goals feature, designed to help you plan, track, and reach your financial milestones with greater ease. You can now create dedicated targets for specific priorities, such as an emergency fund, a home down payment, or an upcoming trip. The tool allows you to establish a target amount, choose an end date, and monitor your progress as your balance grows. To keep your savings consistent, you can also set up automatic, recurring transfers directly toward any active goal. This update is available now in your account dashboard, where you can select the savings section to set up your first goal.
