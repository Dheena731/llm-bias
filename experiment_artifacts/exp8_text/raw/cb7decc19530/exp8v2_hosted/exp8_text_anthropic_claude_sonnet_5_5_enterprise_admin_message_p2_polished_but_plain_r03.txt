Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Beginning Next Month

Dear team,

Beginning on the first of next month, we will introduce a streamlined workflow for approving expenses. The change is meant to shorten reimbursement times, reduce back-and-forth over missing details, and give everyone a clearer view of where a request stands.

Under the new process, all expense reports will be submitted through the finance portal rather than by email. Each submission should include an itemized receipt, a brief description of the business purpose, and the relevant project or department code. Reports that arrive without these details will be returned automatically, so it is worth taking a moment to check them before submitting.

Approval will follow a two-step path. Your direct manager will review the request first and may approve it, return it with comments, or decline it. Once it is approved, the finance team will perform a final review for policy compliance. Expenses under 250 dollars will go straight to finance after manager approval, while anything above that amount will also require sign-off from your department head. Managers and department heads are expected to respond within three business days, and you will receive a notification at each stage.

Approved expenses will be reimbursed in the next scheduled payment run, which takes place every second Friday. Reports submitted by the Tuesday before a payment run will normally be included in that cycle. We also ask that expenses be submitted within 30 days of being incurred, as older claims may need additional justification.

The finance team will host two short walkthrough sessions during the final week of this month, and a recording will be shared afterward for anyone who cannot attend. If you have questions before then, please contact the finance help desk, and we will be glad to assist.

Thank you for your cooperation as we make this transition.

Best regards,
The Administration Team
