**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication for Your Account**

To better protect your account and personal information, we strongly recommend turning on two-factor authentication (2FA). With 2FA, signing in requires both your password and a second verification step, such as a one-time code sent to your phone or generated by an authenticator app.

**How to enable 2FA:**
1. Log in to your account and go to **Settings**.
2. Select **Security**, then choose **Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Follow the prompts to link your phone or authenticator app.
4. Save your backup codes in a secure place.

Enabling 2FA takes only a few minutes and significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access, even if your password is compromised.

If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact our support team.
