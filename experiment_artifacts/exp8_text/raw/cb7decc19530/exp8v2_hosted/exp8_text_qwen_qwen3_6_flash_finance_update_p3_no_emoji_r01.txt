**Update: New Savings Goals Feature**

We are pleased to introduce the Savings Goals feature, designed to help you prioritize and achieve your financial objectives.

With this update, you can now:
*   Set customized targets with specific amounts and deadlines.
*   Monitor your progress through detailed tracking and visual indicators.
*   Automate contributions to ensure consistent growth toward your goal.

To get started, navigate to the "Goals" section in your account dashboard and create your first target. This feature is available immediately in the latest version of our application.

Thank you for using our platform. We look forward to helping you reach your milestones.
