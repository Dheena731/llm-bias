Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection

Beginning Monday, the first of next month, curbside recycling collection in our community will move from a weekly schedule to every other week. Collection will continue to take place on your current pickup day, but residents should check the updated calendar to confirm which weeks are recycling weeks. Printed calendars will be mailed to every household within the next ten days, and a copy will also be available at the municipal office and the public library.

Along with the new schedule, we are updating the list of materials we can accept. Clean paper, cardboard, metal cans, glass bottles and jars, and plastic containers marked with the numbers one, two, and five will still be collected. Plastic bags, foam packaging, and shredded paper will no longer be accepted in curbside bins because they jam sorting equipment and contaminate otherwise good material. Residents can return clean plastic bags to participating grocery stores.

Please rinse containers, flatten cardboard boxes, and place all items loose in the bin rather than inside bags. Bins should be set out at the curb by seven in the morning on your collection day, with the handles facing the house and at least three feet of space between bins and other objects.

Households that need an additional or larger bin because of the longer interval between pickups may request one at no charge by calling the public works department during regular business hours. We appreciate your cooperation and your continued commitment to keeping our community clean.
