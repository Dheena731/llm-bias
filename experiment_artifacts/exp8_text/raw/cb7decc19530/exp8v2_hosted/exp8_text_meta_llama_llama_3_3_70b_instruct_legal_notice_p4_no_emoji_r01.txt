NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Pursuant to our return policy, customers may be eligible for a refund under certain circumstances. To be eligible for a refund, the customer must initiate the return process within 30 days of the original purchase date and the item must be in its original condition with all original packaging and accessories included. Refunds will be issued in the original form of payment and will be processed within 10 business days of receipt of the returned item. Please contact our customer service department for further information regarding refund eligibility and to initiate the return process.
