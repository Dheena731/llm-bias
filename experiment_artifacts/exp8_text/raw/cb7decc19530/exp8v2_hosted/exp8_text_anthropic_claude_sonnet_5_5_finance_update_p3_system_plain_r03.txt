We have added a new savings goal feature to help you set aside money for the things that matter most to you. You can now create a goal for anything you have in mind, whether it is a vacation, a new laptop, an emergency fund, or a down payment on a home. Just give your goal a name, choose a target amount, and pick a date if you want a deadline.

Once your goal is set up, you can track your progress at a glance. A simple progress bar shows how close you are to reaching your target, and the app will estimate how much you need to save each week or month to finish on time. If you fall behind, it will suggest an adjusted amount so you can get back on track without stress.

You can also automate your saving. Choose a recurring transfer from your checking account, or round up your everyday purchases to the nearest dollar and send the difference straight to your goal. Small contributions add up faster than most people expect.

You can create as many goals as you like and rearrange them by priority. When you reach a goal, you will get a notification, and you can move the funds back to your main balance or start a new goal right away.

The feature is available now in the latest version of the app. Open the Goals tab to get started, and let us know what you think. Your feedback will help shape the improvements we make next.
