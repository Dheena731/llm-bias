Tip of the day: stop scheduling everything at the exact start time. Give yourself a 10 or 15 minute buffer before each meeting in your calendar app. Your commute, bathroom break, or that last-minute email will no longer make you late, and you'll walk into every call feeling calm instead of rushed. Most calendar apps let you change the default event length or add an alert, so set it once and your whole week gets a little less stressful. Try it this week and let me know how it goes!
