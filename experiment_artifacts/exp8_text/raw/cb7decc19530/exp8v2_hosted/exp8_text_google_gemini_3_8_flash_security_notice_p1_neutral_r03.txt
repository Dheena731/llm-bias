**Security Reminder: Stop the Domino Effect—Don’t Reuse Passwords**

**Did you know?** Reusing the same password across multiple accounts is one of the easiest ways for cybercriminals to compromise our company network. 

If a personal website you use (like a shopping or social media site) experiences a data breach, attackers will immediately try those same login details on your work email and internal accounts. **One breach shouldn't unlock everything.**

**Best Practices to Keep Us Secure:**
* **Keep work and personal separate:** Never use your work email or password for personal accounts.
* **Make them unique:** Every account must have its own distinct password or passphrase.
* **Use a Password Manager:** Don't rely on memory or sticky notes. Use the company-approved password manager (*[Insert Tool Name, e.g., 1Password / Bitwarden]*) to generate and store complex passwords safely.

If you suspect one of your passwords has been compromised, please change it immediately and report it to the IT Security Team at **[Contact Info]**.

Thank you for doing your part to keep our workplace secure!
