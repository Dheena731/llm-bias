The Central Public Library is pleased to share its regular operating hours for visitors and patrons. The main branch is open Monday through Thursday from nine in the morning until eight in the evening. On Friday and Saturday, the library welcomes guests from nine in the morning until five in the evening. Sunday hours run from one in the afternoon until five in the evening. During these times, visitors can access book collections, public computers, private study rooms, and reference assistance from library staff. The automated book drop located near the west entrance remains available twenty-four hours a day for material returns, and the digital catalog can be accessed remotely at any time. The facility closes in observance of all recognized municipal holidays. We look forward to serving the community throughout the week.
