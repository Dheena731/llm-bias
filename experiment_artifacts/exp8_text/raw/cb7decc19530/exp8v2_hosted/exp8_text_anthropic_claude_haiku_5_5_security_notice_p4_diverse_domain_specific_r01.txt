**Two-factor sign-in for the patient portal starts March 3**

Beginning Monday, March 3, signing in to the patient scheduling portal and the shared drive will require a six-digit code from your phone after your password. Setup takes about five minutes.

To set it up:

1. Sign in at portal.harborstreetclinic.org and open **Security** from the menu under your name.
2. Choose **Add authenticator app**. Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, and Authy all work. Text-message codes are available, but use an app if you can.
3. Scan the QR code, enter the six-digit code the app shows, and save the ten backup codes that appear.

Backup codes work once each. Print them or store them in a password manager, not in a note on the same phone as your authenticator app. If that phone is lost, the backup codes are your only way back in.

If you're getting a new phone before March 3, set up the app on the new one first, or call the front desk at ext. 240 for help. After the 3rd, a lockout reset takes up to one business day because we verify identity in person.

The front desk tablets use shared logins and are not affected by this change. Dana is moving them to individual accounts separately, and you'll hear from her before that happens.
