Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Effective Next Monday

Dear team,

Beginning next Monday, June 3, all expense reports will move through a new approval workflow designed to speed up reimbursements and give finance clearer visibility into company spending. Under the new process, employees will submit expense reports through the updated portal, and each report will route automatically to the direct manager for first-level approval. Reports above $1,000 will also require sign-off from the relevant department head before they proceed to finance for payment.

Managers should aim to review submitted reports within two business days. Reports that remain unapproved after that window will appear on a daily reminder list, and finance will follow up directly on any items pending for more than five business days. Please keep receipts attached to each line item, as reports missing documentation will be returned to the submitter rather than advanced.

Training sessions will be held on Wednesday and Thursday of this week, and a short guide is available on the finance intranet page. If you have questions about how the workflow applies to your situation, please contact the finance operations team before submitting your first report under the new system.

Thank you for your cooperation as we make this transition.

Best regards,
Operations Administration
