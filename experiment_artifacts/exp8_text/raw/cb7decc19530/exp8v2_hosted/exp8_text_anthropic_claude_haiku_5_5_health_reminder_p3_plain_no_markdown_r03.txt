This is a friendly reminder that it may be time to schedule your annual health checkup. Regular checkups help your doctor catch potential health concerns early, review your medications, and keep track of important numbers such as blood pressure, cholesterol, and blood sugar. They also give you a chance to talk about any questions or changes you have noticed in how you feel.

To book an appointment, please contact your primary care office directly or use their online patient portal if one is available. Booking early in the season can help you secure a time that suits your schedule. If you have already completed your checkup this year, please disregard this message.

Taking a little time for your health now can make a meaningful difference later. We look forward to seeing you.
