**Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow**

**To:** All staff and expense approvers
**From:** Administration

We are introducing a new workflow for approving expense requests. Every request will now follow the same path from submission to decision, so each request has a clear owner at each step and every decision is recorded.

**What changes**
- All expense requests go through the same approval steps, in the same order.
- Each request is reviewed by the approver assigned to that step before it moves forward.
- Decisions are recorded in the approval system, not handled informally by email or in person.

**What you need to do**
- **Staff submitting expenses:** Complete every field of your request, including the business purpose and any receipts, before you submit. Requests with missing information may be returned for correction.
- **Approvers:** Check your approval queue regularly and approve or return each request assigned to you, with a short note if you return it.

If you have questions about the new workflow, contact the administration office.
