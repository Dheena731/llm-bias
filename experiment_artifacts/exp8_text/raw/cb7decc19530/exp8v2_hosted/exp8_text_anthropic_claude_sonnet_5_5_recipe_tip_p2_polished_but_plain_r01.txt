To keep fresh herbs vibrant for up to two weeks, treat them like cut flowers or like salad greens, depending on their type. For tender herbs such as parsley, cilantro, and dill, trim the stem ends, stand the bunch in a jar with an inch of water, and loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag before refrigerating. Change the water every few days. Basil is the exception, since cold damages it and turns the leaves black, so keep it in a jar of water on the counter away from direct sun. For sturdier herbs such as rosemary, thyme, and sage, wrap them in a barely damp paper towel, place them in a container or resealable bag, and store them in the crisper drawer. Whatever the herb, wait to wash it until just before using, because excess moisture speeds up spoilage.
