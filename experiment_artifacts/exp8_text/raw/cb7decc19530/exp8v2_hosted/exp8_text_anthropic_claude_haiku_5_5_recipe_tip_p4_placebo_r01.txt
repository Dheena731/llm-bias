Batch-cook one or two versatile components on your least busy day, such as a grain like rice or quinoa, a roasted vegetable tray, and a protein like chicken or chickpeas. Store them in separate containers, then mix them with different sauces or toppings each day so lunches stay varied without requiring daily cooking. Keep a few no-cook extras, like fruit, nuts, or hummus, to fill gaps on the busiest days.
