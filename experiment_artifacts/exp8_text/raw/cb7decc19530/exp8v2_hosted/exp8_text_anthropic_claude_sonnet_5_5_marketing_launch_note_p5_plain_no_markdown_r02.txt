Today we are launching Tongue Twisters, a new podcast for anyone learning a language and wanting to enjoy the process a little more. Each week, we will take one small piece of a language, such as a confusing verb tense, a stubborn sound, or a phrase that never seems to translate well, and explore it through short conversations, real examples, and practical advice from teachers and fellow learners.

Our episodes run about twenty minutes, which is long enough to learn something useful and short enough to fit into a commute or a walk around the block. We speak slowly where it helps, repeat key phrases, and always leave you with one small thing to try before the next episode arrives. You do not need to be fluent, or even close. If you can say hello and want to say a bit more, you are exactly who we have in mind.

The first three episodes are available now. We begin with why adults often feel embarrassed when speaking a new language, and how to stop letting that feeling run the show. Then we look at the habits of people who stick with their studies for years, and finally we try to make sense of why every language seems to have at least one sound that defeats us.

We would love to hear from you. Send us your questions, your funniest mistakes, and the words that trip you up, and we will feature some of them in upcoming episodes. Thank you for listening, and welcome to the first season.
