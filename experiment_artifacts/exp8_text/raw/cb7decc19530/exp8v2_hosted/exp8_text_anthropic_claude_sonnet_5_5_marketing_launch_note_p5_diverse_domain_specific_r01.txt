**Mid-Sentence is live: three episodes, today**

Most Spanish podcasts for learners fall into two camps: slow, tidy dialogues nobody speaks like, or full-speed radio that loses you by the second minute. Mid-Sentence sits in the gap, for people stuck around B1 who can order dinner and can't follow the argument at the next table.

Each episode is one unedited, 20-minute conversation between me (a learner, still making errors) and a native speaker, usually about something ordinary: a landlord dispute in Valencia, why someone quit teaching, how to haggle in Oaxaca. Then comes the part I wish existed when I was learning: we replay three moments where I got something wrong or didn't understand, and unpack what happened. Usually it's small things, like the subjunctive after *cuando*, or *pues* and *o sea* doing quiet work that no textbook mentions.

Every episode has a transcript with my mistakes marked and the native speaker's phrasing alongside them. Nothing is slowed down.

Episodes 1 to 3 are out now, and a new one arrives every Thursday. Start with episode 2 if you want a representative sample. If you catch me making an error I didn't flag, write in. I'll add it to the next episode's breakdown.
