**Subject: Trimbook is live. Your schedule can finally tell a poodle from a doodle.**

Hi all,

I groomed dogs for eleven years, and every shop I worked in had the same problem: a calendar that thought every dog took an hour.

Trimbook is the booking app I wanted back then. It's live today.

**What it does differently**
- **Appointment length comes from the dog.** Breed, coat, and weeks since the last groom set the time. A goldendoodle who hasn't been in for 14 weeks gets 2½ hours, not 90 minutes.
- **Expired rabies certificates block the booking.** You stop finding out at the front desk with the owner standing there.
- **Notes stay with the dog.** "Hates the dryer near his left ear" shows on the day's sheet, not buried in a text thread from March.
- **Deposits for new clients only.** No-shows drop, and regulars aren't charged for being regulars.
- **Rebooking is part of checkout.** You tap "6 weeks" before the owner reaches for their leash.

Reminders go out in your shop's name and use the dog's name. Biscuit gets a text, not "your pet."

**Price:** $29 a month, flat. No per-text fees and no per-groomer fees until you have five chairs.
