We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you better track and manage your expenses. This summary will provide a detailed breakdown of your spending habits, categorizing your transactions into different areas such as housing, transportation, and entertainment. The summary will be sent to you on the first day of each month and will cover the previous month's activity. This new tool is designed to help you identify areas where you can cut back and make more informed financial decisions. If you have any questions or would like to opt out of receiving the summary, please contact our customer service department.
