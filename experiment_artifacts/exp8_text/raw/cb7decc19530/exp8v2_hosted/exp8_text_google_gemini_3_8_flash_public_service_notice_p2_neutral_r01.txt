**PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE**

**Location:** Oak Street (between 5th Avenue and Maple Boulevard)  
**Dates & Times:** Monday, October 14, 2024 – Wednesday, October 16, 2024  
**Daily Hours:** 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM  

Please be advised that Oak Street will be closed to all through traffic between 5th Avenue and Maple Boulevard for scheduled utility repairs and road resurfacing. 

**Detour Information:**  
Traffic will be redirected via Pine Street. Detour signs will be posted to guide motorists. Local access for residents and emergency vehicles will be maintained at all times. 

Motorists are encouraged to allow extra travel time and drive with caution near the work zone. 

We apologize for any inconvenience and appreciate your cooperation.
