**Welcome to *Lingua Lane*: Your Daily Door to New Languages**

Learning a language can feel like standing at the bottom of a mountain. *Lingua Lane* is here to show you the path up, one small step at a time.

Each episode, we'll take you through real conversations, practical vocabulary, and the cultural details that textbooks often skip: the slang people actually use, the jokes that land (or don't), and the everyday moments where language comes alive. Whether you're a complete beginner or brushing off a language you studied years ago, you'll find something you can use today.

Here's what to expect:
- **Short, focused episodes** under 20 minutes, made to fit your commute, workout, or morning coffee
- **Guided practice** that invites you to speak out loud, not just listen
- **Native speakers** sharing their stories, their regional accents, and their favorite expressions
- **Weekly review segments** so new words stick

Our first season explores Spanish, French, and Japanese, with a new language featured every month after that. Tune in, press play, and take your first step down the lane.
