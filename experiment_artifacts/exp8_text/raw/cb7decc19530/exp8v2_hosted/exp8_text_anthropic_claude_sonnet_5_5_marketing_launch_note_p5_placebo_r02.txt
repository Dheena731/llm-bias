**Our podcast is now live**

Our language-learning podcast has launched, and the first episode is ready to play.

It's made for people who are learning a new language and want to practice in everyday moments, like on a commute, on a walk, or while making dinner. Listening regularly is one of the simplest ways to get used to how a language sounds, and we want to make that habit easier to build.

To get started, find the podcast in your usual listening app, press play on the first episode, and follow the show so new episodes appear automatically.

Once you've listened, tell us what worked for you and what you'd like to hear next. Your feedback will help shape the episodes that follow.

Thank you for being here at the start. Happy learning!
