We're pleased to introduce the Clear Month Budget Template, a spreadsheet designed to make personal budgeting simple enough to stick with. It works in both Excel and Google Sheets, and it requires no formulas knowledge, no setup consultation, and no subscription.

Most budgeting tools fail for one of two reasons: they demand too much effort to maintain, or they hide the numbers you actually care about behind complicated dashboards. This template takes a different approach. You begin by entering your monthly income and your fixed expenses, such as rent, utilities, and insurance. Then you assign amounts to flexible categories like groceries, transportation, and entertainment. As you record purchases throughout the month, the template updates automatically to show how much remains in each category and how your spending compares with your plan.

A summary page brings everything together. It shows your total income, total spending, and the amount left over, along with a simple chart that makes it easy to see where your money went. At the end of the month, a single button-free reset carries your savings balance forward and clears your transactions so you can start fresh without rebuilding anything.

The template also includes a savings goals section, where you can set a target amount and a deadline and see how much you need to set aside each month to reach it. A debt payoff tracker is built in as well, useful if you are working down a credit card balance or a loan.

Every cell that expects your input is shaded lightly, and everything else is locked so you cannot accidentally overwrite a formula. Categories can be renamed, added, or removed to match your own life, whether you are budgeting alone, with a partner, or for a household.
