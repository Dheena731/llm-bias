**Season in two stages for better flavor.** Before roasting, toss your vegetables with oil, salt, and pepper so the seasoning clings and helps them brown. Then, right after they come out of the oven, finish with a squeeze of lemon juice or a splash of vinegar and a pinch of flaky salt. Fresh herbs, grated Parmesan, or a sprinkle of spices like smoked paprika or cumin also work best added at this stage, since delicate flavors can burn or turn bitter in high heat.
