Subject: Quarterly Access Review Notice

Dear Team,

This is a reminder that our quarterly access review will begin on the first business day of next month. The review helps us confirm that system permissions remain appropriate and that we continue to meet our security and compliance requirements.

What to expect:

Department managers will receive a list of their team members and the systems each person can access. Please review this list carefully and confirm whether each level of access is still needed. If any access is no longer required, mark it for removal. If you notice any accounts belonging to former employees or contractors, report them right away.

Key dates:

Review period opens: first business day of next month
Manager responses due: ten business days after the review opens
Access changes completed: five business days after responses are received

Action required:

Managers must submit their completed reviews by the deadline. Reviews that are not returned on time will be escalated to department heads, and unconfirmed access may be suspended until verification is complete.

Employees may be contacted to confirm their job duties and access needs. Please respond promptly to any such requests.

If you have questions or need assistance with the review process, please contact the IT Security team at itsecurity@company.com.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our systems secure.

Regards,
IT Administration
