Big news: we just launched Shared Folders, a new way to organize and collaborate on your files with your team. You can now create a folder, invite teammates with a single link, and set who can view or edit each item. Changes sync in real time, so everyone sees the latest version without emailing attachments back and forth. Shared Folders is rolling out to all accounts today, and you can find it in the sidebar under Workspace. We'd love to hear how it works for you, so tell us what you think in the comments.
