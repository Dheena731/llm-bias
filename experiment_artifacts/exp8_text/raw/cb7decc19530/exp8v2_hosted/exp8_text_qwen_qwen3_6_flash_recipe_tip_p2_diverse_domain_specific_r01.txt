Separate your stash by stem lignification. Woody herbs (rosemary, thyme, sage) survive best trimmed and stood upright in an inch of water at room temperature; cover loosely with a ventilated bag to buffer humidity without trapping condensation. Herbaceous greens (cilantro, parsley, dill) rot rapidly in moisture, so wash thoroughly, spin until bone-dry, wrap in a lint-free linen or heavy paper towel, and store vertically in a sealed container within the refrigerator's high-cold zone. Basil is the outlier: it suffers chilling injury and cell collapse, so treat it like the woody group but keep it strictly at room temperature, never refrigerated.
