We are writing to inform you that we will be making a change to our card transaction fees. As of the next billing cycle, the fee for processing card transactions will be 2.5 percent of the transaction amount, plus a small fixed charge of 10 cents per transaction. This change is intended to better reflect the costs associated with processing card payments and to ensure that we can continue to provide a secure and reliable payment processing service. If you have any questions about this change or how it may affect your account, please do not hesitate to contact us. We are always here to help and appreciate your business.
