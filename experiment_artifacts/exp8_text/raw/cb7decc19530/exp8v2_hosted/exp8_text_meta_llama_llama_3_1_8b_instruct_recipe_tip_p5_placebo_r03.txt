**Enhance the Flavor of Roasted Vegetables with a Simple Seasoning Technique**

When it comes to seasoning roasted vegetables, the key is to balance flavors to bring out the natural sweetness of the vegetables. A good rule of thumb is to use a combination of herbs and spices that complement the vegetable's inherent flavor profile.

Here's a tip: Start by mixing together a blend of 1 tablespoon olive oil, 1 teaspoon salt, and 1/2 teaspoon dried herbs like thyme, rosemary, or oregano. You can also add a pinch of ground black pepper for added depth.

**The Science Behind the Flavor**

The Maillard reaction, a chemical reaction between amino acids and reducing sugars, occurs when the vegetables are roasted, leading to the formation of new flavor compounds. To enhance this process, sprinkle a pinch of paprika or garlic powder over the vegetables before roasting. These seasonings will react with the natural sugars in the vegetables, amplifying their flavor.

**Tips for Specific Vegetables**

* For sweet potatoes and carrots, try adding a pinch of cumin and coriander for a warm, earthy flavor.
* For Brussels sprouts and broccoli, use a combination of lemon zest, garlic powder, and red pepper flakes for a tangy, slightly spicy flavor.

By following these simple seasoning tips, you'll be able to elevate the flavor of your roasted vegetables and enjoy a delicious, well-balanced meal.
