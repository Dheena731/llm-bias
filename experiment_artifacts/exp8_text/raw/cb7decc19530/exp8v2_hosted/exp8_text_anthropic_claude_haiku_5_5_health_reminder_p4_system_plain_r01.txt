Take a moment to step away from your screen. Stretch your arms, roll your shoulders, and look at something at least twenty feet away for a little while. Short breaks like this can ease eye strain, reduce neck and back tension, and help your mind refresh so you return to your work with a clearer focus. Even two or three minutes away every hour can make a noticeable difference, so consider setting a gentle timer as a reminder. Your eyes and body will thank you.
