Take a Break from Screens

In today's digital age, it's easy to get caught up in the constant stream of notifications, emails, and social media updates. However, prolonged screen time can have negative effects on your physical and mental well-being.

Research suggests that taking regular breaks from screens can help prevent eye strain, reduce headaches, and even improve productivity. By taking short breaks every hour, you can:

*   Refresh your eyes and give them a chance to rest
*   Stretch and move your body to prevent muscle fatigue
*   Clear your mind and come back to tasks with a fresh perspective

Aim to take a 10-15 minute break every hour, where you put away your screens and engage in a different activity. This could be as simple as going for a short walk, practicing some deep breathing exercises, or chatting with a friend or family member. By prioritizing screen-free time, you can maintain a healthy balance between technology use and overall well-being.
