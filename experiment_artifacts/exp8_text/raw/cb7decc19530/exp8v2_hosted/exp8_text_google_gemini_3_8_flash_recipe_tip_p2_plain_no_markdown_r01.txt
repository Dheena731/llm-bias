To keep fresh herbs lasting longer, treat them like a bouquet of flowers. Trim the bottoms of the stems, place the bunch upright in a small glass jar containing an inch of water, and loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag before placing it in the refrigerator. The main exception is basil, which should be stored using this same jar method but left at room temperature on your counter, as the cold air of the fridge will quickly turn its leaves black.
