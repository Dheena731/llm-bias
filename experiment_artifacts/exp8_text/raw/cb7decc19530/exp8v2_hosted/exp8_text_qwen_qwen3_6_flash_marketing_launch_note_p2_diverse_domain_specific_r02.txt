Most coffee hits your counter weeks after the beans cooled. By then, the volatile aromatics have already retreated.

When you join the roster, the clock starts the moment the batch finishes. We print the roast date on the front of the bag because that number is the only metric that matters. You set the pace—weekly for daily drivers, bi-weekly for slow sippers. Choose your grind for your rig, or take the whole bean if you prefer to dial it in yourself.

First shipment pulls off the roaster Tuesday; arrives by Friday. If the date on the bag is older than four days upon delivery, the next month is on us.

No guesswork. Just beans that taste like they came out of the drum yesterday.
