**OFFICIAL NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS**

Voting for the upcoming election will take place on Tuesday, November 5, from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. 

Voters must cast their ballots at their designated neighborhood polling station. To find your assigned location:

- Visit the official election website at www.elections-example.gov and enter your residential address.
- Check the official voter information card mailed to your home address.
- Call the Board of Elections helpline at (555) 019-2834.

All polling places are fully accessible. Please ensure you bring a valid form of photo identification with you on Election Day.
