Public Notice: Heat Advisory

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for our region, effective from June 15 to June 17. A prolonged period of hot weather is expected, with temperatures forecast to reach the mid-90s during the day and remaining in the 70s at night.

Residents are advised to take precautions to avoid heat-related illnesses. The elderly, young children, and people with chronic health conditions are particularly vulnerable to heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Residents should stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water, limit their physical activity to early morning and evening hours, and seek air-conditioned spaces if they cannot stay indoors.

Pets should also be protected from the heat, and owners are advised to provide them with plenty of water and shade. Outdoor work should be avoided during the hottest part of the day, and workers should take regular breaks in shaded areas.

Residents are encouraged to check on their neighbors, especially the elderly and those with special needs, to ensure they are staying cool and safe. If you or someone you know is experiencing symptoms of heat exhaustion or heat stroke, such as dizziness, nausea, or headaches, seek medical attention immediately.

The city will be providing public cooling centers and offering additional resources to help residents cope with the heat. Further information on these services will be available at local community centers and on the city's website.

Stay safe and stay informed.
