To plan lunches for a busy week, consider setting aside one day to prepare and portion out individual meals that can be easily reheated or grabbed on the go. This can include cooking a large batch of rice, quinoa, or protein, and then using it to make different lunches throughout the week, such as salads, wraps, or leftovers. Having a plan in place can help save time and reduce stress during the week, and can also help ensure that you're eating healthy and nutritious meals even on the most hectic days.
