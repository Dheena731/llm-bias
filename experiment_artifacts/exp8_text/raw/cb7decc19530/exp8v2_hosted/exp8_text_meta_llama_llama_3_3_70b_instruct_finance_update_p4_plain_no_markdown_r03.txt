Your end-of-year tax statement is a document that summarizes the income you earned and the taxes that were withheld from your paycheck during the past year. It is typically provided to you by your employer and is used to help you prepare your tax return. The statement will show the total amount of money you earned, the amount of federal and state taxes that were withheld, and any other relevant tax information. You will need this statement to accurately complete your tax return and claim any deductions or credits you may be eligible for.
