Software updates matter because they keep your devices and programs secure, stable, and useful. Security patches fix vulnerabilities that hackers could otherwise exploit to steal data, install malware, or take control of your system. Developers also use updates to repair bugs and crashes that can cause data loss or frustrating interruptions. In addition, updates can improve performance, add compatibility with new hardware or file formats, and bring new features. Skipping updates over time leaves you exposed to known risks and can eventually make software incompatible with other tools and services you rely on. Keeping software current is one of the simplest and most effective ways to protect your privacy and get reliable performance from your technology.
