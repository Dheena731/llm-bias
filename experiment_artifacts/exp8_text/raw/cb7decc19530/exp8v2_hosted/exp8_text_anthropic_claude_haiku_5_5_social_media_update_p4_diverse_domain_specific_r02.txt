Most calendar apps default new meetings to 60 minutes, which is why your afternoon turns into back-to-back calls with no time to breathe. In Google Calendar, go to Settings > Event settings and change the default duration to 25 or 50 minutes. In Outlook, look for "Shorten meeting and appointment lengths" under Calendar > Event settings. That gap between calls is where you can refill your water, check your notes, or just stand up for a minute.
