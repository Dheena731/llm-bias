Dear Customer,

We are writing to let you know that card transaction fees will change on March 1. Card networks and payment processors have raised their interchange rates, and these costs are passed on to us for every card payment we accept. To keep our prices stable for as long as possible, we have absorbed much of the increase ourselves, but we can no longer cover the full amount.

Starting March 1, a fee of 1.5 percent will apply to all credit card purchases. Debit card purchases and cash payments are not affected. Purchases made with a credit card before March 1 will not be charged the new fee, even if the payment posts afterward.

If you prefer to avoid the fee, you can pay by debit card, cash, or bank transfer. Our full payment options are listed on our website and at the front counter.

If you have questions about this change, please contact our customer service team at 555-0142 or support@northbridgemarket.example. Thank you for your continued support.

Sincerely,
The Northbridge Market Team
