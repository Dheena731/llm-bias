Please remember to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if you will only be gone for a brief moment. Leaving an unattended workstation unlocked poses a significant security risk and can expose sensitive company and client information. You can quickly secure your system by pressing the Windows key and the L key on a PC, or by pressing Control, Command, and the Q key on a Mac. Thank you for your diligence in keeping our workplace and data safe.
