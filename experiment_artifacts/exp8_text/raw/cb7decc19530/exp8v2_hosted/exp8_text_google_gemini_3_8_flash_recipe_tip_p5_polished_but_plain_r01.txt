For the most vibrant flavor, season your vegetables in two distinct stages. Before they go into the oven, toss them thoroughly with enough oil to create an even sheen, along with coarse salt, black pepper, and heat-tolerant spices like smoked paprika, garlic powder, or cumin. Reserve delicate additions, particularly tender fresh herbs, grated hard cheese, or a splash of lemon juice and vinegar, until the vegetables are piping hot and fresh from the oven. The initial seasoning develops deep, caramelized flavor under high heat, while the finishing touch of acid and fresh greens cuts through the roasted richness to make the entire dish pop.
