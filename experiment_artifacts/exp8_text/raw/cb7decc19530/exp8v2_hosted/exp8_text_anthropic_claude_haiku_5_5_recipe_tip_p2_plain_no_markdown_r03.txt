To keep fresh herbs lasting longer, trim about half an inch off the stems and place them upright in a glass with an inch or two of water, much like a bouquet of flowers. Cover the leaves loosely with a plastic bag and store the glass in the refrigerator. Hardy herbs like rosemary, thyme, and sage often last a week or more this way, and you should change the water every few days. Delicate herbs such as basil are better kept at room temperature away from direct sunlight, since cold air can blacken their leaves.
