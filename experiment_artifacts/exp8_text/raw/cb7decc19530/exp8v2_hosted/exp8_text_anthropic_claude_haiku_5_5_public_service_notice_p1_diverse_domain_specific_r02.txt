**NOTICE TO PATRONS: Eastside Branch Hours, Starting January 6**

The Eastside Branch will open at 10 a.m. on Tuesdays and Thursdays, not 9 a.m. Closing time stays at 8 p.m. on those days. Monday, Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday hours do not change (9 a.m. to 6 p.m., Saturdays 9 to 4). The branch remains closed Sundays.

Tuesday and Thursday mornings have had the fewest visitors for the past two years. The extra hour lets staff finish reshelving and clear the holds shelf before the doors open.

- **Book drop:** The outdoor return slot by the north lot is open at all hours. Items dropped on a closed day are checked in the next morning, and no late fines accrue for that day.
- **Holds:** Pickup lockers in the vestibule open at 6 a.m. daily using your pickup code.
- **Questions:** Call 555-0148 or ask at any desk.
