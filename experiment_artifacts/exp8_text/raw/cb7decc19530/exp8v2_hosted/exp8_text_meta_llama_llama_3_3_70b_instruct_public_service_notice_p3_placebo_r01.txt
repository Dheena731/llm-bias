Important Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection Schedule

As of next month, the recycling collection schedule in our area will be changing. The new collection day will be every other Thursday, instead of the current every other Friday. This change aims to improve the efficiency of the recycling program and reduce costs. Residents are advised to ensure that their recyclable materials are placed at the curb by 7am on the designated collection day. For more information and to confirm the new collection schedule, please visit our website or contact the local waste management department.
