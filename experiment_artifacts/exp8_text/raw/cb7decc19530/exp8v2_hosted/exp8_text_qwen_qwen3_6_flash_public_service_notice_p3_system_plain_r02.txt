This notice is to inform residents of upcoming changes to the weekly recycling collection schedule. Starting next Monday, all curbside recycling containers will now be collected on Thursdays instead of Tuesdays. The change is being made to improve route efficiency and reduce operational costs for the waste management department. Please ensure your recycling containers are placed at the curb by seven in the morning on the new collection day. Containers left out after noon will not be emptied until the following week. If you have any questions or require assistance, please contact the public works office during regular business hours. Thank you for your cooperation as we work to maintain a cleaner and more sustainable community.
