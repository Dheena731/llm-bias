Here's a simple tip: store fresh herbs like a bouquet. Trim about half an inch off the stems, place them in a glass with an inch or two of water, and cover the leaves loosely with a plastic bag. Keep the glass in the refrigerator, and change the water every few days. Hardy herbs such as rosemary, thyme, and sage can also be wrapped in a damp paper towel and sealed in a bag, while basil is best kept at room temperature away from direct sun, since cold makes it turn black.
