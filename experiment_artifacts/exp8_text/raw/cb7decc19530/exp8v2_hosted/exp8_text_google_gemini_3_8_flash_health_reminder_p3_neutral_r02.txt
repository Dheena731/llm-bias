Here are a few options, depending on how you want to send it:

**Friendly & Direct (Best for email or calendar reminder)**
> **Subject:** Friendly reminder: Time to schedule your annual checkup!
> 
> Hi [Name],
> 
> Just a quick reminder to prioritize your health and book your annual wellness checkup. An hour of your time today is the best way to ensure peace of mind for the year ahead. 
> 
> Give your doctor’s office a call or book online to get it on the calendar!

**Short & Sweet (Best for a text message)**
> Friendly reminder: It's time for your yearly health checkup! Don’t put your wellness on the back burner—reach out to your doctor’s office today to get on the schedule.
