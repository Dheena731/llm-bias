This is a friendly reminder to request a refill of your prescription before your current supply runs out. Refill requests can take a day or two to process, and some pharmacies need extra time over weekends and holidays, so it is best to contact your pharmacy or prescriber a few days ahead of time. Running out of medication, even for a short while, can affect your health, so planning ahead helps you avoid an unexpected gap in treatment.

To request a refill, you can call your pharmacy, use its website or mobile app, or ask your prescriber's office to send a renewal request. If you are unsure how many refills remain on your prescription, the pharmacy can tell you. When you pick up your medication, please check the label and confirm the name, dosage, and directions are correct. If anything looks different from what you expected, contact the pharmacy right away.

If you have questions about your medication, your side effects, or whether a refill is still appropriate, please reach out to your healthcare provider. Thank you for staying on top of your care.
