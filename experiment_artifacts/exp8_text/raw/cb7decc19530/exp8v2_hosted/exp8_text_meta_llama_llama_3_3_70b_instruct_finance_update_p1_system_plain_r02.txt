We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you keep track of your expenses. This summary will be sent to you at the beginning of each month and will provide a detailed breakdown of your spending from the previous month. The summary will include the total amount you spent, as well as a categorization of your expenses, such as rent, utilities, groceries, and entertainment. This will allow you to see where your money is going and make adjustments as needed to stay within your budget. The monthly spending summary will be available online and can be accessed at any time, giving you a clear and convenient way to manage your finances.
