Hold your ground spices until the final five minutes of roasting. 

Powders like cumin, coriander, garlic powder, and paprika scorch quickly at 425°F, turning bitter long before a carrot or wedge of cabbage softens. Roast with only a high-heat fat, coarse salt, and sturdy woody herbs (thyme, rosemary). If you want deep spice flavor, bloom the ground spices in a tablespoon of warm oil on the stovetop, then toss the vegetables in that oil the second they come off the baking sheet—the residual heat will fuse the aromatics without incinerating them.
