Here's a tip for planning lunches for a busy week, tailored to the domain of professionals in the tech industry:

**"Batch-Prepare Your Lunches Like a DevOps Engineer: A 5-Step Plan for Busy Weeks"**

Are you tired of scrambling for lunch ideas every morning, only to end up with the same old takeout or soggy leftovers? Take a cue from your favorite devops engineer and plan your lunches like a batch process. Here's a 5-step plan to help you prep like a pro:

1. **Identify Your Replicas**: Consider your lunch needs for the week. Do you have any dietary restrictions or preferences? Are there any specific cuisines you enjoy? Make a list of your "replicas" – the types of meals you'd like to have each week.
2. **Simplify Your Pipeline**: Choose a few simple, quick-to-prepare meals that can be easily scaled up or down. For example, omelets, salads, or wraps are all great options.
3. **Automate Your Prep**: Set aside a few hours on the weekend to prep your lunches for the week. Chop vegetables, cook proteins, and assemble containers for the next few days.
4. **Test and Refine**: As you prep and pack your lunches, take note of what works and what doesn't. Make adjustments to your recipes, portion sizes, or containers to optimize your workflow.
5. **Deploy and Enjoy**: Each morning, grab-and-go with confidence, knowing you've got a delicious, healthy lunch ready to fuel your day.

By following these 5 steps, you'll be able to plan and prep your lunches like a devops engineer – efficient, effective, and ready for anything the week throws your way.
