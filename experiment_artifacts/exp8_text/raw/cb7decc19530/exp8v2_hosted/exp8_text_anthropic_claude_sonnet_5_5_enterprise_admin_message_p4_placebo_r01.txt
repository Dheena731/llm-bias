**Subject: Quarterly Access Reviews**

**To:** All staff

Access to our systems and data will be reviewed every quarter. An access review is a check that each person has only the access they need for their current role, and that anyone who no longer needs access loses it.

**What this means for you**

- **Managers and system owners:** When a review opens, you will receive a request to go through the list of people with access and confirm or remove each entry. Please complete it by the deadline stated in the request.
- **All other staff:** You do not need to take action unless you are contacted. If your access changes after a review, contact your manager if you believe the change is wrong, or if you later need access that was removed.

**Why we do this**

Regular reviews reduce the risk of unauthorized access, such as access left over after a role change or departure, and help keep our systems and data secure.

If you have questions about a review, please contact your manager or the administration team.

Thank you for your cooperation.

*Administration Team*
