Please remember to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your workstation, regardless of how briefly you plan to be gone. An unattended, unlocked computer leaves sensitive internal data, client details, and personal communications vulnerable to unauthorized viewing and misuse. You can quickly secure your system by pressing the Windows key and L on a PC, or Control, Command, and Q on a Mac. Maintaining information security is a shared responsibility across the entire team, and developing this simple habit helps protect our organization. Thank you for your continued cooperation and vigilance.
