All staff members are reminded that reusing passwords across different accounts poses a significant security risk to the company. If you use the same password for your work email and a personal social media site, a breach on the personal site could allow attackers to access your work credentials. To protect our data, please ensure that every account you access has a unique and strong password. Avoid writing down passwords in visible places or sharing them with colleagues. If you find it difficult to remember multiple complex passwords, consider using an approved password management tool provided by IT. You must update your passwords regularly and report any unusual login activity to the help desk immediately. Failure to follow these guidelines may result in disciplinary action. Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our workplace secure.
