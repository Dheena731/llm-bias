Subject: Your End-of-Year Tax Statement

Dear Customer,

Your end-of-year tax statement is now available. It summarizes the income, interest, dividends, and other taxable activity recorded in your account during the past calendar year. Its purpose is to give you the figures you need to complete your tax return accurately, so please keep a copy with your other tax records.

The statement is organized by type of income. Each section shows the total amount paid or credited to you, along with any tax that was withheld on your behalf. If you see an amount labeled as withholding, that money has already been sent to the tax authority and should be claimed as a credit when you file. Amounts labeled as gross are totals before any deductions, while net amounts reflect what you actually received.

Before you file, take a few minutes to compare the statement with your own records. Check that your name, address, and taxpayer identification number are correct, since errors can delay processing or cause mismatches with the information we report to the authorities. If any figure looks unfamiliar or seems too high or too low, review your account history for the relevant months. Small differences sometimes arise from timing, such as income paid in late December but credited in January.

If you find a mistake, contact us as soon as possible so we can investigate and, if necessary, issue a corrected statement. Please do not file your return using figures you believe to be wrong, as an amended return is more time-consuming than waiting a few days for a correction.

Please note that this statement is a record of activity and not tax advice. Your personal circumstances, including deductions and credits, will affect what you owe, so a qualified tax professional can help if your situation is complicated.

Thank you for your business. If you have questions, our support team is happy to walk you through any line on the statement.

Sincerely,
