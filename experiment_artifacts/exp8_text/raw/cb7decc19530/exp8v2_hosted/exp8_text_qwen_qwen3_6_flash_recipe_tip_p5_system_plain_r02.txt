Toss your vegetables thoroughly in a bowl with enough oil to coat each piece lightly and sprinkle generously with kosher salt before spreading them on the baking sheet. This allows the salt to penetrate the vegetables as they roast, enhancing their natural sweetness and promoting better caramelization. If using delicate fresh herbs like basil or parsley, stir them in only during the last two minutes of cooking to prevent burning, while heartier dried spices should be added at the beginning to toast alongside the vegetables.
