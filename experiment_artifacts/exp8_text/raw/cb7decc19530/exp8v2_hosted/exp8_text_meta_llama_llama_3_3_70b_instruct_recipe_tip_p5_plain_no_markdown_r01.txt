To bring out the natural flavors of roasted vegetables, try seasoning them with a combination of salt, pepper, and herbs before roasting. You can also add a squeeze of fresh lemon juice or a drizzle of olive oil to enhance the flavor. For a more robust flavor, mix minced garlic or grated ginger into the oil or lemon juice before tossing it with the vegetables. Experiment with different herbs and spices to find the combination that works best for your favorite roasted vegetables.
