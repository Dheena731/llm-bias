**Introducing Savings Goals: Turn Your Dreams into Reality! 🎯**

We’re excited to roll out our newest feature: **Savings Goals**. 

Whether you’re building an emergency fund, planning a dream vacation, or saving for a down payment, staying on track is now easier than ever. 

**Here’s what’s new:**
* **Custom Targets:** Name your goal, set your target amount, and pick a deadline.

Update your app to the latest version, head to the **Savings** tab, and create your first goal today!
