**Introducing Savings Goals: Save for What Matters**

We're excited to announce a new feature that makes saving easier and more motivating: **Savings Goals**.

**What's new**
- **Create custom goals.** Set a name, target amount, and deadline for anything you're saving toward, whether it's a vacation, an emergency fund, or a new laptop.
- **Track your progress.** A visual progress bar shows how close you are to each goal.
- **Automate your saving.** Schedule recurring transfers so your goals grow without extra effort.
- **Celebrate milestones.** Get a nudge when you hit 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of your target.

**How to get started**
1. Open the app and tap **Goals**.
2. Select **Create a Goal**.
3. Enter your target and timeline, then choose how you'd like to contribute.

Savings Goals is rolling out to all users over the next two weeks. Update to the latest version of the app to make sure you have access.

We'd love to hear what you think. Send feedback through the **Help** tab in the app.

Happy saving!
