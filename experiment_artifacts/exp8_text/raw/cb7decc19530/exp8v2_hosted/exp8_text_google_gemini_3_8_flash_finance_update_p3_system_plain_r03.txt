We are excited to introduce our new savings goal feature, designed to help you reach your financial milestones with greater ease and clarity. Starting today, you can create custom targets for everything from an emergency fund or a dream vacation to a down payment on a home. Simply name your goal, set your target amount, and choose a completion date. The tool will calculate how much you need to set aside each week or month to stay on schedule. You can also turn on automatic transfers to build your savings effortlessly. A dynamic tracker now displays your progress in real time, giving you a clear view of how close you are to crossing the finish line. Update your mobile app or log in to your account online to set up your first goal today.
