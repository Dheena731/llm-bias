You didn't name a domain, so I've written three versions. Swap in the one that fits.

**Clinical lab results**
> **No result on file.** The test wasn't run, wasn't resulted, or wasn't entered. It does not mean normal or zero. Left out of trend lines and averages.

**Sensor or telemetry dashboard**
> **No reading received.** The device didn't report in this interval (offline, dropped packet, or power loss). The true value is unknown, not zero.

**Survey responses**
> **Left blank.** The respondent skipped this question or the logic never showed it to them. Excluded from percentages for this item.
