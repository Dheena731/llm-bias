**Introducing "ClearPocket": A Budgeting Spreadsheet Template for Real-World Savings**

As budgets often reflect real-life expenses, not theoretical income, ClearPocket is designed to help you set realistic financial goals. This user-friendly template focuses on categorizing and tracking your spending habits, not just forecasting hypothetical income.

ClearPocket features:

- A "50/30/20" allocation system to help prioritize essential expenses, discretionary spending, and savings
- A 'historical' tracking sheet to visualize past expenses and identify trends
- A "savings challenge" section to help you reach short-term financial milestones
- Automatic calculation of monthly expenses based on historical data

ClearPocket is a straightforward, adaptable tool that acknowledges budgeting is a continuous process, not a one-time event.
