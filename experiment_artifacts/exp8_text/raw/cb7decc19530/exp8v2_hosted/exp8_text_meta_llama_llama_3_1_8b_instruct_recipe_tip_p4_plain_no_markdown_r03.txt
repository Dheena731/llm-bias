To plan lunches for a busy week, consider setting aside a small block of time on Sunday or Monday to prepare for the week ahead. Take stock of any leftovers from the previous week's meals and use them as a starting point for planning new meals. Then, make a list of the ingredients you have on hand and what you need to buy at the store. Use this information to plan out each day's lunch, making sure to include a variety of protein sources, vegetables, and whole grains. Consider preparing a few components of your meals in advance, such as cooking rice or making a big batch of soup, to save time during the week.
