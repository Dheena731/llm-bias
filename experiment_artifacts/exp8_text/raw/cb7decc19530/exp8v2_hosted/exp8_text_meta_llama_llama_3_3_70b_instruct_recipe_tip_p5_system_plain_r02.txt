When seasoning roasted vegetables, it's best to season them twice for the most flavor. First, toss the vegetables with a small amount of oil, salt, and any other desired seasonings before roasting. Then, once the vegetables are tender and lightly browned, remove them from the oven and sprinkle with additional seasonings, such as minced herbs or grated cheese. This second seasoning will add a bright, fresh flavor to the vegetables that can get lost if added too early in the cooking process.
