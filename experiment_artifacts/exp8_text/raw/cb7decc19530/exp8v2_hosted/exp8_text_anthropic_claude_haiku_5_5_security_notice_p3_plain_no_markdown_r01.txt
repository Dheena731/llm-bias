Subject: Please Lock Your Screens When Away From Your Desk

Dear colleagues,

This is a reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even for a few minutes. An unlocked screen can give anyone passing by access to confidential client information, internal emails, and company systems. Locking your screen takes only a moment and helps keep our data and our colleagues' work secure.

On Windows, press the Windows key and L together. On a Mac, press Control, Command, and Q together. If you are unsure how to set this up, please contact the IT help desk and someone will be glad to assist you.

Thank you for taking this small but important step to protect our organization.

Kind regards,

The IT Security Team
