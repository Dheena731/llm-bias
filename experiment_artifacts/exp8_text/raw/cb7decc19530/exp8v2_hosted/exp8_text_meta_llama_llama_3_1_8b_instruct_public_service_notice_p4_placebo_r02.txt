**HEAT ADVISORY ISSUED**

A heat advisory has been issued for the entire metropolitan area due to forecasted temperatures reaching 104°F (40°C) today, with a heat index of 112°F (45°C) by evening. The National Weather Service has warned that the extreme heat will pose a serious threat to public health, particularly for vulnerable populations such as the elderly and young children.

Residents are advised to take precautions to stay cool and safe. This includes staying hydrated by drinking plenty of water, taking regular breaks in shaded or air-conditioned areas, and avoiding strenuous activities during the hottest part of the day (11am-3pm). Outdoor workers and athletes should also take extra precautions to prevent heat-related illnesses.

The City's Emergency Management Department will activate its Heat Wave Response Plan, which includes deploying cooling units and medical personnel to public spaces and providing free water and ice to those in need.

Stay informed about the latest weather updates and follow these simple steps to protect yourself and your loved ones from the heat.
