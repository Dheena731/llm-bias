**Public Notice: Road Closure**

**Attention Motorists**

Please be advised that a section of Main Street will be closed to through traffic commencing 7:00 am on Monday, 19th April 2021. This is to facilitate a scheduled infrastructure upgrade project.

The affected area spans approximately 200 meters, commencing at the intersection with Oak Avenue and terminating at the junction with Elm Street. A detour route will be clearly signposted to direct motorists around the closure.

The project is expected to be completed by 5:00 pm on Friday, 23rd April 2021, weather permitting.

**Alternative Routes:**

* Motorists travelling northbound will be directed to continue on Oak Avenue, then turn left onto Maple Street, and proceed to their destination.
* Motorists travelling southbound will be directed to turn right onto Elm Street, then continue to Oak Avenue, and proceed to their destination.

We appreciate your cooperation and patience during this time. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact our Infrastructure Team at (08) 1234 5678.
