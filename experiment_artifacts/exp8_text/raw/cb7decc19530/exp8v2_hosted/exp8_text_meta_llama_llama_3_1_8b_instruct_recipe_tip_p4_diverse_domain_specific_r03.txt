**Meal Prep for the Busy Week: A Chef's Secret**

As a professional chef, I've seen many home cooks struggle to plan lunches for the week. The key is to think beyond the usual suspects and focus on prep-friendly ingredients that can be transformed into multiple dishes. Here's a tip that'll save you time and stress:

**The "3-2-1" Rule**

 Allocate 3 hours on the weekend to prep for the week's lunches. Divide this time into two segments:

*   **30 minutes**: Chop, dice, and slice ingredients like onions, carrots, and bell peppers. These will become the foundation for various dishes throughout the week.
*   **2 hours**: Prepare 5-7 base components, such as:
    *   Cooked proteins (e.g., chicken, tuna, or tofu)
    *   Roasted vegetables (e.g., broccoli, sweet potatoes, or Brussels sprouts)
    *   Whole grains (e.g., brown rice, quinoa, or farro)
    *   Legumes (e.g., black beans, chickpeas, or lentils)
    *   Sauces and seasonings (e.g., hummus, guacamole, or a simple vinaigrette)

**The "1" Rule**

Use each base component to create at least 3 different meals throughout the week. For example, a roasted chicken breast can be turned into a chicken salad, chicken soup, or a hearty chicken and rice bowl.

**The Benefits**

By following the "3-2-1" rule, you'll:

*   Reduce meal prep time by 50%
*   Increase the variety of your lunches
*   Save money by buying ingredients in bulk
*   Enjoy healthy, balanced meals that fuel your body and mind

So, go ahead and give the "3-2-1" rule a try. Your future self (and your taste buds) will thank you!
