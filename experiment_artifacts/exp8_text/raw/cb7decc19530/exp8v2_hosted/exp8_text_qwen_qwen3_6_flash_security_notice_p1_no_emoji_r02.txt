**SUBJECT: SECURITY NOTICE: Mandatory Prevention of Password Reuse**

All employees are reminded that reusing passwords across work and personal accounts is strictly prohibited.

**Risk:** A breach of a personal account containing reused credentials can provide attackers direct access to corporate systems and sensitive data.

**Action Required:**
*   Immediately update any passwords currently shared between work and non-work services.
*   Assign a unique, complex password to every account.
*   Use the company-approved password manager to generate and store credentials securely.

Compliance with this policy is essential to protect organizational assets. For assistance or to report suspicious activity, contact IT Security.
