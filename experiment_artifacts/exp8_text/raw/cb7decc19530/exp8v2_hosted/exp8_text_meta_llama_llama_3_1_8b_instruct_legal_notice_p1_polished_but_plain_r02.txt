NOTICE OF CHANGE TO ACCOUNT TERMS

Notice is hereby given that the following changes will be made to the account terms effective immediately.

The monthly fee for accounts held in certain currencies will increase by 2.5%. The new fee will be applied to all new accounts opened on or after the effective date and to existing accounts on their next billing cycle.

The minimum balance requirement for accounts to avoid the monthly fee will increase from $100 to $200. Customers with existing accounts will be required to maintain a balance of at least $200 to avoid the fee.

The overdraft facility will be replaced with a new loan facility with a higher interest rate of 18%. Customers with existing overdrafts will be contacted separately to discuss their options.

These changes apply to all accounts held with us and are subject to the terms and conditions of the account agreement.

We reserve the right to make further changes to the account terms and conditions at any time.

By continuing to use our services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood these changes.

Please contact us if you have any questions or concerns regarding these changes.
