**Subject: Lost or stolen laptop: report it within one hour**

If you can't find your company laptop, report it right away, even if you think it's in the back seat or at a coworker's desk. Searching for two days and then telling us is the most common way a lost device becomes a breach.

**Report it to:**
- Security desk, 24 hours: ext. 5511 or security@yourcompany.example
- Or open a ticket under *Security → Lost/Stolen Device*, priority **P1**

If you can't reach either, tell your manager and have them call.

**Have these ready (but don't delay the call to find them):**
- Asset tag or serial number (on the underside, or in the Asset Portal under your name)
- Last place and time you had it
- Whether it was powered off, asleep, or logged in
- Whether anything unencrypted was stored locally, such as exported customer files, spreadsheets, or downloaded databases
- Any hardware token or smartcard that was with it

**What we'll do:**
1. Lock the device and revoke its certificates through MDM.
2. Sign out your sessions and reset your SSO password and MFA enrollments.
3. Rotate any API keys, SSH keys, or VPN credentials tied to the machine.
4. Issue a remote wipe if the device checks in or after 72 hours, whichever comes first.
5. Order a replacement. Expect a loaner within one business day.

**What you should do:**
- Change your password on any personal accounts you were signed into in the browser.
- If it was stolen, file a police report and send us the report number.
- Don't try to remotely wipe or log into the device yourself.

Disk encryption is enforced on all company laptops, so a lost machine is usually a replacement cost rather than a data incident, but only if we hear about it quickly. Reports are not disciplinary. Late reports are reviewed.
