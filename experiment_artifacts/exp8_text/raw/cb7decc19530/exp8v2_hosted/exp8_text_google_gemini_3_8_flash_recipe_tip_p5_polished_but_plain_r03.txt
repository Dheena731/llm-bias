For deeply flavorful roasted vegetables, season them in two separate stages. Before roasting, toss the cut pieces thoroughly with enough oil to coat every surface, then add salt and sturdy ground spices such as cumin, black pepper, or garlic powder. The oil ensures even browning, while the salt penetrates the flesh as it cooks. Reserve delicate fresh herbs, grated cheese, and bright acids like lemon juice or balsamic vinegar for the moment the vegetables come out of the oven. Tossing them in acid while they are piping hot cuts through the rich caramelization and instantly elevates the entire dish.
