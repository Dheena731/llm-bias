Calendar tip of the week: stop using your calendar only for meetings and start scheduling your focus time too. Block off an hour or two for the work that actually needs your full attention, give it a clear name like "Draft project proposal," and treat it like an appointment you can't skip. When someone tries to book that slot, you can honestly say you're busy. Since I started doing this, my to-do list stopped drifting from day to day, and I finally have time to finish things instead of just squeezing them in between calls. Try it for one week and see how it feels.
