Notice of Revised Library Opening Hours

Beginning Monday, the Central Library will operate under new opening hours to better serve the needs of our community. The library will be open Monday through Thursday from 9:00 in the morning until 8:00 in the evening. On Fridays, doors will open at 9:00 in the morning and close at 5:00 in the afternoon. Saturday hours will run from 10:00 in the morning until 4:00 in the afternoon. The library will remain closed on Sundays and on public holidays.

Residents should note that the children's reading room and the computer lab will close thirty minutes before the main building each day. Items may be returned at any time using the outdoor book drop, which is available around the clock. Materials placed in the drop will be checked in on the next business day.

Please plan your visits accordingly, and bring your library card if you wish to borrow materials or reserve a computer. Anyone who needs assistance outside regular hours, including those who require accessible services or special arrangements, is encouraged to contact the front desk by telephone during opening hours so that staff can make suitable arrangements.

We thank patrons for their patience and continued support as we adjust our schedule. Any further changes, including seasonal or holiday adjustments, will be announced at the front desk and on the notice board at the library entrance.
