To streamline your weeknight cooking, always read the entire recipe from start to finish before you begin heating any pans or chopping vegetables. This simple habit allows you to understand the sequence of steps and anticipate when each ingredient will be needed, preventing frantic searches for spices or burnt garlic while the oil smokes. Once you have a clear mental map of the process, gather every item on your counter and set aside any tools required so that the actual cooking time becomes a smooth execution of known actions rather than a race against time. By investing a few minutes in planning and organization, you can transform a chaotic evening into an efficient routine that reduces stress and ensures a hot meal reaches the table quickly.
