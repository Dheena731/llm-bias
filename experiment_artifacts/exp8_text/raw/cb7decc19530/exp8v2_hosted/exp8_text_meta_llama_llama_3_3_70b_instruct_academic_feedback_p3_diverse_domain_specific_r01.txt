"The research question posed in this study lacks clarity, particularly in its conflation of distinct concepts within the domain of neuroplasticity. Specifically, the authors fail to adequately differentiate between synaptic reorganization and functional compensation, leading to ambiguity in their hypothesis and methodology. As a result, it is challenging to discern whether the study aims to investigate the neural mechanisms underlying cognitive recovery post-stroke or the efficacy of a specific rehabilitative intervention. A more nuanced and precisely defined research question is necessary to guide the investigation and ensure that the findings contribute meaningfully to our understanding of neuroplasticity in this context."
