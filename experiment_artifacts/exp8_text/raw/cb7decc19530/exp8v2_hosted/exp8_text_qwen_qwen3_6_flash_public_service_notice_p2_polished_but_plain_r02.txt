Main Street will be closed to all vehicular traffic between First Avenue and Third Avenue starting Monday, October 23 at 9:00 a.m. and is scheduled to reopen Wednesday, October 25 at 6:00 p.m. This closure allows crews to perform emergency repairs on the local water main. Drivers should plan ahead and use Elm Boulevard or Riverside Drive as alternate routes. Sidewalks will remain accessible for pedestrians, though temporary ramps may narrow the walkway near the work zone. Construction activities may cause intermittent delays in surrounding areas. Thank you for your patience while we maintain essential city services. Residents with concerns can reach the Project Hotline at 555-0199 during business hours.
