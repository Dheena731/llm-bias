NOTICE: Immediate Reporting of Lost or Stolen Laptops

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, you must report it immediately. Prompt notification allows our IT Security team to remotely lock the device, wipe credentials, and protect sensitive company data.

Steps to take right away:

1. Contact the IT Help Desk at 555-0199 or itsecurity@company.com.
2. Notify your direct manager.
3. If the device was stolen, file a local police report and obtain a case number.

When reporting, please provide the approximate time and location of the incident, the device asset tag or serial number (if known), and any relevant details. 

Timely reporting is mandatory under our corporate security policy. Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our company data secure.
