Subject: Your end-of-year tax statement

Hello,

Your end-of-year tax statement is now available. This document summarizes the income you received and the taxes withheld or paid on your behalf during the past calendar year. You will need it when you file your tax return, so please keep a copy with your records.

The statement lists your total earnings for the year, including wages, interest, dividends, or other payments, depending on your situation. It also shows the amount of tax already withheld, which is credited toward what you owe when you file. If more was withheld than you owe, you may receive a refund. If less was withheld, you may owe the difference.

Please read the figures carefully and compare them with your own records. If something looks wrong, such as a missing payment, an incorrect amount, or a misspelled name or identification number, contact us as soon as possible so we can issue a corrected statement before you file.

The statement is not a bill, and you do not need to send payment because of it. It is an informational record meant to help you complete your return accurately. If you are unsure how to use it, a qualified tax professional can walk you through the process.

Thank you, and please reach out if you have any questions.
