We are pleased to introduce our new savings goal feature, designed to help you reach your financial targets with greater ease and clarity. You can now create custom goals for specific purchases, emergency funds, or long term plans, and the system will automatically track your progress toward each one. As you deposit money, your dashboard displays a clear timeline showing how close you are to completion. The feature also includes gentle reminders and optional automatic transfers so you can stay on track without constant manual effort. This update is available to all accounts starting today. Simply navigate to the savings section in your app to begin setting up your first goal. Your path to better financial habits just became more straightforward.
