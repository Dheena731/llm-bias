Introducing Ledgerly: Bookkeeping That Fits Into Your Day

We are excited to announce the launch of Ledgerly, a simple financial app built for small business owners who would rather spend their time serving customers than wrestling with spreadsheets. Whether you run a neighborhood bakery, a freelance design studio, or a two-person landscaping crew, Ledgerly brings your income, expenses, invoices, and tax records into one clear place.

Getting started takes only a few minutes. Connect your business bank account and card, and Ledgerly will automatically sort transactions into categories that make sense for your industry. When a payment is unclear, a short prompt asks for a quick confirmation rather than leaving you with a pile of unreconciled entries at month's end. Invoices can be created from your phone in under a minute, sent by email, and tracked until they are paid, with gentle reminders sent on your behalf when a balance comes due.

We built Ledgerly around the questions owners ask most often. How much can I safely spend this month? Am I on track to cover my quarterly tax payment? Which customers pay late? A dashboard answers these at a glance, and monthly summaries arrive in plain language rather than dense accounting jargon. When tax season comes, every deduction you have recorded is organized and ready to export for your accountant.

Your data is yours. Ledgerly uses bank-level encryption, never sells customer information, and lets you export your complete records at any time. The app is available now on iOS and Android and on the web, with a free plan for solo operators and affordable tiers for growing teams. New customers who sign up this month receive 60 days of premium features at no cost.

Ledgerly is our way of giving small businesses the same financial clarity that large companies take for granted. We invite you to try it, and we would welcome your feedback as we continue to improve it together.
