🎉 **Introducing Savings Goals!**

Taking control of your finances just got simpler. With our brand-new **Savings Goals** feature, you can now set specific targets—whether it’s an emergency fund, vacation, or big-ticket purchase—and watch your progress unfold in real time.

✨ **Key highlights:**
• Set custom amounts and flexible deadlines  
• Automatically route a chosen % of each deposit toward your goal  
• Visual progress tracking + milestone badges to keep you motivated  
• Funds are securely separated so you stay on track without temptation  

📱 **Get started:** Open the app → tap **“Savings Goals”** on your dashboard → enter your target amount and timeline. It only takes a minute to set up.
