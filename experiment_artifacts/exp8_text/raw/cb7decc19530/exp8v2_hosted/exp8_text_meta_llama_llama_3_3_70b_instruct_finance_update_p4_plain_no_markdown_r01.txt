Your end-of-year tax statement is a document that summarizes the income you have earned and the taxes that have been withheld from your pay during the year. This statement is usually provided by your employer and is used to help you complete your tax return. It will show the total amount of money you have earned, the amount of taxes that have been deducted, and any other relevant details such as superannuation contributions or other deductions. You will need this statement to accurately complete your tax return and claim any deductions or refunds you are eligible for.
