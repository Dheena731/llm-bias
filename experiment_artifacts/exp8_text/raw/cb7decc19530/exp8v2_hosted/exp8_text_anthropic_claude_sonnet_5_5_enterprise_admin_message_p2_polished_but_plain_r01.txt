Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starting Monday

Hello everyone,

Beginning Monday, all expense requests will follow a new approval workflow designed to speed up reimbursements and give everyone clearer visibility into where their requests stand.

Under the new process, you will submit each expense through the finance portal along with an itemized receipt and a brief note explaining the business purpose. Your direct manager will receive the request automatically and has three business days to approve or return it with comments. Requests over five hundred dollars will then go to the finance team for a second review, which also takes up to three business days. Once a request is fully approved, reimbursement is issued in the next regular payroll cycle.

A few details will help your requests move smoothly. Please submit expenses within thirty days of the purchase, since older requests may require additional justification. Make sure receipts are legible and show the vendor, date, and amount. If your request is returned, you will receive an email explaining what is needed, and you can resubmit directly from the original entry without starting over. Managers who expect to be away should name a backup approver in the portal so that requests are not delayed.

Any expenses submitted before Monday will be handled under the existing email-based process and do not need to be re-entered. We will hold two short training sessions this week, on Wednesday and Thursday at ten in the morning, and a recorded walkthrough will be available on the internal help site afterward.

If you have questions or run into any trouble with the portal, please contact the finance help desk and we will be glad to assist. Thank you for your cooperation as we make this change.

Best regards,
The Administration Team
