**Reminder: Please Lock Your Screen When Stepping Away**

To help keep company and customer data secure, please remember to lock your computer screen whenever you leave your workstation, even if you are only stepping away for a moment. Unattended, unlocked screens can leave sensitive information exposed.

You can lock your screen quickly using these keyboard shortcuts:
* **Windows:** Press the **Windows logo key + L**
* **Mac:** Press **Control + Command + Q**

Thank you for your ongoing commitment to data security.
