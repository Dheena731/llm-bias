Subject: Introducing Your Monthly Spending Summary

We're excited to announce a new monthly spending summary that gives you a clear picture of where your money went each month.

At the start of each month, you'll receive a summary of your spending from the previous month. It shows your total spending, how it compares to the month before, and a breakdown by category such as groceries, dining, transportation, and bills. You can also see your largest transactions and any recurring charges you may want to review.

The summary is available in the Insights tab of your account, and you can also choose to receive it by email. If you'd rather not get the email, you can turn it off in your notification settings at any time.

Your spending data stays private and is visible only to you.

We hope this helps you spot patterns, plan ahead, and stay on track with your goals. As always, send us your feedback and let us know how we can make the summary more useful.
