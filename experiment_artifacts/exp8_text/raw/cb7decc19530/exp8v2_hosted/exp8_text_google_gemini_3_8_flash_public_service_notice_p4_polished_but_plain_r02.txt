A heat advisory is currently in effect for the region through tomorrow evening due to high temperatures and elevated humidity levels. Heat index values are expected to exceed one hundred degrees, creating hazardous conditions that significantly increase the risk of heat exhaustion and heat stroke. 

Residents are strongly advised to remain in air-conditioned spaces whenever possible and to avoid prolonged sun exposure during peak afternoon hours. Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty, and wear loose, light-colored clothing if you must be outdoors. Reschedule strenuous outdoor tasks to the early morning or late evening, and take frequent rest breaks in shaded areas. Never leave children or pets unattended in parked vehicles, as interior temperatures reach lethal levels within minutes. Please check on elderly family members, neighbors living alone, and those without reliable cooling systems. 

If you or someone nearby experiences symptoms of heat illness, such as dizziness, headache, nausea, rapid pulse, or extreme fatigue, move immediately to a cool, shaded place and hydrate. If a person loses consciousness, becomes confused, or stops sweating, treat it as a medical emergency and call for immediate medical assistance.
