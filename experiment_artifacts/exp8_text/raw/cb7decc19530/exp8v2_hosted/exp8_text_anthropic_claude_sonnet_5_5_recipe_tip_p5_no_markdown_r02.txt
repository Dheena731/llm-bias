Toss your vegetables with oil first, then add salt and pepper, so the seasoning clings evenly instead of sliding off. Use enough oil to coat them lightly, spread them in a single layer with space between pieces, and roast at 425°F so they brown rather than steam. For extra flavor, finish with a squeeze of lemon juice or a sprinkle of fresh herbs right after they come out of the oven, since bright flavors fade quickly in high heat.
