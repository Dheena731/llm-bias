Today we are proud to introduce Tallyhouse, a mobile app built to help small business owners run the daily work of their company from one place. Invoicing, expense tracking, appointment scheduling, and customer messages now live in a single, uncluttered workspace, so you can spend less time switching between tools and more time serving the people who keep your doors open.

Tallyhouse is designed for owners who wear many hats. Send a professional invoice in under a minute, snap a photo of a receipt and have it categorized automatically, and see at a glance which payments are overdue. Your calendar and client notes stay in sync, so every booking starts with the right context.

We built Tallyhouse with small budgets in mind. The free plan covers up to twenty invoices a month with no credit card required, and our Plus plan adds payroll exports, recurring billing, and priority support for growing teams.

Tallyhouse is available today on iOS and Android. Download it, set up your business profile in about five minutes, and let us know what would make it more useful for you. Your feedback will shape the next release.
