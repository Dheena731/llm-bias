A section of Main Street will be closed to traffic beginning on Monday and lasting for approximately two weeks. The closure is necessary to allow for repairs to the road surface and will be in effect from the intersection with Oak Street to the intersection with Maple Street. During this time, traffic will be detoured onto adjacent streets and signs will be posted to direct motorists around the closure. Pedestrian access will be maintained throughout the closure period, but motorists are advised to plan alternate routes to avoid delays. The closure is expected to be completed by the end of the month, weather permitting.
