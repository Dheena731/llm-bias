Rinse your rice in a fine-mesh strainer under cold running water, swishing with your fingers until the water runs mostly clear, usually three or four rinses for long-grain varieties. That washes off the surface starch that turns into glue once it hits the pot. Then drain thoroughly, let it sit for five minutes so the grains dry slightly, and cook with a measured ratio rather than a flooded pot. You'll notice the difference in the first forkful: separate grains that hold their shape instead of clumping into a single mass.
