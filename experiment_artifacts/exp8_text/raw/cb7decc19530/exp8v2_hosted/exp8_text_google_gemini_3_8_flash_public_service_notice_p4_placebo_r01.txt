**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

A Heat Advisory is currently in effect due to dangerously high temperatures and humidity. These conditions significantly increase the risk of heat-related illnesses, including heat exhaustion and heat stroke.

Members of the public are strongly advised to take the following precautions:

* **Stay Hydrated:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and high-sugar drinks.
* **Stay Cool:** Remain indoors in air-conditioned buildings as much as possible. If your home lacks air conditioning, spend time in public facilities such as libraries or community centers.
* **Limit Outdoor Exposure:** Reschedule strenuous activities to early morning or late evening hours. Wear lightweight, light-colored, and loose-fitting clothing.
* **Protect Others:** Check on elderly relatives, neighbors, and those with underlying health conditions. 
* **Vehicle Safety:** Never leave children or pets unattended in parked vehicles for any amount of time.

If you or someone around you experiences warning signs of heat stroke—such as dizziness, confusion, nausea, or a high body temperature—call emergency services immediately.
