**MARLOW COUNTY OFFICE OF EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT**
**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY**

**In effect:** 11:00 a.m. Thursday through 9:00 p.m. Saturday

**What to expect:** Heat index readings of 105 to 109°F each afternoon, with little relief at night. Lows in the river valley and downtown will hold near 80°F, so buildings without air conditioning will keep absorbing heat through the weekend. The National Weather Service has not upgraded this to an Excessive Heat Warning, but the overnight lows are the reason we are asking for attention now. The body recovers at night, and this week it won't.

**Cooling centers (free, no ID required)**
- Eastgate Branch Library, 410 Calder Ave: 10 a.m. to 8 p.m.
- Marlow Senior Center, 88 Pruitt St: 9 a.m. to 6 p.m.
- Fire Station 6 community room, 2200 Dunmore Rd: noon to 10 p.m.
- Pets on leash or in carriers are welcome at Eastgate only.

**Who is most at risk**
- Adults over 65 living alone, especially on upper floors
- Anyone taking diuretics, beta blockers, or certain antidepressants and antihistamines, which can impair sweating or raise dehydration risk
- Outdoor workers in their first week on a job or returning from time off
- Infants and young children, and anyone in a parked vehicle

**What to do**
- Move outdoor work, athletic practice, and yard projects to before 10 a.m. or after 7 p.m. Take a shaded break every 20 to 30 minutes.
- Do not rely on a fan alone when indoor temperatures are above 90°F. A cool shower, wet cloths on the neck and wrists, or a few hours at a cooling center is more effective.
- A parked car can pass 110°F within 20 minutes with the windows cracked. Check the back seat every time.
- Run ovens, dryers, and dishwashers before noon or after 8 p.m. to ease the evening strain on the grid.
- Call or knock on two neighbors each day. Do not just text.

**Know the difference**
*Heat exhaustion:* heavy sweating, cramps, nausea, headache, cool and clammy skin. Move to a cool place, loosen clothing, sip water, and apply cool cloths. Seek care if symptoms last longer than an hour.
