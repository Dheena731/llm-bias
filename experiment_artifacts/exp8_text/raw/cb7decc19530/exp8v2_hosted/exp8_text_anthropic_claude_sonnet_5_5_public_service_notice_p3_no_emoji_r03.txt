**NOTICE: Changes to Recycling Collection**

Effective Monday, the recycling collection schedule is changing.

**What is changing**
- Recycling will be collected every other week instead of weekly.
- Collection day will move from Thursday to Tuesday for most addresses.
- Residents should check the updated calendar for their specific pickup dates.

**What you need to do**
- Place bins at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on your collection day.
- Rinse containers and keep items loose in the bin. Please do not bag recyclables.
- Flatten cardboard boxes and keep lids off bottles and jars.

**Accepted items:** paper, cardboard, plastic bottles and containers (#1 and #2), glass bottles and jars, metal cans.

**Not accepted:** plastic bags, foam packaging, food waste, and ceramics.

Bins containing non-recyclable items may be left uncollected with a tag explaining the issue.

For questions or to request a printed calendar, please contact the Public Works Department at the number listed on your municipal bill or visit the city website.

Thank you for helping keep our community clean and our recycling program running smoothly.
