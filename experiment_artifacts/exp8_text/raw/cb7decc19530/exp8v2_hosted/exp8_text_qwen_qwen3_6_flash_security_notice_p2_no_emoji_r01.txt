**NOTICE: PHISHING EMAIL AWARENESS**

**To:** All Employees  
**From:** Information Technology Security  
**Date:** May 22, 2024  

All staff are advised to exercise heightened vigilance regarding incoming email communications. We have detected an increase in phishing attempts targeting company systems.

Please follow these security guidelines:

1.  **Verify Senders:** Scrutinize sender addresses for subtle misspellings or unauthorized domains.
2.  **Do Not Click:** Refrain from clicking links or downloading attachments from unsolicited or suspicious messages.
3.  **Sensitive Data:** Our IT team will never request passwords or financial information via email.
4.  **Report Immediately:** If you receive a questionable email, forward it to security@organization.com and delete the original message.

Your cooperation is essential to maintaining the security of our network and data.

Information Technology Security Team
