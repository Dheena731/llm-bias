📅 **Calendar tip:** Stop using your calendar only for meetings. Block time for your tasks too.

When you add a task to your to-do list, also give it a slot on your calendar. Set a start time and an end time, just as you would for a meeting. Instead of wondering "When will I get this done?", you'll already have the answer, and you'll see right away if your day is overbooked.

**Try it this week:** Pick your three most important tasks for tomorrow and give each one its own time block tonight.
