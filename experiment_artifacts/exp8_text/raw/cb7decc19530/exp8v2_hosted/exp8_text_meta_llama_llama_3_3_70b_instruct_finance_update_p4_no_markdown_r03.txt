Your end-of-year tax statement is a summary of your income and taxes withheld for the year. It will show the total amount of money you earned, the amount of taxes that were taken out of your pay, and any other relevant tax information. This statement is typically used to help you prepare your tax return and ensure you are taking advantage of all the deductions and credits you are eligible for. You will receive this statement from your employer or financial institution, and it is usually available by the end of January each year.
