**Notice: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If you lose a company laptop or believe it has been stolen, you must report it immediately. Prompt reporting helps protect sensitive company data and reduces the risk of a security breach.

**What to do:**

1. **Report it right away.** Contact the IT Service Desk at [phone number] or submit a ticket at [internal link]. Do this as soon as you notice the laptop is missing, even if you are not certain it has been stolen.
2. **Report it to Security.** If the laptop was stolen, or you suspect theft, also notify the Security Office at [phone number or email].
3. **File a police report if required.** For theft, obtain a police report and provide a copy to your manager and the IT department.
4. **Provide details.** Include the asset tag number, serial number, make and model, date and location of the loss, and any files or accounts that may have been stored on or accessed from the device.
5. **Do not attempt to recover the device yourself.** Leave recovery to IT and Security.

**Important:** IT will take steps to secure the laptop remotely, including locking the device, resetting passwords, and wiping data where possible. Delays in reporting can compromise company information, so act quickly.
