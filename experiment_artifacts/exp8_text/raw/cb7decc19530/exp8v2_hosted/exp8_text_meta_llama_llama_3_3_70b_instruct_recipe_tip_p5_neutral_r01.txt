To elevate your roasted vegetables, try seasoning them in two stages! First, toss the veggies with olive oil, salt, and any aromatics like garlic or herbs before roasting. Then, just before serving, sprinkle with a pinch of fresh herbs, a squeeze of lemon juice, or a grind of pepper to add brightness and depth to the dish. This two-stage seasoning approach will bring out the natural flavors of the vegetables and add a burst of freshness to your finished roast.
