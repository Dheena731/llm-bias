**Subject: Expense reports change routing Monday, March 3**

Hi all,

Starting Monday, expense reports in Concur will route differently. Here's the new setup:

- **Under $75 per line, with a receipt attached:** no approval step. These go straight into the Thursday reimbursement batch.
- **$75 to $999.99:** your direct manager approves. Previously this went to two people, so it should be faster.
- **$1,000 and up:** manager approval, then Finance (me or Ravi). We're aiming for two business days. If one is sitting longer, message me directly. Don't resubmit, because that resets the queue.

Some things that will get reports bounced if you miss them:

- Reports submitted before the 3rd stay on the old routing. If your approver has left or is out until mid-March, tell me this week and I'll reassign them.
- Mileage lines need odometer start and end readings. A bare total gets rejected.
- Client meals need attendee names in the description. "Team lunch" isn't enough for the auditors.

Anything approved by the Tuesday before a Thursday payout goes out that week, as usual.
