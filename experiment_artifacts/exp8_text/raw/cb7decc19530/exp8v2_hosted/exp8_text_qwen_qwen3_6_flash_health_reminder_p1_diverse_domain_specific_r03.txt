Open your log from seven days ago. Before you load this week’s plan, cross-check two things: where you actually finished versus where you programmed yourself, and how your recovery markers trended across those sessions. If your average heart rate drifted upward on familiar routes, or your last few reps demanded more focus than usual—note it. Don’t chase a new best into a fatigued window. Drop one set, swap a hard day for movement, and adjust before Wednesday hits. The numbers only move forward when you read them first.
