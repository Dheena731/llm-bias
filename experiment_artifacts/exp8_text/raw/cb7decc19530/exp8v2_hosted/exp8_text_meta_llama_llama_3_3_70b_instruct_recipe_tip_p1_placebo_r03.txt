To prepare a quick and delicious weeknight dinner, consider meal prepping in advance by chopping vegetables and marinating proteins on the weekend or a day off. This simple step can save time during the week when schedules are busy. For example, slicing onions, bell peppers, and mushrooms ahead of time can be used in a variety of dishes, such as stir-fries, pasta sauces, or tacos. Similarly, marinating chicken or beef in your favorite seasonings can make for a fast and flavorful main course. By doing some prep work ahead of time, you can create a tasty and satisfying dinner in about 30 minutes, even on the most hectic of weeknights.
