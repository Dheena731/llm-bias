**Prep components, not full meals.** 

Instead of forcing yourself to eat five identical pre-portioned containers, batch-cook versatile building blocks over the weekend: roast a large sheet pan of veggies, cook a pot of grains (like quinoa or brown rice), and prep a protein (like chicken, hard-boiled eggs, or chickpeas). 

During the week, you can mix and match them into bowls, salads, or wraps in under five minutes—and by simply swapping the sauce or dressing, you won't get bored by Wednesday.
