Please be aware that there has been an increase in phishing emails targeting our organization. These emails are designed to trick recipients into revealing sensitive information such as login credentials or financial information. They often appear to be legitimate and may claim to be from a trusted source, but they are actually attempts to deceive and exploit.

To protect yourself and our organization, it is essential to exercise caution when receiving emails that ask for personal or financial information. Be wary of emails that create a sense of urgency or threaten consequences if you do not respond immediately. Never respond to an email that asks you to verify your login credentials or provide sensitive information.

If you are unsure about the legitimacy of an email, do not hesitate to contact the IT department for assistance. We are working to prevent these types of emails from reaching your inbox, but your vigilance is crucial in helping to protect our organization's security.

Remember that our organization will never ask you to provide sensitive information via email, and any requests for personal or financial information should be treated with skepticism. By being cautious and reporting suspicious emails, you can help to prevent phishing attacks and protect our organization's security.
