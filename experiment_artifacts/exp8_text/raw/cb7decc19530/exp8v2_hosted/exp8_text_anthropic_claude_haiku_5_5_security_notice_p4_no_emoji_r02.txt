**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication for Your Account**

To better protect your account and personal information, we strongly recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA). With 2FA, signing in requires both your password and a second verification step, such as a one-time code sent to your phone or generated by an authentication app.

**How to enable 2FA:**
1. Log in to your account and go to **Settings**.
2. Select **Security** or **Privacy**.
3. Choose **Two-Factor Authentication** and follow the on-screen instructions.
4. Confirm your phone number or set up an authenticator app, then enter the verification code to finish.

Please keep your backup codes in a safe place in case you lose access to your device.

If you have questions or need assistance, contact our support team. Thank you for helping us keep your account secure.
