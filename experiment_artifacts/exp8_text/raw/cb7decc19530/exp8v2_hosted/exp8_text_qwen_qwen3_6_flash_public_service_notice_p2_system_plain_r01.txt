This public notice informs residents and commuters that Maple Avenue will be closed to all vehicular traffic starting at six in the morning on Tuesday, October fifteenth, through five in the evening on Thursday, October seventeenth. The closure is necessary for urgent water main repairs that could not be completed safely during business hours. Traffic will be diverted onto Elm Street and Pine Road. Motorists are advised to allow extra travel time and to follow signage posted at the intersection of Maple Avenue and Fourth Street. Please refrain from entering the work zone while crews are active. We apologize for any inconvenience and appreciate your patience as we complete these essential improvements.
