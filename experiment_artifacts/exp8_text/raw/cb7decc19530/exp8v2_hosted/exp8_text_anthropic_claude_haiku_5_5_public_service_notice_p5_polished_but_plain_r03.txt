**Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations**

Polling station locations may have changed since the last election, so voters are encouraged to confirm where they should vote before election day. Each registered voter is assigned to a specific polling station based on their home address, and voting at the wrong location may mean casting a provisional ballot or being unable to vote.

To find your assigned polling station, contact your local election office or visit the official election website for your area, where you can enter your address to see your location. You may also check the information printed on your voter registration card or the sample ballot mailed to your home.

Polling stations are open from the times listed on the official election calendar. Voters who arrive in line before the closing time will be permitted to cast a ballot. Accessible entrances and voting equipment are available at every polling station, and voters who need assistance may request it from poll workers on site.

If you have questions or need help locating your polling station, please contact your local election office during business hours.
