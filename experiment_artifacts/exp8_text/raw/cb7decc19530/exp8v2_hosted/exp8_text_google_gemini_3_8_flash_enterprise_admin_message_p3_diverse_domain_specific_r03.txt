**Action required: Legacy VPN retirement and client cutover by Friday, Nov 10**

On Friday, November 10 at 22:00 UTC, we are shutting down the old OpenVPN concentrators (`vpn.corp.net`). Access to internal staging VPCs, the CI build farm, and internal admin tooling will switch entirely to our new WireGuard-based client (**Tailscale**).

**What changes for you:**
* **Routing:** Split-tunneling is now hard-enforced. Only traffic targeting `10.120.0.0/16` and internal DNS zones (`*.corp.internal`) will route through the tunnel. Your public traffic and local network remain untouched.
* **Auth:** Static profile certificates are gone. Authentication now runs through Okta SSO with a 7-day machine key expiry.
* **Stability:** The client handles network and sleep/wake transitions automatically without dropping sessions or hung sockets.

**What to do before Friday:**
1. **macOS/Windows:** The new client has already been pushed to corporate laptops via Jamf/InTune. Look for the Tailscale icon in your menu bar/system tray and authenticate using your work email.
