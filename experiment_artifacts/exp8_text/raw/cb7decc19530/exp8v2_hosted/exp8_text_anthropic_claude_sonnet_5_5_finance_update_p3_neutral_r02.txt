**Introducing Savings Goals**

We're excited to announce a new way to make saving money easier and more rewarding.

**What's new:**
With Savings Goals, you can set a target for anything you're working toward, whether it's a vacation, an emergency fund, or a new laptop. Give each goal a name, an amount, and an optional target date, and we'll show you how much to set aside each week or month to get there.

**Key features:**
- **Progress tracking:** See a visual progress bar for every goal at a glance.
- **Flexible contributions:** Add money manually or set up automatic transfers on your schedule.
- **Multiple goals:** Work on several goals at once and prioritize the ones that matter most.
- **Milestone alerts:** Get a nudge when you hit 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of your target.

**How to get started:**
Open the app, tap **Goals**, and select **Create a Goal**. It takes less than a minute.

Savings Goals is rolling out to all users over the next week. We'd love to hear what you think, so send us your feedback through the app.

Happy saving!
