You are receiving this end-of-year tax statement as a courtesy to provide you with a record of the taxes withheld from your paychecks and other income received during the past year. This statement will help you reconcile your income and taxes paid with the information on your tax return.

The statement includes the total amount of wages and other income paid to you during the year, as well as the total amount of federal and state taxes withheld from those payments. You can use this information to complete your tax return and to ensure that you are taking advantage of any credits or deductions to which you are entitled.

If you have any questions or concerns about the information on this statement, please do not hesitate to contact us. We are here to help you with any questions or concerns you may have.

Please retain this statement for your records, as you may need to refer to it when filing your tax return.
