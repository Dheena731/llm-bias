Here's a calendar tip that has saved me a lot of scheduling headaches: stop putting only meetings on your calendar and start blocking time for the work itself. If you need two hours to finish a report, create an event called "Write quarterly report" and treat it like any appointment. Once it's on the calendar, other people can see you're busy, you can see how full your week really is, and you're far less likely to say yes to something that collides with your most important tasks. Try it for one week and see how much calmer your days feel.
