Subject: Understanding Your Year-End Tax Statement

Dear Valued Customer,

Your year-end tax statement summarizes the income you earned and the taxes withheld or paid over the past year. You will need it when you file your tax return, so please keep it in a safe place.

The statement typically includes:

Total income: The amount you earned from wages, interest, dividends, or other sources during the year.

Taxes withheld: The federal, state, and local taxes your employer or financial institution already sent to the government on your behalf.

Deductions and credits: Any pre-tax contributions, such as retirement plan deposits, that reduced your taxable income.

Reporting boxes or codes: Standard identifiers, such as those on a W-2 or 1099 form, that tell the IRS or your state tax agency how to process the information.

If the figures on your statement do not match your records, contact the issuing organization before filing. If you have questions about how to use this information, a qualified tax professional can help you prepare an accurate return.
