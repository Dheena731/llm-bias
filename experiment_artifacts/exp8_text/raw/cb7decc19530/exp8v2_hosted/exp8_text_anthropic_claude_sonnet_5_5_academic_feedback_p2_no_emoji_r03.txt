Thank you for submitting your methods section. It gives a clear outline of your overall approach, and the structure is easy to follow. Below are the main strengths and areas for revision.

**Strengths**

**Areas for improvement**
- **Justification:** Explain why you chose each method, not just what you did. For example, say why this sample size, instrument, or analytic technique suits your question.
- **Detail and replicability:** Add enough specifics (participant criteria, materials, software, settings, procedures) that another researcher could repeat your study.
- **Sampling and variables:** Describe how participants or data were selected, and define your key variables and how you measured them.
- **Analysis:** State which analytic approach you used and how it addresses each research question or hypothesis.
- **Limitations and ethics:** Briefly note any methodological limitations, and include ethical considerations such as consent and data protection.
- **Style:** Use past tense and a consistent, concise tone. Cut any results or interpretation, which belong in later sections.
