Notice of Recycling Collection Changes

As part of our ongoing efforts to improve our recycling program, we are making some changes to the collection schedule and procedures. Starting next week, residents will need to place recyclables in designated bins on the designated collection day.

The collection schedule will be as follows:

* Monday, Wednesday, Friday: Regular household trash collection
* Tuesday, Thursday: Recycling collection

Recyclables must be placed in the bins provided by the city and must be sorted into the following categories: paper, plastic, glass, and metal.

Please note that the collection day may vary depending on your location, so it is essential to check with your local government for specific information.

We appreciate your cooperation and participation in this new program, and we are confident that these changes will help us to improve the effectiveness of our recycling efforts.
