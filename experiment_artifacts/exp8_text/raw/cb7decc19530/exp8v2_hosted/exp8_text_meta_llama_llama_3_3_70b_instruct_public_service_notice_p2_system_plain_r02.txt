A section of Main Street will be closed to traffic beginning on Monday and lasting for approximately two weeks. The closure is necessary to allow for repairs to the road surface and will affect the area between Oak Avenue and Maple Street. Detour signs will be posted to direct motorists around the closed section. Pedestrian access will be maintained throughout the closure, but motorists are advised to plan alternative routes to avoid delays. The closure is expected to be in place until the repairs are completed, at which time the road will be reopened to traffic.
