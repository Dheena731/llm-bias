HEAT ADVISORY ISSUED

A heat advisory has been issued for the entire metropolitan area. Temperatures are expected to reach the mid to high 90s today and tomorrow, with heat index values making it feel like the low 100s.

Residents are advised to take precautions to protect themselves and others from the heat. This includes staying hydrated by drinking plenty of water, taking regular breaks in cool areas, and checking on vulnerable individuals such as the elderly and young children.

If you or someone you know is experiencing heat-related symptoms such as dizziness, nausea, or headaches, please seek medical attention immediately.

Stay cool and stay safe!
