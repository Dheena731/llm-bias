**Administrative Notice**

**Quarterly Access Reviews**

As part of our ongoing effort to maintain the security and integrity of our systems, we will be conducting our quarterly access reviews. This process ensures that all users have the appropriate level of access to our systems and data, and that access is revoked for individuals who no longer require it.

**What to Expect:**

* Department managers will be contacted to review and verify access for their team members.
* Access will be reviewed for all systems, including but not limited to, network drives, software applications, and sensitive data.
* Any access that is no longer necessary will be removed.

**Timeline:**

* The review process will begin on [Date] and is expected to be completed by [Date].
* Department managers will be notified of any changes to access and will have the opportunity to request updates or modifications.

Please reach out to the IT department at [contact email or phone number] if you have any questions or concerns about the quarterly access review process.

Thank you for your cooperation and assistance in maintaining the security of our systems.
