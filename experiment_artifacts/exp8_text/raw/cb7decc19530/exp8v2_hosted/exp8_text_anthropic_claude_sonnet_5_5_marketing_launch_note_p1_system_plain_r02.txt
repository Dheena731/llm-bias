Introducing Tallyroom, the app that gives small business owners their evenings back.

If you run a small business, you already know the work that never shows up on a price tag: chasing invoices, juggling appointments, and piecing together last month's numbers from three different spreadsheets and a pile of receipts. Tallyroom brings all of it into one calm, simple place so you can spend less time on paperwork and more time on the work you started your business to do.

With Tallyroom, you can send professional invoices in under a minute, see who has paid and who needs a gentle nudge, and keep your calendar, customer notes, and expenses together on one screen. Everything syncs between your phone and your computer, so the shop counter, the job site, and the kitchen table all show the same up-to-date picture. There is no training course to sit through and no accounting degree required. Most owners are set up and sending their first invoice within fifteen minutes.

We built Tallyroom alongside bakers, electricians, hair stylists, and freelance designers, and their feedback shaped every feature. The result is software that respects your time and your budget. Plans start at a flat monthly price with no hidden fees, and every new customer gets the first sixty days free.

Tallyroom is available today on iPhone, Android, and the web. Download it, set up your business in a few taps, and see how much lighter your week can feel. Your customers will notice the difference, and so will you.
