As part of our ongoing effort to maintain system security and ensure compliance with company policies, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews. During these reviews, we will verify that all users have the appropriate level of access to company systems and data. This review will help us to identify and remove any unnecessary access, which will in turn reduce the risk of unauthorized data disclosure. The reviews will be conducted by the IT department and will involve checking user accounts and access permissions across all company systems. If you have any questions or concerns about the access review process, please contact the IT department for more information.
