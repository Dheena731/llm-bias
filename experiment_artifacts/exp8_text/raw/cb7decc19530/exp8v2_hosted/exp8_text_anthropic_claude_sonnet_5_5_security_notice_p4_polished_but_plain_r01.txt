Notice: Please Enable Two-Factor Authentication

To help keep your account secure, we strongly encourage you to turn on two-factor authentication. This feature adds a second layer of protection to your login. In addition to your password, you will be asked for a temporary code or approval from a device you trust, so a stolen or guessed password alone will not be enough for someone to get in.

Setting it up takes only a few minutes. Open your account settings, find the security section, and choose the option for two-factor authentication. You will be prompted to select a verification method. An authenticator app on your phone is the most secure and reliable choice, though text message codes are also available if you prefer them. Follow the on-screen instructions to link your chosen method, then enter the confirmation code to complete the process.

When setup is finished, you will be given a set of backup codes. Please save them somewhere safe and separate from your password, such as a locked drawer or a reputable password manager. These codes will let you regain access if you lose your phone or cannot receive a verification message.

After two-factor authentication is active, you will be asked for a second verification step whenever you sign in from a new device or browser. If you receive a verification request that you did not initiate, do not approve it. Change your password right away and contact our support team so we can help secure your account.

Thank you for taking this simple step to protect your information. If you have any questions or run into trouble during setup, our support team is ready to help.
