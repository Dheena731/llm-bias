Subject: Update to our card transaction fees

Dear customer,

We are writing to let you know that our card transaction fees will change on the first day of next month. From that date, the fee for each card payment processed through your account will be 1.9 percent of the transaction value, up from the current rate of 1.6 percent. The fixed charge of 10 cents per transaction will stay the same.

This adjustment reflects an increase in the fees we are charged by card networks and partner banks. For several years we absorbed these rising costs rather than pass them on to you, but we can no longer do so without affecting the quality of the service we provide. We have kept the increase as small as we can.

You do not need to take any action. The new rate will apply automatically to all payments processed on or after the effective date, and any transactions completed before then will be charged at the current rate. Your monthly statement will show the fee applied to each payment so you can see exactly how the change affects you.

If you would like to talk through how this may affect your costs, or if you have any questions, please contact our support team by phone or email and we will be glad to help. Thank you for your continued business.

Kind regards,

The Customer Accounts Team
