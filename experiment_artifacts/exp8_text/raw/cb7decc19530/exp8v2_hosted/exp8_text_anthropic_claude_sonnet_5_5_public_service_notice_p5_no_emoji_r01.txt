**NOTICE: Polling Station Locations**

Voters are advised that polling stations will be open on **Tuesday, November 4, from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.** Please go to the station assigned to your precinct, as listed on your voter registration card.

**Polling Locations**

- **Precincts 1-4:** Lincoln Community Center, 120 Main Street
- **Precincts 5-8:** Riverside Public Library, 45 River Road
- **Precincts 9-12:** Oakwood Elementary School Gymnasium, 310 Oak Avenue
- **Precincts 13-16:** City Hall, Council Chambers, 1 Civic Plaza

**Before You Go**

- Bring a valid photo ID.
- Confirm your precinct number on your voter registration card or on the county elections website.
- All locations are wheelchair accessible. Curbside voting is available upon request.
- Anyone in line by 8:00 p.m. will be permitted to vote.

**Questions?** Contact the County Elections Office at (555) 010-2000 or visit the elections office website for the most current information.
