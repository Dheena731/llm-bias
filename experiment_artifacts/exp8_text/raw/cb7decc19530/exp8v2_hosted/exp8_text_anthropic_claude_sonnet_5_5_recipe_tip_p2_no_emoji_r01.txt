**Tip: Store herbs like flowers or wrap them in damp paper towels.**

For tender herbs such as cilantro, parsley, and dill, trim the stems and place them upright in a jar with an inch of water. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and refrigerate. Change the water every few days.

For sturdier herbs such as rosemary, thyme, and sage, wrap them in a slightly damp paper towel and keep them in a resealable bag or container in the fridge.

Basil is the exception. It prefers room temperature, so keep it in a jar of water on the counter, away from direct sun.
