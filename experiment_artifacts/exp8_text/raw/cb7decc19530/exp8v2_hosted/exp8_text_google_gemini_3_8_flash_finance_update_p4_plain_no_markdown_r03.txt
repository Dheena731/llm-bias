An end-of-year tax statement is an official document that summarizes your financial activity with an organization over the past calendar year. It typically reports total earnings, taxes withheld, interest paid or received, and any deductible contributions or expenses. You will need this form to accurately report your financial information when filing your annual income tax return. When you receive your statement, review it carefully to confirm that all personal details and figures match your own records. If the information is correct, keep a copy in a safe place for your permanent files and use it when preparing your taxes or provide it to your tax preparer.
