Subject: Update to our card transaction fees

Dear customers,

Beginning on the first day of next month, we will be changing the fees we charge on card transactions. We want to explain what is changing, why we are making the change, and what it means for you.

The processing fee on card payments will rise from 1.5 percent of each transaction to 1.9 percent. The fixed charge of 20 cents per transaction will stay the same. For a typical sale of 50 dollars, this means the total fee will increase from 95 cents to 1.15 dollars. Payments made by bank transfer or direct debit are not affected, and neither are refunds, which will continue to be processed without an additional charge.

This adjustment reflects higher fees from the card networks and the banks that process these payments on our behalf. We have absorbed these increases for some time, but we can no longer do so without affecting the quality of the service we provide, including fraud monitoring, payment security, and customer support.

You do not need to take any action. The new rate will apply automatically to all card transactions processed on or after the effective date, and any payment made before then will be charged at the current rate. If you would like to estimate how the change will affect your monthly costs, your account dashboard shows your recent transaction volume, and our support team can walk you through the calculation.

If you have questions or would like to discuss alternative payment options that may lower your costs, please reply to this message or contact our support team during business hours. We appreciate your continued trust and will keep you informed of any further changes well in advance.

Sincerely,
The Customer Accounts Team
