This Saturday, join your neighbors for our annual Community Harvest Day at Maple Grove Park from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. There will be live music, a local farmers market, free face painting for kids, and a pie-baking contest at noon. Bring a blanket or lawn chair and plan to stay a while. Admission is free, and all proceeds from food sales will support the town library's summer reading program. Volunteers are still welcome, so if you can spare an hour or two, stop by the welcome table when you arrive. We hope to see you there.
