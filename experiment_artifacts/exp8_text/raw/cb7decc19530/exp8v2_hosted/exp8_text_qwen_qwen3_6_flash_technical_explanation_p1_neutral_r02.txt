Latency—the time between a user's action and the app's response—is critical because it directly shapes how people perceive your web application. Even delays of a few hundred milliseconds make interfaces feel sluggish, triggering frustration, higher bounce rates, and lost conversions. Research consistently shows that a 1-second slowdown can reduce page views by ~7% and cut customer satisfaction by up to 16%. 

Beyond user experience, latency affects business and technical outcomes: search engines like Google use page responsiveness as a ranking signal, so poor latency can hurt visibility. Technically, high latency often points to bottlenecks in network routing, database queries, or frontend rendering, which can strain servers, increase costs, and limit scalability. In short, minimizing latency isn't just about speed; it's about delivering reliable, engaging experiences that retain users and drive growth.
