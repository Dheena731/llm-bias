Hello everyone,

We are excited to share that savings goals are now available in the app. This new feature gives you a simple way to set aside money for the things that matter most, whether that is an emergency fund, a summer trip, a down payment, or a new laptop. Each goal has a name, a target amount, and an optional deadline, so you can see at a glance how close you are to reaching it.

To get started, open the Savings tab and select Create goal. Enter the details for what you are saving toward, choose how much you want to contribute, and decide whether to make a one-time transfer or set up a recurring contribution from your linked account. You can adjust or pause a goal at any time, and the app will recalculate your progress so you always know where you stand.

Your progress is shown as a percentage bar on each goal card, along with the amount remaining and the projected completion date based on your current contribution pace. When you reach a goal, we will send a notification to celebrate the milestone with you.

Savings goals are designed to work alongside your existing accounts. Funds allocated to a goal remain in your linked account and continue to earn any interest that account offers, and you can move money between goals or withdraw it whenever you need to.

We would love to hear how you are using the feature. If you run into any issues or have suggestions for improvements, please send them through the Help menu. Happy saving!
