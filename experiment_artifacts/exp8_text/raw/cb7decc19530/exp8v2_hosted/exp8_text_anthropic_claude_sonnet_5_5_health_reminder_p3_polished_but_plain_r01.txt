Reminder: It's time to book your annual health checkup. Routine visits are one of the simplest ways to catch small concerns early, keep track of changes in your numbers over time, and make sure your vaccinations and screenings are up to date. Even if you feel well, a yearly appointment gives you and your clinician a chance to talk about sleep, stress, diet, exercise, and anything that has been quietly nagging at you.

To make the most of it, call or go online this week to choose a date, and try to schedule a morning slot if your clinician asks you to fast beforehand. Check your insurance coverage so you know which tests are included, and gather any recent records, medication lists, or family history updates you may need. Write down a few questions or symptoms in advance so nothing gets forgotten once you are in the room.

Once the appointment is on your calendar, set a reminder for the day before. A little planning now will save you time later and help you stay on top of your health all year.
