Caching speeds up a website by storing copies of frequently used data—like images, CSS files, JavaScript, and HTML—in quick-access locations closer to the user. Instead of fetching everything from the main server on every visit, your browser or a content delivery network (CDN) pulls these resources instantly from a temporary "cache." This eliminates redundant network trips, lowers latency, and reduces server workload, delivering pages in milliseconds. Think of it like keeping your favorite tools on a workbench instead of driving to a hardware store every time you need one.
