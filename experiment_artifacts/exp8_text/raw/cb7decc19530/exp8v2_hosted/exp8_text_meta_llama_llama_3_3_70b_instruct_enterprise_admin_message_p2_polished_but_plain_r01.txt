We are introducing a new expense approval workflow that will help streamline our financial processes and improve efficiency. The new workflow will allow employees to easily submit expenses for approval and track the status of their submissions. Managers will receive notifications when expenses are submitted for their approval, and they will be able to review and approve or reject expenses online. This new workflow is expected to reduce processing time and minimize errors. If you have any questions or need assistance with the new workflow, please don't hesitate to reach out to the finance department. We will also be providing training and support to help ensure a smooth transition to the new system.
