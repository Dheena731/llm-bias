Please remember to lock your workstation whenever you step away from your desk, even for a moment, to safeguard confidential information and uphold our security protocols. To lock your screen quickly on a Windows computer, press the Windows key and the letter L simultaneously. If you are using a Mac device, press Command, Control, and the Q key together. Consistently applying this habit minimizes the risk of unauthorized access and demonstrates your commitment to protecting our company data. We appreciate your cooperation in maintaining a secure environment for everyone.
