📅 **Calendar tip:** Stop using your calendar for appointments only. Use it for *focus time* too!

Block out 1–2 hours a few times a week and label it something specific, like "Draft project proposal" instead of just "Work." Your future self will know exactly what to do, and coworkers will think twice before booking over it.

If it's on the calendar, it actually happens. ✅
