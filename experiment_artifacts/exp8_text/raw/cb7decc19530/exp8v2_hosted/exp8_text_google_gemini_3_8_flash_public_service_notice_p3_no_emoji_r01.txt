**NOTICE: Changes to Residential Recycling Collection**

Effective Monday, November 4, 2024, the schedule for municipal recycling collection will change. 

**Key Updates:**
* **New Collection Days:** Neighborhood collection routes have been adjusted. Your designated pickup day may have shifted.
* **Pickup Frequency:** Recycling will now be collected every other week instead of weekly. Trash collection schedules remain unchanged.
* **Set-Out Times:** Please ensure all recycling bins are placed at the curb by 7:00 AM on your scheduled day.

To view the updated route map and find your new collection day, please visit **www.cityservices.gov/recycling** or call the Waste Management Office at **(555) 019-2834**.

Thank you for your cooperation as we work to optimize our community collection routes.
