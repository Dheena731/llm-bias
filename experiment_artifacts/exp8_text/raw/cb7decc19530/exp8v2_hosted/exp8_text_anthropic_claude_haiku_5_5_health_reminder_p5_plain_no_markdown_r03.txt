Hi there, this is a friendly reminder that your prescription may be running low and is due for a refill soon. You can request it through your pharmacy's website, their mobile app, or by calling the pharmacy directly. Most pharmacies need a few business days to process refill requests, so it's best to place your order before you run out. If you have any refills remaining on your prescription, you can simply ask the pharmacy to fill it. If you have no refills left, you will need to contact your doctor's office for a new prescription. If you have any questions about your medication or notice any issues, please reach out to your pharmacist or healthcare provider.
