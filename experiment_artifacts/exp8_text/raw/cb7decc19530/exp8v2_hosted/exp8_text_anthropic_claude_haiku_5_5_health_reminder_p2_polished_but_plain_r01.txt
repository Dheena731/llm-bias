Take a moment to check in with your body and make sure you have had enough water today. Hydration supports your concentration, helps regulate your temperature, and keeps your energy steady, yet it is easy to overlook when you are busy. If you tend to forget, try keeping a glass or bottle within reach at your desk, and fill it whenever you pass a sink or water fountain. A useful rule of thumb is to drink a glass with each meal and another between them. Pay attention to your thirst, and remember that darker urine can be a sign you need more fluids. A few small sips throughout the day will serve you far better than a large amount all at once.
