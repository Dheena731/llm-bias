**Elevate Your Roasted Vegetables with Layered Seasoning!**

To take your roasted vegetables to the next level, try seasoning them in layers. First, toss the vegetables with olive oil, salt, and pepper before roasting. Then, halfway through the roasting time, sprinkle with any additional seasonings you like, such as dried herbs (thyme, rosemary), garlic powder, or paprika. Finally, just before serving, squeeze with fresh lemon juice and sprinkle with chopped fresh herbs (parsley, basil) for a burst of brightness and flavor. This layered approach will add depth and complexity to your roasted vegetables, making them truly delicious!
