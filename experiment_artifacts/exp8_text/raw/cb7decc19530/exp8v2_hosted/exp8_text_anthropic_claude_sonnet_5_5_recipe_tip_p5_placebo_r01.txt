**Tip: Season in two stages for better flavor.**

Toss your vegetables with oil and a generous pinch of salt *before* roasting. The oil helps them brown, and the salt draws out a little moisture and builds flavor from the start. Spread them in a single layer so they roast rather than steam.

Then, right after they come out of the oven, finish them with a fresh touch of seasoning. Taste one, and add a bit more salt if needed. A squeeze of lemon juice or a splash of vinegar brightens the flavor, and chopped fresh herbs such as parsley or basil add freshness that would fade in the oven's heat.

**Why it works:** Dried spices and sturdy herbs, such as black pepper, garlic powder, paprika, or thyme, hold up well during roasting. Delicate flavors like fresh herbs and citrus do best when added at the end.
