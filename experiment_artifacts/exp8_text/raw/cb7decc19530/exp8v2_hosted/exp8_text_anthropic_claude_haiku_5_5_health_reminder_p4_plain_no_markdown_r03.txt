Hey, this is your friendly reminder to step away from the screen for a few minutes. Your eyes, neck, and mind all benefit from regular breaks, even short ones. Try following the 20-20-20 rule: every twenty minutes, look at something about twenty feet away for at least twenty seconds. Stand up, stretch, refill your water, or take a quick walk to the window. These small pauses can reduce eye strain, ease tension in your shoulders, and help you return to your work with a clearer head. Your future self will thank you for taking care of yourself today.
