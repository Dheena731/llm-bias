The town library is pleased to announce its current operating hours for all visitors. The facility is open Monday through Thursday from nine in the morning until eight in the evening. On Friday and Saturday, the building welcomes patrons from nine in the morning until five in the afternoon. The library is closed on Sundays and all official municipal holidays. The outdoor drop box next to the front doors remains available at all times for returning books and media outside of regular hours. Visitors are welcome to use the study areas, public computers, and reference services at any time during open hours.
