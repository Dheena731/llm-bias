NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS

Residents are advised that polling stations will be open on Election Day from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Please vote at the location assigned to your voting district.

Polling Locations by District:

District 1: Riverside Community Center, 120 River Road
District 2: Maple Grove Elementary School, 45 Maple Avenue
District 3: Central Public Library, 300 Main Street
District 4: Oakwood Senior Center, 78 Oakwood Drive
District 5: Westside Fire Station, 910 West Park Boulevard

Important Reminders:

All polling stations are wheelchair accessible. Curbside voting is available upon request for voters who cannot enter the building.

Please bring a valid photo ID. If you are unsure of your assigned district or polling place, check your voter registration card or contact the County Elections Office at 555-0142.

Voters who are in line by closing time will be permitted to cast their ballots.

Thank you for taking part in the democratic process.

Office of the County Elections Administrator
