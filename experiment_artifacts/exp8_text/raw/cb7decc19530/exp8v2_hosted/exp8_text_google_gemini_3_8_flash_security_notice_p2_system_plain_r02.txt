Please remain vigilant regarding suspicious emails received in your work inbox. Cybercriminals frequently use deceptive messages that mimic trusted colleagues, vendors, or services to steal sensitive data and gain unauthorized access to company systems. Be cautious of any email that creates a false sense of urgency, requests your login credentials, asks for financial transfers, or includes unexpected attachments. Always examine the sender address closely for subtle spelling errors or unfamiliar domain names. If you receive an email that appears suspicious, do not click on any links or open any attachments. Report the message immediately to the internal information technology team and delete it from your inbox. Thank you for your continued attention to keeping our network secure.
