We have introduced a new Monthly Spending Summary to help you better track and manage your finances. 

Starting this month, you will receive an automated overview at the close of each month. This report includes:

- A clear breakdown of your expenses by category
- Month-over-month spending comparisons
- Insights into your top spending areas and recurring charges

You can view your latest summary at any time by navigating to the Reports section of your account dashboard. Notification preferences for this summary can be adjusted in your account settings.
