**Fortnight 1.0: a budget template for people who get paid every two weeks**

Most budget templates assume income arrives once a month. If you're paid biweekly, twice a year you get a third paycheck, and your rent, which hits on the 1st, lands in a different pay period than your car insurance. Fortnight is built around pay periods instead of calendar months.

**What's in it**

- **Import tab:** Paste in a CSV from your bank. Date, description, and amount are the only columns it needs. A keyword table (`SHELL` → Gas, `TRADER JOE` → Groceries) assigns categories. Anything it can't match turns yellow so it doesn't disappear into "Misc."
- **Sinking funds:** Enter annual or irregular costs, like registration, the vet, or a January insurance premium. Fortnight divides them across your pay periods and tracks the balance.
- **Credit cards:** Purchases count on the day you swipe, not when you pay the statement, so the payment isn't counted twice.
- **Third-paycheck holding row:** The extra check sits unassigned until you decide where it goes. It won't quietly inflate your grocery budget.

**What it isn't**

It doesn't sync with your bank. You'll spend about ten minutes every other Friday pasting and fixing yellow rows. There are no macros or add-ons, so it works the same in Excel and Google Sheets.
