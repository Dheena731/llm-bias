Our company is introducing a new monthly spending summary. This summary will provide a detailed breakdown of all expenses incurred by the company on a monthly basis. The report will include a list of all individual transactions, categorized by type of expense, such as travel, office supplies, and equipment. It will also include a total amount spent in each category, as well as a grand total for all expenses. This information will be available to all employees and will be distributed via email at the beginning of each month.
