**Plan Ahead for Stress-Free Lunches**

To make your busy week easier, try planning and prepping your lunches on the weekend or one day a week. Set aside 30 minutes to:

* Decide on 3-4 simple lunch ideas (e.g., sandwiches, salads, leftovers)
* Make a grocery list and shop for ingredients
* Prep individual portions or assemble lunches in containers
* Store them in the fridge or freezer for easy grab-and-go meals

This simple habit will save you time, money, and reduce lunchtime stress, allowing you to focus on more important things during your busy week!
