NOTICE TO VOTERS: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS

Please be advised that polling stations for the upcoming election have been assigned to specific locations within each voting district. Your assigned polling station is listed on your voter registration card and can also be confirmed through the official election office website or by calling the county election office directly.

Voters are reminded to check their assigned location before election day, as polling stations may differ from those used in previous elections. Some locations have been relocated or consolidated to better serve the community. Accessible entrances and parking are available at all designated sites.

Polling stations will be open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on election day. Voters standing in line at closing time will be permitted to cast their ballots.

For questions or assistance, please contact your local election office during regular business hours. Thank you for taking part in the democratic process.
