To keep fresh herbs like basil, rosemary, and thyme fresh for a longer period, store them in a glass of water on the counter or in the refrigerator. Trim the stems and place them in a glass with about an inch of water, then cover the top with a plastic bag. This will help keep the herbs hydrated and prevent them from wilting, allowing you to enjoy them in your cooking for up to a week. Simply change the water every few days to keep them fresh!
