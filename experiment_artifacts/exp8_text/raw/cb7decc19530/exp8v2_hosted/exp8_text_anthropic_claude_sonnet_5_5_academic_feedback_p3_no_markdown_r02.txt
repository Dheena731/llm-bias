Reviewer Comment: Clarity of the Research Question

The manuscript does not state its central research question clearly, and this weakens the paper as a whole. The introduction describes several related topics, including user engagement, platform design, and long-term behavioral change, but it never says which relationship or phenomenon the study is meant to investigate. The aims listed at the end of the introduction ("to explore factors related to engagement and to understand user experiences") are broad and could describe many different studies. Elsewhere, the abstract implies a causal question about whether design features increase engagement, while the methods and analysis read as descriptive and exploratory.

This ambiguity causes problems later in the paper. It is difficult to judge whether the sample, measures, and analytic approach are appropriate when the target of inquiry is not specified. The results section presents many findings, but without a guiding question I cannot tell which are central and which are incidental. The discussion then draws conclusions about effectiveness that do not clearly follow from the stated aims.

I recommend that the authors state one focused research question, or a small set of them, and say whether the study is descriptive, associational, or causal. They should define the key constructs, specify the population and context, and then align the hypotheses, methods, and conclusions with that question. A tighter framing would make the contribution much easier to evaluate and would likely make the paper more persuasive.
