**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication**

To protect your account and maintain system security, all staff and users are required to enable two-factor authentication on their profiles. Two-factor authentication strengthens login security by requiring both your password and a unique verification code from your mobile device each time you sign in.

Please complete this setup by accessing your account settings, selecting the Security tab, and following the activation instructions. The process requires only a few minutes.

If you encounter difficulties, contact the Help Desk using the contact information provided in your employee handbook or on the organization’s main website. Accounts without two-factor authentication enabled will lose access to protected files and internal tools until the requirement is met.
