**Cook once, use twice:** When you're cooking a grain or protein for tonight's dinner, make a double batch. Roast a second tray of vegetables, cook extra rice, or grill a few more chicken thighs while you're at it. Tomorrow night, you can turn the leftovers into a quick stir-fry, grain bowl, or wrap in under 10 minutes.

**Bonus tip:** Salt your food in layers as you cook, rather than all at the end. It builds deeper flavor with less salt overall.
