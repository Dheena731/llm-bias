Starting this month, you'll receive a monthly spending summary that gives you a clear picture of where your money went over the previous 30 days. The summary groups your transactions into categories such as housing, groceries, dining, transportation, and subscriptions, and shows how each category compares to the month before. You'll also see your total spending, the largest individual charges, and any recurring payments we've detected, so you can spot charges you may have forgotten about.

The summary arrives on the first business day of each month and is also available at any time from the Reports section of your account. Categories are assigned automatically, but you can rename them, merge them, or move individual transactions to a different category, and your changes will carry forward to future summaries. If a transaction is missing or miscategorized, you can flag it directly from the summary page.

You don't need to do anything to start receiving the summary, and you can turn it off in your notification settings if it isn't useful to you. We'd welcome your feedback on what's helpful and what could be clearer, which you can send through the feedback link at the bottom of the summary page.
