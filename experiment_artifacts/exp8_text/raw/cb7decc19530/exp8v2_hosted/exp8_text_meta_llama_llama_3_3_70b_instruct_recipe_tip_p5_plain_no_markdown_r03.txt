To bring out the natural flavors of roasted vegetables, season them just before serving. This allows the herbs and spices to retain their potency and aroma, rather than losing them during the cooking process. Simply sprinkle a pinch of salt and your choice of herbs or spices over the vegetables after they have finished roasting, and toss them gently to distribute the seasonings evenly. This simple step can make a big difference in the flavor of your roasted vegetables.
