To make a delicious and stress-free weeknight dinner, consider preparing a few ingredients in advance. On the weekend or a day off, take some time to chop vegetables, marinate proteins, and cook grains or legumes that can be used throughout the week. This will save you time during the week when you are busy and make it easier to throw together a healthy meal. For example, you can roast a large batch of vegetables on the weekend and use them in different meals throughout the week, such as in salads, stir-fries, or as a side dish. You can also cook a large batch of rice, quinoa, or lentils and use them as a base for different meals. By doing some prep work ahead of time, you can create a variety of tasty and nutritious meals with minimal effort on busy weeknights.
