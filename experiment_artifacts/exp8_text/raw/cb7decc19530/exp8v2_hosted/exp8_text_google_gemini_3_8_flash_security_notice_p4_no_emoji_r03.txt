**Important Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**

To enhance the security of your account and protect your data from unauthorized access, we strongly encourage all users to enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA). 

Adding 2FA creates an extra layer of protection by requiring a secondary verification code in addition to your password when signing in.

**How to enable 2FA:**
1. Log in to your account and go to **Settings** > **Security**.
2. Select **Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions to link your preferred authenticator app or mobile number.

If you encounter any issues during the setup process, please contact the support team for assistance.
