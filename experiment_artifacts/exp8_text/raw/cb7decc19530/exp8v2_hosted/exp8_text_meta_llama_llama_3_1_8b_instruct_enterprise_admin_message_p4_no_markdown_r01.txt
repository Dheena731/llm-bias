NOTICE TO ALL USERS

Quarterly Access Reviews

As part of our ongoing efforts to ensure the security and integrity of our systems, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews. These reviews are an essential component of our security and compliance program and are designed to ensure that only authorized personnel have access to our systems and data.

During each quarter, our security team will review all user accounts and access permissions to ensure that they are still necessary and up-to-date. This may involve:

* Reviewing user account privileges and access levels
* Verifying the necessity of access to specific systems, data, and applications
* Identifying and resolving any security vulnerabilities or compliance issues

If your account is selected for review, you may be contacted by our security team to provide additional information or to discuss your access permissions. In some cases, your access may be temporarily suspended or terminated if we are unable to verify the necessity of your access.

Please note that this process is a routine part of our security and compliance program and is not a reflection on any individual's trustworthiness or behavior. If you have any questions or concerns about the quarterly access reviews, please do not hesitate to contact our security team.

Thank you for your cooperation and understanding in this matter.
