Don't forget to drink enough water throughout the day to stay hydrated and maintain your overall health. Aim to consume at least eight cups (64 ounces) of water daily, and adjust according to your individual needs, activity level, and climate. Drinking water regularly can help boost energy, support digestion, and keep your skin healthy. Make it a habit to drink a glass of water at regular intervals, such as when you wake up, at mealtimes, and before bedtime.
