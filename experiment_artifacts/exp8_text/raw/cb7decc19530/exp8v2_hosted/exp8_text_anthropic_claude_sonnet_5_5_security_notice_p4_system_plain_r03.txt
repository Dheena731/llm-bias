Notice: Please Enable Two-Factor Authentication

To help protect your account and personal information, we strongly encourage all users to turn on two-factor authentication as soon as possible. This security feature requires you to confirm your identity with a second step when you sign in, such as entering a code sent to your phone or generated by an authenticator app. Even if someone obtains your password, they will not be able to access your account without that second piece of verification.

Setting it up takes only a few minutes. Open your account settings, select the security section, and choose the option to enable two-factor authentication. You will be asked to pick a verification method and confirm it with a test code. We recommend using an authenticator app when possible, since it is generally more secure than text messages. Be sure to save your backup codes in a safe place so you can still get in if you lose your phone.

Please complete this step by the end of the month. If you need help along the way, our support team is available to guide you through the process. Thank you for helping us keep your account and our community secure.
