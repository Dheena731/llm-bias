Please be aware that there has been an increase in phishing emails being sent to employees. These emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information such as your username and password. They often appear to be legitimate and may claim to be from a trusted source, but they are actually attempts to compromise our security.

To protect yourself and the company, please be cautious when receiving emails that ask for personal or financial information. Never click on links or download attachments from unfamiliar senders, and never provide sensitive information in response to an email. If you are unsure whether an email is legitimate, please contact the IT department before taking any action.

Remember that the company will never ask you to reveal your password or other sensitive information via email. If you receive an email that appears to be from a company representative and is asking for this type of information, it is likely a phishing attempt and should be reported to the IT department immediately.
