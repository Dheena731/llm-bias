**Feedback on Conference Poster Draft**

Thanks for sending the draft. The core research question is clear, and the layout is clean and easy to scan. Here are my main suggestions:

**Strengths**
- The title is concise and communicates the topic well.
- The color scheme is consistent and professional.
- The methods section is well organized.

**Suggested revisions**
1. **Lead with the takeaway.** Viewers often spend only 30 to 60 seconds at a poster, so add a one-sentence summary of your main finding near the top.
2. **Reduce text in the results section.** Replace some paragraphs with a single labeled figure, and make sure axes and legends are readable from about two meters away.
3. **Define abbreviations on first use,** or move them into a small glossary.
4. **Strengthen the conclusion.** State what the findings mean for the field and what comes next, rather than only restating results.
5. **Check the contact information and QR code.** Make sure they link to the correct paper or data repository.

**Minor edits**
- Use consistent capitalization in figure titles.
- Increase the font size of the references if they are hard to read.

Overall, this is a solid draft. With a clearer headline finding and less text in the results, it should work well for a busy conference hall. Happy to look at a revised version if you'd like.
