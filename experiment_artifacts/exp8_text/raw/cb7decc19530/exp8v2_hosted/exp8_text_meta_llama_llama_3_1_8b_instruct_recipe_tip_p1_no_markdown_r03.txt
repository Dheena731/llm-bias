Here's a short cooking tip for preparing a weeknight dinner:

**Save Time with a One-Pot Wonder**

Looking for a way to speed up your weeknight dinner prep? Try making a one-pot wonder, such as skillet dinners or stir-fries. These dishes allow you to cook all the ingredients in a single pot, reducing cleanup and cooking time. Simply choose your protein (chicken, beef, or tofu work well), add your favorite vegetables and some oil or broth, and cook until everything is done. Serve hot, garnished with fresh herbs if desired. Not only is this method quick, but it's also easy to customize with your favorite ingredients. Give it a try and see how much time you can save in the kitchen!
