**Subject:** [Action] VPN Client update: Legacy binary deprecation & auth latency fix

The legacy client (`vpn_legacy_v3`) will stop establishing tunnels after **Oct 15**.

We replaced the underlying stack to address the packet loss seen on the US-East nodes during peak hours. This migration also allows us to enforce TLS 1.3, which eliminates the handshake loops some users reported when rotating certificates.

**What's different:**
*   Connect time reduced from ~4s to <600ms.
*   Split-tunnel rules now respect DNS overrides without flushing the local cache.
*   Auto-reconnect logic handles brief Wi-Fi drops better (no more full auth re-requests).
