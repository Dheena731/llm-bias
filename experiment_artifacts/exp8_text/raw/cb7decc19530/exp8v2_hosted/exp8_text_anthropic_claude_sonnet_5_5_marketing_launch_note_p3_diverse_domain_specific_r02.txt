**Ledger Ledger 2.0: a paycheck-based budget sheet for Google Sheets and Excel**

I've released a new version of the budget template I've been using since 2021. The biggest change is that it now budgets by paycheck instead of by calendar month.

If you're paid biweekly, you know two months a year contain three paychecks. Most templates either ignore this or let it blow up your averages. Here, those extra checks go into a "third paycheck" line that you assign on purpose, usually to the annual bills or the car fund.

**What's in it**
- **Paste-in Transactions tab.** Drop in a CSV from your bank. The Rules tab matches keywords ("SHELL," "TRADER JOE") to categories, and anything unmatched lands in a yellow Uncategorized list instead of disappearing.
- **Sinking funds.** Car registration, vet bills, and the annual $139 you forgot about each get a monthly set-aside and a running balance.
- **Transfers don't count as spending.** Paying the credit card from checking is no longer counted twice.
- **Purchase date, not posting date.** A Friday dinner that posts Monday stays in the right week.

**What it doesn't do**
No bank sync. You'll paste a CSV every week or two, which takes about ten minutes. There are no macros or scripts, so every formula is visible and editable.
