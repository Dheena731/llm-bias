To reduce stickiness when cooking rice, rinse the dry grains under cold running water until the liquid runs clear. This step washes away excess surface starch that causes clumping during cooking. Measure your ingredients precisely, using approximately one cup of water for every cup of uncooked long grain rice, and keep the lid securely on the pot while it simmers to maintain even steam distribution. Once the timer ends, remove the pot from the heat and let it sit undisturbed for ten minutes before gently lifting the lid and separating the grains with a fork.
