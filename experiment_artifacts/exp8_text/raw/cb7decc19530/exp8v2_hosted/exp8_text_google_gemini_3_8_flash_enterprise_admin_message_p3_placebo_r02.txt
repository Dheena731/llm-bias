Subject: Important Update: Transition to New VPN Software

Dear Team,

We are updating the Virtual Private Network (VPN) client used to access company systems securely from remote locations. This change will improve connection reliability and enhance our overall network security.

The new VPN application will be installed automatically on all company-managed computers. Once the installation is complete, you will be prompted to restart your device and sign in using your standard login credentials. 

Detailed setup instructions and troubleshooting steps are available on the internal IT support portal. If you experience any issues or need assistance, please contact the IT Help Desk. 

Thank you for your cooperation during this transition.
