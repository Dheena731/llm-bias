This is a reminder to book your annual health checkup. It is easy to put off when you feel well, but routine exams can catch problems early and give you a chance to update vaccinations, review medications, and talk through any concerns. Please call your doctor's office or check their online scheduling this week, and choose a date that fits your calendar so it does not slip another month. If you have recent test results or a list of questions, set them aside so you can bring them along.
