PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE

Maple Street Closure Between 4th Avenue and 7th Avenue

The City Department of Public Works announces that Maple Street will be closed to all vehicle traffic between 4th Avenue and 7th Avenue for water main replacement.

Dates: Monday, June 10 through Friday, June 21
Hours: 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., daily (weekends excluded)

Detour: Drivers should use Oak Street to 8th Avenue and return to Maple Street via 3rd Avenue. Detour signs will be posted.

Access: Local residents and businesses will maintain pedestrian access. Emergency vehicles will be able to reach all properties. Residents may experience brief interruptions in water service and will be notified in advance by door hanger.

Parking: Please remove vehicles from Maple Street between 4th and 7th Avenues by 6:30 a.m. on June 10. Vehicles remaining in the work zone may be towed at the owner's expense.

Weather or unforeseen conditions may extend the project schedule. Updates will be posted at the City website and on the Public Works information line.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience as we work to improve local infrastructure.

For questions, contact the Department of Public Works at 555-0142 or publicworks@examplecity.gov.
