**Notice: Quarterly User Access Reviews**

**To:** All Department Managers and System Owners  
**From:** IT Administration  

The quarterly user access review is now underway. 

To maintain organizational security and ensure appropriate data protection, all managers and system owners must review the system permissions currently granted to their team members. 

**Required Actions:**
1. Review the access lists provided for your team and managed systems.
2. Verify that each user’s permissions align with their current role and job responsibilities.
3. Confirm and approve valid access, or mark permissions for removal if they are no longer needed.

Please complete your review within two weeks of receiving this notice. Prompt completion ensures that our systems remain secure and that access is restricted strictly to authorized personnel. 

For questions or assistance with the review process, please contact the IT administration team.
