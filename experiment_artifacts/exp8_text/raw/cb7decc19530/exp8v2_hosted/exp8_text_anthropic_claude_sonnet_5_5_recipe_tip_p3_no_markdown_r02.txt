To make rice less sticky, rinse it before cooking. Place the uncooked rice in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer and run cold water over it, swirling with your hand, until the water runs mostly clear. This washes away the surface starch that causes grains to clump together. For extra fluffiness, use slightly less water than usual (about 1 3/4 cups of water per 1 cup of long-grain rice), keep the lid on while it cooks, and let it rest off the heat for 10 minutes before fluffing gently with a fork.
