PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE: LIBRARY OPENING HOURS

All patrons are advised of the following regular schedule for library operations:

Monday through Thursday: 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM
Friday: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
Saturday: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM
Sunday: Closed

These hours cover all public services, including material circulation, computer stations, meeting rooms, and staff assistance. Please allow sufficient time to complete transactions before doors close. For scheduled holiday modifications, please consult the library’s official website or call the main information desk. Thank you for your visits.
