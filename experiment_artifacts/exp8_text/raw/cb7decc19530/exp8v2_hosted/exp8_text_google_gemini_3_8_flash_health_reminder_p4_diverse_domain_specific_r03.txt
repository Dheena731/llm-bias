Your blink rate drops by roughly half the second you lock onto a monitor, and the muscles in your eyes weren’t built to hold a fixed focal distance of twenty inches for four hours straight. 

Look away from the glass. Find an object outside—a chimney line, a parked car, the far corner of the ceiling—and let your gaze sit on it until your eyes actually adjust to the depth. 

Twenty seconds at infinity focus. Let your shoulders drop out of your ears, take a full breath, and blink. The queue will still be there when you look back.
