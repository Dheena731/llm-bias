One way to make weeknight dinner preparation easier is to do some advance planning and prep work. Consider setting aside some time on the weekend to chop vegetables, marinate proteins, and cook grains or legumes that can be used in various meals throughout the week. This can save you a significant amount of time during the week when you are busy with work and other activities. For example, you can cook a big batch of rice or quinoa on the weekend and use it as a base for different meals, such as stir-fries, salads, or bowls. Similarly, you can roast a large batch of vegetables on the weekend and use them in soups, stews, or as a side dish during the week. By doing some prep work ahead of time, you can create a variety of quick and easy meals that can be ready in no time.
