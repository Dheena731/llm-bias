To make a quick and easy weeknight dinner, consider preparing a one-pot meal such as a hearty stew or skillet dinner. By cooking all the ingredients together in one pot, you can save time on cleanup and reduce the number of dishes to wash. Additionally, one-pot meals often require minimal preparation and can be ready in under 30 minutes, making them a great option for busy weeknights. Simply brown your protein and vegetables, add in your favorite seasonings and liquid, and let the pot simmer while you set the table and prepare any sides.
