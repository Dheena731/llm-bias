**NOTICE OF AMENDMENT TO PRIVACY POLICY**

Larder Foods Ltd ("Larder," "we") gives notice that its Privacy Policy is amended effective **14 July 2025** (the "Effective Date"). The amended Policy replaces the version dated 2 January 2023 in full.

**1. Summary of material changes**

(a) **Order history retention.** We previously deleted itemised order records 24 months after delivery. Under the amended Policy, they are kept for 36 months to support refund disputes and tax reporting in the United Kingdom and Ireland. After 36 months, records are anonymised, not retained in identifiable form.

(b) **Dietary and allergy data.** Allergy and intolerance information you enter at checkout is now stored separately from your delivery address and is visible only to our kitchen quality team. It is no longer included in marketing segments.

(c) **Payment processor.** Card payments are now processed by Brindle Payments Ireland DAC, replacing Corvane Pay Inc. Your card details continue to be tokenised and are not stored by Larder.

(d) **Analytics.** Behavioural analytics cookies are off by default for new accounts. Existing accounts keep their current setting until you change it under Settings > Privacy.

(e) **Data export.** You may request a CSV export of all personal data we hold about you, free of charge, through the Settings page or by writing to the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

**2. What you need to do**

No action is required to accept these changes. If you do not agree with the amended Policy, you may close your account before the Effective Date. Closing your account deletes identifiable profile data within 14 days, except for the order records described in Section 1(a), which are retained for the period stated.

**3. Questions and complaints**

Write to our Data Protection Officer at dpo@larderfoods.example or Larder Foods Ltd, Unit 4, Cobden Yard, Leeds LS2 7PD. If you are unsatisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (UK) or the Data Protection Commission (Ireland), as applicable.
