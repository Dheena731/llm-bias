The recycling collection schedule will be changing as of the first week of April. Residents are advised to check their collection day and ensure that all recyclables are placed at the curb by 7am on the designated day.

Residents who currently have their recyclables collected on a bi-weekly basis will now have them collected on a weekly basis. However, all recyclables must still be sorted into the designated categories before being placed at the curb.

Residents are reminded to rinse and empty all containers, and to remove any plastic bags or lids. Newspaper and cardboard should be flattened before being placed in the recycling bin.

For more information, please contact the waste management department at 555-1234.
