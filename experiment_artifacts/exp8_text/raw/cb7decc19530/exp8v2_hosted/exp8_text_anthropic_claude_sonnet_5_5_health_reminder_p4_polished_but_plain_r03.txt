A quick reminder to step away from your screen for a few minutes. Every twenty minutes or so, look at something at least twenty feet away for about twenty seconds, which gives your eyes a chance to relax and refocus. Once an hour, stand up, stretch your shoulders and neck, and walk around the room or grab a glass of water. These pauses reduce eye strain, ease tension in your back and wrists, and often help you return with clearer thinking. If you tend to lose track of time, set a gentle timer so the habit does not depend on memory. Small breaks taken consistently do more for your comfort and focus than a long one taken rarely.
