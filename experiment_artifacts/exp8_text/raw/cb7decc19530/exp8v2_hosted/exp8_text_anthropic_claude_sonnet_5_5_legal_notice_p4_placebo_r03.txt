# NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

**To:** Customers who have purchased, or are considering purchasing, from us.

**1. Eligibility.** You may receive a refund of the price you paid if all of the following apply:
- (a) You send your refund request in writing within 14 days of the purchase date.
- (b) You provide proof of purchase.
- (c) The product is unused and in its original condition and packaging, or the service has not yet begun.

**2. Ineligibility.** We will not grant a refund if any condition in section 1 is not met.

**3. Your legal rights.** This notice does not limit any rights you have under applicable consumer protection law, including rights relating to faulty or misdescribed goods and services.

**4. How to request a refund.** Send us a written request that includes your proof of purchase and a short description of the reason for the request.

**5. Payment of approved refunds.** If we approve your request, we will refund you to the original payment method within 10 days of approval.

**6. Decisions.** We will tell you in writing whether your request is approved or denied, and if it is denied, we will state the reason.
