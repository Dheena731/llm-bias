This notice outlines the terms governing refund eligibility for all purchases made through our official channels. A refund will be issued only when a valid proof of purchase is provided and the request is submitted within thirty days of the original transaction date. Items must be returned in their original packaging, unused, and in resalable condition with all accompanying materials intact. Digital goods, personalized items, and services already rendered are not eligible for refund unless required by applicable law. To initiate a refund request, customers must submit written notice to the designated support address, including order identifiers and a clear statement of grounds for the claim. We will review each submission within ten business days and communicate our determination in writing. All refund decisions are final and subject to the exclusive discretion of the company, except where otherwise mandated by statutory rights. This policy takes effect immediately and supersedes any prior informal statements regarding returns or credits.
