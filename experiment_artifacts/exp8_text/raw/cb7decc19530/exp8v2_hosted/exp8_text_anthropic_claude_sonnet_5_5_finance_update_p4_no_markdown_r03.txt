Subject: Your End-of-Year Tax Statement

Hello,

Your end-of-year tax statement is now available. This document summarizes your financial activity for the past calendar year and helps you complete your tax return. Here is what you will find in it:

Income: This section lists the money you earned or received during the year, such as wages, interest, dividends, or other payments. Each amount is reported separately so you can match it to the right section of your tax return.

Taxes withheld or paid: This shows the amount of tax already taken out of your income or paid on your behalf. These figures are credited toward your total tax bill for the year.

Deductions and credits: If applicable, this section lists items that may lower your taxable income or your final tax bill, such as mortgage interest, retirement contributions, or education expenses.

Account details: Your name, address, and tax identification number appear at the top. Please check that they are correct, since errors can cause delays in processing your return.

What to do next:
1. Review each section and compare it with your own records.
2. Contact us promptly if you notice any errors so we can issue a corrected statement.
3. Keep a copy for your files and provide it to your tax preparer or enter the figures into your tax software.

Please note that this statement is meant to inform you, not to serve as tax advice. For questions about how these numbers affect your return, consider speaking with a qualified tax professional.
