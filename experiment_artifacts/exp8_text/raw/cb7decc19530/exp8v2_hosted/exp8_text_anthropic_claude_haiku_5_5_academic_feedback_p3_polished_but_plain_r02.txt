The manuscript's central research question, as stated in the introduction, is whether community engagement improves health outcomes in rural areas. While the topic is important and well suited to the journal's readership, the question as written is too broad to guide the study design or to be answered by the data presented. "Community engagement" is never defined, and the paper uses the term to describe at least three different activities: advisory board participation, volunteer outreach, and attendance at public health workshops. Each could plausibly affect health through different mechanisms, so it is unclear which exposure the authors intend to evaluate. Likewise, "health outcomes" spans self-reported wellbeing, clinical biomarkers, and service utilization, and the methods section analyzes only the last of these. I would ask the authors to specify a single, operational definition of the exposure, name the primary outcome, and state the population and time frame explicitly. Refining the question in this way would also clarify whether the reported analysis, which relies on cross-sectional survey data, can support the causal language used in the discussion. With a sharper question, the authors can better align their hypotheses, measures, and conclusions.
