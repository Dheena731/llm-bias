Notice: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop

If your company laptop is lost or stolen, please report it immediately, even if you believe you may find it soon. Prompt reporting is the most effective way to protect company data, customer information, and your own accounts from unauthorized access.

Contact the IT Service Desk as soon as you realize the device is missing. You can reach them by phone at any hour, and a phone call is preferred over email because it allows the team to act right away. Be prepared to share your name, the device's asset tag or serial number if you have it, the time and place you last had the laptop, and whether it was powered on, locked, or left in sleep mode. Also mention any sensitive files or applications you know were stored on it.

Once your report is received, IT will take steps to lock the device remotely, disable your access credentials, and wipe stored data if necessary. You may be asked to change passwords for any accounts you used on the laptop, including email, cloud storage, and internal systems. Please follow these instructions promptly.

If the laptop was stolen, or if you believe it was taken from a vehicle, home, or public place, file a report with local police and give a copy of the report number to IT and your manager. Do not attempt to recover the device yourself or confront anyone you suspect of taking it.

Please also inform your manager the same day. If the laptop contained regulated or confidential information, such as personal data, financial records, or client materials, the Security and Compliance team may need to assess whether further notification is required. Your honesty and speed help them make those decisions accurately.

Reporting a lost laptop will not result in disciplinary action in itself. Delays in reporting, however, can increase risk and may be reviewed. A replacement device will be arranged once your report is complete and your manager has approved it.

Thank you for helping keep our information secure.
