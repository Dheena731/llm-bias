Please be advised that residential recycling collection schedules and procedures will change beginning Monday, November 3. Starting on this date, recycling will be collected every other Wednesday instead of weekly on Fridays. General household trash pickup days will remain unchanged. Under the updated guidelines, all clean cardboard must be flattened and placed completely inside the blue bin, as loose bundles left beside the container will no longer be accepted. In addition, glass bottles and jars should be rinsed and placed in the designated green crate rather than mixed with paper and plastic. Please ensure your bins are placed at the curb by seven in the morning on your collection day and retrieved by eight in the evening. For questions regarding specific neighborhood routes or accepted materials, please contact the municipal waste management office by telephone or visit city hall during regular business hours. Thank you for your cooperation as we work to improve local sustainability and service efficiency.
