Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations

Polling station assignments may have changed since the last election. Each registered voter is assigned to a specific polling station based on home address, so please confirm your location before you go to vote. You can check your assigned station through your local election office, either by visiting its website or calling its office during business hours. Many jurisdictions also provide a lookup tool that requires only your name and address.

Polling stations are typically open from early morning until evening on Election Day. Voters should arrive at their assigned location, not a neighboring one, to ensure their ballot is counted correctly. Accessible entrances, parking, and curbside voting options are available at most locations, and staff can help you find the right accommodations if you need them.

If you are unsure of your polling station, have recently moved, or cannot find your station listed, please contact your local election office as early as possible so that any issues can be resolved before Election Day.
