**NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE**

The New York State Department of Transportation (NYSDOT) announces that a section of State Route 28 (SR 28) in the town of Olive, Ulster County, will be closed to all traffic from Monday, March 21, 2022, to Friday, November 19, 2022. The closure is necessary to facilitate the replacement of the Boiceville Bridge, a critical transportation artery that spans the Esopus Creek.

During this period, motorists are advised to follow the posted detour route, which will direct traffic via County Route 47 (CR 47) and State Route 212 (SR 212). The detour route will add approximately 10 miles to the typical commute, and drivers should plan for additional travel time.

The NYSDOT will maintain access to local businesses and residences throughout the closure, and emergency services will be accommodated as needed. A shuttle service will be provided for pedestrians and bicyclists who normally use the bridge.

For up-to-date information on the road closure and detour route, please visit the NYSDOT website at [www.dot.ny.gov](http://www.dot.ny.gov) or call the NYSDOT Customer Service Center at (518) 457-6195.
