**Lost Company Laptop Reporting Procedure**

If you have misplaced or lost a company laptop, please report it to the IT Department immediately. This is a critical step in ensuring the security and integrity of our company's data.

To initiate the reporting process, please follow these steps:

1. Contact the IT Department via phone at 555-1234 or email at [itdept@company.com](mailto:itdept@company.com).
2. Provide your employee ID number and the serial number of the missing laptop.
3. Inform the IT Department of the last known location and time the laptop was seen.
4. If the laptop was in use at the time of loss, please provide the name of the application or software being used.

Upon receiving your report, the IT Department will initiate a lockout of the laptop's account to prevent unauthorized access. A replacement laptop will be issued to you, and you will be required to return the original laptop to the IT Department as soon as possible.

If you are unsure about what to do, please consult the company's laptop usage policy, available on the company intranet. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT Department.

**Note:** This procedure applies to all company-owned laptops. If you have any questions or concerns, please speak with your supervisor or a member of the IT Department.
