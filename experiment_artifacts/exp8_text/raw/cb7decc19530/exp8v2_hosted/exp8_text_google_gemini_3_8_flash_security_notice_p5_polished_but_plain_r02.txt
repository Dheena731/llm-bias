If you have lost or misplaced your company-issued laptop, immediate reporting is essential to protect confidential company data. Please notify the IT Service Desk and your direct supervisor immediately upon discovering that the device is missing. In your message, include your full name, employee identification number, the laptop asset tag or serial number if available, and the approximate time and location where the device was last seen. If you suspect the laptop was stolen, file a report with local law enforcement as soon as possible and obtain an official incident number for company records. Prompt notification allows the security team to remotely lock the hardware, revoke network credentials, and protect company assets. Your swift cooperation helps minimize potential security risks and expedites the issuance of a replacement device.
