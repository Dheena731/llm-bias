**Update: Changes to expense routing live Monday**

To fix the approval bottlenecks holding up month-end close, we’ve updated the routing rules in the expense portal starting Monday, October 14. 

Here is what changes for submitters and approvers:

* **Under $1,000:** Routes only to your direct manager. No finance sign-off required if an itemized receipt and cost center are attached.
* **$1,000 – $7,500:** Direct manager, then automatically routed to your department head.
* **Software & subscriptions (any amount):** Now automatically flags IT Procurement for duplicate license checks before hitting Finance.
* **48-hour auto-escalation:** If a manager doesn’t review a report within two business days, it skips to their direct skip-level so reimbursements don’t stall during PTO.

**Cutover note:** Any reports submitted before 5:00 PM this Friday will process under the legacy workflow. Unsubmitted drafts will need to be re-saved on Monday to pick up the new cost-center fields.
