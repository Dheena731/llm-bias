Today we are launching Tallyhouse, a simple app built for the people who run small businesses and wear every hat themselves.

If you have ever sent an invoice from a spreadsheet, tracked appointments on sticky notes, or lost an evening to bookkeeping, you already know why we made it. Tallyhouse brings your scheduling, invoicing, and customer records into one calm, easy place. You can book a client, send a bill, and see who has paid, all from your phone in a few taps.

We built it alongside bakers, barbers, contractors, and freelance designers, and their feedback shaped every screen. There are no confusing menus and no features you will never use. Setup takes about ten minutes, and most owners tell us they save several hours a week within the first month.
