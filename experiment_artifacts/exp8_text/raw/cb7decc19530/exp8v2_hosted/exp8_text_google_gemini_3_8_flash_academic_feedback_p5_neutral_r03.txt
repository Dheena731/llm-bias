Here are a few options, depending on the tone you want to set:

**Balanced & Constructive (Best for general feedback):**
> "You make several strong arguments here, but you need to include in-text citations for your data and specific claims. Always credit your outside sources to strengthen your paper's credibility and avoid plagiarism."

**Short & Direct (Best for a margin note):**
> "Please add citations here. Any specific facts, statistics, or borrowed ideas must be properly referenced."

**Firm (Best if it affects the final grade):**
> "Several key claims and outside ideas are presented without sources. Proper citation is essential for academic integrity, and the lack of references significantly weakened this essay."
