As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain security and compliance, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews to ensure that all users have the appropriate level of access to our systems and data. These reviews will help us to identify and remove any unnecessary access, and to verify that all access is still required and valid. 
The reviews will be conducted on a quarterly basis, and will include an examination of all user accounts, roles, and permissions. 
You will be notified if any changes are made to your access as a result of the review. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the admin team.
