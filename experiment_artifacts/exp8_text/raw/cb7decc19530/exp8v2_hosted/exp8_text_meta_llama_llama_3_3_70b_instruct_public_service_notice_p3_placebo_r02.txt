Attention Residents:

As of next month, the recycling collection schedule will be changing. Instead of collecting recyclables every week, they will now be collected every other week. This change is intended to improve the efficiency of the collection process and reduce costs.

The new collection schedule will be as follows: if your recyclables are currently collected on a Monday, they will now be collected on the 1st and 3rd Mondays of each month. If your recyclables are currently collected on a Thursday, they will now be collected on the 2nd and 4th Thursdays of each month.

Please make sure to check the new schedule and adjust your recycling routine accordingly. If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact our waste management department for more information.
