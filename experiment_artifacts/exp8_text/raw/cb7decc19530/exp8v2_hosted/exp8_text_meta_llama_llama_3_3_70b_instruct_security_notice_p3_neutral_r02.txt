**Important Reminder: Screen Locking**

As a reminder, please make sure to lock your computer screens when leaving your workstation unattended. This is an important security measure to protect sensitive information and prevent unauthorized access. Simply press the Windows key + L (or Ctrl + Shift + Eject on a Mac) to lock your screen. Let's work together to maintain a secure work environment!
