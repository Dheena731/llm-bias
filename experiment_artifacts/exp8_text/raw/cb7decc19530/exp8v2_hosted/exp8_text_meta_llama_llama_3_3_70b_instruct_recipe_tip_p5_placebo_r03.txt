To bring out the natural flavors of roasted vegetables, season them thoughtfully. Toss the vegetables with olive oil, salt, and your choice of aromatics like minced garlic or herbs before roasting. After roasting, add a squeeze of fresh lemon juice and any additional seasonings you prefer, such as pepper or dried herbs. This two-step seasoning process allows the initial flavors to meld during roasting and the finishing touches to brighten and enhance the dish just before serving.
