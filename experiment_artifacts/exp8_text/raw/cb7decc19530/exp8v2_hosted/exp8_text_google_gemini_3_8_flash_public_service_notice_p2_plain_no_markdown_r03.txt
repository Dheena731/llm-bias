The Department of Public Works advises motorists that Main Street, between First Avenue and Fourth Avenue, will be closed to all through traffic from Monday, October 23, through Friday, October 27. The closure will be in effect daily from seven in the morning until five in the evening to allow crews to complete scheduled water main repairs and asphalt resurfacing. 

Detour signs will be posted to direct traffic around the work zone using Oak Street and Elm Avenue. Pedestrian access to local businesses and residences will be maintained throughout the project, but curbside parking will be temporarily prohibited in the affected area. Motorists are urged to use caution, reduce speed near the construction zone, and expect minor delays. Thank you for your patience and cooperation as we work to improve our city infrastructure.
