We are pleased to announce the launch of our new monthly spending summary feature designed to provide greater clarity into your financial habits. This comprehensive overview aggregates your transactions over the course of each calendar month to deliver a concise snapshot of your expenditure patterns. You will now be able to view detailed breakdowns by category alongside cumulative totals, allowing for more informed budgeting decisions. The summary is accessible directly from your main dashboard and updates automatically as new charges process. By reviewing this information regularly, you can identify spending trends, monitor progress toward savings goals, and ensure your allocations align with your current priorities. We encourage you to explore this tool at your earliest convenience to take full advantage of these enhanced insights.
