**Reviewer Comment:**

> The manuscript addresses an interesting topic; however, the core research question lacks sufficient clarity and precision. While the Introduction provides a broad overview of the subject, it does not explicitly define the specific research gap or the primary question this study seeks to answer. Because the central objective remains ambiguous, it is difficult to properly evaluate whether the chosen methodology is appropriate or if the conclusions are fully justified. I strongly recommend that the authors clearly formulate their research question(s) (or hypotheses) toward the end of the Introduction and maintain a tighter focus on them throughout the paper.
