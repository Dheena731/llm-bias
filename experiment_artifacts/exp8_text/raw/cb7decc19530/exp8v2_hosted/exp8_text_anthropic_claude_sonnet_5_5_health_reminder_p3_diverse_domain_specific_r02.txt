**Subject: Your annual checkup is due**

Hi Dana,

It's been about 12 months since your last physical (March 14), so it's time to get the next one on the calendar.

**Before you call:**
- Check whether your insurance covers the visit at no cost. Most plans do for preventive care, but confirm the date of your last claim.
- Ask the office if they'll draw blood at the visit or want you to come in fasting. Fasting usually means nothing but water for 8–12 hours, so a morning slot is easier.
- Pull together your current medication and supplement list, plus any family history updates (a parent's new diagnosis, for example).

**Worth raising at the appointment:**
- Blood pressure and cholesterol numbers from last year, to see which direction they're moving
- Whether you're due for any screenings based on your age, such as colon, skin, or vaccines like tetanus or shingles
- Anything you've been putting up with: a nagging cough, poor sleep, a mole that looks different

Appointments with primary care doctors often book out 3–6 weeks, so call this week rather than when you notice you're overdue.
