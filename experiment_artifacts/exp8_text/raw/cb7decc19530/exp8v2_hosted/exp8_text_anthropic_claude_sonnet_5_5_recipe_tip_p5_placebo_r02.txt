**Tip: Season in two stages for better flavor**

Toss your vegetables with oil and a generous pinch of salt *before* roasting. The oil helps them brown, and the early salt seasons them throughout as they cook. Use enough oil to coat the pieces lightly, not so much that they look wet.

Then, right after they come out of the oven, taste one and adjust. Add a little more salt if needed, plus a finishing touch such as a squeeze of lemon juice, a splash of vinegar, or a sprinkle of fresh herbs. Brightness from acid or fresh herbs lifts the deeper, caramelized flavors that roasting creates.

If you want to use ground spices or dried herbs, toss them on with the oil at the start for most vegetables. Keep delicate fresh herbs, like parsley or basil, for the end, since they can burn or turn bitter in a hot oven.

Also, spread the vegetables in a single layer with some space between them. Crowded pieces steam instead of roasting, and they won't brown as well, no matter how well seasoned they are.
