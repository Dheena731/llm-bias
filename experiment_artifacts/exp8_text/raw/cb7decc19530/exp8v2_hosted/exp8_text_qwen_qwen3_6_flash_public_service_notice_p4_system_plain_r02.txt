A heat advisory is in effect for the entire county beginning at seven in the morning on Wednesday and ending at eight in the evening on Friday. Peak temperatures are forecast to reach ninety-nine degrees with heat index values potentially climbing to one hundred and five. Officials urge residents to drink water frequently, limit time spent outdoors during the hottest part of the day, and never leave children or pets unattended in vehicles. Communities are encouraged to check on elderly neighbors and those without air conditioning. If signs of heat illness appear, such as confusion, rapid pulse, or loss of consciousness, call emergency services immediately. Additional updates will be provided through local television, radio, and official municipal channels.
