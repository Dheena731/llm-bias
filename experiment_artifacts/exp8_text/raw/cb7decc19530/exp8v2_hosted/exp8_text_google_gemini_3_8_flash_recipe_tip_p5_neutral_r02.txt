**The "Two-Step" Secret:** 

Toss your vegetables with oil, salt, and heat-resistant spices (like garlic powder, smoked paprika, or rosemary) *before* roasting. Then, the moment they come out of the oven piping hot, finish them with a splash of **acid**—like a squeeze of fresh lemon juice or a drizzle of balsamic vinegar. 

The brightness cuts through the rich, caramelized sweetness and instantly makes the flavors pop.
