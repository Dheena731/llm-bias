**Changes to Recycling Collection: Maple Ridge Township Public Works**

From Monday 3 March, blue-bin collection moves to **every week** for households on Eastside routes E1 to E4. Westside routes W1 to W3 keep their fortnightly schedule.

**What's changing**

- **Glass goes in the blue bin.** Jars and bottles now go in with paper and cardboard. The green crates are no longer collected, so please take them to the Hollow Road depot or put them out only if your route still lists them.
- **Caps back on bottles.** Loose lids are too small for the sorting screens and end up as contamination. Leave them on, or put them in general waste.
- **No bags in the bin.** Shopping bags, bread bags and cling wrap still cause jams at the sorting plant. Last year, 14 loads were rejected for this reason.

Your collection day does not change. To check your route, visit mapleridge.gov/bins or call 555-0142.
