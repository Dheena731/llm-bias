**Weeknight Cooking Tip: Prep While You Preheat**

When you get home, turn on the oven or start heating a pan before you do anything else. Then use that warm-up time to chop vegetables, season your protein, or measure out sauce ingredients. The heat will be ready when you are, so you avoid standing around waiting and can get food cooking sooner.

**Example:** Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). While it warms, cut broccoli and sweet potatoes into bite-sized pieces, toss them with oil and salt, and spread them on a baking sheet. Season chicken thighs or a fish fillet, add them to the pan or a second pan, and roast everything together. Chicken thighs generally need about 25 to 35 minutes, so check that they are cooked through (165°F / 74°C at the thickest part) before serving. Fish usually cooks faster, so add it partway through.

**Why it helps:** Overlapping tasks shortens your total time and keeps the evening less hectic. A single sheet pan also means less cleanup.
