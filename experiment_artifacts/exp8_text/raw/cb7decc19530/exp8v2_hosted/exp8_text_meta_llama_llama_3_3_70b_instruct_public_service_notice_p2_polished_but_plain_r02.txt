A section of Oak Street will be closed to traffic starting on Monday and lasting for approximately two weeks. The closure is necessary to allow for repairs to the road surface and will affect the stretch of Oak Street between Maple Avenue and Pine Boulevard. During this time, drivers are advised to use alternative routes such as Elm Street or Cedar Drive to bypass the closure. Local residents and businesses will still have access to their properties, but may need to use a detour to reach their destinations. The road is expected to reopen on or before Friday of the following week, weather permitting.
