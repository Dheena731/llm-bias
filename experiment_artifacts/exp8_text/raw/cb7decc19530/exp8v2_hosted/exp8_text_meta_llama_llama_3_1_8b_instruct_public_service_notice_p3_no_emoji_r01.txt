**NOTICE OF RECYCLING COLLECTION CHANGES**

Beginning March 1st, the recycling collection schedule will be modified as follows:

* Tuesdays will now be designated for household hazardous waste collection
* Thursdays will be the new regular recycling collection day
* Residents are encouraged to place their recyclables at the curb no later than 7am on Thursday mornings

For more information, please contact our waste management department at 555-1234.
