This is an administrative notice to announce the start of our quarterly access review. All department managers and system owners are required to verify the permissions currently assigned to their personnel across company networks, applications, and databases. Please examine active accounts to ensure access rights strictly align with current job roles, and submit revocation requests for any personnel who have changed positions or left the organization. All reviews must be completed and signed off within two weeks of this notice. Unverified access privileges will be temporarily suspended after the deadline to safeguard system integrity. Contact the internal security team directly if you require assistance with the verification process.
