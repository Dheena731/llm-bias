Reminder: It's time to book your annual health checkup. Routine visits help catch concerns early, update your vaccinations, and give you a chance to discuss anything that has been on your mind, from sleep and stress to changes in energy or appetite.

When you call or go online to schedule, ask whether you should fast beforehand and whether any bloodwork will be done, since that can affect the time of day you choose. Check your insurance coverage so you know whether the visit is covered as preventive care. It also helps to gather your records ahead of time, including a list of current medications and supplements, any recent test results, and your family medical history.

In the days before your appointment, jot down questions or symptoms you want to raise so nothing is forgotten in the moment. If you are due for screenings such as blood pressure, cholesterol, diabetes, or age-appropriate cancer checks, mention them when you book so enough time is set aside.

Please make the call or open the scheduling page today, even if the appointment is a few weeks away. Getting it on the calendar is the hardest part, and future you will be glad it is done.
