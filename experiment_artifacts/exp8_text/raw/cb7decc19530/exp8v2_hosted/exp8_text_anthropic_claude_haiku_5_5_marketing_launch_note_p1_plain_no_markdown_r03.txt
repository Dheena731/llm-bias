Introducing Ledgerly, the simple way for small businesses to manage their daily money matters. Ledgerly brings invoicing, expense tracking, and cash flow forecasting together in one clean app, so owners can spend less time on paperwork and more time serving customers.

Creating and sending a professional invoice takes under a minute, and automatic reminders help you get paid faster without awkward follow-up calls. Receipts can be captured with your phone's camera and sorted into categories automatically, which makes tax season far less stressful. A simple dashboard shows exactly where your money is coming from and where it is going, so you can make confident decisions about hiring, inventory, and growth.

Ledgerly is available today on iOS and Android, with a free plan for businesses just getting started. Early adopters who sign up this month will receive three months of our premium features at no cost. We built Ledgerly alongside the owners who will use it every day, and we are excited to see what small businesses accomplish when their finances finally feel under control.
