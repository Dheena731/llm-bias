The following road will be closed for repairs.

The road will be closed to all traffic from midnight on Tuesday to 6 am on Wednesday. The closure will be in place between the intersection with Main Street and the intersection with Elm Street.

A detour will be in place to direct traffic around the closure. Drivers are advised to follow the detour signs to avoid the closed road.

The road closure is necessary to allow for the installation of new water and gas pipes. The work is expected to take several nights and the road will reopen as soon as the work is complete.
