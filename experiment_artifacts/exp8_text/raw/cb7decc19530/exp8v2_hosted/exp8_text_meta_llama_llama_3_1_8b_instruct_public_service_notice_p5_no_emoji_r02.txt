NOTICE OF POLLING STATION LOCATIONS

The following polling stations will be open on election day:

Station 1: 123 Main St, near the town hall
Station 2: 456 Elm St, in the community center
Station 3: 789 Oak St, in the local library
Station 4: 901 Maple St, in the school gymnasium

Polls will be open from 7:00 am to 8:00 pm. Voters must present a valid photo ID and proof of residency to cast their ballot. For more information, please contact the election office at 555-1234.
