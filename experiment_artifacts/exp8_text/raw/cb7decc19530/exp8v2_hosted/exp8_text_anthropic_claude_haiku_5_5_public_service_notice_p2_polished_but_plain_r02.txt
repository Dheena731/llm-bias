PUBLIC NOTICE: Temporary Closure of Maple Avenue Between Fifth and Ninth Streets

The City Department of Public Works will close Maple Avenue to all vehicle and pedestrian traffic between Fifth Street and Ninth Street beginning at 7:00 a.m. on Monday, March 16, and continuing through 5:00 p.m. on Friday, March 20, while crews replace a section of water main and repave the roadway. Work is weather permitting and may finish earlier than scheduled.

Local residents and business owners will retain access to their properties from either end of the closed segment, but through traffic will not be permitted. Drivers are asked to use Oak Avenue to the north or Cedar Boulevard to the south as alternate routes. Detour signs will be posted at each affected intersection, and the city asks drivers to follow them carefully and allow extra travel time during the closure.

Pedestrians will be directed along a marked walkway on the east side of Maple Avenue. Bus Route 14 will be temporarily rerouted, and riders should check the transit authority's posted stop changes before traveling. Emergency vehicles will have access to the work zone at all times.

Questions or concerns may be directed to the Department of Public Works at 555-0142 or by visiting the department's office at 200 City Hall Plaza during regular business hours. Residents may also call 311 for general city information.

Issued by the City Department of Public Works
