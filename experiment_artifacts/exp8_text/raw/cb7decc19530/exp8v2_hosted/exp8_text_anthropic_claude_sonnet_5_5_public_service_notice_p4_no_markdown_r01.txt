PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY

Issued by the City Emergency Management Office

A Heat Advisory is in effect from 11:00 a.m. Thursday until 9:00 p.m. Saturday. Daytime temperatures are expected to reach 98 to 104 degrees Fahrenheit, with heat index values up to 110 degrees. Overnight lows will remain in the upper 70s, offering little relief.

Who is most at risk: older adults, infants and young children, people with chronic health conditions, outdoor workers, and pets.

How to stay safe:

- Drink plenty of water, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and sugary drinks.
- Stay in air-conditioned spaces whenever possible.
- Limit outdoor activity to early morning or evening hours, and take frequent breaks in the shade.
- Wear lightweight, light-colored, loose-fitting clothing and use sunscreen.
- Never leave children, pets, or vulnerable adults in a parked vehicle, even for a short time.
- Check on neighbors, relatives, and friends who live alone.

Cooling centers: Free cooling centers will be open daily from 10:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. at the Main Public Library, the Eastside Community Center, and the Senior Services Building. Water will be provided. Pets are welcome at the Eastside location.

Signs of heat illness: heavy sweating, dizziness, headache, nausea, muscle cramps, or weakness. Move the person to a cool place, offer water, and apply cool cloths. If someone has a high body temperature, confusion, or loses consciousness, call 911 immediately.

For updates and transportation assistance to cooling centers, call the City Information Line at 555-0142 or visit the city website.
