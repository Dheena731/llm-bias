Notice: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop

If your company laptop is lost or stolen, please report it immediately. Prompt reporting is the best way to protect company data and limit any harm that may result.

Contact the IT Service Desk as soon as you realize the device is missing, even if you are not certain it is gone for good. You can call the service desk at any hour or send an email to the security team. Please be ready to share your name, the device's asset tag or serial number if you have it, the date, time, and place you last had the laptop, and a brief description of the circumstances. Also let us know whether the laptop held any sensitive files, such as customer records, financial information, or confidential project materials.

Once you report the loss, IT will lock the account, disable remote access, and attempt to wipe the device if it connects to a network. Your manager will also be informed. If the laptop was stolen, you should file a report with local police and give a copy of the report number to IT. If the loss occurred while traveling abroad, tell us that as well so we can respond appropriately.

Please do not wait until the next business day or try to search for the device on your own for an extended period before telling us. Delays can increase the risk of unauthorized access. Reporting a loss in good faith will not result in discipline, though repeated carelessness or failure to report may be reviewed.

To help prevent losses, keep your laptop with you or locked away when traveling, never leave it visible in a parked vehicle, and make sure your device remains encrypted and protected with a strong password. Thank you for helping keep our information secure.
