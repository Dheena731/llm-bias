Quick reminder: give your eyes and mind a break. Every 20 minutes or so, look away from your screen and focus on something at least 20 feet away for 20 seconds. Stand up, stretch your shoulders, roll your neck, and take a few deep breaths. Grab some water while you're at it. These small pauses help reduce eye strain, ease tension, and keep your focus sharp. Your screen will still be there when you get back!
