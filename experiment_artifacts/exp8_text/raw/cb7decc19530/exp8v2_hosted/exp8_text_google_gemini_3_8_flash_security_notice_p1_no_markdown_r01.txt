SECURITY NOTICE: AVOID PASSWORD REUSE

Reusing passwords across multiple accounts creates a significant security risk for our organization. If an external service you use is breached, cybercriminals can use those compromised credentials to access your work email, internal systems, and confidential data.

To protect yourself and our network, please follow these guidelines:

1. Always use unique passwords for every work and personal account.
2. Never use your company login credentials on external or personal websites.
3. Use the company-approved password manager to generate and securely store complex passwords.

If you suspect any of your accounts have been compromised, update your password immediately and notify IT Support.

Thank you for your commitment to keeping our workplace secure.

IT Security Team
