**Tip: Rinse your rice before cooking.**

Place the rice in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer and rinse it under cold water, swishing it with your hand, until the water runs mostly clear. This washes away the loose surface starch that makes grains clump together and turn gummy.

A few other things help:

- **Measure the water carefully.** Too much water makes rice mushy and sticky. A common starting point for long-grain white rice is about 1½ to 2 parts water to 1 part rice, but check your package, since brands and rice types differ.
- **Don't stir while it cooks.** Stirring releases more starch.
- **Let it rest.** After cooking, keep the lid on and let the rice sit off the heat for about 10 minutes, then fluff it gently with a fork.

Note that short-grain and "sticky" rice varieties are naturally stickier, so for fluffier results, choose long-grain rice such as basmati or jasmine.
