**Notice: What to Do If You Lose a Company Laptop**

**To all employees:**

If your company laptop is lost or stolen, report it immediately. Do not wait to see whether it turns up. A lost laptop may hold confidential company or customer information, and the sooner we know, the sooner we can act to protect it.

**What to do:**

1. **Tell your manager and the IT department right away.** Use whatever method is fastest, such as a phone call or in-person report.
2. **Give the key details:**
   - When and where you last had the laptop
   - How you think it was lost, or whether you think it was stolen
   - What kind of information was stored on it
   - Whether it was password-protected
3. **If you think it was stolen,** also report the theft to the police, and tell IT and your manager that you have done so.
4. **Change your work passwords** if IT advises you to.

**Please note:** Reporting promptly will not be held against you. Delays make it harder to protect company information.

Thank you for helping keep our data secure.
