**CALLOWAY POINT MARINA**
**Notice of Refund Eligibility: Seasonal Slip License (April 1 – October 31)**

**1. Scope.** This notice governs refunds of the Seasonal Dockage Fee only. It does not apply to haul-out, launch, or crane charges, metered shore power, pump-out cards, or the dock-key deposit, which are addressed in Schedule B.

**2. Surrender before April 1.** A Licensee who surrenders the slip by written notice received on or before March 1 is entitled to a refund of 90% of the Seasonal Dockage Fee. Notice received March 2 through March 31 entitles the Licensee to 50%. The retained portion is liquidated damages for the cost of re-letting the slip and is not a penalty.

**3. Surrender after April 1.** No refund is due for voluntary surrender after the season opens. The Marina will, however, credit the Licensee with 60% of any rent actually collected from a replacement licensee for the same slip, for the period of that replacement license.

**4. Sale or total loss of vessel.** If the licensed vessel is sold or declared a constructive total loss, the Licensee may request a pro-rata refund for full unexpired calendar months, less a $75 administrative charge. Proof of sale (bill of sale) or loss (insurer's declaration) must accompany the request. The license is not transferable to the buyer.

**5. Closure by the Marina.** If the Marina closes the Licensee's slip or basin for more than 14 consecutive days because of dredging, structural repair, or a storm closure ordered by the harbormaster, the Licensee will receive a pro-rata refund or credit for each day beyond the 14th.

**6. Ineligible circumstances.** No refund will be issued where the license is terminated for nonpayment, for breach of Section 9 (insurance and registration), or for a vessel's exceeding the slip's stated length overall.

**7. Procedure.** Requests must be made in writing to the Harbormaster, by mail or at harbormaster@callowaypoint.example. Refunds are issued within 30 days of an approved request, by the original method of payment.

*Effective January 1. Supersedes all prior refund notices.*
