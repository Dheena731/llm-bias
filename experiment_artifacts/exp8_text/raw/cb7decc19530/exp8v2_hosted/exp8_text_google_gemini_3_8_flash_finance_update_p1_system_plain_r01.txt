We are pleased to introduce our new monthly spending summary, designed to give you a clearer picture of your overall finances. Starting this month, you will receive an automated overview at the close of every billing cycle that breaks down your expenses by category, highlights key trends in your habits, and tracks your progress against your personal budget goals. The summary also lets you compare your current activity with previous months, making it easier to see where you are saving and where you might want to make adjustments. You can view your report anytime in your account dashboard, and a copy will be sent directly to your email on the first of each month. We hope this new tool provides valuable insights to help you make more confident financial decisions.
