To keep fresh herbs lasting longer, trim the stems and place them upright in a glass with an inch or two of water, much like a bouquet of flowers. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and store the glass in the refrigerator, changing the water every couple of days. Hardy herbs such as rosemary, thyme, and sage can also be wrapped in a damp paper towel and sealed in a container, which helps them stay crisp for a week or more.
