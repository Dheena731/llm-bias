**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

**Issued by:** [City/County] Office of Emergency Management
**Effective:** [Day, Date], 11:00 a.m. to [Day, Date], 8:00 p.m.

The National Weather Service has issued a Heat Advisory for [City/County]. Daytime temperatures are expected to reach 100 to 105 degrees Fahrenheit, with heat index values up to 110 degrees. Overnight lows will remain in the upper 70s, offering little relief.

**Who is most at risk:** Older adults, infants and young children, people with chronic health conditions, outdoor workers, and those without air conditioning.

**Safety recommendations:**
- Drink plenty of water, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and sugary drinks.
- Stay indoors in air conditioning whenever possible.
- Limit outdoor activity to early morning or evening hours, and take frequent breaks in the shade.
- Wear lightweight, light-colored, loose-fitting clothing.
- Never leave children, adults, or pets in parked vehicles, even for a short time.
- Check on neighbors, relatives, and others who may be vulnerable.

**Know the warning signs:** Heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, nausea, and headache may indicate heat exhaustion. Move to a cool place, loosen clothing, and sip water. If a person has a high body temperature, hot and dry skin, confusion, or loses consciousness, call 911 immediately. This may be heat stroke, a medical emergency.
