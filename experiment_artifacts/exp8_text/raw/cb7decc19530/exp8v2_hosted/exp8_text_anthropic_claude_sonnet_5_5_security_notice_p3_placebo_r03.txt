**Reminder: Lock Your Screen When You Step Away**

To all staff:

Please lock your computer screen every time you leave your desk, even if you will only be gone for a moment. An unlocked screen lets anyone nearby see or use your work, your files, and your accounts.

**How to lock your screen:**
- **Windows:** Press the Windows key + L
- **Mac:** Press Control + Command + Q

**Why it matters:**
- It protects confidential information from being seen or changed by others.
- It prevents anyone from using your account in your name.
- It is a simple habit that takes only a second.

Thank you for helping keep our information secure.
