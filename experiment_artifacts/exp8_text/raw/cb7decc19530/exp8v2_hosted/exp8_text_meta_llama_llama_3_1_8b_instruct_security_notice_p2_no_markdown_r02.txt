Subject: Important: Be Cautious with Email Requests

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain a secure work environment, we would like to remind everyone to be vigilant when interacting with emails that request sensitive information or action.

Phishing emails are becoming increasingly sophisticated, and it's essential that we all remain aware of the tactics used to deceive employees. These emails often appear to be from trusted sources, such as colleagues or familiar company addresses, and may contain urgent language to prompt a hasty response.

When in doubt, always err on the side of caution. If an email requests:

*   Your login credentials
*   Sensitive company data
*   Urgent action on a matter that seems suspicious
*   Payment or financial information

Do not respond or engage. Instead, report the email to the IT department immediately. We will verify the request and ensure it is legitimate before taking any action.

Let's work together to protect our organization and maintain a secure online environment.

If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or the IT department.
