ROAD CLOSURE NOTICE

The following road will be closed to all traffic from Monday, 3rd October to Friday, 7th October:

Main Street, between Park Road and Oak Avenue.

This closure is necessary for essential maintenance works to the road infrastructure. A signed diversion will be in place to direct traffic around the closed section of road.

Motorists are advised to plan their route in advance and to follow the diversion signs to avoid any delays.

We apologise for any inconvenience this may cause and thank you for your understanding.

If you have any queries or concerns, please contact the Council's Maintenance Team on 01234 567890.
