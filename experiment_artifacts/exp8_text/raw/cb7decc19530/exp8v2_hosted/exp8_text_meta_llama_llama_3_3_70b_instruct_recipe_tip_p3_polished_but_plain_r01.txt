To make rice less sticky, it's essential to rinse it thoroughly before cooking. Rinsing removes excess starch from the surface of the grains, which is the primary cause of stickiness. Simply place the rice in a fine mesh strainer and rinse it under cold running water, stirring occasionally, until the water runs clear. After rinsing, cook the rice with the right amount of water, using a general ratio of one cup of rice to one and a quarter cups of water. Also, avoid overmixing the rice while it's cooking, as this can release more starch and make it stickier. By following these simple steps, you can achieve perfectly cooked, fluffy rice that's not sticky.
