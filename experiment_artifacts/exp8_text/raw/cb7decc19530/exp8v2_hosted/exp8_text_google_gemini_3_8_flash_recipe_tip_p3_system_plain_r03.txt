To make your rice less sticky, thoroughly rinse the dry grains in cold water several times until the liquid runs clear, which washes away the excess surface starch that causes clumping. Measure your water carefully to avoid overcooking the grains, and resist the urge to stir the rice while it is on the stove, as agitation releases more starch. Once the cooking time is complete, remove the pot from the heat and let it rest covered for ten minutes before gently fluffing the rice with a fork.
