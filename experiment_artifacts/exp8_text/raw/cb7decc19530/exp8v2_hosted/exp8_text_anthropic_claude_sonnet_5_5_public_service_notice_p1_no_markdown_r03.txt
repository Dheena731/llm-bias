PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE: UPDATED LIBRARY OPENING HOURS

The Central Community Library is pleased to announce its opening hours for the coming season, effective Monday, June 2.

Monday to Thursday: 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
Friday: 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
Saturday: 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
Sunday: Closed

Please note the following:

The library will be closed on all public holidays. Book drops remain available 24 hours a day for returning items. Computer and Wi-Fi services end 30 minutes before closing time. Children's story hour takes place every Saturday at 11:00 a.m.

Library cards may be renewed at the front desk during any opening hours. Please bring a valid photo ID.

For questions or to confirm holiday closures, call the front desk at 555-0142 or visit the library's website.

Thank you for supporting your local library.
