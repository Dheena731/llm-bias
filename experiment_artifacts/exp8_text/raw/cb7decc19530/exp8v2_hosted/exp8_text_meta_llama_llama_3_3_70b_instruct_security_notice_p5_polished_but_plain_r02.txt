If you have lost a company laptop, please report the incident to the IT department as soon as possible. When making the report, please provide the laptop's serial number, the date and time it was last seen, and the location where it was last used. This information will help the IT department to take necessary steps to secure the device and protect company data. You should also inform your supervisor or manager about the lost laptop so that they can take appropriate action. Reporting a lost laptop promptly will help to minimize the risk of data breach and ensure that the company's assets are protected.
