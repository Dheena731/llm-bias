Join us this Saturday for the Riverside Community Fair, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. at Maple Park! Come enjoy live music, local food vendors, kids' games, and a neighborhood art showcase. Bring a blanket or lawn chair and plan to stay a while. Admission is free, and all proceeds from food sales will support our community garden project. Invite your neighbors, bring your friends, and help us make it a great day together. We can't wait to see you there!
