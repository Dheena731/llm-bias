Before the week begins, choose one or two base ingredients and cook them in bulk, such as a pot of grains, a tray of roasted vegetables, or a batch of shredded chicken. Then vary the lunches by changing the flavor around those basics. The same rice and chicken can become a grain bowl with a lemony dressing on Monday, a wrap with hummus on Tuesday, and a quick stir-fry with a spoonful of soy sauce and ginger on Wednesday. This approach keeps shopping and cooking to a single session, yet it spares you from eating the identical meal five days in a row. Keep a few sauces, pickles, or crunchy toppings on hand, since they do most of the work of making leftovers taste new. Finally, pack lunches the night before, when you still have energy, so that your busiest mornings require nothing more than grabbing a bag and heading out the door.
