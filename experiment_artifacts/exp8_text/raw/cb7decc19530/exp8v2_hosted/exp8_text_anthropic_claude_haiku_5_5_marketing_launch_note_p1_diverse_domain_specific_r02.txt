**Hemline is out. Here's what it does.**

If you run an alterations shop, you already know the problem: a pair of trousers comes in Tuesday with a note that says "take in 1 inch, need by Friday for a wedding," and by Thursday it's a pile of tags on the back counter. Hemline keeps track of each garment from the moment it's tagged to the moment the customer pays.

Scan the tag, record the work (hem, take in, replace zipper), set the pickup date, and take the deposit. The app texts the customer when the garment is ready and flags anything that's been sitting past its date. At the end of the week you see what's owed, what's done, and what's been waiting on a customer who never came back.

Hemline works offline in the shop, so a dead Wi-Fi router won't stop the counter. It's built for one- and two-person shops, so there's no setup consultant and no monthly minimum for the first 60 days.

It's available now on iPhone and Android. If you've been keeping tickets on a clipboard, try it for one week. Bring a garment in, and tell us what we missed.
