**PUBLIC NOTICE: Polling Station Locations for the Upcoming Election**

**Election Day:** Tuesday, November 5, 2024  
**Voting Hours:** 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM  

Registered voters are reminded that you must cast your ballot at your **assigned polling station** based on your residential address. 

**How to Find Your Polling Station:**
* **Online:** Visit **[www.electionswebsite.gov/locate]** and enter your residential address.
* **Voter Card:** Check the official polling card recently sent to your address by mail.
* **By Phone:** Call the Election Office Helpdesk at **1-800-555-VOTE**.

**What to Bring:**
Please remember to bring a valid government-issued photo ID and, if available, your voter registration card to ensure a smooth check-in process.

*All polling stations are fully accessible to voters with disabilities.*

**Issued by:**  
The Board of Elections
