The public library wishes to advise residents of its current operating hours. The facility is open Monday through Friday from nine o'clock in the morning until eight o'clock in the evening. On Saturday, the library operates from ten o'clock in the morning until five o'clock in the afternoon. The library is closed on Sundays and all recognized public holidays. Patrons are encouraged to review this schedule when planning their visits. Additional information regarding holiday observances or maintenance closures can be obtained by contacting the front desk during business hours. We appreciate your patronage and look forward to serving the community.
