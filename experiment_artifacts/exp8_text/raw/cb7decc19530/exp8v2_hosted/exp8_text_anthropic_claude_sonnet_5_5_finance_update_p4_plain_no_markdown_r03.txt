Subject: Your End-of-Year Tax Statement

Hello,

Your end-of-year tax statement is a summary of the income and tax-related activity recorded for you during the past year. Its purpose is to give you the figures you need to complete your tax return accurately, and it is also what tax authorities use to check the information you report.

The statement usually begins with your personal details and tax identification number, so please check that your name, address, and number are correct. Next, it lists the income you received, such as wages, interest, dividends, or other payments. Alongside each type of income, you will see the amount of tax that was already withheld or paid on your behalf. These withheld amounts are important because they are credited against what you owe when you file.

Some statements also show deductions, credits, or contributions, such as retirement savings or charitable donations. These can reduce your taxable income or your final bill, so keep the statement with your other records and compare it against your own receipts.

If you notice anything that looks wrong or missing, contact the issuer of the statement as soon as possible so they can correct it before you file. Filing with inaccurate figures can lead to delays, penalties, or follow-up questions later.

Please keep a copy for at least several years, since you may need it if your return is ever reviewed. If you are unsure how to use any part of it, a qualified tax professional can walk you through it.
