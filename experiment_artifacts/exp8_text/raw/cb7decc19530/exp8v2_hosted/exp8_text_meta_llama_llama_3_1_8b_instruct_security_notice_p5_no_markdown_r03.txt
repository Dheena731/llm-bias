Lost Company Laptop

If you have misplaced or lost a company laptop, please report it to the IT department immediately. This will enable us to take the necessary steps to secure the device and protect our company's sensitive information.

To report a lost laptop, please contact the IT department at extension 555 or email them at itdept@company.com. Provide the laptop's serial number, your name, and a brief description of the incident.

Please note that any unreported lost or stolen laptops may be considered a security risk and may result in disciplinary action. Your prompt reporting will help us minimize the risk and protect our company's assets.
