To save time and minimize cleanup on busy weeknights, cook your entire meal on a single baking sheet. Toss chopped vegetables and your choice of protein—such as bite-sized chicken breast, shrimp, or tofu—in cooking oil, salt, and spices. Spread them in an even layer on the pan and roast in the oven at 400°F (200°C) until cooked through. This allows your dinner to cook with minimal supervision and leaves you with only one pan to wash.
