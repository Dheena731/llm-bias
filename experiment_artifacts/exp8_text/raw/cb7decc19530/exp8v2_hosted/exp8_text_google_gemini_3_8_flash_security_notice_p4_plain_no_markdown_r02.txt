To enhance the security of your account, we strongly recommend enabling two-factor authentication. This feature provides an extra layer of defense by requiring a verification code alongside your password when you sign in from an unrecognized device. You can turn this on at any time by navigating to your account settings and selecting the security tab. Taking this quick step helps prevent unauthorized access and ensures your personal information remains safe. If you have any questions or need help setting up your authentication method, please reach out to our support team.
