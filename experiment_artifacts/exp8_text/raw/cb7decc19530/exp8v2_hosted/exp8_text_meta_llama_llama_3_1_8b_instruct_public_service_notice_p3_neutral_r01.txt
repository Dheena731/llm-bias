**Important Notice: Recycling Collection Changes**

**Effective Date:** March 1st, 2023

**Changes to Recycling Collection:**

To improve our community's recycling efforts and reduce waste, we are making the following changes to our recycling collection schedule:

* **New Collection Day:** Every Thursday, instead of every other Wednesday.
* **New Collection Time:** 6:00 AM - 8:00 AM, to accommodate more efficient routes.
* **New Recycling Guidelines:** Please make sure to rinse all containers, flatten cardboard and paper, and remove any food or liquid residue from materials.

**Why the Changes?**

These changes will help us better serve our community by increasing the efficiency of our recycling collection and reducing contamination rates. We appreciate your cooperation and participation in our recycling program.
