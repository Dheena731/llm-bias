**PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE**

**Riverside Public Library: Hours of Operation**

The Riverside Public Library is pleased to announce its regular opening hours for the community:

- **Monday – Thursday:** 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
- **Friday – Saturday:** 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- **Sunday:** 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m.

The library will be closed on major holidays, including New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. Please check our website or call (555) 123-4567 for holiday schedule changes and special event closures.

Library cards are free for all residents. Visit any branch or our main location at 100 Main Street to sign up.

We look forward to serving you!
