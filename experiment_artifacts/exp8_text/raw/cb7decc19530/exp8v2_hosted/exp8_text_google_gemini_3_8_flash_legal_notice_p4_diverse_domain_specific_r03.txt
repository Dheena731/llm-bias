**ADDENDUM B: REMEDY LIMITATIONS AND COMPUTE RESERVATION REFUND ELIGIBILITY**

**1. General Rule of Finality.** Except as explicitly provided under Section 2 of this Addendum, all fees paid for Dedicated Bare-Metal Clusters, Scheduled GPU Allocations, and Custom Model Pipeline Configurations are strictly non-refundable. Capacity is irrevocably committed at the hardware level upon issuance of the cluster provisioning manifest; the Customer’s non-utilization, suboptimal kernel scheduling, convergence failure, or early job termination shall not abate or remit accrued fees.

**2. Conditional Refund Eligibility.** The Customer shall be entitled to an adjustment exclusively in the form of a prorated Service Credit—and not a cash or tender remittance—if and only if:
* (a) The reserved instances remain unreachable due to an unannounced Control Plane or Host Node failure exceeding the SLA degradation threshold defined in Schedule 1;
* (b) The Customer files an infrastructure defect ticket with accompanying host-telemetry logs within forty-eight (48) hours of the initial node failure; and
* (c) Upstream diagnostics verify that the disruption originated entirely outside the Customer-space OS environment, containers, or user-compiled workloads.

**3. Express Exclusions.** Under no circumstances will refund requests or credit adjustments be entertained for:
* (a) Compute cycles consumed prior to the submission of a verifiable defect report;
* (b) Network egress or high-bandwidth interconnect (NVLink/InfiniBand) overhead generated by runaway loops or memory leaks in Customer containers; or
* (c) Allocations cancelled fewer than fourteen (14) days prior to the reserved provisioning window.

All determinations of hardware non-conformance remain within the sole technical discretion of the Provider’s Infrastructure Operations team, based on non-volatile machine telemetry.
