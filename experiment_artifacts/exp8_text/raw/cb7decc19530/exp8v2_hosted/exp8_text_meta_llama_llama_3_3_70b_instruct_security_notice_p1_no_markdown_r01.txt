To: All Staff

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain a secure work environment, we would like to remind everyone of the importance of password security. One common mistake that can put our systems and data at risk is password reuse. Using the same password for multiple accounts, including personal and work-related accounts, can make it easier for unauthorized individuals to gain access to our systems if one of those accounts is compromised.

We strongly advise against using the same password for your work account and any other personal or online accounts. Instead, use unique and complex passwords for each account, and consider using a password manager to help keep track of them.

Additionally, please remember to change your work password regularly and avoid using easily guessable information such as your name, birthdate, or common words. If you have any concerns or questions about password security, please don't hesitate to reach out to the IT department.

Thank you for your cooperation and attention to this important matter.
