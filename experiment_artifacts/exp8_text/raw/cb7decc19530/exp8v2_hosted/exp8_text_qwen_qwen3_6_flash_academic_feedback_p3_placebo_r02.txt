The manuscript would benefit from a clearer articulation of its central research question. As currently presented, the scope and primary objective are difficult to identify, which complicates the evaluation of whether the methodology and analyses align with the intended aims. I recommend revising the introduction to state a single, focused, and testable question that explicitly defines the target population, key variables, and the specific relationship or gap being examined. Strengthening this foundation will improve the logical coherence, feasibility, and overall impact of the study.
