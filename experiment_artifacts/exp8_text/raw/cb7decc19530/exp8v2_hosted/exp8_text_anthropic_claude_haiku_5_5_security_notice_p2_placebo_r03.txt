**Notice to All Staff: Watch Out for Phishing Emails**

Phishing is a scam where someone sends a fake email pretending to be a trusted person or organization, such as a colleague, a bank, or a service we use. The goal is to get you to share passwords, send money, or open a harmful file or link.

Please take these steps:

- **Pause before you click.** Be cautious with any email that asks you to log in, confirm account details, make a payment, or act urgently.
- **Check the sender.** Look closely at the email address, not just the display name. Be wary of small misspellings or unexpected domains.
- **Don't open unexpected attachments or links.** If you weren't expecting a file or link, confirm with the sender through a separate method, such as a phone call or a new message to a known address.
- **Never share your password.** No legitimate request will require you to type your password into an email or a web page sent to you.
- **Report suspicious emails.** If you think an email may be phishing, do not reply or click anything. Report it to the IT or security team right away.

If you have already clicked a link or entered information you think may be unsafe, report it immediately so the team can respond quickly.

Thank you for helping keep our systems and information secure.
