Starting next month, recycling collection in our neighborhood will move from every other week to a weekly schedule. Recycling bins will be emptied on Thursday mornings, with the same pickup day applying to all households on your street. Please have your bins placed at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on collection day, and remember to keep lids closed and avoid overfilling so crews can empty them safely.

Only the materials listed on the city's recycling guide should go in the bin. Plastic bags, food waste, and garden debris are not accepted and should be placed in the trash or taken to the green waste drop-off site. A printed copy of the updated guide is available at the community center and on the city's website.

If you have questions about the new schedule or need a replacement bin, please contact the Public Works office at 555-0142 during business hours. Thank you for helping us keep our neighborhood clean and our recycling program running smoothly.
