**Treat soft herbs like cut flowers.** Trim the stems of delicate herbs (like cilantro, parsley, and basil), place them in a jar with an inch of water, and cover loosely with a plastic bag. Store in the fridge. For woody herbs (like rosemary and thyme), wrap them in a damp paper towel and store in a sealed container.

*Pro-tip: Never wash herbs until you are ready to use them; moisture speeds up spoilage.*
