**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY**

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for the area from 11:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. today. Temperatures are expected to reach the high 90s to near 105 degrees Fahrenheit, with heat index values making it feel even hotter.

**Precautions to take:**

- Stay indoors in air-conditioned spaces whenever possible. If you do not have air conditioning, visit a library, community center, or cooling center.
- Drink water regularly, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and sugary drinks.
- Wear lightweight, loose-fitting, light-colored clothing, and use a hat or sunscreen when outdoors.
- Limit outdoor activities to the cooler morning and evening hours.
- Never leave children, pets, or anyone who relies on you inside a parked vehicle.
- Check on elderly neighbors, people with chronic illnesses, and anyone without reliable cooling.

**Warning signs of heat illness:** Heavy sweating, muscle cramps, dizziness, nausea, headache, or confusion. If you or someone nearby shows these signs, move to a cool place, drink water if alert, and seek medical help. Call 911 immediately for fainting, confusion, or a body temperature above 103 degrees Fahrenheit.

For updates, monitor local news and the National Weather Service website or your local forecast office.
