We have introduced a new monthly spending summary to help you keep track of your expenses. This summary will provide a clear breakdown of your spending over the course of the month, including the total amount spent and the categories where you spent the most. The summary will be automatically generated and sent to you at the beginning of each month, giving you a chance to review your spending habits and make any necessary adjustments. This new feature is designed to make it easier for you to manage your finances and stay on top of your spending.
