To keep rice from turning sticky, rinse it in a bowl of cold water before cooking, swirling it with your fingers and pouring off the cloudy water several times until it runs mostly clear. This washes away excess surface starch that makes grains cling together. Then use slightly less water than the package suggests, cook the rice covered over low heat, and let it rest with the lid on for about ten minutes after the heat is off so the grains firm up. Finally, fluff it gently with a fork rather than stirring, which can crush the grains and release more starch.
