Password reuse poses a serious risk to our organization. When you use the same password across multiple services, a security breach on a single external website can expose your company account. Cybercriminals frequently test leaked credentials from outside breaches against corporate systems to gain unauthorized access. To protect both company data and your personal information, you must create a unique, strong password for every account you use. Never use your personal passwords for workplace systems. If you have trouble managing multiple complex passwords, use the approved company password manager to store them securely. If you suspect that any of your passwords have been compromised, change them immediately and inform the information technology support team.
