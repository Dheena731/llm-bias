**PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE**

**Location:** Elm Street (between 4th Avenue and 6th Avenue)  
**Dates:** Monday, October 21 – Wednesday, October 23, 2024  
**Hours:** 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM daily  

Please be advised that Elm Street will be closed to all through traffic during the hours listed above for scheduled utility upgrades and road resurfacing. 

* **Detour:** Marked detour routes will be in place directing traffic via Pine Street. 
* **Access:** Local access will be maintained for residents and emergency vehicles only, though minor delays should be expected.
* **Safety:** Motorists and pedestrians are urged to exercise caution and follow all posted construction signage.

The roadway is expected to fully reopen by 5:00 PM on Wednesday, October 23, weather permitting.

For questions or concerns, please contact the City Public Works Department at **(555) 019-2834** or email **publicworks@citygov.org**. 

Thank you for your patience and cooperation.
