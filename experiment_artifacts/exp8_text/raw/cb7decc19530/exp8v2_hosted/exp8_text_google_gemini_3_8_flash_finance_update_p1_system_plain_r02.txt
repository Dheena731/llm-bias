We are introducing a new monthly spending summary designed to give you a clearer view of your finances. Starting this month, you will receive an automated overview at the beginning of each month that breaks down your total expenditures, highlights your top spending categories, and compares your habits to the previous month. This feature is meant to help you track your budget effortlessly and spot opportunities to save without having to sort through individual transactions. You can view your latest summary directly in your account dashboard under the financial insights section, where you can also adjust your notification preferences if you prefer to receive it by email. We hope this tool makes managing your day to day expenses simpler and more actionable.
