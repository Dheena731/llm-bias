**Subject: Two-factor sign-in required for firm email, starting March 17**

Starting **Monday, March 17**, you'll need a six-digit code in addition to your password when you sign in to firm email from a new device or browser. Accounts not enrolled by then will be locked until they are. Nothing is lost, but you'll need to call us to get back in.

**Set it up (about five minutes)**

1. Install an authenticator app on your phone. Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator, and 1Password all work.
2. On your computer, go to **account.harlowpike.example/security** and choose **Add sign-in method**.
3. Scan the QR code with the app, then type in the code it shows to confirm.
4. Download your **recovery codes** and print them. Keep the printout somewhere other than your inbox, since you'd need the inbox to find them.

**Please avoid text-message codes** unless you don't have a smartphone. Phone numbers can be hijacked through the carrier, and we've seen it happen to clients.

**Before you replace or reset a phone**, move the authenticator account to the new one first. Both apps above have a transfer option. If you wipe the old phone first, you'll need a recovery code.

**Lost your phone or locked out?** Call the help desk at ext. 214. We'll call you back on the number we have on file before resetting anything. We will never ask for a code over email or chat, and neither should anyone claiming to be from IT.

Questions or trouble with setup? Stop by the IT desk on Thursdays between 10 and 12, or write to helpdesk@harlowpike.example.
