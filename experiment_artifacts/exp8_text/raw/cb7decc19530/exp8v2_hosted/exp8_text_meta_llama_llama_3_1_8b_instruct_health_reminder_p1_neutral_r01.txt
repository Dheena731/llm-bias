Here's a short reminder:

**Don't Forget to Track Your Progress!**

Take a moment each week to review your fitness progress. Ask yourself:

* Have I met my weekly exercise goals?
* Have I made any progress towards my fitness goals?
* Are there any areas where I need to make adjustments or improvements?

Checking in regularly will help you stay motivated, identify areas for improvement, and make adjustments to achieve your fitness goals. Take 5 minutes each week to review your progress and celebrate your successes!
