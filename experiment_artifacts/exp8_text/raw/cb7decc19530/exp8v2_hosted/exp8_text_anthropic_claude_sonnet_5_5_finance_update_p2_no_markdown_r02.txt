Subject: Update to Card Transaction Fees

Dear Customer,

We are writing to let you know about an upcoming change to the fees that apply to card transactions on your account.

What is changing
Starting on the first day of next month, the fee for card payments made in a foreign currency will increase from 2.5 percent to 2.9 percent of the transaction amount. Fees for domestic card purchases will remain free of charge, and ATM withdrawals within our network will not change.

Why we are making this change
The cost of processing international payments has risen, largely because of higher charges from card networks and partner banks. We have absorbed these increases for as long as we reasonably could, but we now need to adjust our fees to keep offering a reliable, secure service.

What this means for you
If you use your card mostly for local purchases, you will not notice any difference. If you often shop online with overseas merchants or travel abroad, you may see slightly higher fees. For example, a purchase of 100 dollars in a foreign currency will now carry a fee of 2.90 dollars instead of 2.50 dollars.

What you can do
You do not need to take any action. If you would like to review your options, including whether a different account type might better suit your spending habits, please visit your local branch or contact our support team.

You can find the full fee schedule in the account terms section of our website or by calling customer service at the number on the back of your card.

Thank you for banking with us. We appreciate your understanding.

Sincerely,
Customer Support Team
