**NOTICE: Beware of Phishing Emails**

**To:** All Employees
**From:** IT Security Team
**Subject:** Recent Increase in Phishing Attempts

We have seen a rise in phishing emails targeting our organization. These messages are designed to trick recipients into sharing passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information, or into installing harmful software.

**Warning signs to watch for:**

- Unexpected requests for passwords, payments, or personal information
- Urgent or threatening language, such as "Act now or your account will be closed"
- Sender addresses that look slightly off or do not match the company name
- Suspicious links or unexpected attachments
- Generic greetings, spelling errors, or unusual formatting

**What you should do:**

1. Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious messages.
2. Do not reply to the sender or forward the email to colleagues.
3. Report the email using the "Report Phishing" button in your email client, or send it to security@yourcompany.com.
4. If you have already clicked a link or shared information, contact IT immediately at ext. 5555 so we can secure your account.

When in doubt, verify the request by contacting the sender through a known phone number or official channel. IT will never ask for your password by email.

Thank you for helping keep our company safe.

**IT Security Team**
