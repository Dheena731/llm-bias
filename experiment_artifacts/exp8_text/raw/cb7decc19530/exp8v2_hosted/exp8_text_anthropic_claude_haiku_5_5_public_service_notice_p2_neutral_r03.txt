**PUBLIC NOTICE: Road Closure**

**Location:** Main Street, between Oak Avenue and Pine Street

**Dates:** Monday, March 10 through Friday, March 14, 2025

**Hours:** 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily

**Reason:** Scheduled water main replacement and road resurfacing

Main Street will be fully closed to all vehicle traffic during the dates and hours listed above. Pedestrian access to adjacent businesses will be maintained where safe.

**Detours:** Please follow posted signs and use Elm Street or Maple Avenue as alternate routes.

**Emergency Access:** Emergency vehicles will have access to the work zone at all times.

We apologize for any inconvenience and appreciate your patience as we work to improve local infrastructure. For questions, contact the City Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567.
