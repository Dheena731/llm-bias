A new expense approval workflow has been implemented to improve the efficiency and transparency of our expense reporting process. This new workflow will require all employees to submit their expenses through a centralized online system, where they will be reviewed and approved by the relevant department managers. The system will automatically notify the employee and the finance team once an expense has been approved or rejected, and will also provide a clear audit trail of all expenses and approvals. Employees are expected to familiarize themselves with the new system and begin using it for all expense submissions going forward. If you have any questions or concerns about the new workflow, please do not hesitate to reach out to the finance team for assistance.
