**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY**

The National Weather Service has issued a **Heat Advisory** for [City/County/Region] from [start time] on [start date] through [end time] on [end date]. Temperatures are expected to reach [temperature range]°F, with heat index values up to [heat index value]°F.

**Who is at greatest risk?**
Older adults, young children, people with chronic illnesses, outdoor workers, and anyone without access to air conditioning.

**Safety tips:**
- Stay in air-conditioned spaces as much as possible. If your home lacks AC, visit a library, shopping center, or designated cooling center.
- Drink water regularly, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol, caffeine, and heavy meals.
- Limit outdoor activity to early morning or evening, and take frequent breaks in shade if you must be outside.
- Never leave children, pets, or anyone who depends on you inside a parked vehicle.
- Check on elderly neighbors, friends, and relatives at least twice a day.
- Wear lightweight, light-colored, loose-fitting clothing and a wide-brimmed hat.

**Recognize the warning signs:**
Heat exhaustion symptoms include heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, nausea, and headache. Move to a cool place, sip water, and loosen clothing. **Heat stroke is a medical emergency.** Call 911 if someone has a high body temperature, confusion, slurred speech, fainting, or stops sweating.
