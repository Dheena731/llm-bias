**Security Notice: Staying Alert to Phishing Emails**

Phishing emails are fraudulent messages designed to trick recipients into revealing sensitive company data, sharing login credentials, or downloading harmful software. 

Please stay vigilant and watch for common warning signs:
* Urgent or threatening language demanding immediate action.
* Unfamiliar sender addresses, especially those attempting to imitate known contacts or organizations.
* Unexpected requests for passwords, personal information, or financial transactions.
* Unsolicited attachments or links.

If you receive a suspicious message, do not click any links, open attachments, or reply to the sender. Forward the email directly to the IT department for verification and delete it from your inbox.
