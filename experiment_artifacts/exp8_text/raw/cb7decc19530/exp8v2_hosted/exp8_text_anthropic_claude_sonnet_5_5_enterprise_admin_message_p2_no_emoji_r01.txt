**Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow, Effective Monday**

Hello team,

Starting Monday, we are moving to a new expense approval workflow to speed up reimbursements and improve tracking.

**What is changing**
- All expense reports must be submitted through the finance portal. Email and paper submissions will no longer be accepted.
- Receipts must be attached to each line item at the time of submission.
- Reports go first to your direct manager, then to Finance for final review.

**Approval thresholds**
- Under $500: manager approval only
- $500 to $2,500: manager and department head approval
- Over $2,500: manager, department head, and Finance Director approval

**Timelines**
- Submit expenses within 30 days of purchase.
- Approvers should respond within three business days.
- Approved reports are reimbursed in the next bi-weekly payroll cycle.

**Next steps**
A short training guide is available on the intranet under Finance > Expense Resources. Please review it before submitting your next report. If you have questions, contact the Finance team at finance@yourcompany.com.

Thank you for your cooperation as we make this transition.

Best regards,
Administration Team
