📅 Calendar tip: Stop using your calendar only for meetings. Block out time for your own tasks too!

Next time you add a to-do to your list, give it a slot on your calendar instead. "Write report, 2:00 to 3:00 PM" is much more likely to get done than "Write report" sitting on a list somewhere.

Bonus: color-code your blocks (work, personal, focus time) so you can see at a glance if your week is balanced.
