**Reminder: Take Short Breaks from Screens**

Take a few minutes away from your screen every so often. Stand up, stretch, look out a window, or walk around the room. Giving your eyes and body a rest can help you feel more comfortable and focused when you return to your work.

A simple habit to try: every 20 minutes or so, look at something far away for about 20 seconds. Then, at least once an hour, stand up and move for a few minutes.

Your screen will still be there when you get back.
