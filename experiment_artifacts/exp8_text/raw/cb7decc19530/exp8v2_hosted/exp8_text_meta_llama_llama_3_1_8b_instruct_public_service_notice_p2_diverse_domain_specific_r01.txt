**NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE**

**Morgan Creek Bridge Reconstruction**

Effective immediately, the Morgan Creek Bridge on Highway 12 will be closed to all traffic for the next 6 months. This closure is necessary to accommodate the reconstruction of the bridge's northern abutment, which will be replaced with a new, reinforced structure.

**Detour Route:**

During the closure, traffic will be rerouted via the alternate route of Lower River Road (County Route 37) and Oakdale Avenue (County Route 42). Please follow the posted detour signs and expect minor delays.

**Impact on Local Businesses:**

The closure is expected to have a minimal impact on local businesses, as the detour route passes through the town center. However, drivers are advised to plan their route accordingly and allow for additional travel time.

**Contact Information:**

For updates on the road closure and reconstruction, please contact the Road Operations Division at (555) 123-4567 or visit our website at [www.countyroads.com](http://www.countyroads.com).

We appreciate your cooperation and understanding during this project.
