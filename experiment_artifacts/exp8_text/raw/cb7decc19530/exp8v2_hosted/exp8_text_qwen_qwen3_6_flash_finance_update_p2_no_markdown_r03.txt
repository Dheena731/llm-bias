Subject: Notice of Card Transaction Fee Adjustment

Dear Merchant,

We are writing to notify you of an upcoming change to our card transaction fees. After careful review of current processing costs and network rate adjustments, we will be updating our fee structure to ensure we can continue providing reliable and secure payment services.

Beginning November 1, 2023, the transaction fee will increase from 2.5% plus $0.10 to 2.7% plus $0.10 per transaction. This change reflects higher interchange costs passed down by the card networks.

Your account statement will reflect these updated rates starting with the first settlement after the effective date. If you have questions regarding this change or wish to review your projected impact based on your sales volume, please contact your account representative or email us at support@paymentservice.com.

Thank you for your business and partnership.

Sincerely,

Billing Department
Payment Service Provider
