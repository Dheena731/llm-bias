**Important Notice: Polling Station Locations**

To ensure a smooth and efficient voting process, please note the following locations for the upcoming elections:

**Polling Station Locations:**

*   Ward 1: Oak Street Community Center, 123 Main St.
*   Ward 2: Oakwood Elementary School, 456 Elm St.
*   Ward 3: City Hall, 789 Park Ave.
*   Ward 4: Community Recreation Center, 901 Broadway

**Polling Station Hours:**

*   7:00 AM - 8:00 PM on Election Day

**Required Identification:**

*   Valid photo ID and proof of residency

**Accessibility:**

*   All polling stations are wheelchair accessible and equipped with voting machines for voters with disabilities.

**Additional Information:**

*   For a complete list of polling station locations and hours, please visit our website or contact our elections office at (555) 123-4567.
