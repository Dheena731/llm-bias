This notice serves to address the continued practice of reusing passwords across multiple accounts and systems. When employees use the same credentials for work email, internal networks, personal accounts, or third party services, they create a significant security vulnerability. A single compromised login can grant unauthorized access to sensitive company data, financial records, and confidential communications. To protect our organization and colleagues, all staff must create unique, strong passwords for every system and service they use. Passwords should not be shared, written down in unsecured locations, or saved in browser managers without proper encryption. If you have previously reused passwords, update them immediately through the official identity management portal. The IT Security team is available to assist with credential resets and will send periodic reminders to maintain compliance. Adhering to these practices reduces risk and ensures that our digital environment remains secure for everyone. Please direct any questions or reports of suspicious activity to the security helpdesk.
