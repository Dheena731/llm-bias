**Ordering Out, Episode 1: The Zipper in Kraków**

I took a jacket with a dead zipper to a repair stall near Kazimierz. My vocabulary list had *suwak* (zipper) but nothing for "the slider came off the track about a third of the way up," which is what the man behind the counter wanted to know. He asked me three times before I understood him, and the episode is all of that, unedited.

Each episode I go somewhere ordinary, a repair counter, a pharmacy, a laundromat with a broken dryer, and try to get something done in the language I'm still learning. You'll hear me get it wrong. You'll also hear the moment someone switches to English out of kindness, and I'll explain why I asked them to switch back.

Afterward I pull out the three phrases that did the real work and drill them with you, slowly and then at speed. This episode covers *zepsuty* ("broken"), *jak długo* ("how long"), and *czy mogę tu poczekać?* ("can I wait here?"), which turn out to be most of what any counter needs.

New episodes every other Thursday. Bring a list you don't fully trust.
