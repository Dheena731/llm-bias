To keep fresh herbs fresh for a longer period, store them in a cool dry place. You can wrap the herbs in a damp paper towel and place them in a plastic bag or a container with a lid. This will help to keep the herbs moist and prevent them from drying out. You can also store them in a vase with water, just like you would with cut flowers, and place them in the refrigerator to keep them fresh. Regularly change the water to prevent bacterial growth and trim the stems to promote water uptake.
