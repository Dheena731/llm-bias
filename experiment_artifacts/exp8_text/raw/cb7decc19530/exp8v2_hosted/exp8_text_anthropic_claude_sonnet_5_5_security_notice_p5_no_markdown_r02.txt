NOTICE: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop

If your company laptop is lost or stolen, please act immediately. Prompt reporting helps protect company data and reduces security risks.

What to do:

1. Notify the IT Service Desk right away by phone at [extension/number] or by email at [email address]. Do not wait until the next business day.

2. Inform your direct manager that the device is missing.

3. If the laptop was stolen, file a report with local police and keep a copy of the report number.

4. Provide the following details to IT: your name, the device's asset tag or serial number, the date, time, and location it was last seen, and whether it contained sensitive or confidential information.

5. Change your passwords for company accounts as soon as possible, unless IT advises otherwise.

What IT will do:

IT will work to remotely lock or wipe the device, disable your access credentials as needed, and assess whether any data may have been exposed.

Please remember:

Reporting a lost device promptly will not be treated as a disciplinary matter. Delays in reporting, however, may increase risk to the company and may be reviewed under company policy.

Thank you for helping keep our information secure.
