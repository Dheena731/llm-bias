Subject: Your End-of-Year Tax Statement

Dear Customer,

Your end-of-year tax statement is now available. It summarizes the income and transactions from the past calendar year that you may need to report on your tax return. Here is a brief guide to reading it.

The first section shows your personal details, including your name, address, and taxpayer identification number. Please check that these are correct. If anything is wrong, contact us promptly so we can issue a corrected statement before you file.

The next section lists your income for the year, such as interest, dividends, or other payments, with each category shown separately. These figures match what we report to the tax authorities, so the amounts on your return should agree with them. If you see a difference between this statement and your own records, it is worth looking into before you file, since mismatches can lead to follow-up questions later.

Another section may show taxes already withheld on your behalf. These amounts count as payments toward your total tax bill, and you can usually claim them as credits on your return. Keep this figure handy when you calculate whether you owe more or are due a refund.

Finally, any notes at the end of the statement explain special items, such as adjustments, foreign income, or amounts that were corrected after the original reporting date. Reading these carefully can save you time and confusion.

We recommend keeping a copy of the statement with your other tax records for at least several years. While we are happy to explain what the statement shows, we cannot offer personal tax advice, so a qualified tax professional is the best person to consult about your specific situation.

If you have questions about any figure on the statement, please reach out and we will be glad to help.
