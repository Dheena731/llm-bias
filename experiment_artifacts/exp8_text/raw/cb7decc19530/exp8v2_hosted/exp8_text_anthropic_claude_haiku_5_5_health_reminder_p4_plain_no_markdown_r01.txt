Hey, this is a friendly reminder to step away from your screen for a few minutes. Your eyes, neck, and mind all benefit from regular breaks, even short ones. Try looking at something at least twenty feet away for a bit, stretching your shoulders, or standing up to get a glass of water. You'll likely come back to your work with a clearer head and more focus. Make it a habit to pause every hour or so, and your body and mind will thank you.
