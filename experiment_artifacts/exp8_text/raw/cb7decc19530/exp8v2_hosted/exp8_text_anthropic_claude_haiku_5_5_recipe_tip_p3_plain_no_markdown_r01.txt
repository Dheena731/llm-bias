One simple way to keep rice from turning gummy is to rinse it before cooking. Put the rice in a fine-mesh strainer or a bowl, run cold water over it, and swish it with your fingers until the water runs mostly clear. This washes away the surface starch that causes clumping. Drain it well, then cook it using the usual water ratio. For extra fluffiness, let the cooked rice rest covered for about ten minutes before fluffing it gently with a fork.
