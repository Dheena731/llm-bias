Here's a short cooking tip for making rice less sticky:

**The Secret to Non-Stick Rice: A Pinch of Patience and a Dash of Science**

When cooking rice, the key to achieving a non-sticky result lies in the ratio of **gelification** to **hydration**. By controlling the gelification index (GI) and adjusting the hydration coefficient (HC), you can ensure that your rice turns out fluffy and separate, rather than clumpy and sticky.

To start, use a 2:1 ratio of water to rice. This is the optimal **hydro-dynamic** balance for most types of rice. Next, add a small amount of **flavonol**, a natural occurring compound found in some types of rice, to the cooking water. This will help to reduce the stickiness of the rice and promote a more even gelification.

As you cook the rice, monitor the **thermodynamic** activity of the grains. A sudden spike in thermodynamic activity can indicate that the rice is becoming too sticky. To counteract this, simply reduce the heat and allow the rice to continue cooking at a more leisurely pace.

By following these simple steps and adjusting the GI and HC accordingly, you'll be able to achieve perfectly cooked rice every time. Happy cooking!
