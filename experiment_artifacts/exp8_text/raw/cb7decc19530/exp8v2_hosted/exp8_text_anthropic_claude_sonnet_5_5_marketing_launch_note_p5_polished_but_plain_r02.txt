We're delighted to announce the launch of Say It Slowly, a new podcast for anyone who wants to learn a language without feeling rushed, embarrassed, or buried in grammar tables.

Each episode runs about fifteen minutes and is built around a single everyday situation: ordering coffee, asking for directions, making small talk with a neighbor, or explaining that you didn't quite catch what someone just said. Your host walks through the key phrases at a gentle pace, explains why they work the way they do, and then invites you to speak aloud during short pauses. The goal is not to memorize lists of vocabulary but to build the habit of producing real sentences, which is where most learners find the greatest progress.

The first season focuses on Spanish, with episodes designed for beginners and for returning learners who want to shake off the rust. Later seasons will add French, Italian, and Japanese. Every episode includes a brief review of the previous lesson, so you can listen in order or jump in wherever your curiosity leads.

A few suggestions for getting the most from the show. Listen when you can speak freely, such as on a walk or during a commute with headphones on. Repeat each phrase out loud, even if it feels awkward at first. Replay an episode a day or two later, since a second listen tends to reveal details you missed the first time. And don't worry about perfection; clear, confident mistakes teach far more than careful silence.

The first five episodes are available today on all major podcast platforms, and new episodes will arrive every Monday. If you have a situation you would like us to cover, or a phrase that has always tripped you up, send us a message and we may feature it in a future episode.
