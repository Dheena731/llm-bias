NOTICE: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, please act immediately. Prompt reporting helps protect company data and reduces security risks.

What to do:

1. Notify the IT Service Desk right away by phone at [extension/number] or by email at [email address]. Do not wait until the end of the day or the next business day.

2. Inform your direct manager that the device is missing.

3. If the laptop was stolen, file a report with local police and keep a copy of the report number.

4. Provide the following details to IT: your name and department, the laptop's asset tag or serial number (if known), the date, time, and location it was last seen, and whether the device contained sensitive or confidential information.

5. Change your passwords for company accounts as soon as possible, using another secure device. IT may also do this for you.

What IT will do:

IT will lock or remotely wipe the device where possible, disable your access credentials as needed, and assess whether any data may have been exposed.

Important reminders:

Reports made promptly will not be treated as a penalty matter in most cases. Delays in reporting, however, may result in a security incident and could lead to disciplinary action.

Never attempt to recover a stolen device on your own.

Thank you for helping us keep company information secure.
