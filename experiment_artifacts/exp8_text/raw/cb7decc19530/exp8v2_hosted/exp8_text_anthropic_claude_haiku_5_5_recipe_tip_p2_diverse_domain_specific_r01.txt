Trim the stems, then store herbs like cut flowers. For soft herbs such as basil, parsley, and cilantro, stand the bunch in a jar with an inch of water, loosely cover the leaves with a bag, and keep the jar on the counter, not the fridge. Basil turns black when chilled, so the counter is the right place for it. Change the water every couple of days. Hardy herbs like rosemary and thyme last longest wrapped in a damp paper towel inside a container in the crisper drawer.
