To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it thoroughly before cooking. Rinsing the rice removes excess starch, which can cause it to become sticky or clingy when cooked. Simply place the rice in a fine mesh strainer and rinse it under cold running water, stirring occasionally, until the water runs clear. Then, cook the rice as you normally would. You can also try adding a small amount of oil or butter to the rice while it cooks to help separate the grains and reduce stickiness.
