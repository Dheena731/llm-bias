Welcome to Words Between Us, the podcast for anyone who has ever wanted to speak a second language without the stiff textbook dialogues and the fear of sounding foolish. Each week, we sit down with native speakers, teachers, and fellow learners to talk about the real rhythms of everyday language: how people order coffee, apologize, tease their friends, and tell stories about their grandparents. Our first season covers five languages, and every episode includes a short segment where we break down a few phrases you can use right away. Whether you are starting from zero or brushing off a language you studied years ago, you will find a place to begin. Subscribe today, press play on episode one, and let's start talking.
