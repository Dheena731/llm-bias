**Public Notice: Heat Advisory**

A heat advisory has been issued for the region, effective immediately. Temperatures are expected to reach a high of 95°F (35°C) with heat index values reaching as high as 105°F (40°C) by the end of the week.

**Action Required:**

* Residents are advised to take precautions to protect themselves and their loved ones from the heat. This includes staying hydrated by drinking plenty of water, taking regular breaks in shaded or air-conditioned areas, and avoiding strenuous activities during the hottest part of the day (typically between 11am and 3pm).

**Precautions:**

* Those with pre-existing medical conditions, the elderly, and young children are at higher risk for heat-related illnesses and are advised to take extra precautions to stay safe.

**Resources:**

* For those experiencing heat-related symptoms, such as dizziness, nausea, or headaches, seek medical attention immediately.

**Additional Information:**

* A heat advisory is not the same as a heat wave, which is typically defined as a prolonged period of abnormally hot weather. This advisory is a warning that the heat poses a risk to public health and safety.

**Stay Informed:**

* Check local news and weather reports for updates on the heat advisory and any other weather-related information.

This public notice is intended for the general public in the affected area.
