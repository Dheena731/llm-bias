Introducing the Simple Budget Planner, a free spreadsheet template designed to help you take control of your money without the hassle of complicated setup. Whether you are tracking monthly expenses for the first time or refining a budget you already use, this template gives you a clear, organized starting point that works in any major spreadsheet application.

The planner includes separate sections for income, fixed costs such as rent and utilities, variable spending like groceries and entertainment, and savings goals. Enter your figures once, and the template automatically calculates totals, shows how much you have left each month, and highlights categories where spending is running over plan. A year-at-a-glance summary lets you see trends across months so you can spot patterns before they become problems.

Setup takes about ten minutes. Simply enter your income and recurring bills, adjust the category names to match your own life, and begin logging transactions as they happen. No macros, add-ins, or subscriptions are required.

The Simple Budget Planner is available today at no cost. We built it for anyone who wants a practical, transparent tool they can understand, customize, and keep for years. We welcome your feedback as we continue to refine the template based on how people use it.
