NOTICE: SCREEN SECURITY REMINDER

All staff are reminded to lock their computer screens whenever they step away from their desk, even for just a moment. Locking your screen prevents unauthorized access to confidential company and client information.

To lock your screen quickly, press the Windows key plus L on PCs, or Command plus Control plus Q on Macs. Please make this a consistent habit throughout the workday.

Thank you for your attention to this important security practice. If you need assistance or have questions about IT protocols, please contact the Help Desk.
