This is a reminder to refill your prescription soon. Check how many doses you have left, and if you are down to about a week's supply, contact your pharmacy to request the refill. Many pharmacies can process requests by phone or through their website or app, which can save you a trip. If you have no refills remaining, call your doctor's office to ask for a new prescription, since approval can take a day or two. Planning ahead helps you avoid missing doses, which matters for keeping your treatment on track. If you have any questions about your medication, your pharmacist can help.
