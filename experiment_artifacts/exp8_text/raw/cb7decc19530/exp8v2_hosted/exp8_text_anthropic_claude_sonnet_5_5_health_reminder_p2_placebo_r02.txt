**Reminder: Stay Hydrated**

Take a moment to drink some water. Staying hydrated helps your body work well and can help you feel more alert and comfortable throughout the day.

A few easy ways to drink enough:

- **Start early.** Have a glass of water when you wake up.
- **Keep water nearby.** Place a bottle or glass on your desk or in your bag.
- **Sip regularly.** Drink small amounts throughout the day instead of waiting until you feel very thirsty.
- **Pair it with routines.** Drink some water with each meal and whenever you take a break.
- **Adjust to your needs.** You may need more water if it's hot, if you're active, or if you're sweating a lot.

If your urine is pale yellow, you're probably drinking enough. If it's dark, try drinking more water.

Have a glass now, and keep it going through the day.
