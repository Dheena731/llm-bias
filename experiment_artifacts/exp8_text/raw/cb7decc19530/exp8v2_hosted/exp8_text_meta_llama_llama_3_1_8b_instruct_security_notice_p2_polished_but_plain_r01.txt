Notice to All Staff

As part of our ongoing efforts to protect the security of our organization, we would like to remind everyone to be cautious when dealing with emails that request sensitive information or prompt you to take certain actions.

Phishing emails are designed to deceive recipients into divulging confidential information, such as passwords, financial data, or other sensitive details. These emails often appear to be from legitimate sources, but can be easily identified by their generic greetings, poor grammar, and suspicious links or attachments.

To protect yourself and our organization, please take the following precautions when handling emails that seem suspicious:

- Be wary of emails that request you to verify sensitive information, such as passwords or account numbers.
- Do not click on links or open attachments from unknown or unfamiliar sources.
- Be cautious of generic greetings, such as "Dear valued customer" or "Dear employee".
- If you are unsure about the authenticity of an email, do not respond or take any action.
- Report any suspicious emails to the IT department immediately.

If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT department. We are here to assist you in maintaining the security and integrity of our organization.
