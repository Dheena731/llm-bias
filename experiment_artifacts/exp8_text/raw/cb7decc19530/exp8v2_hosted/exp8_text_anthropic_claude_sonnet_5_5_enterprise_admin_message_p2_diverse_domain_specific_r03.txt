**Subject: Expense approvals change starting Monday, March 3**

Hi all,

Starting March 3, expense reports go through approval based on amount, not always to your department head.

**Under $250:** Your direct manager approves. Finance doesn't review these unless something is flagged.

**$250 to $2,500:** Your manager approves, then Finance checks receipts and coding.

**Over $2,500, or any single item over $1,000:** Add your department head. Get their sign-off in writing *before* you buy, and attach it to the report.

**Other changes**
- Every line needs a project code. Reports with "TBD" or blanks will be returned, not held.
- Approvers have 3 business days. After that, the report escalates to the next level up, so nothing sits while someone is on leave.
- Corporate card charges still need receipts within 10 days of the statement closing. Missing receipts after that go to your manager, and a third miss in a quarter suspends the card.
- Reports are due by the 5th for reimbursement on the 15th. Anything later rolls to the 30th.

Mileage and per diem rules are unchanged.

If a report is rejected, the reason now appears in the comments field. Fix it and resubmit rather than starting a new report, or it loses its place in the queue.

Questions go to Dana in Finance Ops (ext. 214). I'll hold open desk time Thursday at 2 p.m. in the small conference room if you'd like to walk through a real report.
