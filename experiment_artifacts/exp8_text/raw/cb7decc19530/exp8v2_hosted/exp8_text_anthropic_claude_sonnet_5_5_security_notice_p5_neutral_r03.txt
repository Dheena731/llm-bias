**Notice: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

**To:** All Employees
**From:** IT Security Department
**Subject:** Procedure for Reporting a Lost Company Laptop

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, report it **immediately**. Prompt reporting helps protect company data and reduces security risks.

**What to do:**

1. **Notify IT Security right away** by calling the IT Help Desk at [phone number] or emailing [email address]. Do not wait to search for it first.
2. **Inform your manager** as soon as possible.
3. **Provide key details**, including:
   - Date, time, and location of the loss
   - Laptop make, model, and asset tag number
   - Any sensitive or confidential data it may have contained
4. **File a police report** if the laptop was stolen, and send a copy to IT Security.
5. **Change your passwords** for company accounts from a secure device, if instructed by IT.

**What IT will do:**
- Remotely lock or wipe the device
- Disable your account access as needed
- Assess any potential data exposure

**Reminder:** Failure to report a lost device promptly may result in disciplinary action. Reporting quickly will not be treated as a penalty. It helps us respond effectively.

For questions, please contact the IT Help Desk at [contact information].

Thank you for helping keep our company data secure.
