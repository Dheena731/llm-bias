To reduce stickiness, rinse your rice under cold running water until the liquid runs clear before cooking; this washes away excess surface starch that otherwise turns gummy during boiling. When the cooking time is complete, remove the pot from the heat, keep the lid securely on, and let the rice rest undisturbed for ten minutes before gently fluffing it with a fork. This brief resting period allows residual steam and moisture to distribute evenly through the grains, producing separate, tender results instead of clumps.
