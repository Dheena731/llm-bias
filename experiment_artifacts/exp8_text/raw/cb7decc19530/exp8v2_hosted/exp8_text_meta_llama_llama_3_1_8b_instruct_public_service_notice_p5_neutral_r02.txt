**Notice: Polling Station Locations**

The public is hereby informed that the following polling stations have been designated for the upcoming election:

**List of Polling Stations:**

1. Ward 1 - St. John's Primary School, 123 Main St.
2. Ward 2 - Community Center, 456 Elm St.
3. Ward 3 - City Hall, 789 Oak St.
4. Ward 4 - Library, 1010 Maple St.
5. Ward 5 - School, 1111 Pine St.

**Important Dates:**

* Polls open: 7:00 AM
* Polls close: 8:00 PM
* Voting hours: 7:00 AM - 8:00 PM

**Additional Information:**

* If you are unsure of your polling station location, please contact the election office at (555) 555-5555.
* Voters with disabilities or special needs may contact the election office for assistance.
* Identification and proof of residency will be required to vote.

**Please note:** Polling station locations are subject to change. We recommend checking the official election website for the most up-to-date information.
