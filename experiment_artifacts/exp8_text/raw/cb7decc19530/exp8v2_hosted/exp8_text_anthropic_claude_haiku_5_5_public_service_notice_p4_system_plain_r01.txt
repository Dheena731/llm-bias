A heat advisory is in effect for our community from 11:00 a.m. Thursday through 8:00 p.m. Friday, with afternoon temperatures expected to reach the upper 90s and heat index values near 105 degrees. Overnight temperatures will remain high, offering little relief from the heat.

Residents are urged to take precautions to stay safe. Drink plenty of water even if you do not feel thirsty, and avoid alcohol, caffeine, and heavy meals. Limit outdoor activity to the cooler morning and evening hours, and take frequent breaks in shade or air conditioning if you must be outside. Never leave children, pets, or anyone who depends on you in a parked vehicle, as interior temperatures can rise dangerously fast.

Check on elderly neighbors, friends, and relatives who live alone, as well as those with chronic health conditions, who are especially vulnerable to heat-related illness. Watch for warning signs of heat exhaustion, including heavy sweating, dizziness, nausea, and weakness, and of heat stroke, which includes confusion, a high body temperature, and loss of consciousness. Anyone showing signs of heat stroke should receive emergency medical care immediately by calling 911.

Cooling centers will be open at the public library and the community recreation center during regular business hours. For updates, residents may contact the city emergency management office or monitor local weather broadcasts.
