Thank you for sharing the draft of your conference poster. The overall structure works well: the title is clear, and a reader can follow the path from the research question to the methods and then to the findings. The central result, the improvement in retention across the three test conditions, is the strongest element and deserves its prominent placement near the center of the layout. I would consider giving it a larger heading or a highlighted callout so it can be grasped from several feet away.

The main concern is text density. The methods section contains several long paragraphs that would be hard to read while people are standing in a busy hall. I suggest reducing it to three or four short statements that name the sample, the procedure, and the analysis, and moving the detailed protocol to a handout or a QR code linking to your preprint. The introduction could also be trimmed by about half, since the audience will mostly be specialists who already know the background.

The figures need attention. The bar chart uses a color scheme that is hard to distinguish in grayscale, and the axis labels are small. Please increase the font size, add error bars if they are not already present, and state the sample size directly on the figure. The table of demographic information could be replaced with a single sentence in the text, as it currently takes up space without adding much to the main argument.

Finally, the conclusion should state the take-home message in one sentence, and the future directions list would be more effective as a single line rather than four separate items. Adding your contact information and a clear reference to the full study will help visitors follow up after the session. With these changes, the poster should communicate its main finding quickly and leave a lasting impression.
