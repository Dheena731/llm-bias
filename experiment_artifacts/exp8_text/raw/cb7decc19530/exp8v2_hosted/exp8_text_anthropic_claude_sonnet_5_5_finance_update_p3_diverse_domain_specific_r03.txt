**Savings goals are now in the app**

You can now set aside money for a specific thing, such as a security deposit, a vet bill fund, or a December flight, without opening a separate account.

**How it works**
- Create a goal with a name, a target amount, and an optional date.
- If you add a date, we calculate the per-paycheck amount. For example, $1,800 by October 15 is $150 on each biweekly deposit. You can change the number or leave it.
- Money in a goal stays in your main savings account. It's just labeled, so your balance and interest don't change.
- Goals show as "set aside" in your balance. Your available-to-spend figure drops by that amount, which helps if you tend to dip into savings.

**Details worth knowing**
- You can pause contributions for a month without losing your schedule. This is useful for a month with an unexpected car repair.
- Withdrawing from a goal takes one tap, but we'll ask whether you want to lower the target or keep it and refill later.
- If a goal is behind pace, we'll tell you once, in the goal screen. No push notifications.
- Each account supports up to 10 goals.

**Not included yet**
- Shared goals between two people
- Goals funded by round-ups (planned for the next release)
- Goals on checking accounts

The feature is rolling out over the next week. If you don't see "Goals" under Savings by Friday, update to version 4.12 and check again.
