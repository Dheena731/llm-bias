We have launched a new monthly spending summary to help you track your finances with greater accuracy. The report automatically gathers your recent transactions and organizes them into a straightforward overview. You will see your total monthly outflow, a detailed breakdown of expenses by category, and a comparison against previous periods to highlight any shifts in your habits. The summary appears in your account dashboard and updates on the first business day of each month. You can review or export the report at any time. This feature is designed to simplify budgeting, reveal spending trends, and support smarter financial choices. Should you need guidance on reading the data or locating the report, please contact our support team directly.
