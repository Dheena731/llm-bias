**Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow, Effective Monday**

Hello everyone,

We are introducing a new expense approval workflow to make reimbursements faster and more transparent. Here is what is changing:

**How it works**
1. Submit your expense report through the finance portal, with receipts attached.
2. Your direct manager will review it within two business days.
3. Expenses over $500 will also require approval from your department head.
4. Finance will process approved reports in the next weekly payment cycle.

**What you need to know**
- Reports without receipts will be returned automatically.
- Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date.
- You will receive email notifications at each stage, so you can track the status of your request.

**Getting started**
A short guide and a walkthrough video are available on the intranet under Finance > Expense Resources. If you have questions or run into any issues, contact the finance team at finance@yourcompany.com.

Thank you for your cooperation as we make this transition.
