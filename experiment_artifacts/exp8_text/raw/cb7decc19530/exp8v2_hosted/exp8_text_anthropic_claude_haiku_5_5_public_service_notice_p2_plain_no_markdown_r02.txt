PUBLIC NOTICE

Road Closure on Maple Avenue

The City of Riverton Public Works Department advises residents that Maple Avenue will be closed to all vehicle traffic between Oak Street and Cedar Lane beginning at 7:00 a.m. on Monday, March 16, and continuing through Friday, March 20. The closure is needed so crews can replace a damaged water main and repave the affected section of roadway.

During the closure, drivers should use Elm Street or Birch Road as alternate routes. Local access to residences and businesses on the closed segment will be maintained for pedestrians, and emergency vehicles will be permitted through the work zone at all times. Signs will be posted at each detour point.

Residents with questions or concerns may call the Public Works Department at 555-0142 during regular business hours, Monday through Friday, from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. The city thanks the community for its patience while these necessary repairs are completed.
