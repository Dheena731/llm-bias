The public library is pleased to announce its regular operating hours for all visitors. The facility is open Monday through Thursday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM. On Friday and Saturday, doors are open from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The library is closed on Sundays and official public holidays. Book drops remain available outside the main entrance for returns at any hour. Digital services, including online catalog access and electronic borrowing, continue to operate without interruption. Community members are welcome to visit during these scheduled hours to browse the collection, use the computers, and participate in local programs.
