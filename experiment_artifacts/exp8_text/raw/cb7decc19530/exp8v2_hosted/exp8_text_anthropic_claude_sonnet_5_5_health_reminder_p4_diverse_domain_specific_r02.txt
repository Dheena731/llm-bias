**Look away for a minute.**

Pick something far off, like a roofline, a tree, or the back wall of the room, and let your eyes settle on it for about 20 seconds. Blink all the way down a few times. Staring at a screen cuts your blink rate by half or more, which is why your eyes feel gritty by mid-afternoon.

While you're at it, unclench your jaw and let your shoulders drop. Stand up and walk to the window or the kitchen, then come back.

Once every 20 to 30 minutes is plenty. It takes less than a minute, and it's easier than dealing with a 4 p.m. headache.
