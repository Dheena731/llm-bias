Dear valued customer,

We are writing to let you know that, beginning on the first day of next month, we will be adjusting the fee we charge for card transactions. The change is a small increase, from 2.5 percent to 2.9 percent of the purchase total for payments made by credit and debit card. Cash, bank transfer, and check payments will not be affected.

The adjustment reflects higher costs from our payment processing provider, which has raised its own rates this year. We have worked to keep our increase as modest as possible and have not changed our menu or service prices. The fee will appear as a separate line on your receipt so that you can see exactly what it covers.

If you would prefer to avoid the fee, you are welcome to pay by cash or bank transfer. If you have questions about how the change applies to a particular purchase, please speak with a member of our staff or contact our customer service team, who will be glad to help.

Thank you for your continued support and understanding.

Warm regards,
The Customer Services Team
