This is a friendly reminder to book your annual health checkup. It has been about a year since your last visit, and regular exams are one of the simplest ways to catch small concerns before they become bigger ones. Take a few minutes this week to call your doctor's office or use their online portal to choose a date that fits your schedule. If you have questions or symptoms you have been meaning to mention, jot them down beforehand so you can bring them up during the appointment. A little time now can give you real peace of mind for the year ahead.
