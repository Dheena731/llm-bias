**MUNICIPAL SERVICES NOTICE: Transition to Dual-Stream Recycling & Route Timing Adjustment**

**Effective Date:** Monday, November 4, 2024  
**Applies to:** Wards 3 through 6 (Eastern Service Area)

To meet reduced contamination thresholds set by the Regional Materials Recovery Facility (MRF), collection crews will no longer accept comingled single-stream recycling as of November 4. 

### 1. Mandatory Separation at the Curb
* **Fibre (Yellow Cart / Marked Crate):** Clean cardboard (flattened, tied with twine if loose, max. 60cm x 60cm), boxboard, newsprint, and paper office trim. Wax-coated beverage cartons and grease-soaked paper must go into residual waste.
* **Containers (Blue Cart):** Rinsed metal cans, rigid plastics (#1, #2, and #5 only), and glass bottles/jars. Keep caps on plastic bottles. 
* **Zero Bagging:** Place all items loose in the bin. Soft films, shrink wrap, and plastic grocery bags wrap around MRF sorting shafts and will trigger an automatic load rejection.

### 2. Set-Out Time Shift
Truck routing has been resequenced. All bins must be placed at the curb line with wheels facing the residence no later than **06:30 on your scheduled collection day**. Crews will not return for bins set out after the truck has completed the street run.

### 3. Non-Collection Tagging
Bins containing soft plastics, polystyrene, food residue, or unseparated fibre/containers will be tagged with a non-compliance notice and left at the curb until the following cycle. 

*Updated route maps and print-ready street calendars are available at the District Operations Depot (140 Northline Rd) or via the utility portal.*
