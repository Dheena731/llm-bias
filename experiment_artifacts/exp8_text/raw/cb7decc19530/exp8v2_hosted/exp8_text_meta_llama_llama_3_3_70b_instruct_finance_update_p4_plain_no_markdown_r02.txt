Your end-of-year tax statement is a document that summarizes your income and taxes withheld for the year. It will show the total amount of money you earned, the amount of taxes that were taken out of your pay, and any other relevant tax information. This statement is usually provided by your employer or financial institution and is used to help you complete your tax return. You will need this statement to accurately report your income and claim any deductions or credits you are eligible for when you file your taxes.
