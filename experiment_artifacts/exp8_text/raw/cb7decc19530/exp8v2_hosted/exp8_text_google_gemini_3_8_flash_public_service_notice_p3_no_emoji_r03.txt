**IMPORTANT NOTICE: Changes to Recycling Collection Schedule**

Effective Monday, November 3, 2025, residential recycling collection will move to a bi-weekly schedule. 

Please take note of the following updates:

* **Collection Day:** Your collection day may have changed. Please check the updated zone map to confirm your new schedule.
* **Timing:** Bins must be placed curbside by 7:00 AM on your designated collection day.
* **Accepted Materials:** There are no changes to the types of materials accepted. Please continue to rinse containers and flatten cardboard.

To view the new collection calendar and find your designated week, please visit www.cityservices.gov/recycling or call the Waste Management Office at (555) 019-2834.

Thank you for your cooperation as we work to improve our community services.

**Department of Public Works**
