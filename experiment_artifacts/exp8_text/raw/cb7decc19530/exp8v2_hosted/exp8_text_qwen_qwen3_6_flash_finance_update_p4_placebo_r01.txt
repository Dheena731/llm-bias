This message provides your end-of-year tax statement, which is an official summary of your total earnings and the taxes already paid or set aside during the past calendar year. It serves as the primary record you will use to complete your annual tax return. The document lists your compensation, any taxes withheld from your pay, and other reportable financial activity for the year. Please review the figures for accuracy, retain a copy for your records, and reference it when preparing your tax filing. If you notice any errors, contact the issuing organization immediately so corrections can be made before you submit your return.
