**Notice: How to Report a Lost Company Laptop**

If you lose a company laptop, or think it has been stolen, report it to IT and your manager as soon as you notice it is missing. Quick reporting lets IT lock the device and protect company data.

When you report, please include:

- The time and place you last saw or used the laptop
- Any details that help identify it, such as the model, serial number, or asset tag
- Whether it contained files or access to company systems
- If you suspect theft, whether you have filed a police report

Do not try to track down the laptop by yourself or post about it on social media. Leave recovery to IT and, where needed, the police.

Thank you for acting quickly. Reporting promptly is the best way to limit the risk to the company and to your colleagues.
