**Heat Advisory Issued for the Region**

A heat advisory has been issued for the area, effective from 11am today until further notice. Temperatures are expected to reach the high 90s, with heat indices making it feel like 100-110 degrees Fahrenheit in many areas.

Residents are advised to take precautions to avoid heat-related illnesses, especially vulnerable populations such as the elderly, young children, and those with pre-existing medical conditions. The public is urged to stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water and to limit outdoor activities during the hottest part of the day (usually between 11am and 3pm).

Local authorities will be monitoring the situation closely and will provide updates as necessary. In the meantime, residents can find additional information and resources on the city's website or by calling the heat advisory hotline at 555-HEAT-123.
