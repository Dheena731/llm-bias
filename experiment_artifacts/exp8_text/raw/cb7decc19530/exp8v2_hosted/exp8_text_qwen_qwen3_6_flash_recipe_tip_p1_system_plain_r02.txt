To save time on weeknight dinners, spend ten minutes each Sunday washing and chopping vegetables, then portion proteins into small containers and refrigerate them. When evening arrives, dump everything straight into one hot skillet or baking sheet without measuring or cutting. This method eliminates the most time consuming steps of cooking and dramatically reduces dishes to wash. Drizzle a quick mixture of olive oil, minced garlic, lemon juice, and soy sauce over the food before cooking to build flavor instantly. By keeping preparation separate from cooking, you can deliver a complete dinner to the table in under twenty minutes.
