**Subject: 🔒 Security Reminder: Eliminate Password Reuse**

**Team,**

To protect our organization and your personal data, please ensure you are **not reusing passwords** between work and personal accounts.

**⚠️ Why This Matters:**
If a password is compromised on a third-party website, attackers often attempt to use it on corporate systems (a tactic known as *credential stuffing*). Reusing passwords creates a single point of failure for both your personal privacy and company security.

**✅ Action Required:**
*   **Unique Credentials:** Every work account (Email, HR, DevTools, etc.) must have a distinct password.
*   **No Cross-Over:** Never use a work password for personal sites, and vice versa.
*   **Immediate Update:** If you suspect you've reused a password anywhere, change it immediately via the [Password Reset Portal].
*   **Use Our Tool:** Leverage our approved enterprise **Password Manager** to generate and store complex, unique passwords securely.

Thank you for helping keep our workplace secure.
