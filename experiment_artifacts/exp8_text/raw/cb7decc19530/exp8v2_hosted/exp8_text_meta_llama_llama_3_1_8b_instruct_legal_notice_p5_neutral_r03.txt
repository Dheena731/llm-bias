**NOTICE OF ACCEPTABLE USE OF SHARED NETWORK**

To all users of [Network Name],

This notice outlines the acceptable use of the [Network Name] ("Network") shared among [Company/Organization Name] employees, contractors, and guests ("Users"). The purpose of this notice is to ensure that the Network is used in a manner that respects the rights of others, promotes a productive and efficient work environment, and adheres to applicable laws and regulations.

**PERMITTED USES**

Users are permitted to use the Network for:

*   Official business purposes, including email, internet access, and software applications;
*   Personal use, as long as such use does not interfere with official business purposes or violate these guidelines;
*   Educational or training purposes, provided such use does not compromise the security or performance of the Network.

**PROHIBITED USES**

Users are prohibited from using the Network for:

*   Sending or receiving unauthorized or unsolicited commercial email ("spam");
*   Engaging in or facilitating any form of hacking, cracking, or other unauthorized access to the Network or its resources;
*   Downloading, installing, or running any software or files that are not authorized by [Company/Organization Name] or are not properly licensed or documented;
*   Engaging in or promoting any form of cyberbullying, harassment, or discrimination;
*   Using the Network to access, store, or transmit any materials that are obscene, pornographic, or otherwise violate any laws or regulations;
*   Engaging in any form of online or digital piracy, copyright infringement, or other intellectual property rights violations.

Users are also subject to the following restrictions:

*   Any user account that is inactive for a period of [insert time frame] will be subject to deletion and potential reassignment;

*   Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account login credentials and for taking all necessary measures to prevent unauthorized access to the Network.

**ENFORCEMENT AND AMENDMENTS**

This notice will be enforced by [Company/Organization Name] and may be amended at any time without prior notice. Any changes to these guidelines will be posted on the Network and communicated to Users through email or other reasonable means.

By using the Network, Users acknowledge that they have read, understood, and will comply with these guidelines.
