**Water check**

If you've had only coffee since you woke up, you're already behind. Drink a full glass now, before the next cup.

Rough pacing: one glass with breakfast, one mid-morning, one with lunch, one around 3 p.m. (when the "I need a snack" feeling is often just thirst), one before dinner. That's about 1.5 to 2 liters without a spreadsheet.

Quick gauge: pale straw-colored urine means you're fine. Dark yellow means drink up. A dull headache, dry lips, or trouble focusing in the afternoon usually means you're late.

Keep a bottle where your eyes land, not in your bag. Refill it whenever you get up for the kettle, the printer, or the bathroom.
