**Important Notice: Library Opening Hours**

The city library is open to the public on weekdays from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and on Saturdays from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The library is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Please note that these hours are subject to change, especially during summer and winter breaks. We recommend checking our website or calling us at 555-1234 to confirm the hours before planning your visit.

We strive to provide a comfortable and accessible environment for all patrons. If you require assistance or have any questions, our friendly staff will be happy to help.

Thank you for choosing the city library!
