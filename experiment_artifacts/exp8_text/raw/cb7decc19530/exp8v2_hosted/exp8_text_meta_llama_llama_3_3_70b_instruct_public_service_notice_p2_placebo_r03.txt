NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE

To all motorists and residents,

Please be advised that Oak Street will be closed to through traffic from Monday, March 21st to Friday, March 25th, due to scheduled road maintenance. The closure will be in effect from 8am to 5pm each day.

During this time, local access will be maintained for residents and businesses, but all other traffic will be detoured via Maple Street and Main Avenue. Signage will be in place to direct motorists around the closure.

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your cooperation as we work to improve the road infrastructure. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Public Works department at 555-1234.

Thank you for your understanding.
