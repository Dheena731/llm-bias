**Enable Two-Factor Authentication: Add an Extra Layer of Security to Your Account**

To protect your account from unauthorized access, we strongly recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA). 2FA adds an extra layer of security by requiring both your password and a verification code sent to your mobile device or email.

**Why enable 2FA?**

*   Protects your account from phishing and hacking attempts
*   Reduces the risk of unauthorized access
*   Provides an additional security measure to safeguard your sensitive information

**How to enable 2FA:**

1.  Log in to your account and navigate to the "Account Settings" or "Security" section.
2.  Look for the 2FA option and follow the prompts to enable it.
3.  You may be asked to download an authenticator app or provide your phone number for receiving verification codes.

**What to expect after enabling 2FA:**

*   Whenever you log in to your account, you will be asked to provide both your password and the verification code sent to your registered device.
*   This ensures that even if someone has your password, they won't be able to access your account without the verification code.

**Help and Support:**

If you need assistance with enabling 2FA, please contact our support team for help. We're here to ensure your account is secure and protected.
