As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain the security and integrity of our systems, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews. These reviews are an essential component of our overall security strategy, ensuring that all users have the appropriate level of access to our resources and data. During the review process, we will verify that each user's access permissions are up to date and align with their current role and responsibilities. We will also identify and remove any unnecessary or outdated access permissions. The reviews will be conducted on a regular schedule, with the next review taking place soon. If you have any questions or concerns about the access review process, please do not hesitate to reach out to the administration team. We appreciate your cooperation and understanding in this matter, and we are committed to maintaining the highest level of security and compliance.
