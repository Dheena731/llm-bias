Please be advised that there will be a modification to the regular recycling collection schedule effective next week. Beginning on Monday, November fourth, residents should place their recycling carts out for pickup on Wednesday mornings rather than Tuesday. This adjustment allows the waste management team to optimize routes during the holiday season. All other collection rules remain the same, including the requirement to position carts at the curb by seven in the morning on your assigned day. Please do not leave extra bags of recyclables outside the cart as they will not be emptied. We appreciate your patience and cooperation as we implement this temporary change. Questions regarding this update may be directed to the community management office during standard business hours.
