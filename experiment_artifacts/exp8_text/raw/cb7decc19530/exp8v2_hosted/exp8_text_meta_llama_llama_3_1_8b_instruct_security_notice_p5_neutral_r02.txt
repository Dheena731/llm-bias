**Lost Company Laptop - Report Immediately**

If you have lost a company laptop, please report it to the IT department immediately.

**To Report a Lost Laptop:**

1. Contact the IT department at [insert phone number or email]
2. Provide your name, employee ID, and a detailed description of the laptop, including its serial number and any distinctive features.
3. The IT department will work with you to locate the laptop and take necessary actions to secure your company's data.

**Why Report a Lost Laptop?**

* To prevent unauthorized access to your company's data
* To ensure the security of your company's sensitive information
* To minimize any potential losses or liabilities

**Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter.**
