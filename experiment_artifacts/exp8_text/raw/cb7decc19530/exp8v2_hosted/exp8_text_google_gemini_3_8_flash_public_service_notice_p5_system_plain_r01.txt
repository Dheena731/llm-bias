The municipal election board announces that polling station locations for the upcoming election on Tuesday, November fifth, are now finalized. All voting centers will be open from seven in the morning until eight in the evening. Voters must cast their ballots at their assigned precinct location based on their residential address. Several polling places have changed since the last election due to facility renovations, so residents are strongly encouraged to verify their assigned location before heading out to vote. You can confirm your polling site by checking the voter notification card sent to your home address, by contacting the city clerk office by phone, or by consulting the county election portal online. Please ensure you bring an approved form of identification and arrive before the polls close. Anyone waiting in line by eight in the evening will be permitted to vote.
