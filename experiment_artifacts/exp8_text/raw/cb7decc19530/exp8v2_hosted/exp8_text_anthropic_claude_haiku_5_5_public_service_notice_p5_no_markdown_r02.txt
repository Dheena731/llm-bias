Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations

Please be advised that polling station locations have been assigned for the upcoming election. Each registered voter is assigned to a specific polling station based on their home address. Your assigned polling station is listed on your voter registration card and may also be found by contacting your local election office or visiting the official election website.

Polling stations will be open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on Election Day. Voters must go to their assigned polling station to cast a ballot unless otherwise directed by election officials. Please allow extra time for travel and parking, and be sure to bring a valid form of identification if required.

Accessible voting arrangements are available at all polling stations. If you require assistance or accommodations, please contact the election office in advance so that staff can prepare to serve you.

If you have questions about your polling station or need help locating it, please contact your local election office during regular business hours.

Thank you for taking part in the democratic process.
