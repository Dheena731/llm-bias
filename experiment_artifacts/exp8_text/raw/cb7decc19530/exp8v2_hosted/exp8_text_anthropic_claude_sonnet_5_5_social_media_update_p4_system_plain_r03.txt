Calendar tip of the week: stop using your calendar only for meetings and start scheduling your tasks too. When you give a task its own time block, like "Write project summary, 2 to 3 pm," it stops floating around on a to-do list and becomes something you have actually made room for. If the block gets bumped, drag it to another slot instead of letting it quietly disappear. You will be surprised how much more gets done when your day has a plan you can see. Try it for just one week and tell me how it goes.
