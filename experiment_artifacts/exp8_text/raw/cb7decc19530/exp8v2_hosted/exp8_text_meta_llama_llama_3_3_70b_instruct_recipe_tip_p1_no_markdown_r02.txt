To make a quick and delicious weeknight dinner, consider preparing a one-pot meal such as pasta with tomato sauce and vegetables. Start by sautéing some onions and garlic in olive oil, then add in your choice of protein, such as ground beef or chicken, and cook until browned. Next, add in a can of diced tomatoes, some chicken broth, and your favorite pasta shape, and bring the mixture to a boil. Reduce the heat and let simmer for 15-20 minutes, or until the pasta is al dente and the sauce has thickened. Finally, stir in some chopped vegetables, such as bell peppers and spinach, and season with salt and pepper to taste. This hearty and satisfying meal can be ready in under 30 minutes, making it perfect for a busy weeknight dinner.
