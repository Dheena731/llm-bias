Season roasted vegetables before they go into the oven, not after. Toss them in oil first, since the fat helps salt, pepper, and dried herbs cling to every surface and bloom as they heat. Use about a teaspoon of kosher salt per pound of vegetables, and add delicate spices like garlic powder or paprika at the start too. Save fresh herbs, lemon zest, and grated cheese for the final minutes or for after roasting, because they scorch or lose their brightness under long heat. A finishing sprinkle of flaky salt and a squeeze of citrus will wake up the whole pan.
