**Screen break**

Look up. Pick the farthest thing you can see, like a roofline, a tree, or the end of the hallway, and rest your eyes on it for about twenty seconds.

Blink a few times on purpose. You've probably been doing it half as often as usual.

Unclench your jaw, let your shoulders drop away from your ears, and stand up if you've been in that chair for an hour or more.

Then go back to it.
