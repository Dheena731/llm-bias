Taking regular breaks from screens can help improve productivity and reduce eye strain. Try to set aside short intervals, such as 5-10 minutes every hour, to step away from your device. Use this time to stretch, move around, and focus on your surroundings. A short walk, some deep breathing, or a quick chat with a colleague can help clear your mind and refresh your perspective. By taking these breaks, you can come back to your screen with renewed energy and a clearer focus.
