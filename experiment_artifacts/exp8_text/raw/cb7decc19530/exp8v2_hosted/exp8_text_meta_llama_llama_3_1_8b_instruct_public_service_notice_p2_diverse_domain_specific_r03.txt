Here is a short public notice about a road closure:

**NOTICE TO TRAVELERS AND RESIDENTS OF LAKEVIEW HEIGHTS**

The City of Lakeview Heights Public Works Department will be closing a section of 5th Street East from Oakwood Avenue to Maple Street from Monday, March 27th to Friday, April 7th for a major infrastructure upgrade.

During this time, residents and travelers will need to take alternative routes to avoid the construction zone. A signed detour will be in place to direct traffic through nearby streets.

We appreciate your understanding and patience as we work to improve the infrastructure of our community. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567.

**Please note that while the road will be closed to vehicular traffic, pedestrian access will be maintained through designated routes.**
