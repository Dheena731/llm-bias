The City Department of Public Works advises motorists that Main Street will be closed to all through traffic between First Avenue and Oak Street starting Monday, October 16, through Friday, October 20. The closure will be in effect daily from 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM to accommodate essential underground utility repairs and resurfacing work. Detour signs will be posted directing drivers to use Maple Avenue as an alternate route. Local residents will maintain access to their driveways, though minor delays should be expected. Emergency vehicles will be granted access through the work area at all times. All lanes are scheduled to reopen fully by the evening of October 20, weather permitting. Drivers are urged to exercise caution, reduce speed, and obey all posted signage near the construction zone. For questions or further information, please contact the municipal road maintenance division at 555-0199.
