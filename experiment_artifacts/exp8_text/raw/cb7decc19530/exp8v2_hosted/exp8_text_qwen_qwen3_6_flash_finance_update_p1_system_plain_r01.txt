We are introducing a new monthly spending summary designed to help you track your expenses more clearly. This report breaks down your purchases by category such as groceries, utilities, dining, and transportation, and compares your actual totals against your original budget. You will receive the summary automatically at the end of each month through your online account portal. The updated layout highlights your spending trends over recent months and clearly marks any categories that exceed your planned limits. Reviewing this document regularly will help you identify habits to adjust and keep your finances on track. If you need guidance on reading the report or want to customize your notification preferences, please contact our support team directly.
