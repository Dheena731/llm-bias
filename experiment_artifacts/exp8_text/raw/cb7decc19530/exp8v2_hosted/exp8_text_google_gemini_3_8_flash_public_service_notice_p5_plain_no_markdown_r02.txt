The local election office has finalized the list of polling stations for the upcoming election. Voters are reminded that they must cast their ballots at their assigned location, which is determined by their residential address. Polling places are established in public facilities such as neighborhood schools, community centers, and municipal buildings. To find your specific polling location, please review the official voter information card sent to your home address or contact the municipal elections office by phone. Stations will remain open from seven in the morning until eight in the evening on election day. Please verify your designated site before traveling to vote, as ballots cannot be processed at alternate locations.
