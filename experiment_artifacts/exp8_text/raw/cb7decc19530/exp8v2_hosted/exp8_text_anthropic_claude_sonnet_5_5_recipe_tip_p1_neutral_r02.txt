**Weeknight Dinner Tip: Prep Once, Cook Fast**

When you get home from the grocery store (or on a Sunday), spend 15 minutes chopping a few onions, bell peppers, and garlic and storing them in airtight containers in the fridge. Then on busy weeknights, you can toss them straight into a hot pan for a stir-fry, pasta sauce, fajitas, or omelets without any extra chopping.

**Bonus:** Cook a big batch of rice or grains at the same time. Leftovers keep for 4-5 days and reheat in minutes, so dinner can be on the table in under 20 minutes.
