**PUBLIC NOTICE: Library Operating Hours**

Please be advised of the regular operating hours for the Public Library system, effective immediately. 

**Hours of Operation:**
* **Monday – Thursday:** 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM
* **Friday – Saturday:** 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
* **Sunday:** 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM

The exterior return drops remain open 24 hours a day, seven days a week for all book and media returns. Digital resources, including e-books and online databases, are accessible at any time through our website.

For holiday schedules and branch-specific details, please visit www.citylibrary.org or call (555) 019-2834 during operating hours.
