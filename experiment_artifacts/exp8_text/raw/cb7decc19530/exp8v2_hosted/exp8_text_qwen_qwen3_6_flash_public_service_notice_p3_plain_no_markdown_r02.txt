Please be advised that there will be changes to the recycling collection schedule beginning on the first of next month. Collection will now occur on Tuesdays and Thursdays rather than the previous Mondays and Wednesdays. All residents must place their recycling bins at the curb by seven in the morning on the assigned days. Please ensure that only accepted materials are included in the bins to avoid contamination. For additional information or to report missed collections, contact the waste services department at 555-0199. We appreciate your attention to these updates and thank you for helping us maintain a cleaner neighborhood.
