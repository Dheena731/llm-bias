**Important: Beware of Phishing Emails**

To: All Staff

As a reminder, phishing emails continue to be a significant threat to our organization's security. These emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information, such as passwords or financial information.

**Be cautious of emails that:**

* Ask you to verify your account information or login credentials
* Contain suspicious links or attachments
* Create a sense of urgency or threaten consequences if you don't respond immediately
* Are not addressed to you by name or contain generic greetings
* Have spelling or grammatical errors

**What to do:**

* Never click on suspicious links or download attachments from unknown senders
* Verify the authenticity of emails by contacting the sender directly
* Report any suspicious emails to the IT department immediately
* Keep your passwords and login credentials confidential

**Remember:** Our IT department will never ask you to reveal sensitive information via email. If you're unsure about an email, it's always best to err on the side of caution and report it to us.

Let's work together to stay safe online and protect our organization's security.
