Subject: Upcoming change to card transaction fees

Dear customer,

We are writing to let you know that our card transaction fees will change on the first day of next month. This update applies to all payments processed through our service, whether they are made in person, online, or over the phone.

The standard fee for domestic card payments will increase from 1.4 percent to 1.7 percent of the transaction value. The fee for international cards will rise from 2.9 percent to 3.2 percent. The fixed charge of 20 cents per transaction will stay the same, and refunds will continue to be free of charge.

The change reflects higher costs we now pay to card networks and partner banks. For several years we absorbed these increases ourselves, but we can no longer do so without affecting the quality of our service. We have kept the adjustment as small as we can.

You do not need to do anything to accept the new rates. They will be applied automatically to every transaction made on or after the effective date, and payments made before that date will be charged at the current rates. Your monthly statement will show the new fees clearly so you can see exactly what has changed.

If you have questions or would like to talk through how this affects your business, our support team is available on weekdays and will be glad to help. Thank you for your continued trust in us.

Kind regards,

The Customer Support Team
