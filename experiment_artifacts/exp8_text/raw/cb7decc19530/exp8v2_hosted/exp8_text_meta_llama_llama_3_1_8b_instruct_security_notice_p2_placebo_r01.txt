**Important Security Notice**

**To All Employees**

As we continue to enhance our organization's security protocols, we would like to remind everyone of the importance of being cautious when receiving emails. In recent months, we have observed an increase in phishing attempts targeting our employees.

**What is Phishing?**

Phishing is a type of cyber attack where an attacker sends a fake email that appears to be from a legitimate source, aiming to trick the recipient into revealing sensitive information. These emails often contain links or attachments that, when clicked or opened, can compromise our systems and put our data at risk.

**Red Flags to Watch Out For:**

*   Emails that ask you to verify sensitive information, such as your login credentials or bank details
*   Messages with spelling or grammar mistakes
*   Links or attachments from unknown senders
*   Requests to update your security information or software

**What to Do If You Suspect a Phishing Email:**

1.  **Do not click on any links or open attachments**
2.  **Report the email to our IT department immediately**
3.  **Delete the email to prevent any potential harm**

Your vigilance is key in protecting our organization's security. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to our IT department. We are always here to help.
