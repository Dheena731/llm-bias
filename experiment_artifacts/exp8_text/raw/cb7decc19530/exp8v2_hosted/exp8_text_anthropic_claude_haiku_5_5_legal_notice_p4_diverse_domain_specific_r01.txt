**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY FOR LIVE PLANT ORDERS**
Halvorsen Pike Nursery, LLC ("the Nursery")
Effective March 1, 2025

**1. Scope.** This notice governs refunds for live plants, bulbs, and bare-root stock shipped by the Nursery. It does not apply to seed packets, dry goods, or pottery, which are covered by the Standard Terms of Sale.

**2. Eligible Claims.** A refund or replacement is available where a plant arrives:

(a) dead or irreversibly damaged, meaning no living tissue is visible at the crown or root collar when scratched with a fingernail;

(b) substantially different from the cultivar described on the order confirmation; or

(c) with a shipping-related defect, such as crushed stems, root ball separation from the container, or frost injury when the package was marked "Protect from Freezing" and the carrier's tracking shows a delivery exception.

**3. Reporting Window.** Claims must be submitted through the order portal within 72 hours of the carrier's recorded delivery time. Claims received after this period will be reviewed only at the Nursery's discretion, and only where the claimant shows the damage could not reasonably have been detected on arrival.

**4. Required Evidence.** Each claim must include:

(a) at least two photographs taken on the day of delivery, one showing the whole plant in its packaging and one showing the root ball or bare-root system;

(b) the order number and the carrier's tracking number; and

(c) for bare-root stock, a photograph of the packing material as received, unopened or opened.

Claims lacking these items will be held for up to ten business days pending receipt.

**5. Exclusions.** No refund is owed for:

(a) plants that died after the 72-hour window, including losses from planting error, drought, overwatering, or pests;

(b) damage caused by delivery to a location different from the shipping address on the order, or by leaving the package unattended in direct sun or rain after delivery confirmation;

(c) hardiness mismatches, where the buyer's USDA zone is colder than the plant's listed rating and the buyer did not request winter protection at checkout; or

(d) orders shipped during a carrier-announced weather hold, where the buyer elected to take delivery anyway.

**6. Form of Remedy.** Eligible claims are resolved, at the Nursery's election, by (i) a refund of the item price to the original payment method, excluding original shipping charges, or (ii) a replacement of equal or greater value at the next available shipping date. Return shipping is not required for plants that arrived dead. For plants that arrived alive but damaged, the Nursery will provide a prepaid return label on request.

**7. Timing.** Approved refunds are issued within ten business days. Card issuers may take an additional one to two billing cycles to post credits.

**8. Questions.** Direct inquiries to claims@halvorsenpike.example or to the order portal. Verbal assurances from staff do not modify this notice.

*Issued by the Office of the Nursery Manager, Halvorsen Pike Nursery, LLC.*
