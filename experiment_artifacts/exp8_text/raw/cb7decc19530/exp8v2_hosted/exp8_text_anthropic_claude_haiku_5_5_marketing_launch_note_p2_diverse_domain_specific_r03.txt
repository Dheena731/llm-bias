**Subscription: Marrow Street Standing Order**

Starting March 3, you can have Marrow Street coffee on a schedule instead of reordering when the bag runs out.

We roast on Tuesdays and Thursdays and ship within 48 hours, so every bag arrives with its roast date printed on the front. Choose a delivery every two, three, or four weeks. You can skip or push a shipment any time before the Monday cutoff, with no fee and no phone call.

The first rotation is Lot 14 from Finca El Recreo in Huila, Colombia: washed process, Caturra and Castillo, roasted medium-light. It's sweet and quick to finish, with red apple and brown sugar, and it holds its flavor well in a V60 at 1:16. If you brew it as espresso, pull it a little shorter than you would a darker roast or it will taste sour. We'll put a short brew card in every box.

Each month we'll tell you what's changing and why. When the next lot comes from a different farm or a different harvest, the card will say so, and the price will stay the same through the end of the year.

Subscribers get the first bag at $18 for 12 oz, then $17 per bag. Sign up at marrowstreet.coffee/standing.
