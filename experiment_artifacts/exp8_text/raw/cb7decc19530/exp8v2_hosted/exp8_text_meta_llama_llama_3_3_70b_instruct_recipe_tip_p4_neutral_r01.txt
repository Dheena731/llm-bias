**Tip: Plan Your Lunches in Advance**

To save time and stress during a busy week, try planning your lunches on the weekend or one day a week. Here's a simple approach:

1. **Choose a theme**: Select a few easy-to-prepare lunch options, such as salads, soups, or sandwiches.
2. **Make a list**: Write down the ingredients you need for each lunch.
3. **Shop and prep**: Buy the necessary ingredients and prep as much as you can in advance, such as chopping veggies or cooking proteins.
4. **Portion and pack**: Divide your prepped ingredients into individual containers or bags for each day of the week.
5. **Label and store**: Label each container with the day of the week and store them in the fridge or freezer.

By planning your lunches in advance, you'll save time during the week, reduce food waste, and ensure you have healthy and delicious meals to fuel your busy days!
