NOTICE OF POLLING STATION LOCATIONS

The following polling stations will be open on election day:

* Polling Station 1: 123 Main Street
* Polling Station 2: 456 Elm Street
* Polling Station 3: 789 Oak Street

These polling stations will be open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM on election day. Voters are encouraged to check their voter registration card or contact the local election office for their assigned polling station location.
