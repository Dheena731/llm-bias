Here's a short cooking tip for preparing a weeknight dinner:

To save time on busy weeknights, cook your protein (such as chicken, beef, or pork) in advance. Simply season the protein with your desired spices and cook it in a skillet or oven until it's about 75% done. Then, refrigerate or freeze it until you're ready to finish cooking it. This way, when it's time to cook dinner, you can simply reheat the protein and add it to a quick-cooking sauce or stir-fry, saving you time and effort in the process.
