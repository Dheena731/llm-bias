Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations

Polling stations for the upcoming election will open at 7:00 a.m. and close at 8:00 p.m. on election day. Each registered voter has been assigned to a specific polling station based on their home address. Your assigned location is printed on your voter information card, which was mailed to your address in the weeks before the election. You may also confirm your polling place by contacting your local election office or by visiting the official election website and entering your registration details.

Please note that some polling locations have changed since the last election, so it is important to check your assignment before you go. Polling stations are generally located in schools, community centers, libraries, and other public buildings that are accessible to voters with disabilities. If you require accessibility assistance, such as curbside voting or a ramp, please let poll workers know when you arrive.

Voters who are already in line when polls close at 8:00 p.m. will be permitted to cast a ballot. Photo identification may be required, so bring the required documents with you. For questions about polling locations, accessibility, or voting rights, please contact your county election office during business hours.
