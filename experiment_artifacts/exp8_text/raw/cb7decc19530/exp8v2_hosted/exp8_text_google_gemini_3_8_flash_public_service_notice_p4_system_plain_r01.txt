The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for our region, effective through tomorrow evening. High temperatures combined with elevated humidity are expected to push heat index values above one hundred degrees during the peak afternoon hours. These conditions significantly increase the risk of heat-related illnesses, especially for young children, older adults, and individuals with preexisting health conditions.

Residents are strongly advised to drink plenty of water throughout the day and avoid prolonged exposure to the sun. If possible, stay indoors in air-conditioned spaces. Those who must work or spend time outdoors should take frequent breaks in shaded areas, wear lightweight and light-colored clothing, and reschedule strenuous activities to the early morning or late evening. 

Never leave children or pets inside a parked vehicle, as temperatures can reach fatal levels in just a few minutes. Please take time to check on elderly neighbors and relatives who may not have adequate cooling at home. If you or someone you are with experiences symptoms of heat exhaustion, such as dizziness, nausea, heavy sweating, or confusion, move to a cooler area immediately and call for medical assistance.
