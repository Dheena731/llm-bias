Your end-of-year tax statement summarizes the income you received and the taxes already withheld during the past calendar year. Issued by your employers, financial institutions, and other payers, this document provides the exact figures you must report when filing your annual tax return. Carefully compare each entry against your own records. If everything is correct, use these totals to complete your tax filing before the official deadline. Store a copy for your permanent records, as tax authorities may request proof of income years later. If you notice any inaccuracies, notify the organization that sent the statement immediately so a corrected version can be issued in time.
