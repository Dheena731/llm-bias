Join us this Saturday for the Riverside Community Day, a free event for neighbors of all ages. The fun begins at 10 a.m. in Calloway Park and continues until 4 p.m. Local food vendors, live music, a children's craft tent, and a neighborhood plant swap will be on hand throughout the day. Volunteers will also be collecting gently used coats and canned goods for the Eastside Food Pantry, so please bring a donation if you can. Parking is limited near the park, so we encourage walking, biking, or carpooling. If rain is in the forecast, the event will move indoors to the Riverside Recreation Center, and we will post any changes here by 8 a.m. Saturday morning. Bring a friend, bring a picnic blanket, and come celebrate what makes our community special. We hope to see you there.
