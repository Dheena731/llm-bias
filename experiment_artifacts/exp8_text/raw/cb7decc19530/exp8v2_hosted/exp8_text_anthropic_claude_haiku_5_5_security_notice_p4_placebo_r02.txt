**Protect Your Account: Turn On Two-Factor Authentication**

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second step when you sign in. After you enter your password, you also confirm your identity with a code or approval from a device you control. This makes it much harder for someone else to access your account, even if they know your password.

**To turn it on:**

1. Sign in to your account.
2. Open your security settings.
3. Select the option to turn on two-factor authentication.
4. Choose how you want to receive your second step, such as a code from an authenticator app or a text message.
5. Follow the on-screen instructions to finish setup.
6. Save the backup codes you are given and store them somewhere safe. You can use them to sign in if you lose access to your device.

Turning on two-factor authentication takes only a few minutes and is one of the most effective ways to keep your account secure.
