Notice of Polling Station Locations

Voters are advised that polling stations for the upcoming election will be open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on election day. Each registered voter is assigned to a specific polling station based on the address on the electoral roll, and the station name and address are printed on the voter information card mailed to every household.

Polling stations in the district will be located at the Central Public Library on Main Street, Northside Community Hall on Alder Road, Riverside Elementary School on Mill Lane, and the Eastgate Senior Center on Fairview Avenue. Each of these buildings has step-free access, accessible parking, and a private voting booth for anyone who needs additional space or assistance. Voters who need help on arrival may ask any member of staff at the entrance.

Please bring a valid form of identification, such as a driver's license, passport, or another government-issued card showing your name. If you have lost your voter information card, you may still vote by presenting identification at the station assigned to your address. Voters who are unsure of their assigned location may contact the district elections office during business hours, and staff will confirm the correct station.

To avoid longer waits, voters are encouraged to visit in the middle of the day, when stations are usually less busy. Anyone in line when the polls close at 8:00 p.m. will be permitted to cast a ballot. Thank you for taking part in the election.
