NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

This notice sets forth the conditions under which a customer may be entitled to a refund for a purchase made from the seller. By completing a purchase, the customer acknowledges and agrees to the terms described here.

A customer may request a full refund for any product or service that is returned in its original condition within thirty (30) days of the date of delivery or purchase, whichever is later. Proof of purchase must accompany every refund request. Products that are damaged, worn, altered, or used beyond inspection are not eligible for a refund, except where the damage was caused by the seller or by a defect in the product.

Certain items are excluded from refund eligibility. These include custom-made goods, perishable items, digital downloads once accessed, and gift cards. Sale items marked as final are likewise non-refundable.

Where a product is defective or does not conform to its description, the customer may request a refund regardless of the thirty-day period, provided the defect is reported within ninety (90) days of delivery. The seller reserves the right to inspect the product before issuing any refund.

Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within ten (10) business days of approval. The seller is not responsible for delays caused by a financial institution. Shipping costs are refundable only where the return results from a seller error or a defective product.

Nothing in this notice limits any statutory rights a customer may hold under applicable consumer protection law. Where such law provides greater rights than those stated here, the applicable law shall govern.

The seller may amend this notice at any time. Amendments apply only to purchases made after the effective date of the revised notice.
