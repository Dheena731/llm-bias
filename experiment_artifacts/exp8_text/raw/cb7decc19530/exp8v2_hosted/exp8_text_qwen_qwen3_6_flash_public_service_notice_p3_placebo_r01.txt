NOTICE: RECYCLING COLLECTION UPDATES

Dear Residents,

Please be advised that our community recycling program will change starting next month. To improve processing efficiency and reduce contamination, we are updating both the collection schedule and accepted materials. Paper, cardboard, and plastic containers will now be collected together in a single bin instead of requiring separate sorting. Additionally, all recycling carts will follow a fixed biweekly pickup schedule rather than the current weekly service. Place your recycling cart at the curb by early morning on your assigned collection day, and ensure all items remain loose inside the container. Glass, plastic bags, and food-soiled packaging are no longer accepted through curbside pickup and must be transported to the designated community drop-off center. Complete guidelines and updated schedules are available at the public works office and on community bulletin boards. Thank you for your cooperation as we implement these improvements.

Public Works Department
