We're pleased to announce the Clear Budget Template, a spreadsheet built for people who want to understand their money without wrestling with formulas or complicated software.

The template works in Excel, Google Sheets, and most other spreadsheet programs. You begin by entering your monthly income and your regular expenses, and the sheet organizes everything into a single overview. Spending is sorted into familiar categories such as housing, food, transportation, and savings, and you can rename or rearrange any of them to match your own life. Each month has its own tab, so you can compare one period against another and see how your habits change over time.

We designed it around the questions people actually ask. How much do I have left after bills? Where did my grocery money go? Am I saving as much as I planned? The summary page answers these at a glance, showing your planned spending beside your actual spending and highlighting any category that has run over. A built-in savings tracker lets you set goals for an emergency fund, a vacation, or a large purchase, and it shows how close you are to reaching each one.

Setup takes about fifteen minutes. The template includes sample data so you can see how everything fits together, along with a short guide that explains each section in plain language. Once you clear the sample entries, the formulas update automatically, so there is nothing to repair or recalculate by hand. Cells that need your input are shaded lightly, and everything else is locked to prevent accidental changes.

Whether you are budgeting for the first time or looking for a simpler alternative to a subscription app, the Clear Budget Template gives you a practical place to start. It is available today as a one-time purchase, with free updates for the first year. Your data stays in your own file, on your own device, and under your control.
