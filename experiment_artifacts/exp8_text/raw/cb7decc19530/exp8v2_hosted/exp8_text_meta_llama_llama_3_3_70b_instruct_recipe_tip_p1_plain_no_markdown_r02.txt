To make a quick and easy weeknight dinner, consider preparing a one-pot meal such as a stir-fry or skillet dinner. Start by gathering all the necessary ingredients and chopping any vegetables before you begin cooking. This will help the cooking process go more smoothly and prevent delays. Choose a protein such as chicken, beef, or tofu and add it to the pot along with your favorite vegetables and seasonings. Cook everything together over medium-high heat, stirring frequently, until the ingredients are cooked through and the flavors have melded together. Serve hot over rice or noodles for a satisfying and stress-free meal.
