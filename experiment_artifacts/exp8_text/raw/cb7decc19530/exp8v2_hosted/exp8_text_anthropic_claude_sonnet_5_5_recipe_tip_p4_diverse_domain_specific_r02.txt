**Cook one grain and one sauce on Sunday, then change the third element each day.**

Make a big pot of farro, rice, or barley, and a jar of something sharp: lemon-tahini, miso-ginger dressing, or a chili-garlic oil. Then each night, toss in whatever is about to expire in your fridge: a roasted sheet pan of broccoli on Monday, canned chickpeas and cucumber on Tuesday, a soft-boiled egg and wilting spinach on Wednesday.

The grain keeps for five days. The sauce keeps lunch from tasting like the same lunch. The third element is where you use up odds and ends instead of shopping for a full week of separate recipes.

Two small habits help:

- **Pack the sauce separately** (a little jar or the corner of a container) so the grain doesn't go gummy by noon.
- **Cook the grain slightly underdone.** It will finish softening when reheated, and it won't turn to paste by Thursday.

By Friday, whatever's left becomes fried rice, which counts as a lunch and also as cleaning out the fridge.
