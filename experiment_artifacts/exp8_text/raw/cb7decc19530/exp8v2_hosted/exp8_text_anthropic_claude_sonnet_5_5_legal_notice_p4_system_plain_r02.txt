A purchaser is eligible for a refund only if a written request is submitted to the Company within thirty (30) calendar days of the original date of purchase. Requests received after this period shall be deemed untimely and may be declined at the sole discretion of the Company, except where a refund is required by applicable law. Each request must include proof of purchase and a brief statement of the reason for the request.

Physical goods must be returned in their original condition, unused, and in their original packaging in order to qualify for a refund. Goods that show signs of use, damage, or alteration, or that are returned without the necessary components or accessories, shall not be eligible. The following are expressly excluded from refund eligibility: custom or made-to-order items, perishable goods, items marked as final sale, and any fees that are identified at the time of purchase as non-refundable. Shipping and handling charges are not refundable unless the refund is due to an error made by the Company.
