**Reminder: Protect Patient Confidentiality**

As we continue to work with sensitive patient information, it's essential that we prioritize data security. A crucial step in maintaining confidentiality is locking your computer screen when away from your workstation.

Please ensure that you lock your screen or log off your workstation when:

* Taking a break or leaving your desk
* Attending meetings or training sessions
* Stepping away to consult with colleagues

This simple habit helps safeguard patient records and prevents unauthorized access. Let's work together to maintain the trust and confidentiality that our patients expect from us.

If you have any questions or concerns about our data security protocols, please don't hesitate to reach out to our IT department.
