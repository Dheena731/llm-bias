For the best depth of flavor, season your vegetables in two distinct stages. Before roasting, thoroughly dry the vegetables and toss them with oil, kosher salt, black pepper, and sturdy, heat-tolerant spices like garlic powder or dried oregano, which will bloom in the heat without burning. The moment the tray comes out of the oven, finish the hot vegetables with a bright splash of acid, such as fresh lemon juice or red wine vinegar, along with chopped fresh herbs. The initial seasoning caramelizes and penetrates the vegetables during cooking, while the acidic finish cuts through the rich, roasted sweetness to bring the entire dish into balance.
