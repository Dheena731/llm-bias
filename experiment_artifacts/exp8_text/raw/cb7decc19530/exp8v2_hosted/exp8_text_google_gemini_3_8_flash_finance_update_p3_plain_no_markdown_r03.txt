We have introduced a new savings goal feature to help you take control of your financial future. Starting today, you can create customized targets for specific milestones, whether you are planning a vacation, building an emergency fund, or saving for a major purchase. The tool allows you to name your goal, set a target amount, and establish a target completion date. You can also schedule automatic transfers to steadily fund your objectives without extra effort. Visual progress tracking is built directly into your dashboard, making it simple to see how close you are to reaching each milestone. This update is now live in your account, and you can create your first goal by navigating to the savings section.
