**Introducing BudgetBase: The Simple Budgeting Template That Does the Math for You**

We're excited to announce BudgetBase, a free-to-customize spreadsheet template designed to help you take control of your money without the complexity of expensive apps or the hassle of building a spreadsheet from scratch.

BudgetBase comes preloaded with everything you need to track your finances:

- **Monthly income and expense tracking**, with categories for housing, groceries, transportation, subscriptions, and more
- **Automatic totals and balances**, so you can see where you stand at a glance
- **Savings goal tracker** to monitor progress toward your emergency fund, vacation, or big purchases
- **Visual dashboard** with charts that update as you enter your data
- **Debt payoff planner** to map out your path to becoming debt-free

BudgetBase works in Excel, Google Sheets, and Numbers, and requires no coding or formula knowledge. Simply enter your numbers, and the template handles the rest.

BudgetBase is available now for download at no cost. Whether you're budgeting for the first time or refining a system you already use, BudgetBase gives you a clear picture of your finances in minutes.
