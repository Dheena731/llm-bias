**To:** All Staff  
**From:** SecOps / Internal IT  
**Subject:** Observed Phishing: Spoofed “DocuSign: Q4 Incentive Review”  

Between 08:30 and 10:15 this morning, our mail gateway caught six inbound messages claiming to be DocuSign notifications regarding "FY24 Employee Incentive Adjustments." 

The senders are masked as internal HR, but the actual envelope sender is an external address (`notifications@docusign-secure-portal[.]net`). The embedded links route to a Cloudflare-hosted credential harvester built to mirror our Okta login page.

**What to do:**
* If the email is in your inbox: Do not forward it to colleagues or HR. Click the **Report Message** button in Outlook to pull it into the triage queue.
* If you clicked the link and entered your domain password: Ping **@sec-oncall** in Slack immediately or call the IT help desk at ext. 4401. We need to revoke active session tokens and roll your credentials; do not wait to see if an MFA push happens.

Legitimate equity or compensation updates are distributed through BambooHR notifications, not direct DocuSign links from unknown routing domains.
