**Notice to All Staff: Recognizing and Reporting Phishing Emails**

Phishing emails are messages designed to trick you into revealing passwords, sending money, or opening harmful files. They often look like they come from a colleague, a manager, a bank, or a familiar service.

**Warning signs include:**
- Requests for passwords, account details, or payment, especially if urgent
- Unexpected attachments or links
- Sender addresses that look slightly wrong, such as a misspelled domain name
- Messages that pressure you to act quickly or keep something secret
- Greetings that are generic, or errors in spelling and grammar

**What to do:**
1. Do not click links or open attachments in a suspicious email.
2. Do not reply with personal or login information.
3. If you are unsure whether a request is genuine, contact the sender through a method you already know is correct, not by replying to the email.
4. Report the suspicious email to the IT team right away, even if you are not certain it is malicious. If you already clicked a link or entered a password, report it immediately.

Reporting quickly helps protect you and your colleagues. Thank you for staying alert.
