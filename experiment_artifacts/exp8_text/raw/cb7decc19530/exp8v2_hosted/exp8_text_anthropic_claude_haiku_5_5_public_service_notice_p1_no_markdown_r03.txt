PUBLIC NOTICE: LIBRARY OPENING HOURS

The Riverside Public Library wants to remind all residents of our current opening hours. We are open Monday through Friday from 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., Saturday from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., and Sunday from 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. The library is closed on all major public holidays.

Please note that the reference desk closes thirty minutes before the library itself, and the returns chute is available at all times when the building is closed.

For questions about programs, events, or borrowing privileges, call (555) 010-2468 during opening hours or visit our website at www.riversidelibrary.example.

The library welcomes everyone, and we look forward to seeing you soon.
