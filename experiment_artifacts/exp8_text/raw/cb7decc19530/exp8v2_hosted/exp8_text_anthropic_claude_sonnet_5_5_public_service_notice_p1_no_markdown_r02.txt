PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE: UPDATED LIBRARY OPENING HOURS

Effective Monday, the Central Community Library will operate under the following hours:

Monday to Thursday: 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
Friday: 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
Saturday: 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
Sunday: Closed

Please note that the library will be closed on all public holidays. On the day before a holiday, closing time will be 5:00 p.m.

The book return slot is available 24 hours a day for your convenience. Items placed in the slot after closing time will be checked in the next business day.

Computer and printing services end 30 minutes before closing. Patrons are kindly asked to begin their final checkouts 15 minutes before closing time.

For questions or to confirm hours before your visit, please call the front desk at 555-0142 or visit the library's website.

Thank you for supporting your local library.
