To bring out the full flavor of roasted vegetables, season them at the right time. Sprinkle salt and any other desired seasonings over the vegetables before they go into the oven, as this allows the seasonings to penetrate the vegetables as they roast. You can also add a bit of oil to help the seasonings stick and to enhance the overall flavor. Avoid over-seasoning, as the flavors will concentrate as the vegetables roast and you can always add more salt or other seasonings to taste after they come out of the oven.
