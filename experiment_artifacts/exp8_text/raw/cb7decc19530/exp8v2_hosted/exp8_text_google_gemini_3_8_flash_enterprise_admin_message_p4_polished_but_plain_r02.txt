This is an administrative notice to all department managers and system owners regarding the commencement of our quarterly user access review. In accordance with our security policies and regulatory compliance requirements, all supervisors must audit and validate the system permissions granted to their team members. Please review your staff listings across all enterprise applications, internal networks, and sensitive data repositories. Ensure that permissions align strictly with current job responsibilities, and identify any accounts belonging to departed personnel or transferred employees that require modification or removal. All access approvals and revocation requests must be submitted through the standard identity management dashboard by the close of business next Friday. Your timely participation is essential for maintaining our security posture and safeguarding company resources. If you have questions regarding specific permissions or encounter technical issues during the process, please contact the IT compliance team.
