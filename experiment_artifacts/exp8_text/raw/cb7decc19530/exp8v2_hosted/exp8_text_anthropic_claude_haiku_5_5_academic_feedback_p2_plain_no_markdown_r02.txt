Thank you for sending your methods section. You have clearly described how you recruited participants and what materials you used, and the step-by-step account of the procedure makes it easy to follow what you did. Your decision to report the exclusion criteria is a strength, since readers need that information to judge who your sample represents.

There are a few areas to strengthen before this is ready for submission. The description of your analysis is the most important. You say you ran a series of comparisons, but you do not name the statistical tests or state the significance threshold you used. Please specify each test, the reason you chose it, and whether you checked its assumptions. The sample size also needs a justification. Explain how you arrived at your number of participants, whether through a power analysis or another rationale.

Some sentences shift between past and present tense, and a few passages describe intentions ("we will") rather than what was actually done. Methods sections are written in the past tense, so a careful edit will make the account more consistent. Finally, the measures section would benefit from reporting the reliability of your questionnaire in your own sample, not only the value reported in the original validation study.

Overall, the structure is sound and the content is mostly complete. Address the analysis and sample size points first, as they will matter most to your reviewers, and then tidy the tense and reliability details. I am happy to look at a revised draft.
