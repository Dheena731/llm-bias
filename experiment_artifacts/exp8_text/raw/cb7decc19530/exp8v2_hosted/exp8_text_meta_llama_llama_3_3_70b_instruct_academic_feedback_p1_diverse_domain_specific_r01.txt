Here is a short academic feedback note on a draft literature review:

"I appreciate the effort to synthesize the existing research on the intersection of socio-technical systems and environmental governance. The section on the role of institutional frameworks in shaping sustainability outcomes is particularly strong, and the discussion of Ostrom's work on polycentric governance is well-integrated. However, I think there are opportunities to further clarify the conceptual boundaries between the notions of 'regime stability' and 'system resilience' in the context of environmental policy implementation. For instance, the literature on regime stability tends to focus on the durability of institutional arrangements, whereas system resilience often emphasizes the capacity of socio-ecological systems to absorb and adapt to disturbances. More explicitly distinguishing between these concepts could help to strengthen the overall narrative and set up a clearer research agenda. Additionally, considering the work of scholars such as Meadowcroft and Newig on the governance of sustainability transitions could provide additional insights into the complex interplay between institutional, social, and ecological factors."
