**Exciting News: Introducing Savings Goals! 🎯**

Reaching your financial targets just got a whole lot easier. With our new **Savings Goals** feature, you can now turn your dreams—big or small—into actionable plans.

**What you can do:**
* **Set Custom Targets:** Name your goals (like *"Summer Vacation"* or *"Emergency Fund"*) and set a target amount and deadline.
* **Track Your Progress:** Watch your progress in real-time with visual milestones that keep you motivated.
* **Automate Your Savings:** Set up recurring transfers so you can hit your goals effortlessly without thinking about it.

Ready to make it happen? Head to the **Savings** tab in the app and create your first goal today!
