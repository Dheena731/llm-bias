# Cookie Notice

**1. What cookies are**

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. They allow the website to recognise your device on later visits and to remember certain information about your activity.

**2. How this website uses cookies**

This website uses the following categories of cookies:

- **Strictly necessary cookies.** These are required for the website to operate and to provide security features. They cannot be switched off in our systems and do not require your consent.
- **Performance and analytics cookies.** These collect information about how visitors use the website, such as which pages are viewed most often, so that we can understand and improve how the website works. This information is collected in aggregate form.
- **Functionality cookies.** These remember choices you make, such as language or display preferences, to provide a more consistent experience.
- **Third-party cookies.** Some features of this website may be provided by external services that place their own cookies on your device. Those cookies are governed by the privacy and cookie policies of the relevant third parties.

Performance, analytics, functionality, and third-party cookies are not placed on your device unless you have given consent where consent is required by applicable law.

**3. Your choices**

When you first visit this website, you may be asked to accept or reject non-essential cookies. You may change your choice at any time by using the cookie settings available on this website. You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that if you block or delete strictly necessary cookies, some parts of this website may not function correctly.

**4. Changes to this notice**

We may update this Cookie Notice from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use or in applicable law. The date of the most recent update will be shown at the top of this notice.

**5. Contact**

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please contact us using the contact details published on this website.
