Public Notice

A heat advisory has been issued for the area, effective immediately. Temperatures are expected to reach record highs, posing a risk to public health and safety.

Residents are advised to take precautions to protect themselves from the heat. Stay indoors during the hottest part of the day, and avoid strenuous activities. If you must work or engage in outdoor activities, take regular breaks to rest and hydrate.

Older adults, young children, and individuals with chronic health conditions are particularly vulnerable to heat-related illnesses. Check on these individuals frequently and offer assistance if needed.

The city's cooling centers will be open to provide a refuge from the heat. Residents can visit these locations to stay cool and hydrated.

Residents are also reminded to drink plenty of water, and to limit their use of electrical appliances to avoid overheating their homes.

For further information, please contact the local public health department or visit their website.
