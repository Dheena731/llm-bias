Recycling collection changes are being implemented in the area. Starting from April 15th, the collection day for recyclables will be changed to Thursday. Residents are advised to have their recyclables out for collection by 8am on the new collection day.

Additionally, the types of materials that can be placed in the recycling bins will also be changing. Only paper, plastic bottles and cans, glass, and metal will be accepted in the recycling bins. Other materials such as food waste, textiles, and ceramics will not be collected.

Residents are asked to check their waste collection calendar for further details and to familiarize themselves with the new collection schedule and guidelines.
