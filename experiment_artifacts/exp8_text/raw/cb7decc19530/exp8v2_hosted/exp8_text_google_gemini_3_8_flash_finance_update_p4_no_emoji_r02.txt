An end-of-year tax statement is an official document summarizing your financial activity over the past calendar year. Depending on who issued it, this statement typically details the total income you earned, taxes withheld, interest paid, or charitable contributions you made between January and December.

You will need this document to accurately report your income and claim any eligible deductions when filing your annual tax return. Please review the information carefully to ensure it is correct and keep a copy for your records.
