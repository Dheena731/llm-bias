**🎙️ Now Live: *Fluent Moments***

Learning a language shouldn't feel like homework. That's why we made *Fluent Moments*, a podcast for curious people who want to speak with confidence, one short episode at a time.

**What to expect:**
- **Bite-sized lessons:** Episodes run 10 to 15 minutes, so they fit your commute, workout, or coffee break.
- **Real-life conversations:** Skip the textbook phrases. You'll learn how people actually talk.
- **Culture included:** Idioms, etiquette, and stories that explain why a language sounds the way it does.
- **Practice built in:** Every episode ends with a quick speaking challenge to help new words stick.

**Where to start:**
Our first three episodes are available now:
1. *Hello, and Everything After:* Greetings and small talk
2. *Ordering Like a Local:* Cafés, menus, and polite requests
3. *Mistakes Are Welcome:* Why getting it wrong helps you get it right

New episodes drop every Tuesday.
