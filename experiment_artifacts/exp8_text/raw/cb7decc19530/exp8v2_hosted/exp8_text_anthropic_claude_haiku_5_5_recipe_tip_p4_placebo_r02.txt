Batch-cook one versatile base on the weekend, such as a pot of grains, a tray of roasted vegetables, or a large batch of chicken or chickpeas. Then, each day, combine it with a different sauce or topping, like pesto, salsa, or a squeeze of lemon and herbs, so your lunches stay varied without requiring any extra cooking during the week. Store portions in separate containers in the fridge, and you can grab one on your way out the door.
