To achieve grains that remain separate and fluffy rather than clumping together, begin by thoroughly rinsing your rice under cold running water until the water runs clear. This essential step washes away excess surface starch, which is the primary cause of gumminess. Once drained, measure the water carefully, as using slightly less liquid than the package suggests can help prevent a soft texture. After cooking, remove the pot from the heat and let it sit covered for ten minutes before gently fluffing the grains with a fork to release trapped steam and ensure each grain stays distinct.
