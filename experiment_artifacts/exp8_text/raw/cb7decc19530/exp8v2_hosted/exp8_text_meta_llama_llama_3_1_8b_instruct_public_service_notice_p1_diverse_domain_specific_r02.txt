**Notice to Library Patrons**

As we continue to navigate the complexities of our post-pandemic world, we want to remind you of our current library hours. Effective immediately, our main library branch will be open to the public from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Monday through Thursday, and 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM on Fridays and Saturdays.

Please note that our branch libraries will maintain their own unique schedules. For example, the downtown branch will be open 12:00 PM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, while the north branch will be closed on Sundays. You can always check our website or call us for the most up-to-date information.

We strive to provide a welcoming and inclusive space for our community, and we appreciate your understanding as we adapt to the changing needs of our patrons. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us directly.

**Library Hours:**

* Main Library Branch: Monday - Thursday: 9:00 AM - 8:00 PM; Friday - Saturday: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM
* Downtown Branch: Monday - Friday: 12:00 PM - 6:00 PM; Closed Sundays
* North Branch: Monday - Friday: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM; Closed Saturdays and Sundays

Visit our website for a complete list of branch hours and services.
