**NOTICE OF CHANGE OF POLLING PLACES**
**Harlan County Board of Elections**
For the Tuesday, November 4 General Election

Three polling places have moved since the May primary. If your precinct is listed below, **do not go to your old location.**

| Precinct | Former location | **New location** |
|---|---|---|
| 04 – Millbrook | Millbrook Elementary gym | **Millbrook Fire Hall, 212 Quarry Rd.** (use side entrance, Bay 2) |
| 11 – East Ward | St. Anselm Parish Hall | **East Ward Branch Library, 40 Delancey St.** (meeting room, ground floor) |
| 17 – Route 9 | Grange Hall | **Harlan Township Municipal Building, 1850 Route 9** (rear lot) |

**Why:** Millbrook Elementary is under construction through December. St. Anselm's hall was sold in August. The Grange could not guarantee heat or an accessible entrance.

**Hours:** 6:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Anyone in line at 8:00 p.m. will be allowed to vote.

**Notifications:** Affected voters are being mailed new voter information cards. Mail may arrive after this notice is posted. Signs will be posted at each former location, but they do not replace checking your precinct.

**Accessibility:** All three new sites have level or ramped entrances, accessible parking, and an accessible voting unit. Curbside voting is available at each site. Ask any poll worker, or have someone go in and request it for you.

**If you go to the wrong place:** Poll workers can look up your precinct and direct you. If you can't reach the correct site before closing, you may cast a provisional ballot. It will be counted only for the races you are eligible to vote in, so going to your assigned precinct is better.

**Check your precinct:** harlancountyelections.example/lookup, or call the office at (555) 014-2290, weekdays 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. The line will stay open until 8:00 p.m. on Election Day.

All other precinct locations are unchanged.
