**HEAT ADVISORY // METROPOLITAN COUNTY**  
Issued: July 12, 2024 | Effective: 10:00 AM Tuesday through 7:00 PM Friday  

The National Weather Service has issued a Heat Advisory for Metropolitan County. Daytime heat indices will reach 104–108°F with overnight lows remaining near 77–79°F, limiting nocturnal heat dissipation.

Public Health and Emergency Management directives apply for the advisory window:
• Maintain mechanical cooling at or above 78°F. For residents facing utility disconnection risk, dial 311 to activate the Summer Assistance Program before Friday.
• Outdoor employers must enforce mandatory rest cycles at 60-minute intervals once heat index crosses 98°F. Shaded rest areas and salt/electrolyte supplies are required under county occupational guidance.
• Designated cooling shelters operate 11:00 AM–7:00 PM daily at the Main Branch Library, District Recreation Centers, and the Municipal Auditorium. Walk-in access only. Temperature-controlled parking garages require badge verification.
• Seniors, individuals on diuretic medications, and residents without mechanical cooling may request wellness checks via the county portal. Personnel will perform exterior door observations; interior visits occur solely with occupant consent.

Track live conditions through the county emergency app or wpc.nws.gov. This notice escalates to a Heat Emergency if the heat index remains ≥108°F for two consecutive days.
