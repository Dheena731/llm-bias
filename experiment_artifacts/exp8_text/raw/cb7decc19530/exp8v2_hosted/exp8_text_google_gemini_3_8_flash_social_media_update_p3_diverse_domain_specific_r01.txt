Saturday's Repair Café is at the Legion hall from 10 to 1. 

If you've got a toaster that only toasts on one side, a lamp with a chewed cord, or a winter coat missing a tooth in the zipper, bring it by. We’ve got three soldering irons, a bench grinder for dull garden shears, and someone who actually knows how to time a 1970s Singer sewing machine. 

Rules are the same as usual: you have to hang out while it’s being worked on (no drop-offs), and if it needs a proprietary part, order it ahead of time and bring it in the box. 

Coffee is on by 9:45. See you in the basement.
