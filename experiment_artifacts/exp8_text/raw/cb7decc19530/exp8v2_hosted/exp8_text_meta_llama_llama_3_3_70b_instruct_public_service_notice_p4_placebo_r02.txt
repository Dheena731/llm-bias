**Public Notice: Heat Advisory in Effect**

A heat advisory has been issued for our area, effective immediately. Due to extremely high temperatures and humidity, the heat index is expected to reach dangerous levels over the next 48 hours.

Residents are advised to take precautions to stay safe in the heat. This includes staying hydrated by drinking plenty of water, staying indoors during the hottest part of the day, and avoiding strenuous outdoor activities.

Vulnerable populations, such as the elderly, young children, and those with pre-existing medical conditions, are especially at risk and should take extra precautions to stay cool.

For more information and tips on how to stay safe during the heat advisory, please visit our website or contact local health authorities. Stay cool and stay safe!
