Fresh herbs stay vibrant for much longer when you treat them like cut flowers. Trim about half an inch from the stems, then place them upright in a glass with an inch or two of water, and cover loosely with a plastic bag before refrigerating. Hardy herbs such as rosemary, thyme, and sage can also be wrapped in a damp paper towel and kept in a sealed container. Tender herbs like basil are best kept at room temperature, away from direct sun, since cold air tends to blacken their leaves. Change the water every couple of days, and your herbs should stay fresh for a week or more.
