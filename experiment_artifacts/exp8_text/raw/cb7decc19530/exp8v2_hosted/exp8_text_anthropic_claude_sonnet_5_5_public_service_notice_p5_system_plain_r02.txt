Notice of Polling Station Locations

Voters are advised that polling stations for the upcoming election will be open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on Election Day. Each registered voter is assigned to a specific polling station based on their home address, and you must vote at that location. The assigned station is printed on the voter information card mailed to every registered household. If you have not received your card or have misplaced it, you can confirm your polling place by contacting the Elections Office or by checking the official election website and entering your address.

This year, several polling stations have moved from their previous locations. Residents who voted at the Northside Community Hall in past elections should now go to the Riverside Public Library, and those who previously used the Maple Street School gymnasium should report to the Fairview Recreation Center. All relocated stations are wheelchair accessible and have designated parking for voters with limited mobility. Signs will be posted at the former locations directing voters to the new sites.

Please bring a valid form of photo identification, such as a driver's license or passport. If you do not have photo identification, you may present two other documents that show your name and address, such as a utility bill or bank statement. Voters who arrive before closing time and are in line at 8:00 p.m. will be permitted to cast their ballots.

For questions, assistance with accessibility needs, or information about early and mail-in voting, please call the Elections Office at the number listed on your voter information card during regular business hours. The office thanks all residents for taking part in this important civic process.
