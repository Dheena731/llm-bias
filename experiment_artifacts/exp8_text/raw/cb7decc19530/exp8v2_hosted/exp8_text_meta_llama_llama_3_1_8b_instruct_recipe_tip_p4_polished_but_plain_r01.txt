When planning lunches for a busy week, consider the following approach. Start by identifying the main ingredients you have on hand, such as staples like rice, pasta, and canned goods. Then, choose a few simple protein sources like chicken, beans, or tofu. Next, think about the types of dishes you can make with these ingredients, such as stir-fries, salads, and soups. Consider also preparing a few batches of a single dish that can be reheated throughout the week. This approach allows for flexibility and saves time during the week.
