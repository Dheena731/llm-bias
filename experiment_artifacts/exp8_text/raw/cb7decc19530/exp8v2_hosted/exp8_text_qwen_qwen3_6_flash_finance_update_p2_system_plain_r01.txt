We are writing to inform you of an upcoming adjustment to our card transaction fees. Beginning on the first day of next month, the processing fee applied to all credit and debit card payments will increase from two point five percent to three percent. This change reflects the ongoing rise in payment network costs and regulatory compliance expenses that affect how we process transactions. The adjustment will apply to all future purchases made with cards after the effective date. Previous charges remain unchanged. If you have any questions about this update or need further clarification, please contact our support team directly. Thank you for your continued business and understanding as we work to maintain reliable service for all customers.
