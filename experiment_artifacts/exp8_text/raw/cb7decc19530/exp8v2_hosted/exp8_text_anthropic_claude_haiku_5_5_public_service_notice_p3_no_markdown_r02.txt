NOTICE TO RESIDENTS: CHANGES TO RECYCLING COLLECTION

Please be advised that recycling collection schedules will change beginning Monday, March 3. Recycling will now be collected every other week on Tuesdays, rather than weekly. Your collection week will be indicated on the enclosed calendar.

Please keep the following in mind:

Place recycling bins at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on your scheduled collection day.

Rinse all containers and remove lids before placing them in your bin.

Do not bag recyclable materials. Loose items are accepted, but plastic bags, plastic film, and food waste must go in the regular trash.

Bulky cardboard should be flattened and bundled with twine no larger than three feet in length.

Regular trash and yard waste collection will not change.

For questions or to request a replacement bin, please call the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567 or visit our website at www.citywebsite.gov/recycling.

Thank you for your cooperation as we work to make recycling more efficient for our community.
