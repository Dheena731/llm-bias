Instead of assembling identical meals days in advance, prepare versatile building blocks to mix and match throughout the week. Roast two trays of sturdy vegetables, cook a large batch of a neutral grain, and prepare a flexible protein such as shredded chicken or baked tofu. Storing these components separately allows you to quickly assemble grain bowls, wraps, or greens each morning in less than five minutes. Rotating different sauces, dressings, or crunchy toppings keeps the meals feeling fresh and varied, helping you stay well fed without the fatigue of eating the exact same dish every day.
