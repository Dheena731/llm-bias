**Subject: Your week, before Monday**

Take two minutes to look at the last seven days before you plan the next one:

- Sessions completed against what you planned
- Resting heart rate compared with last week (it shifts with sleep and stress, so a jump often means something)
- Any lift where reps or weight went backward, and whether you can name the reason

If something looks off, change one thing next week, not everything.
