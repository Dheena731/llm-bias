Pick one day, ideally Sunday, and spend about an hour preparing the building blocks of your lunches rather than full meals. Cook a big batch of a grain such as rice or quinoa, roast a tray of vegetables, and prepare one protein like grilled chicken, hard-boiled eggs, or chickpeas. Wash and chop some raw vegetables and mix up a jar of dressing or sauce. Store everything in separate containers in the fridge. Each morning you can combine the pieces into a different lunch in a couple of minutes, such as a grain bowl on Monday, a wrap on Tuesday, and a salad on Wednesday. This keeps your meals from getting repetitive, helps food stay fresh and not soggy, and means you are never starting from scratch when the week gets hectic.
