While the overarching topic of this study is timely and engaging, the central research question remains ambiguous throughout the introduction. The manuscript discusses several broad themes and intentions, but it does not articulate a focused, clearly defined inquiry or testable hypothesis. This lack of precision makes it difficult to assess whether the methodology is appropriately targeted and how directly the results resolve the stated problem. To strengthen the paper, the authors should explicitly define the primary research question near the conclusion of the introduction, clarifying the key variables or phenomena of interest and delineating the scope of the investigation. Doing so will provide the reader with a clear benchmark against which to evaluate the study design and findings.
