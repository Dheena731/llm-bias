Here's a short reminder:

**Take a Break!**

In today's digital age, it's easy to get sucked into screens for hours on end. But your body (and mind) need a break!

Take a few minutes every hour to step away from your screen. Get up, stretch, move around, and refresh your eyes.

**Benefits of taking breaks:**

* Reduced eye strain and headaches
* Improved focus and productivity
* Boosted energy and creativity
* Enhanced overall well-being

**Set a reminder:**

* Set a timer to remind you to take a break every 60-90 minutes
* Use a screen-free time tracker to help you stay on track
* Make breaks a habit by incorporating them into your daily routine

**Your body (and mind) will thank you!**
