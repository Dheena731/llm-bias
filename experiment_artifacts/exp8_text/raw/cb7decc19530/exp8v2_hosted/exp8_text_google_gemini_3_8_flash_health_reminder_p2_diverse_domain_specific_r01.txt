Check the deli quart tucked on the bottom shelf of your prep station. 

If the ice has completely melted and the blue tape on the rim is turning soggy, you haven’t touched it since morning prep. The heat off the salamander and flat-top dries you out long before you actually feel thirsty, and by the time the dinner push starts, a dehydration headache will kill your timing on tickets. 

Kill the rest of that quart now, refill it from the soda gun, and drop two ice cubes in. Don't wait for the mid-service crash.
