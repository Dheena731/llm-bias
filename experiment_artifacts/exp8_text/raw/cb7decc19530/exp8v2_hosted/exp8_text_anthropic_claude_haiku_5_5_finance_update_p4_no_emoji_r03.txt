Subject: Understanding Your Year-End Tax Statement

Hello,

Your year-end tax statement summarizes the income you earned and the taxes withheld or paid during the past year. You will need it when you file your tax return.

Here are the key items to review:

- **Total income:** The amount you earned from wages, interest, dividends, or other sources.
- **Taxes withheld:** The federal, state, and local taxes already deducted from your pay or payments.
- **Deductions and credits:** Any pre-tax contributions, such as retirement or health savings, that reduced your taxable income.
- **Tax identification details:** Your name, address, and taxpayer ID, which should match your tax return.

If the withheld amount is more than you owe, you may receive a refund. If it is less, you may need to pay the difference by the filing deadline.

If anything on the statement looks incorrect, contact the issuing organization for a corrected form before you file. For questions about how these figures apply to your specific situation, consult a tax professional.
