Please be advised that polling station locations for the upcoming election have been finalized. Voters can locate their designated polling place by entering their residential address on the official portal of the local election authority. Polling stations will open at seven in the morning and remain open until eight at night on election day. Residents must bring a valid form of photo identification when they arrive to vote. Anyone who has moved within the jurisdiction since their last registration should verify their current polling location online to ensure they cast their ballot at the correct site. Additional details regarding early voting options and accessibility services are available through the election office telephone line.
