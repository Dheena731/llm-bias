The local library is open to serve the community six days a week. On Mondays, Tuesdays, and Thursdays, the library is open from 9am to 8pm, allowing patrons to visit before or after work, as well as during their lunch breaks. On Wednesdays and Fridays, the library is open from 9am to 6pm, and on Saturdays, it is open from 10am to 5pm. The library is closed on Sundays, as well as on public holidays. Please note that these hours are subject to change, and it is always a good idea to call ahead or check the library's website to confirm hours before planning a visit.
