Polling stations for the upcoming election will be located at various sites throughout the community. Voters can cast their ballots at the local high school, the community center, or the public library. Each polling station will be open from 8am to 8pm on election day, and voters are reminded to bring their identification and proof of residency with them. A map of polling station locations is available at the town hall, and voters can also contact the election office to confirm their assigned polling station. Voters are encouraged to take advantage of early voting options or to vote by mail if they are unable to visit a polling station on election day.
