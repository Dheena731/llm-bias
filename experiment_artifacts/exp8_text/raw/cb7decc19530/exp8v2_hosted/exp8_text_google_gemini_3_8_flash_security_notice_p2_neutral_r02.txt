**SECURITY NOTICE: Stay Alert for Phishing Emails**

**To All Staff,**

We have recently seen an increase in targeted phishing attempts designed to compromise company data and credentials. Please remain vigilant when reviewing your inbox.

**Common Red Flags:**
* **Artificial Urgency:** Demands for immediate action, wire transfers, or gift cards (often pretending to be from executives).
* **Mismatched Senders:** The sender name looks familiar, but the actual email address is unusual or misspelled.
* **Suspicious Links or Attachments:** Unexpected invoices, password reset prompts, or links disguised as legitimate login portals.

**What You Should Do:**
1. **Think before you click:** Never click links or download attachments from unverified or unexpected sources.
2. **Verify independently:** If a request seems unusual, contact the sender through a separate, known channel (like phone or Slack).
3. **Report it:** Do not forward suspicious emails to colleagues. Use the **[Report Phishing]** button in your email client or notify IT immediately at **[IT Email/Extension]**.

Thank you for helping keep our workplace secure.

**The IT Security Team**
