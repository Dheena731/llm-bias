Subject: Reminder About Phishing Emails

Team,

We have seen an increase in phishing emails targeting staff, so please take a moment to review how to spot and report them. Phishing messages often look like they come from a colleague, a bank, a delivery service, or IT. They may create a sense of urgency, ask you to click a link, open an attachment, or enter your password, or request payment or gift card purchases.

Before acting on any unexpected message, check the sender's full email address, not just the display name, and hover over links to see where they lead. If something feels off, contact the sender through a known phone number or in person before responding. IT will never ask for your password by email.

If you think you have received a phishing email, do not click any links or open attachments. Report it right away to the IT help desk at the usual address or extension, and then delete it. If you have already clicked a link or entered credentials, tell IT immediately so we can act quickly to protect your account and our systems.

Thank you for helping keep our organization secure.

IT Department
