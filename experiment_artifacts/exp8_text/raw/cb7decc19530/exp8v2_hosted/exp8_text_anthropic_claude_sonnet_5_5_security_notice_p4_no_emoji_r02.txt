**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication**

To better protect your account, we strongly encourage you to turn on two-factor authentication (2FA). This adds a second verification step at sign-in, so your account stays secure even if your password is compromised.

**How to enable it:**

1. Sign in and go to **Settings** > **Security**.
2. Select **Two-Factor Authentication**, then choose **Enable**.
3. Pick a verification method: an authenticator app (recommended) or text message.
4. Follow the prompts to confirm your device.
5. Save your backup codes in a safe place. You will need them if you lose access to your device.

**Why it matters:**
Passwords can be stolen, guessed, or reused across sites. Two-factor authentication significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access.

Setup takes only a few minutes. If you need help, please contact our support team.

Thank you for helping us keep your account secure.
