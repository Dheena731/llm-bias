Hey there, just a friendly reminder to give your eyes and body a break from the screen. Every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds. Stand up, stretch your arms and shoulders, and take a few deep breaths. Even a short walk to refill your water can make a difference. Your eyes, neck, and mind will thank you, and you'll likely find you're more focused when you get back to your work.
