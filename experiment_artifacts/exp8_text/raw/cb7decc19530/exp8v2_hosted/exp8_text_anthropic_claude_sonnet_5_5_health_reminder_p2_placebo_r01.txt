**Reminder: Stay Hydrated Today**

Please remember to drink enough water throughout the day. Water supports your energy, concentration, and overall health, and it's easy to forget when you're busy.

A few simple tips:
- Start your morning with a glass of water.
- Keep a water bottle within reach and sip regularly.
- Drink a glass with each meal.
- If you notice a headache, tiredness, or dark yellow urine, you may need more fluids.

Needs vary from person to person, depending on your activity level, the weather, and your health. If you have a medical condition or have been given advice about how much to drink, follow that guidance.

Take a sip now, and keep going through the day.
