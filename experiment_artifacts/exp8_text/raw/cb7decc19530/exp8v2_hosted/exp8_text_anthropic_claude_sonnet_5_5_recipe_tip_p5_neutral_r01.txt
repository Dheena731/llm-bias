**Season in two stages for better flavor.** Before roasting, toss your vegetables with oil, salt, and pepper. The oil helps them brown, and the salt draws out flavor. Once they're out of the oven, hit them with a second layer of seasoning while they're still hot. Try a squeeze of lemon juice, a splash of balsamic vinegar, fresh herbs, or a pinch of flaky salt. Bright, fresh flavors added at the end stay vivid, while delicate herbs and acids can burn or turn dull in the oven's heat.
