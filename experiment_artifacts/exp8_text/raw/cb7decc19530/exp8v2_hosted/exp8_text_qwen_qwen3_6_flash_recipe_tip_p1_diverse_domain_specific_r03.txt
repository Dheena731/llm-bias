Use the protein's resting window to build a pan sauce. Deglaze the hot roasting pan with a splash of broth, wine, or even water, scraping up the fond. Reduce slightly, then stir in a knob of cold butter or a drizzle of olive oil until emulsified. Spoon over the meat and sides; you get restaurant-style gloss and depth without extending cook time or dirtying a second saucepan.
