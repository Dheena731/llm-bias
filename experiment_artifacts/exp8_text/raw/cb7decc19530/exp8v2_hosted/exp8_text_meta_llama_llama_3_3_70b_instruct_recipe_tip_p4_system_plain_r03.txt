To simplify your busy week, consider planning your lunches in advance. Take some time on the weekend or one evening to think about what you want to eat for lunch each day. You can look up recipes, make a grocery list, and even prep some ingredients or cook entire meals ahead of time. Having a plan in place will save you time and money, and ensure that you have healthy and satisfying meals to fuel your busy days. You can also consider using leftovers from dinner to make lunch the next day, or packing similar items like sandwiches or salads that can be easily customized with different fillings or toppings.
