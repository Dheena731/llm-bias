NOTICE: IMPORTANT UPDATES TO RECYCLING COLLECTION

Dear Residents,

Please be advised that there are changes to the recycling collection schedule and guidelines. These updates take effect on November 1, 2023.

Schedule Change:
Recycling collection will shift to a bi-weekly pickup on the first and third Wednesday of each month. Previous weekly service is discontinued.

Bin Placement Rules:
Place your blue recycling bin at the curb by 7:00 AM on collection morning. Bins must be spaced three feet apart and free of loose trash. All bins must be returned to your property by 8:00 PM on collection night.

Material Changes:
Glass containers and plastic film/bags are no longer accepted in curbside recycling. Please dispose of these items at the designated drop-off center located at 123 Main Street.

For questions or to request a bulk pickup, please contact the Department of Public Works at 555-0123 or visit www.citysanitation.gov.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our community clean.

Sincerely,

City Sanitation Services
