**Reminder: Refill Your Prescription**

It's time to refill your prescription. Request your refill before you run out so you don't miss any doses.

- **Check your supply.** Look at how much medication you have left and how many refills remain on the label.
- **Contact your pharmacy.** Call, use their app or website, or visit in person to request the refill. Some pharmacies need a day or more to prepare it.
- **If you have no refills left,** ask your pharmacy to contact your prescriber, or contact your prescriber's office yourself to request a new prescription.
- **Don't stop or skip doses** to stretch your supply unless your doctor tells you to. If you've already run out, call your pharmacy or doctor right away for advice.

Keep taking your medication exactly as prescribed, and ask your pharmacist or doctor if you have any questions.
