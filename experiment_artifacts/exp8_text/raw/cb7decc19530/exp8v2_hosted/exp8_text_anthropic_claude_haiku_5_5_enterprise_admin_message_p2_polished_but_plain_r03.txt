Subject: New expense approval workflow starting next month

Dear team,

Beginning June 1, we will be moving all expense submissions to a new approval workflow in the finance system. The change is designed to shorten reimbursement times, create a clearer audit trail, and reduce the back-and-forth that has slowed approvals over the past year.

Under the new process, every expense report will be submitted through the finance portal rather than by email. Expenses under $250 will be approved automatically once the receipt is attached and the category is selected. Expenses between $250 and $2,500 will go to your direct manager for approval, and anything above $2,500 will also require sign-off from the department head. Each approver will receive a notification and will have three business days to act before the request is escalated to the next level.

Before June 1, please review any open reimbursement requests and submit them under the current process so nothing is left in transition. Training sessions will be held on May 20 and May 22, and recordings will be available on the intranet for anyone who cannot attend. If you have questions about a specific expense or need access to the portal, contact the finance operations team at least one week before your first submission.

Thank you for your cooperation as we make this transition. Your patience during the rollout is appreciated.

Best regards,
Finance Operations
