When you get home on a busy weeknight, start by putting a pot of water on to boil or a pan on to heat before you do anything else. Those two steps take the longest to get going, and while they warm up you can chop vegetables, season your protein, or open a can of beans without wasting a minute. A good habit is to cut everything for the meal before you turn on any heat under the food itself, so you are not scrambling while something burns. If you cook a double batch of rice, pasta, or roasted vegetables, the leftovers can become tomorrow's lunch or a quick base for another dinner later in the week.
