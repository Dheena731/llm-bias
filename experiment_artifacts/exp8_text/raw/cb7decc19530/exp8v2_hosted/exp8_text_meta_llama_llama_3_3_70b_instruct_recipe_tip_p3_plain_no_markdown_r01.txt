To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it thoroughly before cooking. Rinsing removes excess starch from the surface of the rice, which is what causes it to stick together. Use cold water and swish the rice around to loosen the starch, then drain and cook as usual. You can also try adding a small amount of oil or butter to the water when cooking the rice, as this will help to separate the grains and make the rice less sticky. Additionally, using the right amount of water is important, as too little water can cause the rice to become sticky and clumpy.
