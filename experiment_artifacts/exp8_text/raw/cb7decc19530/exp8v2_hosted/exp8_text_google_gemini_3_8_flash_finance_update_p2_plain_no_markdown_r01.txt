We are writing to inform you of an upcoming update to our card transaction fees, which will take effect on the first of next month. Due to recent increases in network processing expenses and the ongoing costs of maintaining robust payment security, the standard rate for credit and debit card transactions will adjust to two point five percent plus ten cents per transaction. Transactions made through alternative payment methods, including direct bank transfers, will remain unaffected by this change. We recognize that any price adjustment impacts your operations, and we remain focused on delivering secure, reliable payment processing for your business. If you have questions about how this update applies to your account or wish to discuss your processing options, please reach out to our support team at your convenience.
