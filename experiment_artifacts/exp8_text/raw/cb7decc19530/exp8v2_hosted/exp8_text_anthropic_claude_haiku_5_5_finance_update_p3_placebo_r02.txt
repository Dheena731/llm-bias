**Introducing Savings Goals**

You can now set savings goals directly in the app. Give a goal a name, such as "Emergency fund" or "Summer trip," and set a target amount. Then track your progress toward it as you save.

To get started, open the Goals section and choose "New goal." Once you've created a goal, you can check how close you are at any time, and you can update or remove it whenever your plans change.

We built this feature to make it easier to see your progress and stay motivated. If you have feedback, let us know through the Help menu.
