We have recently seen an increase in phishing emails being sent to employees. These emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information such as your username and password. They often appear to be from a legitimate source, such as a bank or a well-known company, but are actually from a scammer. 

To protect yourself and the company, please be cautious when receiving emails that ask for personal or financial information. Never click on links or download attachments from unfamiliar senders, and never provide sensitive information in response to an email. If you are unsure whether an email is legitimate, please contact the IT department for assistance. 

We will never ask you to provide sensitive information via email, so if you receive an email that appears to be from the company and asks for this type of information, it is likely a phishing attempt. By being vigilant and taking the necessary precautions, we can help prevent these types of scams from succeeding.
