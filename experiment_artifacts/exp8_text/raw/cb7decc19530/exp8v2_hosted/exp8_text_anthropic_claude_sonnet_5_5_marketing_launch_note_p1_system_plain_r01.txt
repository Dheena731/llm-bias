Introducing Tallybox, the simple way to run your small business

Today we are launching Tallybox, an app built for people who run small businesses and would rather spend their time on customers than on paperwork.

If you have ever sent an invoice from a spreadsheet, chased a payment through three different inboxes, or lost track of what you spent last month, you know how quickly the small tasks pile up. Tallybox brings them together in one clean place. You can create and send professional invoices in under a minute, see who has paid and who hasn't at a glance, track expenses by snapping a photo of a receipt, and keep your appointments and customer notes right alongside your numbers.

We designed Tallybox with owners, not accountants, in mind. There is no jargon to decode and no long setup to get through. Most people are up and running in about ten minutes, and everything works just as well on your phone as it does on your laptop, so you can send a quote from the job site or check your week from the kitchen table.

To celebrate our launch, every new account gets its first three months free, with no credit card required. After that, plans start at twelve dollars a month, and you can cancel whenever you like.

We built Tallybox because we grew up around family businesses and saw how much time good people lost to tedious admin. Our hope is that this app gives some of that time back to you.

Try Tallybox today at tallybox.example and see how much lighter your week can feel.
