Treat delicate herbs like parsley and cilantro like cut flowers by trimming the stems and placing them upright in a glass of water on your kitchen counter. For tougher, woody herbs such as rosemary, thyme, and oregano, wrap them loosely in a slightly damp paper towel and place them inside a resealable plastic bag in the refrigerator drawer. Remember to wait until you are ready to cook before washing your herbs, as excess moisture can cause them to wilt and decay quickly.
