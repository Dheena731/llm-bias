We've added a new savings goals feature to help you set aside money for the things that matter to you. You can now create a goal, give it a name, choose a target amount, and pick a date you'd like to reach it by. Whether you're saving for a vacation, an emergency fund, or a big purchase, the app will show you how much you've put away so far and how much remains.

To keep things simple, you can set up automatic transfers from your checking account on a weekly, biweekly, or monthly schedule. If you'd rather save as you go, you can add money to any goal manually at any time. The app will also estimate whether you're on track to hit your target by your chosen date and suggest an adjusted contribution if you're running behind.

You can create up to ten goals at once and adjust or pause them whenever your plans change. When you reach a goal, you'll get a notification, and you can move the funds back to your main account or start a new goal right away.

The feature is available now for everyone. Just open the Savings tab and select Create a Goal to get started. We'd love to hear your feedback as you try it out.
