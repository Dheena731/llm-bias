**Public Notice: Road Closure**

The road connecting Oak Street to Maple Avenue will be closed to all traffic from Monday, March 21st, to Friday, March 25th, due to scheduled maintenance and repairs. The closure is necessary to ensure the road's safety and integrity.

During this time, drivers are advised to use alternative routes, such as Elm Street or Pine Road, to access their destinations. Local residents and businesses will have pedestrian access to their properties, but vehicular access will be restricted.

The road is expected to reopen on Saturday, March 26th. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your cooperation and understanding as we work to improve the road infrastructure. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our public works department at (555) 123-4567.
