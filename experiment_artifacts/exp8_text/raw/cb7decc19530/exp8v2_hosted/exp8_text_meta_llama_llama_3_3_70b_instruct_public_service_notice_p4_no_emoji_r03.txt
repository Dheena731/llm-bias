Public Notice: Heat Advisory

A heat advisory has been issued for our area, effective immediately. High temperatures and humidity are expected to create dangerous heat index conditions, posing a significant risk to public health. Residents are advised to take precautions to stay cool and hydrated, especially the elderly, young children, and those with pre-existing medical conditions.

Stay indoors during the hottest part of the day, drink plenty of water, and avoid strenuous activities. Check on vulnerable neighbors and family members to ensure their safety. Cooling centers are available at local community centers and libraries for those in need.

Please monitor local news and weather reports for updates and follow all instructions from local authorities. If you or someone you know is experiencing heat-related illness, call 911 or seek medical attention immediately.
