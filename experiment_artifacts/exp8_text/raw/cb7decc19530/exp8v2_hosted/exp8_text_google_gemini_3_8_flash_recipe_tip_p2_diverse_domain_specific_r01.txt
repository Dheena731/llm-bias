Split herbs by stem type rather than treating the bunch uniformly:

* **Tender herbs (parsley, cilantro, mint):** Trim 1/4 inch off the stems and stand them upright in a heavy glass or deli container with an inch of cold water. Tent the original produce bag loosely over the leaves and refrigerate. 
* **The basil exception:** Treat it like a cut flower, but **never refrigerate it**. Basil suffers chilling injury below 50°F, which ruptures cell walls and turns the leaves black within hours. Keep it on the counter away from direct sun.
* **Woody herbs (rosemary, thyme, sage, oregano):** Moisture is the enemy here. Do not wash them before storage. Roll the sprigs inside a *dry* paper towel to absorb ambient humidity, slide them into an airtight zip-top bag, press out excess air, and keep them in the coldest part of your crisper drawer.
