A new expense approval workflow has been implemented to enhance the process of reviewing and approving expenses. The updated process aims to provide a more streamlined and efficient experience for all parties involved.

Under the new workflow, all expense requests will be routed to a designated approver for review and approval. This change is intended to reduce the time it takes to process expenses and minimize the need for follow-up requests.

If you have any questions or concerns about the new workflow, please do not hesitate to reach out to the accounting department. They will be happy to assist you with any queries or issues that may arise.
