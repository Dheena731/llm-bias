As you dive into the digital realm, remember to blink – literally. Prolonged screen time can lead to eye strain, blurred vision, and a decrease in productivity. Take a cue from the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, shift your gaze to something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. This brief pause can help reduce the risk of digital eye fatigue and keep your focus sharp. So, look away, stretch, and refocus – your eyes (and your workflow) will thank you.
