Toss your vegetables in oil and salt before they go into the oven, and don't wait until they come out to season them. Salt drawn into the surface during roasting helps moisture evaporate and encourages browning, while seasoning afterward only sits on the outside. Use about a teaspoon of kosher salt per pound of vegetables, and add dried herbs or spices at the start too, but save fresh herbs and a squeeze of lemon for after roasting so they stay bright.
