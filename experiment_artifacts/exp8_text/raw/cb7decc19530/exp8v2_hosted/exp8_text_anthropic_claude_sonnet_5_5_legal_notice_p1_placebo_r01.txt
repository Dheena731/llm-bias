**NOTICE OF CHANGES TO ACCOUNT TERMS**

**To:** Account holders

**1. Purpose of this notice.** We are changing the terms that govern your account (the "Agreement"). This notice explains when the changes take effect and what your options are.

**2. What is changing.** The revised Agreement accompanies this notice and sets out each change. Please read it in full. If the revised Agreement and this notice differ, the revised Agreement controls.

**3. Effective Date.** The changes take effect 30 days after the date this notice was sent to you (the "Effective Date"). Until then, the current Agreement continues to apply.

**4. Your options.**
- **Accept the changes.** You do not need to take any action. If you keep your account open and continue to use it after the Effective Date, you accept the revised Agreement.
- **Decline the changes.** If you do not agree, you may close your account at any time before the Effective Date. The current Agreement will apply to your account until it is closed.

**5. Existing obligations.** These changes do not cancel any obligation that arose under the current Agreement before the Effective Date, and closing your account does not either.

**6. Questions.** If you have questions about this notice, please contact us using the contact details in your account materials.
