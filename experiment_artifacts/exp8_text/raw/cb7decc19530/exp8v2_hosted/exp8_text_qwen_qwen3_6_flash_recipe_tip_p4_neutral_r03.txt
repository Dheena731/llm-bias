🥗 **Use the "Mix-and-Match" Component Method.**

Instead of prepping identical meals, spend 30 minutes on Sunday roasting a batch of veggies, cooking a grain (like quinoa or rice), and preparing a protein (like chickpeas or chicken). Store these components separately.

During the week, simply assemble your lunch by mixing different combinations and adding two store-bought sauces. You'll save time, avoid food fatigue, and have fresh-tasting meals ready in under 2 minutes!
