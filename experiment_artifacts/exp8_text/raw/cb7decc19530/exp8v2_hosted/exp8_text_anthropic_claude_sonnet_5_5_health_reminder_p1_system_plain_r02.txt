Time for your weekly fitness check-in. Take a few minutes to look back at the past seven days: how many workouts you completed, how your energy and recovery felt, and whether you met the goals you set for yourself. Notice what worked well and where you struggled, and be honest without being harsh. Small improvements count, and consistency matters more than perfection. Use what you learn to set one or two realistic targets for the week ahead, and adjust your plan if something needs to change. Keep going, because every week of effort adds up.
