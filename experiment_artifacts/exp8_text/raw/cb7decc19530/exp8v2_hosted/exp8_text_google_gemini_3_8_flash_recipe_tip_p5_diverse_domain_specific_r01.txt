Toss hard vegetables with oil and coarse salt only before they go into the oven. Ground spices—especially paprika, garlic powder, coriander, and black pepper—contain delicate volatile oils and fine particles that scorch into bitter ash over a 35-minute roast at 425°F. 

Instead, pull the sheet pan five minutes before the vegetables finish, dust with your dry spice blend, and return the pan to the heat just long enough for the hot surface oil to bloom the aromatics without burning them.
