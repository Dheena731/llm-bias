Season vegetables before they go into the oven, not after. Toss them in a bowl with olive oil, salt, and pepper so the seasoning clings to the surface and the oil helps it caramelize as the vegetables roast. Use about a teaspoon of kosher salt per pound of vegetables, since salt draws out moisture and helps the edges brown. Add dried herbs or spices like smoked paprika, cumin, or garlic powder at the start as well, because they can handle the heat. Save fresh herbs, lemon juice, and grated cheese for after roasting so their flavors stay bright and don't burn.
