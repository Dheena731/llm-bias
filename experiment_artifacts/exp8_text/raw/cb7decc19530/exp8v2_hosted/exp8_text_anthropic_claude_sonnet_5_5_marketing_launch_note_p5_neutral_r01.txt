**Launching today: *Say It Again*, a podcast for people who are learning a language**

We're excited to share the first episodes of *Say It Again*, a show for anyone who has ever frozen mid-sentence, forgotten a word they knew yesterday, or wondered whether they'll ever sound natural.

Each 15-minute episode covers one practical idea: a useful phrase, a tricky bit of grammar, a pronunciation habit, or a study technique that works. You'll also hear short conversations with native speakers and learners at different stages, so you can pick up real rhythm and everyday vocabulary, not just textbook lines.

**What to expect:**
- **Short, focused episodes** that fit into a commute or a coffee break
- **Repetition built in**, because that's how language sticks
- **Honest talk about mistakes**, since they're part of the process
- **New episodes every Tuesday and Friday**

We're launching with five episodes, starting with "The First 50 Words Worth Learning" and "Why You Understand More Than You Can Say."

You can listen now on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, or wherever you get your podcasts. If you enjoy it, a rating or a share with a fellow learner helps us a lot.

Thanks for learning with us. Let's get started.
