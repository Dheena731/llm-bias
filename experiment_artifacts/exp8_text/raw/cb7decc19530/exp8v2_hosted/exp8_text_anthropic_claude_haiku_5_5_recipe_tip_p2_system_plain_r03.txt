Store fresh herbs like parsley, cilantro, and basil by trimming the stems and standing them upright in a glass with an inch or two of water, as you would a bouquet of flowers. Cover the leaves loosely with a plastic bag and keep them in the refrigerator, changing the water every couple of days. Basil is the exception: it turns black when chilled, so keep it on the counter away from direct sunlight instead. Hardier herbs like rosemary and thyme last longer when wrapped in a damp paper towel and sealed in a container in the fridge.
