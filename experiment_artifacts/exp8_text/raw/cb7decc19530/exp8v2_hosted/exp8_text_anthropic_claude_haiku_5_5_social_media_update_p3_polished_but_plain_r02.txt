Join us this Saturday, June 14, from 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. at Riverside Park for the annual Neighborhood Spring Fair. The day will feature a farmers market with local produce and baked goods, live music from area student bands, a children's craft tent, and a book swap organized by the public library. Food trucks will be on site starting at 11:00 a.m., and parking is available in the lot off Mill Street. Volunteers are still needed for setup at 8:00 a.m. and for the cleanup crew at 2:00 p.m., so if you can spare an hour, please sign up at the community center front desk or reply to this post. Rain or shine, we look forward to seeing you there.
