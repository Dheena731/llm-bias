# NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

**Effective Date:** [Insert Date]

**1. Purpose**
This Notice sets out the conditions under which [Company Name] ("the Company") will issue refunds for purchases made by customers ("the Customer") through [website/store].

**2. Eligibility**
A Customer is eligible for a refund only if all of the following conditions are met:

a. The request is submitted in writing within thirty (30) days of the original purchase date;
b. The Customer provides valid proof of purchase, such as a receipt or order confirmation number;
c. The product is returned in its original condition, unused, and in its original packaging, where applicable; and
d. The product is not listed under Section 3 (Non-Refundable Items).

**3. Non-Refundable Items**
The following are not eligible for refund unless required by applicable law:

a. Digital products or services that have been downloaded, accessed, or fully performed;
b. Customized or personalized goods;
c. Gift cards and promotional credits;
d. Items marked "Final Sale" at the time of purchase; and
e. Perishable goods.

**4. Defective or Misdescribed Goods**
Notwithstanding Section 2, where a product is defective, damaged on arrival, or materially different from its description, the Customer may request a refund or replacement within sixty (60) days of delivery. The Company may require photographic evidence.

**5. Processing**
Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within ten (10) business days of the Company's receipt and inspection of the returned item. Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return results from Company error. Return shipping costs are borne by the Customer unless Section 4 applies.

**6. Discretion and Denial**
The Company reserves the right to deny any refund request that does not satisfy this Notice, or that appears fraudulent or abusive. Denials will be communicated in writing with a statement of reasons.

**7. Statutory Rights**
Nothing in this Notice limits any rights the Customer may have under applicable consumer protection laws.

**8. Contact**
Refund requests and inquiries should be directed to: [email address / mailing address].

**9. Amendments**
The Company may amend this Notice at any time. Amendments apply only to purchases made after the date of the change.
