**Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations**

Polling stations for the upcoming election are now open for voting. Eligible voters are reminded that polling station assignments are based on residential address, and locations may differ from those used in previous elections.

To find your assigned polling station, please:

- Check your voter registration card, which lists your polling place.
- Visit the official elections website and enter your home address.
- Call the local elections office for assistance.

Polling stations are open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on Election Day. Voters in line at closing time will be permitted to cast their ballots.

Please bring a valid form of identification, as required by local law. Accessible entrances and assistance are available at all polling locations. If you require special accommodations, contact the elections office in advance.

For more information, contact your local elections office or visit the official government elections website.
