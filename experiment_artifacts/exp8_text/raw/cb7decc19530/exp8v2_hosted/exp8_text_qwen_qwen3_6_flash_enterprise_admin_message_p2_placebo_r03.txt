Subject: Important Update: New Expense Approval Workflow

Team, we are implementing a new expense approval workflow to improve accuracy and streamline processing. Effective immediately, all expense requests must be submitted through the official expense portal. Each submission requires a clear receipt image, the correct department or project code, and a concise description of the business purpose. Once submitted, your direct supervisor will automatically receive a notification to review and approve the request. Submissions missing documentation or containing incorrect codes will be returned for revision. Approved expenses will advance to the finance department for processing on the standard reimbursement schedule. Step-by-step instructions and policy guidelines are available on the internal knowledge base under Finance Resources. For technical support or clarification, please contact the finance team directly. Thank you for your prompt attention to this update and for maintaining compliance with our financial procedures.
