Please remain vigilant regarding recent phishing emails circulating across our network. These fraudulent messages often attempt to impersonate internal departments, executive leaders, or external vendors in an effort to compromise company data. Common indicators include requests for immediate action, unexpected attachments, slightly altered sender email addresses, and prompts to verify passwords or financial details. If an email appears unusual or raises suspicion, do not open any attachments, reply to the message, or provide personal credentials. Instead, report the email using the built-in phishing reporting tool in your email client or notify the information security team immediately. When in doubt, confirm the validity of the request with the sender through a separate, established communication method such as a direct phone call. Your attention to these details is vital to keeping our workplace and data secure.
