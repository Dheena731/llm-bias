Remember to take short breaks from screens throughout the day to rest your eyes and reduce eye strain. Looking away from your computer, phone, or other device for just a few minutes can make a big difference in how you feel. Try to get up and stretch or move around every hour or so, and avoid screens for at least an hour before bedtime to help you sleep better. Taking breaks from screens can help you stay focused and productive, and can also improve your overall health and well-being.
