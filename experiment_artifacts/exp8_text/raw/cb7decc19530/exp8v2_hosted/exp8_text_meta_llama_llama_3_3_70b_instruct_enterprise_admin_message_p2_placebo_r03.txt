Attention all staff,

As part of our ongoing efforts to improve financial management and efficiency, we are introducing a new expense approval workflow. This new process will go live on Monday and will apply to all employee expenses, including travel, entertainment, and general business expenses.

The new workflow will require employees to submit their expenses through our online portal, where they will be reviewed and approved by their respective department managers. Approved expenses will then be forwarded to the finance team for reimbursement.

Key benefits of the new workflow include increased transparency, improved tracking, and faster reimbursement times. Detailed guidelines and instructions on how to use the new system will be provided to all employees through a separate email and training sessions will be offered to department managers.

If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the finance team. We are committed to making this transition as smooth as possible and appreciate your cooperation in implementing this new process.

Thank you for your understanding and cooperation.
