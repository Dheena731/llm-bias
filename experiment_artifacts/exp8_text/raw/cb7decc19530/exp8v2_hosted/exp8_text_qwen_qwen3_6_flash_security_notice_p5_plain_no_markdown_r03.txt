Please be advised that all employees must immediately report any lost or stolen company laptops to the IT support desk. If you have misplaced your device, do not attempt to locate it through personal means. Instead, contact the helpdesk directly by calling extension five zero one zero or emailing security at company dot com slash it support. You will need to provide your full name, employee identification number, the approximate time and location where the loss occurred, and the serial number if available. Prompt reporting is essential to protect company data and ensure network security. A replacement device will be issued once the incident has been documented and verified. Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining our information security standards.
