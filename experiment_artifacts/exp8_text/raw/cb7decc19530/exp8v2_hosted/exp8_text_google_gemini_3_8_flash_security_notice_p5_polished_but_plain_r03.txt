If you misplace or lose your company-issued laptop, you must report the incident immediately to help protect organizational data and client privacy. Contact the IT Service Desk and inform your direct supervisor as soon as you realize the device is missing, regardless of the hour or whether you believe the loss is temporary. When submitting your report, provide your employee details, the last known location of the device, the approximate time it went missing, and any relevant circumstances surrounding the incident. Prompt notification allows the security team to quickly disable access credentials, lock the system remotely, and safeguard sensitive information from unauthorized use.
