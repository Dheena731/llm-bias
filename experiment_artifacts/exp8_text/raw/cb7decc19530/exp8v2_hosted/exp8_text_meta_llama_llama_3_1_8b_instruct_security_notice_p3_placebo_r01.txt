**Important Reminder: Secure Your Device**

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain the security and confidentiality of our work, we would like to remind all staff members to take a moment to secure their devices when not in use.

**Why Lock Your Screen?**

Research has shown that a significant percentage of security breaches occur when devices are left unattended with screens unlocked. By locking your screen, you are not only protecting our organization's sensitive data but also contributing to a safer and more productive work environment.

**What's the Best Practice?**

To ensure your device is secure, follow these simple steps:

1. Press the Windows key + L (or Command + L on Mac) to lock your screen.
2. Set your device to require a password or PIN to unlock it.
3. Disable auto-login on your device.

**Let's Work Together**

By taking these few seconds to secure your device, you are helping to protect our organization's data and maintain a secure working environment. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the IT department.

**Stay Secure, Stay Productive**

Thank you for your cooperation and attention to this important reminder.
