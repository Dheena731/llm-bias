**NOTICE: Beware of Phishing Emails**

**To:** All Staff
**From:** IT Security Team
**Date:** [Insert Date]

We have recently seen an increase in phishing emails targeting employees. These messages are designed to look legitimate in order to steal login credentials, financial information, or install malicious software.

**Warning signs to watch for:**
- Urgent or threatening language (e.g., "Your account will be closed today!")
- Unexpected attachments or links
- Sender addresses that look slightly off or don't match the company name
- Requests for passwords, payment details, or gift cards
- Spelling and grammar errors

**What you should do:**
1. **Pause** before clicking any link or opening an attachment.
2. **Verify** the request by contacting the sender through a known, trusted channel.
3. **Report** suspicious emails using the "Report Phishing" button or by forwarding them to **security@[company].com**.
4. **Delete** the email once it has been reported.

If you believe you have clicked a suspicious link or shared information, **contact IT immediately** at [phone/extension]. Prompt reporting helps us protect you and the company.

Thank you for staying alert and helping keep our workplace secure.

*IT Security Team*
