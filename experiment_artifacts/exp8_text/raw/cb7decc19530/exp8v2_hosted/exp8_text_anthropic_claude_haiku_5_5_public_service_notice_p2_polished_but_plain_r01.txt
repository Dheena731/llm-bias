PUBLIC NOTICE: Road Closure on Maple Avenue

The Department of Public Works will close Maple Avenue between Fifth Street and Ninth Street to all vehicle and pedestrian traffic beginning at 7:00 a.m. on Monday, March 16, and continuing through Friday, March 20. Crews will be replacing a section of water main and repaving the roadway during this period.

During the closure, drivers should use Oak Avenue or Cedar Street as alternate routes. Local residents and businesses along the closed section will retain access to their properties from either end of the work zone, and crews will coordinate with property owners in advance of any temporary interruptions to driveway access. Pedestrians may use the marked detour along the north sidewalk of Fifth Street, which will remain open throughout the project.

Work hours will generally run from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on weekdays. Emergency vehicles will be granted access at all times. Residents with questions or concerns may contact the Department of Public Works at (555) 014-2290 or visit the city's public works office at 410 Center Street during business hours.

The Department thanks the community for its patience as these necessary repairs are completed. Schedule changes due to weather may occur, and updates will be posted on the city's official website and social media accounts.
