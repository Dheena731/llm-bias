**Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations**

Please confirm your assigned polling station before heading out to vote. 

Polling stations are assigned according to your registered home address, and your voting location may have changed since the previous election. You must vote at your designated polling station for your ballot to be counted. 

To find your specific polling location, check your official voter information card or visit your local election office website. Please verify your station in advance to avoid delays on Election Day.
