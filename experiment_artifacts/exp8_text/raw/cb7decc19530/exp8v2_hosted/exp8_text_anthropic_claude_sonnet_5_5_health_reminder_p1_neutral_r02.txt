**Weekly Fitness Check-In** 💪

It's time to review your progress! Take a few minutes to:

- **Look back:** How many workouts did you complete this week?
- **Celebrate wins:** Did you hit a new personal best or stick to your routine?
- **Spot challenges:** What got in the way, and how can you adjust?
- **Set your goals:** Choose one or two targets for the week ahead.

Small steps add up. Keep going, you're doing great!
