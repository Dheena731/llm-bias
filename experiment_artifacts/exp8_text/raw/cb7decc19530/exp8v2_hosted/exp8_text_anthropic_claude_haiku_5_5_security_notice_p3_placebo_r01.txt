**Reminder: Lock Your Screen When You Step Away**

All staff are reminded to lock their computer screens whenever they leave their desks, even for a short time.

An unlocked screen lets anyone nearby view or use your account, including confidential information and company systems. Locking your screen takes only a moment and keeps our information secure.

How to lock your screen:
- **Windows:** Press the Windows key + L.
- **Mac:** Press Control + Command + Q.

Thank you for helping keep our workplace and our data secure.
