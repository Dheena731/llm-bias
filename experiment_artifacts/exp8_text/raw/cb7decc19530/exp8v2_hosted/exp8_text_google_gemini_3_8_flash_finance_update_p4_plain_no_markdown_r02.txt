Your end of year tax statement provides an official summary of all relevant financial activity associated with your account over the past calendar year. Depending on your relationship with our organization, this document details your total earnings, taxes withheld, or tax deductible contributions made between January first and December thirty first. You will need the figures listed on this document to complete your annual income tax return. Please review the reported amounts carefully to ensure they match your personal records, and store the document in a safe place or provide it to your professional tax preparer. If you spot an error or have questions about how your totals were calculated, contact our support team as soon as possible so we can assist you before you file.
