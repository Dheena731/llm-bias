To reduce stickiness, rinse your rice thoroughly under cold water until the runoff runs clear before cooking, which removes excess surface starch that causes grains to clump. Measure your water carefully according to the specific rice variety, as using too much liquid creates a gummy consistency. Once cooked, allow the pot to sit covered off the heat for ten minutes so the moisture redistributes evenly, then gently fluff the rice with a fork to separate the grains without crushing them.
