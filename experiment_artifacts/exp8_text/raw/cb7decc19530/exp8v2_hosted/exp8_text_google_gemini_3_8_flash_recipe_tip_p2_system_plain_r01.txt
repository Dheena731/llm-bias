To keep tender herbs like parsley and cilantro fresh, treat them like cut flowers by trimming the bottom of the stems and placing them upright in a small glass with an inch of water. Cover the top loosely with a plastic bag and store the jar in the refrigerator, changing the water every few days. For basil, use the exact same water method but leave the jar on your kitchen counter at room temperature, as cold air will turn the leaves black. Hardier herbs such as rosemary and thyme keep best when wrapped in a slightly damp paper towel and sealed in an airtight container in the crisper drawer.
