We are introducing a new expense approval workflow to improve the efficiency and transparency of our financial processes. The new workflow will enable employees to submit expenses online and track the status of their submissions in real time. Managers will receive automated notifications when an expense report is submitted for their approval, and they will be able to review and approve or reject expenses directly within the system. This new workflow is designed to reduce paperwork and streamline the approval process, allowing for faster reimbursement of expenses. If you have any questions or need assistance with the new system, please do not hesitate to reach out to the finance department. We will be providing training and support to ensure a smooth transition to the new workflow.
