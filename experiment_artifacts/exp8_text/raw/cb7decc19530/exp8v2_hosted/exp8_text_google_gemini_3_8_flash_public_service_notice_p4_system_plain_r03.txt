The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for our area, effective from this morning through tomorrow evening. Temperatures are expected to rise into the upper nineties, with humidity pushing the heat index to dangerous levels above one hundred degrees. Prolonged exposure to these conditions significantly increases the risk of heat-related illnesses, including heat cramps, heat exhaustion, and heat stroke. 

Residents are strongly advised to stay indoors in air-conditioned spaces as much as possible, drink plenty of water, and avoid strenuous outdoor activities during the hottest hours of the day. Please check on elderly neighbors, ensure pets have ample water and shade, and never leave children or animals unattended in vehicles. If you must be outside, take frequent rest breaks in shaded areas and wear lightweight, loose-fitting clothing. Anyone experiencing severe symptoms such as dizziness, confusion, nausea, or a cessation of sweating should seek emergency medical attention immediately.
