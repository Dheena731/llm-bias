**Notice: Reporting a Lost Company Laptop**

All employees are required to report a lost or stolen company laptop immediately. Prompt reporting helps protect company data, reduce security risks, and allow IT to take appropriate action.

**What to do:**

1. **Report it immediately** to the IT Help Desk at [phone number] or [email address], ideally within one hour of discovering the loss.
2. **Provide details**, including the laptop's asset tag number, model, the date and time you last had it, and where it was last seen.
3. **File a police report** if the laptop was stolen, and provide a copy to your manager and the IT department.
4. **Do not attempt to recover or access the device** yourself, as this may interfere with security measures.

IT will remotely lock or wipe the device where possible and will update the asset register.

Failure to report a lost or stolen device promptly may result in disciplinary action in accordance with company policy.
