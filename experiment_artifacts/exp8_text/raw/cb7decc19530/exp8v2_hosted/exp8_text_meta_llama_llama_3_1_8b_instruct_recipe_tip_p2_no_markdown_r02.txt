To keep fresh herbs fresh for a longer period, store them in a breathable container such as a paper bag or a mesh bag. Place the herbs in a single layer, away from direct sunlight and moisture. You can also store them in a glass jar with the stem end submerged in water, and cover the jar with a plastic bag to maintain humidity. Change the water every few days to prevent bacterial growth. This will help preserve the herbs' flavor and aroma for several days.
