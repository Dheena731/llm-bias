To keep rice from becoming overly sticky, start by rinsing the grains under cold water until the water runs clear. This removes excess surface starch that naturally causes clumping. Measure your ingredients carefully, using roughly one and a half cups of water for every cup of long grain white rice. Bring the mixture to a rolling boil, then immediately lower the heat to the lowest setting, cover the pot securely, and cook without ever lifting the lid. After the water is fully absorbed, turn off the heat and leave the rice undisturbed under the lid for ten minutes. Finally, fluff it gently with a fork to separate the grains and finish the steaming process, which yields light, distinct kernels rather than a dense mass.
