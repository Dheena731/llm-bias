Here is a short cooking tip for making rice less sticky:

**The Secret to Non-Stick Rice**

Are you tired of serving rice that's as clingy as a bad relationship? Look no further! To achieve perfectly cooked, non-sticky rice, follow these simple steps:

1. **Ratio is key**: Use the optimal 1:1.5 ratio of water to rice. Too little water and your rice will be as dry as the Atacama Desert, while too much will leave it soggy and mushy. Trust us, it's a delicate balance.
2. **Heat control**: Use a medium-low heat to prevent the rice from cooking too quickly. This will also help to prevent the starches from breaking down and making the rice sticky.
3. **The steaming technique**: Once the rice is cooked, remove it from the heat and let it steam for 5 minutes. This will help to dry out the rice and make it less sticky.
4. **Don't over-stir**: Resist the temptation to constantly stir the rice while it's cooking. This will only break down the starches and make the rice sticky.

By following these simple steps, you'll be serving up a fluffy, non-sticky rice that's sure to impress your guests. Happy cooking!
