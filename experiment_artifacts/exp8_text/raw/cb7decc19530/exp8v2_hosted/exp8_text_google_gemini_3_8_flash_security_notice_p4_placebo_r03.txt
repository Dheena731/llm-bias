**Security Notice: Please Enable Two-Factor Authentication**

To strengthen your account security, please enable two-factor authentication. 

Two-factor authentication provides an extra layer of protection beyond your password. When this feature is active, signing in requires two separate steps: your standard password and a second form of verification, such as a temporary security code sent to your mobile device. This extra step ensures that only you can access your account, even if someone else learns your password.

To turn on this protection, sign in to your account, open your account settings, and select the security section to activate two-factor authentication.
