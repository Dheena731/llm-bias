To tame the stickiness of freshly cooked rice, try this trick: as soon as you fluff the rice with a fork after cooking, add a tablespoon or two of rice vinegar or lemon juice to the pot. The acidity will help break down the excess starches on the surface of the grains, making them less clingy and separate. This is especially effective for varieties like Japanese short-grain rice or glutinous rice, which are notorious for their sticky texture. Additionally, make sure to rinse the rice thoroughly before cooking to remove some of the excess starch, and use the right water ratio to prevent the rice from becoming too moist and sticky. By incorporating these steps, you'll be rewarded with a pot of fluffy, separate grains that are perfect for serving alongside your favorite dishes.
