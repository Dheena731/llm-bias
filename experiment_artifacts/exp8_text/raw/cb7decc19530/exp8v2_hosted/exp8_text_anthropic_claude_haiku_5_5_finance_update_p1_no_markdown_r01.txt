We're excited to share a new feature in the app: the Monthly Spending Summary. This report gives you a clear picture of where your money went over the past month, all in one place.

Each summary includes your total spending, a breakdown by category such as groceries, dining, transportation, and bills, and a comparison to your previous month so you can see how your habits are changing. You'll also find a short list of your largest transactions so nothing unexpected slips by.

The summary is generated automatically on the first day of each month and sent to your inbox. You can also view it anytime from the Insights tab in the app. If you'd rather not receive the email, you can turn it off in your notification settings.

We built this feature to make budgeting simpler and less time-consuming. Whether you're trying to cut back or just want to stay on track, we hope the summary helps you make more confident decisions with your money.

As always, we welcome your feedback. If you have suggestions for categories or details you'd like to see, reply to this message or contact our support team.

Thanks for being part of our community.
