**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

**Issued by:** [City/County] Office of Emergency Management
**Effective:** [Day, Date], 11:00 a.m. through [Day, Date], 8:00 p.m.

A heat advisory is in effect for [City/County and surrounding areas]. Daytime temperatures are expected to reach 98 to 104 degrees Fahrenheit, with heat index values up to 110 degrees. Overnight lows will offer little relief.

**Who is most at risk:**
- Older adults and young children
- People with chronic health conditions
- Outdoor workers and athletes
- Those without air conditioning

**Recommended precautions:**
- Drink plenty of water, even if you do not feel thirsty.
- Stay in air-conditioned spaces whenever possible.
- Avoid strenuous outdoor activity during the hottest part of the day, generally noon to 6:00 p.m.
- Wear lightweight, light-colored, loose-fitting clothing.
- Never leave children, pets, or vulnerable adults in a parked vehicle, even briefly.
- Check on neighbors, friends, and relatives who live alone.

**Warning signs of heat illness:** Heavy sweating, dizziness, nausea, muscle cramps, headache, or confusion. If someone has a high body temperature, hot dry skin, or loses consciousness, call 911 immediately and move the person to a cooler place.

**Cooling centers** are open during the advisory at [locations and hours]. Transportation assistance is available by calling [phone number].
