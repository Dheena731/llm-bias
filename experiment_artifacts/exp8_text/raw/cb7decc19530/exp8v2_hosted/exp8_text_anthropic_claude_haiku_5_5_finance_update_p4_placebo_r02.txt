Your year-end tax statement summarizes the income you received and the taxes withheld or paid on it during the past year. You will need its figures when you file your tax return, so keep it with your other tax records.

Check the statement against your own records, such as pay stubs or account statements. If an amount looks wrong or something is missing, contact the organization that issued it and ask for a corrected statement before you file. Filing with incorrect figures can lead to errors or delays.

If you have questions about how to use the statement on your return, a tax preparer or the tax authority's guidance can help.
