Take a moment to check in with your body and your water bottle. Even mild dehydration can leave you feeling tired, distracted, or headachy, and it is easy to go hours without a drink when your attention is on work, errands, or the next task. Try filling a glass or bottle at the start of your day, and keep it within sight so it serves as a quiet reminder. Sipping steadily through the day tends to work better than trying to drink a large amount all at once. If you find the plain taste hard to enjoy, add a slice of lemon or cucumber, or simply pour a fresh glass whenever you finish a meal. Staying hydrated is a small habit with a real payoff in your energy, focus, and comfort, so make a point to drink enough water today.
