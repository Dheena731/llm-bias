Several claims in this essay rely on outside evidence but are never tied to a source. The statistics in the second paragraph on population growth, and the assertion in the fourth paragraph that most researchers agree on the cause, read as authoritative, yet the reader has no way to check where they came from. Without citations, even accurate points look like opinion, and they weaken the credibility of an otherwise well-organized argument.

Please add a citation wherever you use a specific figure, a direct quotation, a paraphrased idea from another writer, or a broad claim about what experts believe. Be consistent in the format you choose, and make sure each in-text reference matches an entry in a final bibliography. If you cannot find a source for a claim, consider softening it or removing it. Fixing this will make your reasoning easier to trust and your essay much stronger overall.
