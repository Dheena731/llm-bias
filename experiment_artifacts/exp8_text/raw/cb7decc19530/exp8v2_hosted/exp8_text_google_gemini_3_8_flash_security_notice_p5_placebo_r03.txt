**Notice: Immediate Reporting of Lost or Stolen Laptops**

**To:** All Employees  

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, you must report the incident immediately. Prompt action is critical to protect sensitive company data and secure company systems.

Please follow these steps without delay:

1. **Notify the IT Department:** Contact IT support right away so technicians can remotely lock the device and secure your accounts.
2. **Inform Your Manager:** Notify your direct supervisor about the missing equipment as soon as possible.
3. **Report Suspected Theft:** If you believe the laptop was stolen, file a report with local law enforcement and retain a copy of the police report for company records.

Every employee is responsible for safeguarding company equipment. Thank you for your prompt cooperation in keeping our information secure.
