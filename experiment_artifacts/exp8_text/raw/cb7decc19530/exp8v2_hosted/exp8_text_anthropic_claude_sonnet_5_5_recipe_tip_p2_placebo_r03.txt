**Tip: Store fresh herbs like a bouquet (for soft herbs) or wrap them (for hardy herbs).**

**Soft herbs** (parsley, cilantro, dill, mint): Trim the stem ends, then stand the bunch in a jar with about an inch of water. Cover the leaves loosely with a plastic bag and refrigerate. Change the water every couple of days or when it looks cloudy. Basil is an exception: it dislikes cold, so keep it in a jar of water on the counter, out of direct sunlight.

**Hardy herbs** (rosemary, thyme, sage, oregano): Wrap them loosely in a slightly damp paper towel, place them in a resealable bag or container, and keep them in the refrigerator.

In both cases, remove any wilted or slimy leaves before storing, and wash herbs just before using rather than beforehand, since extra moisture speeds spoilage.
