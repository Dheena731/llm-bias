**Introducing Your Monthly Spending Summary**

We've added a new Monthly Spending Summary to help you understand where your money goes, without the need for spreadsheets or manual tracking.

**What's included**
- **Total spending:** See your overall spending for the month, with a comparison to the previous month.
- **Category breakdown:** Spending is automatically grouped into categories like groceries, dining, transportation, and subscriptions.
- **Top merchants:** Find out where you shop most often and where most of your money went.
- **Trends and highlights:** Spot unusual spikes or categories where you spent noticeably more or less than usual.

**How it works**
Your summary is generated automatically at the start of each month using your linked accounts and categorized transactions. You'll get a notification when it's ready, and you can view it anytime from the **Insights** tab. Past summaries are saved so you can look back at previous months.

**Why it matters**
A clear monthly snapshot makes it easier to set realistic budgets, catch forgotten subscriptions, and notice habits that may be affecting your goals.

**Getting started**
Make sure your accounts are linked and notifications are turned on. If a transaction is in the wrong category, tap it to edit it, and your future summaries will reflect the change.

We'd love to hear what you think. Use the feedback button in the Insights tab to share suggestions as we keep improving this feature.
