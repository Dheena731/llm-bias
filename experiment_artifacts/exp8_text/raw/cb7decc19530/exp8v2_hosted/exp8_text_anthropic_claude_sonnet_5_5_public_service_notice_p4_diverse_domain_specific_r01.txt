**NOTICE FROM THE HALVERSON COUNTY OFFICE OF EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT**

**HEAT ADVISORY in effect from 11:00 a.m. Thursday, July 17 until 9:00 p.m. Saturday, July 19**

The National Weather Service expects daytime heat index values of 105 to 109°F across the county, including the river valley and Marsh Point. Overnight lows will stay near 79°F in town, so buildings without air conditioning will not cool down at night. That is the main risk of this event: three days of heat with no real recovery period.

**Cooling centers (free, no ID required)**
- Central Library, 210 Grange St.: 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. daily
- Eastside Community Hall, 48 Pike Rd.: 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. daily
- Marsh Point Fire Station 4, lobby: noon to 6 p.m. Friday and Saturday
- Service animals are welcome. Other pets can wait in the Eastside Hall annex.

**What to do**
- Do outdoor work before 10 a.m. or after 7 p.m. If your job requires daytime work, take a shaded break at least every 30 minutes.
- Fans alone will not protect you once indoor temperatures pass the mid-90s. Use a cool shower, wet cloths on your neck and wrists, or go to a cooling center.
- Check on older neighbors and anyone who lives alone, twice a day if you can. Ask if their AC is running and whether they are eating and drinking.
- Do not leave children or pets in a parked car, even briefly. Interior temperatures can pass 120°F in under 20 minutes.
- Some medications, including diuretics, beta blockers, and certain antidepressants, make it harder to tolerate heat. Ask your pharmacist if you are unsure.

**Know the difference**
*Heat exhaustion:* heavy sweating, cramps, nausea, headache, cool and clammy skin. Move to a cool place, loosen clothing, sip water.
*Heat stroke:* confusion, slurred speech, fainting, hot and dry or very flushed skin, temperature above 103°F. **Call 911 immediately.** Cool the person with wet cloths or ice packs to the neck, armpits, and groin until help arrives.

**Power outages:** Report them to Halverson Electric at 555-0142. If you rely on electrically powered medical equipment, call the county at 555-0190 to be added to the priority check list.

**Need a ride to a cooling center?** Call 211 between 8 a.m. and 6 p.m. Rides are limited, so call early.
