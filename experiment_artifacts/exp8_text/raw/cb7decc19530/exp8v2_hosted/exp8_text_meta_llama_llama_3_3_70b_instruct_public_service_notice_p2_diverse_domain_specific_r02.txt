**NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE**

The New York State Department of Transportation (NYSDOT) announces that a section of State Route 28 (SR 28) in the town of Olive, Ulster County, will be closed to vehicular traffic from Monday, March 21, 2022, to Friday, November 19, 2022. The closure is necessary to facilitate the replacement of the Ashokan Reservoir Bridge, a critical infrastructure project that will ensure the continued safety and reliability of the roadway.

During this period, motorists are advised to follow the posted detour route, which will direct them via County Route 2 (CR 2) and State Route 28A (SR 28A). Local traffic, including residents, businesses, and emergency services, will be permitted to access the area, but may experience delays.

The NYSDOT will provide regular updates on the project's progress and any changes to the traffic pattern. Motorists are encouraged to check the department's website or follow @NYSDOT on social media for the latest information.

We apologize for any inconvenience this closure may cause and appreciate the public's understanding as we work to improve the safety and infrastructure of our transportation network.
