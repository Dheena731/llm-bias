**Lost Laptop Incident Report**

**Company:** NovaTech Inc.

**Department:** Research and Development

**Date:** 02/15/2023

**Incident Summary:**

On February 15, 2023, at approximately 10:45 AM, a company-owned laptop was reported missing from the R&D department. The laptop in question is a Dell XPS 13 (serial number: 1234567890) containing sensitive research data and confidential project information.

**Details of the Missing Laptop:**

* Model: Dell XPS 13
* Serial Number: 1234567890
* Last seen in: R&D department, near the secure data storage area
* Operating System: Windows 10
* Contained sensitive data: Research project files, confidential meeting notes, and encrypted data

**Action Taken:**

* The R&D team has been notified and is cooperating with the IT department to investigate the incident.
* A thorough search of the premises has been conducted, and the area has been secured.
* The IT department is reviewing security footage to determine the time and circumstances of the laptop's disappearance.
