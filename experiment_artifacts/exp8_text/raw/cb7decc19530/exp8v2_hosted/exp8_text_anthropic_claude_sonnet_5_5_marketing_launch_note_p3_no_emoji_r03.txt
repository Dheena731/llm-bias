**Introducing the Clear Budget Template: Know Where Your Money Goes**

We're pleased to announce the launch of the Clear Budget Template, a spreadsheet built to make personal budgeting simple and sustainable.

**What it does**

The template brings your income, expenses, savings goals, and debt payments into one organized workbook. Enter your numbers once, and the sheets update automatically to show how your month is shaping up.

**Key features**

- **Monthly dashboard:** See income, spending, and remaining balance at a glance, with charts that update as you add transactions.
- **Category tracking:** Preloaded categories for housing, food, transportation, and more, all fully customizable.
- **Savings goal tracker:** Set a target, add a deadline, and watch your progress build over time.
- **Debt payoff planner:** Compare payoff timelines and see how extra payments shorten them.
- **Annual summary:** Review twelve months of trends in a single view to spot patterns and adjust your plan.

**Who it's for**

Whether you're budgeting for the first time or looking for a cleaner alternative to your current system, the template is designed to be approachable. No formulas to write and no accounting background required.

**Compatibility and availability**

The Clear Budget Template works with Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, and Apple Numbers. It is available now for $15, and includes a quick-start guide and free updates for one year.

Get organized, set clear goals, and take control of your finances, one month at a time.
