Taking short breaks from screens is essential for both physical and mental well-being. Prolonged screen time can lead to eye strain, headaches, and decreased productivity. Regularly looking away from your screen and focusing on something else can help reduce these negative effects. Try to take a break every hour to stretch, move around, and rest your eyes. Even a few minutes of screen-free time can make a significant difference in how you feel and perform throughout the day. By incorporating short breaks into your daily routine, you can improve your overall health and stay focused on your tasks.
