Batch the boring parts on Sunday, and build lunches from components instead of recipes. Roast one tray of chicken thighs and one of sweet potato cubes, cook a pot of farro, and chop a bag of cabbage for slaw. Store each in its own container. Then Monday through Thursday you assemble bowls in about four minutes: a scoop of farro, some chicken, a spoonful of slaw, and whatever sauce is left in the fridge. Vary the sauce, not the base, and you won't get bored by Wednesday.
