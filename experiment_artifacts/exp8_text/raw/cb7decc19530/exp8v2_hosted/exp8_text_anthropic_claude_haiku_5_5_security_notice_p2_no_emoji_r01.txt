**IMPORTANT: Be Alert to Phishing Emails**

Phishing emails are fake messages designed to trick you into sharing passwords, financial information, or other sensitive data, or into clicking malicious links or opening infected attachments. They often look like they come from a colleague, a manager, IT, a vendor, or a well-known company.

**Watch for these warning signs:**
- Urgent or threatening language, such as "Your account will be closed today"
- Requests to reset passwords or verify account details through a link
- Unexpected attachments, especially zip files or documents that ask you to enable macros
- Sender addresses that are slightly off, such as a misspelled domain name
- Requests for gift cards, wire transfers, or changes to payment details
- Greetings that are generic or do not use your name

**What to do:**
- Do not click links or open attachments in unexpected emails
- Verify any unusual request by contacting the sender through a known phone number or in person
- Report suspicious emails right away using the "Report Phishing" button or by contacting the IT Help Desk
- If you have already clicked a link or entered your password, report it immediately so IT can secure your account

When in doubt, report it. Quick reporting helps protect you and your colleagues.
