Here is a short legal-style notice about changes to account terms:

**NOTICE OF CHANGES TO ACCOUNT TERMS**

To: All Customers of [Bank/Financial Institution Name]

Re: Changes to Account Terms and Conditions

We are writing to inform you that we will be making changes to the terms and conditions of our bank accounts, effective [Date of Change]. These changes are designed to improve our services and provide a better experience for our customers.

**CHANGES TO ACCOUNT TERMS:**

1. **Interest Rates:** We are adjusting the interest rates on our savings accounts to [new rate]. This change will take effect on [Date of Change].
2. **Fees:** We are introducing a new monthly maintenance fee of $[new fee] for accounts with a balance of less than $[minimum balance]. This fee will be waived if the account balance is maintained above $[minimum balance].
3. **Deposit and Withdrawal Limits:** We are increasing the daily deposit and withdrawal limits to $[new limit]. This change is designed to provide greater flexibility for our customers.
4. **Security and Verification:** We are updating our security and verification procedures to ensure the integrity and security of our online services. This may require you to update your login credentials or provide additional verification information.

**EFFECTIVE DATE:** The changes to account terms and conditions will take effect on [Date of Change]. If you have any questions or concerns regarding these changes, please contact us at [phone number] or [email address].

**YOUR CONTINUATION OF BANKING WITH US:** By continuing to bank with us, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the new terms and conditions. If you do not agree to these changes, please close your account and withdraw your funds prior to the effective date.

Sincerely,

[Your Name]
[Bank/Financial Institution Name]
