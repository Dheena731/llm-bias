Join us this weekend for the Riverside Neighborhood Block Party on Saturday, June 14, from 11:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. at Maple Street Park. The day will feature live music from local bands, a farmers market with fresh produce and homemade goods, face painting and games for kids, and food trucks serving lunch and snacks. Volunteers are also needed to help with setup starting at 9:00 a.m., so if you can spare an hour or two, please reach out to the community center front desk. Bring your lawn chairs, invite your neighbors, and come enjoy a fun afternoon together. Rain or shine, we hope to see you there!
