The local library is open to the public from 9am to 5pm, Monday through Friday, and from 10am to 1pm on Saturdays. The library is closed on Sundays and public holidays. During these hours, visitors can browse the shelves, borrow books and other materials, and access the library's computers and other facilities. Library staff are also available to assist with research and answer questions. Please note that the library may close earlier than usual on days when severe weather or other unforeseen circumstances occur, so it is a good idea to call ahead to confirm opening hours before visiting.
