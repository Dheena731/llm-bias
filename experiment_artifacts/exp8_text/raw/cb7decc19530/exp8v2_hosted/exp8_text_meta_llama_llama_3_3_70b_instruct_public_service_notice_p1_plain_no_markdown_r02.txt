The local library is open to the public from 9am to 5pm on weekdays. On Saturdays, the library is open from 10am to 1pm. The library is closed on Sundays and public holidays. Please note that these hours may be subject to change, so it is a good idea to call ahead or check the library's website to confirm the hours before visiting. This will help you plan your visit and make the most of your time at the library.
