**Public Notice: Library Hours of Operation**

The public library welcomes all community members to visit during its regular weekly operating hours:

* **Monday through Thursday:** 9:00 a.m. – 8:00 p.m.
* **Friday and Saturday:** 10:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.
* **Sunday:** Closed

During open hours, visitors can borrow materials, use public computers, and access study spaces. The exterior book return drop box is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week, for items returned while the building is closed.
