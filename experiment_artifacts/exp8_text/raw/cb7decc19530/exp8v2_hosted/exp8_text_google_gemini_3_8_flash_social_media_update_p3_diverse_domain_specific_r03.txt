Broke a toaster lever or split a winter coat seam? Bring them over to the Grange basement this Saturday for the monthly fix-it clinic (9 to noon). 

Volunteer benches are confirmed for:
*   Small appliances & lamps (Dave’s got the multimeter, solder, and heat shrink ready)
*   Textiles & mending (two sewing machines and a serger running; please pre-wash anything you bring)
*   Dull garden loppers and kitchen knives (whetstones on the back worktable)

One item at a time so everyone gets seen. Free as always, though coffee donations keep the lights on. If you have any spare 18-gauge wire or odd zipper pulls rattling around in a drawer, drop them in the parts bin by the door.
