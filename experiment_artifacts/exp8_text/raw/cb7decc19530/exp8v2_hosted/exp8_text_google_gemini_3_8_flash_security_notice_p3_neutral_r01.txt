**Subject:** Security Reminder: Lock It Before You Leave It 🔒

Hi Team,

A friendly reminder to please **lock your computer screen** whenever you step away from your desk, even if it’s just for a quick coffee break. 

Locking your device takes just a second and ensures our sensitive data, client information, and your personal privacy remain secure.

**Quick Shortcuts:**
* **Windows:** Press **Win + L**
* **Mac:** Press **Cmd + Ctrl + Q** (or close your laptop lid)

Thank you for doing your part to keep our workplace secure!
