**Subject: Understanding Your End-of-Year Tax Statement**

Hello,

Your end-of-year tax statement is now available. It summarizes your financial activity for the past calendar year and gives you the figures needed to file your tax return. Here is what you will find in it:

**Income and earnings:** This section lists the total amount you earned or received during the year, which may include wages, interest, dividends, or other payments.

**Taxes withheld or paid:** This shows the amount already paid toward your taxes during the year. Comparing it with your total tax owed helps determine whether you will receive a refund or owe a balance.

**Deductions and credits:** Any eligible amounts that reduce your taxable income or your final tax bill will be listed here.

**Account or reference details:** Check that your name, address, and tax identification number are correct. If anything is wrong, contact us promptly so we can issue a corrected statement.

**Next steps:**
1. Review the statement carefully and compare it with your own records.
2. Keep a copy for your files.
3. Use the figures when preparing your tax return, or share the statement with your tax preparer.

This message is general information and not personal tax advice. If you have questions about how the figures apply to your situation, a qualified tax professional can help. You are also welcome to reply to this message or contact our support team.
