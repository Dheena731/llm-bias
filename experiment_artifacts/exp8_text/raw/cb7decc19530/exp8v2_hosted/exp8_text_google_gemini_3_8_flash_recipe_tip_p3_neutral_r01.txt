**Rinse before you cook:** Place your raw rice in a fine-mesh strainer and rinse it thoroughly under cold water until the water runs mostly clear. This washes away the excess surface starch, which is the main culprit behind gummy, clumpy rice. 

*(Bonus: After cooking, let it rest covered off the heat for 10 minutes, then gently fluff it with a fork rather than stirring with a spoon.)*
