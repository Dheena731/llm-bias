Most preventive care happens before anything hurts. 

An annual checkup isn’t a reaction to symptoms—it’s how we track your clinical baseline. It’s the dedicated space to look at cardiovascular and metabolic trends (lipids, A1C, liver/kidney function), update age-appropriate screening intervals, check blood pressure drift, and renew maintenance prescriptions for the year ahead.

If it has been roughly twelve months since your last complete physical, please take a moment to book through the patient portal or call the front desk. 

*(Note: If you plan on doing fasting labs during the visit, we recommend requesting an early morning slot.)*
