Notice of Recycling Collection Changes

Beginning Monday, the first of next month, curbside recycling collection in our community will move from a weekly schedule to an every-other-week schedule. Your recycling day will stay the same, but pickup will now occur only on alternating weeks. A revised calendar showing your collection dates will be mailed to every household within the next ten days, and copies will also be available at the town office and the public library.

Along with the new schedule, we are updating the list of materials we can accept. Clean paper, cardboard, metal cans, glass bottles and jars, and plastic containers marked with the numbers one, two, and five will continue to be collected. Plastic bags, foam packaging, and shredded paper will no longer be accepted in curbside bins because they jam sorting equipment and contaminate otherwise good material. Please take plastic bags to the collection bins at local grocery stores instead.

To help your recycling get collected, please rinse containers, flatten cardboard boxes, and place all items loosely in the bin rather than in bags. Set your bin at the curb by seven in the morning on your collection day, with the lid closed and a few feet of space around it. Bins containing unacceptable items may be left uncollected with a tag explaining the reason.

Residents who need an additional or larger bin can request one at no charge by calling the public works office during regular business hours. We appreciate your patience during this transition and your continued effort to keep our community clean.
