Notice: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop

If your company laptop is lost or stolen, please report it immediately, even if you believe you may find it soon. Prompt reporting is the most effective way to protect company data, customer information, and your own accounts from unauthorized access.

Contact the IT Service Desk as soon as you realize the device is missing. You can reach them by phone at any hour, and you should use the phone rather than email if possible, since a call allows the team to begin securing the device right away. Be ready to provide your name, the laptop's asset tag or serial number if you have it, the last place and time you remember having it, and whether it contained any sensitive files. If you are unsure of any detail, report what you know and add to it later.

Once your report is received, IT will lock or remotely wipe the device, disable its access to company systems, and reset your passwords as needed. Please do not attempt to log in to company services from the missing laptop's location or try to track it yourself. If you believe the laptop was stolen, notify local police and keep a copy of the report number, as IT and Security may request it for their records. Please also inform your manager so that a replacement can be arranged without delay.

Reporting a lost laptop will not be treated as a disciplinary matter in itself. Delays in reporting, however, can increase risk to the company and may be reviewed. Everyone makes mistakes, and the goal is to limit any harm quickly.

To help prevent loss, keep your laptop with you when traveling, avoid leaving it visible in vehicles, and make sure your device is always encrypted and protected by a strong password. If you have questions about this process, please contact the IT Service Desk.
