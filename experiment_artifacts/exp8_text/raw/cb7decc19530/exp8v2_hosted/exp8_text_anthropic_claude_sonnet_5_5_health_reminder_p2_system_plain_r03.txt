A quick reminder to keep drinking water throughout the day. Even mild dehydration can leave you feeling tired, foggy, or a little achy, and it often sneaks up on you when you're busy. Try keeping a glass or bottle within reach, take a few sips whenever you finish a task or check your phone, and have a full glass with each meal. If plain water gets boring, a slice of lemon or cucumber can help. Your body will thank you with better energy and focus.
