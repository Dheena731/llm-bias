Thank you for submitting this draft of your literature review. Overall, you have assembled a strong and relevant body of research that aligns well with your central research question. The breadth of your references demonstrates a commendable initial grasp of the field, and your inclusion of recent empirical studies gives the review solid contemporary grounding.

To strengthen the review in your next revision, you should focus on shifting from summary to synthesis. At present, several sections read as sequential overviews of individual studies, with consecutive paragraphs dedicated to single authors. Instead, aim to group your sources thematically to illustrate how these scholars converse with, contradict, or build upon one another. Organizing the material around key debates, methodologies, or conceptual frameworks rather than individual researchers will make your argument much more dynamic.

Additionally, make sure to assert your own critical voice throughout the text. Rather than merely reporting findings, evaluate the methodological strengths and limitations of the existing scholarship, and explicitly highlight the gaps that your study aims to address. Refining the transitions between themes will also help clarify the overall narrative thread and better demonstrate why this body of work necessitates your proposed research. This is a very promising foundation, and with deeper comparative analysis, it will become an effective and cohesive chapter.
