A heat advisory has been issued for our area due to extremely high temperatures and humidity levels. Residents are advised to take precautions to stay cool and safe during this time. Stay indoors during the hottest part of the day, typically between 11am and 3pm, and seek air-conditioned spaces if possible. Drink plenty of water and other hydrating fluids to help your body regulate its temperature. Avoid strenuous outdoor activities and check on vulnerable neighbors, such as the elderly and young children, to ensure they are staying cool and safe. If you or someone you know is experiencing heat-related illness, such as dizziness, nausea, or headaches, seek medical attention immediately.
