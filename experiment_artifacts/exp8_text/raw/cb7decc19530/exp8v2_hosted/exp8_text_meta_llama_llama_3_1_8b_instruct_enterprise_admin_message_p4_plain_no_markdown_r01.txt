Quarterly Access Reviews

We will be conducting quarterly access reviews to ensure that all users have the necessary access to our systems and data. These reviews are a routine part of our security and compliance procedures.

The purpose of these reviews is to verify that each user's access is still required and to ensure that their permissions have not been duplicated. This process helps to prevent unnecessary access and reduces the risk of unauthorized changes.

During the review, we will be checking the following:

- Current access levels
- Required access levels
- Duplicate access

If you have any questions or concerns about your access, please contact the IT department.
