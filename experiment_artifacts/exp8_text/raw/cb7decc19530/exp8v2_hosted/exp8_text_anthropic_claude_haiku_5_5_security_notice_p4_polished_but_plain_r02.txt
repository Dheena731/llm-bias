Two-Factor Authentication Is Now Available

To help keep your account secure, we now offer two-factor authentication, which adds a second step to the sign-in process. In addition to your password, you will be asked to confirm your identity with a one-time code sent to your phone or generated by an authenticator app. Even if someone learns your password, they will not be able to access your account without that second code.

To turn on two-factor authentication, sign in to your account and open the Security section of your account settings. Select the option to enable two-factor authentication, then follow the on-screen instructions to link your phone number or authenticator app. You will be given a set of backup codes at the end of setup. Store these somewhere safe, as they allow you to sign in if you lose access to your phone.

We strongly encourage all users to enable this feature. It takes only a few minutes to set up and provides meaningful protection against unauthorized access. If you have questions or run into trouble during setup, please contact our support team, who will be glad to help.
