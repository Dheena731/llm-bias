When seasoning roasted vegetables, it's best to season them before and after roasting for the most flavor. Start by tossing the vegetables with a bit of oil, salt, and any other desired seasonings before putting them in the oven. Then, when they come out of the oven, sprinkle them with additional seasonings such as herbs, spices, or lemon juice to add brightness and depth to the dish. This two-step seasoning process will help bring out the natural flavors of the vegetables and add complexity to the finished dish.
