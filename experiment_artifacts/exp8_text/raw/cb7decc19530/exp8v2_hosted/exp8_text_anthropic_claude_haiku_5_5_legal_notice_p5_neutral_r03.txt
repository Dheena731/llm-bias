**ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY FOR SHARED NETWORK RESOURCES**

**1. Purpose and Scope**
This Acceptable Use Policy ("Policy") governs the use of the shared network, computing systems, internet access, email, and related resources (collectively, the "Network") made available by [Organization Name] (the "Organization"). This Policy applies to all users, including employees, contractors, students, and guests (each, a "User").

**2. Authorized Use**
The Network is provided for lawful, business-related, educational, or otherwise authorized purposes. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with Network performance, the User's responsibilities, or the rights of others.

**3. Prohibited Conduct**
Users shall not, without express written authorization:

a. Access, attempt to access, or share data or systems for which they lack authorization;
b. Use the Network to transmit unlawful, harassing, defamatory, obscene, or discriminatory material;
c. Introduce malware, or intentionally disrupt, degrade, or circumvent security controls or network operations;
d. Share login credentials or permit unauthorized persons to use the Network through their accounts;
e. Download, copy, or distribute copyrighted or licensed material in violation of applicable law or license terms;
f. Use the Network for commercial purposes unrelated to the Organization's activities; or
g. Engage in cryptocurrency mining, bandwidth-intensive activity, or other uses that impair shared resources.

**4. Security Responsibilities**
Users are responsible for safeguarding their credentials and devices and must promptly report suspected security incidents, lost devices, or unauthorized access to the IT Department at [contact information].

**5. Monitoring and Privacy**
Users acknowledge that the Organization may monitor, log, and review Network activity to maintain security, ensure compliance with this Policy, and comply with legal obligations. Users should have no expectation of privacy in communications or data transmitted over the Network, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

**6. Enforcement**
Violations of this Policy may result in suspension or revocation of Network access, disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or enrollment, and referral to law enforcement where appropriate. The Organization reserves the right to take any additional action permitted by law.

**7. Amendments**
The Organization may amend this Policy at any time. Continued use of the Network following notice of changes constitutes acceptance of the amended Policy.

**8. Acknowledgment**
By accessing or using the Network, the User agrees to comply with this Policy. Questions regarding this Policy should be directed to [Name/Title, Contact Information].
