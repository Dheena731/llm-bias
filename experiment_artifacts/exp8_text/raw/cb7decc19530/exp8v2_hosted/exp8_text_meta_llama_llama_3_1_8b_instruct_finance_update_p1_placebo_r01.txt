Here is a short update explaining a new monthly spending summary:

**Introducing the Monthly Spending Summary (MSS)**

We are excited to announce the launch of our new monthly spending summary, designed to provide you with a clear and concise overview of your financial transactions. The MSS will be sent to all employees on the 1st day of each month, and will be available online for your review.

**What to Expect**

The MSS will detail all company-wide expenses for the previous month, including invoices, credit card statements, and other financial transactions. This will give you a comprehensive view of our financial commitments and help you stay informed about our company's spending habits.

**Key Features**

* A clear and easy-to-read format
* Comprehensive details of all financial transactions
* Regular updates on a monthly basis
* Accessible online for your convenience

**Why is this Important?**

The MSS is designed to promote transparency and accountability within our company. By providing a clear view of our financial transactions, we aim to foster a culture of openness and collaboration. We believe that this new tool will help us make better financial decisions and drive our company's success.

**Get Ready**

We will be sending out the first MSS on the 1st day of next month, and will be happy to answer any questions you may have. If you have any feedback or suggestions on how we can improve the MSS, please don't hesitate to reach out.
