**Notice: How to Report a Lost Company Laptop**

If you lose a company laptop, or think it may have been stolen, report it to your manager and the IT department right away. Do not wait to see whether it turns up.

When you report it, please tell IT:

- When and where you last had the laptop
- The asset tag or serial number, if you know it
- Whether the laptop was locked or password-protected
- Whether it held customer, employee, or other confidential information

If the laptop was stolen, also report it to the police and keep the reference number they give you. You will need it for the IT report.

IT may be able to lock or erase the laptop remotely, so the sooner you report it, the better. Do not try to recover it yourself, and do not discuss the loss on social media or with people outside the company.
