The city is making some changes to the recycling collection schedule. Starting next month, recyclables will be collected every other week instead of weekly. This change is intended to help reduce costs and make the recycling program more efficient. Residents are encouraged to check the city's website for their new collection schedule and to make sure they are recycling the correct materials. Additionally, the city will be providing new recycling bins to all households to help make the transition smoother. If you have any questions or concerns about the changes, you can contact the city's public works department for more information.
