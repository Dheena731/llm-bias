To maximize the lifespan of fresh herbs, categorize them by the texture of their stems. For soft herbs like parsley and cilantro, trim the stem ends and stand them upright in a small jar with an inch of water, loosely tenting a plastic bag over the leaves before placing the jar in the refrigerator. Basil should be stored the same way, but kept on the counter at room temperature, as cold air quickly turns its leaves black. For hearty, woody herbs such as rosemary and thyme, wrap them loosely in a slightly damp paper towel and seal them inside an airtight container or resealable bag in the crisper drawer to maintain optimal humidity without trapping excess moisture.
