Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations

Polling station locations have been confirmed for the upcoming election. Voters are asked to check the location assigned to them before election day, as some stations may differ from those used in previous elections. Your assigned polling station is listed on your voter registration record and can be confirmed through the local elections office, either by telephone or on its official website. Please allow a few minutes to check, since a station change may not be obvious.

Polling stations will be open from the hours stated on the official election calendar. Voters who need assistance with access, including step-free entry, parking, or support at the polling booth, are encouraged to contact the elections office in advance so that arrangements can be made. Any voter who arrives at the wrong location should go to the station listed for their address, as ballots can only be cast at the correct station.

For questions about polling locations, accessibility, or voter identification requirements, please contact the local elections office during business hours.
