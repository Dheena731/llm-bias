# Cookie Notice

**1. What cookies are**
Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit. They allow the website to function, to remember information about your visit, and to recognise your device on later visits.

**2. How this website uses cookies**
This website uses two categories of cookies:

- **Strictly necessary cookies** are required for the website to work, for example to keep it secure or to remember your cookie choices. We set these without asking for consent because the website cannot function properly without them.
- **Optional cookies** are all other cookies, such as those that remember your preferences or measure how visitors use the website. We set these only if you consent.

**3. Your choices**
You may accept or reject optional cookies using the cookie choices provided on this website. You may change your decision or withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of our use of optional cookies before you withdrew it.

You can also delete or block cookies through your browser settings. If you block all cookies, including strictly necessary ones, some parts of this website may not work correctly.

**4. Changes to this notice**
We may update this notice from time to time. The version published on this page is the current version.

**5. Questions**
If you have questions about this notice or our use of cookies, please contact us using the contact details provided on this website.
