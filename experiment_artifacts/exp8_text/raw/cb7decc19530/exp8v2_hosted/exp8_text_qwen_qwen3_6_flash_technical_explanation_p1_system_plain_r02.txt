Latency refers to the delay between a user initiating an action and the system responding. In web applications, low latency is essential because it determines how responsive and fluid the experience feels to the visitor. When latency is high, pages take longer to load, interactions feel sluggish, and content appears gradually rather than all at once. This delay frustrates users, leading them to abandon the site if it does not perform quickly enough. Research consistently shows that even minor increases in load time can significantly reduce user satisfaction, engagement, and conversion rates, resulting in direct financial loss for businesses. Furthermore, search engines prioritize faster websites in their rankings, meaning high latency can also diminish organic traffic. By keeping latency low, developers ensure that applications remain accessible, enjoyable, and effective at meeting user expectations and business objectives.
