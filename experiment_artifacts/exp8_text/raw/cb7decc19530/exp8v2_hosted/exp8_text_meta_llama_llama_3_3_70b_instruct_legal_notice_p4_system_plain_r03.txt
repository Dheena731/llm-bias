NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Purchases made are eligible for a refund under certain conditions. To be eligible for a refund, the item must be returned in its original condition within thirty days of the purchase date. All returns must be accompanied by the original receipt and any other documentation provided at the time of purchase. Refunds will be issued in the same form as the original payment. Items that have been altered, used, or damaged in any way are not eligible for a refund. Additionally, shipping and handling costs are non-refundable. Refund eligibility is subject to verification and approval by the seller.
