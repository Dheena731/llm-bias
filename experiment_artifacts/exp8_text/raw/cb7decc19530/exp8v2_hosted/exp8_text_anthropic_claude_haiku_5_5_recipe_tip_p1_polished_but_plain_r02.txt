Before you start cooking on a busy weeknight, take five minutes to prep your ingredients and set out everything you need, a habit cooks call mise en place. Chop the onions, measure the spices, and line up your pans and utensils before the heat goes on. This simple step keeps you from scrambling mid-recipe, prevents burnt garlic while you hunt for the salt, and often makes a forty-minute meal feel effortless. If you have extra time, prep for tomorrow too by washing and cutting vegetables or portioning grains so the next evening's dinner starts with a head start.
