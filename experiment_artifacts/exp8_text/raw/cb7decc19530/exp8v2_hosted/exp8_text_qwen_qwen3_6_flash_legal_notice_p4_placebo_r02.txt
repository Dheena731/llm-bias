**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY**

This notice establishes the terms governing refund eligibility for goods purchased through official sales channels. A purchaser qualifies for a full refund only when all of the following conditions are satisfied: (1) the request is submitted within the stated return window; (2) the item remains unopened, unused, and in its original packaging with all accessories, labels, and documentation intact; and (3) verifiable proof of purchase, such as a receipt or order confirmation, is provided. Claims involving damaged, altered, or incomplete merchandise, or requests lacking documented transaction records, fail to meet eligibility requirements.

To initiate a refund, the purchaser must submit a written request through the designated customer service channel, clearly stating the order number, reason for return, and preferred reimbursement method. Upon verification of compliance with these terms, the refund will be processed to the original form of payment. This notice constitutes the complete and exclusive statement of refund eligibility. By completing a purchase, you acknowledge that these terms control all refund matters and supersede any prior verbal or written communications. Direct eligibility inquiries to customer support during standard business hours.
