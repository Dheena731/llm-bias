**Reminder: Don't Let Your Medication Run Dry!**

If you're taking a medication that requires regular refills, don't forget to schedule a refill with your pharmacy before you run out. To avoid any gaps in your treatment, please:

* Check your prescription label for the refill schedule
* Call your pharmacy 3-5 days before you need a refill to confirm availability
* Ask your pharmacist about any additional paperwork or documentation required for the refill
* Make sure to ask about any potential changes to your medication or dosage, as your healthcare provider may have updated your prescription.

Remember, timely refills are crucial for maintaining effective treatment and preventing any potential complications.
