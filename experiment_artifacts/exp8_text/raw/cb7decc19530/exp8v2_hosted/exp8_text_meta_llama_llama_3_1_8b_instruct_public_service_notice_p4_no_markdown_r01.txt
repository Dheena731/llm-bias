HEAT ADVISORY ISSUED FOR THE REGION

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for our area, effective immediately. Temperatures are expected to reach the mid-90s today and will remain elevated throughout the weekend.

Residents are advised to take precautions to protect themselves from the heat. This includes staying hydrated by drinking plenty of water, taking breaks in cool spaces, and avoiding strenuous activities during the hottest part of the day.

Older adults, young children, and people with pre-existing medical conditions are particularly vulnerable to heat-related illnesses. If you or someone you know is experiencing symptoms such as dizziness, nausea, or headaches, seek medical attention immediately.

Check on vulnerable neighbors and friends, and consider visiting them in air-conditioned spaces. Never leave children or pets unattended in a vehicle, even for a short time.

For updates on the heat advisory, follow local news and weather reports. Stay safe and stay informed.
