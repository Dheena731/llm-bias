Here is a concise tooltip that explains what a missing-value marker means in the context of data analysis or survey research:

"Missing-value marker: This symbol (often a red circle or a question mark) indicates that a data point is not available or has not been reported. In survey research, it may represent a respondent who declined to answer or was unsure about the question. In data analysis, it can signify a gap in the dataset that requires further investigation or imputation to ensure accurate conclusions are drawn."
