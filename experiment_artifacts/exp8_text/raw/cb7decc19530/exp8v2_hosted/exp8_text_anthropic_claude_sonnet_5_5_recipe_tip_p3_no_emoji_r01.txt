**Rinse your rice before cooking.** Place the rice in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer and rinse it under cold water, swishing it with your hand, until the water runs mostly clear. This washes away the excess surface starch that makes grains clump together. For best results, also use a slightly lower water ratio, about 1 3/4 cups of water per 1 cup of long-grain rice, and let the cooked rice rest, covered and off the heat, for 10 minutes before fluffing it with a fork.
