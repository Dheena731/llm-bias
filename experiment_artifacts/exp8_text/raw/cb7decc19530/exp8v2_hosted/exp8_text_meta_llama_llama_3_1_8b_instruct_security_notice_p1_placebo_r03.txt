**Secure Your Account: Avoid Password Reuse**

As part of our ongoing efforts to protect company data and maintain a secure work environment, we want to remind everyone of the importance of using unique and complex passwords for all company accounts and systems.

**Why is password reuse a concern?**

Using the same password for multiple accounts increases the risk of unauthorized access to company data. If a single password is compromised, an attacker may be able to access multiple accounts, putting sensitive information and systems at risk.

**What can you do?**

*   Use a password manager to generate and store unique, complex passwords for each account.
*   Avoid using easily guessable information, such as your name, birthdate, or common words.
*   Change your passwords regularly, as required by company policy.

By taking these simple steps, you can help protect the company's data and maintain a secure work environment. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or the IT department.
