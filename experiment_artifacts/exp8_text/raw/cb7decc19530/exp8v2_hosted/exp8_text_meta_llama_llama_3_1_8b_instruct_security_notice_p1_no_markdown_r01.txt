**Workplace Security Notice**

**Password Reuse: A Security Risk**

To all employees,

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain a secure work environment, we would like to remind everyone of the importance of using unique and complex passwords. Password reuse, where the same password is used across multiple accounts, is a significant security risk that can leave our company vulnerable to unauthorized access.

If you reuse the same password across multiple accounts, including personal and work-related ones, it is not only a security risk but also a breach of our company's security policies. In the event of a data breach, an attacker may be able to gain access to all accounts that share the same password, putting sensitive information at risk.

To minimize this risk, we recommend that you:

* Use a unique password for each account
* Change your passwords regularly (every 60-90 days)
* Use a password manager to generate and store complex passwords
* Avoid using easily guessable information, such as names, dates, or common words

If you are unsure about the security of your passwords or have any questions, please contact our IT department for guidance and support.

Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining a secure work environment.
