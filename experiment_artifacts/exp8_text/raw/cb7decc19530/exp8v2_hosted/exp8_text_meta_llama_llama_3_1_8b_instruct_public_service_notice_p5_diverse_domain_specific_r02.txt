**Election Day Polling Station Locations**

To ensure a smooth voting process, please note the designated polling station locations for the upcoming election:

*   Voters registered in Wards 1-3 can cast their ballots at the Community Center (123 Main St). 
*   Residents of Wards 4-6 can vote at the local library (456 Elm St).
*   Those registered in Wards 7-9 should proceed to the high school (789 Oak St).

Polling stations will be open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on Election Day. Voters are required to bring a valid form of identification and proof of residency to cast their ballots.

If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the election office at (555) 555-5555 or visit our website at [elections.gov](http://elections.gov) for more information.
