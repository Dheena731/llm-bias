Notice to All Staff: Be Alert for Phishing Emails

We have recently seen an increase in phishing emails aimed at our employees. These messages are designed to look like they come from a trusted source, such as a colleague, a vendor, a bank, or our own IT department, but their real purpose is to trick you into revealing passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information, or into opening a harmful attachment.

Please be cautious with any message that creates a sense of urgency, asks for login credentials, requests a payment or a change in bank details, or comes from an address that looks slightly different from the one you expect. Poor spelling, an unusual greeting, and unexpected attachments are also common warning signs. Before you click anything, hover over the sender's name and any web address to check where it truly leads.

If you receive a message that seems suspicious, do not reply to it, do not open any attachments, and do not enter any information. Forward it to the IT Help Desk as an attachment so the team can investigate, and then delete it from your inbox. If you have already clicked a link or shared information by mistake, please contact the IT Help Desk immediately. Reporting quickly allows us to protect your accounts and limit any damage, and no one will be penalized for an honest mistake.

Remember that IT staff will never ask for your password by email or phone. When in doubt, verify the request by contacting the sender through a phone number or channel you already know to be genuine. Thank you for helping keep our organization and our data secure.
