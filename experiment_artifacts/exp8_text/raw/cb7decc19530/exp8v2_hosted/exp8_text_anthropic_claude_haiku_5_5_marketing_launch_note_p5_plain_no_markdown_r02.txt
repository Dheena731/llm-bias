Welcome to Words Between Us, a new podcast for anyone who has ever wanted to speak another language but felt stuck somewhere between the textbook and the real world. Each week, we will sit down with native speakers, teachers, translators, and curious learners to talk about how languages actually live: the slang that changes overnight, the grammar rules that bend in everyday conversation, and the small moments of confusion and delight that come with learning to communicate in a new tongue.

Our first season focuses on five languages, and each episode pairs a short lesson with a longer conversation. You will hear how a Lisbon baker greets her regulars, how a Tokyo high school student uses politeness levels with friends versus teachers, and how a Nairobi translator handles idioms that refuse to survive the journey into English. Along the way, we will offer practical phrases you can try out right away, along with honest notes on where learners tend to stumble.

You do not need to be fluent to join us. Whether you are a beginner who can barely order a coffee abroad or an intermediate speaker looking to sound more natural, there is something here for you. Pull up a chair, press play, and let's start talking.
