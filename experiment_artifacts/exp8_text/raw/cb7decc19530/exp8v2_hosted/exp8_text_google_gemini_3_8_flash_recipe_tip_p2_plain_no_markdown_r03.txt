To keep fresh herbs lasting longer, treat them like a bouquet of flowers. Trim the bottom of the stems and place the bunch upright in a glass with an inch of water. For herbs like parsley and cilantro, loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and store the jar in the refrigerator. For basil, keep the jar on your counter at room temperature, because the cold fridge will turn its leaves black. Change the water every couple of days to maximize freshness.
