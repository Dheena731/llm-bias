When you get home on a weeknight, start the slowest part of your meal before you do anything else. If you are making pasta, put the water on to boil first. If you are roasting vegetables, get the oven heating and the pan loaded while you think about the rest. Then use that waiting time to chop, season, and prepare the faster components, like a quick pan sauce or a simple salad. This small shift in order saves you ten to fifteen minutes and keeps you from standing around waiting for something to heat up. It also helps to cut your vegetables and proteins into smaller, uniform pieces, since they cook faster and more evenly. A sheet pan with chicken thighs and chopped potatoes, for example, can be done in about thirty minutes while you put together a simple side, leaving you with very little cleanup afterward.
