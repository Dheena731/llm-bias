Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit it. They allow the site to recognize your browser across visits and to remember information about your activity. Some cookies are set by us, while others are set by third parties whose services appear on our pages.

We use strictly necessary cookies to enable core functions such as page navigation, secure access to protected areas, and the remembering of choices you make during a session. These cookies do not require your consent because the site cannot operate properly without them. We also use analytics cookies, which collect aggregated information about how visitors arrive at and use the site so that we can improve its content and performance. Where we use functional or marketing cookies, we will only place them after you have given your consent through our cookie banner or preference settings.

You may accept or reject non-essential cookies at any time. You can change your preferences by using the cookie settings available on this website, and you can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the site and the services you are able to use.

Some information collected through cookies may be processed by third-party providers acting on our behalf or under their own privacy policies. We take reasonable steps to ensure that such providers handle data in accordance with applicable data protection laws, but we encourage you to review their policies for further detail.

Where cookies involve the processing of personal data, our Privacy Policy describes the legal bases for that processing, the categories of data involved, how long it is retained, and your rights, including rights of access, correction, erasure, and objection. If you have questions about this notice or our use of cookies, please contact us using the details provided in the Contact section of this website.
