**Subject: Lock your screen when you step away**

Going to the printer, the kitchen, or a colleague's desk? Lock first. It takes one second:

- **Windows:** Windows key + L
- **Mac:** Control + Command + Q
- **Shared or hot-desk machines:** sign out completely

An unlocked screen lets anyone who walks past read client files, send email as you, or approve a request under your name. The audit log will record it as you, not them.

Our auto-lock triggers after five minutes of inactivity, which is plenty of time for all of the above. Treat it as the backup, not the habit.
