Before you heat a pan, spend five minutes arranging all your ingredients and tools within reach. Wash and chop vegetables, measure spices, and position utensils so that nothing is missing during the actual cooking process. This simple step prevents the frustration of searching for salt while garlic browns or realizing you are out of oil mid-fry. By eliminating these interruptions, you maintain a steady rhythm, reduce the chance of burning food, and lower the mental load, allowing you to serve a hot, balanced meal efficiently even when time is tight.
