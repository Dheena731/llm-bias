To prepare a quick and delicious weeknight dinner, consider cooking proteins and vegetables in bulk on the weekends or a day off. This approach, known as "meal prep," allows you to grill, roast, or sauté a large quantity of chicken, beef, or vegetables, which can then be refrigerated or frozen for up to three days. During the week, simply reheat the pre-cooked ingredients and combine them with a fresh component, such as a salad or a whole grain, to create a balanced and satisfying meal. This strategy saves time, reduces food waste, and makes it easier to stick to healthy eating habits, even on busy weeknights.
