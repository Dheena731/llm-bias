A simple way to make a weeknight dinner come together faster is to prep your vegetables and aromatics while the pan or oven heats up, rather than after you've started cooking. Chop onions, garlic, and peppers during the first few minutes of preheating, and store them in small bowls near the stove. Once the heat is ready, everything is within reach, so you can move from one step to the next without pausing. This habit also helps prevent the common problem of burning garlic or overcooking vegetables while you are scrambling to chop something mid-recipe.
