We are writing to share an upcoming adjustment to our payment processing rates. Beginning on the first of next month, the standard fee for credit and debit card transactions will update to 2.6 percent plus ten cents per transaction. This change reflects recent increases in network interchange costs and supports ongoing investments in our payment security, reliability, and processing speed.

The new rates will apply automatically to all card transactions processed on or after the effective date, so no changes are required on your end. You can review your recent transaction volume and monitor fee breakdowns directly within your billing portal. If you have questions about how this update applies to your account, or if you would like to discuss volume-based pricing options, our merchant support team is available to assist you.
