**NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE**
**Harlan Creek Road, between Mill Pond Road and Route 9**
Issued by the Dunmore Township Department of Public Works

Harlan Creek Road will be closed to all through traffic from **7:00 a.m. Monday, March 10, through 5:00 p.m. Friday, March 21**, while crews replace the failing culvert under the creek near the Tanner farm lane. The old pipe has collapsed on its downstream side, and the road edge above it is already cracking.

**Detour:** Follow Mill Pond Road north to Quarry Road, then Quarry Road east to Route 9. Allow about 12 extra minutes at peak hours.

**Local access:** Residents with homes on either side of the work zone can drive to their properties from their end. The barricade will be staffed, and crews will move it for emergency vehicles at any hour.

**School buses:** Route 14 will run the Quarry Road detour. Stops on Harlan Creek will be moved to the corner of Quarry and Orchard Lane. Parents should check the district app for pickup times.

**Mail and deliveries:** Carriers will route through the detour. Packages may be held at the Dunmore post office if the driveway is unreachable.

Questions or concerns: Public Works, (555) 0147, weekdays 7:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. After hours, call the non-emergency line at (555) 0199.
