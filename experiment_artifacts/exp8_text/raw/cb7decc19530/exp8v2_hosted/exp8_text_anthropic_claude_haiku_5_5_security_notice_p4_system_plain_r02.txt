Two-Factor Authentication Is Now Available

We are pleased to announce that two-factor authentication (2FA) can now be enabled on your account. This added layer of security helps protect your information by requiring a second verification step when you sign in, in addition to your password.

To turn it on, sign in to your account and open the Security section of your account settings. Select "Enable two-factor authentication" and follow the prompts to link an authenticator app or receive a verification code by text message. Be sure to save the backup codes you are given, as they allow you to regain access if you lose your phone.

We strongly recommend enabling this feature. Accounts protected by two-factor authentication are far less likely to be compromised, even if a password is exposed elsewhere. If you have questions or run into trouble during setup, please contact our support team, and we will be glad to help.
