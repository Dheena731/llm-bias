Subject: New expense approval workflow starting next month

Hello everyone,

Beginning on the first of next month, we are moving to a new expense approval workflow to make reimbursements faster and easier to track. All expense reports will now be submitted through the finance portal instead of by email. Please attach itemized receipts to each report and select the correct project or cost center before you submit.

Once a report is submitted, it will go to your direct manager for review. Managers will have three business days to approve or return it with comments. Reports over five hundred dollars will then move to the finance team for a second review, and approved reimbursements will be paid in the next scheduled payroll cycle. You will receive an automatic notification at each stage, so there should be no need to follow up by email unless a report has been waiting longer than expected.

Please submit any outstanding expenses under the old process by the last day of this month, since requests sent by email after that date cannot be processed. A short training session will be held next week, and a quick-start guide will be posted on the intranet. If you have questions or trouble logging in, contact the admin team and we will be glad to help.

Thank you for your cooperation during the transition.

Best regards,
The Administration Team
