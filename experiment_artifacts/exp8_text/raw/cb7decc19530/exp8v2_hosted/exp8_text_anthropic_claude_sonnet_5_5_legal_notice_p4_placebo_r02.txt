**To:** Customers who have made a purchase from us

(a) the written terms that applied to your purchase provide for a refund, and you meet the conditions those terms state; or
(b) the law that applies to your purchase entitles you to a refund.

**3. Your legal rights.** Nothing in this notice limits any right you have under the law that cannot be waived or excluded by agreement.
