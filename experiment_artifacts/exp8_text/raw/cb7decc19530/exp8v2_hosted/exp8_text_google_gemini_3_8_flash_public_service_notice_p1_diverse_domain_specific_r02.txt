**PUBLIC NOTICE: Adjustment to Branch Operating Hours**  
**Effective Monday, November 4**

Beginning November 4, municipal branch hours will shift to the winter operating schedule. Please note the revised timetable below for reference, circulation, and public terminal access.

**Main Branch (Carnegie Building)**  
* Monday – Thursday: 9:00 a.m. – 8:00 p.m.  
* Friday – Saturday: 9:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.  
* Sunday: 1:00 p.m. – 5:00 p.m.  

**East & West Neighborhood Branches**  
* Tuesday & Thursday: 10:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m.  
* Wednesday: 12:00 p.m. – 8:00 p.m.  
* Friday & Saturday: 10:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.  
* Sunday & Monday: Closed  

**Service Cut-Off Times:**
* Public internet workstations and print stations log off automatically **15 minutes prior to closing**.  
* The local history archive and microfilm room close **30 minutes prior to building closure**.  
* Requests for interlibrary loan pickups and library card renewals must be processed at the main desk no later than **10 minutes before close**.

**After-Hours Services:**  
Exterior automated return drops remain open 24/7 at all locations. Items returned after closing are backdated to the current business day during morning processing. Public Wi-Fi access points remain active in the perimeter plazas of all branches daily from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.

Direct catalog inquiries, digital renewals, and database access remain available continuously via the county network portal.
