**Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations**

To all registered voters,

Please find below the locations of polling stations for the upcoming election:

* Polling Station 1: Located at 123 Main St, in the heart of the city. Voters can access this location by taking the elevator to the 2nd floor.
* Polling Station 2: Situated at 456 Elm St, a 10-minute walk from the downtown area. Please note that this location is wheelchair accessible via the ramp on the east side.
* Polling Station 3: This station is located at 789 Oak St, in a quiet neighborhood. Voters can park their vehicles in the adjacent parking lot.

**Important:** Please bring your valid photo ID and proof of address to the polling station on election day.

**Hours of Operation:** Polling stations will be open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM on election day.
