For a quick and easy weeknight dinner, try cooking your protein and vegetables in the same pan. This technique is called "one-pot cooking" and can save you time and effort. Simply add a bit of oil to the pan, then cook your protein (such as chicken or beef) until browned, then add your chopped vegetables (like bell peppers and onions) and a bit of seasoning. Continue cooking until the vegetables are tender, then serve over a bed of rice or with a side of noodles. This method allows you to cook a complete meal in one pot, reducing cleanup and saving you time for the rest of your evening.
