**Introducing The Rotation: a coffee subscription that changes every two weeks**

Starting March 3, Kettle Row subscribers get a new bag of coffee every other Thursday. Each one is a different lot from our small-batch roasting, with a bag label that says where the beans came from, what farm or cooperative grew them, and what we tasted in the cup.

The first shipment is a washed Guji from Ethiopia, grown by about 400 smallholder farmers and roasted light enough to keep the fruit. In our cupping it tasted like blackcurrant jam with a little lime, and it finished clean. If it doesn't taste that way to you, write to us and we'll send a replacement from the same lot.

How it works:

- Each 12 oz bag is $24 with free shipping in the US.
- We roast on Tuesdays and ship on Thursdays. Nothing sits in a warehouse for a month.
- Every bag has a one-way degassing valve and a roast date printed on the front, not a "best by" date.
- Choose whole bean or a grind for your brewer: espresso, moka pot, V60, drip, or French press.
- Skip, pause, or change your grind any time before the Sunday prior to shipping.

We're limiting the first run to 300 subscribers so we can keep roasting in small batches. Sign up by February 24 to make the first shipment.
