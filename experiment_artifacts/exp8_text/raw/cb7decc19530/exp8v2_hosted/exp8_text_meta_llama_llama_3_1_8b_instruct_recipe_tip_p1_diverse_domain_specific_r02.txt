Here's a cooking tip for a weeknight dinner:

**One-Pot Wonder: Unlock the Flavor of Your Pasta with a Quick Balsamic Glaze**

When time is tight and the weeknight dinner window is closing in, try elevating your one-pot pasta dish with a quick balsamic glaze. Simply reduce 1/4 cup of balsamic vinegar on the stovetop over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until it thickens into a syrupy consistency (about 5-7 minutes). Then, toss it with your cooked pasta, some grated Parmesan cheese, and a sprinkle of chopped fresh parsley for a depth of flavor that's greater than the sum of its parts. This trick adds a tangy, savory element to the dish without adding any extra ingredients or prep time.
