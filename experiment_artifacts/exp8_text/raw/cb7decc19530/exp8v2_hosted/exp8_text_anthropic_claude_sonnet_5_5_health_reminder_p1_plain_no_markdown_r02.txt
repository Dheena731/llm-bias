It's time for your weekly fitness check-in. Take a few minutes to look back at the past seven days and see how you did. Think about how many workouts you completed, how your energy and mood felt, and whether you hit the goals you set for yourself. Notice what went well and where things got in the way, whether that was time, motivation, or recovery. Use what you find to set one or two realistic goals for the week ahead. Small, steady progress adds up, so be proud of what you accomplished and keep moving forward.
