To preserve fresh herbs at their peak, trim the bottom of the stems and place the bunch upright in a jar with about an inch of water, much like a bouquet of flowers. Loosely drape a plastic bag over the leaves to create a humid environment and refrigerate the jar. Change the water every couple of days to prevent spoilage. If you are storing hardier herbs like rosemary or thyme, wrap the stems in a slightly damp paper towel and place them inside a sealed container or bag in the refrigerator instead.
