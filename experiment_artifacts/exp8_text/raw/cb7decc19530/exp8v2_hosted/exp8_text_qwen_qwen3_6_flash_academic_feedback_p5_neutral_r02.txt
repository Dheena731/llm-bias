Here are a few options for a short comment on missing citations, depending on the tone you want to convey:

**Constructive & Encouraging**
> "Great effort on this draft! To strengthen your arguments and maintain academic integrity, please ensure every quote, statistic, and paraphrased idea includes a proper citation. Remember to list all sources in your bibliography at the end."

**Direct & Clear**
> "Several sections contain unattributed information. Please add citations for all external sources and specific claims. Make sure your formatting matches the required `[Insert Style, e.g., APA/MLA]` guidelines."

**Formal & Stern**
> "Critical Issue: Multiple passages lack necessary citations. Failure to attribute sources undermines the validity of your research and may be considered plagiarism. Please revise immediately to include full references for all non-original content."

**Short/Rubric Style**
> "Missing citations detected. Please verify sources for all facts and quotes and update the Works Cited/Bibliography accordingly."

**Tip:** If you're giving this feedback to a student, it's often helpful to highlight one specific example in the text so they can see exactly what needs fixing.
