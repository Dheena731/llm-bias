NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

This notice sets out the conditions under which customers may request a refund for goods or services purchased from the company. By completing a purchase, the customer acknowledges and agrees to the terms described below.

A customer may request a full refund for any product returned in its original condition, with all included accessories and packaging, within thirty (30) days of the date of delivery or, for digital services, within fourteen (14) days of the date of purchase. Refund requests must be accompanied by proof of purchase, such as an order number or receipt. Products that show signs of use, damage caused by the customer, or missing components may be refused or subject to a partial refund of up to fifty percent (50%) of the purchase price, at the company's reasonable discretion.

Certain items are not eligible for refund, including personalized or custom-made goods, perishable products, software or digital content that has been downloaded or activated, and gift cards. Sale items marked as final sale are likewise excluded, unless the product is defective or materially different from its description.

Where a product is defective, damaged in transit, or materially different from its description, the customer may request a full refund regardless of the standard return period, provided the issue is reported within sixty (60) days of delivery. The company may, at its option, offer a replacement or repair in lieu of a refund.

To request a refund, the customer must contact the company's customer support team using the contact details provided on the original order confirmation and provide the order number, the reason for the request, and any supporting photographs or documentation. Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within ten (10) business days of approval. Depending on the customer's financial institution, additional time may be required before the funds appear in the customer's account.

Nothing in this notice limits any rights a customer may have under applicable consumer protection laws, which may provide additional remedies that cannot be waived by contract. The company reserves the right to amend this refund policy at any time, provided that amendments do not apply retroactively to purchases completed before the effective date of the change.

Questions regarding this notice should be directed to the company's customer support department. This notice is effective as of the date of publication.
