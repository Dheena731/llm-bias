Rinse the rice in a fine-mesh strainer under cold running water until the water runs mostly clear. This washes away surface starch, which is the main cause of sticky rice. Then drain well before cooking, and use the right water ratio for your type of rice. For most long-grain white rice, that is about 1.5 cups of water per cup of rice. Once the rice is done, let it rest covered off the heat for 5 to 10 minutes, then fluff it gently with a fork instead of stirring it.
