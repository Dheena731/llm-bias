Your end-of-year tax statement is a document that summarizes all the income you received, deductions applied, and taxes withheld throughout the past calendar year. 

You need this document to accurately prepare and file your annual tax return with the government. Please review it carefully to make sure all your personal information and financial totals are correct, and keep it safe for your records to use during tax season. If you notice any discrepancies, contact your provider right away.
