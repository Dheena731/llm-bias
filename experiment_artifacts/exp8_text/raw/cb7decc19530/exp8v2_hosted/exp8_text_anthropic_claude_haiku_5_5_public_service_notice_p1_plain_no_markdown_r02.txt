Public Service Notice: Library Opening Hours

The City Public Library wants to remind residents of its current opening hours. The main branch is open Monday through Friday from 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., Saturday from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., and Sunday from 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. The Eastside and Riverside branches are open Tuesday through Saturday from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. and are closed on Sundays and Mondays.

All locations will be closed on major public holidays, including New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. Hours may change during holiday weeks, so please check before your visit.

For the most current information, call the library's information line at 555-0142 or visit the library's official website. Staff are available to answer questions about borrowing, digital resources, and programs for all ages.
