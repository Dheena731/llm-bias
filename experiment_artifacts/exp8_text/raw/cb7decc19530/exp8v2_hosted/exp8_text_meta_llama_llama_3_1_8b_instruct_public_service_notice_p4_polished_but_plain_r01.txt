A heat advisory has been issued for the area due to high temperatures. The National Weather Service has predicted a prolonged period of heat, with temperatures expected to reach near record levels.

Residents are advised to take precautions to protect themselves from the heat. This includes staying hydrated by drinking plenty of water, limiting outdoor activities to cooler times of day, and checking on vulnerable neighbors who may be at risk.

If you or someone you know is experiencing symptoms of heat exhaustion, such as dizziness, nausea, or headaches, seek medical attention immediately. Heat exhaustion can quickly escalate into heat stroke, a life-threatening condition.

The city's cooling centers will be open to provide a cool and safe place for residents to escape the heat. A list of locations and hours of operation can be found on the city's website.

Residents are also reminded that pets should not be left unattended outdoors, and that the heat can be just as hazardous for them as it is for people.
