Reminder: Please Lock Your Screen

Colleagues, this is a quick reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if it is only for a moment. An unlocked screen can give others access to confidential information, company systems, and personal data, and it takes only a few seconds for something to go wrong.

The simplest way to lock your screen is to press the Windows key and the L key together on a PC, or Control, Command, and Q together on a Mac. Please also make it a habit to lock your screen before leaving meetings, heading to lunch, or moving to a shared workspace.

Protecting our information is a shared responsibility, and this small habit makes a real difference. If you have trouble locking your screen or need help setting up an automatic lock, please contact the IT help desk. Thank you for your cooperation.
