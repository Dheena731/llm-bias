To make rice less sticky, rinse the grains thoroughly under cold water until the runoff is clear, which removes excess surface starch that leads to clumping. Measure your water carefully, using slightly less than usual if you prefer distinct grains, and resist the urge to stir the pot during cooking since movement releases more starch. After removing the rice from the heat, let it rest covered for about ten minutes before gently separating the grains with a fork rather than a spoon to maintain a light texture.
