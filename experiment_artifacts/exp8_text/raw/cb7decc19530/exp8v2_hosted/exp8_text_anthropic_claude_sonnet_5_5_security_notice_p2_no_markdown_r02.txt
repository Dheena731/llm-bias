NOTICE TO ALL STAFF: BEWARE OF PHISHING EMAILS

Date: [Insert Date]
From: IT Security Team

We have seen an increase in phishing emails targeting employees. These messages are designed to trick you into sharing passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information, or into installing harmful software.

Warning signs to watch for:

- Unexpected requests for passwords, payment, or personal information
- Urgent or threatening language, such as "act now" or "your account will be closed"
- Sender addresses that look slightly off or do not match the company name
- Unfamiliar links or attachments, especially from unknown senders
- Spelling errors or unusual greetings

What you should do:

1. Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails.
2. Do not reply to the sender or forward the message to colleagues.
3. Report the email to the IT Help Desk at [email/extension].
4. Delete the message after reporting it.

If you have already clicked a link or shared information, contact IT immediately so we can secure your account. Quick reporting helps protect you and the entire company.

When in doubt, check before you click. Thank you for helping keep our workplace safe.
