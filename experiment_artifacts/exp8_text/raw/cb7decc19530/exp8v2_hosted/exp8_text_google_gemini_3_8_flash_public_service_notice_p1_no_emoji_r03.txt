**PUBLIC NOTICE: LIBRARY OPERATING HOURS**

The City Public Library is pleased to announce its current schedule of service hours. 

**Weekly Operating Hours:**
* **Monday – Thursday:** 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM
* **Friday:** 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
* **Saturday:** 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM
* **Sunday:** Closed

**After-Hours Services:**
The exterior book drop is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for all returns. Digital resources, including e-books, audiobooks, and research databases, remain accessible at any time via the library website.

Please note that the library will be closed on all recognized public holidays. For inquiries, renewals, or room bookings, please visit our help desk or call (555) 019-2834 during normal operating hours.
