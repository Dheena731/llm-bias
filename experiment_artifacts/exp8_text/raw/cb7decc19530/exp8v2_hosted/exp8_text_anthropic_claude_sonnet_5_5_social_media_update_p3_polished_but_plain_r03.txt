Join us this Saturday for the annual Riverside Community Day at Maple Park, running from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. This year's celebration brings together neighbors, local businesses, and volunteers for a full day of food, music, and activities for all ages. Families can enjoy free face painting, a hands-on garden workshop, and a children's story tent, while local bands perform on the main stage throughout the afternoon. Food trucks will be on site, and a farmers market will offer fresh produce and handmade goods. Admission is free, and parking is available at the library lot on Elm Street, with a short walk to the park entrance. Please bring a reusable water bottle, as refill stations will be set up near the pavilion. We are still looking for a few volunteers for setup and cleanup, so if you can spare an hour or two, stop by the information table when you arrive. We hope to see you there.
