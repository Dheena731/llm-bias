**New: Monthly Spending Summary**

We've added a monthly spending summary to help you see where your money went over the past month at a glance.

Each summary shows your total spending for the month and how it breaks down across your spending categories. You can use it to spot patterns, compare months, and check whether your spending matches your plans.

You don't need to do anything to get started. Your summary will be available at the start of each month, and you can find it in the same place you view your account activity.

If you have questions about the summary or notice something that looks wrong, contact our support team and we'll help.
