When cooking rice to prevent it from becoming sticky, use the right ratio of water to rice. Typically, for every one cup of rice, use one and a half cups of water. This helps to cook the rice evenly and prevents it from becoming too moist and sticky. Also, try using a non-stick pan or a ceramic pot with a heavy bottom, as these materials can help the rice cook more evenly and reduce the likelihood of it becoming sticky. Finally, fluff the rice with a fork as soon as it is cooked to separate the grains and prevent them from clumping together.
