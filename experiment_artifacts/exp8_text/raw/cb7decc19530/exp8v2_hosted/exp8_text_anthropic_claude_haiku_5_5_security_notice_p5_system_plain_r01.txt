Subject: How to Report a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop

If you have lost a company laptop or believe it has been stolen, please report it to the IT Service Desk as soon as possible, and no later than two hours after you notice it is missing. Prompt reporting allows the IT team to lock the device, revoke access to company systems, and reduce the risk of sensitive data being exposed.

To report the loss, call the IT Service Desk at extension 4357 or email itsupport@company.com with your name, employee ID, the laptop's asset tag, and the last place and time you remember having it. If the laptop was stolen, please also file a police report and send a copy of the report number to the IT Service Desk and to your manager.

Do not attempt to recover or access the device yourself. The IT team will handle remote wiping and any further steps. Your cooperation helps protect both you and the company.
