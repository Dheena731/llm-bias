Here's a short tip for planning lunches for a busy week:

**Prep for Success: Plan Your Lunches in Advance**

To avoid last-minute takeout or sad desk salads, take 30 minutes on the weekend to plan out your lunches for the week. Here's a simple strategy:

1. Decide on your lunch goals: healthy, affordable, and easy to prepare.
2. Choose a few staple recipes that can be adapted for the week (e.g. salads, soups, wraps).
3. Make a grocery list and shop for ingredients.
4. Prep individual portions of ingredients (e.g. chop veggies, cook proteins) to save time during the week.
5. Pack and store your lunches in containers or bags for a quick grab-and-go.

By planning ahead, you'll save time, money, and stress – and enjoy healthy, delicious meals all week long!
