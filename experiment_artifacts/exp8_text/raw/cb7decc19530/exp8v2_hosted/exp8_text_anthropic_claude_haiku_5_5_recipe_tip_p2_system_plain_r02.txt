Fresh herbs stay crisp longer when you treat them like cut flowers. Trim about half an inch off the stems, place the bunch in a glass with a little water, and cover the leaves loosely with a plastic bag before refrigerating. Change the water every couple of days. Hardy herbs like rosemary, thyme, and sage can also be wrapped in a damp paper towel and stored in a sealed container. Basil is the exception, since it blackens in the cold, so keep it on the counter in water instead.
