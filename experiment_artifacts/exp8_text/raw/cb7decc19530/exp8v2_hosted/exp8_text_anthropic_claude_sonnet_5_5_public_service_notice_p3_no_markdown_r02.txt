NOTICE: Changes to Recycling Collection

Effective Monday, March 3, curbside recycling collection will move to a new schedule.

What is changing:
Recycling will now be collected every other week instead of weekly. Collection day will move from Thursday to Tuesday for most neighborhoods. Residents will receive a printed calendar in the mail by February 15 showing their specific pickup dates.

What stays the same:
Please continue to use your current blue recycling bin. Accepted items remain unchanged, including clean paper, cardboard, glass bottles and jars, metal cans, and plastic containers marked 1 through 5.

Helpful reminders:
Place bins at the curb by 6:30 a.m. on your collection day. Rinse containers and flatten cardboard boxes. Do not bag recyclables or include plastic bags, food waste, or Styrofoam.

Extra capacity:
Households that need a larger bin may request one free of charge by calling the Public Works Department at 555-0142 or by emailing recycling@exampletown.gov.

Thank you for helping keep our community clean and sustainable. For questions or to view the full schedule, please visit www.exampletown.gov/recycling.
