Elevate a simple weeknight protein by mastering a quick pan sauce instead of relying on store-bought marinades. After searing chicken, pork, or steak, remove the meat to rest and keep the skillet over medium heat. Pour in a splash of dry white wine, broth, or even water to loosen the flavorful browned bits stuck to the bottom of the pan, scraping gently with a wooden spoon. Let the liquid simmer until it reduces by half, then turn off the heat and vigorously swirl in a tablespoon of cold butter until the sauce turns glossy. This technique takes less than three minutes, requires no extra pans to wash, and turns an ordinary cut of meat into a restaurant-quality meal.
