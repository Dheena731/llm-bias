Hi there, this is a friendly reminder that your prescription may be due for a refill soon. Running out of medication can interrupt your treatment, so we encourage you to contact your pharmacy or doctor's office a few days before you run out. Many pharmacies allow you to request refills by phone, through their website, or through a mobile app, which can save you a trip. If you have any questions about your prescription, or if you are unsure how many refills you have left, please reach out to your provider. Thank you, and take care of your health.
