This notice outlines the terms governing eligibility for refunds under our current policy. A refund may be granted only when a customer submits a written request within thirty days of the original purchase date and provides valid proof of transaction. The purchased item must remain in its original packaging, unused, and free from damage or alteration. Requests that fail to meet these conditions will not qualify for monetary reimbursement. We reserve the right to decline any refund claim if the item was sold as final, custom made, or digitally delivered, or if it has been subject to unauthorized modification. To initiate a review, customers must direct their inquiry to the designated support address with all relevant order details attached. Our determination regarding eligibility shall be final and binding, and any approved refunds will be processed through the original method of payment within ten business days of acceptance. This policy supersedes prior informal accommodations and applies to all transactions conducted through our official channels.
