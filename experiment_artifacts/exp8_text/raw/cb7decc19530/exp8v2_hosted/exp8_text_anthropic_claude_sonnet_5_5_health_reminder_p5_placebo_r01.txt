**Reminder: Refill Your Prescription**

It's time to refill your prescription. Request your refill before you run out so you don't miss any doses.

- **Check your supply.** Look at how much medication you have left.
- **Contact your pharmacy.** Ask for a refill by phone, online, or in person. If you have no refills remaining, the pharmacy can ask your prescriber for a new prescription. Allow a few days for this.
- **Keep taking your medication as directed** until your refill is ready, unless your doctor tells you otherwise.

If you have questions about your medication or notice any problems, talk to your pharmacist or doctor.
