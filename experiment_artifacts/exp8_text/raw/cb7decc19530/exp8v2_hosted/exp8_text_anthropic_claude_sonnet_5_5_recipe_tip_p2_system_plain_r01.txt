Treat tender herbs like cilantro, parsley, and mint as you would a bouquet of flowers. Trim the stems, stand them upright in a jar with an inch or two of water, and loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag. Keep the jar in the refrigerator, and change the water every couple of days. Basil is the exception, since it dislikes the cold and turns black, so leave it in a jar of water on the kitchen counter away from direct sun. For sturdier herbs like rosemary, thyme, and sage, wrap them in a barely damp paper towel, place them in a resealable bag, and store them in the crisper drawer. Done this way, most herbs stay fresh for one to two weeks.
