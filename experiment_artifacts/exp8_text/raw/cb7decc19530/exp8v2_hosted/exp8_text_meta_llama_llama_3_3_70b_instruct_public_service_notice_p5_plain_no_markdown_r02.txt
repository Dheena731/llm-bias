Polling stations for the upcoming election will be located at the town hall, the community center, and the local school. The town hall polling station will serve voters from the north side of town, the community center will serve voters from the south side, and the local school will serve voters from the central area. Voters can find out which polling station they are assigned to by checking their voter registration card or contacting the election office. All polling stations will be open from 7am to 8pm on election day.
