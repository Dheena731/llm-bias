Eligibility for a refund is determined by the timing of your request and the condition of the purchased item. You may apply for a full refund within thirty days of the original transaction date, provided the product remains unused, unaltered, and in its original packaging with all accompanying documentation intact. Refunds requested after this period will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis at the sole discretion of the company. To initiate a refund, please contact our support team with your order number and a brief explanation of the reason for the return. Upon approval, refunds will be processed to the original method of payment within ten business days. Please note that shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to an error on our part
