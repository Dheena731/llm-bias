**Introducing Smart Folders**

Organizing your files just got easier. Smart Folders automatically sort your documents, images, and links based on content, so you spend less time searching and more time doing.

What's new:
- Automatic sorting by project, date, or topic
- Custom rules you can set in seconds
- Instant search across every folder

Smart Folders is rolling out today to all users. Update your app to try it out, and tell us what you think in the comments.
