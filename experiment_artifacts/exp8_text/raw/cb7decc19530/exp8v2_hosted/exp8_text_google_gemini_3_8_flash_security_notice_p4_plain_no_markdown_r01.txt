To enhance the security of your account, all users are asked to enable two-factor authentication. This feature adds an essential layer of protection by requiring a verification code in addition to your standard password when logging in, which significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access. You can activate this feature by visiting your account settings, navigating to the security section, and following the prompts to link your mobile device or authenticator application. Taking this quick step helps ensure that your personal information and account data remain safe. Please complete this setup at your earliest convenience.
