**MUNICIPAL ADVISORY: EXCESSIVE HEAT WARNING**  
**Issuing Agency:** Office of Emergency Management & District Health Department  
**Effective Window:** Thursday, July 18, 11:00 AM – Saturday, July 20, 8:00 PM  

The National Weather Service has issued an Excessive Heat Warning for the metro area. Sustained daytime heat indices are projected between 104°F and 108°F, with overnight ambient lows remaining above 78°F, limiting natural structural cooling.

### Administrative & Service Adjustments

* **Public Cooling Sites:** Central Library, Northside Senior Center, and the 4th Ward Gymnasium will operate as designated cooling facilities daily from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM. High-capacity transit passes to these sites are available at no charge upon boarding.
* **Solid Waste Collection:** Curbside pickup will advance to a 5:30 AM start through Saturday to reduce mid-day physical exposure for sanitation crews. Set bins out the night prior.
* **Infrastructure Monitoring:** The Department of Water Resources has suspended all non-emergency line flushes. Residents on localized well networks should anticipate low-pressure advisories if peak electrical demand triggers grid curtailment.
* **Animal Welfare Ordinance:** Under Municipal Code § 14-8, leaving domestic animals tethered outdoors or unattended in stationary vehicles during an active heat warning is prohibited and subject to immediate citation.

**Welfare Checks & Vulnerable Outreach:**  
Outreach units are actively distributing shelf-stable water and coordinating shelter access for unsheltered residents. To request a municipal wellness check for an unmonitored senior, homebound individual, or person without working air conditioning, contact the non-emergency coordination desk at **555-0190** or dispatch via **311**. 

*Report power disruptions directly to the regional utility grid operator at 1-800-555-GRID.*
