Update: New Monthly Spending Summary

We are pleased to introduce our enhanced Monthly Spending Summary. This new report provides a comprehensive view of your financial activity, designed to help you manage your budget with greater ease.

The update includes detailed expense categories to show exactly where your funds are going. You will also find visual charts that display spending trends over time, allowing you to spot patterns quickly. Furthermore, the summary now features a side-by-side comparison with your personal budget goals, highlighting your progress and areas for improvement.

You can access this report by visiting your dashboard and selecting the Reports tab. We encourage you to review your summary each month to maintain control over your finances and make data-driven decisions.
