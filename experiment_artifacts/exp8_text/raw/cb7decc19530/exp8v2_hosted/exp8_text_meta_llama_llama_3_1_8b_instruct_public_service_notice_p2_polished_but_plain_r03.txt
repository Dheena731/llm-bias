NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE

The intersection of Main Street and Oak Avenue will be closed to all traffic from 6:00 AM on Tuesday, March 21, to 6:00 PM on Friday, March 24. This closure is necessary to accommodate a water main replacement project.

During this time, detours will be in place to direct drivers around the affected area. Signs will be posted to guide traffic and inform the public of the road closure. Local residents and businesses will have access to their properties at all times.

Motorists are advised to plan their routes accordingly and allow extra time for travel through the area. Drivers should exercise caution when navigating the detours and follow all traffic instructions provided by flaggers and signs.

This road closure is essential to ensure public safety and the successful completion of the water main replacement project.
