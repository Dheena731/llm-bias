PUBLIC NOTICE: COMMUNITY LIBRARY OPENING HOURS

The Greenfield Public Library is pleased to announce its current operating schedule. We invite all residents to visit, explore our collection, and make use of our study spaces and public computers.

Regular Hours of Operation:
Monday to Thursday: 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM
Friday and Saturday: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
Sunday: 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM

Please note that our digital catalog, e-books, and research databases remain accessible 24/7 online. The exterior book return box is also available at all times for after-hours drop-offs. 

For inquiries, event schedules, or more information, please call 555-0142 or visit our website at www.greenfieldlibrary.org.
