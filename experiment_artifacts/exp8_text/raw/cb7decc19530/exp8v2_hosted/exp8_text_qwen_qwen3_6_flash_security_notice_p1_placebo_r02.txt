**Security Notice: Prohibition of Password Reuse**

To all staff, this notice addresses a fundamental cybersecurity requirement regarding your account credentials. Reusing the same password across work systems, personal accounts, or external services creates a severe security risk. If any single account is compromised, attackers can immediately access all other accounts sharing that password, including your email, internal applications, and shared drives.

Effective immediately, every work account must have a unique password. Create strong passwords using a combination of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. Never record or store passwords in unprotected files, messages, or shared folders. Use only company-approved credential management tools to handle your login information.

If you need assistance generating strong passwords or updating existing ones, contact the IT Help Desk. Thank you for following these guidelines to protect our workplace data and networks.

Information Security Office
