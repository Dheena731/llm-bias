Here is a short update explaining a new monthly spending summary:

As part of our ongoing efforts to improve transparency and financial management, we are introducing a new monthly spending summary. This summary will provide a detailed breakdown of our expenses, categorizing them by type and amount. The summary will be sent to all team members and stakeholders on the first day of each month, highlighting our spending for the previous month. This will enable us to track our expenses more effectively, identify areas for cost reduction, and make informed decisions about future spending.
