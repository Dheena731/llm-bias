Please be advised that there have been changes to the recycling collection schedule and guidelines effective starting next Monday. Recycling bins will now be collected on the second and fourth Wednesday of each month instead of the previously scheduled Tuesdays. Residents must place their bins at the curb by six in the morning on the designated collection day. Please remember that plastic bags and Styrofoam are no longer accepted in the recycling stream and should be disposed of in the regular trash. If you require additional information or have questions regarding these updates, please contact the waste management office during standard business hours. Thank you for your cooperation in helping us maintain an efficient and sustainable recycling program.
