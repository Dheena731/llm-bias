A quick reminder to give your eyes and mind a rest from your screen. Every twenty minutes or so, look away at something far off for about twenty seconds, and let your eyes relax. Every hour, stand up, stretch your shoulders and back, and walk around for a few minutes. Drink some water, take a few slow breaths, and blink often. These small pauses help reduce eye strain, ease tension, and keep your focus sharp, so you will likely get more done when you return.
