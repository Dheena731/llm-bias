# Notice: Recycling Collection Changes

**Effective Monday, [Date]**

Our recycling collection schedule and guidelines are changing. Please review the updates below.

**Schedule Changes**
- Recycling pickup will move from **Thursdays** to **Tuesdays**.
- Bins must be at the curb by **7:00 a.m.** on collection day.

**What's Changing**
- Recycling will be collected **every week** instead of every other week.
- Glass bottles and jars are now accepted. Please rinse them first.

**Reminders**
- Rinse containers and remove food residue.
- Place items in the bin loose. **Do not use plastic bags.**
- Flatten cardboard boxes.
- Items such as plastic bags, foam, and greasy pizza boxes are **not** recyclable.
