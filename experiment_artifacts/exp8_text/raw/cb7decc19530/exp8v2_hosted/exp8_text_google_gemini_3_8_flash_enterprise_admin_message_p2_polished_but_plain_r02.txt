Please be advised that we are introducing an updated expense approval workflow, effective Monday, to streamline reimbursements and reduce processing delays. Under this new process, all expense reports submitted through the standard finance portal will route directly to your immediate manager for initial verification before proceeding to the accounting team for final sign-off and payment. Managers are expected to complete reviews within three business days of receipt. To ensure timely processing, please verify that all itemized receipts are attached to your claims before submitting them. If you have questions regarding these changes, please reach out directly to the finance department.
