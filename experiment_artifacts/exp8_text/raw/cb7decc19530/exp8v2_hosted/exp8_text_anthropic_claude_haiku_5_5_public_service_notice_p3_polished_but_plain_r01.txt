**Notice of Changes to Recycling Collection**

Beginning next month, recycling collection in your area will follow a revised schedule. Recycling bins will be collected every other week, on the same day as your regular trash pickup. Your garbage collection schedule will not change.

Please review the updated list of accepted materials before your first collection under the new schedule. Clean, dry paper, cardboard, glass bottles and jars, metal cans, and plastic containers marked with recycling symbols 1 and 2 remain accepted. Plastic bags, plastic film, food-soiled containers, and electronics should not be placed in recycling bins. Flatten cardboard boxes and rinse containers before placing them in the bin.

Residents who need a larger recycling container or who have questions about the new schedule may contact the Public Works Department at the number listed on your utility bill or on the city website. Printed copies of the full collection calendar are available at the public library and the municipal office.

Thank you for helping keep our community clean and our recycling program effective.
