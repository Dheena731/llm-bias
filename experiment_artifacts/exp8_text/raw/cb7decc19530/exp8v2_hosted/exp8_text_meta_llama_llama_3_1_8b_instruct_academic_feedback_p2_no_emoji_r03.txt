Here is a sample feedback note on a student's methods section:

"Your methods section is well-structured and clear. However, I would like to suggest a few revisions to improve the clarity and concision of the section. Specifically, I think it would be helpful to provide more detail on the sampling strategy and the data collection procedures. Additionally, please ensure that the methods used are described in a way that is easy to follow for readers who may not be familiar with the specific techniques used. Overall, your methods section provides a good foundation for the rest of the paper, but with a few tweaks, it could be even more effective in conveying your research approach to the reader."

Or, alternatively:

"The methods section is clear and well-organized, but could benefit from a few minor revisions. I would like to see more detail on the data analysis procedures, as well as a brief explanation of any statistical methods used. Additionally, please ensure that all relevant details, such as sample sizes and participant characteristics, are included in the section. With these minor adjustments, the methods section will provide an even clearer picture of your research approach and methods."
