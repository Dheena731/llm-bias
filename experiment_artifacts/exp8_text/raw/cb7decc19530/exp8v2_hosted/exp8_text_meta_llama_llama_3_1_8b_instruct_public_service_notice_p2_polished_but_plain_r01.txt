NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE

The public is advised that a section of Main Street will be closed to all traffic for maintenance and repairs beginning at 8:00 PM on Friday, March 17th. The closure will extend from Elm Street to Oak Street and will remain in effect until 6:00 AM on Monday, March 20th.

During this time, vehicles will be detoured around the closure via side streets and alternate routes. Pedestrian access will be restricted to designated sidewalks.

Motorists are advised to plan their travel accordingly and follow all posted signs and directions from traffic control personnel. Postal delivery and emergency services will be accommodated during the closure.

Questions and concerns can be directed to the Department of Public Works at (555) 123-4567.
