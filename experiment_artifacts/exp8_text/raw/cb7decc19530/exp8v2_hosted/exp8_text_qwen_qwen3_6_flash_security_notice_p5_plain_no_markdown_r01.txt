This notice outlines the required steps for reporting a lost company laptop. Employees who cannot locate their assigned device must notify the IT security team without delay. When contacting support, provide the full name, employee ID, asset tag number, and a detailed account of where and when the laptop was last seen. Immediate notification enables the technical team to disable network access and wipe sensitive data to protect company information. All reports should be submitted through the standard incident management portal or by speaking directly with the security manager. Delayed reporting compromises data safety and may violate corporate policy. Please handle this matter with urgency and seriousness.
