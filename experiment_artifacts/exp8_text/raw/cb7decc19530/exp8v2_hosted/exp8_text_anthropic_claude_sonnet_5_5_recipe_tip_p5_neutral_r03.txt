**Season in two stages for better flavor.** Before roasting, toss your vegetables with oil, salt, and pepper so they brown well and are seasoned all the way through. Then, right when they come out of the oven, finish with a squeeze of lemon juice or a splash of vinegar and a pinch of flaky salt. The heat of roasting mellows flavors, so this last step brightens everything up. Add delicate fresh herbs, like parsley or basil, at the end too, since they burn easily in a hot oven.
