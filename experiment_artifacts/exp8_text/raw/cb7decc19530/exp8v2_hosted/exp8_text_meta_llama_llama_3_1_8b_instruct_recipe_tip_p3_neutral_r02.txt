Here's a short cooking tip to make rice less sticky:

**The Secret to Non-Stick Rice:**

To prevent rice from becoming sticky, use the right ratio of water to rice. A good rule of thumb is to use 1 1/4 cups of water for every 1 cup of rice. Additionally, when cooking rice, use a medium heat and don't lift the lid of the pot until the water has been absorbed and the rice is cooked. This will help to prevent steam from building up and making the rice sticky.

By following these simple steps, you'll be able to achieve perfectly cooked, fluffy, and non-sticky rice every time!
