Friendly reminder: take a moment to check in on your water intake today. Your body relies on water for almost everything it does, from regulating temperature and supporting digestion to keeping your energy and focus steady. If you have not had a glass in a while, now is a good time to fill one up. Keep a bottle nearby at your desk or in your bag so drinking becomes a natural habit rather than an afterthought. Your body will thank you for the steady refreshment throughout the day.
