When planning lunches for a busy week, consider the "cook once, use thrice" approach. Identify a protein source, such as grilled chicken or roasted turkey, that can be repurposed into multiple meals. For example, use Monday's chicken breast to make a salad for Tuesday's lunch and then shred the leftovers for Wednesday's wrap. This strategy not only saves time but also reduces food waste and makes meal prep more efficient. Take it a step further by prepping a batch of quinoa, brown rice, or roasted vegetables on the weekend to use as a base for various lunches throughout the week. By doing so, you'll be able to assemble healthy, satisfying lunches in no time, even on the most chaotic of mornings.
