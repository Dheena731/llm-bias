Software updates matter primarily because they protect your devices and personal information from security threats. Over time, weaknesses are discovered in programs that attackers can exploit to steal data or infect your system. Developers release updates to fix these security vulnerabilities before they can cause harm. 

Beyond protection, updates also resolve glitches to prevent crashes, improve performance and reliability, and introduce useful new features. Regularly updating your software ensures your devices remain secure, stable, and compatible with current technology.
