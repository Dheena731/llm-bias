**Subject: Updated Expense Approval Routing – Effective [Date]**

Starting [Date], all expense submissions will follow the revised routing matrix. 

- Claims ≤ $500 route directly to your department manager.
- Claims $501–$2,000 require secondary review by Finance before payout.
- Claims > $2,000 must include a valid project code and documented pre-approval from the budget owner at time of submission.

Receipts must be itemized. Summary receipts, hotel folios without breakdowns, or blurry screenshots will be returned for correction. Fully approved claims enter a 10-business-day processing window. Partially submitted or misrouted claims pause until corrected; reimbursement dates are not extended retroactively.

The new routing logic is live in the expense portal. Managers should confirm their delegation queue and approver hierarchy by [date]. Finance will track routing accuracy through the next two pay cycles—please flag stuck submissions or incorrect approvers to expense-support@ with your claim ID.

Categorization guidance and exception procedures are posted on the internal wiki. For threshold overrides, submit a brief justification note attached to the original claim.
