**Store soft herbs like flowers.** Trim the stem ends, then stand parsley, cilantro, or mint in a glass of water in the refrigerator, loosely covered with a plastic bag. Change the water every few days. Basil is the exception: keep it at room temperature, since cold damages it. For hardy herbs like rosemary and thyme, wrap them in a damp paper towel, place them in a sealed bag or container, and refrigerate.
