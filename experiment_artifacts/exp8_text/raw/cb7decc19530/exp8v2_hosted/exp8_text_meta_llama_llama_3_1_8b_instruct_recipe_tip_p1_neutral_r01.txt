**Quick Tip: One-Pot Pasta**

For a fast and satisfying weeknight dinner, try making one-pot pasta. Cook your pasta, sauce, and protein (such as sausage or chicken) all together in a large pot. Simply add a tablespoon of oil, then sauté your protein until browned, add a can of crushed tomatoes and your choice of spices, then add the pasta and cook until al dente. The result is a hearty, flavorful meal with minimal cleanup.

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes
**Cook Time:** 15-20 minutes
**Servings:** 4-6 people

This tip saves time by eliminating the need to cook separate components, and reduces cleanup by using a single pot.
