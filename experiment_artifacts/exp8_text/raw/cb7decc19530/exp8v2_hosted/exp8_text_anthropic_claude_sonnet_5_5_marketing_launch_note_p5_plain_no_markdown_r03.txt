Today we are releasing the first episode of Tongue & Time, a new podcast for anyone who wants to learn a language without giving up the rest of their life.

Each week, we take one everyday situation, such as ordering coffee, asking for directions, or making small talk with a neighbor, and walk through it slowly in a different language. You will hear natural speech from native speakers, a clear explanation of why people say things the way they do, and a few simple phrases to try out loud before the episode ends. Most episodes run about fifteen minutes, so they fit easily into a commute, a walk, or the time it takes to wash the dishes.

We start with Spanish, French, and Japanese, and we plan to add more languages as the show grows. Beginners are the main audience, but we also include short notes for listeners who want a little more challenge. Our goal is not perfection. It is to help you feel comfortable speaking, making mistakes, and trying again.

The first three episodes are available now wherever you listen to podcasts. A new one will arrive every Monday. If you have a situation you would like us to cover or a language you hope to hear, send us a message and tell us. We read everything, and your ideas will shape what comes next.

Thank you for listening, and welcome to the first lesson.
