**ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY FOR SHARED NETWORK RESOURCES**

**1. Purpose and Scope**
This Acceptable Use Policy ("Policy") governs access to and use of the shared network, including all wired and wireless connections, servers, storage, and connected devices (collectively, the "Network"). All users, including employees, contractors, and guests, must comply with this Policy.

**2. Authorized Use**
The Network is provided for lawful business, educational, and related purposes. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with Network performance, the work of others, or any other provision of this Policy.

**3. Prohibited Conduct**
Users shall not:
(a) access, store, or transmit material that is unlawful, defamatory, harassing, or discriminatory;
(b) attempt to gain unauthorized access to any account, system, or data;
(c) introduce malware or take any action intended to disrupt, degrade, or circumvent Network security;
(d) infringe copyrights, trademarks, or other intellectual property rights;
(e) share credentials or permit another person to use their account;
(f) conduct unauthorized scanning, monitoring, or interception of Network traffic; or
(g) use the Network for commercial activity unrelated to the user's authorized role.

**4. User Responsibilities**
Users are responsible for all activity conducted under their credentials, for safeguarding passwords, and for promptly reporting suspected security incidents to the IT department.

**5. Monitoring**
To the extent permitted by applicable law, the Network owner may monitor, log, and review Network activity for security, maintenance, and compliance purposes. Users should have no expectation of privacy in communications transmitted over the Network.

**6. Enforcement**
Violations of this Policy may result in suspension or revocation of Network access, disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or enrollment, and referral to law enforcement where appropriate. The Network owner reserves the right to take any other action permitted by law or contract.

**7. Disclaimer**
The Network is provided "as is." The Network owner disclaims all warranties, express or implied, and is not liable for any loss of data, interruption of service, or damage arising from Network use, except as required by law.

**8. Amendments**
The Network owner may revise this Policy at any time. Continued use of the Network following notice of any revision constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

**9. Acknowledgment**
By accessing the Network, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to comply with this Policy.
