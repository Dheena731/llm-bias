We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you better track and manage your expenses. This summary will be sent to you via email at the beginning of each month and will provide a detailed breakdown of your spending from the previous month. The summary will include categories such as housing, transportation, food, and entertainment, and will show the total amount spent in each category. This will allow you to easily identify areas where you can cut back and make adjustments to stay within your budget. The monthly spending summary is designed to be a helpful tool in maintaining financial awareness and making informed decisions about your money.
