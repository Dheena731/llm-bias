**Update: Monthly spending summary**

Starting on the 1st of each month, you'll get a one-page summary of the month that just ended. You'll find it under Reports > Monthly, and we'll send a notification if you want one.

**What's in it**
- Total spent, split into recurring charges (rent, subscriptions, insurance) and everything else
- Your top five categories, each compared with your median for the past three months rather than with last month alone. One month with a car repair or an annual premium shouldn't make every other month look cheap or expensive.
- Any single charge over $200 that isn't a recurring payment, listed separately so one-offs don't get lost in category totals

**How it's calculated**
- Credit card payments and transfers between your own accounts are left out. Only the purchases on the card count, so nothing is counted twice.
- Refunds are subtracted from the category of the original purchase, even if they arrive in a later month.
- Pending transactions are excluded at close. If one posts after the 1st, it goes into the month it was made, and the summary updates with a small "revised" tag.

**Known limits**
- Split transactions use the categories you assigned. If a Costco run is filed entirely under Groceries, that's how it will appear.
- Joint accounts show combined totals only. Per-person breakdowns are not available yet.

If a category looks wrong, tap it to recategorize the transactions. The summary recalculates immediately, and your change applies to future charges from the same merchant.

To turn off the notification, go to Settings > Notifications > Monthly summary. The report will still be in Reports.
