This is a friendly reminder to review your fitness progress for the past week. Take a few minutes to look at your workouts, your activity levels, and how you felt during your sessions. Noticing what went well and what felt hard can help you plan the week ahead.

As you review, consider asking yourself:
- Did I meet the workout goals I set for myself?
- Which activities felt good, and which were a struggle?
- Do I need to rest, adjust my schedule, or try something new next week?

Small, steady progress adds up over time. Even a short review can help you stay motivated and on track.

Keep up the great work!
