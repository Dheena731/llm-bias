Store fresh herbs by treating soft varieties like basil, cilantro, and parsley as cut flowers; trim the stems, place them in a jar with an inch of water, and cover loosely with a plastic bag before refrigerating. Wrap hardy herbs such as rosemary and thyme in a damp paper towel and keep them in a sealed container in the fridge. Change the water in the jar every two days to ensure the herbs stay fresh and flavorful.
