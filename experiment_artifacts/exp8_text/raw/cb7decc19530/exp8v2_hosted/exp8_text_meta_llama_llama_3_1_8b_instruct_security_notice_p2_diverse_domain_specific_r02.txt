**Important Notice to All Employees of Oakwood Financial Group**

As part of our ongoing effort to maintain the security and integrity of our systems, we want to remind everyone to be cautious when receiving emails that may appear to be from Oakwood Financial Group, but are not. These emails, known as phishing attempts, are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information or clicking on links that could compromise our network.

Some recent examples of phishing emails that have been reported include:

* Requests for immediate wire transfers or sensitive data to be sent to an external email address
* Claims that your account has been compromised and requires immediate login credentials to be reset
* Links to update or install software, which may be malicious

**What to do if you receive a suspicious email:**

* Do not respond to the email
* Do not click on any links or open any attachments
* Report the email to the IT department immediately
* Verify the sender's email address to ensure it matches the Oakwood Financial Group domain (@oakwoodfg.com)

Remember, if it seems too good (or bad) to be true, it probably is. Your vigilance is crucial in protecting our company's assets and maintaining the trust of our clients.

**Stay safe, stay secure**

The Oakwood Financial Group IT Department
