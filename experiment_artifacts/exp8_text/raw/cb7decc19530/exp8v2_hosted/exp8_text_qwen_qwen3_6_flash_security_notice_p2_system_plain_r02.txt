This notice is to remind all staff about the importance of recognizing phishing emails. These messages often appear to come from trusted sources but are designed to steal login credentials or install malware. Please verify the sender address carefully before clicking any links or opening attachments. If an email requests urgent action, personal information, or payment changes, contact IT security directly through the company directory rather than replying to the message. Report suspicious emails immediately by forwarding them to the security team. Your vigilance helps protect our systems and data. Thank you for your cooperation.
