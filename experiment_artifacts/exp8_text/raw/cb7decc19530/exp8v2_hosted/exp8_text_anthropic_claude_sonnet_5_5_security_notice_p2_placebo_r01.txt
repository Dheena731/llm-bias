**Notice to All Employees: Beware of Phishing Emails**

Phishing emails are fraudulent messages designed to trick you into clicking a harmful link, opening a dangerous attachment, or giving away sensitive information such as passwords or financial details. They often look like they come from a coworker, a manager, a vendor, or a well-known company.

**Warning signs**
- The message creates urgency or pressure, such as "act now" or "your account will be closed."
- The sender's email address looks slightly wrong or doesn't match the name shown.
- The email asks for your password, payment details, or other private information.
- You weren't expecting an attachment or link, or the link's address looks unfamiliar.
- The message contains unusual wording or spelling errors.

**What to do**
- Do not click links, open attachments, or reply to a suspicious email.
- Never send your password by email, even if the request seems to come from someone you know.
- If you are unsure whether a message is genuine, contact the sender through a different method, such as a phone number you already have.
- Report the email to your IT or security team right away.

**If you have already clicked or replied**, tell your IT or security team immediately. Prompt reporting helps limit harm, and you will not be penalized for raising a concern in good faith.

Thank you for helping keep our workplace secure.
