**Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow, Effective Monday**

Hi everyone,

Starting Monday, we are moving to a new expense approval workflow to speed up reimbursements and improve tracking.

**What's changing**
- All expense reports must be submitted through the finance portal. Email submissions will no longer be accepted.
- Receipts must be attached to each line item at the time of submission.
- Approvals will follow this order: direct manager, then department head (for expenses over $500), then finance.

**Timelines**
- Submit expenses within 30 days of purchase.
- Managers should review requests within 3 business days.
- Approved reimbursements will be paid in the next scheduled payroll cycle.

**What you need to do**
1. Log in to the finance portal and confirm your manager is listed correctly in your profile.
2. Review the quick-start guide on the intranet under Finance > Expense Reports.
3. Submit any outstanding expenses under the old process by this Friday.

If you have questions or run into any issues, contact the finance team at finance@company.com or reach out to me directly.

Thank you for your help with the transition.
