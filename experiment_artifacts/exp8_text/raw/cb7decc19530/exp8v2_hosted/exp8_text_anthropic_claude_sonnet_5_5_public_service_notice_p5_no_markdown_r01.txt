NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS

Residents are advised that polling stations for the upcoming election will be open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM on Election Day. Please vote at the location assigned to your precinct.

Polling Locations:

Precinct 1: Central Public Library, 120 Main Street
Precinct 2: Riverside Community Center, 45 River Road
Precinct 3: Oakwood Elementary School Gymnasium, 310 Oak Avenue
Precinct 4: Town Hall, 1 Civic Plaza
Precinct 5: Westside Senior Center, 78 Maple Lane

Important Reminders:

Bring a valid photo ID and, if available, your voter registration card.
All polling stations are wheelchair accessible. Curbside voting is available upon request.
If you are unsure of your assigned precinct, contact the Election Office at (555) 010-2000 or visit the official election website to look up your polling place.
Voters who are in line by closing time will be permitted to cast their ballots.

Thank you for taking part in the democratic process.
