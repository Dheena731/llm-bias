**TOWN OF HARLOW FALLS, PUBLIC WORKS**

**Recycling moves to every-other-week pickup on March 3, and glass comes out of the blue cart**

**Schedule.** Starting Monday, March 3, blue-cart recycling will be collected every other week. Trash stays weekly. Your pickup day does not change. Homes north of Route 9 are on **A weeks**, and homes south of Route 9 are on **B weeks**. The first A-week pickup is March 3 and the first B-week pickup is March 10. A printed calendar will be mailed the week of February 17, and it is also posted at harlowfalls.example/recycling.

**Glass.** Bottles and jars will no longer be accepted curbside, because broken glass has been ruining paper loads at the sorting facility. Take glass to the bins at the Quarry Road Transfer Station, open Tuesday through Saturday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. No permit sticker is needed for glass drop-off.

**Other changes**
- Leave caps on plastic bottles and jugs. Loose caps fall through the sorting screens.
- Pizza boxes: tear off the clean lid and recycle it. Put the greasy bottom in the trash.
- Plastic bags and bagged recycling are not accepted. Put items in the cart loose. Bags go to the collection bins at the grocery store.
- Shredded paper is no longer accepted in the cart.

**Larger carts.** With pickups spaced further apart, households can swap a 64-gallon cart for a 96-gallon one at no charge. Call 555-0142 by March 28 and the swap will be done within two weeks.

**What to expect at the curb.** Crews will tag carts that contain glass or bagged items and will leave them unemptied. The tag will say why. Remove the problem items and set the cart out again on your next scheduled week.

**Holidays.** Pickup shifts one day later after Memorial Day, July 4, and Labor Day. If a holiday lands on your A or B week, your pickup still happens that week, one day later.

Questions: Public Works, 555-0142, Monday through Friday, 7 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., or publicworks@harlowfalls.example.
