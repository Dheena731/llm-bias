NOTICE: PHISHING EMAIL AWARENESS

To all employees, recent monitoring indicates an increase in phishing attempts directed at our organization. Phishing is a type of social engineering attack in which fraudsters send deceptive messages that mimic legitimate senders to trick recipients into sharing confidential data or installing harmful software.

Please adhere to the following procedures:
• Treat any unsolicited request for passwords, financial information, or personal details as highly suspicious.
• Inspect the sender’s full email address for subtle misspellings, extra characters, or unfamiliar domains.
• Hover your mouse pointer over embedded links before clicking to verify the actual web address displayed.
• Do not open unexpected attachments, regardless of the file type or apparent source.
• Immediately forward any suspected phishing message to your organization’s IT security team and delete it from your inbox. Never reply, click, or provide information through such messages.

Your consistent vigilance is the first line of defense in protecting company data and network integrity. Thank you for applying these precautions to your daily communications.

Information Technology Security Team
