Toss your vegetables with oil and salt before they go into the oven, and make sure they are spread out in a single layer with a little space between the pieces. The salt draws out flavor and helps the edges caramelize, while the room on the pan lets moisture escape so the vegetables brown instead of steaming. Save delicate additions like fresh herbs, lemon juice, or a drizzle of vinegar for the moment they come out of the oven, since high heat can turn herbs bitter and dull the brightness of acid. A final pinch of flaky salt and a grind of black pepper at the end will make everything taste more vivid.
