We’re launching a new Savings Goal feature designed to help you save with clarity and confidence. You can now set a specific target amount, define a realistic timeline, and track your progress with an automatic, up-to-date balance counter. The feature sends timely reminders when you’re near your goal and allows you to adjust contribution amounts or pause savings at any time. Multiple goals can be active simultaneously, each stored securely in your account and updated with every deposit. To begin, navigate to your account dashboard, select “Create New Goal,” enter your target amount and preferred schedule, and confirm. Your progress will appear on your home screen until you reach your milestone.
