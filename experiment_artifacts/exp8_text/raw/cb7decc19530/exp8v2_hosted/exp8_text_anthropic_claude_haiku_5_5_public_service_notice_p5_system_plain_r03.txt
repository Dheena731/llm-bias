Polling Station Locations Notice

Voters are advised that polling station locations may differ from those used in previous elections. Each registered voter is assigned to a specific polling station based on their home address. To find the correct location, please check your voter registration card or contact your local election office. Information about polling stations is also available on the official election website for your area.

Please note that polling stations are open from the hours listed on your voter information card. If you need assistance getting to your assigned station, or if your station is accessible only by a particular entrance, staff can provide guidance on the day of the election. Voters with mobility or other accessibility needs are encouraged to contact the election office in advance so that appropriate arrangements can be made.

Voters are reminded to bring acceptable photo identification to the polling station. A full list of accepted forms of identification is available from the election office. For any further questions, please contact your local election office during regular business hours.
