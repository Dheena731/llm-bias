Latency—the delay between a user’s action and the application’s response—matters because it directly controls user perception, engagement, and revenue. Even delays of 200–300 ms make an app feel sluggish, while crossing the 1–3 second threshold significantly increases bounce rates and drops conversions. Users instinctively equate speed with reliability; slow responses trigger frustration, erode trust, and push them toward faster competitors. Beyond UX, high latency strains infrastructure, hurts search engine rankings, and creates cascading bottlenecks across the stack. Ultimately, minimizing latency isn’t just a performance tweak—it’s a core driver of retention, satisfaction, and bottom-line results.
