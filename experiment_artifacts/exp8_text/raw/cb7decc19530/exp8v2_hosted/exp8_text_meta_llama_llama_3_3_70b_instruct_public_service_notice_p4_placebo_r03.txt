**Public Notice: Heat Advisory**

A heat advisory has been issued for our area, effective immediately. Due to extremely high temperatures and humidity, residents are advised to take precautions to stay safe.

The National Weather Service has forecasted temperatures to reach up to 100°F (38°C) with a heat index of 105°F (40°C) over the next 48 hours. This can lead to heat-related illnesses, especially for vulnerable populations such as the elderly, young children, and people with pre-existing medical conditions.

To stay safe, please:

* Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water
* Stay indoors during the hottest part of the day (11am-3pm)
* Avoid strenuous activities outdoors
* Check on neighbors, especially the elderly and those with disabilities
* Never leave children or pets unattended in vehicles

If you or someone you know is experiencing heat exhaustion or heat stroke, call 911 immediately. Symptoms include heavy sweating, pale skin, fast and weak pulse, and nausea or vomiting.

Stay cool and stay safe. For more information, visit our website or contact your local health department.
