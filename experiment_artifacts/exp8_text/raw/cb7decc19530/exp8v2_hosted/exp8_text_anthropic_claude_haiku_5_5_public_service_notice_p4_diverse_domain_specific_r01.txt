**HEAT ADVISORY: Lower Vale and River Flats**
*Issued Tuesday, July 15, 6:10 a.m. Effective 11 a.m. Tuesday through 9 p.m. Thursday.*

Highs will reach 98 to 103°F. Heat index values of 106 to 110°F are expected between noon and 7 p.m. Overnight lows will stay near 79°F, so buildings will not cool down much after dark.

**If you don't have reliable air conditioning**, go to a cooling center. Eastside Library is open 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. and the Hollis Street Rec Center is open 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. Both take walk-ins without ID. Bus Route 9 stops at both.

**Check on your neighbors,** especially anyone over 65, anyone who lives alone, and anyone taking blood pressure or water pill medication. Knock on the door. A phone call can go unanswered for hours.

**Outdoor workers:** Move heavy tasks to before 11 a.m. or after 6 p.m. Take a shade break with water every 30 minutes, whether or not you feel thirsty.

**Pets and cars:** Asphalt can exceed 130°F and burn paws. A closed car can pass dangerous temperatures within minutes. Never leave a child, an elderly relative, or an animal in a parked vehicle, even for a short errand.

**Call 911 right away** if someone is confused, has stopped sweating while hot, feels hot to the touch, or faints. Move them to shade or air conditioning, loosen their clothing, and cool their skin with cold water while you wait.

**If the power goes out,** report it to Riverside Power at 555-0147. Never run a generator indoors or in an attached garage.

*Issued by the Vale County Office of Emergency Management.*
