📅 **Stop the back-to-back burnout!** 🔥➡️🔋

Here's a game-changer: Always add **10–15 min of buffer time** between meetings. 🕒✨

Why it works:
✅ Review notes & prep for the next topic.
✅ Stretch, hydrate, or just breathe. 🧘‍♀️💧
✅ Avoid the stress of running late.

Your brain needs transition time! Protect it like an appointment. 🛡️
