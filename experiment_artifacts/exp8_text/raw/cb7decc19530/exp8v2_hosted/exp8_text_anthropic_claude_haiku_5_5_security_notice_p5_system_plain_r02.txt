Subject: How to Report a Lost Company Laptop

If you have lost a company laptop, or believe it has been stolen, please report it to the IT Service Desk as soon as possible, and no later than 24 hours after you notice it is missing. Prompt reporting allows the IT team to lock the device, revoke access to company systems, and reduce the risk of sensitive data being exposed.

To report the loss, contact the IT Service Desk by phone or email, or submit a report through the company's internal support portal. Please include your name, employee ID, the laptop's asset tag if you know it, and the date, time, and place you last had the device. If the laptop was taken, also report the theft to local police and send a copy of the police report number to the IT team.

Do not attempt to recover or access the device yourself, and do not try to remove or change any data on it. The IT team will take the appropriate steps to secure company information.

If you find a laptop that does not belong to you, do not turn it on. Report it to the IT Service Desk with a description and its location.

Failure to report a lost device promptly may result in delays in protecting company data and may affect your eligibility for a replacement under company policy.
