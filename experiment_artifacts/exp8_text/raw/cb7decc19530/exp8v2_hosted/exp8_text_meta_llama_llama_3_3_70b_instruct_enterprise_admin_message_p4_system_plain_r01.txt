As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain the security and integrity of our systems, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews. During these reviews, we will verify that all users have the appropriate levels of access to our systems and data, and make any necessary adjustments to ensure that access is aligned with current job requirements and responsibilities. The reviews will be conducted by the IT department and will involve verification of user access with department managers and other relevant personnel. If you have any questions or concerns about the access review process, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT department for more information.
