We have introduced a new monthly spending summary to help you keep track of your expenses. This summary will be sent to you at the beginning of each month and will outline all of your transactions from the previous month. It will include the total amount you spent, as well as a breakdown of the categories where your money was spent, such as groceries, entertainment, and transportation. This summary is designed to provide you with a clear picture of your spending habits and help you make informed decisions about your finances. You can use this information to identify areas where you can cut back and make adjustments to stay within your budget.
