Notice: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, please report it immediately. Prompt action helps protect company data, customer information, and your own accounts from unauthorized access.

As soon as you realize the laptop is missing, contact the IT Service Desk by phone at extension 4400 or by email at servicedesk@company.example. If it is after hours, use the emergency security line at extension 4411. Please do not wait until the next business day, and do not spend time searching before you report it. IT can begin remote protections right away, including locking the device, disabling your accounts, and wiping data if necessary.

When you report the loss, be ready to share your name and department, the laptop's asset tag or serial number if you know it, the date, time, and location where you last had it, and a brief description of what happened. Let us know whether the device held any sensitive files, such as customer records, financial data, or confidential project materials. If you believe the laptop was stolen, also file a report with local police and send the report number to IT Security.

After reporting, change your passwords from another trusted device, and let your manager know what happened. Do not attempt to access company systems from the lost device if you recover it before IT confirms it is safe to use.

Reporting promptly will not result in disciplinary action in itself. Delays in reporting, however, can increase risk and may be reviewed under company policy. A replacement laptop will be arranged once the incident has been logged and reviewed.

Thank you for helping us keep company information secure. Questions about this notice can be directed to the IT Security team.
