When cooking for a weeknight dinner, consider marinating your protein of choice in a mixture of olive oil, garlic, and herbs before grilling or pan-frying. This will not only add flavor but also reduce the cooking time by pre-cooking the protein in the marinade. Simply place the protein in a ziplock bag, add your chosen marinade ingredients, and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before cooking as usual. This step can save you time and help you get a delicious meal on the table more quickly.
