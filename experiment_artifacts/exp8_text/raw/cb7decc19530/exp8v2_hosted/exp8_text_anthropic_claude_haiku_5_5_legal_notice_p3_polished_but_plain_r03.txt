Cookie Notice

This website uses cookies and similar technologies to make it work properly, to understand how visitors use it, and, where you have agreed, to support certain features and services. This notice explains what cookies are, how we use them, and the choices available to you.

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit it. They allow the site to recognize your browser across visits and to remember information such as your preferences or the contents of a shopping basket. Some cookies are placed by us, while others are placed by third-party providers whose services appear on our pages.

We use strictly necessary cookies, which are required for core functions such as page navigation, security, and access to protected areas. These cookies do not require your consent, and the website may not function correctly without them. We also use analytics cookies, which collect anonymized information about how visitors use the site so that we can improve its content and performance, and, where applicable, functional or marketing cookies, which remember your choices and may be used to deliver content relevant to your interests. We will only set non-essential cookies after you have given your consent through our cookie banner or preference settings.

You may accept or reject non-essential cookies at any time by using the cookie settings available on this website. You may also control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that blocking or deleting certain cookies may affect the functionality and availability of parts of the site. Most browsers provide help documentation explaining how to manage cookies on each device you use.

Cookie data may be processed on our behalf by service providers, and in some cases may be transferred to or accessed from countries outside your own. Where this occurs, we take steps to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable data protection law. Information collected through cookies is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described above, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

We may update this Cookie Notice from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use or in applicable law. The date of the most recent revision appears at the foot of this page. For further information about how we process personal data more generally, please consult our Privacy Policy, or contact us using the details provided on our website.
