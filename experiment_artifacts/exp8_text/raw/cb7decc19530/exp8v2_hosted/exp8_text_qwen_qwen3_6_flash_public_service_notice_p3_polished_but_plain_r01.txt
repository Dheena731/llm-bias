This notice informs all residents of important updates regarding the recycling collection schedule. Starting on the first day of next month, single-stream recycling will transition to a biweekly routine designed to improve efficiency and lower costs. Pickups will take place only during odd-numbered weeks for approved materials including paper, cardboard, plastic containers, glass jars, and metal cans. There will be no collection service during even-numbered weeks; please store recyclables in a clean bin until the next available date. Residents are reminded to rinse all containers thoroughly and place items loosely inside the bin without using bags. For additional information or assistance, please contact the waste management department
