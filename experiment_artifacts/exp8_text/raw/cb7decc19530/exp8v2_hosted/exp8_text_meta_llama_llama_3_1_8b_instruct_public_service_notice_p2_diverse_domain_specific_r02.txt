**Temporary Road Closure: Oakdale Drive**

To facilitate the installation of a new water main, Oakdale Drive will be closed to through traffic between Park Avenue and Maple Street, effective Monday, March 20th. The closure will be in place for approximately 6 weeks, with the first phase of work concluding by April 1st.

During this time, local residents and businesses will have access to their properties, but through traffic will be detoured via the adjacent neighborhood streets. Motorists are advised to plan their route accordingly and follow the designated detour signs.

The road will be reopened in stages, with the first section reopening by April 10th. Updates on the road closure will be provided via the City's website and social media channels. We appreciate your patience and cooperation during this necessary infrastructure project.
