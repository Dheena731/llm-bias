**Important Voting Information**

For the upcoming election, polling stations will be located at the following sites:

* Local Community Center, 123 Main Street
* Town Hall, 456 Elm Street
* Public Library, 789 Oak Street

These locations will be open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM on election day. Voters are advised to check their voter registration cards to confirm their assigned polling station. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the local election office for assistance.
