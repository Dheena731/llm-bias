**Subject:** The roast calendar, automated.

Coffee hits its stride between day 6 and day 21 post-roast. Buy too early, it's gassy and hard to extract; buy too late from a grocery shelf, the aromatics have flattened. 

We built our subscription around that specific window.

Here is how it works:

* **Roasted to order:** We drop batches every Monday and ship Tuesday morning. By the time the box lands on your counter, the beans have degassed and are ready to grind.
* **The coffee:** Choose between our core profile (dense chocolate, washed sweetness, built for milk or heavy filter) or the **Origins Rotation** (seasonal microlots, wild natural processes, and high-elevation cultivars that change as fresh containers clear port).
* **Brew parameters included:** Every bag comes with our lab’s starting recipes—dial-in ratios, water temperature, and grind targets for flat-bottom, conical, and 9-bar extraction.

You control the cadence (every 1, 2, or 4 weeks) and can pause or swap your bag profile anytime through the portal.
