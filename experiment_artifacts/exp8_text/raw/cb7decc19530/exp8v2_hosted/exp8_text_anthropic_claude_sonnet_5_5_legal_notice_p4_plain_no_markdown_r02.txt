NOTICE REGARDING REFUND ELIGIBILITY

This Notice sets out the conditions under which a purchaser may be entitled to a refund for goods or services bought from the Company. By completing a purchase, the purchaser acknowledges that they have read and accepted these terms.

A purchaser is eligible for a refund only if a written request is received by the Company within thirty days of the date of purchase. Requests received after that period will not be considered, except where applicable law requires otherwise. The request must include the order number, the name used at the time of purchase, and a brief statement of the reason for the request.

Physical goods are eligible for a refund only if they are returned in their original condition and packaging, unused, and accompanied by proof of purchase. The purchaser is responsible for the cost of return shipping unless the goods were defective, damaged on arrival, or sent in error. The Company reserves the right to inspect returned goods and to decline a refund where the goods show signs of use, damage, or alteration not attributable to the Company.

Services are eligible for a refund only if the purchaser has not yet received substantial performance of the service. Where a service has been partly performed, the Company may, at its discretion, issue a partial refund reflecting the portion not yet delivered. Digital products are non-refundable once downloaded or accessed, unless they are materially defective and the Company is unable to correct the defect within a reasonable time.

Customized items, gift cards, and goods sold as final sale are not eligible for a refund. These categories will be identified at the time of sale.

Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within fourteen business days of approval. The Company is not responsible for delays caused by banks or payment processors. Any processing fees charged by third parties may be deducted from the refund amount where permitted by law.

Nothing in this Notice limits any rights that a purchaser may have under mandatory consumer protection legislation. The Company may amend this Notice from time to time, and any amendment will apply only to purchases made after the date of the change. Questions regarding this Notice should be directed to the Company's customer service department.
