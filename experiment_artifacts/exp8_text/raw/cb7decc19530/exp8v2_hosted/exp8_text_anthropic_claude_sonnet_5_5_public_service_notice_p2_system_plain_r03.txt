NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE

The City Department of Public Works advises residents and motorists that Maple Street will be closed to all vehicle traffic between Fourth Avenue and Ninth Avenue from Monday, June 3, at 7:00 a.m. until Friday, June 14, at 5:00 p.m. The closure is necessary to allow crews to replace an aging water main and resurface the roadway.

During this period, through traffic will be detoured along Oak Boulevard and Eleventh Avenue. Detour signs will be posted at all affected intersections. Drivers should expect delays during morning and evening commute hours and are encouraged to allow extra travel time or choose alternate routes.

Residents who live within the closure area will retain access to their driveways, though access may be briefly interrupted at times when work is taking place directly in front of a property. Crews will make every effort to give advance notice when this is expected. Parking will be prohibited on both sides of Maple Street within the work zone, and vehicles left in place may be towed at the owner's expense.

Pedestrian access will be maintained on at least one side of the street wherever it is safe to do so. Emergency vehicles will be able to reach all properties throughout the project. Trash and recycling collection will continue on the regular schedule, though residents may be asked to move bins to the nearest cross street.

The work is weather dependent, and the schedule may change if conditions delay progress. Updates will be posted on the department's website and announced through the city's public alert system.

For questions or concerns, please contact the Department of Public Works at 555-0142 during business hours, Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. We appreciate your patience as we work to improve local infrastructure.

Issued by the City Department of Public Works, May 24.
