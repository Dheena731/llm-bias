**Important Voting Information**

For the upcoming election, polling stations will be located at the following sites:

* Local Community Center (123 Main St)
* Town Hall (456 Elm St)
* Public Library (789 Oak St)

These locations will be open from 7am to 8pm on election day. Voters are advised to check their voter registration cards to confirm their assigned polling station. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the local election office for assistance.
