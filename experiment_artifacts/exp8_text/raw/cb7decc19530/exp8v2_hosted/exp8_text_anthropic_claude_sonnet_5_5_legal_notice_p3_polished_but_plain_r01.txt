Notice Regarding the Use of Cookies

This website uses cookies and similar technologies to operate properly, to improve your experience, and to understand how the site is used. By continuing to browse this website after being shown this notice, you acknowledge the practices described here. Where the law requires your consent, we will ask for it before placing non-essential cookies on your device, and you may decline or withdraw that consent at any time.

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your computer, phone, or other device when you visit. Cookies allow the site to recognize your device, remember your preferences, and carry information from one page to the next. Similar technologies, such as pixels, local storage, and software identifiers, serve comparable purposes and are treated as cookies for the purposes of this notice.

We use several categories of cookies. Strictly necessary cookies enable core functions such as page navigation, security, load balancing, and the retention of items in a shopping cart or form. The site cannot function correctly without them, so they are set without requiring your consent. Preference cookies remember choices you have made, such as language or region. Analytics cookies collect information about how visitors use the site, including pages viewed and errors encountered, so that we can measure and improve performance. Marketing cookies, if enabled, are used by us or by third-party partners to deliver and measure advertising that may be relevant to your interests. Preference, analytics, and marketing cookies are optional and are used only in accordance with your choices.

Some cookies are set by third parties, such as analytics providers, advertising networks, or embedded content services. These parties may collect information about your online activity across different websites and are responsible for their own handling of that information under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies.

You can control cookies in several ways. You may use the cookie settings tool on this website to accept or reject optional categories, and you may change your selections whenever you wish. You may also adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Please note that disabling certain cookies may limit the features available to you or affect how the site displays and functions. Choices made in one browser or on one device apply only to that browser or device.

Cookies remain on your device for different lengths of time. Session cookies expire when you close your browser, while persistent cookies remain until they reach their expiration date or you delete them. Most of our persistent cookies expire within twelve months.

Where cookies collect personal data, we process that data in accordance with our privacy policy, which explains the legal basis for processing, how long data is kept, and the rights you may have, including the rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your information. If you have questions about this notice or wish to exercise those rights, please contact us using the details provided on our contact page.

We may update this notice from time to time to reflect changes in technology, law, or our practices. The date of the most recent revision will appear at the end of this notice, and we encourage you to review it periodically.
