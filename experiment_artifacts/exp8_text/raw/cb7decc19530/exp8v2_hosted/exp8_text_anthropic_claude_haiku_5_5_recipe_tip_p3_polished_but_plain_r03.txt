Rinse your rice before cooking to keep it from turning gummy. Place the measured grains in a fine-mesh strainer and run cold water over them, swirling with your fingers, until the water runs mostly clear. This washes away the surface starch that causes grains to clump together. Drain thoroughly, then cook as usual. For especially fluffy results, let the cooked rice rest with the lid on for about ten minutes before fluffing it with a fork.
