Here is a reminder about refilling a prescription:

**Reminder: Refill Your Prescription**

If you're running low on your medication, don't forget to refill your prescription before it runs out. You can call your pharmacy to request a refill or schedule a pick-up online. Make sure to have your prescription number and ID handy. Most pharmacies offer same-day or next-day refill services, so you can get back to taking your medication as directed.

**Refill Instructions:**

1. Check the expiration date on your prescription label to ensure you're not overdue for a refill.
2. Contact your pharmacy or healthcare provider to request a refill.
3. Provide your prescription number and ID to confirm your identity.
4. Follow up with the pharmacy to confirm your refill is processed and ready for pick-up.

**Important:** If you're unsure about your prescription status or have questions about refilling, consult with your healthcare provider or pharmacist for guidance.
