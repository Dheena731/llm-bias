Here's a tip for planning lunches for a busy week:

**Plan Your Lunches in Advance**

To avoid scrambling for something to eat during the week, take some time on the weekend to plan out your lunches for the next few days. Think about what you have on hand in your fridge and pantry, and make a list of meals you can prepare in advance. Consider making a batch of soup or stew that can be reheated throughout the week, or preparing a week's worth of salads or sandwiches. This will save you time and money, and ensure that you have healthy and delicious meals ready to go.
