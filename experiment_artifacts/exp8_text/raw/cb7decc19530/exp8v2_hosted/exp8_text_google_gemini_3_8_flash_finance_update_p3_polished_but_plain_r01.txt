We have introduced a new savings goals feature to help you plan and manage your financial milestones more effectively. Starting today, you can create customized targets directly within your account, whether you are building an emergency fund, planning a vacation, or preparing for a major purchase. The tool allows you to specify a target amount, set a completion date, and monitor your progress over time through visual tracking. You can also establish recurring transfers to fund your goals automatically, which removes the friction from consistent saving. This update is now live across both our web and mobile applications and can be found under the accounts overview section.
