Cookie Usage Notice

This website uses cookies and similar technologies to help it function, to remember your preferences, and to understand how visitors use our services. A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your device when you visit it. Some cookies are essential for the site to operate, such as those that maintain security or enable core features. Others are optional and are used for analytics, personalization, or advertising purposes.

Essential cookies are placed without requiring your consent, as they are necessary for the site to work as you would expect. Optional cookies are placed only where you have given your consent through our cookie banner or settings panel. You may accept all optional cookies, reject them, or choose specific categories at any time. Your choice can be changed or withdrawn at any point through the cookie settings available on this website.

Most web browsers also allow you to view, delete, or block cookies through their settings. Please be aware that disabling certain cookies may affect the performance or availability of some features on this website.

We may update this notice from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. The date of the most recent revision will be shown at the foot of this page.
