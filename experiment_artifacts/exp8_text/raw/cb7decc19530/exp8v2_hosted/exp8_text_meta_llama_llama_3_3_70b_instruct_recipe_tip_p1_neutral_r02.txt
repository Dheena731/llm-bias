To make weeknight dinners a breeze, try prep-ahead cooking. Chop vegetables like onions, bell peppers, and mushrooms on the weekend or a day off, and store them in airtight containers in the fridge. This way, when you come home from work, you can quickly sauté your pre-chopped veggies with some protein like chicken or tofu, and serve over rice, noodles, or with some whole grain bread for a quick and satisfying meal. This simple trick can save you up to 30 minutes of cooking time and make healthy eating easier on busy weeknights.
