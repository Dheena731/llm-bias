**Reminder: Book Your Annual Health Checkup**

It's time to schedule your yearly health checkup. Regular exams help catch potential health issues early and give you a chance to discuss any concerns with your doctor.

**What to do:**
1. Call your doctor's office or use their online booking system to request an appointment.
2. Choose a date that allows you to arrive prepared. If your doctor asks you to fast beforehand, they will tell you when you book.
3. Write down any questions, symptoms, or changes in your health you want to mention.
4. Bring a list of your current medications and your insurance information, if applicable.

Booking early gives you more choice of appointment times. Take a few minutes today to make the call or send the request.
