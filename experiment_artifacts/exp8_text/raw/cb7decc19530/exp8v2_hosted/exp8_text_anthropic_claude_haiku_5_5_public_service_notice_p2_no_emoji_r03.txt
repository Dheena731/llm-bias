**PUBLIC NOTICE: Road Closure**

**Location:** Maple Avenue, between 5th Street and 9th Street

**Dates:** Monday, June 9 through Friday, June 13, 2025

**Hours:** 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily

The City of Riverside Public Works Department will be closing Maple Avenue to all vehicle traffic while crews repair the water main and resurface the roadway. Work is expected to be completed by the end of the day on Friday, June 13, weather permitting.

**Detours:** Drivers should use Oak Street or Pine Road as alternate routes. Signs will be posted at each affected intersection.

**Access:** Local residents and businesses along the closed section will be able to reach their properties on foot. Emergency vehicles will have access at all times.

For questions or concerns, please contact the Public Works Department at (555) 012-3456 or visit www.riversidecity.gov/publicworks.

Thank you for your patience as we work to improve local infrastructure.
