Join us this Saturday, June 14, from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. at Riverside Park for the annual Neighborhood Block Party!

Enjoy live music, food trucks, a kids' face-painting booth, and a community garden tour. Local artisans will be selling handmade goods, and the library will be hosting a free book swap at the pavilion.

Bring your friends, your family, and your appetite. Parking is available along Maple Street, and bike racks are located near the main entrance.

See you there!
