Your end-of-year tax statement is a document that summarizes your income and tax deductions for the year. It typically includes details such as your total earnings, taxes withheld, and any contributions to retirement accounts or charitable donations. This statement is usually provided by your employer or financial institution and is used to help you complete your tax return. It's an important document to review and keep for your records, as it will help you accurately report your income and claim any eligible tax credits or deductions when filing your taxes.
