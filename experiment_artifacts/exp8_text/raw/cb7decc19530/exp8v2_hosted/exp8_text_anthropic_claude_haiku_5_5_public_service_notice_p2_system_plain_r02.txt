PUBLIC NOTICE: Road Closure on Maple Avenue

The Department of Public Works advises residents that Maple Avenue will be closed to all vehicle traffic between Oak Street and Cedar Lane beginning Monday, March 10, at 7:00 a.m. The closure is needed so that crews can replace a damaged water main beneath the roadway. The work is expected to take approximately five to seven days, weather permitting.

During the closure, detour signs will be posted directing drivers to use Birch Road and Elm Street as alternate routes. Local residents and business owners will still have access to their properties from either end of the closed section, though travel through the work zone will not be possible. Pedestrians may use the marked walkway on the east side of the street at all times.

Emergency vehicles will be permitted through the work zone when necessary. Drivers are asked to plan ahead, allow extra travel time, and obey all posted signs and flaggers. Residents with questions or concerns may call the Department of Public Works at 555-0142 or visit the city website for updates on the project schedule.

We appreciate your patience while this necessary repair is completed.
