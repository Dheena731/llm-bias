NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Pursuant to our refund policy, a refund may be eligible for customers who have purchased a product or service from our company within the last thirty (30) days. To be eligible for a refund, the product or service must be returned in its original condition, with all original packaging and accessories included. Additionally, the customer must provide proof of purchase and a valid reason for the return. Refunds will be issued in the original form of payment and may take up to fourteen (14) business days to process. Please contact our customer service department for more information on our refund policy and to initiate the refund process.
