**Launch Note: *Say It Again* Is Here**

Today we're releasing the first episodes of *Say It Again*, a podcast for people who want to learn a language without feeling like they're back in a classroom.

Each week, we take one everyday situation, such as ordering coffee, asking for directions, or making small talk with a neighbor, and walk through it at a relaxed pace. You'll hear natural conversations, a slow breakdown of the key phrases, and simple tips for remembering them. Episodes run about fifteen minutes, so they fit into a commute, a walk, or a dishwashing session.

We built the show around a few ideas:

- **Listening comes first.** You'll hear real speech early and often.
- **Repetition helps.** We revisit phrases across episodes so they stick.
- **Mistakes are fine.** Everyone sounds awkward at the start, including us.

The first three episodes are available now in Spanish, with French and Japanese tracks coming later this season. You can find us on all major podcast apps.

Give an episode a listen, then try saying a phrase out loud to someone today. If you have a situation you'd like us to cover, send us a note through the show page. We read every message.

Thanks for joining us at the start.
