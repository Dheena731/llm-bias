Using a calendar app can be a great way to stay organized, but it's even more effective if you take a few minutes to set it up properly. One tip is to schedule recurring events, such as regular meetings or appointments, in advance, so you don't have to remember to add them to your calendar every week or month. This can help you avoid double-booking and ensure that you have enough time to prepare for upcoming events. By taking the time to set up your calendar app in this way, you can save time and reduce stress in the long run.
