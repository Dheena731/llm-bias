Thanks for sharing your poster draft. The overall structure works well, and the progression from background to methods to results is easy to follow. Your central finding is clear and well placed in the upper right, where readers will look first. The results figure is strong, and the axis labels are legible from a reasonable distance.

There are a few areas to tighten. The introduction is somewhat long for a poster, and I'd suggest cutting it to two or three sentences that state the gap your study addresses. The methods section currently uses dense paragraphs; a short list of the key design elements or a simple flow diagram would let a passing viewer grasp the approach in seconds. Consider also adding a one-sentence takeaway beneath the results so the conclusion does not depend on the viewer reading the full text.

Finally, check the font sizes. Body text should be readable at about a meter away, and the reference list can be smaller. Make sure your contact information and a QR code linking to the full paper or your email are visible near the bottom, since many attendees will want to follow up.
