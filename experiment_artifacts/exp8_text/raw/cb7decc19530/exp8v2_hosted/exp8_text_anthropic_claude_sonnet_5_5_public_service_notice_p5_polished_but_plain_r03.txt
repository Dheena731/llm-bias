Notice Regarding Polling Station Locations

Voters are advised that polling stations will be open on election day from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Each registered voter is assigned to a specific polling station based on their home address, and ballots can only be cast at that assigned location. Please check your voter information card or contact your local election office to confirm where you should vote before you travel.

Most polling stations are located in public buildings that are familiar and easy to reach, including schools, libraries, community centers, and municipal offices. Every location has been reviewed for accessibility, and each provides step-free entry, designated parking for voters with disabilities, and assistance on request. Voters who need additional support, such as seating, large-print materials, or help marking a ballot, should speak with a poll worker upon arrival.

Please note that some locations have changed since the previous election. Buildings undergoing renovation, those no longer available for public use, and those that did not meet current accessibility standards have been replaced. If you have moved or have not voted in some time, do not assume your polling station is the same as before. Updated address lists are posted at local election offices and public notice boards.

To make your visit as smooth as possible, bring a valid form of photo identification and your voter information card if you have one. Lines tend to be longest early in the morning, at midday, and shortly after the end of the working day, so arriving in the middle of the morning or early afternoon may shorten your wait. Anyone in line when polls close will still be permitted to vote.

If you are unable to reach your assigned polling station or are unsure of its location, contact your local election office as early as possible. Staff can confirm your address, explain your options, and provide information on early or absentee voting where available. Thank you for taking part in the election.
