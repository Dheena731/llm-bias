Introducing the new savings goal feature designed to streamline your financial planning. This functionality allows you to define specific targets for purchases or emergencies by setting a desired amount and a completion date. As you contribute funds, the interface displays real-time progress against your deadline, helping you stay motivated and on track. You can adjust contribution amounts automatically or manually, ensuring that saving remains flexible while building toward meaningful milestones. Whether you are preparing for a major purchase or bolstering an emergency fund, this tool provides clarity and control over your savings journey.
