We're excited to share that our new Savings Goals feature is now live in the app. It's designed to help you plan for the things that matter most, whether that's a vacation, a down payment, an emergency fund, or a new laptop.

Here's how it works. You can create a goal, set a target amount and a target date, and choose how much you'd like to contribute each month. The app tracks your progress automatically and shows you how close you are to reaching your goal. You can also add one-time deposits whenever you have extra funds available.

You can create up to five active goals at once, and you can edit or pause them at any time. When you reach a goal, we'll send you a notification to celebrate with you.

To get started, open the Money tab and tap "Savings Goals." If you have feedback or run into any issues, please reach out to our support team through the Help section in the app.

Happy saving!
