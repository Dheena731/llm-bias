NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Pursuant to our return and refund policy, customers may be eligible for a refund under certain circumstances. To be eligible for a refund, the customer must meet the following conditions: 

1. The item must be returned within the specified timeframe, 
2. The item must be in its original condition, and 
3. The customer must provide proof of purchase.

Refunds will be issued in the original form of payment. Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item is defective or was shipped in error. All refund requests are subject to review and approval. For more information, please contact our customer service department.
