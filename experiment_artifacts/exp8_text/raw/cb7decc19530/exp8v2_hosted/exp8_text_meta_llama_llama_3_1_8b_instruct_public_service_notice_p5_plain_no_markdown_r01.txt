Polling station locations for the upcoming election have been announced. Voters can check the official website or contact their local election office for a list of designated polling stations in their area.

Voters can cast their ballots at the designated polling stations on election day. Each polling station will be clearly marked with signs and will be open from 7am to 8pm. Voters should bring their identification and proof of residency to cast their ballots.

For voters with disabilities, special arrangements have been made at some polling stations. Voters can contact their local election office for more information.

Voters can also check the official website for a list of polling stations that offer early voting. Some polling stations may be open on weekends or in the evenings for voters who cannot make it to the polls during regular hours.
