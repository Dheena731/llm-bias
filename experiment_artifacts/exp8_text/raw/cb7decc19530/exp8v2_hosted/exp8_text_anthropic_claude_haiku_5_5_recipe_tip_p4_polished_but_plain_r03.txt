Batch cook once and build your lunches from that base. On a quiet evening or weekend morning, prepare a grain such as rice or quinoa, a protein like roasted chicken, chickpeas, or baked tofu, and a couple of vegetables you can eat cold or reheat easily. Store each component in its own container so nothing turns soggy, then assemble a different combination each day to keep things from feeling repetitive. A simple formula of grain, protein, vegetable, and a sauce or dressing gives you variety without extra effort. Preparing these pieces once can save you a surprising amount of time and decision-making during the week.
