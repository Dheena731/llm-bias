A quick reminder to give your eyes and mind a rest from your screen. Every twenty minutes or so, look away at something far off for about twenty seconds, and let your eyes relax. Stand up, stretch your shoulders and neck, and take a few slow breaths. Even a minute or two away from the screen can ease eye strain, reduce tension, and help you come back more focused. Your work will still be there, and you will likely handle it better after a short pause.
