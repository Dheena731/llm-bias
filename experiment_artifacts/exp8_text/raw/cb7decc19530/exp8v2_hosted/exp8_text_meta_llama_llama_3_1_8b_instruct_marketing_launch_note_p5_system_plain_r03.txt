Welcome to "Learn with Us," a new podcast for language learners. In each episode, we'll explore a new language, its culture, and its unique characteristics. We'll be joined by native speakers, language instructors, and fellow learners who'll share their experiences and insights. Our goal is to provide a platform for language enthusiasts to connect, learn, and grow together. Whether you're a beginner, an intermediate learner, or a seasoned linguist, we invite you to join us on this journey of discovery and exploration.
