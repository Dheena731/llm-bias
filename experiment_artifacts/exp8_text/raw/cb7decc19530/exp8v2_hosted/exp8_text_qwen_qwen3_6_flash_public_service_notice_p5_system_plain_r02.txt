This notice informs registered voters of their designated polling station assignments for the upcoming election. Your assigned location depends on your residential address as recorded in the voter registry. To find your exact polling place, visit the official election website or call the voter information hotline. Polling stations will be open from seven in the morning until eight in the evening on election day. Please bring a valid government issued photo identification and your voter registration card if you have it. Arrive early to avoid long lines and allow time for processing. If you encounter any difficulties at the polls, speak with the chief election inspector stationed there. For additional assistance regarding accessibility accommodations or language support, contact the elections office directly using the phone number provided on your registration confirmation. Thank you for participating in the democratic process.
