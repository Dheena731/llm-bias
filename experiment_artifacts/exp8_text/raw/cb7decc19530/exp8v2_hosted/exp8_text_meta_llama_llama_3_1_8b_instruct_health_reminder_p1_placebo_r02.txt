**Weekly Fitness Review Reminder**

Take a moment to review your progress towards your fitness goals. Regularly tracking your progress can help you stay motivated, identify areas for improvement, and make informed decisions about your workout routine.

Consider reviewing the following:

* Your workout schedule for the past week
* Any changes in your body weight or measurements
* Your exercise intensity and duration
* Any progress towards your specific fitness goals (e.g. completing a certain number of workouts, increasing your strength or endurance)

By regularly reviewing your progress, you can refine your approach, adjust your routine as needed, and celebrate your successes along the way.
