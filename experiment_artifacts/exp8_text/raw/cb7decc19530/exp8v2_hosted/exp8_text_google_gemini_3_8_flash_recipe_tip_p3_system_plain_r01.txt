To make your rice less sticky, thoroughly rinse the raw grains in a fine-mesh strainer under cold running water until the water runs completely clear. This crucial step washes away the excess surface starch that causes the grains to clump together and turn gummy as they cook. Once the rice is finished cooking, remove it from the heat and let it rest undisturbed with the lid on for about ten minutes, then gently fluff it with a fork instead of stirring it with a spoon.
