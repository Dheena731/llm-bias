Here's a calendar tip that saves me hours every week: schedule a 15-minute "weekly reset" block every Sunday evening. Use that time to review the coming week, move any tasks that don't have a set time into open slots, and set reminders for anything that needs preparation. Knowing your week is mapped out before it starts takes the pressure off Monday morning and makes it much easier to say yes or no to new commitments. Give it a try this weekend and see how much calmer your week feels.
