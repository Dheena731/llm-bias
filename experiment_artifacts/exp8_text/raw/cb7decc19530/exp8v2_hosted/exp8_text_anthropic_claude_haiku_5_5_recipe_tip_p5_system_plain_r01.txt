Toss your vegetables in oil before seasoning, and add salt just before they go into the oven. The oil helps the seasonings cling to every surface, so the flavor is even instead of patchy. Salting right before roasting, rather than long in advance, keeps the vegetables from releasing too much moisture, which can lead to steaming instead of browning. Once they come out, finish with a small pinch of salt and a squeeze of lemon or a sprinkle of fresh herbs to brighten the flavor.
