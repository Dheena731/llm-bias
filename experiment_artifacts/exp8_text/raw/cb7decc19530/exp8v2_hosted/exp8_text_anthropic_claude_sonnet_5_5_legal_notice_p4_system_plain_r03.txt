NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY. This Notice sets out the conditions under which a purchaser ("the Customer") may be entitled to a refund from the seller ("the Company") for goods or services purchased directly from the Company. By completing a purchase, the Customer acknowledges having read and accepted the terms stated herein.

A request for refund shall be considered only if it is submitted in writing to the Company within thirty (30) calendar days of the date of purchase, as shown on the original receipt or order confirmation. Requests received after that period shall be deemed ineligible, except where a longer period is required by applicable law. The Customer must provide proof of purchase and a reasonable statement of the grounds for the request.

Goods are eligible for refund only if they are returned in their original condition, unused, and in their original packaging, and only if the Customer has not damaged, altered, or materially modified them. Services are eligible for refund only if they have not been substantially performed or delivered as of the date of the request. Digital products, custom-made items, perishable goods, and items expressly designated as final sale are not eligible for refund, unless they are defective or materially not as described.

Where a refund is approved, the Company shall issue payment to the original method of payment within fourteen (14) business days of approval. Original shipping charges, handling fees, and any taxes that the Company is not permitted to refund shall be excluded from the refund amount. The Customer shall bear the cost of returning goods unless the return results from the Company's error or from a defect in the goods.

Nothing in this Notice limits or excludes any statutory rights of the Customer that cannot lawfully be limited or excluded. The Company reserves the right to refuse any request that it reasonably determines to be fraudulent, abusive, or inconsistent with this Notice. The Company may amend this Notice from time to time, and any amendment shall apply only to purchases made after the effective date of the amendment.
