Voters are advised to check the location of their polling station before the upcoming election. Polling stations will be located at various schools and community centers throughout the area. A list of polling station locations can be obtained by contacting the local election office or by checking the official election website. Voters can also verify their assigned polling station by checking their voter registration card or by contacting the election office directly. It is recommended that voters confirm the location of their polling station before election day to avoid any confusion or delays.
