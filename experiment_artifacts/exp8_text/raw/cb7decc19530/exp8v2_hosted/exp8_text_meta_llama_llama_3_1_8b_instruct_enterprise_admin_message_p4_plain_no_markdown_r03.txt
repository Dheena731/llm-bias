Quarterly Access Reviews

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain the security and integrity of our systems, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews. These reviews are designed to ensure that all users have the necessary permissions and access to the systems and data they require to perform their duties.

During these reviews, we will be evaluating the current access levels of all users and making adjustments as necessary. This may involve removing access to certain systems or data for users who no longer require it, or granting access to new users who have been added to our systems.

If you are a user who will be subject to an access review, you will be notified in advance and provided with instructions on how to prepare for the review. If you have any questions or concerns about your access levels, please do not hesitate to contact our IT department.

The quarterly access reviews will be conducted on a rotating schedule to ensure that all users are reviewed at least once per quarter. We appreciate your cooperation in this effort and your help in maintaining the security and integrity of our systems.
