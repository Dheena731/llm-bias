**Notice of Refund Eligibility**

To qualify for a refund, you must submit your request within thirty (30) calendar days of the original purchase date. 

To complete your return, the item must be unused, in the same condition that you received it, and in its original packaging. A valid receipt or proof of purchase is required for all refund requests. 

The following items are not eligible for a refund:
* Custom or personalized products
* Downloadable digital content
* Items marked as final sale at the time of purchase

Upon receipt and inspection of the returned item, you will be notified whether your refund is approved. If approved, your refund will be credited to your original method of payment within ten (10) business days. Original shipping fees are non-refundable.
