After pulling a weeknight protein from the skillet, pour off nearly all the rendered fat but leave the browned bits and a thin film of oil. Add half a cup of broth or even plain water, scrape up the fond with a wooden spoon, and let it reduce for thirty seconds. Take the pan completely off the heat, whisk in a tablespoon of cold butter until it turns opaque and glossy, then season with salt and cracked pepper. That instant emulsion clings to roasted vegetables or grain bowls, binding a simple plate together without adding a separate sauce step or extra dishes.
