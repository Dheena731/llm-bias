We are rolling out a new Savings Goal feature that helps you track and reach your financial targets with clear, actionable guidance. You can now define a specific amount and timeline for any savings objective, such as an emergency fund, travel plans, or equipment purchases. As you contribute, an on-screen progress indicator updates automatically so you always know exactly how close you are to your target. If your contribution pace falls behind your planned schedule, the app will send a brief, non-intrusive reminder suggesting a simple adjustment. The feature is live now in your account menu, and creating your first goal requires only a few taps. Begin saving with structure and confidence today.
