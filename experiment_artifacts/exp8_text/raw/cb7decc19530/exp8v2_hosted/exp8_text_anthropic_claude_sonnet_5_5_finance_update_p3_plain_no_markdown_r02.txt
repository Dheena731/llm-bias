We've added a new savings goals feature to help you set aside money for the things that matter to you. You can now create a goal for anything from a vacation to an emergency fund, give it a name, choose a target amount, and set a date you'd like to reach it by.

Once a goal is created, the app calculates how much you would need to save each week or month to stay on track. You can move money into a goal manually whenever you like, or set up automatic transfers so your savings grow without any extra effort. Your progress is shown clearly for each goal, so you can see how close you are at a glance and adjust your plan if your circumstances change.

You can create as many goals as you need and track them side by side. If you reach a goal early, or decide to change its amount or deadline, you can edit it at any time without losing your progress.

The feature is available now to all users. To get started, open the savings section of the app and choose the option to add a new goal. We hope it makes saving feel simpler and more rewarding, and we welcome your feedback as we continue to improve it.
