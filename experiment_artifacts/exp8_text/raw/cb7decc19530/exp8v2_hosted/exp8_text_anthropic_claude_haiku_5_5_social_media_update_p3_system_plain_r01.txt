Hi neighbors! Join us this Saturday for our community picnic at Riverside Park, starting at 11 a.m. and running until 3 p.m. There will be food trucks, live music from local bands, games for kids, and a plant swap for anyone who loves gardening. Bring a blanket or a lawn chair, and feel free to bring a dish to share. Everyone is welcome, and admission is free. We hope to see you there, so please share this with friends and family who might enjoy a relaxed afternoon together!
