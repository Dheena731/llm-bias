Overall, the draft is looking very strong. The central data visual really grabs attention, and the research question is framed clearly right from the start.

Here are a few quick suggestions to help polish it before printing:

First, try to cut down the text volume in the Introduction and Methods sections by about twenty to thirty percent. Posters are meant to be conversational, so leaning more on concise phrases will make it much easier for attendees to skim as they walk past.

Second, increase the font size of your section headers and data labels. The main title is great, but the subsection headings blend in a bit too much with the body text from a few feet away.

Finally, consider increasing the contrast on the caption text below the graphs. The current gray on white might wash out under harsh conference hall lighting.

You are in great shape for the presentation. Send over the next version whenever you are ready, and I would be happy to take another look.
