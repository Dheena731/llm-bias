Attention all staff, 

We are introducing a new expense approval workflow effective immediately. The new process is designed to improve efficiency and transparency in managing company expenses. All employees are required to submit their expenses through the new online portal, where they will be reviewed and approved by the relevant department heads. Please note that expenses submitted outside of this process may not be approved or reimbursed. Further instructions and training on the new workflow will be provided in the coming days. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the finance department.
