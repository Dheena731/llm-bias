Caching speeds up a website by temporarily storing copies of frequently accessed files—such as images, stylesheets, and scripts—in a location that is faster to retrieve than the original source. When you first visit a webpage, your browser downloads these resources and saves a local copy. On subsequent visits, the browser pulls the saved files directly from your device instead of requesting them again over the internet. This eliminates duplicate data transfers, reduces server processing, and cuts waiting time. The result is quicker page loads, lower hosting demand, and a consistently faster experience for visitors.
