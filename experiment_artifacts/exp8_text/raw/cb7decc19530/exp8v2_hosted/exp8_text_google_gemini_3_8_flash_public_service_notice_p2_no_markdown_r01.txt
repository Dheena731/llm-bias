PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE

Please be advised that Oak Street, between 4th Avenue and Elm Boulevard, will be temporarily closed to all vehicular traffic from Monday, November 4, at 7:00 AM through Wednesday, November 6, at 5:00 PM.

This closure is necessary for scheduled water main replacement and asphalt resurfacing. 

Marked detours will direct traffic around the work zone via Maple Street. Local residents and emergency services will maintain limited access, but delays should be expected. 

Drivers are encouraged to use alternate routes and allow extra travel time. 

For inquiries, please contact the City Department of Public Works at 555-0148. We appreciate your patience and cooperation during this infrastructure improvement project.
