NOTICE OF ACCEPTABLE USE OF SHARED NETWORK

The following rules govern the use of the shared network and all users are expected to adhere to them.

Users are responsible for all activities conducted on the network, including but not limited to, any and all data transmitted, accessed, or stored.

Use of the network for any unlawful, unauthorized, or malicious purpose is strictly prohibited. This includes but is not limited to, hacking, cracking, and other forms of unauthorized access to systems or data.

Users are responsible for ensuring that any equipment or software used on the network is properly configured and compatible with the network's security features.

Users are not permitted to install or configure any software or equipment on the network without the prior approval of the network administrator.

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality and integrity of their account and password. Users shall not share their account or password with anyone.

Users are not permitted to engage in any activity that would compromise the security or stability of the network. This includes but is not limited to, overwhelming the network with excessive traffic, disrupting the network, or interfering with the network's services.

The network administrator reserves the right to monitor all activity on the network, and to suspend or terminate access to the network for any reason deemed necessary.

Users are expected to familiarize themselves with these rules and to report any concerns or issues to the network administrator.

 Failure to comply with these rules may result in disciplinary action, including but not limited to, loss of access to the network, termination of services, and other penalties as deemed necessary by the network administrator.

By using the shared network, users acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agree to abide by these rules.
