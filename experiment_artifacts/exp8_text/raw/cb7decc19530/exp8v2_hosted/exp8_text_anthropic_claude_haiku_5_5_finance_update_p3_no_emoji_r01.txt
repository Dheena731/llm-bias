**Introducing Savings Goals**

We're excited to announce Savings Goals, a new feature that helps you set, track, and reach your financial targets.

**What you can do:**
- **Create a goal** with a name, target amount, and deadline, whether you're saving for a vacation, an emergency fund, or a new laptop.
- **Track progress** with a visual summary showing how much you've saved and how much is left.
- **Set up automatic contributions** so money moves toward your goal on the schedule you choose.
- **Adjust anytime** by changing your target, timeline, or contribution amount as your plans evolve.

Savings Goals is available now in the app under the Savings tab. Open it to create your first goal and see how small, consistent steps add up.

As always, we welcome your feedback. Let us know what goals you're working toward and what would make the feature more useful for you.
