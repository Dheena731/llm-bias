Here are a few options, ranging from constructive to direct:

**Option 1: Constructive and standard (Best all-around)**
> "While the topic is timely and engaging, the primary research question remains ambiguous. The Introduction touches upon several broad themes, but it lacks a clearly articulated question or hypothesis to anchor the study. I recommend explicitly defining the core research question at the end of the Introduction and ensuring the methodology directly aligns with answering it."

**Option 2: Short and direct**
> "The manuscript would be significantly strengthened by a more focused research question. Currently, the study’s objective is too broad, making it difficult to assess whether the methodology and conclusions are appropriate. Please state the specific research question(s) explicitly."

**Option 3: Focus on scope and variables**
> "The central research question is currently too vague. It is unclear what specific relationships or phenomena the authors are attempting to measure. Please clearly define the key variables, the scope of the inquiry, and the specific gap in the literature this question aims to address."
