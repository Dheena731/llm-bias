📅 **Calendar tip:** Stop using your calendar only for meetings. Block out time for your *tasks* too!

Next time you add "finish report" to your to-do list, drag it onto your calendar as a 45-minute event instead. Having a specific time slot makes you far more likely to actually do it, and you'll see right away when your day is overbooked.

Bonus: add a 10-minute buffer between events so back-to-back days don't wreck you. 🙌
