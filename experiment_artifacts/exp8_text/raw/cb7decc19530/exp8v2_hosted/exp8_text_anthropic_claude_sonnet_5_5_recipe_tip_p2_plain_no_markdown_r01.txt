To keep fresh herbs vibrant for up to two weeks, treat them like cut flowers or like salad greens depending on their type. For tender herbs such as cilantro, parsley, and dill, trim the stem ends, stand the bunch in a jar with an inch of water, and loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag before placing the jar in the refrigerator. For sturdier herbs such as rosemary, thyme, and sage, wrap them in a barely damp paper towel and tuck them into a resealable bag or container in the fridge. Basil is the exception, since it blackens in the cold, so keep it in a jar of water on the counter away from direct sun. Whichever method you use, change the water or paper towel every few days and discard any leaves that begin to turn slimy.
