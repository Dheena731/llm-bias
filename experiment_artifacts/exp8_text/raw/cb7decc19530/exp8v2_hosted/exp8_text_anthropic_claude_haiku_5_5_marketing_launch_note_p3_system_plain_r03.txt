Introducing the Everyday Budget Planner, a new spreadsheet template designed to take the guesswork out of managing your money. Whether you are tracking monthly bills, planning for a big purchase, or simply trying to see where your paycheck goes, this template brings everything into one clear, easy-to-use workbook.

The planner opens with a monthly overview that summarizes your income, expenses, and remaining balance at a glance. From there, you can record transactions by category, from groceries and utilities to subscriptions and savings goals. Built-in formulas update your totals automatically, so you can focus on your decisions rather than your arithmetic.

No spreadsheet experience is required. Every section comes pre-labeled with simple instructions, and the color-coded cells show you at a glance whether you are on track or over budget. The template works in Excel, Google Sheets, and Numbers, and you can copy it to start a fresh month in seconds.

The Everyday Budget Planner is available starting today for a one-time download. Take control of your finances with a tool that is simple enough to use every day and powerful enough to keep you on course for the long term.
