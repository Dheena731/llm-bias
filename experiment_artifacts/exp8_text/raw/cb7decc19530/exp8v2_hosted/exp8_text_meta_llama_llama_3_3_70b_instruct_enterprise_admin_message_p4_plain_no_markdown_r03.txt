As part of our ongoing effort to maintain the security and integrity of our systems, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews. During this process, we will be reviewing all user accounts and access permissions to ensure that they are up to date and appropriate. This review will help us to identify and remove any unnecessary access, as well as to ensure that all users have the correct level of access to perform their jobs. The review will be completed by the end of the quarter and any changes will be implemented shortly thereafter. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the administration team.
