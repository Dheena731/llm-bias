Here is a short update explaining a new monthly spending summary:

As part of our ongoing efforts to improve financial transparency and accountability, we are introducing a new monthly spending summary. This summary will provide a detailed breakdown of all expenses incurred by the company over the past month, including both direct and indirect costs.

The summary will include the following information:

* A list of all expenditures, categorized by type (e.g. salaries, rent, utilities, etc.)
* The total amount spent in each category
* A comparison to the previous month's spending in each category
* A running total of the company's year-to-date expenses

This new summary will be made available to all employees on a monthly basis, and will be posted to the company's intranet for easy access. We believe that this will help to promote greater transparency and accountability, and will allow us to make more informed decisions about our financial resources.
