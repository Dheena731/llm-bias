Important Notice: Phishing Email Alert

Please be aware that there has been an increase in phishing emails targeting our company. These emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information such as your login credentials or financial information.

To protect yourself and the company, please be cautious when receiving emails that:

- Ask you to verify your account information or login credentials
- Contain suspicious links or attachments
- Create a sense of urgency or threaten to suspend your account
- Are not addressed to you by name or contain generic greetings

If you receive an email that you suspect is a phishing attempt, do not click on any links or respond with your personal information. Instead, report the email to the IT department immediately.

Remember, it is always better to err on the side of caution when it comes to your online security. If you are unsure about the legitimacy of an email, do not hesitate to reach out to the IT department for assistance.
