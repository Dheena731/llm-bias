**Reminder: Prescription Refill**

Your prescription is due for a refill soon. To avoid running out:

- **Check your supply.** Make sure you have at least 5–7 days of medication left.
- **Contact your pharmacy** by phone, app, or website to request the refill.
- **Call your doctor** if you're out of refills or need a new prescription.

Don't skip doses or stretch your medication to make it last. If you have any questions, your pharmacist can help.
