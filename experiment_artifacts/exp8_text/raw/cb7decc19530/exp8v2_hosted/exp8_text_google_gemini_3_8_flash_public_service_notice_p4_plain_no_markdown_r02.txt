A heat advisory is in effect for the local area from noon today through Sunday evening. Dangerously high temperatures combined with high humidity are expected to produce heat index values exceeding one hundred degrees. These conditions significantly increase the risk of heat-related illnesses, especially for the elderly, young children, and individuals with chronic health conditions. Residents are strongly urged to drink plenty of water, stay indoors in air-conditioned spaces, and avoid strenuous outdoor activities during the hottest parts of the day. Never leave children or pets unattended in parked vehicles, as interior temperatures can reach lethal levels in minutes. If you must work outside, take frequent rest breaks in shaded areas. Please check on vulnerable neighbors and family members to ensure they remain safe and cool throughout this extreme heat event.
