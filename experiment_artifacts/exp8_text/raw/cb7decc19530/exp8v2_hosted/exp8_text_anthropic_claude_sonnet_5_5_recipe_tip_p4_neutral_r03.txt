**Tip: Prep components, not full meals.**

On Sunday, spend 30–45 minutes cooking a few versatile building blocks:

- **A grain** (rice, quinoa, or pasta)
- **A protein** (grilled chicken, hard-boiled eggs, or roasted chickpeas)
- **A veggie** (roasted vegetables or a big batch of chopped raw ones)
- **A sauce or dressing** (pesto, vinaigrette, or peanut sauce)

Each day, mix and match into something different, like a grain bowl on Monday, a wrap on Tuesday, or a salad on Wednesday. You get variety without cooking every day, and the lunches won't feel repetitive by Thursday.

**Bonus:** Pack lunches the night before so mornings stay stress-free.
