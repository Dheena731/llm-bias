📅 **Calendar tip:** Stop using your calendar only for meetings. Block time for your *tasks* too!

Put "Finish report" or "Reply to emails" on your calendar as an actual event. When your to-do list has a time slot, it's way more likely to get done, and you'll see right away when your day is overbooked.

Bonus: color-code by category (work, personal, health) so you can see your week's balance at a glance. 🎨
