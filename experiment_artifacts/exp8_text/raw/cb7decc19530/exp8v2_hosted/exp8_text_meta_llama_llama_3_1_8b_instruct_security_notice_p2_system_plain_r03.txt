To: All Staff

The company has become aware of several instances of phishing emails being sent to employees. These emails are designed to trick the recipient into revealing sensitive information or clicking on a link that may install malware on their computer.

Phishing emails often appear to be from a legitimate source, such as a bank or a colleague, and may ask the recipient to verify their account information or login credentials.

The company wants to remind everyone to be cautious when opening emails, especially if they ask for sensitive information or seem to be from an unknown sender. Before responding to an email, verify the sender's identity by contacting them directly.

If you receive a phishing email, do not respond or click on any links. Instead, report the email to the IT department immediately.
