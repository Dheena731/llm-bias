To report a lost company laptop, please contact the IT department immediately. Provide your name, employee ID, and a detailed description of the laptop, including its make, model, and any distinctive features. Also, please provide the last known location of the laptop and the date and time it was last seen. This information will help us to take necessary steps to secure company data and recover the lost device. All reports will be treated as confidential and will be handled in accordance with company policies and procedures.
