**Session State & Protocol Storage Notice**

**Platform:** Apex Clearing & Settlement Gateway (API & Web Interface)

This gateway employs local state persistence—including encrypted HTTP cookies and session storage objects—solely to maintain cryptographic integrity, enforce multi-factor re-authentication intervals, and preserve tenant isolation across distributed transaction queues. 

We do not deploy marketing beacons, cross-site profiling trackers, or third-party behavioral analytics. The indicators generated by this environment are categorized under applicable data protection frameworks as strictly operational:

*   **Authentication Tokens (`__Host-` / `__Secure-`):** Validate hardware tokens, route encrypted WebSocket feeds, and satisfy audit logging requirements under SEC Rule 17a-4.
*   **Load Distribution Vectors:** Direct queries across active-active database clusters to prevent node starvation during high-volatility volume spikes.
*   **State Cache:** Preserves uncommitted trade tickets through transient network drops.

Restricting cookie generation via client-side policies or user-agent security headers will prevent deterministic signature validation, terminating the session and denying access to execution pipelines. Continued interaction with this terminal constitutes verification that local technical storage is authorized for transactional validation and regulatory compliance.
