Beginning next Monday, our community recycling collection schedule will be updated to improve service reliability and processing capacity. Curbside recycling bins will now be collected every other Tuesday instead of the current weekly schedule. Please place your bin at the curb by six in the morning on collection day, ensuring the lid closes securely and all materials are loosely packed inside. We continue to accept paper, cardboard, glass, metal, and clean rigid plastics. Flexible plastic bags and styrofoam are no longer permitted in curbside containers and must be delivered to the municipal drop off center at City Hall. For additional guidance or specific inquiries, please reach out to the Public Works department at five five five zero one two three four. We appreciate your prompt attention to these adjustments and thank you for supporting a cleaner environment.
