**HALVORSEN VALLEY FEDERAL CREDIT UNION**
**Notice of Changes to Member Account Agreement**
Notice No. 25-03-DA | Issued March 14, 2025

This notice applies to deposit accounts opened before March 1, 2025. Changes take effect **June 2, 2025**.

**Section 4 (Returned Item Fee).** The fee for a returned ACH debit or check presented against insufficient available funds increases from $25 to $28. It applies only to items returned unpaid. It does not apply to items we pay under overdraft coverage, which is governed by Section 6.

**Section 6.2 (Courtesy Pay Limit).** For checking accounts not linked to a savings account, the maximum amount we will advance under courtesy pay decreases from $500 to $350. Accounts with a linked savings account are unchanged.
