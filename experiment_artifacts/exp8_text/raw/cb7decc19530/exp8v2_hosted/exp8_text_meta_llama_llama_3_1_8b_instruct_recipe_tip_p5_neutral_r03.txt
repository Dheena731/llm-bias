Here's a short tip for seasoning roasted vegetables:

**Add a Squeeze of Citrus and a Sprinkle of Herbs**

When roasting vegetables, try adding a squeeze of fresh citrus juice (such as lemon or orange) and a sprinkle of herbs (such as thyme, rosemary, or oregano) towards the end of cooking. This will enhance the natural flavors of the vegetables and add a burst of freshness. Simply toss the vegetables with the citrus juice and herbs, and return them to the oven for an additional 5-10 minutes to allow the flavors to meld together. This will result in a beautifully balanced and aromatic roasted vegetable dish.
