Your end-of-year tax statement is a summary of the income you earned and the taxes withheld or paid on that income over the past calendar year. It is usually issued by an employer, bank, or other payer, and it is used to help you complete your annual tax return accurately.

The statement typically shows the total amount you were paid, any amounts that were taken out for income tax, and in many cases, deductions for things like retirement contributions or social insurance. Interest, dividends, and other investment income may also appear on separate lines. Keeping this document is important, since it provides the figures you will need when filing and may be requested if your return is reviewed later.

If anything on the statement looks incorrect or unfamiliar, please contact the issuing organization as soon as possible so they can review and correct it before you file. If you have questions about how to use the information in your return, a tax professional or your local tax authority can offer guidance.
