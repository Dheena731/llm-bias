Mark your calendars for our community gathering this Saturday at Riverside Park from nine in the morning until noon. This friendly neighborhood potluck will feature games, face painting for children, and a brief ceremony to honor our local volunteers. Attendees are asked to bring a dish to share and reusable plates to help keep the park clean. There is no registration required, and the event is completely free. We encourage everyone to come out and enjoy a morning of connection and celebration with their neighbors.
