NOTICE OF CHANGES TO ACCOUNT TERMS

Effective Date: This notice takes effect thirty (30) days after the date it is delivered to you.

To the Account Holder:

Pursuant to the terms of your Account Agreement, we are providing you with notice that certain provisions of that Agreement will be amended as described below. This notice supplements and, where inconsistent, supersedes the corresponding provisions of the existing Agreement as of the Effective Date.

First, the schedule of fees applicable to your account will be revised. The revised schedule will be made available on our website and upon written request. Fees incurred before the Effective Date will continue to be governed by the schedule in force at the time they were incurred.

Second, the section of the Agreement governing dispute resolution will be amended to require that any claim arising out of or relating to your account first be submitted to us in writing, so that we may have a period of sixty (60) days to attempt resolution before either party commences formal proceedings. Nothing in this amendment limits any right you may have under applicable law to file a complaint with a government agency or regulator.

Third, the provisions concerning electronic communications will be updated to provide that notices relating to your account may be delivered to the email address or mobile number most recently on file with us. You are responsible for keeping your contact information current. Notices sent to the contact information on file will be deemed received on the date of transmission.

Your Right to Decline: If you do not agree to these changes, you may close your account before the Effective Date by notifying us in writing, without penalty and without any fee for closure arising from this notice. Your continued use of the account after the Effective Date, or your failure to close it before that date, will constitute your acceptance of the amended terms.

Except as expressly modified by this notice, all other terms of your Account Agreement remain in full force and effect. If any provision of this notice is found to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions will continue in effect.

If you have questions regarding this notice, please contact our Customer Service Department using the contact details on your account statement. Please retain this notice with your account records.

Issued by the Account Services Department.
