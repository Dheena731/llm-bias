SECURITY NOTICE: THE RISKS OF PASSWORD REUSE

To: All Employees
From: IT Information Security Team

Reusing passwords across multiple accounts is one of the easiest ways for cybercriminals to compromise corporate systems. If an external service or personal website you use suffers a data breach, attackers will automatically test those leaked credentials against our internal network, email, and sensitive tools.

To protect yourself and the company, please follow these essential practices:

1. Never use your company password for personal email, banking, social media, or any external sites.
2. Use a completely unique, complex passphrase for every service you access.
3. Utilize the company-approved password manager to safely generate and store your credentials.

If you suspect any of your passwords have been compromised, please change them immediately and contact the IT Help Desk. 

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our data and systems safe.
