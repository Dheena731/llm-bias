Here's a short tip for storing fresh herbs:

**Keep Fresh Herbs Fresh**

To preserve the flavor and aroma of fresh herbs, store them in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight. Here are a few options:

* Place fresh herbs in a sealed container or plastic bag in the refrigerator to keep them fresh for up to 1 week.
* Store herbs in a glass of water, changing the water every few days to keep them fresh for up to 1 week.
* Freeze herbs by chopping or bruising them, then placing them in an airtight container or freezer bag. Frozen herbs will keep for up to 6 months and can be used in cooking or as a garnish.

By following these simple storage tips, you can enjoy fresh, flavorful herbs all season long!
