Subject: Your End-of-Year Tax Statement

Hello,

Your end-of-year tax statement is now available. This document summarizes your financial activity for the past calendar year and is designed to help you prepare your tax return.

What it includes:

- Income: Total wages, interest, dividends, or other earnings reported to the tax authorities under your name.
- Taxes withheld: The amount of tax already paid on your behalf during the year, such as payroll withholding.
- Deductions and credits: Any eligible amounts that may reduce your taxable income or the tax you owe.
- Account activity: For investment or savings accounts, a summary of gains, losses, and interest earned.

How to use it:

1. Review each section carefully and compare the figures with your own records.
2. Keep the statement with your other tax documents, since you may need it if questions arise later.
3. Use the figures when completing your tax return, or give the statement to your tax preparer.

If you notice an error, such as a wrong amount or incorrect personal details, contact the issuing organization as soon as possible so a corrected statement can be issued before you file.

Please note that this statement is informational and does not replace professional tax advice. If your situation is complex, consider consulting a qualified tax professional.
