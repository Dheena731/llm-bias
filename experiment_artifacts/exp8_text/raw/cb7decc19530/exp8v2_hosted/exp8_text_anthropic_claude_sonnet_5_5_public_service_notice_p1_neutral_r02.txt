**PUBLIC NOTICE**

**Library Opening Hours**

Please be advised of the library's regular hours of operation:

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday – Thursday | 9:00 a.m. – 8:00 p.m. |
| Friday | 9:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m. |
| Saturday | 10:00 a.m. – 4:00 p.m. |
| Sunday | Closed |

**Holiday Closures:** The library will be closed on all public holidays. Hours may also be adjusted on holiday eves.

**Good to Know:**
- The book return slot is available 24 hours a day.
- Computer and Wi-Fi access end 15 minutes before closing.
- Please begin checking out materials 10 minutes before closing time.

For questions or updates to this schedule, please contact the front desk at the library or visit the library's website.

*Thank you for supporting your local library.*
