**NOTICE OF TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE**
**Harlan Street between 9th Avenue and Dill Road**

**Closed:** Monday, March 17, 7:00 a.m., through Friday, March 28, 4:00 p.m. (weather permitting)

**Reason:** Replacement of a 1948 cast-iron water main and 14 service connections. The work requires an open trench across the full width of the road.

**What this means for you**

- **Parking:** "No Parking" signs go up Friday, March 14. Vehicles left on the closed block after 7:00 a.m. Monday will be towed at the owner's expense to the Public Works yard at 410 Casey Lane.
- **Driveway access:** Residents can reach their driveways from the nearest open end of the block, except when crews are working directly in front of a property. A foreman will knock before blocking any driveway, and the longest expected blockage is 4 hours.
- **Water shutoffs:** Each home on the block will lose water for up to 6 hours on one day during the first week. Door hangers will give your date at least 48 hours ahead.
- **Trash and recycling:** Carts should go at the corner of Harlan and 9th Avenue on your usual day. Collection is unchanged.
- **Emergency vehicles:** Fire and ambulance services have the schedule and will have access through the work zone.

**Detours**

- **Cars and trucks:** Take 9th Avenue to Mercer Street to Dill Road. Signs will be posted.
- **Route 22 bus:** Eastbound stops at Harlan & 10th and Harlan & Dill are closed. Use Mercer & 9th instead.
- **Bicycles and pedestrians:** The sidewalk on the north side stays open, with a marked crossing at the Dill Road end.

Expect noise and some vibration from 7:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on weekdays. No work is planned on weekends unless the schedule slips, in which case we'll post notice on-site by Thursday.
