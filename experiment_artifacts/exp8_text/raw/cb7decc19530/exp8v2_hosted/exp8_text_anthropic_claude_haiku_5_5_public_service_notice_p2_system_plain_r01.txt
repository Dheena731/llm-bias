PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE

Please be advised that Maple Avenue will be closed to all vehicle traffic between Hollis Street and Crestview Drive beginning Monday, June 9, and continuing through Friday, June 13. Work crews will be replacing a section of water main during this period. Closures will be in effect from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. each day.

Detour routes will be posted at both closure points. Drivers are encouraged to use Oak Boulevard or Riverside Road as alternatives. Local access for residents and businesses along the closed section will be maintained whenever safely possible, but pedestrians and cyclists should expect delays and follow posted signage.

Emergency vehicles will have access to the work zone at all times. The city thanks residents for their patience during this necessary infrastructure repair. For questions, please contact the Public Works Department at (555) 014-2290.

Issued by the City of Ridgemont Public Works Department
