We're excited to announce a new savings goal feature, designed to make it easier to plan for what matters most to you. Whether you're building an emergency fund, saving for a down payment, or setting aside money for a trip, you can now create a dedicated goal, set a target amount and a deadline, and track your progress in one place.

Each goal shows how much you've saved, how much remains, and the pace you need to stay on schedule. If you fall behind, the feature will suggest a revised monthly contribution so you can adjust without starting over. You can also move funds between goals at any time, and every transfer is recorded in your activity history so you always know where your money is going.

To get started, open the Savings tab and select New Goal. Give it a name, choose a target, and pick a date. You can link the goal to an existing savings account or let us automatically set aside a fixed amount from each paycheck. You can pause, edit, or close a goal whenever your plans change.

This feature is rolling out to all users over the next two weeks. If you don't see it yet, please check back shortly. We welcome your feedback as you explore it, and we'll keep refining the experience based on what we hear from you.
