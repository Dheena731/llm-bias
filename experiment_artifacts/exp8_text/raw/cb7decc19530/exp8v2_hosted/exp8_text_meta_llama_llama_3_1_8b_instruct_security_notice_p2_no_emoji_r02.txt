**Important Notice to All Employees**

As part of our ongoing efforts to protect our organization's sensitive information, we would like to remind everyone to be cautious when receiving emails that request personal or financial information.

Phishing emails are becoming increasingly common and can be sophisticated in their appearance and content. These emails may appear to be from a trusted source, such as our IT department or a financial institution, and may request that you provide sensitive information, such as passwords, credit card numbers, or other confidential data.

**Important:** Do not respond to or provide any information to emails that:

* Request sensitive information
* Ask you to click on links or download attachments from unknown senders
* Appear to be from an unfamiliar source, even if the email appears to be from a trusted source

If you are unsure about the authenticity of an email, do not hesitate to contact our IT department directly. They will be able to verify the email and advise on the best course of action.

Let's work together to keep our organization's data safe and secure.

**If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our IT department.**
