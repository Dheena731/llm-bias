Latency, defined as the time delay between a user's interaction and the application's response, is a critical factor in web application performance. It matters primarily because it directly influences user experience and business outcomes.

High latency leads to user frustration, increased bounce rates, and reduced engagement, as users expect near-instant feedback. From a commercial standpoint, slower performance correlates with lower conversion rates and lost revenue. Additionally, search engines prioritize fast-loading sites, meaning high latency can negatively impact organic visibility. On a technical level, excessive latency can cause timeouts, degrade system stability, and waste server resources. Minimizing latency ensures a responsive interface, better retention, and optimal operational efficiency.
