**NOTICE OF ACCEPTABLE USE: SHARED NETWORK**

**1. Who This Notice Applies To**
This notice applies to every person who connects a device to, or uses, the shared network (each, a "User"). The "network" means the shared wired and wireless connections, the equipment that supports them, and any internet access provided through them.

**2. Purpose**
The network is provided for shared use. Each User must use it lawfully and in a way that does not disrupt the network or unfairly limit its availability to others.

**3. Permitted Use**
Users may use the network for lawful purposes, including communication, research, study, and work-related tasks.

**4. Prohibited Use**
Users must not use the network to:

(a) break any applicable law;
(b) access, or attempt to access, any system, device, or data without authorization;
(c) distribute malicious software or otherwise intentionally interfere with the network or any other device connected to it;
(d) infringe copyright or any other intellectual property right;
(e) harass, threaten, or defame any person, or send unsolicited bulk messages;
(f) share login credentials with others or allow others to use a User's access in violation of this notice; or
(g) consume bandwidth at a level that degrades service for other Users.

**5. Security Responsibilities**
Each User is responsible for all activity carried out through the User's credentials and devices. Users must take reasonable steps to keep their devices protected against malware.

**6. Monitoring**
Network activity may be logged and monitored to protect security, maintain performance, and enforce this notice, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

**7. Enforcement**
A violation of this notice may result in the suspension or removal of the User's access to the network. Suspected unlawful activity may be reported to the appropriate authorities.
