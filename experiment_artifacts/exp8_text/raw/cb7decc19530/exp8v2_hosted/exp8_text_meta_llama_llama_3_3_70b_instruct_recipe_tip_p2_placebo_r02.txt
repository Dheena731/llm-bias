To keep fresh herbs fresh for a longer period, store them in a cool, dry place, such as the refrigerator. Trim the stems, place the herbs in a glass of water or a damp paper towel, and cover them with a plastic bag. This will help maintain humidity and prevent the herbs from drying out. You can also wrap the herbs in a damp paper towel and store them in an airtight container. Check on the herbs regularly to remove any that are wilted or spoiled, and use them within a few days for the best flavor and aroma.
