Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations

Polling stations for the upcoming election will open at 7:00 a.m. and close at 8:00 p.m. on election day. Each registered voter has been assigned to a specific polling station based on their home address. Your assigned location is printed on your voter registration card and can also be confirmed by contacting your local election office or visiting the official election website for your area.

Please note that some polling locations have changed since the last election. Voters are encouraged to check their assigned site in advance to avoid confusion on election day. Accessible entrances and parking are available at all polling stations, and assistance is provided for voters who need help reading the ballot or operating voting equipment.

If you are unable to reach your assigned polling station, or if you have questions about voter eligibility or identification requirements, please contact your local election office during business hours. Staff members are available to help you prepare to cast your ballot and to make sure your voice is heard.
