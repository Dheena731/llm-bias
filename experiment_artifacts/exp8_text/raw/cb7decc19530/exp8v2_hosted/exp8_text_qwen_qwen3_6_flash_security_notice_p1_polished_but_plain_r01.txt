Please remember that reusing passwords across multiple systems creates unnecessary risk for our entire organization. When a single account is compromised through a data breach or phishing attempt, attackers can immediately access every other platform where that same combination has been entered. This practice significantly increases the chance of unauthorized data exposure and disrupts routine business operations.

To protect company information, please use a distinct password for every work application, email account, and network resource. Our IT department recommends utilizing the approved enterprise password manager to generate and store complex credentials securely. If you must record temporary access codes, keep them in a locked personal drawer away from your workstation and destroy them after use.

We recognize that managing many different credentials requires attention, but this straightforward habit greatly strengthens our collective security posture. Should you experience lockouts, suspect unauthorized access, or need assistance with credential management, contact the help desk promptly. Thank you for your diligence in keeping our digital environment safe.
