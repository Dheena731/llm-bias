Here is a short admin notice about quarterly access reviews:

**Quarterly Access Reviews**

As part of our ongoing effort to ensure the security and integrity of our systems, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews. These reviews will take place on a regular basis, every 3 months, to verify that users still require access to our systems and resources.

**What to Expect:**

* You will receive an email notification approximately 2 weeks prior to your review, asking you to confirm your access needs.
* If your access is approved, no further action is required.
* If your access is not approved, you will be required to update your account or remove your access.

**Why We're Doing This:**

* To ensure that only authorized personnel have access to our systems.
* To prevent unnecessary access and potential security risks.
* To maintain accurate and up-to-date records of user access.

If you have any questions or concerns about these reviews, please don't hesitate to reach out to our IT department.
