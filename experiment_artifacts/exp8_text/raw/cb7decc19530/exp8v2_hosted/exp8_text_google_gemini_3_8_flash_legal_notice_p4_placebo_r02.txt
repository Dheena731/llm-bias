**NOTICE CONCERNING REFUND ELIGIBILITY**

To qualify for a refund, you must submit your request within thirty calendar days of the original purchase date. 

Eligibility is contingent upon the following conditions:

1. **Proof of Purchase:** You must present a valid receipt, invoice, or order confirmation documenting the transaction.
2. **Item Condition:** Physical goods must be returned unused, undamaged, and in their original packaging, containing all accompanying parts, manuals, and accessories.
3. **Exclusions:** Items designated as final sale, perishable goods, customized or personalized products, and items damaged through misuse or unauthorized modification are strictly ineligible for a refund.

Approved refunds will be credited exclusively to the original payment method used to complete the purchase. Processing times may vary depending on your financial institution. Shipping, handling, and delivery fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to an error on the merchant's part.
