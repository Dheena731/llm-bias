**Subject: Your end-of-year tax statement**

Hello,

Your end-of-year tax statement summarizes the income you received during the tax year and any tax already paid on it for you, such as tax taken out of your pay and sent to the tax authority. It is a record, not a bill.

**What to do with it:**
1. **Check it.** Make sure your name and identifying details are correct, and that the income and tax amounts match your own records.
2. **Keep it.** You will use these figures when you file your tax return, or to confirm whether you need to file, depending on the rules where you live.
3. **Report problems.** If something looks wrong, contact the organization that sent the statement and ask for a corrected copy before you file.

This statement alone doesn't show whether you will owe more tax or receive a refund. That depends on your full tax situation. If you are unsure, your tax authority or a qualified tax adviser can help.
