Subject: Important Update Regarding New Expense Approval Workflow

Hi Team,

Please be advised that we are rolling out a revised expense approval workflow effective November 1st. This update aims to improve processing times and enhance tracking accuracy across all departments.

Under the new system, all expense reports must be submitted through the central portal prior to incurring costs, except for emergency purchases approved verbally by your manager. Additionally, digital receipts are now required for every transaction regardless of amount.

Detailed instructions and FAQs are available on the intranet under Finance Resources. If you encounter issues or require training, please contact the Finance Helpdesk at ext. 5500.

We appreciate your attention to these changes.
