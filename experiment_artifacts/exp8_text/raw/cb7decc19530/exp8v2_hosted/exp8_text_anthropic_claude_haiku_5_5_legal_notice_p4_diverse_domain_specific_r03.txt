**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY**
Brackett & Sons Piano Service, LLC

Effective March 1, 2025

**1. Scope.** This notice covers tuning, regulation, and repair services booked through our office or website. Replacement parts sold separately are governed by the manufacturer's return policy, not this notice.

**2. Full refund.** You are entitled to a full refund of the service fee if you cancel at least 48 hours before your scheduled appointment and our technician has not yet begun work. Work begins when the lid is opened or any action or hammer is removed from the case.

**3. Partial refund.** If work has begun, you may request a refund of the labor not yet performed, minus a $35 trip charge. Parts already installed are not refundable.

**4. Non-refundable cases.** No refund is owed where:
(a) the instrument was moved, altered, or repaired by another party after our visit and before your request;
(b) the cancellation was made less than 48 hours before the appointment, in which case a $40 late-cancellation fee applies; or
(c) the technician was denied access to the instrument on the scheduled date.

**5. Instruments found unsafe to service.** If a technician determines that an instrument cannot safely be tuned because of a cracked soundboard, a loose pin block, or comparable damage, you may cancel and receive a refund of the diagnostic fee only. The technician's written findings will be provided with the refund.

**6. Submitting a request.** Send requests in writing to billing@brackettpiano.example or by mail to the address above. Include your invoice number, the service address, and the reason for the request. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 10 business days. Card refunds may take one or two additional billing cycles to post.
