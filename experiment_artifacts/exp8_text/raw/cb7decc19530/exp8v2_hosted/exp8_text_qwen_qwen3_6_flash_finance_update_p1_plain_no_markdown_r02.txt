We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to provide you with a clearer view of your financial habits. This feature generates a comprehensive report at the end of each billing cycle, detailing your total expenditures and categorizing your purchases for easier analysis. By reviewing this data, you can track your budget more effectively and spot trends in your spending patterns. The summary will appear in your account settings and will also be emailed to you automatically. We encourage you to check the report as soon as it becomes available so you can start using these insights to manage your funds. If you need help accessing the summary or have questions about the breakdown, please contact our support team for assistance.
