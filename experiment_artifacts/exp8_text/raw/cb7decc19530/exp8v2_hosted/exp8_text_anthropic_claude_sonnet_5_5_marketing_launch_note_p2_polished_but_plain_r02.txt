Starting this month, great coffee can arrive at your door without you ever having to remember to reorder it. We're pleased to announce the Daily Ground Coffee Subscription, a simple way to keep your kitchen stocked with freshly roasted beans, delivered on a schedule that suits your routine.

Each shipment is roasted to order and sent within days of leaving the roaster, so what you brew at home tastes the way it should. You choose the roast, from a bright and fruity light roast to a deep, chocolatey dark, and you decide whether you want whole beans or a grind matched to your brewer. If you're not sure where to begin, a short taste profile quiz will point you toward a good first bag, and you can change your selection whenever your preferences shift.

Flexibility is built into the plan. Deliveries can arrive weekly, every two weeks, or monthly, and you can skip a shipment, pause, or cancel at any time without fees or phone calls. Subscribers also receive a small discount on every bag, free shipping on all orders, and first access to limited releases from the small farms and cooperatives we work with.

To welcome new members, your first bag is half price when you sign up before the end of the month. Visit our website, choose your roast and delivery rhythm, and your first shipment will be on its way within a day or two. We think a good morning cup should be the easiest part of your day, and this subscription is our way of making that true.
