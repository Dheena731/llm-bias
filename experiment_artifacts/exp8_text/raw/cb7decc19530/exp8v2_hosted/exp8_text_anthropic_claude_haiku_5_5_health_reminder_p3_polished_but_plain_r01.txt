This is a friendly reminder that it is time to schedule your annual health checkup. Regular checkups give your care team the chance to catch potential concerns early, review your current medications, update your vaccinations, and discuss any changes in your health since your last visit. Many common conditions are easier to manage when they are found early, so keeping this appointment is one of the most useful steps you can take for your long-term well-being.

To book your visit, please call our office at your convenience, or use the online scheduling portal on our website. If you have questions about what to expect or need to arrange a visit at a time that suits your schedule, our staff will be glad to help. If you have already scheduled your appointment, please disregard this message.

We look forward to seeing you soon.
