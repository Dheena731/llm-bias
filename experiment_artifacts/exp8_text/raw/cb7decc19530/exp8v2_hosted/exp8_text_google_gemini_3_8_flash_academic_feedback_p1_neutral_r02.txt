**Dear [Student Name],**

Thank you for submitting this draft of your literature review. You have done a commendable job gathering a wide range of relevant and current sources, and your understanding of the foundational concepts in this area is evident.

To elevate this to the next level, I encourage you to focus on **synthesis rather than summary**. In its current form, several sections read like an annotated bibliography, moving sequentially from one study to the next (e.g., "Smith argues... Jones found..."). 

For your next revision, try to:
* **Organize thematically:** Group your paragraphs around key debates, methodologies, or findings rather than individual authors. Let the themes drive the headings.
* **Highlight tensions:** Explicitly point out where your sources agree, conflict, or leave unanswered questions.
* **Strengthen your own voice:** Use the literature to actively build the rationale for your own research. Ensure every section explicitly connects back to your core research question and helps define the "gap" your study addresses.

You have a very strong foundation here. With a more critical and synthesized structure, this will be a compelling review.
