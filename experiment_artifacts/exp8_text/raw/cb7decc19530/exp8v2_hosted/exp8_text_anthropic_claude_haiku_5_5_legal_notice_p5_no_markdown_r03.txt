ACCEPTABLE USE NOTICE FOR SHARED NETWORK RESOURCES

1. Purpose and Scope

This Notice sets out the terms under which users may access and use the shared network, including all associated hardware, software, accounts, internet access, and stored data (collectively, the "Network"). By accessing the Network, you agree to comply with this Notice.

2. Authorized Use

The Network is provided for lawful, business-related, educational, or other purposes expressly authorized by the Network administrator. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with the Network's operation, consume excessive resources, or violate any provision of this Notice.

3. Prohibited Conduct

Users must not, without express written authorization:

(a) access, attempt to access, or interfere with accounts, files, or data belonging to another user;

(b) introduce malware, disable security controls, or circumvent authentication or access restrictions;

(c) transmit unlawful, harassing, defamatory, obscene, or discriminatory material;

(d) infringe copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property rights, including through unauthorized copying or distribution of software or media;

(e) conduct unauthorized scanning, monitoring, or penetration testing of the Network;

(f) use the Network for commercial purposes unrelated to authorized activities, or for cryptocurrency mining or other resource-intensive activities not approved in advance; or

(g) share login credentials or permit others to use your account.

4. Account Responsibility

Users are responsible for all activity conducted under their accounts. Passwords must be kept confidential and changed when required. Users must promptly report any suspected unauthorized access or security incident to the Network administrator.

5. Privacy and Monitoring

Users acknowledge that the Network is shared and that data transmitted or stored on it may be logged, inspected, or monitored by administrators to ensure security, maintain performance, and enforce this Notice, subject to applicable law. Users should have no expectation of privacy in content stored on or transmitted across the Network, except as otherwise required by law.

6. Reporting Violations

Any person who becomes aware of a violation of this Notice should report it promptly to the Network administrator. Reports will be handled confidentially to the extent practicable.

7. Enforcement

Violations of this Notice may result in suspension or termination of access, disciplinary action in accordance with applicable policies or employment or enrollment agreements, and referral to law enforcement or pursuit of legal remedies where appropriate. The administrator reserves the right to disable any account or restrict access where necessary to protect the Network.

8. Amendments

The administrator may amend this Notice at any time. Continued use of the Network following notice of an amendment constitutes acceptance of the revised Notice.

9. Acknowledgment

By logging in to or otherwise using the Network, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to comply with this Acceptable Use Notice.
