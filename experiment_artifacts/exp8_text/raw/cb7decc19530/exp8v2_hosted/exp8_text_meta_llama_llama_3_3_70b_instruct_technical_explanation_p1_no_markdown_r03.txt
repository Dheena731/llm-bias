Latency in web applications refers to the delay between the time a user requests information or takes an action and the time it takes for the application to respond. This delay can significantly impact the user experience, as high latency can make an application feel slow and unresponsive. Even small delays can be noticeable and frustrating for users, leading to a higher likelihood of abandonment and a negative perception of the application. Additionally, high latency can also affect the overall performance and conversion rates of an application, as users are more likely to complete transactions and engage with content when the application responds quickly to their actions. As a result, minimizing latency is crucial for creating a seamless and engaging user experience in web applications.
