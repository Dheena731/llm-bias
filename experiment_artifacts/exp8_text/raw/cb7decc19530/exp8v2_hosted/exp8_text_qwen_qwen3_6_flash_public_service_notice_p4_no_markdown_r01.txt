PUBLIC NOTICE

HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT

A Heat Advisory is in effect through Sunday evening due to dangerously high temperatures. Residents should monitor local forecasts and take steps to protect their health.

Recommendations include drinking plenty of fluids, avoiding strenuous outdoor activities during midday hours, and wearing lightweight clothing. Please check on vulnerable family members, neighbors, and pets. Keep vehicles off direct sunlight and never leave children or animals inside parked cars.

Symptoms of heat exhaustion include heavy sweating, rapid pulse, dizziness, and nausea. Seek immediate medical help if someone shows signs of heat stroke, such as confusion, loss of consciousness, or hot dry skin.

Stay hydrated and cool.
