To add an extra layer of security to your account, you can enable two-factor authentication. This requires both a password and a second form of verification, such as a code sent to your phone or a fingerprint scan, to access your account. To enable this feature, follow the instructions provided by the website or service you are using. You may need to download and install an authentication app or provide a phone number to receive verification codes. Once enabled, you will be prompted to enter a verification code in addition to your password to log in. This additional step can help prevent unauthorized access to your account even if someone knows your password.
