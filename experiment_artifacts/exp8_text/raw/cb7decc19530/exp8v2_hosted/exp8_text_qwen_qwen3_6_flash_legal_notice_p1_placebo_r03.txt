NOTICE OF UPDATE TO ACCOUNT TERMS

To Our Account Holders:

This notice formally informs you that the Account Terms governing your service access will be revised. The updated terms take effect thirty days after this notice is issued. The primary changes clarify data storage practices, adjust monthly billing cycles, and replace the previous litigation clause with a mandatory mediation process for unresolved disputes. 

You may review the complete revised terms at any time by logging into your account and selecting “Agreement Details” from the settings menu. Your continued use of the service after the effective date constitutes your full acceptance of the updated terms. If you do not agree to these revisions, you must terminate your account before the effective date. Termination instructions are available in the same settings menu.

Any questions regarding this update should be directed to our customer support department. This notice fulfills our obligation to disclose significant modifications to the governing agreement before they become enforceable.
