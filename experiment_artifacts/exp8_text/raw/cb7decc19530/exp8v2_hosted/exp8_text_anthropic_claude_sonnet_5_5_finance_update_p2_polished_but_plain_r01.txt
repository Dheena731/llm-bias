Subject: Update to our card transaction fees

Dear customers,

We are writing to let you know about a change to the fees we charge on card transactions, which will take effect on the first day of next month.

From that date, the processing fee on card payments will be 2.4 percent of the transaction value plus a fixed charge of 20 cents. Until now, the rate has been 2.1 percent plus 20 cents. The fixed portion is unchanged, and there is no change to fees for bank transfers or cash payments.

This adjustment reflects higher charges from the card networks and the banks that process payments on our behalf. We have absorbed these increases for as long as we reasonably could, but we can no longer do so without affecting the quality of service you rely on. We have not added any new fees, and we have not changed the way refunds or chargebacks are handled.

For most customers, the practical effect will be small. On a payment of 100 dollars, for example, the fee will rise from 2.30 dollars to 2.60 dollars, an increase of 30 cents. Businesses that process a high volume of small payments may notice the change more, and you may wish to review your pricing or your preferred payment methods accordingly.

Transactions completed before the effective date will be charged at the existing rate, even if they settle afterward. Your monthly statement will show the new rate clearly, so you can confirm the amounts applied.

If you have questions about how this affects your account, or would like to talk through options for reducing your costs, our support team is happy to help. You can reach them by replying to this message or calling the number on your statement.

Thank you for your continued trust and custom.

Kind regards,
The Customer Accounts Team
