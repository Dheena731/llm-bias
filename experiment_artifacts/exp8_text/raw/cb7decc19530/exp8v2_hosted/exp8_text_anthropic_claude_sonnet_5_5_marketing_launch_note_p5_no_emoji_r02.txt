**Launching Today: *Say It Again***

We're excited to announce the launch of *Say It Again*, a new podcast for anyone learning a language, whether you're just starting out or trying to break through an intermediate plateau.

Each 15-minute episode focuses on a single real-life situation, such as ordering at a café, making small talk with a neighbor, or asking for directions when you're lost. You'll hear natural conversations at a realistic pace, then we'll slow things down, break apart the key phrases, and give you space to repeat them out loud.

What you can expect:

- **Practical vocabulary** you can use the same day
- **Short, focused lessons** that fit into a commute or a lunch break
- **Guided repetition** so new phrases actually stick
- **Cultural notes** that explain not just what to say, but when and how to say it

Our first season covers Spanish, with French and Japanese to follow. Three episodes are available now, and new ones will arrive every Tuesday.

You can find *Say It Again* on all major podcast apps. Subscribe, leave a review if you enjoy it, and tell us which situations you'd like us to cover next.

Thanks for listening, and happy learning.
