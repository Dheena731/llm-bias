**Rinse away the starch:** Before cooking, place your dry rice in a fine-mesh strainer and rinse it under cold water until the water runs completely clear. 

This washes away excess surface starch—the main culprit behind gummy, clumping rice. 

*Bonus tip:* Once cooked, let the pot sit off the heat, covered, for 10 minutes, then gently fluff the rice with a fork rather than stirring with a spoon.
