The manuscript's central research question, as stated in the introduction, is whether community engagement improves health outcomes in rural areas. This question is too broad to be answered by the study design presented, and it leaves several key terms undefined. "Community engagement" could refer to participation in planning meetings, volunteer work, peer-led programs, or formal governance roles, and each of these would require a different measurement approach. Likewise, "health outcomes" is not specified: the authors do not say whether they mean clinical indicators, self-reported wellbeing, service utilization, or mortality. The reader is left unsure what would count as a positive finding. I would ask the authors to restate the question in terms of a specific form of engagement, a defined population, and one or two primary outcomes, and to explain why these were chosen. A sharper question would also make the hypothesis testable and would clarify whether the cross-sectional design can support any causal claim at all.
