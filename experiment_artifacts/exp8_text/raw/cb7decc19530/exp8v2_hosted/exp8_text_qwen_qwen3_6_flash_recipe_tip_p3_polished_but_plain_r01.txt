To achieve distinct, non-sticky grains, begin by rinsing your rice in a fine mesh strainer under cold running water until the liquid runs clear, which removes excess surface starch responsible for clumping. Measure your ingredients carefully, using a standard ratio of one part water to one and a half parts rice for most varieties, and ensure the water reaches a rolling boil before reducing the heat to the lowest setting and covering the pot tightly. Allow the rice to cook undisturbed until tender, then remove it from the heat and let it rest, still covered, for ten minutes to let the moisture equalize, finally fluffing the grains gently with a fork to separate them without mashing.
