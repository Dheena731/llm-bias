**Introducing Savings Goals**

You can now set a savings goal in the app. Decide what you're saving for, set a target amount, and watch your progress as you get closer to it.

Having a clear goal makes saving easier to stick with, and you can always see how much you've saved and how much is left.

To get started, create your first goal and enter the amount you want to reach.
