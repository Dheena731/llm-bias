**Public Service Notice: Library Operating Hours**

The public library is open to all community members during the following regular hours:

* **Monday through Thursday:** 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM  
* **Friday and Saturday:** 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM  
* **Sunday:** 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM  

Visitors are welcome to browse the shelves, use public computers, and access study areas during these times. An exterior return drop is available outside the main entrance for returning borrowed materials twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week.
