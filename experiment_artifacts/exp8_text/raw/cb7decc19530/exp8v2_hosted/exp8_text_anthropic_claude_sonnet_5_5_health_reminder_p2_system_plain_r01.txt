A quick reminder to keep your water bottle close today. Try to sip steadily from morning to evening instead of waiting until you feel thirsty, since thirst often shows up after your body has already started running low. A glass when you wake up, one with each meal, and another whenever you take a break will add up quickly. If the day is hot or you are active, you will need a little more. Your energy, focus, and mood will thank you for it.
