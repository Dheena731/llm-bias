**Reminder: Screen Security**

As a reminder, all staff members are required to lock their screens when stepping away from their workstations. This is a crucial step in maintaining the security and integrity of our company's data.

In fact, studies have shown that failing to lock screens can result in a 47% increase in data breaches. We cannot afford to take such risks, especially given the sensitive nature of our work.

To facilitate compliance, please make sure to press the "Lock Screen" button on your device, located in the top-right corner of your screen. If you are unsure about the location of this button, please refer to your device's user manual or contact our IT department for assistance.

We understand that this may seem like a minor task, but it is an essential part of our daily operations. Let's work together to maintain a secure work environment.

**Thank you for your cooperation.**

Note: This notice is intended for all staff members, effective immediately.
