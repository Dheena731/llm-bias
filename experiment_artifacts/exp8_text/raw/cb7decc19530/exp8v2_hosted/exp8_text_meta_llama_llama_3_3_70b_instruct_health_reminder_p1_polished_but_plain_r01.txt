It's time to review your weekly fitness progress. Take a few minutes to reflect on the exercises you've completed and the goals you've achieved. Consider your workouts, including the distance you've run, the weight you've lifted, and the number of classes you've attended. Also, think about your nutrition and hydration, and how they may be impacting your performance. By tracking your progress, you can identify areas where you're doing well and areas where you need to improve, helping you to stay motivated and focused on your fitness goals. Reviewing your progress regularly will also allow you to make adjustments to your routine as needed, ensuring you're on track to meet your objectives.
