Here's a short reminder for users to review their weekly fitness progress:

**Weekly Fitness Progress Check-in**

Take a moment to reflect on your progress this week! Regularly reviewing your fitness journey can help you stay motivated and focused on your goals. 

To get started, ask yourself:

* Have I met my weekly step count target?
* Am I consuming the recommended daily intake of fruits and vegetables?
* Have I scheduled any physical activity or workout sessions for the upcoming week?

Take a few minutes to review your progress and make any necessary adjustments for the week ahead.
