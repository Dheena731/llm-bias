**Introducing BudgetBase: The Budgeting Template That Does the Math for You**

We're excited to announce the launch of BudgetBase, a free, ready-to-use budgeting spreadsheet designed to help you take control of your money without the hassle of building a system from scratch.

**What's inside:**
- **Monthly budget planner:** Track income and expenses in one clear view, with categories already set up for housing, groceries, transportation, and more.
- **Automatic summaries:** See your remaining balance, spending totals, and savings progress update as you enter transactions.
- **Savings goal tracker:** Set a target, log your contributions, and watch your progress build over time.
- **Debt payoff calculator:** Enter your balances and interest rates to see how quickly you can become debt-free.
- **Visual dashboards:** Charts that show where your money goes each month at a glance.

**Works with the tools you already use.** BudgetBase is compatible with Excel, Google Sheets, and Apple Numbers, so there's nothing new to install.

**Easy to customize.** Rename categories, adjust formulas, or add new sheets. No spreadsheet expertise required.
