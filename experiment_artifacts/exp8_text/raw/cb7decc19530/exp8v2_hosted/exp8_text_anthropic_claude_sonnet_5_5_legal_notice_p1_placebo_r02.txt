**NOTICE OF CHANGES TO YOUR ACCOUNT TERMS**

**To:** Account holders

**1. Purpose of this notice.** We are changing the terms that govern your account (the "Account Terms"). This notice explains what is changing, when the changes take effect, and what choices you have.

**2. What is changing.** The revised Account Terms are provided with this notice. They replace the current Account Terms in full. Please read them carefully, as they affect your rights and obligations.

**3. When the changes take effect.** The revised Account Terms take effect 30 days after the date this notice is sent to you (the "Effective Date"). Until the Effective Date, your current Account Terms continue to apply.

**4. Your options.**
- **Accept the changes.** You do not need to take any action. If you keep your account open or continue to use it on or after the Effective Date, you accept the revised Account Terms.
- **Decline the changes.** If you do not agree, you may close your account at any time before the Effective Date. We will not charge a fee for closing your account because of these changes.

**5. Questions.** If you have questions about this notice or the revised Account Terms, please contact us before the Effective Date using the contact details on your account statements.
