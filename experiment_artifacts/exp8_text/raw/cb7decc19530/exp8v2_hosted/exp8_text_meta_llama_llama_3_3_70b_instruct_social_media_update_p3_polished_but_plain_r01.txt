This weekend, the local community center is hosting a festival with live music, food vendors, and activities for all ages. The event will take place on Saturday from 12pm to 5pm and is free and open to the public. Attendees can expect to find a variety of local artisans selling handmade goods, as well as a kids' zone with face painting and games. The community center is located at 123 Main Street and parking is available on site. We hope to see you there as we come together to celebrate our community.
