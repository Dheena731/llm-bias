Start by rinsing your rice under cold running water until the runoff is clear to remove excess surface starch that leads to clumping. Measure your water carefully, generally using a one-to-one ratio for many varieties, and bring the liquid to a rolling boil before stirring in the grains. Reduce the heat to the lowest setting and cover the pot tightly to maintain steady steam, avoiding any lifting of the lid while cooking. After the water is absorbed, remove the pot from the heat and allow it to rest covered for ten minutes so the moisture redistributes evenly. Finish by transferring the rice to a wide vessel and gently fluffing it with a fork to separate the grains and prevent them from sticking together.
