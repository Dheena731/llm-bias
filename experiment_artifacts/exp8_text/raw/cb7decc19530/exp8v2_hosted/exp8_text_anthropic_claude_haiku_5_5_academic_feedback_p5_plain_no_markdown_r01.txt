This essay makes several claims that rely on outside research, statistics, and the arguments of other scholars, yet many of these claims appear without any supporting citation. For example, the discussion of declining public trust in local institutions and the assertion that policy reforms reduced processing times both read as established facts, but a reader has no way to check where this information comes from or how reliable it is. Adding in-text citations for these points, along with a complete reference list, would strengthen your credibility and allow readers to follow up on the sources that shaped your argument. Where you are drawing on your own analysis or observations, make that distinction clear so that your original contribution stands apart from the work of others. I would also recommend checking that each source you cite directly supports the specific sentence it is attached to, since a citation that only loosely relates to the claim can be as problematic as no citation at all.
