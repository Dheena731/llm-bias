**New Feature: Set and Track Your Savings Goals**

We have introduced a new Savings Goals feature to help you plan and save for what matters most. 

You can now create personalized goals directly inside your account. Whether you are building an emergency fund, planning a vacation, or saving for a major purchase, you can name your goal, set a target amount, and choose a target date. Your progress will update automatically as you add money toward each goal, making it easier to see how close you are to reaching your target.

Log in to your account today to set up your first savings goal.
