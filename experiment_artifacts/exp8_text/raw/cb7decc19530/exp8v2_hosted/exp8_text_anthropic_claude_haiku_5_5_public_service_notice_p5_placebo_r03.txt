**Notice to Voters: Finding Your Polling Station**

Before you vote, check which polling station you are assigned to. Locations can change between elections, so do not assume your station is the same as last time.

To find your polling station:

- Check the polling card sent to your address, if you received one.
- Contact your local election office. Its contact details are on your local government website.
- Ask staff at your local council office.

Please go only to the station assigned to your address.
