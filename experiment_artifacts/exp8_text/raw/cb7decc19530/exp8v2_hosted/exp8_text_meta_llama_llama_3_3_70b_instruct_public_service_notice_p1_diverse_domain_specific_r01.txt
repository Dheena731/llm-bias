"Attention book lovers and researchers! Our library's opening hours have been updated to better serve your needs. As of this month, our main branch will be open from 9am to 8pm, Monday through Thursday, and 9am to 5pm on Fridays and Saturdays. We're also excited to offer extended hours during exam periods, with the library staying open until 10pm on Sundays.

Please note that our children's section will be open from 10am to 6pm, Monday through Saturday, and will offer storytime sessions at 11am and 2pm on weekdays. Our archives and special collections will be available by appointment only, so be sure to call ahead to schedule your visit.

We're committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive space for everyone in our community. Whether you're a student, a researcher, or simply a bookworm, we invite you to come and explore our shelves, attend one of our author talks or workshops, and take advantage of our free Wi-Fi and quiet study areas.

For the most up-to-date information on our opening hours, events, and services, please visit our website or follow us on social media. We look forward to seeing you at the library!"
