Thank you for sending the draft of your literature review. It shows a solid command of the material, and the amount of reading behind it is evident. My comments below are meant to help you move from a competent summary of sources toward a more critical and focused synthesis.

The main strength of the draft is its breadth. You have identified the central studies in the field and describe them accurately and clearly. The opening section also frames the topic well and gives the reader a sense of why it matters.

The main weakness is that the review is organized source by source rather than by idea. Many paragraphs begin with an author's name and then report what that author found, which leaves the reader to work out how the studies relate to one another. I would encourage you to reorganize around themes, debates, or methodological approaches, and to use the sources as evidence within those threads. Doing so will let you show where scholars agree, where they disagree, and why. At the moment the review describes the literature more than it evaluates it. Questions such as whether the samples are adequate, whether the methods support the conclusions, and whether certain studies rest on shared assumptions would strengthen your argument considerably.

Related to this, the draft does not yet make clear what gap or problem your own project addresses. The final section gestures toward it, but the link between the literature and your research question should be explicit and should guide the structure of the whole review. Consider stating this aim near the beginning and returning to it as you move through each theme.

On a smaller level, watch for long quotations that could be paraphrased, and for places where a claim is supported by a single source when the literature offers more. Some transitions between sections are abrupt, and a sentence or two linking each section to the next would help. Please also check your citation formatting for consistency, as there are several small variations in the reference list.

Overall, this is a promising foundation. A thematic restructuring and a sharper critical voice would make it considerably stronger. I would be glad to look at a revised version, or to talk through the reorganization in a short meeting if that would be useful.
