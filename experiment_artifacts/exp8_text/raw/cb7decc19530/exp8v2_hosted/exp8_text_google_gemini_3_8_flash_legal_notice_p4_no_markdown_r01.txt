Digital goods, customized items, clearance merchandise, and services already rendered are strictly non-refundable. 

Approved refunds will be credited exclusively to the original payment method within fourteen (14) business days following receipt and inspection of the returned merchandise. Shipping and handling fees are non-refundable. The Company reserves the right, in its sole discretion, to reject any refund request that fails to satisfy the criteria set forth herein.
