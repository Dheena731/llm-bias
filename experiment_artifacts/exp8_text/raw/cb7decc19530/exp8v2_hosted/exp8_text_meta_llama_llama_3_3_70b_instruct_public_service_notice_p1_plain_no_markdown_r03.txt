The local library is open to the public from 9am to 5pm, Monday to Friday, and from 10am to 1pm on Saturdays. The library is closed on Sundays and public holidays. Please note that these hours may be subject to change, so it's a good idea to call ahead or check the library's website to confirm before visiting. The library offers a wide range of books, media, and other resources, and the staff are happy to help with any queries or research needs. We look forward to seeing you at the library.
