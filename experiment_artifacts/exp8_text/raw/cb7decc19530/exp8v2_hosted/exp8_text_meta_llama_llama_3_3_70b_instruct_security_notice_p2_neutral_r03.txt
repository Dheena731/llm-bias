**Important: Protect Yourself from Phishing Emails**

As a reminder, phishing emails are a common threat to our organization's security. These emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information, such as your login credentials or financial information.

To protect yourself and our organization, please be cautious when receiving emails that:

* Ask for sensitive information, such as passwords or credit card numbers
* Contain suspicious links or attachments
* Have spelling or grammar mistakes
* Create a sense of urgency or panic

If you receive an email that you suspect is a phishing attempt, do not:

* Click on any links or download attachments
* Respond to the email or provide any information
* Use the email to login to any accounts

Instead, please report the email to our IT department immediately. We will investigate and take necessary actions to protect our network and systems.

Remember, it's always better to err on the side of caution when it comes to suspicious emails. If in doubt, don't click!

Thank you for your vigilance and cooperation in keeping our workplace safe from cyber threats.
