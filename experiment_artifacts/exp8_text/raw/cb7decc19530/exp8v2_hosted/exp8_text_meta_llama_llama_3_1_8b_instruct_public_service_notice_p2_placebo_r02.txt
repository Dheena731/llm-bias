**Public Notice: Road Closure**

The County Council is pleased to inform the public that a section of Main Street will be closed to vehicular traffic from Friday, 17th March to Thursday, 23rd March, inclusive.

The road closure is necessary to facilitate essential maintenance works to the drainage system. During this period, the section of Main Street between Oak Road and Maple Avenue will be closed to all traffic, except for pedestrian access.

The works are part of the council's ongoing efforts to improve the infrastructure and ensure public safety. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate the public's cooperation.

Alternative routes will be clearly signposted, and motorists are advised to follow the diversion signs to avoid delays. We appreciate the public's understanding and patience during this period.

For further information, please contact the Council's Customer Service Centre on 01234 567890.
