**Notice of Changes to Recycling Collection**

Dear Residents,

Please be advised that updates to the residential recycling collection schedule and sorting guidelines are now in effect. 

Key details of these changes include:

* **Schedule Adjustments:** Pickup days have shifted for several routes. Please consult the official waste management portal to verify the updated collection day for your address.
* **Bin Placement:** Place your recycling bin at the curb before morning collection begins on your assigned day, ensuring clear access for collection vehicles.
* **Sorting Guidelines:** All accepted materials must be clean, dry, and placed loose directly into the bin. Items packed in plastic trash bags will no longer be collected.

Thank you for your cooperation and your continued commitment to community recycling efforts.
