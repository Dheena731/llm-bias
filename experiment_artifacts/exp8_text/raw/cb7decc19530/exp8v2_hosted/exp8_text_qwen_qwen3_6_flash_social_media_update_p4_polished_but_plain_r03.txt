One effective strategy to enhance productivity and reduce daily friction is to insert buffer time between your scheduled events. Rather than arranging meetings back to back, block out ten to fifteen minutes after each commitment to review notes, prepare for upcoming discussions, or simply reset your focus. This intentional pause prevents the cascade of delays that often disrupts momentum and allows you to approach every new task with presence rather than rushing. By treating these transition periods as non-negotiable parts of your agenda, you safeguard your mental energy and create a more sustainable rhythm throughout your workday.
