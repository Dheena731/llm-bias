A quick reminder to keep your water intake steady through the day. Many people only drink when they feel thirsty, but thirst often shows up after your body is already a little low on fluid. Try starting your morning with a full glass of water, then keep a bottle within reach at your desk, in your bag, or wherever you spend most of your time. Small sips every hour or so are easier to sustain than trying to catch up with large amounts in the evening.

If plain water feels dull, add a slice of lemon or cucumber, or alternate with herbal tea. Drink a little extra when you exercise, spend time in the heat, or have had a lot of caffeine. A simple check is the color of your urine: pale yellow usually means you are well hydrated, while darker shades suggest you need more. Good hydration supports your focus, energy, digestion, and mood, so take a moment right now to pour a glass and drink some.
