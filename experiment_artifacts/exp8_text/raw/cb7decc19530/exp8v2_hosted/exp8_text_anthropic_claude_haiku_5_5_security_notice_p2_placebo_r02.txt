**Notice: Be Alert to Phishing Emails**

Phishing emails are messages designed to trick you into giving away passwords, account details, or other sensitive information. They often look like they come from a colleague, a manager, a bank, or a service we use.

Before you click or reply, check the following:

- **Unexpected requests:** Be cautious of emails asking you to log in, confirm details, or act urgently.
- **Sender address:** Look closely at the full address, not just the display name. Misspellings and unusual domains are warning signs.
- **Links and attachments:** Do not open attachments or click links in messages you did not expect. If in doubt, go to the website directly instead.
- **Verification:** If a request seems unusual, confirm it by contacting the sender through a separate, known channel, such as a phone call or a new message.

**If you think you have received a phishing email, do not reply or click anything. Report it to IT or your manager right away. If you have already clicked a link or entered a password, report it immediately so the risk can be addressed.**

Thank you for helping keep our information secure.
