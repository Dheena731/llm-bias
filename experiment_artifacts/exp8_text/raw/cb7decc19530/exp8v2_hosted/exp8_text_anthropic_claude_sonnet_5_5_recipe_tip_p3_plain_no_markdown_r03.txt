To keep rice from turning sticky, rinse it in cold water before cooking. Place the rice in a bowl or fine mesh strainer, cover it with water, swirl it around with your hand, and drain. Repeat until the water runs mostly clear, usually three or four times. This washes away the loose surface starch that causes grains to clump together. Then use a little less water than you might expect, about one and a half cups of water for each cup of long grain rice, and avoid stirring while it cooks, since stirring releases more starch. Once the rice is done, let it rest covered for ten minutes off the heat, then fluff it gently with a fork so the steam can escape and the grains stay separate.
