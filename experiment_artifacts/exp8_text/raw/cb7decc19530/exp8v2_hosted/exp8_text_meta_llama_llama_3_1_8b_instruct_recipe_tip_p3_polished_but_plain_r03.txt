To prevent rice from becoming sticky, it's essential to use the right water ratio when cooking. Generally, using one and a half to two cups of water per one cup of rice is recommended. However, the ideal ratio may vary depending on the type of rice being cooked. For example, long-grain rice typically requires a higher water ratio, while jasmine or basmati rice prefers a lower ratio.

Another key factor in preventing stickiness is to ensure the rice is not overcooked. Overcooking can cause the rice grains to break down, leading to a sticky or mushy texture. To avoid this, it's best to cook the rice until it's just tender, then let it rest for a few minutes before serving.

Finally, using a non-stick pot or a heat diffuser can also help to reduce the likelihood of sticky rice. These types of cookware distribute heat evenly, preventing hotspots that can cause the rice to become sticky.
