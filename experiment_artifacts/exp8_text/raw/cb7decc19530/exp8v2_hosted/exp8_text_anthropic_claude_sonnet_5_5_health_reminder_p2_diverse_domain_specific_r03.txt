**Water check**

If you haven't had a glass since breakfast, this is your sign. Most people run about a liter behind by mid-afternoon without noticing.

- **Drink a glass now**, before the next email or meeting.
- **Pair it with something you already do.** One glass with each coffee, one when you sit down to lunch, one when you get home.
- **Check your pee.** Pale straw yellow means you're fine. Dark amber means you're behind.
- **Watch for the 3 p.m. slump.** That foggy, slightly headachy, snack-hunting feeling is often thirst, not tiredness.
- **Keep a bottle where you can see it.** Water in a cupboard doesn't get drunk.

A rough target is 2 to 3 liters across the day, more if you're sweating or it's hot. Finish about half by lunch and the rest takes care of itself.
