Your end-of-year tax statement is now available. It summarizes the income you received and the taxes paid or withheld during the past calendar year. Here is what to look for:

1. Total income: This shows all taxable earnings, such as wages, interest, dividends, or other payments reported to the tax authorities.

2. Taxes withheld or paid: This is the amount already sent to the government on your behalf, either through paycheck withholding or estimated payments.

3. Deductions and credits: If applicable, this section lists items that may reduce your taxable income or the tax you owe, such as retirement contributions, mortgage interest, or education credits.

4. Balance or refund: Comparing your total tax owed with the amount already paid shows whether you will receive a refund or need to make an additional payment.

Please review each section carefully and make sure your name, address, and identification number are correct. If you notice an error, contact us as soon as possible so it can be corrected before the filing deadline.

Keep this statement with your records, as you will need it when preparing your tax return. If you have questions or need help understanding any part of it, we are happy to assist.
