**Heat Advisory: Be Prepared for a Scorched Summer**

As we head into the peak summer months, the City of Phoenix is urging residents to take precautions against the dangers of extreme heat. With temperatures expected to soar above 115°F, the risk of heat-related illnesses and injuries is very real.

**Take Action Now**

To protect yourself and your loved ones, please follow these simple steps:

* Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water throughout the day. Aim for at least 8-10 glasses of water per day.
* Schedule outdoor activities during the cooler morning or evening hours.
* Check on vulnerable neighbors, such as the elderly or those with disabilities, to ensure they are staying cool and safe.
* Keep your vehicle parked in a shaded area and turn off the engine when not in use to prevent heat-related damage.

**Special Concerns**

* Pets: Keep your furry friends indoors during the hottest part of the day and provide plenty of water and shade.
* Children: Ensure they stay cool and hydrated by taking regular breaks in a shaded area and providing plenty of water.
* Workers: Take regular breaks in a shaded area and stay hydrated to avoid heat exhaustion.

**Stay Informed**

* Check the City of Phoenix's website and social media channels for updates on the heat advisory.
* Sign up for emergency alerts from the City of Phoenix to receive timely updates on heat-related emergencies.

**Let's Work Together**

By taking these simple precautions, we can all stay safe and healthy during the hot summer months. Let's look out for each other and stay cool!

City of Phoenix
