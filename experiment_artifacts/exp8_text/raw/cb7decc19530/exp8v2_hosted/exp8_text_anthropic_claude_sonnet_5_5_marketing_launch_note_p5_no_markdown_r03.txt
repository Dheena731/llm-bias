Subject: Our new podcast, Say It Like a Local, is live

Hello, language learners!

We're excited to announce that Say It Like a Local is now available wherever you listen to podcasts.

This show is for anyone who wants to speak a new language with more confidence and less textbook stiffness. Each week, we break down the phrases, slang, and cultural habits that real speakers use every day. Episodes run about fifteen minutes, which makes them easy to fit into a commute, a walk, or a coffee break.

Here's what you can expect:

Short, practical lessons built around real conversations, such as ordering food, making small talk, and asking for directions.

Native speakers who share the expressions they actually use, along with common mistakes to avoid.

Simple listening exercises at the end of every episode so you can practice out loud.

Our first three episodes are ready now. We start with Spanish greetings, Japanese café etiquette, and French phrases for making friends. New episodes will arrive every Tuesday.

If you enjoy the show, please subscribe, leave a rating, and share it with a friend who is learning a language. Your support helps more people find us, and we'd love to hear which languages you want us to cover next. Just reply to this message with your suggestions.

Thanks for joining us at the start of this journey. Press play, say something out loud, and let's get talking.
