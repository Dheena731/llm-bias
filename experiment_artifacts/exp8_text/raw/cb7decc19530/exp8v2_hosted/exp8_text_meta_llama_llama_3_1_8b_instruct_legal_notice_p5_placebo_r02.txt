**NOTICE TO USERS OF XYZ ENTERPRISE NETWORK**

This notice is intended to inform all users of the XYZ Enterprise Network (the "Network") of their responsibilities and obligations when using the Network.

**PURPOSE OF THE NOTICE**

The purpose of this notice is to outline the acceptable use of the Network and to inform users of their obligations to respect the rights of others and to comply with applicable laws and regulations.

**DEFINITIONS**

For the purposes of this notice, the following definitions apply:

* "Network" means the XYZ Enterprise Network, including all computers, servers, printers, and other devices connected to the Network.
* "User" means any individual or entity that accesses or uses the Network, including employees, contractors, consultants, and third-party service providers.
* "Acceptable use" means the use of the Network in a manner that does not violate any applicable laws or regulations, and that is consistent with the Network's intended purpose.

**ACCEPTABLE USE GUIDELINES**

Users are expected to use the Network in a responsible and lawful manner. Specifically, users are prohibited from:

* Using the Network to transmit or store any material that is obscene, defamatory, or in violation of any applicable laws or regulations.
* Engaging in any activity that could compromise the security or integrity of the Network, including hacking, cracking, or other forms of unauthorized access.
* Using the Network to engage in any activity that could interfere with or disrupt the operation of the Network, including flooding, spamming, or other forms of network abuse.
* Downloading, copying, or transmitting any material that is protected by copyright, trade secret, or other intellectual property laws without the express permission of the copyright owner.
* Using the Network to engage in any activity that could be considered a violation of company policy or applicable laws and regulations.

**CONSEQUENCES OF MISUSE**

 Failure to comply with the guidelines set forth in this notice may result in disciplinary action, including but not limited to, revocation of access to the Network, termination of employment or services, or other consequences as determined by XYZ Enterprises.

**ACKNOWLEDGMENT**

By using the Network, users acknowledge that they have read and understood this notice and agree to comply with the guidelines set forth herein.

**CHANGES TO THIS NOTICE**

XYZ Enterprises reserves the right to modify this notice at any time without notice to users. It is the user's responsibility to review this notice regularly to ensure compliance with the guidelines set forth herein.
