Season your vegetables in two stages for the best flavor. Before roasting, toss them with oil and a generous pinch of salt so the seasoning clings and the edges caramelize. Then, as soon as they come out of the oven, add a second light sprinkle of salt along with something bright, like a squeeze of lemon juice, a splash of vinegar, or a handful of chopped fresh herbs. Heat can dull delicate flavors and burn spices or fresh garlic, so saving the bright finishing touches for the end keeps the vegetables tasting vivid instead of flat.
