Dear Customer,

Your end-of-year tax statement summarizes the income and tax-related activity on your account for the past calendar year. Its purpose is to give you the figures you need to complete your tax return, so please keep it with your other tax records.

The statement lists the interest, dividends, or other income you received during the year, along with any tax that was withheld on your behalf. If you sold investments, it will also show the proceeds from those sales and, in many cases, the original cost, so you can work out any gain or loss. Fees or charges that may be deductible are usually noted separately.

When you review it, start by checking that your name, address, and tax identification number are correct. Then compare the totals with your own records. If something looks wrong or missing, contact us promptly, because we may need to issue a corrected statement before you file.

Please note that some figures may be finalized later than others, so a corrected version is occasionally sent in the weeks after the first one. If you receive one, use the most recent version when preparing your return.

We cannot give personal tax advice, so if you are unsure how to report any item, a qualified tax professional or your local tax authority can help. We are always glad to answer questions about what appears on the statement itself.
