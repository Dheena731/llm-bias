Today we are launching Tally, a simple app that helps small business owners keep track of invoices, expenses, and customer payments in one place. We built it after talking with dozens of shop owners, freelancers, and contractors who told us the same thing: they did not start a business to spend their evenings buried in spreadsheets.

Tally lets you create and send a professional invoice in under a minute, snap a photo of a receipt and have it sorted automatically, and see at a glance who has paid and who still owes you. Gentle reminders go out on your behalf, so you no longer have to feel awkward chasing a late payment. At the end of the month, your numbers are already organized and ready to share with your accountant.

There is no complicated setup and no accounting degree required. Most people are up and running in about ten minutes, and everything works on your phone as well as your computer, so you can handle the paperwork wherever your day takes you.

To celebrate the launch, every new account gets its first three months free. If you run a small business and want more time for the work you actually enjoy, we would love for you to try it. Download Tally today and see how much lighter your week can feel.
