**Subject: Security Reminder: Please Lock Your Screen**

All staff are reminded to lock their computer screens whenever stepping away from their desks, even if only for a brief moment. Leaving workstations unlocked exposes sensitive company and client data to unauthorized access.

Quick shortcuts to lock your screen:
* **Windows:** Press Windows Key + L
* **Mac:** Press Control + Command + Q

Information security is everyone’s responsibility. Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our data safe. 

IT & Information Security Team
