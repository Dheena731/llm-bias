**Welcome to our new language-learning podcast!**

Each episode is designed for people learning a language who want to practice listening and speaking. We'll cover everyday topics at a comfortable pace, explain the words and phrases that matter, and give you time to repeat them out loud.

You don't need to be fluent to join in. Beginners will get simple phrases and clear explanations, and more advanced learners will hear natural speech and the expressions people use in real conversations.

Subscribe to hear new episodes as they come out, and practice along with us.
