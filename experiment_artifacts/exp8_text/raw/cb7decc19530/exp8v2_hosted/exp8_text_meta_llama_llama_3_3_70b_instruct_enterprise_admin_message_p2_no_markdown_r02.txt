Attention all staff, 

We are introducing a new expense approval workflow to improve efficiency and transparency in our expense reporting process. The new workflow will require all expenses to be submitted through our online portal, where they will be reviewed and approved by department managers before being forwarded to the finance team for reimbursement. This new process will help to reduce paperwork and ensure that all expenses are properly documented and approved. If you have any questions or concerns about the new workflow, please don't hesitate to reach out to the finance team. We will be providing training and support to help you get familiar with the new system. 

Thank you for your cooperation and understanding.
