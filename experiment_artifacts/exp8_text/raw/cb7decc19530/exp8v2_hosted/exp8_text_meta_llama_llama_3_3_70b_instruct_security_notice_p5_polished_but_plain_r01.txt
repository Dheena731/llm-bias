If you have lost a company laptop, please report the incident to the IT department as soon as possible. When making the report, provide as much detail as you can about the circumstances of the loss, including the location and time it was last seen. You should also include a description of the laptop, such as its make, model, and any distinctive features. This information will help the IT department to take the necessary steps to secure company data and to initiate the process of replacing the laptop if necessary. Please note that all company laptops are the property of the company and it is the responsibility of the employee to take reasonable care to protect them from loss or damage.
