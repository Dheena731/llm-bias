The City of Oakridge Department of Public Works advises all motorists and residents that Elm Street, between Fourth Avenue and Pine Street, will be temporarily closed to through traffic beginning at seven in the morning on Monday, October fourteenth, and continuing through five in the afternoon on Friday, October eighteenth. This closure is required to complete essential water main replacements and road resurfacing. A marked detour route will direct traffic along Maple Boulevard and Sixth Avenue for the duration of the project. Local residents will have restricted access to their driveways, though minor delays should be expected, while emergency service vehicles will be granted access at all times. Motorists are urged to use caution, obey all posted signs, and allow extra travel time. For inquiries regarding this project, please contact the municipal public works office.
