**Introducing Your Monthly Spending Summary**

We've added a new Monthly Spending Summary to help you see where your money goes at a glance.

Each month, you'll get a clear overview that includes:

- **Total spending** compared with the previous month
- **Top categories**, so you can see your biggest expenses
- **Recurring charges** such as subscriptions and bills
- **Trends** that highlight changes in your habits over time

You can find your summary under **Insights > Monthly Summary**. It will be available starting on the first day of next month, and you'll also receive a notification when it's ready.

We hope this makes it easier to review your budget and plan ahead. As always, we'd love your feedback. Let us know what you find useful and what you'd like to see added.
