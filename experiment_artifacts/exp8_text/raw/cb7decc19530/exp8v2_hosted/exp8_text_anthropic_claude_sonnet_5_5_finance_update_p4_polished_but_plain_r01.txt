Subject: Your End-of-Year Tax Statement

Dear Customer,

Your end-of-year tax statement is now available. It summarizes the income, interest, dividends, and other taxable activity recorded in your account during the past calendar year. Its purpose is to give you the figures you need to complete your tax return accurately, so it is worth reading carefully before you file.

The statement is organized by type of income. Each section shows the total amount paid or credited to you, along with any tax that was withheld on your behalf. If you see an amount withheld, you can generally claim it as a credit when you file, which may reduce what you owe or increase your refund. Please compare these totals with your own records, such as bank statements and pay slips, to confirm that everything matches.

Some figures may differ from what you expected. This usually happens because of timing, since income is reported in the year it was paid or credited rather than the year it was earned. Reinvested earnings and adjustments made late in the year can also change the totals. If a number still looks wrong after you check your records, contact us promptly so we can review it and, if necessary, issue a corrected statement. Please do not file until any correction has been resolved, as amending a return later takes more time.

Keep a copy of the statement with your other tax documents for at least as long as your local rules require, which is commonly several years. If your situation is complex, for example if you have income from more than one country, significant investment gains, or self-employment earnings, a qualified tax professional can help you interpret the details and identify any deductions you may be entitled to.

If you have questions about any entry, reply to this message or call our support team during business hours, and we will gladly walk you through it.
