As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain a secure work environment, we want to remind everyone of the importance of password security. Using the same password for multiple accounts, both personal and professional, poses a significant risk to our organization's security. If one of your accounts is compromised, an attacker may be able to gain access to other accounts that use the same password, including your work accounts. To protect yourself and our company, please use unique and complex passwords for each of your accounts. If you have any questions or concerns about password security, please do not hesitate to reach out to our IT department for guidance and support.
