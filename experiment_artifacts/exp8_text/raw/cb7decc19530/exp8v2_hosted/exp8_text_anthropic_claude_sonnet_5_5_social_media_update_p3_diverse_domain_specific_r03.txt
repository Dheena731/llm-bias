**Seed & Cutting Swap: Saturday, 9 to noon**
Alder Street Library, side lot (community room if it rains)

Bring what you've got extra: saved seed in labeled envelopes (variety + year), pothos and tradescantia cuttings in jars, divided perennials, the zucchini seed you swore you'd never plant again. Nothing to trade? Come anyway. There's a free table.

Dolores from the garden club will be running germination tests on old seed packets. Bring that 2019 box of mystery beans and find out in a week if they're worth planting.

Also: the tool library is lending out broadforks and soil knives for the day, and the kids' table will be making newspaper pots.

Parking is tight, so use the back lot on Fenn St. and walk through the gate by the book drop.
