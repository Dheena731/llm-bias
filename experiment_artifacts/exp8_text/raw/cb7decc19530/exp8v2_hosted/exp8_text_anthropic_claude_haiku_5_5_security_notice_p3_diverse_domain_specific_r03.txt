**Subject: Lock your screen when you leave your desk**

Starting Monday, the front-desk and billing PCs will lock automatically after 5 minutes idle, down from 15. That won't cover every gap, though. When you step away to grab a chart from the file room or take a call in the hallway, press Win+L (Mac: Control+Cmd+Q) first.

The auditors flagged two claims-system sessions left open and unattended during their visit last month. Those screens showed patient names and insurance IDs to anyone walking past the front counter.

Also, the shared login on the x-ray room PC is being retired. If you use it, email Dana in IT for your own account by Friday.
