Here's a short tip for seasoning roasted vegetables:

**Add Flavor to Roasted Vegetables: The 3-2-1 Rule**

When seasoning roasted vegetables, use the 3-2-1 rule:

* **3**: Use 3 main seasonings, such as herbs (thyme, rosemary, or oregano), spices (cumin, paprika, or coriander), or citrus zest (lemon or orange).
* **2**: Combine 2 acidic or umami ingredients, like vinegar (balsamic or red wine), soy sauce, or miso paste, to balance the flavors.
* **1**: Add 1 aromatic ingredient, like garlic, ginger, or onion, to bring depth and warmth to the dish.

For example, on Brussels sprouts, you could use thyme (3), balsamic vinegar (2), and garlic (1). On sweet potatoes, you could use cumin (3), soy sauce (2), and ginger (1). Experiment with different combinations to find the perfect flavor for your roasted vegetables!
