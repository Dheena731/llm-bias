To make weeknight dinners easier, consider preparing a protein and a few staple ingredients on the weekend, such as grilling chicken or cooking a batch of rice and roasting vegetables. This way, you can quickly assemble a meal during the week by combining your pre-cooked ingredients with some fresh produce and a simple sauce. For example, you can use pre-cooked chicken to make chicken salads, wraps, or stir-fries, and pair it with pre-cooked rice and roasted vegetables for a quick and satisfying meal.
