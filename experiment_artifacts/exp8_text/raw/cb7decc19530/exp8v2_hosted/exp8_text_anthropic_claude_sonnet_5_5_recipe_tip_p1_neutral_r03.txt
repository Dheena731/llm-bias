**Weeknight Tip: Cook once, use twice.**

When you're browning ground meat, roasting vegetables, or making rice, double the amount and set half aside. Tomorrow night, that extra portion becomes a head start on a completely different meal: tacos, fried rice, a grain bowl, or a quick pasta sauce.

**Bonus:** Prep your aromatics (onion, garlic, ginger) in one batch on Sunday. Stored in airtight containers in the fridge, they'll shave 5-10 minutes off each night's cooking and make a quick dinner feel effortless.
