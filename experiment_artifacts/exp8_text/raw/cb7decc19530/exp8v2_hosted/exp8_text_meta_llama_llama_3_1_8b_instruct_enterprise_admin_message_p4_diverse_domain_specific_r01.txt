**Quarterly Access Review Notice**

To ensure the continued security and integrity of our research data, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews for all research projects and datasets stored on our servers. These reviews are a critical component of our data management processes and are essential to maintaining the trust and confidence of our research community.

**What to Expect:**

* You will receive a notification approximately 30 days prior to the scheduled review date, detailing the specific data and access requirements for your project.
* You will be asked to review and confirm the access permissions for your project, including the names of users with read or write access.
* Any changes to access permissions or user roles will need to be updated and verified during this review process.

**Why Quarterly Reviews Matter:**

* Regular access reviews help prevent unauthorized access and maintain data security.
* They ensure that only authorized personnel have access to sensitive research data.
* Quarterly reviews also provide an opportunity to review and refine data management practices, ensuring compliance with institutional policies and regulatory requirements.

**What to Do:**

* If you are the principal investigator or data owner for a project, please ensure that you have reviewed and updated your project's access permissions prior to the scheduled review date.
* If you are a user with read or write access to a project, please confirm your access with the project owner and ensure that your role is up-to-date.
