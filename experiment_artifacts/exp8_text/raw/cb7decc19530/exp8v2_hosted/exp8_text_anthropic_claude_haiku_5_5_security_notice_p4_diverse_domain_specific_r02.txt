**Subject: Two-factor sign-in required by March 31**

Starting March 3, the patient records system and the staff email portal will ask for a six-digit code from your phone in addition to your password. Every account needs this turned on by Friday, March 31. Accounts without it will be locked at the next sign-in after that date.

**Setup takes about five minutes:**

1. Install an authenticator app if you don't have one. Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator, and Authy all work. Text-message codes are accepted but less reliable in the treatment rooms, where cell signal is poor.
2. Log in at portal.marlowdental.example, go to Settings > Security, and scan the QR code with the app.
3. Save the ten backup codes shown on screen. Store them in the office password vault, not in your email. Each code works once if you lose your phone.

The shared front-desk login is being retired on March 17. Individual accounts will be emailed to each person that week, and the shared login will stop working on March 24.

**Getting a new phone?** Call IT at ext. 214 before you wipe or sell your old one. We can reset the authenticator from our side, but only if you ask first.
