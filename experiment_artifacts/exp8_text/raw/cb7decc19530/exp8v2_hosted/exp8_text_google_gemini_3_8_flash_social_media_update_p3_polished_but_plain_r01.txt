Join us this Saturday for the annual Riverside Community Fair at Town Square Park, running from ten in the morning until three in the afternoon. The gathering will feature more than thirty local craft and produce vendors, live acoustic performances by neighborhood musicians, and hands-on activities for children. Admission is free, and the event will proceed rain or shine. Free parking is available across the street at the civic center garage, though walking or biking is encouraged. Bring your family and friends to support local small businesses and connect with your neighbors this weekend.
