Subject: Updates to the Expense Approval Workflow

Hello everyone,

Starting next Monday, we are rolling out a new expense approval workflow to make reimbursements faster and more transparent. Under the new process, all expense claims under $500 will be approved by your direct manager, while claims of $500 or more will also require sign-off from the finance team. Submissions should be made through the expense portal, and receipts must be uploaded at the time of submission.

Please review the updated policy document on the shared drive before the change takes effect. If you have questions about how the new steps apply to your situation, reach out to the finance operations inbox, and someone will respond within two business days. Training sessions will be held on Wednesday and Thursday of next week, and calendar invitations have been sent to all staff.

Thank you for your cooperation as we make this transition.

Administration Team
