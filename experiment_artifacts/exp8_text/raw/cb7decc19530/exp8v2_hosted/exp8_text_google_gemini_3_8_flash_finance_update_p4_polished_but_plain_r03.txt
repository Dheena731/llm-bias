Your end-of-year tax statement provides an official summary of your financial activity with us over the past calendar year. This document details your total earnings, applicable deductions, and any taxes that were withheld during that time. You will need the figures on this statement to complete your annual tax return accurately. Please review the information carefully against your personal records to ensure all details are correct. If you identify any errors or have questions about the figures shown, please contact our support team promptly for assistance. Be sure to retain a copy of this statement for your permanent tax records.
