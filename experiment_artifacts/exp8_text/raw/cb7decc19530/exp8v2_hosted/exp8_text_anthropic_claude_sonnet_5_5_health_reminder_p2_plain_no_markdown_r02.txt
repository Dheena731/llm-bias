A quick reminder to drink enough water today. Staying hydrated helps your energy, focus, mood, and digestion, and even mild dehydration can leave you feeling tired or foggy. Try keeping a glass or bottle within reach, and take a few sips whenever you finish a task or check your phone. Having a drink with each meal also helps, and you may need more if the weather is hot or you are exercising. If your urine is pale yellow, you are probably doing well. Take a moment right now to pour yourself some water.
