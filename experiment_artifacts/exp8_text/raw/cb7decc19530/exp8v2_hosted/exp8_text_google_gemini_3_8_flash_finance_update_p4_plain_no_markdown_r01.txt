An end-of-year tax statement is an official document that summarizes your financial activity with an organization over the past calendar year. Depending on who provides it, the statement may report your total earnings, taxes that were already withheld, interest earned from a bank, or payments you made that could reduce your taxable income, such as student loan interest or charitable donations. You use the details on this document to accurately file your annual income tax return with the government, which helps determine whether you owe additional taxes or are due a refund. You should review the information for accuracy and keep the document in your records for tax purposes.
