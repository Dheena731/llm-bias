**Overall impression**

The draft shows a solid grasp of the core literature on the topic and a clear sense of the field's main debates. The thematic organisation in Section 2 works well. The review would benefit most from moving beyond summary toward a more critical synthesis.

**Strengths**

- The search strategy is clearly described, and the inclusion criteria are justified.
- Section 2 groups studies by theme rather than by publication date, which helps the reader follow the argument.
- Key definitions are introduced early and used consistently.

**Areas for development**

1. **Synthesis over summary.** Many paragraphs proceed study by study ("Smith (2015) found... Jones (2018) argued..."). Try to group findings around claims, noting where studies agree, where they conflict, and why. Explain what the disagreement reveals.

2. **Critical evaluation.** The review rarely assesses the quality, methodology, or scope of the sources. Comment on sample sizes, contexts, or methodological limitations where they bear on the conclusions you draw.

3. **Identifying the gap.** The concluding section states that further research is needed but does not specify what gap exists or why it matters. Link the gap explicitly to your research questions so the reader sees the rationale for your study.

4. **Currency.** Few sources postdate 2019. Check for recent work, particularly in the last three to four years, to ensure the review reflects the current state of the field.

5. **Signposting.** Opening sentences of each section should state the argument that section will develop, not only its topic.

**Minor points**

- Several citations in Section 3 are missing page numbers for direct quotations.
- "Impact" is used in three different senses; define it or choose more precise terms.
- Check consistency between the reference list and in-text citations (two entries appear to be missing).

**Recommendation**

Revise with a focus on synthesis and critical evaluation, then resubmit. I am happy to review a revised section on the thematic organisation before you complete the full draft.
