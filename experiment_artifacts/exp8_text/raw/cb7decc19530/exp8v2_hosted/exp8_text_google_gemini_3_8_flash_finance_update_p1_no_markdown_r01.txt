We are excited to introduce our new monthly spending summary, designed to give you clearer visibility into your finances. At the end of each month, you will now see an organized breakdown of your expenses by category, your top spending areas, and comparisons to previous months. This new view makes it easier to track your budget and make informed financial decisions without sorting through individual transactions. You can access your summary anytime by visiting the reports section of your dashboard. Take a look and let us know your thoughts.
