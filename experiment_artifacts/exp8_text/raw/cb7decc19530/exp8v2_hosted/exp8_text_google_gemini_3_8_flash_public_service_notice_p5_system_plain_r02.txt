The local election office has finalized the list of polling station locations for the upcoming election. Voters are assigned to specific locations based on their residential address to ensure an orderly and efficient voting process. Designated sites include neighborhood public schools, community recreation centers, and the municipal library. All polling stations will be open from seven in the morning until eight in the evening on election day. You can confirm your assigned voting location by checking the address printed on your official voter information card or by contacting the city clerk by phone. Please remember that you must cast your ballot at your specific assigned location, and you should bring a valid form of identification when you arrive to vote.
