**Reminder: Lock Your Screen When Away**

All staff are reminded to lock their computer screens whenever they step away from their desks, even briefly. Unlocked screens put confidential information at risk and can lead to unauthorized access.

**How to lock your screen:**
- Windows: Press Windows key + L
- Mac: Press Control + Command + Q

Please make this a habit every time you leave your workstation. Thank you for helping keep our data secure.

If you have questions, contact the IT department.
