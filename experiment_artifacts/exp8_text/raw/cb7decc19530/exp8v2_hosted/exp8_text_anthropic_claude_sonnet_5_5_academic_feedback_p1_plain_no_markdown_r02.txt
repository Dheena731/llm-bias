Thank you for sharing your draft literature review. It shows a solid command of the field and a clear effort to engage with a broad range of sources. What follows is my assessment of its main strengths and the areas where revision would help most.

The review's greatest strength is its breadth. You have drawn on both foundational texts and recent empirical studies, and your summaries are generally accurate and clearly written. The opening section also does a good job of establishing why the topic matters, which gives the reader a reason to keep going.

The main weakness is that the review is organized largely as a series of summaries, with one study described after another. This approach tells the reader what has been said but not how the pieces fit together. A stronger review would be organized around themes, debates, or methodological approaches, and would show where authors agree, where they conflict, and why. For example, your discussion of the two competing explanations in the middle section would be far more persuasive if you compared the quality of the evidence behind each rather than presenting them in sequence.

Related to this, the review is more descriptive than critical. You rarely comment on the limitations of the studies you cite, such as small samples, narrow populations, or questionable measures. Even brief evaluative remarks would demonstrate your own judgment and help the reader see which findings deserve the most weight.

The review also does not yet make clear what gap it identifies. The conclusion restates the main findings but does not explain what remains unknown or how your project will address it. I suggest drawing the gap out explicitly, ideally building toward it from the start of the review, so that your research question appears as a natural next step rather than a separate addition.

On style, the prose is clear, though some paragraphs run long and combine several ideas. Stronger topic sentences would help. Please also check citation formatting for consistency, as a few entries deviate from the required style, and make sure every in-text reference appears in the bibliography.

In sum, this is a competent draft with a good foundation. Restructuring around themes, adding critical evaluation, and sharpening the statement of the gap would raise it considerably. I would be glad to look at a revised version.
