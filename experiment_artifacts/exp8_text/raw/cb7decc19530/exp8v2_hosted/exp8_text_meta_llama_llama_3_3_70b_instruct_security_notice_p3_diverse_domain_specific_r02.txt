Reminder: Secure Your Workstations

As we continue to handle sensitive patient data and confidential medical records, it's essential that we prioritize data security. Please remember to lock your screens when stepping away from your workstations, even if it's just for a few minutes. This simple habit helps prevent unauthorized access to our systems and protects the confidentiality of our patients' information.

Let's work together to maintain a secure environment and uphold our commitment to patient privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about our data security policies, don't hesitate to reach out to our IT department.
