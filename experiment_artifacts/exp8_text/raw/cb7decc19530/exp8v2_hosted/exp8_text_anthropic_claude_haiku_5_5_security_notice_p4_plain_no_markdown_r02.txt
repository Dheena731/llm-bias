Two-Factor Authentication Is Now Available

To help protect your account, you can now turn on two-factor authentication. This adds a second step when you sign in: after entering your password, you will also be asked for a one-time verification code sent to your phone or generated by an authenticator app.

To enable it, sign in to your account and open the Security section of your account settings. Select the option to turn on two-factor authentication, then follow the on-screen steps to confirm your phone number or link an authenticator app. You will be given a set of backup codes. Store these somewhere safe, because they let you regain access if you lose your phone.

We encourage every user to enable this feature. Even if someone learns your password, they will not be able to sign in without the second verification step. If you have questions or run into trouble during setup, contact our support team, and we will be glad to help.
