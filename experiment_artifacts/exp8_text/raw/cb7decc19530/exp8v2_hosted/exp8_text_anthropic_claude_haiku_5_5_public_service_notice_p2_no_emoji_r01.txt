**PUBLIC NOTICE: Road Closure**

**Location:** Main Street between Oak Avenue and Elm Street

**Dates:** Monday, March 10 through Friday, March 14

**Hours:** 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily

The City of Riverside Public Works Department will be closing this section of Main Street to repair water lines and resurface the roadway. Local access will be maintained for residents and businesses up to the barricades.

**Detour:** Drivers should follow posted signs and use Pine Street or Second Avenue as alternate routes.

**Transit:** Bus Route 12 will follow the detour, with stops relocated to the corners of Pine Street and Second Avenue.

We thank you for your patience as we work to improve local infrastructure. For questions, contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567.
