**Important Security Notice**

**To: All Employees**

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain the security and integrity of our systems, we would like to remind everyone to be cautious when receiving emails that ask for sensitive information or attempt to deceive you.

**What is Phishing?**

Phishing is a type of cyber attack where an attacker sends a fake email that appears to be from a trusted source, such as our IT department or a bank, in an attempt to trick you into revealing confidential information. This could be your password, financial information, or other sensitive data.

**How to Identify Phishing Emails**

Be aware of emails that:

* Ask you to click on a link or download an attachment from an unknown sender
* Contain spelling or grammar mistakes
* Ask for personal or financial information
* Create a sense of urgency or panic

**What to Do**

If you receive an email that you suspect is a phishing attempt, do not respond to it. Instead, report it to our IT department immediately by emailing [security@company.com](mailto:security@company.com).

**Additional Tips**

* Always verify the authenticity of an email by contacting the sender directly
* Never give out sensitive information via email
* Keep your software and systems up-to-date with the latest security patches

**Your Safety is Our Top Priority**

We are committed to providing a safe and secure work environment for all employees. Your cooperation and vigilance are crucial in preventing phishing attacks.

**If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to our IT department.**
