Latency is the delay between a user's action and the application's response. It matters in web applications for three primary reasons:

1.  **User Experience:** High latency causes perceived sluggishness, leading to frustration and abandonment. Users expect immediate feedback, and even small delays can increase bounce rates and reduce engagement.
2.  **Business Impact:** Performance directly correlates with revenue. Slower load times are linked to lower conversion rates, fewer sign-ups, and reduced customer retention. A fast application provides a competitive advantage.
3.  **Search Visibility:** Search engines prioritize fast-loading sites in their rankings. Poor latency can result in lower organic traffic, making optimization essential for discoverability.

Additionally, in complex systems, latency can accumulate across multiple services, degrading overall reliability and complicating maintenance. Minimizing latency ensures a responsive, efficient, and scalable web application.
