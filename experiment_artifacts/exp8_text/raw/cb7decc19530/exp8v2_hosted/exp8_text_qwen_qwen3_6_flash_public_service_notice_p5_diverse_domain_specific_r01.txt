**OFFICIAL BULLETIN: POLLING PLACE ASSIGNMENT – PRECINCT 14-B**  
**Issued:** October 24, 2024 | **Election Date:** November 5, 2024  

Registered voters assigned to Precinct 14-B will report to:  
**Westside Community Center, 840 Elm Street**  
Operating Hours: 6:00 AM – 7:00 PM  

This location supersedes all prior routing. Voters whose registration has not been updated since the last redistricting cycle must verify their precinct through the County Voter Portal before departure.

**Documentation Required:** Current government-issued photo ID. First-time voters who did not meet identity-verification requirements during registration must also present one form of proof of residential address.

**Facility Layout & Accessibility:** Main entrance is ADA-compliant. Wheelchair staging is located at the north loading zone. Language-assisted check-in desks (3–5) are staffed by certified tabulation personnel.
