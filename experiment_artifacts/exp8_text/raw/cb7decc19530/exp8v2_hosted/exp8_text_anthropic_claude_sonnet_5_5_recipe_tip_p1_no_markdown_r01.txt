Cooking tip: Prep your ingredients before you turn on the heat. On a busy weeknight, take five minutes to chop your vegetables, measure your spices, and have your protein ready on a plate. Once everything is within reach, the actual cooking goes quickly and smoothly, and you avoid burning garlic or overcooking food while you scramble to chop the next ingredient. A bonus trick is to cook in one pan whenever possible. Sear chicken or sausage first, remove it, sauté your vegetables in the same pan to pick up the browned bits, then return the protein and add a splash of broth or a squeeze of lemon. You get more flavor and have fewer dishes to wash afterward.
