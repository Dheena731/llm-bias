NOTICE OF CHANGE TO ACCOUNT TERMS

Dear valued customer,

We are writing to inform you that we will be making changes to the terms and conditions governing your account with us. These changes are intended to reflect updates to our business practices and to provide a clearer understanding of the terms and conditions that apply to your account.

The updated terms and conditions will take effect on the 1st day of the next calendar month and will apply to all accounts held with us. The key changes are as follows:

* The minimum balance requirement for our standard account will be increased to £500.
* The interest rate on our savings account will be reduced to 2.5% per annum.
* We will introduce a monthly management fee of £5 for accounts with a balance below £1,000.
* Our policy on overdrafts will be amended to limit them to a maximum of three times the account balance.

These changes are designed to ensure that our account terms are clear and transparent for all customers. If you have any questions or concerns regarding these changes, please do not hesitate to contact us.
