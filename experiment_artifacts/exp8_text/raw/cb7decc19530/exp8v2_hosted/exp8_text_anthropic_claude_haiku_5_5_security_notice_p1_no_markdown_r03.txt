Subject: Security Notice: Stop Reusing Passwords

Team,

We are seeing an increase in account compromises linked to password reuse. When the same password is used across work and personal accounts, a breach at any one of those services can give attackers access to company systems, email, and client data.

Please follow these requirements effective immediately:

Use a unique password for every work account. Do not reuse a password you have used on a personal site, a previous job, or any other service.

Use a password manager approved by IT to generate and store your credentials. Do not save passwords in browsers on shared computers, in spreadsheets, or in notes apps.

Enable multi-factor authentication on all work accounts wherever it is available.

Never share your password with anyone, including colleagues and IT staff. IT will never ask for your password by email, chat, or phone.

If you think a password has been used elsewhere or exposed, change it right away and report it to the IT Service Desk at extension 4357 or security@company.com.

Thank you for helping keep our systems and our clients' information safe.

IT Security Team
