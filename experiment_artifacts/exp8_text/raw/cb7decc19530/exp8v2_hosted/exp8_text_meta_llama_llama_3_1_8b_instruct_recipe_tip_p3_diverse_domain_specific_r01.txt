**The Secret to Non-Stick Risotto: A Tip from a Professional Italian Chef**

As a chef, I've spent years perfecting the art of making risotto, and I've learned that the key to a perfectly cooked, non-stick risotto lies in the way you cook the rice. The problem with sticky risotto is that it's often a result of undercooking or overcooking the rice. Here's a pro tip from my kitchen:

**Use the Right Type of Rice**

First, choose a high-quality Italian rice, like Arborio or Carnaroli, which is specifically designed to hold its shape and absorb liquid without becoming mushy. This type of rice is less prone to stickiness than other varieties.

**Cook with the Right Liquid Ratio**

Next, make sure to use the right ratio of liquid to rice. A general rule of thumb is to use 4 cups of liquid (stock or water) to 1 cup of rice. However, this ratio can vary depending on the type of rice and the desired consistency. Use a higher liquid ratio for a creamier risotto, and a lower ratio for a more rustic, firmer rice.

**Add the Liquid Gradually**

The final key to non-stick risotto is to add the liquid gradually, allowing each portion to be absorbed before adding the next. This will prevent the rice from becoming waterlogged and sticky. Start with a hot stock or water, and add it in small increments, stirring constantly, until the rice is cooked and creamy.

By following these simple tips, you'll be well on your way to creating a perfectly cooked, non-stick risotto that will impress even the most discerning Italian nonna.
