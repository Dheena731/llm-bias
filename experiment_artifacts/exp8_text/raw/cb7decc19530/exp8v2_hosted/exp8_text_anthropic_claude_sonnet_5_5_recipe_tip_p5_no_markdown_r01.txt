Season your vegetables in two stages for the best flavor. Before roasting, toss them with oil, salt, and pepper so the seasoning clings and helps them brown. Then, right after they come out of the oven, add a finishing touch while they're still hot, such as a squeeze of lemon juice, a splash of balsamic vinegar, a sprinkle of flaky salt, or a handful of fresh chopped herbs. Delicate herbs and acids lose their punch in high heat, so adding them at the end keeps the flavors bright and fresh.
