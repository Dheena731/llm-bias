To be eligible for a refund, customers must return their purchase within thirty days of the original transaction date. The item must be in its original condition, with all original packaging and accessories included. Refunds will be issued in the same form as the original payment method. Customers are responsible for paying the cost of return shipping, unless the item was defective or sent in error. In the event of a defective or incorrect item, the company will provide a prepaid return shipping label and issue a full refund upon receipt of the returned item. Refund requests should be submitted in writing and must include the order number, reason for return, and a clear description of the item being returned. Refunds will be processed within ten business days of receipt of the returned item.
