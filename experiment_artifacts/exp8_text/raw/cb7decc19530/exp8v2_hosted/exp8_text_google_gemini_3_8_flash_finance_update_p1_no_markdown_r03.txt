Subject: Introducing Your New Monthly Spending Summary

We are excited to roll out a new Monthly Spending Summary to help you better track and manage your finances. 

Starting this month, you will receive an automated overview at the end of every billing cycle. This summary breaks down your total expenditures by category, highlights your top expenses, and shows month-over-month trends so you can easily spot where your money goes. 

You can view your latest summary today by heading to the Analytics tab in your account dashboard. Take a look and let us know what you think!
