### ✅ Methods Checklist
- [ ] **Can it be replicated?** Would a peer know exactly how to repeat your study?
- [ ] **Participants/Data:** Are inclusion/exclusion criteria, sample size, and recruitment method clear?
- [ ] **Materials/Measures:** Are instruments described with manufacturer/model info and validity/reliability cited?
- [ ] **Procedure:** Is the step-by-step process chronological and unambiguous?
- [ ] **Analysis:** Are statistical tests justified and assumptions mentioned?
- [ ] **Ethics:** Is IRB approval or ethical clearance noted (if applicable)?
- [ ] **Tone & Style:** Is it objective, precise, and consistently in the past tense?
