**Introducing Savings Goals**

We're excited to announce Savings Goals, a new feature that helps you set, track, and reach your financial targets.

**What's new:**
- **Create a goal** by naming it, setting a target amount, and choosing a deadline.
- **Track your progress** with a visual timeline that shows how close you are to reaching each goal.
- **Automatic contributions** let you schedule regular transfers toward your goal, so saving happens without extra effort.
- **Smart reminders** notify you when you're falling behind or when you hit a milestone.

Whether you're building an emergency fund, saving for a vacation, or planning for a major purchase, Savings Goals keeps your plans organized in one place.

Savings Goals is rolling out to all users over the next two weeks. Open the app and tap "Goals" in the menu to get started.
