To make rice less sticky, thoroughly rinse the raw grains in cold water using a fine-mesh strainer until the water runs clear. This washes away the excess surface starch that causes the grains to clump together. Additionally, be careful not to use too much water, and avoid stirring the rice while it simmers, as agitation releases even more starch. Once the rice is finished cooking, remove it from the heat and let it rest undisturbed with the lid on for ten minutes before gently fluffing the grains with a fork.
