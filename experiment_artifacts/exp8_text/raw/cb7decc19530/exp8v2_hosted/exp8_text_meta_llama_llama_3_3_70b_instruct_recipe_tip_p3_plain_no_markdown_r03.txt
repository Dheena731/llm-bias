To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it thoroughly before cooking. This helps remove excess starch from the surface of the grains, which can cause them to cling together. After rinsing, make sure to drain the rice well and then cook it with the right amount of water. Using too little water can cause the rice to become dry and sticky, while using too much water can make it mushy. Once the rice is cooked, fluff it with a fork to separate the grains and help it cool, which will also reduce stickiness.
