One simple change that instantly improves your calendar is leaving fifteen minutes empty between every meeting or task. That small buffer gives you time to stretch, grab water, jot down notes, and mentally prepare for what comes next instead of rushing from one commitment to the next. When you treat those gaps as part of your schedule rather than wasted space, you will feel less rushed, make fewer mistakes, and actually have energy left at the end of the day. Try it tomorrow and notice how much smoother your routine becomes.
