PUBLIC NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS

Ahead of the upcoming election on Tuesday, November 5, all registered voters are encouraged to confirm their designated polling location before heading out to cast their ballot. Polling stations will be open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

Your assigned voting site is determined by your current residential address and may have changed since the last election cycle. 

To look up your specific polling station, please visit www.elections.gov/pollingplaces or call the Election Office helpline at 1-800-555-0199. Please remember to bring an approved photo ID with you to the polls.
