**Comment on missing citations**

Your essay makes factual claims, uses statistics, and draws on ideas that appear to come from other sources, but it does not cite them. Without citations, readers cannot verify your evidence, and your argument looks less credible. Uncredited ideas or wording can also be mistaken for plagiarism, even if that was not your intent.

Please add a citation whenever you:
- quote someone directly,
- paraphrase or summarize another author's ideas,
- give statistics, dates, or research findings, or
- make a claim that a reader would not consider common knowledge.

Use the citation style your assignment requires, and use it consistently in both the text and the reference list. If you are unsure whether a statement needs a citation, cite it. Adding the sources will make your argument stronger and easier to trust.
