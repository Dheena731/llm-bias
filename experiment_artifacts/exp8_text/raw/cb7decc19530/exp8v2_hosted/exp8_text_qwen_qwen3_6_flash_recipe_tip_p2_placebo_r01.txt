To preserve freshness, store tender herbs like basil, cilantro, and parsley stem-down in a jar with about an inch of water. Keep basil at room temperature away from direct sunlight, and refrigerate the rest. For sturdy herbs such as rosemary, thyme, and oregano, wrap them loosely in a slightly damp paper towel and place them in a sealed container or resealable bag in the refrigerator. Always wait to wash herbs until right before use, as excess moisture accelerates spoilage.
