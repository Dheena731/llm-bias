As of January 1, we will be introducing a change to our card transaction fees. Effective immediately, the fee for transactions processed on weekdays will be 1.5% of the transaction amount, and on weekends and holidays, the fee will be 2% of the transaction amount.

This change is intended to reflect the varying operational costs associated with processing transactions on different days. We strive to provide transparent and consistent fee structures, and this adjustment is designed to better align our pricing with the realities of our business operations.

Please note that these new fees will apply to all card transactions, regardless of the card type or the merchant's business type. We will not be adjusting the fee structure for any specific industries or merchants.

We value your business and appreciate your understanding of this change. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
