1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your device, remember certain information about your visit, and perform various functions. Similar technologies, such as pixels, tags, and local storage, may also be used and are covered by this notice.

Marketing Cookies: These cookies may be set by us or by third-party partners to track visitors across websites and deliver advertisements that are more relevant to you. These cookies are only placed with your consent where required by applicable law.
