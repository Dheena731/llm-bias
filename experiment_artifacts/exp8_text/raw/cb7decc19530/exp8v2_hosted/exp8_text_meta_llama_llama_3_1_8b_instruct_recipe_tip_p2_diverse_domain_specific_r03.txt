Here's a short tip for storing fresh herbs:

**Airy Bunches in the Fridge**

Store fresh herbs like basil, cilantro, and parsley in the refrigerator, but don't bunch them like you would with bananas. Instead, place each herb stem in a single layer on a paper towel-lined plate or tray, or in a mesh bag, to maintain airflow. This helps prevent moisture from accumulating and promotes even drying. Check on them daily and use a sharp knife or scissors to snip off any wilted or brown ends.
