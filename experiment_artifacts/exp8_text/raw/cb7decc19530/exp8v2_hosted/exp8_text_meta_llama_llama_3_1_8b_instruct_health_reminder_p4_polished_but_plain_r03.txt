Taking regular breaks from screens can help you stay focused and refreshed. Try to take a short break every 60 to 90 minutes, lasting anywhere from 5 to 15 minutes. During this time, step away from your computer or phone and engage in a non-screen related activity, such as taking a short walk, stretching, or practicing deep breathing exercises. This simple habit can help reduce eye strain, improve productivity, and give you a much-needed moment of mental clarity.
