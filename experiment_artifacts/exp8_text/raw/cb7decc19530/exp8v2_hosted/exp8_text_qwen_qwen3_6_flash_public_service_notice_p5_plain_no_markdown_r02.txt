This notice informs all eligible voters about polling station locations for the upcoming election. Your assigned polling place is determined by your residential address. You may find the exact location and hours of operation by consulting the voter information sheet mailed to your home or by using the voter lookup tool on the official election board website. Polling stations will be open from seven in the morning until nine in the evening on election day. All voters must bring a valid photo identification and a document showing current residency to cast their ballot. If you have changed your address since the last election, please verify your registration status immediately. Contact your local election office if you require assistance locating your polling site.
