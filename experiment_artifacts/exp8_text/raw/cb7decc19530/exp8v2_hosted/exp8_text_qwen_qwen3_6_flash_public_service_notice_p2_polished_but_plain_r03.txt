Public notice is hereby given that Main Street between First Avenue and Third Avenue will be closed to all vehicular traffic from Monday, October 23 through Friday, October 27. This closure is necessary to facilitate emergency water main repairs that will improve service reliability for local residents. During this period, drivers should expect delays and are advised to utilize Oak Avenue as an alternate route to bypass the work zone. Emergency vehicles will retain access throughout the closure, and residents with valid parking permits may obtain temporary access passes at the Department of Transportation office located at City Hall. Please plan your travel accordingly and allow extra time for commutes. For further information or to request a pass, please contact the Project Hotline at 555-0199 during business hours. Thank you for your patience and cooperation while we complete these essential improvements.
