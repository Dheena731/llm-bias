**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication**

To help protect your account and personal information, we strongly recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA). This added layer of security requires a second form of verification, such as a one-time code sent to your phone or generated by an authenticator app, in addition to your password when you sign in.

**How to enable 2FA:**
1. Log in to your account and go to **Settings > Security**.
2. Select **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Choose your preferred method: text message, authenticator app, or security key.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete setup.
5. Save your backup codes in a safe place.

Enabling 2FA takes only a few minutes and significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access, even if your password is compromised.

If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact our support team.

Thank you for helping us keep your account secure.
