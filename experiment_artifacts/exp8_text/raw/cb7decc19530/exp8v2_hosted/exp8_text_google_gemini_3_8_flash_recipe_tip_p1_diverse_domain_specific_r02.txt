To get deeply browned roasted vegetables in twenty minutes instead of the usual steamed, limp result, slide your bare rimmed baking sheet into the oven while it preheats to 450°F. 

Toss your chopped vegetables—especially dense brassicas like broccoli or halved Brussels sprouts—with high-smoke-point oil and salt in a bowl. When the oven is up to temperature, pull the screaming-hot pan out, dump the vegetables flat-side down, and immediately return it to the rack. 

The immediate thermal shock starts conduction searing before the vegetable cell walls collapse and purge water into the pan, guaranteeing hard caramelization on the contact surface while keeping the interior crisp-tender.
