Hi everyone,

We're excited to share that a new savings goal feature is now available in the app. It's designed to help you set clear financial targets and track your progress toward them, whether you're saving for an emergency fund, a vacation, a new home, or something else entirely.

Here's how it works. You can create a goal by giving it a name, choosing a target amount, and setting an optional deadline. Once a goal is in place, you can add funds manually whenever you like or set up recurring transfers to build your savings automatically. The app will show you how much you've saved so far, how much remains, and whether you're on track to meet your deadline.

You can also create multiple goals at the same time, so you can keep your vacation fund separate from your emergency savings. Each goal has its own progress bar and history, making it easy to see where your money is going.

To get started, open the app, tap the Savings tab, and select New Goal. If you have feedback or run into any issues, please reach out to our support team. We'd love to hear how this feature helps you reach your goals.

Happy saving!
