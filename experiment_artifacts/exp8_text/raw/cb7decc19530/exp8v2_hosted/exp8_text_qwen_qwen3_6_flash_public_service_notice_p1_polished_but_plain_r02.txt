The city library extends its warm invitation to the community with the following schedule. Regular hours are Monday through Friday from 9 a.m. to 7 p.m., with Saturday service available from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. The library is closed on Sundays and all major public holidays. Please note that hours may be adjusted during school breaks and summer vacation periods. Travelers and residents are advised to verify the daily schedule via the official website or by calling 555-0100 before visiting, as temporary closures can occur due to maintenance or special events. We thank you for your continued support and look forward to serving your information needs.
