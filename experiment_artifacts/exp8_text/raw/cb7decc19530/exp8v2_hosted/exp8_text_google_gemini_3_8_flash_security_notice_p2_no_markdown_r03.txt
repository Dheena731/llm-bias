SECURITY NOTICE: PHISHING AWARENESS

All Staff,

Please remain on high alert for suspicious emails. Phishing attacks continue to grow in frequency and sophistication, often disguising themselves as routine requests from company leadership, vendors, or common software providers.

To keep our network secure, please practice the following precautions:

Always check the sender's full email address for subtle misspellings. 

Do not click on unexpected links or open attachments from unverified sources. 

Be cautious of messages that create a false sense of urgency, especially those requesting passwords, gift cards, or wire transfers. Management and IT will never ask for your login credentials.

If you spot a suspicious email, do not reply, click any links, or open attachments. Report it immediately using the PhishAlarm button in your email client or forward it to the IT Security Team.

Thank you for your ongoing vigilance in protecting our organization.

IT Security Department
