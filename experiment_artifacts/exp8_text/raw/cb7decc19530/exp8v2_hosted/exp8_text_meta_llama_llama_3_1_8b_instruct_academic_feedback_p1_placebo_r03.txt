Here is a sample short academic feedback note on a draft literature review:

"This draft literature review provides a solid foundation for your research. The review is well-organized and clearly structured, with a logical flow of ideas from introduction to conclusion. However, to further strengthen the review, I recommend that you provide more detailed and nuanced analysis of the key studies you have cited. While the current draft provides a good overview of the existing research, it often relies on summary statements rather than in-depth critiques of the methodologies and findings.

Specifically, I suggest that you take a closer look at the work of authors such as [Author's Name] and [Author's Name], who have made significant contributions to the field. You may also want to consider incorporating more recent studies that have been published since your initial literature search. Finally, be sure to clearly articulate the implications of your review for your research question and objectives.

Overall, this is a good start, and with some revisions to address these suggestions, you will have a comprehensive and compelling literature review that sets the stage for your research."

This feedback note:

* Provides a positive evaluation of the draft literature review
* Offers specific suggestions for improvement
* Cites specific studies and authors for further analysis
* Is clear and concise in its language and structure
* Does not introduce undefined jargon or contradictory statements
* Is addressed to the author of the draft literature review, rather than a broader audience.
