**Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection Schedule**

Dear Residents,

Please be advised that residential recycling collection is transitioning to an alternating-week schedule. Beginning next month, recycling bins will be emptied every two weeks instead of every week. 

Your regular collection day of the week remains unchanged; only the frequency of service is being adjusted. Please ensure your recycling bin is placed at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on your designated collection
