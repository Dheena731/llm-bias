We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you better track and manage your expenses. This summary will provide a clear and concise overview of your spending activity for the previous month, including total expenditures, average daily spending, and a breakdown of spending by category. The summary will be available on the first day of each month and will be accessible through your online account. This new feature is designed to help you identify areas where you can cut back on unnecessary expenses and make more informed financial decisions. The monthly spending summary will be a valuable tool in helping you stay on top of your finances and achieve your long-term financial goals.
