We have recently seen an increase in phishing emails being sent to employees. These emails are designed to look like they come from a legitimate source, but are actually attempts to steal your login credentials or other sensitive information. They often create a sense of urgency, asking you to click on a link or download an attachment immediately. 

If you receive an email that you are not sure about, do not click on any links or download any attachments. Instead, contact the IT department for assistance. We will be able to determine if the email is legitimate or not. Remember to always be cautious when receiving emails from unknown senders, and never give out your login credentials in response to an email. 

We will be providing additional training on how to spot phishing emails in the coming weeks. In the meantime, if you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT department.
