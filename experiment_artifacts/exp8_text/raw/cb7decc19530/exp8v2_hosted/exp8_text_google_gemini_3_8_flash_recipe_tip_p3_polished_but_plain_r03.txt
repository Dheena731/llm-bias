To keep rice light and separate rather than sticky, thoroughly rinse the dry grains before cooking. Place the rice in a fine-mesh strainer and wash it under cold running water, gently agitating the grains with your fingers until the runoff is clear rather than milky. This step removes the excess surface starch that causes clumping and gumminess. After cooking, let the pot sit covered and undisturbed off the heat for ten minutes, which allows the steam to redistribute evenly throughout the grains. Finally, fluff the rice gently with a fork rather than stirring with a heavy spoon, which can crush the grains and release residual starch.
