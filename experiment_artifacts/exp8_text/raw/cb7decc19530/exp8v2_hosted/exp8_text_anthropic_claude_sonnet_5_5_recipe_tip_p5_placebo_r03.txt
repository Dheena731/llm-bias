**Tip: Season in two stages for better flavor.**

Before roasting, toss your cut vegetables with enough oil to coat them lightly, plus salt and pepper. The oil helps the seasoning stick and promotes browning, and the early salt enhances the vegetables' natural flavor.

After roasting, taste the vegetables while they are still hot and adjust. Add a pinch more salt if needed, then finish with something bright, such as a squeeze of lemon juice, a splash of vinegar, or a sprinkle of fresh herbs. Acid and fresh herbs lose their punch in a hot oven, so adding them at the end keeps their flavor vivid.

**Extra note:** Delicate dried herbs and minced garlic can burn at high oven temperatures. If you want those flavors, add them during the last 5 to 10 minutes of roasting, or stir them in right after the vegetables come out.
