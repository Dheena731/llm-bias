Introducing Savings Goals

We're excited to announce a new way to help you reach what matters most. With the new Savings Goals feature, you can set a target amount, choose a deadline, and track your progress all in one place.

Here's what you can do:

You can create multiple goals, such as a vacation, an emergency fund, or a new laptop, and give each one its own name and target. You can set up automatic transfers on a weekly or monthly schedule so your savings grow without extra effort. A progress bar shows how close you are to each goal, and you'll get a friendly notification when you hit key milestones along the way.

To get started, open the app, tap the Goals tab, and select Create a Goal. It only takes a minute to set up.

Savings Goals is rolling out to all users this week. Update your app to the latest version to see it. We'd love to hear what you think, so send us your feedback through the Help menu.

Happy saving!
