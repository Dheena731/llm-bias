A heat advisory is in effect for this region beginning tomorrow morning through Friday evening as temperatures are forecast to rise well above seasonal averages. Residents are advised to limit outdoor exertion to the early morning or late evening hours and to spend time in air-conditioned spaces whenever possible. It is essential to drink water frequently even if not thirsty and to wear lightweight, loose-fitting clothing when outside. Please check on elderly relatives, neighbors, and others who may be vulnerable to extreme heat, and ensure that pets have access to fresh water and shade. Never leave children or animals unattended in parked vehicles, as interior temperatures can become lethal within minutes. Be aware of the signs of heat-related illnesses such as dizziness, nausea, headache, and confusion; if any of these symptoms occur, move the individual to a cool area and seek medical attention promptly. Local shelters with cooling stations will remain open throughout the advisory period, and updated information can be found via official municipal channels and local broadcast media.
