Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection

Beginning Monday, March 3, curbside recycling collection in our community will move from a weekly schedule to every other week. Collection will continue to take place on your current pickup day, but only on alternating weeks. A printed calendar showing the new pickup dates will be mailed to every household by the end of this month, and a copy will also be available at the town office and the public library.

Along with the new schedule, we are expanding the list of materials that can be recycled. Cartons used for milk, broth, and juice are now accepted, as are empty aluminum foil pans and clean pizza boxes with the greasy liner removed. Plastic bags, foam packaging, and food-soiled paper should still be kept out of your bin, since they can contaminate an entire load and cause it to be rejected.

To make the change easier, residents who find that their bin is too small for two weeks of materials may request a larger 95-gallon cart at no charge. Requests can be made by calling the Public Works office at 555-0142 during business hours. Please allow up to three weeks for delivery.

Place your bin at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on collection day, with the lid closed and at least three feet of space around it. Materials should be loose, not bagged, and rinsed of any food residue.

We appreciate your cooperation and your continued commitment to keeping our community clean. If you have questions about the new schedule, please contact Public Works at the number above.
