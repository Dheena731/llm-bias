**Important Voting Information**

For the upcoming election, polling stations will be located at the following sites:

* District 1: Oakdale Community Center, 123 Main St.
* District 2: Riverside Elementary School, 456 Elm St.
* District 3: Downtown Public Library, 789 Oak St.

Polling stations will be open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM on election day. Voters are reminded to verify their assigned polling station by checking their voter registration card or visiting the election office website. Identification and proof of residency may be required to cast a ballot. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the local election office at (555) 123-4567.
