NOTICE: ENABLE TWO-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION

Dear Users,

To enhance the security of your account, we are implementing a requirement for all users to enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).

Two-Factor Authentication adds an essential layer of protection to your login process. By requiring a second verification step in addition to your password, we can significantly reduce the risk of unauthorized access, even if your credentials are compromised.

ACTION REQUIRED:

Please enable 2FA on your account as soon as possible. Follow these steps:

1. Log in to your profile settings.
2. Navigate to the Security tab.
3. Select Two-Factor Authentication and activate the feature.
4. Choose your preferred verification method, such as an authenticator app or SMS text message.

DEADLINE:

All accounts must have 2FA enabled by December 1, 2024. Failure to comply may result in temporary suspension of access until the measure is applied.

SUPPORT:

If you need assistance with setup or have questions, please contact the IT Help Desk at support@company.com or dial extension 5555.

Thank you for your prompt attention to this important security update.
