**INTERNAL NOTICE: CREDENTIAL SEPARATION POLICY**

Using identical passwords across personal accounts, third-party services, and company systems creates a direct attack path. When external vendors suffer breaches, automated tools immediately test exposed username/password pairs against corporate logins. We have repeatedly traced internal compromises to this exact behavior, resulting in forced account suspensions and mandatory forensic audits.

Effective immediately, all personnel must maintain strictly separate credentials for any work-issued platform, including identity providers, HRIS, ERP, collaboration suites, and cloud repositories. 

Action required by Friday:
1. Run a credential audit on your active accounts using the provided internal checklist.
2. Change any duplicated passwords directly through SSO. Do not paste external credentials into system prompts.
3. Activate MFA on all assigned roles; legacy token-only accounts will be decommissioned next quarter.
4. Store new credentials exclusively in the enterprise password manager. Browser-save functions are disabled for corporate domains.

Accounts detected with cross-domain reuse will be placed in temporary quarantine pending verification. Submit a request to SecOps via the internal ticket portal for assistance or to report suspected compromise.

*Issued by Information Security & Access Management*
