Regularly taking short breaks from screens is essential for maintaining your physical and mental well-being. Staring at a screen for extended periods can lead to eye strain, headaches, and decreased productivity. Taking a few minutes to look away from your screen and focus on something else can help reduce these negative effects. Try to take a short break every hour to stand up, stretch, and move around, or simply gaze out a window to give your eyes a rest. This simple habit can make a significant difference in how you feel and can help you stay focused and energized throughout the day.
