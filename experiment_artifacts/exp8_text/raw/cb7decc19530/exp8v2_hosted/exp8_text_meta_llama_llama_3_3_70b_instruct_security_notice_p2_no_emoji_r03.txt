To: All Staff

Please be aware that there has been an increase in phishing emails being sent to employees. These emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information such as your login credentials or financial information.

To protect yourself and the company, please be cautious when receiving emails that:

* Ask for sensitive information
* Contain spelling or grammar mistakes
* Have urgent or threatening tone
* Come from unfamiliar senders

If you receive an email that you suspect is a phishing attempt, do not respond or click on any links. Instead, report it to the IT department immediately. Remember to always verify the authenticity of an email before taking any action.

Your vigilance is crucial in helping us to prevent phishing attacks and protect our company's security. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT department.
