NOTICE: REPORTING A LOST COMPANY LAPTOP

DATE: October 24, 2024
TO: All Employees
FROM: IT Security Division
SUBJECT: Mandatory Procedure for Reporting Missing Company Hardware

This notice provides the required steps for staff members who have lost or had stolen a company-issued laptop. Immediate reporting is critical to protect confidential information and trigger remote security protocols.

If your laptop is unaccounted for, follow these steps without delay:
Contact the IT Help Desk at extension 5000 or email itsecurity@company.com within one hour of discovering the loss.
Change your primary login password and notify your direct supervisor via email or phone.
Complete the Device Loss Incident Report found on the company intranet under Forms and submit it to the IT Security mailbox.
Cease personal attempts to locate the device if it was left in an unsecured or public environment.

Failure to report a missing laptop promptly may expose the organization to data breaches and will be subject to internal review. For after-hours emergencies, call the Security Hotline at 555-0199.

Thank you for your prompt attention to this security matter.

IT Security Division
Corporate Operations
