Public Notice: Heat Advisory in Effect

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for the county and surrounding areas from Thursday at 11:00 a.m. through Friday at 8:00 p.m. Afternoon temperatures are expected to reach the upper 90s to near 105 degrees Fahrenheit, and heat index values could make it feel even hotter. Overnight temperatures may not drop below the upper 70s, which offers little relief for people working or staying outdoors.

Residents are urged to take precautions during this period. Limit time spent outside between noon and 6:00 p.m., when the sun is strongest, and schedule strenuous activities such as yard work, exercise, and construction for early morning or evening. Drink water regularly even if you do not feel thirsty, and avoid alcohol, caffeine, and heavy meals, which can increase dehydration. Wear lightweight, loose-fitting, light-colored clothing, and use a hat or umbrella when outdoors.

Never leave children, pets, or anyone who depends on you in a parked vehicle, even for a short time, as interior temperatures can rise dangerously fast. Check on elderly neighbors, relatives, and people who live alone, and make sure they have a way to stay cool. If your home lacks air conditioning, consider visiting a public library, community center, or other cooled public space during the hottest hours. Pets should have shade and access to fresh water at all times.

Watch for warning signs of heat-related illness. Heat cramps, heavy sweating, nausea, dizziness, and headache may signal heat exhaustion, which requires moving to a cool place, sipping water, and loosening clothing. Confusion, fainting, a body temperature above 103 degrees Fahrenheit, or hot, dry skin may indicate heat stroke, a medical emergency. Call 911 immediately if someone shows these symptoms.

For updates, cooling center locations, and additional guidance, residents may contact the county emergency management office or monitor local news and weather broadcasts. Stay safe and look out for one another during this period of extreme heat.
