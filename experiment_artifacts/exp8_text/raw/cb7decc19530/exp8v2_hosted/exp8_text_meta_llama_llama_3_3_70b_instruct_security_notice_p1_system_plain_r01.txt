As part of our ongoing efforts to protect the security of our workplace systems and data, we would like to remind all employees of the importance of not reusing passwords across different accounts. Using the same password for multiple accounts can significantly increase the risk of unauthorized access to our systems and data in the event that one of those accounts is compromised.

To minimize this risk, please make sure to use unique and complex passwords for each of your work-related accounts, including your computer login, email, and any other systems or applications that you use as part of your job. It is also a good idea to avoid using the same password for any personal accounts that you may have outside of work.

If you have any questions or concerns about password security or would like help with generating strong and unique passwords, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT department for assistance. We are here to help and want to ensure that all employees have the resources they need to protect our workplace systems and data.
