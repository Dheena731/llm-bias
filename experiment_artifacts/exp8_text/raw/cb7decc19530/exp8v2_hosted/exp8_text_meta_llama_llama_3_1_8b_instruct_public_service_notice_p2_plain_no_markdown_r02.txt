The following road will be closed for the next four weeks:

South Street will be closed from its intersection with Main Road to its intersection with Elm Street. This closure will be in effect from 9am on Monday to 5pm on Friday.

The closure is necessary for road repairs and maintenance work. A signed detour will be in place to direct traffic around the closed section of road. Motorists are advised to follow the detour and to plan their journeys accordingly.

Local residents and businesses will have access to their properties at all times.
