NOTICE: UPDATES TO RESIDENTIAL RECYCLING COLLECTION

Please be advised that beginning Monday, November 4, there will be adjustments to the weekly recycling collection schedule. Recycling bins will now be emptied every other Wednesday instead of Thursday. Collection will resume weekly on Thursdays after January 6, pending seasonal review.

To ensure successful pickup, please follow these guidelines:
- Place your blue recycling cart at the curb by 6:00 a.m. on collection morning.
- Only accept clean, dry materials: cardboard, paper, plastic bottles and jugs, metal cans, and glass containers.
- Do not place recycling in bags or sacks. Items must be loose in the cart.
- Flatten all cardboard and rinse containers to prevent contamination.

These changes are being implemented to optimize route efficiency and reduce municipal operating costs. We appreciate your patience and cooperation during this transition.

For questions or to report missed collections, contact Public Works Operations at 555-0198 or email waste.services@citydistrict.gov.

Thank you for your continued commitment to responsible waste management.
