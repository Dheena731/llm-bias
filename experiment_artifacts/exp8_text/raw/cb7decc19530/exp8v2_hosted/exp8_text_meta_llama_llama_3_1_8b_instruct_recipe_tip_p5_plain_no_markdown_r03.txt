To enhance the flavor of roasted vegetables, try seasoning them with a mixture of salt, pepper, and a squeeze of fresh lemon juice. You can also add other seasonings such as garlic powder, dried herbs like thyme or rosemary, or grated citrus zest to give them a boost of flavor. Simply toss the vegetables with the seasonings before roasting them in the oven, and adjust the amount of seasonings to taste. This will bring out the natural flavors of the vegetables and add a depth of flavor that complements their natural sweetness.
