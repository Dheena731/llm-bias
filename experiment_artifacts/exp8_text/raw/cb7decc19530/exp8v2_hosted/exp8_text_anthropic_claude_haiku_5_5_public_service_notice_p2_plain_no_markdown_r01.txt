PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE ON MAIN STREET

The Department of Public Works advises residents that Main Street will be closed to all vehicle traffic between Oak Avenue and River Road from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on Saturday, June 14, while crews repair a damaged storm drain and resurface the roadway. Detour routes will be posted along Elm Street and Second Avenue. Local access will be maintained for emergency vehicles at all times, and pedestrians may use the sidewalks on both sides of the street, which will remain open throughout the work.

Residents and business owners along the affected section are asked to plan ahead, move any vehicles from the closed area by 6:30 a.m., and allow extra travel time on the day of the closure. Questions may be directed to the Public Works office during regular business hours at 555-0142. Work is weather permitting, and any change to the schedule will be announced on the city website and local news outlets.
