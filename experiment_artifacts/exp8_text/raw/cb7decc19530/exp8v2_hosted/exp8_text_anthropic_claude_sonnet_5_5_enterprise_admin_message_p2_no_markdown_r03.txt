Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starting Monday

Hello team,

Beginning Monday, we are moving to a new expense approval workflow to speed up reimbursements and give everyone better visibility into the status of their requests.

What is changing:

1. All expense reports must be submitted through the Finance Portal. Emailed receipts and paper forms will no longer be accepted.
2. Reports under $500 will be approved by your direct manager only.
3. Reports of $500 or more will require approval from your manager and then the Finance department.
4. Receipts must be attached to each line item at the time of submission.

Timelines:

Managers are asked to review requests within three business days. Approved expenses will be reimbursed in the next weekly payment run, which happens every Friday.

Please note that any expenses incurred before Monday should be submitted under the old process by this Friday. After that date, only the new workflow will be available.

A short how-to guide is available on the intranet under Finance > Expense Reports. If you have questions or run into problems, contact the Finance Help Desk at finance-help@company.com.

Thank you for your help making this transition smooth.
