Residents are advised that changes to the recycling collection schedule will take effect on the first day of next month. The new schedule will see recyclables collected every other week, rather than weekly as is currently the case. This change is intended to improve the efficiency of the collection process and reduce costs. Residents are encouraged to check their collection calendars or contact the public works department to confirm their new collection day. Additionally, a revised guide to acceptable recyclable materials will be mailed to all households in the coming weeks, outlining what items can and cannot be included in recycling bins.
