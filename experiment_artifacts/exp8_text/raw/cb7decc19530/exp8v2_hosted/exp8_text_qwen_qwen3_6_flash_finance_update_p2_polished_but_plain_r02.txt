This note advises that effective November first, the processing fee for all card transactions will increase from two percent to three percent. This update aligns with adjustments in interchange rates charged by payment networks and helps us maintain the quality of service and infrastructure we provide. For merchants using our platform, this change applies to every credit and debit card processed through your account. You will see the updated rate reflected on your next settlement statement beginning December first. If you have questions regarding how this affects your specific volume or pricing tier, please reply to this message or contact our support team directly.
