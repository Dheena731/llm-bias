**Public Service Notice: Library Opening Hours**

The Riverside Public Library wants to remind all residents of our current opening hours:

- **Monday through Friday:** 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
- **Saturday:** 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- **Sunday:** 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m.

The library will be closed on all major public holidays. Please check our website or call (555) 123-4567 for holiday schedules and any temporary changes.

Our branches, study rooms, computer labs, and self-checkout kiosks are available during all open hours. We look forward to welcoming you.
