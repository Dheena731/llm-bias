**Introducing Smart Savings Pots**

We've added a new feature to help you take control of your savings: Smart Savings Pots. This intuitive tool allows you to create customized savings goals tailored to your financial needs. Simply set a target amount, choose a frequency for deposits, and select a savings vehicle from our curated list of high-yield accounts and low-risk investments.

Smart Savings Pots offers several benefits:

* **Visual Progress Tracking**: View your savings progress in real-time, with clear milestones and alerts when you reach your targets.
* **Automated Savings**: Set up recurring transfers from your checking account to make saving easier and less prone to being neglected.
* **Diversified Savings Options**: Choose from a range of savings vehicles, each with its own risk level and potential returns, to create a diversified savings strategy.
* **Prioritized Goal Management**: Identify and prioritize your savings goals, ensuring you allocate funds to the most important objectives.

Smart Savings Pots empowers you to achieve your financial goals by providing a structured and flexible way to save. Start building your Smart Savings Pots today and take the first step towards a more secure financial future.
