To enhance the flavor of roasted vegetables, try seasoning them with a mixture of herbs and spices before roasting. A combination of salt, pepper, and olive oil is a good starting point, but you can also add other ingredients to suit your taste. For example, a Mediterranean blend might include oregano, thyme, and lemon juice, while an Italian-style seasoning might feature basil, garlic, and red pepper flakes. Experiment with different combinations to find the flavor you enjoy most.
