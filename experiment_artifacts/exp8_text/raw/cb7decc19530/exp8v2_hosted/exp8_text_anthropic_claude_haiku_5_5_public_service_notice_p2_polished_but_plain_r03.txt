PUBLIC NOTICE: Temporary Closure of Maple Avenue Between Fifth and Ninth Streets

The City Department of Public Works will close Maple Avenue to all vehicle and pedestrian traffic between Fifth Street and Ninth Street beginning at 7:00 a.m. on Monday, June 16, and continuing through 5:00 p.m. on Friday, June 20. The closure is needed so crews can replace aging water main lines and repave the roadway.

During the closure, drivers should use Elm Street or Oak Avenue as alternate routes. Local access to businesses and residences along the closed section will be maintained from both ends of the block, though vehicles may need to approach from the cross streets. Pedestrians may use the sidewalk on the east side of the street, which will remain open except where crews are working directly on the curb line. Signs and detour markers will be posted at all affected intersections.

Bus Route 14 will be temporarily rerouted along Elm Street during this period. Riders can check the transit agency's website or call the customer information line for updated stop locations.

Residents who need assistance with access, deliveries, or accessibility concerns may contact the Department of Public Works at 555-0142 or visit the department office at 200 Civic Center Drive. Work schedules may change due to weather; any changes will be announced on the city's official website and social media channels.

We thank residents and visitors for their patience as we complete these essential improvements.
