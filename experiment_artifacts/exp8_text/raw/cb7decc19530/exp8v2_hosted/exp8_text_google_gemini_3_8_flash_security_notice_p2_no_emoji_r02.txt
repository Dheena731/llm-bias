**SECURITY NOTICE: Stay Alert for Phishing Emails**

Please remain vigilant regarding suspicious emails targeting company inboxes. Phishing attacks often disguise themselves as legitimate communications from senior leadership, internal departments (like HR or IT), or trusted vendors.

**Key Warning Signs to Watch For:**
* High-pressure or urgent requests demanding immediate action.
* Unprompted requests for passwords, multi-factor authentication (MFA) codes, or financial transactions.
* Mismatched sender addresses (the display name may look familiar, but the actual email domain does not match).
* Generic greetings, unusual phrasing, or poor spelling and grammar.
* Unexpected attachments or links prompting you to log in.

**What to Do:**
1. **Do not click** any links, open attachments, or reply to the sender.
2. **Do not forward** the email to colleagues.
3. Report the message immediately using the "Report Phishing" button in your email client, or contact the IT Help Desk directly.

When in doubt, verify the request with the sender through a separate, trusted channel, such as a direct phone call or internal chat.
