This notice serves to remind all employees that reusing passwords across different accounts poses a significant security risk to both personal information and company systems. If a password is compromised on one platform, attackers can easily access other accounts where the same credentials are used, including corporate email, internal networks, and sensitive business applications. To protect our organization and your personal data, you must create a unique, strong password for every work related account and avoid using identical or similar passwords for personal services. Please update any shared credentials immediately through the company password manager provided by the IT department. Regularly changing your passwords and enabling multi factor authentication will further reduce the risk of unauthorized access. If you suspect your credentials have been exposed or experience unusual account activity, report it to the security team without delay. Adhering to these practices helps maintain the integrity and confidentiality of our digital environment.
