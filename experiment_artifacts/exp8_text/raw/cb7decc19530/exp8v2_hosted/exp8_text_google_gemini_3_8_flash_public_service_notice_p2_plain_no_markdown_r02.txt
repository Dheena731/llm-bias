Main Street between Oak Avenue and Maple Street will be closed to all through traffic starting Monday, October 14, at seven in the morning until Friday, October 18, at five in the afternoon. This temporary closure is required for municipal water main repairs and road resurfacing. A marked detour route will guide motorists around the construction zone using Elm Street and Pine Boulevard. Local residents and businesses within the affected area will retain limited access, but delays should be anticipated. Emergency vehicles will be accommodated at all times. Drivers are encouraged to plan alternate routes in advance, observe all posted detour signage, and exercise caution when traveling near work crews. Inquiries regarding this project may be directed to the Department of Public Works.
