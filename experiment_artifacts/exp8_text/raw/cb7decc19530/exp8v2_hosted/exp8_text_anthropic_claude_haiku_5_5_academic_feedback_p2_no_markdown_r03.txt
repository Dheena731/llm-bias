There are a few areas to strengthen before your draft is ready. First, the sampling description needs more detail. You mention recruiting "around 120 students," but please state the exact final sample size, the inclusion criteria, and how many people were excluded and why. A sentence or two linking each test to a specific hypothesis would help the reader follow your logic. Third, the reliability of your survey instrument is not reported. Include the Cronbach's alpha values for each scale, or cite the source that established them.

Also, watch your tense. Methods sections are usually written in the past tense because you are describing what you did, but parts of your draft shift into the present.

Overall, this is a solid foundation. Once you add the missing details, I think the section will be much stronger. Feel free to send a revised version, and I am happy to look at it again.
