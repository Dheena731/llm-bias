**NOTICE OF REVISED PRIVACY POLICY**

Spokeline Technologies Ltd. ("Spokeline," "we," "us") gives notice that its Privacy Policy (the "Policy") is revised effective **14 July 2025** (the "Effective Date"). The revised Policy applies to all users of the Spokeline app and website (collectively, the "Service").

**1. Summary of changes**

1.1 *Route history retention.* GPS route data was previously kept for 36 months. It is now deleted 90 days after the ride ends, unless you save the route to your account. Routes already older than 90 days on the Effective Date will be deleted on or before 14 August 2025.

1.2 *Payment processor.* Subscription billing has moved from Brackenfield Payments to Corvane Pay B.V., a processor based in the Netherlands. Card numbers are held by Corvane Pay and are not stored on Spokeline systems.

1.3 *Crash-reporting data.* Device identifiers are no longer attached to crash reports. Reports are now grouped by app version and operating system only.

1.4 *Marketing emails.* Marketing messages are off by default for new accounts. Existing accounts keep their current setting until you change it.

**2. Your choices**

2.1 If you do not accept the changes in Sections 1.1 through 1.3, you may close your account at any time before the Effective Date through Settings > Account > Delete. Closing your account before that date is treated as objection to the revised Policy and deletes your data under the prior retention schedule.

2.2 Continued use of the Service on or after the Effective Date constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

2.3 To object to the transfer of billing data to Corvane Pay, email privacy@spokeline.example within 30 days of this notice. We will respond within 30 days of receipt.

**3. Your rights**

Under applicable data protection law, including the UK GDPR, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may withdraw consent for marketing at any time. Requests should be sent to privacy@spokeline.example. We will verify your identity before acting on a request.
