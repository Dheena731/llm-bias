Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starting Next Monday

Hello everyone,

Starting next Monday, we are moving to a new expense approval workflow. Under the new process, all expense reports under $500 will be approved by your direct manager, while anything above that threshold will also require sign-off from the finance department. Submissions will be made through the existing expense system, but you will now receive an automatic notification at each stage so you can track where your request stands.

To prepare, please make sure any outstanding expense reports from the previous quarter are submitted by Friday so they can be processed under the old rules. Receipts should be uploaded as clear photos or scans, since reports missing documentation will be returned without review.

A short training session will be held Thursday at 2 p.m. in the main conference room, and a recording will be available afterward for anyone who cannot attend. If you have questions before then, please reach out to the finance operations team.

Thank you for your cooperation as we make this transition.

Best regards,
Administration Office
