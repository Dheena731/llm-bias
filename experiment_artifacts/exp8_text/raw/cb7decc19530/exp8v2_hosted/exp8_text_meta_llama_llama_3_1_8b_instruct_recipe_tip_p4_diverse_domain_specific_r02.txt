**Weeknight Lunch Hacks for Tech Teams**

When planning lunches for a busy week, consider the following:

*   **Prep for the most efficient meals**: On Monday, prep a batch of protein-rich foods like hard-boiled eggs, cooked chicken, or a big pot of lentils. This way, you can grab-and-go during the week.
*   **Incorporate leftovers into your meals**: Use last night's dinner as tomorrow's lunch by packing it in a container and adding a side salad or some fresh fruit.
*   **Don't forget the beverage budget**: Allocate time to prep a few water bottles or a thermos with your favorite hot beverage for the week.
