Using a calendar app can be a great way to stay organized, but it can also become cluttered and overwhelming if not used effectively. One tip for getting the most out of your calendar app is to set reminders for not just the events themselves, but also for the tasks you need to complete in preparation for those events. For example, if you have a meeting scheduled for next week, you might set a reminder for the day before to review your notes and prepare any materials you need to bring. This can help ensure that you are always prepared and that you don't forget important details.
