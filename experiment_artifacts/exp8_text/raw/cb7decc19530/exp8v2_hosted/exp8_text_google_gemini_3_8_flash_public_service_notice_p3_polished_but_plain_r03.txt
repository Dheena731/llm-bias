Please be advised that residential recycling collection schedules and sorting procedures will change beginning Monday, November 3. Under the updated schedule, pickup days for all neighborhoods will shift forward by one day. Households currently serviced on Tuesdays will now be collected on Mondays, and this pattern continues through the remainder of the week, with Friday collections moving to Thursday. In addition to the date change, paper and cardboard must now be separated from metal, glass, and plastic containers. All bins must be placed curbside no later than seven o'clock in the morning on your designated pickup day to ensure service. For complete route maps or questions regarding accepted materials, please call the municipal waste management office during regular business hours. We appreciate your cooperation as we implement these updates to improve local waste diversion efforts.
