**Reviewer 2, Major Concern 1:**

Section 1.2 never cleanly delineates the primary cognitive construct under investigation. The introduction vacillates between framing the paradigm as an assay of proactive cognitive control and treating it as a measure of spatial working memory capacity. Because both processes recruit overlapping frontoparietal cortex, this ambiguity leaves the core hypothesis unfalsifiable: virtually any cluster of BOLD activation in the dorsolateral prefrontal cortex or intraparietal sulcus can be (and later is) retrofitted to support the model. 

Before the analytical choices can be evaluated, the authors must commit to an explicit question. Are you testing whether *preparation interval* modulates rule-encoding fidelity, or are you measuring load-dependent distractor resistance? Please formulate a single, testable hypothesis and specify what dissociation—either in functional connectivity or regional response—would count as evidence *against* your account.
