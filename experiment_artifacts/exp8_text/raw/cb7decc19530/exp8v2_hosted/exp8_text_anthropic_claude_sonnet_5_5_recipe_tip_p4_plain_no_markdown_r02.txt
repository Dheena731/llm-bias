Pick one day, usually Sunday, to prepare the building blocks of your lunches rather than full meals. Cook a big batch of a grain such as rice or quinoa, roast a tray of vegetables, and prepare a protein like grilled chicken, hard-boiled eggs, or beans. Then wash and chop some raw vegetables and mix up a simple dressing or sauce. During the week you can combine these pieces in different ways, so Monday might be a grain bowl, Tuesday a wrap, and Wednesday a salad topped with the same protein. This keeps lunches from feeling repetitive, cuts down on daily cooking, and lets you adjust to whatever your schedule throws at you. Store everything in separate containers so it stays fresh, and pack the sauce on the side to keep your meals from getting soggy.
