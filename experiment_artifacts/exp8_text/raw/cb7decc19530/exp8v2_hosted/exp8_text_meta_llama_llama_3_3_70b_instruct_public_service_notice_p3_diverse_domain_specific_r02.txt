**Important Update: Recycling Collection Schedule Adjustment**

As part of our ongoing efforts to optimize waste management services, we are introducing changes to the recycling collection schedule in your area. Starting from March 1st, recyclable materials will be collected every other Thursday, instead of the current weekly schedule. This adjustment aims to improve the efficiency of our collection routes and reduce our carbon footprint.

To ensure a seamless transition, please note the following key changes:

* Recyclables will be collected on the 1st and 3rd Thursdays of each month
* Organic waste and bulk items will continue to be collected on the 2nd and 4th Thursdays
* The collection time remains unchanged, between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM

We kindly request that you update your recycling calendar and make the necessary adjustments to your waste disposal routine. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to our customer service team at (555) 123-4567 or [recycling@ourcity.gov](mailto:recycling@ourcity.gov).

Thank you for your cooperation and continued commitment to recycling and sustainability.
