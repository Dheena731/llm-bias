NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

This notice sets out the conditions under which customers may request a refund for purchases made from the company. By purchasing a product or service, the customer acknowledges and agrees to the terms described below.

A customer may request a full refund within fourteen (14) days of the date of purchase, provided that the product is unused, in its original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase. Services that have not yet commenced are eligible for a full refund within the same period. For services already in progress, the customer may be refunded a prorated amount reflecting the portion of the service not yet delivered.

Refunds are not available for custom or personalized items, digital downloads once accessed, perishable goods, or sale items marked as final. Where a product arrives damaged, defective, or materially different from its description, the customer may request a refund or replacement at no cost within thirty (30) days of delivery, regardless of the standard return period.

To request a refund, the customer must contact the company's customer support team and provide the order number, the reason for the request, and any supporting documentation, such as photographs in the case of damage. The company will review each request and respond within ten (10) business days. Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within fifteen (15) business days of approval. The company is not responsible for delays caused by the customer's financial institution.

The company reserves the right to decline a refund request that falls outside the stated conditions or that shows signs of misuse or fraud. In such cases, the company will provide the customer with written reasons for the decision. Nothing in this notice limits any rights the customer may have under applicable consumer protection law.

This notice is effective as of the date issued and supersedes all prior refund policies. The company may amend this notice at any time, and amendments will apply to purchases made after the date of the revised notice.
