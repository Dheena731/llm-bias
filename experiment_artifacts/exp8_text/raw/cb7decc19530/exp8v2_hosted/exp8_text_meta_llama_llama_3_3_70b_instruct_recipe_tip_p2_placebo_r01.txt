To keep fresh herbs fresh for a longer period, store them in a cool, dry place, wrapped in a damp paper towel and placed in an airtight container or plastic bag. This will help maintain the humidity around the herbs and prevent them from drying out. You can also trim the stems and stand the herbs in a glass of water, covering the leaves with a plastic bag to create a mini-greenhouse effect. This method is particularly effective for delicate herbs like basil and parsley. By following these steps, you can enjoy your fresh herbs for several days.
