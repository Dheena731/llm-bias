Here is a sample feedback note on a student's methods section:

"Your methods section is clear and well-organized, but there are a few areas that need improvement. 

You do a good job of explaining your study design and sample selection, but the section could benefit from more detail on how you measured and analyzed your variables. For example, what type of data collection methods did you use, and what statistical tests did you employ to analyze your data? Additionally, it would be helpful to include a diagram or flowchart to illustrate the study design and data flow.

Also, while your literature review is thorough, it would be stronger if you were to explicitly link it to your methods and results. How did your study design and data analysis approach relate to the existing research in the field?

Overall, your methods section provides a good foundation, but could benefit from a bit more nuance and detail. Keep working to develop this section and I'm confident it will be stronger by the next draft."
