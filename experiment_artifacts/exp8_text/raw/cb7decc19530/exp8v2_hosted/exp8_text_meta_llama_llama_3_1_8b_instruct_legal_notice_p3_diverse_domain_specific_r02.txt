**COOKIE USAGE NOTICE FOR BAKEHOUSE.ORG**

**Effective Date:** March 1, 2023

**Introduction:**

Bakehouse.org ("the Website") uses cookies to enhance the user experience, provide tailored content, and improve website performance. This notice explains how we use cookies, the types of cookies used, and how you can manage your cookie preferences.

**Cookie Purposes:**

We employ cookies for the following purposes:

1. **Authentication**: Cookies help us authenticate users, ensuring secure access to your account and personalized content.
2. **Session Management**: Cookies maintain session IDs, allowing us to track and manage user interactions on the Website.
3. **Content Personalization**: Cookies enable us to tailor content, such as recommending recipes based on your browsing history and preferences.
4. **Analytics**: Cookies provide us with insights into user behavior, helping us to improve the Website's performance, navigation, and overall user experience.

**Types of Cookies Used:**

1. **Session Cookies**: These cookies expire when you close your browser and do not store any personally identifiable information.
2. **Persistent Cookies**: These cookies remain on your device until they expire or are deleted. They may store information such as user preferences or login credentials.
3. **Third-Party Cookies**: We partner with third-party services, such as social media platforms and analytics tools, to provide additional features. These cookies may be subject to the terms and conditions of the respective third-party service.

**Managing Cookie Preferences:**

You have control over your cookie preferences. You can:

1. **Accept All Cookies**: By default, our cookies are enabled to provide the best possible user experience.
2. **Reject All Cookies**: You can disable all cookies through your browser settings. Please note that this may affect the functionality of certain Website features.
3. **Customize Cookie Preferences**: You can adjust your cookie preferences through our cookie management tool, available in the Website's footer.

**Changes to This Notice:**

We reserve the right to update this Cookie Usage Notice at any time. Changes will be effective upon publication on the Website.

If you have any questions or concerns about our Cookie Usage Notice, please do not hesitate to contact us at [info@bakehouse.org](mailto:info@bakehouse.org).
