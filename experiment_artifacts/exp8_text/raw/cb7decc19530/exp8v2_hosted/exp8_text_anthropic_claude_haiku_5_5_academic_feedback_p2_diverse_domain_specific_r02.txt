**Re: Methods draft (Section 2), stream nitrate and benthic macroinvertebrates**

Maya,

The sampling rationale is clear, and the choice to pair each nitrate reach with a reference reach upstream of the farm outflow is sound. A few things need attention before this goes to Dr. Okafor.

1. **Kick-net effort.** You write "three kick samples per site," but not how long each kick lasted or what area was covered. Without a fixed effort (e.g., 1 m² over 60 seconds), your richness counts aren't comparable across sites with different substrate. Please state the protocol you actually followed.

2. **Replication.** Your three kicks are combined into one sample per site, which is fine, but then the analysis section treats the 14 riffles as independent while two of them share a single reach. Clarify the unit of replication, and if reaches are nested within streams, say so.

3. **Timing.** The sampling dates span October 3 to October 19, and the field log notes a rain event on the 11th. Mention this in the methods, and say whether high-flow days were excluded or flagged. A storm can scour the riffles you're sampling.

4. **Taxonomic resolution.** You identify taxa "to family where possible." Report which groups were pushed to genus (Chironomidae were almost certainly not) and who did the identification. This matters for the richness metric.

5. **Missing specifics.** Give the mesh size of the net, the preservative and its concentration, and the method for nitrate measurement (instrument, detection limit, whether samples were filtered).

The overall structure is good. Once the effort, replication, and timing details are in, the design will be easy to evaluate. Send me a revised draft and I'll go through it again.
