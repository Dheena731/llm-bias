For dense vegetables like carrots, parsnips, or Brussels sprouts, apply salt 20–30 minutes before roasting. This interval allows salt to draw out surface moisture via osmosis; eliminating that water layer is essential for promoting Maillard browning rather than steaming when the vegetables hit the pan. Early salting also drives sodium deeper into the cellular structure, ensuring the seasoning permeates the flesh rather than resting superficially on the skin. Toss the vegetables in fat immediately after salting to emulsify the extracted juices, creating a uniform glaze that distributes heat and seasoning evenly during roasting.
