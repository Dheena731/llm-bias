Latency represents the time delay between a user initiating an action and receiving a response from a web application. This delay matters because users have developed an expectation for near-instantaneous interactions, and even minor lags can disrupt that sense of immediacy. When latency is high, pages take longer to load, forms submit slowly, and navigation feels choppy, which creates a perception of poor quality and unreliability. Frustrated users are more likely to abandon their tasks or switch to a competitor, directly harming engagement metrics and revenue. Furthermore, slow response times can negatively affect search engine rankings, reducing the application's discoverability. Minimizing latency ensures a fluid experience, maintains user trust, and allows the system to handle traffic efficiently without bottlenecks slowing down critical operations.
