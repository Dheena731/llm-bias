To plan lunches for a busy week, consider setting aside one day to prepare and portion out meals in advance. This can be as simple as cooking a large batch of rice, grains, or protein on the weekend, and then using these ingredients to assemble individual lunches for each day of the week. You can also prep vegetables, fruits, and snacks to add variety to your meals. By taking a little time to plan and prepare, you can save time during the week and ensure that you have healthy, satisfying lunches ready to go, even on the most hectic days.
