Subject: New expense approval workflow starting next month

Hello everyone,

Beginning on the first of next month, we will be moving all expense reports onto a new approval workflow. Under the new process, employees will submit expense reports through the finance portal, and each report will route automatically to the employee's direct manager for first-level approval. Reports above 2,500 dollars will also require sign-off from the department head before reimbursement is processed.

The goal of this change is to shorten reimbursement times and give managers clearer visibility into spending within their teams. Most of the steps remain familiar, but reports that sit unapproved for more than five business days will now trigger a reminder to the approver, and the finance team will be able to see where each report is in the queue.

Over the next two weeks, the finance team will hold short training sessions for each department. Please check your calendar for the invitation sent to your team lead, and reach out to the finance operations inbox with any questions before the rollout date.

Thank you for your cooperation as we make this transition.

Regards,
Administrative Operations
