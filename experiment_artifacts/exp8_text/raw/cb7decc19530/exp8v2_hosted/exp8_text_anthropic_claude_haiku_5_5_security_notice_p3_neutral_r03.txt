**Reminder: Lock Your Screen When Away From Your Desk**

Dear Colleagues,

Please remember to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your workstation, even for a short time. An unlocked screen can give unauthorized people access to confidential company information, client data, and internal systems.

**To lock your screen:**
- **Windows:** Press **Windows key + L**
- **Mac:** Press **Control + Command + Q**

Locking your screen is quick and protects everyone's information. Thank you for helping keep our workplace secure.
