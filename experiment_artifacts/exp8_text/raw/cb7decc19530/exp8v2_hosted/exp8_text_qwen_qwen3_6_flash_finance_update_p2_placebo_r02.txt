**Subject: Update to Card Transaction Fees**

Dear Merchants,

This note announces a revision to our card transaction fees. Starting next quarter, all standard credit and debit processing rates will be adjusted upward to reflect current payment network fees and system maintenance costs. The updated fees apply automatically to every qualifying sale; your billing statements will show the new amounts without requiring any account changes. A full breakdown of the revised rates is available in your merchant account portal. For questions regarding your specific transaction volume or pricing tier, please reach out to our merchant support team directly.

Thank you for your continued business.
