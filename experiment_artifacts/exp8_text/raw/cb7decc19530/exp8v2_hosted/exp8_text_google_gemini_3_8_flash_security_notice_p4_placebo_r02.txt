**Notice: Protect Your Account with Two-Factor Authentication**

To improve the security of your account, please enable two-factor authentication. 

Two-factor authentication is an added layer of protection that requires two separate verification steps to log in: your standard password and a secondary confirmation, such as a temporary numeric code sent to your mobile phone. This ensures that only you can access your account, even if someone else learns your password.

To turn on this protection, open your account settings and navigate to the security options to complete the setup.
