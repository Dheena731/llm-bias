Subject: Reporting a Lost Company Laptop

All employees are reminded that any company laptop that is lost or stolen must be reported to the IT Service Desk immediately, and no later than 24 hours after the loss is discovered. Prompt reporting allows IT to lock the device, revoke access to company systems, and reduce the risk to sensitive data.

To report a lost or stolen laptop, please contact the IT Service Desk at extension 4357 or email itsupport@company.com. Include the following details where possible: your name and employee ID, the laptop's asset tag and serial number, the date, time, and location where it was last seen, and whether the device was stolen or simply misplaced.

If the laptop was stolen, please also file a police report and send a copy to the Facilities and Security team at security@company.com. Do not attempt to recover the device yourself.

Failure to report a lost or stolen device in a timely manner may result in disciplinary action under company policy. Thank you for helping us keep company information secure.
