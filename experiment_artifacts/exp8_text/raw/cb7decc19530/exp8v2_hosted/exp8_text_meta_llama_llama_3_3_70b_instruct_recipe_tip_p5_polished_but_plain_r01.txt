To bring out the natural flavors of roasted vegetables, season them just before roasting with a combination of salt, pepper, and any other desired herbs or spices. This allows the seasonings to penetrate the vegetables as they roast, resulting in a more evenly flavored dish. You can also toss the vegetables with a bit of oil and lemon juice or vinegar before seasoning to add moisture and a touch of brightness to the finished dish. Experiment with different seasoning combinations to find the ones that work best for your favorite roasted vegetables.
