**Time for Your Weekly Fitness Check-In**

Take a few minutes today to review your progress this week:

- **Look back at your workouts.** How many did you complete compared with what you planned?
- **Note any improvements.** Did you lift more, walk farther, or feel stronger or less tired than before?
- **Identify challenges.** Were there days you missed or found difficult? Consider what got in the way.
- **Set your focus for next week.** Choose one or two realistic goals, such as adding a workout or going to bed earlier.

Progress isn't always a straight line. Reviewing your week helps you stay consistent and adjust your plan as needed.

Keep going, you're building healthy habits one week at a time!
