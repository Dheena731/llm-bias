**Quick-Stepping to Perfectly Cooked Noodles**

When cooking pasta or noodles for a weeknight dinner, don't overcook them in the pot. Instead, reserve about 1 cup of pasta water and then drain the noodles as you normally would. Then, add the reserved pasta water to the pot and stir in your sauce. This trick allows the noodles to finish cooking in the flavorful liquid, ensuring they stay moist and don't stick together. It's a simple technique that makes a big difference in the texture and taste of your meal.
