We are upgrading our virtual private network client to improve connection stability, performance, and security. Starting on Monday, all employees will need to use the new VPN client to access internal systems remotely. The new software has been automatically installed on company-managed devices and is ready for use. To connect, launch the new client from your applications menu and sign in using your standard single sign-on credentials. The previous VPN client will be retired at the end of the week and will no longer accept connections after that time. If you do not see the new client on your device or if you encounter any difficulties connecting, please contact the IT support team for assistance.
