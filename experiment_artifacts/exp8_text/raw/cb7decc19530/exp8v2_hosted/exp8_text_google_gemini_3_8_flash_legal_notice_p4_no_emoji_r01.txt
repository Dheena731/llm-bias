The following categories are expressly excluded from refund eligibility:
1. Digital products, downloadable content, and software licenses once accessed or delivered;
2. Customized, personalized, or made-to-order goods;
3. Gift cards and promotional credits; and
4. Any items explicitly designated as "Final Sale" or "As-Is" at the time of transaction.

Approved refunds will be credited exclusively to the original method of payment within ten (10) to fourteen (14) business days following receipt and inspection of the returned item(s).
