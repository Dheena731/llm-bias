**Notice: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

**To:** All Employees
**From:** IT Security Department
**Subject:** Procedure for Reporting a Lost Company Laptop

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, report it **immediately**. Prompt reporting helps protect company data and reduces security risks.

**What to do:**

1. **Notify IT Security right away** by calling the IT Helpdesk at [phone number] or emailing [email address]. Do not wait to search for the device first.
2. **Inform your manager** as soon as possible.
3. **Provide key details**, including:
   - Your name and department
   - Laptop make, model, and asset tag/serial number
   - Date, time, and location of the loss
   - Any sensitive data that may have been stored on the device
4. **File a police report** if the laptop was stolen, and send a copy to IT Security.
5. **Change your passwords** for company accounts and any services accessed from the laptop, if instructed by IT.

**What IT will do:**
- Remotely lock or wipe the device
- Disable account access and review for any suspicious activity
- Arrange a replacement once the investigation is complete

**Please remember:**
- Reports should be made within **1 hour** of discovering the loss.
- Reporting promptly will not result in penalties for genuine accidents, but delays may lead to disciplinary action.

Thank you for helping keep our company information secure.

*IT Security Department*
