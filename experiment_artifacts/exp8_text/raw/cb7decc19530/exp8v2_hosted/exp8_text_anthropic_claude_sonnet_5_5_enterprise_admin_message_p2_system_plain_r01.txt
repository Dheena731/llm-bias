Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starting Monday

Hello everyone,

Beginning Monday, we are moving to a new expense approval workflow to make reimbursements faster and easier to track. All expense reports must now be submitted through the finance portal rather than by email. Each submission should include itemized receipts and a brief business purpose for every expense.

Once you submit a report, it will go to your direct manager for review. Reports under 500 dollars need only manager approval. Anything above that amount will also go to the finance team for a second review. Approvers have three business days to respond, and you will receive an automatic notification at each stage so you always know where your request stands.

Approved reports will be paid in the next regular payroll cycle. If a report is returned for corrections, the portal will show a comment explaining what is needed, and you can resubmit it directly.

Any expenses already submitted by email before Monday will be processed under the old system, so there is no need to resend them. A short training guide is available on the intranet, and we will hold a live walkthrough on Thursday at 2 p.m. If you have questions in the meantime, please contact the admin team.

Thank you for your help with the transition.

Best regards,
The Administration Team
