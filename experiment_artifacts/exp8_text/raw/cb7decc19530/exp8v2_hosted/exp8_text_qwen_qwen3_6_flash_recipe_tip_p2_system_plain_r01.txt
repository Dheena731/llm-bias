To keep fresh herbs crisp and flavorful longer, trim the ends of the stems and place them upright in a glass with about an inch of water, just like you would arrange cut flowers. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and store them in the refrigerator. Change the water every two days to prevent spoilage, and this simple method will keep tender herbs like basil, cilantro, and parsley fresh for up to two weeks. For harder herbs such as rosemary and thyme, wrap them loosely in a slightly damp paper towel and place them inside a sealed container in the fridge instead.
