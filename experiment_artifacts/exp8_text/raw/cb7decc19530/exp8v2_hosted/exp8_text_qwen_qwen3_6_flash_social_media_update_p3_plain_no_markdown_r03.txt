Our annual neighborhood street fair takes place this Saturday starting at nine in the morning on Maple Avenue between First and Third Streets. There will be local vendors, live performances by school bands, and activities for children throughout the day. We encourage everyone to arrive early to secure a spot near the main stage. Bring your own chairs and blankets to relax on the grass during the concerts. All residents and visitors are invited to join us for a fun morning of food, music, and community connection.
