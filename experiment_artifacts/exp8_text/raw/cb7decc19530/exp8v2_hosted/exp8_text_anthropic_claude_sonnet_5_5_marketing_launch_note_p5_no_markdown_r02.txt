Subject: Our new language-learning podcast is live

Hello, language learners,

We're thrilled to announce that the first episodes of Tongue Twisters & Turning Points are now available on all major podcast platforms.

This show is built for people who want to learn a language without feeling like they're back in a classroom. Each week, we pair practical, real-world conversation practice with short stories about the people, places, and quirks behind the words you're learning. You'll pick up useful phrases, get a feel for how native speakers actually talk, and enjoy a few laughs along the way.

Here's what you can expect:

Three episodes at launch, so you can settle in right away. New episodes every Tuesday after that. Each one runs about twenty minutes, perfect for a commute, a walk, or a coffee break. Every episode includes a short recap of key vocabulary, which you can find in the show notes.

We're starting with Spanish, French, and Japanese, and we'll be adding more languages based on your requests. Tell us what you'd like to hear next by replying to this message or leaving a comment on our website.

If you enjoy the show, the best way to support us is to subscribe, leave a rating, and share an episode with a friend who's learning too. It helps more people find us.

Thank you for being part of this from the very start. We can't wait to learn alongside you.

Happy listening,

The Tongue Twisters & Turning Points team
