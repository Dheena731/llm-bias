Introducing Savings Goals

We're excited to announce a new feature to help you reach your financial targets: Savings Goals.

You can now create custom goals for anything you're saving toward, whether it's a vacation, a new laptop, or an emergency fund. Just name your goal, set a target amount, and choose a date if you'd like a deadline.

Here's what you can do:

- Track your progress with a simple visual bar that updates as you save
- Set up automatic transfers on a weekly or monthly schedule
- Create multiple goals and see them all in one place
- Get friendly reminders and milestone notifications when you hit 25, 50, and 75 percent

To get started, open the app, tap Savings, and select New Goal. It only takes a minute to set up.

Savings Goals is rolling out to all users over the next two weeks. If you don't see it yet, check back soon. We'd love to hear your feedback, so let us know what you think through the Help section in the app.

Happy saving!
