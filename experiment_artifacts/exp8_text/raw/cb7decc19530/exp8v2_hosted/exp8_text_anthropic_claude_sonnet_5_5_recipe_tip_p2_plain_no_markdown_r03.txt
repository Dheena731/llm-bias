To keep fresh herbs lasting longer, treat them according to their type. For soft herbs like parsley, cilantro, and dill, trim the stem ends and stand the bunch upright in a jar with an inch of water, much like cut flowers. Cover the leaves loosely with a plastic bag and keep the jar in the refrigerator, changing the water every couple of days. Basil is the exception, since it blackens in the cold, so leave it in a jar of water on the kitchen counter away from direct sun. For sturdier herbs like rosemary, thyme, and sage, wrap them in a barely damp paper towel, slip them into a loose bag or container, and store them in the refrigerator crisper drawer. Wash herbs only right before using them, because extra moisture speeds up spoilage.
