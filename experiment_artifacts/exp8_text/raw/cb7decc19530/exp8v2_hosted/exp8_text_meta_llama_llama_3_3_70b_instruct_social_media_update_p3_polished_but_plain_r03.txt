This weekend, our community is coming together to host a family-friendly event at the local park. The event will take place on Saturday from 10am to 2pm and will feature a variety of activities, including a bounce house, face painting, and a picnic area with food and drinks available for purchase. There will also be a raffle with prizes donated by local businesses. Admission is free, although donations to support local charities are appreciated. We hope to see you there as we celebrate our community and the people who make it a great place to live.
