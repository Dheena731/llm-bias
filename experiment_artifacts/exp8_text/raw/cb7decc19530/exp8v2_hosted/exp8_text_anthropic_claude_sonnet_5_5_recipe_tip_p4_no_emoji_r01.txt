**Tip: Cook once, assemble all week.**

On Sunday, prepare two or three components instead of full meals: a grain (rice, quinoa, or pasta), a protein (roasted chicken, baked tofu, or hard-boiled eggs), and a big tray of roasted vegetables. Store them in separate containers, then mix and match each day with a different sauce or topping, such as pesto, salsa, hummus, or a squeeze of lemon and olive oil.

This keeps lunches varied without requiring daily cooking, and it helps food stay fresher than fully assembled meals. Pack dressings on the side, and plan your last lunch of the week around freezer or pantry items so nothing goes to waste.
