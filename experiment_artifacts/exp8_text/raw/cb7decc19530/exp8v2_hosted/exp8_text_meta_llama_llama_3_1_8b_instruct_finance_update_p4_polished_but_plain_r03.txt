You will receive an end-of-year tax statement, which is a summary of your income and tax withholdings for the past year. This statement is based on the information we have on file, including your W-4 form and any adjustments we made during the year.

The statement will show your total income, the amount of taxes withheld from your paychecks, and the total tax paid for the year. It will also show any additional taxes you may have paid, such as through estimated tax payments or direct payments to the IRS.

You should review your statement carefully to ensure that it accurately reflects your income and tax withholdings. If you find any errors, please contact us immediately so that we can correct them.

You will need to use this statement when you file your annual tax return, which is typically due on or before April 15th. You may also need to provide this statement to other tax professionals, such as your accountant or financial advisor.

If you have any questions about your tax statement, please do not hesitate to contact us. We will be happy to explain any information or provide additional support as needed.
