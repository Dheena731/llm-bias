**Password Reuse Security Notice**

To all employees,

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain a secure work environment, we want to remind everyone of the importance of using unique and strong passwords for all company systems and accounts.

**Password Reuse is a Security Risk**

Using the same password for multiple accounts, including personal and company accounts, is a common security risk. If a hacker obtains one of your passwords, they can use it to access all of your accounts, putting sensitive company data and personal information at risk.

**Best Practices for Password Security**

* Use a unique password for each account.
* Use a password manager to generate and store complex passwords.
* Avoid using easily guessable information, such as your name or common words.
* Do not reuse passwords from previous employers or personal accounts.

**Company Requirements**

All company systems and accounts require unique and strong passwords. If you reuse a password, you may be asked to change it to a more secure alternative. Our IT department is available to assist you in creating strong, unique passwords.

Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining the security of our company's data.

**If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the IT department.**
