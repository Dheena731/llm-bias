Notice: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop

If your company laptop is lost or stolen, please report it to the IT Service Desk immediately, and no later than one hour after you discover it is missing. Prompt reporting allows us to lock the device remotely, disable your account access, and reduce the risk that company or customer information will be exposed.

When you call or email the IT Service Desk, please be ready to give your name and department, the laptop's asset tag or serial number if you know it, the date, time, and location where you last had it, and a brief description of what happened. If you believe the laptop contained sensitive data, tell us that as well. You do not need to determine exactly what was stored on it before you report the loss.

If the laptop was stolen, please also file a report with the local police and send the report number to IT Security as soon as you receive it. If the loss happened while you were traveling, let your manager know as well so that any business impact can be addressed.

Please do not wait to see whether the laptop turns up before contacting us. Reporting a device that is later found causes no problem, while a delayed report can create serious security concerns. You will not be penalized for reporting a loss in good faith and on time.

Once your report is received, IT will confirm that the device has been locked or wiped, reset your passwords, and arrange a replacement laptop as quickly as possible. If you have questions about this process, contact the IT Service Desk at any time.
