Join your neighbors this Saturday for the annual Spring Community Fair at Central Park from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. The afternoon will feature local food trucks, live acoustic music, crafts from neighborhood vendors, and activities for children of all ages. Admission is free, and complimentary parking is available in the community center lot on Oak Street. Bring a lawn blanket and spend the day celebrating our town together. We look forward to seeing you there.
