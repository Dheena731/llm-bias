Season your vegetables in two stages for the best flavor. Before roasting, toss them with enough oil to coat them lightly, along with a generous pinch of salt and some pepper, so the seasoning clings and the edges caramelize evenly. Then, once they come out of the oven, taste one and finish with a small sprinkle of flaky salt and a bright touch such as a squeeze of lemon juice or a splash of vinegar. Delicate ingredients like fresh herbs and garlic can burn at high heat, so add them in the last few minutes of cooking or stir them in right after roasting.
