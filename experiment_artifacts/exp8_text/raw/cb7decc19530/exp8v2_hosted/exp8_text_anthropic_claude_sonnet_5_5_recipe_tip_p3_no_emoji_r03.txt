**Rinse your rice before cooking.** Place the rice in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer and rinse it under cold water, swirling with your hand, until the water runs mostly clear (usually 3 to 5 rinses). This washes away the excess surface starch that makes grains clump together.

For even better results, use slightly less water than the package suggests (about 1.5 parts water to 1 part long-grain rice), and let the cooked rice rest, covered and off the heat, for 10 minutes before fluffing it gently with a fork.
