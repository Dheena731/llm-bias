Here is a sample short academic feedback note on a draft literature review:

"Your draft literature review provides a solid foundation for a comprehensive analysis of the existing research on [topic]. The selection of key studies is thorough, and the review effectively highlights the main themes and debates in the field. However, there are a few areas that require further attention.

Firstly, the review could benefit from a clearer structure and introduction that explicitly outlines the research question and objectives. This would help to establish the review's purpose and relevance to the broader field of study.

Secondly, while the review covers a wide range of studies, some of the summaries and analyses could be more concise and critically engaged. For instance, some studies are summarized without sufficient critique or contextualization, which could make it difficult for the reader to fully understand the significance of the findings.

Lastly, the review would benefit from a more explicit discussion of the limitations and methodological approaches of the studies included. This would add depth and nuance to the review, and enable a more nuanced evaluation of the findings.

Overall, this is a promising start, and with some revisions, this review has the potential to make a valuable contribution to the field. Please revise and resubmit by [deadline]."

This feedback note provides constructive criticism and suggestions for improvement, while also acknowledging the strengths of the draft review.
