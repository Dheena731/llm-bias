To keep rice from turning sticky, rinse it in cold water before cooking. Place the rice in a bowl or fine mesh strainer, cover it with water, swish it around with your hand, and drain. Repeat until the water runs mostly clear, usually three or four times. This washes away the loose surface starch that causes grains to clump together. Next, use a little less water than you might think you need, about one and a half cups of water for each cup of long grain rice. Bring it to a boil, then cover and reduce the heat to low, and resist the urge to lift the lid or stir while it cooks, since stirring releases more starch. When the rice is done, let it rest off the heat for about ten minutes, then fluff it gently with a fork to let the steam escape and keep the grains separate.
