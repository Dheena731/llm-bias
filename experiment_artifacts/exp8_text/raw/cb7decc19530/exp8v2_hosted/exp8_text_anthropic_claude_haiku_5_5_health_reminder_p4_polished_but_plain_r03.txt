Take a moment to step away from your screen. Long stretches of looking at a monitor or phone can leave your eyes tired, your neck stiff, and your mind foggy, even when the work feels routine. A short break, even just two or three minutes, can ease that strain and help you return with sharper focus.

Try following the 20-20-20 rule: every twenty minutes, look at something about twenty feet away for at least twenty seconds. Blink a few times on purpose, since we blink less when staring at a screen. Then stand up, stretch your shoulders, roll your neck gently, and walk to get some water or fresh air if you can.

These small pauses add up. They protect your eyes and posture, reduce the pull of constant notifications, and often lead to better ideas than pushing through without rest. Set a gentle timer if it helps, and treat the break as part of your work rather than a distraction from it.
