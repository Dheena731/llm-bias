Please be advised that our new expense approval workflow will take effect on Monday. This update is designed to simplify the submission process, improve tracking, and reduce the time it takes for reimbursements to reach your account. 

Moving forward, all business expenses must be submitted directly through the internal finance portal, where requests will automatically route to your designated manager for review before final processing by accounts payable. Managers are expected to review and approve submissions within three business days to avoid processing delays. Outstanding requests submitted prior to Monday will be handled under the previous guidelines, but all subsequent claims must use the new workflow. 

For questions, guidance, or system support, please contact the finance department.
