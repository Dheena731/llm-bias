Instead of spending hours assembling identical pre-portioned meals, focus on preparing versatile base ingredients in batches. Roast a large tray of sturdy vegetables, cook a pot of a hearty grain like farro or brown rice, and prepare a simple, well-seasoned protein such as baked chicken or spiced chickpeas. Keep these components in separate containers alongside two contrasting sauces or dressings. Each morning, you can combine these elements into a grain bowl, toss them over fresh greens, or roll them into a wrap, giving you a satisfying meal in minutes without the monotony of eating the exact same dish all week.
