**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY**

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for [Area Name] from [Start Time] on [Start Date] through [End Time] on [End Date]. Temperatures are expected to reach [Temperature] degrees, with heat index values up to [Heat Index] degrees.

Extended exposure to high heat can cause heat exhaustion and heat stroke. To protect yourself and your family:

- Stay indoors in air-conditioned spaces whenever possible.
- Drink water regularly, even if you do not feel thirsty.
- Avoid strenuous outdoor activity during the hottest part of the day, typically between 11 a.m. and 4 p.m.
- Never leave children, pets, or anyone who relies on you in a parked vehicle.
- Check on elderly neighbors, people with chronic illnesses, and anyone without air conditioning.
- Wear lightweight, loose-fitting, light-colored clothing and use sunscreen.

Watch for warning signs of heat illness, including heavy sweating, dizziness, nausea, headache, and confusion. If someone shows signs of heat stroke, such as a body temperature above 103 degrees, hot dry or damp skin, or loss of consciousness, call 911 immediately.
