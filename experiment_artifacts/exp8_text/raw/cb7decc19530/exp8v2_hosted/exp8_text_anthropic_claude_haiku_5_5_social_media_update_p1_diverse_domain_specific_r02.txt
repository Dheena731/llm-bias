Mechanics kept getting asked the same thing at the counter: "Has this one been through the shop yet?" Starting today, the app answers that. Every loaner bike now shows its last brake check, tire swap, and the initials of whoever signed off. We added it after a Tuesday when two riders took out the same wobbly rear wheel. Look under the frame size on any bike's page. You still need to squeeze the levers yourself, but now you'll know who last did.
