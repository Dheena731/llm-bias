PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE ON MAPLE AVENUE

The City Department of Public Works advises residents, businesses, and motorists that Maple Avenue will be closed to all through traffic between Fourth Street and Elm Road from Monday, June 9, at 7:00 a.m. until Friday, June 20, at 6:00 p.m. The closure is necessary to allow crews to replace an aging water main and resurface the roadway.

During this period, drivers should use Oak Street and Highland Drive as the main detour route. Signs will be posted at each end of the closure and at major intersections along the detour. Please allow extra travel time, particularly during morning and evening peak hours, and consider using alternate routes where possible.

Local access will be maintained for residents and businesses within the work zone, though short delays should be expected while equipment is moving. Driveways may be temporarily blocked for brief periods during excavation and paving. Crews will notify affected property owners in advance, and residents are encouraged to move vehicles off the street before 7:00 a.m. on workdays. Vehicles left on the roadway may be relocated at the owner's expense.

Garbage and recycling collection will continue on the regular schedule, but containers should be placed at the nearest intersection outside the work area on collection days. Emergency vehicles will have access at all times, and the city has coordinated with fire, police, and ambulance services to ensure response times are not affected. Bus Route 12 will be rerouted along Oak Street, with temporary stops marked along the detour.

Work is weather dependent, and the schedule may change if there are heavy rains or unforeseen conditions. Updates will be posted on the city website and announced through the city's public information line. For questions or concerns, please contact the Department of Public Works at 555-0142 during business hours, Monday through Friday, between 8:00 a.m. and 4:30 p.m.

The city thanks the community for its patience and cooperation while this important infrastructure work is completed.
