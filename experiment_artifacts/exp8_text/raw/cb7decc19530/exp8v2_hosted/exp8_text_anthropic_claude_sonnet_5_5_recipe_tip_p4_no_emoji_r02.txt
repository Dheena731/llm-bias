**Tip: Prep components, not full meals.**

On Sunday, spend 30 to 45 minutes cooking a few versatile building blocks: a grain (rice, quinoa, or pasta), a protein (roasted chicken, baked tofu, or hard-boiled eggs), and a tray of roasted vegetables. Wash and chop some raw produce too, plus make one sauce or dressing.

Each day, mix and match into something different, such as a grain bowl on Monday, a wrap on Tuesday, and a salad on Wednesday. This keeps lunches from getting boring, cuts down on daily decisions, and lets you use up leftovers before they spoil. Store everything in separate containers so ingredients stay fresh and you can assemble lunch in under five minutes.
