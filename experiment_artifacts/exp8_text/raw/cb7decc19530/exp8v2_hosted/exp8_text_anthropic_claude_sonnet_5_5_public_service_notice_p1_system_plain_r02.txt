Notice to Residents: Updated Library Opening Hours

Beginning the first Monday of next month, the Riverside Public Library will adopt new opening hours at all branches. These changes follow a community survey in which residents asked for more evening and weekend access.

The main library will be open Monday through Thursday from nine in the morning until eight in the evening. On Fridays, doors will open at nine and close at five. Saturday hours will run from ten in the morning until four in the afternoon, and the library will now open on Sundays from one until five.

The Eastgate and Millbrook branches will be open Tuesday through Saturday, from ten in the morning until six in the evening, and will remain closed on Sundays and Mondays.

Please note that the library will be closed on public holidays. Hours may also be shortened on the days before major holidays, and any such changes will be posted at the entrance and on the library website at least one week in advance.

Book drops will remain available at all times. Items returned after closing will be checked in on the next business day, and no late fees will be charged for materials placed in the drop during a closure.

Residents who need help with accessibility services, such as large-print materials, assistive technology, or home delivery, may call the front desk during opening hours to make arrangements. Staff will be glad to assist.

We thank the community for its feedback and look forward to welcoming you at the new times.

Riverside Public Library Administration
