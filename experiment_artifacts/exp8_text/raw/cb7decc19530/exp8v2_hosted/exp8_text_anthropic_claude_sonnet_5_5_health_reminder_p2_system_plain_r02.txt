A quick reminder to keep your water bottle close today. Try to sip steadily from morning to evening rather than waiting until you feel thirsty, since thirst often means you are already a little behind. A glass when you wake up, one with each meal, and another whenever you take a break will take you most of the way there. If plain water gets dull, a slice of lemon or cucumber can make it more appealing. Your energy, focus, and mood will thank you for it.
