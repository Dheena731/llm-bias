The Department of Public Works announces that Main Street will be closed to all through traffic between Fourth Avenue and Seventh Avenue from Monday, October 21, through Friday, October 25, between the hours of 7:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. daily. This temporary closure is necessary to complete underground utility repairs and subsequent roadway resurfacing. A clearly marked detour will redirect drivers along Pine Street and Eighth Avenue. Local access will be maintained for residents and business owners within the work zone, though motorists should anticipate minor delays. Commuters are encouraged to plan alternate routes, allow extra travel time, and proceed with caution around road crews and heavy equipment. The city appreciates the community's patience and cooperation while these essential infrastructure improvements are completed.
