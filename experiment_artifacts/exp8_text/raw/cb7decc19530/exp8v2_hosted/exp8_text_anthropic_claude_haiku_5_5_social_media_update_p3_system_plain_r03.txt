Hi neighbors! This Saturday, April 12, join us at Riverside Park from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. for our annual Community Day. There will be live music, food trucks, a kids' craft corner, and a plant swap by the pavilion. Bring a friend, bring a chair, and come say hello. Everyone is welcome, and admission is free. If you have questions or want to volunteer, send us a message or stop by the info table near the entrance. We hope to see you there!
