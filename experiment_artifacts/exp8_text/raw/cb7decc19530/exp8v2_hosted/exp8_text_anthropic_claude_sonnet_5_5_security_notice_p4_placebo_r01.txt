**Notice: Please Turn On Two-Factor Authentication**

We encourage all account holders to enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on their accounts.

**What it is:** 2FA adds a second step to signing in. After you enter your password, you also provide something else, such as a code from an authenticator app, a code sent by text message, or a physical security key.

**Why it matters:** Passwords can be guessed, stolen, or reused from another website. With 2FA on, someone who has your password still cannot sign in without the second step.

**How to enable it:**
1. Sign in and open your account's security settings.
2. Select the two-factor authentication option and choose a method.
3. Follow the on-screen prompts and confirm that it works.
4. If you receive backup codes, store them somewhere safe and separate from your password. You can use them to sign in if you lose access to your usual method.

Menu names may differ slightly depending on your device or the page you are using.

**Keep in mind:** Never share your verification codes with anyone, including people who say they are from support.
