Here is a calendar tip that has saved me a surprising amount of stress: schedule your tasks, not just your meetings. Most of us use a calendar app only for appointments, then keep a separate to-do list that quietly grows out of control. Instead, take the three or four things that truly matter tomorrow and give each one a time block on your calendar. Write the task as a clear action, such as "Draft the project proposal," and set the length based on how long it will realistically take. When a task has a place in your day, it stops competing with everything else in your head. You also see right away when you have planned more than a day can hold, which makes it easier to say no or move something before it becomes a problem. If a block gets bumped, drag it to another slot instead of letting it fall off your radar. A few minutes of planning each evening can make the next day feel far more manageable.
