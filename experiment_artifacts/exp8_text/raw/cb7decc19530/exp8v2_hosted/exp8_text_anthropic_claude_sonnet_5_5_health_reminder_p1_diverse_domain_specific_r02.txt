**Weekly check-in: 5 minutes**

Before you plan next week, look at this one.

- **Sessions:** How many did you finish out of how many you planned? If you missed one, note whether it was time, sleep, or a sore joint. The reason matters more than the count.
- **Top sets:** Did your heaviest working set, longest run, or fastest split move compared with last week? If three lifts in a row stalled, consider a lighter week.
- **Bodyweight:** Look at the 7-day average, not this morning's number.
- **Recovery:** Check your sleep and resting heart rate for any drift in the wrong direction.

Choose one change for next week, such as adding 2.5 kg to squats, moving Thursday's session, or cutting a set from accessories. Then log it.
