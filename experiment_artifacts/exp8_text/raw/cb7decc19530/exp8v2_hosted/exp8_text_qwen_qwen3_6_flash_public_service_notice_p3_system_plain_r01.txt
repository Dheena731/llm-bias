Please be advised that the recycling collection schedule is changing effective October first. Residents who previously had pickups on Monday will now receive service on Tuesday. All carts must be placed at the curb by six in the morning on the assigned day and retrieved by eight in the evening. Bins left out after collection hours may not be emptied until the following week. For further information regarding this update or special pickup requests, please contact the public works department at the number listed on your monthly billing statement. We appreciate your patience and continued participation in the recycling program.
