NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS FOR UPCOMING ELECTION

All registered voters are reminded that polling stations will be open on Election Day, November 5, from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. To locate your assigned voting site, visit www.voterinfo.gov or call the Elections Office at 555-019-2847. Your official ballot will list your specific precinct and nearest station based on your current residential address. Please arrive early if you plan to vote during peak afternoon hours.

Voters must present a valid photo identification and proof of residence. All polling locations are wheelchair accessible and will provide language assistance materials upon request. If you have recently moved or believe your name is missing from the voter rolls, report to the provisional voting table inside the building. Failure to check in at your correct precinct may result in a provisional ballot being issued.

Thank you for participating in our local elections. For additional questions regarding transportation options or accessible voting accommodations, contact the Voter Assistance Hotline at 555-019-2850.

Issued by: County Board of Elections
