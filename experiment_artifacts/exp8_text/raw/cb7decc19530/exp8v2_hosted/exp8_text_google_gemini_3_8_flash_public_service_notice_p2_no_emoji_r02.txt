**PUBLIC NOTICE: TEMPORARY ROAD CLOSURE**

Please be advised that Main Street, between 1st Avenue and Elm Street, will be closed to all through traffic beginning Monday, October 14, at 7:00 AM, through Wednesday, October 16, at 5:00 PM. 

This closure is required to complete urgent water main repairs and resurfacing work. 

A marked detour route will be available via Oak Street and 3rd Avenue. Local access will be maintained for residents and emergency services, but drivers should expect delays and are advised to seek alternate routes. 

For questions or additional information, please contact the City Department of Public Works at (555) 019-2834 or visit www.cityworks.gov/alerts.
