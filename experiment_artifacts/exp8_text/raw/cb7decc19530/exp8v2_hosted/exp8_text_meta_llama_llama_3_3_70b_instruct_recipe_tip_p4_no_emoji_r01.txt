Consider planning and preparing your lunches for the week on the weekend or one day a week. Take some time to think about what you want to eat, make a list, and go shopping for the necessary ingredients. You can then prep individual portions of meals such as salads, soups, or sandwiches, and store them in containers in the fridge or freezer for easy grab-and-go lunches throughout the week. This can save you time and money, and help you eat healthier even on your busiest days.
