**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY**

A heat advisory is in effect. Hot weather and high humidity can make people sick, especially during long periods outdoors. Stay alert and take precautions until the advisory is lifted.

**To stay safe:**
- Drink water regularly, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and sugary drinks.
- Stay in air-conditioned spaces when you can. If you do not have air conditioning, go to a cooler place such as a library, shopping center, or public cooling center.
- Limit strenuous activity outdoors, especially during the hottest part of the day. If you must work or exercise outside, take frequent breaks in the shade.
- Never leave children, older adults, or pets alone in a parked car, even for a short time.
- Check on older adults, people who live alone, and others who may need help staying cool.

**Warning signs of heat illness:**
- Heat exhaustion: heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, nausea, headache, or cold, clammy skin. Move to a cool place, sip water, and loosen tight clothing.
- Heat stroke: confusion, very high body temperature, hot and dry or damp skin, fainting, or seizures. This is a medical emergency. Call 911 right away.
