To: All Staff

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain a secure working environment, we would like to remind everyone of the importance of password security. It has come to our attention that some staff members may be reusing passwords across multiple systems and applications.

Please be aware that reusing passwords can significantly increase the risk of unauthorized access to our systems and data. If a password is compromised in one system, it can be used to gain access to other systems where the same password is used.

To protect our workplace and sensitive information, we strongly advise against reusing passwords. Instead, please use unique and complex passwords for each system and application. Additionally, please ensure that you change your passwords regularly and avoid using easily guessable information such as your name, birthdate, or common words.

Your cooperation and attention to password security are crucial in maintaining the integrity of our systems and data. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT department.
