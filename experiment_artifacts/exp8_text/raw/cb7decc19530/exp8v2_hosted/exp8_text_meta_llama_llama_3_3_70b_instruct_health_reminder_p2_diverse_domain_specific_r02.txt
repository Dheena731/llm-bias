Staying hydrated is crucial for maintaining focus and productivity throughout the day. As you go about your tasks, remember to take regular sips of water to replenish lost fluids. Aim to drink at least 8-10 glasses of water, spaced out over the course of the day. This will help regulate your body temperature, transport nutrients to cells, and even support cognitive function. Make it a habit to keep a refillable water bottle nearby, and take a few minutes to stretch and hydrate every hour or so. Your body - and mind - will thank you!
