Subject: Introducing the new monthly spending summary

We have launched a monthly spending summary that gives you a clear picture of where your money went over the past month. Starting this month, you will receive a summary on the first day of each month, and you can also view it at any time from the Reports section of your account.

The summary groups your transactions into categories such as housing, groceries, transportation, dining, and subscriptions. For each category, it shows your total spending, how that compares with the previous month, and whether you are on track against any budget you have set. A short overview at the top highlights the largest changes so you can see at a glance what deserved your attention.

All of your data stays within your existing account settings, and you can turn off the email delivery or change the categories at any time. If a transaction appears in the wrong category, you can correct it directly from the summary, and the change will carry forward to future reports.

We would appreciate your feedback. If something in the summary is unclear or missing, please reply to this message or use the feedback option in the Reports section. We plan to refine the layout and add year-to-date views based on what we hear from you over the coming weeks.
