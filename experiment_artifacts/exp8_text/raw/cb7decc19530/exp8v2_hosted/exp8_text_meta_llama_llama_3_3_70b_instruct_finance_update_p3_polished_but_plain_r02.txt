We are excited to announce the introduction of a new savings goal feature that allows users to set and track their savings objectives. This feature provides a clear and concise way to define and work towards specific financial targets, such as building an emergency fund, paying off debt, or saving for a major purchase. Users can easily set a goal amount and deadline, and then track their progress over time. The feature also provides regular reminders and updates to help users stay on track and motivated to reach their goals. By providing a simple and effective way to manage savings goals, we aim to help users take control of their finances and achieve long-term financial stability.
