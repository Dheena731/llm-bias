We are excited to announce the launch of a new savings goal feature in our mobile banking app. This feature allows users to set and track specific savings targets, such as building an emergency fund, paying for a vacation, or making a down payment on a house. With this feature, users can easily create and manage multiple savings goals, set deadlines, and allocate funds from their checking account. The app will also provide regular progress updates and reminders to help users stay on track and reach their goals. This new feature is available to all existing and new users, and can be accessed by logging into the app and navigating to the "Savings Goals" section.
