You should refill your prescription before you run out of medication to ensure you do not miss any doses. Check the label on your current bottle to see how many refills you have left and when your prescription is set to expire. If you need a new prescription, contact your doctor to obtain one. You can then take the new prescription to your pharmacy to have it filled. It is also a good idea to set a reminder for yourself a few days before you are set to run out of medication so you can allow time for the refill to be processed.
