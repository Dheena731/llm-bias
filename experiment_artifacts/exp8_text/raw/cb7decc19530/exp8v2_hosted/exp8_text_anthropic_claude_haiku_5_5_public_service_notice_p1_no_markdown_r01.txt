PUBLIC NOTICE: LIBRARY OPENING HOURS

The Riverside Public Library wants to remind all residents of our current opening hours. The library is open Monday through Friday from 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., Saturday from 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., and Sunday from 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. The library is closed on all major public holidays.

Please note that the reference desk closes thirty minutes before the library itself, and the computer lab requires a fifteen-minute reservation window. For questions about holiday hours or special events, please call (555) 010-2740 or visit the library's front desk during open hours.

Library cards are free for all residents, and our staff is always glad to help you find your next great read.
