Here's a short tip for planning lunches for a busy week:

**Prep on the Weekend**

Designate a specific time on the weekend (e.g. Sunday afternoon) to plan and prep lunches for the upcoming week. Here's how:

1. **Take stock**: Check what ingredients you already have at home, what leftovers you can use, and what fresh produce needs to be used up.
2. **Choose themes**: Decide on a few themes for the week (e.g. Mediterranean, Mexican, or soup-based meals).
3. **Plan meals**: Select 5-7 meals that fit your themes and make a list of the ingredients you'll need to buy.
4. **Prep ahead**: Chop veggies, cook proteins, and assemble salads or wraps. Store them in airtight containers in the fridge or freezer.
5. **Pack and go**: On Monday morning, grab your prepped lunches and head out the door with confidence!

By prepping on the weekend, you'll save time during the week, reduce food waste, and ensure healthy, delicious meals even on the busiest of days.
