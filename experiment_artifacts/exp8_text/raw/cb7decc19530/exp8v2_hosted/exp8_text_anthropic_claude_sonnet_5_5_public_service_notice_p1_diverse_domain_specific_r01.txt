**NOTICE TO PATRONS**
**Changes to opening hours, effective Monday 3 March**
Calderbank Public Library, Main Branch and Eastfield Reading Room

**Main Branch, 14 Tanners Row**
- Monday: Closed (was 10–6)
- Tuesday to Friday: 10–7 on Tuesday and Thursday, 10–5 on Wednesday and Friday
- Saturday: 9–1 (was 9–4)
- Sunday: Closed

**Eastfield Reading Room, inside the Eastfield Community Centre**
- Tuesday and Thursday: 2–7
- Saturday: 10–2
- Closed all other days, and whenever the community centre hall is booked for an election or polling day

**Why these changes**
Two full-time posts have gone unfilled since November. Closing Mondays lets us keep the Tuesday and Thursday evening sessions, which are our busiest for homework help and job-search computer use. Saturday afternoons had the lowest attendance of any shift last year, averaging 11 visitors an hour.

**What stays the same**
- The book drop outside Tanners Row is emptied every morning, including Mondays. Items returned over the weekend will not be fined.
- Holds can be collected during any open hour. Holds not collected within 7 days go back on the shelf.
- Printing, scanning and the public computers are available until 30 minutes before closing. Sessions can be booked at the desk or by phone.

**Holiday closures**
Good Friday, Easter Saturday and Easter Monday (both branches). Both branches also close at 1 p.m. on Thursday 1 May for staff training.

**If you can't get here during these hours**
Call 01632 555 0148 and ask about the home delivery service for patrons who are housebound or caring for someone who is. The e-book and audiobook catalogue is available at all times with your library card number.

Comments on the new schedule can be left at either desk or sent to the Library Manager at the address above. The hours will be reviewed in September.
