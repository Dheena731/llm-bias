When seasoning roasted vegetables, it is best to do so just before serving to ensure the flavors stay vibrant and fresh. Simply toss the roasted vegetables with a pinch of salt, a few grinds of pepper, and any other desired herbs or spices, such as dried thyme, paprika, or garlic powder. You can also squeeze a bit of fresh lemon juice over the vegetables for a burst of brightness and acidity. This simple step can elevate the natural sweetness of the roasted vegetables and add depth and complexity to the dish.
