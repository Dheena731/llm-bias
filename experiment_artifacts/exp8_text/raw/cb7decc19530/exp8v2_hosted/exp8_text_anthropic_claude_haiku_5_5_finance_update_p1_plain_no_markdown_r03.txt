We are pleased to introduce the new monthly spending summary, a feature designed to help you see where your money goes at a glance. At the start of each month, you will receive a short report covering the previous month's transactions. It groups your spending into clear categories such as housing, groceries, transportation, dining, and subscriptions, and shows how each compares with your average over the past three months.

The summary also highlights any unusual charges, such as a payment that is much larger than usual or a recurring subscription that has changed price. If you have set a budget for a category, you will see how close you came to that limit, so you can adjust your plans before the next month begins.

You can find the summary in the Reports section of your account, and you can choose whether to receive a notification by email when it is ready. You can also turn the feature off at any time in your settings.

We hope this summary makes it easier to understand your habits and plan with confidence. As always, if you have questions or feedback, our support team is happy to help.

Thank you for being part of our community.
