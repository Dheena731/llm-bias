Please be advised that residential recycling collection schedules will change beginning on Monday, November third. Collections previously scheduled for Tuesdays will now take place on Wednesdays, while all other pickup days remain the same. In addition, clean cardboard and paper may now be placed in the same container as glass, metal, and plastic. Please ensure your recycling bins are placed at the curb by seven in the morning on your designated collection day. If you have questions about your specific route, please contact the municipal services office by phone or visit the local town hall during normal business hours.
