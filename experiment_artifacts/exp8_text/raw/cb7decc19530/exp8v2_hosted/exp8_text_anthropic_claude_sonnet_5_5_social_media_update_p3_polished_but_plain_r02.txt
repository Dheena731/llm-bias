Join us this Saturday for the Riverside Community Day, a free gathering for neighbors of all ages. Doors open at 10 a.m. at Maple Park, and activities will run until 4 p.m. Expect live music from local bands, a family-friendly fun run at 11, a community garden showcase, and more than twenty food and craft vendors from around the neighborhood. Children can enjoy face painting and a petting zoo, while adults can browse the book swap or chat with representatives from local organizations about volunteer opportunities.

Parking is limited, so we encourage you to walk, bike, or take the number 12 bus, which stops right at the park entrance. Please bring a reusable water bottle, as refill stations will be available throughout the grounds. In case of rain, the event will move to the Riverside Recreation Center on Elm Street, and we will post any changes here by 8 a.m. Saturday morning. We would love to see you there, so bring a friend, meet a neighbor, and help make the day a success.
