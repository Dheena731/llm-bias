Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations

Residents are advised that polling stations for the upcoming election will be open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on election day. Each voter is assigned to a specific polling station based on their home address, and voters are encouraged to confirm their assigned location before they travel. Your voter information card, which was mailed to your registered address, lists the name and address of your station. If you have misplaced your card or did not receive one, you may find your location by contacting the county elections office by telephone during business hours or by visiting the office in person.

Most polling stations are located in familiar public buildings such as schools, libraries, community centers, and places of worship. All stations are accessible to voters with disabilities, and curbside voting is available for anyone who cannot enter the building. Accessible parking and seating are provided at every location, and election staff will be on hand to assist voters who need help.

Please note that some locations have changed since the last election because of building renovations and changes in precinct boundaries. Even if you have voted at the same place in the past, check your card or contact the elections office to be sure your station has not moved. Voters who arrive at the wrong station may be directed elsewhere, which could cause delays, and anyone in line when the polls close at 8:00 p.m. will still be permitted to vote.

Please bring a valid form of identification, and allow extra time during the busiest hours, which are typically early morning and just after the end of the standard workday. Thank you for taking part in the election.
