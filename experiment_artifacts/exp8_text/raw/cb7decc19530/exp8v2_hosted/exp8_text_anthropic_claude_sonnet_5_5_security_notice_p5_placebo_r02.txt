**Notice: Reporting a Lost Company Laptop**

**To all employees:**

If your company laptop is lost or stolen, report it immediately to your manager and the IT department. Do not wait to see if it turns up. A lost laptop may hold confidential company or customer information, and quick reporting helps protect that information.

**What to include in your report:**
- Your name and the laptop's make and model, if you know them
- When and where you last had it
- How it went missing, and whether you think it was lost or stolen
- What kind of company information was on it, as far as you know

**What to do:**
1. Tell your manager and IT as soon as you realize the laptop is missing.
2. Follow any instructions IT gives you.
3. Do not try to recover a stolen laptop yourself.

Reporting promptly will not be held against you. Delays make it harder to protect company information.
