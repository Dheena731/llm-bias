**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**

To keep your account secure, we strongly encourage all users to turn on two-factor authentication. 2FA adds a second verification step when you log in, so your account stays protected even if your password is compromised.

**How to enable 2FA:**
1. Go to **Settings > Security**.
2. Select **Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Choose your preferred method: an authenticator app (recommended) or text message.
4. Follow the on-screen prompts to verify your device.
5. Save your backup codes in a safe place.

**Why it matters:**
- Blocks most unauthorized login attempts
- Protects your personal and financial information
- Takes only a few minutes to set up
