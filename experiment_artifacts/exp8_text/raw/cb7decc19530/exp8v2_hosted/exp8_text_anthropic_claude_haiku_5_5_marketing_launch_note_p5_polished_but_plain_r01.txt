Welcome to Words Between Us, a new podcast for anyone who has ever wanted to speak another language with confidence and without the usual anxiety. Each week we sit down with a language learner, a teacher, or a native speaker and talk through the real experience of picking up a new tongue: the awkward first conversations, the words that refuse to stick, and the small moments when a sentence finally clicks.

Our first season focuses on practical listening and speaking. Every episode centers on a single everyday situation, such as ordering a meal, asking for directions, or making small talk with a neighbor, and walks through the phrases, pronunciation habits, and cultural cues that make the exchange feel natural. Guests share the mistakes that taught them the most, and we pause often to practice together, so you can repeat after us at your own pace.

You do not need any background to start. Episode one introduces the basic sounds and rhythms of Spanish, and later episodes will explore French, Japanese, and Portuguese. Whether you are preparing for a trip, reconnecting with family roots, or simply curious about how other people think and speak, we hope you will find a place to begin here.

New episodes arrive every Thursday. Subscribe wherever you listen, and join us for the first conversation, which is about to start.
