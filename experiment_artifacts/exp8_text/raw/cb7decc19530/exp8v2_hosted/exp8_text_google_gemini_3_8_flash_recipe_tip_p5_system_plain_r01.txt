Toss your vegetables generously with oil, salt, and pepper before roasting, ensuring every piece is coated so the heat can properly caramelize the natural sugars. Save delicate dried herbs or garlic powder for this initial toss, but wait until the vegetables come out of the oven to add fresh herbs, grated cheese, or a splash of lemon juice so the heat does not burn them and the acidity brightens the deep roasted flavors.
