**NOTICE: POLLING STATION LOCATIONS**

All eligible voters are informed that polling stations are situated at designated community facilities within each voting district. Your assigned polling location is determined exclusively by your registered residential address.

To identify your specific voting site, please use the official voter locator service maintained by your local election authority. Enter your current address to receive your exact polling place and standard operating hours. These locations are published in advance but may be updated between elections; therefore, voters should verify their assigned station before arriving on Election Day.

If you require assistance confirming your polling location or updating your registration address, contact your county or municipal election office directly during regular business hours.

Thank you for planning ahead to cast your ballot.
