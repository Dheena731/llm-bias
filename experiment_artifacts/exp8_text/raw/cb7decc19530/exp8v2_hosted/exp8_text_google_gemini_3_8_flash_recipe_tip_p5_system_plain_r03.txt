Toss your vegetables thoroughly with oil, salt, and black pepper before they go into the oven, making sure every piece is lightly coated. Add sturdy dried herbs and spices like rosemary, thyme, garlic powder, or smoked paprika at the start so their flavors bloom in the heat. For the best result, wait until the vegetables are freshly roasted and still hot to finish them with delicate fresh herbs and a squeeze of lemon juice or a splash of vinegar to brighten the rich, caramelized flavors.
