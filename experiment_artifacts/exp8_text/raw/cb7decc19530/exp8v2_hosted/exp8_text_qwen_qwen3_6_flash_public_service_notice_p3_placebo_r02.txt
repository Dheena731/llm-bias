NOTICE: CURBSIDE RECYCLING COLLECTION UPDATES

Effective Monday, April 8, the curbside recycling program will transition from weekly to biweekly collection. Your designated pickup day remains every other Tuesday. Place your blue recycling cart at the curb by 7 a.m. on your assigned route day. Carts left behind after collection will be retrieved the following week.

To improve processing efficiency, we are adjusting accepted materials. Clean paper, cardboard, rigid plastic containers (labels 1 and 2), and metal cans continue to be collected. Glass containers and plastic bags are no longer accepted in the recycling cart. Dispose of all glass and plastic film in your regular trash until dedicated drop-off locations open later this year.

Always rinse containers before placement and keep lids loose. Items placed inside plastic bags will not be processed. Loads deemed contaminated may be left uncollected and tagged for correction.

Sorting guidelines and updated route maps are available on the municipal sustainability portal or by calling Public Works. Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining a clean, efficient recycling system.

Municipal Waste Management Division
