Subject: Updates to the Expense Approval Workflow

Hello everyone,

Starting next Monday, we are rolling out a new expense approval workflow to make reimbursements faster and more transparent. Under the new process, all expense claims under $250 will be approved by your direct manager, while claims above that amount will also require sign-off from the finance team. Submissions should be made through the updated expense portal, where you can also track the status of each request in real time.

To help with the transition, please review the new submission guidelines in the shared drive before submitting your next claim. Receipts must be uploaded as clear digital copies, and each claim should include a brief description of the business purpose. Claims missing either item will be returned to the submitter without review.

A training session will be held on Thursday at 2:00 p.m. in the main conference room, and a recording will be posted to the intranet for anyone who cannot attend. If you have questions before then, please contact the finance operations team at ext. 4420.

Thank you for your cooperation as we make this change.
