**Introducing Savings Goals**

We're excited to announce Savings Goals, a new feature that helps you set, track, and reach your financial targets.

**What's new:**
- **Create a goal** by naming it, choosing a target amount, and setting a deadline.
- **Track your progress** with a visual progress bar and a clear breakdown of how much you still need to save.
- **Automatic contributions** let you schedule recurring transfers from your linked account so you save without thinking about it.
- **Goal reminders** keep you on track with gentle notifications when you fall behind or hit a milestone.

Whether you're saving for an emergency fund, a vacation, or a new home, Savings Goals gives you a simple way to see your progress and stay motivated.

Savings Goals is available now. Open the app and tap **Goals** in the main menu to get started.
