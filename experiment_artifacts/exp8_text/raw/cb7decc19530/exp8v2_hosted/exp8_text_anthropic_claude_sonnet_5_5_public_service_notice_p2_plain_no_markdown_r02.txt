PUBLIC NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE

The City Department of Public Works announces that Maple Street will be closed to all through traffic between Fourth Avenue and Ninth Avenue beginning at 7:00 a.m. on Monday, June 10, and continuing until approximately 5:00 p.m. on Friday, June 21. The closure is necessary so that crews can replace a deteriorated water main and repave the roadway.

During this period, drivers should use Oak Boulevard or Elm Street as alternate routes. Detour signs will be posted at each end of the closure. Local residents will be able to reach their driveways from the nearest open intersection, though access may be briefly limited while work is taking place directly in front of a property. Please avoid parking on Maple Street within the work zone, as vehicles left there may be towed at the owner's expense.

Pedestrians and cyclists are asked to follow posted directions and to use caution near the construction area. Emergency vehicles will have access at all times. Bus routes 12 and 15 will be rerouted, and updated stop locations will be displayed at affected stops.

Work is weather dependent, and the schedule may change if conditions delay progress. The department apologizes for the inconvenience and thanks the community for its patience. For questions or updates, please call the Public Works information line at 555-0142 during regular business hours, Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.

Issued by the City Department of Public Works
