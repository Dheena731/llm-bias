We are pleased to announce the launch of our new savings goal feature. You can now define specific targets for your savings by selecting a name, setting a total amount, and choosing a target date. The interface displays a clear visual indicator of how close you are to reaching each goal as you contribute funds. Automated notifications will remind you to make regular deposits so you stay on schedule. This addition provides structure to your saving habits and makes it easier to track progress toward important financial milestones. Visit the savings tab in your account to create your first goal immediately.
