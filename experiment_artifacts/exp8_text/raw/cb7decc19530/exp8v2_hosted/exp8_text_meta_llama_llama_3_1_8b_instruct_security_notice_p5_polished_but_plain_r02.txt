Lost Company Laptop Report

If your company laptop is lost or stolen, please report it to the IT department as soon as possible. This can be done in person, by phone, or via email. The IT department will then take steps to ensure the laptop's security and to cancel any network access or email accounts that may be associated with the laptop.

When reporting the lost laptop, please provide the following information: the laptop's serial number, the date and time it was last seen, and any details of the circumstances surrounding its loss or theft. This information will help the IT department to identify the laptop and take necessary actions to protect company data.

If the laptop is recovered, please notify the IT department immediately so that it can be returned to you. If the laptop is not recovered, the IT department will work with you to determine the best course of action for replacing it.

The security of company data is a top priority, and your prompt reporting of a lost laptop helps to minimize any potential risks.
