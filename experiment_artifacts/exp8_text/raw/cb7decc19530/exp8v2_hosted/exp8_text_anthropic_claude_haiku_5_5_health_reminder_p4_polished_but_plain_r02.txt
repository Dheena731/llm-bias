Your eyes, neck, and mind all benefit from a regular pause away from your screen. Try following the 20-20-20 rule: every twenty minutes, look at something about twenty feet away for at least twenty seconds. This simple habit eases eye strain and helps prevent the dryness and blurred focus that come from staring at a display for long stretches. Use the break to stand up, roll your shoulders, and take a few slow breaths. Even a two-minute walk to refill your water or look out a window can refresh your attention and sharpen your thinking when you return to your work. Set a gentle timer if it helps you remember, and treat these pauses as part of your work rather than time lost from it.
