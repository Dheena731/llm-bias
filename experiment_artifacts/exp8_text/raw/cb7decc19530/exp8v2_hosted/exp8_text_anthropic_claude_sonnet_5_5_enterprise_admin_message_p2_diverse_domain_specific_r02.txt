**Subject: Expense approvals change March 3: who signs off on what**

Team,

For expenses submitted on or after Monday, March 3, approvals will route by amount instead of all going to your PM.

**Who approves**
- **Under $250:** your site superintendent. This covers fuel, PPE, small tools, and the lunch you bought for the inspector's crew.
- **$250 to $2,500:** the PM on the job code.
- **Over $2,500, or anything coded to overhead (9000 series):** the PM, then Priya in accounting.

**What you need to do**
- Photograph the receipt in the app the day you buy it. Fuel card slips count, and the paper copy doesn't need to come to the office anymore.
- Pick the job code before you submit. Entries with no code bounce back to you, not forward to your PM.
- Submit by Friday 5 p.m. to be paid the following Thursday. Anything later rolls to the next cycle.

**What's going away**
The pink paper form and the shared expenses@ inbox. Anything emailed there after March 3 won't be processed.

**Why we're changing it**
In February, 31 expense reports waited more than ten days, mostly because the PM was on site with no signal and nobody else could approve. Approvers now have three business days. After that, the report moves up to the next approver automatically.

Out-of-town per diem is unchanged: flat rate, no receipts, just tick the box.

If your job doesn't have a superintendent assigned in the system, tell Priya (ext. 214) by Friday so your small expenses don't get stuck.
