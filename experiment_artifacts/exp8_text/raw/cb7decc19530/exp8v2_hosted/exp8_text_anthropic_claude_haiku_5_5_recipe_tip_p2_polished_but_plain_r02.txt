Fresh herbs stay vibrant for much longer when you treat them like cut flowers. Trim about half an inch from the stems, then place them upright in a glass or jar with an inch or two of water, much as you would a bouquet. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and refrigerate. Basil is the exception: it turns black when chilled, so keep it on the counter away from direct sunlight and change its water every day or two. Hardier herbs like rosemary, thyme, and sage can also be wrapped in a damp paper towel and sealed in a bag, which keeps them crisp for a week or more.
