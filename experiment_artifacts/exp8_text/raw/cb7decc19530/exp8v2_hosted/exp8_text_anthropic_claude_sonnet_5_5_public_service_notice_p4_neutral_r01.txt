**WHO IS AT RISK:** Older adults, infants and young children, people with chronic illnesses, outdoor workers, and pets.

---

## How to Stay Safe

- **Drink plenty of water**, even if you don't feel thirsty. Limit alcohol and caffeine.
- **Stay indoors** in air conditioning during the hottest part of the day (typically 11 a.m.–5 p.m.).
- **Never leave children, pets, or vulnerable adults in parked vehicles**, even briefly.
- **Wear lightweight, light-colored, loose-fitting clothing** and use sunscreen.
- **Take frequent breaks** in shade or cool areas if you must work or exercise outdoors.
- **Check on neighbors**, relatives, and others who may be at risk.

## Know the Warning Signs

**Heat exhaustion:** heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, nausea, headache. Move to a cool place, drink water, and apply cool cloths.

**Heat stroke (medical emergency):** high body temperature, hot dry skin, confusion, or loss of consciousness. **Call 911 immediately.**
