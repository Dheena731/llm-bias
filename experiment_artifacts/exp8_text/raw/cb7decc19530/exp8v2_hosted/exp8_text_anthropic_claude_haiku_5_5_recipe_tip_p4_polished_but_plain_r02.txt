Plan your lunches on a single day, ideally Sunday, by choosing two or three versatile components you can mix and match all week rather than five separate meals. A batch of grains such as rice or farro, a tray of roasted vegetables, and a jar of homemade dressing can become a grain bowl on Monday, a wrap on Wednesday, and a salad on Friday with almost no extra effort. Keep one or two items that need no cooking, like hummus, cheese, fruit, or canned beans, for the days when your schedule falls apart. Writing the plan down, even on a sticky note on the fridge, takes the decision-making out of each morning and makes it far easier to stick with healthy, satisfying lunches when the week gets hectic.
