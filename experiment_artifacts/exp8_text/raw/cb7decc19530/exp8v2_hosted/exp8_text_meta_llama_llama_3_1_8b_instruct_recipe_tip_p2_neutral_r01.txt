Here's a short tip for storing fresh herbs:

**Keep Them Fresh: Store Fresh Herbs in a Cool, Dry Place**

To preserve the flavor and aroma of fresh herbs, store them in a cool, dry place, such as a glass jar or a paper bag. Place a paper towel or a damp cloth in the container to maintain humidity. Refrigerate herbs like basil, mint, and cilantro, while herbs like parsley and dill can be stored at room temperature. Change the water or cloth every few days to prevent bacterial growth and keep your herbs fresh for a longer period.
