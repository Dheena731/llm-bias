Today we are launching Tally, a simple app that helps small business owners keep track of their money, their customers, and their time in one place.

We built Tally after talking with dozens of shop owners, freelancers, and service providers who told us the same thing: they did not start a business to spend their evenings reconciling spreadsheets. Most of the tools available were designed for companies with accounting departments, and they showed it. They were powerful but crowded, and they asked owners to learn a new vocabulary before they could do something as basic as send an invoice.

Tally takes a different approach. You can create and send an invoice in under a minute, and the app will remind customers politely when payment is late so you do not have to. Expenses can be captured by taking a photo of a receipt, and Tally sorts them into categories you can hand to your accountant at tax time. A weekly summary shows what came in, what went out, and what is still owed, written in plain language rather than financial jargon.

Tally also keeps a short history for every customer, including past jobs, payments, and notes, so you can pick up a conversation without searching through old messages. If you work with a part-time assistant or a business partner, you can invite them to see only what they need.

Tally is available today for iPhone, Android, and the web. The first thirty days are free, and after that plans start at nine dollars a month with no contracts and no setup fees. If you decide it is not for you, you can export all of your data with a single tap.

We are a small team ourselves, and we read every message that comes in. If something in Tally confuses you or if there is a feature that would make your week easier, tell us. The best ideas in the app so far came from people running real businesses, and we expect the next ones will too.

Thank you for trying Tally. We hope it gives you a few hours back.
