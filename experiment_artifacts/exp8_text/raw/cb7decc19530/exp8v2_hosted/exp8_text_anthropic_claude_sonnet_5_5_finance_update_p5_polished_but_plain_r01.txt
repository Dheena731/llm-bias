Subject: Update on Delayed Payment Processing

We want to let you know that some payments are currently taking longer than usual to process. Our team identified the issue earlier this week, and we are working with our payment provider to resolve it as quickly as possible.

At this time, payments submitted over the past several days may appear as pending rather than completed. Your funds are secure, and no payments have been lost. Once the backlog clears, each transaction will be processed in the order it was received, and you will receive a confirmation as usual.

There is no need to resubmit a payment you have already made. Doing so could result in a duplicate charge, which would then require a refund and add further delay. If you see a pending charge on your statement, it will either resolve into a completed payment or be released automatically if the transaction cannot be finalized.

If a payment deadline is approaching and you are concerned about a late fee, please contact our support team with your account details and the date of your payment. We will note your account and waive any penalties caused by this delay.

We expect processing times to return to normal within the next two to three business days, and we will send another update as soon as the issue is fully resolved. We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience while we work to restore normal service.
