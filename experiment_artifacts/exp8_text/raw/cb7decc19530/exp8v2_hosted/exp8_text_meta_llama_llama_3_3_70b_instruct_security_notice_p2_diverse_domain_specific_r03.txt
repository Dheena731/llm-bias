**Important: Protecting Our Systems from Phishing Attempts**

As we continue to work on sensitive projects, it's essential that we remain vigilant about the emails we receive. Recently, there have been instances of phishing emails targeting our domain, attempting to trick staff into divulging login credentials or installing malware.

These emails often appear to come from legitimate sources, such as our IT department or well-known software companies, but they can be identified by:

* Urgent or threatening language, trying to create a sense of panic
* Requests for sensitive information, like passwords or financial data
* Suspicious links or attachments that may contain malware

To safeguard our systems and data, please be cautious when interacting with emails that:

* Ask you to verify your account information or login credentials
* Contain links to unfamiliar websites or request you to download software
* Have spelling or grammatical errors, or an unusual tone
