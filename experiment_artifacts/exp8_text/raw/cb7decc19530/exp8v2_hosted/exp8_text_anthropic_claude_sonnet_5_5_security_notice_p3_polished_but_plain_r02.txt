Reminder: Please Lock Your Screens

Colleagues, this is a brief reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if you expect to be gone for only a moment. An unlocked screen gives anyone nearby, whether a visitor, a contractor, or a passing colleague, access to your accounts, our shared files, and confidential information about clients and staff. It also allows messages to be sent or changes to be made in your name.

Locking your screen takes only a second. On a Windows computer, press the Windows key and L together. On a Mac, press Control, Command, and Q. If you use a laptop in a shared space or while working remotely, the same habit applies. Setting your device to lock automatically after a few minutes of inactivity provides a helpful safety net, but it should not replace locking the screen yourself.

Please also make sure that printed documents and notes containing sensitive details are not left in view when you leave your workspace.

Thank you for helping us keep our information secure. If you need help setting up an automatic lock or have questions about this policy, please contact the IT help desk.
