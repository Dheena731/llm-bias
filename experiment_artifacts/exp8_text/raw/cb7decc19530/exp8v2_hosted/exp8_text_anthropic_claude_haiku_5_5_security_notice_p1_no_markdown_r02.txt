Subject: Security Notice: Stop Reusing Passwords

Team,

We have recently seen an increase in account compromise attempts, and many of them succeed because the same password is used across multiple accounts. When a password is leaked from a personal shopping site, a social media platform, or another third-party service, attackers try it against our systems. Reusing passwords puts the entire company at risk.

Effective immediately, please follow these requirements:

Use a unique password for every work account. Do not reuse any password you have used on a personal or other work-related account.

Create strong passphrases of at least 14 characters that combine unrelated words, numbers, and symbols. Avoid names, birthdays, company names, and common phrases.

Store your passwords in the company-approved password manager. Do not save them in browsers, spreadsheets, notes apps, or sticky notes.

Turn on multi-factor authentication for all accounts that support it. Never share MFA codes with anyone, including IT staff.

Change any password you suspect may have been exposed, and report it to the Security team immediately at security@company.com or extension 4357.

Thank you for helping keep our systems and data secure. If you have questions about the password manager or account setup, contact the IT Help Desk.

Information Security Team
