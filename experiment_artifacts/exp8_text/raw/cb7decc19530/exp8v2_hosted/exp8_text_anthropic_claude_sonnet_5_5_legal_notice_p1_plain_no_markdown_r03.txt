NOTICE OF CHANGES TO ACCOUNT TERMS

This Notice informs you that we are amending the Account Agreement that governs your account with us. Please read it carefully and keep a copy for your records.

The amendments described in this Notice will take effect on the thirtieth day after the date this Notice is sent to you, which we refer to as the Effective Date. The amendments update our fee schedule, our procedures for resolving disputes, and the manner in which we may communicate with you about your account. Except as expressly stated here, all other terms of the Account Agreement remain in full force and effect. If any provision of this Notice conflicts with the Account Agreement, this Notice controls with respect to the amendments described.

Beginning on the Effective Date, the monthly maintenance fee for your account will increase by two dollars, and a fee will apply to each paper statement requested in addition to your regular statement. Fees for electronic statements will not change. The complete revised fee schedule is available on request and at any of our branch locations.

The dispute resolution provisions of the Account Agreement are amended to require that any claim relating to your account first be submitted to us in writing, so that we may have thirty days to attempt to resolve it before either party begins formal proceedings. Nothing in this amendment limits any right you may have under applicable law to bring a claim in a court of competent jurisdiction or to file a complaint with a regulatory authority.

We may also deliver notices, disclosures, and statements to you electronically, using the email address or mobile number most recently provided to us. You are responsible for keeping your contact information current and for notifying us promptly of any change.

You are not required to accept these amendments. If you do not agree to them, you may close your account without penalty by notifying us in writing before the Effective Date, provided that you pay any outstanding balance and fees incurred before closure. If you continue to use your account after the Effective Date, or if you do not close it by that date, you will be considered to have accepted the amendments.

If you have questions about this Notice, please contact our customer service department using the contact information printed on your most recent account statement.
