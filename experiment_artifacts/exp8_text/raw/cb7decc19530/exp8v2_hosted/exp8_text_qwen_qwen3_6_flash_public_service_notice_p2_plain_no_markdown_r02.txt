This is a public notice regarding a temporary closure of River Road between Elm Street and Pine Avenue. The road will be closed to all vehicular traffic starting at 8:00 am on Tuesday, October twenty-fourth, and is scheduled to reopen at 5:00 pm on Thursday, October twenty-sixth. Work is required for urgent water main repairs. Drivers should plan to use Maple Drive as an alternate route while this project is underway. Sidewalks will remain accessible for pedestrians, though some paths may shift near the construction zone. Please allow additional travel time and obey instructions from road workers. For further information, call the City Public Works office at 555-0199.
