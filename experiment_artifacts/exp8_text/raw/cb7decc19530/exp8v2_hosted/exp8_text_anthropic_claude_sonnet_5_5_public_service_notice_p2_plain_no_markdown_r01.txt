NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE

The City Department of Public Works advises residents and motorists that Maple Street will be closed to all through traffic between Fifth Avenue and Ninth Avenue from Monday, March 10, at 7:00 a.m. until Friday, March 14, at 6:00 p.m. The closure is necessary so that crews can replace a damaged section of the underground water main and repave the affected roadway.

During this time, local residents will be able to reach their driveways from the nearest open end of the street, but access may be limited at times while heavy equipment is in operation. Parking will not be permitted on either side of Maple Street within the work zone. Vehicles left in the area after 6:00 a.m. on Monday may be towed at the owner's expense.

Drivers should use Cedar Road and Eleventh Avenue as the designated detour. Please allow extra travel time, particularly during morning and evening commute hours. Pedestrians and cyclists are asked to follow posted signs and to stay clear of the construction area for their own safety. School bus and public transit routes in the area will be adjusted, and affected riders should check with the school district or the transit authority for updated stops and schedules.

Water service may be briefly interrupted on Wednesday, March 12, between 9:00 a.m. and 1:00 p.m. Affected households will receive a door hanger at least 24 hours in advance. Work is weather dependent, and the schedule may change if conditions are unfavorable. Any change will be announced through the city's public information line.

We apologize for the inconvenience and thank the community for its patience. For questions or concerns, please call the Department of Public Works at 555-0142 during regular business hours.
