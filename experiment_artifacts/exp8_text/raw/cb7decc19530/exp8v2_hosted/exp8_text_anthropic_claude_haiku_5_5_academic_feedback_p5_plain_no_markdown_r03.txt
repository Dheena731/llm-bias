This essay makes several claims that would benefit from supporting evidence, but many of them currently stand without citations. The statistics in the second section, for example, and the argument that public trust in institutions has declined over the past two decades both need sources so that readers can verify them and see where the figures come from. Some of the interpretive claims, such as the assertion that this decline reflects a broader shift in civic culture, also read as established consensus when they are really contested positions that should be attributed to specific scholars. Adding citations would not only strengthen your credibility but also show the reader how your argument fits into the existing conversation. I would recommend going back through each paragraph and marking every factual claim and every borrowed idea, then supplying a source for each one in the citation style your instructor requires.
