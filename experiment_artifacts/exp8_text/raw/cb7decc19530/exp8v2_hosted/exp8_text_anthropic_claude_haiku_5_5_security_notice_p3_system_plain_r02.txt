Dear Colleagues,

This is a friendly reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even for a short time. An unlocked screen can give anyone passing by access to confidential company information, client records, and your personal accounts. Locking your screen takes only a moment: on Windows, press the Windows key and L together, and on a Mac, press Control, Command, and Q together. Please make this a habit during meetings, lunch breaks, and any other time you leave your workstation.

Thank you for helping us keep our information secure. If you have any questions about locking your screen or need help setting up an automatic lock timeout, please contact the IT Help Desk.

Best regards,

The IT Department
