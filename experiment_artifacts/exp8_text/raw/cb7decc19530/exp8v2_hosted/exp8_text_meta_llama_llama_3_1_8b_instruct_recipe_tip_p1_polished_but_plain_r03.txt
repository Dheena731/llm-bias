To quickly prepare a weeknight dinner, consider the one-pot method. Choose a recipe that can be cooked in a single pot, such as a stir-fry or a skillet dinner. This approach reduces the number of dishes to wash and saves time during cleanup. Simply add all the ingredients to the pot, cook until the food is done, and serve hot. This method is particularly useful for recipes that require minimal preparation and can be cooked in under 30 minutes.
