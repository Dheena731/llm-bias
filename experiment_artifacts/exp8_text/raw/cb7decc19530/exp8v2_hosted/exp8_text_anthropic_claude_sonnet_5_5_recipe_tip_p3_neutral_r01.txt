**Tip for less sticky rice:** Rinse your rice in cold water before cooking, swirling it with your hand and draining until the water runs mostly clear. This washes away the surface starch that makes grains clump together. Then use the right water ratio (about 1½ to 1¾ cups water per 1 cup long-grain rice), avoid stirring while it cooks, and let it rest covered for 10 minutes off the heat. Fluff it with a fork before serving.
