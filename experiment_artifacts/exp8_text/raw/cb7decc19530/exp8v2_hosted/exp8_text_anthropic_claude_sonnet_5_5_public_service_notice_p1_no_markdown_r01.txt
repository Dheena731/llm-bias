PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE: UPDATED LIBRARY OPENING HOURS

Effective Monday, the Riverside Public Library will operate on the following schedule:

Monday to Thursday: 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
Friday: 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
Saturday: 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
Sunday: Closed

Please note the following:

The library will close early at 2:00 p.m. on the day before public holidays and will remain closed on the holidays themselves.

Book returns are available 24 hours a day through the outdoor drop box beside the main entrance.

Computer and printing services end 30 minutes before closing time.

Children's story hour continues every Saturday at 10:30 a.m.

For questions about hours or services, please call the front desk at 555-0142 or visit the library website. Staff will be glad to help.

Thank you for supporting your local library.
