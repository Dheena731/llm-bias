Season your vegetables in two stages for the best flavor. Before roasting, toss them with oil and a generous pinch of salt and pepper so the seasoning clings and the edges caramelize. Once they come out of the oven, taste one and adjust. This is the moment to add a squeeze of lemon juice, a splash of vinegar, a sprinkle of flaky salt, or a handful of chopped fresh herbs, since delicate flavors and acidity fade under high heat. Finishing this way brightens the vegetables and keeps them from tasting flat.
