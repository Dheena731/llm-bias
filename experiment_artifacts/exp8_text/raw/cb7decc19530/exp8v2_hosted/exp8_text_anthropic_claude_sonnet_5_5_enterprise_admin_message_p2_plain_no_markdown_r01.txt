Subject: Update to the Expense Approval Workflow

Hello everyone,

Starting Monday, we are introducing a new workflow for approving expenses. The goal is to shorten turnaround times and make it easier to see where each request stands.

From now on, all expense reports must be submitted through the finance portal rather than by email. Once you submit a report, it goes first to your direct manager, who has three business days to review it. Reports under 500 dollars are then approved automatically after manager sign-off. Anything above that amount moves on to the finance team for a second review, which should take no more than two additional business days.

Please attach clear receipts to every expense, and include a brief note explaining the business purpose. Reports missing receipts or details will be returned to you, which will delay reimbursement. You will receive an automatic notification whenever your report is approved, returned, or paid, so there is no need to follow up unless a request has been pending longer than the stated timeframes.

Any reports already submitted by email before Monday will be processed under the old system, so you do not need to resubmit them. If you have questions or run into trouble logging in to the portal, contact the finance help desk and someone will assist you promptly.

Thank you for your cooperation as we make this change.

Best regards,
The Administration Team
