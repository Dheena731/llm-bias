A quick reminder to step away from your screen for a few minutes. Every twenty minutes or so, look at something at least twenty feet away for twenty seconds, which gives your eyes a chance to relax and refocus. Once an hour, stand up, stretch your shoulders and neck, and walk around the room or grab a glass of water. These pauses help reduce eye strain, headaches, and stiffness, and they often leave you more alert when you return. If you tend to lose track of time, set a gentle timer so the habit takes care of itself. Even a minute or two away from the screen is worth it.
