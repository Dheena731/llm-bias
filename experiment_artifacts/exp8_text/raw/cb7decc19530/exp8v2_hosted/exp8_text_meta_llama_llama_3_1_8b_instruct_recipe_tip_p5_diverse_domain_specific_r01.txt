Here's a short tip for seasoning roasted vegetables:

"When roasting vegetables, try seasoning with a combination of citrus juice and chili flakes. The acidity of the citrus (such as lemon or orange) helps to balance the richness of the roasted vegetables, while the heat of the chili flakes adds depth and a subtle kick. For example, tossing carrots and Brussels sprouts with lemon juice, olive oil, and a pinch of chili flakes before roasting will result in a harmonious balance of flavors."
