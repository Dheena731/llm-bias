**Tip: Treat tender herbs like flowers.**

For soft herbs like cilantro, parsley, and dill, trim the stems and stand them in a jar with an inch of water. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and refrigerate. They'll stay fresh for up to two weeks. Change the water every few days.

Basil is the exception. Keep it in a jar of water on the counter, away from direct sun, because the cold turns its leaves black.

For woody herbs like rosemary and thyme, wrap them in a slightly damp paper towel and store them in a zip-top bag or container in the fridge.
