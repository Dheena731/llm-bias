Your end-of-year tax statement aggregates all taxable compensation, investment distributions, and withholdings recorded for the calendar year. Box 1 reflects your gross wages, Box 2 shows total federal and state tax withheld, and the supplemental schedules capture freelance income, retirement plan deferrals, and capital gains distributions. Cross-check these line items against your pay stubs, 1099-DIV/BROKER statements, and benefit election confirmations, then verify your SSN and address before forwarding the packet to your preparer. If cost basis looks misaligned or pre-tax deductions don’t match your active elections, flag it by January 10 so we can trace the variance with payroll and custodians before e-file windows open.
