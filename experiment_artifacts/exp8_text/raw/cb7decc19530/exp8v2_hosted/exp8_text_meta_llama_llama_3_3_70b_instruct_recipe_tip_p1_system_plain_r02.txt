To make a quick and easy weeknight dinner, consider preparing a few ingredients in advance. On the weekend or a day off, chop some onions, bell peppers, and mushrooms, and store them in airtight containers in the refrigerator. This will save you time during the week when you are busy and can be used in a variety of dishes, such as stir-fries, pasta sauces, and tacos. Simply saute the pre-chopped vegetables with some olive oil, garlic, and your choice of protein, and serve over rice, noodles, or with some crusty bread for a satisfying and stress-free meal.
