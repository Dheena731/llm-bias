Calendar tip: Stop using your calendar only for meetings. Block out time for the tasks you actually need to finish, like "Draft project proposal" from 9 to 10:30 a.m. When work has a time slot, it's far less likely to get pushed aside by whatever shows up in your inbox.

Bonus: Add a 10-minute buffer between events. Back-to-back scheduling looks efficient, but those small gaps keep your whole day from falling apart when one thing runs late.
