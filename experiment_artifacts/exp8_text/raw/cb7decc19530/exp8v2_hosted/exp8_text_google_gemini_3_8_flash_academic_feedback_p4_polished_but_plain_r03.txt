Thank you for sharing your poster draft; the overall visual appeal is strong, and your central research question comes through immediately. To maximize impact in a crowded conference hall, I suggest reducing the text density, especially in the methodology section, and letting your visuals do more of the heavy lifting. Attendees usually view posters from a few feet away, so increasing the font size of your section headers and primary takeaways will significantly improve scannability. Your figures are well-chosen, but labeling the key trends directly on the charts rather than relying on dense captions would help people digest the data much faster. Lastly, consider creating a single, prominent conclusion statement at the bottom right to ensure your core finding sticks with the audience. With these minor layout adjustments, this will be a compelling and highly engaging presentation.
