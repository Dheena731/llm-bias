**Subject: Monday roasts, at your door by Thursday**

Starting this month, you can subscribe to Fernway Coffee Roasters instead of racing us to the shelf on Saturday.

**How it works**
We roast on Mondays. Your bag ships Tuesday and usually lands Wednesday or Thursday, so you open it around day 3 off roast. That's past the worst of the degassing and well before the flavor starts to fade. Most coffee does best between days 5 and 21, and every bag is stamped with its roast date so you can check.

**What you get**
Every two weeks, one 250 g bag of our rotating single-origin filter coffee. Right now that's a washed Nyeri lot from Kenya, with blackcurrant and a sharp, clean finish. Next up is a natural from Guji, Ethiopia, which is jammier and softer.

Prefer espresso? Choose the Huila, Colombia roast instead. It's a little darker, and it's forgiving if your grinder is a bit off.

**Price:** $17 per bag, shipping included. Skip a delivery, switch roasts, or cancel from the link in your confirmation email. No call required.

We ship whole bean only. Ground coffee goes stale in about a week, and we'd rather you didn't pay for that.
