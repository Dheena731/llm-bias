**Lost Company Laptop**

If you have lost or misplaced a company laptop, please report it to the IT department immediately. This will help us to take necessary steps to secure the device and protect our company's data.

To report a lost laptop, please contact the IT department at [insert contact information]. Provide the following details:

* Laptop serial number (if available)
* Description of the laptop (make, model, color)
* Last known location of the laptop
* Any other relevant information

The IT department will take care of the rest, including notifying the relevant authorities and taking steps to recover the laptop if possible.
