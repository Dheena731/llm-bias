**NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE**
**Harlow Creek Road, between Mill Race Lane and Old Ferry Road**
Township of Pruitt Hollow Public Works

Harlow Creek Road will be closed to all traffic from **7:00 a.m. Monday, March 9, through 5:00 p.m. Friday, March 20**, while crews replace the culvert under the creek. The work cannot be done with traffic running, and the creek is high this time of year, so the closure will not be shortened.

**Detour:** Southbound traffic should take Old Ferry Road to State Route 14, then Pruitt Road back to Harlow Creek. Northbound traffic should reverse that route. Expect about 9 extra minutes.

**Residents:** Homes on Harlow Creek Road remain reachable from the Mill Race end until the work zone at the creek, and from the Old Ferry end on the other side. Crews will move the work zone to let vehicles reach your driveway, but expect short waits. Mail carriers have been notified. Residents who receive trash pickup on Tuesdays should set bins at the Mill Race intersection.

**School buses:** Route 7 will use the Pruitt Road detour both ways. Pickup times at the Harlow Creek stops will shift about 10 minutes earlier.

Emergency vehicles will have access through the work zone at all times.

Questions or concerns: Public Works, (555) 214-0170, or publicworks@pruitthollow.example.
