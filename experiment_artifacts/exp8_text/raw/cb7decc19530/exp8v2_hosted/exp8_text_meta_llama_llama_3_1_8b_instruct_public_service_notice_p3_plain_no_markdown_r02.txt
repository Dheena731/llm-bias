Recycling Collection Changes

The recycling collection schedule for our community will be changing starting from next week. The new schedule will be as follows: every other week on the same day of the week, the recyclables will be collected instead of every week. This change is intended to improve the efficiency of the collection process and to reduce the number of trucks needed.

Residents are asked to make sure to place their recyclables out on the designated collection days. If you have any questions or concerns about the new schedule, please contact the municipal office.
