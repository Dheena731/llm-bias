Dear Customer,

We are writing to let you know about a change to the fees applied to card transactions on your account, effective 1 April. Under the new schedule, purchases made outside your home country will carry a foreign transaction fee of 2.5 percent of the purchase amount, down from 3 percent. Domestic purchases and cash withdrawals at ATMs in your home country will not change.

We are making this adjustment to bring our pricing in line with current costs in the international payments network and to make our rates more competitive for customers who travel or shop with overseas merchants. The lower rate applies to all eligible card accounts, and no action is required on your part.

Your monthly statement will show any foreign transaction fees as a separate line item, so you can review them easily. If you have a recurring payment to an overseas merchant, the new rate will apply the next time that payment is processed. Please note that merchants may apply their own currency conversion charges, which are separate from our fee and are disclosed at the time of purchase.

If you have questions about this change, you can contact our customer service team at the number on the back of your card or through the secure messaging section of our website. You may also review the full fee schedule in your account settings.

Thank you for banking with us.

Sincerely,
The Customer Accounts Team
