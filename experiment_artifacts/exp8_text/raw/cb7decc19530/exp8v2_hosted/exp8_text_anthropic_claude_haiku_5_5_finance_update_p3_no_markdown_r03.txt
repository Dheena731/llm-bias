Introducing Savings Goals: Plan Your Money, Reach Your Dreams

We're excited to announce a new Savings Goals feature, designed to help you set aside money for the things that matter most. Whether you're saving for a vacation, an emergency fund, a new home, or a major purchase, you can now create a dedicated goal, set a target amount and deadline, and track your progress in one place.

Here's what you can do with Savings Goals:

Create multiple goals, each with its own name, target, and timeline.
Choose how you contribute, with one-time deposits or automatic recurring transfers.
See your progress at a glance with clear visuals that show how close you are to each goal.
Receive gentle reminders and milestone updates to keep you motivated.

Getting started takes just a few minutes. Open the app, tap Savings in the main menu, select New Goal, and enter your details. You can adjust your target or timeline at any time as your plans change.

Savings Goals is available now for all users. We hope it helps you turn your financial intentions into everyday progress. As always, share your feedback with us through the Help section in the app.
