Join us this Saturday, May 18, from 10 AM to 3 PM at Riverside Park for the Neighborhood Spring Festival!

Enjoy live music, food trucks, a kids' craft corner, and a plant swap where you can trade seedlings with your neighbors. There will also be a free bike tune-up station courtesy of Maple Street Cycles, so bring your two-wheeler along.

Everyone is welcome, and admission is free. Bring a blanket, a friend, and your appetite. We'll see you there!
