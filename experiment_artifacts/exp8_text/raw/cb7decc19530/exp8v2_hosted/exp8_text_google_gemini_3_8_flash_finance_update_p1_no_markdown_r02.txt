We are excited to introduce our new Monthly Spending Summary, designed to give you a clearer picture of your finances. 

Starting this month, you will receive a personalized breakdown of where your money went over the last thirty days. The summary highlights your top spending categories, tracks month-over-month changes, and shows how close you stayed to your budget goals. 

You can find your latest report directly in your account dashboard under the Insights tab, and a brief overview will also be delivered to your inbox on the first of every month. We hope this update makes managing your money simpler and more transparent.
