**Introducing Your Monthly Spending Summary**

We've added a new Monthly Spending Summary to help you understand where your money goes, without digging through individual transactions.

**What's included:**
- **Total spending** for the month, with a comparison to the previous month
- **Category breakdown** showing how much you spent on groceries, dining, transportation, bills, and more
- **Top merchants** where you spent the most
- **Trends and highlights**, such as categories that rose or fell significantly

**How it works:**
Your summary is generated automatically at the end of each month and appears in the Insights tab. You'll also get a notification when it's ready. Tap any category to see the transactions behind the numbers.

**Why it matters:**
A clear monthly snapshot makes it easier to spot patterns, catch unexpected charges, and adjust your budget before small habits become big expenses.

**Getting started:**
No setup is needed. If you'd like to customize categories or turn off notifications, go to **Settings > Insights**.
