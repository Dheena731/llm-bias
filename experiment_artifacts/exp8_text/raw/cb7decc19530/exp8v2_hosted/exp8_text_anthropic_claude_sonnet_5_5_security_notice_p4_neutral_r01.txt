**Notice: Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**

To better protect your account, we strongly encourage you to turn on two-factor authentication. 2FA adds a second verification step at login, so your account stays secure even if your password is compromised.

**How to enable it:**
1. Go to **Settings > Security**.
2. Select **Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Choose your method: an authenticator app (recommended), text message, or security key.
4. Follow the prompts to confirm your setup.
5. Save your backup codes in a safe place.

**Why it matters:**
- Blocks most unauthorized login attempts
- Protects your personal and financial information
- Takes only a few minutes to set up
