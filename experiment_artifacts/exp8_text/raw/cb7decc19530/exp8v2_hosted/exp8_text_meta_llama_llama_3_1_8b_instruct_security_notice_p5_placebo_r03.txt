**Lost Company Laptop Reporting Procedure**

If you have misplaced or lost a company laptop, please report the incident to the IT Department immediately. This is a crucial step in preventing unauthorized access to sensitive company data.

To report a lost laptop, please follow these steps:

1. Immediately notify your supervisor and the IT Department (ext. 1234) via phone or email.
2. Provide your employee ID number and a detailed description of the laptop, including its serial number (located on the back of the device).
3. The IT Department will then activate the device's tracking feature, which will help locate the laptop.

**Important:** Failure to report a lost laptop may result in security breaches and compromise of company data.

The IT Department will work with you to ensure that the necessary security measures are taken to prevent unauthorized access to company data. If the laptop is recovered, you will be required to undergo a brief security briefing to ensure that no sensitive information was accessed.

**Contact Information:**

* IT Department: (ext. 1234)
* Email: [itdept@company.com](mailto:itdept@company.com)

Please note that this procedure is subject to change, and the IT Department reserves the right to modify or update the reporting process as necessary.

**Don't hesitate to report a lost laptop. It's essential for the security and integrity of our company data!**
