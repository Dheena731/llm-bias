Phishing Awareness Reminder

We have seen an increase in phishing emails targeting staff, so please take a moment to review how to spot them. Phishing messages often look like they come from a trusted source, such as a colleague, a bank, a delivery service, or the IT department. They commonly create urgency, asking you to act immediately, reset a password, confirm account details, or open an attachment you were not expecting.

Before you click or respond, check the sender's full email address rather than only the display name, and be cautious of messages with spelling errors, unusual requests, or greetings that do not use your name. Do not enter your login details through a link in an email. If a request seems genuine, contact the sender through a known phone number or by visiting the site directly instead.

If you think you have received a phishing email, do not open attachments or click any links. Report it to the IT service desk right away, and if you have already clicked a link or entered information, tell IT immediately so we can act quickly to protect your account and our systems. Prompt reporting is always better than waiting, and no one will be penalized for reporting a mistake in good faith.

Thank you for helping keep our organization secure.
