This end-of-year tax statement is a summary of your financial activity for the past year. It shows the total amount of income you earned, as well as any taxes that were withheld from your paychecks. The statement also includes a breakdown of your tax liability, which is the amount of taxes you owe to the government.

The statement will include information such as your name and address, your tax filing status, and the amount of taxes you paid throughout the year. It may also include information about any tax credits or deductions you are eligible for, such as the Earned Income Tax Credit or the Child Tax Credit.

If you have any questions about the information on this statement, or if you need help understanding your tax liability, you may want to consult with a tax professional or contact the Internal Revenue Service for assistance.
