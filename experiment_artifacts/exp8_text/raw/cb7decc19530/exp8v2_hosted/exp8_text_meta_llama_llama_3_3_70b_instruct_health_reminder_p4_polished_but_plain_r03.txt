It's easy to get caught up in the glow of screens, whether it's a computer, phone, or television, but taking short breaks from them can have a significant impact on your well-being. Regularly looking away from screens can help reduce eye strain, improve your posture, and increase your productivity. Taking a few minutes to stretch, move around, and rest your eyes can also help you stay focused and avoid burnout. Try to take a short break from screens every hour to recharge and come back to your task with renewed energy and clarity.
