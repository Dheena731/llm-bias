We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you keep track of your expenses. This summary will provide a detailed breakdown of your spending habits, categorizing your transactions into different areas such as housing, transportation, and entertainment. The summary will be sent to you at the beginning of each month, giving you a clear picture of where your money is going and helping you make informed decisions about your finances. This new feature is designed to make it easier for you to stay on top of your spending and make adjustments as needed to achieve your financial goals.
