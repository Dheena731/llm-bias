Adding `NOT NULL` to an 80-million row table used to mean sweating over an exclusive table lock while your connection pool drained. 

`schematool` v0.14 now catches this pattern and handles the split under the hood: it adds the column as nullable, attaches a `NOT VALID` check constraint, validates it concurrently without locking reads, and swaps it to enforced. 

You write standard DDL. The engine keeps the table responsive. 

`brew upgrade schematool` to pull it down.
