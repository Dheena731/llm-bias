As part of our ongoing effort to maintain security and compliance, we will be conducting quarterly access reviews to ensure that all users have the appropriate level of access to our systems and data. During this review, we will verify that all access permissions are up to date and align with current job responsibilities. Department managers will be contacted to confirm access levels for their team members and any necessary changes will be made. If you have any questions or concerns about the access review process, please reach out to the IT department.
