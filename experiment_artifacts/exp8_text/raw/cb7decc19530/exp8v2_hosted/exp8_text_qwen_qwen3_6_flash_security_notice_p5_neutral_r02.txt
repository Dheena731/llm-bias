Here is a concise template you can use. Fill in the bracketed information before sending.

**Subject: URGENT: Report of Lost Company Laptop – [Your Name]**

Dear [IT Department / Manager's Name],

I am writing to formally report that my company-issued laptop has been lost.

**Device Details:**
*   **Asset Tag/ID:** [Insert ID Number]
*   **Model:** [e.g., Dell Latitude / MacBook Pro]
*   **Serial Number:** [Insert Serial Number]

**Circumstances:**
*   **Date/Time Lost:** [Date] at approximately [Time]
*   **Last Known Location:** [e.g., Conference Room B, Main Lobby, Commute Route]
*   **Status:** I have retraced my steps and notified [Security/Front Desk], but the device has not been recovered.

**Immediate Action Requested:**
Given the sensitive data on this device, please **remotely disable/wipe the laptop** and reset any associated account passwords immediately.

Please let me know the next steps for the incident report and potential replacement. I can be reached at [Phone Number] for any follow-up.

Best regards,

[Your Full Name]
[Your Job Title]
[Your Employee ID, if applicable]

***

### 💡 Quick Tips:
*   **Send Immediately:** Data security is the top priority. Send this as soon as you confirm the laptop is gone.
*   **Attach Proof:** If you have screenshots of location tracking or a police reference number (if applicable), attach them.
*   **Check Security Protocol:** Some companies require you to call a specific hotline first; check your employee handbook if unsure.
