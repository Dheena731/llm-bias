**Notice: Active spear-phishing attempts targeting vendor accounts and SSO logins**

Over the past week, IT intercepted two distinct phishing campaigns that bypassed initial spam filtering:

1. **Spoofed subcontractor payment updates:** Emails mimicking active engineering partners requesting updated ACH/wire instructions ahead of the mid-month billing run. The display names match legitimate contacts, but the reply-to address is hosted on lookalike domains (e.g., replacing `.com` with `.co` or adding an extraneous `-inc`).
2. **Document review notices:** Messages formatted as standard DocuSign or SharePoint notifications that lead to a spoofed Okta sign-in page designed to harvest corporate credentials and session tokens.

**Required steps:**

* **Verify payment changes out-of-band:** Do not update banking, routing, or remittance details based on an email thread alone. Call the contact directly using the phone number already listed in our ERP/vendor master—not the phone number in the email signature or attached PDF.
* **Inspect authentication prompts:** If an external link prompts you for an Okta MFA push you did not directly trigger, deny the request immediately. 
* **Reporting:** Use the **Report Message** button in the Outlook ribbon. Do not forward suspicious emails to coworkers or the general Slack channels, as this strips the header metadata IT needs to block the sender tenant.

Questions should go directly to the internal IT Helpdesk (ext. 4402).
