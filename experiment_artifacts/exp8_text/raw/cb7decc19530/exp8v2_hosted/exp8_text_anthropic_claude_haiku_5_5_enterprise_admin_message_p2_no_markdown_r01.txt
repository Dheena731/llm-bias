Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starting Next Monday

Hello team,

Starting Monday, June 2, we will be moving to a new expense approval workflow. The goal is to make reimbursements faster and more transparent for everyone.

Here is what is changing:

Expenses under $500 will be approved by your direct manager within two business days.
Expenses of $500 or more will also require sign-off from the department head.
All receipts must be uploaded through the expense portal within 30 days of the purchase date.
You will receive an email notification at each stage, so you can track where your request stands.

Training sessions will be held on May 28 and May 29. Please register through the HR calendar if you have not already done so. A short guide is also available on the intranet under Finance Resources.

If you have questions, contact the Finance team at finance@company.com or reach out to me directly.

Thank you for your cooperation as we make this transition.

Best regards,
Operations Administration
