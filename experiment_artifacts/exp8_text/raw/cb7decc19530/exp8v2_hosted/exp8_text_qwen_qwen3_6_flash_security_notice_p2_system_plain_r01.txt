This notice serves to remind all employees of the ongoing risk posed by phishing emails targeting our organization. Phishing is a cyberattack method where malicious actors send deceptive messages designed to steal credentials, install malware, or extract money. These emails frequently mimic legitimate communications from internal departments, known partners, or popular software platforms. Staff should examine every unfamiliar message before taking action. Pay close attention to the sender address, watch for misspellings or unusual domain names, question any sudden requests for passwords or payments, and hover over links to verify their destination without clicking. Never open unexpected attachments. If an email raises suspicion, forward it exactly as received to the information security team for review and permanently remove it from your account. Prompt reporting allows our technical staff to block threats quickly and keep company data safe. Thank you for staying vigilant and following these guidelines to protect ourselves and our clients.
