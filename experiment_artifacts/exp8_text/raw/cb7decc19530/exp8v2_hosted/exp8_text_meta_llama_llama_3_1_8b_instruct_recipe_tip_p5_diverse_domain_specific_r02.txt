Here's a tip for seasoning roasted vegetables that's specific to the domain and tailored to help you elevate your roasting game:

**Add depth with a citrus-herb glaze on the finishing pass**

When you're roasting vegetables, the most critical moment for adding flavor is usually the last 10-15 minutes of cooking. That's when the natural sugars caramelize and the flavors meld together. To take it to the next level, try brushing your roasted vegetables with a citrus-herb glaze made from a mixture of freshly squeezed orange or lemon juice, olive oil, and chopped fresh herbs like thyme, rosemary, or parsley. The acidity and brightness of the citrus will cut through the richness of the roasted vegetables, while the herbs will add a subtle, savory note. This technique works particularly well with carrots, Brussels sprouts, and sweet potatoes.
