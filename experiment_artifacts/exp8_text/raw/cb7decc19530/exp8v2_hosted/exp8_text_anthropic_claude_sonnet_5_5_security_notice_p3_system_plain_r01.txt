Subject: Reminder to Lock Your Screens

Dear colleagues,

This is a friendly reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if it is only for a moment. An unlocked screen gives anyone nearby access to your files, email, and our internal systems, which puts confidential information at risk.

Locking your screen takes only a second. On Windows, press the Windows key and L together. On a Mac, press Control, Command, and Q. Please also make sure your device is set to lock automatically after a few minutes of inactivity, as a backup.

Thank you for helping us keep our data and our workplace secure. If you have trouble with these settings or have any questions, please contact the IT help desk.

Kind regards,
Information Security Team
