Here is a short notice reminding staff to lock their screens:

**Reminder: Lock Your Screen**

As a reminder, please make sure to lock your computer screen when leaving your workstation unattended. This is an important security measure to protect sensitive information and prevent unauthorized access.

Simply press the Windows key + L (or Ctrl + Shift + Eject on a Mac) to lock your screen. Let's all do our part to keep our workplace secure!
