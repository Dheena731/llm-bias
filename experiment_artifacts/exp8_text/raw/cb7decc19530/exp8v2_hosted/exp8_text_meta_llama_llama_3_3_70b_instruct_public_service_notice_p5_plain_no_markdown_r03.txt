Polling stations for the upcoming election will be located at several sites throughout the area. Voters in the north part of the city will cast their ballots at the community center on Main Street. Those in the south part of the city will vote at the public library on Oak Street. Voters in the central part of the city will go to the city hall building on Elm Street. You can find out which polling station you are assigned to by checking your voter registration card or contacting the local election office. Polling stations will be open from 7am to 8pm on election day.
