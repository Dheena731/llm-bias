Dear valued customer,

We are writing to let you know that, beginning March 1, we will be adjusting the fee we charge on card transactions. The change reflects higher processing costs that our payment providers have passed on to us over the past year. Those costs have risen faster than we expected, and absorbing the full increase has become difficult for us to sustain.

For most purchases, the new fee will be 2.4 percent of the transaction total, up from 2.0 percent. Cash and bank transfer payments are not affected, and there is no change to fees for returns or refunds. We have kept the increase as small as we could while continuing to offer the card payment options you rely on.

If you have questions about how this affects a specific purchase or your account, please contact our customer service team at the number or email address on our website. We appreciate your continued support and your understanding.

Thank you,

The Customer Accounts Team
