**IT Security Notice: Recognizing and Reporting Phishing Emails**

**Date:** [Insert Date]
**From:** IT Security Team

Phishing emails are fake messages designed to trick you into clicking malicious links, opening infected attachments, or sharing passwords and personal information. They often look like they come from a colleague, a manager, a bank, or a familiar company.

**Warning signs to watch for:**
- Urgent or threatening language ("Your account will be locked today!")
- Requests for passwords, login details, or payment information
- Unexpected attachments or links
- Sender addresses that look slightly off (e.g., "support@micros0ft.com")
- Greetings that are generic, such as "Dear User"
- Spelling or grammar errors

**What to do:**
1. **Do not click** links or open attachments in suspicious emails.
2. **Do not reply** or share any information.
3. **Report it** immediately using the "Report Phishing" button in your email client or by forwarding the message to [security email address].
4. **If you clicked or entered information,** contact the IT Help Desk at [phone/extension] right away.

When in doubt, verify requests by contacting the sender through a known phone number or in person. Thank you for helping keep our organization secure.

**Questions?** Contact the IT Help Desk at [contact information].
