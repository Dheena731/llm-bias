**NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE**
**Harrow Creek Road Bridge, Tanner Creek Crossing**
City of Millbrook Public Works

**Closed:** Monday, March 9, through Friday, April 3
**Hours:** 24 hours a day. The bridge will be closed to all vehicles and pedestrians for the full period.

Crews will replace the deck on the bridge, which was load-rated down last fall after an inspection found corroded deck beams. The work cannot be done with one lane open.

**Detour:** Northbound traffic should use Wexford Avenue to Route 9. Southbound traffic should use Quarry Lane to Old Mill Road. Follow the orange signs. Trucks over 26,000 lbs should use Route 9 only, because Quarry Lane has a weight limit.

**Local access:** Residents on the west side of the creek can reach their homes from the Harrow Creek Road south end, but not from the bridge. Crews will keep the south approach clear for emergency vehicles.

**Other changes:**
- Bus Route 14 will detour along Wexford Avenue. Stops at Harrow Creek and Mill Street will be temporarily moved to the corner of Wexford and Pine.
- Trash and recycling pickup for the west-side block will run one day late during the closure week of March 9.
- Mail delivery will continue as usual.

Questions or concerns: Public Works dispatch, (555) 0142, weekdays 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. After hours for an emergency, call the non-emergency line, (555) 0199.

Posted March 2. Schedule subject to change based on weather. Updates at millbrook.gov/roadwork.
