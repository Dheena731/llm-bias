To make a quick and easy weeknight dinner, consider preparing ingredients in advance. Chop any vegetables, such as onions, bell peppers, or mushrooms, and store them in airtight containers in the refrigerator as soon as you bring them home from the store. This will save you time during the week when you are busy and need to get a meal on the table quickly. You can also cook proteins like chicken or ground beef in bulk and refrigerate or freeze them for later use in a variety of dishes. By having these ingredients ready to go, you can throw together a tasty and satisfying meal, such as a stir-fry or tacos, in no time.
