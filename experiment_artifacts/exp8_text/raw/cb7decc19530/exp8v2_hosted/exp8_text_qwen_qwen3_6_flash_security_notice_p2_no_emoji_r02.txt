**NOTICE: VIGILANCE REQUIRED REGARDING PHISHING EMAILS**

**To:** All Staff
**From:** IT Security Department
**Date:** October 26, 2023
**Subject:** Phishing Awareness and Reporting Procedures

This notice serves as a reminder regarding the ongoing threat of phishing emails. Employees are the first line of defense against cyberattacks targeting company data and credentials. Please adhere to the following security protocols:

1.  **Verify Senders:** Carefully inspect the sender's email address. Attackers often use addresses that closely mimic legitimate internal or external contacts.
2.  **Avoid Interaction with Suspicious Content:** Do not click on links or download attachments from unsolicited emails, even if the message appears urgent or comes from a known source.
3.  **Check for Red Flags:** Be cautious of requests for sensitive information, password resets, or wire transfers conducted via email without prior verification through a separate communication channel.
4.  **Report Immediately:** If you suspect an email is a phishing attempt, do not reply. Forward the message to security@company.com or mark it as phishing using the dedicated button in your email client.

Your prompt reporting helps us identify and neutralize threats quickly. Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining a secure workplace environment.

Regards,

IT Security Department
