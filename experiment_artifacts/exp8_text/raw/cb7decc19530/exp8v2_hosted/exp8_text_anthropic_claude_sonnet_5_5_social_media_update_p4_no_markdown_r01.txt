Calendar tip: Stop putting only meetings on your calendar. Block time for the tasks you actually need to finish, too. 📅

Try scheduling a 30-minute "focus block" for that report, email cleanup, or workout, and treat it like any other appointment. Once it has a time slot, it's much harder to let it slide.

Bonus: color-code your blocks (work, personal, health) so you can see at a glance if your week is balanced.
