Here's a short tip for seasoning roasted vegetables:

**Add a Squeeze of Fresh Citrus and a Sprinkle of Herbs**

To take your roasted vegetables to the next level, try adding a squeeze of fresh citrus juice (such as lemon or orange) and a sprinkle of herbs (such as thyme, rosemary, or parsley) during the last 10-15 minutes of roasting. The acidity and brightness of the citrus will balance out the earthy flavors of the vegetables, while the herbs will add a depth of flavor and aroma. Simply squeeze a bit of citrus juice over the vegetables and sprinkle the herbs on top - it's a simple yet effective way to elevate your roasted vegetable dish!
