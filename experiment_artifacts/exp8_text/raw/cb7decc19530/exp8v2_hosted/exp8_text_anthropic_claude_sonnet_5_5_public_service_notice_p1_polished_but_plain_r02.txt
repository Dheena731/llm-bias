Notice to Library Patrons: Updated Opening Hours

Beginning the first Monday of next month, the Riverside Public Library will adopt a new schedule designed to give residents more time to visit during evenings and weekends.

The library will be open from nine in the morning until eight in the evening, Monday through Thursday. On Fridays, doors will open at nine and close at five. Saturday hours will run from ten in the morning until four in the afternoon, and the library will be open on Sundays from one until five. The library will remain closed on public holidays, and any additional closures will be posted at the front entrance and announced by phone message at least one week in advance.

Please note that the children's room and the computer lab will close thirty minutes before the main library each day. Staff will begin gathering materials and shutting down public computers at that time, so patrons are encouraged to save their work and complete any printing a little early. The book drop outside the main entrance remains available at all hours for returning items, and materials placed there will be checked in on the next business day.

Residents who need assistance outside regular hours, such as those requesting accessible services or arranging group visits, are welcome to call the front desk during opening times and speak with a staff member. We will do our best to accommodate every request.

We thank our patrons for their patience during this transition and look forward to welcoming you. For questions about the new schedule, please visit the circulation desk or telephone the library during business hours.
