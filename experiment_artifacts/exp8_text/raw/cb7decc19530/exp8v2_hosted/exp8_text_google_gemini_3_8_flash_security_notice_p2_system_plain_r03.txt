Please be advised that our organization has recently seen an increase in phishing attempts designed to compromise company data. Phishing emails often appear to come from legitimate senders, such as executive leadership, human resources, or familiar vendors, and they typically urge you to take immediate action. Be cautious of messages that contain unexpected attachments, suspicious links, requests for sensitive personal or financial information, or sudden demands for login credentials. Always examine the sender email address carefully for misspellings or unfamiliar domain names. If you receive an email that looks suspicious, do not click on any links, open any attachments, or reply to the message. Instead, report it immediately to the information technology security team using the phishing report button in your email client or by forwarding it directly to the help desk. Maintaining our network security requires vigilance from everyone, and your cooperation is greatly appreciated.
