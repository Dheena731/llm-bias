Time for your weekly fitness check-in. Take a few minutes to look back at the past seven days and see how you did. Think about how many workouts you completed, how your energy felt, and whether you stayed close to your goals for movement, sleep, and nutrition. Notice what went well and celebrate it, even if the wins were small. If something fell short, treat it as useful information rather than a failure, and decide on one simple adjustment for the week ahead. Steady progress comes from consistent reflection, so set your intentions now and head into the new week ready to keep moving forward.
