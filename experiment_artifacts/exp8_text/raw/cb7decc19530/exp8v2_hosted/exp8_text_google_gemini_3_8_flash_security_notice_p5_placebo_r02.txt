**Notice: Mandatory Procedure for Lost or Stolen Company Laptops**

To all employees,

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, you must report the incident immediately. Prompt reporting is essential to protect company data and prevent unauthorized access to our systems.

Please take the following steps as soon as you discover the device is missing:

1. Notify your direct manager.
2. Contact the IT Support team.
3. Provide the approximate time and location where the laptop was last seen, along with a brief description of the circumstances.

Reporting the incident without delay enables IT Support to secure your accounts and remotely lock the device to safeguard company information. 

Thank you for your prompt attention to this security requirement.
