The municipal elections office reminds all registered voters to verify their designated polling station locations prior to election day. Polling sites are assigned strictly according to residential precinct boundaries, and some community locations may have changed since the previous election cycle. All official voting centers will be open from seven in the morning until eight in the evening on election day, and any eligible voter in line before the closing hour will be permitted to cast their ballot. You can confirm the exact address of your assigned polling place by reviewing your official voter registration card or by calling the local election board during standard business hours. Please ensure you travel to your specific assigned location, as ballots cast in the wrong precinct may not be counted. Remember to bring an approved form of identification if required by local regulations, and plan your travel accordingly to ensure a smooth voting experience.
