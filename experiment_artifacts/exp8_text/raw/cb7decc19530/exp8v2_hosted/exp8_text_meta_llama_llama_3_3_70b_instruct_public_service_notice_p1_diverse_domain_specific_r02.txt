Attention all book lovers and researchers!

As we settle into the new semester, don't forget to plan your study sessions and literary escapes around our library's opening hours. Our doors are open Monday to Thursday from 8am to 9pm, Friday from 8am to 6pm, and Saturday and Sunday from 10am to 5pm.

Please note that our library will be closed on public holidays, including Easter Monday and ANZAC Day. We'll also have reduced hours during the mid-semester break, from 9am to 5pm, Monday to Friday.

Before you visit, check our website for any updates on special closures or events that may affect our hours. You can also follow us on social media to stay in the loop about author talks, book clubs, and other exciting programs happening at your local library.

We look forward to welcoming you to our shelves and helping you discover new worlds and ideas!
