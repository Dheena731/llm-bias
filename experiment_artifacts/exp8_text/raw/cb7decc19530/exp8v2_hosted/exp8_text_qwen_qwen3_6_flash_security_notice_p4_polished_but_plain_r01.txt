This notice serves to inform all users that two-factor authentication will soon be required for all system accounts. Two-factor authentication adds a second verification step to your login process, typically by requiring a time sensitive code sent to your mobile device or generated by an authenticator application. This additional layer of security significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access to your account. To comply with this policy, you must activate two-factor authentication on your account before the end of the current month. The setup process can be completed through your account settings under the security section. You will be prompted to link a mobile number or scan a provided code with a compatible authenticator app. If you encounter any difficulties during registration or require assistance, please contact the IT support team at extension five zero one or visit the internal knowledge base. We appreciate your prompt attention to this matter and thank you for helping us maintain a secure working environment.
