**PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE**

Effective 7:00 AM on Monday, October 21, Main Street will be fully closed to all vehicular and pedestrian traffic between Oak Avenue and Pine Boulevard. The closure will remain in place until 5:00 PM on Wednesday, October 23 while utility crews complete essential water main repairs.

Drivers should use Elm Drive and Maple Lane as an alternate route through the area. Pedestrians may access the opposite side of Main Street via the temporary crossing near City Park. Expect delays during peak travel hours and obey all signage and directions from traffic control personnel.

For real-time updates or to report issues, contact the Department of Public Works at 555-0192 or visit cityservices.gov/traffic. We appreciate your patience as we maintain critical infrastructure.
