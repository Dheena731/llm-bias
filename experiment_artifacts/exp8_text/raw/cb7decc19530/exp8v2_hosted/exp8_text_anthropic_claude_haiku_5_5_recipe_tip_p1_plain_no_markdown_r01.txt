Before you start cooking on a busy weeknight, take five minutes to chop your vegetables, measure out your spices, and set out everything you need on the counter. This simple habit, often called mise en place, keeps you from scrambling for ingredients once the pan is hot, which is when dinner tends to go wrong. You will also find the cooking moves more smoothly and the kitchen stays calmer, so the meal feels less like a rush and more like a relaxed routine.
