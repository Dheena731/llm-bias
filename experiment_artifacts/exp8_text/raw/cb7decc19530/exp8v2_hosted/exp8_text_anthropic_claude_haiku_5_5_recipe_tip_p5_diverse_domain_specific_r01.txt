Salt your vegetables before they go in the oven, and do it at about a teaspoon of kosher salt per pound, tossed with the oil. Salt draws out moisture at the surface, and if that moisture is still wet when the vegetables hit the pan, they steam instead of browning. Hold off on fresh herbs and garlic until the last few minutes, or add them after roasting, because minced garlic and delicate herbs scorch long before the carrots or potatoes are done. Dried spices like smoked paprika or cumin can go on at the start, since they toast without burning.
