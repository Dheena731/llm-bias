NOTICE: CHANGES TO RECYCLING COLLECTION

Please be advised that starting Monday, November 10, residential recycling collection schedules will change. Collections previously scheduled for Thursdays will now take place on Tuesdays. 

Additionally, the municipal facility will no longer accept soft plastic bags or styrofoam in curbside bins. Cardboard, paper, metal cans, glass, and rigid plastics numbered 1 and 2 are still accepted.

Please ensure your recycling bins are placed at the curb by 7:00 AM on your new collection day. 

To view the complete updated calendar and recycling guidelines, please visit www.cityservices.gov/recycling or call the Public Works Department at 555-0199.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our community clean and sustainable.
