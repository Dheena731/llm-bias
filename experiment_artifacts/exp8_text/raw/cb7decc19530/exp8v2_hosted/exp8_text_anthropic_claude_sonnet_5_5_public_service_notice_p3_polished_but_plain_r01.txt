Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection

Beginning Monday, March 3, the curbside recycling schedule in our community will change. Collection will move from every Thursday to every other Tuesday, and the first pickup under the new schedule will take place on Tuesday, March 11. Please note that there will be no Thursday collection during the week of March 3, so any recycling you have accumulated by then should be held for the March 11 pickup.

Along with the new schedule, the program will accept a wider range of materials. Cartons used for milk, broth, and juice may now be placed in your bin, as may clean aluminum foil and pie tins. Plastic bags, foam packaging, and shredded paper remain unacceptable, since they jam sorting equipment and can contaminate otherwise good loads. Please rinse containers briefly and keep them loose in the bin rather than bagged.

Because pickups will be less frequent, residents who find their bins filling quickly may request a larger container at no charge. Requests can be made through the public works office by phone during regular business hours, and delivery usually takes about two weeks. Bins should be placed at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on collection day, with the wheels facing the house and at least three feet of space between the bin and any vehicle or mailbox.

Holiday weeks will follow the same adjusted rhythm, so collection will shift by one day only when a holiday falls on or before your regular Tuesday pickup. A printed calendar showing every collection date for the year will be mailed to each household by the end of February. If yours does not arrive, copies are available at the town hall and the public library.

We appreciate your continued effort to recycle well. Sorting carefully keeps costs down and helps ensure that what you set out is actually reused.
