**Digital Detox Reminder**

As you dive into your coding projects, data analysis, or software development tasks, don't forget to give your eyes (and brain) a break from the screen glow. Every 60-90 minutes, take a 5-10 minute pause to stretch, grab a snack, or simply gaze out the window. This brief respite can help reduce eye strain, prevent burnout, and even boost your problem-solving skills. Set a reminder, use a Pomodoro timer, or schedule a walking meeting to ensure you're taking regular breaks and maintaining a healthy work-life balance in the digital realm.
