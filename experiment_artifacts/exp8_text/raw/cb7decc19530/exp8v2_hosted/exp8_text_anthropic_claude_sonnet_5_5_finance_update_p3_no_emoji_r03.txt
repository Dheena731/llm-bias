**Introducing Savings Goals**

We've added a new Savings Goals feature to help you plan and track the things that matter most to you, whether that's an emergency fund, a vacation, or a down payment.

**What you can do:**

- **Set a target:** Name your goal, choose an amount, and pick a target date.
- **Track progress:** See a clear progress bar and how much you have left to save.
- **Automate contributions:** Schedule weekly or monthly transfers so you stay on track without thinking about it.
- **Adjust anytime:** Change your target, deadline, or contribution amount whenever your plans shift.

**How to get started:**

Open the Savings tab, select "Create a Goal," and follow the prompts. You can set up multiple goals and prioritize them in the order you prefer.

Savings Goals is rolling out to all users over the next week. If you don't see it yet, check back soon. As always, we welcome your feedback to help us keep improving.
