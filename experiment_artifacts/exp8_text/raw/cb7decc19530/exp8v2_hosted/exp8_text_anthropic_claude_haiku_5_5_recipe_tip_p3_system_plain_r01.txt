Rinse the rice in cold water before cooking. Put it in a bowl, cover it with water, swish it around with your fingers, then pour off the cloudy water. Repeat until the water runs mostly clear. This washes away the surface starch that makes grains clump together. For best results, drain the rice well, then cook it using a slightly measured amount of water, and let it rest covered for about ten minutes after cooking so the grains firm up and separate when you fluff them with a fork.
