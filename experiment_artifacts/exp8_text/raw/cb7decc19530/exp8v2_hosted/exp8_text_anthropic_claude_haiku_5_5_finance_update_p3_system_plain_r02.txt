We're excited to announce Savings Goals, a new feature that helps you set and track the money you're working toward. Whether you're saving for an emergency fund, a vacation, or a down payment, you can now create a named goal, choose a target amount and a deadline, and watch your progress update as you contribute.

Each goal shows how much you've saved, how much remains, and whether you're on pace to reach it by your chosen date. If you'd like a nudge, you can set up automatic transfers from your checking account on the schedule that works for you. You can also move money between goals at any time, so funds are never locked in place.

Savings Goals is rolling out to all users over the next two weeks. You'll find it in the Savings tab of the app. We'd love to hear how it works for you, so please send any feedback through the Help section.
