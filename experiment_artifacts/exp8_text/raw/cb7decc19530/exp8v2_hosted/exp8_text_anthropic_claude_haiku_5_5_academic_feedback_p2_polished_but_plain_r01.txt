Dear Maya,

Thank you for your methods section. Your description of the participant recruitment is clear, and the step-by-step account of the survey administration makes the procedure easy to follow. Those strengths give the section a solid foundation.

The main area to strengthen is justification. You state what you did but rarely explain why. For example, when you chose a sample size of 84, a sentence on how you estimated it, such as a power analysis or a practical constraint, would help readers judge whether the study could detect the effects you hoped to find. The same applies to your choice of the anxiety scale: a brief reason for selecting it over alternatives would strengthen the case for your measurement.

Some details are also missing. You mention that responses were coded, but not who coded them or how disagreements were resolved. Please also report the reliability of your scale in this sample, since a reader will want to know whether the instrument performed well with your participants.

Finally, tighten the wording in the second paragraph, where several sentences begin with "It was decided that." Active phrasing such as "I distributed the survey" is more direct and easier to follow.

Overall, this is a careful draft. If you add the justifications and the missing details, the section will be much more persuasive. I am happy to look at a revised version.
