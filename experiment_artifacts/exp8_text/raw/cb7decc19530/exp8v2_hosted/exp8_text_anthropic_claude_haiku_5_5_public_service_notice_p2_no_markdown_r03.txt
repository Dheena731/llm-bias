PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE

The City Department of Public Works will close Maple Avenue between 3rd Street and 7th Street from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on Saturday, June 14, and Sunday, June 15, to allow for pavement repair and resurfacing work.

Detour routes will be posted at each closure point. Drivers are encouraged to use Oak Avenue or Pine Street as alternate routes. Local access to residences and businesses along the closed section will be maintained where possible, though travel may be slower than usual.

Pedestrians and cyclists can continue to use the sidewalks on both sides of the street, which will remain open throughout the work period.

We thank residents and visitors for their patience. For questions or concerns, please contact the Department of Public Works at (555) 123-4567 or visit www.cityexample.gov/roadwork.

Issued by the City Department of Public Works
