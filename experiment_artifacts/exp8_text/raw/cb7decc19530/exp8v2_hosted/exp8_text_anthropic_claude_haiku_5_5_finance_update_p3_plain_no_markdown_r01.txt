We're excited to announce the launch of Savings Goals, a new feature that helps you turn your money plans into clear, trackable targets. You can now create a goal for anything you're saving toward, whether that's an emergency fund, a vacation, a new laptop, or a down payment on a home. Simply give the goal a name, set a target amount, and choose a date if you have one in mind.

Once a goal is set, the app shows your progress at a glance, including how much you've saved so far, how much remains, and whether you're on pace to reach it by your chosen date. You can add funds manually at any time or set up recurring transfers so your progress grows automatically with each paycheck. If your priorities change, you can pause a goal, adjust its target, or move money between goals without losing your history.

Savings Goals is available starting today in the latest version of the app. To get started, open the Goals tab from the main menu and tap the option to create your first one. We'd love to hear how you use the feature, so please send us your feedback through the Help section.
