Here is a short update explaining a new monthly spending summary:

As of this month, we are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you better track and understand your expenses. This summary will provide a comprehensive breakdown of your spending habits, including detailed categorizations of your income and expenses. The new summary will be sent to you on the 10th of each month, and will include a comparison of your spending to your previous month's spending, as well as a year-to-date comparison.

The new summary will include the following information:

* A list of all income received during the month
* A detailed breakdown of expenses by category, including housing, transportation, food, entertainment, and more
* A comparison of your spending to your previous month's spending, as well as a year-to-date comparison
* A total overview of your monthly spending, including a summary of all income and expenses

We hope this new summary will help you gain a better understanding of your spending habits and identify areas where you can cut back and save. If you have any questions or concerns about the new summary, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
