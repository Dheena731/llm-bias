**Hydration Reminder**

Take a moment to drink some water. Staying hydrated helps you feel more alert, keeps your energy steady, and supports your body throughout the day.

A few simple ways to make it easier:

- Start your morning with a glass of water.
- Keep a bottle or glass within reach while you work or study.
- Drink a little at each meal and whenever you take a break.
- Sip regularly rather than waiting until you feel very thirsty.

Needs vary from person to person, depending on activity, climate, and health, so listen to your body. If you have a medical condition or have been given specific fluid guidance, follow your doctor's advice.

Pour a glass now and take a few sips. Your body will thank you.
