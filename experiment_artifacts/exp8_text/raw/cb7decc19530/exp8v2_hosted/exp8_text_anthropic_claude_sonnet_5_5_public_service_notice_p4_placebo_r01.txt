# PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT

A heat advisory is in effect for our area. This means expected hot weather could make people sick, especially if they are outdoors or in homes without cooling. Please take precautions.

## How to Stay Safe
- **Drink water regularly,** even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and sugary drinks.
- **Stay in air conditioning when possible.** If your home is not cooled, spend the hottest part of the day in a public building such as a library or shopping center. Contact your city or county for the locations of any official cooling centers.
- **Limit outdoor activity.** If you must be outside, rest often in the shade and wear light, loose clothing and a hat.
- **Never leave children or pets in a parked vehicle,** even for a short time, and even with the windows open.
- **Check on others,** especially older adults, infants, people with health conditions, and neighbors who live alone.
- **Bring pets inside,** or give them shade and plenty of fresh water.

## Know the Warning Signs
**Heat exhaustion:** heavy sweating, weakness, cool and clammy skin, headache, nausea, or dizziness. Move to a cooler place, loosen clothing, sip water, and apply cool, wet cloths.

**Heat stroke is an emergency:** confusion, fainting, a very high body temperature, or hot skin that may be dry or damp. **Call 911 immediately.** While waiting for help, move the person to a cooler place and cool them with wet cloths or a cool bath if possible.

For updates, follow your local National Weather Service forecast or local news.
