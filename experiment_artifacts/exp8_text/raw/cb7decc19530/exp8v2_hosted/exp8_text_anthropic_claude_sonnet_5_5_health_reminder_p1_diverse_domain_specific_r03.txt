**Sunday check-in: 5 minutes**

Your week is logged. Before you plan the next one, look at three things:

- **Sessions:** How many did you finish compared with how many you planned? If you missed one, was it the schedule or the workout?
- **Load:** Did any lift or run pace move up, even by one rep or 2.5 lb? If nothing moved for three weeks, change something.
- **Recovery:** Check your average resting heart rate and sleep against last week. Higher heart rate plus shorter sleep usually means you should ease off.

Look at your bodyweight as a 7-day average, not a single morning's number.

Then adjust one thing for next week, such as a set, a rest day, or a target pace.
