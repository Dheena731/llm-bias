**Reminder: Prescription Refill**

Your prescription is due for a refill. Please contact your pharmacy or doctor's office within the next few days to avoid running out of your medication.

When you call, have the following ready:
- Your name and date of birth
- The medication name and dosage
- Your prescription number, which is on the bottle label

If you have no refills remaining, your pharmacy can request a new authorization from your doctor. This may take a day or two, so it is best to act soon.

Thank you, and please let us know if you have any questions.
