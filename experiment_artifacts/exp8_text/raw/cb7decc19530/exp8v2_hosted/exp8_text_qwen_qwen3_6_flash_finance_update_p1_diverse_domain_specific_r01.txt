The new Monthly Spending Summary compiles all posted expenses into a single report grouped by cost center, GL account, and active project code. Each line displays the transaction date, approver, and variance against the approved budget. Policy exceptions—including unauthorized vendors, threshold breaches, and duplicate invoices—are marked inline instead of siloed in alert digests. Data is frozen at 00:00 UTC on the first business day of the month, applies the latest published FX rates for non-USD charges, and aligns with your organization’s chart of accounts. You can filter by department, drill into invoice-level attachments, or export a flat CSV formatted for GL import. This replaces the previous ad-hoc tracker and now distributes automatically through the standard controller mailing list.
