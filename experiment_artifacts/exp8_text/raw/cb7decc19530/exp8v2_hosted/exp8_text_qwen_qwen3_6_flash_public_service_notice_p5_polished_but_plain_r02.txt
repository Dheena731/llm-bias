Please be advised that all registered voters must cast their ballots at their officially assigned polling station on election day. You may verify your specific location and precinct details by visiting your state election board website or calling your local county clerk office. Standard voting hours run from seven in the morning until eight in the evening, though some jurisdictions may adjust opening times, so please confirm your local schedule in advance. Every voter must present a valid government issued photo identification, and bringing your voter registration confirmation card is recommended to expedite check in. If you require wheelchair accessible entrances, translation services, or ballot assistance, contact your local election office to request accommodations ahead of time. Should any polling location experience a temporary relocation or closure due to unforeseen circumstances, updated addresses will be published immediately on the official election portal and announced through local news outlets. Thank you for fulfilling your civic duty.
