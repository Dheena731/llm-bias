Subject: Reminder to Lock Your Screen

Dear Colleagues,

This is a friendly reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even for a short time. Leaving a screen unlocked can give others access to confidential information, client records, and company systems.

To lock your screen quickly, press Windows key + L on a Windows PC, or Control + Command + Q on a Mac. Making this a habit helps protect our data and keeps our workplace secure.

Thank you for your continued attention to this important responsibility.

Kind regards,

IT Department
