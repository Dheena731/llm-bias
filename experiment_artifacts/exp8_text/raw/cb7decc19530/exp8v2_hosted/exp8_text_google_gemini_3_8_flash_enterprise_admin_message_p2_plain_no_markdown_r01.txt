Please be advised that we are introducing an updated expense approval workflow effective next Monday. Under this new process, all submitted expenses will automatically route to your direct manager for review before moving to the finance team for final processing. This change is designed to speed up reimbursement times and improve overall budget tracking across departments. You can access the updated submission forms through the finance section of the employee portal. If you have any questions about the transition or need help submitting a claim, please contact the finance team.
