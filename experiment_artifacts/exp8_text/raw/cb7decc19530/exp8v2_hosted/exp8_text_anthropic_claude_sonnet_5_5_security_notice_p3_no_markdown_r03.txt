NOTICE TO ALL STAFF: Lock Your Screens

Please remember to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if it is only for a moment. Unattended screens can expose confidential information and put our systems at risk.

Quick reminder:
- Windows: press the Windows key + L
- Mac: press Control + Command + Q
- Always lock your screen before leaving meetings, breaks, or the office

Thank you for helping us keep our data and our workplace secure.

IT Security Team
