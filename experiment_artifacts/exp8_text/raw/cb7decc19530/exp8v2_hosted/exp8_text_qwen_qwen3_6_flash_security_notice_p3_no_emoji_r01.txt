**NOTICE: WORKSTATION SECURITY REMINDER**

**To:** All Staff  
**From:** Management  
**Subject:** Please Lock Your Screen When Away from Desk

Dear Team,

Please remember to lock your computer screen every time you step away from your workstation, even for a brief moment.

Securing your screen is essential to protect sensitive company data and maintain our security standards. Please make this a habit whenever you leave your desk.

**Quick Tip:**  
*   **Windows:** Press `Windows Key + L`  
*   **Mac:** Press `Control + Command + Q`

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our workplace secure.

Best regards,

Management
