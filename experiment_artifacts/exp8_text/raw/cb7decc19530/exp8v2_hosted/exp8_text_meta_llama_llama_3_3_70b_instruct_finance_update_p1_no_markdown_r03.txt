We are excited to announce the launch of our new monthly spending summary feature. This tool is designed to help you keep track of your expenses and stay on top of your finances. At the beginning of each month, you will receive a detailed summary of your spending from the previous month, broken down into categories such as housing, transportation, and entertainment. This summary will provide you with a clear picture of where your money is going and help you identify areas where you can cut back and save. With this new feature, you will be able to make more informed decisions about your spending and work towards achieving your financial goals.
