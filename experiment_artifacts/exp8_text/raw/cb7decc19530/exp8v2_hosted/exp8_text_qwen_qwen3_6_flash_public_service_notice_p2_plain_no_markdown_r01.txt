Public Notice regarding the temporary closure of Elm Avenue. Construction crews will perform emergency sewer repairs on this street starting Tuesday morning at seven am through Thursday evening at six pm. All vehicular traffic must use Maple Drive as an alternate route. Pedestrian access will remain open along the north sidewalk. Motorists are advised to expect delays and plan their journeys accordingly.
