**Notice of Changes to Recycling Collection**

Dear Residents,

Starting next month, residential recycling collection services will transition to an updated schedule. 

Key changes include:

* **Collection Frequency:** Recycling will now be collected every two weeks instead of every week. Your assigned day of the week will remain the same.
* **Placement Time:** Recycling bins must be placed curbside by 7:00 a.m. on your scheduled pickup day.
* **Preparation:** All recyclable items must be clean, dry, and placed loose inside the bin. Materials placed in plastic bags will no longer be collected.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our community clean and sustainable.
