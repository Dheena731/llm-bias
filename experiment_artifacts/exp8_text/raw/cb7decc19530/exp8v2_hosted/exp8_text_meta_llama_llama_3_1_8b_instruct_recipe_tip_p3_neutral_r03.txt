Here's a short cooking tip for making rice less sticky:

**Use the right water ratio**

To prevent sticky rice, use a 1:1.25 to 1:1.5 water-to-rice ratio. This means for every 1 cup of rice, use 1 1/4 to 1 1/2 cups of water. Using too little water can cause the rice to steam and become sticky, while using too much water can lead to mushy rice. By using the right water ratio, you'll achieve perfectly cooked, non-sticky rice every time!
