Reminder: Please Lock Your Screen

Colleagues, this is a reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if only for a moment. An unlocked screen can give anyone nearby access to confidential files, emails, and internal systems, and it only takes a few seconds for information to be viewed, copied, or altered without your knowledge.

The quickest way to lock your screen is to press the Windows key and L together on a PC, or Control, Command, and Q together on a Mac. Please also make sure your device is set to lock automatically after a short period of inactivity.

Locking your screen is a simple habit that protects you, your coworkers, and our clients. Thank you for helping keep our information secure.
