Attention residents: the Central Public Library will have updated opening hours beginning Monday. The library will now open at 9:00 a.m. on weekdays and remain open until 8:00 p.m. on Monday through Thursday. On Fridays and Saturdays, the library will open at 9:00 a.m. and close at 5:00 p.m. The library will be closed on Sundays and on all public holidays.

These changes are intended to make it easier for visitors to use library services, including book lending, computer access, study spaces, and community programs for children and adults. Branch locations may keep different hours, so please check with your local branch before visiting.

For the most current schedule, or for questions about special events or holiday closures, please call the main library desk during opening hours or visit the library's website. Thank you for your continued support of your public library.
