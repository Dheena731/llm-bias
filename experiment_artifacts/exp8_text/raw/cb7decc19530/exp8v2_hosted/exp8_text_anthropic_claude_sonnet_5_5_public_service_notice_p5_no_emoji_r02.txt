**NOTICE: Polling Station Locations**

Voters are advised that polling stations will be open on **Election Day from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.** Please go to the polling station assigned to your address, as shown on your voter registration card.

**Primary Polling Locations**

- **Precinct 1 (Wards A–F):** Central Public Library, 120 Main Street
- **Precinct 2 (Wards G–M):** Riverside Community Center, 45 River Road
- **Precinct 3 (Wards N–S):** Oakwood Elementary School Gymnasium, 310 Oak Avenue
- **Precinct 4 (Wards T–Z):** Town Hall, Council Chamber, 1 Civic Plaza

**Before You Go**

- Bring a valid photo ID and your voter registration card, if available.
- All locations are wheelchair accessible. Curbside voting is available on request.
- Check your assigned polling place on the election office website or by calling the office at (555) 013-2200.
- If you are in line when polls close, you will still be allowed to vote.

Please note that some polling locations may differ from those used in previous elections. Confirm your assigned station before heading out.

*Issued by the County Election Office*
