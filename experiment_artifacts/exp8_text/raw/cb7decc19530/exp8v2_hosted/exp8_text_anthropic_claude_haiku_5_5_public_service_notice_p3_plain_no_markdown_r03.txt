NOTICE OF CHANGES TO RECYCLING COLLECTION

Beginning next Monday, the city's recycling collection schedule will change for all residential addresses. Recycling will now be picked up every other week instead of weekly, on the same day your regular trash is collected. Please check the city's website or your collection calendar to confirm your new pickup dates.

Residents are also asked to make sure recyclables are clean, dry, and loose rather than placed in plastic bags, as bagged items cannot be processed at the sorting facility. Glass containers, plastic film, and food-soiled paper should continue to be placed in the trash. Bins should be placed at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on collection day and returned to your property after pickup.

Bins that are contaminated or damaged may not be emptied, and a tag will be left explaining the reason. For questions or to request a replacement bin, please contact the Public Works Department at the number listed on the city's website.

Thank you for helping keep our community clean and our recycling program effective.
