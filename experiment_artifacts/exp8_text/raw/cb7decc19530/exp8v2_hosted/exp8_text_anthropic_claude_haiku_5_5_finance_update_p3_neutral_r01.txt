**Introducing Savings Goals: Plan Today, Reach Tomorrow**

We're excited to announce our newest feature, Savings Goals, designed to help you turn your financial aspirations into reality.

With Savings Goals, you can:

- **Set a target** for anything you're saving toward, whether it's an emergency fund, a vacation, or a down payment.
- **Choose a deadline** and let the app calculate how much you need to set aside each week or month.
- **Track your progress** with clear visuals that show how close you are to reaching each goal.
- **Automate contributions** from your linked account so you can save without having to think about it.

Savings Goals is available now in the app under the "Goals" tab. Simply tap "New Goal," name it, enter your target amount and date, and you're on your way.

We'd love to hear how you're using the feature. Share your feedback through the in-app support menu, and stay tuned for more updates as we continue building tools to help you save smarter.
