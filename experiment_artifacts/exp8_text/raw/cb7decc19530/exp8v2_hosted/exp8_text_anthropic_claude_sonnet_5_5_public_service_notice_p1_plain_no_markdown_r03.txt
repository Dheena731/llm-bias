Notice of Revised Library Opening Hours

Beginning Monday, the Riverside Public Library will operate under new opening hours designed to better serve our community. The library will be open from nine in the morning until eight in the evening, Monday through Thursday. On Fridays, doors will open at nine and close at five. Saturday hours will run from ten in the morning until four in the afternoon. The library will remain closed on Sundays and on public holidays.

Please note that the children's reading room will close thirty minutes before the main library each day, and the computer lab will accept its final sign-ups one hour before closing. Book returns may be made at any time through the outdoor drop box, which is emptied each morning.

During the first month of the new schedule, staff will be available at the front desk to answer questions and gather feedback. If you need help arranging a visit, or require accessible services such as large-print materials or assistance with mobility, please call the front desk during opening hours and a staff member will be glad to assist you.

We thank all of our patrons for their patience and continued support, and we look forward to welcoming you.
