To minimize stickiness when cooking rice, try rinsing the grains in a fine mesh sieve under cold running water for about 30 seconds before cooking. This simple step helps remove excess starch from the surface of the rice, which is a primary contributor to stickiness. Additionally, use a 1:1.2 to 1:1.5 water-to-rice ratio, depending on the type of rice you're using, and avoid over-stirring or lifting the lid too frequently during cooking. For extra fluffy results, try adding a tablespoon of white vinegar or lemon juice to the cooking water, as the acidity will help break down any remaining starches and result in a lighter, more separate grain.
