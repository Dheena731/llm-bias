Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starting Monday

Hello team,

Beginning Monday, we are moving to a new expense approval workflow to speed up reimbursements and improve visibility into spending.

What is changing:
- All expense reports must now be submitted through the Finance Portal. Email and paper submissions will no longer be accepted.
- Expenses under $500 will be approved by your direct manager.
- Expenses of $500 or more will require approval from your manager and your department head.
- Receipts must be uploaded with each submission. Reports without receipts will be returned.

Timeline:
- Managers have three business days to review submitted reports.
- Approved reports will be reimbursed in the next weekly payment cycle, which runs every Friday.

What you need to do:
- Log in to the Finance Portal before Monday to confirm your access and manager assignment.
- Submit any outstanding expenses under the old process by this Friday so they are not delayed.

A short how-to guide is available on the intranet under Finance Resources. If you have questions or trouble with access, contact the Finance Help Desk at finance-help@company.com.

Thank you for your cooperation as we make this transition.

Best regards,
Office of Administration
