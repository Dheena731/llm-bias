A quick reminder to give your eyes and mind a rest. Every twenty minutes or so, look away from your screen and focus on something at least twenty feet away for about twenty seconds. Stand up, stretch your shoulders and neck, and take a few slow breaths. If you can, walk to a window or refill your water. These small pauses ease eye strain, loosen tight muscles, and help you come back with a clearer head. Your work will still be there, and you will do it better after a short break.
