**Cookie Notice**

**1. What cookies are**
A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your device when you visit it. Cookies allow the website to recognise your browser and remember certain information about your visit.

**2. Types of cookies used on this website**
(a) *Strictly necessary cookies* are needed for the website to work as you request it, for example to keep you signed in to areas that require a login or to remember your cookie choices. These cookies are used without asking for your consent because the website cannot provide those functions without them.

(b) *Optional cookies* are used for other purposes, such as analytics or personalisation. We set optional cookies only if you agree to them.

**3. Your consent**
When you first visit this website, you will be asked whether you accept optional cookies. You may accept or decline them. If you decline, the website will still work, although you will not receive the features that depend on optional cookies.

**4. Changing your choices**
You may change or withdraw your consent
