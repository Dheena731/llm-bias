**NOTICE CONCERNING REFUND ELIGIBILITY**

**1. Standard Eligibility Period**  
To be eligible for a refund, a formal request must be submitted within thirty (30) calendar days of the verified purchase date. Proof of purchase (such as a valid order confirmation or original sales receipt) is strictly required for all claims.

**2. Condition of Goods**  
Physical merchandise must be returned in its original, unused, and undamaged condition, including all original packaging, tags, components, and documentation. Any item showing signs of wear, alteration, or misuse shall be deemed ineligible for reimbursement.

**3. Exclusions and Non-Refundable Items**  
The following categories are expressly excluded from refund eligibility:
* Downloadable digital products, software, or digital licenses.
* Customized, personalized, or made-to-order goods.
* Items designated as "Final Sale," "As-Is," or "Clearance."
* Perishable goods and intimate/hygienic items.

**4. Method of Reimbursement**  
Approved refunds will be processed exclusively to the original payment method within seven to ten (7–10) business days following inspection and confirmation of eligibility. Original shipping fees, handling charges, and processing fees are strictly non-refundable.

**5. Reservation of Rights**  
The Company reserves the sole discretion to deny any refund request that fails to comply with the terms set forth herein, subject to applicable state and federal consumer protection laws.
