**Subject: Your end-of-year tax statement**

Hello,

Your end-of-year tax statement is a summary of the income you received during the year and any tax that was already paid on it. This may include tax your employer or payer took out of your income before you received it, which is called "tax withheld."

**What it's for:** You use it to complete your tax return, or to confirm that the tax already paid matches what you owe.

**What to do:**
1. **Check the details.** Make sure your name, address, and tax identification number are correct.
2. **Compare the figures.** Check that the income and tax-paid amounts match your own records, such as pay slips or bank records.
3. **Keep a copy.** You will need it when you file your return, and it is useful to have if questions come up later.
4. **Report mistakes quickly.** If something looks wrong, contact the organization that issued the statement and ask for a corrected version before you file.

The name and layout of the statement vary by country and by who issued it. If anything on yours is unclear, ask the issuer or a qualified tax professional.
