**Introducing Smart Folders**

We just launched Smart Folders, a new way to keep your work organized without the manual effort. Set your own rules, and your files will sort themselves as you add them.

What you can do:
- Create folders based on file type, date, or keyword
- Update rules anytime to match how you work
- Find what you need faster with less clutter

Smart Folders is available now for all users. Update your app to try it out, and let us know what you think in the comments.
