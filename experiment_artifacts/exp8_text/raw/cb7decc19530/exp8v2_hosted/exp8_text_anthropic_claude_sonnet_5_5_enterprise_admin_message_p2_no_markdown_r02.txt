Subject: New Expense Approval Workflow Starting Monday

Hello team,

Starting Monday, we are moving to a new expense approval workflow to speed up reimbursements and improve tracking.

What is changing:
- All expense reports must now be submitted through the Finance Portal instead of by email.
- Expenses under $500 will be approved by your direct manager only.
- Expenses of $500 or more will require approval from your manager and the Finance team.
- Receipts must be uploaded with each submission. Reports without receipts will be returned.

Timeline:
- Submit reports within 30 days of the expense date.
- Managers have 3 business days to review and approve.
- Approved reports will be reimbursed in the next bi-weekly payroll cycle.

Any expense reports submitted by email after Monday will not be processed, so please use the portal going forward. A short how-to guide is available on the intranet under Finance > Expense Reporting.

If you have questions or run into any issues, please contact the Finance Help Desk at finance-help@company.com.

Thank you for your cooperation.

Best regards,
Administration Team
