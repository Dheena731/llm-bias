Official notice regarding polling station locations for the upcoming election. Polling stations will be open on election day from seven in the morning until eight in the evening. Electors are reminded that they must cast their ballots at their designated precinct location, as ballots cannot be issued at unauthorized sites. You can confirm your assigned polling location by reviewing the registration card mailed to your residential address or by contacting the municipal election office by phone. Neighborhood voting centers include local public school gymnasiums, community recreation centers, and designated town halls. All official locations comply with accessibility standards for voters with disabilities. Please ensure you bring a valid government-issued photo identification when you arrive to vote. Any registered voter who is waiting in line at their correct polling station at the time of closing will be permitted to cast their ballot.
