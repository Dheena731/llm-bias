A quick reminder to keep your water intake up today. Try to sip steadily from morning to evening rather than waiting until you feel thirsty, since thirst often means you are already a little dehydrated. Keep a glass or bottle within reach, have a drink with each meal, and refill whenever it runs low. If your day involves heat, exercise, or lots of coffee, you will likely need a bit more. Pale yellow urine is a handy sign that you are doing well. Your energy, focus, and mood will thank you for it.
