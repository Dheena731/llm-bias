Your end-of-year tax statement is a document that summarizes all of your relevant financial activity with us over the past calendar year. It details important figures such as total earnings, taxes withheld, interest paid, or tax-deductible contributions, depending on the type of account you hold. You will need the information on this form to accurately prepare and file your annual tax return with the government. Please review the details carefully to ensure your personal information and totals are correct. We recommend saving a copy for your personal financial records and providing it to your accountant or tax preparer when you file. If you notice any errors or have questions about the numbers reported, please contact our support team for assistance.
