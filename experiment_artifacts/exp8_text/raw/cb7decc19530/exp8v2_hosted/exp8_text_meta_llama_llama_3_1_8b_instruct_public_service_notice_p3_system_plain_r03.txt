Notice of Recycling Collection Changes

The city's recycling collection program is being modified to increase efficiency and reduce costs. As of January 1, residents will need to separate their recyclables into two distinct bins: one for paper products and another for plastics and glass. This change will allow the city to better sort and process the collected materials.

Residents are advised to rinse all containers before placing them in the recycling bins. Food and liquid residue can contaminate the recyclables and render them unusable.

The city's recycling collection schedule will remain the same, with collections occurring every other week on the designated days. Residents are encouraged to check their calendars to ensure they do not miss a collection.

Further information and guidelines on the new recycling collection program can be found on the city's website or by contacting the public works department.
