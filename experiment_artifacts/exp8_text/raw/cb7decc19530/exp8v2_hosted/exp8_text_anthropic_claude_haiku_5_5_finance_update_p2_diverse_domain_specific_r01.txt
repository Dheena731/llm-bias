**Subject: Change to card processing fees, effective April 1**

Starting with April 1 billing, the per-transaction rate for mid-tier rewards cards moves from 2.49% + $0.10 to 2.61% + $0.10. The card networks raised their interchange and assessment charges in January, and we pass those costs through at cost without markup. Debit, prepaid, and standard consumer credit rates are unchanged.

Here is what it looks like on a typical sale. A $68.50 rewards card purchase currently costs $1.81 in processing fees. From April 1 it will cost $1.89, an increase of 8 cents. Across 400 such transactions a month, that comes to about $32.

Two things are not changing. Your monthly service fee stays at $25, and the card-not-present rate for keyed or invoiced payments stays at 3.19% + $0.25.

You may notice a new line on your statement labeled "Network Assessment Adj." This is the increase itself, shown separately so you can match it against the new rate. It is not an additional charge on top of it.

If your volume mix is heavily weighted toward rewards cards, your effective rate will rise more than an average merchant's. If you'd like to model that against last month's statement, reply to this note or call merchant support at 800-555-0142 and we'll run the numbers with you.
