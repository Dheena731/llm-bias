Batch cook one versatile base on the weekend, such as a pot of grains, a tray of roasted vegetables, or a large batch of shredded chicken or chickpeas. Store them in separate containers, then mix and match through the week by adding different sauces, greens, or toppings so no two lunches feel the same. This cuts daily cooking time to a few minutes, keeps you from relying on expensive takeout, and makes it easy to pack a healthy meal even on your most hectic mornings.
