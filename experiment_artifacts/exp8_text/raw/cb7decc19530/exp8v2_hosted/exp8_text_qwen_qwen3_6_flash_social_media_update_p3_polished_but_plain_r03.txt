We invite neighbors to gather this Saturday at Oak Park from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. for our seasonal community celebration. The day features live performances by local musicians, a selection of food trucks, and designated play areas for children. Attendees are encouraged to bring folding chairs and water bottles to support sustainability efforts. Free street parking opens along Maple Avenue starting at 9 a.m. Questions may be directed to the community center by calling 555-0199. We look forward to sharing a wonderful afternoon together.
