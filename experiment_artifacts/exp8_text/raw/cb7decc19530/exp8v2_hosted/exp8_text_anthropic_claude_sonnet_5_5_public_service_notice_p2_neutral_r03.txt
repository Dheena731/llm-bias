**PUBLIC NOTICE**
**Temporary Road Closure: Maple Avenue**

**What:** Maple Avenue will be closed to all through traffic between 4th Street and 8th Street for road resurfacing and drainage repairs.

**When:** Monday, June 9 through Friday, June 13, 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily. Work is weather-dependent, and the closure may be extended by one or two days if rain delays the project.

**Detour:** Drivers should use Oak Street and 9th Street to bypass the closed section. Detour signs will be posted.

**Access:**
- Residents and businesses within the work zone will have local access, though short delays should be expected.
- Emergency vehicles will have full access at all times.
- On-street parking is prohibited in the work zone during construction hours. Vehicles left in the area may be towed.

**Transit:** Bus Route 12 will be rerouted along Oak Street. Stops on Maple Avenue will be temporarily out of service.

**Questions:** Contact the Department of Public Works at (555) 123-4567 or visit www.examplecity.gov/roadwork.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience as we work to improve our roads.

*Issued by the City Department of Public Works*
