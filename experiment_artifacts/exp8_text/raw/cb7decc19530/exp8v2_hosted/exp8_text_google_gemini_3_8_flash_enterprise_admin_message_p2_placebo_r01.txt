Subject: Update: New Expense Approval Process

Hello Team,

We are updating our expense approval workflow, effective immediately. 

Under this new process, all submitted expense reports will first be routed to your direct manager for initial review. Once your manager approves the report, it will automatically transfer to the finance team for final verification and payment processing. 

Please follow this updated procedure for all future expense submissions and ensure you attach itemized receipts to every request. 

If you have any questions about this process, please reach out to the finance team. 

Thank you for your cooperation.
