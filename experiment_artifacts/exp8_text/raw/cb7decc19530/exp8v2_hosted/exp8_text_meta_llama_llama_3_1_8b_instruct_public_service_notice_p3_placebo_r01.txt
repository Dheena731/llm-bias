**Notice of Recycling Collection Changes**

As of April 1st, 2023, the City of [City Name] is implementing changes to its recycling collection program. The primary change is the introduction of a new recycling truck design, which will improve the efficiency of our collection process.

**New Collection Schedule:**

* Residential recycling collection will now occur on Tuesdays, instead of Thursdays.
* Commercial recycling collection will be on Fridays, previously it was on Mondays.
* The collection schedule remains the same for both residents and businesses, but the collection times may vary by neighborhood.

**Important Reminders:**

* All recyclables must be placed in the designated recycling bins provided by the City.
* Materials must be sorted according to the guidelines provided on the City's website.
* Residents and businesses are encouraged to rinse and remove any contaminants from recyclables.

**Additional Changes:**

* A new "Sorting Station" will be available at the local community center for residents to learn about and practice proper recycling techniques.
* A new "Recycling Hotline" will be available for residents and businesses to report any issues or concerns regarding the collection process.

**Why These Changes?**

The City has implemented these changes to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of our recycling program. We believe that with these adjustments, we can better serve our community and promote a more sustainable waste management practice.
