**Introducing the Simple Monthly Budget Spreadsheet**

Know where your money goes each month without learning complicated software. Our new budgeting spreadsheet template is ready to download and works in common spreadsheet programs.

Enter your monthly income, list your expenses by category, and the template calculates your totals and shows how much you have left over. Each category has its own row, so you can see at a glance where your spending is highest. You can adjust the categories to fit your household, whether you're covering rent, groceries, transportation, or savings goals.

The template is built for people who want a clear picture of their finances, not for accountants. Nothing needs to be set up beyond typing in your own numbers.

Download the Simple Monthly Budget Spreadsheet today and take your first step toward a budget you can actually follow.
