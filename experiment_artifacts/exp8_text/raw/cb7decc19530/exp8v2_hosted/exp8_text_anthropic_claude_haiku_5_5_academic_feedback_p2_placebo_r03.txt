**Feedback on your Methods section**

Your methods section follows a clear sequence, so a reader can see the order in which you did things. The main task now is to make it complete enough that another researcher could repeat your study without contacting you.

**What to address:**

1. **Justify key choices.** For each major decision, such as how you selected participants or why you chose a particular measure, add a sentence explaining why it fits your research question.
2. **Add replication detail.** State exact materials, versions, settings, time frames, and the number of participants or observations at each stage.
3. **Explain your analysis.** Name the analyses you ran, the software you used, and the criteria for inclusion, exclusion, and significance.
4. **Report deviations.** If anything departed from your original plan, say what changed and why.

**Next step:** Revise with these additions, then reread the section as if you knew nothing about your project. Mark every point where you would have had to guess, and fill those gaps.
