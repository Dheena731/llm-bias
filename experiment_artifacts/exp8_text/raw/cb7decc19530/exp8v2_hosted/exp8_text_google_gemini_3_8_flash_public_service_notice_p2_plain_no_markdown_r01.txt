The City Department of Public Works announces a temporary road closure on Elm Street between First Avenue and Fourth Avenue. The closure will be in effect from Monday, November 4, through Wednesday, November 6, daily between the hours of 7:00 AM and 5:00 PM. 

This closure is necessary to allow crews to perform critical water main repairs and repave the roadway. Through traffic will not be permitted during work hours, though local access for residents and emergency vehicles will be maintained at all times. 

Motorists are advised to follow posted detour signs along Oak Street and to allow for extra travel time. Elm Street will reopen to regular traffic each evening and is expected to be fully reopened by 5:00 PM on Wednesday. The city appreciates the patience and cooperation of residents and commuters during this essential maintenance work.
