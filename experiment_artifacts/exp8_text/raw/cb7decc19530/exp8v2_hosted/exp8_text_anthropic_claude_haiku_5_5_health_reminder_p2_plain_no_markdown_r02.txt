Just a friendly reminder to drink enough water today. Your body relies on it for energy, focus, and keeping everything running smoothly, and it's easy to forget when your day gets busy. Try keeping a glass or bottle nearby as a simple cue, and sip regularly rather than waiting until you feel thirsty. If you're spending time outdoors, exercising, or in a warm environment, you may need a little extra. Taking a few moments to hydrate now will help you feel more alert and comfortable as the day goes on.
