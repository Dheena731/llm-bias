NOTICE OF RECYCLING COLLECTION CHANGES

The town's recycling program is undergoing changes to improve efficiency and effectiveness. As of the next collection day, the following changes will take effect:

* Collection days will be moved to the first and third Thursday of each month.
* Residents are now required to sort recyclables into separate bins for paper, plastic, and glass.
* The new collection truck will be larger, allowing for increased capacity and reduced frequency of collection.

Residents are asked to review and adapt to these changes to ensure a smooth transition. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the town's public works department at (phone number).

Thank you for your cooperation and participation in the recycling program.
