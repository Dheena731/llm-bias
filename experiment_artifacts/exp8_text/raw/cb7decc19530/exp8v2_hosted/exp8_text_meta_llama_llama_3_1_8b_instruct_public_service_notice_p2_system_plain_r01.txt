NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE

The public is advised that a section of Main Street will be closed to through traffic effective Monday, March 14, 2023. The closure is necessary for a utility installation project. The affected area will be between Oak Street and Maple Street.

Detour signs will be in place to direct traffic around the closed section. Local access will be maintained and residents will be allowed to enter their properties from designated entry points.

The closure is expected to last for approximately six weeks, weather permitting. Work will be carried out during regular working hours.

Motorists are advised to follow the detour signs and exercise caution when driving in the area.
