Notice to Residents: Changes to Recycling Collection

Beginning Monday, March 3, the city's recycling collection schedule will change for all residential addresses. Recycling will now be picked up every other week on Tuesdays, rather than weekly. Your garbage collection day will remain the same.

Please keep recyclable materials stored in your blue bin until your next scheduled pickup date. The updated collection calendar is available from the public works department, and copies can be requested by calling the customer service line during regular office hours.

Also note that plastic bags, food waste, and loose plastic film should not be placed in recycling bins, as these items can damage sorting equipment and lead to contamination charges. Flatten cardboard boxes and make sure containers are rinsed before they are placed in the bin.

We appreciate your cooperation as we make these changes. If you have questions, please contact the public works department for assistance.
