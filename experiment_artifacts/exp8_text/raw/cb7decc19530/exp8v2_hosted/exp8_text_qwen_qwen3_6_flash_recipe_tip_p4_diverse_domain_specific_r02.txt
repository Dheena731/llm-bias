**Audit for thermal resilience before you chop.**

Most meal-prep failures aren't about flavor; they're about texture collapse. Run every candidate recipe through a strict "reheat stress test": if the dish depends on leafy greens staying vibrant, fish remaining moist, or a mayonnaise-based dressing holding its emulsion, ban it from your hot containers. Instead, anchor your lineup with items that tolerate thermal shock—chili, stews, dense grain bowls with root vegetables, or curries where flavors meld rather than degrade.
