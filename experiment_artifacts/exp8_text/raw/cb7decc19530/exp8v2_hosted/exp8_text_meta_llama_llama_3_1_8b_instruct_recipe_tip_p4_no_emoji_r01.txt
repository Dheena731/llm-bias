Here's a short tip for planning lunches for a busy week:

Plan your lunches in advance by taking 30 minutes on Sunday evening to prep for the week ahead. Grill or cook a large batch of proteins like chicken, turkey, or beans, and portion them out into individual containers. Additionally, prepare a variety of vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins, and assemble healthy lunch combinations such as salads, wraps, or soups. This will save you time during the week when you'll be too busy to think about what to eat.
