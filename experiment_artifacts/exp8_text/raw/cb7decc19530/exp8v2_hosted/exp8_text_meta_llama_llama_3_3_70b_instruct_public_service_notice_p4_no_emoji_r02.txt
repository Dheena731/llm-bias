Public Notice: Heat Advisory

A heat advisory has been issued for our area due to extremely high temperatures and humidity. Residents are advised to take precautions to stay cool and safe. Stay indoors during the hottest part of the day, drink plenty of water, and avoid strenuous activities. Check on vulnerable neighbors, especially the elderly and young children, to ensure they are staying cool. If you or someone you know is experiencing heat-related illness, seek medical attention immediately. Stay tuned to local news for updates on the heat advisory.
