**Notice: Two-Factor Authentication Required**

All account holders must enable two-factor authentication on their accounts. This security feature requires a second verification method, such as a code from an authenticator app or a text message, in addition to your password each time you sign in. Activating this protection significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized account access.

To complete setup, log into your account, navigate to the Security settings, and follow the on-screen prompts. The process takes only a few minutes and provides immediate confirmation once successful. Please enable this feature promptly, as accounts without active two-factor authentication will be temporarily restricted until the configuration is finished.

For step-by-step guidance or technical assistance, refer to the resources in your account dashboard or contact our support team directly. Thank you for taking this essential step to safeguard your information.
