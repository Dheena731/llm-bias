**Security Reminder: Lock Your Screen When Stepping Away**

Dear Staff,

Please remember to lock your computer screen whenever you leave your workstation, even if only for a few moments. 

Leaving an unattended screen unlocked exposes sensitive information to unauthorized viewing and compromises the security of our network.

To quickly lock your screen:
* **Windows:** Press the **Windows logo key + L**
* **Mac:** Press **Control + Command + Q**

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our systems and data secure.
