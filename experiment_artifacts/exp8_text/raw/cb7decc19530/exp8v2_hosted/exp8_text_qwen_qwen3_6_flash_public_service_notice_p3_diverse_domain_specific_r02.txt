**OPERATIONAL NOTICE: ROUTE SHIFTS & CONTAMINATION PROTOCOLS**  
**Effective: October 14, 2024**

**1. Collection Window Tightened**  
Routes are consolidating to manage increased tonnage. Containers must be curbside and accessible by **5:30 AM**. Placing carts after this window results in automatic bypass until the next scheduled date. Early morning access is not guaranteed due to traffic patterns.

**2. Material Adjustments (Blue Cart)**  
To lower rejection rates at the sorting facility, the following changes apply immediately:
*   **Plastic Film & Bags:** No longer accepted. These jam optical sorters; take to designated grocery drop-offs.
*   **Loose Lids:** Discard in residual waste. Caps must remain screwed onto bottles/jars to stay on the belt during processing.
*   **Grease-Soiled Cardboard:** Pizza boxes and fast-food packaging are prohibited. If >25% is stained, compost or trash only.
