SUBJECT: Password Reuse Policy

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain a secure work environment, we want to remind everyone of the importance of password security. Password reuse is a significant risk to our network's integrity and can compromise sensitive information.

We have observed that some employees have reused their passwords across multiple accounts, including personal and company accounts. This is a serious breach of security protocol and can leave our network vulnerable to unauthorized access.

We require all employees to use unique passwords for all company accounts and to avoid using the same password for personal and company accounts. Using a password manager or generating complex passwords can help to minimize the risk of password reuse.

If you have reused a password, we recommend that you update it immediately to a unique and complex password. You can do this by following the guidelines for generating and resetting passwords that are outlined in our company's security policies.

If you have any questions or concerns about password security, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT department. We are available to assist you and provide guidance on maintaining secure passwords.

Thank you for your cooperation and attention to this matter.
