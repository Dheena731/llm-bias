**Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations**

Before you vote, confirm where your polling station is. Locations can change between elections, so do not assume you will vote at the same place as last time.

**How to find your polling station**
- Check any voter card or notice you received from your local election office. It should list your assigned location.
- Visit your local election office's official website or contact the office directly to look up your location.
- If you have recently moved, make sure your registered address is up to date. In many areas, your polling station is assigned based on that address.

**Before you go**
- Check the opening and closing hours for your polling station.
- Find out whether you need to bring identification and, if so, which types are accepted.
- If you need accessibility assistance, contact your local election office in advance to ask about the arrangements at your polling station.

If you are unsure about any of this, contact your local election office. Staff can confirm your polling station and answer questions about voting.
