HEAT ADVISORY NOTICE

A heat advisory is in effect for our region from Tuesday morning through Thursday evening. Daytime temperatures will reach the low nineties with elevated humidity. Residents should limit outdoor activities during peak heat hours, drink plenty of water, wear light clothing, and check on vulnerable neighbors and pets. Seek air conditioning if you experience dizziness, nausea, or excessive fatigue. Cooling centers are open to the public at all community recreation facilities. For real-time updates and emergency assistance, call 555-0198 or visit the official city website. Please take precautions to prevent heat-related illness and stay safe.
