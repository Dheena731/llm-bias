**Subject:** Your bike's been making that noise since October

**Preview:** Third Spoke Cycle Repair opens Saturday on Alder Street.

Hi there,

You know the noise. A tick every pedal revolution, a rub at the front brake, a shift that hesitates before dropping to the big cog. Most of the time it's something small: a dry link, a stretched cable, a rotor a millimeter out of true. Left alone, it gets expensive. A chain run past 0.5% wear takes the cassette with it.

We're Third Spoke, a repair shop that opens Saturday at 214 Alder Street. We're run by two mechanics who've spent a combined 17 years in other people's shops and decided to do it our way.

**What that means in practice:**

- **We measure, not guess.** Chain wear gets a checker, wheels go on the truing stand, and bolts get a torque wrench, especially carbon bars and seatposts.
- **You get the old parts back** if you want them, and a written note on what we found and what can wait.
- **Two-day turnaround** on standard tune-ups ($85). Brake bleeds, tubeless setup, bottom bracket work, and hanger alignment are listed with flat prices on our site, so there's no mystery at pickup.
- **Pickup within 3 miles** for $10, if getting the bike to us means putting it on a car rack you don't own.

We work on everything from commuter steel to e-bikes (non-proprietary drive systems only for now) to the gravel bike you've been meaning to convert to 1x.

**Opening week:** book a tune-up before April 30 and we'll replace your bar tape or grips free.
