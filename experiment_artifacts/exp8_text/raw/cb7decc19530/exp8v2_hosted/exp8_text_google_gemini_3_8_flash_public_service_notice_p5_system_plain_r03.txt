The municipal election board announces that official polling station locations have been designated for the upcoming general election. Voting precincts will be open to the public from seven in the morning until eight in the evening on election day. Registered voters must cast their ballots at their specific assigned neighborhood location, which may have changed since the previous election cycle due to redistricting and venue availability. You can confirm your designated polling place by reviewing the official voter notification card recently sent to your registered address by mail. Residents may also verify their site by contacting the city clerk office directly by telephone or by visiting the local election administration center during standard business hours. Please ensure you verify your polling location prior to arriving to vote, and remember to bring a valid form of identification if required by your jurisdiction.
