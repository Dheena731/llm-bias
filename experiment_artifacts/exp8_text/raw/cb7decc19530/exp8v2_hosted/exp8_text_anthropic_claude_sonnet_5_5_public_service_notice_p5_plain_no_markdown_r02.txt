Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations

Residents are advised that polling stations for the upcoming election will be open from seven in the morning until eight in the evening. Voters must cast their ballots at the station assigned to their address, which is printed on the voter information card mailed to every registered household.

Stations are located in public buildings across the district, including the Central Library on Main Street, Riverside Community Center, Oakwood Elementary School, and the Municipal Hall on Fairview Avenue. Each location is accessible to people with disabilities, with step-free entrances, designated parking, and staff available to assist anyone who needs help.

Please bring a valid form of photo identification, such as a driver's license, passport, or government-issued identity card. If you have lost your voter information card, you may still vote by presenting identification at your assigned station, where staff can confirm your registration.

Voters who are unsure which station serves their address may call the Elections Office during regular business hours or visit the office in person at the Municipal Hall. Staff will confirm your assigned location and answer any questions about the voting process.

To avoid longer lines, consider voting in the middle of the morning or early afternoon, as the periods just after opening and just after the end of the working day tend to be the busiest. Anyone in line at closing time will be permitted to vote.

Thank you for taking part in the democratic process.
