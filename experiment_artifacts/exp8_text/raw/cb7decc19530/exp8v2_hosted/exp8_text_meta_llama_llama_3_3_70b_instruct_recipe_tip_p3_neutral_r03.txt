To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it thoroughly before cooking and adding a small amount of oil or butter to the pot. Rinsing the rice removes excess starch, which can cause stickiness, while the added oil or butter helps to separate the grains. Additionally, using the right water ratio (generally 1:1.5 for white rice) and not overcrowding the pot can also help to prevent stickiness. Finally, fluffing the rice with a fork after cooking can help to separate the grains and make it less sticky.
