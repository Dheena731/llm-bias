Salt your chicken thighs the night before, then pat them dry before they hit the pan. Skin that's been dry-brined in the fridge for a few hours crisps far better than skin seasoned right before cooking, and you skip the wet, steamy sear that leaves it flabby. Start them skin-side down in a cold pan over medium heat, and let the fat render slowly for about 10 minutes before you touch them. The whole dinner takes under 30 minutes, most of it hands-off.
