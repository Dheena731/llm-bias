We have introduced a new monthly spending summary to help you track your finances with greater clarity. 

Starting this month, you will receive an overview at the end of each billing cycle showing your total monthly expenditures alongside a breakdown of your spending by category. This feature is designed to give you a clear, consolidated view of your expenses and help you better understand your month-to-month purchasing habits. 

You can view your summary at any time by logging into your account and visiting the spending section.
