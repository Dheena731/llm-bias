Subject: Update to card transaction fees

Dear customer,

We are writing to let you know about a change to the fees we charge on card transactions, which will take effect on the first day of next month.

The processing fee for card payments will increase from 1.9 percent to 2.4 percent of each transaction. The fixed charge of 20 cents per transaction will stay the same. For example, a payment of 100 dollars will now carry a total fee of 2.60 dollars, compared with 2.10 dollars today. Fees on refunds, bank transfers, and cash deposits are not affected.

This adjustment reflects higher charges from the card networks and the banks that issue cards, which we have absorbed for as long as we reasonably could. We have kept the increase as small as possible, and we do not plan to make further changes to this fee for at least twelve months.

You do not need to take any action. The new rate will apply automatically to every card payment processed on or after the effective date, and payments made before then will be charged at the current rate. If you would like to see how the change affects your account, your monthly statement will show the new fee on each transaction, and our fee calculator in your account settings can estimate your costs based on recent volume.

If you have questions or would like to talk through options, such as encouraging lower-cost payment methods, our support team is available on weekdays from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. at the contact details on your account page. We appreciate your business and are happy to help.

Kind regards,

The Customer Accounts Team
