NOTICE: Changes to Recycling Collection

Starting next Monday, recycling collection in our area will be moving to a new schedule. Recycling will now be picked up every other week on Mondays, rather than every week. Please check the collection calendar on the city website or the mobile app to confirm your pickup dates.

Several other updates are also taking effect:

Glass bottles and jars must now be placed in the separate blue bin provided by the city.
Plastic bags, including grocery bags, should no longer be placed in recycling bins. Please bring them to a participating store drop-off location instead.
Bins should be placed at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on collection day and returned from the street within 24 hours of pickup.

If you need a replacement or additional recycling bin, please contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567 or visit the office during business hours. Residents who have questions about these changes can also reach out to the same number or email recycling@cityservices.gov.

Thank you for helping us keep our community clean and sustainable.
