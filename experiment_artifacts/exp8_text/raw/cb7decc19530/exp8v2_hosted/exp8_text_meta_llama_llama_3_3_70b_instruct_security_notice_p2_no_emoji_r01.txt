To: All Staff

Please be aware that there has been an increase in phishing emails being sent to company email accounts. These emails are designed to look legitimate but are actually attempts to steal your login credentials or sensitive information.

To protect yourself and the company, please be cautious when opening emails from unknown senders or those that ask you to click on links or provide personal information. If you are unsure about the authenticity of an email, do not respond or click on any links. Instead, contact the IT department for assistance.

Remember, the company will never ask you to provide sensitive information via email. If you have any concerns or suspect that you have received a phishing email, please report it to the IT department immediately.

Thank you for your vigilance in helping to protect our company's security.
