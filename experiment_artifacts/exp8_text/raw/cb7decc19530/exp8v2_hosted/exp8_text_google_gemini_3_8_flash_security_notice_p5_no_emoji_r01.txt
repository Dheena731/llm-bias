**NOTICE: Reporting Lost or Stolen Company Laptops**

If you have misplaced your company-issued laptop or suspect it has been stolen, you must report the incident immediately to safeguard company and client data.

**Immediate Actions Required:**

1. **Notify IT Security:** Contact the IT Helpdesk immediately at [Phone Number] or [Email Address]. Immediate notification allows us to remotely lock and wipe the device.
2. **Provide Details:** Include the laptop asset tag (if known), the date and time it went missing, and the last known location.
3. **Reset Passwords:** From a secure secondary device, immediately change the passwords for your company email, VPN, and any accessible internal systems.
4. **File a Police Report:** If the device was stolen, report the theft to local law enforcement and forward the police report number to the IT department.

Failure to promptly report a missing device compromises company security and violates internal IT policy.
