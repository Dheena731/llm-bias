We are introducing a new expense approval workflow to improve the efficiency and transparency of our expense reporting process. The new workflow will require all expenses to be submitted electronically and will automatically route them to the appropriate approver based on the employee's department and expense type. This new process will help to reduce paperwork and minimize delays in reimbursing employees for their expenses. All employees who submit expenses will be required to use the new system starting immediately, and training sessions will be available for those who need assistance with the new workflow. If you have any questions or concerns about the new expense approval workflow, please do not hesitate to reach out to the finance department for support.
