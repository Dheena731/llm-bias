To enhance the security of your account and protect your personal information, we now require all users to enable two-factor authentication. This security measure adds an extra layer of defense against unauthorized access by requiring a verification code in addition to your regular password. Please log in to your account, visit the security section in your settings, and follow the simple prompts to connect an authenticator app or register your phone number for text alerts. Taking a few moments to complete this setup today will ensure your account remains safe and uninterrupted. If you experience any issues, please reach out to our support team for assistance.
