**Welcome to the podcast**

This show is for adults learning a second language who want more practice with listening and speaking than a textbook or classroom usually offers. Each episode is a short spoken lesson built around one everyday situation, such as ordering food, asking for directions, or booking an appointment by phone. We speak slowly and clearly, pause so you can repeat phrases aloud, and explain the grammar that comes up in English.

You don't need to be fluent to start. If you know a few basic words, you can follow along. Listen once to get the meaning, then again to practice speaking.

New episodes come out every week. Subscribe so you don't miss one, and tell us which situations you'd like us to cover next.
