NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

This notice sets out the conditions under which a customer may be eligible for a refund of purchase price paid for goods or services.

1. Eligibility Period. A request for a refund must be submitted within thirty (30) days of the date of purchase, as shown on the original receipt or order confirmation.

2. Conditions of Eligibility. To qualify for a refund, the purchased item must be unused, in its original packaging, and in resaleable condition. Services are eligible for a refund only where they have not been substantially performed.

3. Exclusions. Refunds will not be issued for items marked as final sale, personalized or custom-made products, digital content once downloaded or accessed, or items damaged through misuse, negligence, or normal wear.

4. Proof of Purchase. A valid proof of purchase, such as an original receipt, order number, or account record, must accompany every refund request.

5. Method of Submission. Refund requests must be submitted in writing through the designated customer service channel or the company's official website.

6. Processing. Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within ten (10) business days of approval. The company is not responsible for delays caused by third-party financial institutions.

7. Statutory Rights. Nothing in this notice limits any right a customer may have under applicable consumer protection law. Where such law provides greater rights, those rights shall prevail.

8. Right to Amend. The company reserves the right to amend this refund policy at any time. Amendments apply only to purchases made after the effective date of the amended policy.
