**Reminder: Lock Your Screen When You Leave Your Desk**

All staff are reminded to lock their computer screens whenever they step away from their workstations, even for a short time.

An unlocked screen allows anyone nearby to view confidential information, access files, or use your account. Locking your screen takes only a moment and protects both company data and your own work.

To lock your screen:
- **Windows:** Press the Windows key + L.
- **Mac:** Press Control + Command + Q.

Please make this a habit, every time you leave your desk, including for meetings, breaks, and visits to colleagues.

Thank you for helping keep our information secure.
