**Subject: Security Notice: Stop Reusing Passwords**

Team,

We are reminding everyone to avoid reusing passwords across work and personal accounts. When a password is reused, a breach at any one site can expose every account that shares it, including company systems.

Please follow these guidelines:

- **Use a unique password** for every work account.
- **Use a password manager** to generate and store strong passwords. IT has approved options available on the intranet.
- **Enable multi-factor authentication** on all accounts that support it.
- **Never share passwords** via email, chat, or phone, even with colleagues or IT staff. IT will never ask for your password.
- **Report suspicious activity**, such as unexpected login prompts or password reset emails you did not request, to the IT Service Desk immediately.

If you believe a password you use for work has been exposed or reused, change it now and contact the IT Service Desk.

Thank you for helping keep our systems and data secure.

IT Security Team
