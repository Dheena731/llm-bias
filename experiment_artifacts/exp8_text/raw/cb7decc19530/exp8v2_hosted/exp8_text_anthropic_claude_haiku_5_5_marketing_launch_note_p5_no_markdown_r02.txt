Welcome to Words Between Us, the podcast that makes learning a new language feel like a conversation with a friend.

Whether you are starting from zero or brushing up on a language you studied years ago, this show is for you. Each week we'll explore everyday phrases, cultural quirks, and the surprising stories behind the words we use. You'll hear from native speakers, language teachers, and fellow learners who are figuring it out one sentence at a time.

Every episode is built for busy listeners. Keep it short on your commute, or pause and practice along with us during a longer walk. We'll break down pronunciation, share useful vocabulary, and end each episode with a simple challenge to try before the next one.

Our first episode drops this Thursday, and it starts with the greetings you'll actually use in your first week abroad, or at your next dinner with new friends. Subscribe wherever you listen, and bring a notebook. Let's start talking.
