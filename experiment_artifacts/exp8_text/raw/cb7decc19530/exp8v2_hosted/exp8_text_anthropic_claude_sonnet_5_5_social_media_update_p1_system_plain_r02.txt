Big news: shared playlists are now live! You can invite friends, family, or coworkers to build a playlist together, and everyone can add, reorder, and remove tracks in real time. No more sending long lists of song suggestions back and forth. Just start a playlist, tap Invite, and let the collaboration begin. The update is rolling out today on iOS and Android, so check your app store for the latest version. We can't wait to hear what you create together, so tell us about your favorite shared playlist in the comments.
