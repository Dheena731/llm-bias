Please enable two-factor authentication on your account to help protect your personal information. Two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security by requiring a verification code in addition to your password when you log in. You can activate this feature by visiting the security settings page in your account dashboard and following the setup instructions. We ask that all users complete this step by the end of the current month to maintain uninterrupted access to our services. If you encounter any difficulties during the setup process, please contact our support team through the help center or reply to this message with your account details for guidance. Thank you for taking this important step to keep your account secure.
