Please be advised that the company has recently seen an increase in phishing attempts targeting employee email accounts. Phishing emails are designed to look like legitimate communications from trusted colleagues, clients, or service providers, but their true goal is to steal sensitive information, passwords, or company data.

Always scrutinize unexpected emails carefully, especially those that convey a sense of extreme urgency, ask for login credentials, or request financial transactions. Check the actual sender address rather than just the display name, and look out for unusual phrasing, spelling errors, or generic greetings. If you receive an email containing unexpected links or attachments, do not click on them or download any files. 

If you suspect an email is fraudulent, forward it immediately to the information technology help desk and delete the message from your inbox. When in doubt, contact the alleged sender through a known, separate communication channel to verify the request. Thank you for your continued vigilance in keeping our network secure.
