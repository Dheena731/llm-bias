To keep fresh herbs vibrant for as long as possible, treat them like a bouquet of cut flowers. Trim the ends of the stems and place them upright in a jar with an inch of water, loosely covering the leaves with a plastic bag before refrigerating. This method works best for tender herbs like cilantro, parsley, and dill. For woody varieties such as rosemary, thyme, and oregano, wrap the bunch gently in a slightly damp paper towel and store it inside a sealed container or resealable bag in the crisper drawer to maintain humidity without causing rot.
