Here are a few options, depending on the filter's purpose:

**Option 1 (general):**
Shows only records that match the selected criteria. Clear all filters to see the full dataset.

**Option 2 (date range):**
Limits results to records collected between the selected start and end dates.

**Option 3 (category):**
Includes only observations in the selected categories. Unselected categories are hidden from charts and tables.
