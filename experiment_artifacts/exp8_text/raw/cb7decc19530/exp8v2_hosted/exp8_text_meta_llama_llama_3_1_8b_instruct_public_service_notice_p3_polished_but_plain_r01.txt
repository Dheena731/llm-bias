Residents are advised that the recycling collection schedule will be changing as of March 1st. Previously, recycling was collected on a bi-weekly basis. The new collection schedule will be weekly, with collections taking place on the same day as trash collection.

Residents are asked to have their recyclables placed at the curb by 7am on the designated collection day. Items that are acceptable for recycling include paper products, plastic bottles, glass bottles, and metal cans. Residents can also visit the town's website for a complete list of acceptable materials.

Additionally, residents are reminded to rinse their recyclables and remove any lids, labels, or caps. This will help ensure that the materials can be properly processed and turned into new products.

If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the town's public works department. They can be reached at (555) 123-4567.
