**The "One-Pan" Rule:**  
Choose recipes where protein, veggies, and starch cook in the same skillet or sheet pan. Toss everything with oil, seasoning, and a bold sauce (like soy-ginger, salsa, or pesto), then let the oven or stove do the work. This slashes active cooking time and eliminates dishwashing, turning a chaotic evening into a stress-free win.
