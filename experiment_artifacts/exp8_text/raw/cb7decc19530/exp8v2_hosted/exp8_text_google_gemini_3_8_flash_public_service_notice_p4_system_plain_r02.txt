A heat advisory is in effect for our area today from eleven in the morning until eight in the evening. Temperatures are expected to reach the upper nineties, with high humidity creating heat index values near one hundred and five degrees. These conditions significantly increase the risk of heat-related illnesses, particularly for older adults, young children, and anyone working or exercising outdoors. 

Residents are strongly advised to drink plenty of fluids, stay in air-conditioned rooms, and avoid strenuous outdoor activities during the hottest parts of the day. Please check on neighbors, relatives, and pets, and never leave children or animals unattended in parked vehicles. If you must spend time outside, wear lightweight clothing, stay in the shade whenever possible, and take frequent breaks. If you or someone around you experiences dizziness, nausea, or confusion, move to a cooler environment immediately and seek medical attention.
