**Season in two stages for better flavor.** Before roasting, toss your vegetables with oil, salt, and pepper so the seasoning clings and helps them brown. Once they come out of the oven, finish with a squeeze of lemon juice or a splash of vinegar, plus a pinch of flaky salt and any fresh herbs. Delicate ingredients like garlic, fresh herbs, and citrus zest can burn or turn bitter in high heat, so adding them at the end keeps their flavor bright.
