Here is a short notice about reporting a lost company laptop:

**Lost Company Laptop**

If you have misplaced or lost your company-issued laptop, please report it to the IT department immediately.

**What to do:**

* Notify the IT department as soon as possible
* Provide your name, employee ID, and a detailed description of the laptop (including make, model, and any distinctive features)
* If the laptop has been damaged or is no longer functional, please let us know

**Why report it:**

* To prevent unauthorized access to company data
* To ensure the laptop is not used by someone else
* To initiate the process of replacing or recovering the device

**Contact:**

* IT Department: [insert contact information, such as phone number, email, or online reporting form]

Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter.
