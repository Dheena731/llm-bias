**Lost Company Laptop Reporting Procedure**

If you have misplaced a company laptop, please follow these steps to report the incident:

1. **Immediately notify your supervisor**: Inform them of the situation as soon as possible, so they can be aware of the situation.
2. **Fill out the Laptop Incident Report Form**: You can find this form on the company's intranet site under "Employee Resources" > "Lost/Stolen Equipment". Please provide detailed information about the laptop, including its serial number, model, and any distinctive features.
3. **Contact the IT Department**: Reach out to the IT team at [it@company.com](mailto:it@company.com) or 555-1234 to report the lost laptop. They will guide you through the next steps and arrange for the laptop to be remotely wiped or flagged for security purposes.
4. **Activate your personal account lockout feature**: This will help prevent unauthorized access to your company account. You can do this by visiting the "Account Management" section of the company's website.

**Important Notes**

* The company's Data Protection Policy requires that all lost or stolen devices be reported and secured within 24 hours of discovery.
* In cases of catastrophic loss, such as a theft or severe damage, please notify your supervisor and the IT Department immediately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the IT Department or your HR representative.

**Reporting a Lost Laptop is a necessary step to ensure the security of company data. Your prompt attention to this matter is greatly appreciated.**
