Here's a calendar tip that changed how I work: stop using your calendar only for meetings and start scheduling your tasks too. When you block out an hour for writing a report or answering emails, you can see how full your day really is before you agree to anything new. It also keeps you from treating every open slot as free time. Give each task a start time and a realistic length, and if it slips, drag it to another day instead of letting it linger on a to-do list. After a week or two, you'll have a much clearer sense of how long things actually take.
