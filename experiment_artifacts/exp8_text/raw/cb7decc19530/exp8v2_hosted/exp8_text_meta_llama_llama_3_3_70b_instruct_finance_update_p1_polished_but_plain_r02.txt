We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you keep track of your expenses. This summary will provide a clear and concise overview of your spending habits, allowing you to easily identify areas where you can cut back and make adjustments to stay within your budget. The summary will include a breakdown of your total spending for the month, as well as a categorization of your expenses into different areas such as housing, transportation, and entertainment. You will receive this summary at the beginning of each month, giving you a timely and accurate picture of your financial activity from the previous month. This new feature is designed to make it easier for you to manage your finances and make informed decisions about your money.
