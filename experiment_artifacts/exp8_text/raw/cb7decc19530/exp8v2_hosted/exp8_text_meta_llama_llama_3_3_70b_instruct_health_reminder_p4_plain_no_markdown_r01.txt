It's easy to get caught up in work or entertainment on our devices, but it's essential to take short breaks from screens throughout the day. Taking a few minutes to look away and stretch can help reduce eye strain and improve focus. Regular breaks can also help prevent headaches and improve overall well-being. Try to take a break every hour to stand up, move around, and rest your eyes. Even a short walk or some stretching exercises can make a big difference in how you feel. Remember to prioritize your health and take breaks from screens regularly.
