**NOTICE OF POLLING PLACE CHANGES**
**Harlow County Board of Elections**
Effective for the General Election, Tuesday, November 4

Three precincts will vote at new locations this year. The old sites are closed for this election.

| Precinct | Former Location | New Location |
|---|---|---|
| 07 (Eastside) | Lincoln Elementary gym | Eastside Library, Community Room, 412 Pine St. |
| 19 (Route 9 corridor) | Grange Hall | Route 9 Fire Station No. 2, Bay 1 entrance |
| 22 (Millbrook) | St. Brendan's Parish Hall | Millbrook Senior Center, north lot entrance |

Voters in these precincts who received a card in the mail with the old address should disregard it. Your correct polling place is the one listed above.

**Other things to know:**
- Polls are open 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Anyone in line at 8:00 p.m. will still be allowed to vote.
- The Eastside Library and Senior Center entrances have ramps. The Route 9 station's accessible entrance is on the east side of the building, marked with a blue sign.
- To confirm your precinct and location, enter your street address at harlowvotes.example or call the elections office at (555) 014-2290 before Election Day.

Questions about this notice can be directed to the Elections Office, 90 Courthouse Square, Room 104.
