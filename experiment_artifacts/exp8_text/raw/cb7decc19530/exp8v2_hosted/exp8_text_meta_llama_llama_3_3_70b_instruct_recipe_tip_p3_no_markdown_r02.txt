To make rice less sticky, try rinsing it thoroughly before cooking to remove excess starch. Then, add a small amount of oil or butter to the water when cooking the rice, as this will help to separate the grains and prevent them from sticking together. Additionally, using the right ratio of water to rice and not overcrowding the pot can also help to achieve fluffy and separate grains. Finally, fluffing the cooked rice with a fork can help to break up any clumps and make it less sticky.
