Reminder: Book Your Annual Health Checkup

It's time to schedule your yearly health checkup. Regular exams help catch potential issues early and keep you on track with your health goals.

To do this week:
- Call or go online to your doctor's office to request an appointment
- Check your insurance coverage for preventive visits
- Make a list of any questions or symptoms you want to discuss
- Gather your medication list and any recent health records

Choose a date that works for you and add it to your calendar so it doesn't slip by. A little time now can make a big difference later.
