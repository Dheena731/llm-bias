Here is a short, clear message you can use:

***

**What is an End-of-Year Tax Statement?**

An end-of-year tax statement is an official document that summarizes all of your financial activity with an organization over the past calendar year (such as total income earned, interest received, or charitable donations made). 

You receive this document in January or February, and it provides the official information you (or your tax preparer) need to accurately file your annual income tax return. Please keep it in a safe place with your important financial records.
