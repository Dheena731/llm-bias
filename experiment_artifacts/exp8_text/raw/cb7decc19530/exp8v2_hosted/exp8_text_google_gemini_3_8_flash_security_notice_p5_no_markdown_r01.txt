URGENT NOTICE: REPORTING LOST OR STOLEN COMPANY LAPTOPS

All employees are reminded that any lost or stolen company-issued laptop must be reported immediately. Prompt notification allows the IT Security team to remotely lock the device and safeguard company data.

If your laptop goes missing, take the following steps without delay:

1. Contact the IT Service Desk immediately at extension 4357 or via email at security@company.com.
2. Notify your direct supervisor.
3. If you suspect theft, file a report with local law enforcement and provide the police report number to IT.

When reporting, please provide the last known location of the device, the approximate time it went missing, and the laptop asset tag number if available. 

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our organization secure.
