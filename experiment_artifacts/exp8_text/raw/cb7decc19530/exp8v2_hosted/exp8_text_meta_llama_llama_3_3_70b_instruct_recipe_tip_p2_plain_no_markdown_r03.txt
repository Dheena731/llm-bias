To keep fresh herbs fresh for a longer period, store them in a cool dry place, away from direct sunlight. You can wrap the herbs in a damp paper towel and place them in a plastic bag or airtight container to maintain humidity and prevent drying out. For herbs like basil and mint, you can also store them in a glass of water, like a bouquet of flowers, and keep them in the refrigerator to keep them fresh for up to a week. Regularly trimming the stems and changing the water can help extend the life of the herbs.
