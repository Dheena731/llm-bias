**ACTION REQUIRED: Reporting an Unaccounted-for or Missing Laptop**

If your company-issued machine is missing—whether left in transit, stolen, or misplaced—report it immediately. Do not spend hours retracing your steps before notifying IT. Our remote-wipe and credential-revocation protocols only work if initiated before a device is taken offline or modified.

**Immediate Steps (from your phone or another device):**

1. **Alert the Security Desk:** Call the 24/7 Incident Line at **ext. 8811** (or +1-555-019-8811) or post directly in the Slack channel `#soc-urgent`.
2. **Provide device context:** 
   * Asset tag or serial number (if known; otherwise, your username is sufficient).
   * Time and location last seen.
   * State of the machine when lost: powered off, sleeping, or logged in/unlocked.
   * Any peripheral access items lost with it (YubiKey, physical building badges, MFA devices).
3. **Stand by for token revocation:** SecOps will invalidate your active Okta, VPN, and Google Workspace sessions server-side. Do not attempt manual password resets until SecOps has preserved the necessary authentication logs.

*Policy Reminder:* Lost hardware is an operational reality; a delayed report is a compliance violation. You will not face disciplinary action for reporting a lost machine immediately. Disciplinary reviews only apply to incidents where reporting was withheld or delayed.
