**Subject: Update: Introducing Your New Monthly Spending Summary**

Starting this month, we are rolling out a new monthly spending summary to help you track your expenses more efficiently. Each report provides a clear, consolidated view of your total spending, broken down by category and date. The summary will be generated at the beginning of every month and includes only confirmed, completed transactions. You can access the full report through your usual portal or request a copy directly from our support team. Please review the document each cycle and reach out if you have questions about any charges or need clarification on how categories are assigned. This update is intended to give you greater visibility into your spending patterns and make your financial reviews quicker and more accurate.
