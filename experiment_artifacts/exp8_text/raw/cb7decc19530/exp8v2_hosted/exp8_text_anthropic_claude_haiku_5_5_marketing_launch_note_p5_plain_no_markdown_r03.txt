Welcome to Words Between Us, a new podcast for anyone who has ever wanted to speak another language without feeling like they have to become someone else to do it. Each episode, we sit down with a learner, a teacher, or a native speaker and talk through the small, human side of language: the phrase that finally clicked, the embarrassing mix-up at the market, the grandmother whose kitchen vocabulary you still carry around.

We are starting with a simple idea. Language learning is not a race to perfection. It is a series of conversations, some awkward and some beautiful, that slowly widen the world. In our first season, we will explore how people build daily habits around a new language, how they stay motivated after the initial excitement fades, and what it feels like the first time someone responds to you in the language you have been practicing alone.

Whether you are a complete beginner, a returning learner, or someone raising children in a bilingual home, this show is for you. Pull up a chair, bring your rough accent and your half-remembered verbs, and join us. The first episode drops next week.
