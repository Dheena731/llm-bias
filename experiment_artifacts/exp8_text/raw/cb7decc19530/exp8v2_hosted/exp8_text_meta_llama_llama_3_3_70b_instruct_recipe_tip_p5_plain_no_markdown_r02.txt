To bring out the best flavor in roasted vegetables, season them before roasting with a combination of salt, pepper, and any other herbs or spices that complement their natural flavor. For example, rosemary and thyme pair well with root vegetables like carrots and Brussels sprouts, while oregano and garlic are a good match for vegetables like broccoli and cauliflower. You can also add a squeeze of lemon juice or a drizzle of olive oil to enhance the flavor and texture of the roasted vegetables. Experiment with different seasoning combinations to find the ones that you enjoy the most.
