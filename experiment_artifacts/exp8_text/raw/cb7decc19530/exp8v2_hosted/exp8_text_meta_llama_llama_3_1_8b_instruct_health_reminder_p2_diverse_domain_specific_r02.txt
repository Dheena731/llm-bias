Here's a reminder that's specific to a domain where water intake is crucial: the outdoors.

**For Hikers and Campers:**

Don't let dehydration ruin your adventure. Drink at least 2-3 liters of water per day, especially on hot or humid days. Aim to drink a full liter before each hike or trek, and take sips throughout the day to stay hydrated. Even mild dehydration can sneak up on you in the wilderness, making it harder to navigate, think clearly, and enjoy the scenery. So, grab that water bottle or hydration pack and stay ahead of the game. Your body – and the trails – will thank you.
