PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY

A heat advisory is in effect as temperatures and humidity levels remain elevated through Thursday evening. Please take immediate steps to stay safe. Drink water frequently throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty. Limit outdoor exercise and labor to the early morning or late evening. Wear light-colored, loose-fitting clothing and minimize direct sun exposure. Never leave children or animals inside parked vehicles. Check on elderly neighbors, young children, and individuals managing chronic illnesses, as they are most vulnerable to overheating. If you notice signs of heat exhaustion such as dizziness, nausea, headache, or heavy sweating, move to a cool environment right away and seek medical attention if symptoms worsen. Community cooling shelters are operating on extended schedules; consult local news broadcasts and official weather channels for nearby locations. Monitor conditions closely and follow public health guidance to protect yourself and your household.
