A heat advisory is now in effect for the region from Tuesday morning through Friday evening as temperatures are forecast to remain above normal with overnight lows offering little relief. Residents should take precautions to stay safe during this period. Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty. Limit strenuous activity to early morning or late evening hours. Check on elderly neighbors, young children, and those with preexisting health conditions who may be at higher risk. Never leave children or pets unattended inside parked vehicles, as interior temperatures can rise rapidly within minutes. If you must spend time outdoors, wear lightweight, light-colored clothing and apply sun protection regularly. Local emergency services will remain fully staffed, but please direct nonemergency inquiries to the municipal information line. Additional updates will be posted through official government websites and local broadcast stations. Stay vigilant and look out for one another.
