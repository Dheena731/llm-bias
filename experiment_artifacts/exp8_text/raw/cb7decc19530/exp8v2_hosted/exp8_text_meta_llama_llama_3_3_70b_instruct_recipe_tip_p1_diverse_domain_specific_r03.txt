When cooking a weeknight dinner, consider the "5-10-15" rule to streamline your prep time. This means selecting a protein that can be cooked in under 5 minutes (such as pan-seared chicken breast or shrimp), a vegetable that can be sautéed in 10 minutes (like bell peppers or broccoli), and a grain that can be cooked in 15 minutes (like quinoa or brown rice). By choosing ingredients with complementary cooking times, you can have a balanced and satisfying meal on the table in about 30 minutes, without feeling rushed or overwhelmed. Plus, this approach allows for flexibility and creativity, so you can swap out ingredients based on what's in season and what your family loves. Give it a try and see how it simplifies your weeknight dinner routine!
