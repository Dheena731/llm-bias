As a reminder to all staff, it is essential to lock your computer screens when leaving your workstation unattended. This is a critical step in maintaining the security and confidentiality of our organization's data. By locking your screen, you can prevent unauthorized access to sensitive information and protect against potential data breaches. Please make it a habit to lock your screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if it's just for a short period of time. If you have any questions or concerns about screen locking procedures, please don't hesitate to reach out to the IT department for assistance.
