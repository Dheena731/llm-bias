Update on Delayed Payment Processing

We want to let you know that some payments are currently taking longer than usual to process. Our team identified the issue earlier today, and we are working with our payment partners to resolve it as quickly as possible.

At this time, payments submitted over the past day may appear as pending rather than completed. Please do not resubmit a payment you have already made, since this could result in a duplicate charge. Once processing resumes, all pending payments will be completed in the order they were received, and you will receive a confirmation when yours goes through.

We expect most delayed payments to clear within the next one to two business days. If a payment has not been completed after that time, please contact our support team and we will look into it right away. We will also waive any late fees that result from the delay.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience. We will share another update as soon as we have more information.
