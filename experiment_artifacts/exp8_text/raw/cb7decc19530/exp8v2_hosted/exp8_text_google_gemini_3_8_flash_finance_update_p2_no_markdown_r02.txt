We are writing to inform you of an upcoming update to our card transaction fees, effective November 1, 2024.

Due to rising processing costs from major card networks and investments in our payment security systems, the standard processing fee for credit and debit card payments will adjust from 2.5% to 2.7% plus $0.15 per transaction.
