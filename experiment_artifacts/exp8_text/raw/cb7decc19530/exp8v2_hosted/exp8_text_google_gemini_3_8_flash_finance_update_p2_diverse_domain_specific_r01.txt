**Subject: Update to card processing schedule effective November 1, 2024**

Hi [Merchant Name],

Starting November 1, 2024, we are adjusting the pass-through interchange and scheme fees applied to your card-not-present (CNP) transactions. 

This update reflects the semi-annual interchange revisions from Visa and Mastercard, alongside increased scheme assessment costs for non-tokenized transactions. 

**Summary of adjustments:**

* **Consumer Credit (Domestic):** Variable rate shifts from 2.15% + $0.20 to **2.25% + $0.20** per settled transaction.
* **Network Tokenization Discount:** Transactions routed via network tokens (VTS/MDES) will receive an automatic **$0.03 rebate per authorization**, offsetting the variable adjustment for authenticated traffic.
* **International Card Processing:** The cross-border assessment fee increases by **15 bps** (from 1.10% to 1.25%) on all transactions issued outside the domestic clearing region, independent of currency conversion.
* **Authorization Retries:** In line with updated card brand integrity fees, authorizations exceeding three retries within a 24-hour window will incur an additional **$0.12 network compliance surcharge**.

These adjustments will automatically reflect on your November billing statement (generated December 1). If you are on an Interchange Plus Plus (IC++) pricing schedule, these network fee changes will pass through directly without alterations to your contracted acquirer margin. 

Your full, updated fee schedule and historical BIN-level reporting can be exported directly from the **Billing & Settlements** tab in your dashboard.

Please direct any questions regarding specific settlement impacts or tokenization implementation to your account manager or merchantbilling@[provider].com.
