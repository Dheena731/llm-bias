Here's a short notice about recycling collection changes specific to the domain of a coastal town with a strong focus on marine conservation:

**Important Update: Recycling Collection Changes in Coastal Cove**

As part of our ongoing efforts to reduce waste and promote a more sustainable community, the Coastal Cove Sanitation District is introducing changes to our recycling collection program. Effective immediately, residents are required to sort recyclables into one of two designated bins: paper and cardboard, and plastics and glass.

**Key Changes:**

* The "mixed" bin previously used for collecting all recyclables will be phased out, as we work to increase the quality and quantity of materials being recycled.
* Residents are encouraged to rinse containers and remove lids, labels, and caps before placing them in the designated bins.
* Special collection events for bulk items, such as textiles and electronics, will be scheduled quarterly at the Coastal Cove Community Center.

**Tips for Successful Recycling:**

* Check the Coastal Cove website or contact the Sanitation District for a detailed list of accepted materials.
* Help us keep our beaches clean by disposing of hazardous waste, such as batteries and fluorescent light bulbs, at the designated collection sites.

**Why the Changes?**

By streamlining our recycling program, we aim to reduce contamination rates, increase the value of recyclables, and ultimately protect the marine ecosystem that makes our community so unique. Your cooperation and participation are crucial to the success of this initiative.

Thank you for your attention to this matter and for your commitment to a more sustainable Coastal Cove.
