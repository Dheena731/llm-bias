Set aside a dedicated block of time once a week to prepare three distinct base ingredients such as a grain, a protein, and a roasted vegetable that can be combined in various ways throughout your schedule. By storing these elements separately in reusable containers, you create a flexible system that allows you to assemble fresh-tasting meals quickly without relying on repetitive cooking. This strategy minimizes daily decision fatigue and ensures you always have a balanced option available, freeing up mental space and time for your most important responsibilities.
