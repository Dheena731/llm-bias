**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY**
Harlan County Emergency Management | Issued Tuesday, July 15, 6:10 a.m.

Heat index readings of 103 to 108°F are forecast from 11 a.m. Tuesday through 8 p.m. Thursday. Overnight lows will stay near 77°F, so homes without air conditioning will not cool down at night. The river valley and the Route 9 corridor are expected to run hottest.

**Cooling centers** are open 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. daily at the Eastside and Mill Street branch libraries and the Harlan Senior Center. Bring a water bottle. Route 4 and Route 9 buses will carry passengers to any center free of charge, with no sign-up needed.

**Check on people who live alone**, especially anyone over 65, anyone using oxygen or dialysis, and anyone taking heart or blood-pressure medication. A phone call or a knock in the afternoon is enough. In past heat events, most deaths in this county occurred in homes where no one realized the resident was struggling.

**Know the warning signs.** Heavy sweating, nausea, headache, or muscle cramps mean move to shade or air conditioning and sip water slowly. Confusion, hot dry skin, or vomiting means call 911 immediately.
