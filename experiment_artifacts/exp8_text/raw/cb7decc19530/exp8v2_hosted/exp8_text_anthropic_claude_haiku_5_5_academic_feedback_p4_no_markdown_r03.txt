Hi Maya,

Thanks for sending the draft of your poster on soil microbiome responses to drought. The central finding is clear and worth showing, and the color scheme makes the layout easy to scan.

The biggest issue is the title. At the moment it reads as a topic rather than a claim. Consider stating your main result in the title, such as "Drought reduces fungal diversity in semi-arid soils," so viewers know what you found from a distance.

The methods section is too dense for a poster viewer who has 30 seconds. Cut it to three short points: site selection, sampling schedule, and sequencing approach. Move the full protocol to a handout or QR code.

The main figure needs larger axis labels and a legend placed inside the plot area. As it stands, the text would be hard to read from more than a meter away.

Please add a one-sentence takeaway in a highlighted box near the bottom. Many visitors will skip the full text, so this gives them the key point.

Finally, check that your contact information and the QR code for the full paper are in the lower corner and large enough to scan.

Overall, this is in good shape. Tightening the title and trimming the methods should make the biggest difference. Happy to look at a revised version before you print.
