We are writing to inform you of an upcoming update to our card transaction processing fees. This change reflects recent increases in card network interchange costs and our ongoing investments in payment security and platform reliability. We understand that fee adjustments directly impact your business, and we remain committed to providing you with the highest level of service and transaction speed. If you have any questions about how this change applies to your account, please reach out to our customer support team at your convenience. Thank you for your continued partnership.
