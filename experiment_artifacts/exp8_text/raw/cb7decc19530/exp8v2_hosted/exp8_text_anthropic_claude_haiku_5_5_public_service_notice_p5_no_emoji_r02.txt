**NOTICE: Polling Station Locations**

Dear Residents,

Please be advised that polling station locations have been assigned for the upcoming election. Each registered voter is assigned to a specific polling station based on their residential address.

To find your polling station:

- Check your voter registration card, which lists your assigned location.
- Visit the official election website and enter your address in the polling place lookup tool.
- Contact your local election office during business hours.

Polling stations are open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on Election Day. Voters in line at the closing time will be permitted to cast their ballots.

Please note that polling locations may differ from those used in previous elections. Confirm your location before Election Day to avoid confusion.

For accessibility assistance or additional information, please contact your local election office.

Thank you for participating in the democratic process.

Local Election Office
