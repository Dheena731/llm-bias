Please be aware that there has been an increase in phishing emails targeting our organization. These emails are designed to trick employees into divulging sensitive information such as login credentials or financial information. They often appear to come from a legitimate source, but may contain spelling or grammar mistakes, or create a sense of urgency to prompt you into taking action without thinking.

To protect yourself and our organization, it is essential to be cautious when receiving emails that ask for personal or sensitive information. Never respond to an email that requests your login credentials or other sensitive information, and never click on a link or download an attachment from an unfamiliar sender. If you are unsure about the authenticity of an email, please contact the IT department for assistance.

Additionally, please remember that our organization will never ask you to verify your account information or provide sensitive data via email. If you receive an email that appears to be from our organization and is requesting this type of information, it is likely a phishing attempt and should be reported to the IT department immediately.

Your vigilance in this matter is greatly appreciated, and we are available to provide any guidance or support you may need to stay safe online.
