Voters are reminded that polling station locations for the upcoming election are now finalized. Polling places will be open from seven in the morning until eight in the evening on election day. Because district boundaries and venue availability can change, your assigned polling station may be different from where you voted in previous years. You must cast your ballot at your specific designated precinct for your vote to be counted. To confirm your assigned location, please check the official voter information card recently sent to your registered address by mail, or contact the local elections office directly by telephone. Please verify your voting location before heading out to the polls on election day.
