The recycling collection schedule in our area is changing. As of next month, recyclables will be collected every other week instead of weekly. This change is intended to help reduce costs and make the recycling program more efficient. Residents are advised to check their collection calendars for the new schedule and to make sure they are putting out their recyclables on the correct day. The types of materials that can be recycled have not changed, but residents are reminded to rinse all containers and remove lids and labels before putting them in the recycling bin.
