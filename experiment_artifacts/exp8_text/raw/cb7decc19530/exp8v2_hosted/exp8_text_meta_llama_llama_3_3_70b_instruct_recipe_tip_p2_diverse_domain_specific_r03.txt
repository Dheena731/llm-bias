When storing fresh herbs, try treating them like a bouquet of flowers. Trim the stems at an angle, and place them in a glass of water or a vase, just as you would with fresh cut flowers. Then, cover the leaves with a plastic bag or a damp paper towel to maintain humidity. This will help keep your herbs, such as parsley, basil, or cilantro, fresh for up to a week. For more delicate herbs like mint or lemongrass, you can also wrap them in a damp paper towel and store them in the refrigerator to prevent bruising and browning. By giving your fresh herbs a little extra TLC, you'll be able to enjoy their bright flavors and aromas in your cooking for days to come.
