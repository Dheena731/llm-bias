NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

This notice is provided to inform you of the refund eligibility of purchases made from our company.

Unless otherwise specified, purchases made from our company are eligible for a refund if the item is returned within thirty days of the original purchase date. To be eligible for a refund, the item must be in its original condition, with all original tags, packaging, and accessories still intact.

If you wish to return an item for a refund, you must notify us within the thirty-day period and obtain a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number from us. The RMA number must be included with the returned item.

Refunds will be issued in the original form of payment, minus any restocking fees that may be applicable. If a refund is issued, it will be processed within five business days of receipt of the returned item.

Please note that certain items, including personalized and custom-made items, are not eligible for refunds. Additionally, items that are damaged or have been used in a way that goes against their intended purpose may not be eligible for a refund.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding refund eligibility, please do not hesitate to contact us.
