Plan your weekly lunches around a single batch-cooked base, such as grains or roasted vegetables, that can be easily repurposed each day. On Sunday, divide the base into four or five containers and store dressings or sauces in small separate lids or packets to preserve texture. Rotate simple, ready-to-use proteins like canned beans, rotisserie chicken, or firm tofu across the days to add variety without additional cooking. This method guarantees consistent, ready-to-eat meals, eliminates daily decision-making, and fits naturally into a packed schedule.
