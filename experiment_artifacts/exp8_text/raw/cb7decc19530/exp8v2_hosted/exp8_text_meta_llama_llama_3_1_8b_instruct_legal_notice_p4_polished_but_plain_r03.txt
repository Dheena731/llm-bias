NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Any customer who has made a purchase from our company and is not satisfied with the product or service may be eligible for a refund, provided that the request is made within a reasonable time period and in accordance with our refund policy.

To be eligible for a refund, the customer must have purchased the product or service within the last 30 days and must have used the product or service in a manner consistent with its intended use. Refunds will be issued in the original form of payment, and may be subject to a restocking fee of up to 20% of the purchase price.

Customers who wish to request a refund must contact our customer service department in writing, by mail or email, and must provide a detailed explanation of the reason for the request. Refunds will be processed within 5 business days of receipt of the request.
