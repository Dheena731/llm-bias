PUBLIC SERVICE ANNOUNCEMENT: HEAT ADVISORY

A Heat Advisory is in effect for our area today from 11:00 AM until 8:00 PM. High temperatures and humidity will result in heat index values exceeding 100 degrees, creating a significant risk for heat exhaustion and heat stroke.

All residents are strongly advised to take the following safety precautions:

Drink plenty of water and avoid strenuous outdoor activities, especially during the afternoon hours. 

Remain indoors in air-conditioned spaces as much as possible. If you do not have air conditioning, consider visiting public locations such as the library or community center.

Never leave children or pets unattended in parked vehicles, as interior temperatures can reach lethal levels in minutes.

Check regularly on elderly neighbors, outdoor workers, and those with medical conditions.

If you or someone around you experiences confusion, rapid pulse, dizziness, or loss of consciousness, call 911 immediately, as these are signs of heat stroke.

Issued by the Local Office of Emergency Management.
