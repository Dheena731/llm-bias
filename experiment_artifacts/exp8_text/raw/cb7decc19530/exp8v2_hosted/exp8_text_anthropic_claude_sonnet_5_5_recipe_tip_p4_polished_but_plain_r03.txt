Choose a lunch formula on Sunday instead of planning five separate meals. A simple one is a grain, a protein, a vegetable, and a sauce. Cook a big batch of rice or quinoa, roast a tray of vegetables, prepare one protein such as chicken thighs or chickpeas, and mix a jar of dressing or sauce. Store each component in its own container, then assemble each day's lunch in a couple of minutes. Because the pieces are separate, you can vary them: the same ingredients become a grain bowl on Monday, a wrap on Tuesday, and a salad on Wednesday. Using the sauce to change the flavor keeps the week from feeling repetitive. Before you shop, check your calendar for days with late meetings or lunch plans, and prepare fewer portions for those days so nothing goes to waste. Finally, keep one backup meal on hand, such as soup in the freezer, so a rushed morning never turns into an expensive and unsatisfying lunch.
