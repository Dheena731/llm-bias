Join us this Saturday for the annual community fair at Riverside Park from ten in the morning until four in the afternoon. We will have live music, local food vendors, games for children, and a showcase of handcrafted goods from area artisans. Admission is free, and neighbors of all ages are welcome to attend. Pack a blanket, invite your friends, and spend the day celebrating our vibrant neighborhood. Check the municipal parks website for parking details and the full event schedule.
