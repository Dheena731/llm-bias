To make rice less sticky, rinse it thoroughly in cold water before cooking to wash away excess surface starch. Place the dry grains in a fine-mesh strainer or bowl, swirl them with your hands, and change the water several times until it runs mostly clear. While the rice is simmering, avoid stirring it, as agitation damages the grains and releases more starch into the liquid. Once the cooking time is up, remove the pot from the heat and let it rest covered for ten minutes before gently fluffing the grains with a fork.
