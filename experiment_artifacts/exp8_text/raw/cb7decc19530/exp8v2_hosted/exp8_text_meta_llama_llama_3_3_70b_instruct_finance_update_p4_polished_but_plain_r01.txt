As the year comes to a close, you will receive an end-of-year tax statement that summarizes your financial activity for the past twelve months. This statement is an important document that provides a detailed record of your income, deductions, and tax payments, and it will be used to prepare your tax return. The statement will include information such as your total income, any taxes withheld, and any contributions you made to retirement accounts or other tax-deferred savings plans. You should review the statement carefully to ensure that all of the information is accurate and complete, as this will help you to avoid any errors or discrepancies when filing your taxes. If you have any questions or concerns about your end-of-year tax statement, you should contact the relevant financial institution or tax authority for assistance.
