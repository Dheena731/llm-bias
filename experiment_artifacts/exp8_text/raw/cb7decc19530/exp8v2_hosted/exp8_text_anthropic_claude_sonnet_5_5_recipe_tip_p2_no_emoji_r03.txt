**Tip: Store fresh herbs like a bouquet.**

For tender herbs such as cilantro, parsley, and dill, trim the stems and stand them upright in a jar with an inch of water. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and refrigerate. Change the water every couple of days, and they should stay fresh for up to two weeks.

For basil, skip the fridge and keep it in a jar of water on the counter, away from direct sunlight, since cold temperatures cause it to blacken.

For sturdy herbs like rosemary and thyme, wrap them in a slightly damp paper towel and store them in a resealable bag or container in the refrigerator.
