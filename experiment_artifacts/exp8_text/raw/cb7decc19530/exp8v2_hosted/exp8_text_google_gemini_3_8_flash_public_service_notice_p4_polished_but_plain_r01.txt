A heat advisory is in effect for the local area from noon today through eight in the evening tomorrow. Dangerously high temperatures combined with high humidity are expected to produce heat index values exceeding one hundred degrees. These conditions pose a significant risk of heat exhaustion and heat stroke, particularly for young children, elderly residents, and individuals working or exercising outdoors.

Members of the public are advised to stay indoors in air-conditioned environments as much as possible and drink water frequently, even if not feeling thirsty. Limit strenuous outdoor activities, especially during the peak afternoon hours, and wear lightweight, loose-fitting clothing when outside. Never leave pets or passengers unattended in a parked vehicle under any circumstances. Please check on neighbors and relatives who may live alone or lack reliable cooling. If someone shows signs of heat-related illness, such as dizziness, confusion, or nausea, move them to a cooler location immediately and seek medical assistance.
