Join us this weekend for the Riverside Neighborhood Block Party on Saturday, June 14, from 11:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. at Maple Park, just off Elm Street. There will be live music from local bands, food trucks serving everything from tacos to fresh lemonade, and a kids' corner with face painting and a bounce house. Local artists will also be selling handmade crafts along the main path, and the community garden club will be offering free seedlings to anyone who stops by their table. Parking is available along Elm Street and at the lot behind the library. Bring your family, bring your neighbors, and bring a folding chair if you have one. Rain or shine, we'll be there, and we hope to see you soon.
