Subject: Upcoming Email Migration This Weekend

Hello everyone,

We will be moving our company email to a new platform this weekend, beginning Friday at 6:00 PM and finishing by Sunday at 6:00 PM. This move will give us more storage, better security, and improved search and calendar tools.

During the migration, you may notice that email delivery is delayed and that some messages arrive out of order. Please avoid deleting or reorganizing your mailbox from Friday evening until the migration is complete, since this can interfere with the transfer. Your existing messages, folders, contacts, and calendar entries will be moved automatically, so there is nothing you need to back up or export yourself.

On Monday morning, you will be asked to sign in with your current username and to set up a new password. Instructions for setting up the mobile app and desktop client will be sent to you on Friday afternoon. If you use email on your phone, you will need to remove your old account and add it again after the migration.

If you run into any problems, please contact the IT help desk by replying to this message or calling extension 4400. Extra support staff will be available on Monday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Thank you for your patience and cooperation as we make this change.
