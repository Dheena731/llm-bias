**Notice: Staying Alert to Phishing Emails**

Please remain vigilant regarding phishing emails—fraudulent messages sent by scammers to trick you into revealing sensitive information or downloading malicious software.

Watch for these common warning signs:
* Urgent requests for passwords, personal information, or financial details.
* Sender email addresses that look unusual or slightly misspelled.
* Unexpected attachments or links directing you to unfamiliar websites.

If you receive an email that looks suspicious:
1. Do not click any links or download any attachments.
2. Do not reply to the sender.
3. Forward the email directly to the IT support team and delete it from your inbox.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our workplace secure.
