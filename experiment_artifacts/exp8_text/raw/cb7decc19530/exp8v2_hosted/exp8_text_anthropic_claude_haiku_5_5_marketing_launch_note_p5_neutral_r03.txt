**Welcome to *Lingua Lounge*: Your Daily Dose of Language and Culture**

Do you ever wish you could order coffee in Madrid, joke with friends in Seoul, or read a poem in its original French? Starting today, *Lingua Lounge* is here to help you get there, one conversation at a time.

Each week, we'll bring you short, engaging episodes that make language learning feel less like homework and more like a window into the world. You'll hear native speakers, practice real-life phrases, and pick up the cultural context that textbooks often leave out: the slang teenagers actually use, the etiquette behind a dinner invitation, the stories behind everyday expressions.

Whether you're a complete beginner or brushing up on a language you studied years ago, there's a seat for you in the lounge. Grab a cup of something warm, press play, and let's start talking.
