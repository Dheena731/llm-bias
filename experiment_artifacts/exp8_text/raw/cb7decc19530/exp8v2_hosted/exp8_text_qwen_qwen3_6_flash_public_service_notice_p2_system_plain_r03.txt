The city will close Main Street between Oak Avenue and Elm Drive from June twelve to June fourteen. Construction crews will begin work each morning at six o clock and continue until seven o clock in the evening. The closure is necessary to replace an aging water main beneath the pavement. Motorists must follow posted signs and use Maple Boulevard as the official detour route. Sidewalk access remains open but pedestrians should anticipate brief pauses near the work zone. For schedule changes or further details, contact the municipal public works office at five five five zero one two three four. Thank you for your cooperation as we complete this essential improvement.
