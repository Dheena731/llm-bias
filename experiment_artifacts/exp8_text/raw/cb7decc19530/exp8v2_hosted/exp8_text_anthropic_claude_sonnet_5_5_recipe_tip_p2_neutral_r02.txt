**Tip: Treat tender herbs like flowers.**

For soft herbs like cilantro, parsley, and mint, trim the stems and stand them in a jar with an inch of water. Loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag and refrigerate. (Basil is the exception. Keep it in a jar on the counter, since cold turns its leaves black.)

For woody herbs like rosemary and thyme, wrap them in a slightly damp paper towel and store them in a zip-top bag or container in the fridge.

Change the water or paper towel every few days, and your herbs should stay fresh for 1–2 weeks.
