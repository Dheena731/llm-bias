This notice sets forth the conditions under which a customer may qualify for a refund on a purchase. Eligibility requires that the refund request be submitted within thirty days of the purchase date and that the item be returned in its original condition with proof of transaction intact. Services already rendered, digital downloads upon access, and customized orders are not eligible for reimbursement. Requests must be directed to our customer service department via email including the relevant order details and a statement of reason. Upon approval, refunds will be issued to the original form of payment within ten business days. Management reserves the sole discretion to modify this policy or deny claims that fail to satisfy these requirements or suggest misuse of the refund process.
