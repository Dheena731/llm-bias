Here is a short feedback note you could adapt for a student's methods section.

Thank you for submitting your methods section. You have clearly described your overall research design and the rationale for choosing a survey-based approach, which helps the reader understand why the study was structured as it was. Your description of the participant sample is a real strength: you specified the recruitment setting, the inclusion criteria, and the final sample size, which allows the reader to judge who the findings apply to.

There are a few areas to strengthen before resubmission. The procedure section moves quickly from recruitment to data collection, and it would benefit from a clear step-by-step account of what participants actually did, including how long the survey took and whether it was completed in person or online. The measures are named but not described; please state what each instrument assessed, how many items it contained, and what evidence supports its reliability or validity for your population. Finally, the data analysis paragraph mentions statistical tests without explaining why those tests suit your research questions or what assumptions you checked. Adding a sentence on how you handled missing data would also make the section more complete.

Your writing is generally precise and well organized. With these additions, the methods section will give a reader enough detail to evaluate and, if they wished, replicate your study. I would be glad to look at the revised version.
