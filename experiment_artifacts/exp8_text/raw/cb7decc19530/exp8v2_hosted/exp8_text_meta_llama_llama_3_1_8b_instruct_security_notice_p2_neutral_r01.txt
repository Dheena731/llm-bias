Here is a sample workplace notice about phishing emails:

**Important: Phishing Emails Alert**

To all employees,

As we continue to work together to maintain the security and integrity of our organization, we wanted to remind everyone of the importance of being vigilant when it comes to emails.

**Phishing Scams: A Growing Threat**

Phishing emails are becoming increasingly sophisticated and can be a significant threat to our organization's security. These emails may appear to be from a trusted sender, but are actually designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information or clicking on malicious links.

**Protect Yourself and Our Organization**

To avoid falling victim to phishing scams, please remember the following:

* Never respond to emails that ask for sensitive information, such as your login credentials, financial information, or personal details.
* Be cautious of emails that are urgent or threatening, as these are often tactics used by phishers to create a sense of panic.
* Verify the authenticity of emails by contacting the sender directly through a phone call or by checking the organization's official website.
* Never click on links or download attachments from unknown or untrusted sources.

**What to Do If You Receive a Suspicious Email**

If you receive an email that you suspect may be a phishing attempt, please report it to our IT department immediately. You can forward the email to [IT department email] and we will investigate and take necessary action.

**Together, Let's Stay Safe Online**

We are committed to maintaining a secure and trustworthy work environment. Your vigilance and cooperation are essential in preventing phishing scams and protecting our organization from potential threats.

Thank you for your attention to this matter, and for your ongoing commitment to our organization's security.

Best regards,
[Your Organization]
