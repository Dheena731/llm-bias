NOTICE OF RECYCLING COLLECTION CHANGES

As part of our ongoing efforts to improve our recycling program, we are making some changes to our collection schedule and guidelines.

Effective immediately, the recycling collection day will be changing from Wednesday to Saturday. This change is necessary to ensure that all materials are collected in a timely manner and to reduce the likelihood of contamination and incorrect disposal.

Additionally, we will be introducing a new system for separating recyclables. Please make sure to sort your recyclables into the following categories:

* Paper and Cardboard: newsprint, cardboard boxes, paper towels, and paper products
* Glass: clear, brown, and green glass bottles and jars
* Plastic: #1 and #2 plastic bottles and containers
* Metal: aluminum and steel cans and containers

Please make sure to rinse all materials before placing them in the recycling bin. We also ask that you do not bag your recyclables, as this can cause contamination and reduce the effectiveness of the recycling process.

If you have any questions or concerns about these changes, please do not hesitate to contact us at (555) 123-4567.

Thank you for your cooperation and participation in our recycling program. Together, we can make a positive impact on the environment.
