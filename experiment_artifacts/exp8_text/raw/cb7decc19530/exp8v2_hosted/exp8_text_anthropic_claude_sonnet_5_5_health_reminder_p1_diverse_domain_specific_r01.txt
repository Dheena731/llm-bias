**Weekly check-in: 5 minutes**

Open last week's log and look at three things:

- **Sessions:** How many did you plan, and how many did you finish? If you skipped one, write down why (schedule, tired, achy knee). The reason matters more than the count.
- **Numbers:** Did any lift, pace, or distance move? One extra rep at the same weight counts.
- **Recovery:** Check sleep and soreness. A flat squat day often traces back to two short nights before it.

Then pick one change for the week ahead: add 2.5 lb to your top set, move Thursday's run to the morning, or take the deload you've been putting off.
