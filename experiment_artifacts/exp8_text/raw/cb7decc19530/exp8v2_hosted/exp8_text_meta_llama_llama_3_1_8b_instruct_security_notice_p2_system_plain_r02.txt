Important Notice to All Staff

Phishing emails are a significant threat to our organization's security. These emails are designed to trick recipients into revealing sensitive information or clicking on links that may compromise our systems.

Be aware that phishing emails often appear to be from legitimate sources, but are actually attempts by scammers to gain unauthorized access to our data. They may ask for information such as login credentials, credit card numbers, or other sensitive details.

If you receive an email that asks for sensitive information or seems suspicious, do not respond to it. Instead, delete the email immediately and report it to the IT department. We will investigate the email and take necessary action to protect our systems.

Your vigilance is essential in preventing phishing attacks. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT department.
