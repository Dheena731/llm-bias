To all employees, we would like to remind you to be cautious when receiving emails that ask for sensitive information or prompt you to click on links. Phishing emails are becoming increasingly common and can be disguised as legitimate messages from within our organization or from reputable companies. These emails often try to trick you into revealing your login credentials, financial information, or other personal data. 

When receiving an email that seems suspicious, do not click on any links or download any attachments. Instead, delete the email and report it to the IT department. Legitimate emails from our organization will never ask you to reveal sensitive information via email. If you are unsure about the authenticity of an email, you can always contact the sender directly using a phone number or email address that you know is legitimate. 

It is essential that we all remain vigilant and take the necessary precautions to protect ourselves and our organization from these types of threats. If you have any questions or concerns about phishing emails, please do not hesitate to reach out to the IT department for assistance.
