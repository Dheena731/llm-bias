Calendar tip: Stop using your calendar only for meetings. Block out time for the tasks you need to finish, too.

When I started scheduling things like "Draft project proposal" or "Reply to emails" as actual events, my to-do list stopped piling up. Giving each task a specific time slot makes it far more likely to get done, and it shows you right away when your week is overbooked.

Try it for one week and see how it changes your planning.
