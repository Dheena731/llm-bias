We’ve deployed a new Savings Goal framework. You can now define exact target balances, assign fixed or percentage-based contributions, and designate multiple funding sources. Progress tracking uses daily interest accrual instead of flat projections, and milestone markers recalculate automatically if you adjust the target mid-cycle. Contributions pause instantly on request without resetting your scheduled rate, and all parameters export to CSV for external accounting tools. Configure your goals under Accounts → Savings Goals. Detailed configuration guides and known edge cases are published in the help center.
