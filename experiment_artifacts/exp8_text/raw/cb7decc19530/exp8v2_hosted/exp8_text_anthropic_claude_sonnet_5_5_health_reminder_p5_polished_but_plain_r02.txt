Reminder: Your prescription is due for a refill soon. To avoid running out, please contact your pharmacy or your doctor's office at least a few days before your last dose, since some refills require approval and can take time to process. Have your prescription number or the medication name ready when you call, and check whether you have any refills remaining. If you have none left, ask the pharmacy to send a renewal request to your doctor, or schedule an appointment if a review is needed. If you are close to running out and cannot get a refill in time, let your pharmacist know right away, as they may be able to offer a short emergency supply. Please do not skip doses or stretch out your medication to make it last longer without speaking to a healthcare professional first.
