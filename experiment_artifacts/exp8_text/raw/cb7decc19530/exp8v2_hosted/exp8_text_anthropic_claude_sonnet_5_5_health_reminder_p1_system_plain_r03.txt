Time for your weekly fitness check-in. Take a few minutes to look back at the past seven days and see how you did. Think about how many workouts you completed, how your energy and mood felt, and whether you moved closer to your goals. Celebrate what went well, even if the wins were small, and notice anything that got in your way so you can plan around it. Then choose one or two simple adjustments for the week ahead, such as scheduling your sessions in advance or adding a bit more rest. Progress comes from steady effort, so keep going at a pace you can maintain.
