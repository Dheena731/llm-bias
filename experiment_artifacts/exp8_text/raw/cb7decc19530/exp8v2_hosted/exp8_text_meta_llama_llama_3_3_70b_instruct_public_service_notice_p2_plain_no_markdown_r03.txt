A section of Main Street will be closed to traffic starting on Monday and lasting for approximately two weeks. The closure is necessary to allow for repairs to the road surface and will be in effect from the intersection with Oak Street to the intersection with Maple Street. During this time, traffic will be detoured onto nearby side streets. Drivers are advised to plan their routes accordingly and to expect delays in the area. The closure is expected to be completed by the end of the month, weather permitting.
