Introducing Savings Goals

We're excited to announce a new feature to help you reach your financial targets: Savings Goals.

You can now create custom goals for anything you're working toward, whether it's a vacation, a new laptop, or an emergency fund. Just give your goal a name, set a target amount, and choose a date you'd like to reach it by.

Here's what you can do:

- Track your progress with a simple visual progress bar
- Set up automatic transfers on a weekly or monthly schedule
- Create multiple goals and prioritize which ones matter most
- Get friendly reminders and celebrate milestones along the way

To get started, open the app, tap the Goals tab, and select "Create a Goal." It only takes a minute to set up.

Savings Goals is rolling out to all users over the next two weeks. If you don't see it yet, check back soon or make sure your app is updated to the latest version.

We'd love to hear what you think. Send us your feedback anytime through the Help section in the app.

Happy saving!
