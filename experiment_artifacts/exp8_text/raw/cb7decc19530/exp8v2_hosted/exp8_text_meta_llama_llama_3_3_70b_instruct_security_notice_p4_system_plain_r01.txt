To enhance the security of your account, we recommend enabling two-factor authentication. This feature requires both your password and a verification code sent to your phone or email to access your account. Enabling two-factor authentication will add an extra layer of protection against unauthorized access and help keep your information safe. You can enable two-factor authentication by going to your account settings and following the prompts to set up the feature. If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact our support team.
