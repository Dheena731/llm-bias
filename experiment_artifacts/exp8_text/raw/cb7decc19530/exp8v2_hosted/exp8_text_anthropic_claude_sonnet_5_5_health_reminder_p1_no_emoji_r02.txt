**Weekly Fitness Check-In**

It's time to review your progress for the week. Take a few minutes to:

- Look over your completed workouts and note any you missed
- Compare your results to your goals, such as steps, distance, weights, or active minutes
- Notice what went well and what felt challenging
- Set one or two realistic targets for the week ahead

Small, consistent improvements add up over time. Log your stats today and plan your next steps.
