Notice: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop

If you have lost a company laptop or believe it has been stolen, you must report it to the IT Security team immediately, and no later than two hours after you become aware of the loss. Prompt reporting allows us to secure company data, disable access, and reduce the risk to our systems and clients.

To report the loss, please take the following steps:

1. Contact the IT Service Desk by phone at extension 4357 or by email at itsupport@company.com. Available 24 hours a day.
2. Provide your name, employee ID, department, and the laptop's asset tag number, which is found on the bottom of the device and in your asset records.
3. Describe where and when the laptop was last seen, and whether it was lost or stolen.
4. If the laptop was stolen, file a police report and send a copy to Security at security@company.com.

The IT team will remotely lock or wipe the device where possible and will advise on any further action. Do not attempt to recover or access the device yourself.

Failure to report a lost or stolen device promptly may result in disciplinary action under company policy. If you have questions, please contact your line manager or the IT Service Desk.

Issued by: Information Technology Department
