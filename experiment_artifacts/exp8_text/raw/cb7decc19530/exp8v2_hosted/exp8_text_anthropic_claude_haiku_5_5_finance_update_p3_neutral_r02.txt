**Introducing Savings Goals: Plan Your Future, One Step at a Time**

We're excited to announce our newest feature: **Savings Goals**. Whether you're saving for a dream vacation, an emergency fund, a new home, or a big purchase, Savings Goals helps you set a target, track your progress, and stay motivated along the way.

**What you can do with Savings Goals:**
- **Set a goal** with a name, target amount, and deadline
- **Track progress** with visual indicators that show how close you are
- **Contribute on your terms** through one-time deposits or recurring transfers
- **Get reminders** to keep you on pace toward your target

Getting started takes just a minute. Open the app, tap **Goals** in the main menu, and choose "Create New Goal." From there, enter your target and we'll help you map out a plan.

Savings Goals is rolling out to all users over the next two weeks. Look for it in your dashboard, and let us know what you think in the feedback section. We can't wait to see what you achieve!
