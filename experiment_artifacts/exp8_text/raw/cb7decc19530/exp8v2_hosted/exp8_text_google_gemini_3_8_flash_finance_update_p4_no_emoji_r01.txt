An end-of-year tax statement is a document that summarizes your financial activity with an organization over the past calendar year. Depending on the provider, it may detail total earnings, taxes withheld, interest paid or earned, or charitable contributions made. 

You or your tax preparer will need this information to report accurate figures when filing your annual income tax return. Please review the statement carefully to ensure all details are correct, and keep a copy for your personal tax records.
