Every episode opens mid-scene. No greetings, no pacing drills. Just two speakers working through a misrouted shipment, a regional dialect clash, or a booking error—and we stop only when the language itself stumbles. We isolate the exact moment a learner would typically default to textbook English, then replay it with transparent notation on why the native phrasing branches differently: pragmatic hedging, preposition drift, register collapse, and the collocations that never show up in flashcards.

This podcast treats L2 acquisition like signal processing, not vocabulary accumulation. Each installment targets one high-friction interface between L1 and L2—false friends, topic-comment inversion, deference markers, aspectual nuance—and builds repeated, contextual exposure. Audio mixes prioritize natural prosody over enunciation; transcripts carry marginal calls on syntactic trade-offs and cultural pragmatics, not line-by-line translations.

If your current practice leaves you fluent in prompts but lost in replies, this is the correction. Episodes release biweekly. Open the transcript. Don’t pause for comprehension; pause for pattern recognition. Repeat until the syntax feels like reflex instead of reconstruction.
