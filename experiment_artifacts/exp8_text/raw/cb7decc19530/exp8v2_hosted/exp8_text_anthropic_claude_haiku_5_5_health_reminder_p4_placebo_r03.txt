Take a Screen Break

You've been looking at screens for a while, so it's a good time to step away for a few minutes. Stand up, stretch your shoulders and neck, and look at something at least 20 feet away for a moment to rest your eyes. Get some water, or simply walk to another room. These small pauses help reduce eye strain, stiffness, and fatigue, and they can help you think more clearly when you return. Your future self will thank you for the break.
