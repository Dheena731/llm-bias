This is a friendly reminder to all employees to lock your computer screens whenever you step away from your desk, even if it is only for a brief moment. Leaving workstations unlocked poses a serious security risk and can expose sensitive company and client information to unauthorized access. You can easily lock your screen by pressing the Windows key and L on a PC, or by pressing Command, Control, and Q on a Mac. Thank you for doing your part to keep our workplace and data safe.
