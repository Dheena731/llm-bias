**Mutations: a Welsh podcast for people who keep getting it wrong**

For two years I've been losing arguments about Welsh grammar to my nine-year-old.

Her school teaches in Welsh, so by Year 4 she was correcting my pronunciation over breakfast. "Fy nghi," I'd say, *my dog*, and she'd wince. "*Ci* becomes *nghi* after *fy*. The first letter changes." English has nothing like it, and the apps mention it once, in a footnote, if at all.

This show is my attempt to explain it properly. Each episode takes one feature of Welsh that trips up adult learners: the consonant mutations, the two different ways to say "to be," the fact that *yes* and *no* are really repeats of the verb rather than separate words. We work through it with real sentences, the mistakes I've made, and recordings from people who've put up with me: my kids, a bus driver in Pwllheli, and a retired vicar in Llŷn who corrects my letters in red ink.
