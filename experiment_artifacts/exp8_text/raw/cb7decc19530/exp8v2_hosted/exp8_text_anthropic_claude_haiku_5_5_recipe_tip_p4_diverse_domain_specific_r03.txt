Batch the grains and proteins on Sunday, but keep the sauces and crunchy toppings separate until you pack each lunch. A pot of farro, a tray of roasted chicken thighs, and a jar of tahini-lemon dressing will hold for four or five days in the fridge. Soggy cucumbers and wilted greens are what make a week of meal prep feel like a chore, so add those fresh on Wednesday or Thursday, or pack them in a separate container. Label each box with the day it's meant for, and you'll stop staring into the fridge at 12:15 wondering what you packed for yourself.
