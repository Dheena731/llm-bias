Here is a template you can customize. It includes placeholders for your specific details and covers the essential information needed for a clear, professional notification.

### **Subject:** Important Update: Change to Card Transaction Fees

**Dear [Customer/Merchant Name],**

We are writing to inform you of an upcoming adjustment to our card transaction fees.

**Effective [Date],** the fee structure will change as follows:

*   **Current Rate:** [e.g., 2.9% per transaction]
*   **New Rate:** [e.g., 3.1% per transaction]
*   **Applicable to:** [e.g., All credit/debit card transactions]

[**Optional:** *Briefly explain the reason if appropriate, e.g., "This adjustment reflects ongoing increases in third-party processing costs and enhanced security measures."*]

These changes will apply automatically to all qualifying transactions processed after **[Date]**. No action is required on your part.

If you have any questions or would like to discuss this further, please contact us at **[Email/Phone Link]**.

Thank you for your continued partnership/support.

Best regards,

**[Your Name]**  
**[Your Title]**  
**[Company Name]**

---

### 💡 **Tips for sending this note:**
*   **Timing:** Send this well in advance of the effective date (usually 30 days) to give recipients time to prepare.
*   **Clarity:** Ensure the "New Rate" is bolded and easy to spot.
*   **Tone:** If fees are increasing, keep the tone professional and empathetic. If fees are decreasing, you may want to add a sentence highlighting this benefit to build goodwill.
*   **Legal Check:** Depending on your industry
