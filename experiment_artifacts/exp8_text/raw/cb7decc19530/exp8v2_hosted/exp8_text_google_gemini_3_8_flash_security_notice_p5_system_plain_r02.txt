If you misplace or have your company-issued laptop stolen, you must report the incident immediately. Contact the IT service desk right away so the security team can remotely lock the device, protect company accounts, and safeguard sensitive data. After contacting IT, inform your direct supervisor about the loss. In the event that the laptop was stolen, file a report with local law enforcement and share the police report number with the security team as soon as possible. Prompt reporting is critical to maintaining network security and preventing unauthorized access to confidential information.
