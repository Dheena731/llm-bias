Taking regular breaks from screens is essential for maintaining productivity and overall well-being. When we spend extended periods in front of a screen, whether it's a computer, phone, or tablet, we can become distracted, tired, and prone to eye strain. 

Research suggests that taking short breaks every 60 to 90 minutes can be beneficial. During these breaks, it's best to step away from the screen and engage in an activity that doesn't involve a screen. This could be as simple as stretching, going for a short walk, or chatting with a colleague or friend. 

These short breaks can help to refresh the mind, reduce eye strain, and increase focus when returning to the screen. They can also provide an opportunity to recharge and refuel, reducing the likelihood of burnout.
