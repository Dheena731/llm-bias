Join us this Saturday at Maple Park for our annual Neighborhood Day Celebration! Doors open at 10 in the morning and the fun runs until 4 in the afternoon. There will be live music from local bands, a farmers market with fresh produce and baked goods, free face painting and games for the kids, and a community potluck at noon. Bring a dish to share if you can, and bring a blanket or lawn chair to settle in. Volunteers are still welcome, so stop by the welcome tent if you can spare an hour. Admission is free, and everyone is invited. Come meet your neighbors, support local makers, and enjoy a great day together. In case of rain, we will move everything to the community center on Elm Street. We hope to see you there!
