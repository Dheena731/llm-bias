We are writing to remind everyone to remain cautious of phishing emails. These are fraudulent messages that attempt to trick users into sharing confidential information or downloading harmful software. Please take a moment to examine the sender's address carefully before responding to any unexpected requests. Do not click on links or open attachments from unknown sources. If you suspect an email is not genuine, report it immediately to the IT department by forwarding the message to the security help desk. Your attention to detail helps keep our company data safe. Thank you for your cooperation.
