Roasted vegetables achieve their best flavor when you season them in layers rather than waiting until the end. Toss the raw vegetables with enough oil and a generous amount of salt before they go into the oven, as this allows the salt to penetrate the vegetables and draw out moisture for better caramelization. Once the vegetables are cooked and tender, finish them with a splash of bright acid like lemon juice or vinegar and a handful of fresh herbs to lift the deep, savory flavors developed during roasting.
