To plan lunches for a busy week, consider setting aside one day to prepare and portion out meals in advance. This can be as simple as cooking a large batch of rice, grains, or protein on Sunday evening, and then using those ingredients to assemble individual lunches for each day of the week. By doing so, you can save time during the week and ensure that you have healthy, ready-to-go meals to fuel your busy schedule. Additionally, consider keeping a list of your favorite lunch recipes and ingredients on hand to make planning and grocery shopping easier.
