"The research question posed in this study, namely 'what are the effects of climate change on coastal ecosystems?', is overly broad and lacks specificity. For instance, it is unclear whether the authors are investigating the impacts of sea-level rise, changes in ocean temperature, or shifts in precipitation patterns on coastal ecosystems. Furthermore, the question does not articulate which particular aspect of coastal ecosystems is being examined, such as biodiversity, shoreline morphology, or water quality. To enhance the study's focus and validity, the research question should be revised to target a more precise aspect of the complex interactions between climate change and coastal ecosystems, such as 'how do projected changes in sea-level rise affect salt marsh vegetation composition in temperate coastal zones?'"
