Notice: Reporting a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop

If your company-issued laptop is lost or stolen, please report it immediately. Prompt reporting is the best way to protect company data, our customers, and you.

Contact the IT Service Desk as soon as you realize the device is missing, even if you are not certain it is gone. You can call the Service Desk at the number listed on the intranet or send an email to the security team, but a phone call is preferred during business hours so that action can begin right away. If the loss happens outside business hours, use the after-hours security line and follow up by email the next morning.

When you report the loss, please be ready to share your name and department, the laptop's asset tag or serial number if you have it, the date, time, and location where you last had the device, and a brief description of what happened. Let us know whether you believe the laptop held sensitive information such as customer records, financial files, or login credentials saved on the device.

Once your report is received, IT will remotely lock the laptop, disable your account access from that device, and wipe it if necessary. You may be asked to change your passwords right away. If the laptop was stolen, please also file a report with local police and send a copy of the report number to the security team.

Reporting a lost device promptly will not result in disciplinary action in most cases. Delays in reporting, however, can increase risk and may be reviewed under company policy. A replacement laptop will be issued once the incident has been documented.

Thank you for helping us keep company information safe.
