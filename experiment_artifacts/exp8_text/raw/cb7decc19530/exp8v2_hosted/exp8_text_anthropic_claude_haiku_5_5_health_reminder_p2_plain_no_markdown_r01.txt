Hello there! This is a friendly reminder to make sure you drink enough water throughout the day. It is easy to get busy and forget, but your body relies on water for energy, focus, digestion, and keeping your temperature steady. Try keeping a glass or bottle nearby as you work, and take a few sips each time you check your messages or step away from your desk. If you notice you feel tired, headachy, or unfocused, a glass of water is often a good first step. Take care of yourself today, and keep hydrating!
