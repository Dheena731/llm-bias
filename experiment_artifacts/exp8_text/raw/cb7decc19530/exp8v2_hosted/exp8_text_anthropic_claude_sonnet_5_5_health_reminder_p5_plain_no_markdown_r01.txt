This is a reminder to refill your prescription before you run out. Most pharmacies need a day or two to prepare a refill, and some medications require approval from your doctor first, so it helps to request it a few days early. Check how many pills you have left and call or visit your pharmacy to place the order. If you have no refills remaining, contact your doctor's office so they can send a new prescription. Taking your medication consistently matters, so try not to let it lapse.
