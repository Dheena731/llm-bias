Here's a short reminder:

**Weekly Fitness Check-in**

Don't forget to review your progress from the past week! Take a moment to:

* Reflect on your fitness goals and how you're tracking towards them
* Update your workout log or journal to track your progress
* Make any necessary adjustments to your routine to stay on track

By regularly checking in with yourself, you'll be able to:

* Celebrate your successes and stay motivated
* Identify areas for improvement and make adjustments
* Stay focused on your long-term fitness goals

Take 5 minutes today to review your progress and set yourself up for success in the days to come!
