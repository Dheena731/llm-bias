**Reminder: Take Short Breaks from Screens**

Looking at a screen for a long time can leave your eyes tired and your body stiff. Give yourself a short break every so often.

- **Look away.** Focus on something far from you, such as a window or the other side of the room, for about 20 seconds.
- **Move.** Stand up, stretch, or walk around for a minute or two.
- **Blink and rest your eyes.** Blink fully a few times, or close your eyes briefly.
- **Make it a habit.** Set a timer or reminder to prompt you, such as every 20 to 30 minutes for a quick eye break and once an hour for a longer stretch.

Short breaks take very little time, and they can help you feel more comfortable and focused as you work.
