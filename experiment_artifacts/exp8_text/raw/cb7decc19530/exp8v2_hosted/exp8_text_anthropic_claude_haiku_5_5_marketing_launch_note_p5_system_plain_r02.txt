Welcome to Words Along the Way, the new podcast for anyone who has ever wanted to speak another language but wasn't sure where to start. Each week, we sit down with native speakers, teachers, and curious travelers to explore the everyday phrases, surprising grammar, and cultural habits that make a language come alive. Whether you are a complete beginner learning your first sentence or an intermediate speaker trying to sound more natural, you will find something here to practice and enjoy. Our first season focuses on Spanish, Japanese, and Swahili, with short episodes designed to fit into your commute, your morning coffee, or your evening walk. No pressure, no quizzes, just good conversation and a few new words to carry with you. Subscribe today, and let's start talking.
