A section of Main Street will be closed to traffic from Monday to Friday of next week due to road maintenance. The closure will be in effect from 9am to 3pm each day and will affect the area between Oak Avenue and Maple Street. Motorists are advised to use alternative routes, such as Elm Street or Pine Road, to avoid delays. Pedestrian access will be maintained, but pedestrians are cautioned to follow designated detours and instructions from traffic control personnel. The road is expected to be fully reopened by the end of the week, weather permitting. For more information, please contact the municipal public works department.
