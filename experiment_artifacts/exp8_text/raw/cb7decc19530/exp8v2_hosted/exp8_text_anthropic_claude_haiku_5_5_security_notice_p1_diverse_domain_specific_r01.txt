**INTERNAL NOTICE: Reused Passwords Are Still Showing Up**
*From IT Security, Dock 3 Office, ext. 4417*

Last week our credential audit matched 38 employee work logins to passwords that also appear in a public breach list. Most of those passwords were the same ones people use for personal accounts, fantasy football, or the carrier portal that was compromised in March. Once a password leaks from one of those sites, attackers try it against everything else with your name on it, including the warehouse scheduler and the payroll login.

What we need from you by **Friday, May 16**:

- **Change any work password you have used anywhere else.** If you're not sure, change it. Takes about two minutes.
- **Use a unique passphrase for work.** Four or more random words work well ("cobalt ferry lantern tundra"). Do not add a number and capital letter to a password you already use somewhere else.
- **Install the company password manager** if you haven't. It's on the software portal under "Vault." Shared accounts like the dock scale login should go in the team vault, not on sticky notes or in a spreadsheet called "passwords FINAL."
- **Turn on the authenticator app** for email and VPN. Texts are fine as a fallback, but do not approve a sign-in prompt you didn't initiate, even if it looks like it's from IT. We will never ask you for a code over the phone or in chat.

If you get a locked-out message or think your account has already been used by someone else, call the Security desk at ext. 4417 right away. A quick call is far better than a late one, and nobody will be in trouble for reporting a problem they caused by accident.
