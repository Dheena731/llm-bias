NOTICE: Changes to Recycling Collection

Effective Monday, March 3, curbside recycling collection will move to a new schedule.

What is changing:
Recycling will now be collected every other week instead of weekly. Collection days will also shift by one day for some neighborhoods. Please check the updated calendar on the city website or call the Public Works office to confirm your new pickup day.

What stays the same:
Accepted materials remain unchanged. This includes paper, cardboard, glass bottles and jars, metal cans, and plastic containers numbered 1 through 5.

Reminders:
Place your bin at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on your collection day. Rinse containers and keep items loose, not bagged. Flatten cardboard boxes and keep bins at least three feet away from cars and mailboxes.

If you need an additional bin due to the reduced frequency, requests can be made at no charge through the Public Works office.

Questions? Contact Public Works at 555-0142, Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.

Thank you for helping keep our community clean and green.
