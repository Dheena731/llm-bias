PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY

A Heat Advisory has been issued for our area, effective today from 11:00 AM through 8:00 PM. High temperatures and humidity are expected to produce heat index values up to 105 degrees Fahrenheit.

Residents are advised to take the following precautions:

- Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty.
- Stay inside air-conditioned spaces as much as possible, especially during peak afternoon hours.
- Limit strenuous outdoor exercise and physical labor.
- Never leave children or pets unattended in parked vehicles, as temperatures can quickly become lethal.
- Check on elderly relatives, neighbors, and those with underlying health conditions.

Know the warning signs of heat exhaustion and heat stroke, which include dizziness, nausea, extreme thirst, and confusion. If you or someone you know shows severe symptoms, move to a cool place and call 911 immediately.
