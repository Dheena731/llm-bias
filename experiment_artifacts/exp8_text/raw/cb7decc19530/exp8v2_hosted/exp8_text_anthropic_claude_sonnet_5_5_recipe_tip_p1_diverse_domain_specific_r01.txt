**Salt your pasta water, then steal a cup of it.**

On a weeknight, the fastest dinner is often a pot of pasta and whatever's in the fridge. The trick that makes it taste like you tried harder is to ladle out a mug of the starchy cooking water just before you drain.

Say you've got half a jar of olives, a few anchovies, and some garlic softening in olive oil. Drain the pasta (keep it a little underdone, about two minutes shy of the package time), then tip it straight into the skillet. Add a splash of the cloudy water and toss over medium heat for a minute or so. The starch binds with the oil and turns what would be a slick, separated mess into a glossy sauce that clings to every piece. If it tightens up, add another splash.

Two details matter here. The water should taste like mild broth, so use about a tablespoon of kosher salt for four quarts. And don't rinse the pasta, since that washes away the starch you're counting on.

Total time: about 20 minutes, with one pot and one pan.
