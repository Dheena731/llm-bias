Thank you for submitting your methods section. You have clearly described your participant recruitment and the survey instrument you used, and the step-by-step procedure is easy to follow. Your ethical considerations are also well handled, with the consent process and data anonymisation explained in plain terms.

There are a few areas to strengthen before your next draft. First, the sampling strategy needs more justification. You state that you recruited 60 participants from one university, but you do not explain why this approach suits your research question or how it might limit generalisability. A sentence or two acknowledging this would help.

Second, the description of your data analysis is brief. Please name the specific statistical tests you used, state the significance threshold, and explain why those tests were appropriate for your data type. Readers should be able to replicate your analysis from this section alone.

Third, check that your past and present tenses are consistent. Methods sections usually describe what you did, so past tense is generally preferred throughout.

Finally, consider adding a short note on how you dealt with missing responses, since you mention that eight questionnaires were incomplete but do not say how they were handled.

Overall, this is a solid foundation. Once these points are addressed, the section will be much clearer and more rigorous. I am happy to look at a revised version if you would like further feedback.
