**NOTICE: Changes to curbside recycling, effective Monday, March 3**

**Pickup is moving to every other week.** Trash collection stays weekly. Your recycling week is either A or B. Check the sticker under your cart lid or look up your address at the Public Works website. Week A pickups start March 3, and Week B starts March 10.

**Glass is no longer accepted in the curbside cart.** Broken glass has been embedding in paper bales at the sorting facility, and loads are being downgraded or rejected. Bottles and jars can go to the drop-off bins at the Alder Road Transfer Station (open Wed-Sat, 8 a.m.-4 p.m.) or behind the Fire Station on Pell Street (open 24 hours).

**Cartons are now accepted.** Milk, broth, and juice cartons can go in empty and with caps on. No need to flatten them.

**Unchanged:** Cans, plastic bottles, tubs, jugs, paper, and cardboard are still accepted. Cardboard must be broken down to fit inside the cart or stacked flat beside it, no larger than 3 ft. per side.

**Please note:**
- Put items in the cart loose. Bagged recycling is treated as trash and left behind.
- The lid must close fully. Carts with open lids may be skipped.
- Carts should be at the curb by 6:30 a.m. The new schedule means a missed pickup is a two-week wait.

Missed collection or damaged cart? Call Public Works at 555-0142 within 24 hours of your scheduled day.
