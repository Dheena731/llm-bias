Notice to Voters: Polling Station Locations

Please be advised that polling station locations have been updated for the upcoming election. Voters are encouraged to confirm their assigned polling station before election day.

To find your polling station, visit the official elections website or contact your local elections office. You may also check your voter registration card, which lists your assigned location.

Polling stations will be open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on election day. Voters who arrive at their polling station before closing time will be permitted to cast a ballot, even if they are waiting in line when the polls close.

If you require accessible facilities or assistance, please contact the elections office in advance so that arrangements can be made.

For questions or further information, please contact your local elections office during regular business hours.
