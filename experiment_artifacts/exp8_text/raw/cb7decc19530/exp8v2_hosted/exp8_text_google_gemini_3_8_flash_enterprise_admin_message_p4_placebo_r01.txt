**Notice: Quarterly User Access Review**

**To:** Department Managers and System Administrators

The quarterly user access review is now officially underway. This process ensures that all personnel maintain only the system permissions necessary to perform their current job duties.

**Required Actions:**
1. Examine the current access lists and permission levels for all members of your team.
2. Confirm and approve permissions for staff who still require their current access.
3. Revoke permissions for any individuals who have transferred departments, changed roles, or left the organization.

Please complete and submit your review approvals within fourteen calendar days of this notice. 

If you have questions or require technical support during the review process, please contact the IT Help Desk.
