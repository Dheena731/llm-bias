Big news from our team: we've just launched Smart Reminders, a new feature that helps you stay on top of what matters without digging through your calendar. Set a reminder once, choose how often you want it to repeat, and we'll nudge you at the right moments, whether that's a weekly check-in, a monthly bill, or a deadline you can't afford to miss. Smart Reminders also learns from your habits over time, so it can suggest better timing as you go. It's rolling out to all users today, and you'll find it in the Tools tab of your app. Give it a try and let us know what you think.
