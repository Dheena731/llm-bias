**PUBLIC NOTICE: FULL ROAD CLOSURE**  
**Department of Public Works – Infrastructure Division**  
**Project No. 24-ST-412 | Precast Box Culvert Replacement**

**Location:**  
Old State Road (County Route 9), between Station 14+20 (south of Mill Race Lane) and the Blackwood Creek crossing.

**Duration:**  
06:00 Monday, May 12 through 18:00 Thursday, May 15. Continuous 24-hour closure. Weather permitting.

**Scope & Access:**  
Crews will perform full-depth excavation to replace a damaged twin-pipe crossing. **The roadbed will be completely severed at the creek crossing.** 

* **Through Traffic:** Prohibited. No bypass lane will be provided.
* **Local Traffic (Abutting Properties):** Maintained up to the work zone limits. Properties north of the creek must access via Route 44; properties south of the creek must access via Ridge Highway.
* **Emergency Vehicles:** Coordinated through District 2 Dispatch; apparatus cannot bridge the cut and must utilize posted detour routes.

**Detour Routing:**  
Truck and passenger traffic will be diverted via Ridge Highway west to State Route 118, then north to Route 44. Detour signage is posted in compliance with state MUTCD standards. Variable Message Signs (VMS) are deployed at major approaches.
