We're pleased to announce the release of the Clear Month Budget Planner, a spreadsheet template designed to make personal budgeting simple and sustainable. It works with Excel, Google Sheets, and other common spreadsheet programs, so you can start using it right away without installing anything new.

The template brings your income, fixed bills, variable spending, savings goals, and debt payments together in one place. You enter your expected amounts at the start of the month, then record what you actually spend as the weeks go by. Formulas automatically compare the two and show you where you are ahead or behind, so there is no need to do the math yourself.

We built Clear Month for people who have tried budgeting before and found it too complicated to keep up. Categories are already set up but easy to rename, add, or remove. A summary page gives you a one-glance view of your month, including how much money remains unassigned and how close you are to each savings target. A yearly overview tab tracks your progress across all twelve months, which makes it easier to spot patterns and plan for irregular expenses like insurance premiums or holiday gifts.

Setup takes about ten minutes, and a short written guide inside the file walks you through each step. After that, most people spend only a few minutes a week keeping it current.
