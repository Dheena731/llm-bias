We are launching a new monthly spending summary to help you track and understand your recent expenses more efficiently. Each summary consolidates all recorded transactions into a single overview that categorizes spending, shows month-over-month comparisons, and flags any amounts that go beyond set thresholds. You will receive the updated report automatically, and it will also be available in your account dashboard under the “Reports” section by the fifth business day of each month. Please review your summary each cycle to confirm accuracy and note any irregularities. If you need assistance interpreting the breakdown or updating your notification settings, reach out to our support team. This tool is designed to simplify expense monitoring and support clearer budget planning.
