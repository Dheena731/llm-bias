**PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE**

**Road:** Maple Street, between 4th Avenue and 9th Avenue

**Dates:** Monday, June 10, through Friday, June 14

**Hours:** 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily

**Reason:** Water main repair and road resurfacing

**What to expect:**
- Maple Street will be closed to all through traffic during the hours listed above.
- Local residents will have access to their driveways, but may experience short delays.
- Posted detour signs will direct drivers to Oak Street and 10th Avenue.
- On-street parking will not be permitted along the affected section. Vehicles left in the work zone may be towed.
- Emergency vehicles will have access at all times.

Pedestrians and cyclists should use caution near the work area and follow posted signs.

Work is weather dependent. If rain delays the project, the closure may extend through Saturday, June 15. Updates will be posted on the City website.

**Questions?** Contact the Department of Public Works at (555) 012-3456 or publicworks@examplecity.gov.

We appreciate your patience while we complete this important work.
