Calendar tip I wish I'd figured out sooner: stop putting tasks on your calendar as all-day events.

They pile up at the top of the day like a guilt shelf, and by Wednesday you're ignoring them. Instead, give each task a real 30-minute block, even if it's a rough guess. "Reply to landlord about lease" at 2:30 on Thursday gets done. "Landlord" floating above Thursday does not.

Bonus: when a block gets bumped by a meeting, you can see right away that you don't have room for it, so you move it on purpose rather than discovering on Friday that it never happened.

Also, color-code by *who it's for*, not by project. One glance tells you if the week is all other people's stuff and nothing is yours.
