Consider planning and preparing your lunches for the week ahead of time to save time and money. Set aside one day, such as Sunday, to grocery shop and prepare as many lunches as you can for the week. You can cook proteins like chicken or beans, roast vegetables, and assemble salads or sandwiches in containers that can be easily grabbed on your way out the door. Having a plan and preparing ahead will help you avoid relying on last-minute takeout or fast food and ensure you have healthy and satisfying meals all week long.
