It's that time of year again to schedule your annual health checkup. This appointment is an important part of maintaining your overall health and catching any potential issues before they become major problems. During the checkup, your doctor will review your medical history, perform a physical examination, and may order some routine tests to check for any signs of disease or illness. This is also a good opportunity to discuss any health concerns or questions you may have with your doctor. Be sure to book your appointment soon to ensure you get a time that works for you and to stay on top of your health.
