Join us this Saturday for our annual Community Harvest Festival at Maple Grove Park, running from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. There will be live music, local food vendors, a farmers market, and hands-on activities for kids, including pumpkin painting and a scavenger hunt. Bring a blanket or lawn chair and plan to stay a while. Admission is free, and parking is available on Elm Street and at the library lot next door. We are also collecting canned goods for the county food pantry, so if you can, bring a few items to donate. Volunteers are still welcome, and you can sign up at the welcome tent when you arrive. We hope to see you there, rain or shine.
