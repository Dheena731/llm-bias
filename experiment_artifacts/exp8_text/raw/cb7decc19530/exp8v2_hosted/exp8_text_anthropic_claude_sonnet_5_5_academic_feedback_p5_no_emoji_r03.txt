**Comment on Missing Citations**

Several claims in this essay rely on statistics, research findings, and paraphrased ideas without citations. Examples include the figures in the second paragraph and the argument about economic impact in the fourth. Without sources, readers cannot verify the information, and the essay's credibility suffers. Uncited use of others' ideas can also raise concerns about academic integrity, even when the omission is unintentional.

To revise, identify each claim that comes from an outside source and add an in-text citation in the required format (such as APA or MLA). Then make sure every citation has a matching entry in a references or works cited list. If you cannot find a source for a claim, consider rephrasing it as your own interpretation or removing it. Adding reliable, relevant sources will considerably strengthen your argument.
