Here's a quick cooking tip for preparing a weeknight dinner:

**Prep Your Veggies in Advance**

One of the most time-consuming parts of cooking a weeknight dinner is often chopping and preparing vegetables. To save time, try chopping all your vegetables at once on the weekend and storing them in airtight containers in the fridge for up to 3 days. This way, you can quickly assemble your meals throughout the week, without having to spend precious time on prep work. Simply pull out what you need, add it to your recipe, and cook away!
