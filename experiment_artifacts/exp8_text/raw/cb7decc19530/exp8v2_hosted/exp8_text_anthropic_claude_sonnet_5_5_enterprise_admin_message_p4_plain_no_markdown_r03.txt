Subject: Quarterly Access Reviews Begin Next Week

Quarterly access reviews will begin on Monday and must be completed by the end of the month. These reviews help us confirm that every person has the right level of access to our systems, applications, and shared data, and that access no longer needed has been removed.

Department managers will receive a message from the IT Security team with a list of their team members and the permissions each person currently holds. Please review each entry carefully and confirm whether the access is still appropriate. If a person has changed roles, left a project, or no longer needs a particular permission, mark it for removal. If you are unsure about any item, contact the system owner before making a decision.

Reviews that are not returned by the deadline will be escalated to department heads, and any access that remains unconfirmed may be suspended until the review is complete. We want to avoid disruption, so please set aside time early in the month rather than waiting until the final days.

Employees do not need to take any action unless contacted by their manager. If you notice access you believe you should not have, please report it to the IT Help Desk right away.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our systems and information secure. Questions about the process can be sent to the IT Security team at any time.
