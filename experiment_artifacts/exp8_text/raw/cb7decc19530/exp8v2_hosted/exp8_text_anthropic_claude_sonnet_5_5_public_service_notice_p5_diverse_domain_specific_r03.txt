**NOTICE OF POLLING PLACE CHANGES**
Harlan County Board of Elections
For the November 4 General Election

**Four polling places have moved since the May primary.** If you voted at any of these locations before, check the list below before you go.

| Precinct | Previous location | Location for Nov. 4 |
|---|---|---|
| 07 – Millrace | Millrace Grange Hall | Harlan Public Library, community room (door on Pearl St.) |
| 12 – East Fork | East Fork Elementary gym | East Fork Fire Station No. 3, 410 Dunmore Rd. |
| 19 – Taylor Heights | Taylor Heights Senior Center | Taylor Heights Baptist Church, fellowship hall |
| 23 – Quarry Rd. | Quarry Rd. Volunteer Fire Hall | Same building, **side entrance** only (front lot closed for paving) |

**Why the changes:** The Grange Hall has no step-free entrance. East Fork Elementary is holding a district in-service day. The senior center is closed for roof repair. Precinct 23 stays put, but its parking has changed.

**Hours:** 6:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. Anyone in line at 7:30 p.m. will be allowed to vote.

**Find your precinct:** It is printed on your voter registration card and on the sample ballot mailed in mid-October. You can also call the office at 555-0142 or look up your address at harlancountyelections.example/precinct. If you are unsure, call before Election Day. Poll workers cannot always tell you which building is yours.

**If you go to the wrong place:** Poll workers there will direct you to the correct location. A ballot cast in the wrong precinct may not count for all races, so it is worth the extra drive. Provisional ballots are available if your name is not on the roll.

**Accessibility:** All four locations listed above have step-free entrances and accessible voting equipment. Curbside voting is available at every polling place. Ask the first person you see, or call the number posted at the door.

**Bring:** A photo ID is requested but not required. If you don't have one, you may sign an affidavit and vote a regular ballot.

Questions: Harlan County Board of Elections, 22 Court St., Room 104. Open weekdays 8:30–4:30, and until 7:30 p.m. on Election Day.
