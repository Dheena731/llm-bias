IMPORTANT SECURITY NOTICE: PHISHING EMAILS

To all staff,

Please be aware of an increase in phishing emails targeting our company. These fraudulent messages may appear to come from colleagues, vendors, or official services and often ask for login credentials, sensitive data, or immediate payment.

Guidelines:

Do not click links or open attachments from unsolicited emails. Always check the sender's address for slight misspellings or unusual domains. Verify urgent requests through a separate communication channel such as a phone call or instant message. Report suspicious messages immediately to the IT Help Desk.

If you receive an email that seems suspicious, do not reply. Forward it to the security team or contact IT directly.

Thank you for your vigilance in protecting our organization's data.

Information Technology Department
