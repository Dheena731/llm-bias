PUBLIC NOTICE: ROAD CLOSURE

Effective Monday, November 4 through Friday, November 8, from 7:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. daily, the stretch of Oak Ridge Road between Birch Lane and Willow Court will be closed to all motor vehicles. Crews require full lane access to repair damaged underground water pipes. Drivers must follow posted signs to use Maple Avenue and River Trail as an alternate route. Sidewalks remain open for pedestrians, and bicycle lanes are temporarily shifted to the shoulder of Maple Avenue. Please allow extra travel time during these hours. For daily status updates, check the municipal transportation website or call Public Works at 555-0142. We appreciate your cooperation while we complete essential utility repairs.
