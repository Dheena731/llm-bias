**NOTICE: Lock Your Screens**

Staff are reminded to lock their computer screens whenever they leave their workstations, even for a short time.

Unattended, unlocked devices can expose confidential company, client, and personal information to unauthorized individuals. To protect our data and systems:

- **Lock your screen** before stepping away (Windows: Win + L; Mac: Control + Command + Q).
- **Set up automatic locking** after a short period of inactivity if you haven't already.
- **Never share your password** or leave it written near your workstation.

Thank you for helping keep our information secure.

*IT Department*
