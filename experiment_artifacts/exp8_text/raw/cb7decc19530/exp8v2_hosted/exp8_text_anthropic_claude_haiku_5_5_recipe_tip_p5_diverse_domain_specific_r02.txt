Salt your vegetables before they go in the oven, not after. Toss them in a bowl with oil and about three-quarters of a teaspoon of kosher salt per pound, then spread them out on the pan. The salt draws out surface moisture and seasons the flesh as it cooks, so the edges brown instead of steaming. Add dried spices like cumin or smoked paprika at the same time, but hold back fresh herbs, garlic slivers, and any sugar-based glaze until the last few minutes, since they scorch quickly at high heat.
