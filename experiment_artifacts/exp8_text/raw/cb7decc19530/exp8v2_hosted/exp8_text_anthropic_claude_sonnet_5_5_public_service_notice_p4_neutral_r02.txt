**A Heat Advisory is in effect.** Temperatures are expected to reach **[95–105°F]**, with heat index values up to **[110°F]**. Prolonged exposure may cause heat-related illness.

## Stay Safe

- **Drink plenty of water**, even if you don't feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and sugary drinks.
- **Stay indoors** in air conditioning when possible. Limit outdoor activity to early morning or evening.
- **Dress lightly** in loose, light-colored clothing, and wear sunscreen and a hat outdoors.
- **Never leave children, pets, or vulnerable adults in parked vehicles**, not even briefly.
- **Check on** elderly neighbors, relatives, and anyone without air conditioning.

## Know the Warning Signs

**Heat exhaustion:** heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, nausea, headache. Move to a cool place, drink water, and apply cool cloths.

**Heat stroke (EMERGENCY):** confusion, hot/dry skin, high body temperature, loss of consciousness. **Call 911 immediately.**
