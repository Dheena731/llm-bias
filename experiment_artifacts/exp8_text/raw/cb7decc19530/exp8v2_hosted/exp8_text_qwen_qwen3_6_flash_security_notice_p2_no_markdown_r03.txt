WORKPLACE NOTICE
IMPORTANT: PHISHING EMAIL AWARENESS

All Staff,

We are writing to remind everyone to remain cautious regarding suspicious emails. Recent activity indicates an increase in phishing attempts aimed at stealing login credentials and sensitive information.

Please follow these guidelines:

1. Verify the sender's email address before opening any message.
2. Do not click on links or download attachments from unknown or unexpected sources.
3. Look for poor grammar, urgent requests for money, or generic greetings as warning signs.
4. If you receive a suspicious email, do not reply. Instead, forward it immediately to it-security@company.com or contact the IT Help Desk.

Your vigilance helps protect our company and your personal data. Thank you for your cooperation.

Best regards,

IT Security Team
