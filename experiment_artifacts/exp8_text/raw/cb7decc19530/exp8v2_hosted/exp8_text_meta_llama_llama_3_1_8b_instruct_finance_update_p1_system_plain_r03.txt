We are introducing a new monthly spending summary that will be available to view online. This summary will provide a detailed breakdown of your spending for the past month, including all transactions made on your account. The summary will be updated on the first day of each month and will include information such as total spending, largest transaction, and top categories spent in. This information will be available to view online and can be accessed by logging into your account and navigating to the designated section. If you have any questions or need assistance accessing the summary, please contact our customer service department.
