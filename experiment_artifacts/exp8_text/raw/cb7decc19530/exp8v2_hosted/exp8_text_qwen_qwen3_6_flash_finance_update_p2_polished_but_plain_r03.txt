This note serves to inform you of an upcoming adjustment to our card transaction fees. Beginning on November first, all domestic credit and debit card purchases will carry a standard processing rate of two percent. This update reflects increased network assessment costs and enhanced security requirements across the payment industry. The revised rate applies to all transactions processed through our platform and replaces the previous flat fee structure. Existing agreements will continue under their original terms until December thirty-first, after which the new percentage will automatically apply to future activity. We have structured this change to remain competitive while ensuring reliable service and consistent fraud protection. If you require assistance understanding how this adjustment affects your specific account, please contact our support team directly using the telephone number provided on your billing statement. Thank you for your continued partnership.
