**Introducing the Monthly Budget Spreadsheet Template**

Keeping track of where your money goes doesn't have to be complicated. Our new budgeting spreadsheet template gives you a simple, clear way to plan your income and expenses and see how each month is going.

Enter your income, list your bills and spending by category, and the template keeps running totals so you can see at a glance what's left over. Use it to plan ahead for the month, or review where your money went after it's already spent.

It's designed for everyday people who want a practical budget without learning complex software. No experience with spreadsheets is required.
