**Why Latency Matters in Web Applications**

Latency, the delay between a user's action and the application's response, has a significant impact on the user experience in web applications. High latency can lead to frustration, decreased engagement, and ultimately, a loss of users. Here are key reasons why latency matters:

* **User Experience**: Fast response times create a seamless and interactive experience, while slow responses can make an application feel unresponsive and outdated.
* **Conversion Rates**: Studies have shown that even small delays can result in significant decreases in conversion rates, with users more likely to abandon a slow-loading page.
* **Search Engine Optimization (SEO)**: Search engines like Google consider page load times when ranking websites, with faster sites receiving preference in search results.
* **Competitive Advantage**: In today's fast-paced digital landscape, a responsive web application can be a key differentiator, setting a business apart from its competitors.

By prioritizing low latency, developers can create web applications that are not only more enjoyable to use but also more effective at driving business results.
