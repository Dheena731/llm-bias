We are excited to introduce our new savings goal feature, designed to help you reach your financial milestones faster and with less effort. Starting today, you can create personalized targets for anything you are working toward, whether that is building an emergency fund, planning a vacation, or putting together a down payment for a home. The tool allows you to name your goal, set a target amount, and choose a deadline that fits your budget. You can easily track your progress over time and even set up automated recurring transfers to keep your savings on schedule without any extra work. You can start using this feature immediately by logging into your account and selecting the goals tab on your dashboard.
