📅 Calendar tip: Stop using your calendar only for meetings. Block out time for your own tasks, too!

Try scheduling 30-minute "focus blocks" for things like answering emails, working on a project, or even taking a lunch break. When it's on your calendar, it's far more likely to actually happen, and others can see when you're busy instead of booking over your time.

Bonus: color-code your blocks (work, personal, health) so you can see at a glance if your week is balanced.
