Here is a concise, professional message template you can adapt. Be sure to fill in the bracketed information before sending.

**Subject:** Your End-of-Year Tax Statement for [Year] is Ready

**Message:**

Hi [Name/Team],

Your end-of-year tax statement is now available. This document summarizes your [earnings/account activity] and any [taxes withheld/interest earned] for the year.

Please review the details for accuracy and save a copy for your tax filing records. If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to reach out.

Best regards,

[Your Name]
[Your Title/Company]
[Contact Information]

***

**💡 Tips for sending:**
*   **Attachments:** If the statement is a PDF, make sure to attach it. If it's hosted on a portal, replace "available" with "available via secure link here."
*   **Specifics:** Depending on your region, you may want to specify the form name (e.g., W-2, 1099, T4) rather than just "tax statement."
*   **Disclaimer:** If applicable, consider adding a line such as, *"For specific advice regarding your taxes, please consult a qualified tax professional."*
