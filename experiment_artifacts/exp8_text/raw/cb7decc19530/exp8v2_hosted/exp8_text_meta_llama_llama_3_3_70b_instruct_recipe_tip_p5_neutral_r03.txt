To elevate your roasted vegetables, try seasoning them in layers! First, toss the vegetables with olive oil, salt, and pepper before roasting. Then, halfway through the roasting time, sprinkle with any additional seasonings you like, such as dried herbs, garlic powder, or paprika. Finally, finish with a squeeze of fresh lemon juice and a sprinkle of chopped fresh herbs, like parsley or thyme, just before serving. This layered seasoning approach will add depth and brightness to your roasted vegetables, bringing out their natural flavors.
