Here is a short update explaining a new monthly spending summary:

We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you stay on top of your finances. The summary will include a breakdown of all your expenses, income, and savings for the past month. It will be delivered to your email inbox on the 5th of each month, covering the previous month's transactions.

The summary will include:

* A list of all transactions, categorized by type (e.g. housing, transportation, food, entertainment, etc.)
* Total income and expenses for the month
* Savings and debt repayment progress
* A comparison to the previous month's spending habits

This summary will provide you with a clear and concise picture of your financial situation, allowing you to make informed decisions about your money. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
