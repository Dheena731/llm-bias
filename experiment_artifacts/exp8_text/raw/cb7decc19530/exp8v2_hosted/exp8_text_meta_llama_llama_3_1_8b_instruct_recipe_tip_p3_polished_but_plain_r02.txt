To prevent rice from becoming sticky, cook it with a generous amount of water and then drain off any excess liquid. The key to non-sticky rice is to cook it in a way that allows excess starch to be washed away. Use about 1 1/2 cups of water for every 1 cup of rice, and bring the mixture to a boil before reducing the heat to low and covering the pot. The excess water will be absorbed by the rice as it cooks, helping to prevent the formation of sticky clumps.
