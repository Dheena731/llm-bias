HEAT ADVISORY NOTICE

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for the city and surrounding areas from 11:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. today. Temperatures are expected to reach the mid-90s, with heat index values near 105 degrees. Conditions like these can cause heat exhaustion and heat stroke, especially for older adults, young children, people who work or exercise outdoors, and anyone with a chronic medical condition.

Residents are urged to limit time outdoors during the hottest part of the afternoon. If you must be outside, wear lightweight, light-colored clothing, take frequent breaks in shade, and drink water regularly even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and heavy meals, which can worsen dehydration.

Never leave children, pets, or anyone who depends on you inside a parked vehicle, as temperatures inside a car can rise to dangerous levels within minutes. Check on elderly neighbors, relatives, and friends who may not have air conditioning or who may need help staying cool.

Cooling centers will be open at the public library on Main Street and the community center on Oak Avenue during regular business hours. Anyone without reliable cooling at home is encouraged to visit one of these locations.

If you or someone near you experiences dizziness, confusion, nausea, fainting, or a body temperature above 103 degrees, call 911 immediately. For updates on this advisory, monitor local news and the National Weather Service website.
