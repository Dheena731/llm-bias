**Subject: Update to Card Transaction Fees**

Dear Customer,

We are writing to let you know about an upcoming change to the fees that apply to card transactions on your account.

**What is changing**
Starting on the first day of next month, a fee of 1.5% will apply to card purchases made in a foreign currency. Domestic card purchases and ATM withdrawals within your home country will remain free of charge.

**Why we are making this change**
The fees that card networks and partner banks charge us for processing international payments have increased. Rather than raise costs for all customers, we have chosen to apply this adjustment only to the transactions that incur these higher costs.

**What this means for you**
If you rarely use your card abroad or for overseas online purchases, you are unlikely to notice any difference. If you do make these purchases often, you may want to review your spending habits or consider a multi-currency account, which does not carry this fee.

**Next steps**
You do not need to take any action. The updated fee schedule is available in your online account under "Fees and Charges," and our support team is happy to answer any questions.

Thank you for banking with us.

Sincerely,
Customer Support Team
