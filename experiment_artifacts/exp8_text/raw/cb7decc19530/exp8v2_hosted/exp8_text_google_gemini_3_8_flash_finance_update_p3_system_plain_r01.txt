We are excited to introduce our new savings goal feature, designed to help you reach your financial milestones with greater ease. Starting today, you can create custom targets for anything you are working toward, whether it is an emergency fund, a vacation, or a major purchase. Simply name your goal, set the amount you want to reach, and choose a timeline that works for your budget. You can track your progress as you save, set up automatic recurring transfers, and receive helpful updates as you get closer to the finish line. This feature gives you better control over your money and makes planning for the future straightforward and achievable. The new tool is available now in the latest version of the mobile app and through your online account.
