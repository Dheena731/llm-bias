All employees must immediately report any lost or stolen company laptop to the IT Service Desk and the Corporate Security team. Prompt reporting is essential so that technical staff can disable access, protect internal networks, and initiate remote data wiping procedures. If you cannot locate your assigned device, please call the IT support line immediately and inform your direct supervisor. In the event of theft outside the office, you are also required to file a report with local law enforcement and provide the incident number to the security office. Timely communication helps safeguard company data and minimize security risks.
