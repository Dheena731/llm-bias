Please be advised that the residential recycling collection schedule will change starting on the first of next month. Recycling will now be collected every Wednesday instead of on Fridays. Collection times may vary as drivers adjust to the new routes, so please ensure your recycling bin is placed at the curb by seven in the morning on your pickup day. The types of materials accepted for recycling will remain the same, and regular trash collection days will not be affected. If you have any questions or experience a missed pickup, please contact the municipal services department during regular business hours. Thank you for your patience and cooperation as we work to improve our community services.
