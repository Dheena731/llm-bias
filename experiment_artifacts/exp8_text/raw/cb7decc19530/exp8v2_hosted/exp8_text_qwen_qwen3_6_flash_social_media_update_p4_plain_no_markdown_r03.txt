One simple way to make your calendar app work harder for you is to assign a specific color to each type of event. Use one shade for work meetings, another for personal errands, and a third for family time. When you look at your week at a glance, the visual breakdown instantly shows where your time is going and helps you spot empty slots before you book them. Keep the palette consistent so your brain learns to read the schedule without reading every label. Try it out this week and notice how much clearer your planning becomes.
