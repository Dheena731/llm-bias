The public library is open Monday through Thursday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and Friday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The building is closed on Sundays and municipal holidays. Visitors are welcome to browse the shelves, use public computers, and access quiet study areas throughout operating hours. The outdoor return drop box, located near the main entrance, remains accessible twenty-four hours a day for your convenience. Please contact the front desk during open hours for assistance with account renewals or research inquiries.
