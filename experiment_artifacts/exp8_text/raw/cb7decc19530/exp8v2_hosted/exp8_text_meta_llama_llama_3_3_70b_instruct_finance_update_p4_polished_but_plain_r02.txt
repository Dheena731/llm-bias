Your end-of-year tax statement is a document that summarizes your financial activity for the past year, providing a detailed record of your income, deductions, and tax obligations. This statement is typically issued by financial institutions, employers, and other entities that have paid you or withheld taxes on your behalf. The information on the statement will help you complete your tax return and ensure you are taking advantage of all eligible deductions and credits. You should review the statement carefully to verify its accuracy and contact the issuer if you have any questions or concerns. The statement will also show any taxes that have been withheld, as well as any additional taxes that may be due or refunds that you are eligible to receive.
