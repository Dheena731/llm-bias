When prepping a weeknight dinner, consider the "15-minute mis en place" rule: before starting to cook, take 15 minutes to meticulously chop, slice, and measure out all the ingredients for your recipe. This upfront investment of time will pay dividends in the long run, allowing you to focus on the cooking process and ensuring that each component of the dish is added at the optimal moment. For example, if you're making a stir-fry, use this 15 minutes to finely chop your aromatics, slice your protein into uniform strips, and measure out your sauces and seasonings. By doing so, you'll be able to cook with a sense of flow and confidence, and your finished dish will be all the better for it.
