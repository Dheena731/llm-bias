The library is open to the public Monday through Thursday from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM, Friday and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Sunday from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM. The library is closed on Sundays during the summer months. Please note that these hours are subject to change and may be adjusted during holidays and special events. It is always a good idea to check the library's website or call the information desk to confirm the hours before planning a visit.
