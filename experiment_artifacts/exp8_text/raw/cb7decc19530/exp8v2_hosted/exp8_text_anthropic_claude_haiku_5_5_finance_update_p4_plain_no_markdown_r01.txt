Your end-of-year tax statement summarizes the income you received and the taxes withheld or paid over the past calendar year. It is meant to help you prepare your tax return and confirm that your records match what was reported to the tax authorities.

The statement typically lists your total income from wages, interest, dividends, or other sources, along with the amounts that were withheld from your pay or payments for taxes. It may also show deductions, credits, or contributions that affect the final amount you owe or the refund you may receive. Please compare each figure against your own records, such as pay stubs and bank statements. If you notice a discrepancy, contact us as soon as possible so we can review it and, if needed, request a corrected form.

Keep this statement in a safe place along with your other tax documents for at least three years, as you may need it if your return is ever reviewed. If you have questions about any entry or how it applies to your situation, please reach out and we will be glad to help.
