Introducing the Simple Budget Planner, a new spreadsheet template designed to help you take control of your money without the complexity of traditional finance software. Whether you are tracking monthly bills, planning for a large purchase, or simply trying to understand where your paycheck goes each month, this template gives you a clear and organized starting point.

The planner includes separate sections for income, fixed expenses, variable spending, and savings goals. Enter your monthly earnings once, and the template automatically calculates your remaining balance as you log each expense. A built-in summary page shows your spending by category, so you can spot patterns and adjust your habits before small costs add up.

Setup takes about ten minutes. There are no subscriptions, no accounts to create, and no sharing of your financial data with third parties. The template works in any major spreadsheet program, and your information stays on your own device.
