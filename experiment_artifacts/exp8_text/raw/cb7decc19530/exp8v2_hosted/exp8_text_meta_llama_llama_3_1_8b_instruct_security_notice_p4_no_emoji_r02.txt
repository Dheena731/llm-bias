**Enhance Your Security with Two-Factor Authentication**

To add an extra layer of security to your account, we highly recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA). This feature requires both your password and a verification code sent to your phone or email to access your account.

To enable 2FA, follow these simple steps:

1. Log in to your account.
2. Go to your account settings.
3. Look for the 2FA option and click to enable it.
4. Follow the prompts to set up 2FA.

Once enabled, you'll be required to enter a verification code sent to your phone or email in addition to your password every time you log in. This will significantly reduce the risk of unauthorized access to your account.

We encourage all users to enable 2FA to protect their account and data from potential security threats.
