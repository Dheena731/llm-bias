**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY: BARE-ROOT AND DORMANT STOCK**
Harrow Lane Orchard Nursery · Effective for orders shipped 1 January – 30 April

**1. Scope.** This Notice applies to bare-root fruit trees, canes, and crowns ("Stock") sold by the Nursery and shipped while dormant. It does not apply to potted stock, tools, or soil amendments, which are governed by the General Returns Policy.

**2. Inspection on receipt.** The Buyer must unpack and inspect the Stock within 72 hours of delivery. Roots must be kept moist and out of direct sun during this period. Stock left in sealed packaging beyond 72 hours is received at the Buyer's risk.

**3. Eligible claims.** A refund or replacement will be issued, at the Nursery's election, where the Buyer shows by dated photograph that:
 (a) the Stock arrived with roots desiccated, frozen, or visibly mouldy;
 (b) the cultivar or rootstock differs from that stated on the order confirmation; or
 (c) the main stem is broken below the graft union.

Claims under (a) and (b) must be submitted within 5 days of delivery. Claims under (c) must be submitted within 72 hours.

**4. Failure to leaf.** Where Stock was heeled in or planted within 7 days of delivery, in accordance with the enclosed planting sheet, and shows no bud break by 15 June, the Buyer may claim a one-time replacement for the following dormant season. No cash refund is payable under this clause. Failure to leaf following waterlogging, root-zone herbicide exposure, or planting with the graft union below soil level is excluded.

**5. Ineligible claims.** No refund is available for:
 (a) cosmetic variation in branching, caliper, or height within the tolerances stated in the catalogue;
 (b) loss resulting from late-spring frost, deer, voles, or drought after planting;
 (c) Stock the Buyer has pruned back to the trunk before notifying the Nursery of a defect; or
 (d) change of mind, or unsuitability of the cultivar for the Buyer's site or hardiness zone.

**6. Remedy and return.** The Nursery will not require return of Stock that has been shipped bare-root. The Buyer must retain it, labelled and in its packaging, for 14 days pending any request for inspection. Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within 10 business days. Delivery charges are refunded only under clause 3.

**7. Statutory rights.** Nothing in this Notice limits any right of the Buyer under applicable consumer protection law.

**8. Claims.** Submit order number, photographs showing roots and label, and delivery date to claims@harrowlanenursery.example.
