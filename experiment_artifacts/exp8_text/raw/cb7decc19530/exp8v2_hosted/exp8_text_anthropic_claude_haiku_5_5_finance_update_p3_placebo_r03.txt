**Introducing Savings Goals**

We've added Savings Goals, a new way to set aside money for something specific, such as an emergency fund, a trip, or a big purchase.

To start, create a goal, give it a name, and enter the amount you want to reach. The feature shows how close you are to that target, so you can see your progress at a glance.

Savings Goals is meant to make it easier to keep saving without having to track the numbers yourself. If you have questions about how it works, please reach out to our support team.
