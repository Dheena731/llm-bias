To plan lunches for a busy week, start by writing down your schedule for the week, including work hours, appointments, and any other commitments. Then, decide on the types of meals you want to make, such as soups, salads, or leftovers. Consider making a batch of a staple food, such as rice or quinoa, that can be used in different meals throughout the week. Make a list of the ingredients you need for each meal and go shopping on the weekend. On the days you are busy, prepare the ingredients for your meals in advance, such as chopping vegetables or cooking proteins. This will save time during the week when you are too busy to cook.
