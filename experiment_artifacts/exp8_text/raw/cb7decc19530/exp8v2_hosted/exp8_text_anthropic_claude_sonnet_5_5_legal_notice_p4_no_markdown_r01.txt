NOTICE REGARDING REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Effective Date: [Insert Date]

1. Purpose
This Notice sets forth the conditions under which a purchaser ("Customer") may be eligible to receive a refund from [Company Name] ("Company") for products or services purchased directly from the Company.

2. Eligibility Period
A request for refund must be submitted in writing within thirty (30) calendar days of the original date of purchase. Requests received after this period shall not be considered, except where required by applicable law.

3. Conditions for Eligibility
A Customer may be eligible for a refund only if all of the following conditions are satisfied:
(a) The Customer provides valid proof of purchase;
(b) The product is returned in its original condition and packaging, where applicable;
(c) The service has not been substantially performed or consumed; and
(d) The purchase is not within a category designated as non-refundable under Section 4.

4. Non-Refundable Items
The following are not eligible for refund: (a) gift cards and promotional credits; (b) custom or made-to-order items; (c) digital goods that have been downloaded or accessed; and (d) items marked "final sale" at the time of purchase.

5. Method and Timing of Refund
Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within ten (10) business days of approval. The Company is not responsible for delays caused by third-party financial institutions.

6. Deductions
The Company reserves the right to deduct reasonable shipping, handling, or restocking fees from any approved refund, as disclosed at the time of purchase.

7. Determination
All refund determinations are made at the sole reasonable discretion of the Company, in accordance with this Notice and applicable law. Submission of a request does not guarantee approval.

8. Statutory Rights
Nothing in this Notice limits any rights a Customer may have under applicable consumer protection laws.

9. Contact
Refund requests and inquiries should be directed to [Contact Email or Address].

This Notice is subject to modification at the Company's discretion. Changes shall apply only to purchases made after the effective date of the revised Notice.
