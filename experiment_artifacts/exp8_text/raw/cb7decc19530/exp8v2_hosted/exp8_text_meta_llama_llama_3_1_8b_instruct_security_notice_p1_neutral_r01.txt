Here is a short workplace security notice about password reuse:

**Important: Password Security Reminder**

As part of our ongoing efforts to protect company data and systems, we want to remind all employees of the importance of maintaining strong, unique passwords.

**Do not reuse passwords** for multiple accounts, including company systems, email, and online applications. This includes but is not limited to:

* Using the same password for your company email account and personal online accounts
* Using a password that is also used for a family member or roommate's account
* Using a password that is easily guessable (e.g. using your name, birthdate, or common words)

**Why is this important?**

Reusing passwords can compromise the security of our company's systems and data. If a single account is compromised, an attacker may be able to access other accounts using the same password.

**Best practices:**

* Use a unique and complex password for each account
* Change your passwords regularly (at least every 60 days)
* Avoid using easily guessable information
* Consider using a password manager to generate and store unique passwords

If you have any questions or concerns about password security, please reach out to our IT department for assistance.

**Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining the security of our company's systems and data.**
