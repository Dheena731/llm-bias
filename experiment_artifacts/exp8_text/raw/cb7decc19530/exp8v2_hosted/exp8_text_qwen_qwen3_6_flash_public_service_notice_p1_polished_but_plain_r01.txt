The municipal library would like to share its regular opening hours with the community. We are open Monday through Friday from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. and on Saturdays from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. The library remains closed on Sundays and all statutory public holidays. Occasional adjustments to these hours may occur during school vacations or private events, so we encourage patrons to check our posted notices or contact the front desk for confirmation. Our staff is available to assist with inquiries throughout operating times. We look forward to welcoming you to the library.
