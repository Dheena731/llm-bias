Notice to All Users

As a part of our ongoing effort to maintain and improve the services provided to you, quarterly access reviews will be conducted on all user accounts. This review process will ensure that access to systems, networks, and other sensitive areas is up-to-date and reflects the current needs and responsibilities of each user.

During the review, accounts that are no longer required or are in need of adjustments will be updated or deactivated as necessary. Users will be notified if any changes are made to their access levels.

Please note that it is essential to inform the relevant authorities of any changes in your responsibilities or access needs to ensure that your access is adjusted accordingly. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the relevant department.
