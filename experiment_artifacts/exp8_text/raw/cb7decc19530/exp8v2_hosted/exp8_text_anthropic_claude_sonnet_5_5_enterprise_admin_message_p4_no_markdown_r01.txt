Subject: Quarterly Access Review Notice

Dear Team,

This is a reminder that the quarterly access review for all systems and applications will begin on the first business day of next month. The review helps us confirm that user permissions remain appropriate and that our data stays secure.

What to expect:

Department managers will receive a list of their team members and the access each person currently holds. Please review these lists carefully and confirm whether each permission is still needed. Flag any accounts belonging to former employees, contractors whose engagements have ended, or staff who have changed roles.

Key dates:

Review period opens: first business day of next month
Manager responses due: 10 business days after opening
Access changes completed: 5 business days after responses are received

Action required:

Managers should complete their reviews by the deadline. If no response is received, access for unconfirmed accounts may be suspended until a review is completed. Individual staff do not need to take action unless contacted by their manager or the IT team.

If you have questions or need an extension, please contact the IT Security team at itsecurity@yourcompany.com.

Thank you for helping us keep our systems secure.

Regards,
IT Administration
