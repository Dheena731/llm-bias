**TALLGRASS FEDERAL CREDIT UNION**
**Notice of Change in Terms: Share and Checking Accounts**

Notice date: March 3, 2025
Effective date: April 7, 2025

**To:** All members holding a Basic Share, Everyday Checking, or Youth Savings account

This notice amends the Membership and Account Agreement and the Fee Schedule (together, the "Agreement"). Under Regulation DD (12 C.F.R. § 1030.5) and Regulation E (12 C.F.R. § 1005.8), we are giving you at least 30 days' advance notice of changes that may be adverse to you.

**1. Changes that increase cost or reduce access**

a. *Dormant account fee (Fee Schedule, § 4.2).* An account with no member-initiated transaction for 12 consecutive months will be charged $6 per month, but never more than the account balance. Automatic dividends, credits, and our own fees do not count as member-initiated activity. The fee previously applied after 24 months and was $3.

b. *Paper statements (Agreement, § 9).* Members enrolled in e-statements who revoke consent will be charged $2 per statement cycle. Members who have never enrolled are not affected until June 1, 2025.

c. *Outgoing domestic wire (Fee Schedule, § 7.1).* The fee rises from $20 to $25. Incoming wires remain $10.

**2. Changes that benefit you**

a. The returned-item (NSF) fee is eliminated for items presented again after an initial return. Only the first presentment may be charged, at $28 as before.

b. The daily limit on mobile check deposits rises from $2,500 to $5,000. Holds under Regulation CC continue to apply.

**3. What does not change**

Dividend rates, minimum balance requirements, and the overdraft transfer from savings ($5 per transfer) remain as stated in the current Agreement.

**4. Your options**

You may close an affected account at no charge at any time before April 7, 2025, and we will not assess an early-closure fee for closures made for this reason. Please bring a written request to any branch or send it through secure message in online banking. If you take no action, continued use of the account after the effective date means you accept the changes.

**5. Conflicts and governing terms**

If this notice conflicts with the Agreement, this notice controls for the changes described. All other terms remain in force. The Agreement is governed by federal law and the laws of the State of Kansas.
