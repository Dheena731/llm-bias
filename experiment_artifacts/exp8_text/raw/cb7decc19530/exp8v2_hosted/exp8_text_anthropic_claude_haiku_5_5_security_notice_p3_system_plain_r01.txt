Dear colleagues,

This is a friendly reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even for a few minutes. Leaving a session open can expose confidential client information, internal documents, and our systems to anyone who passes by, whether they mean harm or not.

Locking your screen takes only a moment. On Windows, press the Windows key and L together. On a Mac, press Control, Command, and Q together. If you are unsure how to set this up or would like help configuring an automatic lock after a period of inactivity, please contact the IT help desk.

Thank you for helping us keep our information secure.

Kind regards,
The IT Department
