Subject: Upcoming Email Migration This Weekend

Hello everyone,

We will be migrating our company email to a new platform this weekend, beginning Saturday at 8:00 p.m. and finishing by Sunday at 6:00 a.m. During this window, you may notice delays in sending and receiving messages, and you may be briefly unable to log in.

Before the migration, please make sure you have closed Outlook or any other mail application on your computer and phone by Friday evening. You do not need to back up your messages, since all mailboxes, folders, and calendar entries will be moved automatically. Please do, however, delete any old messages you no longer need, as this will help the transfer go faster.

When you return on Monday, you will be asked to sign in again and set up your new password. Your email address will stay the same. Instructions for adding your account back to your phone will be sent separately on Sunday morning.

If anything looks missing or you have trouble signing in, please contact the IT help desk at helpdesk@example.com or call extension 4100. Our team will be available all day Monday to assist.

Thank you for your patience while we make this improvement.
