Public Notice: Heat Advisory

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for the county effective from 11:00 a.m. Thursday through 8:00 p.m. Friday. Afternoon temperatures are expected to reach the low to mid 100s, with heat index values near 105 degrees. Overnight temperatures will remain elevated, offering little relief.

Extended exposure to high heat can cause heat exhaustion and heat stroke, particularly for older adults, young children, people with chronic illnesses, and those who work or exercise outdoors. Residents are urged to limit time outside during the hottest part of the day, drink water regularly even if not thirsty, and wear lightweight, light-colored clothing. Never leave children, pets, or anyone who depends on you inside a parked vehicle.

Check on neighbors and relatives who may not have air conditioning or who live alone. Cooling centers will be open at the public library and the community recreation center during regular business hours. Anyone experiencing dizziness, confusion, nausea, or a body temperature above 103 degrees should seek medical attention immediately or call 911 in an emergency.

For updates, residents should monitor local radio and television broadcasts or contact the county emergency management office during business hours.
