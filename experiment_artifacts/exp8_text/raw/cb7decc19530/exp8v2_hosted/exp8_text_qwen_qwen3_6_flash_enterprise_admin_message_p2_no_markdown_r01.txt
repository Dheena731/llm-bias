Subject: Important Update: New Expense Approval Workflow

Hello Team,

Starting Monday, we will implement a new workflow for submitting and approving expense reports. This update aims to reduce processing times and ensure consistent compliance across the company.

Key changes include:

1. All expense claims must now be submitted through the finance portal. Email submissions will no longer be accepted.
2. Department managers are required to approve requests within three business days.
3. Any single expense over fifty dollars requires an uploaded digital receipt with itemized details.

A step-by-step guide is available on the intranet under Finance Resources. If you encounter issues during your first submission, please contact the Finance Helpdesk.

Thank you for your cooperation as we make these improvements.
