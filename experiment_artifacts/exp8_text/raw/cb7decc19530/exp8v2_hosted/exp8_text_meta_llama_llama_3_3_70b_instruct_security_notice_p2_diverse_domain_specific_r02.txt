These emails may appear to come from a legitimate source, such as a manager or IT department, but often contain subtle red flags, including:

* Urgent or threatening language
* Requests for sensitive information, such as passwords or financial data
* Suspicious links or attachments
* Poor grammar or spelling

* Ask you to verify your account information or login credentials
* Contain links to unfamiliar websites or request that you download attachments from unknown sources
* Create a sense of urgency or panic
