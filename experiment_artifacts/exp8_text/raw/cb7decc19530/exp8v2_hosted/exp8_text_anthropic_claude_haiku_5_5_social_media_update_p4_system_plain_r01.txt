Want to stop missing meetings? Try setting your calendar app to send reminders at two different times for every important event. A first alert a day ahead gives you time to prepare, and a second one 15 minutes before lets you wrap up what you're doing and actually show up. It takes about thirty seconds to set up for recurring events, and it has saved me from more than a few last-minute scrambles. Give it a try this week and see how much smoother your schedule feels.
