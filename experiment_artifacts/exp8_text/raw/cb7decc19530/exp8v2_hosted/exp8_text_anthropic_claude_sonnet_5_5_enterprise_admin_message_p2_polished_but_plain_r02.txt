Subject: Update to Expense Approval Workflow

Hello everyone,

Beginning Monday, we are introducing a new expense approval workflow designed to reduce delays and make reimbursement status easier to track. Please take a few minutes to review the changes below, as they will apply to all expense submissions going forward.

All expenses must now be submitted through the finance portal rather than by email. Each submission should include an itemized receipt, a brief description of the business purpose, and the appropriate project or department code. Requests that are missing any of these details will be returned to you before review, so completing them at the outset will save time.

Approvals will follow a tiered structure based on the amount. Expenses under 250 dollars will go to your direct manager for a single approval. Expenses between 250 and 2,000 dollars will require approval from your manager and then from your department head. Anything above 2,000 dollars will also need sign-off from the finance director. Approvers have been asked to respond within three business days, and you will receive an automatic notification at each stage so you always know where your request stands.

Once a request is fully approved, reimbursement will be issued in the next scheduled payroll cycle. Please submit expenses within 30 days of the purchase date, as older claims may not be eligible without special approval.

If you have pre-approved expenses already in progress, you do not need to resubmit them. Finance will complete those under the previous process. For anything new, however, please use the portal from Monday onward.

A short training guide is available on the company intranet, and the finance team will host two open question sessions next week. If you run into any issues with access or submission, contact the finance help desk and someone will assist you promptly.

Thank you for your cooperation as we make this transition.

Best regards,
Office Administration
