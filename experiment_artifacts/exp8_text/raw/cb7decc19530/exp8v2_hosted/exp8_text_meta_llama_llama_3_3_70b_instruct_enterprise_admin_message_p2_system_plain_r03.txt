We are introducing a new expense approval workflow that will go into effect immediately. This new process is designed to streamline and simplify the way expenses are approved and reimbursed. All employees will now be required to submit their expenses through the new online portal, where they will be reviewed and approved by their respective managers. The manager will then forward the approved expenses to the finance department for reimbursement. This new workflow is intended to reduce paperwork and increase efficiency, and we appreciate your cooperation in implementing this new process. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the finance department for assistance.
