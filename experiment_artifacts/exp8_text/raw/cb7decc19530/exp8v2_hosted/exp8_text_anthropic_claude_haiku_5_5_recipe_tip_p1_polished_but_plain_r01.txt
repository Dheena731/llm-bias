A simple way to make weeknight dinners easier is to prep your vegetables and aromatics while you preheat the oven or heat the pan, rather than after. Chop the onions, mince the garlic, and cut the peppers before you start cooking, and set them in small bowls near the stove. Once the heat is going, the actual cooking moves quickly, and you avoid the common scramble of searching for ingredients while something burns. Even ten minutes of this kind of setup can turn a stressful meal into a calm, controlled one.
