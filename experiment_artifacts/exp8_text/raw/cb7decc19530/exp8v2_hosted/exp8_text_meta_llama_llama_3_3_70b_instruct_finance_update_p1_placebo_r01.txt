We are introducing a new feature to help you better manage your finances: a monthly spending summary. This summary will provide a clear and concise overview of your expenditures, categorized by type, for each month. The summary will be available on the first day of every month and will cover the previous month's spending. It will include the total amount spent, as well as a breakdown of the amounts spent in different categories, such as housing, transportation, and entertainment. This new feature is designed to help you track your spending habits and make informed decisions about your budget. You will be able to access your monthly spending summary through your online account or mobile app.
