Here's a calendar tip that changed my week: schedule your tasks, not just your meetings. Instead of keeping a to-do list that floats around in the background, give each task a specific time block on your calendar. Even a rough thirty-minute slot for answering emails or finishing a report makes it far more likely to actually get done, and you can see at a glance whether your day is realistic. If something slips, just drag it to a new time instead of letting it quietly pile up. Try it for one week and see how much calmer your Monday feels.
