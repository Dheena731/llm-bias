Instead of fully assembling identical meals that you might grow tired of by midweek, focus on preparing versatile base components. Roast a large sheet pan of vegetables, cook a batch of sturdy grains like quinoa or farro, and prepare a neutral protein like shredded chicken, lentils, or baked tofu. Storing these elements separately allows you to assemble bowls, salads, or wraps in under five minutes each morning, letting you change the flavor profile daily simply by swapping out your sauces and dressings.
