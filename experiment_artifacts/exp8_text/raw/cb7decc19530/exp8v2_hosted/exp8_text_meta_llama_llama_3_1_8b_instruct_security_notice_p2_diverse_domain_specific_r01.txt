**Important: Protecting Our Network from Phishing Scams**

As part of our ongoing effort to safeguard our company's network and assets, we want to remind everyone to remain vigilant when interacting with emails.

Recently, there have been instances of phishing emails targeting our domain. These emails often appear to be from trusted sources, such as our HR department or IT team, but are actually attempts to trick employees into divulging sensitive information or installing malicious software.

**Red Flags:**

* Urgent or threatening language
* Requests for sensitive information, such as passwords or financial data
* Links or attachments from unknown senders
* Misspellings or grammatical errors

**What to Do:**

* Never respond to or engage with suspicious emails
* Verify the sender's identity before taking any action
* Report any potential phishing attempts to our IT department immediately
* Keep your anti-virus software up to date and running on your work device

Your attention to this matter is crucial in preventing unauthorized access to our network and protecting our company's data. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to our IT team.

**Together, we can keep our network secure!**
