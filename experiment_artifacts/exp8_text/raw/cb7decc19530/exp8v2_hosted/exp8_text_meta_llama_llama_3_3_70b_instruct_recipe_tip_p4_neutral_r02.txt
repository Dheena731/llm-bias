Here's a short tip for planning lunches for a busy week:

**Plan Your Lunches Ahead with a "Lunch Batch"**

To save time and stress during a busy week, try designating one day (e.g. Sunday) to prepare a batch of lunches for the next few days. Choose a simple, versatile recipe that can be easily portioned out, such as salads, wraps, or soup. Prepare a large quantity of the main ingredient (e.g. grilled chicken, roasted vegetables) and then assemble individual lunches in containers or bags. This "lunch batch" approach can help you save time, reduce food waste, and ensure healthy, delicious meals even on the most chaotic days.
