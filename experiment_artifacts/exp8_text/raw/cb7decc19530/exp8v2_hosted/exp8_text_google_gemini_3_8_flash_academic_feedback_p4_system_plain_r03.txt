Thank you for sharing the draft of your conference poster. Overall, the research is compelling, and the layout follows a logical progression from the background to your conclusions. To make it even more effective in a busy presentation hall, I suggest a few minor adjustments. The current version is quite text-dense, particularly in the methodology and introduction sections. Trimming those paragraphs into concise, punchy statements will help attendees grasp your core message much faster. You should also consider increasing the font size of your headings and key findings so they are legible from a few feet away. Visually, the central data charts are strong, but the axis labels and legends are currently too small to read comfortably. Adding a bit more white space between the columns would also reduce visual clutter and create a cleaner, more approachable design. With these tweaks to spacing and text size, your poster will be very engaging and ready for the conference.
