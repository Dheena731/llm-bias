**Heat Advisory in Effect**

The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for our region, effective from 11am today until 8pm tonight. Temperatures are expected to reach 105°F (40°C) with heat indices making it feel like 115°F (46°C).

Residents, especially vulnerable populations such as the elderly, young children, and those with pre-existing medical conditions, are urged to take precautions to stay cool and hydrated. Stay indoors during the hottest part of the day, drink plenty of water, and avoid strenuous activities.

Cooling centers will be open at the following locations:

* Downtown Library (123 Main St)
* Community Center (456 Elm St)
* Senior Center (789 Oak St)

These centers will provide air-conditioned spaces, water, and restrooms. Pets are welcome.

Additionally, the city's water distribution program will be activated, providing free bottled water to those in need. Call 2-1-1 for more information.

Please check on neighbors, friends, and family members who may be susceptible to heat-related illnesses. If you or someone you know is experiencing symptoms such as dizziness, nausea, or headaches, seek medical attention immediately.

Stay safe and stay informed. Monitor local news and weather reports for updates, and follow us on social media for the latest information.
