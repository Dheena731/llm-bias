Hello everyone,

We are excited to share a new feature in the app: a monthly spending summary. At the end of each month, you will receive a clear overview of how your money was spent. The summary groups your transactions into categories such as groceries, transportation, dining, and bills, so you can see at a glance where most of your funds went.

The summary also compares your spending with the previous month. If a category rose noticeably, you will see that change highlighted, which can help you spot patterns and decide where to adjust your budget. Nothing about your account settings changes, and you can turn off the summary at any time from the notifications section of your profile.

To view your first summary, open the Insights tab after the month closes. If you have questions or notice anything that does not look right, please reach out through the help center, and our team will be glad to look into it.

Thank you for being part of our community, and we hope this tool makes managing your finances a little easier.
