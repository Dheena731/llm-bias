For the best flavor, season your vegetables in two stages. Before roasting, toss them with a generous amount of oil, salt, and pepper so the seasoning clings evenly and helps the edges caramelize. Then, as soon as they come out of the oven, finish them with something bright and fresh, such as a squeeze of lemon juice, a splash of good vinegar, or a handful of chopped herbs. Heat dulls delicate flavors, so adding acidity and fresh notes at the end wakes everything up. If you want to use garlic or spices like smoked paprika and cumin, add them in the last ten minutes or so of roasting, since they can turn bitter if they cook too long at high heat.
