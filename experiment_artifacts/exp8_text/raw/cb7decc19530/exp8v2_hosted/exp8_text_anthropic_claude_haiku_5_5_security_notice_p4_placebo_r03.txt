**Turn On Two-Factor Authentication to Protect Your Account**

Two-factor authentication adds a second step when you sign in. After you enter your password, you also enter a short code sent to your phone or generated by an app on your phone. Even if someone learns your password, they cannot sign in without that second step.

**To turn it on:**

1. Sign in to your account and open your security settings.
2. Select the option for two-factor authentication and choose a method, such as a code from an authenticator app or a text message sent to your phone.
3. Enter the code shown or sent to you to confirm the setup.
4. If backup codes are offered, save them somewhere safe. You can use them to sign in if you cannot access your phone.

If you change your phone number or get a new phone, update your settings before you lose access to the old device.
