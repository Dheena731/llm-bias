**Lost Company Laptop**

If you have misplaced or lost a company laptop, please report it to the IT Department immediately. This will ensure that the device is secured and any sensitive company information is protected.

To report a lost laptop, please contact the IT Department at [insert contact information]. Provide the laptop's serial number, which can be found on the bottom of the device, and a brief description of the laptop's make and model.

The IT Department will work to secure the device and take steps to protect company data. Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter.
