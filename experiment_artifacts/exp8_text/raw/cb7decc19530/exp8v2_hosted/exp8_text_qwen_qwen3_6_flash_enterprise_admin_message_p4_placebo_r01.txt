Subject: Quarterly Access Review Reminder

To all employees and team supervisors,

We are beginning the quarterly access review cycle. This routine verification ensures that every user account and software permission matches your current job duties. Accurate access levels protect organizational data and prevent unauthorized usage.

Supervisors must review the account permissions listed under their teams. Confirm each entry is necessary, remove any unused privileges, and adjust roles where responsibilities have changed. Individual contributors should check their own assigned tools and notify their supervisor if any access appears incorrect or excessive.

Please complete all verification steps within ten business days from today. On-time confirmation allows us to maintain security compliance and streamline system performance. The review form is available in your standard login area.

Direct questions to the help desk during standard operating hours. Thank you for your cooperation.
