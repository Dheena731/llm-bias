**HEAT ADVISORY ISSUED**

A heat advisory has been issued for the area, effective from 11am today until 8pm tomorrow. Temperatures are expected to reach the mid-90s, with heat indices reaching up to 105 degrees.

Residents are advised to take precautions to protect themselves and their loved ones from the heat. This includes staying hydrated by drinking plenty of water, taking breaks in cool spaces, and avoiding strenuous activities during the hottest part of the day.

If you or someone you know is experiencing symptoms of heat exhaustion or heat stroke, such as dizziness, nausea, or headaches, please seek medical attention immediately.

Stay cool and stay safe!
