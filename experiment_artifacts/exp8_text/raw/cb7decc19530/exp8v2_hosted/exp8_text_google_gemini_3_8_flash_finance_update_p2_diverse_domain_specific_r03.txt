**Subject: Scheduled updates to Card-Not-Present and Scheme Fee schedules (Effective July 1)**

Starting July 1, 2024, we are adjusting processing rates on select card categories to reflect updated card network assessment costs and cross-border settlement fees. 

**Summary of changes:**

* **Domestic Card-Not-Present (CNP) Credit:** The processor margin will increase by 4 bps (+0.04%). Fixed per-transaction capture fees remain unchanged at $0.12.
* **Commercial and Purchasing Cards:** Pass-through network assessments will increase by 7 to 11 bps, depending on tier, aligned with the latest interchange revisions from Visa and Mastercard.
* **Non-EEA Cross-Border Transactions:** The cross-border acquiring surcharge will increase from 1.15% to 1.30% to account for revised FX clearing mandates.
* **Card-Present (Point-of-Sale) and domestic PIN-debit rates will remain at their current contracted pricing.**

No configuration changes are required on your terminals or gateway integrations. An itemized table showing the old versus new effective rates across all card classes is now available under **Dashboard > Billing > Rate Cards (July 2024)**. 

These adjustments will be visible on your August billing statement for all volume captured on or after 00:00 UTC, July 1.
