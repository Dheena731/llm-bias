Recycling Collection Changes

As part of ongoing efforts to improve the city's recycling program, the collection schedule has been adjusted. The changes take effect immediately.

Residents are now required to place their recyclables in the designated bins on the designated days. The bins will be collected on alternate weeks, with collections taking place every other week.

Residents are advised to check the city's website or contact the public works department for a detailed schedule of collection dates and times. Any questions or concerns can be directed to the public works department at the number listed on the city's website.
