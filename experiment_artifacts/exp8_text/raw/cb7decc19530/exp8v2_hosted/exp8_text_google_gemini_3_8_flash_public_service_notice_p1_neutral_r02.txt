**PUBLIC SERVICE NOTICE**

**Community Library Operating Hours**

The Community Library is pleased to welcome all patrons during our regular operating hours:

* **Monday – Thursday:** 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM  
* **Friday – Saturday:** 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM  
* **Sunday:** 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM  
* **Public Holidays:** Closed  

**Need after-hours access?**  
Our exterior book drop is open 24/7 for returns. You can also browse our digital catalog, borrow e-books, and access research databases anytime at **www.yourlibrary.org**.

For inquiries or special accommodations, please call **(555) 019-2834** or speak with a librarian during open hours. 

*Your library is here to help you learn, discover, and connect.*
