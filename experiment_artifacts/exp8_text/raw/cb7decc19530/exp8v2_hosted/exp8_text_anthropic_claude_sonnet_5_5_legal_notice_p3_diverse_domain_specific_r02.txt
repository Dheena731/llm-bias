# Cookie Notice: Skerry Line Ferries Ltd

**Last revised:** 14 March 2025

**1. Scope.** This notice applies to skerryline.example and its booking subdomain, book.skerryline.example. It covers cookies and similar storage technologies (local storage, session storage) placed on your device when you use either site. It does not cover the onboard Wi-Fi portal, which has its own notice.
