**Notice of Recycling Collection Changes**

**Attention All Residents of Oakdale**

Due to the recent upgrade of our waste management facility, the Oakdale Waste Management Department is announcing changes to the recycling collection schedule. As of January 2, 2024, the recycling collection day will be shifting from every Thursday to every **first and third Tuesdays** of each month.

**Important Details:**

* Collection times remain unchanged, with pickups taking place between 8:00 AM and 12:00 PM.
* Residents are still required to place recyclables in their designated blue bins, with a limit of three bins per household.
* Accepted materials include paper, plastic, glass, and metal products.
* Contaminated or improperly sorted materials will not be accepted and may be subject to fines.

**Upcoming Collection Dates:**

* January 9, 2024 (first Tuesday)
* January 23, 2024 (third Tuesday)
* February 6, 2024 (first Tuesday)
* February 20, 2024 (third Tuesday)

For any questions or concerns regarding the new collection schedule, please contact the Oakdale Waste Management Department at (555) 123-4567 or visit our website at oakdale.gov/waste.
