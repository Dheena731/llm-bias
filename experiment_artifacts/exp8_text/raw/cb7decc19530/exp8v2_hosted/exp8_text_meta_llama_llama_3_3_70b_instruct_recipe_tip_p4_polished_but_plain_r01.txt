To plan lunches for a busy week, start by taking some time on the weekend to think about your schedule and dietary needs for the upcoming days. Consider what ingredients you already have at home and what can be prepared in advance, such as soups, salads, or grains that can be cooked and reheated as needed. You can also make a big batch of something like rice or quinoa and use it as a base for different meals throughout the week. Think about what leftovers you can use for lunch and what can be packed and taken on the go. Having a general plan in place can help save time and reduce stress during the week, and can also help ensure that you are eating healthy and satisfying meals.
