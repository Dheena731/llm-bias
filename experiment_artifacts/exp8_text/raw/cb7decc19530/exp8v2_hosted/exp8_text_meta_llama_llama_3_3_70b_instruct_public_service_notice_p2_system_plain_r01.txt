The city has announced that Main Street will be closed to traffic from Monday to Friday of next week. The closure is due to construction work being done to repair a damaged sewer line. The road will be closed from the intersection of Main Street and Oak Avenue to the intersection of Main Street and Elm Street. Drivers are advised to use alternative routes during this time. Detour signs will be posted to help guide traffic around the closure. The city apologizes for any inconvenience this may cause and thanks residents for their patience and understanding.
