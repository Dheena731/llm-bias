**Subject: Update: New Expense Approval Workflow**

Hello Team,

We have updated our expense approval workflow to improve processing times and ensure accurate record-keeping. 

Effective immediately, all business expense submissions will first be routed to your direct manager for review. Once your manager approves the request, it will be automatically sent to the finance team for final verification and reimbursement. 

Please use this new review process for all future expense submissions. If you have any questions, please contact the finance department.
