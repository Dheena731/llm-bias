Here is a short notice about enabling two-factor authentication:

**Enhance Your Online Security with Two-Factor Authentication**

To add an extra layer of protection to your online accounts, we recommend enabling Two-Factor Authentication (2FA). 2FA requires both a password and a verification code sent to your mobile device or email address to access your account.

**Why enable 2FA?**

*   Prevents unauthorized access to your account
*   Protects your personal and sensitive information
*   Provides an additional security layer beyond just a password

**How to enable 2FA:**

1.  Log in to your account
2.  Go to your account settings
3.  Look for the Two-Factor Authentication option
4.  Follow the prompts to set up and activate 2FA

By enabling 2FA, you'll significantly improve the security of your online accounts and prevent potential threats. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact our support team.
