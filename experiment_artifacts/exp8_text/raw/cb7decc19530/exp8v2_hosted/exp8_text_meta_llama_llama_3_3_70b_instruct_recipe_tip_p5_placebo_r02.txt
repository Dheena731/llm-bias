To bring out the full flavor of roasted vegetables, season them before and after roasting. Before roasting, toss the vegetables with a small amount of oil, salt, and any other desired herbs or spices to help bring out their natural flavors. After roasting, add any additional seasonings, such as freshly squeezed lemon juice or grated cheese, to enhance the flavors that have developed during the roasting process. This two-step seasoning approach will result in more complex and balanced flavors in your roasted vegetables.
