Dear colleagues,

This is a friendly reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even for a few minutes. An unlocked screen can give anyone passing by access to confidential client information, internal emails, and company systems, and it only takes a moment for something sensitive to be seen or changed.

To lock your screen on a Windows computer, press the Windows key and L at the same time. On a Mac, press Control, Command, and Q together. Locking your screen takes a second and keeps your work protected while you are away, including during meetings, lunch breaks, and short trips to the printer or kitchen.

We also ask that you not share your password with colleagues or leave it written down near your workstation. If you notice an unlocked computer that is unattended, please lock it if you can do so safely, or let the IT team know so they can follow up.

Thank you for helping us keep our information secure. If you have any questions or need help setting up automatic screen locking, please contact the IT help desk.

Kind regards,
