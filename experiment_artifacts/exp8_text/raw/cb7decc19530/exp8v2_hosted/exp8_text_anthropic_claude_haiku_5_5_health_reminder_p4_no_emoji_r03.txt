Take a Break from the Screen

You've been focused for a while, so it's a good time to pause. Stand up, stretch your shoulders, and let your eyes rest by looking at something at least 20 feet away for a few seconds. Take a few slow breaths, drink some water, and take a short walk if you can. A few minutes away from the screen can refresh your focus and ease tension, so you'll return to your work feeling clearer and more energized.
