**Introducing Savings Goals**

We've added a new Savings Goals feature to help you plan and track the things that matter most to you, whether that's a vacation, an emergency fund, or a big purchase.

**What you can do:**

- **Set a target:** Name your goal, choose an amount, and pick a target date.
- **Track progress:** See a clear progress bar and how much you have left to save.
- **Automate contributions:** Schedule recurring transfers so you stay on track without thinking about it.
- **Adjust anytime:** Change your target, timeline, or contribution amount whenever your plans shift.

**How to get started:**

Open the Savings tab, select "Create a Goal," and follow the prompts. It takes less than a minute to set up.

Savings Goals is available now to all users. We'd love to hear what you think, so please send us your feedback through the Help menu.
