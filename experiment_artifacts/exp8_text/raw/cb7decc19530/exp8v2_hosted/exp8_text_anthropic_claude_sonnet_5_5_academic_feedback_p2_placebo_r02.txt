**Feedback on your methods section**

The main test for a methods section is replicability: could another researcher in your field repeat your study from what you've written? As you revise, check your draft against these points:

1. **Design and rationale.** State the overall approach and say briefly why it suits your research question.
2. **Participants or materials.** Describe who or what you studied, how they were selected or obtained, and the sample size.
3. **Procedure.** Describe the steps in the order they happened, with enough detail that nothing important is left to guesswork.
4. **Measures and instruments.** Name what you used to collect data and explain how it was applied or scored.
5. **Analysis.** Explain how you processed the data and why you chose those techniques.
6. **Ethics and limitations.** Mention consent or approval where relevant, and acknowledge any choices that could affect your results.

Keep the section factual and in past tense. Put results and interpretation in later sections. Define any specialized term the first time you use it.
