Here's a short cooking tip for preparing a weeknight dinner:

**Quick and Easy Weeknight Dinner Tip:**

To save time and reduce stress on busy weeknights, try preparing a "base" protein and vegetable mixture on the weekend or a day off, and then use it to create multiple meals throughout the week. For example, cook a large batch of grilled chicken or roasted vegetables, and then use them to make different meals such as chicken salads, stir-fries, or wraps. This will not only save you time during the week, but also reduce food waste and make meal planning a breeze. Simply reheat and add your favorite seasonings or sauces to create a delicious and healthy meal in no time!
