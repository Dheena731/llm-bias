Welcome to "Learn with Us", a new language-learning podcast where we'll be exploring the intricacies of language and culture through conversations, stories, and interviews with native speakers. Each episode will take you on a journey to a new country and introduce you to its unique customs, traditions, and ways of life. Whether you're a seasoned traveler, a language enthusiast, or simply someone who wants to broaden their cultural horizons, we invite you to join us on this linguistic adventure.
