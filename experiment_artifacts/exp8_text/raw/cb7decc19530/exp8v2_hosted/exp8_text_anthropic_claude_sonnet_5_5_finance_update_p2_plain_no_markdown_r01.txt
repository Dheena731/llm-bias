Subject: Upcoming change to card transaction fees

Dear customer,

We are writing to let you know that our card transaction fees will change on the first day of next month. We review our pricing from time to time, and this update reflects the rising costs we pay to card networks and payment processors, which we have absorbed for as long as we reasonably could.

From that date, the fee for each card payment will be 2.4 percent of the transaction value plus a fixed charge of 20 cents, replacing the current rate of 2.1 percent plus 20 cents. The fixed charge is unchanged. Refunds, chargebacks, and monthly account fees are not affected by this update.

For a typical sale of 50 dollars, the new fee will be about 1.40 dollars, compared with about 1.25 dollars today. You will see the new rate applied automatically to all transactions processed on or after the effective date, and your monthly statement will show the fees itemized as usual.

You do not need to take any action. If you would prefer not to accept the new terms, you may close your account without penalty before the change takes effect. If you have questions about how the new rate will affect your business, or if you would like us to review your account for a more suitable pricing arrangement, our support team is happy to help by phone or email during business hours.

Thank you for your continued trust in us. We appreciate your business and are committed to keeping our pricing clear and fair.

Kind regards,

The Customer Accounts Team
