Residents are advised that the recycling collection schedule will be changing as of next week. The new schedule will see collections take place on alternate weeks, with collections for paper and cardboard products occurring on one week and for plastics and glass on the following week.

The collection days will remain the same, but the type of materials collected will be rotated. Residents are encouraged to check their collection calendar for specific details on what will be collected on each week.

Residents are also reminded to rinse and sort their recyclables before placing them at the curb. The city's recycling program is designed to make it easy for residents to participate and make a positive impact on the environment.

Residents with questions or concerns about the recycling collection schedule are encouraged to contact the city's public works department for assistance.
