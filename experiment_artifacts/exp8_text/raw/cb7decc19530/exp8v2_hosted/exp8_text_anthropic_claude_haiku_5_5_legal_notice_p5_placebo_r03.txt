**Notice of Acceptable Use of the Shared Network**

**1. Who this notice applies to.** This notice applies to every person who connects to or uses the shared network (the "Network"), including employees, contractors, and visitors.

**2. Purpose.** The Network is shared by all users. You must use it in a way that does not harm the Network, other users, or the data stored on or transmitted across it.

**3. Permitted use.** You may use the Network for lawful purposes connected with the reason you were given access to it.

**4. Prohibited use.** You must not:

(a) use the Network for any unlawful purpose;

(b) access, copy, change, or delete data, accounts, or files that you are not authorized to access;

(c) share your login credentials or use another person's credentials;

(d) introduce malicious software, or take any action intended to disrupt, overload, or degrade the Network or other users' access to it;

(e) attempt to bypass security controls or access restrictions; or

(f) use the Network to send threatening, harassing, or unlawful material, or to infringe copyright or other intellectual property rights.

**5. Monitoring.** Activity on the Network may be logged and reviewed to maintain security and to enforce this notice. Using the Network means you accept this monitoring.

**6. Consequences of violation.** A violation of this notice may result in suspension of your access to the Network, and may lead to further action under applicable policy or law.

**7. Acceptance.** Continued use of the Network means you have read and agree to comply with this notice.
