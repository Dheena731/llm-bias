Dear valued customer,

We are writing to let you know that, beginning on the first day of next month, we will be adjusting the fee we charge for card transactions. The new fee will be 2.4 percent of the transaction value, up from the previous rate of 1.9 percent. For a typical purchase, this will add a few cents to a few dollars to your total, depending on the amount.

The change reflects higher processing costs that we have faced from our payment providers over the past year. We have worked to keep the increase as small as possible, and we have not changed the fee for cash payments or bank transfers, which remain free of charge.

If you have a recurring subscription or a standing order with us, the new rate will apply automatically from the effective date, and you do not need to take any action. Your receipts will show the fee as a separate line so you can see exactly what you have paid.

If you have questions about this change, please contact our customer support team by replying to this message or calling the number on our website. Thank you for your continued trust and for choosing to do business with us.

Kind regards,
The Customer Service Team
