**Notice: Reporting a Lost Company Laptop**

If you have misplaced or lost your company-issued laptop, report the incident immediately to the IT Security team. Prompt reporting is required to protect sensitive company data and comply with our asset management policy.

To file your report, access the internal IT support portal and select “Report Lost or Stolen Device.” Complete the form with your full name, department, laptop serial number, and the date and location of the loss. If you cannot access the portal, call the IT Help Desk through the internal phone directory or visit the IT Security office in person.

Once your report is submitted, our security team will immediately suspend your device access and initiate remote data protection protocols. You will receive clear instructions regarding temporary equipment replacement and account recovery within one business day.

Do not attempt to retrieve the device yourself if it was stolen, and do not share your login credentials with anyone during this process. Thank you for your cooperation in keeping company information secure.
