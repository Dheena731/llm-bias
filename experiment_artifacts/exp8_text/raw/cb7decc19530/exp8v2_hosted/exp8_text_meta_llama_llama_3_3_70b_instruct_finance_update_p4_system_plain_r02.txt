Your end-of-year tax statement is a document that summarizes the income you earned and the taxes that were withheld from your pay throughout the year. It is typically provided to you by your employer and is used to help you complete your tax return. The statement will show the total amount of money you earned, the amount of taxes that were withheld, and any other relevant tax information. You will need this statement to accurately report your income and claim any deductions or credits you are eligible for when you file your taxes.
