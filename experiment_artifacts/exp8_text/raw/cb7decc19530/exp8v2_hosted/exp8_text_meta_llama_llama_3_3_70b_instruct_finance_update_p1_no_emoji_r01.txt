We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you keep track of your expenses. This summary will provide a detailed breakdown of your spending habits, including total expenditures, category-wise spending, and trends over time. The summary will be sent to you via email at the beginning of each month, allowing you to review and adjust your spending habits as needed. This new feature is designed to provide you with a clearer understanding of your financial situation and help you make more informed decisions about your money.
