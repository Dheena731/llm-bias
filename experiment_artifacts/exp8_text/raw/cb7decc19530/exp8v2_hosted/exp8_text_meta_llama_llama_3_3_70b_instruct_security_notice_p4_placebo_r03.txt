Important Security Notice: 

To enhance the security of your account, we strongly recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA). This additional layer of protection requires a second form of verification, such as a code sent to your phone or email, in addition to your password. Enabling 2FA will significantly reduce the risk of unauthorized access to your account. You can enable 2FA by logging into your account settings and following the provided instructions. If you need assistance, please contact our support team.
