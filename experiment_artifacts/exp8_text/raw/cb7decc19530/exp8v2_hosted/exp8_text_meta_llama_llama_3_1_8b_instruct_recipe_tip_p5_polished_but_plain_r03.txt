When seasoning roasted vegetables, it's best to add aromatics such as garlic and onions to the pan before adding the vegetables. This allows the aromatics to caramelize and develop a deep flavor that will enhance the overall taste of the dish. Additionally, it's a good idea to use a mixture of seasonings that complement the natural flavors of the vegetables. For example, rosemary and thyme pair well with root vegetables, while a Mediterranean blend of oregano, basil, and lemon juice works well with eggplant and zucchini.
