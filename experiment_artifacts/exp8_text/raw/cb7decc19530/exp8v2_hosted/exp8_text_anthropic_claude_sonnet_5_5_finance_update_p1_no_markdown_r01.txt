Subject: Introducing Your New Monthly Spending Summary

Hello,

We're excited to share a new feature designed to help you understand your finances more clearly: the Monthly Spending Summary.

Starting this month, you'll receive a concise overview of your spending at the end of each billing cycle. The summary breaks down your purchases by category, such as groceries, dining, transportation, and subscriptions, so you can quickly see where your money is going.

Here is what you'll find in each summary:

- Your total spending for the month
- A category-by-category breakdown with percentages
- A comparison to the previous month, highlighting increases and decreases
- Your top five merchants by amount spent
- A list of recurring charges, so nothing catches you by surprise

You can view your summary in the app under the Insights tab, or have it delivered to your email inbox. To manage your delivery preferences, open Settings and select Notifications.

We designed this feature to make budgeting simpler and to help you spot trends, catch unexpected charges, and make more confident decisions. Your data remains private and secure, and the summary is visible only to you.

We'd love to hear what you think. Send us your feedback through the Help section in the app, and let us know what else you'd like to see in future updates.

Thank you for being a valued customer.
