We're pleased to announce the launch of the Simple Budget Planner, a spreadsheet template designed to make managing your money clearer and less stressful. It works with both Excel and Google Sheets, so you can use it on whichever platform you already prefer.

The template brings your monthly income, fixed bills, variable spending, savings goals, and debt payments together in a single workbook. You enter your numbers once, and the sheets update automatically to show where your money is going and how much is left at the end of each month. A yearly overview helps you spot trends, such as seasonal spending spikes or categories that regularly run over, so you can adjust before small problems become large ones.

We built the planner for people who want structure without a steep learning curve. Every cell that needs your input is clearly marked, formulas are already in place, and a short guide on the first sheet walks you through setup in about ten minutes. If you like to customize, the categories are easy to rename, add, or remove, and nothing is locked or hidden.

The Simple Budget Planner is available today for a one-time purchase, with no subscription and no account required. Once you download it, the file is yours to keep and reuse every year. We hope it helps you build confidence in your finances, one month at a time.
