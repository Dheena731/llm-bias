Thank you for your methods section. Its main job is to let a reader understand exactly what you did and repeat it if they wanted to, so I read it with that goal in mind.

**What works:** Describing the study in the order it happened makes it easy to follow.

**What to strengthen:**

1. **Explain your choices.** For each major decision, such as who took part, how they were recruited, or why you chose a particular measure, add a sentence on why it suits your research question.
2. **Give enough detail to repeat the study.** Specify the number of participants, how they were selected, the materials or instruments used, and the steps in order. Where a number or timing matters, state it.
3. **Connect the analysis to the question.** Name the analysis you ran and say what it tells you about your question. A reader should not have to guess how the results were produced.
4. **Name any limitations in this section.** If the design or sample constrains what you can conclude, say so briefly here, and save the full discussion for later.
