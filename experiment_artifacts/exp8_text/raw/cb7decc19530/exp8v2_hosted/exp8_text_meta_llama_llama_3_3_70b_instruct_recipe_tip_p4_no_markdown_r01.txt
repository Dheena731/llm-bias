To plan lunches for a busy week, consider setting aside one day to prepare and portion out your meals for the next few days. This can be as simple as cooking a large batch of rice, grains, or protein, and then using those ingredients to assemble individual lunches. You can also prep vegetables, fruits, and other toppings to add some variety to your meals. Having a plan and some pre-prepared ingredients can help save you time and reduce stress during the week, and ensure that you have healthy and satisfying lunches to fuel your busy days.
