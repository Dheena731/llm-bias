**Notice to Residents: Changes to Recycling Collection**

Beginning Monday, March 3, recycling collection in our neighborhood will move from Tuesdays to Thursdays. Recycling bins should be placed at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on Thursday, and they may be set out the evening before. Trash collection will continue on its current Monday schedule and is not affected by this change.

Please note that glass bottles and jars must now be sorted into a separate container and are no longer accepted in the blue bins. Plastic bags, bagged recyclables, and food-soiled containers should also stay out of the recycling bin, as they can damage sorting equipment and cause entire loads to be sent to landfill. Flattening cardboard boxes before collection helps keep the route on schedule.

Holiday weeks may bring further adjustments. Updated pickup dates will be posted on the city website and sent to registered households by email. Questions can be directed to the Public Works office at 555-0148 or to publicworks@example.gov.

Thank you for helping keep our community clean and our recycling program effective.
