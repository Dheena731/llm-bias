NOTICE: CHANGES TO RECYCLING COLLECTION SCHEDULE

Dear Residents and Business Owners,

Please be advised that starting Monday, October 16, there will be updates to the weekly recycling collection schedule. Household recyclables will now be collected on alternating Tuesdays instead of Mondays. Mixed paper and cardboard will continue to be picked up every Monday as usual.

This adjustment allows our sanitation team to improve sorting efficiency and reduce material contamination. To ensure a smooth transition, please place your recycling bin at the curb by 7 AM on your scheduled day and rinse all containers before setting them out.

For a complete collection calendar and updated item guidelines, please visit our municipal website or contact the Public Works office at 555-0199.

Thank you for your continued support in keeping our neighborhood clean and environmentally responsible.

Sincerely,
Department of Sanitation
Municipal Services Division
