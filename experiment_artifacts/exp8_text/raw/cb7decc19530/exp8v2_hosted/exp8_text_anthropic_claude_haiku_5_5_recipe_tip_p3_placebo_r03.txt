Rinse your rice in cold water before cooking. Place it in a fine-mesh strainer and run cold water over it, swirling with your fingers, until the water runs mostly clear. Rinsing washes away the surface starch that makes cooked grains clump together. Drain well, then cook using the standard water ratio for your variety. For extra fluffy results, let the cooked rice rest covered off the heat for about 10 minutes before fluffing it gently with a fork.
