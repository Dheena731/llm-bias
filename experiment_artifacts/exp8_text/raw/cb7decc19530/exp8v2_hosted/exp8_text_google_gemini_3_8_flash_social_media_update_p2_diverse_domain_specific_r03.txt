**Domain:** *Bioacoustics / Open-source Nocturnal Flight Call monitoring*

***

Last night, a condenser mic sitting on a shed in Duluth caught the archive’s 500,000th verified flight call: a Swainson’s Thrush cutting through the low hum of a passing freight train. 

When this started, the database was mostly rain clipping, leaf blowers, and porch cats. 

Thank you to everyone who spent October squinting at spectrograms at 1:00 AM, arguing over ambiguous warbler chips, and leaving microphones duct-taped to broomsticks in the frost. 

The spring weights are live and the full audio archive remains open for download. Back to the nets.
