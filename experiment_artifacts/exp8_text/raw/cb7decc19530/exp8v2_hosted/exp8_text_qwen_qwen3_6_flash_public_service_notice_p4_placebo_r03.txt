**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY**

A Heat Advisory is in effect from Monday at 10:00 AM through Wednesday at 8:00 PM. During this period, both daytime highs and overnight lows will remain unusually elevated, creating conditions that significantly increase the risk of heat exhaustion, heatstroke, and dehydration.

Residents are advised to take the following precautions:
• Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty.
• Limit strenuous outdoor activities to early morning or late evening hours.
• Check regularly on elderly neighbors, young children, and individuals managing chronic health conditions.
• Never leave children or animals inside parked vehicles, regardless of window shade or duration.
• Seek immediate medical attention if you or someone else experiences confusion, dizziness, nausea, flushed skin, or a rapid heartbeat.

Public buildings equipped with air conditioning will serve as designated cooling centers. For real-time updates and emergency information, monitor official local news broadcasts and your regional emergency alert system.

Issued by: Local Public Health Department & Regional Weather Services
