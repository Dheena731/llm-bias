Tip: Prep your lunches in components on Sunday instead of making full meals. Cook a big batch of one grain (like rice or quinoa), one protein (like grilled chicken or chickpeas), and chop a few vegetables. Store them in separate containers, then mix and match each day with a different sauce or seasoning. This keeps lunches from getting boring, cuts down on daily cooking time, and lets you use up leftovers before they spoil. Bonus: pack dressings and sauces separately so everything stays fresh until you are ready to eat.
