The Central Public Library wishes to remind all community members of its standard operating schedule. The main branch is open to the public Monday through Thursday from nine in the morning until eight in the evening. On Fridays and Saturdays, the building is open from nine in the morning until five in the afternoon. The library is closed on Sundays and on all officially observed municipal holidays. External book drops located next to the primary entrance remain accessible for returning borrowed materials at all times, including outside of regular operating hours. Patrons can continue to access digital resources, including electronic books and research databases, online at any time. For further information or assistance during open hours, please speak directly with a librarian at the front desk or contact the library by telephone.
