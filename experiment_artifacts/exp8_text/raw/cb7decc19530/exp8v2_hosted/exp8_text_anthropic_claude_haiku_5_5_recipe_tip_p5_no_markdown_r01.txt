Toss your vegetables in olive oil before they go into the oven, and season them with salt and pepper while they're still coated. The oil helps the seasoning cling and encourages even browning, so every bite is flavorful. For extra depth, add garlic powder, smoked paprika, or a pinch of dried herbs during the first few minutes of roasting, and finish with a squeeze of lemon juice or a sprinkle of fresh herbs once they come out of the oven.
