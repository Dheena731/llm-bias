When planning lunches for a busy week, start by making a list of the meals you need to prepare for each day. Consider any dietary restrictions or preferences you have, as well as any ingredients you may already have on hand. Next, decide on a few different meal options and consider how you can reuse ingredients throughout the week. For example, if you're making a big batch of rice on Monday, you can use leftovers in a stir-fry on Tuesday. This will help reduce food waste and save you time. Finally, take some time on the weekend to prep as much as you can, such as chopping vegetables or cooking proteins, so that during the week, all you have to do is assemble your meals.
