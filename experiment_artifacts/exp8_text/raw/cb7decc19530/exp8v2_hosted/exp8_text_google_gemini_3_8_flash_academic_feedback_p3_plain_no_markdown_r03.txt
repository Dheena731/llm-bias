The central research question of this manuscript remains ambiguous and requires substantial refinement. While the introduction provides adequate background information, it does not clearly articulate the specific problem the authors aim to solve or the primary hypothesis being tested. Because the core objective is broadly stated, it is difficult to determine whether the chosen methodology and resulting conclusions directly align with the intended scope of the project. I recommend that the authors revise the introduction to present an explicit, tightly focused research question, clearly define their key terms, and explain how answering this question advances the current state of the field.
