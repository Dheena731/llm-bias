The National Weather Service has issued a heat advisory for the region beginning tomorrow morning and lasting through Friday evening. Temperatures are expected to reach highs between one hundred five and one hundred ten degrees Fahrenheit. Residents are advised to limit outdoor activities during the hottest parts of the day and drink plenty of water. Those who must work outside should take frequent breaks in shaded or air-conditioned areas. Elderly individuals and those with health conditions are at greater risk for heat-related illness, so neighbors should check on each other regularly. Please keep pets indoors and never leave children or animals in parked vehicles. If you experience symptoms such as dizziness, nausea, or excessive sweating, seek medical attention immediately. Stay cool and follow these safety guidelines to protect your health during this period of extreme heat.
