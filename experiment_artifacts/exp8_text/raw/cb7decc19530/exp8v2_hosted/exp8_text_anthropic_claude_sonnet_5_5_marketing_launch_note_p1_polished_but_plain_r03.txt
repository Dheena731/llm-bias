Introducing Tallyfold, the simple way to run your small business from your phone.

If you own a small business, you already know that the work doesn't stop when the doors close. Invoices need sending, appointments need confirming, and receipts pile up in places you will never think to look. Tallyfold brings those tasks into one calm, uncluttered app so you can spend less time on administration and more time on the work you actually enjoy.

With Tallyfold, you can create and send a professional invoice in under a minute, then see at a glance who has paid and who needs a gentle reminder. Your calendar and client list live in the same place, so booking a job, recording a payment, and following up with a customer all happen without switching between tools. When tax season arrives, your income and expenses are already sorted and ready to export for your accountant.

We built Tallyfold after talking with dozens of shop owners, freelancers, tradespeople, and studio founders who told us the same thing: most business software feels designed for companies ten times their size. So we kept the setup short, the language plain, and the features focused on what a small team needs every day. There are no long training videos to sit through and no complicated menus to learn. Most people are up and running within fifteen minutes.

Tallyfold is available today for iPhone and Android, and a web version is ready for those who prefer a bigger screen. To celebrate our launch, every new business can use the full app free for the first 60 days, with no card required to start. After that, a single affordable monthly plan covers your whole team.

Download Tallyfold today, set up your first invoice, and see how much lighter your week can feel. We would love to hear how it goes, and our support team is ready to help at any point along the way.
