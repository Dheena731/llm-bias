To plan lunches for a busy week, start by taking some time on the weekend to think about your schedule and dietary needs for the upcoming days. Consider what ingredients you already have at home and what leftovers you can use. Make a list of the lunches you want to prepare and the ingredients you need to buy. You can also prep some components of your lunches in advance, such as cooking proteins or chopping vegetables, to make assembly easier during the week. Having a plan in place will help you save time and ensure you have healthy and satisfying lunches even on the busiest of days.
