For most fresh herbs, the best way to keep them lasting longer is to treat them like cut flowers. Trim a little off the stem ends, then stand them upright in a glass with an inch or two of water, and cover loosely with a plastic bag before refrigerating. Change the water every couple of days. Basil is the exception, since it does not like cold; keep it on the counter in water instead. Hardy herbs like rosemary, thyme, and sage can also be wrapped in a damp paper towel and stored in a sealed container in the fridge.
