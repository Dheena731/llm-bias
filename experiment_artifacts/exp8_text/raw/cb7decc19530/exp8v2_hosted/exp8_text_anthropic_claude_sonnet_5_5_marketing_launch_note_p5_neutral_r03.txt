# Now Playing: *Say It Again*, a Podcast for Language Learners

We're excited to announce that **Say It Again** is live and ready for your commute, your walk, or your dishes.

**What it is**
Short, friendly episodes (10 to 15 minutes) that help you learn a language the way people actually use it. No stiff textbook dialogues, no pressure to be perfect. Just real phrases, useful context, and plenty of repetition.

**What you'll get**
- **Everyday conversations** you can use right away, from ordering coffee to making small talk
- **Culture notes** that explain the "why" behind the words
- **Repeat-after-me moments** so you practice speaking out loud, not just listening
- **Mistake of the Week**, a common slip-up and how to fix it

**Who it's for**
Beginners building confidence, returning learners shaking off the rust, and anyone who wants to keep a language alive in their daily routine.

**Where to listen**
Episodes 1 through 3 are available now on all major podcast apps. New episodes drop every Tuesday.

**Start here**
Episode 1, "Hello, and Everything After," covers greetings, introductions, and the one phrase that will save you in any conversation: *"Could you say that again?"*

Press play, say it out loud, and say it again. 🎧
