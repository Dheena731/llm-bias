**NOTICE: RECYCLING COLLECTION CHANGES**

Dear Residents,

Please be advised that the curbside recycling program will implement two operational updates to improve processing efficiency and reduce contamination.

First, the monthly recycling collection schedule has been revised to align with optimized service routes. Your new pickup day will be listed on the updated calendar distributed to all households. Second, accepted materials have been adjusted to exclude plastic film and expanded foam packaging, while continuing to accept clean paper products, flattened cardboard, glass jars, and metal cans.

Residents must rinse all containers, keep recyclables loose in the designated bin, and place the bin at the curb by early morning on the scheduled day. Loads that are overfilled or contaminated will not be collected.

Complete sorting guidelines and the revised calendar are available through the official municipal services portal. For assistance, contact the waste management office during standard business hours.

Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining a clean and sustainable community.

Waste Management Division
