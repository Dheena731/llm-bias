Join us this weekend for the Maple Street Neighborhood Block Party on Saturday, June 14, from 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. in Riverside Park. There will be live music from the Eastside Jazz Trio, a family picnic area, face painting for kids, and a bake sale benefiting the local library's summer reading program. Local food trucks will be on site, and volunteers are needed to help set up tables at 9 a.m. If you can lend a hand, send a message to our page or stop by the welcome booth. Bring your lawn chairs, your neighbors, and your appetite. We hope to see you there!
