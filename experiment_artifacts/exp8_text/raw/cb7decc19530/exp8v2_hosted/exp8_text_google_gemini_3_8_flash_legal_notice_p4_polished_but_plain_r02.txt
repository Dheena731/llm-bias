This policy outlines the criteria and procedures governing eligibility for monetary refunds. Customers may request a refund within thirty calendar days from the original date of transaction, provided that the request is accompanied by valid proof of purchase, such as an official receipt or verified transaction number. To remain eligible for a refund, physical goods must be returned in their original packaging, unused, unaltered, and in the exact condition as initially received. In the case of digital content, software, or professional services, fees are deemed non-refundable once access credentials have been issued or service performance has commenced, unless a material defect or breach of service delivery is established. Certain categories, including customized merchandise, final clearance items, and gift certificates, are strictly excluded from refund eligibility. Approved refunds will be credited exclusively to the original method of payment within ten to fourteen business days following the receipt, inspection, and verification of the returned item or claim. The company reserves the right to deny any refund request that fails to satisfy these requirements or that exhibits evidence of fraud, unauthorized modification, or excessive wear.
