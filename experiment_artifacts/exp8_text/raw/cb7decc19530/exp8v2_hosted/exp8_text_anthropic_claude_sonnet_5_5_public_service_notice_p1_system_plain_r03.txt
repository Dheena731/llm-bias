Notice to Residents: Updated Library Opening Hours

Beginning the first Monday of next month, the Riverside Public Library will operate on a new schedule designed to better serve the needs of our community.

The library will be open from nine in the morning until eight in the evening on Monday through Thursday. On Fridays, doors will open at nine and close at five in the afternoon. Saturday hours will run from ten in the morning until four in the afternoon. The library will be closed on Sundays and on all public holidays.

The children's reading room will open thirty minutes after the main library each day and will close thirty minutes earlier. Staff will be available at the information desk throughout all opening hours to help with borrowing, research questions, and computer access.

Residents who use the book drop outside the main entrance may continue to do so at any time, day or night. Items returned after closing will be checked in on the next working day, and no late fees will be charged for materials placed in the drop during a closure.

Please note that during the week of the schedule change, some services may be briefly limited while staff update systems and signage. We appreciate your patience and understanding.

For questions about the new hours, or to ask about special arrangements for school groups and community organizations, please speak with a member of staff at the front desk or call the library's main office during opening hours.

Thank you for supporting your local library.
