Please remain vigilant regarding suspicious emails, as we have recently seen an increase in phishing attempts targeting our organization. Phishing emails often pretend to come from trusted colleagues, management, or external vendors, and they typically create a false sense of urgency to trick you into sharing passwords, personal information, or financial details. Always check the sender address carefully before opening unexpected attachments or downloading files. If an email seems unusual, demands immediate action, or asks for sensitive credentials, do not reply to it or click on any contents. Instead, forward the message directly to the IT help desk or use the reporting tool in your email client. Taking a moment to verify suspicious communications helps keep our company data and network secure. Thank you for your cooperation.
