Take a Moment to Rest Your Eyes

You've been looking at screens for a while now, so it's a good time to step away for a few minutes. Look at something about 20 feet away for twenty seconds, stretch your neck and shoulders, and take a few slow breaths. Stand up, get some water, or simply look out a window. These small pauses help reduce eye strain, ease tension, and clear your mind so you can come back refreshed and focused.
