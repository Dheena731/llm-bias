Today we are launching Second Tongue, a weekly podcast for people learning a new language as adults, and we could not be happier to finally share it with you.

Each episode runs about twenty minutes and follows a single idea. One week we might look at why verb endings feel so slippery in the early months. The next, we might talk with a hospital interpreter about what she does when a word has no clean translation. We keep the tone relaxed and the examples real, drawn from conversations you might actually have at a market, on a train, or at a friend's dinner table.

You will not find a rigid method here, and we will not promise fluency in thirty days. What we offer instead is honest company for the long middle stretch of learning, when progress feels invisible and motivation comes and goes. Expect practical tips on listening practice, memory, and speaking with confidence, along with stories from learners who have been exactly where you are.

The first three episodes are available now, and new ones arrive every Tuesday. Start wherever you like, since each one stands on its own. If you have a question, a stubborn grammar problem, or a story about a memorable mistake, send it our way, because we plan to feature listener voices often.

Thank you for being here at the beginning. Press play, get comfortable, and let us learn together.
