**Tip for fluffier, less sticky rice:** Rinse your rice in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer under cold water until the water runs mostly clear, usually 3 to 4 rinses. This washes away the surface starch that makes grains clump together. Then use the right ratio, about 1 cup of rice to 1½ cups of water for long-grain white rice. Once it's cooked, let it rest covered for 10 minutes off the heat, then fluff it gently with a fork.
