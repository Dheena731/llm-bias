The local library's opening hours are as follows:

The library is open Monday through Thursday from 9am to 7pm and Friday and Saturday from 9am to 5pm. On Sundays, the library is closed.

During the summer months, the library's hours may be adjusted. Please check the library's website or call the library directly to confirm the hours for a specific day.

The library is also closed on major holidays, including New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. On these days, you can check the library's website for any alternative hours or services.
