Welcome to Words Between Us, the podcast that makes learning a new language feel like a conversation with a friend.

Each week, we'll take you through the everyday moments where languages come alive: ordering coffee in Lisbon, asking for directions in Seoul, joking with a neighbor in Mexico City. You won't need to be a grammar expert to join us. We'll break down phrases, explain the reasoning behind them in plain language, and share the mistakes we've made so you can skip a few of your own.

Our first season covers the basics across four languages: Spanish, French, Japanese, and Swahili. Every episode includes a short lesson, a listening practice segment, and a challenge you can try during your week. Whether you're starting from zero or brushing up on something you studied years ago, there's a place for you here.

New episodes drop every Thursday. Subscribe wherever you listen, and tell us which language you're learning. We'd love to hear where your words take you.
