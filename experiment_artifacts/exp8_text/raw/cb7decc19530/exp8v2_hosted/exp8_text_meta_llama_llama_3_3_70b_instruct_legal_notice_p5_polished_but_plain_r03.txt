The shared network is provided for the legitimate use of authorized individuals. Users are expected to utilize the network in a responsible and ethical manner, respecting the rights and privacy of others. The network may not be used for any purpose that is unlawful, malicious, or disruptive to the normal operation of the network or the activities of other users. This includes, but is not limited to, the transmission of unsolicited or fraudulent communications, the distribution of viruses or other malicious software, and the unauthorized access or attempted access to systems, data, or other network resources. Users are also prohibited from using the network to harass, intimidate, or otherwise infringe upon the rights of others. By using the shared network, users acknowledge that they have read, understand, and agree to comply with these acceptable use guidelines, and that failure to do so may result in the suspension or termination of their access to the network.
