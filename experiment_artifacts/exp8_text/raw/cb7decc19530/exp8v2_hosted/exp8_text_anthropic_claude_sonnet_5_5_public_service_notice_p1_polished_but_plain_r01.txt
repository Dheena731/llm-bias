Notice to Library Patrons: Updated Opening Hours

Beginning the first Monday of next month, the Riverside Public Library will operate on a revised schedule designed to better serve our community. We have listened to feedback from residents and adjusted our hours to offer more access during the times people have told us they need it most.

The library will be open from nine in the morning until eight in the evening on Monday through Thursday. On Friday, doors will open at nine and close at five. Saturday hours will run from ten in the morning until four in the afternoon, and the library will be open on Sunday from one until five. Holiday closures will be posted at the front desk and on our website at least two weeks in advance.

Please note that the children's room and the computer lab will close thirty minutes before the main library each day, so that staff can complete shelving and equipment checks. Book returns will remain available around the clock through the outdoor drop box beside the main entrance, and items placed there will be checked in on the next business day.

If you are planning a visit for a specific service, such as meeting room reservations, notary assistance, or research help from a reference librarian, we recommend calling ahead. Some of these services are offered by appointment only and may not be available during the final hour of each day.

We appreciate your patience as we make this transition, and we welcome your comments about the new schedule. Staff at the front desk are happy to answer questions in person or by telephone during opening hours. Thank you for continuing to make the library a vital part of our neighborhood.
