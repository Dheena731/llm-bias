**Cookie Usage Notice**

This notice applies to all visitors and users of our website. By accessing or interacting with our site, you acknowledge and agree to the cookie practices described below.

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that enable us to recognize your browser, maintain your session, and remember your preferences. We utilize cookies in three defined categories. Strictly necessary cookies provide core functionality, including security verification, system stability, and transaction processing. These cookies operate automatically and cannot be disabled without impairing essential services. Analytical cookies gather aggregated, anonymized metrics about traffic volume, page engagement, and technical performance to support site maintenance and improvement. Preference and marketing cookies record your choices and monitor browsing patterns across platforms to deliver customized content and advertising aligned with your interests.

We do not use cookies to track personal information across third-party websites unless you have expressly permitted this practice. Non-essential cookies activate only after you provide affirmative consent through our cookie preference center, located in the footer of every page. You may modify or withdraw your consent at any time by returning to this center. Alternatively, you may configure your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Be advised that restricting cookies may limit feature availability or alter expected site behavior.

This notice reflects our current practices and will be updated as technology or legal standards evolve. Revised versions remain posted on this page without separate alert. Direct any questions regarding this notice to our privacy department through the official contact form on our website.
