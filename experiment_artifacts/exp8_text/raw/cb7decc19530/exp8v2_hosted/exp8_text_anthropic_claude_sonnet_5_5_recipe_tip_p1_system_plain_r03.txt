When you get home on a weeknight, start the slowest part of dinner first and build everything else around it. Put a pot of water on to boil or slide a tray of chopped vegetables into a hot oven before you do anything else. While that works, you can prepare the faster elements, like a simple sauce, a protein that cooks in ten minutes, or a quick salad. Cutting your vegetables into small, even pieces helps them roast in twenty minutes rather than forty, and a hot oven at around 425 degrees gives them nice browning without much attention. Finish the meal with something bright, such as a squeeze of lemon or a spoonful of good vinegar, which makes even a plain dinner taste thoughtfully made. If you cook a little extra rice or roasted vegetables, you will also have a head start on tomorrow night.
