All employees are reminded to be cautious when receiving emails that ask for sensitive information. These emails, known as phishing emails, may appear to be from a legitimate source but are actually attempts to deceive and obtain confidential information.

Phishing emails often contain spelling and grammar errors, and may ask you to verify or provide personal or financial information. Never respond to these emails or click on any links they may contain. If you receive an email that asks for sensitive information, report it to the IT department immediately.

The IT department will work to verify the authenticity of the email and will notify you if it is legitimate. In the meantime, do not respond to or interact with the email in any way. Remember, if an email seems suspicious or asks for information, it is best to err on the side of caution and seek guidance from the IT department.
