**OFFICE OF THE REGISTRAR OF VOTERS**  
**NOTICE OF POLLING PLACE ASSIGNMENTS | MAY 7 SPECIAL ELECTION**

Electors must cast ballots at their assigned precinct on Election Day. Due to ongoing municipal facility renovations, two polling locations have been relocated for this cycle only:

* **Precinct 04-B:** Relocated from the Community Center to the **Fire Station No. 3 Training Annex** (612 E. Lincoln Ave. Entrance via the north bay door).
* **Precinct 11-A:** Relocated from St. Mark’s Hall to **Oakridge Middle School** (Auxiliary Gymnasium, Gate C off 4th Street).

All other precinct designations remain unchanged from the November general register. 

Polling locations open at 6:30 a.m. and close at 7:30 p.m. Voters standing in line at 7:30 p.m. will be permitted to vote. Accessible voting machines and curbside ballot intake will be operational at all sites.

To confirm your precinct assignment or check individual registration status, consult the street directory index at **elections.county.gov/lookup** or contact the elections supervisor at (555) 019-8240.
