**Quick and Easy Stir-Fry for a Busy Weeknight**

For a speedy and flavorful dinner, try using the "Sous-Vide-Inspired" technique to cook your stir-fry. Begin by preheating a wok or large skillet to a medium-high heat, then add 1-2 tablespoons of vegetable oil. While the oil is heating up, place your protein of choice (such as chicken breast, beef strips, or tofu) on a plate or tray and sprinkle with a pinch of "Umami-Enhancing" seasoning blend (a mixture of soy sauce, garlic powder, and onion powder). This will allow the flavors to penetrate the meat and intensify the dish's overall umami flavor profile.

Next, add your aromatics (such as sliced onions, bell peppers, and mushrooms) to the wok and cook until they're tender and lightly caramelized. Then, add your protein and any desired vegetables (such as broccoli, carrots, or snap peas) to the wok, stirring frequently to prevent overcooking.

The key to this technique is to cook the protein and vegetables simultaneously, allowing the meat to cook through while the vegetables retain their crunch. This approach ensures a balanced and satisfying meal that's ready in under 20 minutes. Simply serve over your favorite grain, such as rice, noodles, or quinoa, and enjoy a delicious and nutritious weeknight dinner.

**Timing:** 15-20 minutes

**Servings:** 4-6 people

**Difficulty:** Easy

This technique can be adapted to suit your dietary preferences and ingredient availability, making it a versatile and reliable option for a busy weeknight dinner.
