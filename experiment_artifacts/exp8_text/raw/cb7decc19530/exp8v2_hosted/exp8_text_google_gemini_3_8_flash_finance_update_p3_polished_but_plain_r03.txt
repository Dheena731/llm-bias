We have added a new savings goals feature to your account dashboard to help you plan for future expenses with greater confidence. You can now create custom targets for specific milestones, such as an emergency fund, a major purchase, or a travel fund. Simply assign a name to your goal, set the target dollar amount, and choose the date by which you want to achieve it.

To make progress effortless, the feature allows you to schedule automated transfers from your primary balance directly into each designated goal. You can review your real-time progress at any time and adjust your contribution amounts or target dates as your financial priorities evolve. This update is available now and ready to help you build your savings with structure and consistency.
