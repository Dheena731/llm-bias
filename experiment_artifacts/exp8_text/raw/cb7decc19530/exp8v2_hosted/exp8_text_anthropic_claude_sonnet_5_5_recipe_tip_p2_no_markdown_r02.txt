Tip for storing fresh herbs: Treat tender herbs like parsley, cilantro, and mint as you would a bouquet of flowers. Trim the stems, place them upright in a jar with an inch of water, loosely cover the leaves with a plastic bag, and keep the jar in the refrigerator. Change the water every couple of days, and the herbs should stay fresh for up to two weeks. For sturdier herbs like rosemary and thyme, wrap them in a slightly damp paper towel and store them in a resealable bag in the fridge. Basil is the exception, since cold air turns its leaves black, so keep it in a jar of water on the counter away from direct sunlight.
