**PUBLIC NOTICE: HEAT ADVISORY IN EFFECT**

A Heat Advisory has been issued for the area, effective from 11:00 AM to 8:00 PM today. High temperatures and elevated humidity levels are expected to create dangerous heat index values reaching up to 105 degrees Fahrenheit. 

Extreme heat significantly increases the risk of heat-related illnesses, such as heat exhaustion and heat stroke.

**Recommended Precautions:**
* **Stay Hydrated:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day, even if you do not feel thirsty. Avoid alcohol and excessive caffeine.
* **Limit Sun Exposure:** Reschedule strenuous outdoor activities to early morning or late evening hours.
* **Stay Indoors:** Remain in air-conditioned buildings as much as possible. 
* **Check on Others:** Monitor the elderly, young children, and those with underlying health conditions. 
* **Vehicle Safety:** Never leave children or pets unattended in vehicles for any amount of time.

If you or someone you know shows signs of heat exhaustion (dizziness, heavy sweating, nausea), move to a cool place immediately. If you suspect heat stroke (high body temperature, confusion, loss of consciousness), call 911 immediately.

For a list of local public cooling centers, please contact local emergency management or visit the city website.
