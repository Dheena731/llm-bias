**DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS | SOLID WASTE & RECYCLING DIVISION**  
**MEMORANDUM**

**DATE:** October 28, 2024  
**TO:** Route 4 Curbside Customers (South of Baseline Road)  
**SUBJECT:** Transition to Split-Stream Collection and Schedule Adjustment  

Effective **Monday, November 11, 2024**, processing guidelines at the regional Materials Recovery Facility (MRF) require a shift from single-stream to split-stream collection for all Route 4 addresses. 

Please review the following operational adjustments:

* **Separation of Fiber and Rigid Containers:** Paper and cardboard may no longer be mixed with containers. Blue rolling carts are now reserved strictly for clean rigid containers: aluminum/tin cans and plastic containers stamped #1, #2, and #5. All corrugated cardboard must be flattened, tied with natural twine in bundles no larger than 3' x 3', and placed beside the cart. Wet paper or loose cardboard will not be processed.
* **Glass Prohibited in Carts:** Due to high rates of compaction breakage degrading fiber bales, curbside glass collection is suspended. Glass containers must be brought to the self-serve collection bunkers at the East Transfer Station.
* **Collection Day Shift:** Route 4 collection days will move from Wednesday to **Tuesday**. Carts must be curbside by 6:00 AM with handles facing your property and at least 3 feet of clearance from parked vehicles.

**Enforcement:**  
Drivers will not tip contaminated bins. Carts containing film plastics (grocery bags, bubble wrap), food-soiled packaging, or glass will receive an orange non-collection notice and must be corrected before the following cycle.

Direct route inquiries to the Sanitation Dispatch Desk at (555) 019-2831.
