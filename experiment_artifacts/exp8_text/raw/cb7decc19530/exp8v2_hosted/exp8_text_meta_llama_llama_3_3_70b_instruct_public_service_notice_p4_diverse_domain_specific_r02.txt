**EXCESSIVE HEAT WARNING**

A prolonged period of extreme heat is expected to affect our area from Wednesday through Friday, with temperatures soaring to 105°F (40°C) and heat indices reaching as high as 115°F (46°C). The National Weather Service has issued a Heat Advisory, warning of potentially life-threatening conditions, especially for vulnerable populations such as the elderly, young children, and those with pre-existing medical conditions.

To stay safe, please take the following precautions:

* Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water and electrolyte-rich beverages
* Avoid strenuous outdoor activities, especially during the hottest part of the day (11am-3pm)
* Seek shade or air-conditioned spaces to cool off
* Check on neighbors, friends, and family members who may be at risk
* Never leave children or pets unattended in vehicles

 Cooling centers will be available at local libraries and community centers for those in need. For more information, including a list of cooling center locations and heat-related safety tips, visit our website or call (555) 123-4567.

Stay cool, stay safe!
