**Rinse thoroughly before cooking:** 

Place your rice in a fine-mesh strainer and rinse it under cold running water until the water runs completely clear. This washes away the excess surface starch that causes the grains to become gummy and clump together. 

*Bonus tip:* Once cooked, let the rice rest covered off the heat for 10 minutes, then fluff it gently with a **fork** instead of a spoon to keep the grains separate.
