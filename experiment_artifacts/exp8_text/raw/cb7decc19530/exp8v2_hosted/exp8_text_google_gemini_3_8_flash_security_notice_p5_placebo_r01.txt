**Notice: Reporting a Lost Company Laptop**

If you misplace or lose your company-issued laptop, you must report the incident immediately. Prompt reporting is essential to protect company information and prevent unauthorized access to internal systems.

Please take the following steps as soon as you discover the device is missing:

1. Contact the IT support team and inform your direct manager right away.
2. Provide any relevant details, including when and where the laptop was last seen.
3. If you suspect the laptop was stolen, file a report with local law enforcement and notify the IT support team.

Do not delay notification. Timely reporting allows technical staff to remotely lock the device and secure company data.
