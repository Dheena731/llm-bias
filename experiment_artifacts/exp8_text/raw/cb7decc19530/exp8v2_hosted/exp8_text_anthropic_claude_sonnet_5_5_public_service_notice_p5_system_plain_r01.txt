Notice of Polling Station Locations

Residents are advised that polling stations for the upcoming election will be open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on Election Day. Your assigned polling station is printed on the voter information card mailed to your registered address. Voters are encouraged to vote at their assigned location, as ballots and voter lists are prepared for each station individually.

Several locations have changed since the last election. Voters in the northern district will now cast their ballots at the Riverside Community Center on Maple Avenue, which replaces the old library branch. Voters in the central district will continue to use City Hall, and those in the southern district will vote at Lincoln Elementary School. All three sites are wheelchair accessible and have reserved parking for voters with limited mobility.

If you are unsure of your assigned location, or if you have not received your voter information card, please contact the County Elections Office or check the official elections website before Election Day. Staff can confirm your registration and direct you to the correct station. Please bring a valid form of identification when you go to vote.

Thank you for taking part in the democratic process.
