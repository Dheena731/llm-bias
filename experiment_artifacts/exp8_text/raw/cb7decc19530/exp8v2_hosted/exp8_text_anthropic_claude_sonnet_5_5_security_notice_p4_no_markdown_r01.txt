NOTICE: Please Enable Two-Factor Authentication

To help keep your account secure, we strongly encourage all users to turn on two-factor authentication (2FA). This adds an extra layer of protection by requiring a second form of verification, such as a code sent to your phone or generated by an authenticator app, in addition to your password.

How to enable it:

1. Sign in to your account and open Settings.
2. Select Security, then choose Two-Factor Authentication.
3. Pick your preferred verification method: an authenticator app or text message.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to link your device.
5. Save your backup codes in a safe place in case you lose access to your device.

Why it matters: Passwords alone can be stolen or guessed. With 2FA enabled, an unauthorized person cannot access your account without also having your verification device.

The setup takes only a few minutes. Please complete it by the end of the week. If you need help, contact our support team.

Thank you for helping us keep your information safe.
