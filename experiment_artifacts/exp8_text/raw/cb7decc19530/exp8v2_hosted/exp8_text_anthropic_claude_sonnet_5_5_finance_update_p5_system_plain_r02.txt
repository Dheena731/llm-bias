Subject: Update on Delayed Payment Processing

We want to let you know that some payments are currently taking longer than usual to process. A technical issue with one of our payment partners began earlier this week and has slowed the confirmation of transactions. Payments are not being lost, and no one has been charged twice as a result of this delay.

Our team is working closely with the partner to resolve the problem. At this time we expect processing times to return to normal within the next two business days. Payments submitted during the delay will be completed in the order they were received, and you do not need to resubmit anything. Resubmitting could cause duplicate charges, so we ask that you wait until the original payment clears.

If your payment is time-sensitive, such as one tied to a deadline or due date, please contact our support team and give us your transaction reference. We will note the delay on your account so that no late fees or penalties are applied. Refunds for any confirmed errors will be issued promptly.

We apologize for the inconvenience and appreciate your patience. We will send another update as soon as the issue is fully resolved, or sooner if the timeline changes.
