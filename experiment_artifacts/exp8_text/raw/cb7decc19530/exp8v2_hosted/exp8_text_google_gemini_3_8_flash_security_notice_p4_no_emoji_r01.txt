**Subject: Action Required: Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**

To enhance the security of your account and better protect your data, all users are required to enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) by [Date]. 

2FA adds an essential layer of defense by requiring a secondary verification code in addition to your password when signing in.

**How to set it up:**
1. Log in to your account and go to **Settings**.
2. Select the **Security** tab.
3. Click **Enable Two-Factor Authentication** and follow the on-screen prompts to link your authenticator app or phone number.

Failure to enable 2FA by the deadline may result in restricted account access. If you experience any issues during setup, please contact IT Support at [Contact Information].

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our systems secure.
