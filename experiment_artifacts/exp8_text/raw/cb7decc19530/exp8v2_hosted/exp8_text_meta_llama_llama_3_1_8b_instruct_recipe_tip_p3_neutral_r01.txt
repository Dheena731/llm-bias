Here's a short cooking tip for making rice less sticky:

**Use the Right Water Ratio and Cooling Method**

To prevent sticky rice, it's essential to use the right water ratio and cooling method. Here's a simple trick:

1. **Use a 1:1.25 to 1:1.5 water-to-rice ratio**: For every 1 cup of rice, use 1.25 to 1.5 cups of water. This ratio helps to cook the rice evenly and prevents excess moisture from making it sticky.
2. **Don't overmix**: After cooking, fluff the rice with a fork to separate the grains. Don't overmix or stir the rice too much, as this can cause it to become sticky.
3. **Cool the rice quickly**: Once cooked, spread the rice out in a shallow dish or tray to cool it down quickly. This helps to stop the cooking process and prevents the rice from becoming sticky.

By following these simple tips, you'll be able to achieve perfectly cooked, less sticky rice every time!
