To improve account security, we are requiring all users to enable two-factor authentication. This measure adds an extra layer of safety by asking for a verification code along with your password whenever you log in. You can configure this feature using a mobile authenticator app or through text messages sent to your phone. Please take a moment to activate two-factor authentication on your account today. Accounts without this security setting will lose access after Friday unless updated. For help with the setup, contact the technical support desk at extension 5550. We appreciate your cooperation in keeping our systems secure.
