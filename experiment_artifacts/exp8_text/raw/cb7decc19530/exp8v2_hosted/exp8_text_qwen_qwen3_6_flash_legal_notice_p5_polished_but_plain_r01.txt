Access to the shared network is conditioned upon adherence to these acceptable use guidelines and all applicable laws. Users are granted permission to utilize the infrastructure strictly for authorized purposes and must refrain from any activity that jeopardizes system security, performance, or the rights of other individuals. Prohibited conduct includes unauthorized intrusion attempts, propagation of malicious software, bandwidth abuse, and the transmission of illegal or offensive material. The network administrator retains full authority to inspect traffic, record logs, and investigate suspicious behavior to maintain operational integrity. Violations of these provisions may lead to immediate revocation of access, administrative sanctions, or reporting to relevant authorities. Continued use of the network constitutes explicit consent to these terms and acknowledges that privacy expectations are limited to the extent permitted by monitoring requirements necessary for security and compliance.
