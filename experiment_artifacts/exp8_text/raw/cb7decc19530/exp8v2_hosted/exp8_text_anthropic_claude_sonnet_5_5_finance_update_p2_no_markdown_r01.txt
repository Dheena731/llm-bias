Subject: Update to Card Transaction Fees

Dear Customer,

We are writing to let you know about an upcoming change to the fees we charge for card transactions. Beginning on the first day of next month, the fee for each card payment will change from 1.5 percent to 1.8 percent of the transaction amount.

Why we are making this change: The fees that card networks and payment processors charge us have increased over the past year. We have absorbed these costs for as long as we reasonably could, but we now need to pass a small portion on to keep our services reliable and secure.

What this means for you: For a typical purchase of 100 dollars, the fee will rise from 1.50 dollars to 1.80 dollars. Fees for bank transfers and cash payments are not changing. If you would like to avoid the new card fee, you can switch to a bank transfer in your account settings at any time.

If you have questions or would like help reviewing your payment options, please contact our support team by phone or email. We are happy to walk you through the details.

Thank you for your continued trust and business.

Sincerely,
The Customer Support Team
