This is a friendly reminder to book your annual health checkup. It has been a while since your last visit, and a routine exam is one of the simplest ways to catch small concerns before they become bigger ones. Call your doctor's office or use their online scheduling system to pick a time that works for you, and try to choose an appointment early in the day if you will need to fast for bloodwork. Gather a list of any medications you take, questions you want to ask, and any changes in your health since your last appointment. It only takes an hour or so, and it is a worthwhile investment in your long-term well-being. Please make the appointment this week so it does not slip off your list.
