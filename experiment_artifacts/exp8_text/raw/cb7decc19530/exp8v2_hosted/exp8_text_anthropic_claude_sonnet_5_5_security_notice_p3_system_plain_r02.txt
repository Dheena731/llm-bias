Reminder: Please Lock Your Screens

Dear colleagues,

This is a friendly reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if it is only for a moment. An unlocked workstation gives anyone nearby access to your files, email, and our internal systems, and it can put confidential information about our clients and our company at risk.

Locking your screen takes only a second. On a Windows computer, press the Windows key and L together. On a Mac, press Control, Command, and Q. Please also make sure your device is set to lock automatically after a few minutes of inactivity, as a safety net in case you forget.

Thank you for helping us keep our information secure. If you have any questions or need help adjusting your settings, please contact the IT help desk.

Kind regards,
Office Administration
