The library's operating hours are as follows:

Monday through Thursday, the library is open from 9:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. On Fridays, it is open from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. The library is closed on weekends and holidays.

Patrons are advised to check the library's website or call the information desk to confirm the hours before planning a visit. It is also recommended that visitors check the library's website for any special hours or closures due to inclement weather, construction, or other unforeseen events.

During regular operating hours, the library's facilities are available for use, including study areas, computer workstations, and the reading room. The library's collections and databases are available for research and borrowing during these hours.
