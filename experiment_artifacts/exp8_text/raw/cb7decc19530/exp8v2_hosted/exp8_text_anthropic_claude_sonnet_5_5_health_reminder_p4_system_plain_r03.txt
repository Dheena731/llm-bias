A quick reminder to give your eyes and mind a rest. Every twenty minutes or so, look away from your screen and focus on something at least twenty feet away for about twenty seconds. Stand up, stretch your shoulders and neck, and take a few slow breaths. If you can, walk to a window or grab a glass of water. These small pauses help reduce eye strain, ease tension in your body, and keep your thinking clear, so you will likely get more done when you return.
