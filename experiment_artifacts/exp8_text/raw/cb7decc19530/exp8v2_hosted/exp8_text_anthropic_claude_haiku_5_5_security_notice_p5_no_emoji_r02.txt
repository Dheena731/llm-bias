**Notice: How to Report a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If you have lost a company laptop or believe it has been stolen, you must report it immediately. Prompt reporting helps protect company data and reduces security risks.

**What to do:**

1. **Report it right away.** Contact the IT Service Desk as soon as possible, and no later than 24 hours after you notice the laptop is missing.
2. **Provide details.** Include the laptop's asset tag number, serial number, make and model, and the last time and place you remember having it.
3. **Notify your manager.** Let your direct supervisor know about the loss.
4. **File a police report if stolen.** If the laptop was stolen, report it to local law enforcement and provide the police report number to IT.
5. **Do not attempt to recover it yourself.** Leave any recovery or investigation to the appropriate authorities.

**What IT will do:**

- Lock or remotely wipe the device to protect company data.
- Revoke access to company systems linked to the laptop.
- Update the asset inventory and record the incident.

**Contact:** IT Service Desk, ext. 4357, or servicedesk@company.com

Failure to report a lost or stolen device promptly may result in disciplinary action under company policy.
