**TRAFFIC ADVISORY / TEMPORARY CLOSURE**  
**ISSUING AGENCY:** District 4 Division of Highways  
**PROJECT REF:** S-24-CULV-09  

**Location:**  
Route 112 (Blackwood Road) between Milepost 14.6 (Cedar Creek Crossing) and the intersection of State Line Road.

**Dates & Timing:**  
06:00 Monday, April 14 through 18:00 Thursday, April 17.  
*Continuous 24-hour closure. Work is weather-dependent.*

**Operational Scope:**  
Full-depth pavement reclamation and installation of a precast concrete box culvert to replace failing corrugated metal drainage at Station 77+20. 

**Traffic Control & Access:**  
* **Through Traffic:** Closed in both directions. No drivable surface or steel plating will be present across the cut during active excavation.  
* **Detour:** Non-local traffic will follow marked detour via State Route 9 south to County Line Road, returning north via Old Post Highway (adds approximately 6.2 miles). Heavy freight vehicles exceeding 5 axle combinations must utilize the designated Class I truck bypass on Interstate 84.  
* **Local & Emergency Access:** Residents with properties between Milepost 14.0 and 14.5 must approach from the western closure point. Properties north of Station 77+20 must enter from State Line Road. Emergency dispatchers have been provided with dead-end turnaround coordinates; no cross-trench passage is available for rescue vehicles.
