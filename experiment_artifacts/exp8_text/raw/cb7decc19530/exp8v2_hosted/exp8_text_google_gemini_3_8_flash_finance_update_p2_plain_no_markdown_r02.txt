We are writing to inform you of an upcoming update to our card transaction fees, which will take effect on the first of next month. Due to recent increases in processing and network costs, transactions completed with credit and debit cards will now be subject to an updated processing fee of two percent. This change allows us to maintain secure payment processing systems and cover the rising administrative expenses associated with card networks. Alternative payment methods, such as direct bank transfers, will continue to be processed without any additional fees. If you have any questions regarding this change or would like assistance in adjusting your preferred payment method, our support team is available to assist you. We appreciate your understanding and continued business.
