Here is a short, clear template you can use for an email or Slack/Teams announcement:

***

**Subject:** Action Required: Transition to New VPN Client on [Date]

Hi Team,

To improve our network security and connection speeds, we are switching our VPN client from **[Old VPN Name]** to **[New VPN Name]** on **[Date of Change]**. 

**What you need to do:**
1. **Download/Install:** Follow the setup guide here: **[Link to Instructions]** (or: *The new client has been automatically pushed to your device*).
2. **Log In:** Open **[New VPN Name]** and sign in using your standard company credentials by **[Deadline/Time]**.
3. **Uninstall Old Client:** Once connected, you may delete the old VPN client.

*Note: Access through the old VPN will be permanently disabled on [Date].*

If you run into any issues, please reach out to the IT Helpdesk at **[Email/Slack Channel]**.

Thank you for your cooperation!

Best regards,  
**[IT/Security Team Name]**
