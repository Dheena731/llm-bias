To store fresh herbs, place them in a sealed container or plastic bag to maintain moisture and prevent wilting. Keep the container away from direct sunlight and heat sources, as these can cause herbs to dry out and lose their flavor. Store the container in the refrigerator at a temperature of around 40 degrees Fahrenheit. Herbs such as basil and mint can be stored with the stems in water, while those like parsley and cilantro are best stored wrapped in a damp paper towel. Check the herbs regularly and discard any that are wilted or show signs of decay.
