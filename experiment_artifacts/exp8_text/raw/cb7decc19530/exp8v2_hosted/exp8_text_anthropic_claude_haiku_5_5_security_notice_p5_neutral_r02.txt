**Notice: How to Report a Lost or Stolen Company Laptop**

If you have lost a company laptop or believe it has been stolen, you must report it immediately. Prompt reporting helps protect company data and sensitive information.

**What to do:**

1. **Report it right away.** Contact the IT Help Desk at [phone number] or [email address] as soon as you notice the laptop is missing, ideally within one hour.
2. **Notify your manager.** Let your supervisor know what happened.
3. **File a police report if theft is suspected.** Obtain a copy of the report and provide it to IT and HR.
4. **Provide details.** Include the laptop's asset tag number, serial number, time and place of loss, and any files or accounts that may have been stored on the device.
5. **Do not attempt to recover or access the device yourself.** IT will take the necessary steps to secure company data remotely.

**Please note:** Failure to report a lost or stolen device promptly may result in disciplinary action under company policy.
