As part of our ongoing commitment to data security, please remember to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even if it is only for a brief moment. An unattended workstation leaves sensitive company and client information vulnerable to unauthorized viewing and potential security breaches. You can quickly secure your screen by pressing the Windows key and L on a PC, or Command, Control, and Q on a Mac. Cultivating this simple habit takes only a second and ensures our shared workspace remains safe for everyone. Thank you for your diligence in protecting our network.
