It's time for your weekly fitness check-in. Take a few minutes to look back over the past seven days and note how your workouts, activity levels, and recovery measured up against your goals. Consider how many sessions you completed, whether your effort felt sustainable, and if anything, such as sleep, nutrition, or a busy schedule, made it easier or harder to stay on track.

Celebrate what went well, even if the progress seems small, because consistency is what builds lasting results. If you fell short somewhere, treat it as useful information rather than a setback. Choose one or two realistic adjustments for the week ahead, such as scheduling workouts at set times or adding an extra rest day, and write them down so they are easy to follow. A brief review now will help you start the new week with clarity and momentum.
