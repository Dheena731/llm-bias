Introducing the Monthly Budget Planner: a free spreadsheet template that helps you see where your money goes and plan where it should go next.

The template is built for everyday use without any setup headaches. Enter your income once, list your recurring bills and expenses, and the sheet calculates your monthly totals, remaining balance, and percentage of spending in each category. A summary page updates automatically as you enter transactions, so you can check your financial position at a glance instead of digging through bank statements.

Three features set this template apart. First, a flexible category system lets you rename or add categories to match how you actually spend, whether that means groceries and transportation or something more specific like pet care or savings goals. Second, a twelve-month tracker shows how your spending changes over the year, which helps you anticipate seasonal costs like holiday gifts, insurance renewals, or back-to-school expenses. Third, built-in savings targets let you set a goal for each month and see whether you are on track.

The planner works in any major spreadsheet application, and no advanced formulas or financial background are required. Each section includes short instructions, and sample data shows how a completed month should look before you begin.

The Monthly Budget Planner is available now at no cost. Download it, enter your first month of figures, and see how much clearer your finances can look with a simple plan in place.
