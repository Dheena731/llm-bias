**Introducing Savings Goals: Plan Today, Reach Tomorrow**

We're excited to announce our new Savings Goals feature, designed to help you turn your financial dreams into reality.

With Savings Goals, you can:

- **Set a target** for anything you're saving toward, whether it's a vacation, an emergency fund, or a new home.
- **Choose a deadline** and let us calculate how much to set aside each week or month.
- **Track your progress** with a visual dashboard that shows how close you are to reaching each goal.
- **Automate your savings** by linking a recurring transfer so you can save without thinking about it.

Getting started takes less than a minute. Just open the app, tap "Goals" in the main menu, and create your first one.

We built this feature based on your feedback, and we'd love to hear what you think. Share your thoughts through the in-app feedback button or reach out to our support team.

Here's to your next milestone!
