**Public Notice: Updated Service Hours**  
**Effective Monday, November 4**

The Central Library will transition to winter building hours next week. 

* **Monday – Thursday:** 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM  
* **Friday & Saturday:** 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM  
* **Sunday:** 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM *(Ground floor, holds pickup, and public terminals only)*

**Daily Service Cutoffs:**
* **Public Terminals & Wi-Fi:** Public desktop sessions end and networked printers shut down 15 minutes prior to building closure. Unprinted jobs will not clear to the queue the following morning.
* **Returns:** The exterior walk-up drop box on Lincoln Avenue remains open 24 hours. Please do not deposit museum passes, interlibrary loans, or audiovisual equipment in the exterior drop; these must be returned directly to the circulation desk during open hours to clear your account.
* **Holds Pickup:** Held items are shelved by the last four digits of your patron barcode. Paged materials requested from off-site storage are delivered at 11:00 AM on weekdays and held for seven days.

For inquiries regarding the Local History Room or proctoring services, which operate on limited independent schedules, please contact the Reference Desk at ext. 204.
