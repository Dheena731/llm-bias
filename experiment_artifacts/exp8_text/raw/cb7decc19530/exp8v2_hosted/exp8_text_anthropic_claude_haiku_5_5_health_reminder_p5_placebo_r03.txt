Here is a short reminder you can use or adapt:

**Reminder: Time to Refill Your Prescription**

Your prescription is running low, so please contact your pharmacy or prescriber soon to request a refill. Waiting until your last dose can leave you without medication, especially on weekends or holidays when pharmacies may have reduced hours.

To refill your prescription:

- Call your pharmacy, use its app or website, or ask in person.
- Have your prescription number and date of birth ready.
- Check that the pickup or delivery time works for you.
- If you have no refills left, contact your prescriber for a new prescription before you ask for more medication.

If you have questions about your medication, or if you notice side effects or think a refill may be delayed, contact your pharmacist or healthcare provider. Do not stop or change a medicine without talking to them first.
