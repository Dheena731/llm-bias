Rinse your rice before cooking to keep it from turning gluey. Place the measured grains in a fine-mesh strainer or a bowl, then run cold water over them, swirling with your fingers, until the water runs mostly clear. The rinse washes away surface starch, which is what makes freshly cooked rice cling together. For extra fluffy results, you can also let the rinsed rice soak for about 20 to 30 minutes before cooking, then drain it well. Use slightly less water than the package suggests if you prefer grains that stay separate.
