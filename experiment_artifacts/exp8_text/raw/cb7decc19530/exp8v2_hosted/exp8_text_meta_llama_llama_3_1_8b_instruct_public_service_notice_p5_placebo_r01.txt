**Polling Station Locations**

To ensure the integrity and accessibility of the voting process, the following polling stations have been designated for the upcoming election:

*   **Station 1**: Located at 123 Main St, this polling station is wheelchair accessible and has a designated area for voters with disabilities. It will be staffed by trained election officials who can assist with voting procedures.
*   **Station 2**: Situated at 456 Elm St, this polling station offers a secure and private voting area. It is also equipped with electronic voting machines for voters who prefer a paperless experience.
*   **Station 3**: Found at 789 Oak St, this polling station is equipped with audio ballots and large-print voting materials for voters with visual impairments.
