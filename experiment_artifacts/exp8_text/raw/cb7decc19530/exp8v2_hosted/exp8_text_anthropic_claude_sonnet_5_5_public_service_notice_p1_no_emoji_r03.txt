**PUBLIC NOTICE: Library Opening Hours**

Please be advised of the following opening hours for all branches of the Municipal Library, effective the first of next month.

**Regular Hours**
- Monday to Thursday: 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
- Friday: 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
- Saturday: 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
- Sunday: Closed

**Holiday Closures**
The library will be closed on all public holidays. Book return slots remain available at every branch, day and night.

**Helpful Reminders**
- Borrowed items may be renewed online or by phone at any time.
- Study rooms may be reserved up to seven days in advance at the front desk.
- Last entry is 15 minutes before closing.

For questions or updates to these hours, please contact the main desk at 555-0142 or visit the library website.

Thank you for supporting your local library.

*Issued by the Office of Library Services*
