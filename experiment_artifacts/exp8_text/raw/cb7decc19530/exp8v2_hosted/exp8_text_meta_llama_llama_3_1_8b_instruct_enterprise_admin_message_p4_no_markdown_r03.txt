Quarterly Access Reviews

As a reminder, all users are required to undergo a quarterly access review to ensure their continued need for access to sensitive information and systems.

These reviews are a critical component of our security protocols and are designed to ensure that only authorized individuals have access to sensitive information and systems.

The review process typically involves a review of your current role and responsibilities within the organization, as well as a determination of whether your access to sensitive information and systems is still necessary.

If you are found to no longer require access to sensitive information and systems, your access will be removed and you will be notified of any necessary changes to your account permissions.

Please note that quarterly access reviews are mandatory for all users with access to sensitive information and systems, and failure to comply with the review process may result in the revocation of your access.

If you have any questions or concerns about the quarterly access review process, please do not hesitate to reach out to your administrator or security officer.
