We are thrilled to announce the launch of Word by Word, a new podcast for anyone who has ever stared at a menu in another language and wished they could simply read it. Whether you are starting from zero, dusting off the Spanish you studied in school, or preparing for a move abroad, this show was made with you in mind.

Each episode runs about fifteen minutes and focuses on one small, useful idea: a handful of phrases for ordering coffee, the trick behind a tricky verb ending, or the story of why a certain expression means what it does. We speak slowly at first, repeat often, and always explain the reasoning behind the language rather than just asking you to memorize it. You will hear conversations with native speakers, short listening exercises, and a few mistakes of our own, because we believe learning is easier when everyone admits how messy it can be.

Our first three episodes are available today wherever you listen to podcasts, and new ones will arrive every Tuesday. We would love to hear what languages and topics you want us to cover next, so send us a message and tell us where you are on your journey. Thank you for joining us at the very beginning. Press play, say the first word out loud, and let's get started.
