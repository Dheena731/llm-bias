NOTICE OF CHANGES TO ACCOUNT TERMS

This Notice informs you of changes to the terms that govern your account with us. Please read it carefully and keep a copy for your records.

Effective Date. The changes described in this Notice take effect thirty days after the date this Notice is sent to you (the "Effective Date"). Until the Effective Date, your existing account terms continue to apply without modification.

Summary of Changes. We are amending the Account Agreement to update our fee schedule, revise the procedures for resolving disputes, and clarify how we may communicate with you about your account. Specifically, the monthly maintenance fee will be adjusted as set out in the revised fee schedule enclosed with this Notice. The dispute resolution provision will now require that you first submit any claim to us in writing and allow sixty days for good-faith resolution before commencing formal proceedings. In addition, we may deliver legal and account notices by electronic means to the email address or secure message center associated with your account, unless you have requested paper delivery.

Your Right to Decline. You are not required to accept these changes. If you do not agree, you may close your account or opt out of the changes by contacting us in writing before the Effective Date. Closing your account will not result in any early closure fee, and you will remain responsible for any balances, charges, or obligations incurred before closure. Any opt-out request must identify the account holder and the account concerned, and must be received by us before the Effective Date to be effective.

Acceptance. If you do not close your account or notify us of your objection before the Effective Date, and you continue to use the account afterward, you will be treated as having accepted the revised terms. Continued use includes making deposits, withdrawals, payments, or transfers, and maintaining an open balance.

Effect on Existing Rights. These changes apply only going forward. They do not alter any rights or obligations that arose before the Effective Date, and they do not affect any dispute that you have already submitted to us in writing. Except as expressly amended, all other provisions of the Account Agreement remain in full force. If any provision of the revised terms is found to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions will continue to apply.

Conflicts. If this Notice conflicts with the Account Agreement, this Notice controls with respect to the changes described above. If it conflicts with applicable law, the law controls, and the affected provision will be read to comply with it.

Questions. If you have questions about this Notice or the revised terms, please contact our customer service department using the address or telephone number printed on your account statement. Please do not include sensitive personal information, such as full account numbers, in any email correspondence.

Thank you for choosing to bank with us.
