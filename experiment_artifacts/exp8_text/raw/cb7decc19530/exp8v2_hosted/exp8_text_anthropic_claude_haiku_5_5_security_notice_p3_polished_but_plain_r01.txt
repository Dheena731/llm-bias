Subject: Reminder to Lock Your Screen When Away From Your Desk

Dear colleagues,

This is a friendly reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your workstation, even for a few minutes. An unlocked screen can give anyone passing by access to confidential client information, internal documents, and our email and business systems, and it only takes a moment for someone to misuse that access.

Locking your screen is quick and easy. On Windows, press the Windows key and L together. On a Mac, press Control, Command, and Q together. If you are unsure how to set this up or would like your screen to lock automatically after a short period of inactivity, please contact the IT Help Desk and they will be glad to assist.

Thank you for helping us keep our information and our colleagues' work secure.

Kind regards,
The IT Department
