The easiest way to streamline lunches for a busy week is to prepare versatile base ingredients in advance rather than cooking full individual meals. Spend an hour cooking a large batch of grains, roasting a tray of mixed vegetables, and preparing a simple protein like chicken or beans. Store everything separately in the refrigerator so you can quickly assemble fresh salads, grain bowls, or wraps in the morning with different sauces and dressings, ensuring you save time while keeping your meals varied.
