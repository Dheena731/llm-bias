Thanks for sending over the poster draft. The core message is solid, and the data presentation is impressive. To make the poster even more effective for the conference environment, please consider the following adjustments:

Layout and Flow: The current arrangement makes it slightly difficult to follow the narrative arc. Reorganize the columns so the reader naturally progresses from the problem statement to methods, results, and implications. Adding subtle visual dividers or numbered sections will help guide the audience's eye.

Text Reduction: The introduction and background sections contain too much dense text. Condense these areas significantly using bullet points and bolded keywords within the sentences. Viewers typically spend only a few seconds per poster, so the main takeaway must be grasped immediately. Aim to cut approximately 30% of the words without losing essential context.

Readability and Visuals: Verify that the font size meets the minimum requirement for standing viewing distance. Body text should be no smaller than 24pt, and headings should be prominent. Ensure all graphs are colorblind-friendly and have legible axis labels. It would also be beneficial to add a single summary figure or infographic that captures the key finding at a glance.

Contact Information: Include a clear call-to-action near the conclusion. Make sure your email, QR code, and social media handles are placed in the footer where they are easily accessible.
