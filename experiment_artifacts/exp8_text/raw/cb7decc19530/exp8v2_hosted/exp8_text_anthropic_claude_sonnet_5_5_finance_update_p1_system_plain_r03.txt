We have added a monthly spending summary to your account. At the start of each month, you will receive a clear overview of where your money went during the previous one, so you no longer need to piece it together from individual transactions.

The summary shows your total spending for the month and how that total compares with the month before. It groups purchases into familiar categories such as groceries, transportation, dining, bills, and subscriptions, and it points out the categories that changed the most. If a category rose or fell noticeably, you will see that right away without having to dig for it.

The summary also lists your largest single purchases and any recurring charges it found, which makes it easier to spot subscriptions you forgot about or payments you no longer need. If a transaction has landed in the wrong category, you can correct it, and the summary will update to reflect your change.

The summary is generated automatically and will appear in your account on the first day of each month. You can also choose to receive it by email in your notification settings. We hope it gives you a quick, useful picture of your habits and makes planning the month ahead a little easier. If you have suggestions for improving it, we would like to hear them.
