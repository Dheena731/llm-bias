Please be reminded that using the same password across multiple accounts poses a serious security risk to our organization. When a password used for both a personal service and a work account is exposed in an external data breach, unauthorized individuals can easily use those credentials to access our internal systems and sensitive company information. Every employee is required to maintain a unique, complex password for their work profile that is not shared with any other platform. To simplify managing these credentials, the IT department provides an approved password manager. If you are currently reusing passwords across accounts, or if you suspect any of your login information has been compromised, please update your credentials immediately and contact the security team for assistance.
