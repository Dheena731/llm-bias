Introducing Smart Reminders: your tasks now find the right time to surface.

Instead of guessing when to check your to-do list, Smart Reminders learns from your daily habits and nudges you at the moments you're most likely to act. Set a goal once, and we'll handle the timing.

Smart Reminders is rolling out to all users starting today. Update the app to try it out, and let us know what you think in the comments.
