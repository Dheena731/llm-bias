To: All Staff

We have recently seen an increase in phishing emails being sent to our organization. These emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information, such as your login credentials or financial information.

To protect yourself and our organization, please be cautious when receiving emails that:

* Ask you to verify your account information or login credentials
* Contain suspicious links or attachments
* Create a sense of urgency or threaten consequences if you do not respond immediately

If you receive an email that you suspect is a phishing attempt, do not respond or click on any links. Instead, please forward the email to our IT department at [IT email address] for investigation.

Remember, our organization will never ask you to reveal sensitive information via email. If you are unsure about the legitimacy of an email, it is always best to err on the side of caution and verify its authenticity before responding.

Thank you for your vigilance in helping to protect our organization's security.
