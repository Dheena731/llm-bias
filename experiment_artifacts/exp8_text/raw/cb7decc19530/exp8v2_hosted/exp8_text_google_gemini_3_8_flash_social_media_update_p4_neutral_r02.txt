Game-changer calendar tip: Stop scheduling back-to-back meetings. 🛑

Go into your settings today and turn on **"Speedy Meetings"** (Google Calendar) or adjust your default end times (Outlook) to end meetings 5–10 minutes early. 

That tiny built-in buffer gives you time to:
☕ Grab water
📝 Jot down quick action items
🧠 Reset your brain before the next call

Tiny shift, massive difference for your focus. Your future self will thank you! 🙌
