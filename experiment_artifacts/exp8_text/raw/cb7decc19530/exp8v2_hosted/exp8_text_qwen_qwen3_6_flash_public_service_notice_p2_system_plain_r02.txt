This is to inform residents that Main Street will be closed to all vehicular traffic starting at six in the morning on Monday the tenth until five in the evening on Friday the fourteenth due to emergency water main repairs. Motorists are advised to use the detour route via Oak Avenue and Maple Drive to reach their destinations. Pedestrian access along the sidewalk remains open throughout the closure period. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your cooperation as we work to complete these essential repairs safely and efficiently. For further information, please contact the Department of Public Works at five five five zero one two three four.
