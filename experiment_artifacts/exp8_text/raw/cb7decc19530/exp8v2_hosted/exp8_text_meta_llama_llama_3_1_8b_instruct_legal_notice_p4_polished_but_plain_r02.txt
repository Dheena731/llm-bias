Refund Eligibility Notice

In accordance with our return and refund policies, eligible customers may be entitled to a refund for defective or unsatisfactory merchandise. To be eligible, a customer's item must be in a condition that is not suitable for use or resale, must be returned within a reasonable time frame, and must be accompanied by the original sales receipt or proof of purchase.

A refund will be issued in the original form of payment, minus any applicable restocking fees. Refunds will be processed within a certain number of business days after receipt of the returned merchandise. If a refund is denied, the customer will be notified in writing, stating the reasons for the denial.

Refund claims must be made in writing to our customer service department, and must include the customer's name, address, a description of the item, and the reason for the return. The customer is responsible for paying the cost of return shipping, unless otherwise specified.

Refund amounts will be calculated based on the original sale price of the item, minus any applicable discounts or promotions. A refund check will be mailed to the customer at the address on file, or a credit will be issued to the customer's original form of payment.

By purchasing from us, the customer acknowledges that they have read and understood these refund eligibility terms.
