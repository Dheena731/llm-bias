Subject: Procedure for Reporting a Lost Company Laptop

If a company laptop is lost or stolen, employees must report it to the IT Service Desk as soon as possible, and no later than two hours after the loss is discovered. Prompt reporting allows the company to secure its data, disable access to corporate systems, and meet any legal or contractual notification obligations. To report the loss, contact the IT Service Desk by phone or email and provide the laptop's asset tag number, the date and approximate time of the loss, the location where it was last seen, and a brief description of the circumstances. If the laptop was stolen, also file a police report and send a copy of the report number to the IT Service Desk. Do not attempt to locate, access, or recover the device yourself, and do not share your password or access details with anyone who contacts you about it. The IT team will remotely lock or wipe the device where possible, update your account credentials, and open a case for tracking. Employees who fail to report a lost device promptly may be subject to review under company policy. Questions about this procedure should be directed to the IT Security team.
