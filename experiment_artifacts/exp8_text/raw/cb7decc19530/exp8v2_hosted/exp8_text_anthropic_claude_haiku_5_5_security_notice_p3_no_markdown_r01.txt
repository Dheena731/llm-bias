Subject: Reminder to Lock Your Screens

Dear Colleagues,

This is a friendly reminder to lock your computer screen whenever you step away from your desk, even for a short time. Locking your screen takes only a moment and helps protect confidential company information, client data, and your own accounts from unauthorized access.

On Windows, press Windows key + L. On a Mac, press Control + Command + Q. Please make this a habit, including during meetings, breaks, and when leaving your workstation at the end of the day.

Thank you for helping us keep our information secure.
