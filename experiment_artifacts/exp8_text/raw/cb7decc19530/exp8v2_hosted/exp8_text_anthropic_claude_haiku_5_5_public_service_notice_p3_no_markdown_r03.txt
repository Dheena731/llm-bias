Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection

Please be advised that recycling collection schedules will change beginning Monday, March 3. Recycling will now be collected every other week on Tuesdays instead of weekly. Your next recycling pickup will take place on Tuesday, March 4, and your bin should be placed at the curb by 7:00 a.m.

Please note the following updates:

Recycling bins should be placed at the curb the evening before collection or by 7:00 a.m. on the scheduled day.

Only items accepted under the city's recycling guidelines should be placed in your bin. Plastic bags, loose plastic film, and food-contaminated containers should not be included.

Bins should be kept at least three feet apart and away from parked vehicles and mailboxes to allow safe collection.

Garbage and yard waste collection schedules remain unchanged.

For a full list of accepted materials, the updated collection calendar, or questions about these changes, please visit our website or contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567.

Thank you for helping us keep our community clean and sustainable.
