**Subject: Stanchion is live: scheduling built around a grooming van, not a salon**

Hi all,

Stanchion is now available for iPhone. It's a booking and route app for mobile groomers, and it came out of watching one groomer, Dana, run her Tuesday out of three apps and a sticky note on her dashboard.

What it does:

- **Books by coat, not by time slot.** Enter "doodle, 9 months since last cut" and it blocks 2.5 hours instead of 1.5. Breed and coat length set the defaults, and you can override them.
- **Counts the drive.** Appointments are sequenced so you're not crossing town twice. The gap between stops includes parking and the walk to the door.
- **Handles matting before you're on the porch.** Send the client a photo request the night before. If the coat is worse than described, the surcharge approval goes out by text and they tap yes or no while you unload.
- **Tracks vaccines.** Rabies expiry dates sit on each dog's profile, and the app won't confirm a booking that lands after one lapses.
- **Suggests rebooking intervals.** Poodles at 5 weeks, goldens at 8 to 10. Clients get a reminder with two open times, not a "we miss you!" blast.

It costs $19 a month with no per-booking fee. The first 60 days are free, and you can export your client list as a spreadsheet anytime.

Android is planned for spring. For now, it's iPhone only.

Download Stanchion on the App Store, or reply to this email. I read every one, and I'm usually in a parking lot between appointments when I do.
