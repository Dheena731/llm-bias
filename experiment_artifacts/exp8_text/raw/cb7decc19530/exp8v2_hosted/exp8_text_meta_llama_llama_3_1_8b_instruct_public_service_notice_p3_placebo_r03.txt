**NOTICE OF RECYCLING COLLECTION CHANGES**

**Effective Date:** March 1, 2023

**Residents of Oakwood Township:** Please take note of the upcoming changes to our recycling collection schedule.

**New Collection Day:** Our recycling collection day will now be every other Wednesday, starting on March 1, 2023. This change is intended to improve efficiency and reduce traffic congestion in our neighborhoods.

**Collection Time:** The collection time remains the same, between 6:00 AM and 12:00 PM.

**What to Recycle:** Please continue to recycle the following materials:

* Paper and cardboard products
* Glass bottles and jars
* Plastic bottles and containers
* Metal cans and foil

**Important:** Please do not place recyclables in your regular trash. Instead, use the designated recycling bins provided by the Township.

**Questions or Concerns:** If you have any questions or concerns about the recycling collection changes, please contact the Township's Public Works department at (555) 123-4567.

We appreciate your cooperation and participation in our recycling program.
