Hydration Alert

As you navigate your busy day, don't forget to prioritize your body's most essential nutrient: water. Every cell, tissue, and organ relies on it to function optimally. Aim to drink at least 8-10 cups (64-80 ounces) of water throughout the day, adjusting according to your activity level and climate.

Make it a habit to take a few sips:

* Upon waking to rehydrate after a night of sleep
* Before and after exercise or physical activity
* With each meal to aid digestion
* Mid-morning and mid-afternoon to stay focused and alert

Keep a refillable water bottle with you as a constant reminder to drink up and reap the benefits of proper hydration, from boosted energy to healthier skin. Cheers to a hydrated you!
