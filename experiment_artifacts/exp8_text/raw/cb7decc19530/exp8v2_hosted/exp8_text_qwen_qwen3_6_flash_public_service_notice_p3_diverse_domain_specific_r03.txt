**NOTICE: CURBSIDE RECYCLING SCHEDULE & MATERIAL UPDATES**  
Effective Monday, April 7  

Collection routes in Zones 2 and 3 have been resequenced to align with new Materials Recovery Facility intake windows. Adjustments apply to pickup days and acceptable contents.

**What’s changing:**  
• Paper, cardboard, and mixed containers now share a single curbside day. Previous staggered days are discontinued.  
• Plastic resin codes #3 through #7, rigid polystyrene foam, and all glass are removed from curbside collection. These items will not be processed at the current MRF and will result in missed pickups if placed in your bin.  
• Collection frequency for organics remains unchanged.

**What you must do:**  
• Divert glass, foam, and #3–#7 plastics to the County Transfer Station or accept drop-off locations listed on the district portal.  
• Rinse containers; food residue triggers automatic rejection.  
• Leave bin lids open and place cans inside, not next to, the container. Closed lids block optical sorters and delay processing.  
• Bins flagged for contamination will be tagged and left unemptied. Residents may request one re-pickup per quarter after correcting the load.
