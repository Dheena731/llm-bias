Tip: Prep your lunch building blocks on Sunday instead of making full meals. Cook a big batch of one grain (like rice or quinoa), roast a tray of vegetables, and prepare one protein (such as grilled chicken or boiled eggs). Then store them in separate containers. Each day, mix and match them with a different sauce or topping, like pesto, salsa, or a squeeze of lemon with olive oil. You get variety without cooking every day, and lunch takes only a couple of minutes to pack.
