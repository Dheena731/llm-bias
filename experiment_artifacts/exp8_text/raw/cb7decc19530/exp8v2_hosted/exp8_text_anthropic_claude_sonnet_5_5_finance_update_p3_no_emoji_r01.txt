**Introducing Savings Goals**

We've added a new Savings Goals feature to help you plan and track the things that matter most to you, whether that's an emergency fund, a vacation, or a down payment.

**What you can do:**

- **Create custom goals.** Name your goal, set a target amount, and choose a deadline.
- **Track your progress.** See at a glance how close you are with a clear progress bar and projected completion date.
- **Automate your savings.** Set up recurring transfers on a schedule that fits your budget.
- **Adjust as you go.** Change your target, timeline, or contribution amount at any time.

**How to get started:**

Open the app, select the Goals tab, and tap "New Goal." Setup takes less than a minute.

Savings Goals is available now to all users. Update to the latest version of the app to access it. We'd love to hear what you think, so please send us your feedback through the Help menu.
