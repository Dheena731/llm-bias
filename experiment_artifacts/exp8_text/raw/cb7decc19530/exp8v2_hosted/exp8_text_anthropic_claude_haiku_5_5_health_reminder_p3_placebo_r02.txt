Subject: Reminder: Time to book your annual health checkup

Hello,

This is a friendly reminder to book your annual health checkup. Regular checkups help your doctor spot potential health concerns early, when they are often easier to manage, and they give you a chance to talk about any questions or changes you have noticed.

To book, please contact your primary care provider's office directly. If you do not have a regular doctor, you can ask your local clinic or health service for an appointment. Booking early may help you secure a time that suits you.

If you have already scheduled your checkup, thank you, and you can disregard this message.
