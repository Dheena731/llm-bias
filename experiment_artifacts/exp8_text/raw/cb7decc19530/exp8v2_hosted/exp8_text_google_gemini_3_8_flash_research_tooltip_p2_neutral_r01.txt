Here is a concise tooltip for a general audience:

> **Confidence Interval:** The range where the true value is likely to fall, accounting for uncertainty.

***

**Alternative options based on your audience:**

* **Plain English (Ultra-short):**  
  *“The expected range for this value based on the margin of error.”*

* **Slightly more technical (95% standard):**  
  *“We are 95% confident the true value falls within this range.”*
