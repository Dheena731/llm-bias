We've just launched Saved Searches, a new way to get back to what matters most. Whenever you run a search you expect to repeat, you can now save it with a single tap and give it a name that makes sense to you. Your saved searches will appear right on your home screen, so you can jump straight to fresh results without retyping filters or keywords. You can also turn on alerts and we'll let you know when something new matches. Saved Searches is rolling out to everyone this week, so update your app to try it. We'd love to hear how it works for you, so tell us what you think in the comments.
