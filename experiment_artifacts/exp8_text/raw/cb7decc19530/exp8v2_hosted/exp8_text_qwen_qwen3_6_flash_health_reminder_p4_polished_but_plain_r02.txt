Consider pausing your current activity to grant your eyes and mind a brief respite. Prolonged exposure to digital screens frequently results in accumulated tension in the eyes, neck, and shoulders, alongside a decline in mental sharpness. Shift your attention to a point in the distance for roughly twenty seconds to allow your eye muscles to relax. Stand up to gently stretch your arms and legs, promoting blood flow, and inhale deeply to clear your thoughts. Even a minute spent stepping away can significantly reduce physical strain and restore focus, enabling you to resume your tasks with greater comfort and efficiency.
