🗓️ **My favorite calendar hack for avoiding meeting fatigue:**

Turn on **"Speedy Meetings"** in your settings (or manually set defaults to 25 and 50 minutes instead of 30 and 60). 

That automatic 5–10 minute buffer gives you time to:
☕ Grab water
📝 Jot down quick action items
🧠 Actually reset before the next call

Stop living in back-to-back chaos—your future self will thank you! ✨
