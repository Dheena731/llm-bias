**Introducing Your Monthly Spending Summary**

We're excited to announce a new feature: the Monthly Spending Summary. At the start of each month, you'll receive a clear overview of how you spent your money in the previous month.

**What's included:**
- **Total spending** across all categories
- **Breakdown by category**, such as dining, transportation, and shopping
- **Comparison to prior months**, so you can see how your habits are changing
- **Notable transactions**, including large or recurring charges

**Why it matters:**
The summary is designed to help you spot trends, identify areas where you might cut back, and stay on track with your budget goals, all without having to dig through individual transactions.

**Getting started:**
The summary is available automatically in your account under the Insights tab. You can turn off the monthly email notification at any time in Settings > Notifications.

Your financial data remains private and secure. The summary is visible only to you.

We hope this new tool helps you feel more in control of your finances. As always, we welcome your feedback, so let us know what you think.
