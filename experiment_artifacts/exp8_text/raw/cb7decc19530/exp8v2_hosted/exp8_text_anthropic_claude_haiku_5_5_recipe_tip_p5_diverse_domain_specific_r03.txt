Salt your vegetables right after they come out of the oven, not just before they go in. Heat drives off moisture and opens up the surface, so a pinch of flaky salt lands on something that's still hot and sticky with its own oil and sugars. Salt applied before roasting draws water out of the vegetables, which can steam them and dull the browning. Save the pre-roast salt for dense things like potatoes or carrot coins that benefit from it, and finish everything else with salt, a squeeze of lemon, and a little grated hard cheese while it's still steaming.
