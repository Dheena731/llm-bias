All registered voters are reminded that polling stations will operate from seven in the morning until eight in the evening on election day. Official locations can be accessed through the official state elections website or by calling the voter assistance hotline. Printed maps showing each precincts nearest polling place will be mailed to households two weeks before the election. Please bring a current photo identification and your voter registration confirmation card when you arrive. If you require translation services or have mobility accommodations, please contact the local elections office at least forty eight hours in advance. Thank you for taking part in the election process.
