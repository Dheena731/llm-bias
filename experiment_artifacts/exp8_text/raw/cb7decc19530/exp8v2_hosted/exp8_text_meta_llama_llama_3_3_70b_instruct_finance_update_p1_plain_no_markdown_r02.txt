We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you keep track of your expenses. This summary will be sent to you at the beginning of each month and will include a breakdown of your spending from the previous month. It will show the total amount you spent, as well as the categories where you spent the most money. This will allow you to see where your money is going and make adjustments as needed to stay within your budget. The summary will also include some suggestions for ways to reduce your spending and save money. We hope this new feature will be helpful in managing your finances.
