To: All Employees

As part of our ongoing efforts to protect our company's data and systems, we want to remind everyone to avoid reusing passwords across different accounts. 

Using the same password for multiple accounts, including personal ones, can increase the risk of a security breach. If a hacker gains access to one account that uses a reused password, they may be able to access other accounts that use the same password.

To minimize this risk, please ensure that you use unique and complex passwords for all of your accounts, both work-related and personal. You can use a password manager to generate and store complex passwords for you.

If you have any questions or concerns about password management, please speak with your supervisor or our IT department.
