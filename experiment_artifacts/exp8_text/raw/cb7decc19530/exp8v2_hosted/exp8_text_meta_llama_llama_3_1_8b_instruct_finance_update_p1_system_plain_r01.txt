We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you better understand your financial situation. This summary will provide a detailed breakdown of your income and expenses for each month. It will include categories for fixed costs such as rent and utilities, as well as discretionary spending like dining out and entertainment. By reviewing this summary, you will be able to see exactly where your money is going and make informed decisions about how to allocate your funds. The summary will be available on the first of each month, covering the previous month's activity.
