NOTICE OF ROAD CLOSURE

Due to scheduled maintenance and repairs, a section of Main Street will be closed to all traffic starting on Monday, March 21st and will remain closed until Friday, March 25th. The closure will be in effect from 7am to 5pm each day.

Motorists are advised to use alternative routes, such as Elm Street or Oak Avenue, to avoid the closure. Detour signs will be posted to guide traffic around the affected area.

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and appreciate your patience and understanding as we work to improve our infrastructure. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Public Works Department at 555-1234.
