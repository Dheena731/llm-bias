Salt your chicken thighs the night before, or at least forty minutes ahead, and leave them uncovered in the fridge. The salt pulls moisture to the surface, dissolves into it, and gets pulled back in, so the meat seasons all the way through instead of just tasting salty on the outside. The uncovered rest also dries the skin, which is what gets you a crackly sear in a hot pan without the usual splatter-and-steam routine. Nothing else on a Tuesday needs to change.
