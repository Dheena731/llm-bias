The City Department of Transportation advises residents and motorists that Main Street, between Oak Avenue and Maple Boulevard, will be completely closed to all through traffic starting Monday, October 14, at seven in the morning through Friday, October 18, at five in the afternoon. This temporary closure is required to facilitate emergency underground water main repairs and subsequent roadway resurfacing. During this period, local access for residents and emergency vehicles will be maintained, but all other drivers must follow the posted detour routes along Elm Street. Commuters should expect minor delays and are encouraged to plan alternate routes in advance. The city appreciates the patience and cooperation of the public as these critical infrastructure improvements are completed. For further inquiries, please contact the municipal public works office during normal business hours.
