Dear Account Holder,

To help keep your account secure, we strongly recommend turning on two-factor authentication. This added step asks you to confirm your identity with a second piece of information, such as a one-time code sent to your phone or generated by an authenticator app, whenever you sign in from a new device. Even if someone learns your password, they will not be able to access your account without that second step.

Enabling two-factor authentication takes only a few minutes. Sign in to your account, open the Security section in your account settings, and select the option to turn on two-factor authentication. Then follow the on-screen instructions to link your phone or authenticator app. Please save the backup codes you are shown, since they will let you regain access if you lose your device.

If you have any questions or need help with setup, please contact our support team, and we will be glad to assist you.

Thank you for helping us protect your account.

Sincerely,
The Security Team
