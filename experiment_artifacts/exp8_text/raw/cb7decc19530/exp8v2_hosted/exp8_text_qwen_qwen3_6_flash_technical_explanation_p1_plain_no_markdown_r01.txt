Latency represents the delay between a user performing an action and the system providing a response. This delay is critical in web applications because users have become accustomed to immediate feedback, and even small lags can significantly degrade the perceived performance of a site. When latency is high, page loads take longer, interactive elements respond sluggishly, and overall usability suffers, which often leads to user frustration and abandonment. High latency can also negatively affect search engine rankings, reducing organic traffic, and may increase server resource consumption as connections remain open for extended periods. By minimizing latency, developers ensure a smoother, more responsive experience that retains engagement and supports business goals.
