To keep fresh herbs lasting longer, treat them according to their type. For tender herbs like parsley, cilantro, and dill, trim the stem ends and stand the bunch in a jar with an inch of water, much like cut flowers. Cover the leaves loosely with a plastic bag and keep the jar in the refrigerator, changing the water every couple of days. For sturdier herbs like thyme, rosemary, and sage, wrap them in a slightly damp paper towel, place them in a container or resealable bag, and store them in the crisper drawer. Basil is the exception, since cold turns its leaves black, so keep it in a jar of water on the counter away from direct sunlight. Wait to wash any herbs until just before you use them, because extra moisture speeds up spoilage.
