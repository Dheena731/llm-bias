Please be aware that there has been an increase in phishing attempts targeting our organization recently. Phishing emails are fraudulent messages designed to trick recipients into revealing sensitive information or installing malware. To protect yourself, carefully review the sender's email address and look for generic greetings, urgent language, or requests for passwords. Be cautious of any attachments or links, even if the message appears to come from a colleague or known vendor. If you receive a suspicious email, do not reply, click on any links, or download attachments. Instead, forward the message to the IT security team immediately and delete the original email. Thank you for your vigilance in keeping our workplace secure.
