Here's a short cooking tip for preparing a weeknight dinner:

**Quick Tip: Prep Ahead with a "One-Pot" Meal**

On a busy weeknight, save time and stress by preparing a "one-pot" meal. Choose a recipe that cooks in a single pot, such as a skillet, Dutch oven, or large saucepan. This can be a pasta dish, a stir-fry, or a hearty soup. By prepping all the ingredients ahead of time, you can simply toss them into the pot and cook until done. This technique not only saves time but also reduces cleanup and allows you to focus on other tasks, like helping with homework or enjoying a glass of wine with dinner. Try it tonight and see how it simplifies your weeknight cooking routine!
