If you have misplaced or if a company laptop has been stolen, you are required to notify the IT Security team without delay. Contact the helpdesk via phone at extension 5550 or through the internal email address it-support@company.com. When submitting your report, please provide the full asset tag number, the serial number found on the device base, and the specific location where the laptop was last in your possession. Additionally, you must immediately change all relevant work passwords and inform your direct supervisor of the situation. Prompt reporting allows the technical team to initiate remote locking protocols and mitigate potential data breaches. Assistance with device replacement will be coordinated once the incident is logged.
