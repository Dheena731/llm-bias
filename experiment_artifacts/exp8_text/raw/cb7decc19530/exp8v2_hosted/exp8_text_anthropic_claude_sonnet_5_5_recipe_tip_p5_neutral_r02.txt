**Tip: Season twice.** Toss your vegetables with oil, salt, and pepper *before* roasting so the flavor gets into them and they brown well. Then, right when they come out of the oven, finish with a pinch of flaky salt, a squeeze of lemon juice or splash of vinegar, and fresh herbs. The first round builds depth, and the second makes the flavors taste bright.

Bonus: Use enough oil to coat the vegetables lightly, and spread them in a single layer so they roast instead of steam.
