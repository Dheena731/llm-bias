We are introducing a new expense approval workflow that will go live on Monday. This new process is designed to make it easier for employees to submit expenses and for managers to review and approve them. All expense reports will now need to be submitted through the online portal, where they will be automatically routed to the appropriate manager for approval. Employees will receive an email notification when their expense report has been approved or if any additional information is needed. If you have any questions about the new workflow, please don't hesitate to reach out to the finance department for assistance.
