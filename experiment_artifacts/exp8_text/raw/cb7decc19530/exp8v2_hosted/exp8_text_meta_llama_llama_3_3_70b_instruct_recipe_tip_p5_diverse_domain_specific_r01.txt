To elevate your roasted vegetables from mundane to magnificent, try seasoning them in two stages. First, toss the vegetables with a light hand of olive oil, salt, and your choice of aromatics (such as minced garlic, grated ginger, or chopped fresh herbs like thyme or rosemary) before roasting. This initial seasoning will help bring out the natural flavors of the vegetables as they caramelize. Then, just as the vegetables are finishing their roast, sprinkle a pinch of more assertive seasonings – perhaps a smoky paprika, a pungent sumac, or a bright, citrusy zest – to add depth and complexity to the dish. This two-stage seasoning approach will add layers of flavor to your roasted vegetables, making them a true standout on the plate.
