**Security Notice: Password Reuse**

**Date:** [Insert Date]
**From:** IT Security Team
**To:** All Employees

We have identified an increase in accounts compromised through reused passwords. When the same password is used across personal and work accounts, a breach at any one service can give attackers access to company systems.

**What you need to do:**

- **Use a unique password for every work account.** Do not reuse passwords from personal sites or from other work systems.
- **Use a password manager** approved by IT to generate and store strong, unique passwords.
- **Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA)** on all work accounts where it is available.
- **Never share your password** with colleagues, vendors, or anyone claiming to be IT support. IT will never ask for your password.
- **Report suspicious activity** immediately, including unexpected password reset emails or login alerts, to [Security Contact / Help Desk Email].

If you are unsure whether a password you use is reused, change it now and contact the help desk if you need assistance.

Thank you for helping keep our systems and information secure.

**IT Security Team**
