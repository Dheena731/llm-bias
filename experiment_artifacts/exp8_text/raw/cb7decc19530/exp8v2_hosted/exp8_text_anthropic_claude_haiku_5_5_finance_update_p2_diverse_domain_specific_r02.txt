**To:** Merchant account holders, Harbor Street Payments
**Re:** Change to processing fees for card-present sales, effective March 1

Starting with statements dated March 1, the processing rate on in-person card sales will go from 2.60% + $0.10 to 2.75% + $0.10. Online and keyed-in transactions are not changing.

Most of the increase comes from card network assessment fees, which Visa and Mastercard adjusted in January. Those fees are passed through to merchants at cost and are not a markup by us. The remaining 0.05% is our own change, which covers the expanded fraud screening we added to terminals last fall.

Some practical notes:

- If you run a high volume of small-ticket sales, the flat $0.10 is the bigger share of your cost. A $4 coffee now costs about $0.21 to process, up from $0.20.
- Refunds and voids are not charged a fee.
- Amex rates are set by American Express and are not affected by this change.
- If you want to see your effective rate on last month's batches, the statement breakdown in the dashboard now shows network fees on their own line.

If the change affects your margins enough to justify a conversation about pricing or a different terminal plan, call the merchant desk at 800-555-0147 before March 1 and we can run your last quarter's volume against both structures.
