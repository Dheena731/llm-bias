To keep fresh herbs vibrant for up to two weeks, treat them like cut flowers. Trim about half an inch off the stems, then place the bunch upright in a glass or jar with an inch or two of water, much as you would a bouquet. Cover loosely with a plastic bag and refrigerate, and change the water every few days. Hardy herbs such as rosemary, thyme, and sage do well this way, while basil and cilantro are best kept at room temperature away from direct sunlight, since cold air will blacken their leaves. Pick off any wilted or slimy leaves promptly so they don't spoil the rest.
