A heat advisory is in effect for the region today from noon until eight in the evening. Dangerously high temperatures and humidity levels are expected, which can significantly increase the risk of heat exhaustion and heat stroke. Residents are strongly advised to stay indoors in air-conditioned spaces as much as possible, drink plenty of water, and avoid strenuous outdoor activities during the hottest parts of the day. Never leave children or pets unattended in parked vehicles, as interior temperatures can become lethal within minutes. Check on elderly relatives, neighbors, and those without reliable home cooling. If you must work or spend time outside, take frequent breaks in shaded or cool areas and wear lightweight, loose-fitting clothing. Anyone experiencing symptoms such as severe dizziness, confusion, nausea, or fainting should seek immediate medical attention.
