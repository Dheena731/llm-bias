**NOTICE: Changes to Recycling Collection**

Effective Monday, the first of next month, recycling collection will move to a new schedule.

**What is changing**
- Recycling will be collected every other week instead of weekly.
- Collection day will move from Thursday to Tuesday for most households.
- Collection will begin at 6:00 a.m., so please set out your bins the night before.

**What is not changing**
- Trash collection remains on its current schedule.
- Accepted materials remain the same: clean paper, cardboard, glass bottles and jars, metal cans, and plastic containers labeled 1, 2, and 5.

**Reminders**
- Rinse all containers and remove food residue.
- Flatten cardboard boxes.
- Do not bag recyclables. Place items loose in the bin.
- Plastic bags, foam, and food-soiled paper are not accepted.

**Need more information?**
A printable collection calendar and a lookup tool for your address are available on the city website. For questions, contact Public Works at the number listed on your most recent utility bill.

Thank you for helping keep our community clean and our recycling program running smoothly.
