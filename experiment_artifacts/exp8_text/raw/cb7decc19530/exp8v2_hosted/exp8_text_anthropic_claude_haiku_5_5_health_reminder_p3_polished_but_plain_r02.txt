Friendly Reminder: Schedule Your Annual Health Checkup

It is that time of year again to book your annual health checkup. Regular checkups are one of the simplest ways to stay on top of your well-being, since they can catch potential concerns early, when they are often easier to manage. Your doctor can also review your vaccinations, discuss any changes in your medications, and help you set goals for healthy habits in the year ahead.

To schedule your appointment, call your primary care office or use the patient portal to find an open time that suits you. Appointments tend to fill up quickly during the busy seasons, so booking soon will give you more options. If you have questions about your coverage or what the visit will include, your office staff will be glad to help.

Taking an hour for your health now is a worthwhile investment in the months ahead. Please make the call this week.
