Here are a few options depending on your audience:

**Option 1: Standard (Recommended)**
> **Confidence Interval:** The range where the true value is likely to fall. Wider bands indicate more uncertainty in the data.

**Option 2: Non-technical (Plain English)**
> Shows the margin of error. A narrower range means the estimate is more precise.

**Option 3: Ultra-short (Mobile or tight spaces)**
> The expected range of the true value based on a [95]% certainty level.
