**Prep your aromatics first, then cook in one pan.**

Before you turn on the stove, chop your onion, garlic, and any vegetables, and set them in small piles on your cutting board in the order they will go into the pan. Heat a large skillet, sear your protein (chicken thighs, sausage, or shrimp work well), then remove it to a plate. In the same pan, cook the aromatics in the leftover browned bits, add a splash of broth, wine, or water to loosen them, and return the protein to finish cooking.

This approach builds deep flavor in under 30 minutes and leaves you with only one pan to wash. Serve over rice, pasta, or crusty bread to soak up the sauce.
