Here's a short cooking tip for preparing a weeknight dinner:

**"Searing chicken breasts with a flavorful twist: Use a mixture of oil and vinegar as a marinade, then add a sprinkle of Korean chili flakes (gochugaru) to the pan before searing. The acidity in the marinade will help break down the protein, while the chili flakes will add a depth of heat and a hint of smokiness. This trick will elevate your weeknight dinner from ordinary to extraordinary."**
