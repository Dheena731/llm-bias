To make a quick and delicious weeknight dinner, try preparing a one-pot pasta dish. Cook your favorite pasta, such as penne or linguine, along with some diced vegetables like cherry tomatoes and bell peppers, and your choice of protein like chicken or sausage, in a large pot of boiling water. Add some olive oil, garlic, and herbs for flavor, and season with salt and pepper to taste. This easy and efficient method allows you to cook everything in one pot, reducing cleanup and making it a great option for a busy weeknight dinner.
