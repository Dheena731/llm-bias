Friendly reminder: it's time to book your annual health checkup. Regular exams help catch small issues before they become bigger ones, and they give you a chance to talk with your doctor about any questions or concerns. Take a few minutes this week to call your clinic or schedule online, and choose a time that fits easily into your calendar. If you have recent test results, a list of medications, or any symptoms you want to discuss, jot them down beforehand so the visit goes smoothly. Looking after your health now is one of the simplest ways to protect your well-being for the year ahead.
