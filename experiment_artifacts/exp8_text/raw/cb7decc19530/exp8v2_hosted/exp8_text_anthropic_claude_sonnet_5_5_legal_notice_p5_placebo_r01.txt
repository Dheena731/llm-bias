# Notice of Acceptable Use: Shared Network

**1. Scope and Acceptance**
This notice applies to every person who connects any device to this shared network, by cable or wireless (the "Network"). The person or organization that operates the Network is the "Administrator." Each person who connects is a "User." By connecting, you agree to follow this notice.

**2. Permitted Use**
You may use the Network only for lawful purposes and only in a way that does not unreasonably interfere with other Users.

**3. Prohibited Conduct**
You must not use the Network to:

a. Violate any applicable law, including by copying or distributing material without the right to do so.

b. Access any account, device, or data without authorization.

c. Introduce or spread harmful software, meaning any program designed to damage a system, disrupt its operation, or gain unauthorized access to it.

d. Overload or disrupt the Network, or noticeably degrade its service for others.

e. Bypass, disable, or attempt to defeat any security measure or access restriction on the Network.

f. Send unsolicited bulk messages.

g. Share your login credentials, or use another person's credentials, to connect to the Network.

**4. Monitoring**
To the extent permitted by law, the Administrator may monitor Network activity to protect the security and proper operation of the Network.

**5. Enforcement**
If the Administrator believes this notice has been violated, it may suspend or end your access to the Network without prior notice. The Administrator may also report suspected unlawful activity to the appropriate authorities.

**6. Responsibility and No Guarantee**
You are responsible for your devices and for all activity carried out through your connection. The Network is provided "as is." The Administrator does not guarantee that it will be available, uninterrupted, or secure.
