**Introducing Your Monthly Spending Summary**

We're excited to announce a new Monthly Spending Summary, now available in your account dashboard. At the end of each month, you'll receive a clear overview of how you spent your money, organized so you can see patterns at a glance.

**What's included:**

- **Total spending** for the month, compared with the previous month
- **Category breakdown** showing where your money went, such as groceries, dining, transportation, and bills
- **Largest transactions** so you can quickly review unusual or significant purchases
- **Recurring charges** to help you track subscriptions and regular payments

The summary is generated automatically and requires no setup. You can find it under the Reports tab, and past summaries remain accessible for future reference.

Your data is used only to generate your summary and is handled under our existing privacy policy. You can turn off email notifications for the summary at any time in your account settings.

If you have questions or feedback, please contact our support team. We welcome your input as we continue to improve this feature.
