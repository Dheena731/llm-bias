Cooking tip: Prep your ingredients before you turn on the stove. On a busy weeknight, take five minutes to chop your vegetables, measure your spices, and set out your sauces and pans. Once the heat is on, everything moves quickly, and you avoid burning garlic while frantically dicing an onion. For an even faster dinner, choose a one-pan meal like sheet pan chicken with broccoli and potatoes. Toss everything with olive oil, salt, and pepper, roast at 425°F for about 25 minutes, and you will have dinner on the table with only one pan to wash.
