Turn on "Speedy meetings" in Google Calendar (Settings → Event settings → Speedy meetings).

Now every 30-minute event I create defaults to 25 minutes, and every 60 to 50. That gap is when I refill my coffee, skim the next agenda, and write down what I just promised to do. Before, I did all of that during the first five minutes of the next call, badly.

It only affects events you create, so you'll still get booked into full 30s by other people. But it's a nice quiet dent in the back-to-back wall.
