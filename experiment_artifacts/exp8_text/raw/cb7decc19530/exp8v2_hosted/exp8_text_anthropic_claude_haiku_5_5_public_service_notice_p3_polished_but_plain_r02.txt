**Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection**

Beginning Monday, [date], recycling collection in our area will be moving to a every-other-week schedule. Recycling will now be picked up on the same day as your regular trash collection, on alternating weeks. Please check your collection calendar, which is available at [website or phone number], to confirm your new pickup dates.

To help ensure your recycling is collected, please rinse containers, keep paper and cardboard dry, and place only accepted items in your bin. Plastic bags, food waste, and garden materials should not go in the recycling bin. A full list of accepted materials is available from the same source.

Bins should be placed at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on your collection day. Collection crews may not return for materials left out after pickup has begun.

Thank you for your continued cooperation in keeping our community clean and sustainable. If you have any questions, please contact [department name] at [phone number] or [email address].
