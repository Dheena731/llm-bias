Please be aware of potential phishing emails that may be circulating through our email system. Phishing emails are designed to deceive users into revealing sensitive information such as passwords or financial data. They may appear to be sent from a legitimate source, but are actually attempts to compromise our security.

If you receive an email that asks for sensitive information, do not respond to it. Instead, contact the IT department to verify the email's authenticity. We will check the email's origin and advise you on whether it is safe to interact with it.

Additionally, be cautious of emails that:

- Ask you to click on a link or download an attachment from an unknown sender
- Request sensitive information such as passwords or financial data
- Create a sense of urgency or panic to prompt you into taking action
- Contain grammatical or spelling errors

If you have any doubts about an email, it is always better to err on the side of caution and contact the IT department for guidance.
