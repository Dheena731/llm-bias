One of the easiest ways to keep rice fluffy and separate is to rinse it before cooking. Place the rice in a fine-mesh strainer and run cold water over it, swirling with your fingers until the water runs mostly clear. This washes away the surface starch that causes grains to clump together. Drain it well, then cook it using the usual water ratio. For extra separation, you can also let the cooked rice rest with the lid on for about ten minutes before fluffing it gently with a fork.
