Cookie Notice

This website uses cookies and similar technologies to operate properly, to improve your experience, and to understand how the site is used. A cookie is a small text file that is placed on your computer, phone, or other device when you visit a website. Cookies allow the site to recognize your device, remember your preferences, and gather information about your browsing activity.

We use several types of cookies. Strictly necessary cookies are required for the site to function, such as those that maintain your session, support security features, or remember items in a shopping cart. These cookies do not require your consent and cannot be switched off in our systems, although you may be able to block them through your browser settings, which may cause parts of the site to stop working. Preference cookies remember choices you have made, such as your language or region, so that you do not need to enter them again on each visit. Analytics cookies collect information about how visitors use the site, including the pages viewed, the time spent on each page, and any error messages encountered. We use this information to measure performance and improve content and design. Marketing cookies, where used, help us and our advertising partners deliver relevant advertisements and measure their effectiveness across different websites and services.

Except for strictly necessary cookies, we will place cookies on your device only after you have given your consent through the cookie banner displayed when you first visit the site. You may accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or choose your preferences by category. You may change your decision or withdraw your consent at any time by reopening the cookie settings from the link provided in the footer of each page. Withdrawing consent will not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before the withdrawal.

Some cookies are set by third parties, such as analytics providers, advertising networks, or embedded content services. These parties may collect information about your online activity over time and across different websites. We do not control the cookies they set, and their use of your information is governed by their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

Cookies remain on your device for different lengths of time. Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser, while persistent cookies remain until they expire or until you delete them. Most cookies used on this site expire within thirteen months of being set.

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings, which usually allow you to view, delete, or block cookies from particular sites or from all sites. Please note that restricting cookies may affect the functionality of this website and your experience of it.

Where cookies involve the processing of personal data, we handle that data in accordance with our Privacy Policy, which explains the purposes of processing, the legal bases we rely on, how long we retain data, and the rights available to you, including the rights of access, correction, deletion, and objection. If you have questions about this notice or our use of cookies, please contact us using the details provided on our contact page.

We may update this notice from time to time to reflect changes in technology, law, or our practices. The date of the most recent revision will appear at the top of the notice, and your continued use of the site after any update indicates that you have read the revised version.
