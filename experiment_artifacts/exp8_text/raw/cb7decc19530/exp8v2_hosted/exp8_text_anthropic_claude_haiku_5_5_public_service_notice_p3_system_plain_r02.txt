Recycling Collection Changes

Starting next month, recycling collection in our neighborhood will move from every other week to a weekly schedule on Tuesday mornings. Residents should place their bins at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on Tuesdays. Collection will continue to cover paper, cardboard, glass, plastics marked with recycling codes one through seven, and aluminum and steel cans.

Please rinse containers before placing them in the bin, and flatten cardboard boxes to save space. Items that are not accepted include plastic bags, food waste, and electronics, which should be taken to the county drop-off center on Mill Road.

Bins should be returned from the curb by the end of Tuesday. Your current collection calendar will be replaced by the new schedule, which is available on the city website and at the public library. For questions, please contact the Department of Public Works at the number listed on your utility bill.
