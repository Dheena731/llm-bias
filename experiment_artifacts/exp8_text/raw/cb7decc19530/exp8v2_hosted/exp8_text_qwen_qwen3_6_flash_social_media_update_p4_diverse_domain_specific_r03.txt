Stop color-coding your calendar by project. It's lying to you. 📅🛑

When you group by "Design" and "Sales," you end up back-to-backing three high-focus tasks and wonder why you're fried by noon.

Switch to **Cognitive Load** coloring:
🔴 Deep Work (Zero distractions)
🟡 Context Switch (Meetings/Comms)
⚪ Low Gear (Email/Batching admin)

The rule: Never stack two 🔴 blocks. Even if they're different projects. Your brain needs a recovery interval between deep dives. Fill that gap with a walk, a snack, or pure ⚪ work.

Protect your focus like a billable hour.
