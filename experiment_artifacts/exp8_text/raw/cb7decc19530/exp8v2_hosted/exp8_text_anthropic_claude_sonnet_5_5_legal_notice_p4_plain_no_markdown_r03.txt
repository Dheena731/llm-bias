NOTICE REGARDING REFUND ELIGIBILITY

This Notice sets out the conditions under which a purchaser may be entitled to a refund for goods or services purchased from the Company. By completing a purchase, the purchaser acknowledges and agrees to the terms stated below.

A purchaser is eligible for a full refund if a written request is received by the Company within thirty days of the date of purchase, provided that the goods have not been used, opened, or altered, and that the purchaser submits proof of purchase. For services, a full refund is available only if the request is made before the service has begun or within seven days of the start of the service, whichever occurs first.

Refunds will not be issued where the request is made after the applicable period has expired, where the goods have been damaged through misuse or neglect, or where the goods or services were expressly identified at the time of sale as final sale, custom-made, or non-refundable. Digital products that have been downloaded or accessed are not eligible for a refund except where required by applicable law or where the product is materially defective and the Company is unable to correct the defect within a reasonable time.

Where a refund is approved, the Company will issue payment to the original method of payment within fourteen business days of approval. The Company may deduct reasonable costs of return shipping or any applicable processing fees where such deductions were disclosed at the time of purchase. The Company is not responsible for delays caused by the purchaser's financial institution.

Nothing in this Notice limits any rights that a purchaser may have under mandatory consumer protection laws in the purchaser's jurisdiction. In the event of a conflict between this Notice and such laws, the applicable law shall prevail to the extent of the conflict.

The Company reserves the right to amend this Notice at any time. Amendments apply only to purchases made after the date of the amendment. Questions regarding eligibility should be directed to the Company in writing before any return is initiated.
