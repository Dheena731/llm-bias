**Cook one grain, one protein, and one sauce on Sunday, then change only the sauce.**

Make a big pot of farro or rice, roast a tray of chicken thighs (or bake a block of tofu), and whisk together one sharp, oily sauce, like a lemon-tahini or a scallion-ginger oil. Pack the first three lunches as bowls with whatever raw vegetables are in the crisper. Keep the sauce in a separate jar so nothing goes soggy.

By Wednesday you'll be tired of it, so that's when you change the sauce. Stir the same chicken and grain into a jar of chili crisp and a splash of rice vinegar, or tuck them into a tortilla with hummus and pickled onions. The base stays the same, so the work is already done, but the lunch tastes different enough that you won't be tempted by the takeout menu.

Two small things help:
- Cook the grain slightly underdone. It finishes softening when reheated and won't turn mushy by Thursday.
- Keep a "rescue" item in the office drawer, such as a tin of sardines, a packet of instant miso, or crackers. If the Tuesday meeting runs long and you skip your lunch, you can stretch the prepped food to Friday instead of throwing it out.
