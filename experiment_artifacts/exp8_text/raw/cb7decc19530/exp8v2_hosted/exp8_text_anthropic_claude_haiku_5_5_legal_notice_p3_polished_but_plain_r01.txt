Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. Some cookies are strictly necessary for the site to operate, such as those that remember items in a shopping cart or maintain a secure login session. Others are used for analytics, which help us measure traffic and improve our pages, or for preferences and marketing, which remember your settings and may show you content based on your interests. Strictly necessary cookies are set without requiring your consent. All other cookies are set only after you have indicated your agreement through our consent banner.

Those parties may process information about your visit in accordance with their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review. We do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal data, and we do not sell information gathered through cookies.

You may accept or decline non-essential cookies at any time through the cookie settings available on this website. You may also control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the site and your ability to use some of its features.

Continued use of the website after an update takes effect constitutes acknowledgment of the revised notice, although we will seek fresh consent where the law requires it.
