**NOTICE TO PATRONS: Eastside Branch Monday and Tuesday Hours Change March 3**

Starting Monday, March 3, the Eastside Branch will open at 10 a.m. on Mondays and Tuesdays instead of 9 a.m. Closing times are unchanged: 8 p.m. Monday through Thursday, 5 p.m. Friday, and 1 to 5 p.m. Saturday. The branch remains closed Sundays.

The later Monday and Tuesday opening gives staff time to shelve weekend returns and sort holds before the doors open. Items dropped in the book slot after closing Friday will be checked in by 11 a.m. Monday, so holds placed for pickup that morning may not be ready until then.

The book return slot stays open 24 hours. The holds shelf is accessible only during open hours.

Questions? Call 555-0142 or ask at the circulation desk.
