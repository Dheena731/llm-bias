Staying adequately hydrated is one of the simplest ways to support your energy, focus, and physical well-being throughout the day. Thirst is often a late signal that your body is already running low on fluids, so it is best to sip water consistently rather than trying to catch up all at once. Keeping a glass or bottle within sight at your workspace creates an effortless visual prompt to drink. Taking a few sips every hour will help keep your mind sharp, stave off fatigue, and ensure your body functions at its best.
