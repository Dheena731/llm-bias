**Quarterly Access Review Reminder**

As part of our ongoing commitment to ensuring the security and integrity of our systems, all users are required to participate in quarterly access reviews. This process helps us to maintain accurate and up-to-date access controls, ensuring that only authorized personnel have access to sensitive information and systems.

Please be aware that your access will be reviewed and updated on a quarterly basis. You will receive an email notification prior to the review, and you will be required to confirm or update your access permissions within the specified timeframe.

If you have any questions or concerns about your access or the review process, please do not hesitate to contact the IT department. Your cooperation and prompt response are greatly appreciated.

Thank you for your understanding and assistance in maintaining the security of our systems.
