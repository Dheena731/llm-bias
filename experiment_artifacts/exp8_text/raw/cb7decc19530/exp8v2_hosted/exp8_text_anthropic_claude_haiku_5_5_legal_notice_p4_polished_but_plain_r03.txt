NOTICE REGARDING REFUND ELIGIBILITY

This notice sets out the conditions under which customers may request a refund for products or services purchased from the company. By completing a purchase, the customer acknowledges and agrees to the terms described below.

A customer may request a full refund within fourteen (14) days of the date of purchase, provided the product is unused, in its original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase. Services that have not yet commenced are eligible for a full refund within the same period. Once a service has begun, refunds will be prorated based on the portion of the service that has been delivered, and no refund will be issued for the completed portion.

Refunds are not available for custom orders, personalized items, digital downloads that have been accessed or redeemed, or products marked as final sale at the time of purchase. Items damaged through customer misuse, or returned more than thirty (30) days after the date of delivery, are likewise ineligible, except where the law requires otherwise.

Where a product is defective or materially different from its description, the customer may request a refund or replacement at no cost regardless of the timeframes stated above, provided the issue is reported within sixty (60) days of delivery. The company may require photographs or other reasonable documentation to verify the claim.

To request a refund, the customer must contact the company's customer support team through the address or channel listed on the original receipt or the company's official website, stating the order number and the reason for the request. Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within ten (10) business days. The company is not responsible for delays caused by a customer's financial institution.

Nothing in this notice limits any right a customer may have under applicable consumer protection law. In the event of a conflict between this notice and such law, the law shall prevail. The company reserves the right to amend this notice at any time, and amendments will apply to purchases made after the date of revision.
