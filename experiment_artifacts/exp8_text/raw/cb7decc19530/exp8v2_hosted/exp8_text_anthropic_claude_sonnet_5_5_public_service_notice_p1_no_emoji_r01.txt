**PUBLIC NOTICE: Updated Library Opening Hours**

Effective Monday, the Central Public Library will operate under the following hours:

**Monday to Thursday:** 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
**Friday:** 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
**Saturday:** 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
**Sunday:** Closed

**Holiday Closures:** The library will be closed on all public holidays. Hours may be adjusted on the preceding day, so please check the posted schedule.

**Returns:** The outdoor book drop remains available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Items returned after closing will be processed on the next business day.

**Services:** Computer and printing services end 30 minutes before closing. Please begin your last checkout 15 minutes before closing time.

For questions or the latest updates, please contact the front desk by phone or visit the library's website.

Thank you for supporting your local library.
