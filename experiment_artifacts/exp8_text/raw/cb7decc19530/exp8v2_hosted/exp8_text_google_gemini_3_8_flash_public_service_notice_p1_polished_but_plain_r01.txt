The City Public Library is pleased to announce its current schedule of service hours. The main branch is open Monday through Thursday from 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., Friday and Saturday from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., and Sunday from 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. The exterior book return box is accessible twenty-four hours a day for the return of all physical materials. Digital services, including e-books, online research databases, and audiobook downloads, remain available around the clock for all active library cardholders. Visitors are welcome to use the public computers, study spaces, and reference desk during any regular open hours. Please speak with the front desk staff during service hours for further information regarding seasonal schedules and upcoming library programs.
