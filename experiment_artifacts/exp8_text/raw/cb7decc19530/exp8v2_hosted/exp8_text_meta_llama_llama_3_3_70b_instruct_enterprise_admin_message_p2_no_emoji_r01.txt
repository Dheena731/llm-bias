Attention all staff, 

We are introducing a new expense approval workflow to improve efficiency and transparency in our financial processes. The new workflow will require all expenses to be submitted through a centralized online portal, where they will be reviewed and approved by department managers and financial administrators. This new process will help to reduce errors and ensure that all expenses are properly documented and accounted for. 

Further information and training on the new workflow will be provided in the coming weeks. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the finance department. 

Thank you for your cooperation and understanding as we implement this new process.
