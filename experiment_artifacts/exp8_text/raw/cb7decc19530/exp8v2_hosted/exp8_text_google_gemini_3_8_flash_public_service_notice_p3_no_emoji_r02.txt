**Important Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection Schedule**

Effective Monday, November 1, 2023, the municipal recycling collection schedule will be updated for all residential zones. 

Key changes include:

* **New Collection Days:** Your designated pickup day may change. Please review the updated neighborhood route map.
* **Placement Times:** All recycling bins must be placed curbside by 7:00 AM on your scheduled collection day.
* **Accepted Materials:** Plastic bags and styrofoam are no longer accepted in curbside bins and must be taken to designated drop-off centers.
