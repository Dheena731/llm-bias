We just launched Saved Searches, a new feature that lets you keep your most-used filters one tap away. Set up a search once, give it a name, and come back to it whenever you need it without rebuilding it from scratch. You can also turn on alerts, so you will be notified when new results match what you are looking for. Saved Searches is rolling out to all users this week. Update your app to try it, and tell us what you think. Your feedback will help shape what we build next.
