Set aside one hour each weekend to prepare reusable meal components rather than complete dishes. Cook a large batch of grains like rice or quinoa, roast two trays of mixed vegetables, and prepare a couple of proteins such as chicken thighs or beans. Store these elements in separate containers so you can quickly combine different portions throughout the week without repeating the exact same flavor combination daily. Add fresh herbs, citrus juice, or a simple vinaigrette when you pack your bag to keep each lunch tasting freshly made while completely eliminating weekday prep work.
