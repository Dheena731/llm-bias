To elevate your roasted vegetables, try seasoning them in layers. Before roasting, toss your vegetables with a neutral oil, salt, and any aromatics like minced garlic or ginger. Then, halfway through the roasting time, sprinkle on some acidity - a squeeze of fresh lemon juice or a drizzle of apple cider vinegar - to brighten the flavors. Finally, just before serving, add a pinch of fresh herbs like thyme or rosemary, and a grind of black pepper to add depth and complexity. This layered approach will help balance and enhance the natural sweetness of the vegetables, resulting in a rich and savory finish.
