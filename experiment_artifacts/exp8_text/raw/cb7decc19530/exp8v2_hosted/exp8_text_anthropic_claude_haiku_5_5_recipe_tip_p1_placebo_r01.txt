Cook extra rice or pasta on the weekend and store it in the fridge. On a busy weeknight, you can then make a quick stir-fry or fried rice in about ten minutes by tossing the cold grains in a hot pan with a little oil, a handful of frozen vegetables, and an egg or leftover protein. Cold, day-old rice fries up better than freshly cooked rice because it's drier and less likely to turn mushy.
