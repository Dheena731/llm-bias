A quick reminder to keep drinking water throughout your day. Staying hydrated helps your energy, focus, and mood, and it is easy to forget when you are busy. Try keeping a glass or bottle within reach, and take a few sips whenever you finish a task or start a new one. If plain water gets boring, a slice of lemon or a cup of herbal tea can help. Pay attention to your body too, since thirst, headaches, and tiredness can all be signs that you need more fluids. Take a drink right now, and check in with yourself again in an hour or so.
