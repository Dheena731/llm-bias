We are writing to inform you of a change to our card transaction fees. As of our next billing cycle, the fee for processing card transactions will be 2.5 percent of the transaction amount, plus a fixed charge of 10 cents per transaction. This change is intended to better reflect the costs associated with processing card payments and will help us to continue providing you with a secure and reliable payment system. If you have any questions or concerns about this change, please do not hesitate to contact us. We are always here to help and appreciate your business.
