**Subject: Quarterly Access Reviews**

**To:** All staff and managers

Each quarter, we will review who has access to our systems and data. The purpose of an access review is to confirm that each person's access matches their current job responsibilities, and that no one keeps access they no longer need.

**What this means for you**

- **Managers:** You will receive a list of the people on your team and the access each person has. Confirm the access that is still needed, and flag any that should be changed or removed. Please respond by the deadline stated in your review request.
- **All staff:** You may be asked to confirm that the access you have is still necessary for your work. If you notice access you don't need, tell your manager or the administrator who sent the review.

**What happens next**

- Access that is not confirmed as needed may be removed.
- If you lose access you still need, contact your manager to request that it be restored.

Regular reviews help protect company information and keep access accurate as roles change. Thank you for completing your part of each review promptly.

If you have questions about the process, please reply to this notice.
