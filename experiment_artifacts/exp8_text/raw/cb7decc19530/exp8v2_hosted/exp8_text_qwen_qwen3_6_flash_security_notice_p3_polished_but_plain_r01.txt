To maintain the security of our information systems, please remember to lock your workstation screen whenever you step away from your desk, even for a brief moment. Locking your screen prevents unauthorized individuals from accessing sensitive data while you are absent. You can quickly lock your screen by pressing the Windows key and the L key simultaneously on Windows devices, or Command-Control-Q on Mac systems. We appreciate your diligence in protecting our company assets and ensuring compliance with our security policies. Thank you for your attention to this matter.
