Friendly reminder: Refill your prescription soon

Your prescription is running low, so it's a good time to request a refill. Please contact your pharmacy or your prescriber's office before you run out so you don't miss any doses.

Here are a few tips:
- Call or use the pharmacy's app or website to request the refill.
- Allow a day or two for processing, especially on weekends or holidays.
- If you have no refills left, contact your prescriber's office for a new prescription.
- If you have questions about your medication, ask your pharmacist or care provider.

Please do not stop taking your medication without speaking to your care provider first.
