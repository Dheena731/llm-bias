**METROPOLITAN EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY**  
**HEALTH & HUMAN SERVICES DIVISION**  

### ADVISORY: LEVEL 2 EXCESSIVE HEAT WARNING  
**ISSUED:** 06:00 EDT | Wednesday, July 17  
**EFFECTIVE:** 11:00 EDT Thursday, July 18 through 21:00 EDT Saturday, July 20  

The National Weather Service has issued an Excessive Heat Warning for the metro basin. Ambient temperatures are forecast to exceed 98°F (36.7°C), with afternoon heat index values projected between 106°F and 111°F. Overnight low temperatures are not expected to drop below 79°F, severely limiting physiological recovery for residents without mechanical cooling.

---

### MUNICIPAL OPERATIONAL CHANGES

* **Shifted Solid Waste Collection:** Residential collection will begin one hour early, at 05:30, on Thursday and Friday. Set bins out the night prior.
* **Low-Income Cooling Sites:** Public libraries (Central, East District, and Mill Creek branches) will operate as designated low-barrier cooling sites from 09:00 to 20:00 daily. Pets on leashes or in carriers are permitted in designated ground-floor vestibules. Water bottle filling stations are open.
* **Transit Fares:** Fixed-route municipal bus fares are suspended system-wide between 12:00 and 19:00 through Saturday to facilitate transit to cooling centers.

---

### CODE ENFORCEMENT & MANDATES

* **Outdoor Labor:** Municipal Code § 14-88 requires mandatory 15-minute shaded rest breaks every two hours for commercial construction and permitted outdoor worksites when the wet-bulb globe temperature (WBGT) exceeds 87°F.
* **Animal Welfare:** Per Ordinance 21-C, tethering dogs outdoors during a declared Level 2 Heat Advisory is strictly prohibited. Enforcement officers are authorized to issue immediate citations.
* **Utility Shutoffs:** In accordance with state public utility rules, involuntary electric service disconnections for non-payment are suspended for the duration of this advisory.

---

### VULNERABLE POPULATIONS & WELFARE INQUIRIES

Residents residing in multi-story brick or uninsulated walk-up structures without functional air conditioning face elevated risk of heat-induced syncope and hyperthermia. 

To request a wellness check for an isolated senior, or to direct street outreach teams to unhoused individuals in distress, call **311** (TTY: 555-0144). For symptoms of heat stroke—confusion, cessation of sweating, vomiting, or loss of consciousness—call **911** immediately.
