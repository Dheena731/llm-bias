To tame the stickiness of freshly cooked rice, try incorporating a simple step into your routine: rinsing the rice grains before cooking. This pre-cooking rinse helps remove excess starch from the surface of the grains, which is a primary culprit behind clingy, sticky rice. After rinsing, make sure to drain the water thoroughly and then cook the rice with the appropriate water ratio. For most types of rice, a general rule of thumb is to use about 1 1/4 cups of water for every 1 cup of rice. Adjusting the water ratio can also help, as too little water can cause the rice to dry out and stick together, while too much water leads to a mushy, unappetizing texture. Lastly, once the rice is cooked, fluff it gently with a fork to separate the grains, further reducing stickiness. This method works particularly well for varieties like jasmine and basmati, which are known for their naturally higher starch content.
