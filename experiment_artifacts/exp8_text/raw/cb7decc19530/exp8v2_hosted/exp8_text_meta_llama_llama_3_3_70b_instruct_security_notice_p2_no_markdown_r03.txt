Important Notice to All Staff

We have recently seen an increase in phishing emails being sent to staff members. These emails are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information such as your login credentials or financial information. They often appear to be legitimate emails from reputable sources, but may contain spelling or grammatical errors, or urgent requests that require immediate action.

To protect yourself and the company, please be cautious when opening emails from unknown senders and never click on links or provide sensitive information in response to an email that you are not certain is legitimate. If you are unsure about the authenticity of an email, please contact the IT department before taking any action.

Some common signs of phishing emails include:

* Urgent requests for action
* Spelling or grammatical errors
* Requests for sensitive information
* Links or attachments from unknown senders

If you receive a suspicious email, please delete it immediately and report it to the IT department. Remember, it is always better to err on the side of caution when it comes to protecting your personal and company information.
