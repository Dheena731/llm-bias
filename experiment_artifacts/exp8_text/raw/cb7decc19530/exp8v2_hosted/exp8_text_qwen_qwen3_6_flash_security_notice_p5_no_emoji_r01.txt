**NOTICE: REPORTING A LOST COMPANY LAPTOP**

All employees are required to report any lost, misplaced, or stolen company-issued laptop immediately to the IT Security Department. Delayed reporting may expose corporate data to unauthorized access and create network security risks.

Please provide the following details when submitting a report:
• Full name and employee ID
• Department and position
• Date, approximate time, and last known location of the device
• Laptop model and serial number (if available)

Submit your report by emailing itsecurity@company.com, filing a ticket through the internal IT portal under “Device Loss,” or calling the IT Helpdesk at extension 5500. Do not attempt to locate the device independently if it contains sensitive client or proprietary information; notify security first.

A replacement workstation will be issued only after the incident is logged and standard security procedures are completed. Timely reporting is mandatory to protect company assets and maintain data compliance.

Thank you for your prompt attention.

IT Security & Operations
