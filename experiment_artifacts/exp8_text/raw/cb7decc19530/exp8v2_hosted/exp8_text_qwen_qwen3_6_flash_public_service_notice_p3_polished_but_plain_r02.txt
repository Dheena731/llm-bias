Please be advised that there will be adjustments to the recycling collection routine effective next month. The pickup day shifts from Tuesday to Wednesday, so please set your bins out by seven in the morning on the new day. Residents must also note that plastic bags are no longer permitted in curbside recycling containers due to equipment issues. These items should be collected in clean, rigid plastic buckets or brought directly to the central drop-off location. Your attention to these updates helps maintain efficient operations and ensures materials are processed correctly. We appreciate your cooperation as we implement these improvements.
