**SECURITY NOTICE: Phishing Awareness**

Please remain vigilant regarding suspicious emails. Cybercriminals frequently impersonate company leadership, internal departments, or trusted vendors to compromise company data and systems.

**Key warning signs to watch for:**
* Urgent or threatening requests for sensitive information, password resets, or wire transfers.
* Mismatched sender addresses or slight misspellings in domain names (e.g., @cornpany.com instead of @company.com).
* Unexpected attachments, especially zip files or macro-enabled documents.
* Generic greetings and poor grammar or spelling.
* Hyperlinks that preview a different URL than what is displayed.

**What to do:**
If you receive an email that looks suspicious:
1. Do not click any links or download attachments.
2. Do not reply to the sender.
3. Report the message immediately using the "Report Phishing" button in your email client, or forward it to the IT Help Desk.

When in doubt, contact the sender through a known, separate communication channel (such as Slack or phone) to verify the request.
