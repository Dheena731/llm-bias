To assist with budgeting and financial planning, a new monthly spending summary has been introduced. This report will provide a detailed breakdown of expenditures by category, highlighting areas where costs can be optimized and providing insights into spending patterns.

The summary will include a comprehensive list of all transactions, organized by type, including income, fixed expenses, variable expenses, and savings. Each category will be presented with a clear total, as well as a percentage of the total monthly spending, allowing for a quick and easy visual assessment of where the majority of funds are being allocated.

In addition to the categorized breakdown, the report will also feature a comparison of current spending to prior periods, highlighting any notable increases or decreases in certain areas. This will enable users to identify trends and make informed decisions about their financial management strategies.

The new monthly spending summary will be available online and can be accessed by authorized personnel. This report will be generated on a regular basis, providing timely and accurate information to support effective financial management and decision-making.
