Our email system will be undergoing a migration in the near future. This process will involve transferring our email accounts to a new platform. The purpose of this change is to improve the reliability and functionality of our email service.

The new system will provide users with enhanced features and a more efficient experience. However, during the transition period, some minor disruptions may occur. Users may notice changes to their email interface, and some features may not be immediately available.

A detailed schedule of the migration will be communicated to all affected users in advance. Instructions on how to prepare for the change will be provided. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the IT department.

We appreciate your understanding and cooperation during this process.
