Subject: Implementation of the New Expense Approval Workflow

Team,

We are updating our expense submission process to improve accuracy and reduce approval delays. Starting immediately, all reimbursement requests must follow the new approval workflow outlined below.

Please note the following changes:
• Submit all expense claims through the company expense portal before forwarding invoices to accounts payable.
• Expenses under five hundred dollars will be approved by your direct supervisor. Expenses of five hundred dollars or more will require secondary review from the finance department.
• Every claim must include a clear, itemized receipt in PDF or image format. Missing documentation will result in automatic rejection.
• You can track the status of your submissions in real time using the approval tracker in your portal dashboard.

Complete step-by-step instructions, including screenshots and troubleshooting tips, are posted on the internal knowledge base under the Finance section. If you encounter issues during your first submission, please contact the finance support team through the internal helpdesk portal.

Thank you for adapting to this updated process. Your timely submissions help us maintain smooth operations and consistent reimbursement timelines.

Best regards,
Administration Office
