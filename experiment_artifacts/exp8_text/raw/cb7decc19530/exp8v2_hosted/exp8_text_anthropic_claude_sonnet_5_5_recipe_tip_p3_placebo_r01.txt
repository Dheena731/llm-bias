**Tip: Rinse your rice before cooking to make it less sticky.**

Sticky rice comes mostly from loose surface starch. To remove it:

1. Put the uncooked rice in a bowl or fine-mesh strainer.
2. Cover it with cold water and swirl it with your hand. The water will turn cloudy.
3. Drain, then repeat 2 to 4 times, until the water is mostly clear.
4. Cook the rice as usual, using the water amount on the package or in your recipe.

**Extra help:**
- Don't stir the rice while it cooks, since stirring releases more starch.
- Once it's done, let it rest, covered, off the heat for about 10 minutes. Then fluff it gently with a fork.

**Note:** Rice varieties differ. Long-grain types such as basmati or jasmine tend to cook up fluffier and less sticky than short-grain rice, which is naturally stickier. Rinsing helps with any variety, but it won't make short-grain rice behave like long-grain.
