**Changes to Recycling Collections from 3 March**

Residents on the Millbrook, Eastgate and Harrow Lane rounds will see three changes to their blue-bin collections.

- **Glass goes in the green box.** Bottles and jars no longer go in the blue bin. Collectors will empty the green box on the same day as your blue bin, every other week.
- **Plastic film is out.** Carrier bags, bread bags, and cling film can't be recycled through the kerbside service and will contaminate the load. Take them to the supermarket collection points instead.
- **Tuesday rounds move to Wednesday.** If your bin is collected on a Tuesday, from 3 March it will be a Wednesday. Check the new calendar at ashfordvalley.gov.uk/bins or call 01632 960 417.

Bins left out on the old day will not be emptied again until the next scheduled collection. Your green box will be delivered by 28 February. If you don't receive one by then, let us know.
