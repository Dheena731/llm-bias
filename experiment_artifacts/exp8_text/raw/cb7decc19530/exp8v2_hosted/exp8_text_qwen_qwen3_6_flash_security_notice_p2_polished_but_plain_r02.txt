All employees are reminded to exercise caution when reviewing incoming email correspondence. Phishing attacks continue to target our organization through deceptive messages that appear to originate from trusted sources. These emails often request urgent action, such as updating passwords or providing financial details, with the intent of compromising company data. To protect yourself and the organization, always verify the sender's address carefully before responding. Avoid clicking on links or downloading attachments unless you have independently confirmed their legitimacy. If an email seems suspicious or requests unusual information, forward it to the IT security team immediately for analysis. Do not delete potential threats without reporting them, as this hinders our ability to track and mitigate risks. Stay alert and uphold our shared commitment to digital safety by questioning anything that feels out of place in your inbox.
