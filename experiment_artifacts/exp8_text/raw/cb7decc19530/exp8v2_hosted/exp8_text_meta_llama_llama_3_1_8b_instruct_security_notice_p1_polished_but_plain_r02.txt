Password Reuse Security Notice

In order to maintain the security of our systems and protect sensitive information, all employees are reminded to follow best practices when creating and managing their passwords.

Reusing passwords from personal accounts or other work-related accounts is not acceptable. This practice can create a vulnerability in our system if an unauthorized party gains access to one of your accounts. Passwords should be unique and not linked to any other account.

To maintain the security of our systems, we recommend that you create strong, unique passwords for all of your work-related accounts. If you have any concerns or questions about password security, please reach out to the IT department for guidance.

Your cooperation in maintaining strong, unique passwords will help protect our systems and ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our sensitive information.
