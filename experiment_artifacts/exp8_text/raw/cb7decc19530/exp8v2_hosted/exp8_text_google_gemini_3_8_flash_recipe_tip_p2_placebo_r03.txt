To keep fresh herbs crisp and flavorful, treat tender varieties like parsley and cilantro like a bouquet of cut flowers: trim the bottom of the stems, place them upright in a jar with about an inch of water, and cover the leaves loosely with a plastic bag before storing the jar in your refrigerator. 

*(Note: Use this same water-jar method for fresh basil, but keep it on your kitchen counter at room temperature instead, as cold refrigerator air will turn basil leaves black.)*
