Starting next month, recycling collection in our neighborhood will move from every other week to a weekly schedule. Recycling bins will be picked up on the same day as your regular trash, so please check the updated calendar that will be mailed to all households. Residents are asked to rinse containers, flatten cardboard boxes, and keep plastic bags out of the recycling bin, as these items can damage sorting equipment. Bins should be placed at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on collection day. If you have questions or need a replacement bin, please contact the Public Works Department at the number listed on your calendar. Thank you for helping us keep our community clean and sustainable.
