Your end-of-year tax statement summarizes the income you earned and the taxes withheld or paid over the past year. It is usually issued by your employer, bank, or other payers, and it is the document you will use to complete your tax return. The statement typically shows your total earnings, any amounts deducted for taxes such as income tax or social security contributions, and in some cases interest, dividends, or other investment income. Please keep it with your other financial records, since you may need it if the tax authority asks questions or if you apply for a loan or other financial service.

If any figure looks incorrect or you do not recognize an entry, contact the issuer as soon as possible so they can review and correct it before you file. If you have questions about how to use the statement in your return, a tax professional or the official tax agency's guidance pages can help.
