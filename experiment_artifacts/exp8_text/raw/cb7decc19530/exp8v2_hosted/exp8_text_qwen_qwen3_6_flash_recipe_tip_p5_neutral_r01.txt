**Season generously before, brighten after.**

Toss your vegetables in oil and **salt well before roasting**. This draws out moisture, ensuring better caramelization and seasoning that penetrates deep into the veg. 

Once they're out of the oven, **finish with acid**: a squeeze of lemon juice, a splash of balsamic vinegar, or a sprinkle of flaky sea salt will instantly brighten the natural sweetness and elevate the flavor profile.
