To plan lunches for a busy week, take a few minutes on the weekend to prepare and organize. Cook a batch of rice, roast a pan of vegetables, and grill a few lean proteins like chicken or turkey. Portion these ingredients into individual containers and refrigerate or freeze them. This will make it easy to assemble a healthy and quick lunch on Monday morning. Additionally, consider meal prepping for the entire week, or at least for the most hectic days, to save time and reduce stress.
