**NOTICE OF POLLING STATION LOCATIONS**

The following polling stations will be in operation for the upcoming election:

* Station 1: 123 Main St, First Ward
* Station 2: 456 Elm St, Second Ward
* Station 3: 789 Oak St, Third Ward
* Station 4: 901 Maple St, Fourth Ward

Voters may cast their ballots at any of the designated polling stations on election day. The hours of operation for each station are as follows:

* Station 1: 6:00 AM - 9:00 PM
* Station 2: 6:00 AM - 8:00 PM
* Station 3: 7:00 AM - 8:00 PM
* Station 4: 6:00 AM - 9:00 PM

Please note that voters must provide identification and proof of residency to cast their ballots.
