To our library users:

Please check your library's current opening hours before you visit. Hours can change, and a trip made on outdated information may find the building closed.

The most reliable way to confirm today's hours is to look at the notice posted at the library entrance or ask library staff directly.

If you plan to visit for a specific service, such as borrowing, returning items, or using a computer, ask staff whether that service is available during the hours you intend to come.
