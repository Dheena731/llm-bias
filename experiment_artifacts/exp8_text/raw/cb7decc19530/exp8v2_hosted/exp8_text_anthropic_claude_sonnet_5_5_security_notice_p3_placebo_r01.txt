**Reminder: Lock Your Screen When You Step Away**

To all staff:

Please lock your computer screen every time you leave your desk, even if you will only be gone for a moment.

An unlocked screen lets anyone nearby view or use your account, which can expose confidential information and put our work at risk.

**How to lock your screen:**
- **Windows:** Press the Windows key + L
- **Mac:** Press Control + Command + Q

Making this a habit takes only a second and helps protect you, your colleagues, and the people whose information we handle.

Thank you for your cooperation.
