To plan lunches for a busy week, set aside some time on the weekend to prepare and pack healthy meals in advance. Consider making a big batch of something like soup, salad, or grains that can be portioned out and reheated or grabbed on the go. You can also prep individual ingredients like fruits, cheeses, and meats to assemble into quick and easy lunches throughout the week. Having a plan and some pre-prepared options can save you time and stress during the week, and help you stick to your healthy eating goals.
