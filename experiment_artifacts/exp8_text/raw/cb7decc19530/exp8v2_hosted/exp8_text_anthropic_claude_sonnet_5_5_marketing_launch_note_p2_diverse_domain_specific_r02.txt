**Subject: Fresh bags on a schedule, starting March 3**

Until now, if you wanted our coffee every other week, you had to remember to order it every other week. People mostly didn't, and then ran out on a Thursday and bought something grey from the grocery store. We'd like to stop that.

**The Standing Order** is a subscription with three settings:

- **Filter or espresso.** Filter ships 4 days after roasting, espresso after 8, so neither arrives flat or gassy.
- **Every 1, 2, or 3 weeks.** A 12 oz bag lasts a two-cup-a-day household about 10 days.
- **Rotating or locked.** Rotating gets whatever we're roasting that week. Locked keeps you on one coffee for as long as we have it, which right now includes the washed Caturra from Huila that most of you keep asking about.

$18 a bag, shipping included. The roast date is printed on every bag. Skip a week, swap beans, or cancel from the account page, no email required.

We're capping the first round at 150 subscribers, because that's what our 5 kg roaster can handle on Mondays without cutting corners. Sign up at fenwickstreetcoffee.com/standing-order.
