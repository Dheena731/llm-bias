**Cookie Notice**

**1. Purpose of this notice**
This notice explains how this website uses cookies and how you can control them. A cookie is a small text file that a website saves on your device when you visit it.

**2. Types of cookies we use**
- **Essential cookies.** These are necessary for the website to work, for example to maintain your session or keep the site secure. They are set without your consent because the website cannot function properly without them.
- **Optional cookies.** These include analytics cookies, which help us understand how visitors use the website, and marketing cookies, which are used to show or measure advertising. We set optional cookies only if you consent. Some may be set by third-party services rather than by us.

**3. Your choices**
- You may accept or reject optional cookies when you first visit the website, and you may change or withdraw your choice at any time through the website's cookie settings.
- You may also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, parts of the website may not work.
- Rejecting optional cookies does not restrict your access to the website.

**4. Changes to this notice**
We may update this notice from time to time. The current version is always the one published on this page.
