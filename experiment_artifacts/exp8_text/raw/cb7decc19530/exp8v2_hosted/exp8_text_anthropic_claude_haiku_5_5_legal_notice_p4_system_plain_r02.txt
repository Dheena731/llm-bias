Notice of Refund Eligibility

This notice sets out the conditions under which a customer may request a refund for a purchase made from the seller. By completing a purchase, the customer agrees to the terms described here.

A customer may request a full refund for any product returned in its original condition, with all packaging, accessories, and proof of purchase, within thirty (30) days of the date of delivery. Refund requests made after this period will be considered only where the product is defective, damaged in transit, or materially different from its description.

Products that have been used, altered, or damaged by the customer after delivery are not eligible for a refund, except where the defect was present at the time of delivery. Custom, personalized, or perishable goods are excluded from refund eligibility unless they arrived in a damaged or incorrect state.

To request a refund, the customer must contact the seller's customer service department in writing, identifying the order number and the reason for the request. The seller will review the request within ten (10) business days and will notify the customer of its decision. Approved refunds will be issued to the original method of payment within fourteen (14) business days of approval, and the timing of funds becoming available may depend on the customer's financial institution.

The seller reserves the right to deny a refund request that does not meet the conditions stated in this notice. Nothing in this notice limits any statutory rights the customer may have under applicable consumer protection law.
