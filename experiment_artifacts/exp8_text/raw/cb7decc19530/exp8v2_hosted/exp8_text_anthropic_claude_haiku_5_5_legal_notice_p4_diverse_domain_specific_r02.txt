**NOTICE OF REFUND ELIGIBILITY**
**Harrow & Vell Letterpress LLC** ("Harrow & Vell," "we")
Effective March 1, 2025 · Applies to all custom print orders placed on or after this date

**1. Scope.** This notice governs refunds for custom letterpress and foil-stamped orders ("Orders"). It does not govern stock catalog items, which are covered by the return policy printed on each invoice.

**2. Deposits.** A non-refundable deposit of 50% of the quoted total is due when an Order is placed. The deposit secures a place in our press schedule and covers plate-making and paper ordering.

**3. Proof approval.** After you receive a digital proof, you must sign it or reply "approved" in writing. Once a proof is approved, the Order is final. Changes after approval are billed at the rates in Section 6, and no refund is issued for an approved design that you later decide against.

**4. Eligible refunds.** You are entitled to a full refund of amounts paid, excluding the deposit where noted in Section 5, if any of the following apply:

(a) We cancel the Order before printing begins;
(b) Printed items do not match the approved proof in color, text, or die placement, and we are unable to reprint within 15 business days;
(c) Items arrive damaged in transit, provided you report the damage in writing within 48 hours of delivery with photographs of the damaged items and the outer packaging.

**5. Partial refunds.** If you cancel after printing has begun but before shipment, you will receive the total paid less the deposit and the actual cost of materials consumed. Materials cost is calculated at the paper and ink invoice rate and documented on request.

**6. Client-caused errors.** No refund is owed where a defect traces to text, spelling, dimensions, or artwork supplied or approved by you. Reprints for such errors are available at 60% of the original print price.

**7. Requesting a refund.** Send requests to orders@harrowvell.example with your order number and the reason under Section 4 or 5. We will respond within five business days. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 10 business days.

**8. Limits.** Refunds under this notice are the sole remedy for defects in printing. Our liability for any Order is capped at the amount you paid for it.
