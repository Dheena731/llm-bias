Please be advised that we are introducing an updated expense approval workflow effective next Monday. Moving forward, all reimbursement requests must be submitted directly through the employee portal. Once entered, your report will automatically route to your direct supervisor for review before moving to the finance team for final verification and payment. This change is intended to speed up reimbursement times and provide better visibility into the status of your claims. Guides detailing the new submission process are available on the internal shared drive. If you have any questions or encounter issues with your next submission, please contact the finance department.
