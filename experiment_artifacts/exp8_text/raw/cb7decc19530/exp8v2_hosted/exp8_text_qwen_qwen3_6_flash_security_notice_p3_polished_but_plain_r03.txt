Please remember to lock your computer screen whenever you leave your workstation, even for a brief moment. This simple practice protects sensitive company information and ensures compliance with our security policies. To lock your screen quickly, press the Windows key and L simultaneously on Windows computers, or Command, Control, and Q together on Mac computers. You may also select the lock option from your system menu. Unattended machines pose a significant risk to data privacy and network security, so please make this a consistent habit throughout the workday. If you have questions about screen locking procedures or need assistance, contact the IT support team at their usual extension or helpdesk portal. Thank you for helping us maintain a secure workspace.
