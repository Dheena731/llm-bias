When seasoning roasted vegetables, consider the natural sweetness that develops during the roasting process. To balance this, try using acidic ingredients like a squeeze of fresh lemon juice or a splash of apple cider vinegar to cut through the richness. Additionally, herbs and spices that complement the vegetable's inherent flavor profile can elevate the dish - for example, pairing earthy carrots with cumin and coriander, or enhancing the bitterness of Brussels sprouts with a pinch of red pepper flakes. Experiment with different combinations to find the perfect harmony of flavors for your roasted vegetables.
