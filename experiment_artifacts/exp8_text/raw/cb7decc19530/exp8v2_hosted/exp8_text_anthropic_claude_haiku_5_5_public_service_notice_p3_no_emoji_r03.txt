**Notice: Changes to Recycling Collection Schedule**

Effective Monday, March 3, recycling collection will change for all residential addresses. Collection will now take place every other week on Tuesdays instead of weekly.

**Key changes:**
- **Collection frequency:** Every other Tuesday, beginning March 4
- **Collection day:** Tuesday (previously Thursday)
- **Bin placement:** Please place recycling bins at the curb by 7:00 a.m. on collection days

**Accepted materials remain the same:** Clean paper, cardboard, glass bottles and jars, metal cans, and plastic containers marked 1 or 2. Please rinse containers and flatten cardboard boxes before placing them in your bin.

Your green waste and general waste collection schedules are not affected by this change.

For questions or to request a replacement bin, contact the Public Works Department at (555) 123-4567 or visit our website.

Thank you for your cooperation.
