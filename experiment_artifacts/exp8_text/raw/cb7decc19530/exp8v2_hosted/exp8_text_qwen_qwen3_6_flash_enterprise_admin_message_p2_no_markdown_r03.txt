Subject: Implementation of New Expense Approval Workflow

Hello Team,

We are rolling out a new expense approval workflow designed to improve efficiency and streamline reimbursements.

This change takes effect on November 1, 2023. Please note the following updates:

All expense reports must now be submitted via the updated online portal. Expenses exceeding $500 require secondary approval from department heads. Additionally, digital copies of all receipts are mandatory; scanned images are no longer accepted for verification purposes.
