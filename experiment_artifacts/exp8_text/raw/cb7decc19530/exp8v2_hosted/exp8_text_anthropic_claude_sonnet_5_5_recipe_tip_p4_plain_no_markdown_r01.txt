Cook once and assemble all week. On the weekend, make a big batch of two or three basic components, such as a grain like rice or quinoa, a roasted vegetable, and a protein like chicken, beans, or hard-boiled eggs. Store them separately in containers so nothing gets soggy. Each morning or the night before, combine them in a different way: a grain bowl one day, a wrap the next, a salad topped with the leftover protein after that. Keep a couple of sauces or dressings on hand to change the flavor, and your lunches will feel varied without requiring much effort. If you also set aside ten minutes to decide which days you will bring lunch and which you might eat out, you will avoid both wasted food and last-minute scrambling.
