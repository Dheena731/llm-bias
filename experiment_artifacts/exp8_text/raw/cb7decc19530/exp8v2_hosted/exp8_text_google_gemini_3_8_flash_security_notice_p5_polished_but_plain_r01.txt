If you have misplaced your company-issued laptop or suspect it has been stolen, you must report the incident immediately to the IT Help Desk and the Information Security team. Prompt reporting is critical because it allows our technical team to quickly lock the device, revoke access credentials, and remotely erase sensitive business data to prevent unauthorized access. When you reach out, please provide your employee ID, the approximate date and time the laptop went missing, its last known location, and a brief summary of the circumstances. If the device was stolen in a public space or during travel, you must also file a report with local law enforcement and provide the police report number to the security team once it is available. Company policy prioritizes data protection above all else, so please report the loss right away, even if you are not entirely certain whether the device is permanently lost or merely misplaced.
