Rinsing rice before cooking is one of the simplest ways to keep it from turning gummy. Place the measured grains in a fine-mesh strainer and run cold water over them, gently stirring with your fingers, until the water runs mostly clear. This washes away the surface starch that causes grains to clump together. Drain well, then cook using your usual water ratio. For extra fluffy results, let the cooked rice rest covered off the heat for about ten minutes before fluffing it with a fork.
