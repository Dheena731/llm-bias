**Toss before the pan, and finish with acid.** 

Toss your vegetables in a large bowl with oil, salt, and dried spices *before* spreading them on the baking sheet to ensure every piece is evenly coated. Then, the moment they come out of the oven, hit them with a squeeze of fresh lemon juice or a splash of vinegar—the hit of acid cuts through the richness and makes the caramelized flavors pop.
