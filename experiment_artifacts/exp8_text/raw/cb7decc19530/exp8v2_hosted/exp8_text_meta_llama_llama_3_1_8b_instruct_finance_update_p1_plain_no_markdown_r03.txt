We have introduced a new monthly spending summary that will provide a detailed breakdown of your expenditures. This summary will be sent to you at the beginning of each month, highlighting your total spending for the previous month, as well as categorizing your expenses into areas such as housing, transportation, food, and entertainment. The summary will also include a comparison to your previous month's spending, allowing you to track any changes or trends in your spending habits. This new summary is designed to help you stay on top of your finances and make informed decisions about your money.
