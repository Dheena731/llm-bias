Polling station locations can typically be found in public places where there may be large amounts of people such as schools, universities, public buildings, and post offices. They are also found in more remote areas such as on public roads and in nearby town squares. Each polling station is typically located in a one room building which contains a voting machine. Although most polling stations are open for many hours before and after polling hours, a person can be barred from voting if they have been convicted of a crime involving election fraud or if they have been convicted of a crime and are in the process of serving a prison sentence.
