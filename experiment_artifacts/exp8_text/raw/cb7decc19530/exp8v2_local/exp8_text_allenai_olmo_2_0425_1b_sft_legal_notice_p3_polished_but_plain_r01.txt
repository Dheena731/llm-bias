Cookie Usage Notice

Cookies on this site are placed on your computer to provide enhanced functionality and a better browsing experience. This notice explains how we use cookies.

What are Cookies?

A cookie is a small amount of data stored on your computer by a website. A cookie cannot contain harmful or malicious code. Cookies are used to:

1. Save data about how users interact with our site.
2. Load and display content based on user preferences.
3. Collect anonymous information about how users use our site.
4. Allow site administrators to see which pages users have visited.

How Do We Use Cookies?

Cookies are used to:

1. Optimize performance. By using cookies, we can improve the performance of our website.
2. Collect anonymous information to help us improve the site.
3. Provide targeted advertising.
4. Provide personalized content.

Do Not Use

We ask that you do not use any part of the cookie to:

1. Track or record your browsing history.
2. Track personal information about individuals.
3. Collect personal information.
4. Collect sensitive information, such as passwords.

If you do not want cookies to be placed on your computer, you may adjust your privacy settings. To do this, please look for a setting for cookies on your browser and turn off the option to accept cookies. 

If you do not accept cookies, you may see less functionality on our site. If you have any questions about cookies or our use of them, please contact our support team.
