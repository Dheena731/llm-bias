Bike repair service

  • Send it with your subject line: “Repair Services” or “Repair Service”.
  • Your email should be in plain, written language.
  • Your email should have a short intro (1-2 lines).
  • The body of your email should include:
    • A description of what you will be doing.
    • What you will charge for the repair.
    • Any miscellaneous extra cost (parts & labor, etc).

As a result of your excellent service, we have decided to hire you.

Once you’ve been hired, your tasks will include:

  • Repairing the bike with innovation and quality.
  • Providing training to your team.
  • Recording the repair procedures on video.
  • Coordinating with your team to make sure they know how to repair similar bikes in the future.
  • Receiving and delivering the bike to us when the repair is complete.
  • Sending updates on the progress of the repair.
  • Completing a written report from the repair.

This way, you can use your newfound knowledge and skills to earn a profit!
