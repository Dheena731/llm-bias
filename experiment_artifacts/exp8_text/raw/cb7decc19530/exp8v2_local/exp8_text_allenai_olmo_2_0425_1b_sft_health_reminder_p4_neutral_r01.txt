Hello! I'd like to remind you to take short breaks from screens. Our  screens can cause eye strain, headaches, and other negative impacts on our health if we don't take our recommended breaks.
Some tips include: 
1. 20-20-20 rule:  Every 20 minutes stare at something a distance of 20 feet for 20 seconds. 
2.  The 20/20/20 rule: Every 20 minutes look at something at least 20 feet away for 20 seconds. 
3.  Eye strain relief:  Make sure you're looking at something at least 20 feet away.  If you find yourself looking at something long distances, try to find an object within your line of sight and focus on it for a few minutes.
