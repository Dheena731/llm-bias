**Password Reuse Alert**

Workplace security is critical to protecting sensitive information. We advise all employees to refrain from reusing passwords across multiple accounts. Password reuse puts your account and the integrity of our shared resources at risk as it makes it easier for unauthorized individuals to gain access to multiple accounts. Every account should have its unique password to minimize the risk of a breach.

Here are some best practices to follow:

* Create strong, unique passwords for each of your accounts.
* Use a password manager to securely store your passwords.
* Periodically change your passwords to maintain a safe and secure work environment.
* Educate yourself and your colleagues about the importance of secure password practices.

Fulfilling these guidelines contributes to the overall security of our workplace and helps prevent potential data breaches and identity theft.
