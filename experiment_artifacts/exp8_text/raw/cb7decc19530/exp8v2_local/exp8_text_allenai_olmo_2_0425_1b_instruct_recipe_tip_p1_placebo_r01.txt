The best part of cooking a weeknight dinner involves a simple yet delicious recipe such as Chili con Carne (Spiced Beef or Chicken Chili) on the stove top. Place a heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat and add 2 pounds of quality beef chuck or cubed chicken. Season generously with salt, pepper, and your favorite chili powder. Once the beef or chicken starts to brown, add ½ large onion diced, 2 cloves of garlic minced, a can of crushed tomato purée, 1 can of your preferred brand of chili sauce, 2 cans of your favorite brand of corn, and 2 cups of your preferred type of beans (like black beans or pinto beans). Stir well to combine all ingredients and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for about 30 minutes or until the beef or chicken is perfectly cooked and the chili is luxurious and creamy from the sauce. Serve with tortillas, a fresh green salad, and a fresh slice of lime for an indulgent and comforting dinner that's ready in no time!
