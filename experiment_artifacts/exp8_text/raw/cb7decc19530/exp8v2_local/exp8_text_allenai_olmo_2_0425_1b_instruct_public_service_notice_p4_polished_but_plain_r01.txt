Heat Advisory in Effect

Due to high temperatures this week we are issuing a heat advisory. The mercury is expected to stay in the upper 90s and even into the 100s for most of the week. High humidity levels will exacerbate the heat making it feel even hotter. It is important for people to take precautions against the intense heat:

* Stay indoors as much as possible
* Avoid being outside during the heat of the day
* Drink plenty of water
* Monitor your heart and respiratory rates
* Avoid using elevators when possible

Stay safe and cool!
