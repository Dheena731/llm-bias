The card transaction fees have historically been charged at a flat rate per transaction, regardless of the actual amount processed. To make the service more competitive and support the financial institutions, the card transaction fees have been increased. This means that transactions will now be charged at a higher rate per transaction.

This policy will supposedly lead to more customers using cards for their transactions and ultimately lead to more profits for the bank. It should be noted that the increased fees are also being passed on to the merchants, which should result in increased prices for end users.
