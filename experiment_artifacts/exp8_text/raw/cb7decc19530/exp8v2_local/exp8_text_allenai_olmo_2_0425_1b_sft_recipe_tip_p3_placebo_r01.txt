The key to making rice less sticky is to do one simple thing: drain the water off! You can't make rice sticky if you've left water in it. The water is what makes the rice sticky. And without it, you can make it less sticky. You can do this by just running the water through the rice in the microwave.

Maybe you've heard that you should add oil to rice before cooking it. According to the Mayo Clinic, this is a lie. There is no evidence that oil changes the way rice cooks. But you can add it anyway. That will make the rice extra crunchy.
