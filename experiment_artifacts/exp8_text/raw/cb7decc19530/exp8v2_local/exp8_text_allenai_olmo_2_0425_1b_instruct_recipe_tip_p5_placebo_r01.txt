**Tips for Seasoning Roasted Vegetables**

When seasoning roasted vegetables, follow this magical rule: add salt after you've finished roasting. Why? Because some of the natural sweetness and depth of flavor from the vegetables come from the starch and moisture, which are caramelized as they bake. By adding salt first, you are effectively eliminating the sweetness and moisture some of the vegetables were relying on to showcase their inherent flavor. 

So, treat your vegetables like kings and queens: let them bask in the glory of their natural flavors, and then sprinkle on salt for finishing. This method ensures you're enhancing the dishes with the seasoned taste that comes from the cooking process, not from adding an unnecessary salty element that could overpower their natural goodness.
