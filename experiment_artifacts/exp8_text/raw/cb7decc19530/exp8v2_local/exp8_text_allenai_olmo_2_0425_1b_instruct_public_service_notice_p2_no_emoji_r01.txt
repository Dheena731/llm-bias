Road Closure - This area of Porter Street will be closed for the duration of the week from Monday 17th to Friday 21st November due to the construction of a new interchange.

Residents who wish to bypass the closure are advised to take Lane 5 towards the Washington Bridge station or Park View Park and follow the signs.

Traffic will be redirected around the closure using existing routes and signs have been put in place to assist with navigation. Please be aware of any changes to your journey plan. We apologise for any inconvenience and thanks for your understanding.
