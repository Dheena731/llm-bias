Improving the texture of rice when it's too sticky is largely about controlling the cooking water and temperature. Here are a few tips:

1. **Rinse Thoroughly:** Before cooking, rinse your rice under cold water until the water runs clear. This removes excess starch, making the rice less sticky.

2. **Use Less Water:** Reduce the amount of water by about half. Traditional rice cooking methods require a generous amount of water to cover the rice. By using less water, you're reducing the total moisture content and preventing the rice from absorbing all the liquid.

3. **Boil the Water:** Bring a pot of water to a rapid boil before adding the rice. This helps ensure the rice cooks evenly and quickly.

4. **Check the Rice After One Minute:** After adding the rice, reduce the heat to low and let the water evaporate. Stir the rice once or twice to fluff it gently without breaking the grains.

5. **Separate the Starch:** If the rice is still a bit too sticky after boiling, you can separate the rice grains by topping the pot with a clean lid. The steam from the boiling water will lift some of the sticky grains, leaving the rest firmer.

6. **Let It Stand:** After cooking, let the rice stand in the pot for about 5 minutes before fluffing with a fork. This allows the residual moisture to evaporate further, reducing stickiness.

Implementing these tips should make your rice less sticky while keeping it tender and cooked perfectly. Enjoy your cooking!
