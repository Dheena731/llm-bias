Whether you are hitting the gym, going for a jog, or doing yoga, it’s important to regularly review your weekly fitness progress. Take a moment each week to assess how well you've met your goals, set new ones, or adjust your plan as necessary.

**Here are some ways to track your fitness journey:**

- **Set SMART goals:** Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound. They will help you focus your efforts and know when you’ve achieved success.
- **Keep a fitness diary:** Write down your workouts, the intensity, duration, and any noteworthy changes in your body or mood.
- **Use a fitness app:** There are plenty of apps designed to help you keep track of your workouts, progress, and even reward yourself for milestones. This digital record can be a powerful motivator.
- **Reflect on your week:** What went well? Were there any unexpected challenges? How can you improve moving forward?
- **Avoid comparing yourself to others:** Fitness is personal; what works for someone else might not work for you. Celebrate your individual progress and achievements.

Remember, reviewing your progress is a crucial part of any fitness journey. It keeps you motivated and helps prevent plateaus or unwanted setbacks that could deter your progress. So keep a mental note and keep going!
