No structure

A poor summary will be illogical and unstructured. We want clear and easy to understand.


A summary for an article or report should correctly identify and explain the main point of each paragraph. 

Formal writing 

A summary should be formal. This means staying away from slang or colloquial language. 

No originality

Avoid paraphrasing entire paragraphs. Summaries, by definition, should condense the content. That means you will be re-communicating key points verbatim.

A good summary paragraph should give the reader an idea of what to expect in the content.

The body of the content should always be consistent with the ideas proposed in the summary. The only reason you need to make a summary is because it's a short and concise version of the content.


A summary paragraph can be a helpful device to condense an entire blog or article. It's a great tool to communicate the main point of the content. It shouldn't be too long or too short, but should contain the crux of the article.


You should be very careful with summaries. The risk is that your summary may mark the difference between the article and the “scrap” of the article. A summary is never a summary: rather it is a condensation of the content. If you want a good summary, read the article carefully, and then write in a way that summarizes only the main points. Don't summarize it word for word. It's not a summary. It's a summary paragraph.


A summary is a useful tool that enables your readers to get a condensed version of your content. If you use it correctly, you can communicate to your audience the most important information. A summary is a good way to explain the concepts of the article, but be careful not to write a summary word for word. A summary is not a summary, but a summary paragraph.

Use a summary

A summary can be a great way to communicate the essence of the content to other people who might not have the same level of expertise as you. A summary can be a useful tool for professionals to discuss the issues and communicate as necessary. The key is to pay attention to the details and make sure the summary is concise and accurate.
