Alaska: Thunderstorms will be common along the Gulf Coast

A series of thunderstorms will move across the Gulf Coast of Mississippi and Louisiana as temperatures reach the mid-80s Fahrenheit. In addition to possible heavy rain, the storms will allow the air to cool and produce gusty winds. Expect a few downpours and possible isolated severe thunderstorms. 

The thunderstorms are expected to develop through the evening and overnight. Thunderstorms will persist overnight on Sunday into Monday, though the air will still be quite warm and humid.

Nevada: Heat will lead to increased humidity

Throughout the Las Vegas Valley, temperatures will reach into the 90s Fahrenheit. Along the eastern side of the valley, at least a few afternoon thunderstorms are expected to develop with isolated severe thunderstorms. The most likely severe threat will be wind and hail. 

The days will be warm through Saturday evening, with the high temperature in the 90s. On Sunday and through the end of the week, temperatures will gradually cool as the air begins to dry out.

A heat index of 113 to 119 degrees Fahrenheit is expected for much of the valley, with the valley at 114 degrees Fahrenheit. Temperatures above 120 degrees Fahrenheit are not expected for this time period.
