We want to stress the importance of vigilance in detecting and avoiding phishing emails. Here are some common indicators that an email may be phishing:

- Emails that ask for sensitive information, such as passwords, credit card numbers, or personal identification numbers, under false pretenses.
- Emails with spelling and grammatical errors.
- Emails that come from unfamiliar sources or sources that send spam.
- Emails that contain urgent or threatening language.
- Emails that request immediate action, such as clicking on a link to change personal information.
- Emails that request personal information via email and don’t provide a secure method (such as a website address beginning with "https://").

If you encounter such emails, do not click on any links or provide any personal information. If you suspect phishing, please report it to your IT department immediately.
