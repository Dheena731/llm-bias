Please remember to lock your computer screens when you step away from your workstations. Leaving your screens unlocked could expose sensitive information to potential security risks. We value your time and the integrity of our company's data, and we ask that you take a moment to secure your workstation. It is as simple as unlocking your screen lock feature and ensuring that it automatically turns off when not in use to contribute to a safer and better working environment for everyone.

Here are some tips for securely locking your workstation:
- Use strong, unique passwords and consider enabling multi-factor authentication.
- Consider using screen locking services integrated with your operating system if available.
- Be cautious with who you share your work area with; ensure anyone entering your workspace knows the proper procedures.
- Regularly review and update your knowledge about cybersecurity best practices for your organization. 

Thank you for your attention to this reminder. It's a small step that makes a big difference in safeguarding our collective information.
