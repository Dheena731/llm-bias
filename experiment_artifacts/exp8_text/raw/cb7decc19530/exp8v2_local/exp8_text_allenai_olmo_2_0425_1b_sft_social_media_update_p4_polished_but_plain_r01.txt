Keep a calendar in your phone for work events, personal activities, and social gatherings. 

Most calendar apps have a 30 day free trial, so take advantage of how much you'll use a calendar. 

For social events, you can't always count on an invitation, but adding the date helps you remember when events are. 

For work, a calendar app can remind you of projects due, deadlines, and holidays. 

Many calendar apps offer additional features like setting reminders for school, work, or personal errands. 

Keep your calendar app organized and you'll be set for life.
