Every couple of years we go through a major update to our website and one of the recommended changes is enabling two-factor authentication. Here’s a quick overview of why you should enable this additional layer of security.

Two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of protection to your account. It prevents unauthorized access even if your password is compromised. Instead of just relying on a password, two-factor authentication requires a second form of verification after you enter your password. This could come in the form of a one-time code sent to your phone or generated by a hardware security token.

The most common way to get this code is via a text message or through a push notification on your phone. This immediately identifies you as the rightful user and prevents anyone who intercepts your login attempt from gaining access.

Here’s how you can enable it on our website:

1. Go to your account settings.
2. Find the security section.
3. Check the box that says “Enable two-factor authentication.” (It looks like a lock icon or a two-headed key.)
4. You may need to receive authentication codes; follow the instructions for setting this up on your phone or device.

By implementing two-factor authentication, you’re significantly bolstering the safety of your account and making it much harder for attackers to gain entry. Remember, it’s just one more thing to remember, but the peace of mind it provides is well worth it.
