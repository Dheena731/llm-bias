A missing-value marker (also known as an "infinite" marker or "NaN" in some contexts) is a symbol used in data analysis and statistical modeling to indicate values that are unknown or cannot be computed. When data collection is incomplete or an attribute doesn't exist (like a missing birth date), it's represented by NaN to flag these spots where data is lacking. Essentially it serves as a placeholder for further analysis or to indicate that a data point is not available or doesn't fit within the current dataset schema.
