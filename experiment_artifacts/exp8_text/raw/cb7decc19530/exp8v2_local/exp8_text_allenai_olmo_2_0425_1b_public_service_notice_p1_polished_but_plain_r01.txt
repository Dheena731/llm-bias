Put special emphasis on the following:

  1. Check the library schedule for the number of hours. Does it match what is on your calendar?
  2. Note the hours in the phone number listed on the library website.
  3. Visit the library on a weekday to verify the hours and the times you visited, if you can.
  4. Make sure the library is open if you have a student ID card and the library says it is open.

What to Do If You're Not Sure

  • If you still aren’t sure, call the library. Ask the person on the phone what he or she thinks.
  • If you have visited the library multiple times and are still not sure, consider asking your librarian to give you a tour (some libraries have a free tour available every Monday and Wednesday afternoon).
  • If you still aren’t sure, call the library. Ask a librarian for help about what to do.
  • If you like, you can write a public-service notice with reminders, but do not use emoji, Markdown, headings, bullet points, numbered lists, bold text, italic text, tables, decorative symbols, or links.
