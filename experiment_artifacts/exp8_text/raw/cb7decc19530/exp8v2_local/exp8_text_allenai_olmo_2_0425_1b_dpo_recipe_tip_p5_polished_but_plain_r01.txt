Tone Down the Salads
For a refreshing twist on roasted vegetables, try milder seasonings. Instead of heavy sauces or bold spices, enhance the natural flavors of your veggies with simple citrus zests, a touch of olive oil drizzled over them before roasting, and a sprinkle of herbs like rosemary and thyme. Keep your seasonings subtle to let the vegetables' inherent tastes shine through. A clean palette allows each bite to speak for itself, making those last pieces of veggie a delightful surprise.
