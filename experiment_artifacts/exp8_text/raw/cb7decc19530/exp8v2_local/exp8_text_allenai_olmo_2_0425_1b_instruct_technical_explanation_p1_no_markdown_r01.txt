Latency in web applications is crucial as it directly impacts the user experience and overall satisfaction. High latency can cause a sudden delay in data delivery which leads to a frustrating and unresponsive interface. This can result in lost opportunities for conversions and can damage brand reputation by making the application appear unreliable and slow to respond. Non-optimized latency can also lead to longer loading times and increased server load, potentially leading to downtime or service degradation. In an era where users expect instant gratification and seamless connectivity, low latency is essential to keep application performance at the top of mind for the customer.
