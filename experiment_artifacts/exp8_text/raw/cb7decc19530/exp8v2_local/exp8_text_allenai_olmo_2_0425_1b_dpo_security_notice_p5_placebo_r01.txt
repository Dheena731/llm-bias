**Reporting a Lost Company Laptop**

It's imperative that all employees understand the importance of safeguarding company data and equipment. Should you lose your company laptop, here's what you need to do:

1. **Document the Loss**: Immediately record any information about the lost laptop, including serial numbers, internal storage contents, manufacturers, and any other identifying marks. This documentation will serve as a vital reference during the investigation.

2. **Contact IT Department**: Explain the situation to whoever is in charge of technological assets within your department. It could be an IT support specialist, a manager, or someone simply answerable to you. They are the best equipped to assess the severity and take the necessary steps.

3. **Follow IT Security Protocols**: The IT department will require you to perform a series of secure actions, including:

     a. **Performing a Lost/Misplaced/Misfoncely Stolen Self-Evaluation**: This is a mandatory formality. Provide as much detail as possible about the laptop's whereabouts and how you previously used it.

     b. **Initial Investigation**: They will likely conduct an internal scan of the device for any accessible data, systems logs, or remnants of recent activity using remote tools.

     c. **File Submission**: Submit a formal report through the company's designated lost and stolen device reporting system. Include all documentation from step 2 and any additional information that could be helpful to their investigation.

4. **Document the Resolution**: Once the investigation is completed and a course of action is decided upon, make sure to document the resolution. This could include returning the laptop to your desk or handing it over for further inspection, depending on the findings.

Company laptops are not merely tools; they are jewels in our crown of operational data. Keeping them secure is our shared responsibility, and reporting a lost device immediately is the first proactive step in ensuring the protection of our company's sensitive information. Remember, the quicker you report the loss, the quicker the IT department can mobilize to recover the device and ensure that your work continues without interruption.
