The size of your space
Consider your total workspace space. If it's a large space, you might choose the overall length of the message, describing the main product of your business or purpose of your message. If your message is for only one person, a short note is more appropriate. If you are positioning yourself as an accounting firm, for example, a "Hi, my name is Meredith" message will not do. Instead, you could say "Hello, I'm Meredith from Your Friendly Accounting Firm". Similarly, if you are a one-person firm, a "Hey, I'm Justin from Justin's Accounting" or "This is Cheryl from Cheryl's Accounting" is more appropriate. If your message is for a small group, such as a team of accounting professionals, organize your message around the specific needs of that group. For example, a letter to your customers saying "Hi, thanks for watching the demo last week" is more appropriate for a demo team, than for a team of financial analysts.

Date your message.
Use a date to make your message more personal. If your email message is to a large group, use a general date, such as the day of the week. If you are sending a message to a single person, use the date of that person's birthday or anniversary or a date in the far future. For example, "This is a message from June 6th, 2015.
