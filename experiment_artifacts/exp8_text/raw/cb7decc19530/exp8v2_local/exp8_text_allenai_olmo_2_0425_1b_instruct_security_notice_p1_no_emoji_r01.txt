Workplace Security Notice: Password Reuse - 

It is risky to reuse passwords across multiple accounts. This increases the risk of unauthorized access to your workplace resources and other online accounts. Every account should have a unique password. If you have previously used a password at your workplace, consider changing it or using a password manager to securely store and generate new, unique passwords. 

Please be aware that phishing attempts and password guessing attacks are becoming more sophisticated. Ensure your online security is robust by using up-to-date antivirus software, enabling multi-factor authentication when available, and being vigilant about suspicious activity. 

Thank you for your attention to this important matter.
