Refund Eligibility

All refund claims need to contain a description of the equipment, make and model of the vehicle, and a detailed description of each claim.

Since there are so many variables that could impact a claim, this requires a lot of detailing.

An email or fax copy of the original bill for the month is required.

If you are not sure if your bill is correct, please contact us to be sure.

If you have any questions, please call our office to speak with one of our authorized representatives. We can answer your questions and provide you with the necessary forms.

Your Refund Request will be processed and a refund will be issued within 4-5 business days.
