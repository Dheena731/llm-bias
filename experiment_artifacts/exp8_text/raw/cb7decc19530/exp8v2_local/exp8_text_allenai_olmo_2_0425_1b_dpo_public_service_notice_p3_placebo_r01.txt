**Note on Recycling Collection Changes**

Effective January 1, 2023, the City of [Your City/Organization Name Here] is making significant alterations to our recycling collection schedule. As a community, we are committed to fostering an environment that promotes sustainability and reduces waste. Here’s what you need to know:

* **Recycling Schedule Update**: Starting this year, our residential recycling program will operate on a Monday through Friday schedule from 7 AM to 7 PM. Please ensure your recycling bags are prepared and ready for collection on these days only.

* **Bulk Bin Shift**: To accommodate this schedule change, our bulk bin division will be moving the collection times around. Your local bin locations will see a shift from an afternoon drop-off to an earlier morning collection. Check your local bin map for the new hours.

* **Additional Pickup Days**: We are adding two additional days to our recycling collection calendar, Saturday and Sunday, which will provide a broader window for residents to participate. Remember to stockpile your recyclables in readiness for these extended collection periods.

* **Inventive Stencils**: For simplification purposes, we have introduced a new set of "inventive stencils." These are simple shapes and symbols that represent what can be recycled. They are intended to help streamlining the sorting process and ensure that residents understand exactly what can be recycled.

* **Current Recycling Guidelines**: Please ensure all your recyclable materials are in the assigned stencils to ensure they make it to the recycling facility intact. Terms like plastic, paper, glass, aluminum, and biodegradable are included. However, we encourage creativity and adaptation; if there’s anything you don’t recognize in the stencil, please drop it off at your local collection point for further review.

* **Public Awareness Campaign**: We are rolling out a comprehensive public awareness campaign to educate residents on the new recycling guidelines and encourage more efficient waste management. Stay tuned for social media updates, local presentations, and our website for further instructions and tips.

**Wrap-up**: Our changes aim to enhance the efficiency of our recycling program while keeping it accessible and user-friendly. We invite feedback and observations, as we strive to improve our waste management system even further.

**Reminder**: Don't forget, regular recycling collection hours are adjusted starting January 1, 2023, Monday through Friday from 7 AM to 7 PM. For detailed information on new collection hours at your specific location, please visit our website or contact our customer service team.

**Stay green, stay informed, and most of all, recycle responsibly!**
