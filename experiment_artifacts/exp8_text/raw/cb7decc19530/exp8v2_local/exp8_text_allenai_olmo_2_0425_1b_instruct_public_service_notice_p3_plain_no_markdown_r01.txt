If you've been following along with our community efforts to promote environmental responsibility then you might have noticed that our recycling collection schedule has undergone some changes. We understand that managing waste and ensuring recycling is done correctly can be a bit confusing but rest assured that we're making these updates for the benefit of everyone in the community.

The recyclable materials that were previously accepted at home might not be picking up as frequently now. Let's talk about what remains the same and what has been adjusted.

1. **Plastic:** We're proud to notify you that plastic bottles, jugs, caps, and trays are still highly recyclable. Always remember to check your local curbside pickup schedule or visit our website for specific collection days for plastic.

2. **Paper:** Our community's paper collection remains active. Make sure to bundle your newspapers, magazines, and cardboard together and place it in your respective recycling bin. Clear plastic bags and coffee cups are also categories that we now collect.

3. **Mixed Recycling:** The blue bin that contains paper, glass, and metal for recycling will be collected as usual. Please ensure the contents are clean and dry as contamination is a large issue for our recycling team.

4. **Battery and Electrical Waste:** Computers, telephones, and other electronic devices with lithium-ion batteries should be taken to local drop-off locations for proper disposal, as recycling them incorrectly can harm the environment.

5. **Glass:** Glass bottles and jars are always welcome at the curb. Clear glass with no markings or labels is ideal for collection but any clean and unmarked glass can be included.

If you have any questions or concerns about our new collection schedule, contact our recycling center directly at [info@communityrecycle.com]. Your willingness to participate in our recycling efforts makes a big difference in our mission to reduce waste and protect our environment. Thank you for being a part of this important community endeavor!
