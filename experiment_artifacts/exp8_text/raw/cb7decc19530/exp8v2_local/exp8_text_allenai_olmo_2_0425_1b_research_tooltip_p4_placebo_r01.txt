Are the required fields required?
Do not ask questions about the description. A missing-value marker does not mean that the description is missing.

Are the required fields required?
Do not ask questions about the agreement or the terms. A missing-value marker does not mean that the consent or terms are missing. For example: "Do you consent to this use of your data?" and "Do you agree to this use of your data?"

Are the required fields required?
Do not try to "sell" the user on the use of the data. The user is presented with a message box, and is not persuaded to engage.

Do not put "complete my survey" at the end.

Do not put "yes" or "no" in the question. "Yes" is the default answer and "no" does not need to be entered.
