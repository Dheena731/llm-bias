To make a simple yet satisfying weeknight dinner, start with a base of pre-cooked chicken thighs. toss them with a basic balsamic glaze and let them warm slightly in the oven. while they cook, puree cooked carrots and celery in a blender. add in softened garlic and a splash of sherry or dry white wine. season with salt and pepper. spoon the veggie mixture back into the chicken, along with a drizzle of glaze. bake until the chicken is tender and the sauce thickens to a nice depth. serve with rice or stuffed bell peppers for a twist. this dish is perfect for five minutes of prep and minutes more of ooey-gooey goodness.
