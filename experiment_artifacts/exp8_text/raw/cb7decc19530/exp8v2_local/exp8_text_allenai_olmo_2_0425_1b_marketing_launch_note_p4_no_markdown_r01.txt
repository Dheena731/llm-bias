Get ready to deliver

If you're doing a lot of copywriting, then you're probably already generating and pursuing your leads. Delivering your copy is key to your success, and you can't leave it to chance. If you're just beginning, there are numerous ways to begin delivering your copy. 

The first option is to connect with an agency, and the second is to stand on your own. Both options are great, and you can pick and choose which you prefer.

If you're already delivering your copy, then you should make it easy for your audience to request the content. Use a dedicated landing page (see next section), and make it easy to share links with friends and colleagues using tools like Facebook and Twitter.


What's a landing page?

In the world of online marketing, a landing page is a page that serves a specific purpose. Landing pages often have one main value proposition. To be useful, a landing page should also have clear navigation. It should be easy to navigate to a particular value proposition and then take action. Any value proposition that you're promoting should be shared with an email list. 

A landing page should contain clear call to action (CTA) buttons or links that clearly, and immediately, direct the reader to where you want them to go.

Your landing page should be a standalone piece of content with links, credentials, and contact information. 

There are numerous ways to build a landing page.

Landing pages can be built from scratch or can be based on existing templates. You can build a landing page in a variety of ways. Most landing pages are built in WordPress through the Visual Editor. You can also build a landing page in WordPress through the Builder, but you need to know how to use the visual editor. 

Landing pages are generally built by marketers who specialize in landing pages. Landing page design and development requires expertise in HTML, CSS frameworks like Bootstrap, and advanced JavaScript. A landing page may be built from scratch or may be a copy and paste of a template. Landing pages can be built in WordPress by using the Visual Editor or in WordPress Builder.

The choice is up to you.

If you pick a template, then you need to tune it to fit your project, but it's less work than if you were to build everything from scratch. When you choose a template, you get a simple landing page with a header, a body, and navigational elements on the side. 


You can use a landing page to drive traffic to your website. You can also use a landing page to drive traffic to a copywriting service such as a content marketing agency.

Your landing page will be specific to the copy you're promoting.
