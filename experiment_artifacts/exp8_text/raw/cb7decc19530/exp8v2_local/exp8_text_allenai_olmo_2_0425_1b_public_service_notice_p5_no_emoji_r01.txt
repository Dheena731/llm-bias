In this section you'll need to identify the location of polling stations, their available hours and their entrance requirements (such as being a registered voter or a citizen of the country). Make sure that the information is accurate. If necessary, ask the local electoral body for more information, because some polling stations are not publicly available. When you've finished, highlight the text between the double quotes (") to indicate the information has been included.

Include the following:

  • The name of the polling station
  • The location of the polling station
  • The opening hours of the polling station, including if they are open on Sundays and other public holidays
  • The name and contact details of the person who can help you to vote at the polling station
  • The information about the entrance requirements to the polling station (such as being a registered voter or a citizen of the country for example)
  • The location of the polling station

Note: If you have an iPhone use the Public polling station finder in the App Store. You can search by postcode or name.
