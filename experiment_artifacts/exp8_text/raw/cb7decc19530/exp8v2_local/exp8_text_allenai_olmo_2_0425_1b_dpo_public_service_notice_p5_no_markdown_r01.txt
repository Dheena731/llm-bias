Here is a short notice about polling station locations:

Polling stations are located in various public places such as schools libraries town halls gymnasiums libraries post offices grocery stores and other community centers. These locations are chosen to ensure accessibility for all eligible voters. Each polling station is staffed by trained election officials who manage the voting process and ensure the security and integrity of the election. Polling stations typically open around 7 AM and close shortly after 8 PM on election day. It is important to visit your designated polling station on Election Day to exercise your right to vote. For details on specific polling station locations or further information on the voting process, please visit the official election website.
