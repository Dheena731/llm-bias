Reminder for Prescribing Professionals: When the refills on your prescription run out, remember to: 

1. **Verify the Prescription:** Confirm the prescriptions are still accurate by comparing with the original doc note and records.
   
2. **Check Stock Levels:** Use your electronic prescribing system to see if there are remaining prescriptions to be refilled.
   
3. **Prepare Necessary Documentation:** Ensure you have all relevant patient information and any necessary refilling instructions ready for the pharmacy upon deposit or upload.
   
4. **Coordinate Refills:** Communicate with the patient to set reminders for refills and maintain continuity of care, especially if the patient has multiple prescriptions.

5. **Record Refills:** Update your electronic prescribing system with each refill to reflect the current prescription status and to streamline interactions with the pharmacy for future refills.

Remember, consistency in pharmacy procedures enhances patient care and reduces errors, contributing positively to both your professional reputation and patient satisfaction.
