If you have discovered a lost company laptop, it is crucial to report the incident as soon as possible. Lost company laptops contain sensitive information, company data, and sometimes financial records, which must be securely handled and reported to the appropriate authorities and the IT department for proper secure disposal or recovery.

**Action Steps:**

1. **Identify the Laptop:** Confirm if the laptop is truly lost or has been physically stolen.
2. **Contact IT Department:** Reach out to your IT department or IT service provider immediately to report the find and explain the situation.
3. **Follow the Procedure:** Follow any internal reporting guidelines your company has in place. This may involve filling out an IT asset disposition form, notifying your supervisor or manager, and sometimes reporting the incident to local law enforcement if the laptop has personal or sensitive information on it.
4. **Secure Information:** If the data on the laptop is still accessible, secure it by wiping it clean or using data destruction tools. Contact professional services if you're unsure about data recovery processes.
5. **Document the incident:** Keep a record of the date, time, and circumstances of finding the laptop, including any details that could help identify its owner.

**Why Report?**

- **Retention of Information:** Data on lost company laptops must be properly disposed of to prevent unauthorized access.
- **Legal Obligations:** Depending on the sensitivity of the data and the location where it is stored, there may be legal obligations to properly manage the laptop's contents.
- **Professional Reputation:** Missing laptops can damage your company's professional image, so it's important to handle the situation effectively.

By promptly reporting the incident, you're contributing to the security and integrity of your company's data, and ensuring that any potential risks are mitigated.
