We are writing to inform you about the risks associated with password reuse. When two or more users have the same password, it creates a vulnerability in the system that makes it easier for unauthorized individuals to access the information. This can result in data breaches, account takeover, and unauthorized access to sensitive information or systems. To protect yourself and your company from these risks, we request that you create and use unique passwords for each of your accounts.

If you are unable to remember all of your passwords, we recommend using a password manager. These programs can help you securely store and manage your passwords without compromising your security.

Once a password is compromised for one of your accounts, it can be used to breach other accounts. Therefore, the best practice is to change passwords frequently and avoid reusing them. 

Please be aware of the consequences of password reuse and act accordingly.
