Plan ahead for lunches on a busy week. Start by identifying meals that can be made on Wednesday or Sunday for easy weekday respite. Create a shopping list to ensure you have the essentials and avoid last-minute rushes. Prioritize meals that require ingredients to be stored in the refrigerator or freezer, such as sandwiches and salads, to save time on prep. Experiment with different meal combinations for variety. Divide meals into prep and cook days to manage workload efficiently. Consider leftovers for lunches on days when there is a last-minute crunch for meals. Adjust portion sizes based on the number of people and any dietary restrictions that need to be addressed. Don't forget to factor in breaks and downtime on crowded workdays.
