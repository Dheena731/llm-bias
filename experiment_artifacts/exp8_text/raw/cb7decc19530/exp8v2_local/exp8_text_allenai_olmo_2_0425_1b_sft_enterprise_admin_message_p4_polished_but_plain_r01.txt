Dear valued member,

In our continuing journey to enhance our infrastructure and support our members, we are once again reaching out to inform you of our quarterly access review schedule. 

Our team of experts diligently conducts these review sessions to ensure that all accounts remain secure and accessible to you. These reviews are conducted to validate that your account meets our rigorous security standards and provide you with the best possible user experience.

Due to the sensitive nature of your information, access review periods are scheduled between the third and fifteenth weeks of each quarter. We do not conduct these reviews during these periods, nor do we accept new accounts during this time. 

We understand that these frequencies may present challenges for some members. We ask that, at the end of each quarter, members inform us of their availability for the next review. We will ensure that your access is not affected by these reviews.

Should you require assistance with your account or any questions regarding your access, please reach out to our support team. We are here to help and are committed to ensuring the continued security of your account.

Your continued support and use of our services is invaluable to us. Thank you for your continued trust, and we look forward to serving you to the best of our ability moving forward.
