The End-of-Year Tax Statement is a critical document that summarizes your federal, state, and any additional taxes paid for the year. It's important to understand each section to ensure accurate report of your tax obligations for the upcoming year. Key components typically include:

1. **Total Tax Liability:** Summarizes the total tax you owe for the year, whether it's income tax, excise taxes, sales taxes, etc.

2. **Tax Bracket Information:** Details the tax rates applied to your income at different brackets (e.g., 10%, 12%, 13.3%, etc.).

3. **Federal Income Tax:** Breakdown of taxes paid at each federal income tax bracket. It shows how different sources of income (like wages, investment income, etc.) have been taxed during the year.

4. **State Tax Obligation:** Information about state income tax, if applicable, and any other relevant state taxes paid.

5. **Other Tax Obligations:** Might include self-employment tax, dividend and interest taxes, etc.

6. **Total Tax Paid:** Combines federal and state tax amounts to give the complete picture of what you owe.

Understanding and keeping a tally of these figures is essential for making informed decisions with your tax affairs going into the new year. It's also a good practice to review your tax return periodically, to ensure accuracy and to understand your tax obligations better.
