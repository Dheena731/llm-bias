A missing-value marker, sometimes referred to as an NA/NaN (Not Available/Not Numberable) symbol, is a placeholder in a dataset that indicates a missing or unknown value. It's crucial to understand these markers when interpreting and working with datasets, as they can affect analyses and interpretations. For example, in statistical models, missing data can lead to biased results if not appropriately handled, so it's essential to address them when performing data cleaning or analysis. Always note the presence of these markers in your data to accurately represent its completeness and integrity.
