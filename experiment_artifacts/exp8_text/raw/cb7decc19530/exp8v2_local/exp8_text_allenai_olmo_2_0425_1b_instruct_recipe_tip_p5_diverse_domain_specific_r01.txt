Tip: For incredible flavor, embrace the natural sweetness and complexity of roasted vegetables with the right seasoning. Instead of relying on pre-packaged mixes, experiment with a selection of high-quality salts, herbs, and spices. Start with a simple blend of sea salt, finely chopped fresh herbs like rosemary, thyme, and marjoram, and cracked black pepper. The resolution blending these into your roasting vegetables will result in a vegetable that speaks of the earthy notes of the earth itself, complemented by the piquant crackling of the roast. Remember, the key is to let the imperfect flavors emerge, so use a base that allows you to gauge the degree of flavor you desire in each piece of roasted vegetable.
