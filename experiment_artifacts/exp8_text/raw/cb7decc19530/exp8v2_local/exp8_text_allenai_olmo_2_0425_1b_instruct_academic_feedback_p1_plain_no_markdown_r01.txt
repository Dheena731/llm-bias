The literature review presented in the draft is a commendable effort that provides a solid foundation for further research. However there are some areas that could be refined to enhance clarity and thoroughness.

Firstly the review seems to lack a clear structure. It would benefit from a logical organization that starts with a broad overview and proceeds to more specific themes. This will help readers navigate the content more effectively and identify key themes and gaps in the existing literature.

Additionally the citations are mostly concise but could be improved by utilizing parenthetical references for academic sources. This practice not only enhances readability but also strengthens the argument by providing clear evidence for the claims made in the review.

Furthermore the discussion of argumentative gaps is insightful but could be more elaborate. Each identified gap should be examined in detail, providing examples or quotes from relevant studies to support the claims made.

Overall the draft demonstrates a foundational understanding of the topic. With a more organized structure and clearer citations it will be well-positioned to guide future research in this area.
