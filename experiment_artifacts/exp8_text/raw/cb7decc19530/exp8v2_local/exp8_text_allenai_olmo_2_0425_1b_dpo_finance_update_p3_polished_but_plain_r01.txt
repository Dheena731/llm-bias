A new savings goal feature has been introduced! Users can now set specific targets to reach in their savings accounts from the comforts of their personal dashboard. This feature is designed to help individuals track their progress and stay focused on their financial objectives.

With the new feature, users can set up recurring deposits and receive customized alerts to stay informed about how close they are to their goals. Additionally, this addition allows for more detailed tracking of various expenses, giving users a clear picture of where their money is going and how best to allocate it for savings.

To set up a new savings goal, users simply enter the amount they aim to save, the frequency of deposits, and a date range. This information is securely stored on the platform, ensuring user privacy. The platform also provides tips and tricks to help users make the most of their savings efforts.
