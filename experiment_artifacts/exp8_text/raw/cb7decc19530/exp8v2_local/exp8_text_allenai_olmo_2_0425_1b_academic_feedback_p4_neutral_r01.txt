The study was conducted to explore the effectiveness of the listening focus group and virtual conferencing for students with special educational needs and is the first study to examine the impacts of virtual conferencing on students’ academic progress in post-secondary education.

(5 minutes)

Posters for the meeting

Decide on a poster draft for your meeting and paste it up on a wall in the room. Invite the other participants to comment on it.

(5 minutes)

Cite your sources

Evidence check

Consider the following question: is this information a statement of your own ideas or a quotation from your sources? If it's a quotation from your sources, where in the source is it? In what context in the source did you find it?

If the information is a statement of your own opinion, explain who or what your source is. If it puts you in the place of someone else, use quotation marks to indicate this.

(5 minutes)

References and citations

Write out a list of all the information that did not come from you. If it came from someone or something other than you, make that clear. If it came from someone or something that you did not name, cite that person or thing.

(5 minutes)

Other and optional prompts

Describe the poster's focus

What are the poster's main points? What do you want people to take away from reading it?

Describe the poster's structure

What is the first thing a reader sees? What is the second thing? What is the third thing?

Describe the poster's evidence

What makes your poster interesting and useful to other people?

Name the paper

Who wrote the paper? Is it a paper in your class? A paper in a class outside of this one? Do you have to cite the paper if it's a paper in a class outside of this one?

Describe the poster's style

Does the poster have many pictures and colors? What part of the poster was colored? What colours are the computer background and the text?

Describe the poster's layout

What documents does the poster have? How do you know that?

(5 minutes)

Review the poster

Look at the poster and think about what is missing. What detail is missing? What information are you left with a question about?

(5 minutes)

Display the poster

Display the poster around the room.
