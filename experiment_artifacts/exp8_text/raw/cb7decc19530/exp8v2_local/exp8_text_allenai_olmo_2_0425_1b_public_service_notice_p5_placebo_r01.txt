3. If the students have to vote multiple times, make sure you keep track of the number of ballots they have filled in.

5. Contact me if you need help with questions regarding the questions and answers or about the polling station location.

One thing that you should probably not do is to just assign your students a polling station and tell them to go there and vote. Polling stations are usually "closed", or closed to the public. It would be illegal for you to give them the polling station address and go there yourself. It would be illegal for them to go there and vote on their own without you.

So do not just tell your students to vote without giving them the polling station address and polling station name.
