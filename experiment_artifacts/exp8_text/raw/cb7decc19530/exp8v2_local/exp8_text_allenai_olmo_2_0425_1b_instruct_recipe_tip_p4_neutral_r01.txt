Planning lunches for a busy week can seem daunting, but with a few strategic steps, you can ensure that you're always serving nutritious and delicious meals. Here's a short tip:

**Create a Meal Plan with Repetition:**

*   Plan meals that can be prepared in batches. For instance, wrap up a variety of fillings and toppings in reusable wraps or containers and create a week-long meal rotation.
*   Use versatile ingredients that can be used in multiple dishes. This way, you can easily rearrange and repurpose meals to suit changing week demands.
*   Designate 'chalet days' or 'one-week meals' where you plan a mix of dishes, keeping it exciting yet familiar.

**Opt for Quick and Easy:**

*   Choose easy meal components (like pre-cooked meats, pre-packaged salads, or hearty grain bowls) that require minimal prep time.
*   Prepare ingredients in advance for the week, such as on-the-day components like vegetables, cheese, or pre-washed greens. This reduces planning time and increases the chances you'll eat your planned meal.

**Consider Your Dietary Constraints:**

*   Be mindful of dietary restrictions or allergies when planning. This might require extra planning but ensures everyone is fed healthy and safe.
*   Incorporate some 'flex days' into your meal plan, allowing for spontaneous or indulgent meals when you're not in the mood for planning.

By structuring your week around a few consistent meals with variations and a focus on preparation and prep convenience, you can ensure that you're always serving healthy, easy, and satisfying lunches throughout the week.
