When refilling your prescription, be sure to follow these steps:

1. Check expiration date and make sure it's within the recommended range. 

2. Locate the medication you need a refill for and make sure it has been prescribed to you.

3. Find the prescriber and make sure the information matches up. 

4. Visit the pharmacy to get your prescription refilled. 

5. Check the new prescription against your original one to be sure of what medication is being dispensed. 

6. Take your new prescription to a licensed medical professional for a proper evaluation. 

7. Refill your prescription as many times as you need to follow up with any ongoing treatments. 

8. Be sure to follow any instructions on the prescription to ensure your safety.
