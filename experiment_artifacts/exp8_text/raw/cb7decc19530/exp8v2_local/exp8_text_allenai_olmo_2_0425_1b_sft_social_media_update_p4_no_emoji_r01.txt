I have a calendar app that I absolutely love. It helps me stay on top of my work commitments and keeps my life organized.

I never use the "no mark" option.

I'm a sucker for a reminder. I love that it sends me a notification whenever I've got a meeting or an event.

I keep my important appointments in a separate folder and only use the Calendar app for social events or to make sure I haven't forgotten something.

If I have a task for the day, I'll start it with "To Do" on Monday and that way it stays on my mind all day. 

If I forget something, I'll mark it with "Completed" on Tuesday.

I love that I can pin events on the calendar so I can see at a glance what's important!
