If you have misplaced a company laptop please notify your supervisor as soon as possible. The missing device could contain sensitive information such as customer data or company files. Make sure to save any backups or recent snapshots that might still have the data. It is important to report the loss immediately to prevent further unauthorized access or potential data theft. The IT department can be contacted to recover the lost device and may require the laptop to be turned in or scanned for any remaining data. Please do not attempt to access the lost laptop yourself to avoid any risk of further loss or damage.
