To foster better screen time habits and help reduce the strain on our eyes and mental health, it’s important to remember the value of short breaks from screens. Here’s a quick reminder:

**Screen Breaks Matter:**

1. **20-20-20 Rule:** Every 20 minutes, focus on something 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds. This simple rule can help reduce eye strain and distraction from your screen.

2. **Schedule Reviews:** Set aside specific times throughout the day for breaks from screens. These can be times to relax, stretch, drink water, or walk around.

3. **Vision Wellness:** Incorporate activities that improve your overall eye health into your breaks. This could include reading a book, doing some light exercise, or simply taking a few minutes to reflect on your surroundings.

4. **Limit Prolonged Usage:** While it's tempting to spend long hours in front of screens, it’s essential to recognize the toll it can take on our bodies. Taking regular breaks can make a significant difference in maintaining your productivity without compromising your health.

By incorporating these reminders into your day, not only can you avoid the worst effects of screen overuse on your eyesight and overall wellbeing, but you can also improve your daily focus and productivity. Remember, your eyes deserve rest and along with that, a chance to rejuvenate and recharge.
