Please format your note as follows:

  • Use + for headings.
  • $ for math.
  • ** for bold text.
  • _ for italic text.
  • # for numbered lists.
  • | for tables.

Structure and content

We want you to write a notecard that is 350-500 words in length. The body of the note should be a synthesis of the literature review. Be sure to answer each of the following questions (if any) in the body of the note. Each answer should be 50-100 words in length.

  • What are the major arguments or points of your review?
  • Which sources do you think you agree with most?
  • Which sources do you think you disagree with most?
  • Which sources do you think are difficult to read or understand?
  • Which sources do you think use the most evidence?
  • Which sources do you think are most widely cited?
  • Which sources do you think have the best main argument?
  • Which sources do you think offer the most readable main argument?
  • Which sources do you think are the best in terms of balance?
  • Which sources do you think show important limitations? Why/why not?
  • What are the major strengths and weaknesses of your review?
  • Are there any gaps in your review?
  • Are there any sources you would like to include but haven’t found?
  • Did you encounter any major problems when writing your review?

Please do not hesitate to add any additional relevant information. We recommend that you include a link to the full review in your body as well.
