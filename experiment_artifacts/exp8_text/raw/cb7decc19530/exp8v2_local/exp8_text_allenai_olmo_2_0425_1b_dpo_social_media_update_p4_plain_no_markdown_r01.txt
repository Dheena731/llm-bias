Are you struggling to keep track of your important appointments and deadlines? Use a calendar app to take full advantage of your planner! Create multiple calendars for different tasks or seasons, and color code them for quick identification. Set reminders for your favorite days of the week and change the alarm sound to something cheerful. Remember to look at your calendar often and mark off tasks as you complete them. This will help you make the most of your free time and keep you from feeling overwhelmed.
