**Short Cooking Tip for Making Rice Less Sticky**

To keep your rice from becoming too sticky, ensure you’re using the right amount of water. For every 1 cup of rice, use about 2 cups of water. If you add more water, the rice can become overly sticky. Stirring the rice halfway through boiling water ensures that the grains cook evenly and retain less moisture, preventing them from sticking together. 

**Note:** While the amount of water can affect stickiness, it doesn’t affect the flavor or nutritional content of your rice.
