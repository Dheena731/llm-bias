Are you tracking your weekly fitness progress? It's important to review your efforts so you can better understand how far you've come and adjust your strategies as needed. Here's a quick reminder to help you take stock:

1. **Date** - Make sure you're keeping your weekly calendar updated with the date.
2. **Activity**, **Total Distance** or **Time** - Note down the specific workouts you did during the week.
3. **Measurement** - Check your chosen fitness indicators such as weight, body measurements, or energy levels.
4. **Results** - Compare your current performance to past weeks to spot improvements or areas needing more effort.
5. **Notes** - Add reflections on what went well, challenges you faced, or areas you'd like to focus on moving forward.

Remember, tracking your progress is a personal journey. Use this review as a tool to guide your future efforts and stay motivated. Happy tracking!
