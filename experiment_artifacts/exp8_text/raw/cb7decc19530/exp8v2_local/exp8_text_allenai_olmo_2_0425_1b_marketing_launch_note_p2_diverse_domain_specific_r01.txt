When should I use it?

Use it when you want to make a new coffee subscription announcement in a short brand- and market-specific note, branded to the specific products, featuring a unique product teaser line. Additionally, it can be used to promote a new product, or to announce a launch of a product that has been made available to the public. 

How do I use it?

1. Prepare a brief explanation of the product, the benefits, and the new features it brings.

2. Describe the marketing strategies that will be used to promote the product, including the target audience and planned marketing channels.

3. Write a short note that clearly explains the product, its benefits, and why it should be purchased. For example:

+ Coffee from Mulch and Orchards of America: This premium coffee is a blend of coffee beans cultivated in the western part of the United States.

+ This coffee is great for customers who love a nice, rich coffee, but prefer the taste of the slightly sweet and fruity coffee grown in the western region of the United States. 

+ For customers who love to get their coffee from the best brands, this is the coffee they need 

4. Include a teaser line that projects a compelling line of thought, such as "It's the coffee you love but with a twist!" or "A unique blend of ingredients that will bring a different taste to your cup of coffee" 

+ Mulch & Orchards Coffee from the US is a specialty coffee blend made from American grown coffee beans and is a great choice for customers who love a rich, full-bodied coffee that is smooth, sweet, and fruity.

+ A unique blend of ingredients that will bring a different taste to your cup of coffee.

+ Customers who love to get their coffee from the best brands, this is the coffee they need.

+ This coffee is great for customers who love a nice, full-bodied coffee, but prefer the taste of the slightly sweet and fruity coffee grown in the western region of the United States. 

5. Add a link to a product page if you'd like customers to be able to see the full description of your product.

What can I expect from it?

This note will be branded to the specific products, feature a unique product teaser line that will help customers decide whether they want to purchase the product, and include links to the product description. 

What should I consider when using it?

Think about the audience that you are targeting, and the content that will be most effective in engaging with your intended audience. Think about the unique features of your product, and what makes it different from the competition.
