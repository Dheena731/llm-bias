A lost company laptop is a significant concern. When you notice something has gone missing from your desk or computer bag, take immediate action to report it to your IT department or supervisor. Be specific about what happened, where you saw it last, and why you think it might have been lost. Here are some key points to include in your report:

- Describe the laptop in detail. What is its make and model? What color is it? Does it have any unique symbols, serial numbers, or identification numbers? Are there any write-ups or labels on it?

- When it was last used and by whom? Knowing the user or employees who might have interacted with it can help in locating it.

- Offer any photographs or videos of the device if available. This can be particularly helpful if the IT department needs to retrive it.

- If you have a company app for tracking devices, log in and check if the device is reported as lost or found.

- Contact the IT support team to fill out any necessary forms or provide them with your report details.

Remember to be calm and professional throughout the reporting process. Your concern for company property is important, and proactive reporting can prevent further loss and help retrieve the laptop with minimal disruption to your work.
