Planning lunches for a busy week can be challenging but here is a short tip: Use a meal planner app or website to help you plan meals in advance. Choose meals that are easy to prepare and can be stored for leftovers. Consider meals that can be made ahead and are easy to reheat. Be sure to include a variety of options so you can rotate meals and keep things fresh in your mind. Use leftovers creatively for new meals and have snacks ready for days when you're short on time.
