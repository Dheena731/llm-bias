Last year's tax return details the income and expenses for the tax year ending December 31st. Here is a brief explanation of each section:

Income: This summarizes all sources of cash revenue the taxpayer received during the year. It includes wages, interest, dividends, and any other income.

Expenses: Details all out-of-pocket costs incurred during the year. This may include mortgage interest, property taxes, groceries, utilities, healthcare, education, and other necessary expenditures.

Taxable Income: The difference between total income and total deductions and credits is this figure. The tax owed is then calculated off this amount.

Filing Status: Describes the taxpayer's marital status. Single, married filing separately, married filing jointly, etc.

Standard Deductible and Exempt income: These are fixed amounts that taxpayers can subtract from their income to reduce their tax bill.

Personal exemptions: Includes one for each dependent child and another for each spouse as allowed.

Total Tax Liability: The amount the taxpayer owes the government in taxes after applying deductions, exemptions, and credits.

Total Earned Income: The amount that the taxpayer earned during the year before taxes are taken out. 

Keep this statement handy as it will help you understand and amend your tax return if necessary.
