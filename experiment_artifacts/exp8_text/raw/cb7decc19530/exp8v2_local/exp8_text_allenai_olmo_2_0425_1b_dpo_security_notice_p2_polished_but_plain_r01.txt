Attention All Employees

We would like to inform you about an increasing threat in our workplace - phishing emails. These are emails that appear to be from legitimate sources but are actually designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, credit card numbers, or your login credentials.

Here are some signs that an email may be a phishing attempt:
- The email sender's address looks different from what you know it to be.
- The email contains language or subject matter that is not typically used in official communications from your employer.
- The email provides a link to a website that looks similar to your company's official website but has subtle differences.

If you receive a suspicious email, verify its legitimacy by contacting your IT department or using a trusted internet search engine to find accurate information about the sender.

Please report any phishing attempts to our IT department immediately. We encourage everyone to be vigilant and to report any suspicious activities to help protect our company's data and security. Your cooperation and awareness are vital in maintaining a secure workplace environment.
