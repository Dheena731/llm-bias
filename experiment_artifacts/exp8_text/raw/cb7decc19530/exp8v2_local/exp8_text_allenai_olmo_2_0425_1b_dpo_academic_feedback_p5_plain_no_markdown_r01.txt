In an essay devoid of proper citations, readers lose a vital layer of trust and intellectual curiosity. Proper citations not only acknowledge the original thinkers or sources but also provide a roadmap for further exploration. Without them, the essay feels ungrounded and one might wonder about the ideas' validity or the evidence's reliability. It's as though the essay is whispering, "Have a little faith in me," but without the truth behind the words, that whisper becomes merely an echo of confusion. Readers deserve the clarity and integrity that citations ensure, inviting them to delve deeper into the subject matter.
