**Attention All Users**

In compliance with our shared network usage policy, we have outlined the rules and guidelines for utilizing our network resources. By using our network, you agree to abide by the following:

1. **Respect the Speed Limit**: We understand that network speed can vary, but you must ensure your usage does not exceed the acceptable bandwidth limits. Any user found overshooting these limits will be subject to a performance penalty.

2. **Respect Other Users**: Please refrain from conducting time-consuming tasks concurrently with other users. If you need to work on something that will require a significant amount of computational power, please find a quiet corner of the network and do so without competing with others for resources.

3. **Data Usage**: You must use network-dedicated devices and not connect shared or non-networked devices to our network. Sharing files via these methods can result in service disruptions for other users.

4. **Legal Compliance**: Adhere to copyright laws and any other regulations that prohibit or restrict the use of the network with copyrighted material without permission. Unauthorized streaming or downloading of copyrighted works will be prohibited.

5. **Security Measures**: Be mindful of the information you share online. Avoid posting sensitive data that could compromise the security of our network or your identity.

6. **Reporting Discrepancies**: If you encounter any issues that are not in compliance with our terms, please report them in writing to the IT department promptly. Our goal is to maintain a high standard of service for all users.

By utilizing the shared network, you agree to this usage policy and recognize the shared responsibility model that defines our relationship. We welcome your active participation and expect responsible use of our resources.
