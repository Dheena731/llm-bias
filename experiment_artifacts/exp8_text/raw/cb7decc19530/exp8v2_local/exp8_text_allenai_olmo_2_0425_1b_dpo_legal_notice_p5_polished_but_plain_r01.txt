In order to make effective use of this shared network, we must adhere to the following guidelines:

1. Respect for others - kindly refrain from downloading or uploading large files that could disrupt the network for others. If you need to transfer large files, consider utilizing compatible cloud storage services that are designed for high bandwidth transfers.

2. Maintenance of system integrity - ensure that your personal devices are up-to-date with the latest software patches and security updates. This will help protect against malware and other cyber threats that may compromise the network.

3. Compliance with policy - abide by our network policy which outlines acceptable use cases and expectations. If you encounter any restrictions, please report the issue so we can address it promptly.

4. Ethical behavior - refrain from engaging in any activity that is illegal or against our company policies. This includes, but is not limited to, sharing copyrighted material without permission, engaging in inappropriate conversations, or uploading sexually explicit content.

5. No unauthorized access - access the network only through the provided login credentials. Unauthorized use of any network resources or systems can lead to disciplinary action and is strictly prohibited.

Our shared network is intended to facilitate productivity and collaboration. By adhering to these guidelines, you contribute to a safe and productive environment for all members of the organization. Should you have questions regarding these policies or if you believe your use of the network has been adversely affected, please contact the IT department for clarification and assistance.
