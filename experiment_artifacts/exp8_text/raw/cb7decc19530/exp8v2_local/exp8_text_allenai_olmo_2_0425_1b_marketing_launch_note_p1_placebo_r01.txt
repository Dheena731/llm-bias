How to write a beginning of the story

How to write a beginning of the story

The beginning of the story is where your audience is introduced to your core concept. A good beginning shows your vision of the world and sets the pace.

The beginning may not be as important as the end. A good beginning will make the audience want to read the rest of the story. If you get the beginning wrong, you can lose the audience.

Avoid giving away the ending (except if death or apocalypse is required). The end can be a resolution of some sort, but not a complete closure. This is the part of the story your audience will be waiting for. It will not be satisfied with a simple ending that moves the story to a new point. Think twice before you close the story.

Your beginning should not be boring or predictable. A good beginning will make your audience want to read the rest of the story.
