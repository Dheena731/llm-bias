SavingsGoal: $500 per month for 12 months

The new SavingsGoal feature has been launched to encourage more people to save regularly and track their progress towards their financial goals. This feature allows users to set a monthly savings target and receive reminders each month to make sure they are on track. Users can also track their total savings and make adjustments to their target as needed. The feature is optimized for accessibility and intuitive use making saving money easier than ever before.
