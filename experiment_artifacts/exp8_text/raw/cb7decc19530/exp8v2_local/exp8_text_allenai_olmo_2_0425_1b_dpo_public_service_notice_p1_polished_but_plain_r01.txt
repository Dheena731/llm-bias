Library openers are happy to assist you in finding information or just relaxing in a cozy environment. The library is open 7 days a week, 9 am to 9 pm. Kids under 12 are free with library cards, and student IDs accepted for services. If you need assistance finding a book or require help with computer access, please feel free to ask. The library staff is here to help with any questions or concerns you may have or to assist you in utilizing our extensive collection of books, movies, music, digital resources, and more.

Visit us at your convenience for the best resources and community atmosphere.  We hope to see you soon!
