# YourToken

## What is a token?

A token is a unique code that can only be used to log in. Without it, you won't be able to log in to your account.

## How do you get a token?

You can get a token by going to this address (as of writing this article, it's 5v6yCho4pDkyc1JZ7jcwZJYeaOloP7b) and pressing the "Generate a code" button.

## Just double-clicking it isn't enough

When you click the button, React will show you an alert with the code. If you've enabled 2FA, you first need to enter the code into the 2FA field at the top of the page.

## How do I use it?

After you have copied the code, you should paste it into the field below and enter your email to confirm that you are, in fact, the person who owns the account.

## Tips and Tricks

* If you don't see the button, check that you're in the correct country or region.
* You can copy the code from the URL and paste it into a text editor, such as Notepad.
* Don't share the code with anyone.
