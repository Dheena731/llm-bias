If you have lost a company laptop, please report it immediately. The sooner we become aware of the missing item, the faster we can take action to recover the data and ensure the safety of the information.

Here’s what you need to do:  
* Locate the laptop: Be sure you've located the device.  
* Check its location: Ensure you know where you previously left the laptop.  
* Document the loss: Collect as much information as possible about the situation.  
* Contact IT department: Speak with your IT support team as soon as possible.  
* Follow their instructions: Be prepared to provide details about the device, its contents, and any steps taken to recover it.  

In case you’ve already reported the issue, or if you need more information about what to do next, here are a few additional steps you might consider:

* Report the loss to your company's IT department or IT security officer.  
* Look for any security alerts or notifications about the lost or stolen device.  
* If the laptop contains sensitive information, take steps to protect it. Install a firewall, encrypt the drive, or back up important files.  
* Follow any internal procedures for data recovery or bear witness to internal efforts to recover the information.
