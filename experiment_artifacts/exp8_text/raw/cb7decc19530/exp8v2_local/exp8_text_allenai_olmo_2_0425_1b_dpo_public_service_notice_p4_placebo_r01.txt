Attention all residents and visitors:

As of this hour, we are placing a heat advisory in effect for our area. This advisory is in place to ensure public safety and comfort by urging everyone to take necessary precautions to stay cool. Here are some important steps to keep yourself and your loved ones safe during this heatwave:

* Stay indoors when possible.
* Avoid prolonged outdoor activity.
* Drink plenty of fluids, especially water and non-alcoholic beverages.
* Wear loose-fitting, light-colored clothing to help regulate your body temperature.
* Use air conditioning whenever possible.
* Monitor your body temperature and seek shade when necessary.
* Check on neighbors and family members who might be more vulnerable to heat exhaustion.

Additionally, we would like to remind you that local health departments and emergency services are on standby to respond to any heat-related emergencies. If you have any concerns or need additional information, please do not hesitate to contact us.

For your safety and comfort, please follow these guidelines and stay hydrated.
