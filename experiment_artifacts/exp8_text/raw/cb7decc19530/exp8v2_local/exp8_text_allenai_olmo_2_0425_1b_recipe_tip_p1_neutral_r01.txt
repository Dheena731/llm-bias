If you take the time to plan ahead, you can prepare a weeknight dinner in less than 30 minutes. Ask the kids to help you plan the menu. You don't have to have a fancy dinner every night. Just follow these easy tricks to make a delicious and healthy meal.

1. Don't wash your produce until you're ready to use it. If you have to wash fruits or vegetables right before serving, use a paper towel to blot off any water so you don't have to scrub.

2. Serve fruits and veggies with a salad. A side salad with fresh, different vegetables gives your kids a colorful way to get the nutrients they need.

3. Use lean cuts of meat. Lean cuts of meat are also easier to chew and digest. Ask your kids to help you cut the meat and serve it with a side salad.

4. Don't serve your kids just one type of protein. Instead, add a protein-rich vegetable like eggs, beans, or tofu to your meal so they get a variety of protein sources.

5. Add a healthy side dish to your meal to make it even healthier. For example, instead of a sugary cereal, serve a bowl of olives, grapes, or nuts.

6. Add a healthy dip to your meal. If you're serving a healthy meat, such as grilled chicken, try making homemade hummus or homemade guacamole instead of using sour cream or mayonnaise. Ask your kids to help you make it!

7. Serve your meal with a big glass of water. A glass of water will help you stay hydrated and will possibly cut down on your kids' snacking.

Yerba mate

Yerba mate is a traditional yerba mate tea that is served after meals in South America. It has many healthy calcium and vitamin B6 properties. As with any tea, yerba mate can cause problems for people with heartburn, ulcers, and acid reflux. It also contains caffeine, which might cause problems for children, and pregnant women. Yerba mate also contains large amounts of oxalates, which are naturally present in certain foods and are not harmful. However, oxalates can be problematic for people with kidney stones or other kidney problems. Yerba mate also contains tannins, which may cause problems for some people. Yerba mate also contains compounds called phytoestrogens, which are similar to the estrogen found in many women’s bodies. These compounds may interfere with the way your body uses estrogen and cause problems for people with irregular periods, low estrogen, or hormone-related conditions. Yerba mate also contains high levels of aluminum. Pregnant women and children should avoid powdered yerba mate, because the aluminum in it can cause neurological problems in children.
