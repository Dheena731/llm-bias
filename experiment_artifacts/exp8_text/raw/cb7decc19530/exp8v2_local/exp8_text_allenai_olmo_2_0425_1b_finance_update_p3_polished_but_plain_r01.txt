 • Avoid emotional or angry writing. Be polite and factual.
  • Avoid swearing. We don't allow expletives or offensive language.
  • Avoid jokes that are unlikely to be shared by others.
    • You can make good jokes with other writers, but the stories you share with friends likely to be able to understand them, so you can be sure that we'll see them. Why worry about what's funny to only a few?
  • Seek or accept other feedback from the moderator and other writers. Discussing writing will help you understand where you're going wrong or how to improve.
  • Never delete your writing.
    • Even if you think something is a mistake, keep it. If you come back later you might be able to see it in a different light and fix it.
  • Share your writing. Let's write together!
