To prevent overdoses and ensure effective treatment, it's essential to refill your prescription as quickly as your medication reaches the end of your supply. Always follow the pharmacy's scheduling instructions. Be sure to keep your prescription and any refill instructions somewhere safe, especially if you're not near your medicine cabinet or pharmacy. Also, remember to dispose of your unused medication properly, according to local regulations to prevent drug abuse. If you have any questions about your medication, don't hesitate to consult a healthcare professional.
