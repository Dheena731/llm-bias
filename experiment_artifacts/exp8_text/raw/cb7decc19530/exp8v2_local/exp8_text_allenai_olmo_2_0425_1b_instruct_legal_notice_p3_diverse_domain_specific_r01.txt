**What are Cookies?**

Essentially, cookies are tools used by websites to store small pieces of text on your computer. Websites often use cookies to identify your device, remember your preferences, and customize your experience. 

**How We Use Cookies**

We use cookies for various purposes including:

- **Logging In:** To help you log in to our website more easily.
- **Retrieving your preferences:** These can include settings about things like font size, colors, and whether to display an invite popup when you first visit the site after a period of inactivity.
- **Performance and analytics:** To gather information about how users interact with the site so we can improve it.

**Your Choices:**

You may adjust your cookie settings on your browser. Here are the steps to generally disable all cookies:

1. **Find the Settings:** Navigate to your browser's "Preferences" or "Settings" menu.
2. **Access Cookies:** Look for an option related to "Cookies," "Cookies and other similar web features," or similar within this section.
3. **Manage Cookie Settings:** Choose the setting that suits your preference, such as "Never," "Always," "Through log in," or "Custom."
