Start your weekly dinner prep on Sunday night. Gather your ingredients and wash all vegetables and meats thoroughly. Then, chop everything up and place it in separate containers or bags to keep everything organized as you prepare each dish. For example, slice the vegetables for your main course, dice the meat for a stew or stir-fry, and mince any herbs you’ll use. This method saves time and ensures everything is ready and ready to go on Monday night. Have chicken thighs for a stew, ribeye for a steak, and zucchini and peppers for a colorful side dish. Refrigerate the prepped ingredients until you have time to cook your main meal. This tip saves you time and ensures you have everything prepared when you need it, saving you from last-minute stress on Monday night.
