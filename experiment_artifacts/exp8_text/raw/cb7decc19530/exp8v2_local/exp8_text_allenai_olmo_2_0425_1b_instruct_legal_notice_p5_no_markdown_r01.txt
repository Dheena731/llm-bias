To provide a shared network for our community office we request that all users abide by the following rules for acceptable use:

1. No unauthorized access to or use of the network
2. All user conduct is respectful and professional
3. No distribution of harmful, sensitive or harassing information
4. No unauthorized sharing of proprietary or confidential materials
5. No unauthorized storage of personal files on the network
6. No unauthorized use of network resources for commercial gain
7. All devices and equipment connected to the network must comply with our internal policies regarding security and proper maintenance
8. Any complaints regarding inappropriate use of the network will be addressed by management immediately

Thank you for your cooperation and respect as we continue to build a supportive and productive shared environment.
