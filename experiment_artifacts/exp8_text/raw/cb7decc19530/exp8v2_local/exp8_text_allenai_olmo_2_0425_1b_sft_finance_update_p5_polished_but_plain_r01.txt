If you have ever had trouble getting a response from your bank when you try to dispute a delayed payment, you will be pleased to learn that the U.S. government has finally passed a law that may make things a bit easier for those who feel they have been treated unfairly.

Early last year, a group of banks and credit card companies sued the IRS claiming that it failed to provide the necessary information to them when it came to the processing of payments. In light of this, the banks argued that the Internal Revenue Service should change its policy of not providing all interest and fees that are charged on a delayed payment.

The new law is expected to take effect in July 2018. If you would like to read more about the law, you can find more information on the IRS website.

It is hoped that this law will make it easier for those who have been delayed in payment processing to get a response from the IRS when they try to dispute a delayed payment.
