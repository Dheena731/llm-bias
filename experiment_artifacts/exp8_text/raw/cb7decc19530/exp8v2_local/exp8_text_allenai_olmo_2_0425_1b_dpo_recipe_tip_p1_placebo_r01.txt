For a quick yet delicious weeknight dinner, create an "Instant Marinade" using simple ingredients like soy sauce, garlic, and citrus zest. 

1. Start by blending your choice of citrus zest, minced garlic, and a generous drizzle of soy sauce in a food processor or blender until fragrant and creamy.
2. Season the mixture with a pinch of salt and pepper.
3. Serve over rice or noodles, topped with fresh herbs, sliced vegetables, and a squeeze of fresh lime juice for a zesty kick.

This "Instant Marinade" can be ready in minutes, and the result will be a savory, tangy side that complements any protein. Remember to save some leftovers for another night, as this marinade can be versatile – perhaps use it on roasted chicken or add it to a stir-fry.
