For storing fresh herbs, make sure they are kept in a dry, dark place where it's cool. A refrigerator is perfect for this, as it ensures they stay fresh for as long as possible. If you're storing herbs in the fridge, make sure they have some air circulation by using a small, tight container or cup. You can also wrap them in a damp kitchen towel to help keep them from drying out. This way, you'll always have fresh herbs on hand and ready to use.
