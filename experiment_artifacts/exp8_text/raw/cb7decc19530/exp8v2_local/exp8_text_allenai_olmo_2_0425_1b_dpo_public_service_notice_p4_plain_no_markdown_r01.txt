Attention residents and visitors!

We are issuing a heat advisory due to the warm weather forecast for this weekend. It is expected to be hotter than normal, with temperatures reaching 90 degrees and above. The intense heat can pose serious health risks so it is important to take precautions to stay safe.

Here are a few steps you can take to stay cool and healthy:

* Stay in well-airated spaces
* Limit outdoor activity to early morning or late evening when the sun is lower in the sky
* Drink plenty of fluids, especially water and decaf tea or coffee
* Eat cooler foods like frozen fruits and vegetables
* Wear loose-fitting, light-colored clothing
* Avoid overexerting yourself outdoors
* If you are feeling fatigued or dizzy, seek shade, rest, and call for medical advice

Please use common sense and stay alert to your body’s signals. Remember that staying hydrated is crucial in combating the heat. If you have any health concerns or are concerned about your well-being during this heatwave, please consult a healthcare professional.

Stay safe and enjoy the sun responsibly!
