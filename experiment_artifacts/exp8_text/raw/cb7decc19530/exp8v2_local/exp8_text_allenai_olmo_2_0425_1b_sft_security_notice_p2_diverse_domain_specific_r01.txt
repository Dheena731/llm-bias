Attention: Employees

We want to remind you to be extra cautious when interacting with any emails that ask for your personal information, whether they're coming from your company or another company. Fraudulent attempts to obtain sensitive information are happening more often and are unfortunately effective.

If you receive any emails that are suspicious, do not click on any links or provide any personal or sensitive information. The best course of action is to simply delete the email. Always remember to be vigilant to the tactics used in these attacks.

Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining our workplace's security.
