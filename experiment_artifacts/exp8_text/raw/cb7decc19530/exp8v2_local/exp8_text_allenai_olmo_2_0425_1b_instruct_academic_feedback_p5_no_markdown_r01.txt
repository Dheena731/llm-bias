Incorporating citations properly is crucial for academic integrity and ensuring the credibility of one's work. When citations are missing from an essay it can undermine the author's argument and lead to reduced trust from the reader who may question the validity of the information presented. Always remember to include in-text citations for direct quotes or paraphrases as well as references at the end of the essay. Proper citation not only adheres to academic standards but also contributes to a comprehensive and truthful representation of the content.
