At the end of the year, you will receive a tax statement telling you how much money the government has taken from you in taxes. This statement includes all the income you earned during the year, such as wages, salaries, interest, and dividends. It also shows the amount of taxes you paid and any refunds you may have been eligible for. The statement will list any credits or deductions you claimed and explain how they affected your total tax bill. Be sure to review your statement carefully so you can keep track of any changes to your tax situation for next year. It's important to submit your tax return on time to avoid being charged penalties and interest. If you have any questions, don't hesitate to reach out to a tax professional or use the government's online tax help tools.
