Missing citations in an essay can significantly undermine its credibility and can potentially jeopardize the author's academic standing or professional reputation. When citations are omitted, the reader may find it challenging to verify the accuracy and legitimacy of the claims or arguments put forth in the essay. Even if the intended audience is assumed to be familiar with the subject matter, references can serve as a means of verification for more general readers or those with specialized knowledge in the field. Therefore, it is crucial for writers to ensure that their essays are correctly cited, whether they aim to inform the general public or a specifically knowledgeable audience. Without these citations, holes in the argument are left exposed, which can be detrimental when attempting to persuade readers or defend one’s work against counter-arguments. In essence, citing sources not only supports the validity of the writer’s assertions but also enhances the overall persuasiveness of the essay.
