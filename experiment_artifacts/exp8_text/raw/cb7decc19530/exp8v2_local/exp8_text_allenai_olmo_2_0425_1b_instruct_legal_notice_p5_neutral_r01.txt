6. **No Personal Data Sharing**: Do not share personal data without the explicit consent of the person whose information is being shared.

7. **Privacy and Security**: Respect the privacy of others. Do not access or disclose personal information without the individual's permission.

8. **No File Sharing of Hazardous Content**: Do not share data that is illegal to distribute or that may pose a risk to the health, safety, or well-being of others.
