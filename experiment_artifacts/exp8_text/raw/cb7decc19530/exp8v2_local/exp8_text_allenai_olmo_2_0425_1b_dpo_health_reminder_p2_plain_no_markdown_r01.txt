Stay hydrated by drinking water regularly throughout the day. Your body needs water to function properly, and it can easily become dehydrated without consistent hydration. Aim to drink at least eight 8-ounce glasses of water per day, but remember that factors such as temperature, physical activity, and your diet can affect your daily water needs. Your mouth, skin, and even joints may signal dehydration before you feel thirsty, so be mindful of these signs. Staying hydrated supports your overall health and can improve concentration, energy levels, and skin health.
