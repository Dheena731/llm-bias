Roasted vegetables are a delightful way to boost your healthy eating habits. Here’s a short and sweet tip for seasoning roasted vegetables: Try using a mix of salt, pepper, and your favorite herbs like rosemary, basil, or thyme. The combination not only imparts a flavorful kick but also enhances the natural antioxidants and nutrients in the vegetables. Remember, too much of any spice can overpower the natural flavors of the vegetables, so use sparingly and enjoy the vibrant, savory bites of your roasted masterpiece.

Enjoy your cooking!
