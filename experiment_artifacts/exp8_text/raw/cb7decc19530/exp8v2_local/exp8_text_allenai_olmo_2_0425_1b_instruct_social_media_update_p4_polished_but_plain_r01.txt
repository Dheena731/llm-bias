Think ahead to your week. Start by deciding on your calendars. You can use a physical calendar for your to-dos and stick notes to mark appointments or deadlines. For the longer term, you can use a calendar app. The trick is to set aside specific time slots for different activities. For example if you always want to exercise in the morning then schedule it from Monday to Friday. If you have a yearly goal like paying off debt then allocate time for that on a monthly basis. Keeping a structured schedule will help keep your life organized and make big tasks more manageable.
