If you wish to renew any books, or to check out more books, please let the librarian know that you are interested. He or she will refer you to the reserve shelves or to the bookshelves in the study room.

You can also browse the library’s collections on the internet.

If you are looking for a book that is out of stock at the library, you can use the inter-library loan service. Ask the librarian to help you out.

Helpful hint!

If you move out of your home town and give up your library card, just bring the card to the town office of the library in the town you live in and we will provide a new card and you will keep your membership, free of charge.

Bookstore library

The University bookstore library has a great collection of books from the University of Otago and the greater Otago area. Most books are available in English and in both Maori and English. The library also has a collection of books and audio materials in other languages. Most textbooks are available in hard copies, but some may be available as ebooks.

The library will occasionally buy used books and magazines from local sellers. The library will also occasionally pay for new books and magazines for the library, if there are many people who want them.


I am not a student but want to borrow a book to read on my own. How do I borrow a book?

If you are not a student, you can use the library’s public computer to check out books and the internet.

You must have a valid Otago student, staff or employee card to borrow books.

If you want to borrow an e-book, you must have a valid Otago Student ID Card. The library will provide you with a machine where you can download e-books.

Can I check out a book if I am going to use it at another library?

If a library can provide you with a copy of the book you wish to borrow, then you must check it out at that library. However, if the book is not available at the library you are using, we will search for it at other libraries in the Otago region and if we find one, we will send it to you.

If you want to borrow a book that is only available in another library that is not in the Otago region and is not in our collection, we will search for the book in our library and will order it for you. You may have to pay a fee for the book to be delivered to you.

What happens if I lose my library card?

If you lose your library card, please let us know as soon as possible. We will then issue a new library card.
