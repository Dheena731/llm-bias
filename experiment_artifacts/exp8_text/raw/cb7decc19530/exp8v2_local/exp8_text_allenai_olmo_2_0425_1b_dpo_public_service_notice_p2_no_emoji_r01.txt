ROAD CLOSURE FOR DURING THE EVENING WEEKEND

A portion of Valley Boulevard will be closed for repairs starting this Friday evening. Please plan your commute accordingly and avoid driving on this road during the scheduled closure. The road will be reopened sometime Sunday morning. 

Residents and visitors are advised to use alternative routes to get to their destinations during this closure. If you depend on your car for getting around, consider parking your vehicle at a nearby garage or using public transportation.

For updates, please refer to local news channels or contact the County Transportation Authority. Additional information can be found on our website under 'Road Maintenance and Repairs.' Thank you for your patience and understanding.

Construction work does not extend indefinitely, and we anticipate the closure will be brief, but please stay informed for any changes in update times.
