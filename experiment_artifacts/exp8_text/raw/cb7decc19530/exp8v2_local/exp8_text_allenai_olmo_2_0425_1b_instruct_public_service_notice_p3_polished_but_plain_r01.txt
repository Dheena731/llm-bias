Citywide recycling collection hours are changing this month. The city has expanded the hours for residential recycling pickups to ensure that as many residents as possible keep their recycling out of their garbage cans and minimize waste sent to landfills. Residents who had been recycling before or collecting curbside recyclables once every other week will now do so every week. To accommodate this change, please ensure your recycling bins are properly labeled and placed outside your garage or front door on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 7 AM to 7 PM.

For more information or if you have questions about the recycling process, contact the City Clerk's office at (555) 123-4567 or visit our website at www.cityofxyz.com for updates and guidelines. Thank you for being a part of our community effort to reduce waste and promote sustainability.
