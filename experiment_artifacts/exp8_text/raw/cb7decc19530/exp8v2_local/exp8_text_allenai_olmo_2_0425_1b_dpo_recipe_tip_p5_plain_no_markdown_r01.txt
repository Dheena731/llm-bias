When seasoning roasted vegetables, start with a basic patina of salt and pepper to set the stage. For an extra zing, add a drizzle of olive oil or butter to the vegetables while still hot from roasting. Incorporate a variety of spices like paprika, cumin, chili flakes, garlic powder, or dried herbs like basil or oregano, depending on your taste preferences and the rest of your meal plan. You can also squeeze in lemon juice, vinegar, or a splash of red wine for a richer flavor. Finishing with a sprinkle of parmesan cheese or pine nuts adds a pleasant crunch. Remember, the key to flavorful vegetables is not overloading on seasoning—just enough to enhance, not mask, the natural sweetness and richness of the roast.
