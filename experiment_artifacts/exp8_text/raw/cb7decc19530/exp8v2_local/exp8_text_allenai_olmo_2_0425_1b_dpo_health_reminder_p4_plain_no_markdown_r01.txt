Take regular short breaks from screens to give your eyes a rest. Stepping away for just a few minutes can actually improve your focus and productivity when you return. Here's a simple reminder to incorporate these breaks into your daily routine:

Imagine you're on a long road trip, and you need to stop occasionally to stretch your legs and enjoy the scenery. Your eyes acting like the car windows, they're always seeing and adapting to text on screens. Just as you would every few hours, take a few seconds to look somewhere far away, blink more times, and take a deep breath to reset your vision and brain.

Here's what you can do:

1. **Gaze Away:** Set a timer (like 5 minutes) and check something far off, like a tree outside your window or a book.
2. **Stretch:** Roll your shoulders and neck in small circles or do some gentle face exercises.
3. **Blink More:** Swallow and blink deliberately, as this helps to clear your eyes of dryness.
4. **Stay Engaged:** When you return, engage in a different activity that requires concentration or movement.

These simple actions can alleviate discomfort and improve your visual health, ensuring your screen time is sustainable and mindful.
