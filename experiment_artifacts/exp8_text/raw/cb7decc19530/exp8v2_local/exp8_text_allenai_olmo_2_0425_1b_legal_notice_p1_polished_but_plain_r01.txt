What you need to know

  • You must write a notice about changes to your terms of service. You must provide a warning to the users of your site, and to your users and visitors that these terms have changed.

  • You can change your terms without giving anyone a chance to object. However, you must give the community a chance to object.

  • You must notify new users that the terms have changed; you should also update the URL to match the new terms.

  • You don't need to give any user a chance to object to the change, since you have already changed the terms.

  • You must not remove any existing user information or content. If the content is protected by a Creative Commons license, the license is still valid.
