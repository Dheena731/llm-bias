When an essay lacks citations, it becomes difficult for readers to verify the claims and source the author's arguments. Citations not only help acknowledge the original authors of ideas but also create a transparent source trail, allowing readers to explore further the literature cited. Without citations, an essay risks being seen as a personal exposition rather than a scholarly exploration, as it fails to provide a framework within which the claims are placed. 

For academic integrity and credibility, it's crucial to ensure a thorough citation of all sources. Each citation should include the author's name, the title of the work, the year of publication, the publication format, and, when applicable, a link to an online version of the work. This not only adheres to academic standards but also supports the reader's pursuit of knowledge and encourages them to engage with the sources cited.
