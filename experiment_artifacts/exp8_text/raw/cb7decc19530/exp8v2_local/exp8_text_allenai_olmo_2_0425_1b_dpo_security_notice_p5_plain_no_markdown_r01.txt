If you have found a lost company laptop and want to report it please follow these steps:

1. **Document the Find**: Take photos or make a short video of the laptop and save it to your computer or a cloud storage service.

2. **Contact your IT Department**: Call the IT department of your company. Explain the situation and mention the location of the laptop along with any photos or details you have taken.

3. **Follow IT Department's Instructions**: The IT team will guide you through the next steps. They might instruct you to log into the laptop remotely to confirm it's your find or to secure it if it's not.

4. **Inform Security**: If you believe the laptop contains sensitive information or if it belongs to someone else, report it to the company's security department immediately.

5. **Be Patient**: Security procedures can take some time, so be patient and vigilant in case others start looking for it.

Remember to only report your lost equipment to your company; do not attempt to handle it yourself without proper authorization as it could compromise its security or your job security.
