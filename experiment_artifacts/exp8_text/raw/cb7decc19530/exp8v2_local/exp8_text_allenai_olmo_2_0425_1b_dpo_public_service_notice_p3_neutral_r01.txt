Attention all residents,

We are pleased to announce the new weekly recycling collection schedule that will commence from April 1st. Our recycling service is moving to a weekly cycle, with collections on Tuesdays and Thursdays only. 

For residents living further from our main collection points, we recommend checking our mobile recycling units which will be strategically placed at selected locations around the city.

Please remember to ensure you separate your recyclables into different bins, correspondingly labeled for paper, plastics, metals, and organic waste. This initiative aims to further enhance our community’s circular economy and encourage residents to participate in sustainable waste management practices.

For any questions or concerns, please contact our recycling hotline at 123-456-7890 or visit our website for detailed instructions and additional information.

We encourage everyone to embrace a greener lifestyle by participating in these changes.

Thank you for your continued support.

Sincerely,
The Recycling Team
