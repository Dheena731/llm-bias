When your prescription runs out before your next refill date, reach out to your healthcare provider’s office to schedule a new prescription. This ensures you continue to get the medication you need without any interruptions in your treatment. Refilling a prescription at the pharmacy ahead of time is not the same as a new prescription, as the new prescription will contain the latest medication dosage and specifications while the previously filled prescription may not. Always keep your original prescription fill confirmation as proof that you have the prescribed medication and follow the instructions provided by your healthcare provider.
