Attention Residents!

Local councils have announced new changes to our recycling collection schedule. Effective immediately, single-stream recycling has been transferred to Monday morning pickups. This means we'll sort our recyclables at home rather than at the curb on Monday and Thursday mornings.

Here's what you need to know:

* **Where to sort:** Place all recyclable materials in their respective bins: plastic, glass, paper, metal, and compostable materials. Do not confuse recyclables with biodegradable materials.

* **Tightened restrictions:** Be aware that glass, plastic, and aluminum items have been reduced in quantity collected. Ensure you separate these items correctly according to the new rules.

* **Consistent schedule:** These changes are in place for good and will help manage our local resources more efficiently.

* **Stay informed:** For further details and questions, visit our website or contact our recycling team.

We thank everyone for their continued participation in our recycling program and appreciate your cooperation as we work towards a greener community.

Happy Recycling!
