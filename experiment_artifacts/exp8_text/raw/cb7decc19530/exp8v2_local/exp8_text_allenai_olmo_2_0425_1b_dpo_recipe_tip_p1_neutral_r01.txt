Cook a pot of rice or pasta and have it ready in less than 20 minutes. Place the rice or pasta in a large pot and fill it with boiling water, or cook it up in a saute pan over medium heat. Allow it to boil until al dente (for rice) or aliviately until ready to serve (for pasta). Quickly toss it with a simple sauce or leave it plain, depending on your preference. Enjoy and have a great weeknight dinner!
