A polished essay benefits from proper citation as it acknowledges the sources that inform the writer's ideas and confirms the credibility of the written work. Neglecting to include citations can undermine the essay's validity and reveal a lack of research. Remember, each mention of a source (book, article, website) must be accompanied by either an in-text citation (e.g., (Smith, 2019)) or a full reference in the bibliography or endnotes/endnotes section. This practice ensures that the reader can locate the original source, contributing to a respectful academic discourse and enhancing the author's reputation should their work be scrutinized.
