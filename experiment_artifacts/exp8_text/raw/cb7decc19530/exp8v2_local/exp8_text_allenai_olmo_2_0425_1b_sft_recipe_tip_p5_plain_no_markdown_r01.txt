To season roasted vegetables, start by quenching them with fresh herbs like parsley, rosemary, or thyme, and adding a splash of lemon juice or vinegar for flavor and color. The natural oils in these herbs will help lock in the flavors against the heat of the roasting. You can also sprinkle them with Parmesan or Feta cheese for added texture and flavor. Six hours before roasting, toss them with olive oil, salt, and pepper, and roast in the oven until tender and golden. Always be aware of how the oven bakes your vegetables, as different areas can heat up unevenly. This will help your veggies retain their shape and ensure even seasoning.
