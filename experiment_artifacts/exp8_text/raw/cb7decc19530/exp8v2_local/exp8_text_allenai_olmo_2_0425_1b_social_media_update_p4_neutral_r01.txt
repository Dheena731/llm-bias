Our top tip for using a calendar app.

With the rise of budgeting apps like Mint, Pocketbook and the Likeability App, it’s easy to think that our money is now managed in one place. While this is great for most of us, it’s actually a good idea to have a few go-to apps for tracking important details like our events, birthdays and most importantly, money.

Here at Likeability we use the Likeability Calendar App, which gives us the best of both worlds: a calendar and a to-do list. You can set up alerts and reminders for upcoming events, and easily see all your to-do things and birthdays all in one place.

Plus, when you’re on vacation and not able to log in to your other apps, you can still keep track of your events and to-do’s for the week – no matter where you are.
