Calendar apps can be a breeze to set up but getting the most out of them takes a bit of finesse. Here's a tip that can save you time and keep you organized: customize your app settings. Choose a color scheme that stands out on your phone's screen and set up recurring events like birthdays and work meetings. Use tags or keywords to categorize activities. And remember, your calendar is your ally, so spend a bit of time to make it your friend.
