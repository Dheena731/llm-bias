**Password Reuse in the Workplace**

When using a password, it's important to remember not to reuse passwords across multiple accounts. This could increase the risk of unauthorized access to different accounts. Always create a unique password for each service and don't share them with anyone.

Here are some best practices for managing your passwords:

- Never share your password with anyone.
- Never write your password down and keep it in a secure location.
- Allow your computer's password manager to create a unique password for you.
- Never save your password in your browser or a word processor.
- If asked to provide your password, never say it out loud.
- Keep track of all your passwords in a secure location where you can find them easily.
