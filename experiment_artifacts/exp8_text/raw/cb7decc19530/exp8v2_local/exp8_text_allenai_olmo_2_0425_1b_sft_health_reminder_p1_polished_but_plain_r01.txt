To members, please review your weekly fitness progress.

In this journal, you'll find a list of things you've done in the last week, and how they affected your body and mind. You will see the number of calories you have burned, the amount of water you have drunk, the food you have eaten, and how rested you've slept.

Whenever you have a week marked, click the calendar and draw a line. You can also write a brief note in the box below. Make sure the line is straight, and the note is informative but clear.

The journal will help you stay on track, so review it often.
