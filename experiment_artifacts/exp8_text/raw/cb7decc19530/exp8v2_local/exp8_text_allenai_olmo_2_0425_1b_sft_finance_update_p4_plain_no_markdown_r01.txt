If you received a tax statement at the end of the year, it likely includes information about your overall tax liability. The statement will detail the amount of taxes you owe, any credits or deductions you’ve claimed, and the total amount you need to pay. It’s a great opportunity to review your tax history and make sure your tax return is accurate.

To personalize the tax statement, you'll typically need to sign it. Remember to keep a copy for your records. If you have questions about your tax return, contact the tax authority in your area or consult with a tax professional. They can provide assistance in understanding your statement and helping you complete any necessary forms.
