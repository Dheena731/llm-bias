Attention all employees! Here are the instructions for reporting a lost company laptop:

**If you have found a lost or stolen company laptop:**

* **Identify the Laptop**: Make sure you are sure this is the lost laptop. Double-check the serial number and logo. If you're unsure, contact IT for verification.

* **Contact IT**: If it's confirmed to be your laptop, notify our IT department immediately via the electronic form on our company intranet, or call us at [insert contact number]. Our IT team is trained to handle such incidents and will take immediate action to secure the device and act swiftly to recover any necessary data.

* **Protect Your Information**: As you wait for IT to locate and recover the laptop, ensure that the device is not accessed by unauthorized individuals. Use a passcode or encryption to safeguard personal and sensitive data.

* **Returning the Lost Device**: Once the laptop is returned, follow any additional instructions provided by IT regarding data recovery and protective measures.

**Remember:** Our company is committed to safeguarding your personal information and ensuring the security of our assets. In case of loss or theft, swift action is crucial. If you observe suspicious activity on behalf of an unauthorized individual, report it immediately to Corporate Compliance or your supervisor.

Thank you for your vigilance and cooperation. Together, we will ensure the secure return of our laptops and protect the integrity of our work environment.
