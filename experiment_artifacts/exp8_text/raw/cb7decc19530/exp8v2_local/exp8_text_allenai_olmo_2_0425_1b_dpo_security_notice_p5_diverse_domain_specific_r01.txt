**Reporting a Lost Company Laptop**

Attention all employees and team members: We urge you to report any lost company laptop immediately. Our IT department has strict security measures in place to protect our data and sensitive company information. If you find yourself with a lost laptop, please follow these steps:

1. **Return the Device**: Do not attempt to retrieve the device yourself. Return the laptop to your desk or designated IT department storage area without attempting to access any data unless instructed by a supervisor. 

2. **Contact IT**: Upon returning the laptop, immediately contact our IT support team at support@company.com or via the company messaging system. Provide them with all relevant details, including the serial number, the exact location where the laptop was lost, and any information someone might have inadvertently saved to the device (e.g., passwords, login credentials).

3. **Document the Incident**: Keep a detailed record of the incident, including when it happened, who found it, and any notes about what the device contained (if anything).

4. **Proceed with Investigation**: The IT team will perform a thorough investigation to locate and recover the lost device. This may involve scanning hard drives for data recovery and studying wireless logs, among other steps.

5. **Follow Up**: Once the laptop is recovered, be sure to notify your supervisor and the relevant department (e.g., finance, marketing) about the return and the restoration of your accounts and data access.

Securing our company’s assets is critical to our operations and data integrity. We appreciate your vigilance in safeguarding our company's resources. Please take this matter seriously and act promptly when you suspect a laptop has gone missing.  Remember, handling lost property responsibly not only helps maintain the integrity of our systems but also upholds the trust of our company's clientele and partners.

For any questions or further assistance regarding this matter, please reach out to our IT support team.
