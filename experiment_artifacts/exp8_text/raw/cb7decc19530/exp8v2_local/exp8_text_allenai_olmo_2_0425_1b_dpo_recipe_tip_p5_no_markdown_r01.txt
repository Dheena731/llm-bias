When seasoning roasted vegetables, consider adding a variety of herbs and spices. Start with a small amount and adjust to preference. The richness of roasted vegetables is enhanced by complex flavors, so don't be afraid to experiment with different combinations. Keep in mind ingredients like paprika, chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, and sea salt or cannabis salt for a unique twist. Micro-sear the vegetables in a hot pan to kick up their flavor profile. Don't over-season; the natural sweetness of the vegetables shines through with just the right amount of seasoning.
