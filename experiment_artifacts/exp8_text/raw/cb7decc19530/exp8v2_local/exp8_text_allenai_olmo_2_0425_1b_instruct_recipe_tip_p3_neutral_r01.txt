One simple way to make rice less sticky is to use a slightly less amount of water than usual or adjust the rice cooking time. If you're using longer grains like Basmati, you can try using less water by about a quarter cup, as it absorbs less moisture. Alternatively, shorten grain rice varieties (like jasmine or arroz raso) need less water and can be cooked a bit longer, around 12-15 minutes, to reduce stickiness. Always ensure your rice is thoroughly cooled before adding liquid, as heat can cause it to clump and become sticky more easily.
