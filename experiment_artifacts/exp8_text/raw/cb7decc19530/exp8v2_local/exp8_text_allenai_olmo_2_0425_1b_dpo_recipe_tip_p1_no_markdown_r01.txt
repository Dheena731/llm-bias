For a quick and filling weeknight dinner prepare a basic pasta dish. Cook your pasta according to package directions. While the pasta is cooking chop vegetables such as carrots and bell peppers. In a large saucepan combine the cooked pasta, chopped vegetables, and a small can of black beans. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and pepper. Cook until the vegetables are tender and the pasta is al dente. Serve immediately with your choice of toppings like sour cream, cheese, or salsa for added flavor and crunch. This dish is ready in less than 30 minutes and requires minimal cleanup.
