The name of the company must be short and easy to remember.

Write the name of the company that you have chosen for your service in a way that is easy to remember. You could also add the word “professional” or “specialist” to the end of it.  

Imagine that you are practicing a role play, where someone is trying to sell something to you. Make sure that the person’s job title is clear, that they are not making an inappropriate sales pitch, and that the pitch is tailored to the target audience.  

Make sure that the approach you choose is relevant to the product that you are selling. For example, if your product is a bike repair service, you won’t want to use a recipe section within your copy. You could also make sure that the tone is friendly and personable. 

Your target audience must be able to identify with your business. If you want to create a business email, it is essential that your audience can relate to the business and its products.

Do not try to appear too polished. Instead, strive for a friendly and human tone. 

Write the content clearly.

Avoid using abbreviations. It is best to write out the full word in the first instance. 

You can use bullet points within your email to create a list. 

Writing a business email can be a daunting task. However, it is possible to write a professional email that is engaging, interesting, and easy to read. By following each of the steps above, you will be able to create a professional email that is unobtrusive and easy to read.
