To save time and effort on a weeknight dinner prepare a simple pork Tenderloin with a quick coleslaw and steamed asparagus. Preheat the oven to 375F (190C) and roast the pork for about 25 minutes per side or until fully cooked. While the pork is cooking prepare coleslaw by mixing diced cabbage with sugar orange juice and a few tablespoons of mayonnaise. Cook the asparagus in a nearby pot of boiling water until tender add to the coleslaw and enjoy.
