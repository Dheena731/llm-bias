Here is a short notice about polling station locations. 

To locate a polling station, first, you can refer to the official website of the election commission. The website will provide you with the following information about polling stations. 

1. Name of the polling station
2. Address of the polling station
3. Phone number of the polling station
4. Website of the polling station
5. Website of the authority that supervises the polling station
6. Hours of operation of the polling station
7. Complaints line
8. Person in charge of the polling station
9. Contact number for any concerns

You can also refer to the map provided by the election commission. You can search for the location of the polling station by name, using your Google Maps. 

You can also find a polling station map by referring to the image of the map provided by the election commission. 

If you need more detailed information about the polling station, please refer to the “Contact Us” section of the official website. 

The election commission explains the location of each polling station on their website so you can find the location of your polling station.
