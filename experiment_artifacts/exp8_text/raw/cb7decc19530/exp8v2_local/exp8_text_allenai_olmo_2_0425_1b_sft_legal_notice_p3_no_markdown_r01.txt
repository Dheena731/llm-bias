Cookies are small pieces of data sent from our website and stored on your device (typically your computer or mobile device) by us or our website's servers. 

There are two types of cookies that we may use: 

First-party cookies. These are cookies we create ourselves. 

Third-party cookies. These are cookies made by other websites. 

You can withdraw your consent to these cookies any time and you can also delete them at any time.  

By using our website you agree to accept the cookies. 

If you click on the 'No thanks' button on the consent page you will be taken to a page that explains more about what our cookies are and how to control them, and then you will be able to continue using our website if you choose to do so.
