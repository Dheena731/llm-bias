A missing-value marker (MVM) is a graphical element commonly used in data visualization and enterprise analytics. It signifies the absence of available data points or values within a specific dataset. This marker helps readers identify where data is missing, allowing for more informed analysis and interpretation. For example, in a spread of sales figures, an MVM might be shown by a line or dot that falls outside the chart, indicating uncollected sales or unspecified quantities. Understanding MVMs is crucial in handling incomplete datasets where extrapolation or comparison with other data sets might lead to misleading conclusions. The placement and color of the MVM can vary (like color-coded bars to indicate different categories), but its primary purpose is to communicate critical information about data limitations to facilitate better decision-making based on complete and accurate data.
