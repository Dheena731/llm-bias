When planning lunches for a busy week, consider batch cooking on Sundays to create a variety of easy-to-prepare meals. Prepare leftovers to incorporate in your lunches throughout the week, choosing healthy options like salads, sandwiches, or soups. Use reusable containers to transport lunches and take into account different dietary restrictions or preferences among team members when selecting food options. Finally, consider creating a simple lunch prep checklist to streamline your planning process and ensure you cover all meal components efficiently.
