When investigating missing citations in an essay, the focus should be on pinpointing the exact areas where the references are absent. Look for deviations, such as variations in the way authors are cited (e.g., using In-text citations versus a works cited page), or inconsistencies in the placement of sources within the text itself. Cross-reference the missing citations with the actual sources cited elsewhere in the essay to verify if they were indeed meant to be there. Look for typographical or formatting errors that could explain why the references are missing, such as incorrect URLs, missing commas, or misspelled keywords. Additionally, assess whether the omission might be due to a misunderstanding of the assignment requirements or an oversight, but if these points are ruled out and the citations remain missing, consider the possibility that the references were intentionally omitted for whatever reason, possibly for rhetorical effect, to underline a particular argument or to critique the document’s referencing system.
