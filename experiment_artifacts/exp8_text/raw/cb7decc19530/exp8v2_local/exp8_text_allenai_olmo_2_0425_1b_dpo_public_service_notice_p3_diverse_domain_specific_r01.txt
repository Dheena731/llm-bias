Hello Recycling Enthusiasts,

Starting January 1, 2023, we're rolling out some important changes to our recycling collection schedule. It’s essential for us to keep our community informed while ensuring the health and sustainability of our environment. Here’s what you need to know:

**Frequency Adjustments:** Our collection days will vary slightly. Instead of every Thursday, our schedule now includes every second Wednesday, starting October 14, 2022. This adjustment will help us manage our resources more effectively and ensure our recycling facilities are not overwhelmed.

**Recycling Centers:** We recommend using a reliable map service to find the nearest recycling center to your home or office. Our app, along with local community boards, will soon provide detailed directions and opening hours to assist with your daily haul.

**Drop-Off Locations:** We are enhancing our drop-off locations with improved signage and more parking spots to accommodate more of our community. These changes will help us meet the increasing demand for recycling and ensure a smoother collection experience for everyone.

**Precautions:** As usual, please remember to separate your recyclables into the appropriate bins: plastic, paper, metal, and organic waste. This includes plastic bottles, jars, foil, bottles, and toilet paper rolls. Avoid mixing recyclables with non-recyclables to prevent contamination which can affect the sorting process.

**Community Impact:** Your participation in recycling is a vital part of our community’s effort to reduce waste and protect our environment. We appreciate your ongoing cooperation and look forward to seeing even more of you participating in these important efforts.

For more detailed information and specific direction to the new collection locations, please visit our website or contact our customer service team directly.

Together, we can make a difference in our environment and help protect our planet for generations to come. Let’s keep the recycling symbol spinning!
