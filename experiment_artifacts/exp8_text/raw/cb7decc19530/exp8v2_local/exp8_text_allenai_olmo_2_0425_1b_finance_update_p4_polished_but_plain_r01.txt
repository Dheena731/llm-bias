Optional but useful: If you included an image, include a link to the image and an (optional) link to where you found it.

Use the judgement of the tax preparer. If your tax preparer is thinking, "That's a great way to make my job harder," then don't use that technique.

If you have more than a few years of experience using tax software, feel free to omit the part of the post about the tax software. This stuff is already common knowledge for anyone who has been using tax software for more than a few years.

The post ends with a list of things to avoid. You can skip that part if you don't think it's relevant to your situation. For example, I think you could skip the point about making a hobby into a business.

...but if you are in doubt, you can try this test: If you're not sure if your business is a hobby, then it's not. Then you can skip it.
