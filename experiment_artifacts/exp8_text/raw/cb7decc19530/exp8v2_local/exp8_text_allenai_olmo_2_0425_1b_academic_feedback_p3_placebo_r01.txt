References shouldn't be cited in the summary, they should be cited in every manuscript and figure report. Please see the WITS tutorial for more information on how to cite your references.

For references that are not listed in the reference list, please cite them in the text, and if you need to cite more than one author, just list all their names.

Always, always cite your references when reporting on the work of others in your paper. If you are not citing any of the references you use in your paper, that means you have not reported on the work of others, and is a blatant violation of the First Author's agreement. If in doubt, please cite. If you are not sure how to cite your references, please ask a librarian.

Reviewers should also cite their references.

Submit your review at the journal homepage.

The abstract should be a concise summary of the paper. It is not used to report on the work of other authors, except in cases where the abstract is a review paper. For review papers, please always cite the articles and other reviews in the reference list.
