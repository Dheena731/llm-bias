If your company provides a shared network, use it responsibly. Misuse can lead to disciplinary action. Here are some prohibited activities:
- Tampering with network devices
- Installing spyware or malware
- Unauthorized peer-to-peer file sharing
- Violating confidentiality agreements
- Unauthorized use of company resources for personal gain
- Unauthorized modification of company property
- Engaging in or promoting illegal activities
Use this information responsibly. If you have any questions about this policy, please speak with the IT department.
