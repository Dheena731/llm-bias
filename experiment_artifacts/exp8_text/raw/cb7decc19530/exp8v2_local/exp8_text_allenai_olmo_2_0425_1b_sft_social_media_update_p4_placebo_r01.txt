Did you know you can create a new calendar to make your day-to-day easy and efficient? It’s simple! 
1. Make a new calendar with a simple color scheme that you prefer.
2. Set your desired dates and recurring events.
3. Add photos and lists as needed.
4. Finally, save it in a location that’s easily accessible. 

Now, you can easily keep track of deadlines, important dates, and more. The best part? You can access this calendar on your phone or computer, so you never miss a beat.
