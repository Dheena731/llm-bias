Write legibly and clearly. Avoid downlining, excessive use of bullet points, font size 11 or smaller, or other typographic elements that are difficult to read. Do not use excessive paragraph breaks.

Use the same font, size, styling, and layout as the rest of the document. Don’t break up a monospaced font like Courier with a non-monospaced font like Times New Roman. Don’t use spaces between words or sentences to increase readability: leave spaces between paragraphs and use a single space between sentences.

Insert links to other sources. Remember: not everything is web pages. There are PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and PDF zipped attachments. Don’t assume that everyone has the same document format.

Don’t insert blank lines, page breaks, or images. This can cause confusion about where your post ends or begins and what you have written.

Don’t use footnotes or linking to footnotes. Footnotes can be used to add additional information that pertains to an earlier paragraph, but too many footnotes can make reading the rest of the post difficult.

If you’re not sure how to do something, ask about it. It’s easier to add a question mark or two than to explain something later. Your readers will appreciate the effort you took to find the answer. This can also help prevent future problems.

Formatting an essay



The longer the essay, the more important the introduction: this should be one of the most important parts of the essay.

The introduction should provide the general point of the essay in one or two sentences. It should also make clear what the essay is primarily about. The introduction may include a quote that supports the essay’s main point.

The introduction should provide a general picture of the essay’s topic, but not including the question that the essay will answer.

The thesis statement should be clearly stated in the introduction and should be supported by examples.


Support your thesis by including evidence in the body of the essay, supporting each claim with examples. For example, if the thesis statement is that our society has become more violent, you could provide evidence about the number of gun fatalities per year and suicide rates per year.

The first body paragraph should be the strongest and most convincing and should summarize the main examples that the essay will provide. The second and third body paragraphs would then support the first claim with additional examples.

The conclusion should restate the essay’s main points and provide an overall impression that the reader should have after reading the essay. The conclusion should not repeat the thesis statement or the points made in the body paragraphs.


The conclusion may also contain a question that was posed in the introduction, with the expectation of a more thorough answer in the essay.
