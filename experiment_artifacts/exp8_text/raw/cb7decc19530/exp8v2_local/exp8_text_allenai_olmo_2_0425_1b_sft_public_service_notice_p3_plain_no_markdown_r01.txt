Dear Residents,

We would like to inform you about the recent changes to our recycling collection schedule in order to ensure a more efficient and environmentally friendly process for the residents. We understand that these changes may come as a surprise, but we hope you will find that, overall, it benefits us all.

Starting from now, we have introduced a new collection schedule for our recycling bags and bins. We will collect the following items on the 2nd and 4th Tuesdays of each month:

- Glass bottles and jars
- Plastic bottles and jugs
- Metal cans and lids
- Paper and cardboard
- Styrofoam and plastic bags
- Tins and pots
- Glass jars and jars
- Glass bottles and bottles
- Plastic bags and bags
- Tins and pots

We would like to remind everyone to make sure their recycling bags and bins are ready for collection on the specified dates to avoid any delays in our operations. If your bag or bin is not collected, you can put it out several days earlier in the following month.

We hope you will take this change in stride and find the new schedule more convenient for you. We will continue to keep you updated on any further changes to our recycling program. Thank you for your continued support.
