Road Closure - Heading up Hill

We are in the process of closing the northbound lane of Denman Road between the junction with Goldsmith Avenue and the roundabout with Helen Street from Sunday, November 16, until further notice due to heavy snow and ice. Please exercise caution when driving on this road and adjust your travel plans accordingly.

If you have plans to drive up the hill on this road on the stated dates, please consider detouring to either the southbound lane on Rampart Street or finding alternative routes. The road is expected to reopen after the snow has melted.

Please check back here for updates, as road conditions may change throughout the closure period.

Vehicle traffic must adhere to public safety measures, including slow speeds and extra caution.

Thank you for your understanding and cooperation.

This road reopening date cannot be guaranteed so if you plan to drive there after this notice, you may need to adjust your schedule in advance.
