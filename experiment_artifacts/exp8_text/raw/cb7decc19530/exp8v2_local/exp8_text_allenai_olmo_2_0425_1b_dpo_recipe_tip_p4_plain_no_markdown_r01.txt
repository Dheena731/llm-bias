Plan ahead and prepare a variety of meals to keep things interesting. Start by researching popular recipes that can be whipped up quickly and easily. Consider your dietary restrictions and preferences to ensure you have options that work for you. Create a shopping list to avoid last-minute rushes and ensure you have all the necessary ingredients. Pack simple yet nutritious meals in reusable containers with wrap or sandwich covers. Take along a reusable water bottle to cut down on waste and stay hydrated. Use kitchen gadgets or appliances on the weekends to save time during the week. If you have time over the weekend, preheat your crockpot or oven to save on time on weekdays. Remember to pack a backup lunch in case your plans change or if you need to grab a meal during a busy meeting.
