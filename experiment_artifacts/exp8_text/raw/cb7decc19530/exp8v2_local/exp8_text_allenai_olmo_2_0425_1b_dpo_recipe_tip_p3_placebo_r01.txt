To make rice less sticky, start by washing the rice thoroughly under cold water. Use high-quality long-grain rice to ensure it's dry and less likely to clump together. Once washed, drain the rice and let it air dry for a few minutes on a clean kitchen towel. To prevent the rice from sticking while cooking, add a little less water than usual—about two-thirds the recommended amount. Let the rice cook uncovered until it reaches your desired level of doneness, then fluff with a fork to release the starch and prevent it from becoming too sticky. This method not only keeps your rice from being too sticky but also enhances its flavor with less starchy residue, resulting in a perfect, non-sticky side dish.

**Note:** While it's generally good to let rice air dry after washing, it's important to note that using damp rice can increase the risk of germ contamination. Always ensure your rice is completely dry before cooking to maintain food safety.

Facts: Long-grain rice, high-quality rice, washing rice, draining rice, stirring rice, adjusting water content, cooking without cover, fluffing rice, and keeping rice dry post-wash can all contribute to less sticky rice.
