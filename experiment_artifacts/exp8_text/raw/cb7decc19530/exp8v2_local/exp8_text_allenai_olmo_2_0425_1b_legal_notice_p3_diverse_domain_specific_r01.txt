A. Privacy policy

1. Purpose

Your privacy policy should explain what data you collect, why, and what you do with it. In a nutshell, the ultimate goal of a privacy policy is to protect user data. It should explain what users can expect from your site or app.

2. Domain

You should include the domain of your website in your privacy policy, because privacy policies are primarily used by your website visitors. If a user accesses the website from a browser other than Chrome, they will see your site’s privacy policy.

3. Contact

Your privacy policy should include a contact information section for user inquiries or concerns. This information should be easy to access, and you should consider providing a physical mailing address and email address in the event that you ever want to mail your users a copy of your privacy policy.

4. Terms of service

You should explain what your users can expect from your online service and how you will use their information. The terms and conditions section is typically used in a contract to define the user’s obligations and rights. Your terms and conditions is your basic guarantee to your users that you respect their privacy.

5. Privacy statement

A privacy statement is typically used by companies that are already well-established. Your privacy statement should be legally binding, and include information about what data you collect and how you use it. You should always keep your privacy statement up-to-date.

6. Information security policy

A privacy policy should also include a section that explains how the policies of your website will be implemented. Your website’s privacy policy should be managed by a person that is qualified to handle sensitive information. For example, you may not be qualified to handle the sensitive financial data of your visitors, so you should appoint an employee to handle it instead.

B. Terms of use

1. Purpose

Your terms of use should explain what your users are allowed to do on your website. A terms of use agreement is used to set boundaries and expected behavior. It should be legally binding and include information about how you will use the user’s information.

2. Website and app

Your terms of use should include a description of your website and how it will be used. Your terms of use should include all of your website’s services, including e-commerce, subscription services, and anything else that your users will take advantage of. You can also mention third-party services if you want to refer to them.

3. Website and app permissions
