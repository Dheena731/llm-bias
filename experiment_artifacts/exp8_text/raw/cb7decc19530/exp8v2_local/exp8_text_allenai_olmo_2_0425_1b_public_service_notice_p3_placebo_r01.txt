A word about the surprising cost of recycling

We are always happy to help with your recycling and we are happy to pay, but if we do, then it is to help keep the price down for consumers.

Article 7

Every item being recycled must be taken to a collection centre by a valid recycler. If you have any questions about this you should ask your recycler, or the recycling company it works with. They should be able to give you details of the collection centre nearest to you. If they can not give a specific site please ask if they are able to give you the address of the company responsible for collecting your waste.

  • Please do not try and collect your waste yourself. Your recycler will not accept any items which you have not taken to them.
  • We are happy to pay the recycler the amount they quote for the items they collect. We also pay the charge for packaging that is used in the collection process if it is of good quality.
