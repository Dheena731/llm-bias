The end-of-year tax statement explains the tax calculation for your income during the year. It shows how much tax was withheld from your pay and how much you owe the government. The statement includes details about your total taxable income, deductions, credits, and any exemptions you might have. It explains any amendments to the previous year's return and highlights any taxes owed this year. Tax recommendations are provided, along with instructions on how to pay any remaining taxes if they exceed your scheduled payment. It's an important document that helps you understand how much money you've paid in taxes so far and what you need to pay next.
