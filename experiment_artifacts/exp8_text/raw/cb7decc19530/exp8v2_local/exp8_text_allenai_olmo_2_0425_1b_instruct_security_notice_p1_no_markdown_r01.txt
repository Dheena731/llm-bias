**Password Reuse: A workplace security risk**

In the interest of maintaining the security and safety of our workplace we strongly advise against password reuse. Each individual should create unique passwords for each of their respective accounts. Password reuse can result in a single compromised password exposing all accounts and personal information of the user. It is essential to regularly change passwords as per your organization's security policy and adopt a strong password policy. By following these guidelines we can enhance the security of our workplace. Remember security starts with you.
