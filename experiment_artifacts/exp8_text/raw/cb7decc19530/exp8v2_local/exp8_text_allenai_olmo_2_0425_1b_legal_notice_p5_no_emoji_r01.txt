Do not mention the logo of the email provider.

Do not use abbreviations or acronyms unless they are widely recognized.

Format the notice with the following components in the format you prefer.

These components must be in the order shown.

  1. If the network is not restricted, include a brief description of the network and its purpose.
  2. If the network is restricted, include a list of devices that are allowed to use it, such as a list of computers and mobile devices.
  3. If the network is restricted to a specific group of people, such as employees, make a note of the people and their IT administrator email addresses.
  4. If the networking service is provided by a 3rd party, such as a cell phone provider, include the relevant details about the service.
  5. If the service is provided by a 3rd party, such as a cell phone provider, include the relevant details about the service.
  6. If the network is restricted to a client group, such as a college or school, make a note of the client group.
  7. If the network is restricted to a specific user, make a note of the user and their IT administrator email addresses.

If the network is being used for a purpose other than what is stated in the Notice of Use, include that fact.

If there is a link, link to the resource you are sharing from your notice.

If the notice was written by a group or committee, include the name of the group or committee above.

If the notice has been written by a third party, make sure that the 3rd party is given credit in the notice (see Attribution and Acknowledgments).

Include the following items in the notice:


  • A copy of the notice, including all necessary information, including the notice's date, the name of its author, the author's email address, and the author's phone number.
  • A copy of the notice's copyright information (see Copyright).
