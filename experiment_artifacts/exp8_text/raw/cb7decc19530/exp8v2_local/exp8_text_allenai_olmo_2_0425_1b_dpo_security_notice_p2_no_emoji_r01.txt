Attention employees,

We are aware that phishing attacks have been on the rise recently, and we want to remind you all about the importance of being vigilant and cautious when receiving unsolicited emails, especially those requesting personal information or claiming to be from our organization in order to steal your credentials or compromise the security of our systems.

Here are some key points to remember about phishing emails:

1. **Unusual Source**: Phishing emails often mimic our organization and its communications to be as realistic as possible. Look closely at the sender's email address and be cautious if it differs significantly from our official communications.

2. **Unsolicited Attachments or Links**: Pay attention to emails that request you to forward attachments or click on links without your direct knowledge or our official contact information for support.

3. **False Urgency**: Be cautious of emails that create a sense of urgency or fear to prompt you to act quickly without verifying the authenticity of the request.

4. **Incorrect Grammar and Spelling**: Legitimate organizations typically have professional communication standards, so be skeptical of emails with glaring grammar or spelling errors.

5. **Verify the Source**: If you have any doubt about an email’s authenticity, contact our help desk or IT department to verify the accuracy of the information before taking any action.

To help prevent falling victim to phishing scams, please acquaint yourself and your colleagues with our official communication practices and pass this information along to others. By fostering a culture of security awareness, we can work together to maintain the integrity and safety of our digital environment.

Thank you for your vigilance and commitment to protecting our workplace.
