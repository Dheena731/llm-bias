5. Don't forget the thank you

Not all applicants are asked to submit a thank you letter - it is optional. If you do send a thank you letter, make sure that you don't spend too much time on the actual writing. Keep it short and sweet. 

Sample text = "I would like to thank you for your time, and for the opportunity to submit a resume. I am looking forward to learning more about your company and seeing how I can contribute to your team."
