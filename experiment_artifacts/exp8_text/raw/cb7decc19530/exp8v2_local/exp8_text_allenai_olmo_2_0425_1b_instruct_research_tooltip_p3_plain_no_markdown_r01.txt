When using a date-range selector, this tool tip explains how to use it. Select the dates you want to consider by choosing them from a range indicated on the screen. The date range can be moved, expanded, or contracted by dragging the selection box around the desired period or the minus (-) sign to exclude a period, or the plus (+) sign to include a period. This quick and easy way to filter data ensures you only see relevant information, making it more efficient to focus on the relevant timeframe.
