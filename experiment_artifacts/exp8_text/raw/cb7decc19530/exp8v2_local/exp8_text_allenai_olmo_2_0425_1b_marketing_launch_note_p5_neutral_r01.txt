The nice thing about podcasts is that they are very easy to publish. There are plenty of websites out there that will help you with that. But if you want to do it by yourself, then just put the mp3 file into the box several times until it stops uploading.


Even better, I would recommend you to create two podcasts, one in French (CD) and one in German (CD). When you have one for each language, then you will be able to label the mp3 files accordingly, making it easier for people to follow your steps. Then, you can later edit them to be the same.

And no need to worry about translations, I think that the purpose of the podcast is to learn a language, not to translate one. Just let the listeners know if they are interested in knowing the original language.
