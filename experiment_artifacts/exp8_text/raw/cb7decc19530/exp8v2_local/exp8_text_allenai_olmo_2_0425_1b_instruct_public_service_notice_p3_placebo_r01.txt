**New Recycling Collection Schedule: Get Informed and Get Involved**

As part of our continuous efforts to promote sustainability and reduce the environmental footprint of our community, we are making some updates to our recycling collection schedule. Starting this fall, our recycling bins will be collected on a weekly basis rather than every other week. This change is part of our ongoing commitment to ensure that recyclable materials are properly sorted and reused, minimizing waste and enhancing our local environment.

Here are the new collection times for your convenience:

- **Monday:** Collection will take place from 7 AM to 7 PM.
- **Tuesday:** Recycling is collected from 7 AM to 7 PM.
- **Wednesday:** Collection starts from 7 AM to 7 PM.
- **Thursday:** Recycling collected from 7 AM to 7 PM.
- **Friday:** Recycling is collected from 7 AM to 7 PM.
- **Saturday:** Recycling collection is from 7 AM to 7 PM.
- **Sunday:** Our final collection is Monday through the week.

**Why the Change?**

The decision to change the recycling schedule was made after a thorough analysis of community feedback and waste reduction initiatives. By collecting recyclables more frequently, we hope to increase the rate at which communities can sort and recycle their materials, reducing the amount of waste sent to landfills and promoting a culture of sustainability among residents.

If you have any questions about this change or need further assistance, please do not hesitate to reach out to our team. Our goal is to support a greener environment, and your participation is vital.
