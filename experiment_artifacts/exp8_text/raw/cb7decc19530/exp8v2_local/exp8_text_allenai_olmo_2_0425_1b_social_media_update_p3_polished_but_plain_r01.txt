Be specific. Do not write a general update about your work. Write about a specific part of your project. For example, if you’re writing a Tweet, you could write “My design for this year’s Halloween costume is nearly done,” instead of writing “I am working on my design for the Halloween costume.”

What to Do if You Can’t Write a Blog Post About Your Work

If you don’t feel like writing a blog post about your work, don’t worry. There are other ways to share your experience. Here are some ideas:

Write a summary. Write a few sentences about what you’ve done and the lessons you learned. For example, if you were working on a project at your job, you might write something like “I wrote this blog post about my work experience. I learned that being patient and persistent can help me do a good job.”

Share your experience on social media. Post your experience on Twitter, Facebook, or Instagram. Post about it on your company’s website if it has one. Tell your friends and family about it. If you don’t have a company website, tell people about it on your personal social media accounts.

Use these tips to write better blog posts about your work experience. Writing better blog posts about your work experience will help you get better feedback from your team, and it will make you look like a more capable worker.
