If you're not already using one, I highly suggest you check out the calendar app. We all use calendars so it makes sense for us to use an easy-to-use and high-quality calendar app. The best ones allow you to add events, view your upcoming schedule, and see what's coming up. They also give you options for reminders, due dates, and simple reminders. It's a good habit to have, but if you don't it may well be the best thing you've ever done.
