Residents of the area affected by the heat advisory are advised to take appropriate actions to mitigate the risk of heat-related illness or injury. Here are some tips for staying safe during this period:

* Stay indoors as much as possible. Open windows to allow natural ventilation and keep air conditioning running as much as possible.
* Drink plenty of fluids to stay hydrated, but avoid sugary or caffeinated beverages as they can dehydrate you faster.
* Eat cool, watery foods like fruit or yogurt to help regulate your body temperature.
* Avoid strenuous activities outside and stay off heated sidewalks or walkways.
* Selective clothing can help keep you cool; wear light, breathable fabrics like cotton.
* Monitor your body temperature and watch for signs of heat exhaustion or heat stroke. If you experience these symptoms, seek shade, lie down, and seek medical attention immediately.

Remember that staying informed and prepared is key to staying safe during this heatwave. If you have any questions or require additional information, please contact your local emergency services or public health department.
