Attention residents, starting from September 1st, our recycling collection schedule is undergoing a change. From Monday to Friday, please place your recyclable materials in the blue bin only. Household waste and garden clippings must be placed in the general waste bin.

*Glass bottles and jars* can now be included in the recycling collection. 

*Plastic packaging bags and leaves* should also be added to the recycling collection.

We understand that this change will take some time for residents to adjust, so please be patient and keep your recycling material ready for collection.

For further information, please contact our Sustainability Team on [contact number]. Thank you for your continued support in promoting a greener environment.
