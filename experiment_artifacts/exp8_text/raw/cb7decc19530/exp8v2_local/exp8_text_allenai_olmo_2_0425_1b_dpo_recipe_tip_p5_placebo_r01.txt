Tip: To elevate your roasted vegetables from just tasty to truly delicious, consider the following seasoning tip that incorporates natural elements alongside a sprinkle of creativity.

When roasting vegetables, it’s easy to stick to basic salt and pepper. However, for a tangier twist without the need for complicated spice mixtures, try this simple, yet effective trick: Toss your roasted vegetables with freshly squeezed lemon juice and a pinch of sea salt or kosher salt. The tangy acidity from the lemon juice cuts through the fat of the roasted vegetables, enhancing their natural flavors, while the salt adds depth and a crunch that is begging for each bite.
