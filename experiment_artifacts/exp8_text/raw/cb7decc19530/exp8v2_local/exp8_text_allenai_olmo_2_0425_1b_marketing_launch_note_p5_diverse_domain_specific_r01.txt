6. Consider the audience. The topic of a launch note may be more appropriate for a blog post than for a newsletter, which is why it's best to have a single topic per note.

7. Be mindful of the time and space limitations of your note. Try to keep your note to 500 characters or less. Consider the metrics that you're tracking. Remember that most podcasts are short and fit in their notes within 2 to 5 minutes. Many newsletters are also limited in length.

8. Consider the length of your note as a percentage of your overall total content. In general, I think about 10% of the content being a dedicated launch note is a good balance. If your newsletter is 50% dedicated to content, launch notes should be no more than 5%. And if your newsletter is 1% dedicated to content, a launch note could be as short as 1 character.

9. Think about adding a short video or image to your launch note to help the reader (and listener, in the case of a podcast) understand what the latest launch note is about. A good example is our recent launch note on "Replacing your old refrigerator with a new one."
