Here’s a quick, personal nudge to incorporate short screen breaks into your day: 

Imagine you're a novelist, crafting a deeply engaging plot for your latest novel. Now, picture yourself taking a "chapter-break" to jot down thoughts or potential plot twists. This isn't about killing creativity; it's about sharpening your brain's edge. 

Short breaks from screens aren't just about your eyes straining from the brightness; they're about giving your mind a rest. Your digital passion projects will thank you when you come back with renewed ideas and focus. Each time you take a break, consider writing down a few notes, doing a quick mindfulness exercise, or engaging in a short physical activity that gets your blood flowing and your mind refreshed. This not only boosts productivity later on but also improves your creative output. 

So, go ahead and set your alarm for a 5-minute break, a short walk, or a quick sketch or doodle. This little digital hiatus isn't a sign of weakness; it's a strategic move to maintain your productivity and creativity's sharpness. Trust me, it'll make a world of difference!
