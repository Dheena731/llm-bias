Use Different Calendar Apps and Tags to Keep Your Sanity Sane!

Ever find yourself floundering in a calendar app trying to keep up with work commitments, family events, and personal schedule? Here’s how to tame the beast:

1. **Custom Groups:** Create custom calendar groups for work, play, social events, doctor's appointments, and whatever else your daily rhythm requires. This way, you can easily see what’s happening without having to dig through mountains of events.

2. **Share and Tag:** Use the tagging feature to easily share events with friends and family. You can label an event with a specific tag (like 'Family bonding weekend') to make it easy for others to see and RSVP to.

3. **Notifications:** Set your calendar app to notify you of meetings, anniversaries, and other important events. This way, you’ll never miss a crucial date, and you can react more quickly to everyone’s schedules.

4. **Light Up Your Calendar:** Use custom colors and fonts to make your schedule pop and stand out. Bright green at a holiday party? Sure! But also a subtle reminder for a dentist’s appointment.

5. **Only Important Dates Viewable:** Filter your calendar to only show the dates that are important to you. This helps keep the clutter to a minimum and lets you focus on what’s truly important.

By implementing these strategies, you can transform your calendar from a daunting tool into a helpful companion that keeps your life organized and stress-free.
