If you must make a memorial announcement, do so sparingly, and only to announce the death. Do not make announcements for the funeral. Do not use this page for announcements unless it is absolutely necessary.

To memorialize someone, you can:

  • Write a memorial announcement
  • Send a letter to a friend or relative
  • Contact a funeral director

If you are writing a memorial announcement, you may want to include specific details about the person's life, such as first and last name, relationship to you, occupation, any significant events that happened in the person's life, or whether they had any major accomplishments.

Prepare for a Public Announcement

If you want to make a public announcement, you need to let the owner of the address know.

Remember that this is a public announcement, and be careful not to reveal private information about the deceased.

To make a public announcement, you can:

  • Contact the person's family before you make a public announcement
  • Make the announcement public
