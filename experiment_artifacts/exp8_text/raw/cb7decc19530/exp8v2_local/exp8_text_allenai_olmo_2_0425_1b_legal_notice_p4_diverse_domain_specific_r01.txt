Example 2

How to Write a Legal Notice About Refund Eligibility

Car Company, Inc. is happy to announce the launch of our new all-new Car. Model 18. The Car, like all Car 18s, is a top-of-the-line model. All cars are equipped with state-of-the-art computer-controlled power steering and power brakes. Each Car comes with a two-year warranty. Any Car that is not returned to Car Company, Inc. within two years of purchase will be returned.

We will be offering a 25% refund on the purchase price on all Cars that are returned within two years of purchase. Please contact [email protected] to register your interest in receiving a refund.

It is important to note that this is a generic legal notice.
