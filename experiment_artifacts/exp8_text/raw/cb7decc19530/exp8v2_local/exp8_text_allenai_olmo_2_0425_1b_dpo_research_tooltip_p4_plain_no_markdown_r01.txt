A missing-value marker, also known as an FV marker or simply a missing-fill marker, is a symbol used in statistical analysis, data visualization, and datasets to indicate points where the data is absent or undefined. Think of it as a placeholder where you'd expect to find some information, but it's not present. For example, in a scatter plot of sales against customer visits, an FV marker would be a small square without a point, indicating that the corresponding data point (visits per sale) was not collected or is not shown in the data series.

In statistical modeling, this marker helps in understanding the model's fit and generalizability. It assists analysts in identifying where data quality or collection methods fell short, allowing them to adjust their approach or inputs to improve data accuracy for future analyses.
