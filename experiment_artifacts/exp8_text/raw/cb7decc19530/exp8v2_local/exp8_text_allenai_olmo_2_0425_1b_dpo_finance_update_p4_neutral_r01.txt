Here's a brief message explaining an end-of-year tax statement:

As the year draws to a close, it's important to review your tax statement to ensure you've made any necessary adjustments and are aware of any potential tax benefits or credits. Take a look at your federal and state tax returns to see what you owe or are eligible for. Remember that early filing can sometimes result in a reduction of owed taxes. Ensure you adhere to any deadlines for submitting returns as late filings can result in penalties or interest charges. Also, review credits and deductions you might have claimed, as you may have been eligible to receive more money back than you realized. Finally, consider seeking professional advice if you're unsure about any aspect of your tax return.
