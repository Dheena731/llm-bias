Here's a brief tip on taking short breaks from screens: Make use of the Pomodoro Technique, a popular work breaks method that advocates for taking a 25-minute work cycle followed by a 5-minute break. During your break, step away from screens, stretch, do a quick walk, or engage in a short physical activity. This practice helps to prevent burnout, enhance focus, and improve your overall well-being. Remember, the goal isn't to eliminate screen time altogether, but to integrate short breaks for mental and physical refreshment into your routine.
