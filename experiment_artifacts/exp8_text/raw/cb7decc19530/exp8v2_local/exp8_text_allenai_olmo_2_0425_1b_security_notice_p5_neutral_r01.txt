How to dispose of your laptop

There are several options for disposing of a laptop, but not all are appropriate. The following is a description of each as requested by the Australian Taxation Office.

Computers sold through a computer shop.

The accounts department of the computer shop must report the sale to the Australian Taxation Office (ATO).

The ATO advises that the sales report must include sufficient details to enable the laptop to be traced if it is stolen.

The shop, and the person who buys the laptop, must also supply a written certificate stating that the sale is exempt from GST. The certificate must accompany the sale report to the ATO.

Laptops bought from an auction or trade sale.

The ATO states that the auctioneer or the person who buys the laptop must report the sale to the ATO, which will then issue a GST invoice to the buyer. As with the computer shop, the seller must supply a certificate stating that the sale is exempt from GST.

Laptops purchased from a computer shop that were purchased before January 1, 2013.

If you purchased the laptop from a computer shop before January 1, 2013, you are not required to report it to the ATO. A receipt of purchase is sufficient proof that the sale is not taxable.

The ATO advises that there is no reportable period for purchase from the internet or from a flea market.
