Steer clear of eye-strain and burnout by setting a timer for brief screen-free periods. An average adult spends about five hours daily in digital realms and repeatedly peeking away tears the eyes and taxes the mind. Even a few minutes of downtime away from screens can refresh focus and energy levels. Consider a 10-to-15-minute break every hour, engaging in activities such as taking a walk, doing a quick yoga session, gardening, or simply closing your eyes and inhaling deeply. If you're working from home, try closing your eyes for a few breaths before starting a new task, and return to your screen with renewed clarity and ease. Remember, taking these short breaks is not a sign of weakness but a smart move to maintain peak cognitive performance and prevent long-term health issues associated with excessive screen use.
