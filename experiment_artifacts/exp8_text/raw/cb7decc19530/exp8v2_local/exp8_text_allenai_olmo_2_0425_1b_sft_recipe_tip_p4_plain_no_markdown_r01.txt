Tip: Plan your lunches first to save time and energy. Start by checking your calendar to see which days you have lunch breaks and what you need for each day. Make a list of meals you want to eat and what ingredients you need to buy. Once you have done this, find a place where you can eat lunch like a cafe or coffee shop and pick a time to visit. You can also make a meal plan and write it down. On a piece of paper, write down the meals you want to eat, the ingredients that you need to buy, and the time you want to eat. Cut up the ingredients that you need and store them in the fridge or pantry. You can use frozen vegetables or cook them but do not use pre-made soups. Make sure you have utensils, glasses, and a plate. You also want to leave space for leftovers.
