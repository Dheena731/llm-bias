Here’s a short cooking tip to help you whip up a delicious and satisfying weeknight meal. Start by sautéing a small onion and a handful of frozen mixed vegetables in some olive oil until they’re soft and aromatic. Then add canned clams or mussells and let them cook until they open up a bit. Toss in some garlic cloves and cook a few more minutes until aromatic. Pour in a can of chunky Tomato sauce and stir well. Finish with a sprinkle of parmesan cheese and serve over a bed of cooked spaghetti or pasta of your choice. This quick and flavorful dish is perfect for feeding a crowd and is easy to put together on a Friday evening before heading out the door.
