When you're heading to your polling station this Election Day, make sure to know where to go and what to do. It's crucial to understand the layout of your polling station to ensure a smooth and efficient voting process. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

1. **Polling Stations by Borough**: Each voting district in the UK is assigned to a specific polling station. Look up your local area on the electoral roll website. This will give you a clear map of where your polling station is located.

2. **Accessibility**: Some polling stations may have amenities like toilets and parking spaces, especially if they are in isolated areas. Check before you go to ensure there are facilities available.

3. **Opening Hours**: Typically polling stations are open from 07:00 to 20:00 on polling day. Ensure you check the specific opening times of your polling station to avoid any disappointments if they are longer or shorter.

4. **Location Placement**: In urban areas, polling stations are often situated in public spaces such as libraries, schools, or community centres. For those in more rural or remote areas, you might find them in local community rooms or additional buildings designated for election day activities.

5. **Security and Privacy**: Be aware of the security measures in place at your polling station, including any security personnel and the measures to protect your personal information.

Remember, these are general guidelines, and the specific location of your polling station may differ. Use the electoral roll site and local media for the most accurate and current information.

Happy voting!
