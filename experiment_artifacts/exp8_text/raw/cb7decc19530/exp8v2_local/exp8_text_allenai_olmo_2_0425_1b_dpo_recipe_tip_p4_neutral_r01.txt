Start by setting aside two days for planning so you aren’t scrambling on the menu day. Choose meals you can prep ahead of time or in small batches. Use a rotating menu so you don’t get too stuck in a rut. Be sure to consider dietary restrictions or allergies, and grab leftovers from the previous day’s meal for a quick lunch. Keep a fridge stocked with reusable containers and leftovers to easily assemble a lunch. Consider sharing meals with colleagues to save time and money, and enjoy the conversation!
