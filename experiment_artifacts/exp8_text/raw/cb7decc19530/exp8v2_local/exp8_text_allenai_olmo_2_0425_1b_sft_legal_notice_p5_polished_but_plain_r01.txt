To ensure the safety and efficient use of our shared network, we have set the following rules for acceptable use:

1. Keep your messaging civil and respectful. No harassment or cyberbullying.
2. Keep the content appropriate for all users. No graphic violence or explicit material.
3. Do not use the network to steal, cheat, or engage in any form of malicious activity.
4. Do not use the network for personal gain at the expense of others.
5. No unauthorized copying, distribution, or other unauthorized use of the network's resources.
6. Do not use the network for illegal activities.

Needless to say, any violation of these rules may result in disciplinary action.

We appreciate your cooperation in maintaining a safe and efficient network for all.
