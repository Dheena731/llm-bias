## Description of the expense

The field to be completed is "Description of the expense".

Example of good input:

#### Description of the expense

The field to be completed is "Description of the expense".
The field is only used to quickly describe a specific expense (the explanation of the expense).
A good description of the expense as original receipt can be:
- The date of payment and the amount of payment.
- The date of the signature on the receipt and the amount of payment.
- The duration of the work, the same date.
- The duration of the works.
- The date of acceptance.
- The temporary location, the same date.
- A reference number from the original receipt, a unique identifier.
- A reference number the same date and type of work.
- A proof of payment (cash or card), the same date.

Do not use Markdown formatting.
