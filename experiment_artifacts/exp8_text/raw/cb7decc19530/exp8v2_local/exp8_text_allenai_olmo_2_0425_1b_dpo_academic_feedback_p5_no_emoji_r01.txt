In an essay, the absence of proper citations can seriously undermine the credibility of the author's arguments or ideas. Citations are essential for verifying the sources used, providing readers with the opportunity to explore the original works themselves and understand the context, authority, and particulars discussed in the essay. Without citations, the essay risks being viewed as an unsupported opinion, potentially alienating an audience seeking factual backing. Remember, in academic work and many professional fields, adherence to citation standards is crucial for maintaining scholarly integrity and avoiding plagiarism. Including citations within the text ensures that readers can follow the argument, discover the original sources, and respect the author’s contribution to the discussion.
