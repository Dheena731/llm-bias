Attention all employees!

We have received malicious phishing emails at this workplace. Please be aware of the following:

- If you receive an email from the company that you believe may be fraudulent, report it to the designated email address (usually "info@company.com") and please do not reply or click on any links in the email.
- If you are unsure if you received a legitimate email from the company, contact your manager or HR to verify the sender.
- The email may contain links or attachments that appear to be from the company but are actually from a phishing site.
- Do not share personal information, passwords, or financial information with any source that may be sending you these emails.
- Be cautious of emails asking for personal information or claiming to be from the company if they are not verified or are suspicious.
- Follow these steps to help protect yourself from phishing attacks.

If you receive any phishing emails at work, please report them to the designated email address for reporting phishing attempts.
