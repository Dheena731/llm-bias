Recycling collection schedules are undergoing a minor change starting next week. Residents are advised to refer to their local recycling program's website for the new collection dates. Previously, residents would have collected their recyclable items at specific times on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Starting next week, recycling collections will now occur on Wednesdays only. Customers should ensure their items are separated into correct recycling bins such as plastic, glass, metal, paper, and compost. This change was made to enhance sustainability and streamline recycling processes. For further information or questions, contact your local recycling authority directly as detailed instructions may vary depending on the region.
