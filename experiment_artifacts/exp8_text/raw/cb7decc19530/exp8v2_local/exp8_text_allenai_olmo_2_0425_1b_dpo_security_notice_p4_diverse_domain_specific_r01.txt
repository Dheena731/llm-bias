**What is Two-Factor Authentication?**

Two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security to your accounts by requiring not just something you know (your password) and something you have (like a mobile device or a hardware token), but something you are (your biometric data like a fingerprint or facial recognition code). This method is more secure against unauthorized access and is a standard best practice in securing online accounts.

**Why Implemented?**

Our commitment to protecting your data and privacy remains unwavering. By implementing 2FA, we are setting a strong bar against cyber threats and phishing attacks that unfortunately pose a constant and growing risk online.
