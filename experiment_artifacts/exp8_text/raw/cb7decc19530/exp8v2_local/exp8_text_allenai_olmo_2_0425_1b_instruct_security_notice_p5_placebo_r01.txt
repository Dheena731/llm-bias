**Important Notice: Loss of Company Laptop**

At [Company Name], we value the security and confidentiality of our electronic assets above all else. We are reaching out to alert our valued employees to the immediate reporting of any lost or stolen company laptop. This policy is in place to ensure that stolen or misplaced laptops do not fall into the wrong hands, compromising sensitive information, proprietary data, or proprietary technologies.

**What to Do:**
If you find a lost or stolen laptop, please immediately notify your supervisor, the IT department, or call the [ companysafe number] to report it. It is crucial that you not attempt to retrieve or use any data from the device without proper authorization from your supervisor and IT personnel.

**Facts to Consider:**
* The company laptop may contain confidential company information, customer details, financial records, or proprietary technology.
* The stolen laptop is likely to be possession of, or associated with, an unauthorized person.
* Physical locations to check for the laptop include common areas, office spaces, the reception area, and under lockers or tables in designated IT facilities.

**Guidelines:**
* Never attempt to recover data from the device without proper authorization.
* Maintain confidentiality regarding the incident, sharing only with authorized personnel.
* Keep any evidence related to the laptop loss, including any unique identifiers or serial numbers, until the issue has been resolved and you have received confirmation of its return.

**Reporting an item lost versus stolen:**
* A lost laptop is something you inadvertently leave behind.
* A stolen laptop is something taken by someone else with intent to use or sell its contents.

Please act promptly and follow our established procedures to prevent data breaches and maintain the integrity of our company's information systems.
