Ask yourself what the aim of your piece is and how the target reader will react to it.

Here is some sample text for a marketing note:

Dear [target reader],

We recently launched our new subscription service. It's a great way to introduce yourself to coffee lovers in your region.

For example, you could subscribe to our weekly coffee subscription where you receive one coffee every week.

Or you could subscribe to our monthly subscription, where you receive one coffee each month. This is a great way to meet new people and try a variety of coffees.

Subscribe today for a great price of £10.50/€12.50/US$14.00.

If you're interested, please contact us by email.

Yours sincerely,
[Marketing Director]

You can see how the marketing note is written to the target reader and how it is written to persuade the reader to subscribe.

If you write your own marketing note, don't forget to write it to the right target audience and to include the appropriate information about the service you are marketing.

By writing a marketing note, you can become more successful in your coffee marketing and in your business in general.
