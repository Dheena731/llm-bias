Two-factor authentication (2FA) is a way to add an extra layer of security to your accounts by requiring a second form of verification in addition to your password. This means that even if your password is compromised, the attacker still would not have direct access to your account unless they also have the second factor on hand. The second factor can be something that only you have on you, like a physical token or a fingerprint scanner, or something that only you know, like a secret phrase or a piece of information only you should know. By turning on 2FA for your accounts, you are taking a proactive step towards making your digital life more secure. It's a small change that can make a big difference by adding an extra barrier against unauthorized access.
