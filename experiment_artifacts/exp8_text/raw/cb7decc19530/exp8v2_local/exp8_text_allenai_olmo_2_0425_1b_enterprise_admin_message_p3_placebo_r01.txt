Marketing Committee

There is a new group on the mailing list, called Marketing Committee. Here is a brief explanation of what it is and when it will be useful.

Marketing Committee was the idea of a group of people from the first meetings, but was left dormant for a number of years. Now it is being revived for two reasons. First, you have started getting more regular members. Second, a change in the use of the mailing list has occurred.

Today, the marketing committee is an informal standing group that meets irregularly. It’s called Marketing Committee because it has a few important tasks. Not because it has a formal title, which is determined by how many people are on the list. And not because the majority of the people on the list want it to be.

The goal of the marketing committee is to encourage the list to remain useful and full of valuable information for all the people who use it. To do that, it handles the following tasks:

  1. Selecting and approving the topic for a discussion.
  2. Setting and reviewing ground rules for the list.
  3. Setting time for and discussing the topic.

The topics chosen for discussions are largely an opportunity to brainstorm about web design and development. The ground rules for the list are largely an opportunity to make the list more open and welcoming.

The topics and ground rules are set and reviewed by the Marketing Committee. The ground rules are reviewed once a month. The topics are discussed for a few weeks, and then the Marketing Committee suggests the next topic or recommends that no new topic is chosen.

The topic is the interaction between the Marketing Committee and the list. If the Marketing Committee approves the topic, the members of the Marketing Committee are expected to handle the discussion. If the list owners agree to the topic, the Marketing Committee should be used to announce the topic. And similarly, if the Marketing Committee rejects the topic, the list owners should be used to announce that.

In short, the Marketing Committee is for two reasons: to help with the two most important tasks for a list: setting ground rules and maintaining focus on the main topic. It should not be used for everything.

What is a topic? A topic is a discussion that is essentially a single topic. As a rule, a topic means something like this:

  1. A question or a series of questions that is addressed to the list.
  2. A short statement that is addressed to someone. You can address the list, but you don’t have to. You can address a specific person.
  3. A statement that is addressed to a specific person. You can address the list, but you don’t have to. You can address a specific person.

Why a specific person? Because the Marketing Committee is a standing group. These posts are for those who participate regularly in the list, but that does not mean that they all have the time to watch every post. To make the list reliable, the Marketing Committee is specifically addressed to someone who is assumed to be watching the list carefully.

What is the ground rules? The ground rules are the first door that people gain access to the list and the first step into using the list. The Marketing Committee is in charge of them. The ground rules are not a draft that may be changed, nor a list of rules that may be ignored. They are the rules of the list, which are subject to change only by consensus.

The ground rules are necessary to keep the list relevant. The ground rules are also necessary to keep the list polite.
