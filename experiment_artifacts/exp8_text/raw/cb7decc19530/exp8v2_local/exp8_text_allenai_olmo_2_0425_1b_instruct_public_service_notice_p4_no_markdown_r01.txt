**Heat Advisory Issued for Greater Springfield**

The City of Greater Springfield is currently under a Heat Advisory. A heat advisory is in effect for the following reasons:

* High temperatures are expected to remain above 90 degrees Fahrenheit for the next 48 hours.
* Heat strain can cause health problems such as heat exhaustion and heatstroke. 
* Residents are urged to take necessary precautions to stay cool such as staying inside during the hottest parts of the day, using fans, taking cool showers, and staying hydrated.
* Public facilities may have misters or hoses available for cooling.

For further information please visit the City of Greater Springfield's website at www.cityofgrsp.org or call the emergency hotline at 555-HAT-SAF.
