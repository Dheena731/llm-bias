Attention all employees and team members:

We have received reports of phishing emails being sent to our company's email systems. Phishing emails, which masquerade as legitimate emails from trusted sources, are designed to trick you into revealing sensitive information, such as usernames, passwords, or financial details. These threats can compromise our company's security and personal information, and are considered serious breaches of our corporate security policies.

To protect yourself and our company:

* Do not click on any links in these emails.
* Do not provide any personal information unless you are certain of the sender's identity.
* Report any suspicious emails to our IT department immediately.
* Keep your company's email system updated with the latest security patches.
* Educate yourself and your colleagues about recognizing phishing attempts.

We appreciate your vigilance and commitment to maintaining a secure and safe working environment.

Thank you for your cooperation.

Team, stay safe.

(Written by the HR Department)
