Recycling Collection Changes in Your Area

Starting next week, starting to be effective in October, recycling collection hours in our community will be changing. This is a part of efforts to increase the amount of recycled materials diverted from the waste stream.

Starting in October, recycling will be collected every Thursday from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM. On the other days, we will have full-day collection from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Night collection will continue to run from 7:00 PM to 7:00 AM.

To make sure you don't forget, please make plans to bring your recycling along with your regular trash to your curbside in the coming weeks. For the most accurate information, please check our website through December and beyond.

Thank you for your continued support in keeping our community clean and sustainable!
