Drinking enough water throughout the day is essential for your health. It helps with digestion, keeps your skin hydrated, supports your metabolism, and keeps your organs functioning optimally. It's recommended to drink about 8 glasses of water a day (or around 2 liters), but individual needs vary based on factors like climate, activity level, and overall health. Keep a reusable water bottle with you throughout the day and try to sipping water regularly - you'll likely need it! Remember, water is the perfect accompaniment to any meal and should not replace meals, but rather aid in their consumption.
