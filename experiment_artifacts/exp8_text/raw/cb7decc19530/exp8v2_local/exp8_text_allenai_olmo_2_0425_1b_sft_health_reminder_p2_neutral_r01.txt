Drink plenty of fluids! And by that, I mean water. Lots of it. Water helps us digest food, keep our skin healthy, and lubricate our joints. But remember, this is a general reminder. It could mean many different things to different people. If you're a teenager or older, for example, you might need more water in hot weather. If you're pregnant, breastfeeding, or doing a lot of exercise, you might need even more. So please don't hesitate to ask me for more information on how much water you should drink each day, if this sounds more helpful.
