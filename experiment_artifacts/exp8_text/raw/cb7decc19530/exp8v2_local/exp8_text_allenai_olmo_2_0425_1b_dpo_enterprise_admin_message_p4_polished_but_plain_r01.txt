Attention all subscribers!

We are conducting quarterly access review processes to ensure your service continues to meet your needs. During these reviews, we will check the following:

1. Subscription status: We will verify that you have signed up for the correct plan and are enjoying your access.

2. Usage totals: We'll review the number of hours or minutes you have accessed your account in the past quarter. If you've surpassed your usage limit, we may charge you.

3. Usage patterns: We will examine your usage data to see if there are any unusual patterns and determine if you may require a different plan.

If your usage falls within the prescribed limits, you can expect to receive your statements and enjoy your service without interruption. However, if there has been significant usage over the threshold, you may be subject to charges for the excess. To avoid any fees, please keep track of your usage and communicate with us if you anticipate needing additional access.

Should you have any concerns about your access or billing, please contact our customer support team at info@yourdomain.com. We appreciate your understanding and look forward to serving you for the coming quarter!
