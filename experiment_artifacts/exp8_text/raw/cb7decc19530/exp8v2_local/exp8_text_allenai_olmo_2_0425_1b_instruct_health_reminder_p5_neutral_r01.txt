Refilling a prescription is important for maintaining the correct dosage and ensuring your health. Make sure to: 
1. Check the expiration date on your original prescription.
2. Obtain a new prescription from your healthcare provider.
3. Locate the pharmacy on your list where you can drop off the old prescription for refilling.
4. Follow the pharmacy's refilling procedures, which typically involve returning the old prescription, signing a form, and either presenting identification or proof of identity if required.
5. Keep track of your refills and don't exceed the number prescribed by your doctor.

Please ensure that you follow your healthcare provider's instructions and don't stop taking medication without their approval.
