When selecting a date range, the tool tip will give you an overview of how to choose the correct date range. To begin, you need to select the years. As you scroll through the options, you will find multiple options to choose from. Continue horizontally, and you will find multiple options to select the months. Lastly, the time is on the left side of the list, and you will see options for both the day and date. Once all options are set, you will be given the tools to select the dates you want to choose from.
