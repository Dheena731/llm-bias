Choose a few nutritious options that are easy to prepare so you don’t have to waste time. Pack a variety like a sandwich with turkey and greens or a wrap with hummus and veggies. Consider leftovers from a previous meal or pre-cooked dishes that need reheating. Select a simple salad or a pre-assembled pasta dish. Remember to include a drink and maybe a small snack like a banana or an apple to keep energy levels up throughout the day. Keep all items in reusable containers or bags to reduce waste and make cleanup easy.
