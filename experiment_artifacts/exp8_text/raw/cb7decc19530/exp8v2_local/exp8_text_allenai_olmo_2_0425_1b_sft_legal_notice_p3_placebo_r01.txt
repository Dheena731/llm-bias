Cookie Usage Notice

We, [Your Brand Name], understand the importance of your privacy and the importance of knowing what we do with your data. We're here to help you understand how we use cookies with your data.

What are cookies?
Cookies are small pieces of data sent by a website to your browser and back when you visit. They are stored on your computer or mobile device by your browser (your browser is your browser) and are used by a website to remember who you are when you are a user.

Why are we using cookies?
We are using cookies to remember who you are when you visit, so you don't have to keep telling us who you are. We will also use cookies to remember which products you have in your cart so you don't have to add everything to your cart. We use cookies to provide you with a better experience.

Which cookies do we use?
We use cookies to identify users, log in, protect users, provide personalized content, measure usage, access users' personal information, and remember what pages you have visited.

How long do we keep cookies on your computer?
We will keep cookies on your computer for a certain period of time before they expire:

• For cookies used to remember personal data, we will keep those cookies for at least one year after you log out.

• For all other cookies used to remember users, we will keep those cookies for two years after you log out.

• For our cookies used to remember who you are, we will keep those cookies for 30 days after you log out.
