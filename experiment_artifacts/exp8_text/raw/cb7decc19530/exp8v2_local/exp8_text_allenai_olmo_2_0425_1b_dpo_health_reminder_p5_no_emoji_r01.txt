Here's a quick reminder about refilling your prescription:

**Make sure you've filled your prescription accurately and completely.**

**Check the expiration date on your prescription.**

**If your medication looks or smells bad, or if it doesn't look right, don't use it.**

**Use your medicine as directed until it's time to refill your prescription.**

**It's important that you take your medication as directed to prevent side effects and to keep you healthy.**

**If you think your symptoms haven't improved or if you're having side effects, contact your healthcare provider for guidance.**

**Never share or use someone else's prescription.**

**Be sure to bring your original prescription with you when you fill your refill at the pharmacy.**

**Stay informed by checking your prescription records online, if your healthcare provider provides this service.**

**Maintain good communication with your healthcare provider about how well you are responding to your treatment plan.**

If you have any questions or concerns about your medication, don't hesitate to ask your doctor or pharmacist.
