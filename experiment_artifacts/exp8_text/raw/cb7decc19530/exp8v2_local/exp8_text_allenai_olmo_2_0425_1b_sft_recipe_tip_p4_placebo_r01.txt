Tip: Identify your priorities for each lunch. Are you preparing to work or need a serene break? 

If you need a mix of work and relaxation, look for a lunch that will give you the energy to tackle tasks before or after your meal. Try grilled chicken salads or quinoa bowls with seasonal veggies. 

For days you need a light focus, a lunch that won't distract from work tasks may be the best choice. Remember, a long hike in nature can be as rejuvenating as a delicious salad when you don't want to juggle a recipe.  

If you are out to eat, consider a side of the meal you normally do at home. This will help keep you full from the breaded fish tacos to the chopped salad with cranberries and goat cheese. If you are filling up on the side items of a menu, you'll save money and calories. 

Remember, the key to a great lunch is choosing what you will eat, not what you won't.
