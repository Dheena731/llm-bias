Make it polite

"Apologies all around" works best, but "I am sorry" or "I'm sorry" do not hurt, either.

"My sincerest apologies" takes it one step further, but I've unironically used this in real life before.

"Sorry" should be a generally appropriate word in most contexts. "I'm sorry" is more informal and rude sounding than "I apologize", but note it is in fact rude sounding.
