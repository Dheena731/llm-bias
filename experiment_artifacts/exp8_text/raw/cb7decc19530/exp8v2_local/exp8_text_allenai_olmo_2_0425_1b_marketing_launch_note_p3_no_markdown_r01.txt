* **Basic Expenses**: A budget template is used to track the basic expenses the person needs to pay each month.
* **Net Income**: A budget template is used to track the monthly income a person needs to pay their bills.
* **Net Expenses**: A budget template is used to track the monthly expenses a person needs to pay each month.
* **Wage**: A budget template is used to track the monthly income for a person.
* **Tax Deduction**: A budget template is used to track the monthly tax deduction a person needs to pay each month.
* **Net Income**: A budget template is used to track the monthly income a person needs to pay their bills.
