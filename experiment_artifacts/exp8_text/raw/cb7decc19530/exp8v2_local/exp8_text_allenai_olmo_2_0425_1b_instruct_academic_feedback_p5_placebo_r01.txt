When analyzing an essay with missing citations, it becomes evident that Proper citation is a cornerstone of academic integrity. Without citations, the essay loses its credibility, as readers are left to wonder about the veracity and source of the information presented. Proper citations not only give due credit to the original authors but also serve as a roadmap for other researchers to locate and validate the claims made in the essay.

For instance, imagine a statement like "According to Smith (2019), nutrient X has been proven to improve health outcomes exponentially..." without the original source being flagged. This absence creates an unreliable foundation for the argument within the essay, as it raises questions about the validity of the claim without a basis in verifiable evidence or reliable references.

In conclusion, missing citations not only detract from the academic rigor of the essay but also hinder the broader discourse by tilting it toward unsupported assertions. It is imperative to ensure each idea is supported by pertinent and complete citations for a coherent and credible argument.
