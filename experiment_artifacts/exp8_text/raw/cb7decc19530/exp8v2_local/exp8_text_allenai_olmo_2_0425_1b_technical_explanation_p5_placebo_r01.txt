There are two common types of rate limits: Application Rate Limits (ARLs; also called Application Rate Limiting) and User Rate Limits (URL Access Rate Limits; also called User Access Rate Limits).

ARLs limit the rate at which an application can request content from a service. These limits are controlled by the application and should be permitted by the service, such as when a user creates a profile on a social networking service and begins sharing content with their friends.

URL Access Rate Limits (URL ALs) or User Access Rate Limits (URL LES) limit the rate that requests can be made to a URL. These limits are controlled by the user, and should be permitted by the service, such as when a user sets up a profile on a social networking service and begins sharing content with their friends.
