Refilling your prescription is something you should do before your medication runs out. Always follow the instructions on your prescription label for the best results. Irregularly stopping medication can lead to withdrawal symptoms and potential health risks, so it's important to maintain a consistent medication regime prescribed by your healthcare provider.

Here are some steps to remember when it's time to refill your prescription:

1. **Read the Label:** Before you pick up your prescription, carefully review the label to ensure you're taking the right medication and the appropriate dosage.

2. **Determine the Prescription Limit:** Know the total number of refills you have been granted under the prescription. This information is generally listed on the prescription itself.

3. **Check Expiry Date:** Ensure the refill doesn't exceed the expiration date. Prescription medications should be used before their expiration date to ensure they are safe and effective.

4. **Contact Your Pharmacist or Doctor:** If you have any questions or concerns about extending your prescription, don't hesitate to speak with your pharmacist or your healthcare provider. They can guide you on safe and legal practices and ensure you receive the correct refill.

5. **Follow the Process:** When you go to the pharmacy to refill the prescription, follow their standard procedure. This typically includes presenting the original prescription, signing a new prescription form if necessary, and submitting payment for the refill.

Refilling your prescription correctly helps maintain your health and safety and respects the guidelines set by your healthcare provider.
