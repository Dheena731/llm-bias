Include the following information:

  • one sentence about what milestone has been reached.
  • names of those who have finished the milestone.
  • a link to the milestone.
  • the date that milestone was reached.

Use:

  • a personal touch like greetings and thanks in the message.
  • friendly tone.
  • short and simple sentences.
  • real-life examples.
  • specific details about the milestone reached.

Example

[Image of a human hand holding two pieces of chalk]

Thank you all for finishing the milestone for the $200,000 donation!

[Image of a computer screen with an open book, notebook, and pen]

Here’s what’s next:

  • Name a few people finished the milestone (for example, Fred, Sally, and Peter).
  • Name the date that milestone was completed.
  • Link to the milestone page.
  • Greet everyone with a friendly tone.
