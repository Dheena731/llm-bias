A year-end tax statement summarizes all your income and any deductions you've taken during the tax year. It helps you understand how much tax you owe or are entitled to and prepares you for filing your return. On this statement you'll find lines for various sources of income such as wages or salaries, self-employment income, interest, dividends, capital gains, social security, and any other sources of funds. You'll also see amounts for taxes withheld from your paycheck, estimated payments you made during the year, and any remaining balance. At the bottom of the statement are instructions for filing your return. Don't forget to review your deductions and credits as they can often lower your tax liability.
