Tip: Keep fresh herbs in a cool, dry place out of direct sunlight. A kitchen chalkboard can be a great visual reminder or a simple shelf where they can thrive. Use a glass or a pots and pans storage box to keep them organized and prevent them from drying out or being crushed. Trim excess stems as needed and water sparingly. If your herbs frequently require fertilizing, consider using a well-balanced fertilizer designed for garden plants rather than herb-specific ones to avoid affecting their flavor.
