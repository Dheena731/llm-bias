Attention all employees:

We need to be vigilant against phishing emails that could compromise our business information and systems. Here’s what you should know:

1. **Beware of Suspicious Sender**: Be cautious of emails coming from your bank, your IT department, or any other organizations you routinely interact with. Legitimate organizations will never ask for sensitive information via email.

2. **Be Skeptical of Urgency**: Scammers can create a sense of urgency to prompt you to act quickly. Take your time to review any request for action before responding.

3. **Check the Source**: Look at the email address – verify it’s official. Sometimes attackers use domain names that are similar to the real ones but with slight variations.

4. **Do Not Click on Suspicious Links**: Avoid clicking on any links in the email. Instead, use your browser’s address bar to verify the link’s authenticity.

5. **Report Suspicious Activity**: If you receive an unexpected email that you believe to be phishing, report it immediately to your HR department or the IT support team.

Remember, your vigilance is vital in protecting our company and its assets. Let’s stay informed and stay secure.
