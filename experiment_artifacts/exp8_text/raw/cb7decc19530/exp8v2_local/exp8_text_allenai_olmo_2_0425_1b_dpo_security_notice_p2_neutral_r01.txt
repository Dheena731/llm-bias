Attention Team,

It's important to be vigilant against phishing emails in our workplace. These emails often appear to come from trusted sources but may actually be attempts to deceive you into providing confidential information or clicking on malicious links. Here are some ways to identify phishing emails:

1. **Poor Spelling and Grammar:** Phishing emails frequently contain numerous spelling and grammatical errors.
2. **Unusual Requests:** Be cautious of requests for sensitive information, especially those asking for passwords, account numbers, or personal identification.
3. **Poor Contacts:** Verify the email source by contacting the supposed sender directly through a known, trusted channel.
4. **Unusual Links:** Avoid clicking on links in suspicious emails. Instead, type the URL directly into your browser to verify its authenticity.
5. **Unexpected Claims:** Phishing emails often claim that important information is at risk or that immediate action is required.
6. **Requests for Immediate Action:** Take the time to seriously review all communication, especially urgent or alarming messages.

To protect yourself, educate your colleagues about these tactics and encourage a culture of skepticism and alertness. If you receive a suspected phishing email, please report it to your IT department without responding or sharing the information.
