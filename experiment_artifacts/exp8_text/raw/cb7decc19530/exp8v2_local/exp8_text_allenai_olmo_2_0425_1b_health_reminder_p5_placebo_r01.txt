5. Conclusion

In our case, it was a new medication to treat a specific disease. In summary, patients who are prescribed a new medication should be encouraged to follow up with their healthcare provider for regular monitoring as the course of treatment may vary over time. For example, a person may be taking medications to treat shortness of breath, but over time may require an inhaler in addition to the medications for treatment of the underlying cause of the shortness of breath. Additional medications for an underlying condition may be prescribed to complement the initial medication and to treat the specific condition.
