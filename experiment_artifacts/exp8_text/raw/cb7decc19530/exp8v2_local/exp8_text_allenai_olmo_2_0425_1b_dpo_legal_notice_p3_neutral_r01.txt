**What are cookies?**

Cookies are small pieces of data sent from a website and stored on your device (your computer, smartphone, or tablet) while you are browsing the site. They are used to remember information such as your preferences, website activity, and login details, assisting in providing a more personalized and efficient browsing experience.

**Types of cookies**

1. **Session Cookies:** These cookies are used by a web browser to maintain your browsing session while you navigate between different pages on our site. This allows us to provide a seamless experience when you return.

2. **Persistent Cookies:** These cookies remain on your device after your session ends and can be accessed by the website’s server up to your predetermined expiry date, or until you manually delete them.

3. **Third-Party Cookies:** Sometimes we use external websites to host our content, widgets, or services (like social media). These third-party cookies are used by those sites to analyze usage, track your visit, or enable social sharing features.
