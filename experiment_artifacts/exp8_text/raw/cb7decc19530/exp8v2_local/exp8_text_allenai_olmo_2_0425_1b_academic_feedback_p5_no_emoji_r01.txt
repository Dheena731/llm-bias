Glossary of terms

Index of terms
The body of a written work, consisting of a collection of entries arranged in logical order.
A section of a book which contains information about the work's contents, the author and the publication date (the date when the work was published).
In law and in computer science, a section of a word processor which contains instructions for how to fold a document.
Periodical, publication, journal, magazine, or newspaper. This term is often incorrectly used to refer to a periodical that has been published for many years.
A printed or electronic printed document.
A document which is distributed at a given time.
A character representing a mathematical or chemical quantity such as the number 1, the symbol for the Natural Number 1, or the chemical symbol for lead.
To add a footnote to a text, or to set a text as a footnote.
A reference to a particular idea or statement in an essay or article, as opposed to an index entry.
A reference within a work to an external work, such as another book, article, or work of art.
The study of the written or printed word.
A person who is employed by a newspaper or other periodical to check spelling and grammar.
A reference made in the body of an essay or article to a specific piece of information that has been given in an essay or article as an index entry.
Index entry
A reference to information within an essay or article, such as a speciality, a particular argument, or a chemical symbol.
A sheet of paper or other material.
An arrangement of data according to some particular rule.
The field or field of study of the material arts, which encompasses all kinds of artistic works.
A visual presentation of information, often including charts, graphs, tables, and diagrams.
The writing of a work of fiction, such as a story or a novel, or of poetry, or other forms of art.
The body of an essay or article which is written in a straight line.
A collection of essays, articles, speeches, and other writing. The term essay is often used only to refer to an essay written by an individual author.
An essay published on the web by an author or group of authors.
An illustration, photograph, drawing, or other visual representation. The term is also used in the study of written or printed words.
An illustration that accompanies a text, such as an illustration of a chemical formula, or a photograph of the author's office.
The act of finishing a piece of work, such as a story, a novel, or a poem.
A selection of published essays, articles, or other written work.
A form of writing in which the names of the authors are omitted.
A book or collection of books containing numerous entries which are not arranged in logical order.
A reference to an external work such as a poem or a painting, or to an external entity such as a person or a place.
The act of reading aloud.
The study of various aspects of a particular topic, such as a government, a nation, or a corporation.
The act of arranging information in a particular way, which often has no practical significance.
The act of writing a new book or a new edition of an existing book.
To copy a work by hand to a new medium, such as a computer.
A group of people who have some common interest, such as a business, a profession, or a political group.
The study of the structures and materials present in living things, and the processes that affect their activity.
