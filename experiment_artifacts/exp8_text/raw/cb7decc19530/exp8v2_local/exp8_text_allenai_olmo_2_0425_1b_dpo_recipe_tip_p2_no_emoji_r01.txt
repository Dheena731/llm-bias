Store fresh herbs in a cool, dark, and dry place away from direct sunlight. If your herb collection is close to a window, consider using a glass container or airtight jar to shield them from sunlight. The temperature should be around 60°F to 70°F (16°C to 21°C), as this is their ideal growing environment. Avoid moisture buildup by using a dry dish or cloth to cover the pot when not in use. This will help prevent mold and keep your herbs fresh for longer periods.
