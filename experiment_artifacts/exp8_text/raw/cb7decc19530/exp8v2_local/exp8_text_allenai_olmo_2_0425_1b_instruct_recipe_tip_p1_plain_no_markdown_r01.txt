To make a delicious and satisfying weeknight dinner, start by preparing a simple pasta dish. Sauté some onions and garlic until soft in a large pan. Stir in a jar of your favorite pasta sauce and season with salt and pepper. Cook the pasta until al dente then toss it with the sauce. Serve hot along with your choice of vegetables like green beans or zucchini. This easy and straightforward recipe is perfect for a rushed night and ensures you'll have a complete, satisfying meal ready to eat.
