Attention

Use of the shared network is governed by the following rules:

1. Only authorized personnel are permitted to use the network.
2. Unauthorized sharing of files and information is strictly prohibited.
3. laser printers must be used responsibly and only for legitimate business purposes.
4. Internet browsing sessions must be brief and focused on work-related activities.
5. Power consumption must be minimized to prevent network congestion.
6. Laptops and other electronic devices must be turned off or placed in sleep mode when not in use.

Failure to adhere to these guidelines may result in Network Enforcement Actions.

For further information, please contact our IT department.
