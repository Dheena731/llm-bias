To prepare a delicious weeknight dinner, begin by sautéing vegetables such as carrots, zucchini, and bell peppers in olive oil or butter until they soften. Add a small amount of garlic for a burst of flavor, then stir in canned tomatoes with your chosen seasonings, such as basil, oregano, or parsley. Simmer the mixture for a few minutes to meld the flavors together. Serve over a bed of cooked rice, quinoa, or pasta for a satisfying and nutritious meal. Remember to adjust the seasoning to your personal preference, and enjoy your homemade weeknight dinner!
