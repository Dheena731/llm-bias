To make rice less sticky, drain off some of the cooking water as soon as the rice is done. If you were making it in a pot, you could use a slotted spoon to let the excess liquid drain away. Use a rice cooker if you're making it in a cooker. If you're cooking it in a pan, try not to overcook the rice in the first place. If you have to overcook it, add some extra rice to your recipe. However, if you're trying to make a sticky dish, add a little cornstarch to your water or broth before you're ready to pour it into the rice.
