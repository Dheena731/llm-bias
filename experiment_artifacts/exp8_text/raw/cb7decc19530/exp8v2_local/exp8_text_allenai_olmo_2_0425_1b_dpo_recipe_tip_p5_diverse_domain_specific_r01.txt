Tip: Spice Up Your Roasted Vegetables the Right Way

When it comes to enhancing the flavor of roasted vegetables, the choice of seasonings is key. Here's a straightforward and personalized tip that takes into account the unique tastes and textures of the vegetables you're cooking:

**Use Smoky Bay Leaves:**

*Combining smoky and aromatic flavors, bay leaves are an excellent choice for adding a depth of flavor to roasted vegetables. Not only do they lend a subtle smokiness, but their own subtle sweetness helps balance the sharp, caramelized textures of roasted Brussels sprouts, carrots, or zucchini.*

*To infuse your vegetables with bay leaves, start by picking fresh leaves, not the patted-out kind. Twist them slightly to ensure good contact with the vegetables. Before roasting, place the leaves around the edges of your roasting pan and roast until they start to crisp and turn colors, about 10-15 minutes. Remove and discard the leaves, and let the vegetables cool slightly. Finish them with a sprinkle of ground black pepper and sea salt before serving to accentuate their smoky and aromatic goodness.*

**Give Spice Bits a Try:**

*Not all spices are created equal. Cumin, coriander, and paprika are well-known for their savory and umami-rich qualities that work particularly well with roasted vegetables. For a hint of spice, try mixing in ground cumin and coriander into your seasoning mix about 10 minutes into roasting. This combination will give your vegetables a flavorful depth of umami and a pop of spice.*

**Pulse in Pesto or Dried Herbs:**

*For a fresh yet aromatic touch, blend a bit of pesto into your vegetable oil before tossing them with fresh herbs like parsley, chives, and basil. The herbs’ oils will infuse the vegetables with vibrant green colors and a fresh, tangy flavor. Alternatively, sprinkle dried herbs such as oregano, marjoram, and sage over the vegetables during roasting for a burst of aromatic goodness.*

By incorporating these specific flavors, you'll create a dish that resonates more personally with your palate, ensuring each bite is a memorable experience that elevates the simple act of roasting vegetables to something delightful and satisfying.

Remember, the key is to be specific to your vegetables’ personalities and preferences, allowing each dish to shine. Enjoy your cooking adventure!
