Store fresh herbs in a cool, dry place. Keep them cut into pieces or in thin strips to allow air to circulate and prevent fibers from drying out. Avoid storing herbed water or dry herbs together to prevent cross-contamination and ensure freshness. If you frequently use a particular herb, consider cutting it into small, easily identifiable pieces to keep it fresh longer. Always check the packaging or label for storage tips and use them as guidance to maintain the best results from your herbs.
