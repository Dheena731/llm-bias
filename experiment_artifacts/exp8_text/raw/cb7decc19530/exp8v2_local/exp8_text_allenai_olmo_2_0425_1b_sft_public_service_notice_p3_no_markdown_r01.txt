Dear Residents,

We are pleased to let you know about the new recycling collection schedule. From June 1st - September 30th, the recycling will be collected on weekdays only. This will require you to empty your bins on Tuesday and Thursday as usual. The new collection site is located at the corner of Maple and Cedar Sts. Please ensure you bring your recycling bags to this location to collect and dispose of your recyclable items. This change will help us improve our community's sustainability and encourage residents to recycle more. We appreciate your cooperation and support as we strive to make our community a greener and healthier place to live. Thank you for your continued support.

Best regards,
The Village Planning and Development Board
