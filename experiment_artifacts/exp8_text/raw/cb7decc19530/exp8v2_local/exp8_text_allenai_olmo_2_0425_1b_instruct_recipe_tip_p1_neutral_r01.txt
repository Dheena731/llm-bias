Spinach Garlic Spinach Casserole is a quick weeknight dinner that provides a burst of freshness with fresh spinach and garlic. Here’s a quick and delicious recipe: 

Ingredients:
- 1 box of frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed dry (about 1 cup)
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon butter
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese (optional)
- Cooked pasta (or noodles) of your choice 

Instructions:
1. In a large bowl, combine the spinach, onion, and garlic. Drizzle with olive oil and butter. Toss to combine. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Mix in more Parmesan cheese, if desired.
2. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
3. Press the spinach mixture into an ungreased 9x13 inch baking dish. If using, top with cooked pasta.
4. Bake for 25-30 minutes, or until golden and bubbly.

Enjoy this Easy Spinach Garlic Casserole! It's perfect for feeding a crowd or when you're short on time and feel like enjoying a delicious, healthy meal.
