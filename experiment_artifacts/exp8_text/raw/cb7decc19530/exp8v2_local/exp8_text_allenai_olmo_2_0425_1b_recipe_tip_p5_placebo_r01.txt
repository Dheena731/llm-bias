How to Choose 5 Paragraph Essay Topics?

After you have settled on a 5 paragraph essay topic, the next step is to choose a 5 paragraph essay title. What should you to do to choose the best topic for your essay? Use the following steps.

How to choose 5 paragraph essay topic

  1. Choose a topic that you are interested in. Find an essay topic you would be excited to write about. This will make the writing process easier, as well as make the essay more interesting to read.
  2. Research thoroughly. Check out credible sources that will be trustworthy. Search for facts, cite them, and make sure that they are relevant to your essay. Try to avoid general topics.
  3. Brainstorm with others. Talk to your friends, family, classmates. Discuss the topic with them. List some ideas, discuss them, and try to narrow down the list as much as possible.
  4. Think about different perspectives. Consider the different factors like your age, gender, race, religion, and other features that you can bring into the discussion.

How to Write a 5 Paragraph Essay?

Now that you have settled on a suitable 5 paragraph essay topic, the next step is to write a strong 5 paragraph essay. Follow the steps below.

Steps to write a 5 paragraph essay

  1. Find the main point. Take the 5 paragraph essay topic and narrow it down into a specific idea. What is your thesis statement?
  2. Make an outline. Write an outline for your essay. Write down the main points that you want to cover in your essay.
  3. Do your research. Use credible sources to verify the information.
  4. Write the introduction. Write a good 5 paragraph essay introduction. A good introduction should be interesting and should briefly state your thesis sentence.
  5. Write the body paragraphs. Now that you have your thesis statement, it is time to write your body paragraphs. Each paragraph should be focused on one main point.
  6. Write the conclusion. Write a good conclusion for your essay. A conclusion should be powerful, well organized, and should leave a lasting impression on the reader.
  7. Edit and proofread your essay. Read your essay and make sure that it is free of grammar and spelling errors.
  8. Format your essay according to the requirements. Make sure to follow the required formatting style.
