Quick and Simple Veggie Stir Fry: Overwhelmed with dinner planning? Transform your weekly meal prep into a scrumptious, healthy, and effortless vegan stir fry. Ingredients: Mixed veggies (e.g., broccoli, carrots, bell peppers), soy sauce, wheat thin noodles, peanut butter, sesame oil, ginger, garlic. Tip: Blend garlic, ginger, and a pinch of sugar into soy sauce for a zesty flavor. Toss all ingredients in a wok or large skillet, adding soy sauce and sesame oil just before serving. Season with chili flakes for a kick. Happy cooking!
