Date of Posting

The date of posting should be included in the summary, but it shouldn’t be the first word.

Use an ical-formatted date with the full year.

The date of posting may be abbreviated to DD/MM/YYYY (e.g. 30/07/14)

The date of posting may also be abbreviated to MM/DD/YYYY (e.g. 07/30/14)

Avoid including text such as “today” or “yesterday” in a summary unless it is the main point of the comment.

Human Interface Guidelines

Avoid leaving comments with links to your own website, service, or product.

Comments should be general, not promotional, and should not be about self-promotion.

If you have a commercial product, service or software, avoid including that in the comments.

Avoid including comments like “This page is protected by copyright.”.

Do not link to a personal blog or other social media profile.

Do not make personal comments. Personal comments are not appropriate on public forums.

Do not link to a product you are selling or promoting; instead, create your own article or blog post.
