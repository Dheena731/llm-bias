Monitor your monthly spending summary in real time

This section will outline how you can monitor your spending in real time using your monthly spending summary. The specific steps for this will differ slightly depending on which method you use to generate your monthly spending summary.

  1. Generate your monthly spending summary. As explained above, you can view your monthly spending summary in one of two ways:

    • Annual Spending Summary option: Generate an annual spending summary and then click "View" to see a monthly version of the summary. This is the most basic way to view your spending in real time.

    • Monthly Spending Summary option: This option generates monthly spending summaries directly in the Money Dashboard. You can download the summary to your computer, or it can be viewed using third-party online tools such as a spreadsheet program.

  2. View your spending in real time.
