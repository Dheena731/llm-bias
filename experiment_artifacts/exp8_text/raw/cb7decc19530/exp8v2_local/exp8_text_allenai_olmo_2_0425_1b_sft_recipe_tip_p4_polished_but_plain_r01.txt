Planning lunches for a busy week can be a hassle. Make a meal plan that will last you a week. Plan your meals by cutting out what you eat, then cook that meal. Buy groceries in bulk to save money and time. Consider what you are going to eat for the week, and make that your meal plan. There are so many healthy foods that you can eat on a budget. As you are planning a meal for the week, think of different ways that you can prepare the meal. You can prep a week long worth of meals by cutting up vegetables in advance, and cooking them all at once. 

Use leftovers from the week to make lunch the next day, by using them in a wrap, or a salad.
