If you are not currently working on a phishing campaign, you could create a training material for your end users explaining how to react (or not) to phishing emails.

In order to create a training material, you need to select a training resource you are familiar with (such as a well-written book, an interesting video, or a blog post). You could also create a training material yourself. More elaborated training materials are available below.

Do not use Markdown formatting.

The job is to explain the following:

  • the content (e.g. phishing email contents)

  • the context (what is the user supposed to do, e.g. click a link, or not)

Important: please make sure your training material explains what the user should do in a clear and actionable way. We strongly discourage training material for your end users that is more like an academic paper on the subject or that explains in detail the nature, goals, and methods of phishing, rather than what to do in the real case.

Explain the context (what the user is supposed to do, e.g. click a link, or not) as follows:

The user should not open this email, and should not click any links in the email.

Explain what is meant by not opening or clicking the links (i.e. the user cannot open the link, and should not click the link).
