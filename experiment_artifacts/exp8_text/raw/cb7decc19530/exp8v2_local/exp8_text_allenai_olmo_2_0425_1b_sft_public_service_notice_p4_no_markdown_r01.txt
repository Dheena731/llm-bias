This is a public notice to remind everyone to take precautions to stay safe from the heat. High temperatures are expected to continue this week.

Please be aware that heat-related illnesses can be serious if they go untreated. Stay indoors and cool off as much as possible. Avoid activities that could lead to overheating or dehydration such as excessive exercise and strenuous physical activity. If you were unable to stay in air-conditioned spaces or if you cannot find air conditioning, please try to stay cool and not get dehydrated. If you begin to feel the effects of heat exhaustion such as dizziness or fatigue or if you have been outside for several hours and have not been able to get inside, please see a healthcare provider.
