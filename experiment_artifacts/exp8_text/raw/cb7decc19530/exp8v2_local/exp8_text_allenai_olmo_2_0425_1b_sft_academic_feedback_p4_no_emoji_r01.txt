Title: Effectiveness of Tai Chi in Enhancing Mental Health

Abstract:
This study investigates the potential benefits of Tai Chi as a form of physical activity on mental health. Using a randomized controlled trial design, participants engaged in Tai Chi sessions for six weeks. Results indicated a significant improvement in mood and tension, with participants reporting lower stress levels and greater overall well-being. Further analysis revealed that the mental health benefits were more pronounced in female participants. Recommendations suggest that Tai Chi should be incorporated into mental health programs to promote overall health and well-being.
