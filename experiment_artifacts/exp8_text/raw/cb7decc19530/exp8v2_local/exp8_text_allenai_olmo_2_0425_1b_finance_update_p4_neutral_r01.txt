When tax time rolls around, there are many things we would like to be able to avoid doing. However, it’s inevitable that we will, at least once a year, spend hours on our taxes. Unfortunately, many of us have to worry about our tax returns because we are self-employed, and our business-related expenses are not deductible.

One way to reduce the amount of time you spend on your tax paperwork is to process your 2018 tax return as soon as possible and stick to the submission deadline for your country. The IRS will provide you with a form, likely 1040 or 1040-A. It is available in English, Spanish, and French.

Another option is to file through TaxAct. TaxAct is a digital service that provides tools and objects that make it easy to file your tax return. TaxAct provides detailed step-by-step instructions and international filing options for the United States. TaxAct also gives you the option to connect to TurboTax or H&R Block through a mobile app and web portal.

The IRS has a convenient filing option for tax returns that is called free e-file. The IRS will provide you with a form, likely 1040EZ or 1040A, through which you can file your tax return. It is available in English, Spanish, and French.

So, there you have it, three ways to help reduce the amount of time you spend on your tax paperwork. We hope you have enjoyed reading about this topic, and we wish you a happy and prosperous 2019!
