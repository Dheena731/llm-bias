Shared Network Usage Guidelines

We reserve the right to monitor and control the use of our shared network. Users are expected to abide by the following rules:

1. **Respect Privacy:** Do not access or distribute any private or confidential information. Respect other users' privacy and personal data.

2. **Acceptable Usage:** Use the network only for legitimate purposes. Do not engage in illegal or unethical activities such as hacking, cheating, or any other form of unauthorized access.

3. **Respect Technology:** Understand and comply with our security policies and guidelines. Avoid actions that could compromise the network’s security or damage our system.

4. **Authorized Devices:** Only use the network with authorized and registered devices. Unauthorized devices may be removed from the network.

5. **Network Usage:** Do not overload the network with excessive data transfer or usage. Utilize the network as intended and in moderation.

 violators of these guidelines may face restrictions and penalties as determined by our administrators. We encourage cooperation and respect for the shared resources provided by our company.

Thank you for your understanding and adherence to these guidelines.
