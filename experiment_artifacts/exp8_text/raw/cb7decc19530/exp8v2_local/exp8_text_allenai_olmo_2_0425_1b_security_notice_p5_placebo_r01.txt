Media Objectives

Use the media object to emphasize your big idea. Use a photo and headline to make the world notice you. Media objects have text and a graphic.

Media Object Text: Write a clear, interesting description of your big idea.

Note: A widely read media object is like a book on the best-seller list. A well-crafted media object is a valuable way to get attention and will be read by the right people. If you use a photo, headline, and descriptive text, the reader will understand what your ad is about, without having to read copy. This will save you time and money.

The goal is to create a media object that will sell. Think of your media object as a sales aid.

Does it explain your big idea to your target audience? Does it identify the problem it will solve? Does it show what your solution looks like? Does it get the attention and interest of your target audience? Does it contain a unique, interesting, or funny story or piece of information?

Media Object Case Study

Actual Headline: Eberlestock: Who Knew Sitting on a Mountain Was So Profitable?

Notice the headline says "Who Knew Sitting on a Mountain Was So Profitable?" The headline is an example of an "attention grabber" — something great, unusual, or interesting that immediately captures your reader's attention. The media object is a photo of a man sitting on a mountain. The headline grabs your attention and draws you in.

Note: If you write a headline and picture for a media object, you will have to send the copy to the media outlet. But you will not need to write the body copy for the news release. If the media outlet publishes the media object, they will want to write the copy for it.

Media Object Copy: This is a great opportunity to explain how your product or service solves a problem or creates a new market. The media object did not just show a picture of someone sitting on a blank mountain. Instead, it showed how that person is sitting on a mountain and is making lots of money. What a great problem to solve — people sitting on mountains making lots of money!

Media Object Copy: We are all aware that sitting on a mountain is not a great way to make money. But we are not aware of a mountain-sitting industry. For the first time, there is a business model where people can make money sitting on mountain. This is a great business opportunity, and we are proud to announce that we have the solution.

Media Object Case Study 2: The Gorman Bike Rental Program

Actual Headline: Gorman Bike Rental Program: A Great Idea for a PTO

The headline says "Gorman Bike Rental Program: A Great Idea for a PTO." The headline is an example of an attention grabber — a catchy phrase that gets your attention and leads you to read the copy that follows. Notice the headline contains a slight variation on the same theme. "Gorman Bike Rental Program: A Great Idea for a PTO" is the same idea, just put in a different way to keep your attention.

The headline is an example of a "catchy" headline. It's a clever way to write a headline. It's a great idea for a PR message. It's very catchy and interesting.

The headline gives the reporter a reason to call the quoted source and ask, "Who is Amy Gorman, and why should I be interested in her?"

The main point is that if a question is interesting to you, you will also be interested in what the person who answered the question has to say.
