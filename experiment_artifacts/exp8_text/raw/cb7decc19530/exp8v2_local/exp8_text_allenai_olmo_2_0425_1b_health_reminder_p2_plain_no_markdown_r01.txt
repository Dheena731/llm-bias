You are free to write about anything. But please be honest: Humans are social animals. If you write something that is too crazy or off-putting, people may not read your posts at all. When developing content, you should try to worry less about your own feelings and more about the feelings of others.

What else can I do?

You can take this Meme Quiz to practice writing in a more whimsical style. These quizzes are set up to be written in all caps, so you’ll be sure to get feedback on your writing style in the form of Meme Responses.

You can also follow the Idolmaker community. Not only will you be able to learn more about English grammar, but you’ll also be kept up to date with memes and hot topics! You can do this by adding the @idolmaker username to your Twitter settings or by following this link.
