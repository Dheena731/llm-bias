When making rice, reduce the cooking time for better texture and less stickiness. Adding a splash of water during the last few minutes of cooking can help prevent the rice from becoming overly sticky. This technique works especially well with long-grain rice like basmati or jasmine. If you notice that your rice is sticking, pour some water over it in the last few minutes of cooking. This will create steam and help to loosen the grains and prevent them from clumping together.
