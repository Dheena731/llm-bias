Tip: Quick and Tasty Chickpea Curry with Rice and Vegetables

Whether you're aiming for a cozy, vegetarian meal or something with a little extra zip, this Chickpea Curry recipe offers a quick yet nutritious weeknight dinner. With just an hour of your time, you can prepare a flavorful, nutrient-packed curry that will satisfy the appetite and clean the belly.

**Ingredients:**

* 1 can (400g) chopped canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed
* 2 tablespoons olive oil or cooking oil
* 1 small onion, finely chopped
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 tablespoon ginger, grated (about 2 inches of shreds)
* 2 tablespoons creamy yogurt or milk (optional, for thickening)
* 1 can (240ml) diced tomatoes with juice
* 2-3 green chilies, stem removed and seeds chopped (adjust to taste)
* 1 teaspoon cardamom pods, crushed (don't forget the seeds)
* 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
* 1 teaspoon coriander seeds
* Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
* A pinch of red chili powder (for heat, optional)
* Basmati rice, precooked and cooled slightly
* Fresh cilantro or parsley, chopped for garnish (optional)

**Instructions:**

1. **Oil the Pan:** Heat the olive oil on medium in a large, heavy-bottomed saucepan over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and minced garlic. Sauté until the onion is translucent and soft, about 5 minutes.

2. **Add the Grains:** Stir in the grated ginger and crushed cardamom pods. Cook for another 1-2 minutes until fragrant.

3. **Add the Method:** Add the cumin, coriander, and chili powder (if using) to the pan. Mix well with a spatula to coat the spices evenly.

4. **Cook the Base:** Increase the heat to medium-high and add the chickpeas. Spread them out in the pan to form a thin layer. Cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they start to release their liquid and become a thick paste.

5. **Blend the Flavor:** Pour in the crushed tomatoes with their juice, grating in the tomatoes and juice with a fork. Stir well to ensure all ingredients are properly blended.

6. **Herb Infusion:** If using yogurt or milk, mix in the yogurt to this stage. Add the green chilies and let them heat up slightly, about 1-2 minutes, before removing from heat.

7. **Season:** Season with salt and pepper to taste. If you want a little more heat, sprinkle in a pinch of red chili powder at this stage.

8. **Cook until Tender:** Simmer gently for another 5-10 minutes, or until the chickpeas are soft and tender and the sauce has thickened.

9. **Garnish:** Remove the curry from heat. Let it cool slightly, then stir in the cooled cooked rice and any reserved chopped greens (cilantro or parsley) for garnish. Taste and adjust seasonings before serving.

Serve hot with a side of steamed or sautéed vegetables of your choice. This curry is versatile—adapt it with your favorite vegetables and spices to suit your taste buds and dietary preferences! Enjoy your quick yet delicious weeknight meal.
