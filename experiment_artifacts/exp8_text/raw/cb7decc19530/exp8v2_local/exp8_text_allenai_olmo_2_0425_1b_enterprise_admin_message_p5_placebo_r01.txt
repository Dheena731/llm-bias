Don’t ask people to do something that you don’t want or need to be done. Explain what you need, suggest a solution, and then ask for an answer.

Show your willingness to improve. Ask for feedback, and then, when you get it, respond with actions.

Write on a professional level, even if you are familiar with the topic. Your audience won’t be familiar with it. Use a tone of voice that is strong and authoritative, not one that sounds like you’re talking to a friend.

When writing to clients, customers, or other stakeholders, be aware of who you are writing for. Do your research and make sure that you are familiar with who they are and what they want. Your tone should reflect this.
