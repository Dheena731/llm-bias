When making white rice, to avoid it from becoming overly sticky and clumping together, start with a cold water ratio of about 4 parts water to 1 part rice. Let the rice soak for about 15-20 minutes, then transfer it to a Characters or rice cooker. Bring the water to a boil and add in the rice, then reduce heat to 'soft' or 'medium' setting. This method helps to evenly cook the rice while reducing any excess moisture, thus preventing it from sticking together.
