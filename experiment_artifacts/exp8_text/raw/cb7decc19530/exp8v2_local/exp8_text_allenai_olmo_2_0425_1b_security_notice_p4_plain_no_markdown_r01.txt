Write in third person. Personal pronouns such as "I" and "you" should not be used.

Write in a conversational tone. Avoid using technical terms or abbreviations. Present the results as though you were talking to someone face-to-face.

Now that you know how to write a press release, let's take a closer look at what makes a good press release and consider some tips for creating your own.

What Makes a Good Press Release?

A good press release should:

  • Include the reporter's name, contact information, and company address.
  • Reiterate the headline and the company name in the first paragraph.
  • Summarize the important facts in a single sentence and introduce the story in a second paragraph.
  • Offer a telephone number for further information.
  • Be written in clear, concise language.
  • Use the active voice.

How to Write a Press Release

Writing a press release is an important part of the communication process in the media. The media might only print a press release in full in some cases, but there are many other uses for a press release.

  • Use it to capture media attention for an event or news item before a reporter covers it.
  • Use it to publicize a new product or service.
  • Use it to share an important company news with employees or customers.
  • Use it to announce or promote an upcoming event or other event.

Here are some tips for creating a high-quality press release:

  • Don't use a cliché or promotional language. Be descriptive and clear.
  • Use the active voice. Writers tend to use the first-person form of the verb "to be" in press releases. For example, "The company is a leader in the XYZ industry." In this case, it's better to write "The company is a leader in the XYZ industry."
  • Use an easily readable font like Times New Roman or Arial.
  • Keep the length of your press release between 200 and 400 words.
  • Avoid overly technical language. Try to use ordinary language and avoid jargon.
  • Make sure the release is complete and strong on its own.
  • Ask a colleague to proofread your press release for grammar and spelling.
  • Distribute your news release to targeted journalists and media contacts.
