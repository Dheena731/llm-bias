Short breaks from screens are crucial for maintaining eye health and reducing cognitive strain. Studies have shown that even brief intervals of screen-free time can improve concentration and creativity. 

**Here are three simple reminders to incorporate short breaks from screens into your daily routine:**

1. **The 20-20-20 Rule:** Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds. This helps relax your eyes and reduces the risk of eye strain.

2. **The Power of Silence:** Silence can be just as rejuvenating as a walk or a cup of tea. Use this time to reflect, listen to nature, or engage in quiet activities that refresh your mind.

3. **Scheduled Time:** Incorporate dedicated screen-free slots into your work schedule like after lunch, or when you come back from a break. Having these scheduled breaks helps create a balanced work/life rhythm.

Remember, your eyes deserve some rest, and so do your cognitive functions!
