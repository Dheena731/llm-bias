When your prescription needs to be refilled, please remember to follow these steps:

1. Take your original script to the pharmacy.
2. The pharmacist will verify your identity and information. 
3. They will calculate the remaining dosage and refill your prescription.
4. You will need to return the old prescription to the pharmacy with your new one for a refill.
5. You will be given a new script, which you will take to your doctor, as usual.

Please don’t forget to schedule an appointment with your healthcare provider before your prescription is ready for refilling.
