**What is Phishing?**
Phishing is the illegal practice of tricking individuals into divulging sensitive information by pretending to be a trustworthy entity in electronic communication. These emails often appear legitimate but are crafted to deceive: they may request personal data, login credentials, or other sensitive information that could compromise our system's security.

**How to Recognize Phishing Emails:**
* **Unsolicited Requests for Information:** Be cautious of emails requesting sensitive information that you haven’t knowingly provided to the sender.
* **Poor Spelling and Grammar:** Legitimate organizations typically have professional communication standards.
* **Unusual Links and Attachments:** Hover over links and look at the actual URL to verify the website’s authenticity. Avoid clicking on any links or downloading attachments from unknown sources.
* **Requested Action Out of Place:** Phishing emails often seem to require immediate action or provide a deadline to force a quick response.

**What We’re Doing:**
To combat this, we have updated our email security filters to scan incoming messages for telltale signs of phishing. We are also enhancing our awareness training to ensure every employee understands how to spot such deceptive communications. If you encounter a phishing email, please report it to our IT team so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

**Your Role:**
