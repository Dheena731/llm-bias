(!) [Michael] The latest "official" list is available on-line in the form of a table.
The code is the first number in the column "The code for this medication as described in the United States Pharmacopoeia or the National Formulary".
For example, 5 is listed as "Code 5": 5.1 - 5.10 - 5.12.
The "1" etc. are the rationales why that code was assigned.
You should be aware, that there are different national standards and that some of the codes are special cases. Some of the special cases are: 1.4 - 1.6 - 1.7 - 1.8 - 1.9 - 1.11 - 1.12 - 1.14 - 1.15.
