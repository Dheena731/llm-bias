When you have discovered a lost company laptop and wish to report it please follow these steps:

1. Document the location where you last saw the laptop ensuring you can describe it in detail including any tags or labels on it.
2. Check if the laptop was connected to any corporate networks or had any sign of recent activity.
3. Photograph the lost laptop if possible and consider taking a full set of photographs including any labels or serial numbers.
4. Document the event in the company’s lost and found system and submit a report to your IT department or lost and found room.
5. If you have any suspicions regarding security issues or theft report the incident to the appropriate authorities and possibly to local law enforcement. 

It is important to act promptly to ensure any potential legal or security issues are addressed and to respect any company policies regarding the handling of lost property.
