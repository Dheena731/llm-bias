**Reminder: Refilling your prescription**

Refilling your prescription is an important step in ensuring that your treatment continues to be effective. It might seem simple, but here are a few crucial reminders to keep in mind:
* **Check the Prescription:** Always verify that the prescription you are refilling matches the one you originally received from your healthcare provider.
* **Expiration Date:** Make sure the prescription is not expired. If it's close to its expiration, consult your healthcare provider for alternative options.
* **Do Not Skip Readjustment:** Some medications need readjustment after a certain period, so follow your doctor’s instructions on the recommended times for refills.
* **Follow the Procedure:** Be sure to follow the specific instructions your pharmacy provides regarding refilling medications. This might involve specific dates, dosages, or formulation changes.
* **Safety First:** Always use your medication as directed. Self-medicating based on incorrect information could be dangerous.

Remember, refilling your prescription timely helps maintain the efficacy of your treatment and ensures you receive the appropriate care.
