When refilling a prescription you have to follow these steps. First,  check if the prescription is still valid and only thing you need from the pharmacy is the refilling form. Depending on the type of prescription you have this form may be on a CD or on a pill bottle. If it is on the CD you can put your old prescription in the CD cover and put it in the mail. If it is on the pill bottle you will have to use a transfer pen to stick it on the new prescription. Make sure to use the transfer pen in the same way as the original prescription was filled.
