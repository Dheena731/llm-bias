Attention all employees: In light of our recent loss of a company laptop, please follow these steps if you have encountered a lost or stolen device: 

1. **Contact your department head immediately**: Ensure copies of the lost device are sent to the IT department and file a lost device report with your supervisor and department head. 

2. **Document the lost laptop**: Take photos or create a detailed written description of the laptop, including its serial number, make, model, and any associated data or passwords.

3. **Report to IT**: Schedule a time to personally report the lost laptop to the IT support team at your desk. They will handle the recovery process and investigate the loss. 

4. **Follow any internal reporting instructions**: If instructed, report the loss to Loss Prevention, and if applicable, to your supervisor or HR department.

5. **Be vigilant**: Keep your device secure until it is recovered. Do not share it or disclose any personal information unless absolutely necessary.

We appreciate your help in preserving the integrity of our company information and maintaining our security. Losses of sensitive data can have significant implications for our operations and we are committed to quickly addressing this issue.

Thank you for your attention and cooperation.
