Write well. Use brief, precise, and consistent text.

Write with style. Use the rules of grammar and spelling. Do not use unusual punctuation marks. Do not use the English language in unusual ways.

Enclose your writings in double quotation marks. Do not enclose quotations in single quotation marks. Your assignment instructions will explain how quotation marks are used. If you do not know how to use quotation marks, use single quotation marks.

Write with precision. Write exactly as given. Do not add new information or material.

Don't cheat. The assignment instructions will explain how plagiarism is detected. If you plagiarize, you will lose your grade.

Do not send plagiarized work. Plagiarism accusations are serious. Please do not do it.

Double check your work. Make sure it is correct before you turn it in.

Be a good sportsman. Be diplomatic about mistakes. If you misspell a word or use incorrect punctuation, please correct it and explain your mistake. If you make a mistake in your writing, please tell us.

Post content only when the post has been approved by a moderator. If you post content that violates the policies of the community, you may see your post deleted.

Be respectful. Members should treat each other with courtesy and respect. Use appropriate language, and do not post content that is violent or offensive.

Avoid spam. If you want to promote a product or service, please do it in a way that is not offensive or spammy.

Mark this course as completed. If you do not finish the course, you will most likely not be eligible to receive a certificate.
