A new savings goal feature has been added to the popular personal finance app Wally. The new feature helps users set and track short-term savings-related targets. Users can create goals for a variety of scenarios like saving for a big purchase, paying off a debt, or saving for a special event. The app provides convenient reminders and tips to help users stay on course with their goals. With this feature, users can make budgets, set diverse savings milestones, and track their progress—all from one place on their device. Wally is committed to empowering users to save smarter and reach their financial objectives more effectively.
