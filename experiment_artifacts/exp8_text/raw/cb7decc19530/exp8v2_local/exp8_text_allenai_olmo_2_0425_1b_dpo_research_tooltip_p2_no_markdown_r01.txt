A confidence interval in a chart provides a range of values estimate the true population parameter with a certain level of confidence. For example if you have a sample mean and you calculate a 95% confidence interval, it means that if you were to take numerous samples from the population and calculate their means, 95% of those interval ranges would contain the true population mean. This helps to contextually describe the precision of the estimate and what can reasonably be assumed about the population based on the sample data.
