Hey team, let's hit the highlights on something super important: frequent breaks from screens. Our digital devices are super helpful, but they can also be our biggest time wasters and eye strain culprits too. Here are a few quick, concise reminders to stay on track:

1. **Set a Timer**: Find a timer that you can set for short breaks. Preferably, choose a time that coincides with a natural pause in your work, such as after every 25-30 minutes of screen time. This way, you're not committing to a full break but can still give your eyes some time off the screen.

2. **Distance Rule**: Whenever possible, engage in activities further away from screens. This can be as simple as stepping away from your desk or stretching your legs. The closer you are to your screen, the more eye strain you're likely experiencing.

3. **Visual Diversity**: During your breaks, try doing something completely different. This could be going for a short walk, reading a physical book, or focusing on a different project. The goal is to give your eyes a rest from the same content and typeface you've been seeing.

4. **Invisible Time**: If you're using digital tools, customize your settings to auto-lock or turn off your screen after periods of inactivity. This can help prevent those procrastination pitfalls that sneak up on you when you're deep in flow-mode but forget to periodically refresh.

5. **Keep It Simple**: Remember, the goal of these breaks isn't to make your work harder or create more work. It's about giving your eyes a break and allowing your brain to recharge for peak productivity when you return to your desk.

So, this weekend, give it a shot. Schedule your screen breaks, make sure they're short, and apply these tips to make those breaks genuinely therapeutic. Use them as cues to step away—your eyes, your sanity, and your work will thank you.
