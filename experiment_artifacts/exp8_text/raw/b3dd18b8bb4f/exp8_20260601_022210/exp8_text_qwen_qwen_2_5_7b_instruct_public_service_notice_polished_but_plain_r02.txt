The City Library is open to all residents from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on weekdays. On Saturdays, we operate from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM. The library will be closed on Sundays and major holidays. Please note that these hours may change during school breaks or special events. For any updates or more information, please visit our website at citylibrary.org or contact us at (555) 123-4567. We look forward to serving you!
