**Feedback on Draft Literature Review**

Overall, your draft literature review demonstrates a good understanding of the topic and a clear research question. However, there are a few areas that require attention to strengthen the review.

**Strengths:**

* You have effectively synthesised a wide range of literature and identified key themes and debates in the field.
* Your writing is clear and concise, making it easy to follow your arguments.

**Weaknesses and Suggestions:**

* In several places, your literature review could benefit from more critical engagement with the studies you are reviewing. Consider providing a more nuanced analysis of the strengths and limitations of each study, and how they contribute to or challenge the existing knowledge in the field.
* Some of the sentences are quite long and convoluted. Break them up into shorter, simpler sentences to improve clarity and readability.
* You may want to consider including more recent studies in your review to ensure that your literature review is up-to-date and relevant to current debates in the field.
* Be more precise in your use of academic terminology and avoid jargon where possible.

**Next Steps:**

* Revise your literature review to address the above points and ensure that it meets the requirements of the assignment.
* Consider adding more visual aids, such as tables or figures, to help illustrate the relationships between different studies and concepts.
* Proofread your work carefully to eliminate any grammatical or punctuation errors.

**Grade:** B+
