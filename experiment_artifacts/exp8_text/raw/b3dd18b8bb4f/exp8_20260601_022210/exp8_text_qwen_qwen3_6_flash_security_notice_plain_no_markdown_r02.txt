Please be aware that reusing passwords across multiple accounts creates a serious vulnerability for our organization. When you apply the same password to both personal services and work systems, a compromise of one account can lead to unauthorized access to your professional credentials. Attackers frequently use stolen password lists to attempt login access across various platforms. To safeguard company information, you are required to use a distinct and complex password for every work account and personal service you maintain. We recommend utilizing an approved password manager to handle these unique credentials effectively. Do not share your passwords with colleagues and never write them on physical notes attached to your workspace. If you suspect your information has been exposed, contact the IT security team right away. Your diligence in maintaining separate passwords is essential to our collective security posture.
