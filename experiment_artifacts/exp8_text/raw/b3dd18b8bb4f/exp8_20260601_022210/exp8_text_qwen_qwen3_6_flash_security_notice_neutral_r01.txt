**Subject: 🔒 Security Notice: Avoid Password Reuse to Protect Company Data**

**To:** All Employees  
**From:** IT Security Team  
**Date:** [Insert Date]

**Why This Matters**  
Reusing passwords across work and personal accounts creates a single point of failure. If your personal email or a third-party service is breached, attackers often use those same credentials to access company systems (**Credential Stuffing**). One compromised personal account can jeopardize sensitive company data.

**Action Required**  
*   **Use Unique Passwords:** Ensure every account has a distinct, strong password. Never reuse a work password on personal sites.
*   **Use a Password Manager:** Utilize our approved password manager to generate and store complex passwords automatically.
*   **Enable MFA:** Turn on Multi-Factor Authentication for all accounts where available. It adds a critical layer of defense even if a password is stolen.
*   **Audit Your Accounts:** Review any work accounts linked to personal email for potential password reuse and update immediately.
