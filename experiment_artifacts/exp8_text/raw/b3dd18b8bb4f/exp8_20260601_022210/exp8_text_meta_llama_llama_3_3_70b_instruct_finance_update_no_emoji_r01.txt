We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you stay on top of your finances. This summary will provide a detailed breakdown of your spending habits, including total expenditures, category-wise spending, and any transactions that may have triggered alerts. The summary will be sent to you via email on the first day of each month and will cover the previous month's activity. This new feature is designed to give you a clearer picture of your financial situation and help you make informed decisions about your spending.
