Subject: Update: Introducing the Monthly Spending Summary

We are pleased to announce the launch of the new Monthly Spending Summary. This feature provides a detailed breakdown of your expenses for the current billing cycle, categorized by type to help you track spending habits and identify financial trends.

You can access the summary by logging in and navigating to the "Reports" section on your dashboard. This tool is designed to assist with budgeting and improve financial oversight.

If you have any questions regarding the new summary, please contact our support team.
