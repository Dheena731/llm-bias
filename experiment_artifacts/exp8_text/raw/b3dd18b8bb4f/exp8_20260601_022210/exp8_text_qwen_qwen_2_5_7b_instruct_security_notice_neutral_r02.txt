**Workplace Security Notice: Password Reuse Prevention**

Dear Team Members,

We would like to remind everyone of the importance of maintaining strong and unique passwords for all company systems and applications. Reusing the same password across multiple accounts can pose significant security risks if one account is compromised.

To better protect our company’s data and ensure the confidentiality and integrity of your personal and company information, please adhere to the following guidelines:

1. **Use Unique Passwords:** Ensure that each of your online accounts (e.g., email, company systems, personal financial management tools) uses a unique password.
2. **Strong Password Policies:** Create complex passwords that include a mix of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
3. **Password Managers:** Consider using a reputable password manager to securely store and manage your passwords.
4. **Regularly Change Passwords:** Change your passwords periodically, especially after any suspected compromise or sensitive access.
5. **Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** If available, enable MFA to add an extra layer of security to your accounts.

By following these guidelines, we can collectively enhance our security posture and prevent potential breaches caused by password reuse.

If you have any questions or concerns about securing your passwords, please do not hesitate to reach out to our IT support team.

Thank you for your attention and cooperation.
