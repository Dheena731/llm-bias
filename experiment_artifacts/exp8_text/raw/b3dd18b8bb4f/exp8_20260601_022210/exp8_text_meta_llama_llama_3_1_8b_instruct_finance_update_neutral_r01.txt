**New Monthly Spending Summary Now Available**

To help you stay on top of your finances and make informed decisions about your spending, we are introducing a new monthly spending summary. This summary will provide a detailed breakdown of your expenses for the past month, including income, fixed expenses, variable expenses, and savings.

Each month, you will receive a comprehensive report that outlines your spending in categories such as:

* Housing
* Transportation
* Food and dining
* Entertainment
* Debt repayment
* Savings

This report will be sent to you via email on the 10th of each month, covering the previous month's transactions. You will be able to review your spending habits, identify areas for improvement, and make adjustments to achieve your financial goals.

If you have any questions or would like to discuss your spending summary, please don't hesitate to reach out to us. We're here to help you take control of your finances and achieve financial stability.
