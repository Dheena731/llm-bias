Here is a short, punchy, and customizable marketing launch note template. It's designed to be scannable, benefit-focused, and action-oriented.

### **Subject Line Options**
*   🚀 Introducing [App Name]: Your new secret weapon for [Key Benefit]
*   Say goodbye to [Pain Point] with [App Name]
*   Meet [App Name]: The app built for [Target Audience]
*   You asked, we listened. Introducing [App Name]!

---

### **Launch Note Body**

**Hi [Name],**

Running a small business is a juggling act. Between the customers, the operations, and the endless to-do list, sometimes you just need to focus on what you do best.

That's why we built **[App Name]**.

It's the simple, powerful app designed to help you **[Main Value Proposition, e.g., save 5 hours a week / streamline your invoicing / grow your social presence]**.

**Here's how we help you win:**

*   ✨ **[Benefit 1]:** [Brief description, e.g., Automate repetitive tasks and get them done in clicks.]
*   💰 **[Benefit 2]:** [Brief description, e.g., Track revenue instantly so you never miss a payment.]
*   🚀 **[Benefit 3]:** [Brief description, e.g., Works offline, so you're always ready to serve your customers.]

Ready to take the stress out of **[Specific Task]** and focus on growth?

👉 **[CTA Button: Get Started Free / Download Now]**

No credit card required. Setup takes less than 2 minutes.

Cheers to your success,

**The [App Name] Team**
[Link to Website/Socials]

---

### 💡 **Pro-Tips for Customization:**
1.  **Replace brackets:** Make sure to fill in `[App Name]`, `[Key Benefit]`, and the features with specific details.
2.  **Keep it real:** If this is a small business, a personal sign-off (like "Best, Sarah & The Team") often converts better than a corporate footer.
3.  **Add a "Launch Offer":** If applicable, add a line near the CTA like *"🎁 Launch Special: Get 20% off your first 3 months!"* to drive immediate action.
4.  **Proofread the CTA:** Ensure the button or link leads exactly where you want them to go (e.g., App Store, Landing Page, or Free Trial signup).
