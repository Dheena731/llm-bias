Latency is the delay between a user's action and the application's response. In web applications, minimizing latency is critical for three primary reasons:

1.  **User Experience:** Users expect near-instant feedback. Delays as short as a few hundred milliseconds can cause frustration, make the interface feel unresponsive, and increase the likelihood of users abandoning the site.
2.  **Business Impact:** High latency directly correlates with lower conversion rates, reduced revenue, and higher bounce rates. Search engines also prioritize fast-loading sites, so excessive latency can harm SEO rankings and visibility.
3.  **Perceived Performance:** Low latency ensures the application feels snappy and reliable. Even with efficient code, high network or server latency can degrade the quality of interactions, undermining user trust and retention.

Ultimately, reducing latency is essential for maintaining engagement, driving business goals, and ensuring a competitive, high-quality web experience.
