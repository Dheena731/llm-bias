To elevate your weeknight dinner game, try employing the 'mise en place' technique, a French culinary principle that translates to 'everything in its place.' Before you start cooking, take 10-15 minutes to prep all your ingredients: chop those onions, mince the garlic, and measure out your spices. This simple step will streamline your cooking process, reduce stress, and allow you to focus on the actual cooking – searing that perfect chicken breast or simmering a rich tomato sauce. By having all your ingredients ready to go, you'll be able to cook efficiently and confidently, even on the most chaotic of weeknights.
