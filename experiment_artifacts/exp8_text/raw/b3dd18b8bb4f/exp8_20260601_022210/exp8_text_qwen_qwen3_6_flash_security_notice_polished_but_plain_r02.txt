This security notice addresses the risk of password reuse across personal and work accounts. Using the same password for multiple systems creates a single point of failure. If one account is compromised through a breach or phishing attempt, attackers can easily access your other accounts, potentially exposing sensitive company data and client information. To protect our networks and maintain compliance with company security standards, every employee must use a unique password for each work system and external service. We strongly recommend using a company-approved password manager to generate and store complex, distinct credentials. If you currently reuse passwords across any work or personal accounts, change them immediately and establish a new pattern of unique credentials. Should you need assistance selecting a password manager or resetting an account, contact the IT security team at the internal help desk. Your attention to this practice is essential to maintaining the integrity of our digital environment.
