The local library is open to serve the community six days a week. On Mondays, Tuesdays, and Thursdays, the library is open from 9am to 8pm. On Wednesdays and Fridays, the library is open from 9am to 6pm. On Saturdays, the library is open from 10am to 5pm. The library is closed on Sundays. During these hours, patrons can browse the shelves, use the computers, and access a wide range of programs and services. Please note that hours may be subject to change during holidays and special events, and it is always a good idea to call ahead or check the library's website to confirm hours before visiting.
