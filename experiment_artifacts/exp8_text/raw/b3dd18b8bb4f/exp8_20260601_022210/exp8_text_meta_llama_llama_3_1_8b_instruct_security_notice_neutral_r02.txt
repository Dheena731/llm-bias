Here is a short workplace security notice about password reuse:

**Important: Protect Your Company's Digital Assets**

**Password Reuse: A Security Risk**

To ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our company's digital assets, we remind all employees to use unique and strong passwords for each of their accounts.

**Why is password reuse a security risk?**

* If one account is compromised, all other accounts with the same password are also vulnerable to unauthorized access.
* Password reuse can lead to a single point of failure, allowing an attacker to gain access to multiple systems and sensitive data.

**Best Practices for Password Security:**

* Use a unique and complex password for each account.
* Avoid using easily guessable information such as your name, birthdate, or common words.
* Change your passwords regularly (every 60-90 days).
* Consider using a password manager to generate and store unique, complex passwords.

**What happens if you reuse passwords?**

* You may be subject to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.
* The company may take steps to reset all passwords and re-secure compromised accounts.

**If you have any questions or concerns about password security, please reach out to the IT department.**

Thank you for your attention to this important security matter.
