Don't forget to take a moment to review your weekly fitness progress. It's easy to lose track of your exercise routine and milestones, but regularly checking in can make a big difference in your overall fitness goals.

Take a few minutes each week to look back at your workouts, noting what you've accomplished and what you're looking to improve on in the coming days. This can help you identify patterns and areas where you need to make adjustments to stay on track.

Consider tracking your progress in a log or journal to visually see how far you've come. Additionally, using a habit-tracking app or spreadsheet can also be a helpful tool.
