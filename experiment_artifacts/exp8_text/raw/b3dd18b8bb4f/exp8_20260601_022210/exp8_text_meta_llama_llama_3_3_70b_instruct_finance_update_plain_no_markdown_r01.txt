We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you keep track of your expenses. This summary will provide a clear overview of your spending habits and will be sent to you at the beginning of each month. It will include a breakdown of your total spending, as well as a categorization of your expenses, such as rent, utilities, and entertainment. The summary will also highlight any unusual or large transactions, allowing you to quickly identify and address any potential issues. This new feature is designed to make it easier for you to manage your finances and make informed decisions about your spending.
