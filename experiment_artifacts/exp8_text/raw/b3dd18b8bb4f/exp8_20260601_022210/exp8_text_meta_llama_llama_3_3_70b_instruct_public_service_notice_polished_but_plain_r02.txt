The local library is open to the public Monday through Thursday from 9am to 8pm, and Friday and Saturday from 9am to 5pm. On Sundays, the library is open from 1pm to 5pm. These hours apply throughout the year, except for public holidays when the library is closed. It is recommended that you call ahead or check the library's website to confirm hours before visiting, as they are subject to change. The library offers a wide range of services and resources, including books, media, and programs for adults and children, and the staff are happy to assist with any questions or requests you may have.
