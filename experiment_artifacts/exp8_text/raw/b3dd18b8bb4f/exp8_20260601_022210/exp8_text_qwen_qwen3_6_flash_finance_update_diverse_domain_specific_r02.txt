We’ve added a Monthly Spending Summary to your workspace. It replaces the raw transaction feed with a month-end close view: all activity is routed through your category mappings and spending thresholds, then grouped into fixed costs, variable outflows, and threshold breaches. Transactions that don’t match an existing rule sit in the Untagged row so you can assign them before the monthly cutoff. The summary refreshes on the 1st and exports in a column structure that maps directly to standard accounting templates, so you won’t need to rearrange headers. You’ll find it under Workspace > Reports > Monthly View.
