Here's a reminder that's specific to the domain of fitness and tailored to encourage users to reflect on their progress:

**Check-in Time: Review Your Weekly Fitness Progress**

Take a moment to reflect on how your body has responded to this week's workouts. Did you:

* Increase your endurance by completing more sets or reps?
* Improve your form and technique, reducing the risk of injury?
* Notice improvements in your flexibility or range of motion?
* Feel more confident and energized throughout the day?

By regularly reviewing your progress, you'll be able to:

* Identify areas for improvement and adjust your training plan accordingly
* Celebrate your successes and stay motivated to continue making progress
* Refine your technique and prevent plateaus

Take 5 minutes to review your workouts from the past week, and use this feedback to inform your approach moving forward. Your body will thank you!
