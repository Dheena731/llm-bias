**Quick and Easy Tip for a Weeknight Dinner: One-Pot Pasta**

For a fast and flavorful weeknight dinner, try making one-pot pasta. Cook pasta, tomato sauce, and your choice of protein (such as ground beef or sausage) in a large pot with some olive oil and herbs. This method saves time and effort by eliminating the need to boil water for the pasta and then cook the sauce separately. Simply add all the ingredients to the pot, bring to a boil, reduce the heat, and let simmer until the pasta is al dente and the flavors have melded together. Serve with grated Parmesan cheese and a sprinkle of fresh basil for a delicious and satisfying meal that's ready in about 20-25 minutes.
