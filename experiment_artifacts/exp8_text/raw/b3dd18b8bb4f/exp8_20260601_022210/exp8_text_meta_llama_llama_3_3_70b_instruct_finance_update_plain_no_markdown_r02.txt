We are introducing a new feature to help you keep track of your finances. Starting this month, you will receive a monthly spending summary that outlines your total expenditures for the previous month. This summary will be sent to you by email and will include a breakdown of your spending by category, such as housing, transportation, and entertainment. The summary will also show your total income for the month and any savings or debt payments you made. This new feature is designed to help you stay on top of your finances and make informed decisions about your money. You can use the information in the summary to identify areas where you can cut back on unnecessary expenses and make adjustments to your budget as needed.
