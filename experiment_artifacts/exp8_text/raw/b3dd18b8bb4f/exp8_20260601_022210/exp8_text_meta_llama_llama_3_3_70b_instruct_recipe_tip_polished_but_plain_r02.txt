When preparing a weeknight dinner, one of the most effective ways to save time is to cook ingredients in bulk and use them throughout the week. For example, roasting a large batch of vegetables such as carrots, Brussels sprouts, and sweet potatoes on a Sunday or Monday can provide a quick and easy side dish for several nights. Simply portion out the cooked vegetables into individual containers and refrigerate or freeze them for later use. This approach can also be applied to proteins like chicken, beef, or tofu, which can be cooked in advance and then reheated as needed. By doing some prep work ahead of time, you can create a variety of healthy and delicious meals with minimal effort during the busy weeknights.
