**One-Two Punch for a Speedy Shrimp Stir-Fry**

When you're racing against the clock on a weeknight, a shrimp stir-fry can be a lifesaver. To get dinner on the table in under 20 minutes, try this trick: prep your aromatics and protein simultaneously. While your shrimp are marinating in a mixture of soy sauce, garlic, and ginger, slice your onions and bell peppers. This way, as soon as your shrimp are cooked, you can toss them with the prepped veggies and a splash of oil, and stir-fry everything together in a single, sizzling pan. This streamlined approach ensures a flavorful, well-rounded dinner without sacrificing precious time.
