We are pleased to announce the launch of our new monthly spending summary. This feature provides a consolidated view of your financial activity, making it easier to track expenses and manage your budget. The summary breaks down your spending by category, shows your total monthly expenditure, and allows you to compare your current results against previous months. Data is updated daily to ensure accuracy, and you can access the report directly from your account dashboard. We encourage you to review your first summary to see how the new layout helps you understand your spending patterns and make informed financial decisions.
