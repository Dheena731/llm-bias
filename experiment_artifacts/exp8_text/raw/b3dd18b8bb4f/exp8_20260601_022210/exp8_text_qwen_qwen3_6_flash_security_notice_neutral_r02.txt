**Security Notice: Avoid Password Reuse**

**Date:** [Insert Date]  
**To:** All Employees  
**From:** IT Security Team  

**Subject: Protect Company Data by Using Unique Passwords**

To protect our company network and sensitive data, we are issuing a reminder regarding password best practices. **Reusing passwords across different accounts poses a significant security risk.**

**Why this matters:**  
If a hacker compromises a password from a personal account (e.g., social media or a retail site), they may attempt to use that same credential to access company systems. One outside breach could lead to unauthorized access to our internal data.

**Action Required:**  
✅ **Use a unique password** for every work and personal account.  
✅ **Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** wherever available.  
✅ **Use our approved password manager** to generate and store complex, unique passwords securely.  
✅ **Change passwords immediately** if you suspect any account has been compromised.

Please review your accounts today and update any reused passwords. Your vigilance is our first line of defense.

Thank you for helping keep our workplace secure.

**IT Security Team**
