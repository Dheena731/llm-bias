Here is a short reminder:

**Weekly Check-In: Review Your Fitness Progress!**

Take a few minutes to reflect on your fitness journey this week:

* How did you stay active and move your body?
* Did you hit your step or workout goals?
* What workouts felt successful or challenging?
* Are there any areas where you'd like to improve or focus on next week?

By reviewing your progress, you'll be able to:

* Celebrate your accomplishments
* Identify areas for improvement
* Set new goals and motivation

Take a few minutes to review your fitness log, journal, or mobile app to reflect on your progress. Let's keep moving forward and crush our fitness goals!
