Here's a short cooking tip for preparing a quick and delicious weeknight dinner:

**One-Pot Wonder**

To save time and reduce cleanup on a busy weeknight, try cooking a one-pot dinner! Choose a recipe that can be cooked entirely in one pot, such as a hearty stir-fry, skillet pasta, or chicken and rice bowl. Simply sauté your protein and veggies, add your grains and seasonings, and let everything simmer together until cooked through. Not only will you have a tasty meal on the table in no time, but you'll also have fewer dishes to wash at the end of the night. Give it a try and enjoy a stress-free weeknight dinner!
