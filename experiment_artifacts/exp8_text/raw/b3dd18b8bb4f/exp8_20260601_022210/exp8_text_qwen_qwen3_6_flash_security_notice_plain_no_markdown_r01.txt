This notice serves as an important reminder about password security. Using the same password for multiple accounts, including personal and work systems, creates a serious risk to our network. If a password you reuse is exposed in a third-party breach, attackers can use that same credential to access company data and internal tools. To protect your information and the organization, please ensure every account has a unique, strong password. We recommend using a company-approved password manager to generate and store these credentials safely. Change any passwords you currently reuse immediately and avoid writing them down or sharing them with others. If you need assistance setting up secure credentials or have questions about this policy, contact the IT security team at the help desk extension. Thank you for your cooperation in keeping our workplace systems safe.
