To speed up your weeknight dinner prep, try making a big batch of rice or grains on the weekend and storing it in the fridge or freezer for later use. This way, you can quickly reheat it to serve with a variety of weeknight meals, such as stir-fries, roasted vegetables, or grilled meats. Simply cook a big pot of rice, quinoa, or other grains, let it cool, and then portion it out into individual servings or airtight containers for easy reheating throughout the week.
