The public library announces its standard operating hours for the current term. The facility is open Monday through Friday from nine a.m. to eight p.m. and on Saturdays from ten a.m. to five p.m. The library is closed on Sundays and observed public holidays. Patrons are encouraged to arrive at least fifteen minutes before closing to return materials and complete transactions. For real-time updates regarding holiday schedules or temporary adjustments, please consult the library website or contact the information desk at 555-0123. Thank you for visiting our community resource.
