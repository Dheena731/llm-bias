Here's a short cooking tip for a quick and easy weeknight dinner:

**One-Pot Wonder: Simplify Your Weeknight Dinner**

When short on time, try making a one-pot pasta dish. Cook pasta, protein (such as chicken or sausage), and vegetables all in one pot. Simply sauté the protein and veggies in a bit of oil, then add in your pasta, broth, and any desired seasonings. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, and let simmer until the pasta is al dente. This method saves time on cleanup and allows for a hearty, satisfying meal to be ready in under 30 minutes!
