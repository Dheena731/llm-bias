Start with a simple protein like chicken breasts or a can of chickpeas. Season them well and cook until fully done. While the protein is cooking, chop some vegetables such as bell peppers, onions, and zucchini. Use these to make a quick sauté which can be thrown together in minutes. Serve the protein over cooked rice or quinoa and top with the vegetables. This meal requires minimal fuss but delivers a satisfying dinner that won't take long to prepare.
