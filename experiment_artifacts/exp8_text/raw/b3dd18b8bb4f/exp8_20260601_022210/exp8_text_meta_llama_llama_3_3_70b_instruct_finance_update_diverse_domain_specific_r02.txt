Our new monthly spending summary is now live, providing a detailed breakdown of your expenditures across various categories. This feature is designed to help you better understand your financial habits and make informed decisions about your money.

Each summary will include a categorized overview of your spending, highlighting areas where you may be able to cut back and allocate funds more efficiently. You'll also see a comparison of your current month's spending to the previous month, allowing you to track changes in your spending patterns over time.

To access your monthly spending summary, simply log in to your account and navigate to the 'Spending' tab. From there, you can view your current summary, as well as archives of previous months. We've also added the ability to download your summaries as a CSV file, making it easy to import the data into your preferred budgeting tool or spreadsheet.

Our goal with this new feature is to provide you with a clearer picture of your financial situation, and empower you to take control of your spending. If you have any questions or feedback about the monthly spending summary, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
