To streamline your weeknight dinner preparation, consider preparing a large batch of grains or roasted vegetables early in the day. This can be done in as little as 30 minutes, and the results will remain fresh for several meals. Choose a neutral flavored grain like brown rice, quinoa, or farro, and roast a variety of vegetables such as carrots, sweet potatoes, and Brussels sprouts. Once cooked, let these ingredients cool and store them in airtight containers in the refrigerator. When you're ready to assemble your dinner, simply reheat the grains and vegetables in the oven or on the stovetop, and add your preferred protein source, such as grilled chicken or sautéed tofu. This strategy can save you time and effort during the week, and will allow you to create a variety of meals without the need for extensive meal planning.
