The draft literature review demonstrates a solid foundation in the key debates surrounding the intersection of environmental governance and global value chains. However, to further strengthen the review, it would be beneficial to delve deeper into the empirical studies that examine the implementation of environmental standards in global production networks. The work of Cashore and colleagues on the emergence of non-state market-driven governance systems could provide a useful framework for analyzing the interactions between these actors and the firms operating within global value chains. 

Overall, the draft literature review provides a good starting point for exploring the complex relationships between environmental governance and global value chains.
