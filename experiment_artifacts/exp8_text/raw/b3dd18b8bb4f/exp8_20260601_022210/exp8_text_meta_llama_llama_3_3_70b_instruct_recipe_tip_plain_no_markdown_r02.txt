When preparing a weeknight dinner, consider planning your meal around a protein that can be cooked quickly, such as chicken breasts or fish fillets. You can season these proteins in advance and store them in the refrigerator until you are ready to cook them. To save time, prepare any vegetables you plan to use while the protein is cooking, and keep them ready to add to the dish as needed. This approach allows you to have a complete and healthy meal ready in about thirty minutes, which is ideal for a busy weeknight.
