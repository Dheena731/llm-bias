When preparing a quick weeknight dinner, opt for one-pot wonders like pasta or rice dishes. For instance, try making a hearty tomato basil pasta with cherry tomatoes, garlic, and fresh basil. Start by toasting some pine nuts in a dry pan until golden, then add them to your pasta as it's draining. This step adds a delicious crunch that elevates the dish. Pour in the hot pasta cooking liquid over the cherry tomatoes and garlic, letting them cook for a minute before folding in the toasted pine nuts and fresh basil. This method keeps cleanup simple and the flavors vibrant, perfect for a busy weeknight.
