Attention all book lovers and researchers: 

As we head into the new season, please note that our library's opening hours will be adjusting slightly. From Monday to Thursday, our doors will be open from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, allowing you to study, browse, or attend one of our many author events and workshops during the evening. 

On Fridays, we'll be open from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and weekends will see us operating from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM on Saturdays and 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM on Sundays. 

It's also a good idea to check our website or social media before planning your visit, as we occasionally close for special events or staff training. We're looking forward to seeing you soon and helping you discover new titles, authors, and topics to explore.
