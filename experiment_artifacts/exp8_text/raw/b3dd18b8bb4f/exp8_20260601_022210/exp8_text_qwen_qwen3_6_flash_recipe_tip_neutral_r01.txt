**Tip: Master the One-Pan Roast.**

Toss your protein (like chicken thighs or tofu), chopped veggies (such as broccoli, bell peppers, or potatoes), and a drizzle of oil directly onto a sheet pan. Season well and roast at 400°F (200°C) for 20–25 minutes. You'll get a complete, flavorful meal with minimal prep and almost zero cleanup, leaving you more time to relax on busy weeknights.
