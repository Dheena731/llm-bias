Here is a sample short academic feedback note on a draft literature review:

**Feedback on Literature Review Draft**

Dear [Student],

Thank you for sharing your draft literature review with me. Overall, I think you have made a good start on synthesizing the existing research on [topic]. However, there are a few areas that require attention to ensure your literature review meets academic standards.

**Strengths:**

* You have identified a clear research question and provided a good overview of the main themes in the literature.
* Your organization is clear and logical, making it easy to follow your argument.

**Areas for Improvement:**

* In some sections, the analysis of the literature feels a bit superficial. I encourage you to dig deeper into the research findings and identify potential contradictions or limitations in the existing literature.
* The transition between paragraphs could be smoother. Consider using more explicit linking words or phrases to guide the reader through your argument.
* There are a few places where the writing is a bit unclear or wordy. Make sure to edit your text carefully to ensure it is concise and free of grammatical errors.

**Recommendations:**

* Conduct a more thorough analysis of the literature, paying attention to the methodological approaches used, the results, and the implications of the findings.
* Use more nuanced language to describe the relationships between the different studies and theories.
* Consider including more critical perspectives on the literature to add depth and complexity to your review.

**Next Steps:**

* Revise your literature review based on the feedback above and submit a revised draft by [due date].
* Meet with me to discuss your revisions and ensure you are on track to meet the assignment requirements.

Best of luck with your revisions. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
