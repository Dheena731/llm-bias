**Quick Fix: Pan-Seared Chicken Breasts with a Twist**

When cooking weeknight dinner, it's all about speed and flavor. For a game-changing twist on classic pan-seared chicken breasts, try this simple trick: sear the chicken in a mixture of olive oil and rendered pancetta fat. The smoky, savory flavor of the pancetta adds depth to the dish without adding extra time or ingredients. Simply cook diced pancetta in a skillet until crispy, then add olive oil and your chicken breasts. Sear the chicken for 2-3 minutes per side, then finish with a squeeze of fresh lemon juice and a sprinkle of chopped parsley. This technique yields a juicy, aromatic chicken breast that's perfect for a weeknight dinner.
