Here is a sample workplace security notice about password reuse:

**Important Security Notice for All Employees of XYZ Corporation**

As a valued member of the XYZ Corporation team, we want to remind you of the importance of password security in protecting our company's sensitive data. With the increasing number of high-profile data breaches in our industry, it's essential that we maintain strong password practices to prevent unauthorized access to our systems.

**Don't Reuse Passwords Across Multiple XYZ Corporation Systems**

We've noticed that some employees have reused passwords across multiple internal systems, including email, network drives, and software applications. This practice puts our entire network at risk of a single-point-of-failure breach.

**Why Reusing Passwords is a Problem**

Reusing passwords across multiple systems creates a vulnerability that can be exploited by attackers. If an attacker gains access to one system using a reused password, they may be able to access other systems, potentially leading to:

* Unauthorized data access and theft
* System compromise and disruption
* Reputation damage and financial loss

**What to Do**

To protect our company's data and systems, please take the following steps:

* Ensure that each password is unique and not reused across multiple systems
* Use a password manager to generate and store complex, unique passwords
* Change your passwords regularly (at least every 60 days)
* Avoid using easily guessable information (e.g., birthdates, family members' names)

**Additional Resources**

If you need help with password management or have questions about our password policies, please contact the IT department at [it@xyzcorp.com](mailto:it@xyzcorp.com).

Thank you for your cooperation and commitment to maintaining the security of our company's data.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
XYZ Corporation Information Security Team
