**Quantitative Finance / Risk**

**Key structural notes for research tooltips:**
*   **State the action immediately:** "Apply..." or "Filter by..."
*   **Define the exclusion consequence:** Research tools require transparency on data loss (e.g., "Excludes variants...", "Removes illiquid...").
*   **Flag downstream effects:** Warn how the filter changes derived metrics (e.g., "Impacts...", "Adjusts...").
*   **Use native terminology:** Use domain standards (VAF, bps, P99) rather than generic terms like "high quality" or "fast."
