When searing protein for a quick weeknight meal, leverage the fond instead of reaching for a jarred sauce. Once the meat is removed, lower the heat and deglaze the pan with a splash of sherry vinegar or dry white wine. Scrape the caramelized bits into the liquid, then remove the pan from the heat and whisk in a tablespoon of cold butter until glossy. This forty-second emulsification creates a rich, balanced sauce that clings to the protein, masking dryness and adding depth without extra dishes or simmering time.
