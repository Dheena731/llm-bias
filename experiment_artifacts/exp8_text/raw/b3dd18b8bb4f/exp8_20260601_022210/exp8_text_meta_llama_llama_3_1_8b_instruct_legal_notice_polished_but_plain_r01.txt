NOTICE OF CHANGES TO ACCOUNT TERMS

Effective as of the date of this notice, the following changes will be made to the terms and conditions governing your account with our company.

1. The minimum balance requirement for maintaining a checking account has been increased from $100 to $500. If your account balance falls below $500, a monthly maintenance fee of $10 will be assessed.

2. The interest rate on savings accounts has been changed to 1.5% APY, effective on all deposits made after the date of this notice.

3. The overdraft protection plan will now require a fee of $25 per transfer, in addition to the transfer fee charged by the bank from which the transfer is made.

4. Our online banking system will no longer be available on Saturdays and Sundays, but will be accessible 24/7 on weekdays.

5. The address for sending mail to our customer service department has been changed to 123 Main Street, Anytown, USA 12345.

These changes are made to comply with regulatory requirements and to improve the overall service we provide to our customers. Your continued use of your account after the effective date of these changes will be considered acceptance of the new terms and conditions.
