Here is a short academic feedback note on a draft literature review:

"The current draft provides a solid foundation for exploring the intersection of spatial reasoning and embodied cognition in the context of math education. I appreciate how you've woven together the work of Nora Newcombe and Liz Spelke to highlight the role of spatial skills in mathematical development. However, I think there's an opportunity to further unpack the distinction between 'intrinsic' and 'extrinsic' spatial abilities, as discussed by Michotte and colleagues. Specifically, it would be helpful to clarify how these different types of spatial skills relate to the use of manipulatives and gesture-based instruction in math classrooms. Additionally, I'd love to see more attention paid to the cultural and socioeconomic factors that influence access to spatially-rich educational experiences, as highlighted in the work of Gutierrez and Ramos. By incorporating these perspectives, the review could provide a more nuanced understanding of the complex relationships between spatial reasoning, embodiment, and math learning."
