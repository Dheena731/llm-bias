LIBRARY OPENING HOURS

All patrons are advised of the following operating schedule for the Main Public Library:

Monday through Friday: 9:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.
Saturday: 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
Sunday: Closed

Please note that the library will be closed on all statutory holidays. Occasional schedule adjustments may be made for maintenance or special events. For the most up-to-date information, please visit the library website or contact the front desk.

Thank you for supporting our community services.
