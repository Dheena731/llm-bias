Subject: New Monthly Spending Summary Now Available

We are pleased to introduce the new Monthly Spending Summary. This feature offers a detailed breakdown of your expenses, categorizing spending to help you track financial habits and identify trends.

The summary is now accessible in the Reports section of your account. It updates automatically at the end of each month, providing a clear overview to support better budget management.

Please review your latest summary to gain insights into your spending patterns. For assistance with navigation, refer to the user guide.
