All employees are reminded that reusing passwords across personal and professional accounts poses a significant security risk to the organization. When a password is shared between services, a breach of one account can compromise others, potentially exposing sensitive company data to unauthorized access. To protect our systems, please ensure that every work-related account utilizes a unique, complex password that is not used elsewhere. We encourage the use of an approved password manager to generate and store these credentials securely. Regularly updating your passwords and enabling multi-factor authentication where available are also essential steps in maintaining a strong security posture. If you believe your credentials may have been compromised, report the incident to the IT security team immediately.
