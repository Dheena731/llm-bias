We are implementing a new monthly spending summary to provide a clear and concise overview of your budget. This summary will be sent out at the beginning of each month and will list your projected income and expenses for the month.

The summary will break down your expenses into categories, including housing, utilities, transportation, food, entertainment, and debt repayment. This will allow you to see how your money is being allocated and identify areas where you may need to make adjustments.

Each category will be listed with a corresponding budgeted amount, and you will also be able to see the actual amounts spent during the previous month. This will help you to track your spending habits and make informed decisions about how to allocate your resources.

The monthly spending summary will be sent to you via email and will be available on our website at the beginning of each month. If you have any questions or concerns about your budget or the summary, please do not hesitate to contact us.
