When preparing a quick weeknight dinner, try using a one-pan chicken and vegetable dish. Start by slicing some bell peppers, onions, and zucchini into uniform pieces for even cooking. Season the chicken with a combination of garlic powder, paprika, and salt. In a large oven-safe skillet, brown the chicken on both sides over medium heat. Then, add the vegetables and a splash of chicken broth. Cover the pan with aluminum foil and bake in a preheated oven at 375°F (190°C) for about 25 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the vegetables are tender. This method not only saves time but also keeps the kitchen clean since you’re essentially using just one pan.
