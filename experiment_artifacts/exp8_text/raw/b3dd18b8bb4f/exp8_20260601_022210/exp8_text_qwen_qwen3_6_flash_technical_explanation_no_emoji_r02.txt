Latency, the delay between a user action and the application's response, is a fundamental factor in web application performance. High latency degrades user experience by creating friction; even minor delays can increase bounce rates, reduce engagement, and drive users to competitors.

From a business perspective, speed directly influences revenue. Research consistently shows that increased load times correlate with lower conversion rates and lost sales. Additionally, search engines prioritize fast-loading sites, meaning high latency can negatively impact SEO and organic traffic.

Technically, excessive latency can strain server resources, lead to connection timeouts, and cause cascading failures under heavy load. Maintaining low latency ensures a responsive interface, optimizes conversion metrics, improves search visibility, and supports system stability.
