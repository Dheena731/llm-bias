**SECURITY NOTICE: PASSWORD REUSE POLICY**

**Date:** October 26, 2023
**To:** All Employees
**From:** Information Security Team
**Subject:** Requirement for Unique Passwords

To safeguard organizational assets, all staff must adhere to the following password standards effective immediately:

1. **No Password Reuse:** You must never use the same password for a corporate account that is also used on a personal email, social media, or third-party service. A compromise of a personal account can lead to unauthorized access to company systems via credential stuffing attacks.

2. **Unique Credentials:** Each work account must have a distinct, complex password. Do not reuse passwords across different company applications.

3. **Password Management:** Employees are required to use the approved enterprise password manager to store and generate credentials securely.

4. **Reporting:** If you suspect any account compromise, or if you have used the same password for work and personal accounts in the past, submit a ticket to the IT Help Desk immediately for remediation.

Compliance with this policy is mandatory. Failure to adhere to these requirements may result in disciplinary action. Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining our security posture.

**IT Security Operations**
