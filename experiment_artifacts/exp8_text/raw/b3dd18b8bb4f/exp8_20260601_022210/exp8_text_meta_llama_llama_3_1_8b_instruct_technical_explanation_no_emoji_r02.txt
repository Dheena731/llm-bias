Latency in web applications refers to the delay between a user's action and the time it takes for the application to respond. This delay can be frustrating for users and can have a significant impact on their overall experience.

There are several reasons why latency matters in web applications:

*   **User experience**: High latency can cause users to feel like the application is unresponsive or slow, leading to frustration and potentially even abandonment.
*   **Conversion rates**: Studies have shown that even small increases in latency can lead to significant decreases in conversion rates, as users are less likely to complete a task if it takes too long to load or respond.
*   **Search engine optimization (SEO)**: Search engines like Google take into account the user experience of a website when ranking its search results. High latency can negatively impact a website's SEO, making it less likely to appear at the top of search results.
*   **Revenue**: In e-commerce applications, high latency can lead to lost sales and revenue, as users are less likely to complete a purchase if the application is slow to respond.

Overall, reducing latency in web applications is crucial for providing a fast, responsive, and engaging user experience.
