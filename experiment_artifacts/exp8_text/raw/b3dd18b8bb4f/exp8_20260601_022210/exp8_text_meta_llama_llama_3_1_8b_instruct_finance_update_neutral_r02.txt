**Introducing Our New Monthly Spending Summary**

As part of our ongoing efforts to provide you with clear and transparent financial information, we are introducing a new monthly spending summary. This summary will provide a detailed breakdown of your monthly expenses, including income, fixed costs, variable expenses, and savings.

The new summary will be sent to you at the end of each month, and will include the following information:

* A summary of your income for the month
* A breakdown of your fixed costs, such as rent/mortgage, utilities, and minimum debt payments
* A detailed list of your variable expenses, including groceries, entertainment, and travel
* Your total savings for the month
* A comparison of your spending to your budgeted amounts

This new summary will help you stay on top of your finances and make informed decisions about your spending. If you have any questions or concerns about the new summary, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.

We hope this new tool will help you achieve your financial goals and take control of your finances.
