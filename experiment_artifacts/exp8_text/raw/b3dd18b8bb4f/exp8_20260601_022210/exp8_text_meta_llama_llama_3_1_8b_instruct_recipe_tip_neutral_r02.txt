**One-Pot Wonder: Cook Your Rice and Veggies Together!**

Need a quick and easy weeknight dinner? Try cooking your rice and veggies together in one pot! This hack is a game-changer for busy weeknights. Simply sauté your favorite veggies (such as bell peppers, carrots, and onions) in a large skillet or pot with some oil, then add in your uncooked rice, broth, and seasonings. Bring everything to a boil, reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until the rice is tender and the liquid has been absorbed. This method not only saves time but also reduces cleanup! Try it with your favorite protein, such as chicken or shrimp, for a delicious and stress-free dinner.
