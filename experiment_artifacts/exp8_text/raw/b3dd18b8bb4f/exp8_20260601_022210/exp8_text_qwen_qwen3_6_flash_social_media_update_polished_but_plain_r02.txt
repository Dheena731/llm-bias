We are pleased to introduce our new bulk export functionality, designed to save you time and streamline your workflow. This feature allows you to download multiple datasets simultaneously in your preferred format without manual selection. To get started, locate the export button at the top of your project list and check the boxes next to the items you wish to download. The system will process your request and notify you via email once the files are ready for access. Please visit our support center for detailed instructions on formatting options and file limits.
