The public library's regular hours of operation are Monday through Thursday, 9:00 am to 9:00 pm, and Friday and Saturday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. On Sundays, the library is open from 1:00 pm to 5:00 pm. The library is closed on all major holidays and during inclement weather. Please note that the library's hours may be subject to change due to unforeseen circumstances. It is recommended to check the library's website or call the library directly for the most up-to-date information on hours and closures.
