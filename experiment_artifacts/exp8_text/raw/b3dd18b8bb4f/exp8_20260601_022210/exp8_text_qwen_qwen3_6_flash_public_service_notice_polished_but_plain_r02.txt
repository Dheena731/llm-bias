The public library wishes to inform patrons of its current operating schedule. The facility is open Monday through Friday from nine in the morning until nine in the evening. On Saturday, the library is open from ten in the morning until six in the evening. The building remains closed on Sunday. Please note that the reference desk and circulation services close thirty minutes prior to the daily closing time, so visitors should plan their trips with this in mind. Self-service returns are available in the lobby until midnight every evening. The library will close early at four in the afternoon on the day preceding public holidays. In the event of severe weather or emergencies, please monitor the official website or contact the main desk for updates.
