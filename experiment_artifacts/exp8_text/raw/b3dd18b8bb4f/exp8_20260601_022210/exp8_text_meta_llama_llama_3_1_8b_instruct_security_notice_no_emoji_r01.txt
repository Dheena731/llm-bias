**Important Security Notice**

To ensure the security and integrity of our company's systems and data, we are reminding all employees of the importance of using unique and complex passwords.

**Password Reuse Policy**

As part of our ongoing effort to protect against cyber threats, we are strictly enforcing a no-password-reuse policy. This means that you should never use the same password for multiple accounts, including your company email, network login, or any other sensitive systems.

**Why Password Reuse is a Risk**

Reusing passwords can put your company's data and systems at risk of unauthorized access. If a hacker gains access to one of your accounts, they may also be able to access other accounts that use the same password.

**Best Practices for Password Security**

* Use a unique password for each account
* Choose complex passwords that are at least 12 characters long and include a mix of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters
* Avoid using easily guessable information, such as your name, birthdate, or common words
* Consider using a password manager to generate and store unique, complex passwords

**What to Do If You Have Used the Same Password**

If you have used the same password for multiple accounts, please change it immediately to a unique, complex password. If you need assistance with password management or have questions about this policy, please contact the IT department.

Thank you for your cooperation and help in keeping our company's systems and data secure.
