Here's a concise update on our new monthly spending summary feature:

**Introducing Monthly Insights**

We've enhanced our expense tracking to provide a clearer picture of your financial activity. Our new monthly spending summary groups your transactions into categories, such as rent, groceries, and transportation, making it easier to identify trends and areas for improvement. You'll also see a breakdown of your income and total expenses, as well as a comparison to the previous month.

This feature is designed to help you refine your budget and make more informed decisions about your money. You can access your monthly spending summary directly from your account dashboard, and we'll also send you a summary email at the start of each month.
