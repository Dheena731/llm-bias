We have introduced a new monthly spending summary to help you track and manage your expenses more effectively. This summary provides a clear breakdown of your spending across different categories, allowing you to easily identify areas where you can cut back and make adjustments to stay within your budget. The summary will be sent to you at the beginning of each month and will include information on your total spending, average daily spending, and the categories where you spent the most. This new feature is designed to give you a better understanding of your financial habits and help you make more informed decisions about your money.
