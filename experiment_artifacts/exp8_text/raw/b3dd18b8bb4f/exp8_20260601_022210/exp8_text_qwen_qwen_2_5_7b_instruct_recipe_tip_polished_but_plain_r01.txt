When preparing a weeknight dinner, choose ingredients that can be cooked quickly and efficiently. Opt for proteins like chicken breasts or fish fillets which can be baked or pan-seared in a few minutes. Have all your ingredients prepped and ready to go before you start cooking to save time. Using one-pot or sheet-pan meals can also simplify the process, allowing multiple components to cook simultaneously. Add fresh vegetables towards the end of cooking to preserve their crispness and nutrients. Keeping a well-stocked pantry of basic staples such as canned beans, pasta, and frozen vegetables ensures you can whip up a delicious meal even on the busiest nights.
