### 💡 Tips for sending:
*   **Add a visual:** If possible, include a screenshot or a GIF of the summary in action.
*   **Highlight the "Why":** Make sure the recipient knows how this benefits them (e.g., saves time, reduces errors, provides clarity).
*   **Call to Action:** Always include a direct link or button to the summary to drive engagement.
