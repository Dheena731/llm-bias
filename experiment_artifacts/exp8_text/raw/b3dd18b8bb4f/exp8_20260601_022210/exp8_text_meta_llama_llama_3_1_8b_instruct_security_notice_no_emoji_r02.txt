**Important Security Notice**

As part of our ongoing efforts to protect company data and systems, we are reminding all employees of the importance of maintaining strong and unique passwords.

Reusing passwords across multiple accounts and systems is a significant security risk. If a single password is compromised, it can lead to unauthorized access to sensitive company data and systems.

To help prevent this, we are enforcing a policy of no password reuse. This means that each password should be unique and not used for any other company account or system.

Please take a moment to review your password usage and ensure that you are not reusing passwords. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the IT department.

Thank you for your cooperation in maintaining the security of our company's data and systems.
