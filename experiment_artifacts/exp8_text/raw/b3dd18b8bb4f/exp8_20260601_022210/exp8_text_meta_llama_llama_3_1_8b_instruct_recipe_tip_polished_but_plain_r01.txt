When preparing a weeknight dinner, consider the concept of mise en place. This French culinary term translates to "everything in its place," and it can greatly streamline the cooking process. By having all the ingredients and tools you need within arm's reach, you can quickly and efficiently chop, sauté, and season your way to a delicious meal. Take a few minutes to prep your ingredients before starting to cook, and you'll find that the rest of the process is much more manageable.
