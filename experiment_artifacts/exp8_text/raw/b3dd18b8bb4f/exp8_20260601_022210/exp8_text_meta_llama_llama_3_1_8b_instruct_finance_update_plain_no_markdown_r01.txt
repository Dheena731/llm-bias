We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you stay on top of your finances. This summary will provide a detailed breakdown of all your expenses for the month, including categories such as housing, transportation, groceries, and entertainment. It will also include a total of all your expenses, as well as a comparison to your budgeted amounts.

The summary will be sent to you on the first day of every month, and will cover the previous month's expenses. This will give you a clear picture of where your money is going and help you identify areas where you can cut back and save. We hope this new summary will be a valuable tool in helping you manage your finances effectively.
