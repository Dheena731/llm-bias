Here is a short workplace security notice about password reuse:

**Important Security Reminder**

**Password Reuse: Protect Your Account and Our Network**

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain a secure work environment, we want to remind everyone of the importance of using unique and complex passwords for all company accounts and systems.

**Why is password reuse a security risk?**

* If you use the same password for multiple accounts, a single compromised password can give hackers access to multiple accounts and sensitive information.
* Password reuse can also increase the risk of a "password dump" attack, where hackers obtain a large list of reused passwords and use them to gain unauthorized access to multiple accounts.

**Best Practices for Password Security**

* Use a unique password for each account and system.
* Avoid using easily guessable information such as your name, birthdate, or common words.
* Use a combination of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
* Consider using a password manager to generate and store complex, unique passwords.

**What to Do if You've Reused a Password**

If you suspect that you've reused a password or have concerns about your password security, please contact our IT department immediately. We can help you update your passwords and ensure that your accounts are secure.

Thank you for your cooperation and attention to this important security matter.
