**One-Pot Pasta**: Choose a hearty pasta like penne or rigatoni and cook it in a large pot with plenty of water. Simultaneously, sauté your favorite vegetables and protein (like chicken, beef, or vegetables) in a bit of olive oil. Once the pasta is cooked, add the sautéed ingredients to the pot and mix everything together. Add some pasta sauce or a splash of tomato paste for flavor. This method not only saves time but also makes a dish that's full of flavor and easy to prepare. Enjoy your quick, comforting meal!
