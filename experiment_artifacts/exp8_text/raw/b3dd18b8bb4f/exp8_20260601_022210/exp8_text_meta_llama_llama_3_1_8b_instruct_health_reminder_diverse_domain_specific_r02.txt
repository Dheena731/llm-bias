Here's a short reminder that's specific to the domain of fitness and tracking progress:

**Don't Let Progress Slip Through the Cracks**

Take a few minutes to review your weekly fitness progress. Check your log or app to see:

* How many workouts you completed
* Which exercises you nailed (and which ones need more practice)
* Any noticeable changes in your body composition or performance metrics
* If you're on track to meet your goals for the week

Reflecting on your progress helps you stay motivated, identify areas for improvement, and make adjustments to optimize your routine. Use this opportunity to fine-tune your approach and get back on track.
