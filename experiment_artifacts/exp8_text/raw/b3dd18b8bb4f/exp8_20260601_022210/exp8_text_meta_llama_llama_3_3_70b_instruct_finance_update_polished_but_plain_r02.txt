We are introducing a new monthly spending summary to help you keep track of your expenses. This summary will provide a clear overview of your spending habits, making it easier to identify areas where you can cut back and make adjustments to your budget. The summary will be sent to you at the beginning of each month and will include a breakdown of your total spending, as well as a categorization of your expenses by type. This will allow you to see exactly where your money is going and make informed decisions about how to allocate your funds in the future. We hope this new feature will be a useful tool in helping you manage your finances and achieve your financial goals.
