When prepping a weeknight dinner, consider the 'mise en place' approach. This French culinary term refers to having all your ingredients measured, chopped, and ready to go before starting to cook. For a dish like chicken fajitas, take a few minutes to slice the onions and bell peppers, marinate the chicken, and warm the tortillas. This strategic prep work will streamline your cooking process, reducing stress and allowing you to focus on searing the chicken and sautéing the vegetables to perfection. By doing so, you'll be able to get a flavorful and satisfying meal on the table in about 30 minutes, making it an ideal solution for busy weeknights.
